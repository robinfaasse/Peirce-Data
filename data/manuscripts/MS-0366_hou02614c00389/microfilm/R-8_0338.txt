inquiry, and if belief is of the nature of a habit,
why should we not attain the desired end, by taking
any answer to a question which we may fancy, and
constantly reiterating it to ourselves, by dwelling on
all which may conduce to that belief and learning
to turn with contempt and hatred from anything
