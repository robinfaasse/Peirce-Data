4. A single [u]e[/u] ending a [del]syllabled[/del] word, or a constituent
of a compound word, or the stem of a word, is not sounded
if the word can be sounded without it. It must be sounded
at the end of words which would otherwise be without a
vowel.

If follows that in order to spell a long vowel not
ending a syllable, we must either add an [u]e[/u] so that it

add a vowel so as to make a diphthong or double vowel,
