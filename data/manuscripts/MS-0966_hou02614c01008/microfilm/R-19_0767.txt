[pn]Ms 964[/pn]

Take 5 points $X, Y, Z, U, V$. These
lie in one 3-dimensional space. [del]Hence,[/del]
We express this by writing
$$AX + BY + CZ + DU + EV = 0$$
$$A + B + C + D + E = 0$$
No four of them are in one plane; so
that $ABCDE \neq 0$.

Let $W$ be collinear with $X$ and $Y$, and
coplanar with $Z, U, V$. Then
$$AX + BY + (C+D+E)W = 0$$
$$CZ + DU + EV + (A+B)W = 0$$

[del]Write[/del] $AX = x$ $BY = y$ $CZ = z$ $DU = u$ $EV = v$
Then if we write $(C+D+E)W = w$, we have
$$w = -(x+y)$$
and similarly we shall have in all 10 points besides
the first five $-(x+y), -(x+z)$, etc.
