[u]Remarkable points of a triangle[/u], certain points having
remarkable relations to the triangle, viz.:— (1) the
centroid, or intersection of lines through the angles
bisecting the opposite sides, (2) the orthocentre, or
intersection of perpendiculars from the angles upon
the opposite sides, (3) the circumcentre, or centre of the
circumscribed circle, (4) the centre of the ninepoint
circle, (5) the incentre, or centre of the inscribed circle,
(6) the radical centre of the escribed circles, (7) the point
the sum of whose distances from the sides is a mini-
mum, (8) the Grebe, or Lemoine, point, the sum of the
squares of whose distances from the sides is a
minimum
