[u]Introduction[/u]. [del]What awfully nice times[/del]
[del]to open[/del]. Beautiful oysters revealing
remembrances of wealth. Alas!
Reasonable endeavours very often
let vice establish ruin. Anger never
does. Cries and reproaches turn
rapidly into dangerous gifts easily
sent. Whirlwinds in the heavens
open up thoughts. Drat every
lesson at yuletide! Hardihood
and valour injure no girls. Rather
every autumn season overthrows
[del]many[/del] plums. Try often.
Think how invariably nobody knows.
Think how angels think! Behold yon-
der! Behold Esau in Noah's garden! Knaves
never own weakness. Talk openly. Have all
voyages ended? Overtures never end.
