CLARK UNIVERSITY,
WORCESTER, MASS.

[date]July 12. 1890.[/date]

Mr C. S. Peirce,
My dear Sir,
I have hoped each meeting I
might reply to your note by a definite
'yes' or 'no', but I find to my regret
that our Board were unable to consider
the matter at their last meeting for
want of time. I suppose now it will
have to go over till September.
Outside lecturing has never yet been
adopted by them so that I think the
large general question will be discussed
&, as I wrote you first, I fear the chances
are a little against their appropriating
much money just now in so early a
stage of our development. Such a course as
you outline however would interest & stim-
ulate every man on the grounds in a most
admirable way.
I am,
Most Sincerely Yours,
G Stanley Hall
