[date]Jan 28 - 93[/date]

Mr Charles S. Peirce,

My dear Sir:

I am drawing up a somewhat extended article which attempts to set forth without criticism or comment, but in the briefest & most concise way possible, a history of Psychology in this country, including everything of importance published down to at least [date]Jan 1 - 1893[/date]. Psychology to be interpreted in the largest possible sense, to include Logic, Aesthetics, History of Philosophy, Metaphysics, etc.

My plan is to say A. B. has published so and so - naming titles, place of publication, number of pages, etc. of all works & then to give the briefest possible digest. If you could kindly assist me:
[u]I[/u] By giving me titles of your own works.
[u]II[/u] By sending to me, either to be retained or as a loan, a copy of all your publications not readily accessible - not purchasable or in the usual files of journals.
[u]III[/u] By giving the briefest possible digests of different works or articles yourself (selbstanzeigen) it would be a very material aid in this work.

Any suggestion of names, topics, or anything else would be valuable.

Very Truly Yours,
G Stanley Hall
