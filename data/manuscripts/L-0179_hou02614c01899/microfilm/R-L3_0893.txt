CLARK UNIVERSITY,
WORCESTER, MASS.

PRESIDENT'S OFFICE

[date]Feb. 27-00.[/date]

My dear Mr. Peirce,

Your favorable impressions of my work give me rare pleasure, indeed there are few people in the country or world whose good opinion on such matters I value so highly. We have had a hard struggle here with our limitations severe in the way of money, but hope the day of deliverance is at hand.

If Garrison does not accept your notice possibly you might get it into Science, or the Popular Science Monthly might like it?
