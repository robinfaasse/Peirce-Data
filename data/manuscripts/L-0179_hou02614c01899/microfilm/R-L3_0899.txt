CLARK UNIVERSITY,
WORCESTER, MASS.

[date]July 23-00.[/date]

My dear Professor Peirce,

In preparing a lot of Dr. Hall's papers for the binder I have come across a few parts of your Logic of Science & beg to ask if the work has yet appeared in book form, or, if you can supply the parts that have been printed?

With high esteem, I am,
Most Truly Yours,
Louis N. Wilson
Secretary.
