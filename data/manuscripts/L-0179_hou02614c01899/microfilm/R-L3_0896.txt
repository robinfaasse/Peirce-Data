CLARK UNIVERSITY,
WORCESTER, MASS.

PRESIDENT'S OFFICE

April 25, 1900.

My dear Professor Peirce,

I am very much pleased with your notice in Science and am also glad to have the galley as we keep a University scrap book.

I am very sorry to observe all these misprints and I think we shall correct our edition that has not gone out yet. I was much pleased with your review of Royce.

I am writing you under great pressure of time and so with most cordial greetings, believe me

Very truly yours,

G Stanley Hall
