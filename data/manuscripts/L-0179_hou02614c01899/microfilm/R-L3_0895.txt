CLARK UNIVERSITY,
WORCESTER, MASS.

PRESIDENT'S OFFICE

[date]Mch 19-00.[/date]

My dear Professor Peirce,

I think there is nothing I would like to have said that you do not appear to have well in mind. We have aimed high at some cost of numbers & of local - yes, general - appreciation. Indeed I should not object to your saying what you say you think but do not intend to write, that if no millionaire will help us we may get along awhile on a subscription.

Our Founder is at the point of death & this is our most critical moment.

I am glad Garrison will even take an abridgement - that is something.

I am, most cordially yrs,

G. Stanley Hall
