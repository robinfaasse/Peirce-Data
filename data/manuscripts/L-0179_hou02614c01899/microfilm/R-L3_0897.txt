CLARK UNIVERSITY,
WORCESTER, MASS.

PRESIDENT'S OFFICE

[date]June 1, 1900.[/date]

My dear Professor Peirce,

You certainly do not overestimate my high appreciation of the value of your own work or of the very great interest I have always felt in everything you write and of your power to inspire students. At present we do not know the contents of Mr. Clark's will and when it is made public, there are understood to be complications which may take a year to so clear up that we shall be able to use anything even if we have it. When that time comes and if all is favorable, there will probably be a very careful revision of the whole situation first and then we shall be in a position to say something definite.
