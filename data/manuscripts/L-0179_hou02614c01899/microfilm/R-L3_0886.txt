CLARK UNIVERSITY,
WORCESTER, MASS.

[date]Mch 17. 1890[/date]

My dear Professor Peirce,

In reply to your note I am very sorry to have to beg off if it is wanted within the next month or two. I am just out from several weeks of sharp illness with diptheria & all the accumulated matters of this busy time of year loom up before me & will fill every moment of time & absorb all my strength for the immediate present.

The paper you send is at first hasty reading full of interest and suggestion to me, as indeed everything you write has always been.

If the spirit should move & time should make it possible I will surely do better than I now think I shall be able.

I am, Most Truly Yours,
G. S. Hall,
