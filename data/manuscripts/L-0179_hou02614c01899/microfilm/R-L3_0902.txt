CLARK UNIVERSITY,
WORCESTER, MASS.

PRESIDENT'S OFFICE

[date]Jan. 29-01.[/date]

My dear Mr. Peirce,

The Trustees of the Wor. Polytechnic, with whom I am in conference, want a young man who is an engineer & I suppose much depends upon finding openings for the graduates. As connected with another institution, which some of them look upon as a rival, I have little influence, but have mentioned your name. I think, however, you would agree with me, if you knew the situation, that such a position is not the one you are best fitted to fill.

( I had the great pleasure last Sunday week of expressing to your brother at my house my own sense of the value of your work & what I owe to it. )

Ever, Faithfully Yours,
G Stanley Hall
