CLARK UNIVERSITY,
WORCESTER, MASS.

PRESIDENT'S OFFICE

[date]Sep. 1 - 00.[/date]

My dear Professor Peirce,

We only wanted to know if the old Popular Science Monthly papers were accessible in order to bind them for our library.

All your letters are preserved & have been replied to, so far as I know, but the one you refer to as "my last" cannot have been received as I do not at all understand your reference to it. I have only this morning returned from a month's absence & it is possible it may have miscarried in forwarding from one place to another.
