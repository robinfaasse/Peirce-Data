**Clark University,**
**Worcester, Mass.**

[date]Jan. 2 - 94.[/date]

My dear Mr Peirce,

I have read your prospectus with very great interest, as I have done everything you ever wrote. If you are able to carry out such a scheme as this it will be of great importance for both the philosophical work & representation in this country.

Of course, I subscribe, herewith, for one copy & wish I were able to do more to help & hasten the matter along.

Most Truly Yours,
G Stanley Hall
