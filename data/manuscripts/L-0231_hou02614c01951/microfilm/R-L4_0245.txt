Fig 7. asserts that [u]Some boy is industrious[/u]. By the 1st permission it can be changed
to Fig. 8, which asserts that [u]there is a boy and that there is an industrious person[/u].
This was asserted in Fig. 7, together with the identity of some case. Fig. 9. asserts
[u]either there is nothing known for certain or else there is no communication with
anybody[/u]. By the same permission this can be changed to Fig. 10 which asserts that
[u]no communication with anybody deceased is known for certain[/u]. But this is fully
included in the state of things asserted in Fig. 9.
