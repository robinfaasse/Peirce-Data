In continuation of the consideration of
Probable Induction, that a finite propor-
tion is true, of course, that a finite collec-
tion of an infinite collection is infinite.
But then, even if there be any collection
of single objects that is infinite, which is
open to question, it would seem presumable
that man can never have experience of
such a multitude. For no single man can
have distinct recognition of such a mul-
titude of objects; and all the men there ever
will be must be finite in number. An in-
finite [del]multitude[/del] collection must, there-
fore, it would seem, if we are to know it
be either a collection of [u]can-bes[/u] or else of
[u]would-bes[/u].
