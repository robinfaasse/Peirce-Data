$$(g-f)(y-f)(v-x) = 0$$
$$(g-f)(v-y)(v-x) = 0$$
Adding these two equations, we get
$$(g-f)(v-f)(v-x) = 0$$
But the middle factor, $v-f$, can-
not be zero, since $v$ and $f$ are dif-
ferent numbers. Consequently,
we have a right to divide by
this middle factor, and doing
so we get
$$(g-f)(v-x) = 0$$
which means, If $g$ is true, $x$ is true,
the correct conclusion from the
premises.
