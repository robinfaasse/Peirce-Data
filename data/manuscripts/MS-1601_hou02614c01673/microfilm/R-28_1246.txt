[u]Peirce.[/u]

Thomas Peirce was the man who brought our
family to these shores. We first hear of him, in
Charlestowne, where he was admitted to the
church in [del]1635[/del] [ins]1634[/ins], February 21, his wife Elizabeth
having been admitted January 10th of the same year.
He was made a freeman of the colony on the 6th of
following May. On September 27th, 1642, the Court
having ordered that Saltpeter heaps should be
made by all the farmers, 21 Commissioners were
appointed to oversee the work, one of whom was
Thomas Peirce. He died [date]1666 Oct 7[/date]. Here follows
his will.

Cambridge in New England [date]Nov. 7. 1665[/date]
I, Thomas Peirce of Charlestowne aged about 82
years, being throw the goodness of the Lord of sound
Judgement and memory and in some measure of
bodily health do ordain and hereby declare my
last will and testament in manner and form
