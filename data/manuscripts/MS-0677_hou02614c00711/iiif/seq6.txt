[fig]1[/fig] A SKETCH OF LOGICAL CRITICS.
PART II.
Thus far I have been loosely and rudely sewing together,—
basting together, to use the sewing-woman's word,— a few reflex-
[fig]punch hole[/fig] ions which seemed to me to be equally useful to those readers
who have never bestowed any thought at all upon reasoning in
its generality, and to those who, having themselves mastered the
subject, desire, before reading what I have to say about Logical
Critics, to ascertain where I stand among logicians. I am now
going to begin all over again, keeping this time more close to the
subject, but avoiding mere repetitions.
