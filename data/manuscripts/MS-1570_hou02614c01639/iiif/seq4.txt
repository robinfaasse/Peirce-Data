Your congregation come to church of a Sunday morning
actuated by one of the sublimest sentiments of which human
nature is the vehicle. There are a number of base sentiments
mixed up with that; and the people who have been all the
week obliged to study base sentiments, are far more con-
scious of these than they are of the one which is really the
more potent, but which lies too deep in their nature to
be remarked. They come to our church and they go to the
evangelical churches [del]under[/del] really under the influence of that
deep instinct. But the evangelical churches do not ask
