This part of the art of delivery shows what you must not
do. The other part [del]s[/del] shows what you must do. You wish
to carry a certain series of thoughts & feelings through the
minds of the audience. To do this you have to avail your-
self of the law of mind, the law of the association of ideas.
You start with a certain instinctive capacity for that. Every
man possesses it in some degree; and that natural capacity
you improve upon by the special study of the psychological
and physiological principles involved.

That gives you an idea of what a complete course should
be. It would consist of 36 lessons covering a little over
four months.
