MATHEMATICS.

PHILADELPHIA, [date]September
the 23rd 95.[/date]

Charles Sanders Peirce B.Sc. M.A.
Arisbe, Milford Pa.

Dear Sir,

I fear I might transgress the
limits of propriety by addressing you with
the following request. I should consider it
a great privilege and a personal favor
if you could find the time to review two
little books, which I send you under separate
cover, in the "Nation" or in any magazine
you might find proper.

By doing so you will greatly oblige
yours very truly
I. J. Schwatt.
