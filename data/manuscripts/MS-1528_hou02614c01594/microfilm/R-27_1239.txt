[u]Our Chess Corner.[/u]

Here is a very brilliant Giuoco Piano, played at
Hastings, [date]1895 Aug. 17[/date].

[u]White (Steinitz)[/u] [u]Black (von Bardeleben)[/u]
1. P - K4. P - K4.
2. Kt - KB3. Kt - QB3.
3. B - QB4. B - QB4.
4. P - QB3. Kt - KB3.
5. P - Q4. P x QP.
6. P x QP. B - QKt5 (ch).
7. Kt - QB3.
This is instead of [del]B[/del] B - Q2, the usual move. If
the Kt now takes KP, white will castle and will
probably gain a pawn. Black does not take the
pawn; but whether he does better may be doubted.
7. P - Q4.
8. P x P. KKt x P.
9. Castles. B - K3.
10. B - KKt5.
A deep calculation. To gain a small, but definite, ad-
vantage in position, while brings about a general
exchange.
B - K2.
11. B x Kt. B x KB.
12. Kt x B. Q x Kt.
13. B x B. Ktx B.
14. KR - K.
