is by no means the only [ins]transitive[/ins] relation. Examples of others
are 'being greater than' or 'less than' and it is clear
that the syllogism is equally valid for any kind
of transitive relation whatever so that it is the
same sort of reasoning to say A is greater than B.
B is greater than C therefore A is greater than C
as it is to say A is B B is C therefore A is C.
This was first pointed out by Mr De Morgan.
