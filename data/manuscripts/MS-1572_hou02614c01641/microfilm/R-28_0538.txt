[u]Lecture I[/u]

The purpose of this course is double.

First purpose, though last attained. To make a
scientific criticism of the agnostic and similar
philosophies of the last generation, and to reëxamine
the evidences of the first proposition of natural
religion.

In order to do this it is necessary to make a more
scientific examination of the nature of reasoning
than has ever been made; but in this course, the
demonstrations which are too difficult for such
lectures can only be outlined.

Second Purpose. To examine reasonings as above
described, and to make use of the results to criticize
some reasonings which pass as highly scientific, but
which will be shown to be mere expressions of
foregone prepossessions.
