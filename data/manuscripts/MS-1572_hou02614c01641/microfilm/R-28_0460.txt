The following depends on all three. If $(A \prec B) \prec B$ is scriptible, so is $(B \prec A) \prec A$. For by II either $A \prec B$ or $A$ is scriptible. If $A$, then by I so is $(B \prec A) \prec A$. If $A \prec B$ is scriptible and also $(A \prec B) \prec B$ then by III, $B$ is scriptible. If $B$ is scriptible and $A$ not, then by III $B \prec A$ is not scriptible and then by II $(B \prec A) \prec A$ is scriptible.
