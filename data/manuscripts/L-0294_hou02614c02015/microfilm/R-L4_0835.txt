My dear Mitchell O.H.

The notation of the logic of relatives can be somewhat simplified by spreading the formulae over two dimensions. For instance suppose we write

[fig]b and l connected vertically[/fig]

to express the proposition that something is at once benefactor and lover of something. That is,
$$\Sigma_x \Sigma_y b_{xy} l_{xy} > 0.$$

We can write

[del][fig]crossed out diagram[/fig][/del] [fig]b and l connected horizontally[/fig]

to mean that something is at once benefactor and loved of something, that is, something is benefactor of a lover of itself
$$\Sigma_x \Sigma_y b_{xy} l_{yx} > 0$$
