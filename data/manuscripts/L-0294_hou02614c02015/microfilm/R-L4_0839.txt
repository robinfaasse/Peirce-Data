[fig]two loops connected by a vertical line[/fig] we get first [fig]figure eight shape[/fig] and then by a twist [fig]two loops side by side connected by a curved line[/fig]
from which we get either [fig]two loops side by side connected by a line[/fig] or [fig]two loops side by side connected by a line with a crossbar[/fig].
