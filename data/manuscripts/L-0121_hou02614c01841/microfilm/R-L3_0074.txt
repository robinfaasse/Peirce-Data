[date]1893 Oct 30[/date]

The Hon. L. A. L. Peine Esq

My dear Sir:

The National Academy of Sciences meets in Albany on the 7th. I wish I could get some copies of my prospectus for it.

As I have not heard whether you received my last copy, I send another. [ins]I have made perhaps[/ins] as many abbreviations in this to not overdelay the printers for number of special types, but generally write what you have.

Very truly
C. S. Peirce
