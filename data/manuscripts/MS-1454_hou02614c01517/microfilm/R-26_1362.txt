He was so [del]fully[/del] occupied with his own view that he could not perceive there was any other. He felt himself to be in possession of [ins]the mastery of every arcanum of[/ins] [del]the key of[/del] philosophy, although he could not have distinctly and accurately formulated his idea; and he was so possessed with the impulse to apply that, that he could not stay to listen to another man's thought. This did not prevent him from deriving many an important conception from British thinkers; for to a mind like his a mere hint would be enough.
