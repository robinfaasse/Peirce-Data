Professor Mezes, of the University of Texas, is known to
the general public as a scholar of Howison and as one
the four authors of the symposiac book 'The Conception
of God.' He there gave us the impression of a man of some
power, but as an intellect of not quite such an edge as is
called for in philosophy; and when we compare the present
book with the great land-marks of ethics, the same impres-
sion is renewed.
