We have, hitherto, not crossed the threshold of scientific logic. It is cert-
it is certainly important to know how to make our ideas clear, but they may be ever so clear
without being true. How to make them so, we have next to study. How to give birth to those vital
and procreative ideas which multiply into a thousand forms and diffuse themselves everywhere, adva-
ncing civilization and making the dignity of man, is an art not yet reduced to rules, but of the
secret of which the history of science affords some hints.
