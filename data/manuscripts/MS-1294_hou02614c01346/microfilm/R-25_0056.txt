Everybody knows that Egyptian chronology
is extremely uncertain; and the reason is
that the Egyptians had no chronology in regular
use. They sometimes drew up lists of kings;
but the keeping of time was not regarded by
them as of any particular importance! What
sort of mind does that bespeak? And yet
they always, in the "New" empire, at least, are
fully impressed with the superiority of their
ancestors to themselves! Have those who call the
Egyptians an intellectual people ever put such
facts as these together?

"Ah, but exclaims the egyptologist, you
must remember they lived before the days of
such notions." That is true; they seem to have
lived before the days of notions, generally.

Egyptian chronology is highly uncertain. It
is pretty well agreed, on all hands, that the "New"
Empire, began (with the XVIIIth dynasty) about 1650 B.C.
