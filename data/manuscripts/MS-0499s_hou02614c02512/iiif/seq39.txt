There is, moreover, a conclusive reason
for the precedence of distinctions of
Form over distinctions of Matter in
all natural [del]of[/del] and rational classi-
fications. It is that Form is something
that the mind can assimilate and
comprehend, while Matter is always
foreign [del]to it[/del], as recognizable but
incomprehensible something. Now
natural classifications are intended
to make the whole classified collection
comprehensible.
It follows then that if there are any
indecomposable constituents, or elements,
of the phaneron, their highest classi-
fication, — like that of the chemical
elements, — must be according to
their valencies.
