There is no apparent reason why
there should not be univalent or monadic
Elements of the Phaneron. We should
expect them to be present in enormous
number. Each of these will likewise be
"absolute", but not in the sense in which
'die alle Wahrheit' is [del]so[/del], but only in the
sense that it is whatever it is regardless
of anything else. [del]As such[/del] [ins]Anything of which this is[/ins]
