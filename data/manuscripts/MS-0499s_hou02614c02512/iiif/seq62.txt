Whether we think of the will
as a cause or a force or a
function, we must assume
a self or person or ego or
monad or subject as the
ground out of which the will
proceeds. We cannot conceive
a will which is not somebody's
will. There is no such thing
as an abstract will existing
by itself. In the human
spirit, the will is always
dependent on the other functions
of consciousness. It is always
the result of intelligence
and feeling.
