$\frac{l}{a} + \frac{h}{b} + \frac{k}{c} = 1$   $\left. \begin{matrix} \frac{hl}{a} + \frac{h^2}{b} + \frac{hk}{c} = h \\ \frac{k^2}{a} + \frac{kl}{b} + \frac{hk}{c} = k \end{matrix} \right\}$
