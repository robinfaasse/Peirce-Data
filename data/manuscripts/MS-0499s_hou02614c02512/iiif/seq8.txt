[del]In that hour there was nothing but our love and the great
white moon and the blue-black star-filled night. We were
the only living creatures left in the world. And so we
lay and whispered to each other while the world was
new and we were the children of the morning.[/del]

[del]She was mine and I was hers and there was nothing
but love and the night and the stars.[/del]

[del]Ah, but it is better that you should go, oh, my beloved
for you are not meant for this world. It is better that you
should return to the stars and the moon and the night
than that you should stay and be soiled by the earth.
For you are a star and a moonbeam and a breath of
the night wind and you were not meant for the day.[/del]

[del]Go then, oh, my beloved, go and be happy among
your brothers and sisters the stars. And sometimes,
when the night is very still and the moon is white
and the stars are very bright, look down on this old
earth and think of me. For I shall be waiting for
you and thinking of you and loving you, always,
until the end of time.[/del]
