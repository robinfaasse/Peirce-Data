If [ins]$\frac{d\rho}{S} = \frac{dr}{T}$[/ins]
$\log g + P = \log r$

$(\frac{T}{R})^2 + (\frac{2Z-z}{Z})^2 = 1$

[del]$1 - (\frac{T}{R})^2 = (\frac{2Z-z}{Z})^2$[/del]  $(1 - \frac{z}{Z})^2 = 1 - (\frac{T}{R})^2$

$\frac{2Z-z}{Z} = \sqrt{1 - (\frac{T}{R})^2}$  $1 - \frac{z}{Z} = \sqrt{1 - (\frac{T}{R})^2}$

Is this $\frac{d\rho}{g} = \frac{dr}{r}$ satisfied by

$z = Z - \frac{Z}{R} \sqrt{R^2 - r^2} = Z (1 - \sqrt{1 - (\frac{r}{R})^2})$

and $g = r - \frac{dr}{dz} z + \sqrt{z^2 + 1 + (\frac{dr}{dz})^2 z^2}$

$\frac{dz}{dr} = + \frac{1}{2} \frac{2r}{R \sqrt{R^2 - r^2}} = \frac{r}{R} \frac{\frac{r}{R}}{\sqrt{1 - (\frac{r}{R})^2}}$

$\frac{dr}{dz} = \frac{1}{2} \frac{R}{Z} \frac{\sqrt{1 - (r/R)^2}}{r/R}$

$\frac{dr}{dz} z = \frac{1}{2} R \frac{\sqrt{1 - (r/R)^2}}{r/R} - \frac{1}{2} R \frac{1 - (r/R)^2}{r/R}$

$\sqrt{1 + (\frac{dr}{dz})^2} = \sqrt{1 + \frac{1}{4} (\frac{R}{Z})^2 \frac{1 - (r/R)^2}{(r/R)^2}}$

$z \sqrt{1 + (\frac{dr}{dz})^2} = Z - $

$1 + \frac{1}{4} \frac{R^2}{Z^2}$
