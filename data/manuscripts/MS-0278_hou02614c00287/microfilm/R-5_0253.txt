The prevalent idea is that finite numbers are
the only ones that we can reason about, as Boole, in
any ordinary mode of reasoning. But I long ago
showed that finite collections are distinguished from
infinite ones only by the applicability to the former
of the very peculiar and rare mode of inference called
by its discoverer, De Morgan, "syllogism of transposed
quantity." The following is an example of this form
of argument:

[del]Every Hottentot kills a Hottentot;
But no Hottentot is killed by more than one person:
Hence, every Hottentot is killed by a Hottentot.
if the premises are true of any finite collection[/del]
