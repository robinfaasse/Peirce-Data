There are [del]two[/del] points where ball, putty, air, and water join.

Two rigid things, irregularly enough shaped, can be made to touch in six points. Bend or twist a door key so as to remove all symmetry of shape. Bring it down into a box until it touches at one point. Roll it over that point till it touches at another place. Roll it over the two till it touches at a third. Move it up till it touches the end of the box. Rotate it till it touches at a fifth point. Then it can still be twisted so as to touch at a sixth point. [ins]Two[/ins] Bodies cannot touch one another at more than 6 points unless they are fitted to do so.
