[u]Theorem[/u]. [u]Addition is a commutative mode of combination[/u].

[u]Demonstration[/u]. Let X and Y be any numbers whatever. Then, I say that X + Y = Y + X.

For in the definition of addition there is nothing to distinguish one term from the other.
