Given two lapses of time, A and B, of which B is subsequent to A, [del]but[/del] while there is no lapse of time at once subsequent to A and precedent to B, then there is something called an [u]instant[/u] subsequent to A and precedent to B. This instant is said to be contained in the lapse of time that contains both A and B and that contains no lapse of time having no lapse in common with either A or B. This instant may be said to belong to A and to B. Between any two distinct instants there is a lapse.
