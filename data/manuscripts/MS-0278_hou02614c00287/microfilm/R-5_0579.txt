graphics are skipped. A few paragraphs will give a slight
resumé of their most fundamental propositions.

(a) Topology treats of the connections of places. For that purpose
it studies the modes of displacement of bodies which are
flexible, plastic, or fluid, but which are supposed to be so
cohesive as not ordinarily to be capable of being broken.
