TABLE OF THE "HEADINGS" OF THE NUMBERS

The heading of any whole number, $m$, is $\nu$, the least [ins]positive[/ins] integer
such that $(2^{\nu}-1)/(2m+1)$ is a positive whole number; which, since I shall
employ the secunda[del]l[/del], instead of the decimal, system of numerical notation,
(writing $1, 10, 11, 100, 101, 110, 111, 1000, 1001, 1010$, etc for
$2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10$, etc.), I shall express by saying that $\nu$ has
such value as to [del]make[/del] [ins]give[/ins] $(10^{\nu}-1)/(10m+1)$ the least positive integral
value for each integral value of $m$ that is not negative.
