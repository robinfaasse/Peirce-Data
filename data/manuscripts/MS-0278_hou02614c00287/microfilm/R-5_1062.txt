[del]made[/del] They [del]copy[/del] [ins]adopted[/ins] the aristocratic party.
They rigorously maintained the exact doctrine
of their master, whom they regarded as super-
human, [del]dividing[/del] [ins]and silenced all opposition[/ins]
by the dictum αὐτός ἔφα, "himself has said it."
But the doctrine was kept profoundly secret
and the least divulgation was terribly punished.
In order to keep it secret, each doctrine received
from Pythagoras himself a symbolical or
emblematic expression, which could be used
mnemonically.
