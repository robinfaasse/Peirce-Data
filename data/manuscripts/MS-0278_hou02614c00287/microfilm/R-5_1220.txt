0 gives Sunday
1 " Monday
2 " Tuesday
3 gives Wednesday
4 " Thursday
5 " Friday
6 gives Saturday.

<u>Example.</u> To find the day of the week on which 25 December 1894 will fall:
Century Index 2
Year Index 1
Month Index 2
Day Index 25 (or 4, deducting 21)
30 rem. 2
Hence the day will be Tuesday.

With a little practice, all this computation can easily be performed mentally.

<u>Note.</u> The Gregorian Calendar, or New Style, was introduced by Pope Gregory XIII. on Friday, 5 October 1582 (O.S.), which became 15 October 1582 (N.S.). It was adopted by Great Britain Saturday, 3 September 1752 (O.S.) = 14 September 1752 (N.S.). The Julian Calendar, or Old Style, is still used in Russia and by the Greek Church. The difference of the two calendars is now twelve days.
