SIR:

A SPECIAL MEETING will be held at the House of the Academy, No. 28 Newbury Street, on Monday, the 24th of February, at eight-thirty o'clock P.M.

WILLIAM WATSON,

Boston, February 15th, 1913.

COMMUNICATIONS.

Professor Barrett Wendell will give a brief account of the College de France.

M. Henri Bergson, Professor of Modern Philosophy in the Collège de France, will address the Academy.

After the adjournment of the meeting an informal reception will be held in honor of Professor Bergson.

No business is to be transacted at this meeting.

Each Fellow may invite a lady or gentleman to accompany him.
