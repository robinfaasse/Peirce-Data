5th. Draw a horizontal line under the array and bring down into a row beneath it the 1s remaining in the array, without altering their secundal places, filling the vacant places between them and in the l place with 0s. Mark the same barred place and put dots over the same two places. The result is the sum required.

Example. Add the first twenty odd numbers, after attaching to them the following circulates: [u]10[/u], [u]101[/u], [u]01[/u], [u]010[/u], and repeating this succession of circulates four times. For the operations on the circulates I use brown and blue ink alternately, for those on the serious red and green.
