[u]Theorem[/u]. The addition of 1 to a number gives the number next higher.

[u]Demonstration[/u]. For suppose this true for any number $N$. Namely, [del]suppose[/del] [ins]let[/ins] $N'$ be the number next higher than $N$ and $N''$ the number next higher than $N'$. If then $1 + N = N'$ then, by axiom 6, $1 + N' = N''$

But for $N = 0$ it is true for 1 is the number next higher than 0, and $1 + 0 = 1$, by the last corollary. Hence it is true for all numbers.

[u]Corollary[/u]. The addition of any number to 1 gives the number next higher.
