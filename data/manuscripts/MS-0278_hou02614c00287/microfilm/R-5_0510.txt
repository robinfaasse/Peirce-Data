[del]divisor of two numbers[/del].

smaller by the remainder, the first remainder by the
[del]second[/del] and so on, until [del]there remainder is[/del] Then, the
last remainder before the remainder zero, is the greatest
common factor. If it is a divisor it is the greatest common
divisor; if not, there is no common divisor and the
numbers are prime to one another.
