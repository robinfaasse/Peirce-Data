this is not true. We have, then, three classes
of collections: —

1. Finite collections;
2. Infinite numerable collections;
3. Innumerable collections.

Of finite collections, we can enumerate the
individual members. Other numerable collections
consist of individual members; for if any
part of them, already counted, constitute such
a collection, then these together with the next
to be counted will constitute such a collection.
