Hyperbolic geometry
Synthetic treatment

Suppose sum angles triangle less than $180^{\circ}$

[fig]Right triangle ABC with extended lines to D and E[/fig] Let AB be perpendicular to BC and let A be taken as large as possible to make a triangle ABC. Take CD perpendicular to AC and DE perpendicular to DC. Join AE then
$\text{DCE} = 90^{\circ} - \text{ACB}$
$\text{CDE} = 90^{\circ}$
$\text{CDE} + \text{DCE} + \text{CED} < 180^{\circ}$
$\therefore \text{CED} < \text{ACB}$ and $\text{ACE} = 180^{\circ} - \text{ACB}$
$\text{ACE} + \text{AEC} < 180^{\circ}$
$\text{CAE} > 0^{\circ}$
Then $\text{EAB} > \text{CAB}$ until $\text{ACB} = 0$
