Let us next consider the conception of a unidimensional
continuum, without introducing measurement. Take, for ex-
ample, the points on an oval. Given, any two points $A$ and
$C$, all others are either on the right-handed path from
$A$ to $C$ or on the right-handed path from $C$ to $A$. $A$ and $C$
themselves are said to be on the limits of the two paths.
If $B$ is on the right-handed path from $A$ to $C$, then $C$ is
on the right-handed path from $B$ to $A$.
