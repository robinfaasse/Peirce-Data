Part I. Calendar & Astronomical Ephemeris

The calendar is chiefly to show where to open [del]the prayer-book.[/del]

Moon-light is also of consequence

Also, in some places, [u]tides[/u].

And in the country, the time of apparent noon.

All times must be expressed in [u]Standard Time[/u]

The World Almanac only gives Local Times.

Other important data are
Moon's phases
Eclipses
Striking occultations and conjunctions
The places of planets (including morning & evening stars.)
[ins]Brightness of planets (Not given in any almanac)[/ins]
Varying [del]School[/del] Aspect of the heavens with the names of the hundred brightest stars.

Duration of twilight.
