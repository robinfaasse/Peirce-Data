6x' - 2y + 1z - 6 &= 0 \\

4x' + 3y - 4z + 7 &= 0 \\
0x' + 1y + 0z - y &= 0
\end{aligned}$$
In like manner let us substitute $z = 6z'$, and so multiply the
third column by $6$. We thus get;
$$\begin{aligned}
6x' - 2y + 6z' - 6 &= 0 \\
8x' - 1y - 12z' + 7 &= 0 \\
4x' + 3y - 24z' + 7 &= 0 \\
0x' + 1y + 0z - y &= 0
\end{aligned}$$
We shall not hesitate to multiply even the $y$ column. It is evident
we have the same right to do so, and it decidedly simplifies matters
since it will leave us only one $y$ to attend to down in the corner.
We will substitute then $y = -3y'$. We thus get
$$\begin{aligned}
6x' + 6y' + 6z' - 6 &= 0 \\
8x' + 3y' - 12z' + 7 &= 0 \\
4x' - 9y' - 24z' + 7 &= 0 \\
0x' - 3y' + 0z' - y &= 0.
\end{aligned}$$
We have the same right precisely to multiply the last column. It may
be a useless thing; but still, to establish our rights, we will do so.
