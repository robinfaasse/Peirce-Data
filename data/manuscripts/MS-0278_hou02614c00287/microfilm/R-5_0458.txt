The totient of any cardinal number, N, is the number of cardinal numbers greater than any assigned value but not greater than that value added to N, which are prime to N.

The above definitions satisfy all recognized formulae.

Thus, it is [ins]commonly[/ins] said that the number of divisors of a number is equal to the continued product of [del]its[/del] [ins]the exponents of[/ins] prime factors, [del]after[/del] each exponent has been increased by unity. For example, [del]12[/del] [ins]12[/ins] = $2^2 \cdot 3^1 \cdot 5^0 \cdot$ etc and the divisors of 12 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12 or 6 = $(2+1) \cdot (1+1) \cdot (0+1)$ etc. So the sole divisor of 1 is 1 and $1 = 2^0 \cdot 3^0 \cdot 5^0 \cdot$ etc and $1 = (0+1) \cdot (0+1) \cdot (0+1)$ etc. But every number is a divisor of $0 = 0^{\infty} \cdot 2^{\infty} \cdot 3^{\infty} \cdot 5^{\infty} \cdot$ etc.

So if any number [ins]a[/ins] is raised to a power whose exponent is the totient of a number, n, prime to a, and the power is diminished by 1, it is said, that the remainder is divisible by n. Thus $10^2 - 1 = 99$ which is divisible by 3, of which 2 is the totient. For 1 and 2 are the numbers greater than 0 but not greater that 0+3 which are prime to 3. So $5^1 - 1$ = 4 is divisible by 2 of which 1 is the totient. $N^1 - 1$ is divisible by 1 of which 1 is the totient. $1^N - 1$ is divisible by whatever number N is the totient of, all number being prime to 1. $N^0 - 1 = 0$ and every number is prime to 0 of which 0 is the totient. $0^4 - 1$ is not divisible by 5 of which 4 is the totient because 0 is not prime to any number, though every number is prime to 0.
