§ Definitions. [del]A factor[/del] [ins]A factor[/ins] of a whole number is a whole number unequal to that number [del]but[/del] which multiplied by a suitable whole number, called a divisor of the first number, gives that number as the product. Thus, the factors of 6 are 1, 2, 3, while its divisors are 6, 3, 2.

A [del]prime[/del] [ins]prime[/ins] number is a whole number with just one factor.
