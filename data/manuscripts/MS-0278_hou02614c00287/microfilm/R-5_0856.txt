$$D\tan x = 1 + \tan^2 x$$
$$D^2\tan x = (2\tan x) D\tan x = (D^0 + D^0)\tan x$$
$$D^3\tan x = 2(D\tan x)^2 + (2\tan x) D^2\tan x$$
$$D^4\tan x = 4 D\tan x \cdot D^2\tan x + 2 D\tan x \cdot D^2\tan x + 2\tan x \cdot D^3\tan x$$
$$= 6 D\tan x \cdot D^2\tan x + 2\tan x \cdot D^3\tan x$$
$$D^5\tan x = 6 D^2\tan x \cdot D^2\tan x + 6 D\tan x \cdot D^3\tan x$$
$$+ 2 D\tan x \cdot D^3\tan x + 2\tan x \cdot D^4\tan x$$
$$= 6 D^2\tan x \cdot D^2\tan x + 8 D\tan x \cdot D^3\tan x + 2\tan x \cdot D^4\tan x$$
$$= (D^0 + D^0)^4 \tan x$$
