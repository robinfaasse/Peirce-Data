In order to calculate values from a slowly converging
or slowly diverging series.

Let the series be $u_0 + u_1 + u_2 + u_3 + u_4 + \text{etc}$

First form the quantities $\frac{u_0}{u_0-u_1}$, $\frac{u_1}{u_1-u_2}$, $\frac{u_2}{u_2-u_3}$, etc

The first series of approximations are
$v_0 = u_0 \frac{u_0}{u_0-u_1}$, $v_1 = u_0 + u_1 \frac{u_1}{u_1-u_2}$, $v_2 = u_0 + u_1 + u_2 \frac{u_2}{u_2-u_3}$, etc

Calculate also $v_1 - v_0 = u_1 (\frac{u_1}{u_1-u_2} - \frac{u_0}{u_0-u_1})$ $v_2 - v_1 = u_2 \{\frac{u_2}{u_2-u_3} - \frac{u_1}{u_1-u_2}\}$, etc

Difference the series of these so as to get
$v_2 - 2v_1 + v_0$ $v_3 - 2v_2 + v_1$ etc

Then the second series of approximations are
$w_0 = v_0 - \frac{(v_1-v_0)^2}{v_2-2v_1+v_0}$ $w_1 = v_1 - \frac{(v_2-v_1)^2}{v_3-2v_2+v_1}$ etc

Form a third approximation
$\text{Final approx.} = w_0 - \frac{(\Delta w_0)^2}{\Delta^2 w_0}$

In order to illustrate the use of this method (which of
course does not necessarily work well, although it will
do so practically in nine cases out of ten, at least)
I will apply it to a problem to which I have not ap-
plied it namely to calculate nat. log 2.1 from the formula
$\log (1+x) = x - \frac{1}{2}x^2 + \frac{1}{3}x^3 - \frac{1}{4}x^4 + \frac{1}{5}x^5 - \frac{1}{6}x^6$
