(55) Let $[O']$ be $[15']\{13''\}$. Then we have
[fig]Geometric diagram with lines and numbered points[/fig] $[1], [3], [5]$
successively projected upon $[1'], [3'], [5']$
upon $[1''], [3''], [5'']$
upon $[5], [3''], [5'']$
and upon $[5], [1], [3]$

Thus, points opposite to $[5]$, relatively to $[1]$ and $[3]$ will be projected ultimately upon points opposite to $[3]$ relatively to $[5]$ and $[1]$, and in short all points will be carried in one direction. For instance, a range $[1], [2], [3], [4], [5], [6]$ will be projected upon $[5], [6], [1], [2], [3], [4]$.

In order to construct such a mode of displacement let us take upon $\{t\}$ a range $[1], [2], [3], [4], [5]$, etc of which the first three points are situated anywhere [del]and[/del] [ins]while[/ins] the following ones are situated at points to be determined later.
