Thus we see that $(D_{\theta}r^{-1})^{2}$ can be put in the form

$(D_{\theta}r^{-1})^{2} = C_{1}^{2} + 4 C_{2}(r^{-1}-C_{0}) + (6\frac{C_{3}}{C_{1}}-1) (r^{-1}-C_{0})^{2} + \text{etc}$

So that
$D_{r}(D_{\theta}r^{-1})^{2} = -\frac{1}{r^{2}} \left( 4C_{2} + 2(6\frac{C_{3}}{C_{1}}-1) (r^{-1}-C_{0}) + \text{etc} \right)$

In other words the force is expressible as
a series
$F = E_{2}\frac{1}{r^{2}} + E_{3}\frac{1}{r^{3}} + E_{4}\frac{1}{r^{4}} + \text{etc}.$

But unless $E_{4}$ etc are $= 0$ the body [del]may[/del] [ins]will[/ins]
fall into the centre (of course, with infinite
velocity), and indeed generally will do
so and unless $E_{3} = 0$ it will approach
nearer & nearer that centre forever
in a spiral.

The same holds for any orbital motion.
