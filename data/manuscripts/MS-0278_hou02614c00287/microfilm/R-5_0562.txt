[u]Art[/u] . [u]Problem[/u] . To solve a system of linear equations.

[u]Solution[/u]. To fix our ideas, let us, at first take a special system, namely, the following (almost any other would answer)

That our sum added to twice [u]z[/u] and diminished by 4 times [u]x[/u] leaves only 7 millions, and that three times our sum added to twice [u]x[/u] is short of [del]4[/del] [ins]our[/ins] [del]z[/del] [ins]equation[/ins] by only 7 millions. How much is [u]y[/u]?[/ins]
[u]3x[/u] - 2[u]y[/u] + [u]z[/u] = 6,
4[u]x[/u] - [u]y[/u] - 2[u]z[/u] = -7,
2[u]x[/u] + 3[u]y[/u] - 4[u]z[/u] = -7.

We will begin by adding to each the negative of the second member. We thus get
3[u]x[/u] - 2[u]y[/u] + [u]z[/u] - 6 = 0
4[u]x[/u] - [u]y[/u] - 2[u]z[/u] + 7 = 0
2[u]x[/u] + 3[u]y[/u] - 4[u]z[/u] + 7 = 0
