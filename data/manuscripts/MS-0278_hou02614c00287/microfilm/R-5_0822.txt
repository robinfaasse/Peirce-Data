$3! = 6$
$2! = 6/3 = 2$
$1! = 2/2 = 1$
$0! = 1/1 = 1$
$-1! = 1/0 = \infty$
$-2! = \infty/-1 = -\infty$
$-3! = -\infty/-2 = \infty$

$\frac{(x+y)!}{x! y!}$ Put $y=0$ $\frac{(x+0)!}{x! 0!} = \frac{1}{1} = 1$

Put $y = -1$ $\frac{(x-1)!}{x! (-1)!} = \frac{\frac{1}{x}}{\infty} = 0$ [ins]unless $x=0$ when it is $0/0$[/ins]

Put $y = -2$ $\frac{(x-2)!}{x! (-2)!} = \frac{\frac{1}{x(x-1)}}{-\infty} = 0$ [ins]unless $x=0$ or $1$[/ins]
