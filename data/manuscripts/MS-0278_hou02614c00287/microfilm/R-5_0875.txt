To ascertain whether the equation
(0) $d\rho/\rho = \sqrt{(dr)^2 + (dz)^2} / r$
is satisfied when
(1) $(\frac{r}{Z(1+E)})^2 + (\frac{z}{Z} - 1)^2 = 1$ or $(1\frac{1}{2})$ $(\frac{r}{1+E})^2 = 2Zz - z^2$
by putting is satisfied absolutely or approximately by
(2) $\rho = r - \frac{dr}{dz} z + \frac{\sqrt{(dr)^2 + (dz)^2}}{dz} z$

To find that out, we must deduce from $(1\frac{1}{2})$ the value of $r$ in terms of $z$
and that of $dr$ in terms of $dz$. Now we have from $(1\frac{1}{2})$
(3) $r = (1+E) \sqrt{2Zz - z^2}$
(4) $dr = (1+E) \frac{2Z - 2z}{2\sqrt{2Zz - z^2}} dz = (1+E) \frac{Z - z}{\sqrt{2Zz - z^2}} dz$
$(dr)^2 = (1+E)^2 \frac{(Z-z)^2}{2Zz - z^2} (dz)^2 = (1+E)^2 (\frac{Z^2}{2Zz - z^2} - 1) (dz)^2$
$(dr)^2 + (dz)^2 = (1+E)^2 \frac{Z^2}{2Zz - z^2} (dz)^2$ [del]$- (1+E)^2 (dz)^2 + (dz)^2$[/del]
$\sqrt{(dr)^2 + (dz)^2} = (1+E) \frac{Z}{\sqrt{2Zz - z^2}} dz$
whence, by (3)
(5) $\frac{\sqrt{(dr)^2 + (dz)^2}}{r} = \pm \frac{Z}{2Zz - z^2} dz$
so that (0) is satisfied, provided
(6) $d\rho/\rho = \pm \frac{Z}{2Zz - z^2} dz = \pm \frac{dz}{z(2 - \frac{1}{Z}z)}$
the integral of which is of the simplest, viz
$\log \rho + \text{Const} = \pm \frac{1}{2} \log \frac{z}{2 - \frac{z}{Z}}$
or $\rho = \sqrt{\frac{z}{2 - \frac{z}{Z}}} \times \text{Const}$
