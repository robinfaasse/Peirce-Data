If a line and a surface previously separate are brought together
at [ins]an ordinary[/ins] point, the effect is to produce a [del]2-line[/del] [ins]2-branch[/ins]-1-sheet node and
[del]the[/del] chorisis is diminished by 1 or the cyclosis is increased by one.
That is [del]the[/del] X-K is diminished by 1.

If the point of the line is an extremity, the extremity disappears and
a 1 branch-1 sheet node takes its place, X-K being diminished by 1

If the point of the line is a node, this node disappears and is replaced
by N-branch-1-sheet node.

Thus, in all these cases the nodicity is increased by 2 branches and 1 sheet.

If the point of the surface is a nodal point of $\nu$ sheets, this becomes for
an ordinary point of the line a 2 branch-$\nu$ sheet node. That is the nodicity
in sheets is not affected. But the [del]nodose[/del] [ins]branch-nodicity[/ins] is increased by 2.
