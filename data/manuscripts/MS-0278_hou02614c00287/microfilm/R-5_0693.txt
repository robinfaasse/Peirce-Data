$\tan^{2} \alpha = \frac{1 - 2 \cos a + \cos^{2} a}{1 + 2 \cos a + \cos^{2} a} = \frac{2 - \sin^{2} a - 2 \cos a}{2 - \sin^{2} a + 2 \cos a}$

$\sec^{2} \alpha = 1 + \tan^{2} \alpha = \frac{2 [del]4[/del] - \sin^{2} a}{1 + \cos a - \frac{1}{2} \sin^{2} a}$
