[u]Art. . Definitions[/u] . A part of a whole number,
N, is a whole number unequal to that number, which,
multiplied by a suitable number, called a factor, or
divisor, of N, gives the first number, N, gives that num-
ber as the product. Thus, the parts of 6 are 3, 2, 1; while
its factors or divisors are [del]1, 2, 3[/del] 2, 3, 6.

A [u]prime number[/u] is a whole number with a single
part.

A [u]prime factor[/u] of a whole number is a prime number
that is a [ins]factor[/ins] [del]part[/del] of it.

A [u]homogeneous[/u] number is a whole number with a
single prime factor. Thus, 27 = 3.3.3 is a homogeneous
number. The [u]multiplicity[/u] of the factor is the number of
times it has to enter as a factor to give the whole.

A [u]heterogeneous[/u] number is a whole number having
more than one prime factor.

A [u]composite[/u] number is one which is a product of
two or more homogeneous parts.
