Mathematics is the science which traces out the [del]conse-[/del]
quences of simple assumptions.

All other sciences set out from precise observations of fact.
I here mean by "precise" as nearly true as the most refined arts of
observation have rendered possible. [del]What[/del] [ins]These[/ins] arts are we have
here no occasion to consider, inasmuch as the mathematicians,
alone among men of science, do not hold themselves in the least
responsible for the truth of the statements from which they
set out, but only for what they assert would be true in case
those initial statements should be exactly true.
