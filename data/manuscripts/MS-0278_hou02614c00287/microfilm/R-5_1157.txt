For example, suppose the four lines assumed have for their equations in Cartesian coordinates

$$\begin{matrix}
x=0 & y=0 \\
x=0 & z=0 \\
y=0 & z=0 \\
x+my=0 & x+nz=0
\end{matrix}$$

$$\begin{matrix}
A = & my+nz+w=0 \\

C = & my \phantom{+nz} +w=0 \\
