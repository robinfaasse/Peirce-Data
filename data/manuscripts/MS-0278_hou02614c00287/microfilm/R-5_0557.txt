[u]Definition 38[/u]. By the [del]system[/del] [ins]cardinal[/ins] of [del]whole[/del] numbers we mean that simple, [del]wise[/del] sequence of numbers, which has 0, for its first object, and [ins]next[/ins] after any object has the next [del]greater[/del] [ins]or higher[/ins] number, and has no last object.

[u]Axiom 6[/u]. The effect of substituting in a sum for either term (supposing it to be a whole number) the number next greater is to change the value of the sum to the number next greater.

[u]Axiom 7[/u]. 0 + 0 = 0.

[u]Theorem 10[/u]. [u]The sum of two whole numbers is a whole number[/u].

[u]Demonstration[/u]. For, by Axiom 6, if this is true of any two numbers, it is true when for one of them we substitute the number next higher. But, by Axiom 7, it is true when both are 0. Hence, etc.
