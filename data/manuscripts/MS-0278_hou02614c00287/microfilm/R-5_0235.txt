[u]Counting[/u] is an operation establishing a relation,
I, of a class [u]$\gamma$[/u], to a number, N. Assume that the
relation, Q, has the property just attributed
to it. If then, $O_1$ be a relation such that everything
that belongs to the class, $\gamma$, is in that relation to a
number, but not to two different numbers, and
no two members of the class to the same number,
and if, moreover, whatever number X may be,
either something belonging to the class, $\gamma$, is in the
relation, $O_1$ to X, or else nothing belonging to the
class, $\gamma$, is in the relation, $O_1$ to a number, Q of
to X, then if something belonging to the class $\gamma$
is O of N, but nothing belonging to the class $\gamma$ is
O of a number Q of N, then and then only is
$\gamma$ in the relation, I, to class, N.
