Now let us turn to the disadvantages. These fall into two classes; [del]1st[/del] [ins]I[/ins] the disadvantage of decimal division in making things & in doing things; [del]2nd[/del] [ins]II[/ins] the disadvantage of attacking the predominance & sovereignty of our standards.

The disadvantage of decimal subdivision is shown conclusively, [u]a priori[/u] and [u]a posteriori[/u]. How great it is does not concern the 1 percent of labor that is spent in ciphering but all the other 99 percent in the actual processes of manufactures. It is well below the mark to say that a decimal division in manufacturing would necessitate the employment of another wage earner for every hundred now employed. But that would make many millions of dollars a year. There are various other disadvantages of this which seem to come to quite as much.
