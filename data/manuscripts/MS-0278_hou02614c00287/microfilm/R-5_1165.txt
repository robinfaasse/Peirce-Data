Several antecedents & final consequent is a single
letter, Z.
Their all being true, makes Z true.
$\therefore$ Z must be consequent of one of them.
Consider antecedents of this. It must be true
if the rest are true. $\therefore$ Must be deducible from
[del]others[/del] [ins]them[/ins].

Thus there is a shorter formula on
which this depends.

$$\{ (Z \prec B) \prec A \} \prec Z$$
