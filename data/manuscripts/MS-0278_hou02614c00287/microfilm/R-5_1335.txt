Consider a ray very near an axis of translation in one of the invariable laminae through it. It crosses the axis; that is going in one sense along the axis from the point of intersection it is on one side of the axis, going in the other sense on the other side.

Hence [del]of[/del] a [del]point[/del] [ins]particle[/ins] moving along the axis till it returns to its original point, returns with all its surrounding points reversed, and it must make the circuit twice before it restores them.
