Now take the case in which $E$ does not vanish.

$$Ey^4 + (Cx^2 - a^2)y^2 = -x^4 - a^2x^2$$

$$4E^2y^4 + 4(Cx^2 - a^2)Ey^2 = -4Ex^4 - 4Ea^2x^2$$

$$2Ey^2 = -Cx^2 + a^2 \pm \sqrt{(Cx^2 - a^2)^2 - 4Ex^4 - 4Ea^2x^2}$$

$$= -Cx^2 + a^2 \pm \sqrt{(C^2 - 4E)x^4 - 2a^2(C + 2E)x^2 + a^4}$$

$$2E \frac{d(y^2)}{d(x^2)} = -C \pm \frac{(C^2 - 4E)x^2 - a^2(C + 2E)}{\sqrt{(C^2 - 4E)x^4 - 2a^2(C + 2E)x^2 + a^4}}$$

Putting this equal to $2E \frac{y^2}{x^2}$

$$-C + \frac{a^2 \pm \sqrt{(C^2 - 4E)x^4 - 2a^2(C + 2E)x^2 + a^4}}{x^2}$$

$$= -C \pm \frac{(C^2 - 4E)x^2 - a^2(C + 2E)}{\sqrt{(C^2 - 4E)x^4 - 2a^2(C + 2E)x^2 + a^4}}$$

Clearing from fractions, and cancelling the term $-C$

$$\pm a^2 \sqrt{(C^2 - 4E)x^4 - 2a^2(C + 2E)x^2 + a^4}$$

$$\pm [del](-C^2 + 4E)x^4 + 2a^2(C + 2E)x^2 - a^4[/del]$$

$$+ (C^2 - 4E)x^4 - a^2(C + 2E)x^2$$

$$= a^2(C + 2E)x^2 - a^4$$

Dropping the factor $a^2$ and squaring

$$(C^2 - 4E)x^4 - 2a^2(C + 2E)x^2 + a^4$$

$$= (C^2 + 4[del]E[/del][ins]C[/ins]E + E^2)x^4 - 2a^2(C + 2E)x^2 + a^4$$

or $x^4 = 0$
