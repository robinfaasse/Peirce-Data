Suppose, now, we were to make an exact per-
spective picture of this plain, this plane plain.
For this purpose, we might fix a needle in
the top of a post, and set up a piece of glass
to draw upon, with suitable ink. To find where
upon the glass to mark the representation
of any object upon the ground, we should
put our eye so that the object was just
covered by the needle point, and mark it
at the point on the glass in the same line
of sight.
