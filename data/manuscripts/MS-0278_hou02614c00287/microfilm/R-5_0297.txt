$$\Sigma^4 - 2(a+d)\Sigma^2 = -(a-d)^2 - 4bc$$

$$\left[\Sigma^2 - (a+d)\right]^2 = 4(ad-bc)$$

$$\Sigma^2 - (a+d) = 2\sqrt{ad-bc}$$

$$\Sigma^2 = a+d \pm 2\sqrt{ad-bc}$$

$$\Sigma = \pm\sqrt{a+d \pm 2\sqrt{ad-bc}}$$

$$\Delta = \frac{a-d}{\Sigma}$$

$$y = \frac{b}{\Sigma}$$
