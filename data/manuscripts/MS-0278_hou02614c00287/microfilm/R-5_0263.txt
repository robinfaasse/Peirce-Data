Lines cut the line at infinity at two coincident points. That is, the line at infinity being a circle of infinite radius is two coincident lines (Easily adapts itself to common sense this.) Hence we may say that the character which all [del]lines[/del] [ins]pairs[/ins] which [del]inter[/del] cut the sky at any one point is direction. Hence two angles whose legs are parallel considered as relations of directions are absolutely the same.

Now if two angles subtend segments of the same line at infinity we will say they are summed in the angle that subtends the sum of the two segments. We will further assume that two triangles which have their three sides equal, and similarly [del]arranged[/del] [ins]ordered[/ins], have their corresponding angles equal.
