$u_{a,b}$ is the probability that after $a$ favorable and $b$ unfavorable
independent events, [del]the patient will die[/del] $m$ before $n$
[del]there will be $x$ favorable and $y$ unfavorable events[/del]
making [del]$a+x : b+y \geqq m$[/del] before
$a+x : b+y \leqq n$

where $p$ is the probability of a favorable event

$u_{a,b} = p \cdot u_{a+1,b} + \bar{p} \cdot u_{a,b+1}$
$= p^2 \cdot u_{a+2,b} + 2p\bar{p} \cdot u_{a+1,b+1} + \bar{p}^2 \cdot u_{a,b+2}$

If $a=0$ $b=0$ $u_{a,b} = p$
