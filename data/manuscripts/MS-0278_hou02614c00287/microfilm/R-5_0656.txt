Puncturing a bag and thus diminishing periphraxis by one diminishes
singularity by 1. $\Pi$ and $S$ on opposite sides.

Thus the singularity is one less of a nodal line one part of which is a
simple split and the rest an edge, than if all were split. That is its
singularity is the sum of singularities of its parts.

So less by one than the singularity of a double split is a double split
with two extremities.
