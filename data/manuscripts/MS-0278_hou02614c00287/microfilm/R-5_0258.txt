For the present purpose we may assume,

$$\theta - \omega = A \sin \left( t \sqrt{\frac{g}{R}} + \epsilon \right)$$

$$\cos (\theta - \omega) = 1 - \frac{1}{2} (\theta - \omega)^{2} = 1 - \frac{1}{2} A^{2} \sin^{2} \left( t \sqrt{\frac{g}{R}} + \epsilon \right)$$

$$D_{t} (\theta - \omega) = \sqrt{\frac{g}{R}} A \cos \left( t \sqrt{\frac{g}{R}} + \epsilon \right)$$

$$(D_{t} (\theta - \omega))^{2} = \frac{g}{R} A^{2} \cos^{2} \left( t \sqrt{\frac{g}{R}} + \epsilon \right) = \frac{g}{R} A^{2} - \frac{g}{R} A^{2} \sin^{2} \left( t \sqrt{\frac{g}{R}} + \epsilon \right) = \frac{g}{R} A^{2} - \frac{g}{R} (\theta - \omega)^{2}$$

$$D_{t}^{2} l = \frac{[1 + A^{2}] - \frac{3}{2} (\theta - \omega)^{2}}{\cos \omega} g - \frac{E}{m} l + \frac{C}{m}$$

Now we have [del]accurately[/del], $\omega = \theta (1 - \frac{l}{R})$ But $\frac{l}{R}$ differs [del]extremely[/del] [ins]very[/ins] little from unity and $\theta$ is also a small quantity consequently $\omega$ is wholly negligible and $\cos \omega$ may be taken as unity. We have therefore

$$D_{t}^{2} l = - \frac{3}{2} g (\theta - \omega)^{2} - \frac{E}{m} l + (1 + A^{2}) g + \frac{C}{m}$$

[del]Now $D_{t}^{2} (\theta - \omega)^{2} = 2 (D_{t} (\theta - \omega))^{2} + 2 (\theta - \omega) D_{t}^{2} (\theta - \omega)$[/del]

[del]But $D_{t}^{2} (\theta - \omega) = - \frac{g}{R} (\theta - \omega)$[/del]

[del]So that $D_{t}^{2} \{ (\theta - \omega)^{2} \} = 2 \frac{g}{R} A^{2} - 4 \frac{g}{R} (\theta - \omega)^{2}$[/del]

Now $(\theta - \omega)^{2} = \frac{1}{2} A^{2} - \frac{1}{2} A^{2} \cos (2 \sqrt{\frac{g}{R}} t + 2 \epsilon)$

And we may write $\psi = \frac{1}{2} A^{2} \cos (2 \sqrt{\frac{g}{R}} t + 2 \epsilon)$ so that $(\theta - \omega)^{2} = \frac{1}{2} A^{2} - \psi$

Then

$$D_{t}^{2} l = \frac{3}{2} g \psi - \frac{E}{m} l + (1 + \frac{1}{4} A^{2}) g + \frac{C}{m}$$

and or putting $L$ for the mean value of $l$ $D_{t}^{2} (l - L) = \frac{3}{2} g \psi - \frac{E}{m} (l - L)$. Now

$$D_{t}^{2} \psi = - 4 \frac{g}{R} \psi$$

and hence

$$D_{t}^{2} \left\{ \frac{4 \frac{g}{R} - \frac{E}{m}}{\frac{3}{2} g} (l - L) + \psi \right\} = - \frac{E}{m} \left\{ \frac{4 \frac{g}{R} - \frac{E}{m}}{\frac{3}{2} g} (l - L) + \psi \right\}$$
