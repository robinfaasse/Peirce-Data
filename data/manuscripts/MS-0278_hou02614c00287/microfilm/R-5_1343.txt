[u]Postulate[/u]. The firmament of the rigid motion of ordinary
measurement is a plane.

[u]Corollary[/u]. Since the circle which is a plane has a quadrant
radius, and since the firmament is infinitely distant from every
point of space, it follows that a quadrant length is infinite. [del]And
every point of space is equally distant from the firmament.[/del]

[u]Definition[/u]. Parallel [del]lines[/del] [ins]rays[/ins] are rays which meet
upon the firmament.

[u]Corollary[/u]. Since the firmament is a plane, it follows
that through a given point there is but one ray parallel to
a given ray.

[u]Theorem[/u]. Parallel rays are cut by any third ray at
equal angles.
