[fig]small loop diagram[/fig] A nodal line of nodality 2 may have extremities.
It may also have two points in which it splits into two one of nodality 1
and the other nodality -1.
Or it may split into two each with nodality -1
or each with nodality 1.

A nodal line of even nodality may split into an even number
of [del]line[/del] branches with odd nodality.
A nodal line of odd nodality if it splits must have an odd
number of branches of odd nodality.
At any node of a nodal line, there are an even number of branches
of odd nodality.

The cyclosis of a nodal line without singularities equals its chorisis.
