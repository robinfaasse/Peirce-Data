3/ is not as small as [del]$\frac{M}{L}$[/del] [ins]$\frac{M}{I}$[/ins] and is not
as great as [del]$\frac{G-M}{L}$[/del] [ins]$\frac{G-M}{I}$[/ins] while $\frac{G-M-G+Y}{L-N}$ is
not as great as $\frac{G-M}{I}$ but $G-M-G+Y$ is
the same as $M-Y$, so that the same
fraction of denominator $L-N$ is not
as small as $\frac{M}{L}$ and not as great as $\frac{G-M}{I}$

<u>Corollary</u>. But if there be a fraction $\frac{X}{N}$
of denominator $N$ which is both as great and
as small as $\frac{M}{I}$ where $I$ is greater than $N$,
then $\frac{M-X}{I-N}$ is also both as great and as
small as $\frac{M}{I}$.
