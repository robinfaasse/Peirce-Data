<u>Scholium</u>. It is perfectly proper to speak of the "sides" of
a plane, provided what is meant be that there are two opposite
contrary ways of moving through it. But if it be meant that there
are two distinct "floors," so to speak, so that a fly walking upon
one side could not get upon the other side without doing
something different from walking along, that is a mistake. If
the fly performs a circuit of walking it will [del]reach the same[/del] [ins]return to its original[/ins]
[del]point[/del] starting-point on the plane, only it will be upon the
other side.
