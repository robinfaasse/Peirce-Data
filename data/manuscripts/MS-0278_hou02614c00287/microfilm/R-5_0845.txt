$$z^2 = Z^2 \left[1 - \left(\frac{r}{R}\right)^2\right] \qquad (dz)^2 = \frac{Z^4}{R^4} \frac{r^2}{Z^2 \left(1 - (r/R)^2\right)} (dr)^2$$
