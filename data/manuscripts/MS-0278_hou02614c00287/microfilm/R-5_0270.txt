(2) In the last example, substitute for the first premise:
everybody has urged everybody to shoot all the persons
to whom he has abused him.

Then the premises become
$$\Pi_i \Pi_j \Pi_k \quad \bar{v}_{ijk} \text{ + } u_{ijk}$$
$$\Sigma_x \Pi_y \Sigma_z \quad v_{xyz} \cdot \bar{u}_{xzy}$$

We write
$$\Sigma_x \Pi_y \Sigma_z \Pi_i \Pi_j \Pi_k \quad (\bar{v}_{ijk} \text{ + } u_{ijk}) \cdot v_{xyz} \cdot \bar{u}_{xzy}$$

Identifying as before we get
$$\Sigma_x \Pi_y \Sigma_z \quad u_{xyz} \cdot \bar{u}_{xzy}$$

There is a man who has urged everybody to shoot a certain
person without urging him to shoot anybody. (There is
a slight contradiction here.)

But we can also identify
$$\Sigma_x \Pi_y \Sigma_z \quad \bar{v}_{xzy} \cdot v_{xyz}$$

There is a man who has abused everybody to one man
whom he has not abused to anybody. (Note the same
contradiction.
