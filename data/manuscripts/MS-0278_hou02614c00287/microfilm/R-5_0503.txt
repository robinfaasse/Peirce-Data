A part of a whole number, [ins]N,[/ins] is a whole number unequal to that number, but which, multiplied by a suitable number called a [u]factor[/u], or divisor of the original number, [ins]N,[/ins] gives that number as the product. Thus, the parts of 6 are 1, 2, 3 and its factors or divisors are 6, 3, 2.

A [u]prime number[/u] is a whole number with just one part.

A whole number is said to be [u]prime to[/u] a whole number when they have no common divisor.
