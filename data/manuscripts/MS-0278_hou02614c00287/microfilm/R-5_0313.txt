Here then is a whole world of observation, to which
we have been systematically blind, simply because of
a [del]many[/del] metaphysical prejudice. One of the most ac-
complished men of science in the country went so
far as to say to me the other day, that there was no
reason to suppose that the sensations of color of one
person had any resemblance to those of another!
Violently as this flies in the face of the principles of
