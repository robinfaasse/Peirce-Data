Now, the definition of $5$ is $4+1$ and of $8$ is
$7+1$. Hence,
$$7+5 = 7+(4+1) = (7+1)+4 = 8+4.$$
In this way we should show that
$$8+4 = 9+3 = 10+2 = 11+1 = 12.$$
The proposition $7+5=12$ is thus proved from
definitions.
