"He says the intellectual powers come out of eggs"

"That is a paradox. That men grow from eggs is
obviously true, and means more than to say that a
statue comes from a quarry. There is also a certain
sense in which it can be said that the intellectual
powers come out of eggs; namely, the very same sense
in which one might say that the power of a statue
to fill the soul with delight was quarried in Ferrera.
Among the intellectual powers, is our knowledge of the
alphabet. [del]Now[/del] I have always been interested in the
puzzling question of the origin of the Phenician
alphabet. So Brooks says it comes out of an egg.
That is a theory not hitherto broached. How original
Brooks is! Well, how does he prove it?"
