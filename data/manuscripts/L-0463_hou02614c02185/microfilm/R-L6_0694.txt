I want to help you with existential graphs. Let us see under what circumstances a partial graph can be eliminated by iteration followed by deiteration. I first call your attention to the fact that iteration and deiteration under a single enclosure cannot amount to much. And iteration in an odd enclosure is simply iterating a particular graph where you have a right to scribe any graph you please; and so deiteration from an evenly enclosed area is only erasing where you are entitled to erase whatever you like.
