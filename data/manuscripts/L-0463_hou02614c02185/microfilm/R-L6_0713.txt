[u]ESSAYS ON SIGNIFICS.[/u]

It is proposed to bring together a number of representative studies of [del]a critic-[/del]
al and constructive character bearing upon the group of topics which Lady [del]Welby[/del]
has so long endeavoured to bring into prominence. The word which she has [del]fixed[/del]
upon as covering the whole range of these questions is "Significs"—the study of the
use of signs in all their bearings. For this purpose it is desirable to [del]issue a[/del]
and appeal for co-operation to those who have shown sympathy for her general [del]aims.[/del]
As indicating the kind of subjects that will come within the scope of the [del]volume,[/del]
the following suggestions are offered:

1. **The Analogical Extension of Interpretation from one Field of Experience to**
**another.**

2. **Value and Purpose as Factors in Significance.**

3. **The Function of Metaphor in Thought.**

4. **The Figurative Element in the Conception of Space and Time.**

5. **The Relation of Mathematical Symbolism to Significance.**

6. **The Sources of Obscurity and Ambiguity in the Use of Words.**

7. **The Extent of Voluntary Control over Language.**

8. **The Means of Developing in Children a true Insight into the Nature and [del]Use of[/del]**
**Language.**

Further suggestions as to desirable subjects will be welcomed.

The Essays will be edited by Professor Stout, The University, St. [del]Andrews,[/del]
and Dr. Slaughter, Elmwood, Bickley, Kent.
