The ten respects according to which the chief divisions of signs are determined are as follows:

1[sup]st[/sup], According to the Mode of [del]Being[/del] [ins]Apprehension[/ins] of the Sign itself;
2[sup]nd[/sup], According to the Mode of Presentation of the Immediate Object,
3[sup]d[/sup], According to the Mode of Being of the Dynamical Object,
4[sup]th[/sup] According to the Relation of the Sign to its Dynamical Object,
5[sup]th[/sup], According to the Mode of Presentation of the Immediate Interpretant,
6[sup]th[/sup], According to the Mode of Being of the Dynamical Interpretant,
7[sup]th[/sup], According to the [del]Nature of the[/del] [ins]Relation of the Sign to[/ins] the Dynamical Interpretant,
8[sup]th[/sup], According to the Nature of the Normal Interpretant,
9[sup]th[/sup], According to the Relation of the Sign to the Normal Interpretant,
10[sup]th[/sup] According to the Triadic Relation of the Sign to its Dynamical Object and to its Normal Interpretant.

The ten divisions appear to me to be all Trichotomies; [del]but it is possible that some of them are not properly so.
