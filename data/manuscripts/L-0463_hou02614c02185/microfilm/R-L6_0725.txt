[date]Oct 19 1909[/date]

DUNEAVES,
HARROW.

My dear Mrs Peirce

Though, alas, I cannot emulate the delicacy of your dainty handwriting, my grateful and affectionate thanks are due to you for your letter, and [del]to you both[/del] for your generous invitation.

Alas, I can only retain working power by strict economy of strength & avoidance of extra risk.

So, though I have been more than once invited [del]by my[/del] [ins]by[/ins] American friends, I have been compelled to forego what to me would have been a delightful experience. — I shall of course have specially valued an opportunity of seeing something of your husband & you — And I must [ins]be[/ins] allowed to send, in return for yours, much and warm love to you both, from

Victoria Welby
