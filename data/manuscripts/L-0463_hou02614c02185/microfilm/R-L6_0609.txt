[pn]22[/pn] [del]III[/del] [ins]In[/ins] respect to the Nature of their Dynamical Objects, Signs
I found to be either

1. Signs of Possibles. That is [u]Abstractives[/u] such as Color, Mass,
Whiteness etc.

2. Signs of Occurrences. That is [u]Concretives[/u] such as Man, Charle-
magne.

3 Signs of Collections That is [u]Collectives[/u] such as Mankind, the
Human Race etc

By [u]Abstractives[/u] I meant signs of immediate abstractions; but
reason some doubt what to do with abstractions resulting from
experiment. I thought it would be requisite to study subdivisions
of these classes but never went into that research.

I was of the opinion that if the Dynamical Object be a mere
Possible the Immediate Object could only be of the same nature,
while if the Immediate Object were a Tendency or Habit then
the Dynamical Object must be of the same nature. Consequently
an Abstractive must be a Mark, while a Type must be a Col-
lective, which shows how I conceived Abstractives and Collectives

IV. The fourth Trichotomy is the one which I most frequently use
