[pn]26[/pn] the language employed in discoursing to the reader, and the language employed to express the thought to which the discourse relates should be kept distinct and each should be selected for its peculiar fitness for the purpose it was to serve. For the discussing language I would use [u]English[/u], which has special merits for the treatment of logic. For the language discoursed about, I would use the system of Existential Graphs throughout, which has no equal for this purpose.
