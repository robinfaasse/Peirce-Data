19, GRAY'S INN RESIDENCES,
HOLBORN, W.C.

[date]August 20th/04[/date]

Dear Sir,

I wrote to you some months ago but fear my letter has failed of its destination. My [u]object[/u] was to secure your cooperation in the venture described in the enclosed memorandum. Professor Stout very much hopes that you will find it possible to contribute an essay, & indeed, feels that the book would be incomplete without it. The length of essay must suit the wishes of the contributor but should be within nine or ten thousand words. It is hoped that the book may be ready for publication in January. You will be glad to know that Lady Welby continues well; she is at present in Scotland.

Yours faithfully, J. W. Slaughter
