The copulants are likewise indispensable and have the property of being Continuant. What I mean is that [del]As[/del] the sign 'A is red' can be decomposed so as to separate 'is red' into a Copulative and a Descriptive, thus: 'A possesses the character of redness' But if we attempt to analyze 'possesses the character' in like manner, we get 'A possesses the character of the possession of the character of Redness'; and so on ad infinitum. I call all such sign Continuants. They are, all Copulants and are the only pure copulants.
