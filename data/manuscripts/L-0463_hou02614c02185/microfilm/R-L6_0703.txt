The best way is to find out first what you want to want, what you desire to desire, and then to use the machinery of self control to cause yourself to want what you want to want. Let me see what else struck me — "Language is only the extreme form of Expressive Action." Good; only I think you do not quite express what you mean. Perhaps you mean that Language is the most effective engine for enabling a man to control the conduct of a man.

Wasn't it in the same paper that you said "Life itself may be considered as Expression" and I thought perhaps you might have said "it should be recognized" as such.

But there are a good many remarks that I am sure you wouldn't have made if you were first to study Greek, — or even Latin. It makes me angry with the whole British conception of "women's sphere" (a [del]sphere[/del] [ins]sphere[/ins] is the shape an envelope assumes when it puts the maximum constraint on its contents) to think that a woman of your brilliancy and earnestness, should devote her life to the significance of language, & yet should not know Greek! That could not happen here I am sure.
