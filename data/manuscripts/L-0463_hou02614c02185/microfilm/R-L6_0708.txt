[u]Significance[/u].

[date]1909 March 14[/date].

Heavens! To think that I should not, in the middle of March, have yet finished a letter begun in January, and what is much worse [del]still[/del], that I should have let all this time slip by without so much as acknowledging your letter! It is really dreadful. What can you think of me? But really I have not had any time at my command; I am shockingly behindhand in everything through having more to do than I possibly could. Nor could I escape doing anything I have done.

Draft of Lieb p. 34
