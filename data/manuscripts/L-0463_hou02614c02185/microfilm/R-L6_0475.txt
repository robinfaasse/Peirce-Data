I have three kind letters to thank you
for but have been taken severely
ill and even now cannot write
more than this —

Your last letter (Dec 4) had no
Syllabus in it, so I am still
hoping for it as I suppose it
was accidentally omitted.

I will write as soon as I can

Excuse this

V. Welby

Harrow — England
[date]Dec 17 1903[/date]

I send this to Milford as your
Lectures will be over — Their titles
on the Ticket sound most interesting
to me, especially the three last.
