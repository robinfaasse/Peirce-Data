TEMPLE, E.C.

April 25th, 1911.

Dear Dr. Peirce,

I am venturing to write again regarding the volume of Essays which friends of Lady Welby are contributing. We now have all the materials except your contribution, and are greatly hoping that your health has permitted you to prepare a paper. If so, we shall be glad to receive it soon, in order to get the volume through the press some time this summer. If you require some further time, we can proceed with the printing, being certain that your contribution will come.

Yours truly,

J. W. Slaughter

Dr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Pennsylvania,
U.S.A.
