There are four objections that would probably be raised against my doctrine; but I will not lengthen this letter with the refutations of them. I have carefully considered them, and have found them to be answered.

From the summer of [date]1905[/date] to the same time in [date]1906[/date], I devoted much study to my ten trichotomies of signs. It is time I reverted to the subject, as [del]I[/del] [ins]I now[/ins] know I could now make it much clearer. But I dare say some of my former names are better than these I now use. I formerly called a Potisign ... a Tinge or Tone
an Actisign ... a Token
a Famisign ... a Type

a Descriptive ... an Indefinite (but this was bad)
a Denominative ... a Designation
a Copulative [ins](which is bad)[/ins] ... a Distributive which is much better

I think Potisign Actisign Famisign
might be called Mark Token Type (?)

while
Descriptive Denominative Copulative
might be called Descriptive Denominative Distributive

I have now given as much time to this letter as I can afford and I cannot now reexamine the remaining Trichotomies, although I must do so as soon as possible. So I just give them as they stood two years and more ago. In particular, the relations I assumed between the different classes were the wildest of guesses, and cannot be altogether right, I think.
