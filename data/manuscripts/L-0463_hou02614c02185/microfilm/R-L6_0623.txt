hold that a Sign which is in its own nature a Habit, or General, can
have no other kind of Object than a General. That a Famisign, that
is a Habit that is a Sign, like a word (in the sense in which we
usually speak of a "word") cannot be Descriptive we have already
seen. But in this additional contention of mine, I ask how
it is possible that a Habit should be the sign of an individual
thing or individual event, or individual occurrence of any
sort? A "Copulant" is the sort of sign which everybody usually has
in mind when he speaks of a word or other sign as "general". He
does not mean that the word in itself is general; for every word, -
such as Charlemagne is that. What he means is that it is a Sign
of a General Object.
