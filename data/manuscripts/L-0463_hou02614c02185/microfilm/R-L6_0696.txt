This has, I doubt not, had a good deal to do with my devotion to logic; [ins]and[/ins] I have no doubt my intellectual left-handedness has been of the utmost service to my studies in logic; causing me to study its literature much more deeply and fundamentally than I otherwise should have been; and it has given me so much caution in forming my opinions and so much diffidence of my own opinions, that I have been able to produce little; and half, or more than half, of what I have written seems to me rather immature.
