Yet I do not think so ill of the present
state of science. The concepts of
Body and cell, atom and grain,
Molecule, corpuscle, ion, and all,
Magnet, electron,
do not seem to me to be mere "bricks"
"well fitted to build a wall," "to keep out the
message of light" [ins]and[/ins] "more deadly than
any plague." I protest against that;
especially, as what you would put
in their place is mere feeling, the state
of mind of an infant. It is feeling, —
pure feeling, — that according to me is
mere "bricks" that build an obscurant
wall.

Still less do I agree in your
passionate disclaimer of all
modern thought, — or rather of
all thought, "Shame on our thinking,
— skeletal, bony
Worship of quantity."
