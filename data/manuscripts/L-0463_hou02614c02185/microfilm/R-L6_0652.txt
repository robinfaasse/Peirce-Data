[pn]31[/pn] We now assume a number of new units, one more [del]than the[/del] [ins]in[/ins] number [del]than the[/del] [ins]than the[/ins] fundamental units, $i, j, k,$ etc of the system. We do not define [del]these[/del] [ins]them[/ins] at all at first; they shall [del]mean[/del] [ins]denote[/ins] nothing except that the formulae into which they enter shall be true. Just as the word [u]liberty[/u] or any other abstraction, such as [u]expense[/u], denotes nothing except the truth of certain forms of propositions under certain circumstances. These new units are $A$ and a capital letter for each of the units $i, j, k,$ of the system; they are then $A, I, J, K,$ etc. They will never be multiplied together, but may be multiplied by numerical coefficients and the products may be added. And multiplied by $0$ any one of them vanishes. So any expression such as $aA + a_{1}I + a_{2}J + a_{3}K$ may occur. Now let what will call a capital-pair composed of two of these capital letters with a colon between the two and the pair enclosed in parentheses as $(A:I)$ or $(J:K)$ denote the operation of substituting the first letter of the capital-pair in place of the second. Thus
$$\{(A:I) + (J:K) - (K:A)\} [aA + bI + cJ + dK]$$
will convert the operand $aA + bI + cJ + dK$ into
$$-aK + bA + dJ$$
for $(A:I)J = (A:I)K = (A:I)A = 0$
but $(A:I)I = A$ etc.

Let us also assume (since we can assume any formulae for expressions not otherwise defined, so long as our assumptions do not conflict) that whatever capitals $P, Q, R, S$ may be $[(P:Q)R]:S = (P:Q)(R:S)$ so that if the last capital of a multiplier that is a capital pair is the first capital of the its multiplicand capital pair, as $(P:Q)(Q:S)$, this by the rule just given is $[(P:Q)Q]:S$ that is since $(P:Q)Q = P$ is $(P:S)$ so that the product is the capital pair of which the first capital of is that of the multiplier and of which the second capital is that of the multiplicand; while in the other alternative, namely, when the second capital of the multiplier capital pair and the first capital of the multiplicand capital pair are not the same, the product vanishes $(P:Q)(R:S) = 0$.
