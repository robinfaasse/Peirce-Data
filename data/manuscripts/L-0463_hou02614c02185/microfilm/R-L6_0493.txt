In regard to their relations to their objects signs may be divided in two ways, according to what object be considered. Considered with reference to its dynamic object, a sign is either an Icon, an Index, or a Symbol.

An Icon is a sign which represents its object by virtue of its affinity with it in its own internal nature simply. It may be a qualisign, or a sinsign similar or analogous to the object, or it may be a legisign the general type of something similar.
