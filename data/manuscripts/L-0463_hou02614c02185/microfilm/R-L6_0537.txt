My dear Mr. Peirce

I must have to thank you for the kind thought of sending me the number of the Monist with your remarks on Mr. Peterson's 'Discussion' and also for the review of Mr. Gore on Sir Thomas Browne's work. I found much interest.

I am sending you by this post a copy of the recently started "University Review" which is finding a considerable circulation; as you will see that it contains some readable [del]matter[/del] [ins]stuff[/ins].

Such an attractive photograph. People ask me if you are not intending to publish a book, [del]representative[/del] [ins]representative[/ins] of some portion of your life's work?

As for me I am mainly content with copying short letters & papers of [del]your[/del] [ins]your[/ins] friends.
