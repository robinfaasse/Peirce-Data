[pn]27[/pn] [del]Derivations of points in the barycentric calculus, which by the
way is the same as the combination of forces according to the
parallelogram of forces, — let us say, in general, barycentrically, —[/del]
is my discovery that any [ins]dyadic[/ins] relative may be put in the form

$$\begin{aligned}
& a_{11} (A\colon A) \mathbin{\pmb{\Psi}} a_{12} (A\colon B) \mathbin{\pmb{\Psi}} a_{13} (A\colon C) \mathbin{\pmb{\Psi}} \text{etc. through the universe} \\
\mathbin{\pmb{\Psi}} & a_{21} (B\colon A) \mathbin{\pmb{\Psi}} a_{22} (B\colon B) \mathbin{\pmb{\Psi}} a_{23} (B\colon C) \mathbin{\pmb{\Psi}} \text{etc} \quad \text{"} \qquad \text{"} \qquad \text{"} \\
\mathbin{\pmb{\Psi}} & a_{31} (C\colon A) \mathbin{\pmb{\Psi}} a_{32} (C\colon B) \mathbin{\pmb{\Psi}} a_{33} (C\colon C) \mathbin{\pmb{\Psi}} \text{etc} \quad \text{"} \qquad \text{"} \qquad \text{"} \\
& \text{etc} \\
& \text{through the universe}
\end{aligned}$$

when $A, B, C,$ are the monadic individuals of the universe
while $(A\colon B)$ is such a relative that $(A\colon B)B = A$ while
$(A\colon B)A = 0$ $(A\colon B)C = 0$ etc through the universe —

It is my discovery of this combined with Sylvester's subsequent
discovery (which, [ins]as[/ins] his airs seemed to say, took the wind out of my
sails, — and I may remark that mathematicians often indulge
in such airs and [ins]in[/ins] the ideas that lie behind the airs; but they
would be most despicable in a logician) — his discovery [ins]that[/ins]
[u]in general[/u] (a phrase which has a peculiar meaning in
mathematics, [del]which meaning[/del] I will explain presently,) —
that [u]in general[/u], I say, any expression such as
$$\begin{aligned}
& a_{11} (A\colon A) + a_{12} (A\colon B) + \text{etc} \\
{} + {} & a_{21} (B\colon A) + b_{22} (B\colon B) + \text{etc} \\
{} + {} & \text{etc}
\end{aligned}$$
