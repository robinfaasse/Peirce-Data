[marginalia]I am just beginning the crucial
effort of my life.
[date]Dec 31. 1911[/date][/marginalia]

Dear Mrs Peirce

I am getting to be very unhappy at
hearing nothing from your husband
for so sadly long a time: and it is
a great disappointment not to hear
anything of the [ins]promised[/ins] contribution to Prof.
Stout's book of Essays for which we
have so eagerly looked.

I am afraid it means that Mr.
Peirce is ill or suffering.

Forgive me these confessions, to you
and your husband and all the great
honour he has done me has been
so much to me! -

It would be a joy to hear and a
double joy to find that the promised
essay was on its way. With affectionate
wishes for you both I am yr faithful friend
Victoria Welby
