attacks which consist in my stiffening out and being
unable to move a muscle for perhaps a quarter of an
hour or less. When they first came on they came only
once in several months. But when they came, I would
fall like a stone without being able, in any way to lessen
the force of the fall. I thus got severely hurt several
times, & that made me timid. I then observed that the
