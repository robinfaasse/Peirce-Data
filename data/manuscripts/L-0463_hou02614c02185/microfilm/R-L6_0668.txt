But owing to the independence of the units, this cannot be unless
the two sums the one according to $q$ and the other according to $s$ are
equal term by term; so that, putting $s=q$,
$\sum_p [m,n,p] [l,p,q] = [del]\sum[/del] \sum_r [l,m,r] [r,n,q]$
which is another expression of the associative principle.
