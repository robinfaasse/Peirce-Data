My dear Lady Welby:

I could not content myself with answering your very kind inquiry on a Post-Card, and several days have elapsed before I could write this letter. The reason I have not written is somewhat complex. In all has been ultimately due to the need of repairs on the House to make it habitable for next winter & the consequent need of my getting together a considerable sum of money. For the real-estate agents assured us that by some further repairs than were required for mere habitability we should certainly be successful in disposing of the place, and have our choice
