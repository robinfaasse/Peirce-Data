To show you that I have taken them in, I will just jot down a gem or two, as fast as pen can scribble. — those only that linger in my mind: [u]Ex To Wit[/u] Man as translating vegetal and brute strength into intellectual and spiritual vigor — Good! — That word "translating" bears a volume in its womb! The one dated "All-Souls" contains [del]a truth[/del] [ins]truths[/ins] very familiar to me but that is far from being as familiar to men & women generally as it deserves to be. I haven't time to set it down. I will just jot what ought to show I enter into your thoughts. [u]Ethics[/u] I would teach my son, if I had one, is the study that will teach you to get what you desire. Just that. The first step is the one that costs. It consists in planning out, & not [del]yet[/del] how you shall contrive to get it, but what it is you really [u]do[/u] want. Because a large majority of successful men will testify that success, — just what they planned to get, — turned to dust & ashes in the mouth. So first make very sure indeed of what you want. It is a terrible mistake, I should say ruin, for a man to think he shall always like plum-pudding as well as he does the first mouthful.
