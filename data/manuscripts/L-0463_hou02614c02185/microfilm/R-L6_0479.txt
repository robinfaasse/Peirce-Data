My dear Lady Welby:

This is dreadful paper to write a letter on; but it happens that I have no other except small note paper by me. My stock needs replenishing; but it is hard to suit oneself without going personally to a shop, and here I am far away even from the most unsatisfactory shops. So I beg you to excuse the paper.

My Metric System paper has developed a very fundamental difference between us. You are a rationalist and a radical; while I am a conservative of the most convinced kind. Neither of us is likely to bring the other over, yet I think we had better have it out; for each of us is likely to learn something and to see more truth in the opposite view than we had
