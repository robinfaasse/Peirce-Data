I was so afraid of extending unduly
my already long letter, that I left
out something I now venture to
add. As I am now preparing for
a second edition of "Who is M?", may
I ask if you will add to your kindness
by indicating (even on a postcard)
those parts of the book which you
[del]will[/del] consider especially weak.
I might in some cases at least
be able either to suppress or
strengthen them.
I am especially glad of your
endorsement of my view of the
"primitive mind".

Yrs very [del]truly[/del]
W. B. R.

Harem, England.
[date]Nov 20 1903[/date]
