My dear Lady Welby— [u]Entre nous[/u], I am seriously thinking of selling this place at once and accepting a consulate in the tropics. I feel that life in the open air is my wife's only chance, and [del]besides[/del] [ins]then[/ins] the care of this rambling house is too much for her, especially as a speck of dust deranges her equanimity. Besides, small as the salary of an American consul is, I imperatively need the money. For the present I do not speak of this plan; but it may be executed very soon. I am thinking that if I go, I will try to take England on my way in order to see your Ladyship as well as those Oxford men [del]who ph[/del] whose philosophical opinions have taken a turn in my direction.
