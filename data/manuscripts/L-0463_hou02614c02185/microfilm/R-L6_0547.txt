I for the [u]Nation[/u], I have to put up
with [del]things[/del] spellings etc. excessively
contrary to my convictions, such as
[u]skeptic[/u] for [u]sceptic[/u], which former is
preferred because forsooth it agrees
with the pronunciation, which according
to me is the best of reasons for avoiding
it. For I do not want the delicate
earthen pot of the vernacular to be
so banged up against the iron pot of
print. As for the [u]Monist[/u], it is simply
what it is. Language cannot go further.
To find [u]definitive[/u] changed to [u]definite[/u]
specially enrages me.
