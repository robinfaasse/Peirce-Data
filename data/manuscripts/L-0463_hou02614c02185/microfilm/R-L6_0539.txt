My dear Lady Welby:

Thank you very much indeed for your pragmatoidal poem which falls in quite with my own way of thinking. I must say that it contains one unjustifiable slander; but I suppose you thought yourself privileged as to that. There is one word about which I have to make the humiliating confession that I do not understand it.

The truth is that our spoken dialect in America is the worst I know. In Germany, Italy, France (in parts)
