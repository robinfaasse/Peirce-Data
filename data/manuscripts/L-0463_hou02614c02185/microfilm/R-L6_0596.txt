[ins]also?[/ins] As to our use of the phrase 'belief in God' I [ins]sus-[/ins]expect that it betrays an un-

As for Argumentation it is becoming more and more a plague of futility, as any Critical
Journal witnesses. And Play too; which we so often connect, not with the Play of
cosmical forces or of controlled impetus, but with the mere random wandering, a sign of
mental disease, which no true Play can be.
