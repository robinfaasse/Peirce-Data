It is important to distinguish between the two functions of a word [ins]1[sup]st[/sup][/ins] to denote something - to stand for something and [ins]2[sup]d[/sup][/ins] to mean something - or as Mr. Mill phrases it - to connote something.

What it denotes is called its [u]Sphere[/u]. What it connotes is called its [u]Content[/u]. Thus the [u]sphere[/u] of the word [ins]for me[/ins] man is [del]every[/del] [del]man I know[/del]; and for each of you it is every man you know. The [u]content[/u] of man is all that we know of all men, as [del]being[/del] [del]two-legged, having souls, having language &c &c.[/del] It is plain that both the [u]sphere[/u] and the [u]content[/u] admit of more and less. Thus the [u]sphere[/u] of negro is less than the [u]sphere[/u] of man; while the [u]content[/u] of negro is greater than the [u]content[/u] of man, because it contains being black, which the latter does not. Now the sphere considered as a quantity, is called the [u]Extension[/u], and the content considered as quantity, is called the [u]Comprehension[/u]. Extension & Comprehension are also termed Breadth & Depth. So that a wider term is one which has a greater extension; & a narrower one is one which has a less extension. [pn]45[/pn]

A higher term is one which has a less Comprehension and a lower one has more.

The narrower term is said to be contained under the wider one; and the higher term to be contained in the lower one.

We have then

| [u]What is denoted[/u] | [u]What is connoted[/u] |
| Sphere | Content |
| Extension | Comprehension |
| Breadth [bracket] [ins]wider[/ins] [ins]narrower[/ins] | Depth [bracket] [ins]lower[/ins] [ins]higher[/ins] |
| [u]What is contained under[/u] | [u]What is contained in[/u]. |

The principle of explicatory or deductive reasoning then is that

Every part of [del]its[/del] [ins]a words[/ins] Content belongs to every part of its Sphere.
or Whatever is contained in a word belongs to whatever is contained [ins]under it[/ins],

Now this maxim would not be true if the Extension & Comprehension were directly proportional to one another this is to say, if the greater the one the greater the other. For in that case, though the whole Content would belong to the whole Sphere; yet only a [ins]particular[/ins] part of it would belong to a part.
