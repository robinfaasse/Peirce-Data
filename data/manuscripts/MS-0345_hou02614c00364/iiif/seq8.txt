It is important to distinguish between the two func-
tions of a word 1st to denote something — to stand for something and
2nd to mean something — or as Mr. Mill phrases it — to connote
something.

What it denotes is called its [u]Sphere[/u]. What it connotes
is called its [u]Content[/u]. Thus the sphere of the word [u]man[/u] is[ins]for me[/ins] every
man I know; and for each of you it is every man you know.
The [u]content[/u] of [u]man[/u] is all that we know of all men, as [del]being[/del]
two-legged, having souls, having language &c &c. It is plain
that both the [u]sphere[/u] and the [u]content[/u] admit of [u]more[/u] and [u]less[/u].
Thus the [u]sphere[/u] of [u]negro[/u] is [u]less[/u] than the [u]sphere[/u] of [u]man[/u]; while
the [u]content[/u] of [u]negro[/u] is [u]greater[/u] than the [u]content[/u] of [u]man[/u], because
it contains [u]being black[/u] which the latter does not. Now the
[u]sphere[/u] considered as a quantity is called the [u]Extension[/u], and
the [u]content[/u] considered as quantity is called the [u]Comprehension[/u].
Extension & Comprehension are also termed [u]Breadth[/u] & [u]Depth[/u].
So that a wider term is one which has a greater exten-
sion; a narrower one is one which has a less extension 45

[7]
A higher term is one which has a less Comprehension and
a lower one has more.

The narrower term is said to be contained [u]under[/u] the wider
one; and the higher term to be contained [u]in[/u] the lower one.

The principle of explicatory or deductive reasoning then
is that

Every part of its [ins]a words[/ins] Content belongs to every part of its Sphere.
or whatever is contained [u]in[/u] a word belongs to whatever is contained [ins]under it.[/ins]

Now this maxim would not be true if the Extension
& Comprehension were [del]directly[/del] proportional to one another
this is to say if the greater the one the greater the other.
For in that case, though the whole Content would belong to
the whole Sphere; yet only a [ins]particular[/ins] part of it would belong to a part.
