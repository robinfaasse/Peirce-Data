him; so that he would be available. A man of his capacity
who is at the same time absolutely trustworthy, as I have every
proof that he is, is not always easy to find. So I think it would
be very much to the interest of all of us to have him have some
place in the Power Co. where he would be useful, and at the
same time would be at hand if he were wanted for anything else.
I think he would be quite disposed to accept almost any business
position in that company which might be offered him, provided
it afforded a prospect of working into a place of some importance.
