Dear Sir:-

After careful examination of the enclosed drawings, we cannot see as to how this can become practicable for the following reason: as the water enters one compartment and drips upon the carbide, it will, of course, generate the gas all right, until the carbide in that chamber is exhausted, but by the time it is exhausted the water then, as we understand it, has to fill that chamber before it overflows into the next and considering the time it would take for the water to fill the chamber, the question arises, how is gas generated in the interim? This point is not clear to us and we should be glad to have you explain the matter.

Greatly obliging,

Yours very truly,
Stewart & Co
