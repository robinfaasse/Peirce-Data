New York, Oct. 6th, 1897.

CABLE ADDRESS
GARDYNE NEW YORK

Prof. C. S. Peirce, Esq.,
108 W. 89th St.,
City.

Dear Sir:-

Your favor of 5th received. At the present time I do not see that there would be any position vacant in the St. Lawrence Power Company, which would be available, as everything is now in the hands of the contractors and will be until December of next year. I would be pleased should occasion arise to keep you in mind.

As to investment in the Company's stock, I do not believe that there is any for sale at the present time, at least so understand it.

By the way, we have never received the balance of the report on Acetylene, and we hope you will find it convenient to send it soon, as that we now have in our possession, without being complete, is absolutely useless to us.

With kind regards,

Yours very truly,

[signature]
