84 Broad St. [date]1896 Dec 3[/date]

My dear Mr. Stewart:

Wishing to do something to earn the $25 while waiting for Mr. Bogart's calculations & for the business of analyzing the carbide etc. which you have promised me, I thought, as there was not enough to commence an analysis, the best thing I could do would be to collect facts relating to acetylene from [u]the original memoirs[/u] on the subject. It was discovered 60 years ago, and there have been at least 120 memoirs relating to it. I have read all of these for the first 40 of the 60 years and have made brief notes, filling 16 foolscap pages, thus obtaining much information not in any single book. I consider that this is a very large equivalent for the $25. Should you like to have me bring the work down to date, as there [del]are[/del] [ins]must[/ins] certainly be more papers to follow, I shall hold myself ready to do so.

Mr. Bogart's report is so much delayed that I suppose it may be assumed that he finds the 200000 HP quite impracticable. I trust Mr Crosby will soon be heard from, so that I can go ahead with the [del]at[/del] magnesian limestone investigation.

Yours very truly
C. S. Peirce
