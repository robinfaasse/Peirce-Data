Sirs :

YOU WILL PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that by virtue of an
agreement heretofore made between the undersigned and
John Edward Comte d'Aulby, N. S. Easton and Albert Bierstadt,
I am the owner of and entitled to receive a one-fifth share
of any stock of the St. Lawrence Power Co.[ins], of Massena, New York,[/ins] which may be
issued or may have been issued in payment for services of
said parties or any of them in placing the bonds of said
Company, and I hereby require that you will not part with
or dispose of said stock to any other person without my
consent in writing.

Dated New York, December [blank] 1896.

Yours, &c.,


To
The St. Lawrence Power Co.

Messrs. Stewart & Co.
