Prof. C. S. Pierce,

Dear Sir:-
Replying to your favor of 14th. I regret that the large demands made upon me at the present time, will not permit of my making the advance requested. The great burden that I am carrying in the Massena matter, for which I have not yet been reimbursed, strains my resources, otherwise I would be very glad to accomodate you. With kind regards.

Yours very truly,
