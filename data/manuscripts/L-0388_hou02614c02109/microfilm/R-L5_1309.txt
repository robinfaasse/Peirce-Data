Dear Sir:-

Replying to your favor of 20th, contents of which has our
careful consideration. Our reason for delaying matters in regard to the
perfection of machines has been on account of the fact that it became
quite evident to us that machines had absolutely no value unless we were
prepared to furnish calcium carbide at a price which would make it a
commercial commodity, and we have directed every energy towards perfect-
ing a process that would enable us to manufacture it more economically
than by any other system now known, and at the same time avoid infringe-
ment upon any other patents. This we have now accomplished and expect
to organize for its development very shortly. It will take us at least
ninety days or four months before we are prepared for business, even on
a small scale, and we have so advised Count D'Aulby, as our own corres-
pondents have written us that it would be folly to present the matter in
Europe without being fully equipped both by the use of machines and the
manufacture of the carbide.

Pending these developments we should be glad to to confer with
you at any time you may find it convenient to drop in at our office,
relative to the development of your special device, which to us at the
present moment, seems impracticable.

Very truly yours,
[u]Stewart & Co[/u]
