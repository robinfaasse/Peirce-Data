New York, April 10, 1897.

C. S. Pierce, Esq.,
84 Broad St.,
Jersey City, N.J.

My Dear Mr. Pierce:-

I think Mr. Easton explained to you personally what could be done in regard to the sale of some of the St. Lawrence Power Company's stock. You understand of course that there is none of this stock on the market and we only could make a transfer of it by private arrangement and as a favor to yourself. This refers to your proposition to purchase, of course,

Very truly yours,

Stewart & Co

K.O.E.
