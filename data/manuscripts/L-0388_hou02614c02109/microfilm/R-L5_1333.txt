Memorandum that it has heretofore been agreed between the
undersigned, Nelson H. Easton, Albert Bierstadt, and Charles S.
Peirce that all stocks and moneys received by any of us on account of
using our endeavors to place the bonds of the St. Lawrence Water Power
Company of Massena New York shall be equally divided between us. But it
is this day agreed that if one hundred thousand dollars of stocks
is received, Mr. Easton shall have forty thousand and Messrs Bierstadt
and Peirce thirty thousand each. In testimony whereof we have all set
our hands this [date]4th day of January, 1897[/date], in the City of New York
