To make the expression homogeneous put
$$w_x = \frac{u_x}{v_x}$$

so that
$$\frac{u_{x+1}}{v_{x+1}} = \frac{a u_x + b v_x}{c u_x + d v_x}$$

Further assume
$$u_{x+1} = a u_x + b v_x$$
$$v_{x+1} = c u_x + d v_x$$

or
$$(E - a) u_x = b v_x$$
$$(E - d) v_x = c u_x$$

Then we have for either $u_x$ or $v_x$
$$(E - a)(E - d) = bc$$

Let $\varepsilon_1$ and $\varepsilon_2$ be the roots of this equation. Then we have
$$u_x = C_1 \varepsilon_1^x + C_2 \varepsilon_2^x$$
$$v_x = B_1 \varepsilon_1^x + B_2 \varepsilon_2^x$$
