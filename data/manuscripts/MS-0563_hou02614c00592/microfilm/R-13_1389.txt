The relation of the B's to the C's is
determined by the equations
$C_1 (\mathcal{E}_1 - a) = b B_1$     $C_2 (\mathcal{E}_2 - a) = b B_2$

or, what is the same,
$B_1 (\mathcal{E}_1 - d) = c C_1$     $B_2 (\mathcal{E}_2 - d) = c C_2$

Then write
$\alpha = \frac{C_1 b}{C_2}$     $\beta = b$

$\gamma = (\mathcal{E}_1 - a) \frac{C_1}{C_2}$     $\delta = \mathcal{E}_2 - a$

$e^i = \frac{\mathcal{E}_1}{\mathcal{E}_2}$
$- \log e^i = \theta$

and we have

[del]$W_x = \frac{\alpha e^x + \beta}{\gamma e^x + \delta}$[/del] = $\frac{\alpha e^{ix} + \beta e^{-ix}}{\gamma e^{ix} + \delta e^{-ix}}$

$W_x = \frac{\alpha + \beta \cos \theta x + \dots}{\dots}$

This is [del]evidently[/del] of course periodic. The
condition of the period being real would
easily be found.

$u_{x+1} = a u_x + b v_x$
$v_{x+1} = c u_x + d v_x$
