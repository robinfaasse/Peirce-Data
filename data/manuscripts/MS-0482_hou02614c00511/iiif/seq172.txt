Rule II. Given two medads, if an unencircled bond of each be
broken, and the broken ends of the one be joined to those of the other,
the veritability of the result will be as high as one or other of the
given medads.
Thus, if the medads of Fig. 15 be both true, so is that of Fig. 16.
