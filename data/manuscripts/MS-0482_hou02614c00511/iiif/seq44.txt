Rule VII. Derived Illative Transformations.
[u]Contraposition.[/u]
[fig]figure[/fig] 1. If any transformation is illatively valid under even enclo-
sures, the reverse transformation is valid under odd enclosures, and
vice versa.
[fig]figure[/fig] 2. [u]Reassociation.[/u] Under {[del]even[/del] [ins]odd[/ins]} enclosures, any graph
can be extracted from within double enclosure; and under {[del]odd[/del] [ins]even[/ins]}
enclosures, any graph can be introtracted into [ins]a[/ins] double enclosure.
[fig]figure[/fig] 3. [u]Segmentation and Fusion.[/u] Under {[del]even[/del] [ins]odd[/ins]} enclosures, two
circles one within another may be each twice [del]broken[/del] [ins]severed[/ins], and each
part of each [del]drawn[/del] [ins]extended[/ins] so as to close it and form two double enclosures;
and under {[del]odd[/del] [ins]even[/ins]} enclosures, two pairs of circles, one circle of each
pair enclosed in the other, may be severed and reclosed so as to
form one [ins]such[/ins] pair of circles.
