§9. The fundamental illative transformations of graphs seem to
be six pairs of opposite processes which may be named as follows:
[u]1st Pair[/u]: Contraposition, consisting Double Encircling and Mutual
Annullment;
[u]2nd Pair[/u]: Insertion and Omission of graphs;
[u]3rd Pair[/u]: Identification and Diversification of subjects, by Joining
and Severing graphs;
[u]4th Pair[/u]: Reassociation; composed of Involution and Evolution;
[u]5th Pair[/u]: Fusion and Segmentation of circles;
[u]6th Pair[/u]: Development and Elimination.
It is possible some of these processes can be shown to be derivative.
