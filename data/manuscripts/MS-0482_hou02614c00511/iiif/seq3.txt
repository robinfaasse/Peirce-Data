Besides the Listing theorem, the following second census-proposition holds of valental graphs. The valency of a graph equals the sum of the valencies of its spots less twice the number of connecting bonds. It follows, as a corollary, that no perissid can be composed exclusively of artiads; and this constitutes a fundamental distinction between artiads and perissids.
Between Listing's theorem and this second census-proposition the number of connecting bonds may be eliminated.
