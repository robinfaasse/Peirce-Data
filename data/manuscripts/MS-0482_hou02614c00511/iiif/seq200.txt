The second proposition going to define being smaller than is,

[fig]diagram[/fig] Nothing is smaller than itself.

The third proposition completing the formal definition of being smaller than is, (in a universe of magnitudes)

[fig]diagram[/fig] Take any two magnitudes, then either one is smaller than the other or they are one identical magnitude.

I now proceed to define the [del]set[/del] series of whole numbers. The universe is supposed to be a universe of whole numbers.

1st [fig]diagram[/fig] No number is smaller than the number zero.

2nd [fig]diagram[/fig] Every number has a number greater than it, but not greater than any third number intermediate between them. That is, there is a number [u]next[/u] greater than any given number.

3rd [fig]diagram[/fig] The jagged line refers to the universe of characters.

[fig]diagram[/fig] Every character that is possessed by a number is possessed by some number that is [ins]identical with or[/ins] greater than every number that possesses that character.
