[ins]Contraposition.[/ins]
¶ 1. If any transformation is illatively valid under even enclosures, the reverse transformation is valid under odd enclosures, and vice versa.

¶ 2. [u]Reassociation[/u]. Under {[del]odd[/del] [ins]even[/ins]} enclosures, any graph can be extracted from within double enclosure; and under {[del]even[/del] [ins]odd[/ins]} enclosures, any graph can be introtracted into double enclosure.
