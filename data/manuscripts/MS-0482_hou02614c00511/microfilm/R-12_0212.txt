[u]Segmentation[/u] is substantially the illative passage from denying
a copulative or disjunctive combination of propositions to asserting
the [del]contrary[/del] [ins]opposite[/ins] combination of the denials of those propositions, while
[u]Fusion[/u] is the illative passage from treating them severally to
treating them in combination.
