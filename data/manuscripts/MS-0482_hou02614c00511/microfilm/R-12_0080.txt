[u]Thirdly[/u], when a monad of nullity is bonded to a triad of diversity, the meaning is the same as if that monad and its bond were suppressed, thus destroying the triad. Hence, the monad and bond are a meaningless addition and should be omitted. Thus, Fig. 51 has the same meaning as Fig. 11. Thus, the dyad of non-unity is a mere ordinary place on a bond, [del]and the triad of non-unity[/del] is a mere node of point of furcation.
