[u]Description of the Graphical Method of Representing Relations[/u].

A [u]graph[/u] is a diagram composed of [u]spots[/u] and [u]bonds[/u], or simple lines, of each of which one end, at least, connects with a spot, the other end being either loose or connected with a spot.

A [u]valental[/u] graph is a graph from each spot of which proceeds a number of bonds [del]depending[/del] [ins]determined[/ins] solely by the inherent nature, or [u]valency[/u], of that spot; and the different bonds proceeding from a spot are distinguished as proceeding from different parts of the spot.
