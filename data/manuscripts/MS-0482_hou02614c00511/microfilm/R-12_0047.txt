[u]Reassociation[/u] is the inferential step whereby the groupings of terms are changed without change in the order of their connection. Thus, in algebra, from [del]to + b[/del] [u]x = (a+b)+c[/u] we pass illatively to [u]x = a + (b+c)[/u]. But this is a complex operation, consisting, first, in inserting something in one group and second, in removing it from the other.
