Of course, though the relatives of non-unity can be omitted, the circles

Circles have two effects, first, their effects upon the grouping, and
second, their negativing effect. The latter extends not merely to
the spots but also to the bonds. Namely, a connecting bond both
ends of which are within one circle is negatived by that circle. When
two circles [ins]one[/ins] [del]immediately[/del] outside the other surround precisely the same
graph, they destroy one another's effect. Thus, if from the medad of
Fig. 38 the dot be omitted, we get Fig. 39; and the two circles may as
