$$\Sigma_i \Pi_j (u_i v_j) = \Pi_j \Sigma_i (u_i v_j)$$

For
$$\Sigma_i \Pi_j (u_i v_j) = \Sigma_i u_i (\Pi_j v_j)$$
$$= \Sigma_i u_i \cdot \Pi_j v_j$$
$$= \Pi_j \{ \Sigma_i u_i \cdot v_j \}$$
$$= \Pi_j \Sigma_i (u_i \cdot v_j)$$

And in a similar way
$$\Sigma_i \Pi_j (u_i + v_j) = \Pi_j \Sigma_i (u_i + v_j)$$

Having in this way got [del]our[/del] [ins]those[/ins] [del]subscripts together[/del] operational symbols together which needed to be brought together, we have to make their subscripts the same.

We shall, for the present, suppose that the two universes are the same; though one letter is distinguished from the other by an accent.
