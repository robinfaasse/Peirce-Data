To deal with this we introduce
the [del]sub[/del] term $0$ and we will
write
$$\Pi_{j} (l_{ij} + 0_{jk}) = (l \dagger 0)_{ik}$$
meaning that $i$ is a lover
of everything coexistent with
$k$. That this is the meaning
is easily seen for if there be any
thing coexistent with $k$ of which
$i$ is not said to be the lover
let $j$ be this thing then $l_{ij} = 0$
But $0_{jk} = 0$ hence the factor vanishes
and the whole expression is ab-
surd. We have then
$$\Pi_{i} \Pi_{v} u_{iv} = \Pi_{i} (u \dagger 0)_{ii}$$
