My dear Prof. Moore:

I wish you every success and happiness for the year 1904.

It may be you have written a second time about my argument. But I have received nothing. I am curious to see your comments. I now write to communicate another proof. But first I will explain the terminology I use.

<u>Collection</u> is Cantor's <u>Menge</u>. He professes to define it, using a method which is a favorite of Kant's. But that method produces no definition at all. A definition should be an explicit statement of what difference it makes to affirm or deny the definition. A mathematical definition states what difference it makes to the course of mathematical demonstration.
