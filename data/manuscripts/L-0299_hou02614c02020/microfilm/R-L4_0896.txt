[pn]The University of Chicago[/pn]

[date]Dec. 8, 1903[/date]

My dear Dr. Peirce,
I hope you will promptly send in an affirmative vote on the Osgood National Academy nomination. His election will depend largely upon how nearly unanimously our section supports him in this initial vote.

Yours very sincerely,
E. H. Moore
