the relation $\alpha$ to $j$ for $i$’. $\Pi_x$ means ‘Take any individual you
like, and call it $x$’, $\Sigma_y$ means ‘There is an individual which I
will call $y$’. The operator precedes the operand Thus $\Pi_\alpha \Sigma_i q_{\alpha i}$

the character $\alpha$; while $\Sigma_i \Pi_\alpha q_{\alpha i}$ means ‘There is an individu-

sion of predication negatives it. Thus $\Sigma_\alpha \Pi_i \bar{q}_{\alpha i}$ means ‘There is
