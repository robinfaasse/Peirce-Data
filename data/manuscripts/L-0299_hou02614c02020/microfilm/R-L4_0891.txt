The University of Chicago
[date]August 13, 1902[/date]

My dear Dr. Peirce,
Pardon me!! The books are leaving today:
Klein's report on the first award of the Lobat. prize (to Lie) (I will send the Lie books if you wish);
Killing's Grundlagen der Geometrie,
Whitehead's Universal Algebra,
between whom second award was divided;
Hilbert's Grundlagen der [del]Euclidischen[/del] Geometrie (Euclidischen).

These I think are the most noteworthy of the recent writings.
Please tell me the best source of information concerning your work on the Algebra of Relatives; how far do you approve of Schröder's exposition?
Pray bear in mind the thought of writing out your work on the Cantor theories and on the lines leading to Topical Geometry.
With cordial greetings, I am
Yours very sincerely
E H Moore
