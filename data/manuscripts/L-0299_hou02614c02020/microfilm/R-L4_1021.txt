Milford Pa [date]1904 Nov 21[/date]

My dear Professor Moore:

As this letter will be a long one, I shall append a table of contents. This will enable you to read parts which I shall mark in the table by pointing hands thus [fig]pointing hand[/fig] before you have time to read the whole, though I hope the whole will have some interest for you. I begin with a sketch of a paper I gave the Academy last week. [ins]which I call ordinals here (without defending this name)[/ins]

My paper gave two definitions of the system of whole numbers, Now the object is to analyze the properties of the system into as many independent characters as possible.

[u]Ordinal[/u]. A fine real positive integer number. I do not defend this def.
