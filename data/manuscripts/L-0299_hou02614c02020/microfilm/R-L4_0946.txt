lection, but with a difference I proceed to
explain. Given any character, whatever
or monadic rheme, whatever, there is a
collection of whatever there may be in
the universe that possesses that character.
I call a collection in that sense a [u]sam[/u].
The word is formed by dropping the [u]e[/u] from
[u]same[/u]. It reminds one of German [u]Sammlung[/u]
and of Latin [u]simul[/u]. The identity of the sam
is not altered by finding more or fewer
objects of which the monadic rheme is
true. Consequently, there is a sam of
nothing. The sam is thus an [u]ens rationis[/u],
and therefore the sam of suns, when there
is but one sun, is not identical with that
sun, for this need not be an [u]ens rationis[/u]
