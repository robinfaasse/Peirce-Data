But I say the sam of nothing is a single
sam and does not exist. The sam of the
sun (there being but a single sun) is not
identical with the sun in the heavens, I
also speak of a gath meaning that whose
existence consists in the existence of the
members of a sam, and which has no
possibility except existence, and no
reference to the characters of the
objects. Its identity consists in the iden-
tity of the members of it. There is thus
no gath of nothing, and the gath
of the sun is the very sun itself.

A collection I call [u]enumerable[/u] if the syllogism of
transposed quantity is valid of it.
Every woman is preferred by some woman above all others
$\therefore$ Every woman prefers some woman to all others.

A collection not enumerable, I call [u]innumerable[/u]
or [u]infinite[/u].
