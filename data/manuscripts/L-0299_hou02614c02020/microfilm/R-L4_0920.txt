[date]Dec. 31, 1903[/date]

My dear Dr. Peirce,

Your letters of [date]Dec. 23"[/date] and [date]29"[/date] are at hand. As I think you know, the Cantor theorem is one of much interest to me, and I find your considerations very suggestive indeed.
