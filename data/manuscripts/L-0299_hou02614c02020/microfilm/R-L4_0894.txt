The University of Chicago
[date]Dec. 21, 1902[/date].

My dear Dr. Peirce =
I have your letter with pleasure, and regret however, that you seem to prefer to hold back the publication of the mathematical-logical work for the whole series - I am strongly in favor of having you put out such series under auspices of the Carnegie Institution, and, as I have written you, I have no doubt the Carnegie Trustees will be very anxious to do this as soon as you can convince them that the work is really practically finished - Why don't you simply finish a first memoir and offer it to them or else to the Transactions through me?

[marginalia]
Let me have this memoir for the Transactions -
