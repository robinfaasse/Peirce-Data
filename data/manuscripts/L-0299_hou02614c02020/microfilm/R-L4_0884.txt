TRANSACTIONS OF THE
AMERICAN MATHEMATICAL SOCIETY

CHICAGO, [date]May 1, 1902[/date].

Dr. Charles S. Peirce,
Milford, Pennsylvania.

Dear Dr. Peirce:-
I recall our conversations in Washington and in particular your remarks concerning the transfinite cardinal numbers obtained by a certain sequential powering of the base 2, and also your suggestions which led me to understand that you had obtained a system of axioms suitable for the establishment of an abstract topological geometry. It would give me much pleasure if you would prepare on these subjects two articles for The Transactions. I should, of course, be pleased to receive other mathematical articles from you.

With best greetings,

Yours truly,
E H Moore
