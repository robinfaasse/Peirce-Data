TRANSACTIONS OF THE
AMERICAN MATHEMATICAL SOCIETY

CHICAGO [date]July 22, 1902[/date]

My dear Dr. Peirce,
Your MS is at hand. I thank you. I have not had time to read it critically. The series certainly seems to be of interest. May I note down a number of ways in which by slight amplification the work of the reader will be much simplified?

[u]1)[/u] P2 The theorem: [ins]"if $2^n$ be ...", then[/ins] "Every rational fraction .... $N(P - 2^{\frac{m}{n}}) / Np$, there ...." This $m$ should be $n$? The explanatory clause "if $2^n$ be ..." would confuse less, i.e., explains better, if set after the formula?

The proof is omitted. Even though obvious it might better be inserted, since the preceding has been so sudden a plunge into the defin-ition of the series.

[u]2)[/u] It should be said that "table" P5 yields series when it is read line by line, the arrangement being by the $n$ and $m$ of $P$, $n$ vertical, $m$ horizontal.
