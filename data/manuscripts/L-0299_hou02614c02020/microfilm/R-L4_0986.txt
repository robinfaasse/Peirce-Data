sams of pairs [ins]each[/ins] of an [u]m[/u] and an [u]n[/u].

In each sam each [u]m[/u] is paired to
a sam of [u]n[/u]s. Call that sam of [u]n[/u]s the
'[u]n[/u]-sam' of that [u]m[/u].

Suppose that in every one of the sams
of pairs, some sam of [u]n[/u]s is not the [u]n[/u]-sam
of any [u]m[/u].

Suppose that in every one of the
sams of pairs some [u]m[/u] has not the
sam of [ins]any[/ins] single [u]n[/u] as its [u]n[/u]-sam.
