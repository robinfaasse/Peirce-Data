But while every such statement is a definition of the system, yet
the purpose of a definition is to make the different properties of the
system distinct, and the further the analysis is carried the better.
The aim should be to make as many distinct clauses stating indepen-
dent relations as possible; though in some cases there may a limit
to this. Of course, if the objects had any other being than as creations of
thought, and were N in number, there would be N independent charac-
ters, and many of these would be of no present interest, and their state-
ment could only be confusing. But with creations of thought this is not
usually, if ever, the case. It should be the intention to make the different
clauses of the definition as independent as possible; but I do not pro-
pose to take any elaborate pains to avoid implying anything in one
clause that is implied in another, but leave the definitions to be
revised so as to effect this object.
