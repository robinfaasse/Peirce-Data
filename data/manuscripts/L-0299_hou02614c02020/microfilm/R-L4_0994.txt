My dear Professor Moore:

§1. This letter being a long one, I shall divide it into sections numbered so that § m, n is the n[ins][u]th[/u][/ins] division of the m[ins][u]th[/u][/ins] section [ins]etc.[/ins] I will append a table of contents. In this table I mark with a pointing hand (thus: [fig]pointing hand[/fig]) certain parts that I should like to have you read even if you have not time to read the rest, though I shall not [del]select[/del] insert anything that I do not think will prove interesting to you, unless it is something that I particularly desire to bring to your attention and therefore mark [fig]pointing hand[/fig].

§2. As you urged me some time ago to draw up some mathematico-logical paper, I now propose to sketch the contents of a paper I gave the Academy last week. The whole thing is completely worked out & I will explain the obstacles to my writing it out & printing it, with a suggestion of how you might perhaps help me to do so.

§ 2, 2. The paper contains two definitions of the system of positive integer numbers.

[u]Definition of Ordinal[/u]. By an [u]ordinal[/u] I mean a positive integer number. I do not defend the designation. But having nothing to do in this paper with Cantor's ordinal numbers, it is convenient here to use this word in this sense.
