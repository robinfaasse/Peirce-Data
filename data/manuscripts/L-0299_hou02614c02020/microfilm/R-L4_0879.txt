[marginalia]VB3b#3[/marginalia] Northwestern University,
428 Sheridan Road, Evanston, Ill.
[date]Feb. 25, 1892[/date]

Dear Sir -
In the New York Mathematical Society
Bulletin of December, 1891, is a mention
of a paper by you before the National Academy
of Sciences, concerning astronomical methods
of investigation of question of negative
curvature of space. The matter is
one in which I have considerable
interest, and if or when the paper is
published, I should consider it a
great favor if you would kindly
send me a copy.

I am, with high respect,
Yours truly,
E. H. Moore
