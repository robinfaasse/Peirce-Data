The principle that if of a class of propositions each one is possible in every possible state of the others as to truth & falsity, then all are possible simultaneously, is a corollary from the definition of possibility.

A sam is an individual object whose essential being consists in the possibility of the existence of whatever [del]individuals[/del] [ins]sams[/ins] may be of a given description. Thus white is a description. Anything that is white is an individual of that description. The fact that there [del]may[/del] [ins]can[/ins] be white things constitutes the being of the general. It is a general possibility, but in addition it constitutes the possibility, or essential being, of an individual object which is the same whatever things are actually white, or even if it happens that none is white.
