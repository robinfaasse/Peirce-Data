My dear Professor Moore:

Wishing you a merry Christmas &
a prosperous new year, I add a bit to
my last letter.

positions each one is possible in every
possible determination of the others as
to truth and falsity, then all are simul-
taneously possible, is a corollary from
definitions of possibility and simultane-
ity. It is necessary, in applying it, to
beware of certain fallacies. Three kinds
