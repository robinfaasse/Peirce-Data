This the the story of a
mountain that I saw when I
went to Lake Geneva. It was
a very beautiful sight.
One morning when we were 
taking a walk on the bank of
Lake Geneva we saw a beautiful
mountain on the other side of
the lake. It was a very
pretty sight. It was a high
mountain with a cap of
snow on top. This mountain
is famous and people come
from all parts of the world to
see it. The name of this mountain
was Mont Blanc. We saw 
the mountain again the next
morning and then we never saw
it any more because it was
covered with clouds.
This mountain is located in the 
South of France.
It is the highest mountain in 
Europe. It is 15781 feet high.
I thought it was a very
fine sight and I will never
forget it.
