COLUMBIA UNIVERSITY
NEW YORK

DEPARTMENT OF
PHILOSOPHY AND PSYCHOLOGY

[date]December 18th, 1903.[/date]

Mr. Charles S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

You have doubtless heard from Professor Cattell that I have undertaken the editorship of the Journal of Philosophy, Psychology and Scientific Methods, proposed by him in a recent letter. I hope that you will give me the support in this enterprise which you would have given him, and help to assure the success of the new Journal. It is expected that the first number will appear on January 1st, 1904.

I hope you will be able to contribute to the early numbers. An article of from 2000 to 3000 words on some topic of your special interest, or a critical review, of about the same length, of a recent book is suggested. If you could send in copy at once it would be a particular accommodation.

I should especially appreciate something dealing with any new material you may have in hand, bearing your interesting philosophy of Tychism.

Very truly yours,
Frederick J. E. Woodbridge
