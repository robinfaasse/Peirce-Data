As to the rapidity of the convergence, in the case of quadratic equations, the rule coincides with that of the solution by continued fractions, which is well-known to be the most rapidly convergent possible. But generally speaking, one can only say that it gives an indefinitely close approximation, at last. It cannot even be said that in all cases the last approximation is closer than the one which preceded it. It is usually very rapid; yet in some cases is most provokingly slow; so that it had better then be abandoned in favor of the differential [del]pro[/del] method.
