$10 \times \frac{1}{16} = 0 \frac{10}{16}$, $10 \times \frac{10}{16} = \frac{100}{16} = 6 \frac{4}{16}$, $10 \times \frac{4}{16} = \frac{40}{16} = 2 \frac{8}{16}$, $10 \times \frac{8}{16} = \frac{80}{16} = 5 \frac{0}{16}$, $10 \times \frac{0}{16} = 0 \frac{0}{16}$

$\therefore \frac{1}{16}$ decimally expressed is $\cdot 0 6 2 5 \dot{0} \dot{0} \dot{0}$

$6 \times \frac{1}{16} = 0 \frac{6}{16}$, $6 \times \frac{6}{16} = \frac{36}{16} = 2 \frac{4}{16}$, $6 \times \frac{4}{16} = \frac{24}{16} = 1 \frac{8}{16}$, $6 \times \frac{8}{16} = \frac{48}{16} = 3 \frac{0}{16}$, $6 \times \frac{0}{16} = 0 \frac{0}{16}$

$\therefore \frac{1}{16}$ sextally expressed is $\cdot 0 2 1 3 \dot{0}$

The following is an example involving a [del]center[/del] circulating fraction in [del]both of the[/del] two systems (but I have something to communicate about circulating fractions, to which I think I will devote [del]my next chapter[/del] [ins]another[/ins]). Required to express $\frac{1}{7}$ decimally and sextally.

$10 \times \frac{1}{7} = 1 \frac{3}{7}$, $10 \times \frac{3}{7} = 4 \frac{2}{7}$, $10 \times \frac{2}{7} = 2 \frac{6}{7}$, $10 \times \frac{6}{7} = 8 \frac{4}{7}$, $10 \times \frac{4}{7} = 5 \frac{5}{7}$, $10 \times \frac{5}{7} = 7 \frac{1}{7}$. Now $\frac{1}{7}$ is the original fraction. Hence, $\frac{1}{7}$ decimally expressed $= \dot{1} 4 2 8 5 \dot{7}$

$6 \times \frac{1}{7} = 0 \frac{6}{7}$, $6 \times \frac{6}{7} = 5 \frac{1}{7}$. Hence, $\frac{1}{7}$ sextally expressed $= \dot{0} \dot{5}$
