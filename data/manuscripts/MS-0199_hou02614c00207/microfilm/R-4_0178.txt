N.B. It is here to be explained that in secundal arithmetic each secundal place bears, and is designated by, the exponent of the power of two that a 1 in that place denotes. Thus, in 101.001, whose decimal equivalent is 5.125, the first one is in the place called "the +2 place;" that is to say, the $2^2$ place; the second 1 is in the place called "the 0-place" that is to say, the unit place; and the third 1 is in the place called "the [del]minus[/del] [ins]-3[/ins]-place," that is to say, the $2^{-3}$ place.
