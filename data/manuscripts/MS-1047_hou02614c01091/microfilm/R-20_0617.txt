In analysis by the moist way, the body in solution is
mixed with reagents and the resulting phenomena upon
which we depend are 1. effervescence 2 precipitation
3 smell & color.

In analysis by the dry way, the substance is heated either
alone or in conjunction with reagents in the flame
of the lamp (in Preliminary examination & in Spectrum
analysis) in that of the blowpipe (blowpipe analysis)
by electric sparks (in Spectrum analysis). The
results are either 1 phenomena of colour & light
2 General form & appearance 3 chemical properties.

Lately, Bunsen & Kirchhoff have originated the idea of
passing this light through a prism. Pure white light can
thus be analyzed into a spectrum, as in the rainbow
& why could not this be applied to these colored lights.
The experiment has been tried & it has been found that the
spectrum of the different bodies are extremely different.
That of Potassium, for instance, has a spectrum about
half as long as the solar spectrum containing principally
the spectrum of
the red & blue colors at both ends. Sodium on the con-

the spectrum of course, that is along the rainbow) The spectra
