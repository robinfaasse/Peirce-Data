We have seen that Quantitative Analysis must be
the proof or criterion of the existence of a new element.
It can hardly, however, discover an element - the sug-
gestion must arise from some peculiarity of proper-
ties. That systematic examination of bodies with a
view to ascertaining their constituents, which takes cog-
nizance only of their physical & chemical proper-
ties without nice weighing & measuring is called
Qualitative Analysis. Qualitative Analysis, there-
fore, is to Quantitative, as [del]something[/del] [ins]invention[/ins] is to logic,
and is much the more fascinating pursuit.
