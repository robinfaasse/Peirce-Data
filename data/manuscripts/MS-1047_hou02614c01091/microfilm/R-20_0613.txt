[pn]No 2[/pn] [u]Chemical Method.[/u]

To discover the true method of science we must
know distinctly what science is. Last time, we
agreed that it was the study of the immaterial in
the material. And that this was divisible into the
study of forms & the study of forces. But are the
semblances of clouds to lions, camels, & whales, which
are certainly forms, & the sequence of defeat to
an attack, [del]or[/del] [ins]or[/ins] of being drowned (for little boys)
to swimming on a particular day of the week, which
is a force of course, matters of science? and if not
why not? No, because they are imaginary, you will
say. But even if all matters of this kind were false,
which I do not admit, still they are forms & forces
in the imagination & they are derived from the
Material; why then are they not matters of science? Of
course they are not. Nature is a book which science
interprets, and yet all its poetry which is a form
and all its fables which is a force are foreign to science.
