To me, a manifestation external is no more surprising than
my own consciousness which is a manifestation internal.
There is this which is unexplained (not always to be so,
I think) the connection between volitions & actions. This
is puzzling, because the volition & the action are not the
same force but two quite different manifestations. Yet even
here the difficulty is not that no possible explanation supports
itself but that we do not know which explanation to take.

[u]HEAT.[/u]

Heat is the only force of nature which has been recog-
nized as expansive. Heat expands all bodies. Even
such things as bricks, wood &c which contract in the
oven, do so by virtue of losing a part of their sub-
stance in the form of steam.

The conception of force thus becomes perfectly clear. I will
now say one word about the law of Causation. It is an
axiom that no manifestation of life can take place through
an event which will not take time, for every motion, change,
event, takes time. Even if that which is manifested is a-
absolute unit, its manifestation must spread over time.
For how much time is indeterminable; therefore, over all time
equally. The law of causation, therefore, is the simplest con-
dition of any manifestation.

Perhaps you will think that the endowment of matter
with life is occult. I cannot see this, as we know nothing
of matter except that it is that which manifests life to us.
