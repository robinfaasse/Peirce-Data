[pn]CHEMISTRY.[/pn]

Chemistry is the science of kinds of matter. I shall
devote this number to the illustration of this.

As you know every natural science belongs
to one of two great divisions; it a branch
either of Natural Philosophy or of Natural History.
Natural Philosophy, more properly called Physics,
is the study of forces. If I ask why a top spins,
I ask a question in physics; because [del]I call an[/del] [ins]"What[/ins]
force, acting how, makes it turn round and stand
up?" Optics the study of light, Acoustics the study
of sound, Mechanics the study of those forces
which produce motion, Electricity, in short
any study of a peculiar force is a branch of
physics. Now there are certain forces with which
chemistry alone deals.
