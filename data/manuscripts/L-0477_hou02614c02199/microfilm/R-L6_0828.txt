Dr. Charles S. Peirce,
Milford,
Penn..

My dear Sir:

I remember some years ago you did me the honor to interest yourself in a certain publication of mine called "Heredity in Royalty" and so now I am taking the liberty of sending you another book which has just appeared, "The Influence of Monarchs". It is along the same lines, the application of scientific method to the philosophy of history.

In the somewhat lonesome labour along this path, I have often been encouraged and flattered by rereading your kind letters in which you commend the logic of my work and state that my book "ought to make an epoch". It has not made any epoch yet, as far as I know, but I am still plugging at the idea which seems to be slowly taking hold, and is meeting with some decided encouragement, and also what is not so bad, considerable decided contempt, misunderstanding and opposition.

With regard to "Heredity in Royalty", you kindly volunteered to write a review; but I am afraid the matter afterwards escaped your interest, or at any rate I never got any notice of a review.
