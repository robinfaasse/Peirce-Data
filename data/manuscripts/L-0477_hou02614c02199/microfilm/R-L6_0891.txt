eat and drink. Royce said that in the old days
in Cambridge if Peirce got hold of five dollars
he would hurry to Boston, and have the best
dinner and bottle of Burgundy possible.

I remember Professor Sedgwick told me one
day that he had just met Charles Peirce coming
out of a leading grocery store in Copley Square.
They stopped for a moment and Peirce said "I
came here to get some Rocquefort cheese. This
is the only place to get Rocquefort cheese." As
