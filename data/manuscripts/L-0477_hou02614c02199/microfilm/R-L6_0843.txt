The circumstance that at the age of 12 I did not know the meaning of the word "logic" even vaguely, although my father was a mathematician, leads me to mention that he had a low opinion of the utility of formal logic, and wished to draw directly upon the geometrical instinct rather than upon logical deductions from the smallest number of axioms. This disposition of his is mani-fest in his "Analytic Mechanics" of 1852 and still more in his interesting text-book of Elementary Geometry ([date]1837[/date]). This was one of a number of allied points in which I have always disagreed with my father, mainly because I do not think instinct is an absolutely infallible source of truth.
