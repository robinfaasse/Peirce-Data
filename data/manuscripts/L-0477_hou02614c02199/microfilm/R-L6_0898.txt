153 RIVERSIDE DRIVE
NEW YORK

This envelope contained the first letter
that Charles S. Peirce ever wrote
to me. I lent it to a friend
to have it copied. He lost it.
As near as I can recall
the letter was substantially the
following. I read it many times
and so remember it pretty well.
Peirce was staying at the St Botolph
Club at the time, attending a meeting
of the National Academy of Sciences.
He happened to come across a
copy of my Heredity & Royalty
