Dr. C. S. Peirce. 2.

I should not wish that your time should be taken up without recompense, so that if it should be for some journal that does not pay for such reviews, I should wish to pay for it myself.

I know that it is suggesting a good deal that you should pause in your own work to write a review, and I should never think of doing so if you had not so graciously volunteered to furnish the other upon "Heredity in Royalty".

The part upon which I especially want your printed opinion, is the validity of the idea called "a paradox of historiometry", pages 21-36, and also as much as possible on Chapters XVII-XVIII.

[del]If you can not give so much time as this, perhaps you will[/del] allow me to quote something written in a letter.

With highest esteem, believe me

Very truly yours,

Frederick Adams Woods
