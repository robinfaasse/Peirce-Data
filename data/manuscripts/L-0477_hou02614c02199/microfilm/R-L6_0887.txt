My dear Dr. Woods
1006 Beacon Street
Brookline, Mass

On [date]Oct. 20 last[/date] I sent
you a letter by Registered Mail.
It was, I believe, owing to my state of
health and to constant interruptions
by business and far removed from
any thing like science as could well
be imagined, the very worst letter I
ever wrote, as a piece of writing, and
to it I built it added the crime of being
(if I remember rightly) some 50± pages
long, — which however shows I made
some effort about it. I had mislaid
your letter & did not remember the
address. So I addressed it to you
at the Mass. Institute of Technology.
Boston.

and get it, so that I shall get the
