My dear Dr. Woods:

I have duly received the copy of your new work, and have already read pp. iii-xvi, 1-46, and 196-417, and have found it perfectly convincing! so that my only criticism relates to Chap. [del]XVIII[/del] [ins]XIX[/ins], which, though convincing to me, might, I think, be improved by introducing, in some form, some considerations like the following:

A conditional proposition, — say "If A, then B" is equivalent to saying that "Any state of things in which A should be true, would (within limits) be a state of things in which B is true." It is therefore essentially an assertion of a general nature, the statement of a "would-be".
