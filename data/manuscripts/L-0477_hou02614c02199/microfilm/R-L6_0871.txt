My quasi-limit is an ultimate value to which there is a never-ceasing approach; only this approach is not incessant, but irregular so that there are small recedings from it as well as crossings over to the other side of it. It is only an approach of the mean of a sufficiently large number of terms; and no finite number can be expected to be invariably or everlastingly sufficient.
