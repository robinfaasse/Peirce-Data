I premise that I use the
word [u]quasi-limit[/u] in such a
sense as not to disturb the
usual definition of "limit",
although I must remark
that that definition is rather
a definition of the mode of
approach contemplated than
of the limit itself. In harmony
with my definition of [del]an in-[/del]
[del]finitesimal[/del], I ought to say
that the limit of a series [ins]or of a sum[/ins] is
the value of the last term (or
of the final sum) when further
modifications become "infi-
nitesimal." The usual defi-
nition of "limit" ought to be
considered as referring to a
regular approach to a limit.
