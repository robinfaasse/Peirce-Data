Dear Professor Woods:

Please pardon my long delay in answering your letter regarding the friends of Charles S. Peirce whom I met in Washington. The reason I have not answered is that I have wanted to look in some old volume of Who's Who in America to verify the name of the man whom I thought I remembered, though I was not quite sure. I knew that he was a prominent geologist and that his name was Becker. I have been away from Rome (in the country) for a long time, but have now found him in an issue of 1908.
