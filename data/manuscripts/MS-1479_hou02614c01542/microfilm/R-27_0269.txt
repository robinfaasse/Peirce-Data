1861 Tiedemann d. ([date]Jan. 22[/date]) Liebig elected.
1863 Mitscherlich d.
1864 Plana d. ([date]Jan. 2[/date]) De la Rive and Wöhler elected, the latter doubtless to Mitscherlich's place, the former to Plana's.
1867 Faraday d. ([date]Aug. 25[/date])
1868 Brewster d. ([date]Feb. 10[/date]) Kummer and Murchison elected. The less inappropriate arrangement is to regard Kummer as following Brewster and Murchison, Faraday.
1871 Murchison d. ([date]Oct. 22[/date])
1872 Herschel d. ([date]May 11[/date]) Agassiz elected to succeed Murchison and Airy to succeed Herschel.
1873 Liebig d. ([date]Apr. 18[/date]) Agassiz d. ([date]Dec. 14[/date]) De la Rive d. ? Wheatstone elected probably to succeed De la Rive.
1874 Alphonse De Candolle and Tchebichef elected, the former succeeding his compatriot Agassiz and therefore the latter following Liebig.
1875 Wheatstone d. ([date]Oct. 19[/date])
1876 Ehrenberg d. ([date]Jan. 27[/date])
1877 Sir Wm. Thomson (Kelvin) and Dom Pedro d'Alcantara elected, the former succeeding Wheatstone, the latter Ehrenberg.
1882 Wöhler d. ([date]Sep. 23[/date]) Bunsen elected.
1892 Dom Pedro d.? Airy d.? Owen d. ([date]Dec. 18[/date]) Helmholtz and Van Beneden elected, the former succeeding Airy, the latter Dom Pedro.
1893 Kummer d.? De Candolle d. Lister and Nordenskiöld elected.
