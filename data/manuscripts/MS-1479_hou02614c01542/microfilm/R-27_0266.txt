[date]1703[/date] Viviani d. (Sep. 22) Poli elected.
[date]1705[/date] Jacob Bernoulli d. (Aug. 16) Bianchini elected.
[date]1708[/date] Tschirnhausen d. (Oct. 11) Sir Hans Sloane elected.
[date]1710[/date] Guglielmini d. (July 11). "Lord Pembroke" elected. Who was he?
[date]1713[/date] Poli became French.
[date]1715[/date] Marsigli elected in Poli's place, and the Duc d'Escalone, Spanish

[date]1716[/date] Leibniz d. Nov 14.
[date]1725[/date] De Bronses, a mathematical logician elected to Leibniz's place.

[date]1726[/date] Manfredi elected in Hartsoeker's place.
[date]1727[/date] Newton d. (March 31) [del]Ruysch[/del] Fredrik Ruysch elected in his place.
[date]1729[/date] Bianchini d. (March 2) and Halley elected in his place.
[date]1730[/date] Marsigli d (Nov. 1) and Boerhaave elected in his place.
[date]1731[/date] Ruysch d. (Feb. 22) and Morgagni elected in his place.
[date]1733[/date] Christian Wolffius elected and this must have been to the place
