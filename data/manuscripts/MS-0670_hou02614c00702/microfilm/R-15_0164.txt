[u]Deductive Reasoning.[/u]

The word "Deduction" will here be used, in a generalized sense, to denote any necessary, or mathematical, reasoning, — any reasoning of which the premises, — or, as they will here often be called, the "Copulate Premiss, — having been asserted, the conclusion cannot consistently be denied by the same assertor.
