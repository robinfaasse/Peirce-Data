[pn]N10[/pn] The utility of the symbols 1, 2, 3, etc can be increased by
using them as follows

$1_i$ $1_{ij}$ $1_{ijk}$ $1_{ijkl}$ etc mean that the indices denote
one individual existent.

$2_{xi}$, $2_{xij}$, $2_{xijk}$ etc mean that the individuals denoted by the indices
all belong to the doublet $x$ (and therefore in
case of 3 or more some are identical, for non-
existent objects are never denoted by indices
which always refer objects of the universe)

$2_{ij}$, $2_{ijk}$, $2_{ijkl}$ etc mean that the indices [ins]denote[/ins] [del]are[/del] [ins]2[/ins] individuals [del]with[/del]
[del]respect to, 2 would mean that it is[/del]
employed ambiguously.

3, 4, 5, etc to be used in the same manner.
