$\cdot$ The utility of the symbols $2, 3,$ etc can be further increased by using
them as follows:

$\begin{aligned}
2_{xii} & = 2_{xi}\cdot 2_{xj}\cdot \mathsf{T}_{ij} \\
3_{xii} & = 3_{xi}\cdot 3_{xj}\cdot \mathsf{T}_{ij}
\end{aligned}$

$\begin{aligned}
1_{xi} & & 1_{xij} & = 1_{xi} \cdot 1_{xj} \\
2_{xi} & & 2_{xij} & = 2_{xi}\cdot 2_{xj}\cdot \mathsf{T}_{ij} & 2_{xijk} & = 2_{xi}\cdot 2_{xj}\cdot 2_{xk}\cdot \mathsf{T}_{ijk} \\
3_{xi} & & 3_{xij} & = 3_{xi}\cdot 3_{xj}\cdot \mathsf{T}_{ij} & 3_{xijk} & = 3_{xi}\cdot 3_{xj}\cdot 3_{xk}\cdot \bar{2}_{ijk} \\
4_{xi} & & 4_{xij} & = 4_{xi}\cdot 4_{xj}\cdot \mathsf{T}_{ij} & 4_{xijk} & = 4_{xi}\cdot 4_{xj}\cdot 4_{xk}\cdot \bar{2}_{ijk}
\end{aligned}$
