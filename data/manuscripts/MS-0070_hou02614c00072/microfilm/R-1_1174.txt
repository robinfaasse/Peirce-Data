[u]Addition[/u].

Let $(i+j)_k$ mean that the integer $k$ can result from adding the integer $i$ to the integer $j$. This can be negatived by an obelus over it like any other expression.

Addition is definable by the following six formulae:

(10) $\Pi_i \Pi_j \Sigma_k (i+j)_k$

(12) $\Pi_k \Pi_j \Sigma_i \Sigma_\alpha s_\alpha \cdot q_{\alpha i} \cdot \bar{q}_{\alpha k} + (i+j)_k$

(13) $\Pi_i \Pi_k \Sigma_j \Sigma_\alpha s_\alpha \cdot q_{\alpha i} \cdot \bar{q}_{\alpha k} + (i+j)_k$

(14) $\Pi_i \Pi_j \Pi_k \Pi_u \Pi_v \Sigma_\beta (i+j)_k + (u+v)_k + S_\beta \cdot (q_{\beta u} \cdot \bar{q}_{\alpha i} + q_{\beta v} \cdot \bar{q}_{\beta j}) + \bar{s}_\beta + \bar{q}_{\beta u} + q_{\beta i}$

(15) $\Pi_i \Pi_j \Pi_k \Pi_u \Pi_v \Sigma_\beta (i+j)_k + (u+v)_k + S_\beta \cdot (q_{\beta u} \cdot \bar{q}_{\beta i} + q_{\beta v} \cdot \bar{q}_{\beta j}) + \bar{s}_\beta + \bar{q}_{\beta v} + q_{\beta j}$

(16) $\Pi_i \Pi_j \Pi_k \Pi_u \Pi_v \Sigma_\beta (i+j)_k + (u+v)_k + S_\beta \cdot (q_{\beta u} \cdot \bar{q}_{\beta i} + q_{\beta v} \cdot \bar{q}_{\beta j}) + \bar{s}_\beta + \bar{q}_{\beta v} + q_{\beta j}$
