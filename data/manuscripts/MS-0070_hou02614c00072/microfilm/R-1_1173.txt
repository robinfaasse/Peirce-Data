[u]Analysis of some Demonstrations concerning definite Positive Integers[/u]

Let the universe of l.c. italics be the aggregate of [del]all[/del] [ins]definite[/ins] Integers not negative,
Let the universe of Greek minuscules be the aggregate of possible characters of such integers,

Let $q_{\alpha u}$ mean, as in [u]Am. J. Math.[/u] Vol VII. p. 199, that the integer, $u$, has the character, $\alpha$.

[u]Hypotheses.[/u]

(1) $\Pi_{\alpha} \Sigma_{\beta} \Pi_{u} (q_{\alpha u} \bar{q}_{\beta u}) (\bar{q}_{\alpha u} \bar{\Psi} \bar{q}_{\beta u})$ i.e. Every character has a negative.

(2) $\Pi_{\alpha} \Pi_{\beta} \Sigma_{\gamma} \Pi_{u} (\bar{q}_{\alpha u} + \bar{q}_{\beta u} + q_{\gamma u}) (q_{\alpha u} q_{\beta u} \bar{\Psi} \bar{q}_{\gamma u})$ [marginalia]i.e. Of every two possibilities there is a compound possibility[/marginalia]

Instead of introducing an unanalyzed relation of "as small as", let us, at first, conceive a character [del]consisting[/del], consisting in each of certain characters belonging to every integer lower than an integer to which it belongs.

(3) $\Pi_{u} \Sigma_{\alpha} q_{\alpha u} \cdot S_{\alpha}$ Every Integer has a character common to all lower Integers [del](&amp; to no)[/del]

(4) $\dot{\Pi}_{\alpha} \Sigma_{u} \bar{S}_{\alpha} \bar{\Psi} \bar{q}_{\alpha u}$ i.e. There is no highest Integer.

(5) $\Sigma_{u} \Pi_{\beta} \Pi_{v} q_{\bar{b} a} + \bar{q}_{\alpha v}$ i.e. There is a lowest Integer (i.e. Zero).

(6) $\Pi_{\beta} \Pi_{v} \Pi_{u} \bar{S}_{\alpha} \bar{\Psi} \bar{q}_{\alpha v} \bar{\Psi} q_{\alpha u} \bar{\Psi} \bar{q}_{\beta v} \bar{\Psi} \bar{q}_{\beta u}$ [marginalia]i.e. an Integer having [del]exactly[/del] [ins]one more[/ins] than another has[/marginalia]

(7) $\Pi_{\alpha} \Pi_{\beta} S_{\alpha} (q_{\alpha u} \bar{\Psi} \bar{q}_{\alpha v}) (q_{\beta u} \bar{\Psi} \bar{q}_{\beta v}) \bar{\Psi} q_{\alpha u} \bar{q}_{\beta v} \bar{\Psi} \bar{q}_{\alpha u} \bar{q}_{\beta b}$

[marginalia]i.e. Unless one of any two integers has some S-character which the other has not, they are identical.[/marginalia]
