[pn]N12[/pn] Intimately connected with these abbreviations are others, some
of which I have mentioned elsewhere. The rules of their application
would form an elaborate logical doctrine, which I have not time
to develope, because I am working at more fundamental parts of
logic. Whenever undertakes it in the light of what I have said
here and elsewhere will have other symbols forced upon his
attention.

I pass to another and very simple abbreviation, which
consists in using the symbol $\sigma$ so that $\sigma_{ij}$ shall mean that
$j$ is [ins]at least[/ins] [del]as low[/del] an integer as $i$. That is,

(20) $\sigma_{ij} = \Pi_{\alpha} \bar{s}_{\alpha} \mathbin{\psi} \bar{q}_{\alpha i} \mathbin{\psi} q_{\alpha j}$

It immediately follows that

$\begin{array}{l} \text{(21)} \\ \text{(22)} \end{array}$ $\Pi_{i} \sigma_{ii}$ and $\Pi_{i} \Pi_{j} \Pi_{k} \bar{\sigma}_{ij} \mathbin{\psi} \bar{\sigma}_{jk} \mathbin{\psi} \sigma_{ik}$
