[u]MS 44[/u] [u]Clause 1.[/u] Of any two non-identical ordinals, there is a branch-character
of a certain [del]system of[/del] [ins]discrete system of[/ins] branch-characters (here to be designated simply
as "the system") which one of them possesses while the other lacks it.

[u]Clause 2.[/u] The branch-characters of the system are singular.

[u]Clause 3.[/u] It would be logically possible for an object susceptible of
any [del]of the[/del] branch-character of the system and [ins]actually[/ins] having one of them to
change to one other and to change from every character to which it
had [del]been[/del] [ins]one branch-character of the system[/ins] changed to [del]one[/del] [ins]some[/ins] to which it had never been changed, without
ever being restored to its first character.

[u]Clause 4.[/u] On the other hand, this would be logically impossible if the
changes were restricted to those branch-characters of the system that are
possessed by any one ordinal.
