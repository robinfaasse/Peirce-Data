[u]The Rules of Right Reasoning[/u] Ms. 694

[u]Introduction.[/u]

The study of right methods of reasoning has been
the principal occupation of my life, for about forty-
five years. I have pursued it with the sincerest purpose
and most intense desire to find out the real truth of the
matter as far as I could. I must be a precious fool, then,
if I have not something useful to communicate upon
the subject. I have published papers upon various topics
of logic which have been more or less approved by those
who were prepared by previous studies to benefit by
them. I have never yet published a book upon the whole
subject. I have written half a dozen; none of them have
approved themselves to me. The main difficulties were two.
