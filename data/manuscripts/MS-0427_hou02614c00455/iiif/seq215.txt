Three items only of Brinton's Somatology belong to psychognosy.
They are, 1[ins]st[/ins], prosopology, so far as that refers to the dubious science of
physiognomy, 2[ins]nd[/ins], psychology, and 3[ins]rd[/ins], criminal anthropology.
A great part of his Ethnography simply considers men as biological forms.
