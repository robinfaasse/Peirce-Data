I shall, then, recognize that Nomological Psychognosy must
separate into two Suborders; the first of General Psychonomy, the
second of Special Psychonomy, or Nomological Psychology. The
former will study the law of final causation and seek to formulate
it with exactitude, while the latter will study subordinate laws of
mind, of which that of Association is the first.
