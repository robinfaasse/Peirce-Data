Note. A most interesting problem is the true affiliations of those agglu-
tinative languages which are spoken by [del]Whites[/del] [ins]men supposed to be of the White race.[/ins] These are the Polynesian,
Basque, Australian, Dravidian, East Caucasian, and West Caucasian.
Concerning the two last I have no information. Of the first three I have said some-
thing. It would seem that Thibetan and perhaps Burmese, though apparently
[ins]predominantly[/ins] Chinese languages [del]have[/del] been influenced by White speech. From what little I know
of Dravidian, it appears that it has some remarkable features in common with
Australian, and also some in common with Tagala, which seems to be a highly
developed Polynesian with Malay resemblances. But what is Malay? It is not without
some resemblance to Egyptian. The principal living Dravidian language, Tamil, has
one curious resemblance to Indo-European. Namely, we know that gender has a
tendency to disappear except in the singular. Now in Tamil there is an approach to
three genders in the singular; namely, one form for irrational things (as we talk of irra-
tional things as "it") and forms for male and female rational beings. Considerin
what an unusual thing three genders constitute, this feature is not insigni-
ficant. If my theory of the origin of the White race is correct, we should natu-
rally suppose that features common to Pacific and Atlantic languages belonged
to the language before [ins]the White[/ins] this race left the cradle of the Genus Homo.
