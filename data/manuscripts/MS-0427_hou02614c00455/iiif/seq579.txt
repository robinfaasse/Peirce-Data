[ured]Three Families[/ured]
[ured]of History.[/ured]
The second suborder of Descriptive Psychognosy is 
Narrative, or History. This Suborder divides into 
three Families. Family 1 is Biography and Genealogy. 
Family 2 is the History of Communities and of 
their intercourse, chiefly Wars. It naturally divides 
into [del]B[/del] two subfamilies of Political and Military History.
