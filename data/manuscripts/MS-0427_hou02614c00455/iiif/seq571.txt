But here I [del]foresee[/del] [ins]hear[/ins] an exclamation that numismatics is a study of classes, since it is not a single coin that is struck, but many. The same objection, if it were just, would apply to printed books. There would have to be, according to this, a Suborder of Classificatory Psychogeny which should study certain classes of productions. I am convinced that there is no such Suborder. If a production manifests mind, it is in the performance that the mind comes in. But where is the psychical performance in the striking of a coin, or the printing of a book? Is it the men who wield the sledges in the one case, (or work the capstan bar, if it is modern medal,) or who pull the [ins]bar of the[/ins] printing-press? That is where the multiplication comes in?
