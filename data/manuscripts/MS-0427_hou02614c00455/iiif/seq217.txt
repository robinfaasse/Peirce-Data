Three
Guises of
[ured]Finality.[/ured]

Let us[ins]now[/ins] with Dr. Brinton's list before us, endeavor to survey Psychognosy and make out its Orders. In the first place, final causality, which is the object of psychical science, appears in three guises; first, quite detached from any biological organism; second, in biological individuals as vehicles; third, in societies, [ins]ranging[/ins] from the family to that public which includes an indefinite "posterity."
