Genera
what

Different Genera of the same Family of science are
studies which have [ins]precisely[/ins] the same general character, but
of which each is strongly distinguished by some marked
feature. This is [del]vague[/del] a sufficiently vague statement,
not to have the effect of imposing an artificial clas-
sification, while it is clear enough to be of material
service in enabling us to determine whether a given
subdivision is a division into Families or into Genera.
