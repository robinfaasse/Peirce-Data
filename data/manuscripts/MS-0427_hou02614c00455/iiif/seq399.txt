The explanation of how terrestrial phenomena
came to be such as they are also depends at
several points upon the explanation of how
celestial phenomena came to be as they are.
Astronomy and Geognosy, then, will be the two
Families of this Order. Astronomy has two Genera;
The first studies the facts as they are during the
present state of things; the second produces a
[del]theory[/del] of how the present state of things came
about. The first Genus has three Species. The first
produces a virtual picture of the heavens. It catalogues
the heavenly bodies, and describes their appearance,
configuration [ins]and changes of appearance[/ins], etc. The second Species ascertains
their [ins]regular[/ins] motions. The third studies their radiations.
