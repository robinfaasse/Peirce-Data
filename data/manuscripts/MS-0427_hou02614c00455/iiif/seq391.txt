The Order of Classificatory Physiognosy resolves itself into two Suborders. In the first, the things classified are possibilities, not necessarily actually existing in nature, but having mostly to be produced before they can be classified. The classification of things physically possible comes near to being a study of physical laws. There are here two Families. The first is a classification of geometrical-statical forms, where the particular kind of matter in which a form happens to be embodied is regarded as a fact of secondary importance.
