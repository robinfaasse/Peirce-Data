What is the use of a dictionary, then?
It has many uses. One is to put it up
on a shelf in plain sight near your
office table in order to inspire your
visitors with proper respect, and make
them feel that in talking with you
they must be careful how they express
themselves. That is in fact the only
use of a dictionary, unless you
sometimes look into it.
