To come back to this book. Its title-page
is torn away, perhaps because it carried the
autograph of some illustrious man to whom this
copy had belonged. It certainly once possessed
a title-page, though title-pages had not
then long been in vogue. The older printed
books, the incunabula as they are called, had
no title pages; for they imitated the manu-
scripts on parchment, and it would
have been unseemly to surrender a whole
recto of vellum to merely naming a book,
and nobody would pay for it. Moreover, it
was impossible to give at the beginning of a large
manuscript the date of its issue, because it
