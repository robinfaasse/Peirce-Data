What is a [u]dictionary[/u]? This word, like
almost every other, has several meanings;
but in the [del]ordinary[/del] sense, in which I mean
to talk to you about dictionaries, it is a
list of [del]the[/del] words of a language (or
a branch of a language) alphabetically
arranged with explanations of their
meanings. So much a dictionary [del]must[/del]
be to deserve the name, at least in the
[del]usual[/del] sense. Besides that, it may
give the pronunciations of words; when
it is called a pronouncing dictionary.
[del]And it[/del] may give the derivations of words;
when it is called an etymological dictionary.
It may, also, give explanations of the
nature [ins]and construction[/ins] of the things that the words denote;
