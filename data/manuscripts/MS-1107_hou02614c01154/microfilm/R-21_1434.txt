[del]Be[/del] There are no other forms of consciousness except the
three that have been mentioned, Feeling, Altersense, and Medisense.
They form a sort of system. Feeling is the [ins]momentarily[/ins] present
contents of consciousness taken in its pristine simplicity, apart from
anything else. It is consciousness in its first state, and might be
called "primisense". Altersense is the consciousness of a directly
present other or second, withstanding us. Medisense is the consciousness
of a thirdness, or medium between primisense and altersense, leading
from the former to the latter. It is the consciousness of a process of bringing
to mind. Feeling, or primisense, is the consciousness of firstness;
altersense is consciousness of otherness [del]or secondness[/del]; medisense is the
consciousness of means or thirdness. Of primisense there is but
one fundamental mode. Altersense has two modes, Sensation and
Will. Medisense has three modes, Abstraction, Suggestion, Association.
