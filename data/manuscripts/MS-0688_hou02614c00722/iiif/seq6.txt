things." Thereupon I spoke severely. "Now," said I, "I positively know that
you are the thief, and have evidence to send you to Sing Sing. But it will
cost me trouble and money too. Still, it shall be done, unless you behave
reasonably. No pawn broker will give you more than fifty dollars for the
things; and you will still be left in peril; in worse than peril, for you
will be sure to be taken. Take up with my offer. I am a man of honor,
