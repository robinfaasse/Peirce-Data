Ushered into the presence of Mr. Bangs, the head of the New York office, I said, "A negro on the Fall River Boat, named so and so (I gave his name) has stolen my watch, chain, and light overcoat. He will come off the boat at nine o'clock, and will immediately go to pawn the watch. I want you to have him shadowed, and when he pawns the watch, let him be arrested." Said Mr. Bangs, "What makes you think he has stolen your watch?"
