A great and important part of logic consists in [del]the[/del] analyzing the meanings of familiar words. We must endeavor to take up these terms in systematic order, and the first one which calls for our attention is "is". We say that Julius Caesar [u]is[/u] dead, [del]that[/del] [ins]that a griffin is a fabulous animal,[/ins] [del]that[/del] [ins]that[/ins] A four-sided triangle [u]is[/u] an absurdity, and that nothing [u]is[/u] that which does not exist.
