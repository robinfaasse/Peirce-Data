[u]Definition[/u]. [u]Quantity[/u] is that character of an object by
virtue of which it is, in respect of a given quality, more or less, and
precisely [u]so[/u] much, that is, less than some things and more than others.

[u]Illustration[/u]. Thus, the multitude of a collection [ins]in general,[/ins] is a quality,
but the precise multitude which makes a given collection count up
to just so many is its quantity.
