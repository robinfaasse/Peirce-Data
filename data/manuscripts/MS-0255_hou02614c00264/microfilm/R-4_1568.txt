<u>Definition</u>. Modular quantity is the quantity of difference between two quantities of the same line of variation.

<u>Corollary</u>. Any particular quantity of [del]a[/del] [ins]given[/ins] line of variation differs from another by a [ins]definite[/ins] least modular quantity.

<u>Scholium</u>. Certain apparent exceptions to the above corollary must be noticed. In the first place, the line of variation may return into itself. In that case, [ins]any[/ins] one quantity of that line of variation differs from any other by a least amount, and also by the whole circuit less that amount, and also by either of those amounts increased by any number of times the whole circuit. Still, there will always be one of these which is the least. In the second place, the scale of numbers may be so applied to the expression of differences on the line of variation, that a certain difference is expressed by $\infty$.
