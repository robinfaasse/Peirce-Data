The word [u]logic[/u] is supposed to have originated with the early peripatetics (i.e. followers of Aristotle). It is used in two senses, which were distinguished by Duns Scotus, [ins]the Doctor Subtilis,[/ins] as [u]logica docens[/u] and [u]logica utens[/u]; the former being the science, the latter the practice of logic. [u]Logica docens[/u], [del]says the venerable inceptor[/del] [ins]says Ockham,[/ins] is that science which teaches us to define, to divide (or make distinctions), and to distinguish truth from error by means of arguments.
