Composition of [del]Dual[/del] [ins]Triple[/ins] Relatives

The relatives $(A|B:C)$ and $(C:D|E)$ may be multiplied in this order just like duals; thus,
$$(A|B:C)(C:D|E) = (A|B:D|E)$$

The following are nearly of the same nature
[del]$A|B$[/del]
$$(A:B|C)(C:D|E) = (A:B|D|E)$$
$$(A|B:C)(C|D:E) = (A|B|D:E)$$
So we may write
$$(A:B|C)^{(C:D|E)} = (A:B|D|E)$$
etc.

The following [del]is[/del] [ins]are[/ins] a little different
$$(A:B|C)(C|B:D) = (A:D)$$
$$(A:B|C|D)(D|C:E) = (A:B,E)$$

Corresponding to the [del]relative[/del] [ins]dual[/ins]
$\sum (B_i:D_i)$ we have the triple $\sum_i (B_i B_i : D_i)$
If $\sum (B_i:D_i)$ is denoted by $t$ we may denote
$\sum (D_i|B_i:D_i)$

$\sum (C_i:D_i)$ we have the triple $\sum_i (C_i D_i : D_i)$. If the first
is denoted by $l$ we may denote the latter by $l'$

The product
$$(A:D|C)(\sum C_i:D_i) = \sum (A:D|D_i). \text{ If this is multi-}$$
plied by $\sum_i (D_i|D_i:E)$ it gives $A$
