Mr. McColl and I find it to be absolutely necessary to add some new sign to express existence; for Boole's notation is only capable of representing that some description of object does not exist, and cannot say that anything does exist. Besides that, the sign of equali[del]ality[/del]ty, used by Boole, expresses a complex relation, as De Morgan showed forty years ago. To say that
African = Negro
implies two things, that every African is a negro and that every negro is an African. For these reasons, McColl and I make use of [del]two[/del] signs of inclusion and of non-inclusion.
