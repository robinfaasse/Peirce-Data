[u]The taking of a random drawing[/u]

The drawing of objects at random is an act
in which honesty is called for; and it is often
hard enough to be sure that we have dealt
honestly with ourselves in the matter, and still
more hard to be satisfied of the honesty of another.
Therefore, one method of sampling has come
to be preferred in argumentation; namely, to
take all the objects of the class to be sampled
of which we have the necessary knowledge.
But sampling [ins]however[/ins] is a real art well deserving an
extended study by itself, and [del]into[/del] this study it would
lead us aside from our main purpose to enlarge
upon the subject here.
