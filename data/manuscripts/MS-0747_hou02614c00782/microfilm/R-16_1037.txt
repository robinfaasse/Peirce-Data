From this point of view, a rectangle is equal
[del](but not identical with)[/del] to the product of its base by its altitude, because
each unit of the base has to be paired with
each unit of the altitude in counting the num-
ber of units of the area. If we chose to mea-
sure areas by triangles instead of by squares
the area of a rectangle would be twice the
product of the base by the altitude. The product
of a [ins]uniform[/ins] velocity by a time gives a [del]space[/del] [ins]distance[/ins], because
the whole velocity has to be equally related to
every part of the time to generate the [del]space[/del] [ins]distance[/ins].
