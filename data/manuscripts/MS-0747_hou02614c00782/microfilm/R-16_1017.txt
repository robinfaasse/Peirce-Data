[del]The signs of addition and multiplication
are taken in their usual logical significations[/del]

The order of two $\Pi$'s or $\Sigma$'s is indifferent.
That is
$\Pi_i \Pi_j = \Pi_j \Pi_i$     $\Sigma_i \Sigma_j = \Sigma_j \Sigma_i$

Using the sign $\prec$ to denote that the proposition placed after it follows from that placed before it, we have
$\Sigma_i \Pi_j \prec \Pi_j \Sigma_i$
