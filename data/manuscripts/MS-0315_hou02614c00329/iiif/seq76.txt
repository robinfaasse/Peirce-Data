The other half of the cell is the nucleus. 
It consists of protoplasm which is 
highly specialized. It is bounded 
by a nuclear membrane which 
is firmer & more resistant than 
the cell protoplasm. 
The protoplasm inside the nu-
cleus is called the nucleoplasm 
and is composed of two substances: 
1. Linin which consists of a 
faintly staining substance 
in the form of a network 
or reticulum.
2. Chromatin which exists as 
numerous darkly staining gran-
ules or threads supported by 
the Linin reticulum. This 
is the most important sub-
stance in the cell for it con-
tains the hereditary characters 
which the parent transmits 
to its offspring. In addition 
to the above parts there 
is also a nucleolus which 
is a small globule of some 
undetermined substance. Its use 
is not known but it is thought 
to be a reserve storehouse of 
food material for the cell. 
Finally, the nucleoplasm fill-
ing all the spaces in the 
chromatin & linin network.
