Italian lake scenery. It seemed tame after that. The
sun set, and the moon rose as we steamed into the
lake. We took leave of our fellow-passengers and
mounted the omnibus which was to take us to
Vevay. It was a lovely, moonlight night, but the
scenery between Lausanne and Vevay didn't strike me
as being remarkably beautiful; of course it was fine,
but it was too much the same. At last we reached
Vevay, and drew up at the Trois Couronnes, a fine
building on the edge of the lake. I was very glad to
be at the end of my journey. [fig]figure[/fig] Lausanne and
Geneva are at the two ends of the lake; and Vevay,
though a small place, is one of the most important
