17.
I sat and looked at these blue hills
so soft & pure with starry dew all day
And I wondered if there was anything to do
And I found that it was enough to be
this little child
And so I grew up and did many things
and played and cried and smiled & saw
the moon and the stars
And then I grew up and became a woman
and I loved and was loved and had children
and I worked and played and cried and
smiled and I knew the moon and the stars
And then I grew old and I sat and looked
at these blue hills so soft and pure
with starry dew all day
And I wondered if there was anything to do
And I found that it was enough to be
this little child.
