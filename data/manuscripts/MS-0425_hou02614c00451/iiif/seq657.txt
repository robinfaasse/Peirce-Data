either Terms, Propositions, or Arguments. A term
is a sign which leaves its Object, and [u]a fortiori[/u] its
Interpretant, to be what it may. A Proposition is a
sign which distinctly indicates the [del]Object[/del] [ins]called its Subject[/ins] which it
denotes, [del]but[/del] leaves its Interpretant to be what it may.
