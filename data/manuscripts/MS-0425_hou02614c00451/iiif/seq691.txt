Having considered the three fundamental modes
of arguments, I now pass to the consideration of
arguments which mingle the characters of these.
Here belongs in the front rank the argument from
analogy, followed by four ways of supporting hypotheses
by uniformities, arguments from likelihood,
from the resemblance of the falsified to the unshaken etc.

I now go back to consider other theories of the
validity of induction and hypothesis than my own.

identify with inductions have in some cases no
