Originality is being such as that being is, regard-
less of aught else.

Obsistence (suggesting obviate, object, obstinate, [u]ob-
stacle[/u], [u]insistence[/u], [u]resistance[/u], etc) is that wherein
secondness differs from firstness; or, is that element
which, taken in connection with Originality, makes
one thing such as another compels it to be.

Profinity

Transuasion (suggesting [u]translation[/u], [u]transaction[/u],
[u]transfusion[/u], [u]transcendental[/u], etc.) is mediation, or the
modification of firstness and secondness by third-
ness, taken apart from the secondness and firstness;
or, is being in creating Obsistence.

Although Originality is the most primitive, simple,
and original of the categories, it is not the most obvious
and familiar. We have thus far considered the cate-
gories in their original aspect. We now pass to the
easier study of their obsistential shapes.
