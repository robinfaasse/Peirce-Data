This seems incomprehensible; but we ought to know
what the idea of [ins]brute[/ins] force is, since we undoubtedly derive
it from our own sense of effort. We can make no effort
where we experience no resistance, no reaction. It is a
two-sided sense, revealing a something within, and a
simultaneous other something without. It is not, however,
the feeling which we are concerned with, but the fact
that sense reveals, or seems to reveal. That fact is binarity.
In this form, the idea is disembarrassed from the accident
of time. To say that [del]an[/del] a phenomenon is forced, whether by
