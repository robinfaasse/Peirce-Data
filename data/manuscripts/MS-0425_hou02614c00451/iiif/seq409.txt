It was a matter open to the remark of every mathe-
matician, even before Weierstrass, when mathema-
tical reasoning was far less strict than it has
since become. The more recent philosophers show
no improvement in this respect. You may search
the whole library of modern metaphysics from
Descartes to Royce, and hardly find a single
argument which does not leave room to drive a
coach and four through it, commodiously. The effect
upon the minds of those who have been nourished on
such food mainly becomes deplorably patent to
everybody who finds himself in contact with them.
Their natural sense of logic is enfeebled and diseased.
