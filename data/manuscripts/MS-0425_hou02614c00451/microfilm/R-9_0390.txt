be classed among modes of defending the natural light
of reason, although it professes as much to destroy as
to construct, and it does not deal with the deliverances
of reason in one undistinguished mass, but criticizes
each by itself. State this method in general terms, and
it is unimpeachable. You set out from some proposition
absolutely indubitable, such as that man has some knowledge.
