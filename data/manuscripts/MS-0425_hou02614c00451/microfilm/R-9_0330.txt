The biography of Pythagoras rests on very poor evidence, at
best; but the only logical way is to follow the best authorities we
have, so far as if is possible to do so, and to account by some
theory for the falsity of their testimony when we are obliged to reject
consider it false. This method no doubt leaves us liable to error,
but it cannot be improved upon.
