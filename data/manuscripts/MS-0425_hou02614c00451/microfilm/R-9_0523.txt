[ins]§3. Different[/ins] proofs of Logical Laws.

[del]The[/del] [ins]melancholy[/ins] disintegration of philosophic thought appears in
the [del]multitudinous[/del] [ins]different[/ins] methods which are employed by different
logicians for establishing the truths of our science. I
propose to set down here the principal of these
necessarily in abridged forms with such criticisms
of them as can be made intelligible in the present
incipient stage of our inquiry.
