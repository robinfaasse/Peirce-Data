This is not to be confounded with the passion of those ardent souls who delight to believe at the dictates of their religion what they know to be absurd. This is different. It consists in that what seems absurd is not so, because the church declares that it is true.

It might be the part of good sense for a man to employ a method of reasoning he did not understand say the method of least squares, on the recommendation of another; but it is not a scientific proceeding. Knowledge cannot be advanced by authority.
