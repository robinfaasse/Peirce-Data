At this point, I interpose a psychological chapter,
not for the purpose of introducing psychological matter
into logic, but for the purpose of making its irrele-
vancy quite clear. All our thoughts are signs, and I
here consider how they are so, and show that it is in their
character as signs that logic is applicable to them. Further,
logic is applicable to all signs whether they are directly
mental or not.
