Passing to the Proposition we have seen that it is
a sign which distinctly indicates its Object but leaves
its Interpretant to be what it may. A Proposition may
be either a Symbol or an Index. In the former case, since
its interpretant is severed from it, it will conveniently be
illustrated by [del]a statemen[/del] an assertion made by a
Speaker to an Auditor. If it is an Index, this illustra-
tion will not answer, unless the assertion consists
