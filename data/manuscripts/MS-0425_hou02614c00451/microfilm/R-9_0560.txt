Whether or not space is to be regarded as an entity is a metaphysical question which may possibly affect the psychological theory, but cannot be in the least affected by it. All of this must be totally foreign to logic for this reason among others, that logic has to do with how thought ought to be conducted. Now the intuition of space is not an idea which we can accept or reject, as we think proper. It is absolutely forced upon us.
