[u]Originality[/u] is being such as that being is, regardless of aught else.

[u]Obsistence[/u] (suggesting obviate, object, obstinate, obstacle, insistence, resistance, etc) is that wherein secondness differs from firstness; or, is that element which, taken in connection with Originality, makes onething such as another compels it to be.

[marginalia]
Profinity
[/marginalia]

[u]Transuasion[/u] (suggesting translation, transaction, transfusion, transcendental, etc.) is mediation, or the modification of firstness and secondness by thirdness, taken apart from the secondness and firstness; or, is being in creating Obsistence.

Although Originality is the most primitive, simple, and original of the categories, it is not the most obvious and familiar. We have thus far considered the categories in their original aspect. We now pass to the easier study of their [del]obsi[/del] [ins]obsistential[/ins] shapes.
