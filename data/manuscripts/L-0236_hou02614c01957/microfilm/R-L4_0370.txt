LAW OFFICES.
HARRY E. KNIGHT.
PATENT CAUSES.

87 NASSAU ST.
NEW YORK.

April 6th, 1897.

Charles S. Peirce, Esq.,

84 Broad St., (Room 6) City.

Dear Sir:-

In the matter of Interference between your application and that of Deroy & Kemp; I have today received notice from attorney for Kemp that he will proceed with the taking of testimony on behalf of Kemp in Baltimore on the 9th of April. As I have heard nothing from you about this matter I will, of course, not attend nor do anything in the case unless I hear from you.

Yours very truly,

Harry E Knight
