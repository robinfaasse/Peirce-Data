Not the smallest flaw in the reasoning could escape
challenge, and the man who committed it would be
confronted by a disputant in the presence of bystanders
who would not permit any evasion of the obli-
gations of disputation. All this has unhappily passed
away. Philosophers write long [del]and[/del] essays contain-
ing hundreds of vaguely stated and ill-supported
assertions; and nobody takes any notice of what
they say, except in equally discursive and diffuse
and vague essays. In physics, a man can make
no erroneous statement of the result of an experi-
ment without two or three contradictions in a
few months; and he must sustain his position
or be discredited. But the reviewer has read certainly
upwards of fifty German [del]logic[/del] [ins]philosophical[/ins] treatises on logic;
and there has not been a single one which has
not contained a [del]logic[/del] palpable logical fallacy.
