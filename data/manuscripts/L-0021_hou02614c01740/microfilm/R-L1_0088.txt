My dear Sir:

I am glad to say that it seems possible for us to go on with the translation of Dr. Hirsch's "Genius and Degeneration". Dr. Hirsch writes me that he would like to confer with you in regard to some of the technical terms[ins],[/ins] [ins]etc.[/ins] His address is 53 East 60th Street.

I have taken the liberty of marking one or two words in your translation where it has occurred to me that [del]a[/del] simpler words might be used. I do this simply to accentuate the point made previously - that we are anxious to have this translation in as simple and readable English as possible. We appreciate the value of your work and feel confident that the entire translation will be turned out satisfactorily.

My understanding is that a payment of $200 will be satisfactory to you for the translation and proof-reading, and that you can finish the book in a month or six weeks. I shall be glad to receive copy from you as soon as possible for I shall send the copy to the printer as fast as you furnish it. I may add also that it is our custom to make payment for translation upon completion of the proof-reading. I send you a copy of the book, and in the hope that we may receive the first installment of the translation early next week, I remain

Very truly yours,
Ripley Hitchcock
