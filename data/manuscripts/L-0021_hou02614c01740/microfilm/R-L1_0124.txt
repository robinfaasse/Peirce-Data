APPLETON'S POPULAR SCIENCE MONTHLY,
72 FIFTH AVENUE,
NEW YORK, [date]March 9th 1900[/date]

Mr C. S. Peirce
Dear Sir

Such an article as you propose would be very appropriate to the Monthly if we could conveniently give it a place within a reasonable time. But owing to the recent loss of a quarter of our space, and the pressure of a large number of accepted articles, some of which are getting very old, we have for two months past been obliged to decline all offers of new material. We shall have to continue this until our older engagements are out of the way.

Very truly yours
W. J. Youmans
