CABLE ADDRESS: HILANDERO.

Dear Mrs Peirce:

I am very glad to hear that everything is now satisfactory.

I presume Dr. Hirsch has now the complete copy and as I hear that the translation is satisfactory to him, I will send Mrs Peirce the cheque. You will of course see that we should get satisfaction to the author of the book. Will you kindly let me know if all the mss. has been sent to Dr. Hirsch?

Yours very truly

Wm W Appleton
