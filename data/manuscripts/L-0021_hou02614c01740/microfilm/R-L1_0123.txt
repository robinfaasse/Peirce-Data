[date]24th March, 1896[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce;-

I have received your letter of March 22nd. It seemed to us best, in order to hasten matters, to have the proofs read here. I cannot send you duplicate galleys as the reading has now gone too far for corrections. I will inquire whether the quotations are needed, and if they are, will send you word.

I am very sorry that you have had so much trouble about this book and hope that it will come out in a satisfactory manner. At all events the reading is being carefully attended to, and Dr. Hirsch seems to be getting it in the shape he desires.

Yours sincerely,

[fig]signature[/fig]

C.T.Peirce Esq.
