January 2nd, 1896.

C. S. Peirce, Esq.,
74 West 35th Street.

Dear Sir:

I have received the first half dozen galleys from the printer and have sent them to Dr. Hirsch, asking him to look over the proofs with a view to any modifications in the use of scientific terms, and explaining to him that any literary corrections will be made by you. I have asked him to send the proofs direct to you in order to save time and to write you in regard to them.

Very truly yours,

Ripley Hitchcock
