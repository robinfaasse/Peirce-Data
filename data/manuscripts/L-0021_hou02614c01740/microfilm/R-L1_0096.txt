D. APPLETON & CO.,
72 FIFTH AVENUE,
NEW YORK.

January 14th, 1896.

C. S. Peirce, Esq.,
74 West 25th Street,
City.

Dear Sir:

I have received your letter of the 13th reporting your conversation with Dr. Hirsch. Of course it is important that his meaning should not be changed, although it is also important that the meaning should be expressed in as readable English as possible. I am glad you had the conversation with him and I think the results will be satisfactory.

I am sorry that there is likely to be any interruption in the progress of the book but I trust there will not be much delay. I have received the first lot of galley proofs revised and have sent them to Dr. Hirsch, who will forward them to you.

Very truly yours,
Ripley Hitchcock

P. S. I will send one of our own boys when necessary. Messenger service is of course expensive.
