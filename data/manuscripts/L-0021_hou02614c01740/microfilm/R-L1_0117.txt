D. APPLETON & CO.
72 FIFTH AVENUE,
NEW YORK.

LONDON OFFICE: 33 BEDFORD ST., (COVENT GARDEN.)
CABLE ADDRESS: HILANDERO.

Dear Mrs. Peirce:

I have only
just heard from Dr.
Harris. It seems that
he has not been well
& has only been able
to read a portion of Mrs.
Peirce’s translation. He writes
that the translation is satis-
factory with a very few minor
changes & I take pleasure
in enclosing cheque for
$150. - receipt of
which we should be

[page break]

very glad to have Mrs.
Peirce send the proofs
for the work, as we have
been doing, if it in any
way interferes with your
plans, we will manage
to get them read elsewhere.

Yours very truly
Wm W Appleton
