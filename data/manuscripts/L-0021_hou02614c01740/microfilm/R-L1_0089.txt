D. APPLETON & CO.,
72 FIFTH AVENUE,
NEW YORK.

December 23rd, 1895.

C. S. Peirce, Esq.,
158 West 50th Street,
City.

Dear Sir:

I beg to acknowledge your letter of December 21st. It will be satisfactory to the Messrs Appleton to make the payment in four installments as the translation is received, in accordance with your request. We will not publish your name as translator since you prefer that this shall not be done. I had already spoken to Dr. Hirsch about your peculiar qualifications and special experience. The question of conference is, of course, merely a detail. It may or may not be necessary for Dr. Hirsch and yourself to meet. What we are interested in is that the translation shall be satisfactory. Dr. Hirsch is in active practice here and it is somewhat difficult to get hold of him as of course he is frequently called away. I do not think there will be any special difficulty, however, about a conference between you should personal meetings be necessary.

I shall hope to receive copy from you as soon as possible. Let me assure you that we appreciate your interest in the work.

Very truly yours,
Ripley Hitchcock

P.S.
Yours of December 23rd is just received. Dr. Hirsch will read the proofs. Of course the expansion which you refer ot is simply an amplification of the meaning tending to simplify the demand upon the reader and I think there will be no objections. please mail me copy as rapidly as may be convenient for you.
