[fig]Circular stamp: COMPOSITION AND ELECTROTYPING DEPARTMENT, THE APPLETON MANUFACTURING COMPANY, 689 TO 707 KENT AVE.[/fig]

BROOKLYN, N. Y., [date]Feb. 28, 1896[/date].

Dear Sir:

Your postal card of the 27th inst. to D. Appleton & Co. was handed to me. We can not send you the MS. for galleys 12 to 20 as that went out with the slip proofs and was not returned.

We send duplicate page proofs — one to be retained and the other to be returned to us. Haven't you the duplicates of the first seventeen pages? I send you now plate proofs of those pages — they are the only pages that we have had back. We have sent 43 slips in all and pages 18 to 31 went out today.

Yours respectfully
E. Werrey
