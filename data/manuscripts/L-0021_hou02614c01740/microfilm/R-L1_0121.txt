City.

Dear Sir:

We wrote Dr. Hirsch some time ago requesting him to send you the original copy of any of the earlier part of the book which he might have in his hands. As the proofs have gone backward and forward so much we think it is probable, as we have explained before, that the copy has not been preserved. As we have said, it is not necessary to insert the originals of the quotations.

We are writing now to ask you to kindly return galleys 21 to 32, which are still in your hands. We think that after this we need not trouble you to read the proofs inasmuch as it is necessary to complete the work upon the book as soon as possible. If, therefore, you will return the proofs that you have we will not send you any others. We presume that this will be quite satisfactory to you.

With thanks for your assistance with the book, we are

Very truly yours,
