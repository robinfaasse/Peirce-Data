D. APPLETON & CO.,
72 FIFTH AVENUE,
NEW YORK.

February 26th, 1896.

Charles S. Peirce, Esq.,
160 West 87th Street,
City.

Dear Sir:

We send you galley proofs 12-20 today. They have, as you see, been read by Dr. Hirsch. Will you kindly look them over at your early convenience. We wish to call especial attention to the omission on galley 12. A page of copy has not been received. Will you kindly supply that page in order that the printers may go ahead. We are sorry to trouble you with this but the page (47) has not been received by us at all. The proofs have been read up to this point and the first pages of the book have been cast. We shall be able now to make more rapid progress with the book and we trust that we shall not tax you unduly with the proof-reading.

With best wishes, we are

Very truly yours,

DAppleton & Co
