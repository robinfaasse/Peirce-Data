[date]28th February, 1896[/date]

C. S. Peirce, Esq.,

160 West 87th street.

Dear sir;-

We have just received your letter of this date.

1st. We have ordered plate proofs to be mailed to you direct from our factory.

2nd. It is doubtful whether we can send you the copy corresponding to these galleys.

There has been considerable delay owing chiefly to the illness of Dr. Hirsch, and as we do not like to take up your time longer, we should be very glad to look after the reading of the proofs ourselves, if you will supply us with the one missing page. We can easily arrange for some one to take charge of the proof reading who will be competent for the work.

Yours truly,
