Dear Sir:

In answer to your esteemed favor of the 8th June, we would say, that we do not publish Cook's Logic and we have no works on this subject at present.

We take pleasure in sending you our topical lists of subjects, which we trust may prove of interest. You may feel at liberty to mention any of our books in your publication. Any of our books you may desire to purchase for this purpose, we will send you at a special rate.

Pleased at all times to serve you, we are

Very truly yours
D Appleton & Co
