When the slightest clue presented itself, Keppler's
promptitude to seize upon it was amazing. The last and
most essential step of his great discovery was
made according to his own account by accident.
Namely, it was due to his remarking that two numbers
which seemed to have no connection with one another
were nearly equal, one 429, the other 432. Keppler
does not remark that an ordinary man's attention
would not have been struck by this near equality,
or if it had, would never have divined its [del]meaning[/del] [ins]significance[/ins].
