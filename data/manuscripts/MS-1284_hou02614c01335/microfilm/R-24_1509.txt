age of 59 while on a journey he died rather suddenly
at Ratisbon of an infectious fever. He had had
in all 12 children.

All the endowments of Keppler's intellect and
heart seem to have been concentrated upon one
function, that of reasoning. In his great work
on Mars, he has laid bare to us all the
operations of his mind during the whole
research; and what better sign of the per-
