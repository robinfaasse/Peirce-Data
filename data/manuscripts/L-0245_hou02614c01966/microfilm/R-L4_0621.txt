29 Washington Square
[date]22d Sept. 1899[/date]

My dear Professor Peirce,

I have been trying in vain till now to get time to reply to your letter - It arrived while I was going through an attack of malarial fever, much severer than the touch that I had at Milford, and which quite incapacitated me for the time being - putting me back with my work and dislocating my plans in general, so that I fear I shall not be able to get to Milford again this Fall, (only five weeks of which remain) in response to the invitation that you so kindly renew.
