29 Washington Square, N.Y.
[date]June 7th 1901[/date]

My dear Professor Peirce,

I received this morning yours of the 5th and have deposited twenty-five dollars to the credit of "C. S. or Juliette Peirce" in the Fifth Avenue Bank as you request. —

You speak of coming to New-York on the 10th or 12th, and if you will please to call with the MS. or send it, as may best-suit your convenience, [ins]as soon as you arrive[/ins] I will tackle it and will ask you to make an appointment so that we can go over it together. — I do not now fix a time in advance because I have several matters coming on together about that time and will have to see how I can fit them in when the moment arrives. —

I am sorry to hear that Mrs. Peirce is not as well as usual, but hope that you are, and to see you early next week. — In great haste,

Yours very truly Francis Lathrop
