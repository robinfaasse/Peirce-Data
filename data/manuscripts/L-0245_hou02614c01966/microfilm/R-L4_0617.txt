Raquette Lake
Hamilton Co N.Y.

My dear Professor Peirce,

I have just received your note forwarded from Delhi, and hope that you will not think me altogether an uncertain quantity when I announce another change of time-table. I find that my friends had planned a little expedition through the woods and lakes, and that this will delay me a few days so that it will be the latter part of the week instead of the first before I can arrive in Milford— (that is the 18[ins]th[/ins] or 19[ins]th[/ins] instead of the 14[ins]th[/ins] as I had expected) when I anticipate much pleasure from meeting you again
