51 West 10th St.
New York, [date]Feb 16th[/date]

Prof. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

My dear Sir;

I am sorry that through my absence your letter did not reach me until last night. I will deposit $150.- to your credit in the 5th Avenue Bk. tomorrow morning, and hope the delay has not caused you any serious inconvenience.

The package I believe is in the Express Co's hands and was brought here by them Friday but they would not leave it in my absence. I hope to get it tomorrow and will let you know if it does not reach me.

Very sincerely yours
H. A. Smith.
