29 Washington Square
[date]11th Feb. 1903[/date] -

My Dear Professor Peirce,

Your letter of the 9th inst. is received, but says nothing of the receipt by you of the typewritten copy of Ms. sent you -

Your previous letter intimating the improbability of any copy of the Aldebaran Chapter's being of any use Peirce, who wrote to me as your representative, and has told me that the intention is to interest the Carnegie Institution in the matter, - a project which you spoke of as having fallen through, but which I hope will come to a happy issue -

Hoping that you are well
Yours Truly
Francis Lathrop
