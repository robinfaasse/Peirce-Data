Rule XII. Two ways of delineating a heavy line which connect the same
partial graphs and the outermost parts of which are within the same ovals
are equivalent. Thus, [fig]graph A-B-C[/fig] and [fig]graph A-B-C[/fig] are equivalent. [fig]graph A-B[/fig] and [fig]graph A-B[/fig] are [ins]containing[/ins] equivalent.

Rule [del]XIII[/del] [ins]XIII[/ins]. A heavy line reaching out through an odd number of ovals [del]connecting[/del]
partial graphs not connected within those ovals is ineffective in connecting those
partial graphs. Thus, [fig]graph sins-sings[/fig] is equivalent to [fig]graph sins-sings[/fig] or to [fig]graph sins-sings[/fig].
So [fig]graph is a man-loves-is a woman[/fig] is equivalent to [fig]graph is a man-is a woman-loves[/fig].

Rule XIV. [del]On or[/del] Two ovals one enclosing the other and nothing else may be
introduced or suppressed at pleasure. Thus [fig]graph sins-sings[/fig] is equivalent to [fig]graph sins-sings[/fig].
