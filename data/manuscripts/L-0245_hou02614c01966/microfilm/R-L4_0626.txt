29 Washington Square
New-York [date]27th May 1901[/date] —

My dear Professor Peirce,

I dare say that you have seen the discussions in the papers à propos of Mr Carnegie's offer to the University of Edinburgh. —

They have set me to thinking on the subject, and I recall that you on several occasions expressed the opinion that the present organization of Universities, both in this country and in Europe, is defective in many ways, and you hoped that an institution might some day be founded on lines which you could indicate as conducive to the better advancement of philosophy and science. I should extremely like to know what sort of an organization you would suggest as affording the greatest facilities for individual and coöperative work toward the solution of the various problems that are coming up in our modern society
