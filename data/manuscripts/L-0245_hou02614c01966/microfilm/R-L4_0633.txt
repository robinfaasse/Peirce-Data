46 Washington Sq
New York [date]June 23d[/date]

Prof. C. S. Peirce
Milford. Penna.

My dear Sir:

Mr. Lathrop has requested me to send you his copy of Lavater for your use during the summer - that is, until Oct. 1st, and to write me to say to you not to hesitate to make any marginal annotations you desire, to facilitate your reference in the reading.

I send it by Wells, Fargo & Co Express - Please advise me at the above address if the volumes are safely received.

Yours very sincerely
H. A. Hammond Smith
