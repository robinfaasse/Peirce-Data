29 Washington Square
[date]25" June 1898[/date]

My Dear Professor Peirce,

The book Panpneumatism
came a day or two after your
letter was received - I should
like to know some time, what
are the important points that you
say have escaped the author.

And for fear that you should
forget about them I send you
another copy (the first was
presented to me by the author)
for you to keep, and if it is
not too much trouble, double
