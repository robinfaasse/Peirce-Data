[date]21" August 1899[/date]

Dear Sir:

I wrote to you some time since from
Delhi, but not hearing from you I
write again to ask when you are
likely to be from the 25" to the 30"
of this month, as it is likely that
I may be in your vicinity about
that time, and in that case I
should like to consult you.

Enclosed please find stamped and
directed envelope for reply.

Hoping to hear from you, I am,
Yours truly
F. Lathrop.

To Dr. Henry Coursey
Apy. N.Y.
