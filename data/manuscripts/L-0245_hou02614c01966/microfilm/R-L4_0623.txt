29 Washington Square
New York [date]Feb 8. 1900[/date] -

My dear Professor Peirce,

I received your letter of the 4th inst. and have been making inquiries to find if anyone I knew would take up the mortgage on your place, but so far without success. Are the two mortgages that you speak of, the first for one thousand and the second for twelve hundred dollars, both on the property extending from the hills to the river, containing about sixty acres, and on which your house and a barn and cottage stand?
