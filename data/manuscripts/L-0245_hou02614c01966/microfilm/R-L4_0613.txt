29 Washington Square
New York

My Dear Professor Parker

I should have
acknowledged sooner
the receipt some time
since of the printed
circular of Anita Villa
which I presume that
you sent me, had not
my time been so entirely
taken up, in addition to
time - Please to send
me a line, care of
S. Sherwood Esq.
Delhi. Delaware Co
N.Y.
to say if you expect to be
at Milford the latter part
of next week, and if it
will be convenient for you
to see me then -

With regards to Mrs Parker
and yourself,
Yours very truly
Francis Lathrop

[date]27 July 1899[/date]
