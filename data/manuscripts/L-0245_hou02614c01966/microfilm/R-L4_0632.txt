29 Washington Square
New-York [date]18th June 1901[/date]

My dear Professor Peirce

Since seeing you I have
spoken with some friends and find so [del]much[/del]
interest expressed in your project of writing a
book on Logic that I feel quite encouraged,
but fear that it will not be possible to get
the matter settled before I leave for Europe.
It may, however, be practicable to make a
beginning. I will try to write again, though
I am pressed for time as I expect to sail
this week, — perhaps on Thursday. On Mon-
day I deposited $35.00 at the Fifth Avenue Bank,
to the credit of "C. S. or Juliette Peirce". This amount
with the $65.00 already paid to you completes the
hundred dollars for the "University" article.
Have you made out the preliminary list of books?
Please send it to me as soon as possible, at the above
address. If it arrives after I sail it will be forwarded
to me. In great haste, Yours very truly, Francis Lathrop
