Camp Pine Knot
Raquette Lake
Hamilton Co. N.Y.

My dear Professor Peirce

I have succeeded in getting at some time-tables and in mapping out a route from here via Utica and Binghampton to Port Jervis, where I expect to arrive on Saturday next — the 19" — at 5.17 p.m. — without fail, unless I should have the misfortune to miss a connection at Binghampton where the Delaware and Lackawanna train from Utica arrives at 1.50 p.m. — the Erie train leaving at 1.53 — Should that occur, I will telegraph the fact to you and take the next train leaving Binghampton at 6. p.m. — and arriving at Port Jervis at 9.55 p.m. — as I see you have noted in your letter — But I hope that nothing will happen to further delay the carrying [del]of[/del] out of the plan of visiting you at Milford that I have been looking forward to so long —

With regards to Mrs. Peirce and yourself

Yours very truly
Francis Lathrop

[date]15" August 1899[/date] —
