Madrid [date]31st January 1902[/date]

My dear Professor Peirce,

I must ask your excuses for not answer-
ing sooner your letter of last month.
I have been trying to get something settled
in reference to the four chapters on Logic
that were the theme of my letter to you from
London at the end of October. Unfortunately
before I received your answer to that letter
my friends left England, and as they have
been traveling from one end of Europe to the
other, and I have not always known where
they were, there has been much delay and
