I hope that the cold that you were
suffering from when you last wrote
has long since disappeared, and that
both you and Mrs Peirce are quite well.
I expect to be in New-York in a few
weeks and to have good news from
you then. Sincerely yours
Francis Lathrop

P.S. In regard to the length of the new
chapters I think that anything from 10000 -
words up would be satisfactory -
I received here in Spain your letter about
sending some books from London, and
therefore could not attend to the matter
as requested - I noticed however that all
but one of them are published by Macmillans
so you have no doubt been able to get them in New
York.
