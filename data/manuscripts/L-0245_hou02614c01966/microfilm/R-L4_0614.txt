I am going on Monday
to visit a friend at Delhi
for a few days, and
on looking at the map
it occurs to me that I
might be able to return
to New York by way of
Port Jervis, stopping over
at Milford for a day,
and carrying out thus
the plan that failed
last year about this
the demands of my work,
with the care of my
poor mother whose health
was gradually failing
during the past year.
You will have seen in
the papers the notices of
her sudden death on
[date]July 4th[/date] after a brief
illness - Since which time
I have myself been far
from well.
