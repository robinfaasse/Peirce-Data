29 Washington Square
New-York [date]31st May 1900[/date]

My dear Professor Peirce

I rec'd this morning your
letter asking me to get you two tablets of
writing-paper, and I am sending them by
this same post. — They are from Brentano's,
[del]the[/del] best that I could find, but did not
cost as much as the cheque for one dollar
and a quarter that you enclosed, so I re-
turn it, and beg that you will not think
of troubling to re-imburse me for the small
expenditure — Hoping that the paper will
prove satisfactory, and that both you
and Mrs Peirce are well,

Yours very truly
Francis Lathrop
