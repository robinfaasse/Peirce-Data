29 Washington Square
[date]December 7 - 1897[/date]

Dear Professor Peirce,

Not having heard from you since the first of last week, I conclude that you have decided to do nothing further about the completion of the syllabus, and perhaps it is just as well, for I think that I have about reached my limit in the two hundred dollars which I find from my accounts that I have paid for lessons and syllabus together -

You are right in surmising that I have been much preoccupied with my big picture, and I cannot tell when it will allow me time for anything else - It is far from being in a presentable state yet, but I hope that the next two or three months will bring it into shape -

Will you please hand to the bearer my little black globe and its stand that I left with you - I will call for the two hoops later on, as soon as I can do so -

Yours truly - Francis Lathrop
