29 Washington Square

My Dear Professor Peirce,

I have your letter of the 9" inst: saying that you have finished the paper on University work, and that you will arrive in town this evening, so I will call at the address given by you, at nine o'clock — Hoping to see you then, Yours very truly

Francis Lathrop

Tuesday —
[date]11" Jan 1901[/date]
