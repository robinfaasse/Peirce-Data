46 Washington Sq.
New York, [date]July 23[/date]

Prof. C. S. Peirce
Milford, Penna.

My dear Sir -

I am in receipt of your letter of the [u]18th[/u] inst and also the package containing manuscript.

I will have this type-written and send you a copy as soon as possible, but there will be some delay about this as the operator to whom Mr. Lathrop wished to give it is now taking her vacation and will probably be away until the end of next week.

I am glad to know the Lanthorn reached you safely.

Yours very truly,
H. A. Hammond Smith
