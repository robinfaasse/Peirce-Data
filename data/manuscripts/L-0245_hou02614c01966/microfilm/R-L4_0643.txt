46 Washington Sq
New York, [date]Oct 2nd[/date]

Prof. C. S. Peirce
Milford, Penna.

My dear Sir;

On Monday I deposited
to your credit in the
Fifth Ave. Bank $100.-
and have to-day sent,
to you a copy of the
manuscript of the 1st chapter.

Trusting it will reach you
safely I am

Yours very sincerely
H. A. Smith
