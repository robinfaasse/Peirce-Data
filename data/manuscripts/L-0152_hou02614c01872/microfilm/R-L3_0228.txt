My dear friend:-

Yours of the 5th has been kept under my eye in the hope that a favorable opportunity to answer it would offer; but as time lapses additional matter comes in, which must be attended to at once, and I must forego, for the present, the pleasure of a long chat with you over whom I had lost all track.

I am very sorry to hear of the misfortunes of Mrs Peirce, and sincerely trust that she is under way to permanent recovery. We have had, also, serious ups and downs in health and worldly fortunes, so that the Universal Trust Company has to draw a zero about me, as endowed with [ins]the[/ins] properties of nothingness.

Even these few lines have been constantly interrupted by urgent calls, so that you must consider this only a provisional acknowledgement of your letter with assurances of my best wishes for your welfare, in which all my family cordially join.

Faithfully yours
E. A. Fuertes
[u]Director.[/u]
