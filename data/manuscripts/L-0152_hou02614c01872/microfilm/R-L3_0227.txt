Prof. C. S. Peirce,

Milford, Pa.

Dear Friend:

I have only time to tell you that President Adams is still with us. He is, however, out of town,— also Dr. Schurman, the Dean of the School of Philosophy. Whilst I shall present your letter to the President as a matter of policy, I shall show its contents first to Dr. Schurman, who may feel inclined to avail himself of the opportunity of having you as a resident non-lecturer in this new School. But everybody is out of town at present, so that I must postpone further news until the beginning of the term about the middle of January.

Mrs. Fuertes joins me in regards to you all, and believe me

Yours sincerely,

E. A. Fuertes

Director, College of Civil Engineering,
Cornell University.
