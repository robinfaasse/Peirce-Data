Johns Hopkins University.

PRESIDENT'S OFFICE

Baltimore, [date]April 27 1881[/date]

Dear Sir,

I consulted the Exec. Committee yester-
-day in respect to our plans for next year, & they
desire me to say to you that they hope you
will consent to remain another year as lec-
-turer in logic at a salary of $2500 per annum.

Yours very truly

D. C. Gilman

Professor C. S. Peirce.
