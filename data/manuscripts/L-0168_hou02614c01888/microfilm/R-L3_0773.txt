[pn]✓[/pn] JOHNS HOPKINS UNIVERSITY
BALTIMORE

PRESIDENT'S OFFICE

[date]April 15, 1895.[/date]

Dear Sir:

I have received your note of the 9th of April, and will bear your request in mind, but most of our young men are in very moderate circumstances, and I doubt whether they will indulge in the luxury of a summer school at Milford. The funds of the University are still so restricted, that we have not means enough to expand in any way our work.

Yours very truly
D C Gilman

Prof. Chas. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.
