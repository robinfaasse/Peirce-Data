THE JOHNS HOPKINS UNIVERSITY OF BALTIMORE,
PRESIDENT'S OFFICE.

My dear Sir

I have had the pleasure
of receiving the note in which
you kindly propose to
associate my name, as
President of the Johns
Hopkins University, with
the comprehensive work
which you are about to
complete & publish, and
you ask whether it will

[page break]

not appear that I
appreciate the spirit
which prompted your
note. I remain

Very truly yours

Daniel C. Gilman

Charles S. Peirce Esq.
