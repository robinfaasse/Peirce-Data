June 14th, 1894. Saw Prof. Pickering today. 
He thought it would be better to make the photographs 
first with the 15-inch telescope, and see if the same 
diffraction rings were visible there. He said he 
thought they would be, but that the size of the 
diffraction disk would be smaller. He suggested 
that we should use a smaller aperture on the 
15-inch telescope and see if the rings were then 
enlarged. He thinks the rings are due to 
diffraction and that their size depends on the 
aperture. I am to see Mr. Gerrish and find 
out when I can have the use of the telescope.
June 20th. Saw Mr. Gerrish today. He says 
I can have the 15-inch telescope on any night 
when it is not in use. He will let me know 
when there is a chance.
June 26th. Went to the Observatory this 
evening. The 15-inch telescope was in use 
by Prof. Bailey, but he kindly allowed me 
to use it for a short time. I first 
used the full aperture (15 inches). The star 
images were very small and sharp. There 
was a very faint ring around each, but 
it was so close to the star as to be 
scarcely visible. I then put on the 
4-inch cap. The images were then much 
larger and the rings were very distinct. 
They were much larger than before. I 
then used the 2-inch cap. The images 
and rings were then still larger. This 
seems to prove that the rings are due 
to diffraction and that their size depends 
on the aperture of the telescope. I 
shall now try to get some photographs 
with the 15-inch telescope using different 
apertures.
