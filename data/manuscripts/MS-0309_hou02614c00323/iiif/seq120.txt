[del]these services should be restricted to members[/del]
[del]of the College, or that their number should be[/del]
[del]limited. With this view I entirely concur.[/del]
[del]I propose therefore to give only four such[/del]

[del]unless they have a ticket which can only be[/del]
[del]obtained from myself or from some other[/del]
[del]member of the College. This would keep the[/del]
[del]attendance within moderate limits, and[/del]
