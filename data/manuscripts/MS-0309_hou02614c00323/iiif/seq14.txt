The Cluster itself is about as bright
as A or B and about 1/4 the diameter
of the field.
The region between A & B is about as
bright as A & B. and between B & C it
is somewhat brighter than C.
the area covered is roughly circular
and is about twice the diameter of
the Cluster itself.

is star No. 11. and about as bright as C.
