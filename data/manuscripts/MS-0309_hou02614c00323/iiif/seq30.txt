[del]And after they went in and sat down in the midst of
them the high priestess had come into the circle
near where the king’s seat was.  At this time
she was dressed in her own garments and had
only a simple robe of white.  She was a woman
past middle life, though she was very beautiful.
She took the branch of olive which had been
given to her by the king.  Then she and the others
bowed themselves and they offered incense.  Then
she went to the altar and began to speak and
the king and all of them were very much amazed.
She was very beautiful to look upon and when
she spoke her voice was very soft and low.
Then she said to them “You have come to
see me and to ask me what you shall do
for the people that are in the city.”  And she
spoke to them for a long time.  Then she
gave the olive branch to the king and she
went out from them.  And then the king
and the other men went out and went to the
house of the king.  And then they stayed there
for a while and then they went out and went
to the city.[/del]
