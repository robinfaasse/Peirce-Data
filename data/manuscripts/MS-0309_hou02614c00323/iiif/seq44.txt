The morning of Thursday November 20. 1913
was very windy and cloudy. After breakfast
at half past seven it began to snow, a 
regular blizzard of snow, very fine and
the wind very strong blowing from the 
North and East. This continued all the 
forenoon and the snow drifted in 
the [del]corners[/del] [ins]hollows[/ins] of the hills and near 
buildings. It was about 28° above
zero. About 2:30 P. M. it cleared
away and the wind changed to
South west and South and blew 
just as hard.
Friday, Nov. 21. was a clear cold day 
with wind from the North and West.
Saturday, Nov. 22. was also cold, and
frozen hard. About 2 P. M. it began 
to cloud up and then snowed a 
flurry. Then it cleared again.
Sunday, Nov. 23. was clear and cold
and quiet.
Monday, Nov. 24. was very bright and
much warmer. The snow melted
fast. In the evening we went
to the School house to practice
speaking and singing for the 
Thanksgiving program.
Tuesday, Nov. 25. was a fine bright
day. At noon the wind changed to
North west and blew very hard
and it grew much colder.
Wednesday, Nov. 26. was a fine day.
bright and cold and not much
wind. In the evening it was
much warmer and a very beautiful
bright moonlight.
Thursday, Nov. 27. was Thanksgiving
Day. It was very bright and warm
and the snow nearly all melted 
in the sun. In the afternoon we 
went to the school house to a 
Thanksgiving program given by the 
children and teacher. We stayed
to supper with the neighbors
as it was a community affair.
