Let us consider for example the spiral whose equation
is
$$\theta = \frac{1}{\log(P-r) - \log(r-Q)}$$
[del]So[/del] It is obvious that the real values of $\theta$ will be those for
which the value of $r$ is less than $P$ and greater than $Q$
Suppose we write $r = pP + (1-p)Q$ where ($p$ varies from $0$ to $1$)
$$P-r = (1-p)(P-Q) \quad \log(P-r) = \log(1-p) + \log(P-Q)$$
$$r-Q = p(P-Q) \quad \log(r-Q) = \log p + \log(P-Q)$$
$$\log(P-r) - \log(r-Q) = \log(1-p) - \log p = \log(\frac{1}{p}-1)$$
