LOGIC.

[del]CHAPTER I. WHAT LOGIC IS.[/del]

The reader will find throughout this book many words printed with initial capitals. This shows that they are not used according to the loose habits of ordinary language, but each of them in a single sense herein explained. Some of them are technical terms that have been [del]in[/del] used by logicians for a long time, or are anglicized form of such terms, while others are formed by the author from the Greek. The writer keenly sympathizes with those readers whose esthetic sense will be offended and whose patience will be tried by such diction; but it is a discomfort incidente to the early stages of study of any science, although of no other so [del]unremittingly[/del]
