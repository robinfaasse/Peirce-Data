For an instant of time that is neither marked by the beginning nor by the ending of a process is a fiction: there is no such element of times.

But until I can furnish the full account of the nature of a continuum, the Reader will best imagine that time consists of a series of instants, remembering that this representation is subject to future correction.

Before undertaking to elucidate the opinion, it will be well to call to mind a familiar case in which phenomena are universally acknowledged to occur that are nearly analogous with those which the opinion in question supposes. One holds a pebble motionless between finger and thumb; one separates these, and abandons the pebble to the action of gravity.
