But it must be clearly understood that, whatever Lotze may have meant by his "Local-zeichen," my Local Signs are nothing but Characters of Feeling; that is to say, whatever is true of them might conceivably be true regardless of anything else. They cannot, therefore, of themselves, indicate what part of the retina are excited, nor can they, of themselves, each for itself, convey any information of a spatial kind, or concerning any other Relations. Accordingly, the excitations of a minute part of the retina can only convey a sense of light without any sense of its being spread over a surface or occupying any position. Since this is true of each part of the retina, it is true of the whole, notwithstanding our inability to imagine a light that does not seem to [del]occup[/del] occupy a surface and to be in some definite direction from us.
