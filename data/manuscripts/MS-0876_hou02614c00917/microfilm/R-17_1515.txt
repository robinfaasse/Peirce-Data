The idea governing this scheme is that there should be
weekly conversations, each beginning with about half an
hour's lecture. But every other week the subject is to form a
part of an account of a system of inquiry in philosophy,
and every other week the subject is to be some light and
curious subject in the history of science or something of the
kind, so that those who choose to come only once a
fortnight will be able to follow the severer or the lighter
course. But the "severer" one will be such as perfectly
untrained intelligences can fully comprehend & carry away.
