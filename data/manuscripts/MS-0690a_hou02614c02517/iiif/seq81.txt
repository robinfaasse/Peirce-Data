Deduction, of course, relates exclusively to an ideal
state of things. A hypothesis presents such an ideal state
of things, and asserts that it is the icon, or analogue of
an [del]experience[/del].

hypothesis predictions as to what the results of experiment
will be, we proceed to test the hypothesis [ins]making the experimenter[/ins] by comparing
these predictions with the actual results of experiments.
