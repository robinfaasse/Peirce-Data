and the probability that all the arguments pro lead to

be
$$\frac{q_1}{p_1 + q_1} \cdot \frac{q_2}{p_2 + q_2} \cdot \frac{q_3}{p_3 + q_3} \cdot etc \times \frac{q_5}{p_5 + q_5} \cdot \frac{q_6}{p_6 + q_6} \cdot \frac{q_7}{p_7 + q_7} \cdot etc.$$
But one or other of these two [del]things[/del] [ins]alternatives[/ins] must be the case;
so that the [u]odds[/u] or ratio of favorable to unfavorable
probability on the whole is simply
$$\frac{p_1 \cdot p_2 \cdot p_3 \cdot etc \cdot p_5 \cdot p_6 \cdot p_7 \cdot etc}{q_1 \cdot q_2 \cdot q_3 \cdot etc \cdot q_5 \cdot q_6 \cdot q_7 \cdot etc} .$$
Now if we suppose that the impression made on the
mind of the wise man is proportional to the logarithm
of the [u]odds[/u] as its exciting cause, then the total
impression will be
$$\log (\frac{p_1}{q_1} \cdot \frac{p_2}{q_2} \cdot \frac{p_3}{q_3} \cdot etc \cdot \frac{p_5}{q_5} \cdot \frac{p_6}{q_6} \cdot \frac{p_7}{q_7} \cdot etc) = \log \frac{p_1}{q_1} + \log \frac{p_2}{q_2} + \log \frac{p_3}{q_3} + etc$$
$$+ \log \frac{p_5}{q_5} + \log \frac{p_6}{q_6} + \log \frac{p_7}{q_7} + etc.$$
