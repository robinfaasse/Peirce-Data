This series being indefinitely extended, no even number will be repeated nor will any odd number be repeated. For every number of the form 2N will be in the 3N place and every odd number of the form 4M+N where N is plus or minus 1 will be in the 3M+N place. To show that the whole is no more numerous than a part, or all the numbers than the even numbers, it suffices to write down [fig]1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9, etc / 2 4 6 8 10 12 14 16 18, etc.[/fig]
