Our seventh prediction was that after the expiration
of the long lull, about 377 B.C. we should expect his

Our eighth prediction was that after Plato's return
from his second voyage to Syracuse in 377 B.C., we
should expect for a considerable time no more moral
dialogues. This is verified by the characters of the [u]Par-
menides[/u], [u]Critias[/u], and [u]Timaeus[/u].
Our ninth prediction was that Aristotle's advent would
be marked by a new departure. That is most complete.
For the [u]Theatetus[/u] affords but slight hints of what was coming.
