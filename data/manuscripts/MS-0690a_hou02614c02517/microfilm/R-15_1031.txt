But in the very same sentence Diogenes says that Plato was born in the year in which Pericles died. Now Pericles did die under the archonship of Epaminon, namely, in the autumn of 429 B.C. The Prolegomena of Olympiodorus, a celebrated Neo-Platonist of the VIth century, says that Plato was born while Pericles was living; but this statement might easily have arisen out of an exaggeration.
