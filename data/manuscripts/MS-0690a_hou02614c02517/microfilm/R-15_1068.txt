There are a few dialogues whose dates may be ap-
proximately fixed with considerable probability. These
are the Apology and Politicus on external grounds;
the Protagoras, Cratylus, Symposium, Phaedrus, Theae-
tetus, and Parmenides, on internal grounds.

As to the Apology, it seems to me probable that Plato
talked it over with Socrates during that month of waiting
for the trireme and therefore that it would be written in
the same summer of [date]399 B.C.[/date]
