[u]On the Logic of drawing History from Ancient Documents[/u]
[u]especially from Testimonies.[/u]
By C. S. Peirce

Ancient history is drawn partly from documents and
partly from monuments. [del]From[/del] The last generation has
afforded so many examples of the refutation by archeology
of the conclusions of the critics of documents as to suggest
the question whether the whole logical procedure of the latter
class of students has not been radically wrong. The pur-
pose of the present paper is to show that this is the case;
that the logical theory upon which the critics proceed is as
bad as logic can be; and to set forth and defend the
true logical method of treating ancient historical documents
