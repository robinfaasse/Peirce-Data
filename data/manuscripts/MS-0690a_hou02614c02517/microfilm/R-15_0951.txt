Now what is meant by "the long run"? The phrase is only
used in saying that the [ins]ratio of[/ins] frequency of an event has such
and such a value in the long run. The meaning is that if the
occasion referred to upon which the event might happen
were to recur indefinitely, and tally were kept of the
occurrences and the non-occurrences, and [del]the[/del] ratio of
the one number to the other as the occasions went on
would [del]indefinitely[/del] [ins]irregularly[/ins] converge toward a definite limit. The word "con-
verge" is here used in a different sense from that which
is usual in mathematics. The common definition is that
a series of values $x_1, x_2, x_3$, etc. converges toward a
limiting value $x$, provided, after any [del]discrepancy[/del] [ins]$\varepsilon$[/ins] has
been named, it is possible to find one of the members of
the series $x_n$ such that for every value of $n$ greater than $N$
$(x_n - x)^2 < \varepsilon^2$. This ought to be called the [ins]finite[/ins] convergence[ins], in the indefinite convergence with which we have to do,[/ins].
