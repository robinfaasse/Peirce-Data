This book and another which will follow it, D.V., owe
their utility, — which will be much greater than their
readers will imagine until years convince them of it, — not to
any power but to my principal deficiency, which is that
my brain is small. This renders me incapable of thoroughly
grasping together any considerable number of details; and
the consequence is that I do not readily pass from [del]one[/del] [ins]one[/ins] subject
or occupation of thought to another; whence, my persistency.
