The lowest kind of conscious reasoning
is where I know what the premise is from
which my belief in the conclusion follows,
and I feel that it follows upon some prin-
ciple, — technically called the [u]leading princi-[/u]
[u]ple of the inference[/u], — but I do not distinctly
know what that principle is. Such an in-
ference is called a [u]simple consequence[/u].

Such inferences are common enough. Un-
educated people seldom reason in a higher
way; and educated people reason so very often.
