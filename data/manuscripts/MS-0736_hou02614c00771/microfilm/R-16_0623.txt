[u]Chapter VI[/u].
The Logical Algebra of Boole.
——

The actual state of things is represented by a single point in the field of possibility; and the reasonings above treated have been concerned with this single point. Categorical propositions, it is true, in one sense refer to whole classes of individuals, but they only take these classes [u]distributively[/u], that is to say, they only consider characters as belonging to any one or some one individual of such a class, not as relations between several individuals.
