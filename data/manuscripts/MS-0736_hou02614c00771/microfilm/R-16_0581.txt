The particular form of Categorical
Syllogism given above (though all
others are essentially of the same nature)
is called Barbara. It may be written
either

Every S is M,
Hence, if Every M is P, every S is P;

or

Every M is P,
Hence, if Every S is M, every S is P.

Applying the [del]principle[/del] [ins]inference[/ins] of contraposition
to the two hypothetical conclusions, we
have the two inferences:

Every S is M
Hence, if not every S is P, not every M is P;
