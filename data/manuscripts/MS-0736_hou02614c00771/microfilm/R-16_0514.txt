For example, a child might reason,
If I am good, my mamma will love me,
Now, I will be good,
And so my mamma will love me.

[del]This is logically perfect reasoning. That
is to say, if the premises are admitted to be
true, we not only feel a blind constraint to
believe the conclusion, but we perceive that
it is true. When we figure to ourselves, by
what [del]images[/del] [ins]or schema[/ins] diagram we may, the truth of the
premises, we have only to contemplate that
schema to recognize the truth of the con-
