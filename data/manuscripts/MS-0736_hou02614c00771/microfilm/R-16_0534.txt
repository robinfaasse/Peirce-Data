If S, then P;
But P is false;
Hence, S is false.

This is, in fact, a direct syllogism. Categorical syllogism is treated separately. Categorical propositions have four forms, denoted by the four vowels, A, E, I, O. (A and I are the first two vowels of affirmo, E and O of nego.)

A. Universal Affirmative. Every S is a P.
E. Universal Negative. No S is a P.
I. Particular Affirmative. Some S is [del]a[/del] [ins]a[/ins] P.
O. Particular Negative. Some S is not a P.

Affirmative and negative propositions are said to differ in [u]Quality[/u]; universal and particular in [u]Quantity[/u].
