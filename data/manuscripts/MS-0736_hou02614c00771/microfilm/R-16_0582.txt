Every M is P,
Hence, if not every S is P, not every S is M.
These give the [ins]following[/ins] [del]two[/del] syllogistic forms

Every S is M,
Not every S is P,
Hence, not every M is P.

This is called Bocardo.

Every M is P,
Not every S is P,
Hence, not every S is M.

This is called Baroko. As concrete ex-
amples we may take the following.

<u>Bocardo</u>

Every Patriarch was a man,
But not every patriarch had to die,
Hence, not every man had to die.
