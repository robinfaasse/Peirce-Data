[u]The Association of Ideas[/u].

In the absence of external impressions, thoughts chase one another through the mind in a sort [del]of[/del] [ins]of[/ins] Bacchic train. Each suggests another. After a while, the clear train of thought is broken, the ideas remain scattered for a time, and then reconcentrate in another train.
