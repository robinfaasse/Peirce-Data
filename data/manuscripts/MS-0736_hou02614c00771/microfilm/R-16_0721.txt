Thirds are capable of two degrees of degene-
racy. The first degree is where there is in the
fact itself no thirdness or mediation but where
there is true duality; the second degree is where
there is not even genuine secondness in the
fact itself. Each of these degrees of degeneracy
exists in several different forms.

tions to two others. A pin fastens [ins]two[/ins] things [ins]together[/ins]
by sticking through one and also through
the other. A mixture brings its ingredients
together by containing each. I term these
accidental thirds.
