If [u]a[/u]+[u]b[/u], then either [u]a[/u] or [u]b[/u].*

* It must be carefully borne in mind that all [del]this[/del] our discourse in this part of the book is about individuals. Give [u]a[/u]+[u]b[/u] a categorical form, and the proposition stated seems false. Namely, it does not follow that because all men are mortal, therefore, either every object is a [ins]non-[/ins]man or else that every object is mortal; but it does follow that each single individual is either not a man or is mortal.
