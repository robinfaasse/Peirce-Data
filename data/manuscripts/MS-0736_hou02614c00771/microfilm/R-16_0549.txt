[u]Canons of Syllogism[/u].

1. A syllogism has three propositions
and three terms.

2. The middle term must be [u]distributed[/u]
once and once only. A term is said to
be distributed when a change in the cha-
racter of a single [del]object[/del] [ins]thing[/ins] denoted by the
term would change the proposition from
true to false. The subject of a universal
proposition is distributed and so is the
predicate of a negative one.
