Chapter IV.

The Syllogism.

If from A as a premise we [del]infer[/del] conclude that B is true, and then taking B as a premise conclude from it that C is true, we do indirectly conclude C from A; so that if B necessarily follows from A, and C from B, then C necessarily follows from A.
