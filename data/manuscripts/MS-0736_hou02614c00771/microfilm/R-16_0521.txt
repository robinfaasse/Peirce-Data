[u]Chapter IV.[/u]
[u]The Syllogism.[/u]

If from A as a premise we conclude that B is true, and then taking B as a premise conclude from it that C is true, we do indirectly conclude C from A. Consequently if B follows from A (logically or materially), and C from B, then [del]neces[/del] [ins]neces-[/ins] sarily C follows in the same sense from A.
