[u]Darapti[/u].
([u]Bramantip[/u])
Every M is P,
Every S is M;
$\therefore$ Some P is S.

[del]Dabitis[/del] [ins]Dimaris[/ins].
([del]Dimaris[/del] [ins]Dabitis[/ins])
Every M is P,
Some S is M;
$\therefore$ Some P is S.

[u]Celantes[/u].
([u]Camenes[/u])
No M is P,
Every S is M;
$\therefore$ No P is S.

[u]Fapesmo[/u].
([u]Fesapo[/u])
Every M is P,
No S is M;
$\therefore$ Some P is not S.

[u]Frisesomorum[/u].
([u]Fresison[/u])
Some M is P,
No S is M;
$\therefore$ Some P is not S.
