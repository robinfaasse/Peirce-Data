[u]Chapter VI.[/u]

[u]Logical Extension and Comprehension.[/u]

This chapter ought perhaps to be called an Excursus. It is not, like the previous ones, to be devoted to the consideration of a general form of inference, but to the exposition of certain conceptions which have been found useful in thinking about reasoning.
