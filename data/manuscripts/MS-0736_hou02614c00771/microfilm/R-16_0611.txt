The precise meaning of the term dilem-
ma has never been settled.[ins]*[/ins] The standard
example given in all the old books, and
taken from Aulus Gellius is as follows: "It
is not good to marry a wife; for if she be fair,
she will be common; if not fair, then loathsome."
The best definition is that it is an inference,
in one of the premises of which alternatives
are given, from [del]all[/del] [ins]each[/ins] of which, as the other
premises show, the same consequent follows.
