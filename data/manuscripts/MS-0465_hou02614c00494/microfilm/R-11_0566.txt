The [u]Third[/u] has a mode of being which consists in the Secondnesses that it determines, the mode of being of a Law, or Concept. Do not confound this with the ideal being of a quality in itself. A quality is something capable of being [ins]completely[/ins] embodied. A Law never can be fully embodied [ins]in its character as a law[/ins]. [del]A quality[/del] [ins]A quality[/ins] is how something may or might have been. A law is how an endless future [del]may[/del] [ins]must[/ins] continue to be. Now in Genuine Thirdness, the First, the Second, and the Third are all three of the nature of third, or Thought, while in respect to one another they are First, Second, and Third. The First is Thought in its capacity as mere Possibility, that is, mere Mind capable of [del]thought[/del] [ins]thinking[/ins], or a mere vague idea, [ins]planning[/ins]. The [u]Second[/u] is Thought in the rôle of a Secondness.
