$$(\underline{x}-\underline{f})(\underline{v}-\underline{x})=0$$
For this shows the product [del]of $\underline{x}-\underline{f}$ and[/del] [ins]equals zero[/ins] $\underline{v}-\underline{x}$ equals
zero, and therefore one or other of these factors
is equal to zero. That is, either
$$\underline{x}-\underline{f}=0\quad\text{i.e. }\underline{x}=\underline{f},$$
or $\qquad\underline{v}-\underline{x}=0\quad\text{i.e. }\underline{x}=\underline{v}.$
Or if the student solves the quadratic equation
$$(\underline{x}-\underline{f})(\underline{v}-\underline{x})=\underline{x}\underline{v}-\underline{x}^2-\underline{f}\underline{v}+\underline{f}\underline{x}=0,$$
he will reach the same result.

Let $\underline{y}$ denote the statement that everything in
the Bible is true; then
$$(\underline{y}-\underline{f})(\underline{v}-\underline{x})=0$$
will mean that it is either [ins]not[/ins] [del]false that[/del] everything in
the Bible is true or it is true the Elijah was caught
up to heaven. Let $\underline{z}$ mean that the Christian religion
is true. Then,
$$(\underline{z}-\underline{f})(\underline{v}-\underline{y})=0$$
will mean that either the Christian religion is not true,
or everything in the bible is true. Multiply, the last
equation but one by $\underline{z}-\underline{f}$ and the last by $\underline{v}-\underline{x}$,
and we get
$$(\underline{z}-\underline{f})(\underline{y}-\underline{f})(\underline{v}-\underline{x})=0$$
$$(\underline{z}-\underline{f})(\underline{v}-\underline{y})(\underline{v}-\underline{x})=0.$$
