[u]1. The resistance and supporting power of the air[/u], a
question of mathematics.

[u]2. The motor, its character and its energy[/u]. Mr.
Chanute begs the question of whether any motor at all
is needed. Birds can soar, those that are heavy enough,
without being carried along by the wind. They resist
the velocity of high winds, without expenditure of energy.
The air-ship ought to fly against the wind without a
motor, and carry a windmill to compress air for
the purpose of breathing. Why not?
