MS. on Vellum XIV century. folio. Consists
of Vellum cover, 4 leaves attached to cover, 6 leaves
fastened together, 8 leaves fastened together.

On the 3[ins]d[/ins] leaf of cover is this memorandum:
Hic continentur xx pecie
questiones ethicorum, questiones phisicorum, questiones ethicorum
questiones veteris logice, questiones thome de anima,
de questionibus priorum, questiones posteriorum,
de sophismatibus, ars priorum
scripta a g. de nogent
questiones a radulpho britone
de anima. questiones ethicorum.

The [del]f[/del][ins]MS[/ins] on 4 leaves begins thus: -

Circa libros politicos in quo subjectum operatio hominis
secundum quod homo est pars communicationis civilis
ideo primo quero utrum de operatione hominis ut
civilis est sit politica sicut de subjecto

There are nearly fifty questions, and the MS breaks off
unfinished "Consequenter quero utrum"
