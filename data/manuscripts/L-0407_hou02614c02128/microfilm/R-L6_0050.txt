December 26, 1895.

Sir:-

I have the honor to acknowledge the receipt of your letter of December 25th, and to inform you that your previous letter failed to reach this Office. My opinion of the chart designed by Mr. C.S. Peirce is embraced in a letter written to Mr. Pierce on March 31st, 1894, a copy of which I enclose; I also enclose a copy of a letter of the same date to Commander C.H. Davis, U.S. Navy, who referred the chart to this Office. The chart was surrendered at the time and has not since been under consideration.

Although the chart has little or no value for navigating purposes, especially as its use involves a detached diagram, it represents an interesting study of the properties of a certain projection and ought, therefore, to be preserved in its proper place. I would suggest that with a full discussion from Mr. Pierce it be purchased for the Library of Congress. I consider its purchase for that purpose as an advisable measure.

Very respectfully,

Commander, U.S. Navy,
Hydrographer.

Honorable Henry Cabot Lodge,
United States Senate,
Washington, D. C.
