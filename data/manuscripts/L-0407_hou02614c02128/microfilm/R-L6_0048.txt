Dear Sir:-

Your plan for the construction of a chart on the Skew
Mercator projection has been examined, and is found to fulfill the
conditions you claim for it. The greatest advantage of such a
chart is the uniform mile-scale for measuring distances. The other
advantages possessed by it are also found in the gnomonic charts
which are published by this Office for purposes of great circle
sailing.

The idea of supplying navigators with sailing charts of
special lanes of ocean travel has been under consideration hereto-
fore. Such charts can be constructed on a larger scale than those
of the whole oceans on the gnomonic projection. The Office has
not taken the matter up because there are so few lines of American
steamships to make use of the charts. Nearly all the vessels en-
gaged in the transatlantic navigation are foreign and are supplied
with foreign charts. The Pilot Chart of the North Atlantic Ocean,
which is published each month by the Hydrographic Office and dis-
tributed to a great many mariners engaged in transatlantic naviga-
tion in return for their meteorological observations, shows the
tracks or lanes both for eastward and westward passages that are
