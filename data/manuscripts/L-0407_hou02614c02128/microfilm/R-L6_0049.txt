The steam lines between New York and the British Islands are
not of the continuous great circle character that would be essen-
tial to application to your proposed chart. You are sent herewith
the following charts, viz:

Great Circle Sailing Chart of the North Atlantic Ocean, on
the gnomonic projection, published by the Hydrographic Office.

Pilot Chart of the North Atlantic Ocean, showing steam lanes,
published by the Hydrographic Office.

North Atlantic Track Chart, published by the Coast Survey,
showing a great circle route between the United States and Great
Britain, but not the route of the steam lanes.

A disadvantage involved in your scheme is the use of the sep-
arate diagram for laying off courses. The use of a chart in two
parts is always a disadvantage at sea; also, the distortion may
affect the two parts differently.

Experience shows that navigators, as a class, greatly dislike
curved or converging parallels or meridians on their charts and
that they will not use them if they can get others.
