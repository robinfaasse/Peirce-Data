§5. We need some further sign to enable us to express
that a given graph could, were it expressed as an entire
graph, be transformed into another given graph. That we
have as yet no way of expressing this, is sufficiently
evident, since we have, as yet, no mode of combining
graphs except one which expresses that they both
happen to be scriptible at once; while a proposition such
as I propose to express would relate not to what happens
to be the case under actual circumstances but what
would be the case under all possible circumstances.
