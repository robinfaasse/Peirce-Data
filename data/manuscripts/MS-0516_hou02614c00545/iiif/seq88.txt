[fig]X[/fig]

[fig]G —— is a patriarch
(G —— is translated)[/fig]

is to be translated "Some patriarch may be designated as G
and nobody is translated unless he is other than G," or "Some
patriarch is not translated." So, the graph

[fig]H —— is a man
(H —— is mortal)[/fig]

may be translated "there is no man or else he cannot be called
H or else there is something mortal that is H;" that is,
" [ins]If any[/ins] [del]Whatever[/del] man can be called H (as, of course, any man can,)
[del]is mortal.[/del] [del]some man[/del] [ins]something[/ins] called H is mortal."
