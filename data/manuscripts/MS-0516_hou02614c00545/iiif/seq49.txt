Rule VIII introduces an entirely new element of notation;
that of a line of identity intersecting an oval. For the rule is
that replicas of the same proper name may [u]always[/u] be joined
by a line of identity. Therefore,
[fig]F —— is a patriarch
( F —— is translated )[/fig]
may be transformed into
[fig]   F —— is a patriarch
( | )
   F —— is translated[/fig]
By deiteration we can now write
[fig]   F —— is a patriarch
   ( | is translated )[/fig]
