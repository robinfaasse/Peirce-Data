Basic Rules
37 Rule V

It may be considered as an addition to Rule I that
A line of Identity may be ruptured wherever it is unenclosed.
For
[fig]A-B[/fig]
expresses that there is a circumstance under which A and B are
both scriptible; while
[fig]A- -B[/fig]
expresses less; namely that there is a circumstance under
which A is scriptible and that there is a circumstance under
which B is scriptible.

The only modification of Rule II that is required (unless
Rule V be considered as such) is this:

Rule VI.
An unenclosed partial graph may be iterated with the same
connections.

For example [fig]is mother of loves[/fig]
may be transformed into
[fig]is mother of loves loves[/fig]
