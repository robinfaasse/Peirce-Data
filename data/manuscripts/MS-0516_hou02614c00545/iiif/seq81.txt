In regard to the meaning of "circumstance", every mathe-
matician will perceive that we have to do with pure algebra,
and that our signs, being purely formal in themselves, may
be joined to any matter we like. For example, we may
write
[fig]figure[/fig] is a patriarch
[fig]figure[/fig] is translated
to mean that some person existing (regardless of tense) is a
patriarch and is translated. Or we may write
[fig]figure[/fig] to be rich
[fig]figure[/fig] to be happy
to mean that it is possible to be at once rich and happy.
Or we may write [del]gives to power[/del]
[fig]figure[/fig] gives to [fig]figure[/fig] power of [fig]figure[/fig] to betray to [fig]figure[/fig] is an enemy of [fig]figure[/fig]
