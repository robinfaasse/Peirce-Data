E —— is a patriarch
E —— is translated.

But here a special privilege is enjoyed by indices. Namely,
Rule VII. Different replicas of the same proper name may be joined by a
line of identity.
We may write
[fig]E —— is a patriarch
E —— is translated[/fig]
That is "Some patriarch shall be called E, and nobody is
translated unless he is not identical with anybody who is E,"
or "Some patriarch is not translated." So
[fig]F —— is a man
F —— is mortal[/fig]
will be translated "Any body you may pick out is not
a man or else he is not to be designated as F, or else
(if any man is designated as F) there is a mortal designated
as F.
