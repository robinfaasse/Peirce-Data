And further, if [fig]A B C inside a capsule[/fig] is scriptible, either A, or
B, or C, is scriptible. For if A is not scriptible [del]A[/del] [ins]Ⓐ[/ins] is
scriptible; if B is not scriptible, [del]Ⓑ[/del] [ins]Ⓑ[/ins] is scriptible, etc. and
then
[fig]A B C with bars over them, inside a capsule[/fig]
is scriptible. Whence,
[fig]A B C inside a capsule[/fig]
is not scriptible. All these consequences easily follow from
principles already laid down. Hence if
[fig]A B C with bars over them, inside a capsule, with a bar over the whole[/fig]
is scriptible, either A, or B, or C, is scriptible.

For this reason, Jevons, and all subsequent students
of Boole, following De Morgan (whose pupil Jevons was)
have adopted the operation of logical aggregation as
fundamental. That it ought not to be considered as a
primitive operation, if copulation and negation, are to be
taken as primitive, appears from my paper of [date]March, 1867[/date].
