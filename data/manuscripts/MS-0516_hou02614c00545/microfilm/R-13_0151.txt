[u]Rule III[/u] requires no modification as long as [ins]no[/ins] the line of
identity cuts the oval; but it may be well to call
attention to the interpretation

[fig]Diagram showing nested ovals with text: is a patriarch, is an antediluvian, is translated[/fig]

means "Something exists [ins]and[/ins] is identical with something
that is a patriarch, [del]and[/del] with something that is a translated
antediluvian."

[fig]Diagram showing nested ovals with text: is a patriarch, is translated, is an antediluvian[/fig]

means "either nothing exists or of there does it is not an
antediluvian patriarch or if it is it is not identical
with anything unless that thing is identical with
something [del]un[/del]identical with anything unless that
thing is untranslated," i.e. "no antediluvian patri-
arch is translated."
