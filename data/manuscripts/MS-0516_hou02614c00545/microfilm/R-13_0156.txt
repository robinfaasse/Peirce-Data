[fig]X-like symbol[/fig]

[fig]Graph: G is a patriarch[/fig]
[fig]Graph: G is translated, with G enclosed in a loop[/fig]

is to be translated "Some patriarch may be designated as G,
and nobody is translated unless he is other than G, or "Some
patriarch is not translated". So, the graph

[fig]Graph: H is a man, H is mortal, with H enclosed in a loop[/fig]

may be translated "There is no man or else he cannot be called
H or else there is something mortal that is H; that is,
"If any [del]Whatever[/del] man can be called H (as, of course, any man can,)
[del]is mortal[/del]" [ins]Something called H is mortal."[/ins]
