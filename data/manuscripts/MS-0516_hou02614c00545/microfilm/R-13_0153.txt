A proper name is a sign of a definite individual. But
since an individual becomes to a certain extent definite
as soon as anything is predicated of it, it is permissible
to invent a new proper name for it. Thus, if we have
[fig]diagram of "is a patriarch" connected to "is translated"[/fig]

and thus we may write
