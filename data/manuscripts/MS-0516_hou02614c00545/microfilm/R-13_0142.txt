[u]Logical addition[/u] is strong, I must admit, since I, in common
with most of the others was led independently to do so. But
a further study of what has been said by Boole and Venn, together
with reflexions of my own, have convinced me that aggregation
is not addition in any properly extended sense, but is only
another operation moreover considerably resembling addition.
Even if aggregation be a sort of addition, I hold that to
represent it by the sign + (a sin into which I myself was
led at one time) is not only highly inconvenient, since ad-
dition and aggregation must often appear in the same
formula, but is also an offence against ethical principles
of terminology which are vital to the health of this branch
of science. [del]If[/del] I am sure that nobody will understand me
that in saying this, I am accusing my esteemed friends who
have so used the sign + [del]for a pred[?]n[/del] [ins]to a disregard[/ins] of morals. I simply
hold that they have overlooked certain considerations which,
duly weighed, must forbid such notation.
