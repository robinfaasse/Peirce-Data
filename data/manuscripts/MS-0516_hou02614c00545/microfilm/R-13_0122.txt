In consequence of this convention, I term the particular
system of symbolization I am here developing [u]Existential[/u]
[u]Graphs[/u], as opposed to another system described by me in
the Monist Vol. [del]VIII[/del] [ins]IX[/ins], which I call [u]Entitative Graphs[/u].
The reasons for these names will appear below.

There is no restriction upon the distance at which
the two simultaneously written graphs shall be placed.
One may follow the other with a short blank. Thus, if A
means 'Art is long' and T 'Time is weary',
A T
will mean art is long, and time is weary. We have thus
adopted the blank as the sign of logical copulation or composition.
It will be convenient to have a second sign for the same
purpose; and I will use a heavy dot above the line; thus:
A • T.
