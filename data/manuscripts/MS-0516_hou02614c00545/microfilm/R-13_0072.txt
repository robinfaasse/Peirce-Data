We are going to use visible marks upon a surface as our
symbols. We will provide ourselves, therefore, with a [del]sheet[/del] [ins]surface[/ins]
which we will call the [u]sheet of assertion[/u], and will pretend
to hold ourselves responsible for, that is to assert, the perfect
conformity to our purpose of whatever proposition we write
upon it. But let us not talk of propositions; but let us call
what is written, or might be written, and might under circum-
stances not necessarily the actual circumstances conform to
our purpose, a [u]graph[/u]. As long as the sheet of assertion re-
mains blank let us conceive that we are irresponsible.
