[pn]Rule 0[/pn]

Whatever graph is written on the sheet of assertion may be allowed to remain.

For we shall make abstraction of tense; and therefore if any graph ever was scriptible, it remains so. But since there is some doubt of the relevancy or utility of such a rule, I shall number it Rule 0.

Since a blank sheet involves us in no responsibility, we have the more positive rule,

[pn]Rule I.[/pn]

A graph written on the sheet of assertion may be erased.

§ A. Suppose that each of two graphs is scriptible; so that we might, conformably to our purpose, write, either of them on the sheet of assertion. Then it will be extremely natural to write both on different parts of the same sheet of assertion, treating these parts as different sheets of assertion, and that without regard to [del]their to their[/del] [ins]greater or[/ins] less distance.
