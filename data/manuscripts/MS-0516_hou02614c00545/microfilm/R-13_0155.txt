But here a special privilege is enjoyed by indices. Namely,
Different replicas of the same proper name may always be
joined by a line of identity.
Thus, if we have written
[fig]F is a plumber, F is poor[/fig]
this may be translated "There is somebody who is identical with a
plumber and who may be called F and somebody is poor and
is F", that is, the name being a proper name, it must be the
same individual who is a plumber and poor; so that we
may write successively
[fig]F is a plumber, F is poor[/fig]
[fig]F is a plumber, F is poor, connected by line[/fig]
[fig]C is a plumber, C is poor, connected by line[/fig]
