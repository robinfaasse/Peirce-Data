Let us adopt this so natural convention, in the following rule:

[pn]Rule II.[/pn] [u]Detached Graphs may be inserted or erased, as if no others were on the sheet, and that regardless of distance and of direction.[/u]

We shall naturally be prepared to find reason, later, to extend or even to restrict the meaning of the word "detached," here.

In consequence of the convention embodied in this rule, I term the particular system of symbolization that is here in process of development that of [u]Existential Graphs[/u], as opposed to a system of [u]Entitative Graphs[/u] which I sketched in (Monist, Vol [del]VII[/del] [ins]IX[/ins] pp. ). The pertinency of the name will appear [del]later[/del] below.
