36[/pn]

stance is some circumstance.

In regard to the meaning of "circumstance", every mathematician will perceive that we have to do with pure algebra, and that our signs, being purely formal in themselves, may be joined to any matter we like. For example, we may write

[fig]bracket grouping two phrases[/fig] [bracket] is a patriarch
is translated [/bracket]

To mean that some person existing (regardless of tense) is a patriarch and is translated. Or we may write

[fig]bracket grouping two phrases[/fig] [bracket] to be rich
to be happy [/bracket]

To mean that it is possible to be at once rich and happy.

Or we may write [del]gives to[/del] [ins] [fig]arrow pointing to "power of"[/fig] gives to [/ins] [del]power of[/del] [ins] [fig]arrow pointing to "to betray to"[/fig] power of [/ins] to betray to [del]an enemy[/del] [ins] [fig]arrow pointing to "A to an enemy of A"[/fig] an enemy [/ins]

To mean somebody, A, gives power to B to betray A to an enemy of A.
