[u]Corollary 11.[/u] If a graph is scriptible, consisting of an
oval enclosing two graphs, then either of these if
written as an entire graph [del]would[/del] [ins]need[/ins] be transformable
into an [del]oval[/del] enclosure containing the other only.

For if [fig]circle with letters A B[/fig] is scriptible, then [del]if[/del] [ins]A[/ins] B is transformable
into any graph C. That is, under any circumstances
under which A and B are both scriptible, C is scriptible.
If, then, A is scriptible, under any circumstances under
which B is scriptible, C is scriptible.
