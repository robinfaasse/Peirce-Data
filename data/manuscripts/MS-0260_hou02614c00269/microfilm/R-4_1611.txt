Note on the Utility of considering Space as a [del]pro-[/del]
[del]jection of a[/del] [ins]section of a[/ins] space of more than 3 dimensions.
By C. S. Peirce.

Suppose that any two linear equations between
four coordinates, [u]x[/u], [u]y[/u], [u]z[/u], [u]u[/u], determine a plane,
after the elimination between them of the unreal
coordinate [u]u[/u]. Then, any 3 linear equations will
determine a right line, and [del]and[/del] 4 a point.
Taking 4 equations, A=0 B=0 C=0 D=0, these
represent a point, and any three of them repre-
sent a line through that point.
