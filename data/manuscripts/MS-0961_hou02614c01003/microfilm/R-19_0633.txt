[u]Affections of Ideas[/u]

But we are met by the question what is meant by saying that one [del]idea[/del] state of mind is affected by another. It is no easy question and to answer it we must trace out the phenomena a little further.

An idea has three elements. The first is its [ins]intrinsic[/ins] quality, as red. The second is the shock it inflicts upon us, its here-and-now-ness in the immediate present, and an infinitely reduced intensity of shock in its relative recency when it is past. The third element is its connection with other ideas.
