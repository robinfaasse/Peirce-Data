The Law of Mind.

Events seem to flow in time. Now, time is a system among certain relations. A process, or anything that dures, has its time relations more or less indeterminate; that is to say, for example, a process occupying the whole of Monday is partly a whole day subsequent to Sunday noon and partly not; it is not all determined one way. But every space of time is separated from other spaces by two instants, or temporal individuals, [del]which are[/del] [ins]and every instant is[/ins] wholly determinate in its time-relations to every other.
