[u]The Law of Mind.[/u]

According to the law of the conservation
of forces the sum of the squares of the
velocities of a system of bodies is propor-
tional to a function of their instantaneous
positions. Here the time only enters raised
to the second power; and consequently
there is no general distinction between
time past and time future. If, for
example, by some miraculous power
the motions of all the particles in the
universe were suddenly reversed, while
the absolute velocities remained unchanged,
the bodies would describe the same paths
