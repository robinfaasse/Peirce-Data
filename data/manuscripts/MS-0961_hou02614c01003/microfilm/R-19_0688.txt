In order to cut off all breaks both finite and
instantaneous, it is necessary to introduce the idea of
measurement as in the following:

4. [u]Condition of continuity[/u]. Given any three instants,
A, B, C, [del]it is[/del] there is a fourth instant D as much
previous or subsequent to C as B is to A.

Time as so defined is [del]a[/del] "hyperbolic" continuum; that
is to say, the [del]instant[/del] infinitely past and the infinitely
future are distinct and do not coincide. This, I
believe, [del]is the[/del] [ins]accords with our[/ins] natural idea of time.

But to say that the continuity of time consists in
its having no breaks, without saying what is to be
understood by a break, is empty [del]gabble[/del] language.
