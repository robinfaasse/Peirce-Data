The following are the properties of time: —

1. Of any two different instants [ins]the[/ins] one is previous to the
other, the latter subsequent to the former; but no instant
is [ins]both[/ins] previous and subsequent to the same instant.

2. Any instant previous to a second instant, that
is previous to a third, is itself previous to that third.

3. If any instant, A, is previous to any other in-
stant, C, a third instant, B, exists which is subsequent
to A and previous to C.
