for encouragement to develop, or at least state, this
philosophy which I have been excogitating for
many years. It seems to me, — and I am, as yet, the
only person who knows what my philosophy is, —
that it is the only system which is, at once, rationally
defensible and [del]at[/del] entirely in harmony with the
