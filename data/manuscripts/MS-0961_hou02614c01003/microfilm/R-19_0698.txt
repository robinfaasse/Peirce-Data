Mental phenomena follow the rule of logic.
This is all the clearer the simpler they are.
Take for example the frog deprived of
cerebral hemispheres.

A peripheral nerve is excited. Say the
side is irritated. The hind leg is raised to remove
the irritation. The irritation is repeated; the same
thing happens; until finally the frog gives a
leap to get out of the way.

Let us describe this from the inside. A feeling
is forced upon the mind. There is the consciousness
of the external force and of the feeling. This
feeling spreads. This spreading is also accom-
panied by sense of reaction. The action in-
creases: the feeling becomes intensified as
it spreads. Finally such action takes place
that the irritation is removed and the
feeling dies out and the action with it.
