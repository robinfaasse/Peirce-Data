For instance, there might be instants at all intervals later than A whose proper approximate expression in ordinary decimals never contains the figure 2. [del]Thus[/del] In that case, between any two such instants there would be a multitude of instants incomparably greater than that of the integers; and yet there would be none between 0.2 and 0.3 of a day later than A; nor between 0.02 and 0.03, between 0.12 and 0.13, etc. In short, there would be an infinite multitude of finite gaps. But even if there were no finite gaps, the series might not be continuous.
