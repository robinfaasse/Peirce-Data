the explorer who first finds out the right method of attaining
it, Hartmann seems to me to have rendered pretty certain.
At present, I will not venture to enumerate it among the
families of nomological psychics. Hitherto, we derive our
knowledge of the laws of mind entirely from the study of
the Psychology of the personal mind, where they must be
modified and overlaid by other laws peculiar to this
