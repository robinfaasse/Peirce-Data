Which do you prefer, one of those ghost-like hypo-
theses about things-in-themselves which anybody
can set up but nobody can refute; or a flesh
and blood hypotheses that nothing prevents one from
[del]overturning[/del] [ins]wrestling with and throwing it[/ins]

except that when you come to try the experiments
they unexpectedly all turn out in favor of the hypothesis?
