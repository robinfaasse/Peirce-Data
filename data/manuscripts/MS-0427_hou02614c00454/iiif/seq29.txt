In the Sub-class of Psychognosy, the first Order consists of General, or Nomological Psychology. This treats of the general properties, or laws, of mind. But it is quite astonishing how our modern psychologists, although they profess to have given up the old notion that the mind is a substance, or centre of force, and in truth have in a measure given it up, [del]and are[/del] [ins]are[/ins] still very considerably entangled in that false idea. They often go quite too far in their endeavor to escape this notion, hardly allowing that there really is a soul. They go too far in the wrong direction, and not far enough in the right direction. They do not see that the soul is an idea, [del]real and[/del] a real agency in the same general way in which the idea of right, in which Truth, is a real agency. They do not see that its agency, most real, as it is, is entirely that of a final cause, and neither much nor little that of an efficient, or blind, cause.
