The use of legs is manifest; for we have legs ourselves. But let us run over the animals that have legs. They are all mammals except the sea-cows and whales, all birds (I believe), all reptiles except serpents and some lizards, [ins]two of the[/ins] [del]most[/del] [ins]three orders of[/ins] batrachians, very few fish, [ins]no [del]lancelet nor[/del] lamprey, nor the lancelet,[/ins] no tunicates, all insects and crustaceans, no mollusks nor molluscoids, no worms, all echinoderms, perhaps, no [del]Coelenterate[/del] acalephs, sponges, or protozoa.
