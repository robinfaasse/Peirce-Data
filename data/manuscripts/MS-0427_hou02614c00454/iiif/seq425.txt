This, however, is not all. There is a third Suborder, unless it should be considered as a Family of the first, as its analogy with a Family of General Nomological Physics makes me suspect it of being. The [del]the[/del] Kinematical Theory of Gases deduces the laws of the elasticity, expansion, viscosity, diffusion, [del]and conduction of gas[/del] and other phenomena of gases from the assumption that a gas consists of an enormous collection of bodies moving independently of one another except when they happen to come [ins]in pairs,[/ins] within [ins]a maximum distance of[/ins] the sensible mutual attraction. A regularity necessarily springs out of [del]multiform[/del] multitudinous irregularity, as the doctrine of chances shows it must. There is an analogous science, where persons are substituted for single molecules, and the market takes the place of the gas. It is Nomological Economics, with some slightly developed extensions that go by the name of Mathematical Psychics. We may call it Nomological Sociology.
