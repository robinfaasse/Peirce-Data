"[del]The visible part of living forms is made up of the[/del] [del]complexed, systematized, and utilized atoms and molecules[/del] [del]of the non-living world.[/del] The invisible life is an organizing power using matter as material, and intelligence, and purposiveness are plain characteristics of the invisible life. Every cell and organ and organism shows presently-acting intelligence, healing power, adaptation to circumstance, resistance to or use of circumstance. It is plain that a practically omnipresent, invisible, living, intelligent force is operating in and through every living thing."

[del]Here,[/del] This is [del]his[/del] [ins]the author's[/ins] argument for the existence of his God. This God, which is Life with purpose and intelligence, discerned in every animal and plant, he names Biologos, Life-thought. This Biologos is wonderfully wise, perhaps so wise that no defect of wisdom is in Him. But He is very far from being omnipotent.
