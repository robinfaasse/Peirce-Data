We want the square root of
$N$ [del]we[/del] suppose $A^2$ is the largest
integer square less than $N$ and
let $R = N - A^2$

Then the first approximation is $A$
the second is $A + \frac{R}{2A}$
the third $A + \frac{R}{2A} - \frac{\left(\frac{R}{2A}\right)^2}{2\left(A + \frac{R}{2A}\right)}$

Suppose for instance you want the
square root of $10$
$10 = 3^2 + 1$     $3 = A$     $R = 1$

The approximations are $3$
$3 + \frac{1}{6}$
$3 + \frac{1}{6} - \frac{\frac{1}{36}}{6\frac{1}{3}}$

The squares are $9$
$10\frac{1}{36}$
$10\frac{1}{36} - \frac{1}{36} + \frac{1}{[del]12^2[/del] 19^2}$
