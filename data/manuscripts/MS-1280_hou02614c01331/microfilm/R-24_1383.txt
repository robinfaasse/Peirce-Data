But an eminent historian of mathe-
matics Moritz Cantor has done a good
service in calling attention to [del]the facts[/del]
[ins]two facts[/ins] and to their significance. The
facts are that Muḥammad ben Mūsā
never defines these operations of al-jebra
and almukâbala, and second that he
scarcely makes any use of them! Then
why did he give this part of his work

evidence, that the Arabians are entitled
to no credit in this matter.
