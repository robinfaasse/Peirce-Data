Observe that I am not saying the
Arabians were deficient in power of thought.
Quite the contrary: it was one of their strong
points; and the philosophies of Avicenna,
of Averrhoes, and others, are of some
interest. They were strong in thought, but
their thought was not of a scientific charac-
ter. It soars too far above, observed facts.
