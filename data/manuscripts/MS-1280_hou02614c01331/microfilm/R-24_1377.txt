But after all the only substantive discovery
in astronomy they made was an inequality
in the moon's motion known as the variation;
and opinions differ as to whether they really
did discover this. If they did, the discovery
was made in the second half of the
10[ins]th[/ins] century by Abûl Wafâ, the Buzjanite.
