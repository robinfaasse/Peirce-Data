[pn]Ms. 961[/pn] [u]Association of Ideas[/u].

Due 1 To a real connection
(Contiguity)

2 To resemblance or
relation of reason.

1 We pass from belief in one fact
[del]to[/del] [ins]to[/ins] belief in another fact in other
cases connected with the first

2 We pass from belief in one fact
to belief in another like it.

Dog reasons like that. He
sees you take a whip and
anticipates pain.

Mythology arose from second kind
of reasoning.

These are the only ways in which
we can reason. But if we reason
about reasoning, consciousness of
control arises, & habits discriminating
reasons further.
