[del]Suggestions of these two kinds[/del] [ins]Blendings of ideas[/ins] characterize not merely dreams and dreamy meditations, but also thoughts referred to the real world, or, in technical language, categorical judgments. Association is the [del]only force[/del] [ins]general name of all forces[/ins] which exists within the intellect; and whatever power of controll[del]ing[/del] the thoughts there may be can be exercised only by utilizing these forces: indeed, the power, and even the wish, to control ourselves can come about only by the action of the same principles. Still, the force of association in its native strength and wildness is seen best in persons whose understandings are so little developed that they can hardly be said to reason at all. Believing one thing puts it into their heads to believe in another thing: but they know not how they come by their beliefs, and can exercise no control over the inferential process.
