I commence without indices, and let it
be understood that every formula without an
index refers to an individual. As I showed
in my paper in the third volume of this
Journal, [del]the[/del] first operative symbol of logic
is [u]⤙[/u]. The formula
$$x \prec y$$
means [del]that[/del] [ins]if[/ins] the individual [del]of which forms the
subject of discourse either[/del] [ins]is[/ins] $x$ is true $y$ is true.
That is to say, [del]the[/del] putting $v$ for true, $f$ for
false,
$$x \prec y$$
$$\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{is true} & \text{is false} \\
\text{when} & \text{when} \\
x=f \quad y=f & x=v \quad y=f. \\

\end{array}$$
