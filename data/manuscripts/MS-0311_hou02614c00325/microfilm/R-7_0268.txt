Ladies and Gentlemen:

I have explained that by Philosophy I mean that department of Positive Science, or Science of Fact, which does not yet busy itself to gather facts, but merely takes those that are already in [ins]undoubted[/ins] possession of us all, and learns what it can from them.

The first task of Philosophy is to examine the universal phenomenon and make out what its elements are. This study I call Phenomenology after Hegel.
