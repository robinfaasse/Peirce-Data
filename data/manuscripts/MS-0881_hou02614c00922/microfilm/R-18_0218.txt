The question however, arises, What do we perceive?
In answer to that it will not serve our purpose to pursue
a scholastic method and to begin by laying down an
arbitrary definition that might be ill-considered and
unadapted to our purpose. Let us rather set out from
familiar instances of perception and notice what their
relation is to the formation of scientific beliefs, and
then frame a definition which shall cover all that
is so related to knowledge and which shall cover
nothing else.
