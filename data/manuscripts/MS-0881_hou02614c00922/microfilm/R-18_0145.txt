In order not to prolong the discussion, I leave some
possible objections to what I have said unanswered. The
most serious of these is that a perceptual judgment may
be revised, so that there is a certain check upon it. The
examination of this point would be lengthy, and it does
not seem to me likely to appear to anybody to be of
fundamental importance. Full justice to it would involve
complications which few would have the patience to follow.
