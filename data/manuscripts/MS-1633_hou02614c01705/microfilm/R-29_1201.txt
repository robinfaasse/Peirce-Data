Certainly, my dear brother, I think you
have much to bear with in your chum. These
personal peculiarities I used to think were
the springs of all our hates and loves. I hope
you are not so frequently or so greatly vexed as to be
unable to study. But do not tell me you do not like
to live with Epes; I know you could not bring
yourself to leave him. And you are right, as I think,
for genuineness is not to be exchanged for the most
graceful virtues if they do not reach the centre of the man.
