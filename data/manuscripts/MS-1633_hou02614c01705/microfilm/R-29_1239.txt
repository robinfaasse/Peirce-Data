[u]Whether great talents are favorable to the happiness of their possessor.[/u]

1. Objections Answered.

[marginalia]
Talent, Ex-
perience, &
education.
[/marginalia]

There is no analogy between the feelings of a talented man & of an educated or of an experienced man. There is no analogy because there is no similarity. Here are three men. The first, the talented man, has the sense of taste naturally in great perfection. He has always lived, however, on rustic fare & has never tasted old madeira. The second, the experienced man, has a poor taste naturally as well as by education. He has, however, tasted old madeira & knows what it is. The third, the educated man, has cultivated a naturally course palate, so as to be a connoisseur of all wines except old madeira. Now some old madeira is given to each of these three men. I contend that the impression it will produce upon each will be peculiar & that therefore there is no analogy between either of the men with reference to the pleasure they experience.
