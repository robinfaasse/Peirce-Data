An Example of the Different Estimation in which
Great Men have been held in Different Times.

It might be said of Galleleo, for example,
that it was merely from an accidental circum-
stance that he was not held in greater es-
timation in his own time. Let us therefore
take as an example, a man of whose great-
ness there has been but one opinion, and see
whether there is nothing in the history of his
fame to illustrate the subject of this theme.
