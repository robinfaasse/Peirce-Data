This is my first forensic & I have not yet
become acquainted with the laws of such compo-
sitions. I do not know into what imaginary positions
the writer is called upon to place himself. Perhaps,
he ought to move back fifty years on the occasion
of such a question. But if not, & if I am seri-
ously to answer this question & to give reasons
for [del]my[/del] [ins]my[/ins] answer, my reply is in the negative be-
cause it was settled long ago by the greatest
statesmen in the negative, & no new facts or ar-
guments have made it worth while to reopen the
discussion. Perhaps they will appear to-day.
Charles S. Peirce
