The second assertion is that those
who do not 'revive' are quiet. If this means

but if it means that their minds are stag-
nant, I think it stands as much in need
of proof as the first assertion. And you
have only to select those men whom you
would say were particularly strong-minded
and those whom [del]you[/del] [ins]one[/ins] would call particularly
weak-minded before the revival, and then
see which of the two it affects to be con-

The third assertion is that these 'lazy and
guilt' men are unable to discern consequences.
This is the coolest assertion of all, for if you
carefully observe the way in which a revival
passes off, you see very clearly that many
of the revivalists are convinced that they did
not foresee consequences.
