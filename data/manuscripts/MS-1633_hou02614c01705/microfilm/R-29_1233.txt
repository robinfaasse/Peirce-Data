[u]Estimation[/u]
[u]The Condition of Shakespear in his own Times[/u]

I have found [del]no reason[/del] [ins]no reason[/ins] to modify my views of this subject as expressed in a former theme; & consequently, since I have no intention of entering into a critical examination of separate passages, I have now little more to do than to repeat my former remarks.

Shakespear was considered in his own time as an excellent writer for the stage, a truthful & elegant delineator of human nature, a poet of a very fresh & original genius and of great ease and sometimes carelessness of expression. This is a very correct opinion. There is nothing to be objected to in it, except the greater regard paid for some traits than for others.
