The American Country Gentleman
The Ideal & the Reality.

I do not know whether the
greatest difficulty in writing a theme
is that one is obliged to say what
does not interest himself or that he
is [del]obliged[/del] prevented from saying what
he wishes to say, on the subject given
out. I never heard of "the American
country gentleman"; I never saw him
I, nevertheless proceed to describe him,
in Ideal and in Reality.

I have applied to my friends and
relations, and speaking-acquaintances, in
vain for fancy-portraits of American
country gentleman, I shall, therefore,
be obliged to draw my own.
