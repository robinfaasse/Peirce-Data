[u]The Strength and the Weakness of the Present
Dynasty in France.[/u]

The last half century in France has seen the Bour-
bon dynasty overthrown and a republic set up, the repub-
lic turned to an empire, the empire overthrown and the
Bourbons restored, the Bourbon replaced by the Orleans fa-
mily, this latter superseded by a republic, and final-
ly Louis Napoleon has restored the empire and es-
tablished the firmest government of all. Without
questioning this, and with no special reference to
the probabilities of a speedy overthrow of this
dynasty, I have merely to determine and weigh
the various strong and weak points common and pe-
culiar to the government of the Napoleons.
