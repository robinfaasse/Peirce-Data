Michael Angelo had the reputation of being a
"stern man," which is no wonder though he
by no means deserved the name. Like all strong-
ly masculine minds, he worked hard and worked
in solitude, but no one can read his letter to
Urbino on the death of his servant, for instance,
and still think he wanted, I will not say
heart, but affection. Raphael's was one of those
