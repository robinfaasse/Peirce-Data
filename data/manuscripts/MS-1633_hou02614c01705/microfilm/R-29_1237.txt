The alleged intolerant and persecuting
conduct of the Pilgrim Fathers. Does it
admit of any valid excuse?

On a certain evening, when the last
day's work is done; ere the glory of the
climacteric sunset has faded from the
heavens, the Western skies will be rent,
and the trump will sound and [del]both[/del]
quick & dead from far and near will
hasten and crowd to the judgement-seat.

And after the book has been opened and
the man with five talents and the man
with two have had their audit, and
the liar and the sorcerer and the harlot and
the murderer and the Malthusian have been
pardoned and received, while Mary Magdalen
& St. Paul stand together, [del]fearless[/del] fearful
