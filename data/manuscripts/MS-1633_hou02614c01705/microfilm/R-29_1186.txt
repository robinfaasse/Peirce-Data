picion would have been true, I shall have no-
thing with which to blame myself; and even
if I find these egotists are sincere men, how
preferable is good opinion when founded on know-
ledge to that which rests on [ins]an[/ins] ill-founded faith.
The philanthropy which springs from ignorance
and from the confidence of youth is little
more than a phase of good-natured conceit.
