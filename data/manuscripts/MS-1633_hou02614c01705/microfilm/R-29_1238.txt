Hopeful waiting for their judgment; the
Puritan's turn will come. He will step forward
and humbly confess his sins and shortcomings —
his want of zeal his want of patience his
cowardice; and then he will take from his
breast a paper labelled "A Valid Excuse
for Hard-Heartedness." And Christ will
take it, for he never refuses to receive
petitions; but some one in that august pre-
sence when he sees the Puritan's excuse
will burst out laughing.

An angel will drag him to the
foot of the throne; and he will be told
by the Judge that He considered it worth
while to see what the puritan had to say.
But that he the scoffer as he judged shall
