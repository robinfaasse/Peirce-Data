All through the animal and vegetable
kingdoms the great end of life is generation; is it not so with man? Man is made in the image of his creator; that is to say,
he is what God is, but that he is finite. Man, then, has the qualities _ though not the quantities _ of his creator _ creator. He creates. This
is not subtlety, nor sophistry: Now then what is life? What is matter? Everything physical, material, objective, is creature.
The life is creator, all else is creature: space and time are creature.

Now let us suppose that God has created an
infinite universe of space; he could create another infinite universe of space, which should bear no relation whatever to the
first _ none of distance (for distance is creature if space is creature and none has been created between them), none of direction &c
But you, O inexperienced reader, do not understand this; you think that space has always, everywhere existed. What then are the
dimensions of your soul? In other words, is not your soul without the universe, the yet infinite universe of space.

When
a man gets to heaven he is made perfect: he was already in the image of his God, now he is made perfect: he is the son of God arrived at man-
hood, and Christ says he is put over ten cities. What are these ten cities? Is he to dare to govern a part of God's creation? and shall he
never exercise his own creative powers? or does not God govern His own creation? Whose creation shall an angel govern but his own?
There are ten thousand creations which bear no material relations to each other and whose deities are angels of God, distinct but
not differing except in order. And the angels of God are in no universe of space. Their spirits commune by the matter of soul, and
that is Heaven. In Hell all have lost their creative power, it is the everlasting death-struggle of the soul. P.S. It is to be observed that every
