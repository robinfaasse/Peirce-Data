[u]Test-Example of Mathematical Reasoning.[/u]

This little inquiry supposes there are [ins]three classes of[/ins] [del]certain[/del] things called 'points', 'rays', and 'planes', and a certain relation called "containing", of which the following seven propositions are true:

1. If a plane contains a [ins]given[/ins] ray, and that ray contains a given point, then that plane contains that point.

2. [u]A[/u]. Any two different points are contained in one [ins]sole[/ins] ray, but in no plane not containing that ray.

2. [u]B[/u]. Any two different planes contain one ray, and no point not contained in that ray.

3 [u]A[/u]. Any point and any ray not containing that point are both contained in one plane, and in no other.
