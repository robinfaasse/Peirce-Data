I will take up these three in reversed order. [u]Idioscopy[/u] has two wings, the [u]Physical[/u], which studies material nature, and the [u]Psychical[/u], which studies the mind and human things. If precedence is to be given to either of these, it must be to the [u]Physical[/u] wing. For psychology depends today greatly upon neurology, linguistics upon acoustics, archeology upon dynamics, etc., history upon physical geography, etc. But [del]though[/del] [ins]it[/ins] seems to me that this dependence is so slight that it is better to regard the two wings as marching side by side.
