2. Unbroken continuity between two lines is to be understood as imposing upon the things they denote the condition of being identical.
3. The writing down of [del]a[/del] [ins]anything which can be [ins]construed as a[/ins] [del]proposition[/del][/ins] [ins]proposition[/ins] is to be understood as asserting it; and the writing of [del]two[/del] [ins]or more[/ins] propositions together is to be understood as asserting them all.
