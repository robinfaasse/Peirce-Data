It is a meaningless proposition to assert that existing things are coexistent. Hence, Fig. 72 may be read, "Some wise man is coexistent with some virtuous man." The negative of a meaningless proposition is selfcontradictory. Hence, Fig. 73 is absurd. But if the bonds be omitted, as in Fig. 74, the meaning is "Whatever is a wise man is not coexistent with whatever is a virtuous man," that is, either no man is wise or no man is virtuous. On the same principle, Fig. 75 means, "Either no man is wise or some man is virtuous," that is, if there is a wise man there is a virtuous man.
