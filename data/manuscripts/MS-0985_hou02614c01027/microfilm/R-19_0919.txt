Objection 5. Lutosławski says that Plato could not have been in Megara because Cicero
thinks the Megaric school owed much to Plato's influence. That is, Lutosławski must
think that Cicero thought that if the Megarics had caught sight of Plato, he would have
lost all his influence upon them. This is too silly. Was Cicero called on to say that Plato had been at Eleusis?
[ins]Megara is about an hour's walk beyond Eleusis.[/ins]
Objection 6. Lutosławski objects that Cicero does not directly say that Plato ever was
in Megara. Does he mean that Cicero is to be considered as a witness against every fact
which he might have known but does not mention? Cicero never has the slightest occasion
to refer to the matter. This is the dregs of Lutosławski's objections.
