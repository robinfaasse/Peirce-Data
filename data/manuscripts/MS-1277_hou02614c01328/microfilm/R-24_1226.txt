But I must not linger over Thales,
interesting a figure as he is. There have
been of late years some discussions from
which it appears probable, though it
is not stated by any ancient authority,
that Thales knew that the [del]S[/del] sum
of the three angles of a triangle is equal
to two right angles. When I say knew, I
mean knew it as well as it is known
now; for there is no reason for supposing
the proposition is absolutely exact.
