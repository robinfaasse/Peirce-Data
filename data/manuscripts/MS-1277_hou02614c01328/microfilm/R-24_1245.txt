[u]The Pythagoreans[/u].

We have what professes to be the Oath of the Pythagoreans. Its [del]is[/del] [ins]genuineness is[/ins] extremely questionable; still, if there really were a secret society, it may be they really used this oath. Now this oath makes much of the secret [del]te[/del] tetractys, by which there is little doubt that 10 was meant, since ten is

[fig]Tetractys represented by ten dots arranged in a triangle[/fig]

And the oath speaks of Pythagoras as the revealer of this number; and calls it the "support of life." So it certainly was for the Pythagoreans if they gained their living by the practice of mathematics.
