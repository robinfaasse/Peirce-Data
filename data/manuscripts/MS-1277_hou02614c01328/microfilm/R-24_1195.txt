Gentlemen & Ladies:

We have just glanced at the scientific achievements of the Chaldeans.

We have seen them pursuing during [del]for[/del] a period of two thousand years, with indefatigable patience, their steady purpose of unravelling the motions of the stars; and we have found that they attained a degree of success, which, in the absence of optical instruments, and of all physical theory, whether good or bad, is truly surprising.
