Whether Thales wrote anything or not we
do not know. He lived in the early days of
alphabetic writing. If there were any Greek
books, it is safe to say there were no regular
and enterprising publishers; and his poem,
for it was before the invention of prose, [del]had[/del]
[ins]was[/ins] been forgotten by Aristotle's time. Yet
we meet with a few expressions which
we are tempted to surmise were extracted
from a poem by Thales.

Certainly none of the Greeks whose
writings have come down to us had
any definite idea of what it was that
Thales had accomplished; but from that
day to this Greeks have never seen any
objection to filling up the blanks of
