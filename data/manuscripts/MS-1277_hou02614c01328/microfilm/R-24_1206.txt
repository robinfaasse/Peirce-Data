are simply the names of units of weight.
[u]Mene[/u] or Mina, [del]Mna[/del], is a pound, Tekel
[del]is[/del] is the Assyrian form of Shekel, roughly
corresponding to an ounce, and Upharsin
means "fractions". Thus, what Belshazzar
saw was the commencement, [ins]or suggestion,[/ins] of [del]the[/del]
[del]history[/del] balance sheet to be drawn between
him and God.
