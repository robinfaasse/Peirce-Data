[pn]Logic 6[/pn]

[u]Chapter I. Common Ground[/u].

I salute you, Mr. Reader! Would I were about to become as well acquainted with you as you are with me. You know as well as I do that all my knowledge of things external to me comes to me through my "five senses", and as far as I am aware I never looked upon your face, nor heard your voice, nor felt your presence.
