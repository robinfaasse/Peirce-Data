7. BRETON. (Brittany.)
Rag evel-se eo en deus Doue caret ar bed,
ma en deus roed e Vab unik-ganet, evit na
vezo ket collet pioubenag a gred ennan, mes
ma en devezo ar vuez eternel.

I know absolutely nothing of the Breton, except that it belongs to the British branch of Celtic, and is called [u]Armoric[/u].

[u]Rag[/u] = but & evel-se = felly = so eo = Irish so, that [del]en[/del] en perhaps like Welsh y there. deus auxiliary verb = did
Doue = God caret = loved p.p. ar the bed world
