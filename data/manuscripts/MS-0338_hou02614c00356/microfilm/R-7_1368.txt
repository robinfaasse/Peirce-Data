[u]Phanerology[/u]
([del]a Proper noun[/del])

By the [u]Phaneron[/u], I mean the [ins]single[/ins] entirety, or total, or whole, of that which the reader has in mind in any sense. This is vague, [del]and is meant to be so;[/del] but the clause "in any sense" renders it less vague, [del]as it thus[/del] [ins]since it thereby[/ins] includes [ins](symbolic and[/ins] habitual cognition[del],[/del][ins])[/ins] [del]One has in mind all that he can think, or think of, and all tones of mental state.[/del] The [u]Phaneron[/u] is nearly enough what I have in mind to be confounded with it for the purposes of this paper, in which I invite the reader to an [ins]observational[/ins] examination of the [u]Phaneron[/u].
