Cornell University,
Ithaca, N.Y. [date]Sept. 21. 1909[/date].

Charles S. Peirce Esq
Dear Sir,

For some time I have been
interested in trying to construct a
rather connected exposition of the
principles of thermodynamics.
The clearness of your views on
scientific matters has impressed
me so deeply that I am venturing
to send you a copy of a preliminary
draft of an exposition of the
thermodynamic laws. I should
value some general expression of
your estimate of this attempt, if

[page break]

you should care to give it. If
it is entirely inconvenient
for you to do this, there is
always the waste-basket -
& my apology for trespass.

Faithfully yours,
J. E. Trevor
