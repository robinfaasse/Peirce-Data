We have thus shown that any proposition may be inserted as
second antecedental, provided it is also inserted as first ante-
cedental, without loss of veritability. This may be called
the [del]rule[/del] [ins]rule[/ins] of antecedental development.

By Rule 1, we can write
[u]S[/u] | X | [u]S[/u] | X true,

and then by Rule 2
[u]S[/u] | [u]P[/u] | X | [u]P[/u] | X true.

If now we have
[u]S[/u] | P true,

Rule 3 gives us
[u]P[/u] | X | [u]S[/u] | X true;

so that the inference is justified
[u]S[/u] | P true,
[u]..[/u]. [u]P[/u] | X | [u]S[/u] | X true.

This may be called the rule of contraposition. It is of the
utmost importance.

Let us apply it to Rule 2. Rule 1 gives
[u]X[/u] | X true;

whence Rule 2 gives
[u]Y[/u] | [u]X[/u] | X true,

and by [del]Rule 3[/del] [ins]the rule[/ins] of transposition
[u]X[/u] | [u]Y[/u] | X true,

and by the Rule of contraposition
[u]Y[/u] | X | Z | [u]X[/u] | Z true.
