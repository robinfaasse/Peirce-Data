Suppose we extend the rule of snipping, as applied
to the sign of inconsequence, in a similar way. Given
$\text{X}\cdot\overline{\text{Y}\cdot\overline{\text{Z}\cdot\overline{\text{W}}}}$ true
that is, X is true and either Y is false or else Z is true
while W is false. Let us [del]to[/del] see what the [ins]following[/ins] inference

$\frac{\overline{\text{X}\cdot\text{Y}\cdot\overline{\text{Z}\cdot\overline{\text{W}}}}}{\text{X}\cdot\overline{\text{Y}\cdot\overline{\text{Z}\cdot\overline{\text{W}}}}}$
It means plainly X is true while this is false, that Y
is true and Z false and W true. Accordingly we
cut the sign of inconsequence into a dot meaning
"and" and a vinculum meaning "not." Thus

is
