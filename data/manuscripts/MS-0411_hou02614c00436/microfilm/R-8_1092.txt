§104. Let us now abandon the Garden-of-Eden Logic, and
glance at the matter from a quite opposite point of
view. Instead of supposing that we are looking at our
own constructions, let us suppose that we are bumping
up against Nature's. In that case, instead of the paradisaical
finding of everything true, we find falsehood, falsehood,
everywhere, disappointment, disillusion, and perfect truth
in nothing. We find something is true but something is
false; for not even the falsity is thorough-going. Let us
