[u]Rules of Logical Aggregation and Composition[/u].


1. The signs [ins]$\Psi$[/ins] and $\cdot$ are associative. That is,
$(x \mathbin{\Psi} y) \mathbin{\Psi} z = x \mathbin{\Psi} (y \mathbin{\Psi} z)$
$(x \cdot y) \cdot z = x \cdot (y \cdot z)$.

2. Any aggregant may be [del]inserted[/del] [ins]omitted[/ins], or any component dropped without decrease of veritability. Any component may be inserted, or any aggregat[del]et[/del] dropped without increase of veritability.[ins]*[/ins]

3. An aggregant the whole or a component of which is identical with a coordinate aggregant, or a component the whole or an aggregant of which is identical with a coordinate component, is without effect.

4. Two superposed signs of negation neutralize each other.
$\overline{x \mathbin{\Psi} y} = \bar{x} \cdot \bar{y}$
$\overline{x \cdot y} = \bar{x} \mathbin{\Psi} \bar{y}$

5.
$\omega \mathbin{\Psi} x = \omega$
$\omega \cdot x = x$
$x \mathbin{\Psi} \bar{x} = \omega$
$0 \cdot x = 0$
$0 \mathbin{\Psi} x = x$.
$x \cdot \bar{x} = 0$.
