A first, second, third, or higher antecedental or consequental
may be said to be of the first, second, third, or higher order, as
the case may be. Coordinate antecedentals are of the
same order. If one expression is subordinate to another and
like it in [del]being covered by[/del] [ins]having[/ins] an even or an odd number of vincula,
it may be said to be evenly subordinate to it.

Let us use the word veritability to mean the character
of being true or false. Let us say that there are two
degrees of veritability, of which the higher is being true,
the lower being false. Let us say that to be necessary is
a higher modal degree of veritability than to be simply
true, and to be impossible lower [ins]or deeper[/ins] than to be simply false.
Logical, or essential, necessity is the highest, substantial
necessity the least high degree of necessity, substantial
impossibility the least deep, logical impossibility the
deepest degree of impossibility.

§96. The fundamental rules of the algebra of the copula
are but [del]three[/del] [ins]four[/ins], as follows:

[u]Rule 1[/u]. We can assume as logically necessary any

[u]Rule 2[/u]. To any expression we can prefix any ante-
cedent, without [del]detriment[/del] lowering its degree of veri-
