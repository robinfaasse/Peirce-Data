That, if from A follows non-B, then from B follows non-A,
is because the relation of otherness, implied by the non is
extended to every case of B. Thus, from No man is a
woman, it follows that no woman is a man. That is if
from a person's being a man it follows he is [del]not[/del] [ins]other than[/ins] a woman,
then from a person's being a woman it follows she is [del]not[/del] [ins]other than[/ins] a
man. That is precisely analogous to the following: If every man
is a lover of every woman it follows that every woman
is [del]a lover of[/del] [ins]loved by[/ins] every man. That is, if from anything, A, being a
man, and anything B being a woman, it follows that A
loves B, then from anything, B being a woman, and anything,
A being a man, it follows that B is loved by A.
