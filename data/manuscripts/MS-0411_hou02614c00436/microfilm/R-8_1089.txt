Since $\overline{0|x}$ true
the rule of antecedental distribution gives
$\overline{\overline{x|0}|0} | \overline{\overline{x|0}|x}$ true

And since by rule 1
$\overline{x|} \ \overline{x|}$ true

Rule 4 gives the inference
$\overline{\overline{x|0}|x}$ true
$\therefore$ x true

Whence by the principle of Barbara
$\overline{\overline{x|0}|0}$ true
$\therefore$ x true

And consequently
$\overline{\overline{x|0}|0}|x$ true.

This may be called the consequential form of the principle
[del]of excluded middle.[/del]
