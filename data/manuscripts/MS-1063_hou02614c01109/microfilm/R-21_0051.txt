In order to integrate it, it is to
be observed that, in general,
$D_y.(D_x y)^2 = D_y x \cdot D_x(D_x y)^2$
$= [del]D_y x \cdot[/del] D_y x \cdot D_x(D_x y) D_{D_x y}(D_x y)^2$

$= 2 D_x^2 y$
