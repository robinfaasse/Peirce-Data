$D_{\phi}^2 = + \frac{3}{4} (\cos\phi - \cos A)^{-\frac{5}{2}} \sin^2\phi$
$+ \frac{1}{2} (\cos\phi - \cos A)^{-\frac{3}{2}} \cos\phi$

$D_{\phi}^3 = + \frac{15}{8} (\cos\phi - \cos A)^{-\frac{7}{2}} \sin^3\phi$
$+ \frac{9}{4} (\cos\phi - \cos A)^{-\frac{5}{2}} \sin\phi \cos\phi$
$- \frac{1}{2} (\cos\phi - \cos A)^{-\frac{3}{2}} \sin\phi$
