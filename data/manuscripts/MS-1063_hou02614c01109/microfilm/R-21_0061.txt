$$\int F d\varphi = F \frac{d\varphi}{d\theta} d\theta$$

$$\sin^2 \frac{1}{2}\varphi = \sin^2 \frac{1}{2}\psi \sin^2 \frac{1}{2}A$$
$$\cos^2 \frac{1}{2}\varphi = 1 - \sin^2 \frac{1}{2}\psi \sin^2 \frac{1}{2}A$$

$$\sqrt{1 - \frac{\sin^2 \frac{1}{2}\varphi}{\sin^2 \frac{1}{2}A}}$$

$$\frac{\sin \frac{1}{2}\varphi}{\sin \frac{1}{2}A} = \sin \frac{1}{2}\psi$$

$$d \sin \frac{1}{2}\psi = \frac{\cos \frac{1}{2}\varphi}{\sin \frac{1}{2}A} d \frac{1}{2}\varphi$$
$$d \sin \frac{1}{2}\psi = \cos \frac{1}{2}\psi d \frac{1}{2}\psi$$

$$\frac{d \frac{1}{2}\psi}{d \frac{1}{2}\varphi} = \frac{\cos \frac{1}{2}\varphi}{\sin \frac{1}{2}A \cos \frac{1}{2}\psi}$$

$$= \frac{\cos^2 \frac{1}{2}\varphi}{\cos^2 \frac{1}{2}\psi \sin^2 \frac{1}{2}A}$$

$$= \frac{1 - \sin^2 \frac{1}{2}\psi \sin^2 \frac{1}{2}A}{\cos^2 \frac{1}{2}\psi \sin^2 \frac{1}{2}A}$$

$$\sqrt{1 - \frac{\sin^2 \varphi}{\sin^2 i}}$$

$$\frac{\sin \varphi}{\sin i} = \sin \theta$$

$$d \sin \theta = \cos \theta d\theta$$
$$= \frac{\cos \varphi}{\sin i} d\varphi$$

$$\frac{d\theta}{d\varphi} = \frac{\cos \varphi}{\cos \theta} \frac{1}{\sin i}$$

$$\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{\sin^2 \varphi}{\sin^2 i}}} d\varphi = \int \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - \sin^2 \theta \frac{\cos^2 \varphi}{\cos^2 \theta} \frac{1}{\sin^2 i}}} d\theta$$

$$= \int \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{1 - \sin^2 \theta \sin^2 i}{\cos^2 \theta \sin^2 i} \sin^2 \theta}}$$
