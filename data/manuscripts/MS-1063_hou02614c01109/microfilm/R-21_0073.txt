$\frac{1}{2} r^2 (D_t \theta)^2 + \frac{1}{2} (D_t r)^2 = \frac{a}{r} + C$

$r^2 D_t \theta = E$

$r^4 (D_t \theta)^2 = E^2$

$\frac{1}{2} r^2 (D_t \theta)^2 = \frac{1}{2} \frac{E^2}{r^2}$

$D_t r = \sqrt{2C + 2\frac{a}{r} - \frac{E^2}{r^2}}$
