[del]proposition[/del] [ins]assertion[/ins] of the figure false if possible. Moreover in this same circle is the proposition that something is a virtuous man and this something is to be selected so as to make the proposition false if possible. The assertion of the figure is that the thing selected is true and is coexistent with a selected thing that is a virtuous man. The assertion is that these [del]three[/del] propositions are true though these selections be made in the manner the most unfavorable. That is, the assertion of the figure is that it is true whatever things these may be. It asserts, then, that they are true of everything. That is to say, the figure asserts that some wise man asserts something which is different from anything that is at once true and is coexistent with a virtuous man. That is, what is asserted by the wise man is [del]either[/del] false or else there is no virtuous man. That is, Some wise man asserts that no man is virtuous.
