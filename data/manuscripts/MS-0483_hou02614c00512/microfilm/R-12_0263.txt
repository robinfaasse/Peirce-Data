Thus, the whole line is a continuum of verbs "— is identical with something that is identical with something that is identical, etc. with —." That is, a heavy black line signifies the identity of its extremities. One of these in Fig 1 is a man that is wise. The other is left wholly vague, except that it is an existing man. Since the graph is written down unenclosed, what is meant is that when this vagueness is removed in a manner favorable to the truth of the proposition, it is true. That is, Fig. 1. asserts that [u]some existing man is wise[/u].
