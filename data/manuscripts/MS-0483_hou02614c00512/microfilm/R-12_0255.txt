[u]Existential Graphs[/u]

[u]Terminology of Logical Graphs.[/u]

A [u]graph of lowest dimensionality[/u] is a diagram composed of spots, and bonds, and enclosures. A part of a graph containing a spot is itself a graph. A graph need not be all [del]in[/del] connected into one piece.

A [u]spot[/u] is a part of a diagram treated as a point, yet having distinguishable sides, and also a quality which makes it like or unlike another spot. But there are no grades or other diagrammatic relations between unlike qualities.

A [u]bond[/u] is a simple line connecting two spots. It must have two extremities and can have no [del]branches[/del] furcations or other nodes.
