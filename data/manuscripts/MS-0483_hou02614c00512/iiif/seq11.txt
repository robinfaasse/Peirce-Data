A valental [u]system of graphs[/u] is one in which all spots of any
given quality have a determinate number of sides at which
bonds are [ins]or maybe[/ins] attached.
A [u]blank[/u] of a spot is a side at which a bonds are attached.
The [u]adicity[/u] (from -αδα the accusative termination of μόναδα,
δυάδα, etc) of a graph is the number of blanks of spots in it to
which no bonds [del]that[/del] are parts of this graph are attached. It is
[del]to be also[/del] explained that though an entire graph may have its
adicity zero, yet [ins]certain[/ins> parts of it may have high adicities, because the
attached bonds do not belong to those parts.
According [del]to[/del] the adicity of a graph is 0, 1, 2, 3, etc. it is to
