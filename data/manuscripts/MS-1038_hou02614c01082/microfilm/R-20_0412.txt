[u]The Chemical Elements[/u].

The accepted classification of the chemical elements is due to Demetrius Mendeleef, being based on his "periodic law" which is, that the properties of the elements (that is, the [del]number[/del] [ins]quantities[/ins] determined by [del]these[/del] properties) are periodic functions of their atomic weights. The periodicity is, however, complicated and this circumstance, together with the impurities which the law of chemical mass-action renders unavoidable, cause [del]all[/del] [ins]the[/ins] regularities in the relations of the properties to be very roughly fulfilled.
