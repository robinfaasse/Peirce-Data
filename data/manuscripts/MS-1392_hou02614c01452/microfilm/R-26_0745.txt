Some astronomical association or other there is
with almost every god. For instance, take the great
gods of Memphis, [u]Ptah[/u], [u]Ra'E[/u] (an inverted 3 represents
the arm, Ayin, a consonant heard in the bleat of a
sheep), Shu, Seb or Gub, [u]As'iry[/u] ([u]Osiris[/u]), Set, and
Har ([u]Horus[/u]). [u]Ptah[/u], the god of truth, has for his
emblem the scarabaeus, which is the constellation
Cancer. [u]Ra'E[/u] is the sun. Shu, plural of Sha3 (we
write 3 for that breathing represented by the hieroglyphic
eagle,) is the constellation Gemini. Osiris is the moon.
Set, the god of darkness, seems to be [ins]indirectly[/ins] connected with
the largest of the constellations, the river-cow. Horus,
being the god of periodicity, is necessarily astronomical.
