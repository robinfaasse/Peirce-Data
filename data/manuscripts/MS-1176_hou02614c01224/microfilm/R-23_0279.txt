The style of writing to be extremely compact,
yet scrupulously elegant. The ideas domi-
nant in each branch of science to be
emphatically indicated, and its leading

The arrangement to be alphabetical.
The length of the articles such as best
subserves economy of space. This generally
forbids very short articles; yet articles of
more than one page should be rare.

The copy to be completed in two years.
As every word would have to be weighed
and every statement verified, it would
cost $10 to $15 a thousand words. The
editor to receive, [u]besides[/u], $3000 a year.
