[u]Art. . Definitions[/u] . A [u]circuit[/u] is the quantity either of a [u]translation[/u] which carries a particle through every point of an axis and leaves it at the initial point, or of a [u]rotation[/u] [del]which carries[/del] [ins]which carries[/ins] a flat film through every plane containing an axis and leaves it at the initial plane. [ins]A half circuit of rotation is called a [u]right angle[/u]. Planes at right angles to one another are said to be [u]normal[/u] or [u]perpendicular[/u]. A half circuit of translation may be called a [u]quadrant[/u].[/ins]

. A [u]revolution[/u] is a movement which leaves every filament in the line from which it started.

. Places may be said to be [u]constructively equal[/u], if the part of the motion which just fills the one would necessarily just fill the other, in case the motion were susceptible of such a displacement as to permit the comparison.

. Places may be said to be [u]equivalent[/u] when it can be shown [del]that[/del] by equations that the measure of one is either equal to or is the negative of the other.

In [ins]either[/ins] this case, we say that |AB| and |CD| are [u]equivalent[/u].
