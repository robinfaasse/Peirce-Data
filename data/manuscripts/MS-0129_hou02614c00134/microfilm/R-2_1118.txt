<u>Corollary</u>. Hence (comparing a triangle with itself, as in the last corollary) if two
angles of a triangle are equal, so also are the sides opposite them.

<u>Theorem</u>. If in three triangles, two sides and an angle
opposite one of them, in one of the triangles, are respectively
equal to corresponding parts of each of the other two [ins]triangles[/ins], then,
in some two of the three triangles, the remaining side and
angles are also respectively equal.
