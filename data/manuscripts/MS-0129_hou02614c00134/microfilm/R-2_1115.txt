[u]Art.[/u] [del]Theorem[/del] [ins]Theorem[/ins]. The sum of the angles of a plane triangle is one circuit, or two right angles.

[fig]Triangle ABC with point D constructed to form quadrilateral ABDC[/fig] [u]Demonstration[/u]. Let ABC be a triangle then I say that <A + <B + <C is one circuit.

For draw {CD} inclined to {CA} equally and the same way as {AB}; and draw {BD} inclined [del]op.[/del] to {BA} equally and the same way as {AC}. Let [D] be the point of intersection of {CD} and {BD}.
