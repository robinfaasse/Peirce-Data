[u]Theorem 7[/u]. [u]Four planes perpendicular to a fifth are copunctual[/u].
[u]Demonstration[/u]. Let the four planes $(\alpha)$, $(\beta)$, $(\gamma)$, $(\delta)$, be all perpendicular to a fifth plane, $(\varepsilon)$, Then, I say, that the common point $[\alpha\beta\gamma]$ lies in $(\delta)$.
