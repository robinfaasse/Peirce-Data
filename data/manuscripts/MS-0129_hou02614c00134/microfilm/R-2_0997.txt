[u]Divisions[/u]. Space measures are either fundamental
or derived.

Fundamental space measures [ins]or lengths[/ins] are either
A. Measures of distance, between two points; or
B. Measures of inclination, or angle, between two planes.

Derived space measures, are either
A. Simple distances, namely, the shortest distances, between
(a) a point and a line, (b) a point and a surface, (c) two lines,
(d) a line and a surface, or (e) two surfaces,
B. Compounded distances, namely
(a) areas of surface-extension,
(b) volumes, or solid contents, of spacious-extensions
C. Simple angles, namely, (a) between two rays, whether
intersecting or not, (b) between two intersecting lines, at their
point of intersection (c) between a line and a surface, at
their intersection.
