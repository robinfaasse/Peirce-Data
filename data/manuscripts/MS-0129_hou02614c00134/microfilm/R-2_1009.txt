Of spatial measures two kinds are elementary. That is to say, all other space measures are modifications or combinations of these. The elementary spatial measures are,

1[sup]st[/sup], distances between pairs of points, each taken the shortest way along the ray through the two points;

2[sup]nd[/sup], inclinations of pairs of planes, each taken the shortest way round the ray in which the two planes intersect.
