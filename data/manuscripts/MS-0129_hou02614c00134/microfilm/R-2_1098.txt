[u]Theorem[/u] . [u]All right angles are equal[/u].
[u]Demonstration[/u]. Let {AC} be a homaloid, cut at right angles at [B] by another homaloid, {BG}. Let {DF} be a third homaloid cut at right angles in [E] by a fourth homaloid {HE}. Then I say $\angle\text{ABG} = \angle\text{DEH}$.

[fig]Two intersecting lines with transverse lines and curves[/fig] [fig]Two intersecting lines AC and DF meeting at J[/fig] For let [J] be the point [{AC}{DF}]. Then let the metron move along {AC} [del]with the flat film in the surface remaining in the surface,[/del] until the particle at [B] comes to [J]. Then the part of the metron filling at first $\angle\text{ABG} + \angle\text{GBC}$ comes to fill $\angle\text{AJF} + \angle\text{FJC}$, so that
.$2\angle\text{ABG} = \angle\text{BJF} + \angle\text{FJC}$
In like manner
$2\angle\text{DEH} = \angle\text{EJC} + \angle\text{CJF}$
But by Theorem , $\angle\text{BJF} = \angle\text{GJE}$
Hence $\angle\text{ABG} = \angle\text{HED}$ Q.E.D.
