[u]Theorem[/u]. In the translation of a rigid metron about an axis cutting the firmament, every particle [ins]in the firmament remains fixed at its point.[/ins] [del]describes a ray cutting the axis in the firmament.[/del]

[u]Demonstration[/u]. For the translation is, by Theorem , a rotation about an axis in the firmament. But, by the definition of rotation, no rotation moves any particle except so far as it moves it out of the plane containing it and the axis. If there are special flat films through the axis that are not moved by the rotation, every particle in one of them remains at its point. Now, the film of the metron in the firmament is such a flat film; for, by the definition of the firmament, no particle moves into or out of it. Consequently, every particle in the firmament remains at its point, during any translation along an axis cutting the firmament.
