[u]Art[/u]. Definition. A metron is an ideal body, filling all space and capable of motion, subject to the following conditions:

[u]1[sup]st[/sup][/u], Every straight filament of the metron always remains straight; and consequently, every flat film remains flat;

[u]2[sup]nd[/sup][/u], Any straight filament of the metron can be brought into any ray in either of two opposite ways; and two motions can thereupon be made, either without or with the other, in any proportion; namely, [ins]first,[/ins] a motion by which particles are carried along the ray, until any [ins]one[/ins] particle is brought to any [del]point[/del] [ins]plane[/ins]; and second, a motion by which flat films are carried round the ray, until any one flat film is brought to any point;

[u]3[sup]d[/sup][/u], A straight filament being in a given ray and a given particle of that filament being in a given point of that ray there are but two other points of the ray (at most) to which any other one particle of that filament can be brought; [del]and[/del]

A straight filament being in a given ray, and a given flat film containing that filament being in a given plane [ins]through[/ins] [del]of[/del] the ray, there are but two planes through that ray into which a given flat film can be brought.
