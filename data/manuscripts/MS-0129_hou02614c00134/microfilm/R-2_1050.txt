[u]Theorem[/u]. [u]It takes two circuits to make one revolution[/u].

[u]Demonstration[/u]. Let {$r$} be an axis of [del]revol[/del] rotation, and {$t$} the conjugate axis of translation. Then I say that when one circuit has be accomplished a particle, at first at the point [A], not on either axis, has not returned to that point but is at the other intersection of the ray with the circle.
