[u]Art[/u] . [u]Theorem[/u] . [u]In every rotation of the metron, there is, through every point, a flat film that never leaves its plane.[/u]

[u]Demonstration[/u]. Let $\{x\}$ be the axis of rotation and [del]$[X]$[/del] [ins]$[Y]$[/ins] any [ins]flat film[/ins] point [del]of[/del] [ins]of[/ins] space. Then, I say, that there is a [del]plane[/del] through $[Y]$ which by no rotation round $\{x\}$ is carried out of its plane.
