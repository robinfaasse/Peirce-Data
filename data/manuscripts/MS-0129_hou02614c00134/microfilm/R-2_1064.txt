[u]Section 3.[/u] [u]The Firmament.[/u]

[del]Art.[/del] [u]Definition[/u] . The firmament is a surface to or from which no particle of the metron can move [del]into[/del] [ins]and[/ins] or away from contact [del]who[/del] with which no flat film of the metron can move.

[u]Theorem[/u] . If the distance between two points is infinite along a ray, it is infinite along every path.

[fig]Three points labeled A, C', and B with a curved dashed line above C'[/fig] [u]Demonstration[/u]. If the distance $|AB| = \infty$ and $|AC|$ is finite, then $|CB| = \infty$. For let the metron be turned in the plane $(ABC)$ round $[A]$ so as to bring the particle at $[C]$ into $[C']$ in $\{AB\}$. Then, $|CB|$ and $|C'B|$ cannot differ infinitely; and $[C']$ is not infinite, because there are but [del]two[/del] [ins]Hence[/ins] points on $\{AB\}$ infinitely distant from $[B]$. [del]Each[/del] $|C'B| = \infty$; and $|CB| = \infty$. Q.E.D.
