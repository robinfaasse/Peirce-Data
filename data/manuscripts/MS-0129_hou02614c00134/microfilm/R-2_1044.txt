[u]Art. Definitions[/u]. A circuit is the measure either of a
translation which carries a particle through every point of
the axis and leaves it at its starting point, or its is a rota-
tion which carries a flat film through every plane containing
the axis, and leaves it at its starting-plane.
A revolution is a movement which leaves every
filament in the line from which it started.
[u]Theorem[/u]. [u]All circuits are equal[/u].

the whole sweep round {a} is in angular measure equal to
that round {b}.
