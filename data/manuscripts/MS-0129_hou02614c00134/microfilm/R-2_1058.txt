But since $\{AR\}$ makes the same angle with $\{t\}$ as does $\{AS\}$, it follows that the same filament of the metron which at one time lies in $\{AR\}$ will by rotation round $\{t\}$ be brought into $\{AS\}$. And since $|AR| = |AS|$, the particle in $[R]$ will be carried to $[S]$. That is particles in $\{r\}$ will be carried along $\{r\}$, and since the particles at $[A]$, $[B]$, etc. in $\{t\}$ will not be moved from their points by the rotation, it follows that the flat films in $(\underline{r}A)$, $(\underline{r}B)$, etc. which are all the flat films through $\{r\}$ will remain in their planes. Thus the rotation round $\{t\}$ as axis of rotation is a translation along $\{r\}$, as axis of rotation. Q.E.D.

<u>Corollary</u>. In every rotation, through each point of the axis of rotation, there is a flat film that [del]remains[/del] [ins]stays[/ins] in its plane, namely, the plane through the axis of translation.

<u>Corollary</u>. In every translation, in each plane through the axis of translation, there is a particle that stays at its point, namely, the particle at the point where the plane is cut by the axis of rotation.
