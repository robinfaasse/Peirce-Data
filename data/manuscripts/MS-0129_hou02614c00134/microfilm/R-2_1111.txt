[u]Problem[/u] . [u]To divide a given limited straight line into[/u] [u]a given number of equal parts[/u].

[u]Solution[/u]. Through either end of the line draw a ray, and upon this mark a point at a convenient distance from the [del]intersection[/del] [ins]end of the given[/ins] line. Lay down a succession of other points at successive equal distances (by parallelograms, or otherwise) until [del]the given[/del] [ins]a number of[/ins] points equal to the required number of parts have been laid down. [del]Join[/del] [del]the last by a ray to the[/del] [ins]second[/ins] [del]end[/del] [ins]Join the last[/ins] of the given limited straight line and, through the rest draw parallels to this ray. Then those rays will cut the given line into equal parts, by the last Theorem.

[u]Second Solution[/u]. Draw a ray parallel to the given ray, and upon this at equal successive distances lay down [del]a[/del] points in number one more than the required number of parts. Join the extreme points to the extremities of the given line by rays, and [ins]and to the intersection of those rays[/ins] [del]join[/del] the intermediate points by other rays. Then the last rays will divide the given line into equal parts.
