[u]Art. 1 Definition[/u]. A sphere is a surface all whose points are at one distance, called the [u]radius[/u], from one point, called the [u]centre[/u].

A [u]circle[/u] is a line in a given plane all whose points are at one distance, called the [u]radius[/u], from one point, called the [u]centre[/u].

A straight line limited by the centre and any point of the sphere or circle is also called a radius.
