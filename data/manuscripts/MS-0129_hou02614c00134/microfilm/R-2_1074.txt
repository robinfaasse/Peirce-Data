[u]Art.[/u] Parallel rays and planes are rays and planes whose intersection is in the firmament.

[u]Corollary[/u]. Since the firmament is a plane, [del]it follows[/del] [ins]the intersection[/ins] of any ray with the firmament is a single point, and the intersection of any plane with the firmament is a ray. Consequently, through any [ins]given[/ins] point there is but one plane parallel to a given plane and but one ray parallel to a given ray. But of two parallel planes either is parallel to every ray of the other.
