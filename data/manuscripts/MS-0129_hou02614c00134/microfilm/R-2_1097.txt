A length is the measure of the extension of a limited
part of a homaloid between two points.

[del]bounded by them[/del] [ins]The[/ins] distance between two points is the least of the two lengths
[ins]upon[/ins] the homaloid through them.

Right lengths are equal lengths [ins]bounded by[/ins] the same pair
of points.

Supplementary lengths are the two lengths bounded by the
same pair of points.

[u]Notation[/u] [del]We will[/del] denote homaloids by braces like rays.

[u]Theorem[/u] . Vertical angles are equal.

Demonstration. Let [del]ray[/del] $\{AB\}$ and $\{CD\}$ be two [del]rays[/del] [ins]homaloids[/ins]
intersecting in $[X] = [\{AB\}\{CD\}]$. Let $[A]$
and $[B]$ be near $[X]$ on opposite sides of
it; and let $[C]$ and $[D]$ be near $[X]$ on
opposite sides of it. Then, I say, $\angle CXB = \angle DXA$.

[fig]Two intersecting lines forming vertical angles[/fig]

Let the metron move, with the [del]point[/del] particle at $[X]$ remaining
there, until the [del]fiber[/del] filament in $\{AB\}$ returns to this homa-
loid but with the part on the side of $[C]$ carried over
to the side of $[D]$. Then, the part previously just filling
$\angle AXB$, now just fills $\angle BXA$ (on the other side). So that
$\angle AXB$ (toward $[C]$) $= \angle BXA$ (toward $[D]$).

or $\angle AXC + \angle CXB = \angle BXD + \angle DXA$.

In a similar way
$\angle CXD$ (toward $[B]$) $= \angle DXC$ (toward $[A]$).

[del]Adding these equations[/del]
[del]Subtracting the last or[/del] $\angle CXB + \angle BXD = \angle DXA + \angle AXC$.

Adding the equations, we get
$\angle AXC + 2 \angle CXB + \angle BXD = \angle BXD + 2 \angle DXA + \angle AXC$

Subtracting $\angle AXC + \angle BXD$ from both sides and halving
$\angle CXB = \angle DXA$ Q.E.D.
