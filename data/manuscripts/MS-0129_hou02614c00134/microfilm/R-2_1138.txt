[u]Theorem 3[/u]. [u]If two sides of a triangle are quadrants, the angles opposite to them are right angles.[/u]

[u]Demonstration[/u]. This is equally true of plane and spherical triangles, and is proved for both in the same way. Hence, it will be sufficient to give the proof for spherical triangles.
