I begin Geometrical Optics by remarking that there is a system of
homaloids which are distinguished [del]from[/del] [ins]by[/ins] all others by the circumstance
that they look differently. Namely, all lines of possible [del]un[/del] undisturbed
vision (I do not say paths of light) [ins]lie in[/ins] are the lines of that system. They
are called rays. This gives me

[u]Postulate 5[/u]. (The Optical Property of Space.) All lines of vision lie in the
lines of a system of homaloids.
