Just now looking into Boole's book of which I only have the first edition. I see that with the notation $x^{(m)}$ to mean $x(x-1)(x-2)\dots$ to $m$ factors, he very absurdly writes $x^{(-m)}$ for
$\frac{1}{x}\ \frac{1}{x+1}\ \frac{1}{x+2}\ \dots$ to $m$ factors

Accordingly $x^{(+0)} = 1$ and $x^{(-0)} = \infty$

He ought to make $x^{(-m)}$ denote
$\frac{1}{x+1}\ \frac{1}{x+2}\ \dots$ to $m$ factors
