largely your own fault. You have again
reminded me of that fascinating but
delusive problem, and I have spent
the time since writing the above in
trying to solve it, but alas! I believe
[/marginalia]

that the case of exception to Kempe's method
[del]requiring that[/del] [ins]demands that[/ins] the map shall
have at least one triangular or quadri-
lateral district, in which case the pentagon
is not the next district to be colored, i.e.
