Dear Sir:

I look upon you as knowing all about the Non-Euclidean Geometry. You can no doubt tell me how many circles can be drawn through 3 points. Also, what are the properties of a set of 4 points through which 2 circles can be drawn? Can a third circle be drawn through such 4 points?
