17 Hammond St.
Worcester, Mass.
[date]June 29, 1899[/date] -

My dear Peirce:

Will you
give me the pleasure of en-
tertaining you at my house
during our celebration next
week &c.? I am to have also
Picard and Fine with me.
Please excuse the tardiness of
this invitation, but I did not
know earlier that you were
coming on.

Sincerely yours,
William E. Story
