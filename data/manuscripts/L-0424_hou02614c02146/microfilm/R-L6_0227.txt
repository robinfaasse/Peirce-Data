17 Hammond Street,
Worcester, Mass.
[date]June 13, 1899[/date]

My dear Peirce =

Having made what
inquiries I could without attracting
attention, I have found no good
evidence that Mendenhall is
dissatisfied here. There has been
lately a report that the presidency
of the Ohio State University had
been offered to him, but I don't
suppose that means anything.
It is commonly supposed that
his present salary is $5000.
but there is not, so far as I can
find, any public announcement
of salaries at the Tech.

Sincerely yours
William E. Story
