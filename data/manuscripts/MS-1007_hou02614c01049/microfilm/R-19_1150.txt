I therefore add to the sense a synopsis, then since sense contains
manifoldness in its intuition, there will always correspond to the
synopsis a synthesis and the Receptivity can only by a Spontaneity
make possible combined experiences. Now this is the ground of a
three-fold Synthesis, which necessarily occurs in all cognition: namely
the [u]Apprehension[/u] of representations as modifications of the mind in
intuition, the [u]Reproduction[/u] of them in the imagination and their [u]Recog-[/u]
[u]nition[/u] in conceptions. Now these lead to three subjective sources of
cognition, which themselves makes the Understanding possible and
through this all Experience as an empirical product of the Understanding.
