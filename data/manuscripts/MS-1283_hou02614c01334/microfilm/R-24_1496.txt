I think there can be no doubt that Galileo used
the pendulum to determine the value of $g$ or
the acceleration of a falling body in a second.
This he did at Padua, and I suspect also
elsewhere.

But it must be remembered that the ecclesias-
tical authorities would not for many years permit
Galileo to publish the least thing; and this discouraged
him from writing.
