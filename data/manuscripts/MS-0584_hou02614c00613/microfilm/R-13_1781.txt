[u]Lectures on British Logicians.[/u]

I was asked by the President to give nine lectures on the
History of Logic. Vastly too wide a subject to be treated in
nine lectures. I have therefore restricted myself to British
Logicians, — that being as small a branch of the subject
as occurred to me, at once. Yet, [ins]even[/ins] this would require 90
lectures instead of 9 to be adequately treated. Therefore, one
must restrict our attention chiefly to a few important
writers and pass over the others with a mere word.
We shall thus get some tolerable understanding of a few
logicians; which is better than getting no notion at all
of the many treated of.
