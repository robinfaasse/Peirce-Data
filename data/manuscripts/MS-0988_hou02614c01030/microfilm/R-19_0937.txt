[u]As 7.[/u] There cannot be a gap of time in
the existence of a substance.
"And if this is so that the living are
produced again from the dead, can there
be any other consequence than that our
souls are there? for surely they could not
be produced again if they did not ex-
ist." Phaedo 70.

sary that all things which have a con-
trary should be produced from nothing
else than their contrary. As for instance,
when any thing becomes greater, is it not
necessary that from being previously
smaller, it afterwards became greater?"
"'Yes.'
"And if it becomes smaller, will it not,
from being previously greater, afterwards
become smaller?"
"'It is so,' he replied.
