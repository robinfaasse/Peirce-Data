[u]PHAEDRUS[/u]
Ax 21 A chain of causation never ceases of itself.
"That only which moves itself, since it does not quit itself, never ceases to be moved" Phaedrus. §51.
Ax 22. Matter cannot move itself.
"For every body which is moved from without is soulless; but that which is moved from within of itself, possesses a soul, since this is the very nature of the soul." Phaedrus §53
Ax 23. The Soul moves itself.

[u]THEAETETUS.[/u]
Prop 4. The Conceptions which each faculty gives us are peculiar to itself.
"Such things as you perceive by means of one faculty it is impossible for you to perceive by means of another" Theæt. 103
Remark This depends upon the following axiom
Ax 24 We only know our faculties by the conceptions or feelings they give us — by their results.
