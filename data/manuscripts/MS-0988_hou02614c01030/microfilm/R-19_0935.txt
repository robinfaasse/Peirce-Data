[del]No. 4. General[/del] Pleasure and Pain, in the
Socratic use of the words, cannot
be present at the same time.

By this must be meant that the judge-
ment "this is pleasant" and the judgement
"this is painful" cannot both be made at
once; for, it is perfectly evident that some
elements of our general feeling at any
one time may be pleasant & others
painful, [del]so[/del] [ins]or[/ins] that we must either sup-
pose that the above is the meaning
or else that it is meant that our
general state cannot be one of pleasure
& one of pain also. It is not certain
which was meant, but the latter seems
an almost puerile remark.
