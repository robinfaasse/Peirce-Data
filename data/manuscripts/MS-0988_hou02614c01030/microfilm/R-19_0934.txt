[del]Ax 3. To return[/del] [ins]Do[/ins] an injury is unjust
"Socr Is it right to do evil, Crito, or not?
Cri. Surely it is not right, Socrates.
Socr. But what? To do evil in return
when one has been evil-entreated, is
that right or not?
Cri By no means.
Socr. For to do evil to men differs
in no respect from committing in-
justice" [u]Crito 10.[/u]
