Ax 1. Conscience judges of actions by the quality of the character they proceed from.
"You do not say well, friend, if you [ins]think[/ins] that a man, who is even of the least value, ought to take into the account the risk of life and death, and ought not to consider that alone [del]whether[/del] when he performs any action, whether he is acting justly or unjustly and the part of a good man or a bad man." Apol 16.
