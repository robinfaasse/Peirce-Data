The immediate, then, I declare to be that
which is such as it is positively all of itself;
and that I declare to be just any absolutely
simple quality of feeling. It must be simple
itself although it may be a perfect match
for a feeling which we can discern to be
highly complex. But [ins]as it is[/ins] [del]in its[/del] [ins]positiveness and[/ins] [del]immediacy and[/del]
absence of all [del]comparison and relation[/del], it
must be absolutely simple. Qualities of feeling
are remarkable for their myriad-million-
milliard-milliasse-fold variety. But this
variety is not in them as they are in their
presentness, but only as they are taken into
comparison [del]and so cease[/del] [ins]to be[/ins] [del]mere[/del] qualities.
As it is, in its being as quality each quality
is its own universe. There is nothing else.
This then is the first element which I
perceive in whatever is before my mind.
[del]It involves a qua[/del] I call this element Quality.
