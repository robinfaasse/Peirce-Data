PARK BENJAMIN,
EXPERT COUNSEL IN PATENT MATTERS,
MEMBER BRITISH INST. OF PATENT AGENTS,
203 BROADWAY, NEW YORK.

New York, December 6, 1895.

C. S. Peirce, Esq.

New York City.

My Dear Sir:-

I am greatly obliged for your kind letter. I shall of course be very glad to have your notes on my book; and as for examining my library, it will give me great pleasure to have you do so at any time you choose. It is here in my office, and I am usually to be found here myself. With regard to possible errors in my book, I think it could hardly be expected that in any work, relying as this does upon such a vast number of other publications, that some errors would not creep in, no matter how much care was taken to prevent them. At the same time, I think that there [del]are[/del] [ins]are[/ins] many statements which will be taken as errors because they run counter to general accepted opinions. In all such cases, I am pretty well provided with defensive armor and a little ammunition for use in reply. I have reversed my own previously expressed opinions in the book several times.

I shall be very glad to see you here whenever it suits your convenience to call.

Very truly yours,

Park Benjamin
