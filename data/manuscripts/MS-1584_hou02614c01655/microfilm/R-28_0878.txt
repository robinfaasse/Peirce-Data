if there is no [ins]one to one[/ins] relation in which

either there is no one to one relation
in which every M stands to an M
that is not one fixed M or else
there is a one to one relation in
which every sum of Ns stands
to an M.

such a relation that if [del]an individual[/del] [ins]an individual[/ins] x is r to
