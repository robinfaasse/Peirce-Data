[u]Notes on Steinthal[/u]

The old stoa recognized (p 290) four
parts of speech [u]μέρη λόγου[/u]. These
were [u]ὄνομα[/u], [u]ῥῆμα[/u], [u]σύνδεσμος[/u],
[u]ἄρθρον[/u].
The [u]ἄρθρα[/u] were pronouns and article
Of course, [u]ὄνομα[/u] is [del]nomen[/del] noun
[u]ῥῆμα[/u] is [del]verbum[/del] verb
[u]σύνδεσμος[/u] is conjunction
[u]ἄρθρον[/u] is [del]articulum[/del] article

[u]ὄνομα[/u] [del]nomen proprium[/del]
and [u]προσηγορία[/u] or
[u]ὄνομα προσηγορικόν[/u]
[del]nomen appellativum[/del]

[del]= προσηγορέω[/del] to greet. The [u]προσηγορία[/u]
was the name by which a man
