Law I If ap·b cp·d
then bc·q ad·q

Law II If ab·p cp·d
then bc·q aq·d

Law III If ac·c
then c=a

Law IV ac·a

Law V "No entity is absent from the
system which can consistently
be present."

<u>§7.</u> If ac·b bc·a
then a=b
For by Law I bb·q aa·q
Hence by Law III b=q=a

<u>§8</u> If ab·c bc·d
then ab·d
For by II bb·q aq·d
by III b=q ∴ ab·d

If ab·c bc·d
then ad·c
For by I ad·r cc·r
but c=r ∴ ad·c

<u>§9.</u> If ab·c ab·d cd·e
ab·e
For from ab·c cd·e by II sa·e b·s
From bd·s ab·d by §8 ab·s
From sa·e ab·s by §8 ab·e
