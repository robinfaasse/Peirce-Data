§1. Property of the Stereographic.
If a point in space X, Y, Z
be projected from the origin
upon the plane of Z = 1, its projection
x, y will be
x = [fraction]X/Z[/fraction]  y = [fraction]Y/Z[/fraction]

If we imagine the sphere through
the origin
X² + Y² + (Z - 1)² = 1
to be cut by the plane
[fraction]X/A[/fraction] + [fraction]Y/B[/fraction] + [fraction]Z/C[/fraction] = 1

we have
[fraction]X²/Z²[/fraction] + [fraction]Y²/Z²[/fraction] = - [del]1[/del] [ins]+[/ins] [fraction]2/Z[/fraction]

[del]and[/del] [fraction]X/AZ[/fraction] + [fraction]Y/BZ[/fraction] = [fraction]1/Z[/fraction] - [fraction]1/C[/fraction]

or eliminating Z
x² + y² + 1 = [fraction]2/A[/fraction] x + [fraction]2/B[/fraction] y + [fraction]2/C[/fraction]
