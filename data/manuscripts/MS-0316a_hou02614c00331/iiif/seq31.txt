assenting to the proposition, it is obvious that a
proposition not assented to has no cognitive value
whatever. But nobody will or can assent to a
proposition without supposing himself to have

last cannot be the case with such a proposition as

perceptual; for the reason that however the
psychologist might class such a compulsory
