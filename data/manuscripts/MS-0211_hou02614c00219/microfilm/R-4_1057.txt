$\frac{29}{60} \frac{u_{x,y-1}}{u_{x,y}}$ is the probability of his gaining the next bet

Let $U_{x,y}$ be the average number of bets after he has

Then
$U_{x,y} = \frac{31}{60} \frac{u_{x,y+1}}{u_{x,y}} U_{x,y+1} + \frac{29}{60} \frac{u_{x,y-1}}{u_{x,y}} U_{x,y-1} + 1$

or
$u_{x,y} U_{x,y} = \frac{31}{60} u_{x,y+1} U_{x,y+1} + \frac{29}{60} u_{x,y-1} U_{x,y-1} + u_{x,y}$

Since $u_{x,y} = \frac{(\frac{29}{31})^{y+1} - (\frac{29}{31})^{x+1}}{1 - (\frac{29}{31})^{x+1}}$

$\frac{u_{x,y}}{(\frac{29}{31})^{y+1}} = \frac{1}{1 - (\frac{29}{31})^{x+1}} - \frac{(\frac{29}{31})^{x}}{1 - (\frac{29}{31})^{x+1}} (\frac{31}{29})^{y}$
