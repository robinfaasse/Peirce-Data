Now the chance of losing one bet is [frac]30[/frac]/[frac]61[/frac]. The chance of losing ten times running is this raised to the 10[sup]th[/sup] power, which calculation by logarithms shows to be 1/870. The odds are therefore 869 to 1 that a man with a fortune of 1023 francs [del]will win[/del] can net 1 franc by a martingale. The fortune of 1,048,575 francs would be exhausted by 20 successive losses at a martingale.
