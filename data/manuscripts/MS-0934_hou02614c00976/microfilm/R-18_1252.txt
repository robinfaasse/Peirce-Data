§27. The three conceptions of Quality, Relation, and Representa-
tion, are numerical. The quality is First, in the sense of the original,
the fresh. [del]The word[/del] Relation is simply otherness, or duality.
Representation is mediation, or thirdness.

There is nothing fanciful in the connection of the conceptions
with those of the numbers; on the contrary, the conceptions are
not truly apprehended [ins]are not conceived in all their generality[/ins] until they are seen to be [del]essentially nothing but the first three vocables of that mystic formula, "Eeny, meeny, mony, mije" etc. I call it mystic, because, though mere nonsense, from it are evolved all the mysteries of numbers, and all the subtleties of metaphysics.[/del]

§28. These three accidents suggest one another by a natural
self-development of thought, which however does not clearly
appear when stated in brief.

We must begin with the First. That which is first must be fresh and
new; and that which is fresh and new must be vivid. That which is
first must be original and free; for the dependent is to be second.
That which is quite free must be unlimited, and therefore endlessly
varied. Endlessly varied spontaneous vividness is the manifold of pure quality.
