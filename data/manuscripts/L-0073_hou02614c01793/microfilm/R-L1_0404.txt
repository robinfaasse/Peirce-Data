By [u]subjects of the same category[/u] I mean [del]subje[/del] two or more
subjects each of which may, without contradiction, be supposed to
be modified, without altering its general nature, so as to take any

are not; for a position cannot burst into two, or move [ins]so as to[/ins]

be more or less widely conceived.

By saying that subjects are [u]more or less independent[/u], I mean,
that they can be supposed, without absurdity, to interchange their
characters, more or less, without losing their identities. Thus, two
atoms can be supposed, in the course of time, to interchange every
instantaneous character; but two [del]qual[/del] colors cannot interchange
many of their characters without becoming different colors.
