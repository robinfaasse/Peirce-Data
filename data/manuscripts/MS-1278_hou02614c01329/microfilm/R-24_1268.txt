The most general practical formula reminds
one of the Imitation of Christ; it was To
follow god. Another thing particularly impressed
upon the disciples was that all human life
is under the continual inspection of the deity.
Hence, severe selfexamination was inculcated.
On going to bed, every night, the disciple must
carefully go over all the acts of the day, and
must seriously upbraid himself for every
infraction of the great principle of universal
amity. This careful review of one's actions
was also practiced for the purpose of
strengthening the memory. Every day, before
rising from bed, all the events of the day before
were to be carefully gone over. After rising
