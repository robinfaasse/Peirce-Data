[u]Pythagoras[/u]

Is said by several good authorities to have come to Rome
under Tarquinius Superbus. Solinus says [del]518 B.C.[/del] in the last
year of that King. Livy that he taught in Italy under Tarquinius
Priscus; but perhaps he confuses the date of his reaching Italy
with that of his leaving Samos, which Jamblichus says was 3
years earlier. Cicero says in the 4[ins]th[/ins] year of Tarquinius Superbus
which would be about [date]531 B.C.[/date] Porphyry and Aristoxenes say
he left Samos to escape the tyranny of Polycrates which would
be a few years before [date]529 B.C.[/date] We may take [date]531 B.C.[/date] as
a pretty well-established date.
