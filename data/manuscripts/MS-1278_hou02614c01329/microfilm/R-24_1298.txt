Remained 12 years in Babylon and
in [date]512[/date] (aged 57 years) with the aid
of Democedes, doctor of Darius, es-
caped to Greece.

Found his parents still living
in Samos. At Delos placed a garland
on the altar of ἀναίμακτου τοῦ
Γενήτορος Ἀπόλλωνος. Tended the sick
bed of Pherecydes till he died of lice.
In Crete, was initiated by Epimenides in
the mysteries of Idean Zeus.

Tried to found a school at Samos. Failed.
In [date]510[/date] (aged 59) landed at Sybaris.
Going from there to Tarentum bought
a whole seine of fish in order to
set them free. At Crotona, he
produced a profound impression.
His public lectures were fashionable
& thronged by persons who ex-
pected to hear curious theories
