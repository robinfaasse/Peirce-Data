[u]Chronology of Pythagoras[/u]

[u]1[sup]st[/sup].[/u] The best date is that of the arrival of Pythagoras in Italy which Cicero sets at [date]529 B.C.[/date] This is the date of the death of Cyrus. Cicero no doubt selected this date because Pythagoras was said to have left Samos to escape the tyranny of Polycrates who obtained possession of the island during the last years of Cyrus. Others simply say he arrived while Tarquinius Superbus was reigning in Rome. Solinus puts the arrival in the last year of that King; Livy says it was in the reign of Servius Tullius, which ended [date]534 B.C.[/date]

[u]2[sup]nd[/sup].[/u] Jamblichus says the Pythagoras was taken prisoner by Cambyses in Egypt. That would suit no date earlier than [date]527 B.C.[/date] and is therefore inconsistent with the date just obtained. In order to account for the story we may suppose he was taken prisoner by the Persians at an earlier date. That could only have been by Cyrus about [date]544 B.C.[/date] in Ionia. How would this hypothesis suit other facts? In the first place, we note that in [date]544 B.C.[/date] the prisoner would not have been carried to Babylon, which only fell [date]538 B.C.[/date] but would have been carried further east where he would have been much more likely to come into relation with Brahmans, as Dr. Schröder says he must have done. In the next place we notice that the statement of Jamblichus that he returned to Samos 12 years after [del]this[/del] [ins]his[/ins] capture and was 3 years in going to Italy suits this hypothesis exactly.
