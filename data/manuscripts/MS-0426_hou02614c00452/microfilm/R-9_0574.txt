[u]Section I. Classification of the Sciences.[/u]

Auguste Comte it was, as far as I am informed, who first had the idea of arranging the sciences in a ladder, each leading to the next. From the point of view of logic, at least, this is decidedly the best arrangement; because in order to prove one thing to be true, if one is to proceed beyond more perceptual facts, it is necessary to assume the truth of something else, while to attempt to prove this something else by assuming the truth of the former proposition would be to complete a vicious circle. It does not [ins]often[/ins] happen that there are [del]several[/del] [ins]two[/ins] independent ways of proving the same thing; and when this does happen, one of those ways will usual be more satisfactory or more fundamental than the other.
