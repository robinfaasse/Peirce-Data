What is the kind of science upon which the arts most call?
This is a question to which almost anybody will, until he comes to
consider it carefully, give a wrong answer. Let us take, as an
example, the art of illumination; and let us suppose it to
be in that stage in which acetylene is considered as an illu-
minant, which was, in fact, the situation from 1885 to 1895.
