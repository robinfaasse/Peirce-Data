Upon these principles the lines between the different sciences are
easily drawn. There never would have been any difficulty if
the classifiers had not stupidly persisted in looking upon science

Classification, however, is not a mere matter of drawing lines.
The essential relations between the classes have to be brought out,
especially the relations of dependence. [del]The man of[/del] [ins]One[/ins] science has to
consult [del]the man of[/del] another, or virtually to do so by being himself that
other. This [ins]circumstance[/ins] [del]fact[/del] greatly modifies the isolation of specialities.
This is a fact which we have to study in some detail.
