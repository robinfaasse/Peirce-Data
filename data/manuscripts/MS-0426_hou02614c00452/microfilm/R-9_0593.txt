I shall have to leave the classification of the arts for a
student of different talents from mine. I have drawn up a
list of over four hundred of them, and have found that some
are almost purely psychical, [ins]like that of placating the invisible powers,[/ins] while others are almost purely
material physical, like gold-beating. Still, it seems to me to be true
that all arts depend both upon psychical and upon physical know-
ledge. [del]But still[/del] [ins]At all events[/ins] I find no reason to doubt that there
are natural divisions between them. Indeed, so far as they con-
stitute different trades, the divisions are easily found. But
when it comes to higher groups, I have not succeeded in making
a satisfactory classification; nor does it greatly concern the
Science of Logic that this should be done.
