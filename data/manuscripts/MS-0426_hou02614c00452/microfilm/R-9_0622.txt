My position is that neither the question about protoplasms nor
that about ether can be answered at present, but that they
are eminently proper questions to spring upon nature as-
whenever an opportunity can be found; and that it will
be worth while to labor hard for an opportunity. Meantime,
to an unanswered question, it is right that the analogical side
should have a slight predominance, and therefore I incline
slightly toward thinking that both protoplasms and ether
are chemical substances. I have no sympathy with the
spirit that wishes to bar experiment and on meta-
physical grounds, and which, had it the power, I am quite
sure, in spite of all denials, would use force and torture to
