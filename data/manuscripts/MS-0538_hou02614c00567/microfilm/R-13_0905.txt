[del]qualitative equiparance.[/del]

[del]The relation of being alternative of something repugnant to is[/del] the relation of being '[u]essential to[/u]' or '[u]subordinate to[/u]'. That is the proposition that a [del]man[/del] [ins]person[/ins] is baptized is essential to his being confirmed, [del]and the[/del] proposition that some animal is immortal is essential to some man being immortal; [del]that is[/del] and the converse relation is [del]that[/del] of being [u]dependent upon[/u] or [u]superordinate to[/u]. That is, either the dependent proposition is false or the essential proposition is true. These and other terms are fully discussed in DeMorgan's [u]Syllabus of Logic[/u].
