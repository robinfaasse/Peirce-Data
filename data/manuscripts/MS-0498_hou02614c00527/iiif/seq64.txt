[del]The first part of the study of the life of
an insect consists in following it through
its several stages of growth, from the egg
to the adult state.
A full life history must go much farther than this;
it must show how it lives, what it eats, what enemies
it has, what it does for the preservation of its
offspring, and its relations to the other things that
surround it.
The life history of an insect, properly treated,
is one of the most interesting studies in the
world, and it is the aim of this book to
place the more important facts of this science
clearly and simply before the student.[/del]
