[u]Demonstration[/u]. For note the way in which the
network is constructed by which designations are
attached to points. It may be started in several
ways, but the result is always substantially the
same.

We may start with five points [del]none[/del] [ins]no[/ins] three coplanar.
Four of these receive asyzygetic designations, say
[O], [X], [Y], [Z]. The designations of any five
points are syzygetic. [del]note[/del] This is at least evident of
these four and any fifth. The fifth point receives
arbitrarily any [del]the[/del] syzygetic designation, say [O+X+Y+Z]
(If it is called, say, [O+X+2Y+3Z], the only effect is
that every multiplier of Y [del]for[/del] in every point is to be mul-
tiplied by 2 and every [del]p[/del] multiplier of Z is to be multiplied by
3.) Calling the fifth point [O+X+Y+Z], [ins]through this[/ins] [del]from[/del] [ins]and[/ins] [del]each[/del]
and each of the other points there is a ray which cuts the
plane of the remaining three, and the point of intersection
is named by [del]dropping[/del] so that the three coradial points
shall have syzygetic designations and the four coplanar
points shall have syzygetic designations [del]s[/del] thus:

Thus, [{O, O+X+Y+Z}(XYZ)] = [X+Y+Z]
[{X, O+X+Y+Z}(OYZ)] = [O+Y+Z]
[{Y, O+X+Y+Z}(XOZ)] = [O+X+Z]
[{Z, O+X+Y+Z}(XYO)] = [O+X+Y]
