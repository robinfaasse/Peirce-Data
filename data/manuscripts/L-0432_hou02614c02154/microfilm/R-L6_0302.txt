[date]Durham N. H. 1904 May 2[/date]

My dear Peirce

I am indeed sorry
to hear of your illness.
I had hoped to see you
at Washington. I was there
but only for a short time.
I am glad to hear that you
are better. Do not write
to me until you are quite
well. I will fill the
blank as you suggest, but
I do not see a teacher's [del]l[/del] [ins]c[/ins]ert.

Very truly yours,

Charles S. Minot
