THE RICE KENDALL COMPANY.
BOSTON, [date]April. 28 1894[/date]
[/marginalia]

Dear Sir;
I have noticed in one of the papers that you are to publish "The Principles of Philosophy: or, Logic, Physics and Physics considered as a Unity, in the light of the Nineteenth Century."
Is this work to embody the lectures you delivered at the Lowell Institute in 1892? I had the pleasure of listening to that course, and would like to read and preserve the matter in book form.

Very truly, John H. Rice.

Charles S. Peirce, Esq.
