Hiroshima, Japan, March 22 1894

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.,
U. S. A. :-

Having seen notice of the twelve vol-
umns which you propose to issue under the general head of
"The Principles of Philosophy", I write to request you to kind-
ly send me a copy of your prospectus and syllabus. I have [del]nev[/del]
never had the pleasure to read any of the articles so highly
spoken of by the various distinguished writers from whom you
quote in the Nation of Feb. 15th but from what they say I an-
ticipate a treat. Please consider me a subscriber for your
first volumn.

Yours very sincerely,

J. Walter Doughty
