**Library of the University of Wisconsin**
**Madison, Wisconsin**

[date]25 Nov'. 95[/date]

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

My dear Sir: —

May I trouble you to inform me on enclosed post-card whether you have yet issued your announced work on the Principles of Philosophy and if not when it may be expected? Our bookseller has been unable to get any information about the work.

Faithfully,
Walter M. Smith,
Librarian
