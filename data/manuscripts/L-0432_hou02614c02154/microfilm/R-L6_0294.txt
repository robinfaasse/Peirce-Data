THOMAS W. DITTY,
Attorney and Counsellor at Law,
LAMAR, MISSOURI.

[date]March 24 1894[/date]

C. S. Peirce, Esq.
Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:—

I have forgotten whether I sent my order to you for your work: "The Principles of Philosophy: or, Logic, Physics, and Psychics, considered as a unity, in the light of the Nineteenth Century." In 12 volumes, $2.50 per vol. If I haven't already done so, you may then consider me as a subscriber and I will pay for same on delivery

Reply
T. W. Ditty
