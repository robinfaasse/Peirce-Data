Yale University,
New Haven, Conn., January 9, 1894.

Dear Mr. Peirce. -

Your letter of January 2d was duly
received, and with it the prospectus of your proposed work on
"The Principles of Philosophy."

I am very glad to know that this great work on which you have
spent so much time and thought is so far advanced towards publica-
tion, and it will be a pleasure to me to [u]subscribe[/u] for it myself,
and to do what I can in other respects to bring it to the notice
of my friends, and of the general public.

Yours, very truly,

O. C. Marsh

Professor Charles S. Peirce.
