Exactly what this last phrase means is not quite clear. I
suspect the πρότερον to be a blunder of Apellicon; because I
have strong reason to think that Aristotle generally put 64
lines of the Berlin text on each sheet of papyrus, and Apellicon,
as we are told on good authority, made out a text by filling
blanks occasioned by insects etc, that had destroyed places
in the original. Now these would be likely to occur at
corresponding places of successive sheets [del]and[/del] [ins]particularly[/ins]
toward the end of each book. But at 41b20 "τὰς ΕΖ"
is certainly wrong, and is probably due to Apellicon. [del]Precisely[/del]
