Vineyard Haven, Mass.
[date]22 May 1912[/date].

Dear Sir:

I trust I am not asking too much in making a respectful request for some sentiment and autograph from you for my collection.

I have letters from nearly all the members of the N.A.S. and wish very much to complete the list. Will you favor me with a few words?

This is my third letter to you, my last was written [date]Feb. 23, 1910[/date].

Faithfully Yours
Homer Norris Jr.

Prof. Charles S. Peirce.
