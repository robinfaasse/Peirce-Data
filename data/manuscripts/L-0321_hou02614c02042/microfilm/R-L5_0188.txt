These rules are mostly very simple appli-
cations of one of the most historically
famous propositions of mathematics called
[u]Fermat's theorem[/u] (or else of an easy exten-
sion of it). It should be stated in every
encyclopaedic dictionary and proved in
every encyclopedia of any pretensions.
The best book on this fascinating subject
is Dedekind's edition Lejeune-Dirichlet's
"Vorlesungen über Zahlentheorie". But
most books give the reader more or less diffi-
culty owing to their not precisely defining their
technical terms and vigorously sticking to their
definitions. Since definitions are in my line, I

here define the principal terms needed.
