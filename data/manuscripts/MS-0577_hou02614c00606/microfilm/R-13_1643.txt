[u]Algebraic Rules[/u].

[u]Rule 1[/u]. The order of antecedents is immaterial.
Thus, a⤙b⤙c is identical with b⤙a⤙c.

[u]Rule 2[/u]. Any antecedent may be inserted under
an even number of parentheses, or none, and
omitted under an odd number. Thus if we
have given (a⤙b)⤙c we can write d⤙(a⤙b)⤙c
or [(d⤙a)⤙b]⤙c or b⤙c.
