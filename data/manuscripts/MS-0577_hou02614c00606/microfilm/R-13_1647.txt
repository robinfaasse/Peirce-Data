[u]The algebra of the copula.[/u]

This is an algebra of two-valued quantity, and the operation is such that if $f$ and $t$ are the two values,
$$ft = t \quad ff = t \quad tt = t \quad tf = f.$$

The number of forms of combination with one letter is one, with two letters [del]one[/del], $ab$, with three letters two $(ab)c$ and $a(bc)$, or as we shall write the latter $abc$, for we shall always omit parentheses extending to the end of the formula or [del]to[/del] the end of exterior parentheses. With four letters there are 5 forms, $[(ab)c]d$, $[abc]d$, $(ab)cd$, $a(bc)d$, $abcd$. With 5 letters there are 14 forms, etc.
