[u]Rule 5 Principle of breaking parentheses[/u]. At any copula
within an even number of parentheses a break may
be made, and the beginning of the inner parenthesis
brought to that point, while all that is to the left of
that to the [ins]next[/ins] outer parenthesis may be carried out
of this latter parenthesis.

• $ft = t$
[del]•[/del] $ff = t$
$tt = t$
$tf = f$
