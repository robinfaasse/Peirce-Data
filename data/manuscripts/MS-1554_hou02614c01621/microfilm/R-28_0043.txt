[u]A.[/u]

The rules are to show, with reference
to the party originally responsible
for the work,
[u]1. Who he is.[/u]
[u]2. How he is to be designated.[/u]

[u]1.[/u]

Who is the party responsible for the
work is determined by
a. A general principle;
b. Certain special rules.

[u]a.[/u]

In general, the party responsible for
the work is the first of the follow-
ing who is ascertainable:—

i. The author;
ii. The editor, [del]stated to be[/del] [ins]named as[/ins] responsible;
iii. The party causing the publica-
tion.
iv. The party [ins]written to[/ins] whose properties
the book [del]is written to[/del] describes.
