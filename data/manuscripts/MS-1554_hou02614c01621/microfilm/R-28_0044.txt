In particular, the responsible party

i. of a translated book is the origi-
nal author not the translator.

ii. of a text with commentary is
the author of the text, not the com-
mentator

iii. of a disputation is he who
starts it; except that in a cri-
minal case, it is the first de-
fendant.

iv. of a forged book is the pretend-
ed author; but this does not
apply to a work attributed to
a fictitious or obscure writer.

v. of a sacred book, the reputed
author under the euphemism
of the name of the collection
as [u]the Bible[/u].
