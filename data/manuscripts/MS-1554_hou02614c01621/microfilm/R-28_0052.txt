[pn]Rules for Cataloguing[/pn]
[pn]C. S. P.'s books[/pn]

This is to be an "author cata-
logue" governed by the rules
1[sup]st[/sup] of Good Sense,
2[sup]d[/sup] of Charles A. Cutter.

Each publication is entered on
a slip having
A. Name of author or party origi-
nally responsible for the work.
B. Title of work
C. Description of the book
D. Place on shelves.

***

[pn]A[/pn]

The party responsible is
[del]i[/del] [ins]i[/ins] The author proper.
ii The editor if named as re-
sponsible
iii The party ordering or causing
the publication.
iv. The party whose proper-
ties are described.

In particular,
Not translator, but author.
Not compiler of concordance
but author.
Not commentator (if text is
given) but author.
The party who starts a dispu-
tation
