[u]RULES[/u]
for cataloguing C. S. Peirce's Books.

The catalogue is to be on slips and the
rules relate
[u]First[/u], to the [del]entries[/del] [ins]writing[/ins] on the slips,
[u]Second[/u], to the treatment of the slips themselves.
In scholastic language the first relate to the slips [del]formaliter[/del] [ins]formally[/ins]
the latter [del]materialiter[/del] [ins]materially[/ins].

[u]First,[/u]
[u]ENTRIES.[/u]

Entries are to be made on two kind of slips
I. [u]Chief slips[/u].
II. [u]Cross-reference slips[/u].

I.
[u]Chief Slips.[/u]

One chief slip is to be appropriated to each
publication, and is to show:
A. The [u]Original[/u], or virtual author;
B. The [u]Title[/u];
C. The [u]edition and Dress[/u], generally;
D. The [u]Contents[/u] (especially defects thereof);
E. [u]Place on shelves[/u].
