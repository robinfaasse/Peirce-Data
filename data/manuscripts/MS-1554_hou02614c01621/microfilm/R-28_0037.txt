b.

i. [del]The designation of the author is to be written[/del]
in its Latinized form if that is as familiar
as another; for Latin is the vernacular of
librarians. Otherwise, in such language
& spelling as the original would himself
have preferred among such as he would
have dared to submit to judicial examination.
If the alphabet is not Roman, Canon
Taylor's transliteration is to be used. But
if the name is Greek it is to be Latinized [ins]so far
as names to be given under Latinized forms if possible.[/ins]

ii. The surname to stand first. Not to be
preceded by a particle except in English &
Dutch where the particle is considered as
part of the name.

iii. If the original is an office or corporation,
crossreferences from any [del]other[/del] entry used by
another library must be used. But here the
effort will be to particularize and not accumu-
late great numbers of cards under one heading.
Hence, Academies sitting usually in particular
towns will be entered under those towns.

iv. Corporations will be entered under their most
usual [del](not the worst conceit)[/del] designations.
