[del]Designation of the Original.[/del]
The rules must determine
a. The chief designation,
b. The Mode of writing it,
c. Secondary designations.

a.
The chief designation is to be
i. The most familiar, if any is familiar to the majority of learned men. Thus, Voltaire, Georges Sand, George Eliot, Lord Bacon, Melanchthon; but not Marbodens Gallus, Puteanus (for Dupuis), nor Zigzag.
ii. If none is generally familiar (and always for cross-reference) the latest strict, formal and most approved legal title (owh inferior to a previous title); or [del]such[/del] [ins]the[/ins] nearest approximation to this known to the cataloguer.
