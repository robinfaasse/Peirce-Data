i. This designation is to be written in its Latinized
form, if that is well known. Otherwise, in
such language and spelling as the man
himself would have preferred in a
formal statement (unless he was an
impostor.) If the alphabet is not Roman,
Canon Taylor's transliteration to be used.
(But if Greek, by the first part of the rule the
Latinized form is taken.)

ii. The surname to stand first. Not to be pre-
ceded by particle except in English & Dutch
where particle is considered part of the name
and not an addition expressive of nobility.

iii. If the original is an office or corporation
(1) if under government, civil or religious, its
hierarchical place is to be shown, the Sove-
reign (under God) to stand first. But
a country or church rather than a King
or Pope.
(2) if an Academy, not under a government
the entry under the place of sitting if any usual
(3) otherwise the usual word describing
[del]the body[/del] to stand first.
