[del]a.[/del] Of any [ins]more[/ins] work or collection
of works, [del]that[/del] editions are to
be placed first which are
in the original language.
b. Next translations into Latin
c. Next translations into English
d. Next translations into other
tongues.
i. Of editions which do not dif-
fer in the above respects, the
earlier are to be placed before
the later.
Finally of those which differ
in none of these respects, the
more valuable is to precede
the less valuable.
