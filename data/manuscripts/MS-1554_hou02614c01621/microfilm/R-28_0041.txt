b.
[u]In particular[/u]
for the original is to be taken
i. The author rather than the translator;
ii. The author of the text rather than the commentator (though with cross-reference);
iii. Of a disputation, the respondent;
iv. Of an inquest or trial, the defendant or accused party;
v. Of the forged book of a celebrity, that man (not so with a mere pseudonym as Gulliver)
vi. Of a sacred book, the name of the collection, instead of the Divinity who is supposed to have inspired it.
vii. In case there are several works by different authors, or one work [del]by[/del] written by several authors in collaboration, the first named is to be taken as Original.
