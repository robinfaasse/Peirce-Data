A.
[u]The Original.[/u]
The rules must show
1. Who is to be taken as such;
2. How he is to be designated.

[u]1.[/u]
[u]Who the Original.[/u]
Who is to be regarded as the original is to
be determined
[del]a.[/del] [ins]a.[/ins] by General Principles,
b. by Special Rules.

a.
[u]In general,[/u]
the original will be the first of the
following ascertainable:
i. The author;
ii. The editor, named as responsible;
[del]iii.[/del] [ins]iii.[/ins] The party causing the publication;
iv. The party whose property the book
is intended to describe.
