The rules show in reference to the
responsible party
[u]a.[/u] What is to be his chief designa-
tion.
[u]b.[/u] How this is to be written.
[u]c.[/u] What secondary designations
are to be used.

[u]a.[/u]

The chief designation is to be the
latest strictly formal and legal title
or name of the party (but not an
inferior title assumed later than
and in addition to another;) or if
the whole of this is not known,
such part of it as may be known;
or if none of it is known that
name or title which most close-
ly approximates to this charac-
ter.
