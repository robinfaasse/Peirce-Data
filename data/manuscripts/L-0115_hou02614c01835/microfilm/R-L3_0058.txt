[marginalia]YB3b#2[/marginalia] 52 Wall St-
[date]Nov. 2/72[/date]

My dear Charley

I have received
today the two charts for
which I am very much
obliged to you and hope
to use them next
summer. I admire
very much the new harbor
charts, and am sure that
I shall at some time
find it very useful. Thanking
you again, I remain,
Your Affectionate Cousin
Fidul. Davis-
