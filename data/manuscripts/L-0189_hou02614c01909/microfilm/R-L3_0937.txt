[ins]✓[/ins] Milford
[date]Nov 7. 1903.[/date]

My dear Sir

Allow me to thank you
very sincerely for your kind
letter, & for your contributions
to the Nation which I much
enjoyed.

As I am an idle man now,
& you are very much the
reverse, perhaps you will
allow me to disregard
ceremony & call upon you.
I need not say that it
would be very much pleasure
us to make your acquaintance.
