There never was on my part any objection

I had expected to send by this
mail to the Supt. some suggestions
in reference to increase of pay in
the Office for the next fiscal year,
but I am prevented from doing so
by the pressure of engagements. Next
mail from Brest will do just as well.

I go again to Brest to-night, having
got the chronographic registration
into good working order at the Paris
Obsy. Exchange with Greenwich is
arranged, and I presume that the
Brest - St Pierre cable will have
been repaired to-day.

Yours very truly
J. E. Hilgard

Chs. S. Peirce Esq
