The following shows the inferences to be drawn from your comparison of German Normal Metre No 49 with the stops of Saxton's comparator.

According to the statement forwarded to me from the office, the metre No 49 is longer than the stop metre by 365.0 [ins]$\mu$[/ins] at 72.8 F° and the stop-metre [ins]at 69.04 F[/ins] equals the committee metre at 32° F, and its coefficient of expansion is 9.84 [ins]$\mu$[/ins] per degree F.

In the Superintendent's Report, pages 273-275, I have shown that the coefficient of expansion of No 49 is 18.83 [ins]$\mu$[/ins] per degree C = 10.46 [ins]$\mu$[/ins] per degree F.
