Paris, [date]21st June, 1872[/date]

My dear Sir,

I was very glad
to receive yesterday your letter
of [date]17th May[/date], which was addressed
to me at Brest, and bears the Brest
stamp of [date]May 31[/date], when I was in that
very place, and inquired concerning &
for letters. There is no accounting for
the stupidity of French Officials.

My present address is care of G. Bossange,
16 rue du 4 Sept. Paris.

I will arrange for a supply of 36 x 49
chart paper, but it is perfectly necessary
to get [del]more than[/del] [ins]a[/ins] couple of reams
of the American manufacture.
