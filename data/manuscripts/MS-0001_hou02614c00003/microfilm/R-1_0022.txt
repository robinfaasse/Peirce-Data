Since this process is by no means exempt from liability
to mistakes (reversals of what is before one's eyes, especially, when
multiple, being the point where mistakes in computation are
most to be feared), I give an easier rule for simple subtrac-
tion, as follows:

Write the subtrahend columnwise, under the minuend, and
draw a horizontal line under them. Start at the right-hand-
most case of a [u]y[/u] under a [u]u[/u], drawing a line which in that
column shall be below the horizontal line and thence being
continued toward the left shall preferably lie above the
horizontal line as long as there is a [u]y[/u] in each column
which it shall loop (looping preferably a [u]y[/u] in the subtrahend)
but passing below the horizontal line [ins]in every column where there[/ins]
that contains no [u]y[/u] above it. And as soon as this line
has looped a [u]y[/u] in the minuend, it shall there stop and go no
further. Then pass to the right to the next case of a [u]y[/u] below a [u]u[/u]
