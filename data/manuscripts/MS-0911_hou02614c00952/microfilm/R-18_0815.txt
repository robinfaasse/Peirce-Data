A dual character may be degenerate,
in the same sense in which mathe-
maticians term [del]two[/del] a pair of straight
lines a degenerate conic, etc. Namely,
a dual character may consist mere-
ly in the two objects having the same
singular character. This is a relation
of similarity. The non-degenerate dual
relation is a real relation. What
are usually called relations of reason
are so only in a loose sense; all relations
except those of similarity are in the
perfect strictness real relations. In
the same way the will is of two kinds,
the external will and self control;
and the discharge of a nerve cell
is either excitatory or inhibitory.
