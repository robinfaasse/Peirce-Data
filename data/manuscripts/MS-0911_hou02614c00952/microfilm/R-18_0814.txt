[marginalia]Ms. 911[/marginalia]

A triple [del]relation[/del] [ins]character[/ins] has two degrees
of degeneracy; namely it may consist
in the medium having separate
dual relations to the two objects between
which it mediates, or it may consist
in the three objects concerned being
all alike. In like manner, a sign may
bring the thought into relation to the
thing signified, by virtue of [del]an[/del] [ins]a[/ins] [ins]habit[/ins]
or association of ideas, in which case
it is a general sign, or as I call it
a [u]token[/u]; or second it may signify
its object by forcibly directing the
thought to that object, like a finger post
and this kind of sign I term an [u]index[/u];
or third it may make the thought
like object because it is itself of the
same description, and in this case
[del]I term it an icon.[/del]
