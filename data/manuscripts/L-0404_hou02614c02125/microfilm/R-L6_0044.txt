[date]April 20th 1903[/date]

Mr Charles S. Peirce
Dear Sir
I hope I am not taking an unwarranted
liberty in asking you if you will kindly tell me, in the
enclosed envelope, all of your published writings. The papers
in the Monist I know, and some in the old Journal of Speculative
Philosophy - but I am anxious to have a complete list, as I
am making a special study of Logic. In particular I want
[del]to[/del] [ins]to[/ins] list [del]of[/del] any work you have done on the [ins]beside, what was in the Monist[/ins] logic of Relatives,
definition of finite & infinite, and any remarks on Cantor's trans-
finite numbers.

Very sincerely yours
Wilmon Henry Sheldon
Ass't in Philosophy
Columbia University.
