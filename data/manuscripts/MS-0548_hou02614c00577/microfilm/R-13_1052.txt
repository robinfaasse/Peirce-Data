[u]Axioms of Number.[/u]
$g = \text{greater than}$.

1 Alio-relative. $g \prec \bar{I}$
2. Transitive $gg \prec g$
3. Simple quantity $I \prec g + \breve{g}$
4. No maximum $\infty \prec \breve{g}\infty$
5. [u]1[/u] minimum $\breve{g} \text{①} \prec 0$
6. Discrete $g \prec (1+g)(\breve{g}\bar{\dagger}\bar{g})$
7 Simply infinite If $G \prec \bar{x}\bar{\dagger}x$ then $g \prec \bar{x}\bar{\dagger}x^{\infty}$
