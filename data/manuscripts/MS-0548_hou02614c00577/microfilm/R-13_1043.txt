In some respects [u]low[/u] has a right to
be considered the fundamental operation
as it requires no other except $\times$.
Association formula is
$[a \circ b] \circ c = a \circ [b \circ c]$
Distrib formula
$\{a \times b\} \circ c = \{(a \circ c) \circ n\} \times \{(b \circ c) \circ n\}$

$a \circ n = n \circ a$

$a = n \circ a \circ [del]n[/del]$

But one may equally well take the negative of
relative multiplication as fundamental
$\overline{a \dagger} = \overline{\dagger a} = \breve{a}$
$\overline{\dagger a \dagger} = \overline{\overline{\dagger a} \dagger} = a$
$a \dagger \overline{\overline{b} \dagger c} = a \dagger b \dagger c$
$(a + b) \dagger c = a \dagger c \times b \dagger c$ But this is inferior to the above.
