If $s$ and $p$ are absolute terms
$s = s \infty$  $p = p \infty$

$\infty \prec s \bar{p}$ means Some $S$ is $P$
$\infty \prec \bar{s} \bar{p}$  "  There is something besides $S$ and $P$
$\infty \prec s p$  "  All $S$ is $P$
$\infty \prec \bar{s} p$  "  [del]Everything[/del] [ins]Anything[/ins] but $S$ is $P$.

But as $\infty$ is its own converse
These are equally
$\infty \prec \bar{p} s$
$\infty \prec \bar{p} \bar{s}$
$\infty \prec p s$
$\infty \prec p \bar{s}$
