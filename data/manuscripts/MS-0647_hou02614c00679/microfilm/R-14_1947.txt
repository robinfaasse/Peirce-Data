In the first place, I venture to think that in Mathema-
tics there is no such thing as Probability. A problem in that science
is either solved or unsolved; but if the latter we are simply quite
ignorant whether it be true or not. I am aware that there are certain
propositions in the Integral Calculus that go by the name of "Propositions
in Geometrical Probability"; but I maintain that such propositions are
only needlessly complicated in enunciation by introducing into them
the idea of probability. One might as appropriately enunciate the pro-
position that not more than one angle of a plane Euclidean triangle
can be obtuse in the form, "The probability that any angle of such a
triangle taken at random is obtuse is either zero or [ins]1[/ins]/3."
