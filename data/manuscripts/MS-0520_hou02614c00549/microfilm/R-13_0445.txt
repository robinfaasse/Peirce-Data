The operation of taking the converse is best indicated by a "short
mark" or line curving up at its two ends, written over the relative
which it affects. There are a variety of such operations in the
logic of plural relations; but if the general algebra of relatives is
employed, [del]they[/del] [ins]signs for them[/ins] will not often be required.

Other operations only occasionally demand special signs; and
a common accord concerning such signs is less important. I should
suggest drawing upon the signs for planetary aspects when unusual
operations have to be indicated.
