Had I, therefore, appropriated that mode of signification to any other
meaning, those who came after me would have been fully justified,
notwithstanding my priority, in correcting so flagrant a violation of
the logic of notation. It would have been incumbent upon them, as
good logicians, to do so. But I recognized the situation and followed
the immemorial usage and the law of intelligence in writing the
correlate directly after the relative as an indication of relative mul-
tiplication. [del]Yet[/del] I cannot pretend that any additional sanction was
given by this prior usage to that which logic imperatively required.
