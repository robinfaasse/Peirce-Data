For by the distributive principle
$\Sigma_{i} u_{i} . \Pi_{j} v_{j} \left(= (u_{1} + u_{2} + u_{3} \dots) v^{1} v^{2} v^{3} \dots \right)$
$= \Sigma_{i} \Pi_{j} u_{i} v_{j} \left(= u_{1} v^{1} v^{2} v^{3} \dots + u_{2} v^{1} v^{2} v^{3} \dots + \text{etc}\right)$
$= \Pi_{j} \Sigma_{i} u_{i} v_{j} \left(= (u_{1} + u_{2} + \text{etc}) v_{1} . (u_{1} + u_{2} + \text{etc}) v_{2} . \text{etc}\right)$

Thus the $\Sigma$'s and $\Pi$'s belonging to
the different premises can be ar-
ranged in any order, so long as
the original order in any one
premise remains undisturbed.
