Office of "The Nation," VB3b #2

No. 5 Beekman Street. (P. O. Box 6732.)

New York, [date]Oct. 1 1872[/date]

My dear Charley: I have rec'd
the maps, for wh. I hereby ex-
press my gratitude. In this bust-
ling city I have passed several
days of the past month, & go
to Boston to settle down, prob-
ably, early next week. There
I shall await yr. return to
Cambridge. Please give my
regards to Mrs. Peirce.

Yours truly
T. S. Perry.
