Dearest Contributor:

How beauteous is the paper on wh. you write, how welcome are the words written! As to the suitability of what you read in C. for the N. A. R. the views of others differ fm. [ins]those[/ins] [del]yr. mod-[/del] [del]esty[/del] dictate. They may not be of general interest but to the particular mind they will be of inestimable value. Still, use yr. own discretion but cull some logical flowers, such as seem the sweetest & send them to me.
