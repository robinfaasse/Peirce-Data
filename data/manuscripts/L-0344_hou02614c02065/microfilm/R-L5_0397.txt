LATE TICKNOR & FIELDS, AND FIELDS, OSGOOD & Co.

Boston, [date]Nov. 25 1872[/date].

My dear Charley: I write to
beg of you to let me have
that paper you read the
other night at Cambridge
for the N. A. R. It ought to
be published, I'll pay you
bountifully & I must
have it. Be a good boy
& send it. My duty to
Mrs. Peirce.

Yrs. ever
T. S. Perry.
