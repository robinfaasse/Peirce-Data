312. Marlborough St.
Boston: [date]Jan. 2, 1894[/date].

Dear Charley: I have just received
your most interesting circular
& I beg that you will put my
name on the list of subscribers
& count me as one ready
to see the whole work. That my
feeble brains can cope with it
all I do not maintain, but there
will be little eddies alongside
of the Niagara like current of
argument in wh. I shall wade.
The [del]p[/del] beginning of Vol. II. seems
to be prepared for just such as
as I shall be (barring youth).
I observe that the world has
taught you wisdom as well as
logic - they are very different
things (as possibly you will
say, vol. XIII., p. 987, no. 995) -
& you at last acknowledge
that the giddy & frivolous
"may skip" that part wh. I
take to be those thrilling papers
on the Logic of Relatives wh.
I remember in Cambridge long
since.

I wish you all success with
this massive undertaking. Do
you know Alfred W. Benn who
wrote a book on Greek Philoso-
-phers some 10 or 12 yrs. ago & fre-
-quently contributes to the Acad-
-emy? Send him a circular
% Academy, as well as an to
that paper, tho' I dare say you
have done so already.

We are thinking of going
abroad this spring, but I write
from my address with you.

Give my regards please to
Mrs. Peirce & believe me

Ever sincerely & admiringly
yours
T. S. Perry.
