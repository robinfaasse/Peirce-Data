CABLE ADDRESS, "DARTMOUTH BOSTON."

THE PUBLIC LIBRARY OF THE
CITY OF BOSTON.

ALL COMMUNICATIONS SHOULD
BE ADDRESSED TO THE
LIBRARIAN.

HORACE G. WADLIN, LIBRARIAN.

BOSTON, MASS., U. S. A., [date]June 26, 1903[/date].

Dear Charles:
Do you know who published the reprint of Duns Scotus ca. 1894? I see in Ueberweg that one appeared, but I can get no trace of it in any catalogue & yet wd. fain add it to the Liby. Here we have D. S. only as he appears in Migne. Ueberweg says the only satisfactory ed. is that of Lyons, 1639, of wh. the '91 or '94 ed. is a reprint. I presume that Ueb. is right: have you agreement or discord w. him in this matter?
All is well here. I haven't seen Jim since the excesses of his Jubilee. He must be a wreck & he will have to drink up all the waters of Carlsbad before he is able to digest a boiled egg.
Remember me most kindly to Mrs. Peirce.

Yours ever
T. S. Perry.

P.S. Do you know anything of price of reprint?
