[del]“[/del]The Principles of Illative Transformation.
Section i. Basic Principles.

The first chapter of this tract was a grammar of
this language of graphs. But one has not mastered
a language as long as one has to think about it in
another language. One must learn to think in it about
facts. The present chapter is designed to show how
to reason in this language without translating it
into another, the language of our ordinary thought.
This reasoning, however, depends on certain first
principles for the justification of which we have to
make a last appeal to instinctive thought.
