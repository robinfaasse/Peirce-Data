[u]Subsection 3. Of Interpretational Corollary No. 3.[/u]

Any two hooks not joined by a connexus, or lines of
identity, may be regarded as joined by the absence
of such connexus; so that one may say that any pair
of hooks have a nexus between them, which is either
a connexus or a dinexus; and has a connexus
signifies the relation of identity, so the dinexus may
be understood to signify the relation of coexistence
(taken in a sense in which it does not exclude identity).
