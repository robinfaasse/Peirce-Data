[u]Rule 4[/u], called The Rule of the Nullity of the Double Sep. Two
seps, one enclosing [del]nothing[/del] [ins]with its contents[/ins] the other [ins]and nothing else[/ins] may
be erased.

[u]Note A.[/u] The ligatures of the contents of the inner sep
will not be broken by the withdrawal of the two seps.
