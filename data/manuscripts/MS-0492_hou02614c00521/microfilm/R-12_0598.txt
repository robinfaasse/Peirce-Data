By means of this principle the rules of illative transformation hitherto given will easily be extended so as to apply to graph with ligatures attached to them, and the one rule which it is necessary to add [del]to[/del] the list will also be readily deduced. In the following statement, each rule will first be enunciated in an exact and compendious form and then, if necessary, two remarks will be added, under the headings of 'Note A' and 'Note B'. Note A will state more explicitly how the rule applies to a line of identity; while Note B will call attention to a transformation which might, with out close particular care, be supposed to be permitted by the rule but which is really not permitted.
