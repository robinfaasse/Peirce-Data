A graph represents the universe to be in some
conceivable state. Hence, if an absurdity be written, as
in Fig. 3, it is not a graph, but may be called a
pseudograph. It would be a violation of Convention No. 1.

[u]Subsection 2. Of Convention No 2.[/u]

Particular facts, [del]which if complex, are composed[/del]
[del]only of simultaneous facts, are not the only facts[/del]
[ins]to be expressed.[/ins] There are presumably laws, as well, that
are true of the universe. A law is expressible by a
conditional proposition, "If A, then C." Such a proposition
means that under whatever circumstances A may be true,
