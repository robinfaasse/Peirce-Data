[u]Subsection 3. Of Conventions 4 [del]and 5[/del] [ins]to 2[/ins].[/u]

Let a heavy dot or dash be used in place
of a noun which has been erased from a pro-
position. A blank form of proposition produced
by such erasures as can be filled each with a
proper name to make a proposition again is
called a [u]rhema[/u], or, relatively to the proposition
of which it is conceived to be a part, the [u]predicate[/u] of
that proposition. The following are examples of
schemata:
— is good
every man is the son of —
— loves —
God gives — to —
