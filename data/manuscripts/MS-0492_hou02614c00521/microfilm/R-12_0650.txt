[u]Transformations to be taken as basic[/u] ought to consist of operations as simple as possible. Since, therefore, any transformation other than an insertion or an erasure may be conceived to result from an insertion followed by an erasure, operations of these two sorts shall alone be explicitly permitted by rules admitted to this section, [del]as basic[/del] [ins]entitled to rank as basic.[/ins] [del]of a basic character[/del] and be ranked as basic.

This restriction will not prevent the permission of any illative transformation. For if [ins]any graph, [u]x[/u],[/ins] [del]it[/del] can be illatively transformed into any other, [u]y[/u], it can be transformed into the graph [u]x y[/u] which merely asserts the truth of both, and this having been done, it will be permitted to erase the [u]x[/u]; so that the transformation into [u]y[/u] will be effected in two steps.
