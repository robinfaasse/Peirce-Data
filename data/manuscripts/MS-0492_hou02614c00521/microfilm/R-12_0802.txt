Rhemata of first intention are those [del]such[/del] rhemata
which express differences of real fact. Rhemata of
second intention are those which express differences
between symbols. The principal [ins]simple[/ins] rhemata of second
intention are as follows:

[u]Medads[/u]
What is well-known to be true.
What is well-known to be false.

[u]Monads[/u]

[u]Dyads[/u]
— is identical with —
— is other than —
