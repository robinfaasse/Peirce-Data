[marginalia]Section iii. Recapitulation.[/marginalia]

The principles of interpretation may now be restated
more concisely and more comprehensibly. In this
resumé, it will be assumed that selectives, which
should be regarded as a mere abbreviatory device, and
which constitute a serious exception to the general idea
of the system, are not used. A person learning to use
the system and not yet thoroughly expert in it might,
however, be led to doubt whether every proposition
is capable of being expressed without selectives. For
