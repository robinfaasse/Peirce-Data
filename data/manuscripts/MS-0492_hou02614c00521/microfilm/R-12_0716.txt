[ins]*[/ins] Every proposition is [del]a[/del] [ins]of[/ins] description of the universe. The logical universe is that object with which the utterer and the interpreter of any proposition must be well-acquainted, and mutually understand each other to be well-acquainted, and must understand that all their discourse refers to it. It is not always the same thing: it may be the Shakespeare's world of the Midsummer Night's Dream; it may be a universe created by the utterer for the nonce. But all these universes have their places in one great universe of the Truth.
