First of all, let us inquire what are the modes of
illative transformation [del]by[/del] [ins]under[/ins] which any graph whatever
standing alone on the sheet of assertion may be trans-

tion might result. Let us confine ourselves, in the
first instance, to transformations not only involving
no connexions, but also involving no [del]entia[/del] rationis
nor seps. Let us suppose a graph, say that of Fig. 59,
to be [del]done[/del] alone upon the sheet of assertion.

In what ways can it be illatively transformed
without using connexions nor seps nor [del]entia[/del] [ins]other[/ins] rationis?
In the first place, it may be erased; for the result of
erasure, asserting nothing at all, can assert nothing
false. In the second place, it can be iterated, as
in Fig. 60; for the result of the iteration
asserts nothing not asserted already.
