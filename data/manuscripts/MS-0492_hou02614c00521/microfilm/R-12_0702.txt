[u]Basic Categorical Rules for the Illative Transformation of All Graphs.[/u]

[u]Rule 1[/u], called The Rule of Erasure and of Insertion. Any graph, evenly enclosed, can be erased; oddly enclosed, can be inserted.

Note A. This [ins]rule[/ins] permits any [del]line of identity[/del] [ins]ligature[/ins] where evenly enclosed, to be severed, and any two ligatures oddly enclosed in the same close to be joined.

It permits any evenly enclosed ligature to be severed [ins]on the inside[/ins] [del]from a sep that encloses the interior[/del] [ins]and to be extended to[/ins] [del]at any point on[/del] [ins]any sep in the same enclosure, [del]or likewise[/del] and permits[/ins] any oddly enclosed ligature to be joined to the inside of a sep [del]which is[/del] [ins]immediately[/ins] within [del]and[/del] [ins]and to be severed[/ins] from the outside of a sep in the same enclosure [del]elsewhere[/del] [ins]where[/ins] [del]it does not[/del] [ins]if the ligature[/ins] has an extremity.
