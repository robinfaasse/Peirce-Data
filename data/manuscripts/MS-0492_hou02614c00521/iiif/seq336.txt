Rhemata are distinguished, according to the numbers of

complete propositions), monads, of one blank each, dyads,

Of course, a proposition may be variously dissected, so as
to be regarded as containing one rhema or another. [u]A[/u]
[u]subject of a proposition is any part of it which may be regarded[/u]
