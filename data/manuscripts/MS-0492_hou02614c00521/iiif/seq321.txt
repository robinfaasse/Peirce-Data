In most languages common nouns, except as parts of verbs, do not exist, or hardly exist. So I find that in analyzing the proposition for the purposes of logic, there is not only nothing to be gained by the separation of "is a man" into verb and common noun, but on the contrary it is better and every way simpler not to do so. Of course, when the [u]term[/u], or ultimate logical part of the proposition, is understood as predicative, an identifying pronoun is all that is needed to complete the assertion.
