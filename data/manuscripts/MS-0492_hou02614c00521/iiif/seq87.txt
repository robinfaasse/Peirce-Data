The respect in which selectives violate the general
idea of the system is this. The outermost occurrence
of each selective has a different significative force
from every other occurrence, — a grave fault, if it
be avoidable, in any system of regular and exact
representation. The consequence is that the meaning
of a [del]graph[/del] partial graph containing a selective
depends upon whether or not there be another part,
which may be written on a distant part of the
sheet in which the same selective occurs further
out. But the idea of this system is that assertions
written upon different parts of the sheet should
be independent of one another, if, and only if,
they have no common part. When lines of identity
