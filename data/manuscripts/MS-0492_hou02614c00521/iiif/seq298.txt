[u]Subsection 3. Of Interpretational Corollary No. 3.[/u]
Any two hooks not joined by a [u]connexus[/u], or line of identity, may be regarded as joined by the absence of such [u]connexus[/u]; so that we may say that any pair of hooks have a [u]nexus[/u] between them, which is either a [u]connexus[/u] or a [u]dinexus[/u]; and has a connexus signifies the relation of identity, so the dinexus may be understood to signify the relation of coexistence (taken in a sense in which it does not exclude identity.)
