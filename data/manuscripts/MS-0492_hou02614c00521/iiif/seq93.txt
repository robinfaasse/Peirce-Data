But the operation is not worthy to be called reasoning
unless it be deliberate, critical, selfcontrolled. In such
genuine reasoning we are always conscious of proceeding
according to a general rule which we approve. It may
not be precisely formulated but still we do think that
all reasoning of that perhaps rather vaguely characte-
rized kind will be safe. This is a doctrine of logic. We
never can really reason without entertaining a
logical theory. That is called our [u]logica utens[/u].
