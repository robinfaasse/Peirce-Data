Every proposition has one predicate and one only. But
what that predicate is considered to be depends upon
how we choose to analyze it. Thus, the proposition
God gives some good to every man
may be considered as having for its predicate either
of the following schemata:
— gives — to —
— gives some good to —
— gives — to every man
God gives — to —
God gives some good to —
God gives — to every man
— gives some good to every man
God gives some good to every [del]mem[/del][ins]man[/ins].

predicate. [ured][ins]¶[/ins][/ured] A schema which has one blank is called
