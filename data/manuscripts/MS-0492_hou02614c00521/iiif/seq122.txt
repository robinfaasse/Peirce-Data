[ins]B[/ins] We now pass to the consideration of graphs connected by lines of identity. A small addition to our nomenclature is required here. Namely, we have seen that a line of identity is a partial graph; and as a graph it cannot cross a sep. Let us, then, call a series of lines of identity abutting upon one another at seps, a [u]ligature[/u]; and we may extend the meaning of the word so that even a single line of identity shall be called a ligature. A ligature composed of more than one line of identity may be distinguished as a [u]compound ligature[/u].
