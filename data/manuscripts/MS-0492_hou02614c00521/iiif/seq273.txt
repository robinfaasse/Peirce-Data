Rhemata of 0, 1, 2, 3, etc blanks are called respectively medads, monads, dyads, triads, etc.; so that a [u]medad[/u] is a complete proposition. By a polyad is meant a rhema of more than two blanks. In drawing graphs, rhemata may be written out in ordinary language or may be represented by lower case letters. [ins]Spot[/ins] But in either case, a special place called a [u]hook[/u], on the periphery of the sign of the rhema, must be recognized as proper to each blank. [del]And[/del] The expression of the rhema with its hooks is called a [u]spot[/u]. If the heavy line is employed (called a [u]line of identity[/u] or [del]line of connexion[/del] [ins]a connexus[/ins]), this is to have an extremity abutting upon a hook in order to [del]repress[/del] [ins]assert[/ins] [del]rhema is true of[/del] that the individual it denotes.
