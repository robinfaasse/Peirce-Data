CHAPTER II.
The Principles of Illative Transformation.
Section i. Basic Principles.

The object of this section is to lay down [del]all the[/del] [ins]principles[/ins] of illative transformation sufficient to justify all such transformations that can be justified. By an illative transformation is meant a transformation permitted by a general rule of permission which will never permit the transformation of a true assertion into a false one.
