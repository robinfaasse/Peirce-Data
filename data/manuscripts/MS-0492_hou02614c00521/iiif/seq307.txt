The other division of representamens is into
sisigns ([u]semel signantia[/u]), bisigns, and tersigns.
A sisign [del]is a sign[/del] [ins]represents its object[/ins] only once.
A bisign represents its object twice, once more
directly, and again as that which is thus represen-
ted; or to state it otherwise, it excites an icon and
refers to an index of the object of that icon.
A tersign is thrice a representamen; [del]first[/del]
twice in a bisign and then adding an intended
interpretant of that bisign.
Sisigns include all icons together with some
indices and symbols. Symbols include all tersigns
together with some bisigns and signs. There are,
besides, bisign indices.
