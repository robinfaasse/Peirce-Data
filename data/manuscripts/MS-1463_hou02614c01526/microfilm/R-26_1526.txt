To say that Prof. Strong solves the problem would be a very different proposition to which we could by no means assent. As far as we can look into the future, we can descry no prospect of the problem's being solved; and, for our own part, we are altogether disinclined to believe that the solution which we may reasonably hope will some day be reached will be of anything like the metaphysical complexion of that which Prof. Strong offers us.
