A cool and painstaking attempt is here made to set forth the [del]present[/del] [ins]contemporary[/ins] issues of the question of the nature of the connection between mind and matter, to subject the different contentions to brief but penetrating criticism, and to develope by original studies the panpsychism of Clifford and Paulsen.
