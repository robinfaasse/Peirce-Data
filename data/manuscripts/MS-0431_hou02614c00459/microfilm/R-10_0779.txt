[u]III[/u]. A quantity written on a sheet of assertion may, [del]without[/del] [ins]without[/ins]
loss of truth, be transformed by the insertion of two oboli over
it.

[u]Demonstration[/u]. Let us assume that [u]x[/u] is that which is
asserted. Then, by the definition of the vinculum and obelus,
[u]x[/u] is false. And since the assertion that this is false is
true, the assertion that [u]x[/u] is true is true.
