$$\frac{i'' - j''}{l'' - m''} = \frac{i - j}{l - m} ; \quad \frac{j'' - k''}{m'' - n''} = \frac{j - k}{m - n} ; \quad \frac{k'' - i''}{n'' - l''} = \frac{k - i}{n - l} ;$$

whence it follows that all formulae concerning the unaccented
differences have reciprocal formulae exactly like them concern-
ing accented differences. It is true that the reverse is not always
true. For example,

$$i'' + j'' + k'' = 0 \quad l'' + m'' + n'' = 0 ;$$

while without the accents these equations would not hold good.
But this very fact will lead us [ins]in due time[/ins] to an analogy, of not only very
pretty, but of the highest importance for logic.
