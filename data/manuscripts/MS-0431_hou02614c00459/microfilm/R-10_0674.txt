[u]VIII[/u]. If a quantity covered by an obelus, could, [ins]were it written on a sheet of assertions,[/ins] [del]without loss of truth,[/del] be transformed [del]of[/del] [ins]into[/ins] another covered by an obelus, then, were the latter written without the obelus, it could, without loss of truth, be transformed into the former without the obelus.

[u]Demonstration[/u]. Let [u]x[/u] represent the former, [u]y[/u] the latter quantity. Then, we assume that [u]x[/u] could be transformed into [u]y[/u], without loss of truth; and I am to show that, under that assumption, [u]y[/u] could be transformed into [u]x[/u], without loss of truth. By [u]VI[/u], [u]y[/u] could be transformed into [u]x̄[/u], and by [u]IV[/u], [u]x̄[/u] could be transformed into [u]x[/u]. Hence, [u]y[/u] could be transformed, in two steps, into [u]x[/u]. Q.E.D.
