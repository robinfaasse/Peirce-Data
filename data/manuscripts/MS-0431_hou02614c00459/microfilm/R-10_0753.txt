As for involution, until somebody can give some
good reason for attaching a given definite interpretation
to [del](120°)[/del] [ins](120°)[/ins], I may be excused from attempting to introduce it
into this algebra. I apprehend that multiple algebra being
essentially linear, there is no demand for any involution
of its units.

It will be seen that, if we are to accept the premises
upon which the addition-table and multiplication-table
are based, we cannot avoid giving peculiar properties
to each of the three values [unclear]δ[/unclear], [unclear]ν[/unclear], [unclear]ο[/unclear], and that the connection
of them with some such sensuous images as day, night,
and dawn, is by no means utterly an idle fancy. Let us
put these tables into form. I add the subtraction-table.
