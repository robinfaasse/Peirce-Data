A [u]function[/u] is said to be a quantity whose value depends upon
the value of another or others, called its [u]arguments[/u]. It is usual
to say that the arguments vary, each of them through the whole
range of possible values; and for each set of possible values
of the arguments the function has one or more values, al-
ways [ins](for a regular function)[/ins] the same number, except when two or more coincide.
The function and arguments are, therefore, called [u]variables[/u];

upon them. Moreover, for a regular function, for [del]any[/del] [ins]any one[/ins]
