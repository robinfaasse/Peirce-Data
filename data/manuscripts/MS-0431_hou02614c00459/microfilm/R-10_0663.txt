It is usual to suppress parentheses, etc, first, when they merely unify combinations of single letters by the dot, and secondly, when the operative sign outside the parenthesis is the same as that inside, and is associative. The latter omission is almost imperative; [del]and I shall always assume there is no such parenthesis, etc.[/del] [ins]the other is objectionable in writing;[/ins] but in speaking about what is written, I shall always assume the vinculum to be there. I shall always call [del]a[/del] parentheses, square brackets, braces, or any equivalent sign, a [u]vinculum[/u]. By an [u]enclosure[/u], I shall mean either a vinculum or obelus.
