[u]Definition of Composition.[/u]
If $x \cdot y = v$, then $x = v$;
If $x \cdot y = v$, then $y = v$;
If $x = v$ and $y = v$, then $x \cdot y = v$;
[u]Definition of Aggregation.[/u]
If $x + y = f$, then $x = f$;
If $x + y = f$, then $y = f$;
If $x = f$ and $y = f$, then $x + y = f$;
whatever quantities $x$ and $y$ may be.

[u]Definition of Negation.[/u]
$x$ and $\bar{x}$ are not both $v$
$x$ and $\bar{x}$ are not both $f$.
