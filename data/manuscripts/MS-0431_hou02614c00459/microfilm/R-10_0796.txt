<u>Topic B. Trichotomic Mathematics.</u>

Although the importance of the mathematics of three objects
or values, is vast, it has hardly been studied. Indeed, I know
nothing relating to this subject in print, except some small
contributions of my own.

In my memoir of [date]1870 Jan 26[/date] ([del]Mem. Am. Acad of Arts and[/del]
[u]Sci.[/u] [u]IX[/u], p.362, or p.46 of the excerpt) I gave an algebra which
Prof. W. K. Clifford wished to call [del]nonions[/del] [ins]novemions[/ins]; but I prefer the
designation novemions. Let there be three [ins]values of[/ins] ingredients of

an ordered pair of objects may be attached each to one of the
three, [u]u[/u], [u]v[/u], [u]w[/u]. Then, since an assertion [ins]concerning a pair[/ins] may either exclude or
