With these two signs, the vinculum (with its equivalents, parentheses, brackets, braces, etc) and the [u]⋏[/u], which I will call the [u]ampheck[/u], (from [greek]ἀμφήκης[/greek], cutting both ways) all [del]proposition[/del] assertions as to the values of quantities can be expressed. Thus;

[u]x[/u] is [u]x ⋏ x ⋏ x ⋏ x[/u]

[u]x[/u] is [u]x ⋏ x[/u]

[del]x[/del] [ins]x[/ins] is [u](x ⋏ x) ⋏ (x ⋏ x)[/u]

[del]v[/del] is [u]x ⋏ x[/u]

[u]x ⋏ y[/u] is [del](x ⋏ y)[/del] [u][x ⋏ y ⋏ (x ⋏ y ⋏ x ⋏ y)] ⋏ [(x ⋏ y ⋏ x ⋏ y) ⋏ x ⋏ y][/u]

[u]x ⊗ y[/u] is [u]x ⋏ y ⋏ (x ⋏ y ⋏ x ⋏ y)[/u]

[u]x = y[/u] is [u](x ⋏ y) ⋏ (x ⋏ y ⋏ y)[/u]

[u]x ∥ y[/u] is [u]x ⋏ y ⋏ (x ⋏ x ⋏ y ⋏ y)[/u]

[u]x ⋏ y[/u] is [u]x ⋏ y ⋏ x ⋏ y[/u]

[u]x ⋏ y[/u] is [u](x ⋏ x ⋏ y ⋏ y) ⋏ (x ⋏ y ⋏ y ⋏ y)[/u] or [u](x ⋏ y ⋏ y) ⋏ (x ⋏ x ⋏ y)[/u]

[u]x ⋏ y[/u] is [u](x ⋏ y ⋏ x) ⋏ (y ⋏ x ⋏ y)[/u]

[u]x ∨ y[/u] is [u]x ⋏ x ⋏ y ⋏ y[/u]

[u]x < y[/u] is [u]x ⋏ y ⋏ y[/u] or [u]x ⋏ y ⋏ y[/u]
