Every invertible mode of combination of two quantities has two
corresponding determinative inverse operations. But these two
are equal if the direct operation is commutative. The operation
inverse of addition is subtraction. Thus, if (A+B) = C then
[del]if C = A+B[/del] A = C-B and B = -A+C, But since if A+B = C,
we have also B+A = C, it follows that B = C-A and -A+C = C-A.

and regressive division. Progressive division may be represented
by the solidus of Stokes (Mathematical and Physical Papers: 1880,
Vol I p. vii) thus: if AB = C, then A = C/B, That is, (C/B)B = C.
