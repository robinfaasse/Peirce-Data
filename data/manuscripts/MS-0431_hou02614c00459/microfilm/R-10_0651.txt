... can put $\alpha$, and in $\eta$ and $\kappa$, can put $\mathfrak{D}$, for $\heartsuit$; putting
$\Upsilon$... all four for $\diamondsuit$. If the parts of the margin intersecting
in $>$ are used, then in one system, in $\gamma, \delta, \varepsilon, \text{F}$ we may put
$\mathfrak{D}$ for $\heartsuit$, and in $\zeta, \eta, \theta, \kappa$, may put $\alpha$ for $\heartsuit$; while in
$\gamma, \varepsilon, \zeta, \eta$, we put $>$, and in $\delta, \text{F}, \theta, \kappa$, we put $<$, for $\diamondsuit$.
Then in $\alpha, \beta, \iota, \lambda$, we shall put $\lambda$ for $\heartsuit$ and $\iota$ for $\diamondsuit$.
In another system of interpretation, we may put in $\gamma, \delta, \varepsilon, \text{F}$,
$\zeta, \eta, \theta, \kappa$, put $\Upsilon$ for $\heartsuit$, while in $\gamma, \varepsilon, \zeta, \eta$, we put $\alpha$
and in $\delta, \text{F}, \theta, \kappa$, we put $\mathfrak{D}$, for $\diamondsuit$. In $\alpha$ and $\beta$, we
shall put $\alpha$, in $\iota$ and $\lambda$, we shall put $\mathfrak{D}$, for $\heartsuit$, [del]and in[/del]
all four shall put $\Upsilon$ for $\diamondsuit$. If the parts of the margin inter-
secting in $\lor$ are used, then, in the first system, in $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta$, we
put $\alpha$ for [del]$\heartsuit$[/del] [ins]$\diamondsuit$[/ins], while in $\eta, \iota, \kappa, \lambda$, we put $\mathfrak{D}$, for $\diamondsuit$, and
in $\alpha, \gamma, \eta, \iota$, we put $\alpha$, in $\beta, \delta, \kappa, \lambda$, we put $\mathfrak{D}$, for $\heartsuit$.
In the other system in $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \eta, \iota, \kappa, \lambda$, we put $\lambda$ for $\heartsuit$,
in $\alpha, \gamma, \eta, \iota$, we put $>$, in $\beta, \delta, \kappa, \lambda$, we put $<$, for $\diamondsuit$. In
$\varepsilon, \text{F}, \zeta, \theta$, we may put $\Upsilon$ for $\heartsuit$ and $\lambda$ for $\diamondsuit$ or else
in $\varepsilon, \text{F}$, we may put $\mathfrak{D}$, in $\zeta, \theta$ we may put $\alpha$, for $\heartsuit$, in all four $\Upsilon$ for $\diamondsuit$.
