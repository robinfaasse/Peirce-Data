We see that the multiplication table [del]has[/del] [ins]gives[/ins] each value a
peculiar property. Multiplication by 0 is overpowering, reducing
everything to 0. Multiplication by 1, on the other hand, has no
effect. While [u]v[/u] alone has the peculiarity of not being its own
square; so that one multiplication by or into [u]v[/u] is undone by
a second multiplication by or into [u]v[/u].
