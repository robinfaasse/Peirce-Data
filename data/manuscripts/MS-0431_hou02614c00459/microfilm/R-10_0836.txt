As for involution, I do not even know what $(120^\circ)^{(120^\circ)}$ is; and I should be at a loss upon what premises to establish it for this trichotomic arithmetic. To be sure, we have "indices" in the theory of numbers. Thus, for modulus 11, $2^1 \equiv 2$, $2^2 \equiv 4$, $2^3 \equiv 8$, $2^4 \equiv 5$, $2^5 \equiv 10$, $2^6 \equiv 9$, $2^7 \equiv 7$, $2^8 \equiv 3$, $2^9 \equiv 6$, $2^{10} \equiv 1$. But then the period of the exponents is one less than that of the power. Let us pass this by.
