[u]VII[/u]. If a quantity, [ins]$\bar{x}$,[/ins] were it written with an obelus over it,
on a sheet of assertions, could be transformed, obelus and all,
into an other quantity, [ins]without loss of truth,[/ins] then the latter were it written with
an obelus over it, could be transformed into the former without
its obelus.

Demonstration. Let $x$ be the former, $y$ the latter quantity.
Then we assume that [ins]in all cases,[/ins] $\bar{x}$ could be transformed into $\bar{y}$, without
loss of truth, and I am to prove that $\bar{y}$ could, in all cases, be
transformed into $x$ without loss of truth. Since $\bar{x}$ could be
transformed into $\bar{y}$, it follows from V that $y$ could be transformed
into $x$. But by IV, $\bar{y}$ could then be transformed into $\bar{x}$; so that
$y$ would be transformable into $x$. Q.E.D.
