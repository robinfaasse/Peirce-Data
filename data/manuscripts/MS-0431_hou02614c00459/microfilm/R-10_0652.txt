In order to form a proposition necessarily true of the form $(x \diamondsuit y) \bigcirc (x \heartsuit y)$, it is only necessary to rewrite it replacing $\diamondsuit$ and $\heartsuit$ by the signs they stand for in their most iconic forms, but with [del]$\times$[/del] [ins]$\bigcirc$[/ins] in place of $\bigcirc$. This middle sign is now to be [del]mod[/del] modified as follows:

If there is a quadrant open in both right and left hand signs, the top quadrant of the middle one must be open;

If there is a quadrant open in the left hand sign [ins]alone[/ins], the left hand quadrant of the middle sign must be open;

If there is a quadrant open in the right hand sign alone, the right hand quadrant of the middle sign must [del]be open[/del] [ins]remain[/ins];

If there is a quadrant open in [ins]neither[/ins] the right nor [ins]the[/ins] left hand sign, the bottom quadrant of the middle sign must be open.

[del]Any[/del] Quadrants which [del]are[/del] [ins]is[/ins] not compelled to be [del]closed[/del] [ins]open[/ins] by this rule, may be closed.
