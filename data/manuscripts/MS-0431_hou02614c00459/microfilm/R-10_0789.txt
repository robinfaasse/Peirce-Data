If $a + b = f$
Then $a + b \prec x$

and, by the 1880 definition,
$a \prec x$ and $b \prec x$;

whence
$a = f$ and $b = f$.

But if $a = f$ and $b = f$,
$a \prec x$ and $b \prec x$,

and by the 1880 definition,
$a + b \prec x$

whence $a + b = f$.
