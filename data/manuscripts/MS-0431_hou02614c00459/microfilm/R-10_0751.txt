But for the purposes of [del]dual[/del] trichotomic mathematics, it should be recognized that each quantity has one of three values. Call them, for the moment, $0^{\circ}$, $120^{\circ}$, $240^{\circ}$; [del]$360^{\circ}$[/del] regarding $360^{\circ}$ as the same as $0^{\circ}$. Or one might call them night, morning, and afternoon. Let us denote the three values by $o$ (for [foreign]ὄρθρος[/foreign]), $\delta$ (for [foreign]δείλη[/foreign]), $\nu$ (for [foreign]νύξ[/foreign]). Then, we must [ins]upon[/ins] have the addition table:

$$\begin{array}{ccc}
o + o = o & o + \delta = \delta & o + \nu = \nu \\
\delta + o = \delta & \delta + \delta = \nu & \delta + \nu = o \\
\nu + o = \nu & \nu + \delta = o & \nu + \nu = \delta
\end{array}$$
