From (11) and (12), it follows that
(13) If from S would follow P, then necessarily from P would follow [u]S[/u].
For if from S would follow P, then if S is true, P is true, whence

true, then by (12), necessarily from P would follow [u]S[/u]. There-
fore, in either case, (13) holds.
