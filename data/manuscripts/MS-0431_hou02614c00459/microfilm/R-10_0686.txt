XIV. As long as the only signs used are those described, any
quantity, into which another could be transformed without loss
of truth if the latter were asserted alone, can, if it occurs any-
where under a single obelus in an asserted quantity, be trans-
formed into that latter.

[u]Demonstration[/u]. Let [u]x[/u] and [u]y[/u] represent the two quantities
[del]x[/del] [ins]so[/ins] that in the assertion that [u]x[/u] has the value [ins]v,[/ins] [u]y[/u] could be
replaced by [u]y[/u], without loss of truth. Then, if I say [del]that if,[/del]
in an asserted quantity, [u]y[/u] occurs anywhere under a single
obelus, it can be transformed into [u]x[/u] without loss of truth.
For let [u]Y[/u] be the entire [del]quantity[/del] [ins]expression[/ins] which is under the same
obelus as [u]y[/u]; and let [u]X[/u] be what [u]Y[/u] would become if [u]y[/u]
were replaced by [u]x[/u]. Then, by XIII, if [u]X[/u] were asserted, it could be
[del]tr[/del] transformed into [u]Y[/u]. And consequently, by V, [u]Y[/u], if it were as-
serted alone could be transformed into [u]X[/u]. Hence, by XIII, situ-
ated as [u]Y[/u] is, not under any obelus, it can be transformed into
[u]X[/u]. But this transformation consists only in replacing [u]y[/u] by [u]x[/u]
under a single obelus.
