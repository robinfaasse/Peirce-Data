[u]In means in the second place that only those sets of values that
have [del]x[/del] [ins]z[/ins] affirmatively are retained;[/u] so that, on the whole, it
admits the following

$x\cdot y\cdot z$
$\bar{x}\cdot y\cdot z$
$x\cdot\bar{y}\cdot z$
$\bar{x}\cdot\bar{y}\cdot z$

Let us now see what $x\cdot\bar{y\cdot z}$ means. If means that $x\cdot\bar{y\cdot z}$ is
to be cut out. Now $\bar{y\cdot z}$ cuts out $y\cdot z$. Let us indicate that
by drawing enclosing $y\cdot z$ in ovals, thus:

($x\cdot y\cdot z$)
$\bar{x}\cdot y\cdot z$
($x\cdot y\cdot\bar{z}$)
$\bar{x}\cdot y\cdot\bar{z}$
$x\cdot\bar{y}\cdot z$
$\bar{x}\cdot\bar{y}\cdot z$
$x\cdot\bar{y}\cdot\bar{z}$
$\bar{x}\cdot\bar{y}\cdot\bar{z}$

But $x\cdot\bar{y\cdot z}$ further admits only what is $x$ so that we have

($x\cdot y\cdot z$)
$\bar{x}\cdot y\cdot z$
($x\cdot y\cdot\bar{z}$)
$\bar{x}\cdot y\cdot\bar{z}$
$x\cdot\bar{y}\cdot z$
$\bar{x}\cdot\bar{y}\cdot z$
$x\cdot\bar{y}\cdot\bar{z}$
$\bar{x}\cdot\bar{y}\cdot\bar{z}$

Now $\overline{x\cdot\bar{y\cdot z}}$ admits what is here enclosed in ovals, and [del]rejects[/del] [ins]excludes[/ins]
the rest. This is plain, then, that it admits all the other admits
and besides [del]x·y·z[/del] $\bar{x}\cdot\bar{y}\cdot\bar{z}$. That is, it has all the possibilities of being
true, that the other has, and more besides. Thus, when we clearly
see what is meant, the truth of it is manifest.
