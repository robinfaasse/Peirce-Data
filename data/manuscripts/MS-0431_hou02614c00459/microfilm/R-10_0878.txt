The operations $\Phi_m$, $\Phi_i$, $\Phi_l$, $\Phi_o$, are idempotent; that
is [del]repetition[/del] [ins]their repetition[/ins] has no effect. $\Phi_j$ and $\Phi_k$ are [del]o-potent[/del]
$o$-potent, as we may say, that is $\Phi_j\Phi_j = \Phi_k\Phi_k = \Phi_o$. $\Phi_n$ is
$m$-potent. $\Phi_n\Phi_n = \Phi_m$. $\Phi_m$ and $\Phi_o$ combine in this fashion,
where for the sake of clearness, the $\Phi$s are omitted

So $\Phi_m$ and $\Phi_l$, $\Phi_m$ and $\Phi_i$, and $\Phi_o$, $\Phi_l$ and $\Phi_o$ all
combine $m$-wise. $\Phi_j$ and $\Phi_o$ and $\Phi_k$ and $\Phi_o$ combine
so as always to produce $\Phi_o$. $\Phi_m$ and $\Phi_n$ combine thus
