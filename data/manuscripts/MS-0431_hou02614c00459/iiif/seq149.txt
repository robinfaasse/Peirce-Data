superfluity of signs. However, in the life of mathematics,
superfluities can ill be dispensed with. But I will proceed
to show that all our operations can be expressed by means of
the dot of composition and the obelus, as well as by the two
signs ⚬ < (for which ⚬ might, through the identity of x ⚬ y
and y ⚬ x, be substituted) and f (or any other sign for a gene-
ral and indefinite object); and by the way, I shall call atten-
tion to sundry other properties and relations of the forms of
information. It will be proper, as a preliminary, to note
some properties of the dot and the obelus, on the one hand,
and of ⚬, ⚬, f, etc. on the other.
