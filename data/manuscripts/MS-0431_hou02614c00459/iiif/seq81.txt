If two sets of values are admitted, they may be such as to fix
the value of one quantity and give no information about the
other. Since there are two values which the former quantity
may have, and it may be either of the two quantities,
there are 2x2, or 4, possible informations of this kind,
namely
$v_x$ , $f_x$ , $v_y$ , $f_y$ ;
or $x=v$, $x=f$, $y=v$, $y=f$.
There remain, then, 6-4, or 2, informations of a different
kind. Namely the sets of values they admit may be
either $v v$ and $f f$ or $v f$ and $f v$.
