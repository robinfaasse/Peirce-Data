[ured]Daniel Webster said that this country's need of liberty and
need of union were One and Inseparable. He did not mean
that the two things could not be distinguished by the mind, but
that they were one in fact and inseparable in fact. So those
the sentences are signs, and, as assertions, they are signs
of the truth. Now the truth is individual; for when we say
Napoleon Bonaparte was an individual, in contradistinction
to 'every man', which is general, what we mean is that while
