The different kinds of imperfection of certitude, and if we had only once acquainted ourselves with their different natures, I shall in these notes endeavour to mark the three ways of falling short of certainty by the three terms [u]probability[/u], [u]verisimilitude[/u] or likelihood, and [u]plausibility[/u]. Just at present I propose to deal only with [del]Probability[/del], but I will so far characterize [u]Verisimilitude[/u] and [u]Plausibility[/u] as mark them off as being entirely different from [u]Probability[/u]. Beginning with [u]Plausibility[/u], I will first [ins]endeavour to[/ins] give an example of an idea which shall be strikingly marked by its very low degree of this quality.
