The relatively degenerate forms of Thirdness are extremely varied. The first degenerate form has itself its relatively genuine and its relatively degenerate form while the genuine form has its relatively genuine, its relatively first degenerate, and its relatively second degenerate forms. I do not know what limit, if any, there may be to this.
