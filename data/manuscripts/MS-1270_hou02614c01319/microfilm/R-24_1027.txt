Three countries only, so far as we know, approached in high antiquity to the scientific stage of civilization, — Babylonia, Egypt, and northern China. All three were flooded every year by the rivers running through them [ins]*[/ins], while at other seasons they required irrigation. The irrigation of such districts can only be carried on under a strong government; and the first irrigators would naturally become so rich as to enslave the rest of the people.
