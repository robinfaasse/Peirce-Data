[u]multiplication[/u]

The correlates of mathematical operations are called [del]quantities[/del] [ins]values[/ins]; a quantity being identified by its satisfying [del]certain[/del] [ins]given[/ins] conditions. But when it is identified by [del]these[/del] purely mathematical relations to other quantities with which we conceive the system of possible quantities to be built up, it is called a [u]value[/u].

[del]Mod[/del] A collection of values such that the sum of any two of them, [del]or the addend[/del] [ins]as well as the[/ins] additive term which combines with an additive term and a sum from among the members of the collection, itself to always a member of the collection is a [u]tribe[/u], and every tribe satisfies this condition. To state the matter otherwise, if [u]a[/u], [u]b[/u], [u]c[/u], [u]d[/u], [u]e[/u], etc. form such a collection, that whatever members of it [u]m[/u] and [u]n[/u] may be, both [u]m[/u]+[u]n[/u] and [u]m[/u]-[u]n[/u] are members of the collection, then this collection is a [u]tribe[/u], while if this condition be not fulfilled the collection is not a tribe.

Multiplication as applied to the members of certain tribes follows certain additional rules.

A [u]clan[/u] is [del]a tribe[/del] the narrowest tribe which [del]embraces[/del] contains an enumerable collection of quantities, which shall be called the [u]totems[/u] of the clan.
