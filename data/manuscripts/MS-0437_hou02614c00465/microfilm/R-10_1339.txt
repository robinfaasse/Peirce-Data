The consequence is that unless the metaphysician is a most
thorough master of formal logic, — and especially of the inductive
side of the logic of relatives, immeasurably more important

practical politician decides as to the weight that ought to be
allowed to different considerations, that is to say, by the impres-

pendous difference, that the one man's impressions are the
resultant of long experiential training, while [ins]with[/ins] such training

who adopts a [ins]metaphysical[/ins] reasoning because he is impressed that it is
