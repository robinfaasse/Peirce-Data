Since this plane and (B), of multiplicities 3 and 2 are both contained in space of multiplicity 4, they must contain in common a homoloid of multiplicity 1, i.e. a point, [{AX}B]. By parity of reasoning there must equally be a point [{AX}C]. Hence the two points lie in one ray; and since both points lie in the plane {AX}, so also, as seen above, does that ray.
