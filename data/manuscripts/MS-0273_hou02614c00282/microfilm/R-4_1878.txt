A ray and a point not contained in that ray must
both be contained in one plane. For the sum of their
multiplicities is 2+1 = 3, while the multiplicity of
the homoloid they both contain is 0. Hence, 3-0 = 3
is the multiplicity of a homoloid containing them
both. Two rays in one plane must contain a
