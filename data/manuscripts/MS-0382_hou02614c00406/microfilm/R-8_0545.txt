According to this, we allow ourselves to say anything we please of
that which does not exist, and there is clearly no falsity
in making any assertion we please in regard to it, since we are
not making a false statement about anything at all; or,
to state the matter in another way, the logical formula, [u]a[/u]
is [u]b[/u], means that whatever [u]a[/u] there may be in existence
is [u]b[/u]. Now there is clearly no falsity in saying, for
example, that whatever griffin there may be in exist-
ence breathes fire. There can be no falsity in it because
the statement is limited to such griffins as exist, and no
griffins exist, so that there is no statement in it at
all, it is only the form of a statement without the
substance.
