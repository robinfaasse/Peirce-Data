It is evident that under the fixed load the form of the cable is a parabola with its vertex at its lowest point, its equation being
$$\frac{y'}{y'_k} = \frac{x'}{x'_k} (2 - \frac{x'}{x'_k}).$$

Hence,
$$\frac{dy'}{dx'} = 2 \frac{y'_k}{x'_k} (1 - \frac{x'}{x'_k}) = 2 (\frac{y'}{x'} - \frac{y'_k}{x'_k}).$$

and
$$\frac{dy'_0}{dx'_0} = 2 \frac{y'_k}{x'_k}$$
