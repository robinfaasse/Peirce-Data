§ . Suppose a cord to be in equilibrium and that
an additional load is put upon it. Required the effect upon
the maximum tension and the deformation.

Let $W$ be the weight of the additional load and
[del]$\Xi$[/del] [ins]$\Xi$[/ins] the value of $x$ for its centre of gravity.

Then the value of $x$ for the centre of gravity of the whole com-
bination is
$$\frac{\omega_N \xi_N + W \Xi}{\omega_N + W}$$
and no reactions can alter this. Before this load was
put on $\omega_H$ had been
$$\omega_H = \omega_N \frac{x_N - \xi_N}{x_N}$$

After the additional loading, it [del]was[/del] becomes
$$\omega_H + \delta \omega_H = (\omega_N + W) \frac{x_N - \frac{\omega_N \xi_N + W \Xi}{\omega_N + W}}{x_N}$$
$$= \omega_N + W - \omega_N \frac{\xi_N}{x_N} - W \frac{\Xi}{x_N}$$
$$= \omega_N \left(1 - \frac{\xi_N}{x_N}\right) + W \left(1 - \frac{\Xi}{x_N}\right)$$

That is
$$\delta \omega_H = W \left(1 - \frac{\Xi}{x_N}\right)$$
$$\delta \omega'_H = W \frac{\Xi}{x_N} .$$
