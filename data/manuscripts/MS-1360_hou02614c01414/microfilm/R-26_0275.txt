Mr. Monson's Bridge

The Bridge has its middle part when unloaded a parabola, the outer parts common catenaries. Take the origin of coordinates at the middle of the bridge, and number the suspenders (supposed to be every 50 feet) + and - from that point. Then the $n^{\text{th}}$ suspender sustains that part of the whole weight from $50n. \text{ feet} \pm 25 \text{ feet}$, with a correction owing to the unequal [del]lin to[/del] linear distribution of the weight of cable and suspenders. The weight comes uniformly on the cable, but is not distributed uniformly horizontally, so that the outer suspenders shorter lengths and the weights sustained by the quantity suspenders are not vertically under them, in a certain sense. But this does not mean that the horizontal stress of the cable is different but only that the middle part of the truss is compressed.
