That is, there are $n$ equations of the form
(2) $- \mathcal{E}_i (\frac{1}{l_i} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{(\Delta x_i)^2 + (\Delta y_i)^2}}) \Delta x_i + \mathcal{E}_{i-1} (\frac{1}{l_{i-1}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{(\Delta x_{i-1})^2 + (\Delta y_{i-1})^2}}) \Delta x_{i-1} = 0$
and $n$ others of the form
(3) $- \mathcal{E}_i (\frac{1}{l_i} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{(\Delta x_i)^2 + (\Delta y_i)^2}}) \Delta y_i + \mathcal{E}_{i-1} (\frac{1}{l_{i-1}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{(\Delta x_{i-1})^2 + (\Delta y_{i-1})^2}}) \Delta y_{i-1} = P_i$
Equations (2) give
(4) $\mathcal{E}_i (\frac{1}{l_i} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{(\Delta x_i)^2 + (\Delta y_i)^2}}) = \frac{C}{\Delta x_i}$

where $C$ is independent of $i$, but is, for the present, unknown. Substituting (4) in (3)
we get
(5) $C \frac{\Delta y_i}{\Delta x_i} = O - \sum_1^i P_j$,
where $O$ is a constant which the multipliers of $d y_1$ in (1) show equals zero.
Multiplying (4) by $\Delta x_i$ and substituting the value of $\Delta y_i / \Delta x_i$ from (5), we
get
[del]$\equiv$[/del] $C = \mathcal{E}_i (\frac{\Delta x_i}{l_i} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 + (\frac{\sum_1^i P_j}{C})^2}})$
or
(6) $\Delta x_i = l_i (\frac{C}{\mathcal{E}_i} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 + (\frac{\sum_1^i P_j}{C})^2}})$
