$$\sin \tau_{n-1} + \sin \tau_n + \sin \tau_{n+1} = \text{Constant},$$
$$\cos \tau_{n-1} + \cos \tau_n + \cos \tau_{n+1} = \text{Constant};$$
or, differentiating,
$$\cos \tau_{n-1} \cdot \delta \tau_{n-1} + \cos \tau_n \cdot \delta \tau_n + \cos \tau_{n+1} \cdot \delta \tau_{n+1} = 0,$$
$$\sin \tau_{n-1} \cdot \delta \tau_{n-1} + \sin \tau_n \cdot \delta \tau_n + \sin \tau_{n+1} \cdot \delta \tau_{n+1} = 0.$$
These equations give
$$\delta \tau_{n+1} = - \frac{\sin (\tau_n - \tau_{n-1})}{\sin (\tau_{n+1} - \tau_{n-1})} \delta \tau_n,$$
$$\delta \tau_{n-1} = - \frac{\sin (\tau_{n+1} - \tau_n)}{\sin (\tau_{n+1} - \tau_{n-1})} \delta \tau_n.$$
or, when the curvature is not infinite $\delta \tau_{n+1} = \delta \tau_{n-1} = - \frac{1}{2} \delta \tau_n$
Under these conditions
$$(\tau_{n+2} - \tau_{n+1})^2 + (\tau_{n+1} - \tau_n)^2 + (\tau_n - \tau_{n-1})^2 + (\tau_{n-1} - \tau_{n-2})^2$$
is to be made a minimum; so that
$$-2(\tau_{n+2} - \tau_{n+1})\delta \tau_{n+1} + 2(\tau_{n+1} - \tau_n)(\delta \tau_{n+1} - \delta \tau_n) + 2(\tau_n - \tau_{n-1})(\delta \tau_n - \delta \tau_{n-1}) + 2(\tau_{n-1} - \tau_{n-2})\delta \tau_{n-1}$$
$= 0$. That is
$$(-\tau_{n+2} + 2\tau_{n+1} - \tau_n)\delta \tau_{n+1} + (-\tau_{n+1} + 2\tau_n - \tau_{n-1})\delta \tau_n + (-\tau_n + 2\tau_{n-1} - \tau_{n-2})\delta \tau_{n-1} = 0$$
