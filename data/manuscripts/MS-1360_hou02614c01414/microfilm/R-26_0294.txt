Let $\varepsilon$ be the product of the elasticity into the "moment of inertia" of the cross-section about a horizontal central axis in it. Then the work of bending the truss is

$$-\int \eta d p + \frac{1}{2} \varepsilon \int \left(\frac{d^2 \eta}{d p^2}\right)^2 d p' = \frac{1}{2} \varepsilon \frac{x'_k}{A_k} \int \left(\frac{d^2 \eta}{d p'_k}\right)^2 d p'_k - \int \eta d p$$

Let $v$ be the product of the elasticity of the truss into its material cross-section. Then the work of compressing or extending the truss is

$$\frac{1}{2} v \int \frac{(d \xi - d x')^2}{d x'} = \frac{1}{2} v \int \left(\frac{d \xi}{d x'} - 1\right)^2 d x' = \frac{1}{2} \left(\frac{p_k}{x'_k} \frac{d \xi}{d p} - 1\right)^2 \frac{x'_k}{A_k} d p.$$

The work upon the virtual back-stays is calculated from $I$, the natural length of each virtual backstay. Under the fixed load, the pull upon the backstays is $\sqrt{A'^2 + p_k^2}$; and by equation (29)

$$A' = \frac{1}{2} p_k \frac{x'_k}{y'_k}$$

Hence, the pull is

$$p_k \sqrt{1 + \frac{x'^2_k}{4 y'^2_k}}.$$
