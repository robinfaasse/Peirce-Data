[date]Saturday 1895 Apr 27.[/date]

My dear Sir

[del]I have not accomplished much today somehow.[/del]

I have only recalculated the horizontal component of the
stress in the cable in a different way in consequence
of the views you expressed. That is, I previously
took no account of the cantilevers in doing this. I
do not know that I have taken the right account
of them now. I have assumed that of the whole
weight of the truss, if unsupported by the cable [ins]$\frac{11}{73}$[/ins] would
come on the outer supports and $\frac{62}{73}$ on the inner
supports. I did this because it seemed as if a rough
guess would be sufficient.

I have also made my calculation more correct in
several respects than before.

But what astonishes me is that I got a totally different
result, and it seems quite impossible. Most likely there is
some error of calculation which I shall be able to detect before
morning. I enclose the old calculation and the new. But you
[del]had better not take them away.[/del]

very truly C. S. Peirce
