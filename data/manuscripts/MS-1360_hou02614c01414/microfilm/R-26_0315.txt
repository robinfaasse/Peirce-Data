where

$\Delta \underline{u} = \underline{u}_{i+1} - \underline{u}_i$

$\Sigma \underline{u} = \underline{u}_{i-1} + \underline{u}_{i-2} + \text{etc} \dots + \underline{u}_0$

Since $\Delta \underline{v}$ is constant

$\underline{v} = (i + C) \Delta \underline{v}$

Then $\delta \int (\frac{\underline{u}}{\underline{v}})^2 d\underline{v}$ becomes
$\delta \Sigma \frac{\underline{u}^2}{(i+C)\Delta\underline{v}} = \delta \frac{\underline{u}_{i-1}^2}{(i-1+C)\Delta\underline{v}} + \delta \frac{\underline{u}_{i-2}^2}{(i-2+C)\Delta\underline{v}} \dots \delta \frac{\underline{u}_0^2}{C\Delta\underline{v}}$
$= 2 \frac{\underline{u}_{i-1}\delta\underline{u}_{i-1}}{(i-1+C)\Delta\underline{v}} + 2 \frac{\underline{u}_{i-2}\delta\underline{u}_{i-2}}{(i-2+C)\Delta\underline{v}} \dots 2 \frac{\underline{u}_0\delta\underline{u}_0}{C\Delta\underline{v}}$
$= 2 \Sigma \frac{\underline{u} \delta\underline{u}}{(\Delta\underline{v})^2} \Delta\underline{v}$
