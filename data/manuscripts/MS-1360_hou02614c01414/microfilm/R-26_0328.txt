The suspenders are so adjusted that being vertical they bring the detached sections into position for rivetting. [ins]The cable and suspenders are weightless.[/ins] The part of the cable outside of the [del]suspended span[/del] [ins]span of suspension[/ins], which is virtually a part of the backstays, passes over a fixed pulley and is anchored. [del]The cable and suspenders are weightless.[/del] The parts of the truss outside the [del]suspended span[/del] [ins]span of suspension[/ins], rests before the riveting upon two fixed points, and though it is thus strained is nevertheless perfectly straight and level.
