Assume $x$ to be measured horizontally increasing with the numbers of the particles
$y$ to be measured vertically upward.

Then,
(1) $\begin{cases} x' = x - x_H, & x_0 = 0, & x'_0 = -x_H, & x'_H = 0 & \text{; } x_{N+1} = \text{the span ;} \\ y' = y - y_H \text{ ,} & y_0 = 0 \text{ ,} & y'_0 = -y_H & y'_H = 0. \end{cases}$

Use $\Sigma$ or $\Delta$, with or without indications of limits, as follows:
$\Delta u_n = \sum_i^{n-1} u_i = u_{n+1} - u_n$
$\Sigma u_n = \sum_i^{n-1} u_i$ [del]+ Arbitrary constant[/del] $= u_{n-1} + u_{n-2} + \dots + u_1 + u_0 + \text{Const.}$

Use,
$s$ for the length of the cord from the origin to the variable point
increasing with the numbers of the stretches,

$\tau$ for the inclination of the variable stretch to the horizon turning from the
positive direction of increase of $x$ round the short way toward the positive
direction of increase of $y$,

$\rho$ for the radius of curvature

$\omega$ for the weight passed in moving along $s$ in the direction of its increase
from the origin to the variable point; and $\omega$ is to take the sign of $s$.
$\begin{array}{lllll} s' = s - s_H & s_0 = 0 & s'_0 = -s_H & s'_H = 0 & s_{N+1} = \text{total length of cord.} \\ \omega' = \omega - \omega_H & \omega_0 = 0 & \omega'_0 = - \omega_H & \omega'_H = 0 & \omega_N = \omega_{N+1} = \text{Total weight.} \end{array}$
