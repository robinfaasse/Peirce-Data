middle sketches must extend between two points fixed as far as
the variation now studied is concerned is expressed thus:
$$\sin \tau_{n-1} + \sin \tau_n + \sin \tau_{n+1} = \text{Constant},$$
$$\cos \tau_{n-1} + \cos \tau_n + \cos \tau_{n+1} = \text{Constant};$$
or, differentially,
$$\cos \tau_{n-1} \cdot \delta \tau_{n-1} + \cos \tau_n \cdot \delta \tau_n + \cos \tau_{n+1} \cdot \delta \tau_{n+1} = 0$$
$$\sin \tau_{n-1} \cdot \delta \tau_{n-1} + \sin \tau_n \cdot \delta \tau_n + \sin \tau_{n+1} \cdot \delta \tau_{n+1} = 0$$
Solving for $\delta \tau_{n-1}$ and $\delta \tau_{n+1}$ considered as unknown
$$\delta \tau_{n+1} = - \frac{\sin (\tau_n - \tau_{n-1})}{\sin (\tau_{n+1} - \tau_{n-1})} \delta \tau_n$$
$$\delta \tau_{n-1} = - \frac{\sin (\tau_{n+1} - \tau_n)}{\sin (\tau_{n+1} - \tau_{n-1})} \delta \tau_n$$

Unless the curvature is infinite the sines equal the angles. The energy
to be made a minimum is proportional to
$$(\tau_{n+2} - \tau_{n+1})^2 + (\tau_{n+1} - \tau_n)^2 + (\tau_n - \tau_{n-1})^2 + (\tau_{n-1} - \tau_{n-2})^2$$
That is, $\tau_{n+2}$ and $\tau_{n-2}$ being fixed
$$-(\tau_{n+2} - \tau_{n+1}) \delta \tau_{n+1} + (\tau_{n+1} - \tau_n) (\delta \tau_{n+1} - \delta \tau_n) + (\tau_n - \tau_{n-1}) (\delta \tau_n - \delta \tau_{n-1}) + [del](\tau_{n-1} - \tau_{n-2}) \delta \tau_{n-1}[/del] [ins]+ (\tau_{n-1} - \tau_{n-2}) \delta \tau_{n-1}[/ins] = 0$$
Rearranging
$$(\tau_{n+2} - 2\tau_{n+1} + \tau_n) \delta \tau_{n+1} + (\tau_{n+1} - 2\tau_n + \tau_{n-1}) \delta \tau_n + (\tau_n - 2\tau_{n-1} + \tau_{n-2}) \delta \tau_{n-1} = 0$$
Substituting the values of $\delta \tau_{n+1}$ and $\delta \tau_{n-1}$ and clearing of fractions
$$0 = (\tau_{n+2} - 2\tau_{n+1} + \tau_n) (\tau_n - \tau_{n-1}) [del]- (\tau_{n+1} - 2\tau_n + \tau_{n-1}) (\tau_{n+1} - \tau_{n-1})[/del] + (\tau_n - 2\tau_{n-1} + \tau_{n-2}) (\tau_{n+1} - \tau_n)$$
$$= (\tau_{n+2} - 3\tau_{n+1} + 3\tau_n - \tau_{n-1}) (\tau_n - \tau_{n-1}) - (\tau_{n+1} - 3\tau_n + 3\tau_{n-1} - \tau_{n-2}) (\tau_{n+1} - \tau_n).$$
That is, the following quantity does not vary with $n$:
$$\frac{\tau_{n+2} - 3\tau_{n+1} + 3\tau_n - \tau_{n-1}}{\tau_{n+1} - \tau_n}.$$
