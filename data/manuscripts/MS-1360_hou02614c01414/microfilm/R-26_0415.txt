[u]Notation[/u].

When a letter is familiarly used with a particular
signification, especially in a book in constant use, like
that of Rankine, it ought not to be used in the
same branch of mathematics, say in statics, in a
different sense. It is far better to go outside the alpha-
bet and employ the symbols of astronomy (which in
statics cannot possibly be supposed to be used in their astro-
nomical senses), etc. In particular, I recognize the
following uses of letters, as so to speak sacred, or
as the President would say, "consecrated."
