Problem 1. From an immovable point hangs a cord of [del]elasticity[/del] [ins]extensibility[/ins] E
and natural length l. To the other end is attached a weight P. Determine
its equilibrium position.

Adopt rectangular coordinates having their origin at the fixed point, and with
y directed vertically downward.

The work is [del]1/2[/del] [ins]1/2[/ins] E [u]($\frac{\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}-l}{l}$)[/u]$^2$ - Py

The condition of equilibrium is

E ($\frac{1}{l}$ - $\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}}$) (xdx + ydy + zdz) - Pdy = 0.

That is,

E ($\frac{1}{l}$ - $\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}}$) x = 0
E ($\frac{1}{l}$ - $\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}}$) y - P = 0
E ($\frac{1}{l}$ - $\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}}$) z = 0

Since neither E nor P is zero, x = 0, z = 0. Hence,

$\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}$ = +$\sqrt{y^2}$

y = l ($\frac{P}{E}$ $\pm$ 1).
