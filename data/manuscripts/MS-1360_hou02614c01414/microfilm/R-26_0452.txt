\int M y dx = \frac{load \cdot Y}{2 X^2} \int_{-X}^{+X} \left( \frac{X^2}{2} - x^2 \right) \left( X^2 - x^2 \right) dx = \frac{Y}{2 X^2} \int_{-X}^{+X} \left[ \frac{X^4}{2} - \left( \frac{X^2}{2} + X^2 \right) x^2 + x^4 \right] dx

= \frac{Y}{[del]3[/del] X^2} \left[ \frac{2}{3} [del]X[/del] X^2 - \frac{2}{15} X^2 \right] X \cdot load

= \frac{2}{3} X^3 \frac{Y}{[del]X^2[/del]} \left[ \frac{X^2}{[del]2[/del]} - \frac{1}{5} \right] load

= \frac{1}{12} l^3 f \left[ \frac{l^2}{[del]2[/del]} - \frac{1}{5} \right] load

\int y^2 dx = \int \left( Y^2 - 2 \frac{Y^2}{X^2} x^2 + \frac{Y^4}{X^4} x^4 \right) = [del]2[/del] \left( Y^2 - 2 Y^2 \left( 1 - \frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{5} \right) \right) X = \frac{8}{15} f^2 l
