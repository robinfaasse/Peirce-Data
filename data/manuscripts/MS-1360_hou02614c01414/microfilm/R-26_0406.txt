[u]3[ins]rd[/ins] Part[/u]. When the horizontal force is just sufficient to bring the suspenders into place, the vertical force on the cable at $x_c$ is
$$\int_0^{x_c} \left\{ K \sqrt{1 + (2 \frac{y_0 - y_c}{x_c})^2} + K' (y_0 - y_c) \right\} dx = \left[ K \sqrt{1 + (2 \frac{y_0 - y_c}{x_c})^2} + K' (y_0 - y_c) \right] x_c$$
Since $\frac{dy}{dx} = 2 \frac{y_0 - y_c}{x_c} \cdot \frac{x}{x_c}$, the horizontal pull is the vertical pull multiplied by $\frac{1}{2} \frac{x_c}{y_0 - y_c}$, or $\left\{ K \sqrt{1 + (\frac{x_c}{2 \overline{y_0 - y_c}})^2} + \frac{1}{2} K' x_c \right\} x_c$. The cable preserving its form the horizontal and vertical forces are thereafter increased in the same ratio. That is, when $H$ is the horizontal pull, the additional load brought on each element of length of the truss is
$$\left[ -2 \frac{y_0 - y_c}{x_c^2} H + K \sqrt{1 + (2 \frac{y_0 - y_c}{x_c})^2} + K' (y_0 - y_c) \right] dx.$$
