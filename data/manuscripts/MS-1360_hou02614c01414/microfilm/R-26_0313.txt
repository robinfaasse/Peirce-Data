Now, the ratio $\frac{A}{\varepsilon}$ will be an important quantity, and it is desirable that the effects under stretching should be comparable with those when $\frac{A}{\varepsilon} = 0$. Hence $\frac{A}{\varepsilon}$ which is the ratio of a horizontal force to a force along the cable ought to vanish with one angle considered, and simplicity is subserved by making the variable angle vanish when the increment of the horizontal abscissa divided by the increment of the arc of the cable vanishes.
