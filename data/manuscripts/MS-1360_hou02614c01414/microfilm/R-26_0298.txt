(3) $W_n = q_n \sin i_n - q_{n-1} \sin i_{n-1}$
(4) $q_n \cos i_n = q_{n-1} \cos i_{n-1}$

From the last equation it follows that $q_n \cos i_n$, the horizontal component of the tension, is constant throughout the cord. Call it $Q$, so that
(5) $Q = q_n \cos i_n$.

Summing successive values of $W_n$ by (3), and supposing $m > n$
(6) $\begin{aligned} \sum_n^m W &= q_m \sin i_m - q_{n-1} \sin i_{n-1} \\ &= Q (\tan i_m - \tan i_{n-1}). \end{aligned}$

If the weights are all positive, it follows that, if [del]m[/del] [ins]$m > n$[/ins] then $i_m > i_n$.

(7) If $h$ be the number of a stretch that is horizontal
(8) $\sum_{h+1}^m W = Q \tan i_m$.
whence, $q_m = \sum_{h+1}^m W \cdot \csc i_m$

$W''_n = - Q \tan i_{n-1}$.
