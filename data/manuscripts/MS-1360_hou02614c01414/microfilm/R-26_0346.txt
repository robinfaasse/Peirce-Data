$$\int_0^S \frac{ds}{\sqrt{1 + \frac{(F's - \omega_{_H})^2}{Q^2}}} = x_{_{N+1}} \qquad \frac{1}{Q} \int_0^S \frac{(F's - \omega_{_H}) ds}{\sqrt{1 + \frac{(F's - \omega_{_H})^2}{Q^2}}} = y_{_{N+1}}$$
