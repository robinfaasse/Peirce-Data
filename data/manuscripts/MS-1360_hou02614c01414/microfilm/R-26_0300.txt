Then, it is evident that, under the fixed load, the form of the cable is a parabola with vertical axis and vertex down, and that the whole weight of the truss is borne by the suspenders. The equation of the cable is
$$y' = y'_+ \frac{x'}{x'_+} (2 - \frac{x'}{x'_+}).$$

Hence, its slope is,
$$\frac{dy'}{dx'} = 2 \frac{y'_+}{x'_+} (1 - \frac{x'}{x'_+}),$$

and
$$\frac{dy'_0}{dx'_0} = 2 \frac{y'_+}{x'_+}$$

The horizontal pull is, by (29),
$$A' = \frac{p x'_+^2}{2 y'_+}$$

The total pull upon the virtual backstay is
$$\sqrt{A'^2 + (p x'_+)^2} = p x'_+ \sqrt{1 + \frac{x'_+^2}{4 y'_+^2}}.$$

The elongation of each backstay is
$$\frac{T}{E} p x'_+ \sqrt{1 + \frac{x'_+^2}{4 y'_+^2}}$$

Hence, the length of cable which would be over the span, were all the weight taken off, would be
$$2 ( [u]l_+[/u] - \frac{T}{E} p x'_+ \sqrt{1 + \frac{x'_+^2}{4 y'_+^2}} ),$$

where [u]$l_+$[/u] is to be determined by means of equation (35), [del]noticing[/del] [ins]having regard to[/ins] the different notation.
