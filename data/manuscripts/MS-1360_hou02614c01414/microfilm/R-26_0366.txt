<u>Other general propositions</u>. Consider any portion of the cord. The pulls upon its ends act along the terminal lines of that portion. Those two lines of force meet in one point; and since their horizontal components are equal and opposite, their resultant is vertical. It is, of course, upward. But since equilibrium exists it does not make a couple with the resultant of the weights attached to the [ins]intermediate[/ins] portion of the cord. It must therefore be in the line of that resultant weight; that is, it lies in the vertical of the centre of gravity of the intermediate weights. This is expressed by the equation
(14) $\frac{\sum_{n+1}^m w x}{\sum_{n+1}^m w} = \frac{x_m \tan i_m - y_m - x_{n+1} \tan i_n + y_n}{\tan i_m - \tan i_n}$
