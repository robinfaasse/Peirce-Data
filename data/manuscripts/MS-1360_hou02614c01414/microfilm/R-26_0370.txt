The expression $\delta\tau/\delta\tau_n$ may best be taken in that sense which implies bendings at the smallest possible number of joints (particles), which number, in view of the fixity of the extremities of the ribbon, is two.

If we take $\theta$ and $\vartheta$ to denote the angles $\arctan \frac{y}{x}$ and $\arctan \frac{y}{x-x_N}$ we have
[del]$\delta y + \delta \frac{\sin\tau}{\dots} \cdot ds + \delta (y + \frac{\partial y}{\partial s} ds) = 0$[/del]
[del]$\delta (y \cot \theta) + \delta \cos\tau \cdot ds + \delta \{ (y + \frac{\partial y}{\partial s} ds) \cot \vartheta \} = 0$[/del]
[del]$= \delta y \cot \theta - \frac{y \delta\theta}{\sin^2\theta} - \sin\tau \delta\tau \cdot ds + \delta (y + \frac{\partial y}{\partial s} ds) \cdot \cot \vartheta - (y + \frac{\partial y}{\partial s} ds) \frac{\delta\vartheta}{\sin^2\vartheta}$[/del]

[del]$\delta y \cot \theta$[/del] $x \delta\theta + \cos\tau \cdot \delta\tau \cdot ds + (x-x_N) \delta\vartheta = 0$
$-y \delta\theta - \sin\tau \cdot \delta\tau \cdot ds - y \delta\vartheta = 0$
$(y \cos\tau - x \sin\tau) \delta\tau \cdot ds - y_N \delta\vartheta = 0$
$\frac{\sin(\theta-\tau)}{\sin\theta} \delta\tau \cdot \frac{ds}{x_N} = \delta\vartheta$
$y x_N \delta\theta + (y \cos\tau - (x-x_N) \sin\tau) \delta\tau \cdot ds = 0$
$\delta\theta = - \frac{\sin(\vartheta-\tau)}{\sin\vartheta} \frac{\delta\tau \cdot ds}{x_N}$
