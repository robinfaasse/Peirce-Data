of $x$ and $y$ for $s = S$, by which to determine the two
unknown constants $Q$ and $w_H$. That is, we have to
determine the constants of the equation
$$Fs = Q \tan \tau + w_H$$
by means of the equations
$$\int_0^S \cos \tau \cdot ds = x_{N+1} \qquad \int_0^S \sin \tau \cdot ds = y_{N+1},$$
which may be written
$$\int_0^S \frac{ds}{\sqrt{1 + \frac{(Fs - w_H)^2}{Q^2}}} = x_{N+1} \qquad \frac{1}{Q} \int_0^S \frac{(Fs - w_H) ds}{\sqrt{1 + \frac{(Fs - w_H)^2}{Q^2}}} = y_{N+1}.$$

The first approximations can be obtained from a table for the
common catenary. These will best be corrected by the double
rule of false, which is less laborious in this case than calculating
the coefficients of linear equations of correction. It will be
found that an increase of $Q$ much increases $x_{N+1}$ while
merely bringing $y_{N+1}$ a little nearer zero, if it is not already
zero, while an increase of $w_H$ much diminishes $y_{N+1}$ while
slightly diminishing $x_{N+1}$.

fundamental one, and is easier, in so far as $Q$ is given. But
