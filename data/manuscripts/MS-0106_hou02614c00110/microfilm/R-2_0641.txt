[del]Three[/del] [ins]Four[/ins] systems of coordinates.

1[ins]st[/ins]. Cartesian system with O as origin

2[ins]nd[/ins]. The point P is defined by drawing a circle through it and the two fixed points A & B, [ins]equally distant from O[/ins] and giving $\rho$ the radius of this circle and $\vartheta$, the angle at the centre from the direction of -X towards or through that of +Y to P.

3[ins]rd[/ins]. Two angles $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are measured from A and B respectively as centres, & from the direction of B, $\beta$ from that of A, and both towards or through +Y to P.

4[ins]th[/ins]. Calling OD = $y_1$ , $s = x + (y - y_1)i$
$t = x - (y - y_1)i$.
