[u]Rules Existential Graphs[/u] 9.

[u]Rule XX[/u]. The reverse operation can be performed-
i.e. any part of a graph may be omitted, which
occurs elsewhere with the same connections
& not within any enclosures other than those
within which lies the part that is to be omitted.

[fig]figure[/fig] The inner [u]sings[/u] can
be omitted, but
the outer [u]sings[/u] cannot.

[u]Rule XXI[/u] (Corollary from XX)
A part of a graph evenly enclosed may be [ins]part[/ins]
carried in into two ovals existing in the other [ins]^[/ins]

E.g.
[fig]figure[/fig] = { There is a man who
loves all women

"man" can be carried into the two ovals, giving: —
[fig]figure[/fig] = { Every woman is
loved by some man or other

Proved by Rules XX & XI.
