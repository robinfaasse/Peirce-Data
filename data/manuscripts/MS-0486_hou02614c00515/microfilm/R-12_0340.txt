[marginalia]Rules -
Existential
Graphs[/marginalia] [pn]10[/pn]

[u]Rule XXII[/u] Any oddly enclosed part of a graph that is in 3 or more enclosures can be carried out from two.

[u]Rule XXIII[/u]. Any connecting line that is oddly enclosed can be protracted beyond any part or the whole of its enclosures.
