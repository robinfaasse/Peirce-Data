[u]Existential Graphs.[/u]

[u]Rule VIII.[/u] If an oval containing only a given graph is illatively transformable into a second graph, then an oval containing the second graph is illatively transformable into the first graph. Example. [fig]oval containing "you pay your fare" and "walk"[/fig]

[u]Rule IX.[/u] Any index of any individual of the universe may be written down with a connecting line attached to it—the other end of the connecting line being left free—

Note A. These connecting lines can be called "bonds."

Note B. This rule was first given thus:— The enclosure of an individual index in an oval has no effect, although the enclosure of a portion of the connecting line has the same effect as upon any graph. Example. [fig]oval containing "John" and "sins" connected by a line[/fig] [fig]oval containing "John" and "sins" with "sins" enclosed in a smaller oval[/fig] Both mean "John does not sin."

[fig]oval containing "John" and "sins" connected by a line[/fig] [fig]oval containing "John" and "sins" with a loop in the connecting line[/fig] Both mean "John is other than somebody who sins"

Note C. "not" means a second person—
E.g. "John does not sin" means "Pick out any person as first & if he sins John is a second person"—

[u]Rule X.[/u] is stricken out.
