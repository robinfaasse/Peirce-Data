[u]B. Derivative Rules.[/u]

[u]Rule XI[/u] Any unenclosed part of a graph can be erased.
Proof. Given graph AB, you can infer B,
because by Rule I I can erase A standing alone,
then by Rule II I can erase it if it is an unenclosed
part of any graph.

[u]Rule XII[/u] (corollary from [u]XI[/u]) Any unenclosed connecting
line may be broken. [fig]Graph showing a line being broken[/fig] can be transformed to [fig]Graph showing a broken line[/fig]
Example. [fig]Graph of "Sins" connected to "Sings"[/fig]
[fig]Graph of "Sins" and "Sings" disconnected[/fig]

[u]Rule XIII[/u] (an extension of [u]Rule II[/u]) Any unenclosed part of a
graph may be repeated with all its connections.
Example. "sins — sings" may be made "sins [fig]Graph showing "sins" branching to two "sings"[/fig] sings"
