[u]Rule V.[/u] Individual indices joined by connecting lines
uncrossed by any line are illatively transformable
each into the other.

[u]Rule VI.[/u] To any connecting line at least one uncrossed
index designating an individual object might be joined,
if we knew what that index were. Example. [fig]John Sings[/fig]
