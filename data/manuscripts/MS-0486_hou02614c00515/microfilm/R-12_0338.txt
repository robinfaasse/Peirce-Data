[u]Rule XVIII[/u] If any transformation can
be made irrespective of the oddness or even-
ness of the enclosures, then the reverse
transformation can be made.
(Except-possibly under no enclosures) -

[u]Rule XIX[/u]. Any insertion may be made,
provided that the partial graph inserted
is already present and within no enclosures
other than those within which the insertion
is proposed to be made -
And the connections must be the same
of the two occurrences or repetitions.
