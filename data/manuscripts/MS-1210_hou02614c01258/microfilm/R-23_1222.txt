-[u]ide[/u], originally used to modify the name of
the non-metallic element of a haloid
salt, as chloride of sodium, NaCl. Now
modifies the name of any residue attached
by an exterior bond to the rest of a
compound, as potassium oxide, iron
carbide, [del]stannic[/del] [ins]silicic[/ins] methide. For [u]amide[/u] see below.
Also ends the irregular word [u]anhydride[/u]

-[u]uret[/u], (obsolete) restricted chiefly to sulphuret
for sulphide.

=[u]ic[/u], an adjective termination, 1[sup]st[/sup] of acids,
as sulphuric acid, acetic acid, etc. 2[sup]nd[/sup]
modifying the names of metals and
of residues replacing metals, especially
in salts, as mercuric iodide, potassic
[del]hydride[/del] hydrate, 3[sup]d[/sup] used still more loosely
to indicate any peculiar constituent as
silicic ethide
