He also gives $0 = \overline{\infty}$ $\infty = \overline{0}$

$0$ is $x \overline{<} \overline{x}$.

In fact it is necessary to begin with
the algebra of the copula.

(1) $x \overline{<} x$.
Principle. If one $y$ follows from $x$
$x \overline{<} y$ may be written. If $x \overline{<} y$ can be written,
then in an individual state of things if $x$ is true
$y$ is true.
(2) $x \overline{<} y \overline{<} x$
(3) $y \overline{<} x \overline{<} x$

The rules of the copula are either rules
stating that a given formula may be written,
