§17.
To find $x$ from
$\begin{aligned}
x & \prec a \\
b & \prec x
\end{aligned}$
$\begin{aligned}
a & \prec x \bar{\lor} b \\
a & \prec b \bar{\lor} x
\end{aligned}$

[del]Algebra[/del]
$x \prec x(b \bar{\lor} \bar{b}) \prec xb \bar{\lor} \bar{b} \prec a \bar{\lor} \bar{b}$
$a\bar{b} \prec (x \bar{\lor} b)\bar{b} \prec x \bar{\lor} b\bar{b} \prec x$
There is nothing gained by writing
$\sum_u x = u(a \bar{\lor} \bar{b})$
this is merely quantifying the predicate.
