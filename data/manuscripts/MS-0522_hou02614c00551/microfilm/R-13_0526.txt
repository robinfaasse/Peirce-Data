[del]Permissible[/del]

Having thus got the rules for the
copula of inclusion, we pass to the
other copula.
Here the rules are just the opposite

<u>Permissible</u> are
Any insertion under odd vincula.
Any omission under even vincula.
Any insertion under even vincula
of anything under a part of those vincula

Thus given
[u]a b [u]c d [u]e f [u]g h [u]i j [u]k l [u]m n [u]o p[/u][/u][/u][/u][/u][/u][/u][/u]

one can insert after p under the four vincula
either [u]n[/u] or [u]j[/u] or [u]f[/u] or [u]b[/u]. But not p

Any omission under odd vincula of
anything under a part of those vincula
Thus one can omit [u]p[/u] provided this is identical
with [u]n[/u] or [u]j[/u] or [u]b[/u].
