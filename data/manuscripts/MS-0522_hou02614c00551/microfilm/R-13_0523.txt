We may insert anything we
please anywhere under an even number
of vincula (or none)

We may omit anything under an
odd number of vincula, so long as
we leave something. If nothing is left,
[del]this to is something accepted or under[/del]
[del]class[/del] but the vincula must remain.

We may omit under an even number
of vincula any repetition of anything
under the same vinculae, or the same less any
even number.

Thus given [u]x7y7x7z[/u]
we may insert [del]x7[/del] thus [u]x7y7x7z[/u]
and may then omit [del]x7[/del] thus [u]y7x7z[/u]
So having accepted
[u]o7j7b7o7p[/u]
we may omit o7 thus [u]j7b7o7p[/u]

We may insert under an odd number of
vincula any repetition of anything under
the same vinculae

Thus having accepted p we
may omit o7p. Having accepted
[u]j7b7p[/u] we may write
[u]j7j7b7p[/u]

Having accepted [u]m7n7p[/u]
we may write [u]n7p[/u]

Having accepted
[u]a7b7d7h7p[/u] we may write
[u]b7d7h7p[/u].

But better say that repetitions of what
are under [del]not a pen or the same[/del] [ins]fewer[/ins] vinculae have no
effect.
