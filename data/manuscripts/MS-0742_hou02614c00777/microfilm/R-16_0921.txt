§1. Logic is the science needed in order to test
arguments.

The science required for any testing is one which
merely divides its object into its natural kinds &
describes the characters of each kind. Thus a
"Bank-Note Detector" affords the knowledge re-
quisite to testing bank-notes and it describes
each kind of bank note merely, without entering
into [del]an observation[/del] [ins]account[/ins] of [del]the[/del] manufacture. Such
a knowledge will be termed a [u]classificatory[/u]
in opposition to a [u]causal[/u] or [u]demonstrative[/u]
science.
