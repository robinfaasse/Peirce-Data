------ No war is long popular; for every war increases taxation; and the popularity of anything that touches our pockets is very short-lived.

------ No carnivorous animals have four stomachs; all ruminants have four stomachs; hence, no ruminants are carnivorous.

------ He that says you are an animal speaks truly; he that says you are a goose says you are an animal; hence, he that says you are a goose speaks truly.

------ Every good statesman is favorable to progress; some members of parliament not being favorable to progress are not good statesmen.

------ Of two evils, the least is to be preferred; occasional turbulence, therefore, [del]being a[/del] less evil than rigid despotism is to be preferred to it.

------ I snatch at the conclusion that every eager desire is an evil thing; since I know that the desire of evil is evil, and that not a few eager desires have evil objects.

------ All plants contain cellular tissue; no animals are plants; therefore, no animals contain cellular tissue.

------ The question whether freight-rates depend on the tariff is difficult to answer. But the price of iron certainly depends on the tariff. Hence, the question whether freight-rates depend on the price [del]of[/del] iron is difficult to answer.

------ If you do not control your small expenses, your living will cost you much more than you calculate. If it is not easy to persuade you that the speedy result of overrunning your income would be to exhaust your capital, you are a poor arithmetician. If your living costs you much more than you calculate, you will be in danger of overrunning your income. It is not easy to persuade you that if your living costs you much more than you calculate, you will be in danger of soon overrunning your income. Hence, if a want of control of your small expenses would put you in danger of overrunning your income, you are a poor arithmetician.

------ If your making your men work in your powder-mill at night would loose you much money, would lead you to insure your property, you would be sure to find no company would take the risk. Hence, if your making your men work in your powder-mill at night would make danger of fire being dropped into a barrel of gunpowder, you cannot get any company to insure your property.

------ If Enoch and Elijah were mortal, the Bible errs. But it is possible that Enoch and Elijah were men, and yet that the Bible does not err. Hence, some men are not mortal.
