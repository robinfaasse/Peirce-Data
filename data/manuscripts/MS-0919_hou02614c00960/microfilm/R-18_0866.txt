[u]On Classification[/u]
[u]§ 1. Of thoughts[/u]

A thought is not a thing. Several things may have some quality in common. This is a thought. All the consciousnesses which contain this thought also have the thought of which they are only expressions. Thought therefore is not personal. But pure form.
