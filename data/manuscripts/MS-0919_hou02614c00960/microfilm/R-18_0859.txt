Chapter I Object of Philosophic Study.

Metaphysics is, by definition and idea, the queen of the sciences. As all knowledge is the unity of a given manifold, so [u]σοφία[/u] (the object of φιλοσοφία) stands in the same relation to everything.

Chapter II. Plans of Philosophic Study.

Since we cannot see and consider every individual fact, to attain the σοφία we must do one of three things.
1. Anticipate the manifold altogether
2. [del]Find[/del] [ins]Find[/ins] some finite manifold which potentially contains every manifold
3. Find the [del]σο[/del]φία in every part of the manifold.

The earliest, most direct, and least artful plan, is to take for granted what we are inwardly forced to believe, and hang the universe on what I say.
