COLUMBIA UNIVERSITY
DIVISION OF
PHILOSOPHY, PSYCHOLOGY AND ANTHROPOLOGY

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y. [date]January 12, 1904[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce:

As I have written to you, Professor Woodbridge has assumed the editorship of the [u]Journal of Philosophy, Psychology and Scientific Methods[/u], and its conduct is entirely in his hands. I am certain that he would greatly appreciate your cooperation, and I should suppose that the audience offered by the journal would be the best now existing. It is of course possible that a journal devoted especially to logic would be still better, but I should doubt whether there are at present sufficient people interested in the subject to warrant its establishment.

If Professor Woodbridge has not replied definitely to your offer to take charge of the department of logic, it is perhaps because he does not understand what you would be willing to do, but I will speak to him of the matter.

Very truly yours,
J. McK. Cattell

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.
