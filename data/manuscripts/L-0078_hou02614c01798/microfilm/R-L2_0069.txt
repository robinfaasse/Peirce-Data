Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y. [date]December 4, 1900[/date]

Dr. John S. Billings,
425 Lafayette St.,
New York City

Dear Sir:-

I beg to vote in favor of the nomination of Professor Dewey. Professor Dewey is doubtless primarily a student of philosophy, but he treats philosophy from the scientific rather than the metaphysical point of view, and he has made contributions to psychology, logic, ethics and education of importance, which may properly be regarded as scientific in character.

Very respectfully,
