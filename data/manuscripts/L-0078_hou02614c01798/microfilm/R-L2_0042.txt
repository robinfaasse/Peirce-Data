SCIENCE
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y. [date]May 7, 1901[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I am much obliged to you for the manuscript on the history of Euclid.

I shall always be glad to see you if you are in New York, and if you can come to the College on Tuesday or Thursday at 1.30 you would be sure to find me and we could have luncheon together.

Very truly yours,

J. McK. Cattell

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.
