THE POPULAR SCIENCE MONTHLY
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y.
September 20, 1904.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I am addressing this letter to those men of science whose help the POPULAR SCIENCE MONTHLY wants, whether or not they have already assisted in its work. We need this journal to keep the scientific worker in contact with the intelligent public, to secure the general interest in science essential to its support and to enlarge the place of science in education and life. The conduct of such a journal is not easy; it was being published by the Appletons at an annual loss of $10,000 when they were compelled to give it up, and it cannot under ordinary circumstances pay for contributions. But I trust that a journal intended to promote the interests of science and of men of science deserves their support. Will you not contribute one article in the course of a year or two and will you not suggest now one or two suitable articles with possible contributors? It is more difficult to secure satisfactory articles in mathematics than in any other subject.

Very truly yours,
John R Cattell

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
'Arisbe'
Milford, Pa.
