SCIENCE
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y. June 25. 1900

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

Mr. Wilson writes to me that he has asked you to return to me the Clark memorial volume, which you kindly reviewed for SCIENCE. There was no reason why he should do this and on the contrary reasons why it should not have been done. Please do not return the book if it has the slightest value to you.

Very truly yours,

J. McKeen Cattell

Dictaed. Signed in Mr. Cattell's absence from home.
