Dear Sir:--

We enclose with this agreement[ins],[/ins] drawn in duplicate in the usual form[ins],[/ins] covering the publishing arrangement for the "History of Science" that you have in preparation for the Science Series.

Both copies of the agreement should be signed by the author and one should be returned for the files of the publishing office.

We have left blank the specification in Clause I as to the date for the delivery of the "copy" of the volume in readiness for the compositors. We have, however, noted that it is the author's present expectation to complete his work before the close of 1899.
