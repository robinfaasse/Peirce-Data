THE SCIENCE SERIES
G. P. PUTNAM'S SONS
NEW YORK & LONDON
OFFICE OF THE EDITOR

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y. [date]May 24, 1898[/date]

Dear Mr. Pierce:-

I examined with much interest the outline of your book, sent by you to the Putnam's. I feel sure that it will be a most important contribution to science. It is possible that as you have yourself suggested, it might be best to make the work longer, devoting the first volume to the history of science prior to the present century, following it with a volume on the history of science in the nineteenth century - should the first volume pay the costs of publication as I feel sure it will. I should like it very much if you would consent to contribute some part of the book as articles to SCIENCE.

If you are in New York, could you take lunch with me at the College, say on Friday next, at one o'clock? I shall be in Room 412, Schermerhorn Hall.

Very truly yours,
J. McK. Cattell

Mr. C. S. Pierce
Milford, Pa.
