We look forward to the [del]whole[/del] [ins]work[/ins] with full hopefulness.

We are now planning to put to press the first of the American contributions for the series. The volume is the work of Professor Israel C. Russell, of the University of Michigan. The subject is the "Rivers of the North American Continent". I am inclined to believe, as far as it is practicable to judge of the matter from the schedule thus far arranged for, that the American contributions will possess a scientific importance fully equal to, and probably greater than, that pertaining to the English volumes. I should also be much disappointed [del]if the American[/del] volumes were not more successful in the literary form of their presentation. I am,

Yours faithfully,
