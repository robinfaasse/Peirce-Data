COLUMBIA UNIVERSITY
DIVISION OF
PHILOSOPHY, PSYCHOLOGY AND ANTHROPOLOGY

Garrison-on-Hudson, N. Y., [date]December 2, 1903[/date].

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

It was decided this week that Baldwin will here-after edit 'The Psychological Review', while I shall edit a new journal on the lines of a 'Centralblatt', to be issued twice a month and to be called [u]The Journal of Philosophy, Psychology and Scientific Methods[/u]. It will contain short articles, preliminary reports, discussions, abstracts, reviews and notes, covering the field of scientific philosophy and psychology.

I trust that the undertaking will have your approval and assistance. It is proposed that the journal shall be edited with the cooperation of those who may assist by contributions or otherwise, and I shall be under great obligations to you if you will permit the use of your name in this connection. The new journal will not, I think, in any way interfere with other journals, but will cooperate with them by increasing the interest taken in scientific philosophy and psychology.

Very truly yours,

J. McK. Cattell

Mr. Chas. Sanders Peirce,
Milford, Pa.
