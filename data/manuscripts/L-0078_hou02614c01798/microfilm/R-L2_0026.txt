THE PSYCHOLOGICAL REVIEW
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y. March 10. 1900

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

Would you consent to write for the PSYCHOLOGICAL REVIEW a re-view of the second edition of Pearson's 'Grammar of Science' It is quite altered, and I think that it is an important book and in many ways connected with your interests.

Very truly yours,
J. McK. Cattell

Mr. C.S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

I have received your note since the above was written. I shall be very glad if you will review the Clark volume for Science. I will send it [del]to you[/del] soon as it reaches me.

J McK C.
