SCIENCE
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y. July 15. 1899

Dear Mr. Pierce:-

It was a great pleasure to me to have such a [del]pleasant[/del] [ins]agreeable[/ins] and I trust to me profitable conversation on our way to Worcester.

You may remember that you told me that you had some material regarding the history of science that you might be willing to contribute to SCIENCE. I should be very glad if you would contribute such an article. That on Basil Valentine appeared to me to be of great value to SCIENCE.

Very truly yours,
J. McK. Cattell

Mr. C. S. Pierce
Milford, Pa.
