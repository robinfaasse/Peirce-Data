April 12, 1898

Dear Mr. Peirce:--

I am obliged to you for having given me an opportunity of examining the introductory chapter for the proposed "History of Science". This impresses me as decidedly interesting and suggestive, although certain of the mathematical topics touched upon are outside of my range of comprehension.

I shall be able, however, to arrive at a more satisfactory publishing impression concerning the promise and character of the work as a whole if you might find it convenient to let me have at some date in the future a scheme showing the ground to be gone over, or an approximately complete table of contents.

I should judge also that with a subject of this compass, the author would himself find a decided convenience, from the literary point of view, in putting such general scheme into shape before completing the writing of any one of the chapters or general divisions.
