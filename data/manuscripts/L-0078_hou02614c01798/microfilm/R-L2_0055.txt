[pn]J[/pn] [u]6[/u] Prescott Hall [date]1903 Dec 18[/date]

My dear Cattell:

Baldwin's attitude about the new journal goes still further to strengthen the impression I got of him the first time I laid eyes on him & which has already been confirmed.

Will you kindly show Prof. Woodward the letter I wrote you & say that I am ready to join him on the same terms as I was ready to join you in this, so long
