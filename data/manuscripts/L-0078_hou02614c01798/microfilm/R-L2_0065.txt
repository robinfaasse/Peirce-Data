COLUMBIA UNIVERSITY
DIVISION OF
PHILOSOPHY, PSYCHOLOGY AND ANTHROPOLOGY

Garrison, N.Y., [date]January 22, 1906[/date].

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

Professor Josiah Royce, of Harvard University, has been nominated for membership in the National Academy. It appears to me that Professor Royce's publications in psychology, mathematics and scientific methods would warrant his election even if it is decided that his work in philosophy should not be considered. The question as to how far the Academy wishes to recognize work in philosophy will I understand be the subject of a report of a committee at the approaching meeting, and I think it would be well if the nomination of Professor Royce, usually regarded as our leading student of philosophy, could be brought before us. I am informed by the home secretary that several members of the committee on anthropology have not sent in the preliminary ballot in reference to Professor Royce. This is natural as Professor Royce's work is less closely related to anthropology than to some other sciences. I should, however, be under great obligations to you if you would send your vote whether favorable or the reverse to the home secretary before February 1.

Very truly yours,

J. McKeen Cattell

Mr. C.S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.
