SCIENCE
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y. April 5. 1900


Dear Mr Peirce:-

I am greatly indebted to you for the review of the Clark University Decennial volume for SCIENCE. This I have not seen it having been sent neither to me nor to SCIENCE. I am now, however, writing to ask them to send me a copy and will forward it to you if it turns up.

In regard to the review of Pearson's 'Grammar of Science' for the 'Psychological Review' we shall be glad for you [del]o[/del] [ins]to[/ins] take as much space as you are willing to give to it. We shall be very glad to have a long and thorough review. For the May number I should have the manuscript soon, for the July number there [del]i[/del] [ins]i[/ins]s of course plenty of time.

Within ten minutes of writing the above, by one of those special providences which philosophers interpret variously, I received by express a copy of the memorial [del]voume[/del] [ins]volume[/ins] and reforward it to you.

Very truly yours,

J. McKeen Cattell
