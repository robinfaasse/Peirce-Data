SCIENCE
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y., September 8, 1902.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

Will you not consent to make public in SCIENCE your views as to how the Carnegie Institution can contribute most effectively to the advancement of science? I enclose proof of an article that I have written on the subject. The expression of the opinion of a number of American men of science would doubtless be of service to the officers of the institution, and would be considered by the trustees in the important decisions to be made at their November meeting.

Very truly yours,

J. McK. Cattell

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Pa.
