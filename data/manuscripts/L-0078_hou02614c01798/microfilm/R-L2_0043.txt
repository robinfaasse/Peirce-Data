COLUMBIA UNIVERSITY
IN THE CITY OF NEW YORK
DEPARTMENT OF PSYCHOLOGY

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y. [date]June 6, 1901[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I am writing to our librarian, Mr. J. H. Canfield, that I should much appreciate any courtesy that he may be able to show you in the way indicated in your letter. I should think that an article on 'Laws of Nature' and how they came to be so called might be of much interest. In regard to Arabic characters, I do not suppose that the printers could set them, but if they were drawn we could have them photographed.

I have not written in reply to your last letter about certain facts in color vision, because I wished to consider the matter and I have been very busy the last few weeks. I can only say now that the color-wheel used by me rotated fast enough - 100 times a second. Helmholtz says that colors fuse at 20 but this is not correct, 50 being the limit in a fairly bright light.

Very truly yours,
J. McK. Cattell

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
'Arisbe',
Milford, Pa.
