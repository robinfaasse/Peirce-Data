SCIENCE
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y., July6, 1898.

Dear Mr. Pierce:-

Sometime since I wrote to you asking if you would consent to contribute to SCIENCE some part of the material that you are preparing for your volume on the History of Science and I also made a suggestion as to the possibility of limiting this volume to Science prior to the 19th. century. You are probably only waiting to come to a decision on the matter, but as it is possible that my letter or yours has been lost I write again on the subject.

Very truly yours,
J. McK. Cattell

Mr. C.S.Pierce,
Milford, Pa.
