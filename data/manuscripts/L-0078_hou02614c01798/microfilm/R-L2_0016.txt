THE SCIENCE SERIES
G. P. PUTNAM'S SONS
NEW YORK & LONDON
OFFICE OF THE EDITOR

27 West 23rd St., N. Y., April 8, 189 8.

Professor C. S. Peirce,
108 West 89th St., City.

Dear Professor Peirce:--

I ought to have sent an earlier acknowledgment of your favor of last week with which you were kind enough to submit the first twenty pages of the manuscript of the "History of Science".

I had, however, placed this manuscript to one side in order to give [ins]it[/ins] [del]it[/del] a personal inspection and in connection with the pressure of annual accounts and with some difficulty with my eyes, the opportunity for such inspection had not yet arrived. In order not to delay acknowledgment further, I am writing to-day. I expect to send word next week when I have examined the manuscript itself, in regard to the matter still to be determined, as to the scope and size of the proposed History.

I remain of the opinion that while there would be certain inconveniences in putting the History into two volumes, we should probably rather accept such disadvantage than to incur the risk of lessening the comprehensiveness or the general interest and value of the proposed History.

I am,
Yours very truly,
G. H. Putnam
