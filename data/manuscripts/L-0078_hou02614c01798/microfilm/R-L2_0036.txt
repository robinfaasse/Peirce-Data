THE POPULAR SCIENCE MONTHLY
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y. [date]December 24. 1900[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I am sorry to say that the publishers of the POPULAR SCIENCE MONTHLY do not give reprints unless the authors pay for them, and 30 would cost as much as 100 namely $5.50 I am, however, sending to you now five copies of the number and can have sent to you two months hence without charge [ins]30[/ins] copies that are returned from the news companies. If you want the reprints please let me know, if not I will have the copies sent.

Very truly yours,
J. McK. Cattell

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.
