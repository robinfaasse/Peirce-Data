C. S. Peirce, Esq.,
Milford, Pa,

Dear Sir:--

We are glad to learn from Professor Cattell that we are authorized to include with the preliminary announcement of our "Science Series" a volume from your pen.

He gives us as the present general title for such volume "The History of Science". You will let us have, at your convenience, such sub-title or further specification of title as you may find occasion to make. It is probable that you will wish to specify the term to be covered by your history, [ins]during the Nineteenth Century,[/ins] [del]during the Nineteenth Century,[/del] or (?).

We will put into shape shortly an agreement for the publication of the book, [ins]there[/ins] in accordance with the arrangement arrived at on our behalf by our editor.

Yours very truly
