COLUMBIA UNIVERSITY
DIVISION OF
PHILOSOPHY, PSYCHOLOGY AND ANTHROPOLOGY

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y. [date]December 28, 1903[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I thank you for sending the syllabus, which I have read with interest. We should like to print the part about the classification of the sciences in the new 'Journal of Philosophy, Psychology and Scientific Methods', of which I have written to you. I assume that you will do so unless you write to me to the contrary. Proof will be forwarded to you.

Very truly yours,

J. McKeen Cattell

Mr. Charles S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dictated. Signed in Mr. Cattell's absence.
