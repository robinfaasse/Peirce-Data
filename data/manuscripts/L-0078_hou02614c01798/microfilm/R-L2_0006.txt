THE SCIENCE SERIES
G. P. PUTNAM'S SONS
NEW YORK & LONDON

OFFICE OF THE EDITOR

Garrison-on-Hudson, N. Y., [date]January 6, 1898[/date]

Dear Mr. Pierce:--

Messrs G. P. Putnam's Sons have asked me to take editorial charge of the Series of scientific books that they have in preparation in cooperation with an English firm.

It is very important to plan this Series so that it may contribute to the advancement and diffusion of science in America, and we should greatly appreciate any suggestions that you might be willing to make in regard to the general conduct of the Series or to the special subjects that might be treated in it. The publishers wish especially to learn of the existence of MSS., completed or in preparation, that might possibly be available for the Series.

I should greatly appreciate any advice you may be able to give me in these directions.

Very truly yours,
J. McK. Cattell

Mr. Charles S. Pierce,
Milford, Pa.
