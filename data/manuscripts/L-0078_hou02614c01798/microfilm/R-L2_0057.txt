Dear Professor Peirce:--

We are obliged to you for giving us the opportunity of considering the publication in book form of the series of Lectures you are delivering at the Lowell Institute upon some topics of logic, etc.

We are very ready to believe, as well on the ground of the selection of this series for delivery at the Lowell Institute, as by reason of the authoritative position occupied by the lecturer himself, that the proposed volume will contain material of distinctive and permanent interest and value.

We have, I am sorry to say, not been able to convince ourselves that we should be likely to secure for a book on so very special a subject matter a sufficiently extended sale to render the publication remunerative, or to place the publishers in a position to make a satisfactory return to the scholarly author.
