THE PSYCHOLOGICAL REVIEW
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT

Garrison-on-Hudson, New York, [date]Sept. 29th, 1900.[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

Professor Baldwin and I have charge of the PSYCHOLOGICAL REVIEW in alternate years. The November number is the last number in my year. I should be very glad if you could let me publish the review of Pearson's book in that number. I should need the copy on [ins]about[/ins] October 10th.

Very truly yours,
J. McK. Cattell

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Arisbe, Milford, Pa.
