May 5, 1898


Dear Professor Peirce:--

I am returning with this the scheme and the preliminary
chapter submitted for the "History of Science".

This schedule has been passed upon by the editor, and,
as far as I understand, it meets with his approval. You have
evidently taken pains, in accordance with my suggestion, to plan
out the general proportion of the sub-divisions of your subject
matter before putting labor into the completion of the preliminary
chapters. I can understand the difficulties that you will en-
counter in handling so large a mass of suggestive material, which
in not a few of its divisions would tempt you into a fuller de-
velopment or into excursions from the main narrative. I only
trust that you will be able to keep the study within the compass
now proposed. The work would certainly be an excellent example
of power of condensation and of taking comprehensive views, and
would in itself present a very interesting example of a scientifi-
cally trained mind.
