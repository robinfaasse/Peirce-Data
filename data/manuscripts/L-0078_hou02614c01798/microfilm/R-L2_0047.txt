Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y. [date]March 25, 1903[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I am glad to learn that you may be able next winter to go on with the 'History of Science', and am [del]alds[/del] [ins]also[/ins] glad to learn of the lectures at Harvard and Boston. I look forward to the pleasure of seeing you at Washington. We can then have some conversation about the Carnegie Institution and other matters.

Very truly yours,
J. McK. Cattell

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.
