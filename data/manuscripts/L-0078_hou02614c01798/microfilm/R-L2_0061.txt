COLUMBIA UNIVERSITY
DIVISION OF
PHILOSOPHY, PSYCHOLOGY AND ANTHROPOLOGY

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y. [date]March 4, 1904[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

Dr. Quackenbos was once professor of rhetoric in Columbia University. He now practises medicine with special reference to the applications of hypnotism. He doubtless means well, but the path he is following is dangerously near to quackery.

Very truly yours,
J. McK. Cattell

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
'Arisbe',
Milton, Pa.
