Dear Sir:-

We write to acknowledge receipt this morning of your favor of the 14th inst. We report as follows concerning the several matters in question:

I. [u]The History of Science[/u]. We are glad to understand that progress has been made with the preparation of this work for our Science Series. We understand that you have the first volume practically completed. We understand further that the nature of the narrative calls for the publication at one time of the completed work in the two volumes. We agree with you that there would be no advantage in beginning the typesetting on the first volume until we had in our hands the copy for the completed work. We can only await your further report of progress later in the year.

2. Dumas' "[u]Notes of Travel[/u]." We regret that our judgment is adverse to the probability of our securing for the proposed translation of this volume a sufficiently extended sale to render its publication remunerative or to place the publishers in a position to secure an adequate return for the labor of the translator.
