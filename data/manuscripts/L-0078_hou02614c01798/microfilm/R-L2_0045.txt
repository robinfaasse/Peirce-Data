COLUMBIA UNIVERSITY
DIVISION OF
PHILOSOPHY, PSYCHOLOGY AND ANTHROPOLOGY

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y. January 17, 1903

Dear Professor Peirce:-

I have the highest possible opinion of Mr. Peirce's abilities, and should regard it as a public misfortune if his ideas could not be made public.

I have consulted with President Butler as to the possibility of offering Mr. Peirce a lectureship in this university, but he does not regard it as feasible.

Very truly yours,

J. McK. Cattell

Professor J. M. Peirce,
Harvard University
