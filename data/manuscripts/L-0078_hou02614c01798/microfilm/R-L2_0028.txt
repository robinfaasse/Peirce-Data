THE POPULAR SCIENCE MONTHLY
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT.

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y., May 7th, 1900

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

THE POPULAR SCIENCE MONTHLY will hereafter be published by The McClure Co., and edited by me. We evidently need this journal to keep the scientific worker in contact with the intelligent public, to secure the general interest in science essential to its material support, and to enlarge the place of science in education and life.

I trust that we may have your support in the conduct of a journal intended to promote the interests of science and of men of science.

Very truly yours,
J. McK. Cattell.

Mr. Charles S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.
