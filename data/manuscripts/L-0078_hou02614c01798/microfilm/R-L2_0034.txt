THE POPULAR SCIENCE MONTHLY
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT

Garrison-on-Hudson N.Y. [date]October 30. 1900[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

Your article on Pearson's book came too late to be
inserted in the November number of the [u]Review[/u], that
is to say, too late to send you proof and for me to see
that the corrections were properly entered. We shall
certainly be very glad to have an article from you in the
[u]Review[/u], but it has occurred to me that, in view of the
general interest of the review, it might be printed to
advantage in the POPULAR SCIENCE MONTHLY. The
MONTHLY pays for articles, but as you know not very
generously.

Very truly yours

J. McKeen Cattell

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dictated. Signed in Mr. Cattell's absence from home.
