SCIENCE
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT

Garrison-on-Hudson, N. Y., [date]Dec. 1, 1900.[/date]

Mr. Charles S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:-

SCIENCE will next year be sent to all members of the American Association for the Advancement of Science. The circulation of the Journal will be increased and its influence and responsibility in an equal degree. SCIENCE is an institution for the advancement of science, not a publishing enterprise. The more widely it is circulated and the more completely its contents represent scientific advance and activity, the greater is its service to science and to men of science.

The Editorial Committee need and have in large measure secured the co-operation of all American scientific men. We hope that this support will be continued and increased now that it will be more effective then hitherto. The Journal needs all kinds of contributions that will forward the advancement and diffusion of science, but it is in the first instance a trade journal or newspaper for men of science. It should report fully and promptly all advances in science and all information regarding those agencies on which science depends. Please send me contributions of this character and suggestions in regard to securing them.

Very truly yours,

J. McK Cattell
Responsible Editor.
