[u]THE SCIENCE SERIES[/u]
G. P. PUTNAM'S SONS
NEW YORK & LONDON
[u]OFFICE OF THE EDITOR[/u]

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y. January 15, 189[ins]8[/ins]

Dear Mr. Pierce:-

I thank you for your kind letter. It is undoubtedly true that your most important work is above the heads of ordinary people, but it seems to me that this ought not to interfere with its publication and I trust that all the MSS. you have prepared or are able to prepare will be published in the near future.

A book such as you suggest on the history of science would I should think be of great value to the SERIES I am planning. It would, however, need to be comparatively brief as the volumes will be fairly uniform in size - 300 to 500 pages of from 300 to 400 words each. I should be glad if you would consider the preparation of such a book, which, however, would not, I should hope, interfere with the preparation of the larger work which is greatly needed and which you would be so competent to prepare.

The Putnam's offer a royalty of 10% with an increase if the sales warrant.

Very truly yours,
J. McK. Cattell

Mr. C. S. Pierce
Arisbe, Milford, Pa.
