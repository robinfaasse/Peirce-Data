SCIENCE
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y. August 15, 1903

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I am forwarding Russell's book. I think that you will find it worthy of review. Thank you for consenting to review it, if your judgment agrees with mine.

Very truly yours,
J. McK. Cattell

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.
