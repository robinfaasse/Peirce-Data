the question, for example, of whether three unlimited straight
lines in a plane make a triangle or have a common inter-
section would not have [del]been[/del] recognized as a question

and astronomy, — the only mathematical liberal arts that
they recognized, — were distinguished from the objects
treated in the other liberal arts, namely, grammar, logic,
and rhetoric, [del]from[/del] by being subjects of quantity, or quanta.
Now a Greek would hardly be so dull as not to see that
the objects of [del]grammar, logic, and rhetoric[/del] [ins]the trivium[/ins] all possess
