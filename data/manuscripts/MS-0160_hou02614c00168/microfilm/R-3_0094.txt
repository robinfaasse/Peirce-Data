Angles and cusps and tangents might be included. An angle is a point on a line where the motion of the generating particle necessarily stops. [del]A tang[/del] point of tangency is a point which two parts of a line-figure of the nature of lines have in common, but where the motion of the generating particle need not stop though it pass from one line to the other. A cusp is a point the two parts of the line proceeding from which may either of them be generated by a particle [del]reaching the cusp with a[/del] moving before it reaches them or the cusp and not checked there. All these singularities of generation, as they maybe called, belong to topics, but as far as I see, are of little interest in regard to the topics of lines. When considered with reference to surface etc. they are somewhat more interesting

A filament is an object which can occupy a whole line in an instant, [del]but[/del] and can be contained in a line.

A Surface is a place which [del]may[/del] [ins]can[/ins] be occupied in the course of time by a filament.

[u]w[/u], the periphraxis of a [del]surface[/del] [ins]place[/ins] of N dimensions, is the number of simple non singular (N-2) dimensional removals of matter which have to be made from the matter filling that place before it can, [del]by gradual shrinkage, be reduced to a[/del] [ins]shrink to [del]one of its limits[/del] [ins]a point or[/ins][/ins] lower dimensionality.
