[u]Charmides[/u]. What is [greek]σωφροσυνη[/greek]?

To show that nothing is advantageous but knowledge,
and no knowledge but that of good and evil.

Socrates tells a dream.

***

Very critical and puts himself in the background, pretending
to have no opinion. Illustrations from medicine, ship-building

Piety cannot be defined as that which is dear to the gods because what is dear to one god is not so to another.
The gods differ about what is good they love the good because it is good & therefore it is not true that
it is good because they love it. Piety cannot be defined as that part of justice which ministers
to the gods, because it does not benefit them. It only pleases them. So after all piety
is doing what is pleasing to the gods. So Euthyphro is proved to be in a complete muddle as
to what is piety. There seems to be covert argument against the traditional theology,
if not against polytheism.
