[u]Alcibiades I.[/u] Importance of self-knowledge
We ought to strive for justice and temperance.
[u]Ion.[/u] Ion is a rhapsodist. He has to expound
Homer. Socrates argues that he knows nothing
about it.
[u]Menexenus.[/u] A regular Athenian funeral oration.
[u]Gorgias[/u] To show that it is a greater evil to do than to
suffer injustice, that it is better to suffer for wrong-doing
than not to so suffer, and that we do, not what we will, but
what we wish.
Rhetoric not an art but a flattery. It is to justice
what cookery is to medicine
Ends with the myth of Minos, Rhadamanthus, Aeacus.
[u]Republic Book X.[/u] Begins by treating of imitation.
1st the idea of the bed, of which the material bed is
the imitation, and the picture of the bed the imitation
of the imitation. Homer and the poets are ignorant
imitators, not having the users of the objects represented
at their elbows. The imitative faculty, low. The
faculty of measuring comes to the rescue of the soul.
Imitative arts have immoral tendencies. There is an
old quarrel between philosophy and poetry.
The immortality of the soul proved.
Vision of Er, and rebirth. Metempsychosis
