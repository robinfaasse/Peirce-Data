[pn]p 37.[/pn] The [u]an[/u] should be omitted before umbrella.
The only possible use of inserting it is to avoid being
understood to mean "a book, or umbrella," where
the comma shows the [u]or[/u] is explanatory. But
common sense must be exercised. It is more
elegant to say "a book or umbrella". To say
"a book or an umbrella" sounds pedantic.

[del]"A boy and a girl could not under
such circumstances fall in love,"[/del] that is,
he could not fall in love with any [del]woman[/del]
nor she with [del]any[/del] man. [del]But[/del] if it be
meant they could not fall in love [u]with
eachother[/u], the article must be omitted.
If it does not mean this, then [u]or[/u] should replace
[u]and[/u].
