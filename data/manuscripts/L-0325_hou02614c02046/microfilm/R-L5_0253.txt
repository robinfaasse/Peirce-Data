1, West Franklin Street.

Baltimore, Md. [date]Oct.24th. 1904.[/date]

Dear Sir:-

On Tuesday, November 1st. there will be a meeting at the Johns Hopkins University, in commemoration of the two hundredth anniversary of the death of John Locke. Would you give me the pleasure of your company at dinner at the Maryland Club at 7.30 the same evening?

Sincerely yours,

W Osler
