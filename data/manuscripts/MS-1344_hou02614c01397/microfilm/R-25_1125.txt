Thus science is divided, in the first place into the
Theoretical, of which the final purpose is knowledge, and
the Practical, or art, of which the final purpose is the
doing of something else. Setting aside Art as not par-
ticularly concerning logic, we may notice that there is
a subordinate distinction of ends among the theoreti-
cal sciences. For some are Heuretic, or bent upon the
discovery of truth, while others or designed to arrange
the truth already known into [del]a[/del] [ins]a[/ins] System. Humboldt's
[u]Cosmos[/u], Comte's [u]Philosophie positive[/u], and Herbert Spen-
cer's [u]Synthetic Philosophy[/u] are examples of Systematic
Science. My purpose is to confine myself to Heuretic
Science.
