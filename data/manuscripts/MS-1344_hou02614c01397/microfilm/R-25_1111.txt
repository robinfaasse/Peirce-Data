Who is fit to reason about the duplicated canals of Mars
but a man who is not only perfectly familiar with
astronomical telescopic appearances in general, but
with the [del]experience[/del] appearances of these very objects?
Nor can observation be properly conducted unless men
reason while they observe. Accordingly, although in
every department of science some men are more
given up to observation and others to reasoning, all
must be observers and all must be reasoners; and
that is why every science is not duplicated into an
observational and a rational part, although almost
every science has its applied mathematics.
