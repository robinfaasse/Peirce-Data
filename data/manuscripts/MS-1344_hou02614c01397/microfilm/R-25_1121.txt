Now in order to ascertain whether the keeping up of the popula-
tion is the final, or formal, cause of the equality of the sexes,
we must examine what happens when that equality is
deranged. Is the [del]population[/del] [ins]end[/ins] then otherwise attained? It
is said that wars which result in depleting the male popu-
lation are [del]followed[/del] [ins]accompanied[/ins] by great fecundity among the women;
and further that where women are imported in great
numbers the balance of the population is maintained by
diminished fecundity; while if the female population
is [del]cut off[/del] down by exposure of infants, or otherwise, [del]a[/del]
great decrease in the death-rate among men [del]follows[/del] [ins]or[/ins]
prolongation of the child-bearing period among women
again effects a balance. If such are the facts, there is
here an action of final causation.
