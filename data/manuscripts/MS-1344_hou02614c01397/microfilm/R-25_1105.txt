But it is difficult to say wherein they agree. Sodium is distinctly [del]as[/del] [ins]a[/ins] monad; gold appears [ins]to be[/ins] a pentad. For this reason no "intelligent" natural classification of such objects are generally possible. Of sciences, on the contrary, an intelligent natural classification is perfectly possible.

In every such case, the first division is into Branches, each Branch having a distinctive governing idea, or purpose. Among the Sciences we recognize two Branches; the one of the [u]Theoretical Sciences[/u], whose final end is knowledge; the other of the [u]Practical Sciences[/u], or Arts, whose final end is the doing of something else.
