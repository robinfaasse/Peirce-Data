[u]Introduction[/u]
[u]Section 1. On the Classification of the Sciences.[/u]

I do not intend, as many have done, to draw up
a scheme of all possible sciences, nor, as others have
done, to confine myself to the science of the past; but
to classify the sciences that I find either living
today or apparently destined soon to have birth.
I do not speak of "science" in the sense of "syste-
matized knowledge," but in the sense of a mode
of life; namely, a life devoted to the search after
truth, in thorough singleness of purpose, [del]and[/del] by
the most enlightened methods of the day, and
in coöperation with [del]the[/del] [ins]other[/ins] students of related
problems.
