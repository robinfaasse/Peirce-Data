[u]Abstract of a Memoir "On the Logic of drawing History
from Ancient Documents, especially from Testimonies."[/u]

The basis of the procedure of historical critics today is
the balancing of likelihoods.

[del]Origin of this Method[/del] [ins]This method[/ins], [del]by[/del] [ins]but[/ins] first perhaps scientifically
formulated by Hume. It is defensible by the doctrine of
probabilities on certain conditions; but I maintain that
these conditions are too rarely fulfilled, even with the
roughest degree of approximation, to justify its being
erected into a general method of historical research.
