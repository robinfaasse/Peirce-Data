The divisions so produced ought to be called Sub-branches. Namely, theoretical sciences either have the discovery of truth as their end, or the systematic presentation of truth. Thus we have [del]S[/del] a sub-branch of [u]Heuretic Sciences[/u], and a sub-branch of [u]Systematic Science[/u]. Such books as Humboldt's [u]Cosmos[/u], Comte's [u]Philosophie Positive[/u], and Herbert Spencer's [u]Synthetic Philosophy[/u] belong to sub-branch of Systematic Science, of which I shall take no further notice.
