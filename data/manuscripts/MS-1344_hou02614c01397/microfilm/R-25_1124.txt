[u]Introduction.[/u]
[u]Section I. The Classification of the Sciences.[/u]

The intention is not, as many have done, to consider what sciences are possible, nor, as others have done, to consider merely what have been the sciences of the past, but to consider the sciences as they exist today and as they have definite prospects of development.

By "science," I do not mean "systematized knowledge," but I mean the search [del]for[/del] [ins]after[/ins] truth, [del]by[/del] in thorough singleness of purpose and by the most enlightened methods of day, conducted by social groups of men devoting their lives to the pursuit.
