distinguished from such an invariable sequence as that of night
upon day. For if by the cessation of the earth's rotation night
were prevented from following upon day, there would be no equi-
valent consequence whatever. In like manner, if anything had
prevented my throwing a stone into the well, I should have
done something else which would have answered [ins]quite[/ins] nearly as well.

In order to illustrate this take the following syllogism:

A people among whom the sexes are in equal numbers will keep up its population;
In this country the sexes are in equal numbers;
Hence, In this country the population is kept up.
