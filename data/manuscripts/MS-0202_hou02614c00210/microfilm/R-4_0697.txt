[u]Some Amusements of Mathematics[/u],

I desire to make two contributions to your [ins]"Mazes [del]curiosities[/del] of mathematics."[/ins] The first of them consists in some commonplace, [ins]yet pertinent,[/ins] remarks concerning a "curiosity" which has been noticed in your pages. The second [del]is[/del] [ins]contribution communicates[/ins] a fact which has surprised every person, including some very eminent mathematicians, to whom I have shown it, and will, I am sure, interest every intelligent reader who shall actually go through the operations I shall describe. But having described the surprising fact, I shall go on to explain it upon well-known and evident principles.
