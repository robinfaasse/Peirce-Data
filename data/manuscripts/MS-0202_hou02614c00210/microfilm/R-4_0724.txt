[u]Rule of Direct Division[/u]. To divide one number, N, by another, D, first find the greatest common divisor, G, of N and D, and [del]divide each by G, and use[/del] [ins]obtain[/ins] the quotients, N' = N/G, as a [del]new numerator[/del] [ins]dividend[/ins] instead of N, and D' = D/G, as a divisor instead of D. Next find the greatest common divisor, G', of D' and B (the base of numeration) and divide both D' and N' by this, using the quotient D'' = D'/G' as the divisor in place of D', and the quotient N'' = N'/G' (which cannot involve a circulating fraction) as the dividend in place of N'.
