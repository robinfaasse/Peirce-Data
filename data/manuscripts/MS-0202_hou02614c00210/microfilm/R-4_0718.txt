20[/pn] | But $10^6 = 1000000$ and $10^6 - 1 = 999999$. This must be divisible
by 7. And since, as nearly as numbers can express quantities
$1 \doteq 0.999999999999999999$ etc ad infinitum
it follows that since $\frac{1}{7} = 0.142857142857142857$ ad infinitum, the whole
number $142857$ is $\frac{1}{7} \times 999999$. To show that it must be so, but is,
in fact, so, I perform the division

[fig]Long division of 999999 by 7 showing remainders and quotient digits[/fig]
$7 \overline{) 999999}$
$\frac{7}{29}$
$\frac{28}{19}$
$\frac{14}{59}$
$\frac{56}{39}$
$\frac{35}{49}$
$\frac{49}{0}$

The reason of the peculiar property of this number is thus apparent.
