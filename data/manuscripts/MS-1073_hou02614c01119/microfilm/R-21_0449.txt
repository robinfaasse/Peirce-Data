[u]Determination of the relative length of
a wave of light and a metre bar.[/u] By
C. S. Peirce.

[u]Part I.[/u]

The operations here in described have been
undertaken in order to obtain a check upon
the spontaneous changes of length [ins]to[/ins] which bars
used as standards of length are known to
be subject.

sequel to the long labours of Mr. Rutherfurd
which have produced the finest ruling-machine
yet constructed, and most of it has been
done in his laboratory with the aid of his
trained assistant Mr. Chapman. In begin-
