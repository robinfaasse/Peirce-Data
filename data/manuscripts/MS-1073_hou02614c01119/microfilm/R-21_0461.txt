The diffraction gratings employed have been ruled upon
the machine of Mr. Rutherfurd. I here give a list of all
that are in my possession, although not all have as
yet been made use of.

Class 1. At each side of these gitters there is a
blank space and then three lines distant from one
another by 10 microns. By the width of the gitter is meant
the width from the middle line on one side to the
middle line on the other.

A. 227 lines per millimetre. Width, 1 cm. Ruled, [date]1879,
March 18[/date], at 68.3 F. Ruling began at 78 3/4 rev. of machine.
The lines of this grating are filled with black lead.

C. 227 lines per millimetre. Width, 2 cm. Ruled [date]1879,
March 21[/date], at 71.2 F. Ruling began at 53 3/4 rev. of machine.
The lines are filled with black lead.

B. 340 1/2 lines per millimetre. Width, 1 cm. Ruled, [date]1879,
March 20[/date], at 71.6 F. Ruling began at 61 3/4 rev. of machine.

D. 340 1/2 lines per millimetre. Width, 1 cm. Ruled, [date]1879,
at 70 F.[/date] Ruling began at 62 3/4 rev. of machine.

E. 340 1/2 lines per millimetre. Width, 2 cm. Ruled, [date]1879,
March 25[/date], at 65.5 F.

F. 681 lines per millimetre. Width, 1 cm. Ruled, [date]1879,
March 29[/date], at 69 F. Ruling began at 62 3/4 revolutions. The
lines are filled with black lead.

G. 681 lines per millimetre. Width, 1 cm. Ruled, [date]1879,
March 6[/date], at 70 F. Ruling began at 62 3/4 revolutions.
