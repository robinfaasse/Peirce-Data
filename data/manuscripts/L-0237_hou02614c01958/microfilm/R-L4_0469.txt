1507 Park Avenue.

My Dear Mr. Peirce,

I am glad
you like the
Symbolic Logic
very willingly, tho
I have of course
already done a
good deal of work
on it. I ought to
elapsed. I am
glad to have been
delivering in other
matters so much
more that I
mean to write a
book!!

Thanks for the
reference to Cantor. I
shall look up the passage.
I am making a collection
of such things.

Yours truly,
C. L. Franklin
