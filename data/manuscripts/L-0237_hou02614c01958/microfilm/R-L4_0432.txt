1507 Park Ave.,
Baltimore,
[date]Nov. 5, 1900[/date].

My dear Prof. Peirce,

It is very good
of you to let me
hear from you
so free — Proposition,
Syllogism, Symbolic
Logic. These will of
full treatment under
that head.

You will have noticed
from the proof-sheets
that many of the articles
are the joint-contribution
of two or more writers. I
should think there would
be easy room for your
views too under most of
Newcomb's topics. He
will make short work
if left to himself, I fear,
of philosophical groundings.
