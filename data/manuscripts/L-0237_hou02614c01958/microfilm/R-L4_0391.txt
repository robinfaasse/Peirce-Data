Given the name of a
mood, there is no artifi-
cial means of knowing
what figure it belongs to.
But such means might be
obtained by a slight change
in the mnemonic lines; for
instance, the letters r, t, l, n
might be taken to indicate
the first, second, third and
fourth figures respectively; as,
(r) Barbara, Cesare, Darii,
Ferioque prioris;
(t) Cesare, Camestres, Festivo,
Baroko secundae;
(l) Tertia Darapti, Disamis,
Datisi, Felapton,
(l) Bokardo, Feliso, habet;
quarta insuper addit
(n) Bamantip, Camenes, Dimanis,
Fesapo, Fresison.
(Cesare is an imperative of the second
conjugation.)
