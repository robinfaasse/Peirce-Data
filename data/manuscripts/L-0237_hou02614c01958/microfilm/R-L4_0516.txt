It is rather a
puzzle than it is worth
as one is leaving
the cradle of so
little of family behind
for so long a
time. But Mrs. Franklin
has a brother
sister-in-law, & niece
will take care of
my last journey,
dear Mrs. Sarah Franklin.

Hahnemann, Paris.
I began to write
you a long letter in
the summer, which
is in some sort of way
finished. I enclose
it now to her—But
there is of it. Please
return it sometime,
as some things in it
I have put in the
way in which I wish
to have them described.
