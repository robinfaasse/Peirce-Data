CSP to Mrs Franklin
About the notation of the algebra of dual relatives.

I consider it a part of the ethics of science to
insist upon the observance of such rules as are
generally requisite to the harmonious working together
of scientific men.

I, therefore, think that

A notation should not be changed without a serious
reason, — that the change [del]tends[/del] [ins]tends[/ins] to advance science.

If it has to be changed, it should be changed as little
as possible, and its first author should have a prior right
to [del]make[/del] [ins]determine what[/ins] the change shall be.

I accordingly hold that

The sign + should be exclusively employed for addition
strictly, following Boole.
