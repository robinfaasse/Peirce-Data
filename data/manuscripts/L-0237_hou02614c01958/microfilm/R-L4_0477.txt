My dear Mrs. Franklin:

I have read your article [u]Syllogism[/u], which is certainly very original. But it would be next to an imperti- nence for me to expatiate on its good points. My business is to carp about the smallest faults, as long as carping can do the least good; if need be, even to set down a [u]veto[/u].

It seems to me quite clear that what is wanted in such an article can be classed under the two heads of Historical Information and Logical Analysis, the for- mer being the more important. Under Historical Infor- mation, the first thing to be discussed is what is denoted by [u]syllogism[/u]. The first business of this Dictionary is to do something to settle terminology. Now where one man has a predilection for one use of a term, another for another, there can be but one way of producing an ultimate settlement, and that is on the basis of a prin- ciple.
