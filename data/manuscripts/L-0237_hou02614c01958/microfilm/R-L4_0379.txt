§ 3.

1. $(\prec) \overset{\times}{*} (\succ) = (=)$
2. $(\prec) \overset{\times}{*} (\overline{\succ}) = (\prec)$
3. $(\overline{\prec}) \overset{\times}{*} (\overline{\succ}) = (\asymp)$

1. (is as small as) $\overset{\times}{*}$ (is as large as) = (equals).
2. (is as small as) $\overset{\times}{*}$ (is not as large as) = (is less than).
3. (is not as small as) $\overset{\times}{*}$ (is not as large as) = (there is a part of each wh. is external to the other).
