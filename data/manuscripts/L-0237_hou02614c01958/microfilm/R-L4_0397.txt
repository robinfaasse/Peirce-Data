Why is not this,
$$S = MS,$$
a very good form in
which to write a proposition?
It utilizes the ease which
one has already acquired
in dealing with the sign
$=$, and it is freed from
all ambiguity. In other
words, it distributes $MS$, and
it says nothing, as it should
say nothing, about the
distribution (or the existence)
of [del]$MS$[/del], $\bar{S}M$, or $\bar{S}\bar{M}$. It has
simply the effect to include
$MS$ and to exclude $M\bar{S}$.
It gives the value of $S_{\bar{M}}$
but not that of $M_{\bar{S}}$.
