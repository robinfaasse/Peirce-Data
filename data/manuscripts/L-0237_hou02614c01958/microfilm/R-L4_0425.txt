Milford Pa [date]1891 Aug 29[/date]

My dear Mrs. Franklin:

If you are going to reply to Schröder's criticisms of me, as my friend, which I should be very glad of and very much indebted to you for, I had better explain myself further.

First let me say one thing I might forget at the end of my letter where it naturally would go. Namely, if Schröder's manner seems a little harsh toward me, that is more than excused by the manner in which I have neglected to write to him. He does not know, and nobody can begin to imagine, the difficulties under which I have labored. Into that subject I won't enter. How many times in going over the proofs of the Century Dictionary have I berated them for inserting ignorant, weak, and irredeemable stuff, and then found I had written it myself; and on examining to find why I had said what I did found that it was because I had pushed my inquiries further that I had later remembered!
