[u]Nota notae[/u] (I repeat that this is nothing but one more and

He has a triumphant air throughout, as if he were producing some-
thing highly original. He probably never had looked into Aristotle;
for Aristotle recognizes that the conversions involve new principles.
In my Classification of Arguments, I showed that the reduction of
the second figure always involves one principle, however it be
performed, and that of the third figure another. It is true that
when you consider the [u]meaning[/u], there is no difference between
No S is P and No P is S; but the[del]n[/del], that is just what you must
not do, if you want to analyze the [u]logic[/u]. You must confine
