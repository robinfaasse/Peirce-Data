Write $S_{\pm M}$ for Socrates [ins]taken[/ins]
with-respect to the quality-
manners or not-manners, &c.
Then the premises given are
$$S_{\pm M} = S \overset{+}{M}$$
$$M_{\pm P} = M \overset{+}{P}$$
$$\overline{\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad}$$
The $M$ in [ins]the[/ins] minor premise
might be taken with-respect-
to any other quality, $x$; but
the major premise asserts that
it is taken with-respect to $\pm P$, &
that when so taken it is of the form
$M \overset{+}{P}$. Then, by the principle
of identity, $M = M_{\pm x}$, we have
$$S = \overset{+}{P} \cdot \overset{+}{M} \cdot S,$$
or our Socrates is the-mortal-
man-Socrates.
