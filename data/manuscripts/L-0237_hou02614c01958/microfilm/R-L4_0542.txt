When two propositions are equivalent, I hold that a system of
formal logic which does not distinguish between them, may
fulfill the functions of formal logic perfectly. Indeed, it is rather
a blemish upon the system if it needlessly distinguishes equivalent
propositions. But there may be a good reason for doing so.
