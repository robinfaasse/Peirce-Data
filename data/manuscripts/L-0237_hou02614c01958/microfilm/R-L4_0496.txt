[u]Proposition[/u]

Why make such a fuss over the definition of proposition. I really don't know what justifies your saying that it is "usually" defined as a judgment expressed in words. Very strange must have been your line of reading in logic. On the contrary [del]a[/del] [ins]proposition[/ins] has usually been defined, after Aristotle, as that which is either true or false. A judgment is what [del]a[/del] proposition [u]means[/u], especially for those [del]who[/del] [ins]that maybe[/ins] think that the meaning of a sign lies in something [del]present[/del] [ins]at[/ins] the mind.
