intended to prove the necessity of a separate copula. It
seems to me, on the contrary, [u]to disprove[/u] that. For if you are,
as here, to regard the copula, no longer as the mark of

on the same principle a new copula would be required to
connect this copula with either extreme, and so on [u]ad in-[/u]
[u]finitum[/u]. This seems to be a [u]reductio ad absurdum[/u].
