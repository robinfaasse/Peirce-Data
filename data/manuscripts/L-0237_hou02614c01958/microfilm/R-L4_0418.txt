The human mind,
when in the universe
of Mathematics, has
reached a point where
it can easily conceive
of the inconceivable. Why
should it continue to
live within such narrow
bounds when in the
universe of Logic? In
Math. the term point no longer
connotes the idea of reality.
We have
Real points + imag. points = all points;
i.e., by point we mean some-
thing which is common to both
real and imag. points. So in Logic
we should say
Real universe + imag. universe
= [u]Universe[/u].
or
$0 + \infty =$ an infinity of a high-
er power.
