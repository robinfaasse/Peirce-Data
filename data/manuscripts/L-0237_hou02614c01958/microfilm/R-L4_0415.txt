If $A = \text{circle}$, $B = \text{square}$, then
"circular squares" is
the only combination
that is wanting. But
there is a certain sense
in which circular squares
may be said to be, —
namely, it is a possible
combination of the
two terms. For a better
example, put $A = \text{two}$
circles in the same
plane having no point
in common, and
$B = \text{their two points}$
of intersection.
