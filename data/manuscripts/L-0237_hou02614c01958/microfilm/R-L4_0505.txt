Does Sylvester use the phrase "principle
of parsimony" in any different sense from

In the proposition "architect-bankers are clever"
"architect-bankers" being a general term has
a definition and its meaning is discovered by
substituting the definition for the definitum. That is,
Whatever is at once an architect and a banker is clever.
In this point of view all that there is that is subject
in the meaning is "whatever is," and the proposition
not being intended to apply to the world of mathematical
possibility but only to the experiential world of the
age and country of the speaker, the whatever implies
or indicates existence. There is nothing in architect, banker
or clever which might not hold for fairy-land. This
