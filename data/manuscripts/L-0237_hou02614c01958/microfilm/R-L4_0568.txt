6 Prescott Hall Cambridge Mass
[date]1903 Dec 8[/date]

My dear Mrs. Franklin:

I send you one of a dozen copies that remain to me, after distribution to my audience, of the first pages of a Syllabus. It is of the nature of a proof sheet since I had no opportunity to see the proofs & it was written piecemeal & in great haste without keeping any copy or having any definite recollection of what I had written to the New York, some formal
