[pn]108[/pn] Monist But Newton will tell him that the change of [del]plane[/del] [ins]effect[/ins] is only a secondary effect. The primary [del]action[/del] is to change the velocity. Now velocity is a relation between positions at given instants. Consequently, the action consists in bringing about a certain relation between three positions, just as the function of a sign is to mediate between two other signs. In fact, if three positions of a particle at given instants are known, and the force is uniform the while, that force and all the other positions can be calculated. So [del]likewise[/del] [ins]they[/ins] can, if the force varies in a known way and we know how many complete orbits have been described in each of the two intervals. (Our mathematics may, of course, be too weak, and there may be more than one possible solution of the problem.) This is mess of the last degeneracy.
