The relations of the class $l$ are altogether
analogous to those of class $kl$. Write [del]$kl$[/del] [ins]$l \equiv kl$[/ins]

A [del]relation[/del] [ins]dyadic term or dyadic[/ins] is a dyad [del]which includes[/del] [ins]or aggregate of dyads.[/ins]

A dyadic aggregate which has every monad of the
ensemble once as antecedent and once as consequent
is a one-to-one correspondence.

A one to one correspondence must form a cycle
of all the monads or [ins]a number of[/ins] cycles of $m, n, p$ etc. monads
[del]respectively[/del]. In the former case, it is to be termed
a monocyclic dyadic. If $m=n=p=\text{etc.}$ its
is to be termed [del]an[/del] [ins]isocyclic[/ins] [del]monad[/del] [ins]dyadic[/ins].

Two dyadic aggregates which are composed of
like dyads similarly related are analogous
and we may write $l \equiv m$.

$\S 6$. An [u]aspect[/u] is a one to one correspondence

ensemble.
