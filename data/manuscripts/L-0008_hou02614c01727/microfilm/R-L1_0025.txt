3. I do speak of "the test of conceivability", and of several other tests
as well; but the bare statement that I thus 'make it something to be trust
to' is misleading; for I not only limit it to certain fields (p. 408), but
also say that it is only applicable if we accept certain presuppositions;
that if anyone supposes the perceptible relations of things to be mere
human ways of imagining them "then that person ought not to use conceiv-
ability as a test of what things in themselves can or cannot be." (p. 422).
