My dear Sir:

Enclosed you will find a letter which I [del]w[/del] wrote with the idea of sending it to 'The Nation'; but now that it is written I feel disposed to send it to you — at least to begin with — and see what you think about it.

It seems to me that your whole review was dominated by the idea that I tried to do something which I had no thought of doing — all because you misunderstood my use of the term 'objective'. This would account for your statement that I am influenced most by Venn's work of similar title.
