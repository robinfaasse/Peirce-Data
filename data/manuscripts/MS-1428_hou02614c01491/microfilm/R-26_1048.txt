[u]History of Ancient Philosophy[/u]. By W. Windelband. Authorized
Translation from the Second German Edition by Herbert Ernest
Cushman. Charles Scribner's Sons. [date]1899[/date]. 8[sup]vo[/sup], pp. 393.

A manual of the history of Greek philosophy in one volume
rather large than small, but not redoubtable in bulk, by giving
as much upon this subject as nine out of ten intellectual persons
care to read (unless it be Plato and parts of Aristotle with some
dippings into later writers) may, in view of the extent to which
it will be used, be a more important publication than if it
were larger. At any rate care is more incumbent upon the
reviewer, since many of its readers will be less able to judge
of its merits unaided. Here, then, is a judicious work, on
most points up to date, whose author does not plume himself
on brilliant theories that set all the evidence at defiance so
much as upon his just and sententious verdicts. On the
