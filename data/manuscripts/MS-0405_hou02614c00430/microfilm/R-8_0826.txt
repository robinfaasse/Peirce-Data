§17. A decapitated frog almost reasons. The habit
that is in his cerebellum serves as a major premise.
The [del]sensation[/del] [ins]excitation[/ins] of a drop of acid is his minor premise.
And his conclusion is the act of wiping it away. All
that is of any value in the operation of ratiocination
is there, except only one thing. What he lacks is the
power of preparatory meditation.
