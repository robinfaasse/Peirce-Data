or [space] $\frac{C}{r} = \frac{\cos\theta}{\tanh a} + \sqrt{\frac{\cos^2\theta}{\tanh^2 a} - 1}$

The two values resulting from the ambiguous sign of the square
root are reciprocals. Multiplying by $r$ and putting
$r\cos\theta = x$ [space] $r^2 = x^2 + y^2$
we get
[del]$(x - \frac{C}{\tanh a})^2 + y^2 = (C \operatorname{cosech} a)^2$[/del]

So that the equation is that of a circle. In order to determine
the value of $C$ we put $\theta = 0$, $r = l$, whence $C = [del]l[/del] [ins]l[/ins] (\tanh \frac{a}{2})^{\pm 1}$. This
gives for the circle either
$[x - \frac{1}{2}l(1 + \coth^2 \frac{a}{2})]^2 + y^2 = (\frac{l}{2\sinh^2 \frac{a}{2}})^2$
$[x - \frac{1}{2}l(1 + \tanh^2 \frac{a}{2})]^2 + y^2 = (\frac{l}{2\cosh^2 \frac{a}{2}})^2$
