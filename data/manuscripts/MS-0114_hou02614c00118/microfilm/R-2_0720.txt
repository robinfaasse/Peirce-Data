It will be noticed that, because
$(x + \sqrt{x^2 - 1})(x - \sqrt{x^2 - 1}) = 1$,
the two values of $\frac{C}{r}$ due to the ambiguous sign of the square root are reciprocals of one another. We may, therefore, equally write
$r = C \left( \frac{\cos \theta}{\tanh a} - \sqrt{\frac{\cos^2 \theta}{\tanh^2 a} - 1} \right)$

In order to determine $C$ we have $r = l$ for $\theta = 0$. or
$C = l \left( \frac{1}{\tanh a} + \sqrt{\frac{1}{\tanh^2 a} - 1} \right) = l \coth \frac{a}{2}$
