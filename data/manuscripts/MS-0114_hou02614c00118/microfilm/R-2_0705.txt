[u]On Hyperbolic Geometry.[/u]

I assume the particular form of this geometry in which the formulae of plane trigonometry become those of spherical trigonometry after substituting in the latter
for every line $a$
For $\sin a$ [ins]$-i \sinh a$[/ins]
For $\cos a$ [ins]$\cosh a$[/ins]

That is for right-angled triangles
$\cosh h = \cosh a . \cosh b = \cot A . \cot B$
[del]$\sin B = \frac{\cos A}{\cosh a}$[/del] $\cos A = \cosh a . \sin B$
$\sinh h = \frac{\sinh a}{\sin A}$ [del]$\cos B = \frac{\tanh a}{\tanh h}$[/del] $\sinh b = \frac{\tanh a}{\tan A}$
$\tanh h = \frac{\tanh a}{\cos B}$

For oblique triangles
$\frac{\sinh a}{\sin A} = \frac{\sinh b}{\sin B} = \frac{\sinh c}{\sin C}$
$\cosh c = \cosh a \cosh b - \sinh a \sinh b \cos C$
$\cos C = -\cos A \cos B + \sin A \sin B \cosh c$
$\sinh a . \coth b = \cot B . \sin C + \cosh a . \cos C$

We have Formulae of the functions
$\cosh^2 x = 1 + \sinh^2 x$
