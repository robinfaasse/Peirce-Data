there is no defence but that they agree with what we observe
in the consequences which can be drawn from them.

Observe, then, that the geometer does not pretend that
there is any positive evidence that space returns into itself;
he only says it simplifies matters to suppose it true, and
can conflict with no observations. It is an assumption
which can do no harm, which cannot be pronounced false,
and which, at any rate, is of the greatest service.

How it is useful, the student cannot understand, until
he sees what the nature of the difficulties of geometry are.
Suffice it to say, that it reduces these difficulties by about
one half.
