xiv. The [u]Law of Homogeneity[/u]: A thought will suggest another of the
same order, in preference to one of a different order.
This was borrowed from Fries.

xv. The [u]Law of Facility[/u]: A thought easier to suggest will be roused
in preference to a more difficult one.
The easier are
a. — Those more clearly and strongly impressed; more undistracted-
ly and attentively received; in youth, in the morning; joined with novelty,
wonder, passion, etc.
b. — Those more recent.
c. — Those more frequently repeated.
d. — Those more isolated from foreign thoughts.
e. — Those more connected with assisting thoughts.
f. — Those more interesting
1, on account of thinking talents,
2, on account of [del]his[/del] [ins]the[/ins] line of thought,
3, on account of the momentary preoccupation.
g. — Those more in harmony with affective dispositions.
1. natural,
2. habitual,
3. temporary.

Hamilton, like Brown, speaks of these, as characters of the suggested idea.
