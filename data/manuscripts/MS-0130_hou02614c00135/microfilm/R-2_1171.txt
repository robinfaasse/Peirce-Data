<u>Scholium</u>. The usage of mathematical language is to apply the term "angles" without an adjective to the internal angles, and the term "sides" to the exterior sides. But it is the interior sides which are really analogous to the internal angles. Thus, we shall find in ordinary plane geometry, [del]measuring with[/del] [ins]by[/ins] a rigid motion, that the sum of the internal angles is one circuit. Now, the sum of the interior sides is also one circuit, by that mode of measurement. [del]When the sum of the int. angles differs from one circuit[/del] [ins]When the sum of the interior sides differs from one circuit,[/ins] the difference measures the enclosure. When the sum of the exterior sides differs from one circuit, the difference measures the exclosure.
