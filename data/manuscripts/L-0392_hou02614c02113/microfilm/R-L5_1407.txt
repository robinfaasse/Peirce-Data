We have to distinguish in the universe of substantive logical possibility the
haecceities, the first intentions, and the second intentions. Let indices of the
Italic alphabet denote haecceities, those of the Greek alphabet first intentions,
and those that are accents and other such non alphabetic characters second
intentions. Then I say that no matter what the description of $m$, $\Sigma_i m_i$
is possible unless it be self-contradictory, so that $m \prec 0$ by logical rules. Hence,
$\Pi_i m_i$ is impossible unless by the rules of logic $1 \prec m$. So $\Sigma_i \Sigma_j l_{ij}$ is possible
under the same limitation. But what is possible for one object is equally possible
for every other. Hence $\Pi_i \Sigma_j l_{ij}$ is possible under the same conditions. Consequently
$\Sigma_i \Pi_j l_{ij}$ (since this is the negative of $\Pi_i \Sigma_j \bar{l}_{ij}$ with another $l$) is impossible.
In like manner $\Sigma_i \Sigma_j \Sigma_k g_{ijk}$ is possible unless self contradictory. Hence
$\Pi_i \Sigma_j \Sigma_k l_{ij}$ is possible under the same limitation.
