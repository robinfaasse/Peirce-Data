[marginalia]Radolfzell in Baden [date]31. Oct. 1898[/date].[/marginalia]

Dear Sir,

Longing to send you four recently published papers
of mine, several of which are costly and rather volu-
minous, I abstain from doing so because of my
uncertainty as to your actual address: my registered
letter of [date]March 2, 1898[/date] (dispatched March 23) having
never [del][illegible][/del] been acknowledged. May I ask, whether it has reached you?

If you are not opposed to receiving any personal
communications from this quarter, you ought
to confirm or renew to me your address, continuing
to keep me well informed as to your whereabouts,
some postcard sufficing.

[del]Though I am closer now than ever to the end of life (I have[/del]
[del]resigned my chair since Oct. 1, 1898),[/del]
[del]I remain[/del]
very truly yours Prof. Dr. E. Schröder
(Volkswohnung)
