The entire development of pragmatism is beyond my powers, even with such assistance as I might derive from others, if they were not too busy to smooth away rudimentary difficulties to pay much attention to those that occupy my mind. Under these circumstances, I try to concentrate my endeavour upon the points at which my work is likely to be relatively the most efficient.
