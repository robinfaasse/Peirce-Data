[u]Pythagorean[/u]

Suppose we can measure [del]objects[/del] in two [del]different[/del] attributes, whose units are $X$ & $Y$. Then the whole measure of a body will be
$$aX + bY.$$
Then $aX + bY$ [del]having been[/del] [ins]raised[/ins] to a certain power of such that $\frac{(aX+bY)^p}{X^{p-1}} = Y$. For $aX+bY$ [del]is an amount[/del] [ins]has a certain[/ins] proportion of $Y$ which $X$ has not (or less if $b$ is negative); if then we take a new object $a'X+b'Y$ [del]which is related[/del] whose ratio to $aX+bY$ is as that of the latter to $X$, this new object will contain a still greater proportion of $Y$ at least untill a maximum is reached.
