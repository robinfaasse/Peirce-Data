There is but one alio-concurrency, the relation of [u]incompossibility[/u]. There is but one anti-alioconcurrency, the relation of [u]identity[/u]. There is but one alioanticoncurrency, the relation of [u]otherness[/u]. There is but one antialioanticoncurrency, the relation of [u]coexistence[/u]. Classing these together as [u]logical relations[/u], we have on the other hand [u]real relations[/u] of five kinds, as follows. 1[ins]st[/ins], [u]alioopponencies[/u], and these are antiopponencies as well; 2[ins]d[/ins], [u]selfconcurrencies[/u], and these are anti[del]concurrencies[/del] [ins]selfrelations[/ins] as well; 3[ins]d[/ins], [u]antialioopponencies[/u], and these are antiopponencies as well; 4[ins]th[/ins] [u]selfanticoncurrencies[/u], and these are antiselfrelations as well; and [del]fifth[/del] 5[ins]th[/ins], [u]variform relations[/u], or those which are at once opponencies and antiopponencies, selfrelations and antiselfrelations.
