The Formal Classification of Relations.

1. Relations may be classified without regard
to the form of their elements

11. As to what they contain.

111. Whether an element or not. The latter class
embraces only the relation 0.

112. Whether [del]they[/del] [ins]a[/ins] pair of elements or not. A
relation embracing one element but not a pair
is individual.

113, etc. This system of classification may
be extended to sets of any number of elements.

1100. Whether an infinity of elements or not. The
names [u]infinite[/u] and [u]finite[/u] may be applied to these
classes

1100. Whether a continuum of elements or not. The
names [u]continuous[/u] and [u]discrete[/u] may be applied
to these classes. Continuous relations may be
classified according to the manifoldness of
the continuum.

12. As to what they exclude, the classifi-
cations being parallel to those under 11.
