[u]A True Statement of my Reading
in Philosophy.[/u]

I believe I have read and sufficiently
studied every important system of logic,
except the second edition of Sigwart.

I have paid little attention to the philosophy
of the beautiful, and don't care very much about
theology. [del]For[/del] I am most devoted to the theory
of knowledge, after that to cosmology. I have
considerable interest in ethics.

1. Have read all the fragments of early Greek
philosophers, the histories of Zeller, Röth,
Gladisch, and parts of other books. Also, such
things as [ins]translation of[/ins] Diogenes Laërtius, some books of Aris-
totle, a good deal of Plutarch, etc. Jamblichus'
life of Pythagoras.

2. Have read rather cursorily translation of Plato,
and Zeller and Grote and a good many things.
But I don't take any extraordinary interest
in Plato.

original. The whole Organon, the Metaphysics,
the Physics, and the de Anima, and parts of other
