[u]Non Euclidean Geometry[/u]

The design of these notes is to endeavor to
find some basic way of treating geometry in
its metric relations by introducing the
absolute synthetically.

My guide is Story (Am. J. Math. V. 180) but
solid non-euclidean geom. is a trifle too hard
for me. I prefer to restrict myself to the plane.

Here the only things to be considered are the
distances of two points, the angle of two lines, and the
distance of a point from the nearest point of a
line, which is the same as the angle between the
line & the nearest line [ins]through a[/ins] [del]on the[/del] point, according to
Dr. Story's luminous statement on p 180.
