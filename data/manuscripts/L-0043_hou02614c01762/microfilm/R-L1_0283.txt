Dear Sir;

The Chronoscope has arrived safely and is now being set up in its old place.

The name chronoscope as applied to instruments for measuring small intervals of time does not seem to be strictly appropriate. The word Chronomicrometer as you suggest would seem to be more correct, certainly, for scientific purposes.
