Active effort is prompted by an image in the inner world, and the effort consists precisely in metamorphizing this object into an object which reacts compulsively upon the agent. In the normal case, that which he at first imagines is transmuted into a similar object perceived. Thus, one takes hold of a chair (though it is only what passes in the mind that concerns us,) with an idea of what one proposes to do with it, which imagination is converted by the action into a perception that the effect is produced in the outer world.
