It is said that one ought to experiment without preconceived ideas. To do so would be to render experiment aimless. Besides, one cannot do it. Everybody has his metaphysics, and knows not how to think without it. He knows not precisely what it is that he virtually assumes. Our very language is soaked in metaphysics, without our being [ins]definitely[/ins] aware of it.
