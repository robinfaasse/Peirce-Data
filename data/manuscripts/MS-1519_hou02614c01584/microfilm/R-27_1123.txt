That we ought to experiment without preconceived ideas is one of those vague logical maxims which characterize the loose reasoner. An experiment has been well called the putting of a question to Nature, which she answers mostly by 'no' or 'yes'. To have no preconceived idea in experimentation is to take an interrogatory position without putting any definite question.
