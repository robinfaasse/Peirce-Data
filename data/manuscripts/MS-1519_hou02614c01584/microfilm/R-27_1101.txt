[u]I. Experience and Generalization[/u].
Experience is the only source of truth. It alone
[marginalia]Poincaré[/marginalia] first gives us new ideas: it alone can certify to them.
These two propositions are quite beyond dispute.
But that being the case, where does mathematics come
in? That is the question.
