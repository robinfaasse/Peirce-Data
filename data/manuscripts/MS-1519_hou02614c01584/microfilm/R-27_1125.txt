The principle of pragmatism is applicable here. In
reference to any particular investigation that we may have
in hand, we must hope that, if it is persistently followed out,
it may ultimately have some measure of success; for if it be
not so, nothing that we can do can avail, and we might as well
give over the inquiry altogether, and by the same reason stop
applying our understanding to anything. So a prisoner [del]makes[/del]
breaks through the ceiling of his cell, not knowing what his
chances of escape may be, but [ins]feeling sure[/ins] [del]knowing well[/del] there is no
other good purpose to which he can apply his energies.
