Hanover N.H.
[date]May 23 1872[/date]

Professor Peirce
Dear Sir
I presume the chipping of the prism may have been caused by some carelessness of my own in packing. Do not trouble yourself about it at all. I do not believe it will be best to cut down the prism on account of it, on account of loss of light, but I can easily gum a piece of black paper over the broken edge.

I should have written before, but your letter accidentally was mislaid.

Yours very truly
C. A. Young
