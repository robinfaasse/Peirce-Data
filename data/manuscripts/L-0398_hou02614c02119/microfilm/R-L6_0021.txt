NEW YORK, [date]March 10, 1898[/date]

My dear Peirce

Your letter [del]of[/del] which would have
been answered at once had I been able to answer
to any purpose) got pushed under a pile of
others on my desk & I have just got down
to it, it appears. I'm sorry to say I don't
know of any book to recommend for trans-
lation - We certainly have nothing on
the stocks or in prospect, and should not
care to go into anything like Ostwald's
chemistry of which you speak. Of course
I know your abilities in this as well as
other literary lines, but with! I don't
know of anything [del]to suggest[/del] for them
to exercise themselves on.

Sincerely yours
E. L. Burlingame
