June 15th, 1894.

Mr. C. S. Pierce,

Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:

In answer to your favor of the 7th, we wish to say that we have no objection to your using such quotations from our publications as you mention, providing they come within the limits given. We send you a copy of our last Text-book Catalogue, and if you will kindly mark such books as you would like to examine, we shall be pleased to send them to you, allowing you a special discount in view of the work which you are doing. If you wish to return the books after looking them over, you have the privilege of doing so at your own expense. We shall be pleased to give you any information that we can, and shall attend promptly to any requests which you may make.

Very truly yours,

CHARLES SCRIBNER'S SONS,
