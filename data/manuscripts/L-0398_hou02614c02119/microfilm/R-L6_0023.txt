CHARLES SCRIBNER'S SONS
PUBLISHERS
153-157 FIFTH AVENUE,
BETWEEN 21ST AND 22ND STREETS.

New-York, June 14, 1900.

My dear Professor Peirce,

Yours of May 21st. has
been duly received and pondered, and
should any occasion arise on which
we should be able to avail ourselves
of your services, we should be most
happy to invoke your aid — the
value of which I need hardly say
your letter was not necessary to
assure us of.

Very truly yours
W. C. Brownell.

Professor C. S. Peirce.
