My dear Mr. Peirce

I have just come back from
a month's absence, & hasten to
answer your note of a fortnight
ago. I am sorry - for I should be
personally interested in it - that
I cannot think it advisable to
take for this Magazine, with
just its needs & public, the
article on Swedenborg which
you suggest. I should be glad if
I could answer otherwise, for
I should like on many accounts
to accept such a proposal from
you; but I can come to no
other conclusion under the
circumstances.

Yours very truly
E. L. Burlingame
