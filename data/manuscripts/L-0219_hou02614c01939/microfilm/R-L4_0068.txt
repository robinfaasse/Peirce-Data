Mr. Charles S. Peirce finds
here a summary of "Lettered
Lines, their Universes, their in-
terrelations of the simpler sort,
a Calculus of the same."
He will see at a glance
whether he thinks it has
any value or any novelty.
It is more pedagogical than
otherwise characterizable.
The science of lettered lines
has as much and as little
to do with metaphysics, episte-
mology, psychology, linguistics
