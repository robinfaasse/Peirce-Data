The equation
$$(a \pm b) = (i = \bar{a} \pm b)$$
is thus established to the eye by the
equality of each member to the same
set of 8 universes, if $(i = \bar{a} \pm b)$ be
taken to mean any other universe
than $(i = \bar{a}b + \bar{a}b + ab)$.

Otherwise
$$(i = \bar{a} \pm b) \neq (a \pm b)$$
$$(i = b \pm c) \neq (b \pm c);$$
which seems inconsistent with the generali-
ty of meaning assigned to the letter symbols.
So $(i = \bar{a}b + \bar{a}b + ab) \neq (i = \bar{a} \pm b)$
