What mathematicians mean by a projection, therefore, is the perspective picture of the drawing-master with the addition of all the other points which are obtained by continuing the lines of sight [del]backward from the eye[/del] [ins]as well as forward[/ins]. It amounts in fact to your first standing with your face due East, making a perspective picture of what is in front of you in that position, and then without moving from your stand, facing about due West and making a perspective picture in that position. Then [del]if[/del] both pictures are cut off at their horizon lines and joined together, [ins]one right side up & the other upside down,[/ins] so that the point due East which appears only on one picture shall fall upon the position occupied by the point due W on the other, the point NE. on one on SW. on the other, and so on;— all opposite points of the horizon falling together.
