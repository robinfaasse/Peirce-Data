THE LIBRARY OF CONGRESS
Washington

January 22 1904

Dear Sir:-

Your letter of January 15th to the Librarian was duly received and, by his direction, referred to the Superintendent of the Reading Room for attention.

The Superintendent now reports that the book you desire is probably,- "De Candolle,- "Histoire des sciences et des savants. Genève, 1873," and the Librarian has taken pleasure in directing that it be sent to you as a loan for a period of two weeks. It will go to you by mail today.

The other works desired have not yet been named by Prof. Newcomb.

Very truly yours,

[ins]Allen R. Boyd[/ins]
Secretary

Prof. Charles S. Peirce
Milford Pa.
