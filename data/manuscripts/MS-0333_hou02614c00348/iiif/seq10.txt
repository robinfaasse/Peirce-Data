This is called the scientific method. Its fundamental
hypothesis stated in more familiar language is this.
There are real things, whose characters are entirely
independent of our opinions about them; These
realities affect our senses, according to regular
laws, and though our sensations are as different
as our relations to the objects, yet [ins]by[/ins] taking advan-
tage of the laws which subsist we can ascertain
by reasoning how the things really are, and any man
if he have sufficient experience and reason enough
about it, will be led to the one true conclusion. The
new conception here involved is that of reality. It may
