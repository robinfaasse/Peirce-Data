6 Prescott Hall, Cambridge, Mass
[date]1903 Dec 8[/date]

My dear Professor Jastrow:

I send by this mail a copy of the first pages of a very hastily composed Syllabus. It was written in a downright hurry & sent off in scraps without retaining a copy or remembering very well what had been sent before & I never saw a proof sheet.

Yours very faithfully
C S Peirce

I return to Milford Pa on Dec 18 where I should always be happy to hear from you, although of course there is no need of acknowledging receipt of this trifle.
