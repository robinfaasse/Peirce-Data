Madison, Wis.
Nov. 22 93.

Prof. C. S. Peirce
My dear Sir,
The second annual meeting of the American
Psychological Association takes place at Columbia
College, New York City, beginning December 27th. As
it is desired to present a printed program at the
opening session, I respectfully invite you to contribute
one or more papers to this meeting, and to submit
as soon as possible the titles and time necessary
for presentation.
Awaiting your reply, which should reach
me not later than December 1st, I am
Very truly yours
Joseph Jastrow
Secretary
