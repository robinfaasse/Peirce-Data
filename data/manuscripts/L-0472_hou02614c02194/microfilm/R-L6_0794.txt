Box 289 North Adams Mass
Station at Hoosac [date]1873 October 12th.[/date]

Prof Joseph Winlock
Director of Harvard Coll. Obsy.

Dear Sir
The following is the statement concerning my photometer work which you desired to have me send you. I am obliged to write by another hand as my own is hurt.

The object of the work is to determine the magnitudes of all the stars in Argelander's Uranometria situated between 40° and 50° of North declination. The design is to determine these magnitudes upon a scale of uniform ratios of light and so that the probable error of a single magnitude shall not exceed the tenth of a magnitude.
