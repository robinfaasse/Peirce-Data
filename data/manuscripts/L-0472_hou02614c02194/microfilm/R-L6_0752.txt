Cambridge [date]Aug 5 1872[/date]

Dear Sr:

The Observatory will pay for printing your table - but I should like to know what it is, how large, and if you want [del]ing[/del] [ins]y[/ins] it printed here.

I have not seen Eliot but I expect to.

I do not care to see your past correspondence with the President. What I meant in my conversation with you to-ther day that I thought it would be well that I should be informed when anything important was about to be said or done.

Yours very truly
Joseph Winlock

C. S. Peirce Esq.
