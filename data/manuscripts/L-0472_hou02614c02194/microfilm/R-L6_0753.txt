Dear Sir:

I have received yours
inclosing diagrams showing
monthly progress of your
work. I have not yet forwarded
your letter with the plates to
the President. And I shall
wait until I hear from you
again. I spoke to Mr. Eliot
in regard to your leave of absence
and arranged it satisfactorily.

work? If he did not I should
advise not sending it. If he approves
