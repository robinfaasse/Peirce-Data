Harvard College Observatory,
Cambridge, Massachusetts, [date]Feb 2 1875[/date]

My Dear Sir:

I recd Yours containing
Your proposal in regard to your work
for the Observatory, and also Your
two of a later date. From my
conversation with Mr. Eliot I
feel authorized to make any arrangement
with you that I choose within
the limit of the sum you name,
$1200, and so he answers you to
[del]est[/del]. My object will be to suit your
convenience as far as possible.

The most urgent necessity is
the publication of a volume as
soon as possible, after that there
would be no hurry in anything that
you may have in hand. I have
already in type about 120 pages
of Meridian Work besides the South
Stars, and I hope to complete
the Volume before the next meeting
