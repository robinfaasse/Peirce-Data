Washington [date]1872 Oct 12[/date]

Prof. Joseph Winlock
Director of the H. C. Observatory
Cambridge Mass

Dear Sir

At your desire, I make the usual report of work done [ins]by me[/ins] during the last twelve months. My work has consisted of Double Star work and Photometer work.

[u]I. Double Star work.[/u]

Soon after the last annual examination, I was obliged to go to Washington by my duties as a Coast Survey officer, and was kept there about six weeks. On my return, I resumed the [del]of[/del] regular observations of double stars and continued them until the arrival of the Zöllner's astrophotometer in the middle of February. Since then, they have been discontinued.
