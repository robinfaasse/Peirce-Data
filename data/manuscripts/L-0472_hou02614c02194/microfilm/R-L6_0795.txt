— $3^{\text{rd}}$ By using a heliometer with prisms upon the
object end so that any two stars however dis-
tant can be thrown into the field at once, and
then by having both halves of the heliometer
out of focus, so that both stars show disks
of light, [del]but[/del] and adjusting the distance of the
objective from the eye piece until the two
disks of light are equally bright, and ~
measuring the amount which the two
glasses are out of focus. This is the
method of Steinheil which was employed
by Seidel. It is exceedingly accurate but
it is not without important sources of
constant error and is inapplicable to stars
below the fourth magnitude.
