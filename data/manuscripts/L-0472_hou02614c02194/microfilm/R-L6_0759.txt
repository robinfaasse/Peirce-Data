[u]II. Photometer work[/u].

My photometric studies have been of four kinds
1. Studies of Uranography, 2. [ins]Preliminary[/ins] Determination of the pho-
tometric value of the scale of magnitudes, 3. Studies of
the instrument, 4. Measures of the light of stars.
