We must next calculate [u]$\vartheta''_m$[/u] and [u]$\Delta''_m$[/u] which are the coördinates
of the moon's [ins]centre[/ins], referred to the sun's centre, by the formulae.

$$\text{Tan } \vartheta''_m = \frac{e \sin E + \Delta' \sin \vartheta'_m - e_m \sin E_m}{e \cos E + \Delta' \cos \vartheta'_m - e_m \cos E_m}$$

$$\Delta''_m = (e \cos E + \Delta' \cos \vartheta'_m - e_m \cos E_m) \sec \vartheta''_m$$

Photographs obtained at different positions may next be com-
bined by the method of least squares, to get the parallax, etc.,
in a way which it is unnecessary here to describe.
