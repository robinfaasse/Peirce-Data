advantage after using the centre of the sun as the origin of
coördinates in measuring the sun's limb, to shift the origin
by a known amount in a known direction so as to bring it
to the centre of the moon before measuring the moon's limb.
This could be done by adding another graduated circle to the
micrometer, between the two micrometer screws. I shall
suppose, in what follows that the measures have been made
in this way. In the case of transits, it makes very little difference.
