Washington [date]1871 Dec 10[/date].

Prof. Joseph Winlock
Dear Sir

I beg leave to set forth a method of computing the parallax and other elements of future solar eclipses and Transits of Venus and Mercury, from photographs taken by your plan without an eye piece, whether with an equatorial telescope or with your stationary telescope.

In the first place, I would call attention to the following points, by attention to which in taking the photographs the computation will be greatly facilitated.

1. The pictures must have sharp edges which can be pointed on without much accidental variation. This is much more important than any delicacy in the photographs. Irradiation is of very little moment, so long as the planet (in the case of a Transit) is not obliterated.
