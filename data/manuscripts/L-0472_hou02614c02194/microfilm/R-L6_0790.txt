Harvard College Observatory,
Cambridge, Massachusetts, [date]Oct. 30 1872[/date].

Dear Sir:
I have waited until after my irritation before answering your letter of the 20th. I will put up the requisite building as soon as I can understand the plan clearly. I send a plan of the structures lately erected west of the Observatory for longitude and photography. Can yours be combined with these? Should not your round building be above the others, you cannot afford to lose the Southern horizon? Could it [del]not[/del] be set off at some distance and use a speaking tube for the recorder.
