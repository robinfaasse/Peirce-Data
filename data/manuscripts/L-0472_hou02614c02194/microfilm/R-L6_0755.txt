Observatory, Cambridge
[date]Sept. 24 1872[/date]

Dear Sir:

The Observatory will
pay for the necessary repairs
of the photometer.

Please send me a summary
account of your work for
the year for my annual report.
[del]I wish[/del] I wish to print it.
You may put into it any
interesting results that you
may have. And state the
general object and plan.
Also give the state of the
double star work.

Yours very truly
Joseph Winlock

C. S. Peirce, Esq.
