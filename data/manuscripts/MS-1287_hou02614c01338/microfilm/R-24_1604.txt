<u>Lecture I</u>.

The subject of this Course of Lectures is the History of Science from Copernicus to Newton. This period of a century and a half, from [date]1543 to 1686[/date], is deeply interesting from many points of view. The [del]fund[/del] elementary phenomena in most of the branches of pure physics were then [del]established[/del] discovered.
