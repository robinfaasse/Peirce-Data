through the whole history of human
knowledge, so that we may see what

what [del]ages[/del] states of science have been in
any measure within sight and hailing
distance of one another. In taking this
backward glance let us let our eyes
rest at each epoch [del]of a whole number[/del] [ins]dated by a multiple[/ins]
of [del]50[/del] [ins]500[/ins] years in our ordinary
reckoning. This will be convenient because
[del]a[/del] 500 years is about long enough to
