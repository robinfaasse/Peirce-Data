I shall say no more about the Pyramids
although it presents some problems in
engineering of some interest; but
shall skip at once over a thousand
years or more, in order to consider
the origin of the constellations.

It will be convenient to narrate this bit
of history backwards, beginning at our
own day and [del]passing[/del] [ins]moving[/ins] into the past. At
present, then, astronomers divide the whole
sphere of the heavens into 88 regions
called constellations, and bearing Latin
names.
