[pn]45[/pn] [u]Convention No. 3[/u]. The Graphist may, with the assent of the Interpreter, take the leaf and scribe upon any blank a graph agreed upon. But if he thereupon delivers the leaf to the Interpreter, this act shall constitute an assertion by him that something such as the graph describes has its being in the universe and the part of the universe indicated by the tincture; excepting only that if, after scribing the graph and before delivery of the same, he makes an incision through the sheet [ins]but wholly within one province,[/ins] so as to cut off the graph from the surrounding parts, this excision shall constitute a denial that there is, in the part of the universe indicated by the tincture, anything such as the graph describes.
