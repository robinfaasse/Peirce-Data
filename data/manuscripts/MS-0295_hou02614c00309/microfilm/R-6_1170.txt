41[/pn] [u]Convention N[sup]o[/sup]: 2.[/u] The Graphs are to be scribed on a leaf, to be called the Quasi-Mind, which is to be, at once, a Seme of the Truth, in the sense of the widest Universe of Reality, and a Pheme of all that is virtually taken for granted between Graphist and Interpreter from the outset of the discussion.
