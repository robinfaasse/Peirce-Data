The object of [del]these[/del] [ins]the following[/ins] distinctions is not to secure metaphysical accuracy, but only such a degree of accuracy as shall be needful in the inferences to be drawn; nor should they be insisted upon more strictly than such need is.

It will sometimes be desirable to distinguish Subdivisions of a Universe, different ways of Actuality, Possibility, and Finality.

When that happens we can avail ourselves of the different metals, colours, and furs. Besides, [u]Argent[/u] for the primary actualities, we can use [u]Or[/u], and different tints of it. Besides [u]Azure[/u] for the usual Possibility, there are [u]Gules[/u], [u]Sable[/u], [u]Vert[/u], and [u]Purpure[/u].

Besides [u]Ermine[/u] for the usual kind of Finality, there are [u]Vair[/u] and [u]Potent[/u].
