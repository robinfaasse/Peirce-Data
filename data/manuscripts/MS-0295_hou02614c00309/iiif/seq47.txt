[ured]48[/ured] Convention [ins]indesignato[/ins] № 4. A heavy dot on any area will denote an individual (i.e. an individual object) having the mode of being that the tincture and tint of that area indicates, and if scribed and delivered by the Graphist will amount to his assertion that such a being there is.
