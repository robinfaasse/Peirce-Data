It will sometimes be desirable to distinguish Subdivisions
of a Universe, different ways of Actuality, Possibility, and Finality.
When that happens we can avail ourselves of the different
metals, colours, and furs. Besides, [u]Argent[/u] for the primary
Actualities, we can use [u]Or[/u], and different tints of it. Besides [u]Azure[/u]
for the usual Possibility, there are [u]Gules[/u], [u]Sable[/u], [u]Vert[/u], and [u]Purpure[/u].
Besides [u]Ermine[/u] for the usual kind of Finality, there are [u]Vair[/u] and
[u]Potent[/u].
