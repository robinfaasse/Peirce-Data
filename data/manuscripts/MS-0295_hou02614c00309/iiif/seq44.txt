[ured]45[/ured] [u]Convention No. 3.[/u] The Graphist may, with the assent of the Interpreter, take the leaf and scribe upon any blank a graph agreed upon. But if he thereupon delivers the leaf to the Interpreter, this act shall constitute an assertion by him that [u]something[/u] such as the graph describes has its being in the universe and the part of the universe indicated by the tincture; excepting only that if, after scribing the graph and before delivery of the same, he makes an incision through the sheet, [ins]but wholly within one Province,[/ins] so as to cut off the graph from the surrounding parts, this [del]excision[/del] shall constitute a denial that there is, in the part of the universe indicated by the tincture, [u]anything[/u] such as the graph describes.
