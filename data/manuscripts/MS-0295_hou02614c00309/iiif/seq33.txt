41[/ured] Convention [u]No. 2.[/u] The graphs are to be represented as scribed on a leaf, to be called the [u]Quasi-Mind[/u], which is to be, at once, a Seme of The Truth, in the sense of the widest Universe of Reality, and a Pheme of all that is virtually taken for granted between Graphist and Interpreter from the outset of the discussion.

In the further description of this leaf, the [del]characters[/del] [ins]appearance[/ins] which it is to be represented as having, together with the significance of the same, shall first be described, and afterwards the manner in which this appearance is to be represented in practice.
