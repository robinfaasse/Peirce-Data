The next number is twenty. The last syllable, -ty, anciently -tig, means "tens," so that twenty is twain-tig, two-tens. In like manner, three tens is thirty, four tens forty, five tens fifty, six tens sixty, seven tens seventy, eight tens eighty, nine tens ninety. To form the name of a number between nineteen and ten tens, the rule is: first put the numbers of tens, using one of the words just given, and then the number of units, or ones, over and above the tens, connecting the two words by a hyphen.
