The Chaldeans, as Berossus says, had ten kings, which he calls by these strange names:

1, Alorus
2, Alaparus
3, Almelon
4, Ammenon
5, Amegalarus
6, Daonus
7, Euedoreschus
8, Amempsinus
9, Otiartes
10, Xisuthrus

These names were invented in Chaldea or Babylonia, or else in Arabia, at different ages.
They are words, which had some resemblance
to the names of those kings...
