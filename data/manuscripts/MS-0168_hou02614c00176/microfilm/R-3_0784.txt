After ninety-nine comes a hundred, which is therefore ten tens. Then the count proceeds: a
hundred and one, and so forth to a hundred and ninety-nine, and then continues two hundred,
two hundred and one, etc. to nine hundred and ninety-nine. The next numeral is a thousand,
which is ten [del]tens[/del] [ins]hundred[/ins]. After a thousand, comes a thousand and one, etc. to a thousand and
ninety-nine, and then comes a thousand and hundred, the word "and" only being used before numerals
less than a hundred. The count goes on, a thousand and one hundred and one, etc. to two thousand,
to three thousand, ... to ten thousand, to eleven thousand, ... to ninety-nine thousand, to a hundred
thousand, to a hundred and one thousand, up to nine-hundred-and-ninety-nine thousand nine hundred
and ninety-nine. The next numeral is a million, which is therefore a thousand thousand. We,
then, proceed, using "and" as before, to nine hundred and ninety-nine million nine hundred and
ninety-nine thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine.

After this, there are two different ways of counting, the English and the so-called French.
The English way, which is preferable, is to go on to a thousand million, a thousand million and
one, ... a thousand and one million, a thousand and one million and one, up to two thousand seven
hundred and sixty-five million four hundred thousand and three, ... ten thousand seven hundred and sixty-
five million four-thousand thousand two hundred and ten, ... two thousand seven hundred and sixty-
five million four hundred thousand three hundred and twenty-one and so on up to nine hundred
and ninety-nine thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine million nine hundred and ninety-nine
thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine. The next numeral is called a billion which is thus a
million million.

A million million is a billion;
a million billion is a trillion;
a million trillion is a quadrillion;
a million quadrillion is a quintillion;
a million quintillion is a sextillion;
a million sextillion is a septillion;
a million septillion is an octillion;
a million octillion is a nonillion;
a million nonillion is a decillion;

And so we proceed to undecillion, duodecillion, tredecillion, quattuordecillion, quindecillion,
sexdecillion, septendecillion, octodecillion, novemdecillion, vigintillion, unun-et-vigintillion, etc.

In the French system, on the other hand, a billion is a thousand millions, a trillion a thousand
billions, etc. But practically, billion, etc. are words little used.

In German we say one-and-twenty, two-and-twenty, [del]and[/del] [ins]as[/ins] we frequently call a thousand, ten hundred,
and count the hundreds up to twenty hundred, and more. Many numbers have special names. Those
which are not decimal will be noticed in another chapter.
