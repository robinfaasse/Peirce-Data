[u]— buys — from — for the price —[/u], is a relative

buyer of — from — for —,
bought by — from — for —,
seller of — to — for —,
paid by — to — for —.
So, [u]he gives — to —[/u], is another relative rhema,
giving the two relative terms,
given to —,
donee of —.
