Dear Sir,

The Nation mentions your edition of P. de Maricourt's epistle on the Lodestone - and I should like a copy. I offer in exchange a copy of my recently published "Bibliography of Chemistry 1492 - 1892" a work of 1200 pages 8vo. Or if you prefer my work on "Alchemy and Numismatics" -

Yours truly
H. Carrington Bolton
