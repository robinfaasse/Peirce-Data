[u]Abacus.[/u] I will number my remarks.

1. The derivation from A.B.C. is unfounded. Such guesses are almost always wrong. Besides, the Greek [u]ἄβαξ[/u] seems to have been pretty near the same thing; and the word is not like A.B.C. but sounds as of of Asiatic origin. [ins]or rather Indian or Bactrian or Babylonian.[/ins] A genius for arithmetic was un-Greek.

2. The logical abacus of Jevons was an aid to a special method of his invention which hardly any logician but him has approved, and therefore it hardly needed entry.
