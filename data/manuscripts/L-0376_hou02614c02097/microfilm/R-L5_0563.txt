COMMONWEALTH OF MASSACHUSETTS.

TOPOGRAPHICAL SURVEY COMMISSION,
OFFICE, 11 MT. VERNON STREET.

Boston, [date]October 11 1888[/date].

Dear Professor:

We write to Asa by this mail, asking him to give our boxes and trunk to the "Milford Freight" man, to be forwarded to us from Port Jervis. We did not know until a few days ago where we shall be located. I hope the things have not been in your way.

I will send you the $25.00 shortly. I could have sent it before, but I was very anxious to pay up a debt of long standing, as I told you before coming away; and I thought you would be willing for me to do so before I paid you.

Please give my regards, and Mrs. R's. also, to Mrs. Peirce.

Yours, very respectfully,
A. D. Risteen.
