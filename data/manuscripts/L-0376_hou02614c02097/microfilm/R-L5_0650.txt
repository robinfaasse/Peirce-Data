Dear Professor:

There has been a misunderstanding about the Century Dictionary, somewhere and somehow. I certainly am not competent to criticize any of your definitions, and I never had any thought of doing so. In acknowledging the receipt of Mr. Smith's check I casually asked if a supplementary volume is to be issued, and said that I had noticed a number of omissions [del]that[/del] and a few unfortunate definitions that could profitably be supplied or changed. I mentioned three or four words -- perhaps more. Among them were two (I think perhaps three) of Saturn's satellites, which were omitted. I supposed the general editors supplied these words. Certainly I had no idea, until I received your letter, that they were in your department, as you had so much to do in mathematics, weights and measures, and philosophy and logic, that it would seem impossible for you to do more.
