I almost forgot to call your attention to the fact
that in the Century Dictionary you state that the gallon
is equal to 3.7853 liters, and that the quart is equal
to 0.9468 liters. Now adopting the value of the gallon
as given in your recent letter, we have 1 gallon = 3.787064
liters, and 1 quart = 0.946766 liters. Preserving only
4 decimals, this gives 1 gallon = 3.7871 liters, and
1 quart = 0.9468 liters. Hence your C. D. "quart" appears
to be correct, but the C. D. "gallon" appears to be in error
by .0018 liter. If it had not been for the disagreement
between the C. D. "gallon" and "liter", I should have adopted
the C. D. "gallon" as my standard.
