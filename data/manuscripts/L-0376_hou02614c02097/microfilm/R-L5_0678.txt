The omission of Abbe's absolute measure of the parallax of
Sirius was due to my ignorance. Klinkerfues's paper
I have not seen. The fact is, Hartford is singularly
deficient in scientific literature, and I have to go out of
town to consult anything in the way of Transactions or
Proceedings of societies, or reports or periodicals of scientific
value. Some of these days I hope to have a good collection
of them myself, but they are forbidden fruits at present.
