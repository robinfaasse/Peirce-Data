When I send on the photos. — which I hope to do soon — I wish you would deduct from the cost of them what you paid Mr. Lundelius, for I feel that it was my fault that the camera was not returned Monday. I don't believe the pictures will all come out good, because the plates turned yellow after fixing and washing — presumably from the formation of silver hyposulphite. I have never had trouble of that sort with the plates that I use, but other members of the Camera Club here have had similar difficulties with Carbutt plates, and I think, therefore, that the trouble was in the emulsion. Lundelius would probably say I did not wash them long enough; I gave them about twice as long a fixing and twice as long a washing as I do the "Seed" plates I use. Have been trying to get the stain out of them, but have not entirely succeeded yet. Someone has recommended very dilute sulphuric acid, so I will try that next.

Sincerely yours,
A. D. Risteen.

Drawer 8. Hartford.
