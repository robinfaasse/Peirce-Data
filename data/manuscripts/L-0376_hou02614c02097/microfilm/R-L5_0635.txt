Inscribed and circumscribed triangle. A triangle whose angles
lie on one or more given curves, and whose sides touch
one or more others. [del]The inscribed and circum[/del] It is
a special case of the inscribed and circumscribed polygon.

Isosceles triangle. A triangle two of whose sides or angles are
equal.

Lemoin point. The point whose distances from the sides a, b, c,
of the triangle [del]A, B, C[/del] ABC are proportional to
a, b and c. Called among Germans the Grebe point.

Medial line of a triangle. A line joining any vertex to the
middle of the opposite side. There are three medial
lines in [del]each[/del] [ins]every[/ins] triangle, and they intersect at
the center of gravity of the triangle.

Nine point circle. See Feuerbach circle.
