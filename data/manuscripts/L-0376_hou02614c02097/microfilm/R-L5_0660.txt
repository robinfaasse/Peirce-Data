I wrote you, quite a long time ago, that I was at work
on a treatise on Thermodynamics. I am still at it, though
I don't get much time for it, now. I suppose it will
be another year before it is ready for the printer.

orbit of the binary star 70 Ophiuchi. Mädler thought the
motion of this star is not elliptical, and the existence of
an irregularity of some kind has been maintained by
many astronomers. My orbit fits the observed position
angles with wonderful accuracy, but there is a systematic
difference between the observed and computed distances. I
haven't published the orbit, because I haven't been able to think
