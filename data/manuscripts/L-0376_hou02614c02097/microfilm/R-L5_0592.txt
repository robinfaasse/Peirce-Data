[marginalia](6/11/91)[/marginalia]

Hartford, [date]June 11[/date].

Dear Professor:

I cannot find "tithonometer" in Sill. Jour. 1843. Is the reference not wrong? I send herewith "three points problem" and "thrust block", for your examination. "Torsion" is a longer word than either of us supposed, but it will be ready in a day or two. "Toucan" also. I have struck a whole raft of "transformations" that were not in your list. I suppose you would like to have them all included, and am going on that supposition. Can't imagine what an "affine" transformation is. Fortschritte says simply that a certain proposition is true, and says that somebody, by an "affine transformation" has indicated an extension of the proposition. Sounds as though it might mean "ingenious" or something of that kind, but so far as I know there is no such word in the German language.
