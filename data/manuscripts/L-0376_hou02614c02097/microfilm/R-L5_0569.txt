Dear Professor:

I append a list of subjects in the order that I thought it might be well to follow. No doubt this could be much improved, so please criticize it freely.

1. Counting. Copying scales. Measuring lengths, weights and volumes. Comparison of measures of the same thing obtained by different pupils. (All metric?) Simple questions in U.S. Money.

2. Notation. Whole numbers and decimals.

3. Numeration. do.

4. Addition. Examples in whole numbers, in U.S. money, then in decimals in general. "Casting out nines."

5. Subtraction. Same order as (4.) "Casting out nines."

6. Multiplication. "Casting out nines."

7. Division. "Casting out nines." Quick division. Circulating decimals.

8. Factoring. Least common multiple, greatest common divisor.
