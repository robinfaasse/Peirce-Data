[u]Lie's transformation[/u]. A transformation in which lines tangent
to one surface, $S$, correspond to spheres tangent to
another surface, $S'$. To all the lines tangent to $S$ at
a point $M$ correspond all the spheres tangent to $S'$
at the point $M'$.

[u]Linear transformation[/u]. A transformation in which the coördinates of
each point in one plane are [del]of[/del] linear functions of the
coördinates of the corresponding point in the second plane.
The transformation is birational. To a right line cor-
responds a right line; and, in general, to any curve
in one plane corresponds a curve of the same order in
the other plane. A node corresponds to a node, and a
cusp to a cusp. See also [u]linear[/u].

[u]Mechanical transformation of polygons[/u]. This transformation consists
in dividing two polygons of equal area in such a
manner that out of the parts of either the other can be
constructed.

[u]Line-point transformation[/u]. A transformation in which to a point
in the first plane there corresponds a line in the
second, and to a line in the first there corresponds a
point in the second. See [u]homographic transformation[/u] (1.)
