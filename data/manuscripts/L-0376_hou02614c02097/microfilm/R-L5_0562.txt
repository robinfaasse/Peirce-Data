It has often occurred to me
that a collection ought to be
made of those properties that
are common to all curves of
given kinds — say, closed curves
— and that perhaps the close
examination of such a set of
general propositions might lead
to others equally general, so
that after a time we should
have a [u]general geometry[/u] in
the truest sense. I do not know
enough about what has been
done to attempt this thing myself,
but I should like very much to
see someone undertake it.
