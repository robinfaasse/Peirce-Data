the coefficients of $\varphi(x)$ and of $f(x)$. It is also possible
to express the coefficients of the equation whose roots are
$\varphi(\alpha_1), \varphi(\alpha_2), \dots \varphi(\alpha_n)$ in terms of $S_1, S_2, \dots S_n$, and
therefore, finally, in terms of the coefficients of $\varphi(x)$
and $f(x)$. Hence the transformation is theoretically
