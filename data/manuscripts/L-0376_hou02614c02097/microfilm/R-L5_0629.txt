[marginalia](7/7/91)[/marginalia]

My dear Sir:

I have sent you the major part of the word "triangle", which has been delayed on account of illness for a few days. Tomorrow the rest of the word follows, together with a few notes concerning certain words and phrases connected with it.

Will you kindly send my account to the Century people? or shall I send it personally? I need the money, and have been counting on it to pay certain bills that I have contracted, and which are already overdue.

In stating the railway fare to [del]Sp[/del] Boston I find I omitted [ins]$.50[/ins] each way, giving the fare from Springfield to Boston in each case, instead of from Hartford to Boston.

Yours, respectfully,
A. S. Risten

Hartford, [date]July 7[/date].
