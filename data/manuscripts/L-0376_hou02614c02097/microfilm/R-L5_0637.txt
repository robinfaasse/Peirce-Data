[u]Rational Prime triangle[/u]. A triangle whose sides are expressible by [ins]whole[/ins] relatively prime [del]whole[/del] numbers, and whose area is rational.

[u]Right triangle[/u], or right angled triangle. A triangle one of whose angles is a right angle.

[u]Seven point circle of a triangle[/u]. See Brocard's circle.

[u]Similar triangles[/u]. Two or more plane triangles are similar when they can be so placed that their corresponding sides are parallel.

[u]Simpson's line[/u]. A line joining the feet of perpendiculars on the three sides, from any point of the circumscribed circle.

[u]Sine triple angle circle[/u]. On the sides BC, CA, AB of the triangle ABC take points D and D', E and E', F and F', in such a manner that $\angle AEF = A = \angle AF'E'$, $\angle BFD = B = \angle BD'F'$, $\angle CDE = C = \angle CE'D'$. The two triangles DEF, D'E'F' have the same circumscribed circle, which is the sine triple angle circle of the given triangle; and

DD' : EE' : FF' = $\sin 3A : \sin 3B : \sin 3C$.
