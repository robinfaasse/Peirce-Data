[u]Modulus of a linear transformation[/u]. See [u]modulus[/u].

[u]Neumann's transformation[/u]. A transformation of La Place's
equation in spherical harmonics, by which it is shown
that a solution of the equation may in certain cases be
obtained in the form of a series.

[u]Order or degree of a transformation[/u]. "When the points of a space
$S$ have a (1,1) correspondence with those of another
space $s$ in such a manner that the planes and the
right lines of $s$ correspond to surfaces $F$ of
$m$ th. order, and to curves $C$ of the $n$ th. order in
the former space $S$, I say that the transformation of
$s$ into $S$ is of the $m$ th. degree, and that the inverse
transformation (of $S$ into $s$) is of the $n$ th. degree." (Cremona.)
