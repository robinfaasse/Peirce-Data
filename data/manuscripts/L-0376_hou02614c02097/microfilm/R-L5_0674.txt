Prof. C. S. Peirce
Milford
Pike Co.
Penn.
U. S. A.

Mrs. Risteen and I have at
last gone on a trip to Europe.
Now at Zürich. We have been
in Hamburg, Leipzig, Berlin,
Köln, Düsseldorf, Mannheim,
Heidelberg, Frankfurt, Homburg,
& Munich. We expect to visit
Luzerne, Interlaken, Lausanne
(perhaps Geneva), Paris, London,
& Stratford. With best regards
to you both, from Mrs. Risteen
and myself,
A. D. R.
