[u]Triangle of forces[/u]. A triangle whose sides are parallel and proportional to three given forces in equilibrium, and whose sides, followed around in order, represent the directions in which the forces act.

[u]Triangle of osculation[/u]. A circle which touches a conic at a point P, also intersects it in a point Q; and conversely, for every point Q on the conic there are always three points, P, P', P", at which a circle passing through Q touches the conic. The lines joining P, P' and P" constitute the triangle of osculation.
