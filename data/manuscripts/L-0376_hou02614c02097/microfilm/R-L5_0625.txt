<u>Transformation of operations.</u> Let $S$ and $T$ represent two
operations, and let $S'$ be inverse to $S$, so that
$SS' = 1$. Consider the compound operation $STS' = T'$.
If $S$ and $T$ are not permutable, $T'$ is different from
$T$; and in this case $T'$ is said to proceed from $T$ by
transformation.

<u>Rational transformation.</u> A transformation in which to
each point, $P$, in the first plane there corresponds
but one point, $P'$, in the second. It does not follow
that to each point, $P'$, in the second plane there corres-
ponds but one point in the first plane. A transforma-
tion in which this holds true is <u>birational</u> (see
<u>birational transformation.</u>) The word rational is often
used in the same sense as [ins]the word[/ins] birational.
