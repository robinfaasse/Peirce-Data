Dear Professor —

I injured my eye
a little while ago, and have
been absent from the Coast Survey
Service for a while, on account
of it. Your letter therefore wan-
dered around somewhat before
reaching me. It was very
thoughtful of you to send me
the proposition, and I shall im-
prove the first opportunity I have to
look up the demonstration of it
in Crelle's Journal. It is cer-
tainly a very interesting fact.
