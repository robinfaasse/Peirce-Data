I wrote that def. of "Tarry point" about midnight,
and I see from what you send that my mind must
have been wandering at the time I began it.
The only error seems to be in starting off. The perpen-
diculars are not to the sides of the triangle, of course.
Here is the statement of the problem of determining
M and M'. "In der Ebene eines Dreiecks ABC
den Ort eines Punktes M so zu bestimmen, dass
die Lote zu den Geraden MB, MC, MA in den
Ecken sich in einem und demselben Punkte M'
schneiden". I think that will prove more
intelligible.

Respectfully,
A. D. Risteen.
