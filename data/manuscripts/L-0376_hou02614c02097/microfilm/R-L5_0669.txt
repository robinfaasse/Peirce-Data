R.M.S.P.
"TAGUS"
[date]Dec. 17. 1911.[/date]
At Sea, 270 miles
S.E. of New York.

Dear Professor Peirce:-

We expect to reach New York tomorrow (Tuesday) upon our return from Bermuda, and I shall have to proceed at once to Hartford, and find out what my new job is going to be like. I have been thinking a good deal about the porous plug experiment, while I have been gone, and I will certainly try to make the apparatus and carry the experiments through to a successful issue when these are regarded as systems that are known to us only by their integral behavior.

I certainly enjoyed myself very much indeed at Milford, and I hope you have not suffered from the late hours we kept.

With kindest regards to Mrs. Peirce,

Yours sincerely,
A. D. Risteen

P.O. Box 70,
Hartford
will be my address hereafter.
