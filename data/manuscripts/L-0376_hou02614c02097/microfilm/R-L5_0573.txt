Wanda Farm [date]1891 March 24[/date]

My dear Risteen:

I propose to see what the evidences of the curvature of space may be. Probably there is no argument on the subject not open to objection. Yet if they all tend one way, it will come to something. The [del]arguments[/del] [ins]methods[/ins] seem to me to be as follows:

[u]Class I. Statistical Methods.[/u]

Subclass 1. From proper motions alone.

1. Consider the [del]components[/del] [ins]components[/ins] of proper motions, athwart the path of the solar system. On the supposition of equable distribution of the stars, there will be a relation between the numbers of stars of different proper motions and the magnitudes of those motions; and this will depend on the constant of space. So, on the supposition that the stars are equably distributed within an infinite plane lamina. Have these proper motions any particular distribution relatively to the milky way?
