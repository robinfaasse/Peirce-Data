Torsional rigidity. The moment of torsion divided by the
twist per unit length.

Torsional spring. A spring whose reaction is caused by
torsional forces.

Torsion balance. (1) An instrument for measuring small
forces by comparing them with the moment of torsion
of a fiber of quartz, [del]glass,[/del] unspun silk, or
other suitable material. Most of the instruments
called torsion balances are not balances at all
in the usual sense of the word balance.
(2) A balance in which thin strips of spring
steel take the place of the knife-edges of the
ordinary balance; an instrument used in pharmacy.

Torsion head. The part of a torsion balance to which the
upper end of the test fiber is attached. It is
usually graduated so that a known amount of
twist can be given to the fiber.
