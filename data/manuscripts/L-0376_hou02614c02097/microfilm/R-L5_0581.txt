Hartford, Conn. [date]May 18 1891[/date]

Dear Professor:

On Saturday I answered the "Century"
company, saying that I thought some arrangement might
be made so that I could assist you, and asking them
for more definite information concerning the work.
This morning I received a telegram from you, advising
me to make a stiff price with them. I don't know
just what to call a stiff price. It means, I
suppose, a price that they would hesitate a little
about paying, but which they would pay, ultimately.
Now since I have never had any dealings with
them, I don't know where this line of indecision would
come. Can you not advise me a little more spe-
cifically?
