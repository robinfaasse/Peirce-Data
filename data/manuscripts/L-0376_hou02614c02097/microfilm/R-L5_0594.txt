Boncompagni's Bulletins is not in the New Haven library.
I have not succeeded in finding Fontenelli's theorem, in
Comment. Bonon IV. 1757. , nor Lehmus's theorem in Element.
Geometry (no reference), nor Brasseur's fundamental theorem
(Mem. Belg. xxix), nor Duhamel's theorem relating to the
convergence of series (Zeuthen (5) III.) I believe that
is all that I have not sent you.

In the biographical line I have learned nothing further
except that the mathematician Rodrigues was certainly
Benjamin-Olinde Rodrigues, born 1794, "gestorben" Dec. 17.
1851. I think the theorem was published about 1820,
but cannot recall my authority for the statement.

I found a large amount of material on "transformation"
and "triangle" in Boston yesterday.

Did not get your telegram till this morning. Then I
telegraphed to New Haven to make perfectly sure that the
Bulletins is not there. Then I sent word to you.

Yours, respectfully,
A. D. Risteen
