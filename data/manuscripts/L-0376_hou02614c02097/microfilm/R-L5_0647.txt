My dear Risteen

The radius of a sphere being r, the constant of space k
its surface is proportional to $(\text{Sinh } \frac{r}{2k})^2$
its volume to $(\text{Sinh } \frac{r}{k} - \frac{r}{k})$.

Supposing that [del]the[/del] [ins]the[/ins] mean distance of a star
of magnitude 5.55 is equal to $2k$, and that in a
certain region of the heavens there are $1391 \frac{3}{4}$ stars
as bright or brighter than 5.25 mag, the following
should be the numbers of different magnitudes
