[u]Symmedian line[/u] of a triangle. The line which is situated
symmetrically to the medial line, with respect to the
bisector issuing from the same vertex.

[u]Symmedian point[/u] of a triangle. A point whose distances
from the sides of the triangle are proportional to
the sides of the triangle. It may also be defined
as the intersection of the lines joining the middle
point of each altitude with the middle point of
the side to which the altitude is perpendicular.
Called also [del]the[/del] Lemoine point, and Grebe point.
