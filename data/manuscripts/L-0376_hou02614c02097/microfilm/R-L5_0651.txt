and I find that you did have the department of general
astronomy also. I regret that I said anything about it.
After the many kindnesses you have done me, I should
feel guilty of treason, almost, to say or do anything
that might reflect upon you in any way. Besides,
I have a very great favor to ask of you, some day,
and even if there were no other reason, it would be
suicidal to take any such action. (I am not ready to
ask it yet, so please be patient!)

The words that I had in mind are general words
— not mathematical at all. I am sure I can find
200 or more of these that are not in the Dictionary;
but as I have no copy of it — Mr. Smith did not
think it [del]does[/del] advisable to give me one, you remember —
I cannot say, now, what they are. I should
like to undertake to find them. How many such
words would be worth a copy of the dictionary to him?

Yours, respectfully,
A. D. Risteen.
