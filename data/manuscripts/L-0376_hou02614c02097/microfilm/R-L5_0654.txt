Hartford, Conn., [date]Feb. 24 1892[/date]

Dear Professor:

Some time ago you wrote me concerning
a scheme you had in mind, the details of which you
said you would write about the first of the year.
I noticed in Science that you have resigned from the
C. & G. S., and I infer from that that you are about to
take up something new. Please let me know about
it as soon as you can, if it is anything you might
want me for, because in Hartford one has to take
a lease of the house he lives in. Our lease expires
on May 1[ins]st[/ins], and we have to give about six weeks
notice so that the owner may have an opportunity to
re-rent in case the tenant elects not to stay.

I like my present place very much, except in some
particulars that I mentioned to you when in Milford.
There is not a very good outlook for advancement
— that is, for advancement in a direction such as I
should like. There is also a sort of religious
