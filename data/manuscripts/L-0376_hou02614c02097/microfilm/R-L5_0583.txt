[date]June 6. 1891.[/date]

Dear Professor:

Your note is at hand. Will look up the subject of "trees" on Monday next.

I have not had much luck with the biographical matters.

[u]Lancret[/u]. The only one of this name that I find is Nicholas Lancret, a French painter. Born 1690. Died 1743.

[u]Giovanni Ceva[/u]. Cannot find him. I find Tommaso Ceva, an Italian mathematician. Born Dec. 20, 1648. Died Feb. 3, 1737.

[u]Earnshaw[/u]. I find only a Thomas Earnshaw, who was born Feb. 4, 1749 and died Mar. 1, 1829. Considering the nature of his theorem, I should say that this man was too early. Perhaps not. There is nothing, however, to make me think he is the one required.

[u]Dr. Graves[/u]. (Theorem in 1841.) A Dr. Robert James Graves was born 1800, died 1853. I see no reason for supposing he is the man, except his title (Dr.) and his age at the time the theorem was published. There was also an R. P. Graves who published a life of Hamilton in 1882.
