Dear professor:

I would suggest that the definition of "rational transformation" (see the enclosed proof) be modified as indicated in the copy send you yesterday and today. Möbius's "circular" transformation I could not find. In the original publication it was printed by title only, and wherever I find it referred to they are very "careful not to give any particulars. "Uniform transformation" I cannot quite make out. Here is what I have been able to find: Uniform transformation of elliptic curves into themselves — "Diese Transformationen sind von zweierlei Art. Sind die Coordinaten der Punkte der Curve elliptische Functionen des Parameters [u]u[/u], dessen Perioden [del]w[/del] [ins]ω₁[/ins] und ω₂ [del]sind[/del] sein mögen, so werden diese Transformationen durch die Relationen [u]u[/u]₁ = ±[u]u[/u] + C (mod. ω₁, ω₂) gegeben, worin C eine Constante ist und man das Zeichen - nehmen muss, wenn die Trans. erster Art, dagegen +, wenn sie von der zweiten ist." Perhaps you will understand this passage better than I can.
