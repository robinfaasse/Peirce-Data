[u]Cayleyan transformation[/u]. The Hessian transformation of the
conic $(a, b, c, f, g, h)(x_1, x_2, x_3)^2$ is
$(m^2 a + 2mf)x_1^2 + (m^2 b + 2mg)x_2^2 + (m^2 c + 2mh)x_3^2$
$- (a + 2m^2 f)x_2 x_3 - (b + 2m^2 g)x_3 x_1 - (c + 2m^2 h)x_1 x_2 = 0$
If the proposed conic is the product of two lines $\lambda_x, \mu_x$,
the discriminant of the Hessian transformation of this product
is obtained by substituting in the general discriminant
of the foregoing Hessian, for the coefficients $a, b, c,$ etc.,
of the conic, the products $\lambda_1 \mu_1, \lambda_2 \mu_2, \lambda_3 \mu_3,$ etc. In
