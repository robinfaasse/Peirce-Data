[u]Triangle.[/u] In geom., a figure having three sides and three angles.

[u]Altitude of a triangle.[/u] The perpendicular distance from any apex to the opposite side.

[u]Annex triangle.[/u] A triangle constructed from a given one, ABC, in the following manner: Through B draw the straight line which, with respect to AB, is symmetrical to BC; and through C draw the straight line which, with respect to AC, is symmetrical to BC. The two lines so drawn intersect at A', and A'BC is an annex triangle to ABC. There are three annex triangles to every given one, one on each side. The centers of the circles inscribed in the annex triangles lie on the circle circumscribed about ABC.

[u]Associated points.[/u] If [math]\alpha, \beta, \gamma[/math] are the homogeneous coördinates of a point O, the points [math]O_a, O_b, O_c[/math] are said to be associated to O, if their coördinates are expressible [del]by[/del] by [math]-\alpha, \beta, \gamma[/math]; [math]\alpha, -\beta, \gamma[/math]; [math]\alpha, \beta, -\gamma[/math], respectively.

[u]Bisector of a triangle.[/u] A line bisecting an angle of the triangle, either internally or externally.
