Hartford, Conn. [date]June 29 1891[/date]

Dear Professor:

Enclosed please find a statement of the time I have spent on the dictionary work. I am very much in need of the money, so if you will kindly give the matter your earliest attention you will relieve me of some embarrassment.

I have made out the account in full, and in the railroad fares I have included parlor car travel, which I presume is legitimate; or does the extra fee come out of the $5. a day? I have forgotten the fare from New York to Port Jervis. Will you kindly introduce this item? You will notice that the fare from Boston to Hartford is not the same as that from Hartford to Boston. $x$ is not numerically equal to $-x$. This is a fact, and is often spoken of by travelers. The Albany road charges to the nearest cent, while the N. Y., N. H. and H. R. R. charges to the nearest five cents. There is also a charge for time on June 30 and July 1.
