Again, many of the stars may be faint on account of
small intrinsic brilliancy, and undoubtedly are so. The point
I was coming at was, that if there is such a group of
stars, [ins]as I have supposed,[/ins] near the most distant point of space, we might reason-
ably expect to see the group in both directions in the heavens;
though it does not follow that there would be any very
close correspondence in general appearance in the two images.
A particularly bright patch in one direction might correspond
to a bright one or to a faint one in the opposite direction,
according as the brightness in the original direction was due, on
the whole, to greater [ins]intrinsic[/ins] brilliancy or to lesser distance. Now
