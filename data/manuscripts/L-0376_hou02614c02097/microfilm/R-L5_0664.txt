UNIVERSITY OF PENNSYLVANIA
DEPARTMENT OF PHYSICS

3909 Locust St.
Philadelphia Jan. 28. 1903

My dear Professor Peirce:-
I write to ask if you
remember a Mr. A. D. Risteen who
says he was associated with
you several years ago in your
pendulum experiments in the
U. S. Coast & Geodetic Survey. If
so can you give me any informa-
tion concerning his scientific
training and his ability as an
investigator. Should you for example
be willing to recommend him
as an instructor or an assistant
in a Research Physical Laboratory?
And from what you know of him
should you be inclined to place
confidence in his work of scientific
investigation? I shall be obliged
for your opinion on these matters.
Yours truly George F. Barker.
