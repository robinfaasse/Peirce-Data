Point-point transformation. A transformation in which to each point of either locus there corresponds a single point of the other locus.

Polar transformation. A transformation in which the polar coördinates of [del]ea[/del] $\rho, \theta$, of each point of one locus are connected with those of the corresponding point of the other locus by the equations $\theta_1 = m \theta$ , $\rho_1 = k \rho^m$. In this transformation the angle of intersection of [del]two[/del] [ins]any[/ins] curves is not changed.

Quadratic [ins]^[del]or[/del]^[/ins] transformation. A transformation in which a quadratic function connects the coördinates of corresponding points; a transformation in which the points $x$ in one plane, corresponding to lines $v$ in the other that pass through a fixed point $y$, lie on a conic; a transformation that is birational, and in which to a line in either plane corresponds a conic in the other.
