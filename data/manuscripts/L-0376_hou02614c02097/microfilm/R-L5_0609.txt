There is one other matter. The Century Company may
think that I couldn't have done much work on [date]June 5th.[/date]
in Boston, if I went and came on the same day.
I was conscientious about it, however, and took
the 2:10 a.m. train from here, reaching here
again, next morning, at 3:10 a.m. And so in
other similar cases.

The work has not been so profitable for me
as I had hoped, for I presume I shall have to
lose the time I was absent from this long-name
company's office. Still it has been a distinct
gain to me, and it was pleasant work.

Please attend to the matter as soon as you can.
I do not want to put you to any trouble, but I need
the money dreadfully, just at present.

Respectfully yours,
A. D. Risteen.
