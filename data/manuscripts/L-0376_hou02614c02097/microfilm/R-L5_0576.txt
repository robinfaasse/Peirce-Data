In the kind of space outlined above I cannot see why a
star should be magnified or blurry when looked at through
a greater distance than $D$. For, imagine a pencil of rays
emanating from the star. These cannot intersect until they
have each passed around space. Hence not more than one
of them can enter the eye of an observer on the earth. Hence
the star will always appear as a point. As I understand
it, you have in mind a space that has a double curvature,
the radii lying in opposite directions. Is that correct? And
do you consider the product of the greatest and least radii to
be constant for all points? My notions of these things
are pretty hazy, and if you will kindly tell me where
I can find something that will clear them up, I shall be
grateful to you, for I think the subject is one of absorbing
interest.
