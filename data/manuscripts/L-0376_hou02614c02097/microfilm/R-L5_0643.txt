<u>Tucker's triplicate ratio circle</u>. Through the symmedian point of a triangle draw lines parallel to the sides. The six points in which these lines intersect the sides lie on a circle, which is the triplicate ratio circle; and the intercepted chords are in the triplicate ratio of the sides.

<u>Escribed circle of a triangle</u>. A circle touching one of the sides of the triangle and the other two sides produced. Every triangle has three escribed circles, the centers of which are at the respective intersections of the external bisectors.

<u>Inscribed circle of a triangle</u>. [del]The circle[/del] A circle lying wholly within a triangle, and touching its three sides.

<u>Circumscribed circle of a triangle</u>. The circle passing through the three vertices of the triangle.

<u>Scalene triangle</u>. A triangle which is neither right angled nor isosceles. An equilateral or isosceles triangle is oblique but not scalene. Compare [del]Oblique[/del] [ins]<u>Oblique</u>[/ins] <u>triangle</u>.
