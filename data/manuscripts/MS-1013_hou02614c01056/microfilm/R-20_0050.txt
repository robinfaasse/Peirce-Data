Now for a few words as to the proper
method of going about this business.

We know that birds can fly, and fish can do
much the same thing; and we have reason
for confident belief that man can fly, if he
is animated with [del]effectual[/del] [ins]sufficient[/ins] desire to do so,
sufficient to cause his setting about it as a
great undertaking. If flying is possible at all
there is reason to believe it can be done on a large
scale more easily than on a small scale; and if
it can be done on a large scale, it will probably
be a matter of commercial importance.
