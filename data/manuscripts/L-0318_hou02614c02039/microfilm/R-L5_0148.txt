To the Editor of the New York Herald,

Sir:

I do not remember that public attention has ever been called to the fact that, according to the Census of 1900, three of our cities are [ins]or then were,[/ins] so nearly equal in importance, that a single death in the most populous of the three, combined with a single birth in the second in order, would reverse the relative ranks of these cities; while the third, [del]was not then so much in arrear that it might[/del] [ins]by this time[/ins] [del]now[/del] [del]may[/del] have attained a secure supremacy over both its rivals. These three cities are

Coronado City, Kansas;
Surf City, New Jersey;
and Rainy Lake City, Minnesota.

Whether you maintain correspondents in these cities or not, I do not know; but I should like to be informed [ins]the scale of[/ins] whether or not their relative positions in population have since undergone any change, and if so, how.

A Constant Reader.

Coronado City is in Wichita County, Ka.
Surf City is in Ocean County, N.J.
Rainy Lake City is in Itasca County, Minn.
