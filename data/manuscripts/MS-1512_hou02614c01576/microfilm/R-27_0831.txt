[del]In fact, that there are different kinds of
air had been well-known since Van Helmont.[/del]
Thus, Boyle, [date]1678 April 1[/date], notes "that common
air doth corrupt bodies; yet it doth so much less,
if it be mixed with factitious air."

As for any general sensation, there is not an
indication of it in Walpole's letters, although he
refers to the Cavendishes over 70 times.
