CLARK UNIVERSITY,
WORCESTER, MASS.

[date]March 5th 1895[/date]

My dear Mr Peirce:

About three years ago
at the time of the death of
Professor Peck of Columbia
you were so kind as to
write a letter on my
behalf for President Low.
There proved to be no
vacancy at the time; and
I was informed of this
before I got your letter;

talk with you about; and I hope I shall
be more fortunate next time.

Yours very truly
Henry Taber
