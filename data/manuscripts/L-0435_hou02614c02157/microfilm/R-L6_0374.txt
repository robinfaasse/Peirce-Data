[org]Clark University,
Worcester, Mass.[/org]

[date]March 13, 1895[/date]

My dear Mr. Peirce, I received
your letter yesterday evening
and I have forwarded
it to President Low.
It is a source of great
gratification to me that
you feel able to commend
my work so highly and
I am very much indebted to you
for your kindness in writing
on my behalf.

Yours very sincerely,
Henry Taber
