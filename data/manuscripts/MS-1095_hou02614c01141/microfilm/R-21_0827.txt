If horizontal forces $f_1$ and $f_2$ be applied to the two knife-edges, the displacements produced will be
$$\begin{array}{l}
s_1 = f_1 p_1 + f_2 q_2 \\
s_2 = f_2 p_2 + f_1 q_1.
\end{array}$$
These give
$$\begin{array}{l}
f_1 = \frac{1}{p_1 p_2 - q_1 q_2} (p_2 s_1 - q_2 s_2) \\
f_2 = \frac{1}{p_1 p_2 - q_1 q_2} (p_1 s_2 - q_1 s_1).
\end{array}$$
The work done will be
$$s_1 f_1 + s_2 f_2 = \frac{1}{p_1 p_2 - q_1 q_2} (p_2 s_1^2 - (q_1 + q_2) s_1 s_2 + p_1 s_2^2).$$
