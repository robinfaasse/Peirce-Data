[u]Correction to the Standard Atmosphere.[/u]

The station numbers have [del]been[/del] [ins]been[/ins] reduced to the stand-
ard atmosphere, [del]of[/del] having a pressure of one million
C.G.S. units and a temperature of 15° C. The effect
of humidity has been neglected; and, in fact, no obser-
