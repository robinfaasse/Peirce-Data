In order to express the Kinetic energy, we must first find the distance of the axis of the joint [ins](which we may denote by $r + \delta r$)[/ins] from the centre of rotation of the second piece. This is given by the equation
$$(r + \delta r)(\theta_0 + \delta \theta) = r \theta_0$$
or
$$\delta r \cdot \theta_0 + r \cdot \delta \theta = 0,$$
or
$$\delta r = - r \frac{\delta \theta}{\theta_0} = r \omega.$$

The Kinetic energy is thus seen to be
$$E = \frac{1}{2} m_0 (h_0^2 + \gamma_0^2) (D_t \theta_0)^2 + \frac{1}{2} m \{(r + r \omega + h)^2 + \gamma^2\} (D_t \theta)^2$$
$$= \frac{1}{2} m_0 (h_0^2 + \gamma_0^2) (D_t \theta_0)^2 + \frac{1}{2} m \{(r + h)^2 + \gamma^2 [del]+[/del] [ins]+ 2[/ins] [del]\gamma^2[/del] [ins]h[/ins] (r + h) \omega\} (D_t \theta_0)^2.$$
