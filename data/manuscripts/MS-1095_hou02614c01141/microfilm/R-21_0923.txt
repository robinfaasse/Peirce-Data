If the differential equations were formed, or were
made linear by the omission of terms of higher
degrees, and were then resolved, the motion
would be seen to consist of two harmonic
components, the amplitudes of which are
arbitrary constants. It is, therefore, possible
for one of these to vanish; and we may as-
sume such an equation between $\theta$ and $\theta_0$
as to bring about this result. This equation
must express the condition that the Kinetic
potency shall be a minimum. It is, therefore,
$$gmh \sin \theta + \varepsilon \delta\theta = 0,$$
or
$$\delta\theta = - \frac{gmh}{\varepsilon} \sin \theta.$$
