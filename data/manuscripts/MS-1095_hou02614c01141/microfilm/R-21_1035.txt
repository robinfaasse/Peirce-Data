Effect of Flexure of Piece carrying Knife

Suppose weight of yard pendulum produces
flexure increasing $h$ by $x$

Then length measured is $h_d + h_u + x$ or
if we consider that in each case $h+x$ is
the real distance is $h_d + h_u - x$

Then the length of the seconds pendulum found
instead of being $\frac{h_d + h_u}{T^2}$ as it ought to be
is $\frac{h_d + h_u - x}{T^2}$ so that the error is $-\frac{x}{T^2}$

In the case of the metre pendulum $x$ is $1.06$
greater that is is $1.06 x$ and since $T^2_{\text{metre}} = T^2_{\text{yd}} + 1.09$
the error for the metre will be about $.97$ of that
of the yard so that the ratio instead of being
unity will be
$$\frac{\lambda - .97 \xi}{\lambda - \xi} = 1 + .03 \frac{\xi}{\lambda}$$
