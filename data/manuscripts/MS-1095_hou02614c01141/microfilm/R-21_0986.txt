[u]Notes on the process of determining
gravity by the reversible pendulum.[/u]

I arrange what I have to say under
the heads of the different dynamical
problems involved.

[u]Problem I.[/u] A rigid pendulum makes in-
finitesimal oscillations in vacuo about a
fixed horizontal axis.

Let $M$ be the mass of the pendulum;
$h$, the distance of the centre of
mass from the axis;
$\gamma$, the radius of gyration about a
parallel axis through the centre of mass;
$g$, the acceleration of gravity;
$t$, the time;
$T$, the period of oscillation;
$\phi$, the instantaneous angular
displacement from the position of
equilibrium;
$\partial$, the ratio of the circumference
to the diameter;
