$$D_x p = -\rho D_t u$$
$$D_y p = -\rho D_t v$$

$$D_x D_y p = -\rho [del]D_x[/del] D_y D_t u$$
$$= -\rho D_x D_t v$$

$$D_x u + D_y v = 0$$
$$D_x D_t u + D_y^2 v = 0$$
$$D_x D_y D_t u + D_y^2 D_t v = 0$$
$$D_x D_y D_t u = D_x^2 D_t v$$
$$(D_x^2 + D_y^2) D_t v = 0$$
$$D_t v = F_1'(x+iy) + F_2'(x-iy)$$
$$D_y p = -\rho F_1'(x+iy) - \rho F_2'(x-iy)$$
$$p = i \rho \int F_1'(x+iy) d(x+iy) - i \rho F_2'(x-iy) d(x-iy)$$
$$D_x p = i \rho F_1'(x+iy) - i \rho F_2'(x-iy)$$
$$D_t u = -i F_1'(x+iy) + i F_2'(x-iy)$$
$$D_x D_t u = -i F_1''(x+iy) + i F_2''(x-iy)$$
$$D_y D_t v = i F_1''(x+iy) - i F_2''(x-iy)$$

$$y=0 \quad x=1 \quad D_t u = -i F_1' 1 + i F_2' 1$$
when $y=0 \quad v=0 \quad F_1 x + F_2 x = 0 \quad F_1 = -F_2$
$$v = \phi(x+iy) - \phi(x-iy) + f_2$$
$$u = -i \phi(x+iy) - i \phi(x-iy) + f_1$$
$$= -2i \phi 1 + f_1 = D_t \xi$$
