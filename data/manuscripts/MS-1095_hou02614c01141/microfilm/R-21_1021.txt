$$dx = dl \sin \theta \quad dy = dl \cos \theta$$
$$x = \int dl \sin \theta \quad y = \int_{0}^{l} dl \cos \theta$$
$$D_{t}x = \int dl \cos \theta \cdot D_{t}\theta \quad D_{t}y = - \int dl \sin \theta \cdot D_{t}\theta$$
