This is evidently a quadratic equation with unequal roots
and we shall therefore have the two equations

$$D_t^2 (\mathcal{M}_1 s_1 + s_2) = -\mathcal{N}_1 (\mathcal{M}_1 s_1 + s_2)$$
$$D_t^2 (\mathcal{M}_2 s_1 + s_2) = -\mathcal{N}_2 (\mathcal{M}_2 s_1 + s_2)$$

The integrals of these equations will be

$$\mathcal{M}_1 s_1 + s_2 = \mathcal{A}_1 \sin (\sqrt{\mathcal{N}_1} t + \epsilon_1)$$
$$\mathcal{M}_2 s_1 + s_2 = \mathcal{A}_2 \sin (\sqrt{\mathcal{N}_2} t + \epsilon_2)$$

and therefore there will be two linear compounds of the
positions of the two pendulums which will each vibrate as
a simple pendulum.

On actually solving the equations for $\mathcal{M}$ and $\mathcal{N}$ we find

$$\mathcal{N} = \frac{G_1 + G_2}{2} \pm \frac{1}{2} \sqrt{(G_1 - G_2)^2 + 4 a^2}$$

Then we shall have,
