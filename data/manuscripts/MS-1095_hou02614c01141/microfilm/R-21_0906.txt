Let $h_1, h_2, h_3$, etc be the distances of the
centres of mass from the upper joints. Let
$\gamma_1, \gamma_2, \gamma_3$, etc be the radii of gyration about
[del]the[/del] axes through the centres of mass and paral-
lel to the axis of $z$. Let $m_1, m_2, m_3$, etc. be
their masses. Let $\theta_1, \theta_2, \theta_3$, etc. be the angu-
lar displacements of the pieces about their cen-
tres. Let [u]$p$[/u] be the number of pieces; and
let [u]$t$[/u] be the time. Then, for any piece,
say the $n^{\text{th}}$, we have

$$x_n = \sum_1^{n-1}{}_m\ l_m \sin \theta_m + h_n \sin \theta_n$$
$$y_n = \sum_1^{n-1}{}_m\ l_m \cos \theta_m + h_n \cos \theta_n$$

$$\text{D}_t x_n = \sum_1^{n-1}{}_m (l_m \cos \theta_m . \text{D}_t \theta_m) + h_n \cos \theta_n . \text{D}_t \theta_n$$
$$\text{D}_t y_n = -\sum_1^{n-1}{}_m (l_m \sin \theta_m . \text{D}_t \theta_m) - h_n \sin \theta_n . \text{D}_t \theta_n$$

$$(\text{D}_t x_n)^2 = \sum_1^{n-1}{}_i \sum_1^{n-1}{}_j (l_i l_j \cos \theta_i \cos \theta_j . \text{D}_t \theta_i . \text{D}_t \theta_j)$$
$$+ 2 \sum_1^{n-1}{}_m (l_m [del]h_n[/del] [ins]h_n[/ins] \cos \theta_m \cos \theta_n . \text{D}_t \theta_m . \text{D}_t \theta_n) + h_n^2 (\cos \theta_n)^2 (\text{D}_t \theta_n)^2$$

$$(\text{D}_t y_n)^2 = \sum_1^{n-1}{}_i \sum_1^{n-1}{}_j (l_i l_j \sin \theta_i \sin \theta_j . \text{D}_t \theta_i . \text{D}_t \theta_j)$$
$$+ 2 \sum_1^{n-1}{}_m (l_m h_n \sin \theta_m [del]\cos[/del] [ins]\sin[/ins] \theta_n . \text{D}_t \theta_m . \text{D}_t \theta_n) + h_n^2 (\sin \theta_n)^2 (\text{D}_t \theta_n)^2$$
