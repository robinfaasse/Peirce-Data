$$\frac{d}{dx} = P \frac{d}{dr} + Q \frac{d}{d\theta}$$

$$P \sin (\theta - \theta_1) + Q r \cos (\theta - \theta_1) = 1$$

$$P \cos (\theta - \theta_1) - Q r \sin (\theta - \theta_1) = 0$$

$$Q r = \cos (\theta - \theta_1)$$

$$P = \sin (\theta - \theta_1)$$
