The mere supposition of $\chi$ satisfies this.

Are the hydrodynamic equations satisfied.
This requires
$$(D_x^2 + D_y^2)^2 \chi = 0$$

$$D_x^2 \chi = D_x (D_x \chi) + D_x^2 y' (D_{y'} \chi) + D_x y' D_x (D_{y'} \chi)$$

Now $D_x = (D_x) + D_x y' (D_{y'})$
$$D_x^2 = \left\{ (D_x) + D_x y' (D_{y'}) \right\} \left\{ (D_x) + D_x y' (D_{y'}) \right\}$$

$$= (D_x^2) + (D_x) D_x y' \cdot (D_{y'}) + 2 D_x y' (D_x D_{y'})$$
$$+ D_x y' \cdot (D_{y'}) D_x y' \cdot (D_{y'}) + (D_x y')^2 (D_{y'}^2)$$
