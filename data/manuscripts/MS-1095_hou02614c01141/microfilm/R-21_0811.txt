[u]On the Mutual Influence of Two Pendulums.[/u]
By C. S. Peirce.

It is important to know whether two pendulums can be set up near to one another without danger of influencing one another. I therefore work out the analysis, supposing that each is on a stand which can move in the direction of the oscillation, and is elastic. The two stands also have an elastic connection.

$g$, the acceleration of gravity,
$\varepsilon_1$, $\varepsilon_2$, the elasticity of the supports
$\varepsilon_3$, that of their connection
