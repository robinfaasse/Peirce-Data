In these experiments, there was no feeling
with the knife-edges, but the planes
were leveled up [ins]with[/ins] a spirit-level of which

The plane was first made so nearly
level that the correction would be in-
appreciable, and at the end of the
swings with each pendulum the level
was re-read & the apparatus again
leveled up. At the beginning of the
swings with Pend. No 4, we find the
forward end high by [del]1[/del] [ins]1.0[/ins] divisions.
At the end of these 4 swings, the for-
ward end was low by [del]1[/del] [ins]1.1[/ins] division.
