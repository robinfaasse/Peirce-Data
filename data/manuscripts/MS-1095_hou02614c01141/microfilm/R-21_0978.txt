$p \sin (\mathfrak{c}_1 + q) = - p' \sin (\mathfrak{c}_2 + q')$

$p \sin (\mathfrak{c}_1 - q) = - p' \sin (\mathfrak{c}_2 - q')$

$\frac{\tan \mathfrak{c}_1 + \tan q}{\tan \mathfrak{c}_1 - \tan q} = \frac{\tan \mathfrak{c}_2 + \tan q'}{\tan \mathfrak{c}_2 - \tan q'}$
