[u]Description of Gravitation and Astronomical Station[/u],
Ebensburg, in Ebensburg, Cambria, Co, Penn.

Gravity determined by Bessel Rev. Pend swinging on
knife edges in the air.

Latitude determined by sextant obs'ns of Sun, Jupiter, &
Polaris.

Longitude by telegraphic exchanges with Allegheny
obsy.

Elevation by levels to Railway station.

The observations were made in the house and grounds
of Mrs. Frances S. McDonald, on Centre Street. The place
is shown on county map by Beers [date]1867[/date], where the house
has marked under it J. M. Donald. It is at the [del]SE[/del] [ins]next[/ins]
corner of the street, south of Highland Street.

cellar of the house — N and — or at the Tran-
