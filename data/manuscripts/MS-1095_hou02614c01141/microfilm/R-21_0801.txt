[del]The[/del] Bessel's reversible pendulum has no slider but $h_d$ and $h_u$, the distances of the centre of mass from the two knife-edges are measured and the formula [del]is[/del] for the [del]period[/del] square of the period of the seconds' pendulum equal in length to the distance between the knife-edges is

$$\frac{h_d u - h_u v}{h_d - h_u} = \frac{u+v}{2} + \frac{h_d+h_u}{h_d-h_u} (v-u) .$$

The advantage of the [del]for[/del] last formula over the last but one is obvious. Besides this advantage, Bessel's reversible pendulum has another even more important, namely that its external figure is perfectly symmetrical in the two positions, in consequence of which the atmospheric effect is completely eliminated.
