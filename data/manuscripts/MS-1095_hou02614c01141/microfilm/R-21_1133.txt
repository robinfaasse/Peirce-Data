$(\gamma'')^{2}$, the squared radius of gyration of the
enclosed air about an axis through its
centre of mass parallel to the knife-
edge $= (\gamma_{0}'')^{2} - (h_{0}'')^{2}$.

$h_{d}''$, the distance of the centre of enclosed
air from the knife-edge functioning
with heavy end down $= \frac{1}{2}(h_{d} + h_{u}) - h_{0}''$.

$(\gamma_{d}'')^{2}$, the squared radius of gyration of the
enclosed air about the same edge $= \gamma''^{2} + h_{d}''^{2}$.

$h_{u}''$, the distance of the centre of enclosed
air from the knife-edge functioning
with heavy end up $= \frac{1}{2}(h_{d} + h_{u}) + h_{0}''$.

$(\gamma_{u}'')^{2}$, the squared radius of gyration of the
enclosed air about the same edge $= \gamma''^{2} + (h_{u}'')^{2}$.

$(\gamma''')^{2}$, the squared radius of gyration of the
displaced and enclosed air about an
axis through the centre of volume of the
pendulum parallel to the knife-edge.
$= J_{0}''' / V'''$

$(\gamma_{d}''')^{2}$, the squared radius of gyration of the
displaced and enclosed air about either
knife-edge $= (\gamma_{u}''')^{2} = (\gamma''')^{2} + \frac{1}{4}(h_{d} + h_{u})^{2}$.
