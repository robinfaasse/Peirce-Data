In order [del]to[/del] [ins]to[/ins] express the Kinetic energy, we
must first find the distance of the axis of the
joint from the centre of rotation of the second
piece, which distance we may denote by $r + \delta r$.
This is given by the equation
$$(r + \delta r) (\theta_0 + \delta \theta) = r \theta_0$$
or
$$\delta r . \theta_0 + r . \delta \theta = 0$$
or
$$\delta r = - r \frac{\delta \theta}{\theta_0} = r \omega.$$

The Kinetic energy is thus seen to be
$$T = \frac{1}{2} m_0 (h_0^2 + \gamma_0^2) (D_t \theta_0)^2 + \frac{1}{2} m \left\{ (r + r \omega + h)^2 + \gamma^2 \right\} (D_t \theta)^2$$
$$= \frac{1}{2} m_0 (h_0^2 + \gamma_0^2) (D_t \theta_0)^2 + \frac{1}{2} m \left\{ (r + h)^2 + \gamma^2 - 2 (h r + h^2 + \gamma^2) \omega \right\} (D_t \theta_0)^2$$
