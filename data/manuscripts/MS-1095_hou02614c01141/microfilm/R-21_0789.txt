[u]End of Swing.[/u]

I On entering the room first light the lamps and place them in position.
II Read internal thermometers Right first then Left, and external thermometer.
III Read pressure and attached thermometer.
IV Observe coincidence.
V Read arc Right and Left.

[u]Beginning of Swing.[/u]

I Start Pendulum with arc of about [ins]in[/ins] 1.1 or [ins]in[/ins] .55 on each side as soon as possible after observing last coincidence having first pumped out if necessary.
II Observe the first coincidence.
III Read arc Right and Left.
IV Read internal thermometers.
V Read pressure and attached thermometer.
VI Read air barometer and attached thermometer.
VII Compare time piece.
VIII Complete record and put out lights (exit)
