$J_d''$, the moment of inertia of air (supposed
of unit density) displaced by the whole pendu-
lum about the knife-edge functioning
with heavy end down $= V''(\gamma_d'')^2$.

$J_u''$, the same for heavy end up $= V''(\gamma_u'')^2$.

$J_d'''$, the moment of inertia of air (supposed
of unit density) displaced and enclosed by
the whole pendulum about either knife-
edge $= J_u''' = V'''(\gamma_d''')^2$.

$\mu$, the viscosity of air at $0^\circ\text{C}$.

$\mu_\tau$, the same at temperature $\tau$.

$D_\tau'$, the density of common air at tempe-
rature $\tau$ and one absolute atmosphere
of pressure.

$\nu_\tau$, the kinematical viscosity $= \mu_\tau / D_\tau'$

[ins]T, the actual period in mean seconds[/ins]

$k-1$, coefficient of viscosity effect on period
$= (2\sqrt{2}/r^{in})\sqrt{\nu T/\pi}$

$k'$, the coefficient of viscosity effect on the
arc $= (k-1) + 1/4(k-1)^2$.

$I_d$, the moment of inertia of the pendu-
lum about the knife-edge bearing
with heavy end down $= M(q^2 + h_d^2)$.

$I_u$, the same for heavy end up $= M(q^2 + h_u^2)$
