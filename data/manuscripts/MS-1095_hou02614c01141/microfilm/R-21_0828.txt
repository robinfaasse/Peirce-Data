$$- M_1 g_1 h_1 \cos \varphi_1 - M_2 g_2 h_2 \cos \varphi_2 + \frac{p_2 s_1^2 - (q_1 + q_2) s_1 s_2 + p_1 s_2^2}{p_1 p_2 - q_1 q_2}$$

The actual energy, as I have shown in my first paper on the flexure of a pendulum support, will be

$$\frac{1}{2} M_1 l_1 h_1 (D_t \varphi_1)^2 + M_1 h_1 \cos \varphi_1 (D_t \varphi_1) (D_t s_1) + \frac{1}{2} M_1 (D_t s_1)^2$$
$$+ \frac{1}{2} M_2 l_2 h_2 (D_t \varphi_2)^2 + M_2 h_2 \cos \varphi_2 (D_t \varphi_2) (D_t s_2) + \frac{1}{2} M_2 (D_t s_2)^2.$$

The canonical equations of Lagrange are then as follows:

$$l_1 D_t^2 \varphi_1 + \cos \varphi_1 D_t^2 s_1 + \sin \varphi_1 D_t \varphi_1 D_t s_1 = - g_1 \sin \varphi_1$$
$$l_2 D_t^2 \varphi_2 + \cos \varphi_2 D_t^2 s_2 + \sin \varphi_2 D_t \varphi_2 D_t s_2 = - g_2 \sin \varphi_2$$
$$M_1 h_1 \cos \varphi_1 D_t^2 \varphi_1 - M_1 h_1 \sin \varphi_1 (D_t \varphi_1)^2 + M_1 D_t^2 s_1 = - \frac{-2 p_2 s_1 + (q_1 + q_2) s_2}{p_1 p_2 - q_1 q_2}$$
$$M_2 h_2 \cos \varphi_2 D_t^2 \varphi_2 - M_2 h_2 \sin \varphi_2 (D_t \varphi_2)^2 + M_2 D_t^2 s_2 = - \frac{-2 p_1 s_2 + (q_1 + q_2) s_1}{p_1 p_2 - q_1 q_2}$$

$$l_2 D_t^2 \varphi_2 + D_t^2 s_2 = - g_2 \varphi_2$$
$$M_1 h_1 D_t^2 \varphi_1 + M_1 D_t^2 s_1 = - \frac{-2 p_2 s_1 + (q_1 + q_2) s_2}{p_1 p_2 - q_1 q_2}$$
$$M_2 h_2 D_t^2 \varphi_2 + M_2 D_t^2 s_2 = - \frac{-2 p_1 s_2 + (q_1 + q_2) s_1}{p_1 p_2 - q_1 q_2}$$
