It will therefore be more convenient to make $A$, $B$, and $C$, all positive. For this purpose, $\psi$ will be made less than $\frac{1}{2}\theta$ and $\omega$ still smaller. $\frac{A}{B}$ will be much smaller than $\frac{B}{C}$.
