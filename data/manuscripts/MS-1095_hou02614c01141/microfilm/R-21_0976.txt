Then we have,

$$D_t^2 s_1 = - G_1 s_1 + a \frac{m_2 \lambda_2}{m_1 \lambda_1} s_2$$

$$D_t^2 s_2 = - G_2 s_2 + a \frac{m_1 \lambda_1}{m_2 \lambda_2} s_1$$

Imagine the first equation multiplied by such a constant $\mathcal{M}$
that when they are afterwards added they give

$$D_t^2 (\mathcal{M} s_1 + s_2) = \mathcal{N} (\mathcal{M} s_1 + s_2)$$

where $\mathcal{N}$ is another constant.

To determine these constants we should have the equations

[del]$$- \mathcal{N} (\mathcal{M} s_1 + s_2) = - G_1 \mathcal{M} s_1 + a \frac{m_2 \lambda_2}{m_1 \lambda_1} s_2 -$$[/del]

$$\mathcal{N} (\mathcal{M} s_1 + s_2) = - G_1 \mathcal{M} s_1 + a \frac{m_2 \lambda_2}{m_1 \lambda_1} \mathcal{M} s_2$$
$$- G_2 s_2 + a \frac{m_1 \lambda_1}{m_2 \lambda_2} s_1$$

This resolves itself into these two

$$\mathcal{N} \mathcal{M} = - G_1 \mathcal{M} + a \frac{m_1 \lambda_1}{m_2 \lambda_2}$$

$$\mathcal{N} = a \frac{m_2 \lambda_2}{m_1 \lambda_1} \mathcal{M} - G_2$$

Multiplying the last by $\mathcal{M}$ and subtracting

$$a \frac{m_2 \lambda_2}{m_1 \lambda_1} \mathcal{M}^2 + (G_1 - G_2) \mathcal{M} - a \frac{m_1 \lambda_1}{m_2 \lambda_2} = 0$$

or

$$\mathcal{M}^2 + \frac{1}{a} \frac{m_1 \lambda_1}{m_2 \lambda_2} (G_1 - G_2) \mathcal{M} = \left(\frac{m_1 \lambda_1}{m_2 \lambda_2}\right)^2$$
