$$Q = c e^{\sqrt{\frac{n}{2\mu'}} (\xi - \xi_1)} \sin \left\{ \sqrt{\frac{n}{2\mu'}} (\xi - \xi_1) \right\}$$
$$P = c e^{\sqrt{\frac{n}{2\mu'}} (\xi - \xi_1)} \cos \left\{ \sqrt{\frac{n}{2\mu'}} (\xi - \xi_1) \right\}$$
