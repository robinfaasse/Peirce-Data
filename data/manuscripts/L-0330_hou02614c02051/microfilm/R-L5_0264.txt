To All whom it may concern:

Be it known that I, [u]Charles Sanders Peirce[/u], of "Arisbe" in the township of Westfall (Post Office: Milford, Pa.) in Pike County, Pennsylvania, have invented a new and useful means of preventing waste and injury to goods in bleaching by electricity and I do hereby declare that the following is a full, clear, and exact description of the same:

The nature of my invention consists in adding to a solution of chloride of sodium a sufficient quantity of sulphate of soda to cause the two salts to be decomposed in equivalent proportions, with a view of preventing the liberation of chlorine. It is known that a solution of hypochlorous acid will do as much bleaching as double the amount of chlorine it contains when free in solution. This, however constitutes no advantage of hypochlorous acid over chlorine as a bleaching agent, since the former also requires twice the expenditure of energy to produce it. But hypochlorous acid has two great advantages over chlorine as a bleaching agent. Namely, first, it does not escape in any important amount into the air, and second, being much less active, it attacks and destroys the coloring matter which adheres to the fibres, without sensibly attacking the fibre itself, nor the wood of the vat, and other things, while chlorine does attack these, and in so doing not works positive injury, but is also itself exhausted and rendered [del]ineffec[/del] useless for bleaching.
