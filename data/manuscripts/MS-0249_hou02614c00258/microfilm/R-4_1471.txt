[marginalia][date]1889 Jan 19[/date][/marginalia]

Of the two quadrilaterals, [u]$\bar{\gamma}\bar{\alpha}\beta\delta$[/u] is finite, and of the 6 trilaterals, $\frac{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}\delta}{\bar{\gamma}}$ and $\frac{\bar{\gamma}\alpha\delta}{\bar{\beta}}$ are finite, as the symbols immediately show.

As to the order of the letters, the fourth line separates $\frac{\alpha\gamma}{\beta}$ into $\frac{\delta\gamma}{\alpha\beta}$ and $\frac{\alpha\gamma\delta}{\beta}$; it separates $\gamma\alpha\beta$ into $\gamma\delta\alpha\beta$ and $\frac{\bar{\gamma}\alpha\delta}{\beta}$; it separates $\bar{\alpha}\beta\bar{\gamma}$ into $\frac{\bar{\alpha}\beta\bar{\delta}}{\bar{\gamma}}$ and $\bar{\alpha}\delta\beta\bar{\gamma}$, and its separates $\bar{\alpha}\beta\bar{\gamma}$ into $\frac{\bar{\alpha}\beta\bar{\delta}}{\bar{\gamma}}$ and $\frac{\delta\beta\bar{\gamma}}{\bar{\alpha}}$.
