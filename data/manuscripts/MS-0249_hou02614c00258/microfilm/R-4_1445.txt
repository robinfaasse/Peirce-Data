[u]Ordinal Geometry[/u]

By ordinal geometry, I mean the theory of the arrangements of segments.

I make use of the notation of the [del]logic[/del] [ins]algebra[/ins] of logic. If [u]a[/u] denote a part of the line, plane, or solid considered, [u]a[/u] with a bar over it denotes the complementary part. "Qualification" is the respect in which [u]a[/u] and [u]a[/u] with a bar over it differ. To change the qualification of a letter is to "requalify" it. [ins]The express [u]a[/u] < [u]b[/u] means a is included in b.[/ins] The product of two segments is their common part. The sum is includes all that is included in either. A fraction, as [u]a[/u]/[u]b[/u] with a bar over it, is equal [del]to[/del] at once to [u]a[/u] and to [u]a[/u][u]b[/u] with a bar over it, implying that [u]a[/u] < [u]b[/u] with a bar over it.
