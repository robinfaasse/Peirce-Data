If $\alpha\beta\gamma$ is the [del]triangle[/del] [ins]region[/ins], the other regions are
$\overline{\alpha}\beta/\gamma$, $\beta\gamma\overline{\alpha}$, $\overline{\gamma}\overline{\alpha}/\beta$, $\alpha\beta\overline{\gamma}$, $\beta\overline{\gamma}/\alpha$, $\gamma\alpha\overline{\beta}$
and $\overline{\alpha}\overline{\beta}\overline{\gamma} = 0$.

If a fourth line, [u]d[/u], be drawn, suppose it
first appears in the region $\alpha\beta\overline{\gamma}$. It may then
pass into $\beta\overline{\gamma}/\alpha$, into $\alpha\beta\gamma$, or into $\overline{\gamma}\overline{\alpha}/\beta$. The
first and last cases are alike. Suppose it passes
into $\beta\overline{\gamma}/\alpha$. Since $\beta$ has been changed, $\gamma$, the
only other letter of the numerator must next
be changed, so that the line passes into
$\gamma\alpha\overline{\beta}$. The only letter remaining to be changed
is $\alpha$, so that the line then passes into $\overline{\alpha}\overline{\beta}\gamma$,
and this, being the reverse of the first region,
must be the last. The triangle $\alpha\beta\gamma$ is not
entered. Suppose this on the positive side of d.
Then its symbol will be $\alpha\beta\gamma/\delta$. The other regions
will be
