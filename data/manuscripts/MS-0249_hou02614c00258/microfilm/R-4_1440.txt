In the former case, the succession of regions
penetrated is
[u]\(\frac{\alpha\gamma}{\beta}\)[/u], \(\bar{\gamma}\alpha\beta\), \(\bar{\gamma}\bar{\alpha}\beta\), \(\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}\bar{\gamma}\).
The unpenetrated regions are [u]\(\frac{\bar{\beta}\alpha}{\gamma}\)[/u], [u]\(\frac{\gamma\bar{\beta}}{\alpha}\)[/u], and \(\beta\gamma\bar{\alpha}\).
Two of these are adjacent; for from [u]\(\frac{\gamma\bar{\beta}}{\alpha}\)[/u] we pass
to \(\beta\gamma\bar{\alpha}\) [ins]by[/ins] a single requalification. But from
[u]\(\frac{\bar{\beta}\alpha}{\gamma}\)[/u] we pass only to \(\bar{\gamma}\alpha\beta\) and to \(\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}\bar{\gamma}\); so that
this is on the other side of the fourth line.
The regions round infinity are
\(\frac{\alpha\gamma}{\beta}\delta\), \(\frac{\alpha\gamma}{\beta}\bar{\delta}\), \(\bar{\gamma}\alpha\beta\bar{\delta}\), [u]\(\frac{\bar{\beta}\alpha}{\gamma}\)[/u]\(\bar{\delta}\), \(\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}\bar{\gamma}\bar{\delta}\), \(\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}\bar{\gamma}\delta\), [u]\(\frac{\gamma\bar{\beta}}{\alpha}\)[/u]\(\delta\),
\(\beta\gamma\bar{\alpha}\delta\).
Comparing these with the unpenetrated regions
we see that all the regions are
\(\frac{\alpha\gamma}{\beta}\delta\), \(\frac{\alpha\gamma}{\beta}\bar{\delta}\), \(\bar{\gamma}\alpha\beta\bar{\delta}\), \(\bar{\gamma}\alpha\beta\delta\), \(\bar{\gamma}\bar{\alpha}\beta\delta\), \(\bar{\gamma}\bar{\alpha}\beta\bar{\delta}\), \(\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}\bar{\gamma}\delta\),
\(\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}\bar{\gamma}\bar{\delta}\), \(\frac{\bar{\beta}\alpha}{\gamma\bar{\delta}}\), \(\frac{\gamma\bar{\beta}}{\alpha\bar{\delta}}\), \(\frac{\beta\gamma\bar{\alpha}}{\delta}\).

In the second case, the succession of regions
penetrated is
\(\frac{\alpha\gamma}{\beta}\), \(\bar{\gamma}\alpha\beta\), [del]\(\bar{\gamma}\bar{\alpha}\beta\)[/del] [ins]\(\frac{\bar{\beta}\alpha}{\gamma}\)[/ins], \(\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}\bar{\gamma}\).
The unpenetrated regions are \(\bar{\gamma}\bar{\alpha}\beta\), [u]\(\frac{\gamma\bar{\beta}}{\alpha}\)[/u], and \(\beta\gamma\bar{\alpha}\).
These regions lie together, for we can pass from
\(\beta\gamma\bar{\alpha}\) to either of the others by a single requalification.
