3. Whenever a fraction is inserted in a space [ins]bounded by[/ins] [del]between[/del] a fraction to the left of it, the sign $=$ is to be inserted to the left of the new fraction; but if the space is bounded by a sign, the same sign is to be inserted to the right of the new fraction.
