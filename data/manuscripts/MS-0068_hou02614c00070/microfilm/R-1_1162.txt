formulae that doubtless hold [del]of it[/del] [ins]good[/ins], there is this, where $l < 2^{m-1} < 2^{n-1}$:
$N2^n \cdot N(2^m + l) = N(2^n + 2^{m+1} + l) + N(2^{m+1} + l - 1)$.
Thus, take $l=2$, $m=3$, $n=4$. Then, $N2^4 = 6$; $2^m + l = 10$. $N10 = 8$; $6 \times 8 = 48$.
$2^4 + 2^3 + 2 = 26$; $N26 = 13$; $2^3 + 2^4 - 3 = 141$; $N141 = 35$; $13 + 35 = 48$.
Take $l=3$, $m=3$, $n=5$. Then, $N2^5 = 7$; $2^m + l = 11$; $N11 = 7$; $7 \times 7 = 49$.
$2^5 + 2^3 + 3 = 43$; $N43 = 18$; $2^7 + 2^4 - 4 = 140$; $N140 = 31$; $18 + 31 = 49$.
