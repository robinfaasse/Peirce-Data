But, well as the mathematicians do the kind of reasoning that it is their proper business to do, individual mathematicians are apt in consequence of their conscious intellectual superiority to other men, to fall into a fatal readiness to meddle with questions quite outside the [del]limits of[/del] narrow range of their competence; and I know no other subject, — unless it be political economy, — that illustrates this better than the calculus of probabilities.
