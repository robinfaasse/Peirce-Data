Now a quality is a consciousness. I do not say a waking
consciousness, — but still, something of the nature of consciousness.
A sleeping consciousness, perhaps.

A possibility, then, or potentiality is a particular tinge of conscious-
ness. I do not say the possibility is exactly a consciousness; but it is
a tinge of consciousness, a potential consciousness. However, the dis-
tinction is little more than verbal.
