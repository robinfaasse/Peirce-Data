[u]On the nature of signs[/u]

A sign is an object which stands for another to
some mind. I propose to describe the characters of
a sign. In the first place like any other thing it
must have qualities which belong to it whether it
be regarded as a sign or not thus a printed word
is black, has a certain number of letters and those
letters have certain shapes. Such [del]material[/del]
[del]qualities[/del] characters of a sign I call its material
quality. In the next place a sign must have
some real connection with the thing it signifies
so that when the object is present or is so as the
sign signifies it to be the sign shall so signify
it and otherwise not. What I mean will
best be understood by illustration a weather-
