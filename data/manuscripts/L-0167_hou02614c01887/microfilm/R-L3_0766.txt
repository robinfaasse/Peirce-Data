✓ Room 223 Oct 28/81
[/marginalia]

My dear Mr. Peirce:

As soon as I could get
at my trunk this morn-
ing I sent my papers to
your room but learned
that you had already
gone out. I have told
the messenger if you are
not now (1.30) to leave
this note and bring back
the papers, and I shall
expect to send it to Phil.
for Pa. tonight by mail.

If you receive this at
once and can still see
me at 1.30 today I
should be very glad indeed
