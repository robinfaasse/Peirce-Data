And Being is (to speak in outline) such as man, horse;
and how much such as two feet, three feet
and of what kind such as white, grammatical
and [del]they[/del] [ins]as to what[/ins] such as double, half, greater
and where such as at market, at [del]Lyceum[/del]
and when such as yesterday, in a year
and to rest such as [del]he reclines[/del], he sits
and to have such as he is shod, he is armed
and to do such as he cuts, he burns
and to suffer such as he is cut, he is burnt.

And each of these itself by itself is by no means said
to be affirmed, but an affirmation arises with their con-
nection with each other. For every affirmation seems to
be either true or false; but nothing spoken unconnectedly
is either true or false, as man, white, walks, conquers.
