About this time, I suppose, he begins to find that what these
people about him say is the very best evidence of fact. So
much so, that testimony is even a stronger mark of fact than
appearances, themselves. I may remark, by the way, that this remains
so through life; testimony will convince a man that he himself
is mad. The dawning of the conception of testimony is the dawn-
ing of self-consciousness. Because testimony relates to a fact,
