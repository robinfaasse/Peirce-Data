[u]Questions concerning Reality.[/u]

As long as the logician contents [del]himself[/del] with tracing out the
forms of propositions and arguments, his science is one of the
most exact and satisfactory. It may be confused; it can hardly
be erroneous. But logic cannot stop here. It is bound, by its
