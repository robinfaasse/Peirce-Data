[pn]9[/pn] I begin with some rudimentary reflexions from Geometry. Three branches of Geometry there are, sharply separated and widely remote in every particular. The most rudimentary is best called Topics. It treats of the modes of connexion of the different parts of places.

Some attention was called to it by the great though perhaps somewhat narrow genius Euler, in enunciating this proposition: In any polyhedron, the sum of the number of faces and the number of summits exceeds by 2 the number of edges. But this is [del]only[/del] true only if the polyhedron has no polyhedral hollow within it [ins]or canal breaking through edges[/ins] Thus we have,
