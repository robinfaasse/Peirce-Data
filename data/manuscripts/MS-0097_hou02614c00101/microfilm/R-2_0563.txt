Into how many such compartments can a projective plane be cut up? Here we have

$$\begin{array}{lc}
\text{Chorisy} & +1 \\
\text{Cyclosy} & \\
\text{Periphery} & +1 \\
\text{Apeiry} & 0
\end{array} \quad +1$$

$$\text{Census Value} \quad \underline{(+2) - (+1) = +1}$$

Into how many can the surface of a sphere be cut up?

$$\begin{array}{lc}
\text{Chorisy} & 1 \\
\text{Cyclosy} & \\
\text{Periphery} & 1 \\
\text{Apeiry} &
\end{array} \quad \begin{array}{c} \\ 0 \\ \\ 0 \end{array}$$

$$\text{Census value} \quad \underline{2} \qquad \frac{7x}{6} - \frac{x^2}{6} = 2 \qquad x = 4$$
