Burlesque is a ludicrous treatment of an
absurd subject in a more or less lofty style.
We do not know where it took its first
origin. But it is difficult to imagine the
learned Chaldeans or the serious Egyptians
indulging in burlesque; and there is not burlesque
in the Bible, for we cannot call the satirical
passage in Job burlesque. [del]There is[/del] "Doubtless
ye are the whole people, and wisdom shall die
with you." It needed the intellectual brightness
of the Greek to strike out burlesque and with
the Greeks it is in full development. The Greek
comedy is [del]nothing but[/del] [ins]almost entirely[/ins] burlesque, varied by
