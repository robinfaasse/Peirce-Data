Required to eliminate $x$ from $(xx, xx, a)$.

$\frac{\phi}{x} = 00, 0; a = a, a$

$\frac{\phi}{xx} = 00, 00; 00; a = aa$

$\therefore \phi = \frac{\phi}{x}, \frac{\phi}{xx}, \frac{\phi}{x}, \frac{\phi}{xx} = aa, aa; aa, aa = aa$

Required to eliminate $x$ from $(xa, a)$.

$\frac{\phi}{x} = 0a, a = aa, a$

$\frac{\phi}{xx} = 00, a; a = aa$

$\therefore \phi = aa, a; aa: aa, a; aa = aa, aa = a$

Required to develop $(ax; b, xx: ab)$ according to $x$.

$\frac{\phi}{x} = a0; b, 00: ab = aa, a; ab = a, ab = a$

$\frac{\phi}{xx} = a00; b, 0; ab = bb, bb; ab = b, ab = b$

$\phi = \frac{\phi}{x} x, \frac{\phi}{xx} xx = ax; b, xx$

Required to eliminate $M$ from $(SS, M; MM, P)$.

$\frac{\phi}{M} = SS, 0; 00, P = SS, SS; SS, SS = SS$

$\frac{\phi}{MM} = SS, 00; 0, P = P, P; P, P = P$

$\therefore SS, M; MM, P = SS, P; SS, P$

which is the syllogistic conclusion
