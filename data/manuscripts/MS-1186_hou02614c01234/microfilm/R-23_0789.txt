whether they retain their original forms or not. The word-
coinage of the modern scientific mint must conform to
the rules of literary English, and has no claim to the
slightest weight in the settlement of these rules. All
everyday words of Greek origin are at present spelled with
an e for both æ and œ; and that practice must have
some authority as a precedent. There remains but a very
