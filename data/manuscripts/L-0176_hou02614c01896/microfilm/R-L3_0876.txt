Philadelphia,
June 5, 1911.

Dr. C. S. Pierce,

Dear Sir,

I am very anxious to know whether there has been collected any bibliography on the "Insolubilia" on which you wrote the article in Baldwin's Dictionary.

If you know of one I should be very grateful if you would let me know where it could be found.

Very respectfully yours,
[ins]E. R. Guthrie[/ins]
