VB

Lincoln, Nebraska.
[date]June 20, 1911[/date].

Dear Mr Peirce, I am very grateful for the card with the account of treatments of the Insolubilia and should like very much to know your own opinion about their treatment.

My own interest in the subject began when I read the discussion between Poincare and Russell in the Revue de Metaphysique which you have of course seen. I hope to be able to devote myself to the question for some time.

Very Respectfully,
Edwin Guthrie.

260 No. 27
Lincoln, Nebr.
