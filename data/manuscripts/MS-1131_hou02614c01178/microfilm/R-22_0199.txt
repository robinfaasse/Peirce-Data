[u]Rain is caused by the cooling of moist air[/u],
for warm air will contain more moisture than
cool air. Hence about a warm centre where there is
a current upward to colder regions it is apt to rain,
and about a cold centre where there is a downward
current, and especially in the south-east of a warm
centre there is generally rain. If a person is south
of the line of progression of a warm centre, he
first feels wind from the south-east accom-
panied generally with rain. The wind then changes
through south to south-west and the weather
clears up. If he is north of the centre he first
feels wind from the north-east, which changes
through north to north-west and then also it
clears up.
