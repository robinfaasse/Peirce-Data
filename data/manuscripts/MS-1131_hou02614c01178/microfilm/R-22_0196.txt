But the earth turns a little to the left under it while

the equator there is no such effect whatever, because the
earth does not turn under it. If it is somewhere between
the equator and the pole, the rotation produces a partial
effect of turning under it, though not so much as at
the pole. It is, therefore, deflected, though not so rapidly
as at the pole. A current of air or any other thing mov-
ing upon the earth is, in consequence of its momentum,
affected in a similar manner to the pendulum. This is
why every current of air or water presses to the right.
