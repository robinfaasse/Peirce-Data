is blowing in on all sides towards a heated spot, from
north, south, east, and west. That which blows from the
north as it approaches the centre is bent round towards the
west, that which blows from the west is bent round towards
the south, that which blows from the south is bent round
towards the east, and that which blows from the east is
bent round towards the north. Thus, north of the spot
the wind will be towards the west, west of the spot towards
the south, south of the spot towards the east, and east of
the spot towards the north. In short, there will be a whirl-
wind, bending towards the left just about the centre.
