the west, with various modifications however. In northern
districts there is, for instance, a somewhat less rainy interval
in the middle of the summer. In the valley of the Missis-
sippi the December rains appear again and October is the
dryest season. Further west, the system of seasons seems to be
quite different. In Arkansas, April and November are
the times of maximum rain-fall, September and January
of minimum. In Indian Territory, the maxima fall in
May and October, the minima in August and January.
In California, the maximum is in December, and there
is no rain at all from the first of June to the first
of October. In Washington Territory, the maximum is in
December, the minimum in July or August. At Sitka, the
maximum is early in October, the minimum late in June,
but 3½ inches falls even in that month.
