[pn]I[/pn] HE brief treatise on the lodestone by Petrus Peregrinus, dated [date]1269[/date], occupies a unique position in the history of the human mind, being without exception the earliest work of experimental science that has come down to us. Nor can we learn that anything of this sort had been written earlier. No doubt experiments had been made earlier.
