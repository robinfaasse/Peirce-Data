The following was copied by C. S. Peirce from a MS in the Bibliothèque nationale,
MSS latins 7378. For the convenience of any person who may wish to compare the copy
with the MS, I have broken the lines where the MS breaks them. The spelling has been fo
lowed as far as possible; but it will be considered that the MS is full of abbre-
viations, which it would have been neither possible nor desirable to reproduce. Nor
has it been thought worth while to imitate the old ways of using capitals, the letters
i and j, u and v, nor the marks of punctuation. The MS, which is hardly legible, is
of the middle of the XIIIth century.
