[u]Roger Bacon’s Opinion of Petrus Peregrinus.[/u]

The family of Joseph Pellerin, the numismatician, had estates in Picardy.* If this explanation of the Peregrinus be admitted, it is clear that Bacon omits this surname from the same motive from which he omits that of John Peckham (?) of London, his other mathematical adept, whatever that motive may have been — probably, to impart a more learned and clerical style to the names.
