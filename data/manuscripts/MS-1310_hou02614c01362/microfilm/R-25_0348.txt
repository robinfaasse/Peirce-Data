[u]Translation of the Dedication[/u].

To the most illustrious and most serene Prince,
the most dread Lord, the Lord John, paternal
uncle of our Lord, the King of France and England,
Regent of the realm of France, Duke of Bedford,
Rollando, physician to the writer of your Highness,
of Lisbon, has written in fulfillment of [del]an[/del] [ins]his[/ins] oath.

Most famous Prince, under whose happy
dukedom all France happily increases with a
certain high consent of the people as beseems
a prince ruling righteously, in your charity
burns within, piety gleams without, and science
sheds her rays both ways. Who purer in feeling?
Who more abundant in well-doing? Who more
certain in clearness of [del]purpose[/del] [ins]counsel[/ins]? I say, weighing
my meaning, that you spend your treasures as
you might scatter ashes. You immolate yourself
as a sacrifice in incessant labors. In order
that you may prepare peace for those subject to
