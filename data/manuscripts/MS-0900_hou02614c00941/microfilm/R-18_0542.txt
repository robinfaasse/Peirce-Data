Of laws not relating to the spatial motions of bodies, some relate to the kinds of [del]collections[/del] [ins]aggregations[/ins] of of bodies, [ins]or chemical laws,[/ins] while others [del]do[/del] not relate to bodies at all. Of chemical laws, [del]the[/del] it is doubtful whether we ought to name first the division of matter into ordinary matter and ether or not. Assuming, however, that the ether is merely a [del]sep[/del] special chemical [del]substance[/del], the first law seems to be that all matter is segregated into atoms, or atomicules.
