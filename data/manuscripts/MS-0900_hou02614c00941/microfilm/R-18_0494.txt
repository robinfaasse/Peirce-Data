[del]¶[/del] Laws which connect phenomena by a syntheses more or less intellectual, or inward, are divided somewhat broadly into laws of the inward relations, or resemblances, of bodies and laws of mind.

The laws of the resemblances and differences of bodies are classificatory, or chemical. We know little about them; but we may assert with some confidence that there are differences between substances, [ins]i.e.[/ins] [del]or[/del] differences in the smallest parts of bodies, and a classification based on that, [del]and[/del] [ins]or[/ins] differences in the structure of bodies and classification based on that. Then of these latter we may distinguish differences in the structure of the smallest pieces of bodies, depending [del]on[/del] shape and size of atomicules, and differences in the manner in which bodies are built up out of their smallest [del]pieces[/del] [ins]parts[/ins]. Here, we have a distinction between that kind of structure which gives rise to forms without [del]its[/del] [ins]power[/ins] of truth growth or inorganic structures and the chemistry of protoplasms which develope living [del]organisms[/del].

Finally laws of mind divide themselves into laws of the universal action of mind and laws of kinds of psychical manifestation.
