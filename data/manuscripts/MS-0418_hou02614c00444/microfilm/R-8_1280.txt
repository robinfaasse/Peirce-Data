For instance, we have the valid inference
$x \cdot (y \psi z)$
$\therefore x \cdot y \psi z$.
This is an associative formula. But write the
premise twice
$x \cdot (y \psi z) \cdot x \cdot (y \psi z)$
and we deduce
$\therefore (x \cdot y \psi z) \cdot x \cdot (y \psi z)$
$\therefore (x \cdot y \psi z \cdot x)$
$\therefore (x \cdot y \psi x \cdot z)$
and we have justified the distributive formula
$x \cdot (y \psi z)$
$\therefore x \cdot y \psi x \cdot z$.
