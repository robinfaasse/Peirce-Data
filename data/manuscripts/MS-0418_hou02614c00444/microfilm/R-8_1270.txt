$$(\bar{S}_{1} \mathbin{\Psi} \bar{P}_{1}) \cdot (\bar{S}_{2} \mathbin{\Psi} \bar{P}_{2}) \cdot (\bar{S}_{3} \mathbin{\Psi} \bar{P}_{3}) \cdot \text{etc}$$

$$\Pi_{i} (\bar{S}_{i} \mathbin{\Psi} \bar{P}_{i})$$

As these symbols $\Sigma$ and $\Pi$ are defined in terms
of the signs of aggregation and composition, there is
no difficulty in deducing the rules for their use.
