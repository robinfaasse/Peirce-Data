[date]1908[/date], the Part of the Oxford Dictionary extending from [u]Polygenous[/u] to [u]Premonious[/u] was published. It gives to some passage from Watts's Logic that I quoted as the earliest use of [u]precisive[/u] in the [del]logical[/del] [ins]logical[/ins] [del]sense[/del] [ins]precisive[/ins] [del]earlier[/del] [ins]sense[/ins] that its 1400 readers have been able to unearth. But three instances of 'precision' for logical prescission are given, from Glanvill's Sadducismus, of [date]1681[/date] [ins](1st Appendix §8)[/ins], Locke's Essay of [date]1690[/date] (III. x. 15), and Berkeley's Principles, [ins][date]1710[/date],[/ins] Introd. §9.
