[u]Development of the method[/u]. (5-12)

1. It is not a historical fact that the best thinking has been done by words, or aural images. It has been performed by means of visual images and muscular imaginations. In reasoning of the best kind, an imaginary experiment is performed. The result is inwardly observed, and is as unexpected as that of a physical experiment. On the other hand, the success of [del]experim[/del] [ins]outward[/ins] experimentation depends on there being a reason in nature. Thus, reasoning and experimentation are essentially analogous.
