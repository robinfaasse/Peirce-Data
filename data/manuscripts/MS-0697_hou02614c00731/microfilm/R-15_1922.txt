[u]Consequences[/u]

A [u]consequence[/u] is the statement that one fact follows from another.
The expression of the former fact is called the [u]antecedent[/u].
The expression of the latter is termed the [u]consequent[/u].

Note. The investigation of consequences constitutes Logic. The
questions of psychology are therefore irrelevant to the science
of logic generally, though they may, no doubt, be of impor-
tance with reference to particular kinds of consequences
where a psychological fact is explicitly or implicitly involved
in the antecedent.
