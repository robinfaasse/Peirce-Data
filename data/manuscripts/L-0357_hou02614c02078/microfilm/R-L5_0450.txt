[date]May 24th 1900[/date].

Dear Mr Peirce:

Yours just at hand. I have done
all that I can as far as money is
concerned that I can do at present.
I have assumed some obligations that will
take every cent I can lay my hands on.
Wish I might find something for you to do.

Sincerely yours
Geo A. Plimpton

I shall sell the wood land & pay off my
mortgage -
