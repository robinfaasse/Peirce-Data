I should prefer to do the work somewhat
on this plan. Your firm to furnish the capital
including pay for my work. How much this
would be we could not tell for certain until
after those 20 days experiments. But supposing
it proved to be what I now estimate, I would
be satisfied with $250 a month, and a proviso
that I should finish in 16 months. After it
was out, the capital should be repaid out of
the profits with 10 per cent interest. After that,
I think you ought to let me in for half the
profits. I only suggest this arrangement,
without pretending to understand the matter.
