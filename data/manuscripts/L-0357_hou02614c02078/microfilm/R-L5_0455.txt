Dictated by G.A.P.

70 Fifth Ave., New York. Feb. 5, 1901

Mr. Charles S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

I have sent my check to the Fifth Avenue Bank, to be placed to your credit ($5).

I send you Nichols' Number and Space, as requested. Wont you give me your idea of the book?

When would you like to finish cataloguing my mathematical books?

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A Plimpton
