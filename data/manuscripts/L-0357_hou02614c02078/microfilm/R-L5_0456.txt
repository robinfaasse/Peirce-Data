Feb. 14, 1901

Mr. Charles S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

What I want to have done in regard to the mathematical books I think can be done in a few days, if you should happen to be in the city. I don't think it is anything that would take a great deal of your time. I find that I am going to be away myself for the next two weeks, and I should advise you to stick to the country until the weather moderates. It must be mighty comfortable up there; here it is about zero.

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A Plimpton
