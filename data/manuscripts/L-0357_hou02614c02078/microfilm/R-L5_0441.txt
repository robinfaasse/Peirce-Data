III. It is very necessary in the case of words that are
not very common, to give a hint as to their peculiar tone
or application. Take for instance the words, sense, meaning,
signification, application, acceptation, acception. To
write with discrimination, one should know that ac-
ception differs from acceptation and how. But it
would be a waste of space to explain distinctions,
however fine, that are generally understood.

The first thing in making a new work would
be to form a new provisional scheme of pigeon-holes
or classes. This I have; but it would pay to spend from
ten to twenty days in revising it. It would still
be provisional, and would only contain about
a thousand classes, which number would naturally
increase as the exigencies developed themselves.
