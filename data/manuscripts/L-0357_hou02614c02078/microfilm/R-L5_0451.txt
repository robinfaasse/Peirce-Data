My dear Mr. Peirce:-

Yours just received. When I see you, I will tell you why you did not get the Grolier Club position. I prefer to tell you personally rather than to write it.

The McClure Cyclopoedia is in statu quo. Nothing has been done yet. It is the McClure people who have it, not Doubleday, Page & Co.

I am glad you have some translating to do.

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A Plimpton
