July 29, 1901

Mr. Charles S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

Our Boston people write us in regard to your sheets as follows:

"We might make Mr. Peirce such an offer as this: We will pay the transportation on the sheets, supposing they are in good condition, will bind them at our expense, and will give him say 40% of all receipts from sales. Perhaps if you thought best we could make it 50%."

Wont you please return that little slip about the Boetius?

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A Plimpton
H.
