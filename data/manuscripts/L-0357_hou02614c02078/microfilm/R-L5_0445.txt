Milford Pa [date]1894 June 23[/date]

My dear Plimpton

It was May 25 when I put in my scheme about the Index. It had been excogitated & experimented upon to a far greater extent than most such undertakings are when the publishers take them up. That I know, because I have seen the formation of a number of important works. A good deal is done; and I have a trunkful of materials.
