half starved condition & my wife here. I became very
ill. They called in a doctor. I never should have done
so myself, — being only too eager to get out of
this world. The doctor after watching my case for
some weeks, told me he didn't think I was long
for this world. This more than his treatment brightened
me up & gave me the strength for the journey home.
