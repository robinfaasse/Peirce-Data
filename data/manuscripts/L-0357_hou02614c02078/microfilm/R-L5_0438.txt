Mr. C. S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

Your esteemed favor through Mr. DeVinne is at hand, and I am very much interested in your letter. My books will have come on probably inside of a week or ten days, and then it would give me great pleasure to notify you, and I should be glad to have you come and look them over. In the meantime, believe me,

Sincerely yours,
George A. Plimpton
