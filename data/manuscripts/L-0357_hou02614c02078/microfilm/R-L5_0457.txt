70 Fifth Ave., New York. June 14, 1901.

Mr. C. S. Pierce,
246 West 46th Street.

My dear Mr. Pierce:-

I am mighty sorry not to have seen you when you were here. If I could have gotten hold of you, I should have liked to have had you stay over. If you are in town now, won't you let me know, please; I should be glad to have you come and spend Sunday with me.

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A. Plimpton
