Professor C. S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Professor Peirce:-

In looking over my papers I find a copy of your logarithmic tables. I remember returning one to you, but I think this is the one which you left at the house. I return it by mail today.

I am sorry that our Boston house was unable to use your logic. It seems to me if you could make a logic after the plan of Jevons, that is, not any larger than that, and as a text-book, pure and simple, it would have a good sale. As I understand it, the only logic that is sold today is Jevons.

Sincerely yours,
George A. Plimpton
H.
