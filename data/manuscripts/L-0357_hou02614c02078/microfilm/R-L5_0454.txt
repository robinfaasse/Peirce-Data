Mr. Charles S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

I enclose herewith the deed, properly signed and stamped; also Mr. Peck's letter, and return that of Mr. Potter.

As I understand it, I am to be reimbursed for the money which I have advanced to you on this land, and I shall be glad to have this money. I hope the amount you receive will tide you over your difficulties until you get some permanent position where you will receive some regular compensation.

Very truly yours,
Geo. A Plimpton
