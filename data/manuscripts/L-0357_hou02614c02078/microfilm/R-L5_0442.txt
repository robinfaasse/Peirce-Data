It would next be necessary to go through a
good dictionary, say the Century Dictionary.
Only about 50000 words would be wanted;
for the jargon that fills up dictionaries would
be excluded. But many words have several
meanings & have to be entered in several
places. It would be necessary to make 20
days experiments to ascertain how this would
best be done, and how long it would take.
Leaving all possible to the clerk, I think
a word would take 2 1/2 minutes on the average
of which the clerk could take off 1/2 minute.
[ins]A superior assistant could take off a minute possibly.[/ins]
If 5 solid hours a day were done, and
it would probably not much exceed that,
all sorts of interruptions & hitches being considered,
there would be a years' work there. Next
the 1200 to 1500 classes would have to be
revised & probably would average 20 a day. This
would occupy then from 2 to 3 months.
