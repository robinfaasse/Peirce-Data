I go on with the study of superposed projective primes. [pn]47[/pn]

Let us suppose that a unidimensional prime of 4 elements,
$1, 2, 3, 4$, is projective with $1, 2, 4, 3$, where two of the
elements are transposed. I am going to show that that
is possible, but not with every such prime.

[fig]Line with points A, B, C, D and intersecting projection lines[/fig] Take the range whose base is $\{ABCD\}$.
If this is projective with $ABDC$, it
must be by one projection into rays
crossing on one side of a [del]second[/del] [ins]sectional[/ins]
ray, and then another projection by
rays crossing on the other side.
