$ds = dx = \sqrt{(dx+d^2x)^2 + (d^2y)^2}$
$(dx)^2 = (dx)^2 + 2dx.d^2x + (d^2x)^2 + (d^2y)^2$
$2dx.d^2x + (d^2x)^2 + (d^2y)^2 = 0$

$(\frac{d\rho}{ds})_1 = \frac{dx\mathbf{i}}{dx} = \mathbf{i}$  $(\frac{d\rho}{ds})_2 = \frac{(dx+d^2x)\mathbf{i} + d^2y\mathbf{j}}{\sqrt{(dx+d^2x)^2 + (d^2y)^2}}$
