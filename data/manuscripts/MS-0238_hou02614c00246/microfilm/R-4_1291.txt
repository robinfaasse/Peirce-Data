In speaking of relations it is necessary to attend to
the order of succession of 'any' and 'some' or their
equivalents. If interlocutors understand one another,
they are both more or less acquainted with a single
collection of objects, which is their universe of discourse.
If, then, either of them says that 'Any [u]m[/u]' has a
certain character, if he uses language as I shall do, he
means that his interlocutor shall be at liberty to
indicate [del]whatever individual[/del] [ins]whatever individual[/ins] of the universe he likes; and if,
