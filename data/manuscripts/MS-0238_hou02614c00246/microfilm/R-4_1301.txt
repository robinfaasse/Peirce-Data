[u]Clause I[/u]. There is among positive integers a certain doubly
non-plural dyadic relation called the relation of [u]being next[/u]
[u]greater than[/u]. There is at least one positive integer which is
not next greater than any positive integer; and one indi-
vidual positive integer of this description is called [u]zero[/u]. Given
any positive integer, N, there is a positive integer next
greater than N.
