All science is either, A, Science of Discovery; B, Science
of Review; or C, Practical Science.

occupy themselves with arranging the results of discovery,
[del]and[/del] beginning with digests, and going on to endeavor to
form a philosophy of science. Such is the nature of Humboldt's
Cosmos, of Comte's Philosophie positive, and [ins]of[/ins] Spencer's
Synthetic Philosophy. The Classification of the Sciences
belongs to this department.
