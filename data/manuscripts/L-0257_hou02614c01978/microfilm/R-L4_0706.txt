Chicago [date]Oct. 9 1903[/date]

Dear Mr Peirce,

My thanks for your letter. It seems to me that you are so well known that to add Milford to your name would be anti-climax, — or hitching a wagon to a star; and while ascending to heaven this may be good for the wagon, I cannot conceive that it adds dignity to the star.

I think I can get many of your diagrams — if not too complex — made for [del]$[/del][ins]$[/ins]35.00 and [del]$[/del][ins]$[/ins]15.00 ought to print your syllabus unless it is very long.
