Oct. 3, 1903.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I enclose herewith an advance copy of the Program of the lectures of the Lowell Institute. May I ask you to examine the statement in regard to your own course and to inform me, if possible by return mail, if it is entirely satisfactory to you. There is still time for minor changes.

Will you also kindly keep me informed of your address from this time forwards so that I can reach you by mail or, if necessary, by wire.

Very truly yours,
W. T. Sedgwick
Curator.

Charles S. Peirce, Esq.,
Milford, Pennsylvania.
