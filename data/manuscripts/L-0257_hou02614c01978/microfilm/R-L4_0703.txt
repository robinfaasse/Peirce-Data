Cottage St.

My dear Sir,

Enclosed I send
you ticket made out in
accordance with Mr. Lowell's
programme — to be revised and
altered as you see fit; and
further to have the subjects
of your several lectures
printed on the back of the
ticket if you wish.

Please add a line or two
of description of your course
for the News-paper advertisement.

Please also return the
[ins]filled up[/ins] ticket to me as soon as you
have arranged it — that I
may send you a printer's-proof
in season for any final ar-
rangement you may wish to
make.

Very truly,
B. E. Cotting [ins]by J[/ins]
