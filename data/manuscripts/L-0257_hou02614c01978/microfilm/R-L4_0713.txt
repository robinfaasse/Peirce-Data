LAKE FRONT
R. H. Southgate,
President.

Chicago, ______ 190

I thought that "discovery
and assistance of exception-
al men" was their great
ambition.

Where will you stay in
Boston? The St. Botolph Club
is a good place, & either
Prof. J. M. Peirce or I could
put you up there. It is rather
cheaper than a hotel and
much pleasanter. Still
cheaper is the Technology
Club, where I could gladly
get you a room. Both are
very near Huntington Hall.

Address me in Boston, please,
& believe me, Sincerely yours,
W. T. Sedgwick
