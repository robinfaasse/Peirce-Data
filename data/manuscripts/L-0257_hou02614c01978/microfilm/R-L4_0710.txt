LOWELL INSTITUTE,
BOSTON, MASS., U. S. A.

A. LAWRENCE LOWELL, TRUSTEE.
WILLIAM T. SEDGWICK, CURATOR.

Nov. 3, 1903.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I find, after consulting three separate printers, that fifty dollars will print about the first hundred pages of your syllabus, which by the way, strikes me as a magnum opus rather than a syllabus! This sum will give you one hundred copies on decent paper and another hundred will be only four or five dollars more. I should think that one or two hundred would be sufficient. If you wish us to go ahead with this at once, as we ought to do, if we are to have it on the 23rd, or any considerable part of it, please send a night telegram to me at the Institute of Technology at my expense, saying so. My secretary will open the telegram and act accordingly. I am leaving to-day for St. Louis and from Thursday to Sunday my address will be Southern Hotel, St. Louis, Mo. After that for a while Auditorium Hotel, Chicago.

Your syllabus looks most interesting and valuable and is really, it seems to me, a valuable book.

Sincerely yours,

W. T. Sedgwick,
Curator.

Per F. M. E.

Professor C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Penn.

P. S. I must have your topics immediately. Please send them to me at the Institute of Technology. I want also very much your bibliography but this I can wait for until the 14th of November.
