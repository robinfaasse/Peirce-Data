**LOWELL INSTITUTE,**
**BOSTON, MASS., U. S. A.**

**A. LAWRENCE LOWELL, [u]Trustee[/u].**
**WILLIAM T. SEDGWICK, [u]Curator[/u].**

Oct. 23, 1903.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I do not wish to hurry you unduly, but I should be glad to have as soon as convenient the topics of your several lectures for use in public advertisements, upon tickets, etc. I send herewith one to each of the current courses in order that you may see the kind of thing which we want.

By an arrangement with the Public Library authorities we are in the habit of printing lists of books of reference for students who desire to read in connection with the lectures. About twenty titles are usually given in by our lecturers and we should like as far as possible to have them in good bibliographic form, that is to say with dates of publication, name of publisher, full name of author, etc. If you find the preparation of such a list too difficult or too time-consuming, we shall of course not insist upon it, but for your own sake and the sake of any students who might wish to follow your lectures closely, I think you might find it worth while to prepare a list of this kind, and I should be particularly glad to have it on or before November 14th if convenient to you, as it will then appear (during your lectures) in the December Bulletin.

Faithfully yours,

W. T. Sedgwick
Curator.

Charles S. Peirce, Esq.,
Milford, Penn.
