Boston 8th Dec 91

My dear Mr Peirce,

The history of Science from Copernicus to Newton is the subject which Mr Boeker suggests & to which he refers in answering his letter. Moreover it is a subject which your studies have led you to explore so deeply that there is probably no one who could treat it with so much knowledge and acumen as you.

Twelve lectures I should suppose would be needed to develop so comprehensive a theme & I am quite content that the Course should extend to that number.

We will therefore, if you please, consider the engagement to be a definite one, the only matter omitted being the time of delivery. If you have any decided preference for one month over another you must let me know; otherwise it will be left to Dr Cotting, the Curator, to arrange with you when the time for making his preparations for another year arrives.

I remain
Yours very truly
Augustus Lowell
