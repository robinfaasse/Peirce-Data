R. H. Southgate, President.

Chicago ____________ 190

Your list of references is being
got ready by the Public Library
people & will be ready on
Dec. 12. This costs you nothing
and you can get a good number
of copies for distribution to
friends (and enemies, if as I
find it hard to believe you have
any). I imagine that not
more than 50-75 people will
need syllabi at the Hall, so
you will have of these also
a goodly number for future
use. I am surprised to
learn that the Carnegie
would not help you out by
a few hundred dollars for
publication.
