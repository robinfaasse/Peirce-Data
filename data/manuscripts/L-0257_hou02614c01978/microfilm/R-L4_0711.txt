R. H. Southgate, President.

Chicago, [date]Nov. 17 1903[/date]

My dear Mr Peirce,

Your pleasant letter of the 7th has found me among travels & I hasten to reply. My secretary (Miss Esterbrook) opened (at my request) your letter and acted according to your instructions in regard to the printing matter. Since you agree with me that it will be unwise to cast your pearls before swine & to waste syllabi upon mere curiosity seekers who may attend the first few lectures there will be time enough to print after you arrive in Boston.
