Boston [date]12th July '92[/date]

Professor Charles S. Peirce,

My dear Sir,

Agreeably to your
suggestion I send you a check for
$450.00 being one fourth of the amount
to be paid by the Lowell Institute
for your course of twelve lectures, to
be given next winter.

I am very glad to have been
able to be of service to you & beg
that you will believe me to be

Yours very truly

Augustus Lowell
