Jan. 1, 1904.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I hope that you got home safe and have only pleasant recollections of your Boston visit.

By registered mail to-day I am forwarding to Milford your copy returned by Mudge. In the hope that it will arrive safe, I am, as always,

Sincerely yours,
[ins]W. T. Sedgwick[/ins]
Curator.

Professor C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Penn.
