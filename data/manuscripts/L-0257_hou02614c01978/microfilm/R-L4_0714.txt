Nov. 23, 1903.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I am very glad to know that you have
arrived in town and I am sending over by
special messenger twenty-five tickets for
your personal use.

Please ask for me in the Lower Hall of
the Rogers Building, 491 Boylston Street
to-night when you arrive, at about ten minutes
of eight, in order that I may have the pleasure
of escorting you to the Office and later to
the platform.

Faithfully yours,
[u]W. T. Sedgwick[/u],
Curator.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
6 Prescott Hall,
474 Broadway,
Cambridge, Mass.
