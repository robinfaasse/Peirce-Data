My dear Sir,

I am sorry to hear that
you have been suffering from the
Grippe. The disease has made terrible
havoc here especially among old people.
It is, certainly, a subject of congratulation
to be done with it.

It is my habit to allow a
reasonable amount for the expenses
of diagrams & slides, where they are used
in the lectures. Perhaps the enclosed
answers to probable questions will give
you all the information you desire.

I remain,
Yours very truly
Augustus Lowell
