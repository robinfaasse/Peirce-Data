Metaphysics is supposed to be the most difficult, not to say
impossible, of sciences; although almost every one of us has
his own little system of heaven and earth all sketched out
in its general and [del]its[/del] [ins]most[/ins] difficult features, the easier
details being unattacked. Whether or not this science really
is so difficult we are not in a condition to say as long
as it fails to conform to the conditions which have made
the modern physical and natural sciences so successful.
The great factors of that success have been of a moral
nature.
