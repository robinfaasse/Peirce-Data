At this point, the matter passes out of the purview of philosophy.

One more lineament of contemporary philosophy seems worth attention while a dictionary lies open to our meditations. It is that, though the literary style is still generally employed in philosophical writing, [ins]and this very work before us is marked by readable[/ins] the days of its application to these subjects seem numbered. The majority of future contributions to philosophy will probably take the form of memoirs, like those of other sciences, such as mathematics, which is no more special than is philosophy. A scientific memoir written in any but the most unadorned style would be as ridiculous as if it appeared in rhymed pentameters. It is a truism to say so; and this truism implies another; namely, that there is such a thing as a good style and a bad style for a scientific memoir.
