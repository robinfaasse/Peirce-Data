During the last half century several branches of philosophy and of psychology have taken rank among the regular sciences. Others have almost attained that position; and students are everywhere putting forth their most strenuous endeavors to bring the remaining parts of these studies to the same stage of development. At such a moment especially a universal agreement upon a terminology, consisting of well-chosen words with exact scientific definitions would immensely further the object that all have so much at heart.
