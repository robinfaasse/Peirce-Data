old man, angrily, "Don't you see you
are spoiling my figure." The soldier slew him
on the spot. It was Archimedes. When
the Roman General Marcellus learned
his fate, he had the body honourably
interred and a monument erected
over the grave, with a figure of a sphere
and a cylinder upon it, in memory of one
of the geometer's greatest achievements.
