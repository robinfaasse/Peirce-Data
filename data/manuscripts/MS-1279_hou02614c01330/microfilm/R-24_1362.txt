<u>centres of gravity</u>

6[sup]th[/sup] If [del]magnitudes[/del] [ins]weights[/ins] placed at certain
distances balance, so equal weights at
the same distances will balance —

7[sup]th[/sup] Every [del]convex[/del] figure [ins]without concavity[/ins] has its centre of
gravity on the inside.

Owing to the loss of certain Archi-
medean books, we have to patch
out the demonstration of Archimedes;
because his demonstrations depend on
what was given in the lost books.
