Now Eratosthenes went to live in [del]Alexandria[/del] [ins]Alexandria[/ins]
about [u]240[/u] B.C. and was there very influential
in regard to the great Edict of Canopus, which
fixed the Ptolemaic calendar, in [u]238[/u] B.C.
This supposes Eratosthenes was at least
35 years old or that Archimedes was
born not later than 284 B.C..

Another thing King Hieron II came to the
throne of Syracuse 270 B.C. and very shortly
after, probably within a year, we find
him consulting with Archimedes about the
celebrated Crown problem. It is impossible
to suppose that Archimedes was then less
than 18 years old. Thus the date 287 B.C.
seems the most probable one for his
birth.
