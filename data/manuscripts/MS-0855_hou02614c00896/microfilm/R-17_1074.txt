§2. Intention of this work.
Three and only three essentially different
kinds of reasoning.
[u]The 3 kinds defined.[/u]
The best proof that there is no fourth kind
is inductive.

§3. My subject of logical Critic coupled
with facts of human life, [ins]purpose[/ins] [del]This[/del] vague
and needs rewriting—that is what the
intention is. Earliest conscious knowledge
consists in percepts. First impression of sense.
The process of formation of percepts perfectly
analogous to reasoning. Yet not reasoning
because not controlled. It may be objected
that reasoning is equally forced upon us. And
this will naturally be joined to denial of Free Will.
Thus we are brought to the question of whether
[del]or not[/del] all phenomena are governed by immu-
table laws. But this had better be post-
poned until the principles of Critic are
settled.
