On the other hand, we cannot but recognize that to whatever
character of the relationship between the atoms the quality of
hardness or any similar quality may be due, that character,
when discovered would be found to consist in the truth of
some conditional proposition expressing that if certain con-
ditions be fulfilled certain phenomena must result. It is im-
possible to imagine of what other nature it possibly could be.
We are thus forced to admit that the truth of a conditional
proposition stating that some state of things will accompany
some hypothetical state of things that may be or may not be,
can constitute a real fact about an individual thing. In
fact all the general phenomena of physics are of that nature.
