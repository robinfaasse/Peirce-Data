Let the small letters denote such grounds. Thus let $A - B = a$.

Let a small letter preceded by a negative sign and enclosed
in brackets denote the inverse of the ground denoted by the
small letter.

Then by the definition of inversion it follows that if $A - B = a$
$B - A = [-a]$

Note This would not follow by ordinary syllogism, by which
it does not even follow that if
