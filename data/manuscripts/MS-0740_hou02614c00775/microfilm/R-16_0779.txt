I call a ground of relationships [u]continuous[/u], when any 
correlate whose relate is related to it upon any such ground, is 
itself in the same relation to a possible new correlate; and when 
the same is true of the relations of such grounds of relation to 
each other.

I call a kind of ground of relationships [u]commutative[/u], when 
every such ground is related to some other possible one upon the 
same ground on which any other such ground is related to another.
