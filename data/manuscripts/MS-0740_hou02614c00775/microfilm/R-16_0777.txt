I say, then, that
[del]A difference[/del] [ins]a difference[/ins] in time is a quantitative, continuous, commutative ground
of disquiparance; and
[u]a difference[/u] in space is a quantitative, continuous, non-com-
mutative ground of disquiparance.

I proceed to explain these phrases. I call a relation quantitative,
when the internal character of the correlate, determined by a relation
is the same[ins]*[/ins] as the internal character which would be determined
in that correlate were it made a relate to something else upon the
same ground. Thus suppose that [u]x[/u] is twice [u]y[/u]; and suppose [u]y[/u]
is twice [u]z[/u]; then [del]y[/del] [ins]x[/ins] & [u]z[/u] being determinate quantities, [u]y[/u] is deter-
mined in the same respect by both relations. It is different if
A kills B and B kills C; by one of these relations B is made a
criminal, by the other a victim. Perhaps the idea of justice is
that all relations are quantitative.
