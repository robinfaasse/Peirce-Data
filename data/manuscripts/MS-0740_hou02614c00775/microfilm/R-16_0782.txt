Let a capital minus a small letter denote the object to which that [del]capital[/del] [ins]object[/ins] is related upon that ground. Thus if $A-B=a$, let $A-a=B$.

Let a capital plus a small letter denote the object to which the object denoted by the capital is related upon the ground denoted by the small letter. Thus if $A-B=a$ so that $B-A=[-a]$ let $B+a=A$.

If $A-B=a$, then $A-B=[-[-a]]$ so that $A+[-a]=B$ but $A-a=B$ so that $A+[-a]=A-a$.

If the correlate of one relation is the relate of a second, let the [del]symbol of[/del] small letter [del]which denotes[/del] [ins]symbol of[/ins] the ground of the former plus the small letter [del]symbol[/del] [ins]symbol[/ins] of the ground of the latter, denote the ground of the relation of the relate of the first relation to the correlate of the second. Thus if $A-B=a$ $B-C=b$, let $a+b=A-C$.
