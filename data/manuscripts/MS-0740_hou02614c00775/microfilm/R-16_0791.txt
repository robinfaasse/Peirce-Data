The reduction of polynomials consists of three elements

$1\overset{\text{st}}{=}$ in associating terms

$2\overset{\text{nd}}{=}$ in adding coefficients

$2\overset{\text{nd}}{=}$ in transposing terms

We have seen that $(\alpha + \beta) + \gamma = \alpha + (\beta + \gamma)$ and any of these plus signs
may be made minus by substituting $\alpha' = -\alpha$ $\beta' = -\beta$ $\gamma' = -\gamma$. Add-
ing coefficients is part of the multiplication of polynomials.
Transposing terms is allowed by the formula $\alpha + \beta = \beta + \alpha$.

$2\overset{\text{nd}}{=}$ in changing all the signs of a polynomial to be subtracted.
