War Department,
SIGNAL OFFICE,
Washington,

[date]November 10, 1902.[/date]

M. [del]r.[/del] [ins]Prof.[/ins] C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pennsylvania.

My dear Mr. Peirce:

It is impracticable for me to take any action in regard to Mr. Samuel D. Mott's invention for a flying machine. I suppose there are on file in this office some four or five hundred similar propositions. I have neither time to examine such projects, of which I know nothing, nor is there any fund available for their construction. Under these conditions, as you fully understand, I can have but one answer to give to an inventor.

Appreciating your kindly interest in the work of this Corps and your complimentary allusions to the pendulum work of the Lady Franklin Bay Expedition, to which you were so prominently connected with regard to the installation,

I am

Yours truly,

[fig]Signature of A. W. Greely[/fig]
Brigadier General,
Chief Signal Officer, U.S.A.
