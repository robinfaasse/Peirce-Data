I simply instance these matters to show that I have not been dis-
posed to go before the public criticizing either the instruments or
the instructions that I have received, but have refrained from making
other than observations bearing on certain statements which it seemed
to me might lead to misapprehension of the facts among those who used
these observations for scientific purposes. I beg to assure you that
I have always been impressed with your earnestness and zeal in con-
nection with these observations, and I know that you were very decided
in insisting upon the conditions under which the work should be done.
I cannot well believe that any one should consider you as desirous of
pulling down a house which has been substantially built with your
hands; for to your assiduity, skill, and knowledge must be credited, as
I have always understood, the [ins]latest and[/ins] [del]most[/del] important advances in the methods of
application of pendulum observations.

Regretting exceedingly that you should have labored under the
misapprehension that I viewed your report as blaming Mr. Israel or my-
self, I am,

Very sincerely yours,
[fig]signature of A. W. Greely[/fig]
Chief Signal Officer.

P.S.--I have marked my letter private, as you have done, but if you
should so desire, I am willing later to give you authority to use it
or any portion of it as you may please.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.
