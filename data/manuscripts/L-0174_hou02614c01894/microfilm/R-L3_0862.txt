[u]Signal Office,[/u]
WAR DEPARTMENT,
Washington City, [date]November 22nd, 1888.[/date]

My Dear Sir:

I have had pleasure in ordering sent to you Volume I of the Lady Franklin Bay Expedition Report, and will send the 2nd Volume as soon as received from the printer.

I have received a few advance extracts of Volume II and I send you to-day 6 copies of that portion relating to Pendulum Observations.

I am, very respectfully,
Your obedient servant,

[fig]signature[/fig]
Chief Signal Officer.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey,
Washington, D. C.
