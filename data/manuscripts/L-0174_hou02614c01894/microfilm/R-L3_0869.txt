Dict.-A.

**Office of the Chief Signal Officer,**
**Washington, D. C.**

[date]December 4, 1888[/date]

Dear Sir:

General Greely directs me to acknowledge the receipt of your letter of 28th ultimo, enclosing two notes for insertion in Volume 2, Report of the Lady Franklin Bay Expedition, and to say he regrets that a short absence from the city on public business prevents his answering your communication at this time, but that he will have pleasure in giving it his attention immediately on his return.

Very truly yours,

[fig]signature of Robt. Craig[/fig]
1st Lieut. 4th Art'y, A. S. O.,
Assistant and Adjutant

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.
