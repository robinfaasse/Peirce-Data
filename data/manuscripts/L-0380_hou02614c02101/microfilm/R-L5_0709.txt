Vienna. Austria
[date]October. 21. 1895[/date]

My dear Sir:

Your letter of August 14
found me after long wanderings.

We have not lived in Philadel-
phia since 1889 when we sold
the Rittenhouse Square house.

Most of the time since that
we have been travelling about
and as I have no house now
and no library I could make
no use of the volumes of the
London Philos. Trans. and in fact
no place to put them.
