Very well. Suppose the tortoise has in the
beginning a start of .1 kilometre ahead of
Achilles and runs .1 of a kilometre every hour
while Achilles runs 1 kilometre every hour.
Then Achilles will reach the spot where the
tortoise first was in .1 of an hour. The tortoise
in that .1 hour will have run .01 kilometre
and [del]the[/del] Achilles will reach that point in .01 hour
longer or .11 hour from the start. But in that
last .01 hour the tortoise will then have run
.001 kilometres & Achilles will reach that point
in .001 hour more or .111 hour from the
start. Thus the [del]distances at[/del] [ins]times at which[/ins] Achilles
will pass those previous places of the
tortoise will be

.1 hour
.11 hour
.111 hour
.1111 hour
.11111 hour
etc

from the start.
