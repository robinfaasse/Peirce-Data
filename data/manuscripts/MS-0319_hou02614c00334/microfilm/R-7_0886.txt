It is time to say what pragmatism is. But first I must remove a great and widespread misapprehension by saying what it is not. It is not a doctrine of metaphysics. I haven't an English testament by me, but I will quote the Vulgate whose language is to my apprehension equally grand and fascinating. Pragmatism is the Johannes Baptista of a new metaphysical light.
