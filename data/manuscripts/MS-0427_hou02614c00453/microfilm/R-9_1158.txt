This seventh Genus is itself stupendous, embracing not
only Speech, but all modes of communication, and
under Speech, not only all dialects, in their Grammar
and vocabularies, but also in their styles of composition.

some notions of the different families of speech. I have
never seen any classification which was not most
crude. The old division into Inflected, Agglutinative,
