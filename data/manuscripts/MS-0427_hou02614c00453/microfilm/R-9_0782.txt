We must not forget, — and the biologists would remind
us of it, if we did, — that a natural classification is
genetic, and that a classification the result of growth
will naturally be hierarchical, or genealogical. The
biologists are constantly crying, — such a form is an
alpine species, such and such is a marine form.
If there are alpine species, why are not genera divided
across, so as to place the alpine species of all genera
on one side of [ins]a[/ins] dividing line; — and why is not
the same thing done with reference to every general
cause of variation? If there is no reason but conve-
nience for not making this cross-classification, let
us break with the biologists on this point. For we only
accord weight to their authority so far as they are observers
and exponents of the ways of nature. Questions of con-
