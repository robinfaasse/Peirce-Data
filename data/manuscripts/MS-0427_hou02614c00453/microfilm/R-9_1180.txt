The fourth is Ethics; of which the [del]proper problem for each
person is simply to determine what it is for which he
is deliberately prepared to wish.[/del] [ins]It is certainly, one of the
very subtlest of studies.[/ins] The whole course of it seems to
consist in painfully extricating oneself from one pitfall
only straightway to fall into another. It might seem that
logic was desirable in this deliberation; but I fear that
logic, as a definite theory, can be of no avail until one knows
what it is that one is trying to do, [ins]which is precisely what ethics is to determine.[/ins] On the contrary, that has to
be settled before one can form any sound system of logic,
as we shall see in due time.
