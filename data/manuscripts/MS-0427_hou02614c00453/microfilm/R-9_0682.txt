All classification, whether artificial or natural, is the
arrangement of objects according to ideas. A natural clas-
sification is the arrangement of them according to those
ideas [ins]from[/ins] which their existence results. No greater merit can a
taxonomist have than that of having his eyes open to the
ideas in nature; no greater [del]more[/del] deplorable blindness can
afflict him than that of not seeing that there are
ideas in nature which determine the existence of objects.
