The dependence of the Psychical sciences upon Philo-
sophy is no less manifest. A few years ago, indeed, regenerate
psychology, in the flush of her first success, not very wisely
proposed to do without metaphysics; but I think that
today psychologists generally perceive the impossibility
of [del]its[/del] doing [ins]such a thing[/ins]. It is true that the psychical sciences are
not quite so dependent upon metaphysics as are
the physical sciences; but, by way of compensation,
they [del]are more dependent[/del] [ins]must lean more[/ins] upon logic. The mind works
by final causation, and final causation is logical cau-
sation [ins]Note, for example, the intimate bearing of logic upon grammatical syntax.[/ins]; moreover, everything in the psychical sciences is
