We can now see how the different departments of Dynamics are related to one another. These departments are, the Dynamics of a particle, the Dynamics of Rigid Bodies, the Dynamics of numerous collections (as in the [del]cinematical[/del] theory of gases), [ins]finally,[/ins] [del]and[/del] Hydrodynamics (including the general [del]theory[/del] [ins]theorems but not the laws[/ins] of elasticity and of pneumatics). It is true that the usual division is into Statics and Dynamics, [ins]or[/ins] into Kinematics, Statics, and Dynamics.
