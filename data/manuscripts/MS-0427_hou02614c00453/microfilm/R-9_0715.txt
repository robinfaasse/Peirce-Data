How far then are the [del]phys[/del] psychical sciences influenced
by physiognosy, or how far ought they to be so influenced?

The theory of psychophysical parallelism would seem to im-
ply that there is and can be no influence at all. But I must
confess myself to be of the party which thinks that no psychical
fact, as such, can be observed. The direct percept, as it first
appears, appears as forced upon us brutally. It has no gene-
rality; and without generality there can be no psychicality.
