[del]and Astronomy[/del]

Astronomy and Geognosy, then, will be the two
Families of this Order. Astronomy has two Genera.
The first studies the facts as they are during the
present state of things; the second produces a
theory of how the present state of things came.

[marginalia]
Genera and
Species
of Astronomy
[/marginalia]

The first Genus has three Species. The first
produces a virtual picture of the heavens. It catalogues
the heavenly bodies and describes their appearance,
configuration, [del]and physical appearance[/del] [ins]etc.[/ins] The second species ascertains
their [ins]regular[/ins] motions. The third studies their radiations.
