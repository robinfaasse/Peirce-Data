This, however, is not all. There is a third Suborder, unless it should be considered as a Family of the first, as its analogy with a Family of General Nomological Physics makes me suspect of being. The [del]Kin[/del] [ins]Cinematical[/ins] Theory of Gases deduces the laws of the elasticity, expansion, viscosity, diffusion, [del]and[/del] conduction [del]of gases[/del] and other phenomena of gases from the assumption that a gas consists of an enormous collection of bodies moving independently of one another except when they happen to come [ins]in pairs[/ins] within [del]maximum distance of[/del] [ins]the sensible[/ins] mutual attraction. [del]A[/del] regularity necessarily springs out of multifarious [del]multitudinous[/del] irregularity, as the doctrine of chances shows it must. There is an analogous science, where persons are substituted for single molecules, and the market takes the place of the gas. This Nomological Economics, with some slightly developed extensions that go by the name of Mathematical Psychics. We may call it Nomological Sociology.
