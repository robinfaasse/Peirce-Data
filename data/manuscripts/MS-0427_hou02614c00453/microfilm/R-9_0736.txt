Three items only of Brinton's Somatology belong to psychognosy.
They are, 1st, prosopology, so far as that refers to the sub-science of
physiognomy, 2nd, psychology, and 3rd, [del]crani[/del] [ins]craniology[/ins] and anthropology.
A great part of his Ethnography simply considers men as biological forms.
So too the physical geography, geology, and paleontology that he includes.
