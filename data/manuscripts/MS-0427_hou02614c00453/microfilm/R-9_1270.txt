Invariably follow the dictates of Instinct in prefe-
rence to those of Reason when such conduct will
answer your purpose: [ins]that is the prescription of Reason herself.[/ins] Do not harbor any expecta-
tion that the study of logic can improve your
judgment in matters of business, family, or
other departments of ordinary life. Clear as it seems
to one that certain dicta of my conscience are un-
reasonable, and though I know it may very well be
wrong, yet I trust to its authority emphatically rather
than to any rationalistic morality. [ins]This is the only rational course (I say it advisedly).[/ins]
