We come now to Descriptive Physiognosy which
is the study of individual things. It divides itself
at once into two Families, according to the two
individual objects which physical nature presents
to our study. These are, first, the Heavens, and second,
the earth. I give them this order because the study
[del]Astronomy[/del] has a decidedly nomological color, and
while Geognosy has an equally marked [del]classifi-[/del]
[ins]catory[/ins] tinge. Astronomy has two Genera, the old
astronomy with two Subgenera; the first relating
to positions and motions, the second to telescopic
appearances, including photometric and photo-
graphic astronomy. The New Astronomy has at
present two Sub Genera, [del]Both[/del] Spectroscopic and
Bolometric astronomy. Geognosy
