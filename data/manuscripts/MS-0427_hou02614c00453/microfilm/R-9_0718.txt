We cannot [ins]now no[/ins] longer postpone the recognition of a
second Sub-branch of theoretical science. It is a department
perfectly well recognized. It belongs by virtue of its purpose
to the Branch of Theory; yet varies enough in its purpose
from the active science to be erected into a Sub-Branch.
It is the subject of Humboldt's [u]Cosmos[/u], Comte's [u]Philosophie Positive[/u],
and Spencer's [u]Synthetic Philosophy[/u]. It is science [u]en retraite[/u],
[u]Wissenschaft a. D.[/u] Its design is to sum up the results of
