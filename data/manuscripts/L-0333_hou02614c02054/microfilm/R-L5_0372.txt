My dear Father

Three objections of Schott's to my paper would amount to nothing if they were correct. But they are incorrect.

The scale he thinks incorrect is correct to [ins]$\frac{1}{100,000}$[/ins] according to Hilgard & affect my result to the last figure must be wrong to the [ins]$\frac{1}{300}$[/ins] part.

been received with favor but I still favor addressing the secretary reinforced by letters from Rood & Mayer and putting my resignation in the hands of Patterson contingently upon a refusal. I prefer working for somebody who will consider the character of my work.

Best love to all at home. Millions of kisses to Mother.

Your loving son
C. Peirce
