U. S. Coast Survey Office.
Washington. June 16 1873

Dear Father

Seeing what [del]an[/del] [ins]little[/ins] interest the scientific [del]of[/del] [ins]of[/ins] people take in pendulum work gives me a strong desire to go because I am confident that without a great expense I could make one of the most valuable pendulum expeditions there have been. Almost every government but ours has sent one.

Repsold is engaged to make me a reversible pendulum as

[page break]

chamber in order to determine the density of the earth & I think this would be an experiment of high value.

I would then go to Europe to the places I have mentioned & personally oversee the completion of my instrument at Repsold's and by being on the ground I could get it finished if they delay about it.

This is a very large scheme I admit but I think its merit is granted that the results would be large & [del]they[/del] would not be affected by the chance of bad weather.

But what would [del]my[/del] pendulum party here do meanwhile? Could I not leave it in charge of Frank Blake? To put this in practice of course it would be necessary to move at once. C.S.P.
