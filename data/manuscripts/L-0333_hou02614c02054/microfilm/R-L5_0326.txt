My dear Father

I have just seen your telegram which pronounces what we have done "tolerably satisfactory." I cannot admit. I think it is no more. I wish to explain only.

I first saw the objectionable paragraph. I carried it to Patterson as I felt bound to do. He at once said Hilgard must answer it. I said I supposed he must be permitted to do so but that I wished it could be otherwise. I said his correction ought not to pass unless it was all that could be desired.

[del]For Patterson[/del] [del]and Mitchell[/del] [del]for then I will see what I wish[/del] [del]to do with them.[/del]

Hilgard will not work against the appropriations because he sees they won't be cut down even if he does. He will therefore give them his strenuous support.

Your loving son
C. S. P.
