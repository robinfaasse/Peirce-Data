You for attending to my roof.
I would write but towards the
end of the day the very little color
I have in that direction is
exhausted. In place of Martha
we have a very respectable per-
son but O what a difference!

I will write to Einbeck privately
& tell him he better write to you
about the transit. I think however
Harkness will have pretty much
his own way.

I dont see the shadow of a rea-
son for giving up your quarters
here.

With best love to mother & to
all I remain affectionately
your son
J. Peirce
