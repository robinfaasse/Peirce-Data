Dear Father

Thanks for your warm sup
port. However I don't see
that my honor is at all en-
gaged in this observation matter
or that a fight would pay.

They can't get along at all
without me in putting the Double
Star through the press so it
will amount to nothing if they
try to get rid of me. I said I
would send copies of some letters
to Winlock but I haven't time.
