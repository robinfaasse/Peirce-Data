Dear Father

I find this letter in
my pocket. It was evidently
for you but I never thought
to give it to you.

Earnshaw professes to solve
Laplace's Equation. The solution
is something like this

$$V = \frac{F(\sqrt{x^2+y^2} + z\sqrt{-1}) + f(\sqrt{x^2+y^2} - z\sqrt{-1})}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2} + z\sqrt{-1}}$$
$$+ \frac{F(\sqrt{x^2+y^2} + z\sqrt{-1}) + f(\sqrt{x^2+y^2} - z\sqrt{-1})}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2} - z\sqrt{-1}}$$
