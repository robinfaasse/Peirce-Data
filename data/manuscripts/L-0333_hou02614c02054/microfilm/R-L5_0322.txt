U. S. Coast Survey Office,
Washington, [date]June 18 1873[/date]

Dear Father

A Mr. Trill, an acquaintance of Bradford's, a competent civil engineer, is a draughtsman in the Hydrographic office.

He came to Bradford yesterday & told him that Capt. Wyman of the Hydrographic office is getting up a scheme to have the C. S. hydrographic work transferred to them & that Sargent is to urge it before congress.
