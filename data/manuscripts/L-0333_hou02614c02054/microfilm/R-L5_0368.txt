CENTURY CLUB.
109 EAST 15TH STREET
[date]1878 June 4[/date]

My dearest Father _

I have been speculating about the Assistant-Secretaryship of the Smithsonian. I think Langley whom Gibbs favors would be an excellent man; so good that perhaps I ought to let the matter go, at that. I think I should also fill the place well. It is undoubt-edly a better place that that which I have now. The pay [del]is[/del] [ins]at[/ins] European and American stations, that my pay should be raised. But I do not believe it will be done, unless some incident brings a pressure upon Patterson to recom-mend it. Should a considera-ble increase, not less than $600 be given me, I should feel satisfied.

My memoir will be finished in a few months.

I shall get on to Cam-bridge if I can manage it. But love to all.

Your loving son
C. S. Peirce
