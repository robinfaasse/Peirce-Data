No time to write. Have had hitherto to put off
accounts because Farquhar leaves the 15[ins]th[/ins].
Experiments finished at Kew. They appear to
be very fine. Thus, length of pendulum on four days
agrees to [del]1/1000[/del] [ins]1/10000[/ins] millimeter. [ins]under golden calibration[/ins] Now remains to fix ac-
counts, scrutinize records to see all is satisfactory,
to make some comparisons of standard in Berlin
which they weren't before ready for. I go there for a
few days. Shall leave tomorrow. Shall do accounts
first. As to classification of vids, I have attempted
something in my Logic of Relatives. I fear it is not very
satisfactory however. As to the fourth unit (quaternions)
I had some remarks which you cut out of my paper
in printing it. An absurdity wanted for greater freedom! Am well
[date]July 12, '76?[/date] & Z too. Love into-nuts to all Jem especially.
