[pn]V 2, 2[/pn]

U. S. Coast Survey Office,
Washington, [date]Aug 2 1872[/date]

Dear Father

I need make no remark on enclosed. I carried letter to Judge Richardson & understood it would be all right. Your inkstands were not in the stores. They are sent for. Would you like places for pens on both sides or only on one?

affectionately
C S P
