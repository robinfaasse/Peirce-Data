Coast Survey Office
Washington, [date]November 30, 1872[/date]

To Mr Charles S. Peirce
Assistant U. S. Coast Survey
Sir
You are hereby directed to take charge of the Pendulum experiments of the Coast Survey, and to direct and inspect all parties engaged in such experiments - and as often as circumstances will permit, you will take the field with a party organized by yourself under the estimates made in the usual manner and approved by the Superintendent.

You will be allowed in the performance of your duty for your subsistence one dollar and a half when engaged in office work, and three dollars while you are in the field.

In combination with the pendulum experiments
