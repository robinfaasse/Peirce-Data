Washington [date]Sep 17 1872[/date]

Dear Father

About the matter of an assistant to Mr. Hilgard, I believe he never applied to you to make such an appointment but that you offered to do it. I don't think that with all due allowance for Mr. Hilgard's having other duties than those I have had here that there is the least difficulty in one man's doing the business. If he has an assistant he will have an immensity of surplus energy which I understand he has done well.

Your loving
C. S. Peirce.
