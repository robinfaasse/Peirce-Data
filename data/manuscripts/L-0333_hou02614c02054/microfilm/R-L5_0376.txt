Allegheny Observatory,
Allegheny, Pennsylvania.
[date]Jan 16[/date]

My dearest Father

On my arrival here last night I found your letter. I send you an official communication. I should feel it as an indignity if my pay were not raised at this time whether the transfer is made or not. If my pay is not raised then office rent in W. G. should at least be allowed me.

I have said something of the [del]same[/del] [ins]reply[/ins] to Sylvester.

I wish I could write more but I cannot spare the time.

Love to Mother & all

Your loving
C. S. P.
