Some time ago I wrote to you asking you to arrange with Ward to renew my letter of credit with the Barings, for [del]£1000[/del] [ins]one thousand pounds[/ins]. I hope you will be willing to do so for me pretty soon. Indeed unless you will do so at once I shall be in rather an awkward position.

Paris is fearfully expensive. I was comparing it with Berlin & there I used to pay for the best dinner possible 5 francs without wine. Here with wine a dinner at 5 francs is not fit to keep a man well on at all. Then the theatre was a thaler. Here it is all the way from 6 to 15 francs & over kind. Generally, one very limited kind at each booth. At one for example, nothing but puzzles, at another only Portemonnaies etc. This year "étrennes utiles" preponderate. Of course the Boulevard has been very different from its usual air of mild gaiety. It has been thronged as I supposed only Demon's Row knew how to be thronged, nearly from the Madeleine to the Port St. Martin & even beyond. The booths reach to the Bastille.
