Your letter to Mr. Stiles has been
received. I wrote & said I would
prefer to answer his letter verbally
if he thought it worth his while to
call here.

A man of about 30 I should
guess named Ormond Stone called
here yesterday & seemed to be in a
very petulant frame of mind. He
wanted to study higher mathematics
under you. I told him he should go
to Cambridge. He said he couldn't but
wished to take a tutor [ins]here[/ins] under your super-
vision. I told him I couldn't tell what
you would do but I didn't think you
would make such an arrangement. He
said he had supposed you were a man who
would take an interest in the furtherance
of pure mathematics. I told him so you
were but you had affairs enough already
I thought. I asked him if he had completed
the Calculus. Yes and had gone further
under Professor Safford & he had the
idea you would be glad to make his
acquaintance. He added that his design
was after this study to apply for a degree
