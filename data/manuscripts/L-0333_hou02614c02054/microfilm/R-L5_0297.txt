Yankeeyellan
[date]June 25, 1912[/date]
[/marginalia]

Dear Sir, I have nothing to
add to the enclosed letter
which came back from Berlin
marked (as I read on the
back) "Undeliverable" and
"Not asked for". The address
I believe was suggested by
Mr Schott and I believe
also that of many letters
sent to Europe from this
office none have been
returned as in the present case.

[marginalia]
Best love to Dick. Dorothy
says, her love also. We are
all quite well. Hope you
are so too.
J. S.
[/marginalia]
