[date]New York 1868 March 1.[/date]

Dearest Father

Searle is going to resign
his position as soon as Winlock
returns in order to enter the
Priesthood. Of course, this is a
secret as yet, as Winlock does
not know it. Mr. S. fears that
it will be difficult to supply my
place, and asked me if he might
say to Winlock that I would
stay on for the present. I told
him that I would, if the Coast
