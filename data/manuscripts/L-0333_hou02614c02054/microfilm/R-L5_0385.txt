[date]Dec 3.[/date]

My dearest Father

It is possible I was mistaken in giving $20 as the price for [u]two[/u] but it certainly includes board. Of course, the room is only one flight up. It is close by the bath room.

Mother need not think I was disturbed that things not belonging to me came. Not at all; nor could I expect anybody to put themselves out about packing my things.

An important discovery in electricity has been made here viz that if the current be supposed to consist of negative electricity then a magnet acts on a current just as it does on the conductor.

My lectures continue to interest
