My dearest Father.

There is a room at Miss Marfield's which You and mother can have. [del]calendar[/del] [ins]for the two[/ins] You can come for $20 a week [ins]for[/ins] board, fire & light included. I don't think you would be any better off at the Mt. Vernon Hotel. I haven't enquired there because I know first what they will do, having made the best arrangement possible with them myself. It is a comfortable

A great electrical discovery has been made here. When a current passes at right angles to a magnet, an electromotive force is developed towards or away from the magnet. I think that is the statement.

Sylvester is working at some theorems about numbers which he has established by many trials but cannot demonstrate. One is that the form
$x^3 - 3xy^2 + y^3$
represents every number whose only prime factors are 3 or ($18n \pm 1$)

I have lost one of my claws although I have been taking great pains with my lectures.

Love to all, especially Mother
Your loving C. S. P.
