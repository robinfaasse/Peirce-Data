Dear Father

I call your attention
to this Determination of
Latitude by Talcott's method
& to the note on p 88.

Love to all

C. S. P.

If Bertie is coming on here
(as I hear indistinctly he is) I
would like to have some things
got in New York. Not that I want him
to come.
