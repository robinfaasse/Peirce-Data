My dearest Father

My friend Mr William Appleton is abroad & his son whom I also know is ill. The only member of the firm now in town is Mr Sydney Appleton who is the richest & who don't care for anything. I will however go to see him & I think you may be perfectly assured that they will print the book on favorable terms. This will be infinitely better than Little

I for my part know nothing about his facts; but I [del]do[/del] [ins]✓[/ins] notice the extreme alacrity of these geologists to contradict one and other, without the least examination. It is a pretty lazy kind of science & we might as well say at once that we know nothing about it & are not likely to for a very long time at least.

All we can do now is to speculate as to the consequences of all sorts of suppositions, without knowing whether they are true or not.

With warmest love to all

Your loving son
C. Peirce
