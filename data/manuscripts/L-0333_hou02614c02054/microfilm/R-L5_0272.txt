Pascagoula. [date]1859. December. 15.[/date]
My dear Father -

I am very grateful to you for your
kind & loving note, but I have very
little of interest to write in return.

Our schooner is still aground and at
present appearances it will remain
there at least till the New Year. This is
distressing as there is no work to be done
but go to the P.O. and I am approach-
ing the end of the schooner's library.

Pascagoula is as nice a place as there
is here I suppose, but I see it is
such a gossipping little town, that I
like to keep of the peoples' reach. It is an
excellent place to learn chess.

This week I have had a bad cold in
my head for we had considerable rain
last week, which leaked through a hole
in the deck big enough to put my hand
in directly down into my berth & wet
the mattress through & through. It is
