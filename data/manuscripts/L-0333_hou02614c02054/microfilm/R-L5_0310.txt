If such an appointment is made I should think it would generally be allowed that I was entitled to ask for it. Perhaps I could not do better. The great arguments for it are first that I like the business while my temperament utterly unfits me for computing. Second that the clerks and mechanics here can without any abuse, be made useful in any researches that it may come in my way to make. Some of my friends seem to think that it is not a good enough place for me but I do see but it is a good deal better than what I have in Cambridge where besides every influence is against my promotion in any direction whereas here I can look forward to the position of chief in charge which seems to me better than any Professorship. Washington is not as agreeable a place to live in as Cambridge.
