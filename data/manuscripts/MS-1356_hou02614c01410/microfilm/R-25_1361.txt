A few miles above [del]the[/del] Montreal the St. Lawrence is divided into
two channels by Long Sault Island some 15 or 20 miles long, and
the fall in 17[ins]1/4[/ins] miles is 53 feet. The southern channel is stated by
Bogart to have a flow of 240000 cubic feet per second. I should
be glad to have more minute information in regard to this. Still, it
is probably not far out; for the river has a tremendous velocity, no
doubt over 6 miles an hour, on the average, as I have convinced
myself by observation, and and the Map of the Lake Survey under
Genl. Comstock shows its average depth is over 20 feet, while it is
1500 to 2000 feet wide. Bogart says its average cross section is 17245
