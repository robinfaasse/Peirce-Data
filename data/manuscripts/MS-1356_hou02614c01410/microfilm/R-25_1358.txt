South is a table land of about the elevation of the bank
of the river. Moderate variations of topography. Some hills,
of no great height [low hills]

2 1/2 miles south from St. Lawrence (at point of nearest approach)
is Grass river. Source in Adirondacks. Empties into St L 2 miles
below lower end of Long Sault Island. Moderate volume and not
rapid flow. [It is not sluggish]

Massena is 7 miles from mouth of Grass River. Two dams.

On S bank of St L 1/2 mile below head of Long Sault Island
a small creek. Only storm season flow. Depression very
favorable to inlet. Surface of water here is 46 feet above
surface of the Grass River at the foot of lower dam at Massena.
