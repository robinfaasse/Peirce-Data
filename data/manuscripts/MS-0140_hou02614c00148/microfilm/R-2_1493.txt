The object of the present work is to lay out the whole subject more logically,
to [del]correct[/del] modify Listing's hypothesis of space, according to which there is only
one point at infinity, so as to bring it into harmony with graphics, or pro-
jective geometry, to follow out the suggestion of Listing that an extremity
is nothing but a one-branched node [ins](or nodal line)[/ins] and an isolated point of a line-figure
or an isolated line of a line-figure is nothing but a zero-branched node
or nodal line, and to extend the subject to four dimensions.
