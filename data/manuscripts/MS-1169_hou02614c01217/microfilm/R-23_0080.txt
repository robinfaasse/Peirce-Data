QUADRATIC residue, a number left as remainder after dividing a square number by a given modulus. Thus, 1, 3, 4, 5, 9, are quadratic residues of 11. For 1 = 1² - 0. 11, 3 = 5² - 2. 11, 5 = 4² - 1. 11, etc. But 2, 5, 7, 8, 10, are quadratic non-residues of 11.

A celebrated problem in probabilities, how much ought to be paid for the assurance of being paid $2ⁿ, where n is the number of times that a coin will be tossed up without coming up heads.
