WAVE-SURFACE. A surface whose equation in rectangular coordinates is
[ins]x²/(1-A²r²)+y²/(1-B²r²)+z²/(1-C²r²)=0[/ins]
If upon every central section of a quadric surface be erected a perpendicular at the centre, and points be taken on this perpendicular at distances from the centre equal to the axes of the section, then the locus of these points will be the wave surface. It is frequently called Fresnel's wave-surface, to distinguish it from Huyghens's wave-surface, which is simply an ellipsoid, the latter being the form of the wave-front of a uniaxial crystal, the former that of a biaxial crystal.
