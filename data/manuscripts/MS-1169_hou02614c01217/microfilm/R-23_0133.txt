RECEPTIVE, a. Add: Receptive faculty, the same as presentative faculty, which see.

To acquire knowledge is to receive an object within the sphere of our consciousness. The acquisitive faculty may therefore, also, be called a receptive faculty. Hamilton, Lect. on Metaph. XXI.

We call [del]acceptability[/del] sensibility the receptivity of the soul, or its power of receiving representations whenever it is in any wise affected. Kant, Critic of the Pure Reason, tr. by Mueller p. [51].
