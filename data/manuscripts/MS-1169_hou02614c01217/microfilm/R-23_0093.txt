SPHERE For the definition, put:
1. A solid figure generated by the revolution of a semicircle about its diameter. This is substantially Euclid's definition; the modern definition is a quadric surface having double-contact-with-the contact with the absolute throughout a conic, and therefore everywhere equidistant from a centre. The surface of a sphere is 4$\pi$R$^2$ where R is the radius, its volume is 4/3 $\pi$ [del]R$^3$[/del].

SPHERICAL CURVATURE, the curvature of a non-plane curve, as measured by the radius of the osculating sphere. Spherical lune, that portion of the surface of a sphere included between two great circles. Spherical indicatrix, the spherical curve traced on the surface of a unit sphere by a radius drawn parallel to the tangent to a non-plane curve. Spherical projection [If this term is ever used as a synonym of the globular projection, it is not here correctly defined. Omit.]

SPHERICS, n. For the definition, put:
Geometry of figures drawn on the surface of a sphere.

SPHERO-CONIC, n. A non-plane curve, the intersection of a sphere with a quadric cone having its vertex at the centre of the sphere. Reciprocal sphero-conic, the envelope of the great circles of which the [del]ar[/del] points on the first sphero-conic are the poles. Cyclic arcs of the sphero-conic, the intersections of the cyclic planes of the cone with the sphere.

The principal spirals which have received attention are the spiral of Archimedes, discovered by Conon the Samian, whose tangent has a constant to its radius vector; the hyperbolic spiral whose radius vector is inversely proportional to the angle; the lituus, the square of whose radius vector is inversely proportional to the angle; and the logarithmic spiral, whose angle is proportional to the logarithm of the radius vector.
