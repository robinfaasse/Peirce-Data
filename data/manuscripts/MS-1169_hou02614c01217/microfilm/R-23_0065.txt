[u]Quadric[/u], a. In [u]alg.[/u] and [u]geom.[/u] - of the second degree, quadratic. Where there is only one variable, the word [u]quadratic[/u] is usually employed, in plane geometry [u]conic[/u], and in solid geometry and where the number of non-homogeneous variables exceeds two, quadric. Thus, we say quadric cone, not quadratic or conic cone, [del]and quadric[/del] [ins]a[/ins] Quadric surface, a surface of the second order.
