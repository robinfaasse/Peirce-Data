RHUMB-LINE, n. For the definition (to avoid the loose use of the word direction,) put:
A curveon a sphere or spheroid, the track of a ship sailing on one course, that is crossing
all the meridians at the same angle. Thus, if a ship were to start from the equator and sail north-
east, and there were no land to intercept it, it would go round and hound the pole in a sort
of spiral, and this curve would be a rhumb-line.
