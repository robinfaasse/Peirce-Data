SYNCHRONISM, n. Add: Synchronism of the circle, the property of the circle stated in the proposition
that a body falling under the influence of a constant force from the highest point of a circle
along any oblique line in the plane of the circle, will reach the circumference at the same time,
along whatever such line it falls.

SYNCHRONOUS curve, a curve the locus of points reached [ins]at the same moment[/ins] by particles falling from a fixed point
along curves of a given family.

SYNODICAL, a. In place of the second sentence of the definition of the synodical revolution of a
planet, put: The period of the synodical revolution of Mercury is 115 days, that of Venus is
584 days, that of Mars is 780 days, that of Jupiter is 398 days, and that of Saturn is 378 days.

SYZYGETIC, a. [Gr. syzygos, really meaning paired, but the person who invented the English word
probably thought the Greek meant joined in any numbers] Pertaining to a linear relation, that is,
to a polynomial linear in the variables. Syzygetic function, a function of the form

Ax+By+Cz+etc.

where x, y, z, are the variables, and A, B, C, are arbitrary quantities. Syzygetic multipliers, the
multipliers of the variables in a syzygetic function.

SYZYGETICALLY, adv. With reference to a linear relation or syzygy.

SYZYGY, n. Add: 3. In alg., a linear function in the variables. See syzygetic.

SATELLITE, n. Add: In geom., the satellite of a given straight line with reference to a given cubic
curve in whose plane the straight line lies is the straight line joining the three points at which
the three tangents to the curve at the points of intersection of the first straight line with it a
again cut the curve.

SEXTTACTIC, a. Pertaining to a six-point contact. Sextactic points on a curve, points at which a
conic can be drawn having six-point contact with the curve.

SIX-POINT contact, a contact due to the coincidence of six-points ; in the case of curves, a conta
contact of the fifth order.

SPINODE, n. In geom., a stationary point or cusp on a curve.

STEINER-HESSIAN, n. Another name for the Cayleyan, which see.

SYNTRACTRIX, n. The locus of a point on the tangent to the tractrix which divides the constant line
into portions of given length.

SCROLL, n. Add: In geom., a skew surface, or undevelopable ruled surface

SPHERO-QUADRIC, n. The curve in three dimensions formed by the intersection of a sphere with a quad
ric cone.
