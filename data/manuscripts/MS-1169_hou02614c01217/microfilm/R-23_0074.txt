<u>Hyperbolic quantity</u>, a system of quantity
such that there are in any direction distances
more remote than an infinite distance, which
are imaginary. Thus, if it were the property
of a yard-stick to shorten on receding from
a fixed centre, this might happen according
to such a law that [del]some points should be
further away than the limits up to which the
[del]yardstick[/del] measurements by the yardst[/del]
[del]no measurements by the yard-stick could
ever[/del] no finite number of layings down
of the yard-stick could carry the measure-
ment further than certain limits, be-
yond which there would however be
points. Such measurement would make
a system of hyperbolic quantity.

<u>Imaginary quantity</u>, a quantity
of the form
$$A + B \bar{i}$$
where $i^2 = -1$; in a wider sense, any
impossible complex quantity.

<u>Improper quantity</u>, the same as in-
tensive quantity, as the quantity of perfection.
