RULE, n. Add: Rule of three, the method of finding the fourth term of a proportion when three are
given. If we conceive the four terms of a proportion to belong to the four corners of a square,
in such a way that any corner is to either of its adjacent corners as the other corner adjacent
to the first is to the corner opposite to the first, then the rule to find the fourth term when
three are given, is to multiply the two which belong opposite to one another and divide by the
third. Rule of false, or rule of double position, a method of approximation, based on the assumptio
that the true value sought will divide the interval between two assumed values in the same ratio
[del]as[/del] [ins]any[/ins] function of the true value will divide the interval between the values of the same func-
tion of the two falsely assumed values.
