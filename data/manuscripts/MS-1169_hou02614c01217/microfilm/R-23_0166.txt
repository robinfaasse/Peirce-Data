Names of fixed stars and constellations from T to the end.

TARAZED. [Ar. shāhīn tārā-zed, star-striking falcon] An orange star of magnitude 2.8, the preced-
ing of two in the left shoulder of the eagle, gamma Arietis.

TAYGETA. The northernmost of the brighter pleiads; the one at a corner of the group with another
near to it. It is a yellowish star of the 4.4 magnitude.

TAURUS- An ancient constellation and sign of the zodiac, representing the forward part of a bull.
It contains the first magnitude star Aldebaran, the second magnitude star Nath, and the striking
group of the Pleiades.
