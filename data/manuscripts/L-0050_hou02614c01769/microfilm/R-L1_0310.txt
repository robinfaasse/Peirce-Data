Mr C. S. Peirce
My dear Sir,
I did not know
your work would be placed
at so large a price when I
made my proposition. My
[u]Bibliography of Chemistry[/u]
(1200 pp.) sells at $3.50
my "[del]Contributions of Numismatics[/del]
[ins]to Alchemy[/ins]" at $1. so I
should be still much in your
debt. I return to America
in June & then we can again
approach the subject.
Your heavy letter was
forwarded to me here & cost
me 45 cents postage! but
you are not to blame.
Allow me to remark that
your claim that [u]Peregrinus[/u]
was the first to devise a piece
of apparatus to obtain an
experimental answer to a question
