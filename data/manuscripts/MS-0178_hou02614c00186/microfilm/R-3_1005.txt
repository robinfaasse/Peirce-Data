In multiplying in the [del]head[/del] [ins]mind[/ins] there are a variety
of devices which should be taught; such as
multiplying by factors of the whole [del]number[/del]
multiplier and of certain figures of it.

Thus if the multiplier is 2571, we can
multiply by 41 and 61 successively and
add to the product the product by 70.

Of course to multiply by [del]powers of[/del] 5 we
divide by the same power of 2 and then
multiply by 10.

If multiplier and multiplicand are one odd and
the other even, the latter should be divided by two
and the product remultiplied by 2. Both being
add or both even, their product is the square of
[del]their[/del] half sum less the square of their half
difference. Thus $41 \times 19 = (\frac{41+19}{2})^2 - (\frac{41-19}{2})^2$
$= (30)^2 - (11)^2 = 900 - 121 = 779$

The use of [del]a[/del] table of quarter squares will
be explained in small print in the higher
arithmetic, since this is designed to serve as
a handbook as well as a text book.

Cross multiplication, which is indispensible
in multiplying in the head, should also be
taught.

Long multiplication ought to be performed
without the use of the multiplication table;
and the following method should be thoroughly
practised.
