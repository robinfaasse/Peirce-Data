Packs of cards are provided, each
card bearing a number, and these
run up to 101 inclusive. The pack
being arranged in order of increasing numbers
from back to face is dealt out into piles.

and by nines

These and all the other modes of counting must be
drilled into them until these operations become easier
and more sure than talking.

There will be also some drill, — not so much, — upon
descending counts. Not much is necessary in order to
render it perfectly easy.
