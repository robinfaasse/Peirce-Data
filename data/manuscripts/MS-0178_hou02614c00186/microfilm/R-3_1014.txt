Continue this process until the initial number
is reproduced as a number to be treated in
the same way.

Then the succession of figures struck off
in their reverse order from last to first
are the circulating decimal.

<u>Examples</u>

To find the circulating decimal representing
$\frac{2}{79}$, 8 is the current multiplier 2 the initial
number

[fig]Arithmetic calculation for 2/79[/fig]

Initial number = 2

$\frac{2}{79} = \dot{0}25316455696\dot{2}$
