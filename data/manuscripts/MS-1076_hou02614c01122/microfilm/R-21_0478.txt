the most northerly station which ever has been, or is ever likely to
be, occupied for exact scientific observations; and it was there
swung, on 16 days, by Lieutenant Greely, aided by Mr. Israel, with a re-
markable degree of skill. [ins]¶[/ins] The directions accompanying the instru-
ment were that the pendulum should be swung on 8 days, once each day
with heavy end down, and twice with heavy end up, the one swinging in
the former position being intermediate in time between the two in
the latter. After these eight days swingings, the knives were to be
removed and interchanged, and eight days more work was to be done in
the same manner in the new position of the knives. This programme
was faithfully carried out; but after the interchange of knives the
periods of oscillation show a large change, and this is of such a
character as not to be eliminated by the formula for the reversible
pendulum. No certain explanation of this change can, at present, be
given.

knife-edges forming a part of it) was left behind; but the pendulum
it self was courageously brought away, and carried down to the camp
