LENSFIELD COTTAGE, CAMBRIDGE, [date]21 MAY, 1886[/date].

MY DEAR SIR,

A LONG TIME HAS ELAPSED SINCE A LETTER PASSED BETWEEN US. I
AM AFRAID THE FAULT IS MINE; FOR THOUGH I HAD BEEN EXPECTING TO
HEAR FROM YOU, YOU HAD STILL BETTER REASON TO EXPECT A LETTER
FROM ME.

A COMMITTEE OF THE ROYAL SOCIETY ARE INTENDING TO MAKE SOME
ADDITIONAL PENDULUM OBSERVATIONS, AND THEY HAVE JUST RECEIVED A
VOTE OF MONEY WHICH WILL SUFFICE FOR A PORTION OF THE WORK.

OUR FIRST OBJECT IS TO MAKE A FRESH COMPARISON BETWEEN GRAVITY
AT THE AMERICAN STATIONS AND GRAVITY AT KEW AND GREENWICH. FOR
THIS PURPOSE WE SHOULD WANT THE PENDULUMS BACK AGAIN IN THE SAME
CONDITION IN WHICH THEY HAVE BEEN EMPLOYED IN THE AMERICAN WORK.
BUT

1. WE DO NOT WANT TO INTERRUPT WORK NOW GOING ON, IF SUCH
THERE BE, IN AMERICA WHICH DEPENDS ON THE CONTINUED EMPLOYMENT
OF THE SAME PENDULUMS.

2. IT MAY PERHAPS BE ADVISABLE BEFORE RETURNING THE PENDULUMS
TO SWING THEM AFRESH AT WASHINGTON, OR NEW YORK, OR BOTH. I
SHOULD BE GLAD TO KNOW YOUR OPINION AS TO WHETHER SUCH A RE-DE-
TERMINATION WOULD BE DESIRABLE. IF THE PENDULUMS HAVE NOT BEEN
SWUNG AT THOSE PLACES WITH LEVELLED AGATES, OR IN DEFAULT OF THAT
ON AGATES THE ERROR OF LEVEL OF WHICH WAS ACCURATELY KNOWN, SUCH
A REDETERMINATION WOULD SEEM TO BE REQUIRED.

3. FOR FEAR OF THE KNIFE EDGES GETTING RUSTED IN THE VOYAGE
IT MIGHT PERHAPS BE WELL TO RUN A DROP OF MELTED PARAFFIN ALONG
THEM. IT COULD EASILY BE CLEANED OFF WITHOUT INJURING THE EDGES
WHEN THEY WERE GOING TO BE USED.

4. CONSIDERING THE WAY IN WHICH PACKAGES ARE LIABLE TO BE
TOSSED ABOUT ON SHIPBOARD, SPECIALLY IN LADING AND UNLADING, IT
WOULD BE DESIRABLE TO ENTRUST THEM SPECIALLY TO THE CARE OF THE
CAPTAIN OF THE VESSEL (UNLESS SOME RESPONSIBLE PERSON CAME OVER
WITH THEM) AND TO HAVE THEM KEPT IN THE CAPTAIN'S CABIN. WE
SHOULD BE PREPARED TO OFFER HIM AN APPROPRIATE HONORARIUM.
