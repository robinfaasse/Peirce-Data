LENSFIELD COTTAGE, CAMBRIDGE, [date]13 FEB. 1886[/date].

MY DEAR SIR,

I RECEIVED YOUR LETTER YESTERDAY. I AM VERY GLAD TO FIND
THAT IN THE AMERICAN SURVEY THE PENDULUMS WERE ALWAYS SWUNG ON
LEVELLED AGATES. I DO NOT THINK THAT THE TOUCH METHOD OF COLO-
NEL HERSCHEL IS A SAFE THING TO TRUST TO, THOUGH HE SEEMS TO THINK
IT IS THE BEST MODE OF ADJUSTMENT.

IF THE PENDULUMS SHOULD NOT HAVE BEEN SENT OFF WHEN YOU GET
THIS, PLEASE KEEP THEM TILL YOU HEAR FURTHER. WE ARE IN NO
IMMEDIATE HURRY FOR THEM, AS SEVERAL ARRANGEMENTS WILL HAVE TO
BE MADE BEFORE WE CAN USE THEM. AND IN CASE OF DOUBT RESPECTING
ONE OR MORE OF THE AMERICAN STATIONS, IT MIGHT PERHAPS BE DESIRA-
BLE TO REPEAT THE OBSERVATION BEFORE THE PENDULUMS WERE SENT OVER,
SO AS TO GET AN ADDITIONAL GOOD CONNEXION BETWEEN GREENWICH AND
KEW AND THE AMERICAN STATIONS. YOUR GOVERNMENT MIGHT PERHAPS BE
INDUCED TO AFFORD THE SUM REQUIRED FOR THAT, WHICH WOULD NOT BE
LARGE, EVEN IF THEY SHOULD HAVE DETERMINED TO PUT A STOP TO THE
INTERESTING GRAVITY SURVEY WHICH WAS GOING ON.
