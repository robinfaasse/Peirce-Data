[u]Series[/u]

$1 + x + x^2 + x^3 + \&c = \sum_0^\infty x^n = \frac{1}{1 - x}$

$0 + 1 \cdot x + \frac{1}{2} x^2 + \frac{1}{3} x^3 + \&c = \sum_1^\infty \frac{x^n}{n} = \log \frac{1}{1 - x}$

$1 + x + \frac{1}{2!} x^2 + \frac{1}{3!} x^3 + \&c = \sum_0^\infty \frac{x^n}{n!} = e^x$

$1 + 2x + 3x^2 + 4x^3 + \&c = \sum_0^\infty (n + 1)x^n = \frac{1}{(1 - x)^2}$

$0 + x + 2x^2 + 3x^3 + \&c = \sum_0^\infty nx^n = \frac{x}{(1 - x)^2}$
