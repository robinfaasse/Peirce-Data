Commentary
[u]The graph[/u]
-loves-
describes the universe as involving the fact that [u]x[/u] loves y
while leaving it indeterminate what [u]x[/u] and y are. They
are individuals of the universe.
The graph [fig]-loves-
is mother of[/fig]
asserts that some existing individual both loves and is mother
of some existing individual.
The graph [fig]-loves-
injures[/fig]
asserts that something both loves and injures itself.
