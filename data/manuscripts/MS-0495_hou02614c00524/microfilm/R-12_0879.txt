The graph [fig]loves or -loves-[/fig]
asserts that taking any individuals, A, B, C, whatever (same or
different), either A does not love B or is not identical with C;
that is, [del]either[/del] C does not love B. That is, nobody loves
anybody.

The graph [fig]hates- loves-[/fig]
asserts that somebody, A, hates somebody, B, and at the same
time is identical with somebody who is not identical with
anybody [del]who loves[/del] unless that person does not love anybody.
That is, somebody is hated by somebody who loves nobody.
