[ured]22[/ured] colour, though [u]relative[/u] to light, and though it is a [u]would-be[/u], and not
an actuality, is perfectly [u]real[/u], because whether a person who only felt the
grass in pitch darkness believed the testimony that it was green to be true
or thought it a lie, it would be true, and experiment would prove it; and
even though instead of being relative to light the greenness were relative to
eye-sight, yet the same experiment would prove it to be real. For that is
real of which whatever is true is true regardless of whether any person
or number of persons think it to be true or not.
