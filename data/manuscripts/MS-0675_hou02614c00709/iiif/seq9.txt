[ured]8 1/2[/ured] Sense in which "reasoning" is here used.
The definition and the utility of a definition require it to specify
everything essential, and to omit all that is inessential, to its [u]definitum[/u]:
though it may be pardoned for calling special attention to an omission in
order to show that it was not inconsiderate. By "[u]Reasoning[/u]" shall here
be meant any change in thought that results in an appeal for some mea-
sure and kind of assent to the truth of a proposition called the "[u]Conclu-
sion[/u]" of the reasoning, as being rendered "[u]Reasonable[/u]" by an already
existing cognition (usually complex,) whose propositional formulation
shall be termed the "[u]Copulate Premiss[/u]" of the reasoning. The reader
will remark, as the point where this definition most markedly breaks
with actual usage, that it refuses the name of reasoning to the synthe-
