[pn]22[/pn] colour, though relative to light, and though it is a would-be, and not an actuality, is perfectly [u]real[/u], because whether a person who only felt the grass in pitch darkness believed the testimony that it was green to be true or thought it a lie, it would be true, and experiment would prove it; and even though instead of being relative to light the greenness were relative to eye-sight, yet the same experiment would prove it to be real. For that is real of which whatever is true is true regardless of whether any person or number of persons think it to be true or not.
