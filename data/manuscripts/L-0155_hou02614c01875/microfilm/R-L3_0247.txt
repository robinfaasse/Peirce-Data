[marginalia]VP Argyle Mo.[/marginalia]
[date]Oct 28-1911[/date]

Dr C. S. Peirce
Milford, Pike Co. Penn.

Dear Sir

Under separate cover I am sending you a free copy of The correlation of all Philosophy & Science, through the Discovery of their Elements.

In my work I have used the Dictionary of Philosophy & Psychology, in which you seem to have largely dictated the definitions of logic, therefore I would appreciate it as a great favor if you would criticise my elements in the use I have put them to.

It has been a great [del]encouragement[/del] to me to find that I could outline a logic from my elements when I know so little about the science.

Yours truly
Geo. H. Gallup.
