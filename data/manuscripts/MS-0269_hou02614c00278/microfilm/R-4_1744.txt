Let the summits adjacent to $A$
be $B, C, D$

[fig]Diagram showing vertices A, B, C, D, E, F with arrows and labels r, s, t[/fig] [del]Then if $(A-C)$[/del]
[del]$r(A-C) = (B-A)$[/del]

Then there is a quantity
$r$ such that
$r(A-C) = (B-A)$

And a number $R$ such that
$r^R = 1$

And if $E$ and $F$ are adjacent to $B$
either $E = r(B-A) + B$
$F = r(B-A) + B$

And if $r(A-C) = (B-A)$ [ins]$r^{-1}(A-B) = C-A$[/ins]
$s(A-C) = (D-A)$
$s r^{-1}(B-A) = (D-A)$
If $t(A-B) = D-A$
$t = - \frac{s}{r}$ [del]$t = -s$[/del]

If $E$ and $F$ are adjacent
to $B$ either
$E-B = r(B-A)$ , $F-B = t^{-1}(B-A)$
or $F-B = r(B-A)$ , $E-B = t^{-1}(B-A)$

That is the summits adjacent to $B$
are
$B - (B-A)$
$B + r(B-A)$
$B + t^{-1}(B-A)$

If $E-B = r(B-A)$ and $F-B = t^{-1}(B-A)$
then of course $E-B = rt(B-F)$
But [del]$rt$[/del] is not necessarily equal to $tr$
