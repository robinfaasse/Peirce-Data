what is meant by such an expression and what can
be meant by it. What idea can be attached to that
of which there is no idea? For if there be an idea of
such a reality, it is the object of that idea of which
we are thinking and which is not independent of
thought. It is clear that it is quite beyond the power
of the mind to have an idea of something entirely
independent of thought - it would have to abstract itself
from itself for that purpose; and since there is
no such idea there is no meaning in the expression.
