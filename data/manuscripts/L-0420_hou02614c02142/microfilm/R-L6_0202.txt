United States Senate

Oct. 13, 1893.

C. S. Peirce, Esq.,

Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:

Your favor of the 10th instant is re-
ceived and contents noted, for which accept thanks. I am glad
you are interested in the silver question, and trust you will keep
up the good work. You can do much to disseminate knowledge among
the people on this all-important question.

Yours, truly,

[signature]
