It is readily seen that the delineation of a
point is a point, and that [del]to[/del] every point in
the picture corresponds a point in the natu-
ral plane. And to a straight line in the natu-
ral plane corresponds a straight line in the
picture. For the first straight line and the point
[del]of view[/del] lie in one plane, and the intersection
of this plane by the plane of the picture is a
straight line.

All this is just as true for the non-Euclidean
as for the Euclidean geometry.
