Let the conic be the absolute.
Let ACB be a given angle.
From C draw CI and CJ
tangent to the absolute. Take any other vertex C',
and draw tangents C'I and C'J to the
absolute. Through I the intersection of CI
and C'I and J the intersection of CJ and
C'J draw the straight line IJ cutting AC and
BC in A and B, Then AC'B = ACB.
