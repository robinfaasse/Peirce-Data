Now when Keppler first joined Tycho [pn]12[/pn]
Brahe, he found that astronomer deep in
the study of the motions of Mars. Tycho's
system was essentially the Copernican
only he made the earth to be stationary
and the sun to revolve around it carrying all
the other planets revolving round it.

He had a theory which seemed to satisfy
the longitudes at opposition; but he could
not satisfy them at any other time, and
he could not satisfy the latitudes.

What could Keppler do for him? Why he
thought he could straighten out the whole thing
by considering the planet when in opposition
to the real sun instead of to the mean sun.
