In the first place, let me point out to you why Mars was the planet above all to be studied. First it was a superior or outside planet and therefore is not hidden in the Suns rays at the time when it is nearest to us. In the second place, it is the nearest to us of any planet and hence any [ins]given[/ins] displacement [del]of it[/del] appears greater as seen from the earth than the same displacement of any other planet. In the third place, its eccentricity is far greater than that of any other planet, except the [del]difficultly observed and exceptional[/del] Mercury.
