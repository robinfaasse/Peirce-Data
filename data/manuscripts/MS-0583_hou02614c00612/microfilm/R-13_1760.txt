Notes on History of algebraical & logical signs

[del]Ahmes[/del] [ins]Ahmes[/ins], uses [fig]hieratic papyrus-roll sign[/fig] which is the hieratic form of the papyrus-roll, the determinative of thought, almost as a sign of equality. But there can be no doubt that this is really the sign of the word [del]dm[u]d[/u][/del] [ins][fig]hieroglyphs for dmd[/fig][/ins], which means "add", or "together".

In like manner [fig]legs walking forward[/fig], the legs, is used as a sign of addition. But this is only the word [del]p[u]e[/u]w[/del] [ins]or n'w[/ins], Coptic [fig]Coptic character[/fig], go; and [fig]legs walking backward[/fig], the legs reversed, is used as a sign of subtraction, but is only the word [del]En[/del] [ins][fig]hieroglyphs for 'n[/fig][/ins], return.

A better sign of equality is [fig]hieroglyphic sign for xpr[/fig] which is [u]Xpr[/u], make.

The truth is the Egyptian language is a good deal like algebra.
