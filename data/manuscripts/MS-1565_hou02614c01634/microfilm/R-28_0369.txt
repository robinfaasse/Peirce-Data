O partitive morning laugh! Thy twin flamivomous piaff
Spoon out of this double room of grey
To let thy smooth upper arch's half
Boom broadest art mid an archic play!

O glomerous midlight laugh, by secant severant scarf
Bisect in a sheath of barbed smile,
Wherein dark clutches the resultant gaff
Of mancraft make over limpid mile!

O will a new time e'er laugh? insight's exclusion of daff
Stream out of the upright vertebrae?
Th'elan turn make of the ages chaff
All seas on, kin of the Sunken Key?
