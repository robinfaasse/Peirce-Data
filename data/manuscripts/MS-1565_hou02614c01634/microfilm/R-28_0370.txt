All-holding Sesame, master-fully divulgent
All-beaming goal Key, spark fangled, Me-fulgent:
Me-g. beacon'd shoaling crank of windy ocean
Outside disposed, whereby the whole world see Thee—
Over the broadest level floor devotion
Can see thee—seethe, to splay all ways commotion
Of Man who'd see Keying sway! Manhood sand's peaky
Key, keeping up ekeing susurus to be the
Real attitude of sea sons that no go shun
Nor insight blind or blink to blank alee:
Insight's Nightsailing Key, dark's prodigy
O'Man surpassing date mean time pivotion:—
From far, up-heres thy peri style, no-table,
Up in high sounding atmosphere's [del]same[/del] raynow
Of loose earth peel—pellucid dome unstable,
Non-antiquating crystal o'er the Now.
