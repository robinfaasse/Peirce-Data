[marginalia][pn]No. 1565[/pn][/marginalia]

No mental eyes of [del]earth[/del] [ins]art[/ins] e'er hadst thou shone;
Thy entailed gnomon had not played so prone;
Not, at all, fact borne ideas grayed as grown,
Nor Man as halo'd tower of nature known.
Thy fearful lift's bed rock stepped arbor type —
Sight bearing wake's upholding spinelike gripe,
In the last inhauling hall o' the endstead, down
Where glues the gloomy stripe of night's tar frown
And stays the rathe of day's eclipse, where lone,
The moon gropes round in purblind monotone,
For there her tender stars are dark as stone —
Now from that indrawn earth's sky-ganging billow
Sees out about, through ash tied back, thy cone,
Whose spread of insight beam far round out-thrown
With make each slippery rising peak doth crown
Pale as the pallor's tip this pile o' pillow.
