[u]Laura[/u] (A maiden of 28 summers.) See, papa! The
heir of all the Astofellerbilts can't keep his eyes off
me!

[u]Papa[/u]. Don't make yourself ridiculous, Laura. —
When a young lady reaches your years —

[u]Laura[/u]. I think, dear papa, it is hardly for
you to throw that in my teeth. You shouldn't have
married so early.
