[pn]22[/pn] [u]The Future Perfect Middle[/u]
which is oftener passive in meaning
([del]sometimes[/del] [ins]sometimes[/ins] active) occurs in but few
verbs.

This formed by adding [u]σομαι[/u] to the
Perfect Middle stem, lengthening
any [ins]short[/ins] vowel that immediately precedes
that ending. Inflected like Future Middle

There are 2 active future perfects
τέθνηξω ([ins]I shall be dead[/ins]) formed from τέθνηκα
and ἑστήξω (I shall stand) from ἕστηκα

There are several more in Homer

Usually expressed by Perfect Part & ἔσομαι
λελυκὼς ἔσομαι I shall have freed
λελυμένος ἔσομαι I shall have been freed
