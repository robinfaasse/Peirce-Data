<u>The Greek Prepositions</u>
are some Simple, some Compound.

The simple prep. may be conjectured to be cases of
pronouns or nouns, originally signifying relative
spatial positions. [del]They first became adverbs, then prepositions.[/del] Under this theory the D after a prep.
shows at what the position referred to is relative; the A
to what, the G from or belonging to what. Or, originally
Gen. general, Acc. limit.
