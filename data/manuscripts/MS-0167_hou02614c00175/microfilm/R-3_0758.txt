The figure 0 is called the cipher, or zero. It only serves to [del]keep[/del] [ins]remove[/ins] [del]separate[/del] the other figures from the decimal point, but itself signifies no number. The effect of removing any figure [del]to the[/del] [ins]one place[/ins] to the right is to [del]multiply its value by ten.[/del] [ins]make its value ten times as great;[/ins] the effect of every remove to the left is to make its value only one tenth as great as it was before. Thus,

1 is one, 2 is two, 3 is three;
10 is ten, 20 is twenty, 30 is thirty;
100 is a hundred, 200 is two hundred, 300 is three hundred;
1000 is a thousand, 2000 is two thousand, 3000 is three thousand
10 000 is ten thousand,
100 000 is a hundred thousand,
1 000 000 is a million,
10 000 000 is ten million, etc.

0.1 is one tenth, 0.2 is two tenths, 0.3 is three tenths,
0.01 is one hundredth, 0.02 is two hundredths, 0.03 is three hundredths
0.001 is one thousandth, 0.002 is two thousandths, 0.003 is three thousandths
0.0001 is one ten-thousandth, 0.0002 is two ten-thousandths, etc.
