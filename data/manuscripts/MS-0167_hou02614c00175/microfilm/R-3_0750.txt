A [u]hectometre[/u] is a hundred metres.

A [u]kilometre[/u] is a thousand metres, being [ins]19 feet 2 inches[/ins] less than [u]5[/u]/8 of a statute mile.

A [u]myriametre[/u] is ten thousand metres.

Ten million metres is twenty-five metres less than the shortest distance [del]on[/del] [ins]along[/ins] the sea-level from the north pole to the equator. [[u]Ar. Clarke[/u] in Encyclopaedia Britannica, Vol vii. p. 607.]

There are also names for one tenth, one hundredth, one thousandth, and one millionth of a metre.

A [u]decimetre[/u] is one tenth of a metre, [del]or[/del] [ins]a little less than 4 inches[/ins]

A [u]centimetre[/u] is one hundredth of a metre.

A [u]millimetre[/u] is one thousandth of a metre, and twenty five and four tenths millimetres make an inch.

A [u]micron[/u] is a millionth of a metre, a microscopic length.
