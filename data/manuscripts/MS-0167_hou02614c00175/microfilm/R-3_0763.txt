There are other names for some numbers. Some
persons say [u]one and twenty[/u], [u]two and twenty[/u], etc. Some
persons count by [del]ten[/del] scores or twenties; thus, sixty
is called [u]three score[/u], seventy [u]three score and ten[/u],
eighty [u]four score[/u]. "Lo, I am this day fourscore and five
years old," says Moses in the [del]old[/del] [ins]common[/ins] translation of the
book of Joshua (xiv. 10.) Twelve is very often called a
[u]dozen[/u], and thirteen a [u]Baker's dozen[/u]. Twelve dozen
make a [u]gross[/u].
