In Russia, a [u]rouble[/u] worth fifty-eight and two tenths cents
($0.582) is divided into a hundred [u]copecks[/u].

In Denmark, Norway, and Sweden, the unit is a [u]Krone[/u]
of the value of twenty-six and eight tenths cents ($0.268) divi-
ded in one hundred [u]öre[/u].

In Turkey, the unit is a [u]piastre[/u], of which one hundredth is an
[u]aspre[/u], and a hundred make a [u]lira[/u]. The value of the piastre
is four and four tenths cents ($0.044).

In British India, the unit is the [u]rupee[/u], worth thirty-four
and six tenths cents ($0.346). In some places, it is divided
into one hundred cents.
