In Germany, a mark is a money of the value of twenty-
three and eight tenths cents. Its hundredth part is called
a pfennig.

In Holland, a florin or guilder is a money of the
value of forty and two tenths [ins]of our[/ins] cents. Its hundredth part
is called a cent.
