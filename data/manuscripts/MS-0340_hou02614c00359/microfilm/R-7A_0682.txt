A symbol in general and as such has three relations. The first is its relation to the pure Idea or Logos and this (from the analogy of the [del]persons[/del] grammatical terms for the pronouns I, IT, THOU,) I call its relation of the first person, since it is its relation to its own essence. The second is its relation to the [del]consciousness[/del] [ins]mind[/ins] as being thinkable, [ins]or to any language as being translatable,[/ins] which I call its relation to the second person, since it refers to its power of appealing to a [del]person[/del] [ins]mind[/ins]. The third is its relation to its object, which I call its relation to the third person or IT.
