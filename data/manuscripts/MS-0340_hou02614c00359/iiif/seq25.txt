I define logic therefore as the science of the conditions
which enable symbols in general to refer to objects.
At the same time symbolistic in general gives a trivium
consisting of Universal Grammar, Logic, and Universal
Rhetoric, using this last term to signify the science of the
formal conditions of intelligibility of symbols.
