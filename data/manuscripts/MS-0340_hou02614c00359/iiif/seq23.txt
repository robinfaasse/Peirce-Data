A symbol in general and as such has three relations.
The first is its relation to the pure Idea or Logos and this
(from the analogy of the [del]personal[/del] grammatical terms for
the pronouns I, IT, THOU,) I call its relation of the
first person, since it is its relation to its own essence.
The second is its relation to the consciousness as being
thinkable, [ins]or to any language as being translatable[/ins] which I call its relation to the second person, since
it refers to its power of appealing to a [ins]mind[/ins] person. The third
is its relation to its object, which I call its relation to
the third person or IT. Every symbol is subject to three dis-
tinct systems of formal law as conditions of its taking up
these three relations. [del]Take an example: [u]Hominem pagina[/u][/del]
