But it is necessary to call attention to [del]difference[/del] [ins]existential[/ins] between the representation of the Phaneror and an ordinary Graph. Namely, in ordinary Existential Graphs each Spot is connected with the Area on which it stands, and may be regarded as having a virtual Peg by which it is so connected. These Virtual Pegs are not counted in determining whether it is a Medad, Monad, Dyad, etc. In the case of the Phaneron, the Sheet is the Mind, and according to the doctrine of Kant, each Graph [del]must be connected[/del] must represent itself as connected with the mind by the "I think"; but it does not appear that one Graph need be so connected twice.
