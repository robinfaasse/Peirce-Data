Why is [u]red cow[/u] not a natural class, while
[u]cow[/u] is one? Because the former can be de-
fined as the common extent of the classes
[u]red[/u] and [u]cow[/u] and nothing can be uni-
versally predicated of [u]red cow[/u] which is
not predicable either of [u]red[/u] or [u]cow[/u]; where-
as [u]cow[/u] cannot be defined as the com-
mon extent of two or more classes such
that nothing can be universally predicated
of the former which is not predicable of one
or other of the latter. In other words [u]cow[/u]
is a term which has an area; [u]red cow[/u] has
no area, except that area which every
term has, namely that it excites a particular
emotion in the mind.
