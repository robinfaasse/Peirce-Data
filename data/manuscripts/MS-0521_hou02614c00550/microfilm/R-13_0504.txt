Every Hottentot kills a Hottentot
But no Hottentot is killed by more than one Hottentot
[ins]∴[/ins] Hence, every Hottentot is killed by a Hottentot.

If the Hottentots are a finite collection, this reasoning is good. But if the Hottentots go on from generation to generation, the reasoning is not good.
