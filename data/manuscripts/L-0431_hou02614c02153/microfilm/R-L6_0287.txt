Princeton, N. J., December 17, 1895.

C. S. Peirce, Esq.,
176 West 87th St.,
New York.

Dear Mr. Peirce,

Please find enclosed check for $6.00 and
send the Pike County Press to Prof. H. B. Fine, Prof. J. G.
Hibben, Prof. A. L. Frothingham and myself, all addresses Princeton,
New Jersey. I sent a notice of this to the Princetonian,
our student paper, but am not sure that they printed it.

My father's attention has been already called to the
D'Auby pictures, and I am under the impression that he has
seen them during a recent visit to New York. I shall be glad
to go with him to see them the next time that I am in New York.
My father, however, is not buying pictures at present and not
likely to do so for some time to come.

Yours very sincerely,
Allan Marquand

P.S. Only the nos. of Pike Co Press containing your
article need be sent.
