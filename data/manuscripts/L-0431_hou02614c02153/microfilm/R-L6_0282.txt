Larch Street,
Cambridge, Mass.
[date]Nov. 8, 1895[/date].

My dear Peirce,

Of course I want your
papers on the Logic of Rela-
tives. I send enclosed my
cheque for five dollars for
one year's subscription to the
Pike County Press — 1.50 —
and the [del]baggaly[/del] balance
for an addition (of my small
power) to the amount it pays
you for your articles. I know
well how underpaid such
work always is, and I in-
sist on my right to help
right a wrong.

Wm. James sent me
your MS. — First Chapter of
A Short Logic, and Introduction
