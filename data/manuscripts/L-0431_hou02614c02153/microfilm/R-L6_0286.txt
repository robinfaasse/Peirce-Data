Oregon, Mo.
[date]Dec. 4th. 1895[/date].

Dear Mr. Peirce:-
On the 11th. of Nov. ult. I sent you money order for $1.50 in payment of sub. to weekly paper to which you are to contribute 12 articles on Logic the 1st. article was to appear in the issue for Nov. 15th. Did you receive my order? The paper has never reached me.
I am [del]anxious[/del] [ins]desirous[/ins] to read your articles, and do not want to miss any of them.
If my money order went astray, I will procure a duplicate.

Very truly Yours,
Thomas W. Evans
