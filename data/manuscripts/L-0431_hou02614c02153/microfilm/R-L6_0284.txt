JOHNS HOPKINS UNIVERSITY,
BALTIMORE.

[date]November 9, 1895[/date].

Dear Sir:-

Mr. Gilman asked me to
enclose one dollar and a half as a
subscription to the Pike County
Press. If there is any printed
statement regarding the proposed
contribution, it will give him pleas-
ure to have a copy of it and to make
it known so far as he can.

Yours truly,
T. R. Ball
Registrar.

Col. Peirce, &c.
