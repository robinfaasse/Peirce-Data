[u]Syllabus
of Plane Analytical Geometry.[/u]

[del]Let[/del] If $P_{1}$ and $P_{2}$ are any two points in the
plane and $\lambda$ is a number, let
$$\lambda P_{1} + (1-\lambda) P_{2}$$
denote any third point in the same line. That is,
if $P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}$ are three points, the condition that they
shall be in a line is that three numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}$ can be found such
that
$$a_{1} P_{1} + a_{2} P_{2} + a_{3} P_{3} = 0$$
$$a_{1} + a_{2} + a_{3} = 0.$$

In like manner, if $L_{1}$ and $L_{2}$ are any two lines in
the plane, and $\lambda$ is a number, let
$$\lambda L_{1} + (1-\lambda) L_{2}$$
denote any third line crossing their intersection.
That is, the condition that three lines $L_{1}, L_{2}, L_{3}$
meet in a point is
$$a_{1} L_{1} + a_{2} L_{2} + a_{3} L_{3} = 0$$
$$a_{1} + a_{2} + a_{3} = 0.$$

[del]In like manner[/del]
If $P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}$ are any points in the plane, any fourth
point $P_{4}$ is in a line [del]through[/del] [ins]with[/ins] $P_{3}$ and some point
in the line [del]betw[/del] through $P_{1}$ and $P_{2}$ and hence
$$P_{4} = x_{1} P_{1} + x_{2} P_{2} + x_{3} P_{3}$$
where $x_{1} + x_{2} + x_{3} = 1$

or
$$x_{1} P_{1} + x_{2} P_{2} + x_{3} P_{3} + x_{4} P_{4} = 0$$
$$x_{1} + x_{2} + x_{3} + x_{4} = 0$$

In like manner, if $L_{1}, L_{2}, L_{3}$ are any lines in
the plane, any fourth line $L_{4}$ passes through the
intersection of $L_{3}$ and some line through the
intersection of $L_{1}$ and $L_{2}$ and therefore
$$L_{4} = u_{1} L_{1} + u_{2} L_{2} + u_{3} L_{3}$$
where $u_{1} + u_{2} + u_{3} = 1$

or
$$\left. \begin{aligned}
u_{1} L_{1} + u_{2} L_{2} + u_{3} L_{3} + u_{4} L_{4} = 0 \\
u_{1} + u_{2} + u_{3} + u_{4} = 0
\end{aligned} \right\}$$
