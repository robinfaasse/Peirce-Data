The proposition describes a state of things; and it not only
describes it but also asserts its existence. Between describing
and asserting there is all the difference between death and life,
between dreaming and doing. A proposition intends to produce
the effect of an experience or reaction with the world of reality.
It is either true or false, and this duality has its origin in the
essential duality of reaction. For reaction, as experienced by man,
is between the inward and the outward worlds. Now the true
proposition is that which comes from the outward, the [del]objective[/del] [ins]objective[/ins];
