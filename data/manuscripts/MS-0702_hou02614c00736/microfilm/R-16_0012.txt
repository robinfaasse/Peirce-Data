[u]Exercise[/u]

Prove from the above formulae alone, without of the definitions, the following:

$(x+y)+z = (x+z)+(y+z)$     $(xy)z = (xz)(yz)$

$(x+y)z = (xz)+(yz)$     $(xy)+z = (x+z)(y+z)$.

These formulae are called distribution formulae.
