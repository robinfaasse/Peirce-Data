It has always seemed to me singular that when we put the question to an evolutionist, Spencerian, Darwinian, or whatever school he may belong to, what are the agencies which have brought about evolution, he mentions various determinate facts and laws, but [del]I[/del] among the agencies at work he never once mentions Chance. Yet it appears to me that chance is the one essential agency upon which the whole process depends.
