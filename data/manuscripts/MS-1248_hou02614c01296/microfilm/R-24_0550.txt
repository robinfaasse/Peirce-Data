[u]Numerals of Many Tongues[/u]

This study was originally undertaken in connection with my hypothesis about Pythagoras, [del]further[/del] [ins]to[/ins] illustrate my Doctrine of Abduction. Namely, I wished to make out where the strange words given by Ralph of Laon came from (See M Cantor Gesch. d. Math. 1[ins]st[/ins] Ed Vol I pp 764 et seqq.).

It appears that some are Finno-Turkish, some Semitic, and one Aryan. Consequently, they must have come from Babylon. They are
1 Igin
2 Andras
3 Ormis
4 Arbas
5 Quimas
6 Calcis or Caltis (the latter better)
7 Zenis
8 Temeniam or Termeniam
9 Celentis
10 Sipos
