OFFICE OF
J. THOMPSON GILL
815 WARREN AVE.

CHICAGO, ILL. [date]2/11 1901[/date]

Mr C. S. Peirce
Milford Pa

Dear Sir
I have been so busy since the reception of
your last letter I have had no time to reply.
To obviate the objections you stated I make the
following proposition.
Send me particulars of the most difficult problem
confronting you & I will send you the equation
for its solution - if it is capable of solution with
the limited faculties of humanity. I mean a real
practical problem. I may have made a mistake
in writing my circular upon the high ethical, religious
plane - Although it has attracted the attention of
European authorities.

With many kind regards
I am sincerely yours
J. Thompson Gill
