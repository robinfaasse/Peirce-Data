Dear Sir
Your favor 17th Inst to hand, I thank you for your kind attention to circular concerning "The Completion of Logic"

Permit me to call attention in an abbreviated way to your remarks concerning the same, wherein you impair the Constructionary Equation, by depriving one of its extremes of that which I have placed both in the extremes & in the origin of the condition (Equation): this you assert or assume is not there, but presume, it, should be, viz "It is so conveniently subdivided into parts, chapters, paragraphs, etc, with a comprehensive index, you need read only such portions as you desire, consulting for others which may be necessary."

You may thus comprehend the subject in an hours time, because the contents, position, etc, of your personality, are more complete than those of the average of the community, as far as this subject is concerned.
