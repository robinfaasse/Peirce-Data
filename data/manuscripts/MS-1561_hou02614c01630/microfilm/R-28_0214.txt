They rejoined the others and ate and drank a little; and
so, after divers brief preparations, each man busy by himself,
Thodores, Lia, Karolo, the two men of the previous day, and three
others set off quietly together. They mounted the lower part of
the western slope of High Olympus, keeping well down among the
oaks, until at about seven o'clock, arrived at a large opening too
rocky for any growth, they found awaiting them twenty-three fine
fellows, constituting the command of Thodores. They were on the
path with occasional halts all day long. It was a tedious march,
