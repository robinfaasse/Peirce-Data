the gravity of his demeanor upon the occasion, though it was real-
ly due to his hesitation, had gone far to win him the hearts of
the family.

In that house, the sojourner was delightfully entertained
for more than a week. The cooking was a thing to fill the mind
with wonder and with praise. At the suggestion, a flood of memo-
ries even now overwhelms his senses,- pilau with stewed pears,
fricassed fowls with chestnuts, exquisite kabobs, blood puddings,
scientific compositions of forcemeat, gourds, and tomatoes, with
other dreams past utterance.
