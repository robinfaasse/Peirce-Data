"Can he arrive tomorrow?"

He left the room, and shortly returned followed by
Thodores himself. For an instant in the dim light neither friend
made out who the other was. Then there was a simultaneous recog-
nition, and they flew into each other's arms.

"So this is your prisoner", said Thodores; "give him to
me. He is my sworn brother; his friends must be my friends and
his enemies my enemies. So, Lia, give him up to me; or else I will
pay his ransom. But in that case, I cannot aid you tomorrow."
