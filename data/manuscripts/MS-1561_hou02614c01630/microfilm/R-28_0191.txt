parties belonged by nature to the universal brotherhood of ardent
young men; and international fraternization was soon carried to the
point of intimacy, and far beyond. Indeed, the Yankee was quite
carried out of himself by these young noblemen; for perfectly
noble they were in every sense of the term, although it was par-
