And Fael Allah was struck with love for me, and discerned that
I was not without prudence. A contract of marriage was agreed to
by which my fortune was to be added to his, and I was to be made his
equal partner in his business. So we were married; and I induced
him to make three great caravans of Kali and dori carpets of
Kurdistan, shawls of Kurk, curtains of Yeza, silks of Teheran,
embroideries, metal-work, tiles, turquoises, pearls, attar, opium,
and tumbak, and to lead one caravan himself and entrust
the other two to his two brothers, and to bring away the whole of
our wealth, so as to carry it into the country of the Franks, to
the great city of Vienna. The three caravans all arrived
safely in Stamboul, where we sold a portion of our wares and
