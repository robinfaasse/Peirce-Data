Again they forged upward; but halts began
to be more frequent. Their fatigues were begin-
ning to tell upon the whole party. At last
the flask of raki was brought into requisition.
It was passed first to Karolo, and by him to
the woman. All tasted it and barely tasted
it. Then another great spurt was made
and the three summits of Olympus, — [u]ta[/u]
[u]tria adhélphia[/u] came into view, impressive
and divine. Karolo could not help repeating
the [del]prayer, mystical prayer[/del] [ins]and untranslatable prayer[/ins],

Ἀπὸ τὸν Ὄλυμπον τὸν κόρυμβον
τὰ τρία ἄκρα τοῦ Οὐρανοῦ
ὅ’ σου αἱ Μοῖραι τῶν Μοιρῶν
καὶ ἡ ἐδική μου Μοῖρα
ἂς ἀκούσῃ καὶ ἂς ἔλθῃ.
