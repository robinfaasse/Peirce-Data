"I am not making fun, I am talking straight out of my
heart. You mix with the society of different races; you compare
their conflicting ideas; and you are carried to a height whence
you can survey all opinions, and view things justly."

"That is what I ought to have done, Thodores; but it is
as far as possible from what I have done."

"You have red men in your country, have you not, Karolo?"

"Yes."

"You are at war with them?"

"More or less so, nearly all the time."

"Now see how different the ideas of different nations
are,- even two peoples so much alike as the Greeks and Americans.

"Even with us, Thodores, abductions are not quite matters

"Indeed! But still, Karolo, it is easy to see it was a
