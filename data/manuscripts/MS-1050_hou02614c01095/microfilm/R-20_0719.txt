Chapter II treats of the method of reducing the different observers scales of magnitudes to one. [del]The method[/del] [ins]The[/ins] scale to which it is sought to reduce all the rest is such that if [u]m[/u] be the numerical magnitude [ins]in the northern heavens[/ins] and N the number of stars, brighter than the star whose magnitude is [u]m[/u], then

$$m = -\frac{1}{3} + \frac{2}{3 \log 2.25} \log N.$$
