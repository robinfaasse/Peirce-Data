Cap. 15.
Proof that no universal exists out of the mind.
1. No universal is a singular substance. If it
exists, then, it is many things. Many singular
things or many universal things? [del]If the latter[/del]
[ins]it is singular.[/ins] If the latter, the question re-
peats itself ad infinitum.
2. If the universal were a substance distinct
from singulars, it would be separable from
them.
3. It would follow that no individual could be
created if another coexisted, or could be anni-
hilated if another remained.
4. The individual would be composed of univer-
sals and so would be more universal than
the universal.
5 Something of the essence of Christ would
be miserable and damned.
Also, he gives a large number of autho-
rities.
