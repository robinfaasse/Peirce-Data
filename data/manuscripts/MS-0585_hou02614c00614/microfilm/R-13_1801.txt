<u>Part. 1.</u>
<u>Cap 1.</u>
Of the term. Its definition and division. Term
is pars propinqua propositionis. Divided into
spoken, written, and conceived. The two first are
signa [u]ad placitum[/u], the last naturaliter. Words are
not properly signs of concepts, but they follow con-
cepts in their significations. Signs means either
what causes cognition or what is made to cause
cognition.

<u>Cap. 2.</u>
Of 3 meanings of the term term. Principally de-
voted to distinguishing material & personal
supposition.

Of the division of incomplex terms. Noun and Verb.
