CHARLES SCRIBNER'S SONS
PUBLISHERS IMPORTERS AND BOOKSELLERS
153-157 FIFTH AVENUE
BETWEEN 21ST AND 22ND STREETS

NEW-YORK, March 27, 1896.

My dear Peirce,

As we for many other
reasons the price and the success of the
book would be too problematical for
us to undertake a translation of either
of Wundt's books that you mention.
The cost of translation, copyright &
manufacture would be strictly prohibitive,
I should say.

Truly yours
W. C. Brownell.
