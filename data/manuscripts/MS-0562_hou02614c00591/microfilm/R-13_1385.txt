But $0 \times \log \infty = 0$.
Writing, now, for short, $\infty$ instead of $\log \infty$, we
have
$\infty \times x = x$,
for this is true whether $x$ be zero or in-
finity. Hence also
$x = \frac{x}{\infty}$.

Bearing these propositions in mind, it is
easily seen that the expression for the lo-
gical aggregate of $x, y, z$, etc. becomes
$\underline{x} + \underline{y} + \underline{z}$
while the expression for the logical compound
remains
$x \times y \times z$.
But the latter might, perhaps more philoso-
phically, be expressed in the form
$-\log (\bar{0}^{-x} + \bar{0}^{-y} - \bar{0}^{-x-y})$
