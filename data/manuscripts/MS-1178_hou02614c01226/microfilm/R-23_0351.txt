Still, until matters are quite revolutionized, the
utterances of the tongue will be winged words, adapted
to the purpose of the moment, and changing their fashion
with every generation. The force of speech is felt to lie
greatly in the freshness of its forms. Modern sentiments
express themselves in modern sounds. Quite taboo in
good society is all bookishness of talk; while smart
slang and fashionable novelties of pronunciation
proclaim one's [del]intimacy[/del] [ins]intimacy[/ins] with whatever is most idle
and elegant. Every community, every class of society,
every coterie, every family, has its own ways of
talking, and as the course of events throws it into
importance, those ways are imitated by its admirers.
