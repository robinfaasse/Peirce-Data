moderate [ins]systematic[/ins] code of maxims resting upon a small
number of theorems. I regret that want of space forbids
my developing this important subject, here. The govern-
ing consideration of the whole matter is [u]economy[/u]. One
[ins]good[/ins] reason which may given for this is, that, notwithstanding
man's aptitude for guessing right, the only safe calcu-
lation must be that any given hypothesis will,
after a time, have to be superseded or at least modified.
