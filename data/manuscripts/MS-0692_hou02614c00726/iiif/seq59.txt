The likelihood of a hypothesis ought not to be considered
in abduction, except so far as it affects the question of
economy; and it is as often good economy to begin
with an unlikely hypothesis as with a likely one. It
is true that we must rely upon a general likelihood in
our hypotheses; but to trust to the likelihood of any
special one is quite a different thing. Any given likeli-
hood may be illusory; the likelihood is that it is so; so
that likihood warns us against its own deceptions.
