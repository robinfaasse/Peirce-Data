The pre-Wundtian psychologists of my youth used
to say that when a dog is observed to act as if he had
reasoned, he really was not acting from reason but
from the association of ideas. It has since been made
clear that all reasoning, as well as all other intellectual
operations, come under the one general law which, rather
narrowly conceived, used to be called the association of ideas.

analogy between the dog's mental operation and that of
a philosopher, that they are, perhaps, in danger of
overlooking the important distinction that those earlier
psychologists emphasized.
