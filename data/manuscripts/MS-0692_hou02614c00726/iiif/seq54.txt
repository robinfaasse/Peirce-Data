he nor anybody else was able to make a good guess
about bodies in motion before Galileo. The reason was
that their guesses were awkward contortions of their
minds. Every inventor knows that it is the idea which
seems, when it is once gained, the most natural and
obvious, which is the most difficult to discover, and
