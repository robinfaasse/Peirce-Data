[date]January, WEDNESDAY 2. 1889[/date]

Down at 8h AM.
Wrote on the book all the
morning with tolerable
success.
Afternoon [del]fix'd up[/del]
[del]sundries.[/del]
Then went out to check
time.
Evening 5 hours on
proof of dictionary
Bar at noon [del]one[/del]
Let watch run down
to my extreme vexation.
Juliette is very poorly
& irritable.
I weigh with clothes
on 152 lbs which alarms
me.
No word from Goble

[date]January, THURSDAY 3. 1889[/date]

Down about 8 AM.
Tried to write on book, but not
successfully. Yesterday was better.
Afternoon 5 P.M. [del]went out[/del]
Returned at 11 PM.
Soley brought 5 book boxes
Juliette weighs 104 with
thick clothes & heavy shawl.
Evening [del]Bacon &[/del] [ins]Gaillot[/ins]
Reading Taine chiefly for
his style.
No word from Goble
Groceries from Mesters
Set my clock level.
& clock

Began a course of K!

Francis C. Russell of Chicago
