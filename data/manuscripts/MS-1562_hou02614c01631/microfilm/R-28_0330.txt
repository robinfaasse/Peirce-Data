O dreadful night! Night of farewells & tears!
I went to all those spots, those spots well-loved
Where during seventeen years my days had past
As one long day of June. [del]I hugged the walls,[/del]
[del]and every family piece.[/del] Upon my knees
I kissed my young girl's bed and sobbed and cried
Oh why, thou fatal hero, did the gods
Lead thee to me? But oh, the worst of all,
The acme of my bitter wretchedness,
Anguish and utter misery, was when
I softly crept into my mother's room.
I stood and listened, but she was asleep.
And I knelt down, all still, beside the bed,
There, close to her, I oft had slept so well.
And so I left, just by her darling head
An offering of my hair. Oh mother dear
Oh fatherland! Oh friends! Ye sacred ones!
See, see my fate, and you will pardon me!
(Buries her face in her hands.)
