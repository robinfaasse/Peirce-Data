[u]CP 42[/u] || by "action at a distance". At a distance from what?
The Boscovichian atom extends through all space. It is
impossible to be at a distance from space. But a cluster
of Boscovichian points may rotate; and there is sup-
posed to be no more [ins]kinetic[/ins] energy of rotation than would be due
to the concentration of the whole mass at these points. This,
therefore, is one thing that is meant by saying that the
action is at a distance: it is at a distance from the
[fig]figure[/fig] total mass. Moreover, the Boscovichian conception
and the conception of the conservation of energy are
