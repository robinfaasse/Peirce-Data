Let the packs so laid down be taken
up in the order
0 1 2 3 etc, (mod P)
Let the position of a card in the new order be $n_1$
Then first suppose $N$ divisible by $P$. Then
are $\frac{N}{P}$ cards in a pile. For the first $\frac{N}{P}$
cards $n_0 = P n_1$
so that for $n_1 = \frac{N}{P} - 1$ $n_0 = N - P$
For the next card $n_1 = \frac{N}{P}$ $n_0 = 1$ instead of $N$
That is the modulus is such that
$N \equiv 1$
or the modulus is $N - 1$ and we have in
general
$n_0 \equiv P n_1 \pmod{N-1}$
$n_0 = R \frac{P n_1}{N-1}$

maximum number of cards in a
pack by $Q = \text{Q} \frac{N}{P} + 1$
