[u]The Evening Post[/u]
Publication Office, 206-210 Broadway,
NEW YORK CITY.
P. O. Box 794. Telephone, Cortlandt 84.

[date]May 21, 1898.[/date]

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:-

Please accept my thanks for your favor of the 20th inst. together with sketch of map enclosed. I think such a scheme as you suggest would prove very interesting, but I fear the shape of the map would make it very difficult for us to handle it to advantage. However, the matter is indefinitely postponed for the present as we have decided to use a map of the West Indies next Wednesday, and whether we will take up anything more expensive later on will depend upon the progress of the war.

Again thanking you for your courtesy, I am

Yours very truly,

Edward P. Call

Publisher.
