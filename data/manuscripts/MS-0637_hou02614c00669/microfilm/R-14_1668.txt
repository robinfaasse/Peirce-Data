There is nothing in it that is extreme or peculiar:

"It may be counted amongst the surest results of the analysis of our knowledge that every assumption of an external world is mediated by thought, and in some way or another derived by unconscious mental processes from the subjective facts of sensation. Thus, except by Thought, we have no means of ascertaining whether we have really achieved our purpose of knowing the Existent; the possibility of comparing our knowledge with things as they exist apart from our knowledge is forever closed to us."

His use of the words 'Existent' and 'exist' here is an inaccuracy [del]in the use of the words[/del]. For "existent" we should read "Real", and for "exist", "really are".
