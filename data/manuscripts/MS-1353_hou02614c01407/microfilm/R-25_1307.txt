[u]New Theory of the Skew Mercator[/u]
[date]1894 March 27[/date]

Suppose a geodesic line to be represented by a straight line & equal arcs by equal lengths, and that the projection is conform.

***

The straight lines perpendicular to this [del]base[/del] [ins]central[/ins] line will represent geodesics. For if from neighboring points on a geodesic, geodesic perpendiculars of equal length be erected (on the same side [del]of the first[/del]) the junction of their extremities will by a known Theorem be perpendicular to them. Therefore the angles will be preserved in making these perpendicular straight lines represent geodesics.

Therefore the poles will not lie on any of them except those that are meridians.
