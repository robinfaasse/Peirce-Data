C. S. Peirce's. Skew-Mercator Map

The idea of this map is that since a
Mercator's chart preserves the shapes [del]or[/del]
[ins]size[/ins] of the globe within 40° of the equator
sufficiently for comparison by the
unaided and not excessively critical eye
and since all the possessions of the United
States (except Hawaii) lie within that angu-
lar distance of a certain great circle (namely,
one [del]with[/del] inclined 60° to the Equator and cutting it
in 35° West Longitude) it follows that a map
which represents that great circle by a straight
line, and preserves all angles unchanged, will
give the best possible eye-view of those posses-
sions.
