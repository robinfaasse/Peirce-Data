[u]The Skew Mercator.[/u]

§1. We may conveniently indicate the position of a point on a plane by writing
$$z = y + xi = r e^{\theta i}$$
For, then, if
$$z' = y' + x'i = r' e^{\theta' i} = Fz = F(y+xi) = F(r e^{\theta i})$$
we shall transform in that way from one projection preserving the angles (or conform projection) to another. This is shown in every work on the theory of functions.
