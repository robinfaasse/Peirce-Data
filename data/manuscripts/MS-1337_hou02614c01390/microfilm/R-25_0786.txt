Still more special combinations or structures are communi-
ties or societies of conscious beings especially men; and
Sociology must be reckoned as the seventh order of
science. It has two main branches, the first
relating to [ins]conduct in general[/ins] [del]general conduct[/del], the second to intercom-
munication. The first branch includes ethics, the-
ology, politics and law. The second branch includes
the theory of art and sciences of language.

Finally, there is an eighth order of sciences
which treat of individual objects or collections of ob-
jects. This divides into sciences of things [ins]or cosmology[/ins] and sciences
of men, or human history. Cosmology consists of
astronomy and geognosy, and geognosy is sub-
divided into geology, geography, meterology, etc.
