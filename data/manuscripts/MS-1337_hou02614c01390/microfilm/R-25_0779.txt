History of Science from Copernicus to Newton
Lecture I.

All that has ever been found out has been the result of correcting or verifying the suggestions of two primitive instincts, or groups of instincts, those of self-preservation and of reproduction. The one has taught us all we know of forces and things, the other all we know of our fellow beings, and most of what we know of ourselves. Thus all science is a development of primitive ideas connected with growth.
