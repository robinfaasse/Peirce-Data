Pure mathematics is analytical or synthetical,
according as algebraical or [del]syn[/del]geometrical
[del]elements[/del] [ins]methods[/ins] predominate. Applied mathematics
is to be divided in the same way as [del]the other[/del]
non-mathematical sciences are divided.

The next branch of science after mathematics
in order of generality is philosophy. Here facts
are considered, but only in their most general
characteristics. It has two branches: first, [u]logic[/u], the
study of the most general intellectual relations; second,
[u]metaphysics[/u], the study of the most general real characters
of things.

The third order of science is [u]nomology[/u], or the
study of laws. These laws are either [u]physical[/u] or
[u]psychical[/u]. Thus we have general physics and
general psychology.

The fourth order of science is the study of kinds
of matter, [u]chemistry[/u], including the determination of
physical constants. The ordinary conception of chemistry
