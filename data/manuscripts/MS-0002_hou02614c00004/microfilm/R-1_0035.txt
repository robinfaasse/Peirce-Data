[u]On the Simplest Branch of Mathematics[/u].
By C. S. Peirce

The purpose of this paper is, first, to lay the foundations of the simplest branch of pure mathematics, to furnish a system of diagrams for the study of it, and to deduce its main propositions; secondly, to apply this mathematics to logic and to draw certain logical corollaries from it; thirdly, to apply this logical doctrine so obtained to the resolution of certain questions relating to multitude; and fourthly, further to extend the same principles to topical geometry and to take some steps toward forming a method of studying that subject.
