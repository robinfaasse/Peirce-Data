Let clearness be applied to the subject
in the same manner as it has been to the
predicate, and it will give propositions
1 determining the whole subject
2. determining a part
3 determining it by the remainder of
its genus.

This gives the following propositions
(Does it not?)

1 [u]All S is all P[/u]
2 [u]All S is some P[/u]
3. [u]No S is P[/u]
4 [u]Some S is all P[/u]
5 [u]Some S is P[/u]
6 [u]Some S is not P[/u]
7 [u]Everything but P is all S[/u]
8 [u]Everything but P is some S[/u]
9 [u]Only some S is P[/u]
