My dear William

There was no representative of Psychology in the National Academy of Sciences. I thought that ought not to continue. I thought the [del]best[/del] [ins]surest[/ins] way to bring that state of things to a close was to put forward some man whose work was sufficiently like that of the members of the Academy to enable them to recognize it as genuine scientific work. I was afraid to name you, for fear the idea should be spread (in the rivalry of different department for recognition) that if your work belonged to the department of literature. I chose Cattell whom I knew to be a fervent admirer of yours. He was elected, I am told, though I was not present. I hope you will understand that if your name was not put first, it was because I wanted to begin by getting an entering wedge, & having my ground perfectly secure before I brought your name into discussion

very affectionately C S Peirce
