[date]1909 March 7[/date]

My dear William:

I adhere more strongly than ever to my tychism and evolutionism as to the laws of nature. But I think it down right immoral to use language inexactly in philosophy; and I need hardly say that I do not think the phrase "devenir réel" is anything better than a [del]deperate[/del] desperate attempt to attract attention by wild abuse of language. In my opinion morality forbids philosophy's being so degraded. I never read anything of Bergson, but his phrase does not attract me.

I don't see how you can say that one event, A, should be [del]caused[/del] [ins]necessarily caused[/ins] (for causation implies necessity) — by another, B, and this B can be caused in the same identical respect by a third C and yet that A is not caused by C. What you mean I cannot guess, but what you say is that If C then B happens [del]and that[/del] if B then A and yet that it does not follow that if A then C.
