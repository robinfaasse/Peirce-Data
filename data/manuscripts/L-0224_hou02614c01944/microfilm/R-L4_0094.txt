From every point of view of logic, then, Renouvier's razor
will cut no ice.

In fact, the only thing that gives it much semblance of truth,

It is easy to sketch out the sort of nominalism that must result
from Renouvier's maxim. Accordingly, looking ahead further than
I have yet read I see a chapter about philosophical "fetishism." Yes,
we know that talk. But the only thing that makes a fetish absurd is
