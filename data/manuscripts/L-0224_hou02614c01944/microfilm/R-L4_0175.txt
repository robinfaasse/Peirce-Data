My dear William:

Your Appendix C appears to me to give a fair idea, as far as it professes to go, of a branch of my opinion which I am disposed to maintain more than ever. I have never, as far as I remember now, read anything of Bergson's; but I cannot imagine anything more opposed to my opinions than the doctrine that "Reality is changeable."
