For abbreviations I will write [equation] M [/equation] for [equation] \sqrt{2k-A^{2}} r_{a} [/equation] and [equation] Q [/equation] for [equation] \frac{\sqrt{M^{2}-r_{c}^{2}} + M}{\sqrt{M^{2}-r_{c}^{2}} - M} [/equation]

value of [equation] r^{2} [/equation] is diminished and the denominator increased. Consequently,
[equation] r^{2} [/equation] will be increased at every revolution. It will be a spiral. But
[equation] r^{2} [/equation] cannot get larger than [del] [equation] M [/equation] [/del] [equation] r_{a}^{2} [/equation], as we have already seen.
