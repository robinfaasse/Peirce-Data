As a matter of fact what we deal with is "Aristotles idea of —", "Darwin's",
Spencer's etc and the evolution of human thought consists (1) in
multiplying any favorable variation of an idea [ins]eg. Darwins variation on
the idea of evolution,[/ins] (2) producing gradually of many minute favorable
variations in the idea of Darwinian evolution (3) uniting two or more of these
into a new idea having the favorable variations of each — an idea always born
in a certain mans mind at a certain place and time multiplied in
other men's minds first as his idea and only when it has accumulated
variations as men's own idea. Of course even here A's idea in B is B's idea
but it is B's idea of A's idea — a positive objective thing in itself and if
as people are so fond of saying B "makes A's idea his own" by thoroughly
assimilating it it must be either that B. has tested the idea by
actual reference to the reality with which the idea has to do or else
that he has forgotten the source — as if a man should put a borrowed
book in his library and forget the owner. or more literally should
plagiarize by "unconscious cerebration". It seems to me that this
this distinction of all ideas (even if they are as far as we can judge
exactly alike save for the difference of position in time and space)
