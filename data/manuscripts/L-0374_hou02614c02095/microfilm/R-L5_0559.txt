My dear Dr. Richardson

I am sending you one of a dozen copies of a the first pages of a Syllabus that remain over after distribution to an audience. It was written in extremest hurry & piecemeal & I never saw any proof of it. It contains something about the classification of the sciences that may possibly interest you. [ins]It will be an item more to your list.[/ins]

Yours very truly

C S Peirce

I return to Milford Pa on [date]Dec 18[/date], but don't think yourself obligated to acknowledge receipt of such a trifle.
