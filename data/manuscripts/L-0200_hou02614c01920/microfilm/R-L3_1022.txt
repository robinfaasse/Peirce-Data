[del]A[/del] [ins]A[/ins] practical rule about fractional values suggested by the above is that the fraction [frac]\frac{A+B}{C+D}[/frac] is always intermediate in value between [frac]\frac{A}{C}[/frac] and [frac]\frac{B}{D}[/frac].

In fact, from this the whole theory of rational numbers can be deduced. To begin with, since [frac]\frac{A+A}{C+C}[/frac] is intermediate between [frac]\frac{A}{C}[/frac] and [frac]\frac{A}{C}[/frac] it is equal to them.

Which is the greatest [frac]\frac{27}{109}[/frac] or [frac]\frac{28}{113}[/frac]? We have the sequence

[frac]\frac{27}{109}[/frac]  [frac]\frac{28}{113}[/frac]  [frac]\frac{1}{4}[/frac]

But [frac]\frac{1}{4}[/frac] = [frac]\frac{28}{112}[/frac] Hence we have the sequence

[frac]\frac{28}{113}[/frac]  [frac]\frac{1}{4}[/frac] = [frac]\frac{28}{112}[/frac]

[frac]\frac{0}{1}[/frac]

[therefore] [frac]\frac{27}{109}[/frac] < [frac]\frac{28}{113}[/frac]
