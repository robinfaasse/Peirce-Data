THE CENTURY ASSOCIATION,
7 WEST FORTY-THIRD STREET.

My dear Peirce / Thank you for your two letters — always interesting & significant. I'm busy about little things & haven't written before.
— I can't do any thing in the way of sending Encyclopaedia work to you for the conditions were so unsatisfactory that I have thrown up the departments I was in charge of. If I couldn't stand their methods you couldn't have stood them either. They are going to have them written by boys in the office!
— Well — let [del]that[/del] [ins]it[/ins] go.
When you write next please send me the names of firms of publishers that you have already spoken to in regard to arithmetics, will you? I think it would be best to try fresh men? different ones? Anyhow I should like to know.
