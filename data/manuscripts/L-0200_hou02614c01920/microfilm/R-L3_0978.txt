LICK OBSERVATORY, UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA,

MT. HAMILTON, [date]Jan. 21[/date], 1896.

MY DEAR SIR:

The Lick Observatory possesses a large number of portraits of astronomers, which are carefully preserved in Albums. We shall be much indebted to you if you are willing to add your own photograph to the collection.

I am, my dear Sir,

Very respectfully and truly yours,

E. S. Holden
Director.

To Charles S. Peirce esqr
