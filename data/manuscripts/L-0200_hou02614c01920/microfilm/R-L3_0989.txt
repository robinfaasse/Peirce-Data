I once had an aid whom I set to duplicating some time observations. He came to me and said that he could not conscientiously do it, because observations taken in the evening were said to be made at $2^{\text{h}}$, $3^{\text{h}}$, $4^{\text{h}}$, etc. I remarked that that was sidereal time. Well, he said, sidereal and mean time can only differ by $16^{\text{m}}$ $40^{\text{s}}$ and therefore he would prefer not to copy the work. All right, I said, I will give you another job. Go down and write your resignation. In my opinion an anonymous hack is in a similar position, and his [del]virtue[/del] [ins]duty[/ins] consists in producing exactly the kind of thing wanted. That is what I profess to be able to do better than the average of the people now employed.

I am particularly good at translating, because my translation produces as nearly as possible the effect on an English reader that the original did on the native of the country where it was written. In translating French I am particularly good, because I understand what the French writer means, which he hardly ever says. He expects his readers to read between the lines, which they do, but which the American won't: for him it is necessary to be a lot more explicit, — a very delicate business, for if overdone it is revolting.

I am also good at editing books, as the Mach's Mechanics will show.

I may mention too that I am quite au fait at cataloguing libraries; and I add notes about the books, not a fac-simile in the library etc. and all this at prices that quite defy competition. I do not charge half so much as the ordinary cataloguer & my work is more accurate in every respect. very faithfully C.S. Peirce
