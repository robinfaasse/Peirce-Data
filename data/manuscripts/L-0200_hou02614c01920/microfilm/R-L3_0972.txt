My Dear Peirce : —

You have given me a genuine pleasure in sending me the five 'Monists' with your articles in them. Two pleasures — for first, I like to be remembered & second, high intellectual pleasure is a thing to be grateful for. I have only read the first two papers — just now — at this time in the morning, but I am going to thank you for all of them at once, & to wish that the "abridger of joys" — the sunderer of societies & the plunderer of palaces may leave you here a long time to do more & many more of them.

I think that what I like most in the papers I have read is the spectacle of a mind freed from trammels — shackles.
