DR. EDWARD S. HOLDEN,
4 E. 43rd St., NEW YORK CITY.
(STOCKBRIDGE, MASS.)

[date]June 14/1900[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I've forwarded yr note addressed to Editor to direct to Walker, with a note from myself. Better wait till you see what comes of it! Remember W. is very busy running a business requiring all his attention — the Magazine suffers, accordingly.

I'll do what I can. No news yet from the proposed Cyclopædia — [del]did[/del] — when news comes, I'll write again — but don't you refuse anything on my account.

Sincerely yours,
Holden
