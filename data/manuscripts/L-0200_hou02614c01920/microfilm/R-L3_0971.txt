Bread & butter [u]is[/u] hard to compass. Have
you entirely made up your mind
that it is worth while? Suppose
it were not for the hostages to
fortune?

I should love to talk to you about
a lot of things — & to listen to you
about many others. But somehow
I respect myself & you too much to
write a modern telegraphic letter
about them — & I've no leisure to
write of them as they deserve. I wish
you would send me your photo-
graph some time. I am going
to send you some of the Publications
of our Astronomical Society. It is a
successful experiment.

I am always
faithfully & cordially yours
Holden
