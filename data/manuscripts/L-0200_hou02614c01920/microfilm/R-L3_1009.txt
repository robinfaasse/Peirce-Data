[u]The Cosmopolitan Magazine.[/u]
[u]Editorial Department.[/u]
Irvington-on-Hudson, N.Y.
[date]June 29, 1900[/date].

Dear Sir:

Your favor of June 28 in regard to price of MS on the senses, by Charles S. Pierce, is at hand. I shall see that the matter is called to Mr. Walker's attention immediately upon his return. He is at present in the West, and will be away for some ten days.

Yours sincerely,
David Strother Walker

My dear Peirce / just to show what is doing - Yours sincerely
Holden

Dr. Edward S. Holden,
Stockbridge, Mass.
