[date]January 2, 1900[/date].

My Dear Peirce —

I do not know how to tell you how disappointed I was when I learned that another man than you had been appd. to the charge of the Bureau of Weights & Measures in the Coast Survey; & the details that you write me fill me with disgust. There really isn't anything to say but that. You know for yourself & everyone else knows too, that you are the best man living for that place [del]& for some others[/del] [ins]>[/ins]. You will not get them, probably, as you have not been appointed to this — & for the same reasons. You are too big a man to be wanted among smaller ones. In a way you ought to be content; but that content doesn't bring an income.

If I see any way to throw work in your direction I will do so — but I do not just know. The publishers do not want a man of your size any more than the C.S.
