DR. EDWARD S. HOLDEN,
[del]4 E. 43rd St., NEW YORK CITY.[/del]
(STOCKBRIDGE, MASS.)

[date]Sept. 25/01[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Thank you for your letter. I shall be very glad if you will write to Garrison. With regard to the Nation & Post set - its relation to me the Hindu saying

What! live in the river, & not friends with the crocodiles?

fits the case exactly.

- I think Douglas, Lowell or Willy Pickering wrote the note - probably Douglas.

- I'm writing for an Edin-burgh publisher a volume
