Socrates was freed from intellectual
shackles — conventions & in another but
similar sense the some great con-
querors have been so freed. Say
Baber the Mogul invader of India.
Most of us peg along as slaves, but
anyway we keep the conception of
what intellectual & moral self-
determination is like, & when we
see it we recognize it, & feel that
somehow or other it is our birth-
right, no matter whether we have
sold it for pottage or not. That is
the use that a great man is in
the world. To show the slaves what
their birthright is, if they choose.
And they recognize it & then lie
down & go on being slaves once
more, but they have had a gleam
of what makes their hearts warm.
