[date]March 22/00[/date] NY

THE CENTURY ASSOCIATION,
7 WEST FORTY-THIRD STREET.

My dear Peirce —
Yes — I think Walker will pay for what he accepts. I think he will accept the three articles I spoke of, though of course I do not even know so much as that. If I were the Editor I should accept your damnation of Hegel & Hegel's brethren instanter — & very likely he will do so. Suppose you try? C'est à vous à jouer.

Yours,
Holden
