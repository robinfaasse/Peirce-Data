THE CENTURY ASSOCIATION,
7 WEST FORTY-THIRD STREET.

My dear Peirce / I haven't any
authority to order any of
the following articles but if
you care to write about
4000 words on each of them
& send them to J. Brisben Walker
Irvington-on-Hudson, N.Y. — &
mark the envelope
< to be delivered at the house >
they'll be considered — & favorably
I believe.
