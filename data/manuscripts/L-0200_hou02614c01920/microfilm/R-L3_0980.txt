My Dear Sir:-

The Lick Observatory has in its halls a large number of portraits of noted astronomers-- Gauss, Bessel, and others-- and many of Americans-- Newton, Chauvenet, Gould and others-- [del]with[/del] [ins]as[/ins] a sort of Walhalla. Besides these we have many albums of photographic portraits (cabinet size). We should very much like a cabinet photograph of your father, for the Albums, and also a larger unmounted photograph or engraving to be framed for the halls.

I have only a small carte-de-visite which he gave me many years since (1874 [ins]±[/ins]). I have asked your brother Charles for these portraits, but despair of getting them. May I ask you to take the matter in hand? We shall be most indebted to you if you will.

I am, Very sincerely yours,
Edward S. Holden

To
Professor James Mills Peirce.
