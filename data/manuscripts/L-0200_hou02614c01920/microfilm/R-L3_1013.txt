DR. EDWARD S. HOLDEN,
4 E. 43rd St., New York City.
[del]STOCKBRIDGE, MASS.[/del]

[date]Oct. 18/1900[/date].

My dear Peirce /

[u]Two[/u] school arithmetics are needed to cover the whole ground.

The field is occupied three times over; but not satisfactorily. If you'll send me your Mss. I will go over them & "submit" them as our joint work to some publishers, personal friends. I'll do my share of the work of making the books & divide the profits equally. Your name shall go first on the title pages. If this suits, & if the Mss. are ready send them if you like. I have n't much hope of success, but I'll try. Yours

Holden
