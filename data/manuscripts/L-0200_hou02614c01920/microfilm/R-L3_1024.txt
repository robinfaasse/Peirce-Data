[date]January 2, 1901.[/date]

Dr. Edward S. Holden,
4 East Forty-third Street, New York.
My dear Doctor Holden:
Please accept my congratulations and kind greetings for the New Year and the New Century.
I have received your letter with reference to Professor Charles S. Peirce and have forwarded it to Messrs. G. & C. Merriam Company. I thank you for the same. I do not think, however, that they are at present going on with any work of revision but I presume that they always have some expert adviser in the line of which you speak and will be glad to get so distinguished a name as that of Professor Peirce.
I have made several distinct plans to see you in New York on my way through but my plans have always been frustrated by some
