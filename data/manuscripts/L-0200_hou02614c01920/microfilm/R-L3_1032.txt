Dear Holden

You like riddles. Here is one. A certain Arabic verb has the following meanings. What is the one idea (not among these mentioned) which explains them all?

to split wide open
to be perplexed
to fear
to be overjoyed
to waste in flesh

The IV[ins]th[/ins] "conjugation" or causative [ins]or probative[/ins] form means
To be consumptive
To meet accidentally
To have a red nose

The V[ins]th[/ins] form [del]"conjugation"[/del] generally expressing experience by subject of verb of effect of action means
To be rich
To be erudite
To meditate profoundly

The X[ins]th[/ins] "conjugation" reflexive of IV[ins]th[/ins] generally expressing asking for action or deeming that it takes place, means
To walk about,
To have full command of language.

I paste a paper over the answer.

ever faithfully
C. S. Peirce

Milford Pa
