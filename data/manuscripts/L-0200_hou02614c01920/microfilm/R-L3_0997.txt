Dear Doctor

Have you thought to suggest to Peirce writing for the International Review just started by Macmillan? Perhaps he could get some editorial work there.

[u]My dear Peirce[/u] — I send you this to consider — perhaps you'll write to them — I will speak to McClure about work on a proposed Cyclopaedia — & I have
