Stockbridge, Mass.
[date]June 12/99[/date]

My dear Peirce —
I sincerely hope that you may be appointed to the place which you are especially fitted to fill; & I hope that you will refer to me as a person who will be glad to testify for you. I should love to come to see you, but my work & engagements will keep me here.
With my best regards to Mrs. Peirce & to you, believe me, faithfully your friend
Edward S. Holden,
