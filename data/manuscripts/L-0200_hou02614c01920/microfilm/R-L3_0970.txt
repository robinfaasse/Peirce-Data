(LICK OBSERVATORY,)
University of California,
MOUNT HAMILTON, [date]Dec. 24, 1891[/date].

C. S. Peirce Esq, Milford, Penn[sup]a[/sup]

My Dear Peirce: —
I know President Jordan of the Stanford Univ[sup]y[/sup], fairly well (& like him) & he has talked to me somewhat about his plans. I infer that he is now collecting his [u]College[/u] Professors & that his University (or highfalutin') Professors will be invited later, one at a time. I know the man he has picked out as Univ[sup]y[/sup] Prof. of Mathematics & I should say he was the best man on the planet to invite. Let us hope that he will invite the best man on the planet in Logic — & get him. I will speak to him about it. The salaries are $3000 I believe.
