DR. EDWARD S. HOLDEN,
4 E. 43RD ST., NEW YORK CITY.
(STOCKBRIDGE, MASS.)

[date]April 5/01[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I sent your Ms. back to you to-day & tried to pay the expressage on it. But the idiot in charge of the office couldn't figure out the expense (not having the U. S. Ex. Co's tariff) & so it had to go collect — or not at all. Please excuse.

I've spoken to Brett (Macmillan Co.) again about the plan, & he is interested. I'll also speak to Ginn & Co. & to others, as occasion serves & we'll try to have them bidding against each other.

Yours faithfully
Holden
