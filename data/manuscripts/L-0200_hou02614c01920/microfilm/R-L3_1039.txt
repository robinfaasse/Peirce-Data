[date]Oct. 24/1901.[/date]

My Dear Sir: I shall not
have my books until some
time in November, but I
came across this cut — which
is the one criticized in the
[u]Nation[/u] (wrongly divided & with
its numbers reversed (I for-
get the exact wording.) The
division is copied from the
Ency. Met. steel plates &
there's nothing essentially wrong
about it. The numbers are
correct — not reversed.
The reviewer also said — in
effect — that when Mars
was examined in a teles-
cope the eye paid attention
to the whole disc at once
when in fact the attention
is concentrated on a very
small area at a time.
