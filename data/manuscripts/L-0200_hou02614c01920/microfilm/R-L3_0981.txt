My dear Peirce - I don't think you'll know
at once, how completely I understand your
letter. And I thank you for writing freely.
- You tell me about Mrs. Peirce's pain -
- & relief? now? I hope so. There is an
entirely new world opened to those who
bear pain - mental or physical. An
astonishing number of us live in the
world of physical pain & have dis-
ciplines that the rest of us know noth-
ing of. Add to the physical the
mental sufferings & you have a huge
mass which seems only explicable
on the Roman Catholic scheme. Granted
its basis & at least it's a consistent
theory. Happy (in one sense) those who
can believe it.
