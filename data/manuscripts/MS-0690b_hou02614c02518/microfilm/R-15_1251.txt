We must next frame some hypothesis in regard to the dates
at which the dialogues were written. For this purpose the first
step will be to frame a hypothesis as to the order in which they
were written. This is, indeed, a far more important problem than
the other. It is so indispensable to any general comprehension of
Plato's philosophy that I cannot but guess that perhaps Plato him-
self drew up a chronological table of them for use in the Academy.
