chimeras, but an attempt to interpret an infantile philosophy of
some non-Aryan, non-Shemitic thinker.

The ground is now cleared for the third, and more signif-
icant, question of why the Pythagoreans made such a mystery of their
doctrine. I believe the question will answer itself if it only
broadened a little. Why is any knowledge made a mystery? The
answer is, because there is a motive, -- a good reason for doing so.
