three independent determinations of the degree of stylistic devel-
opment of each dialogue, and should thus have three equations for
determining the three probable errors of the three different methods
of determination. I made the necessary calculations using the
square roots of the numbers, so that the probable errors should be
independent of the numbers themselves. But when I came to write
the present paper, and got out my former calculations, I was struck
