Street cars are famous [u]ateliers[/u] for speculative modelling.
Detained there, with no business to occupy him, one sets to scrutiniz-
ing the people opposite, and to working up biographies to fit them.
I see a woman of forty. Her countenance is so sinister as scarcely
to be matched among a thousand, almost to the border of insanity,
yet with a grimace of amiability that few even of her sex are suffi-
ciently trained to command; -- along with it, those two ugly lines,
right and left of the compressed lips, chronicling years of severe
discipline. An expression of servility and hypocrisy there is, too
abject for a domestic; while a certain low, yet not quite vulgar,
kind of education that is evinced, together with a taste in dress
neither gross nor meretricious, but still by no means elevated, be-
speak companionship with something superior, beyond any mere contact
