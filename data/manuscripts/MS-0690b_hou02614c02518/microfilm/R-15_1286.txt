less influenced by the Chaldean scientists, yet there would be no
particular and intimate connection between their doctrines and those
of Pythagoras. Accordingly we find no astrology in Pythagoreanism;
no exorcisms of spirits. On the contrary, we find a system of
astronomy very much opposed to the Babylonian. If he introduced
Babylonian weights and measures, which may be doubted, they were
probably already common in Asiatic countries. So there is another
prediction verified, illustrating how much information an erroneous
