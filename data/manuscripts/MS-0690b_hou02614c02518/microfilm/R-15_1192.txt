It is true that this latter argument is weakened by the consideration
that states of things not universal usually come to an end; while
the other is strengthened by the consideration that, time being con-
tinuous, it is reasonable to suppose that, in sufficiently short in-
tervals, there will be no further variation of any given phenomenon.
