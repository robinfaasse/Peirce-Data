Deduction, of course, relates exclusively to an ideal
state of things. A hypothesis presents such an ideal state of
things, and asserts that it is the icon, or analogue of an experience.

Having, then, by means of deduction, drawn from a hypoth-
esis predictions as to what the results of experiment will be, we
proceed to test the hypothesis by making the experiments and compar-
ing those predictions with the actual results of experiment. Ex-
periment is very expensive business, [del]both[/del] in money, [del]and[/del] [ins],[/ins] in time, and in
