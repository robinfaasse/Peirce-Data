generally known to be proficient in any art by which men could gain
a livelihood? Of course. They were proficient in mathematics,
which has, in all ages, supplied a livelihood for experts; and they
were particularly proficient in by far the most useful of the math-
ematical arts, that of computation. Especially in the state of
the art among the Greeks, expert accountants must have commanded
large fees. Let us go on, then, to the second prediction. Is there
any indication of a knowledge by Pythagoras of any branch of math-
ematics by which he may have derived peculiar knowledge in the east?
