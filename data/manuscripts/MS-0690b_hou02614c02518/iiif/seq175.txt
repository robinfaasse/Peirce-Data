for any other purpose than that of restricting their numbers. Such
was that of keeping silent for five years. Another proof that their
numbers must have been restricted is that they had their property in
common. The other kind of mystery, where the numbers [u]are[/u] more or
less restricted, are trades. A community having their property in
common has never been known to be successful for centuries unless
they exercised some industry in which they were better versed than
other people. It is, therefore, likely that the Pythagorean mystery
was a knowledge by which this brotherhood earned their living. Let
us try this hypothesis. What predictions can be based upon it?
