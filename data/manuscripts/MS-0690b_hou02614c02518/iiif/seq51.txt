Induction, on the other hand, is not justified by any relation be-
tween the facts stated in the premisses and the fact stated in the
conclusion; and it does not infer that the latter fact is either
necessary or objectively probable. But the justification of its
conclusion is that that conclusion is reached by a method which,
steadily persisted in, must lead to true knowledge in the long run
of cases of its application, whether to the existing world or to any
imaginable world whatsoever. Deduction cannot make any such claim
