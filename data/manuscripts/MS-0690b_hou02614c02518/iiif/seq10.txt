The odds in favor of a ball being auriferous:

Antecently are            40 : 30       = 4:3

As yellow  are            24/40 : 12/30 = 3:2

As heavy   are            35/40 : 25/30 = 21:20

Hence, on the whole, the odds in favor of a heavy yellow ball being

auriferous are 4/3 . 3/2 . 21/20 = 21/10, which is, of course, cor-

rect.  The demonstration that this is always so, is now extremely

simple.  Write [u]x[/u] for aAp/pAp and [u]y[/u] for pAa/pAp.  Also ξ for aPp/pPp
