[date]1908 Dec 1[/date]

To the Editor of the New York Times

Sir:

You manifestly have accomplished students of literature upon your staff; will not one of you oblige a grateful reader by telling something of the origin and history of that style of prose which is interlarded with scraps of original verse, [ins]such as[/ins] [del]of[/del] The 'Consolations of Philosophy' and Voltaire's 'Temple du Gout'?

Q.

C. S. Peirce
P.O. Milford, Pa.
