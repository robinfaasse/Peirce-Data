[pn]10 3/4[/pn] - We want a Theory of Limits

One prop is that whatever is done up to the limit is either
true at the limit or has the limit of its truth there

Another is that what is true up to the limit
may be taken to be true at the limit for purposes of
reasoning (though this is not necessary.)
