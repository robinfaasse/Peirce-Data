Given any two [del]lines[/del] [ins]rays[/ins] there are two points from which every pair of rays cut off equal linear distances on [del]those rays[/del]

Given any two points there are two rays on which any two points subtend equal angular distances from those points.

These are general propositions true of plane & spherical & non-Euclidean geom. and requiring to be established from the general idea of measurement.
