[del][u]Theorem[/u]. If three points lie in a ray then their designations in the barycentric notation will be related as $[P]$, $[Q]$, $[pP+qQ]$ where $p$ and $q$ are suitable numbers, and conversely if three points have their designations so related they lie in one ray.
