[u]Corollary 5[/u]. The relation of incompressibility transfers no collection (except 0) to any collection. For $\Pi_s \Pi_r \bar{r}_{\{0\}rs} = 0$ and $\Pi_x \bar{l}_{x0} \mathbin{\psi} \Sigma_s q_{xs}$ whence, by (6) $\Pi_m \Pi_n \bar{l}_{n0} \mathbin{\psi} \bar{t}_{\{0\}mn}$

[u]Corollary 6[/u]. The relation of coexistence, [del]C[/del][ins]D[/ins], transfers every collection to every collection except 0. For $\Pi_s \Pi_r \bar{D}_{sr}$ ; whence by (7)
$\Pi_m \Pi_n \bar{l}_{0m} \mathbin{\psi} \bar{t}_{\{D\}mn}$

[u]Corollary 7[/u]. The relation of identity, I, transfers every collection to every collection that includes it. For [del]$\Pi_s \Pi_r \bar{l}_{sr} \mathbin{\psi}$[/del] [ins]$\Pi_s \Sigma_r (\bar{q}_{ms} \mathbin{\psi} q_{mr} \cdot l_{rs})$[/ins] (and to say that one collection $m$ includes another $n$ is to say that $\Pi_s \bar{q}_{ns} \mathbin{\psi} q_{ms}$. The product of these two statements gives $\Pi_s \Sigma_r \bar{q}_{ns} \mathbin{\psi} q_{mr} \cdot l_{rs}$ ; and this multiplied into (7) gives $t_{\{I\}mn}$.

[u]Corollary 8[/u]. The relation of negation or otherness, T, transfers every collection[ins], to[/ins] [del]to[/del] every collection not an individual of [del]$n$[/del][ins]$n$[/ins] and not 0. For to say that $m$ is not an individual of $n$ and is not [del]zer[/del] zero is to say that $\Sigma_r \Sigma_s q_{mr} \cdot (\bar{q}_{nr} \mathbin{\psi} q_{ms} \cdot T_{sr})$. But we have $\Pi_s \Pi_r (l_{sr} \mathbin{\psi} T_{sr})$ Multiplying these we have

$\Pi_s \{ \bar{q}_{ns} \mathbin{\psi} \Sigma_r q_{mr} \cdot T_{sr} \mathbin{\psi} \Sigma_s q_{ms} \cdot T_{sg} \}$
