[[u]Intensity of light[/u], photometric intensity, refers to the degree of [ins]sensorial[/ins] conscious shock to [ins]the[/ins] visual centre. If measured, the principle is that the simultaneous effect of two equal lights shall be considered double that of one. Photometric intensity may apply, 1[sup]st[/sup], to illumination, throwing a strong or feeble light on all objects present; 2[sup]nd[/sup], to light shining on the retina or to a source of emission; 3[sup]rd[/sup], to a body irregularly reflecting a large or small proportion of day-light; [del]For shining of polished surfaces, see Regular reflection No[/del]]
