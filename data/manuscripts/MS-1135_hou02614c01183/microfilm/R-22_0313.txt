[u]Effects of juxtaposition[/u]

[u]Contrast[/u]. Violent, glaring, etc. Some extreme contrasts produce dizziness.
The contrast-colors are pairs, each member of which excites a sensation of
the other as a secondary color. They are nearly the same as the complementary
colors, which are [del]also[/del] pairs of colors which being mixed give white.
