[u]Hue[/u]

Greek: τὸ χρῶμα
Latin: color

[u]General[/u]: hue, color,

[u]Modification of [del]one[/del] [ins]hue[/ins][/u]: tinge, cast, tone, shade (some writers restrict this to darkened colors), tint (some writers restrict this to whitish modifications)

[u]Broad distinctions of hue[/u]:
'warm' (colors approaching orange yellow), 'cool' (colors approaching pure blue). [The warm colors are those which appear more chromatic under [del]increased[/del] [ins]high[/ins] illumination in direct than in diffused sunlight, the cool colors are those of which the reverse is true.]
less refrangible, belonging to the lower part of the spectrum, (yellowish green, yellow, orange, [ins]brown,[/ins] red, crimson-purple), more refrangible, belonging to the upper end of the spectrum (bluish green, blue, violet, violet-purple). [The former are the warm, the latter the cool colors.]
fundamental colors: red, green, blue or violet.
primary colors: red, yellow, blue.
