19. [u]Buying[/u]. The art of buying is one of the purely psycholo-
gical arts. It requires some knowledge of human-nature though
less than the art of selling. Its affinities are with [u]business[/u]
or [u]Money transactions[/u].

[u]Medicine[/u] or [u]Therapeutics[/u] and so is a [u]physiological[/u] art.

It is also an art of forming a [u]receptacle[/u] & must be
referred to under receptacles (under [u]property[/u], though
the value of the contents of a gelatine capsule, is less than
that of a jewel casket.)

22. [u]Chaffing[/u]. The English art of making rude speeches,
which in other countries would lead to "difficulties". It is
purely psychological. An art of producing pain or disagree-
able emotion, chiefly shame & anger, mixed. It is a branch of
[u]rhetoric[/u], or the art of using language to influence men.
