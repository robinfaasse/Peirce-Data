[u]Classification of Ideas & Words[/u].

The broadest classes are numbered 1, 2, 3. The broadest classes under 1 are numbered 11, 12, 13, etc; those under 2, 21, 22, 23, etc., and this system is carried out down to the narrowest divisions. This has sundry advantages, among which is that of enabling me to alter the arrangement in the course of the work with little renumbering.

There are three categories of elements of experience, as follows:

1. [u]Feeling-qualities[/u], or sensible qualities, either unassociated or attached each to an object. In themselves, they are not definitely objectified, since they involve no reflection whatever, and therefore no thought that they are within or without.

2. [u]Consciousness of effort and resistance[/u], is essentially consciousness of a [u]within[/u] and a [u]without[/u], correlative to one another. It is also essentially [u]here and now[/u], differing in this from the qualities which are not definitely located. From this second kind of consciousness [del]is[/del] [ins]are[/ins] derived the ideas of brute force, reality, existence, relation, etc. Under this head I place all the common experiences of life, all that is most real to all men.

3. [u]Consciousness of something as a medium between two things[/u]. This involves the idea of intellectual [del]value or equivalence[/del] [ins]pertinence and of[/ins] significance.
