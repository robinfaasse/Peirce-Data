[pn]323123111111[/pn] [u]Chess[/u].

The game of chess is said to go back into the night of time. It certainly is a very old game. It has been played almost according to the rules of today for 400 years; a game closely allied to it was played in India before the Crusades, and a game somewhat allied to it was played by the ancient Egyptians, 1600 B.C. There is no other important game of comparable ancestry. [u]Implements[/u]: a 64-squared board checkered in light and dark with a light square at each of the two players' right hand, and 32 chess-men, 16 white, 16 black (in fact, often red, but called black). Of the 16 men, 8 are "pieces," 8 "pawns". The pawns are all alike; the "pieces" are of greater value, viz:— "A King," a "Queen" [ins]originally a Vizier,[/ins] (now only valued at about 10 pawns), two "Rooks," or castles [ins]originally elephants[/ins] (valued at about 5 pawns each); 2 "Bishops," originally [del]archers[/del] (valued at about 3 1/2 pawns each); [ins]and[/ins] 2 "Knights," (valued at about 3 1/4 pawns each). [del]The two players play alternately[/del]
