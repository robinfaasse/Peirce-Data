The system of numbering or otherwise systematically
designating the [del]classes[/del] articles need not be definitively
settled, until the work is finished. But as a provisional method
to be used in the progress of the work, I shall use for each article
a series of numbers of which the first designates the broadest
division, [del]the second the[/del] [ins]the second the[/ins] broadest subdivision of that division,
and so on. This is like commencing a new hundred at every [del]cross-[/del]
[del]street[/del] [ins]street[/ins], in numbering the houses of a street. It has the convenience
of permitting rearrangement with little renumbering. Between
the significant figures I insert zeros to show that the ideas deter-
minative of the following numbers are not mere specializations
of those which precede.
