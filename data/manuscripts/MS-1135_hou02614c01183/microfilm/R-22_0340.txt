[u]323123113213 Prayer-Meetings, etc[/u] [ins]Amusement is any form of occupation in which the awakening of consciousness is the principal object. Fairs, dances, etc may have a religious cast, or may be intended to promote marriage which is closely connected with religion.[/ins]
