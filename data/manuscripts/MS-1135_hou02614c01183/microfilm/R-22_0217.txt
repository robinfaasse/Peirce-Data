[pn]A[/pn] A very few slight specimens of defects of Roget.
(Got together by looking through the book 20 minutes)

[u]Travel[/u] is classed under [u]Space; motion[/u].
[u]Consanguinity[/u] is classed under [u]Abstract Relations[/u]
[u]Number[/u] is separated from [u]Quantity[/u], with another class between
[u]Youth, adolescence, etc.[/u] under [u]Abstract Relations of Time[/u]
But [u]change[/u] is [ins]not[/ins] under [u]Time[/u]
[u]Category[/u] and [u]Predicament[/u], Greek and Latin equivalents, [del]are classed as[/del] [ins]contraries[/ins]
[u]Relation[/u] is the character of a thing as member of one of a class of pairs
or other sets. Hence, if it have any opposite, it is a character of
a thing as a separate individual, a [u]Quality[/u]. But Roget gives, as
the contrary of [u]Relation[/u], "dissociation, multifariousness, heteroge-
neity, intrusion" which are various relations having little in common.
[u]Law, morality, title, dignity, and renown[/u] are all classed under [u]Affections[/u]
[u]God, devil, angel, Bible, revelation, orthodoxy, creed, heresy[/u]
are all classed under [u]Affections[/u]
[u]Sorcery, divination, table-turning, astrology[/u] classed under [u]Affections[/u]
But [u]sensibility, sensitiveness, feeling, moral sensibility, consciousness[/u]
are classed (375) under [u]Matter[/u]
[u]Paralysis, numbness, coma, chloroform[/u] under [u]Affections[/u]
