[del]29[/del] [ins]29[/ins] [u]Making "comfortables"[/u] The bed covering called in N.Y. a "comfortable" I was brought up to call a "comforter". The C.D. says it is called a "comfort." It belongs to the art of constructing beds, a physiological art.
