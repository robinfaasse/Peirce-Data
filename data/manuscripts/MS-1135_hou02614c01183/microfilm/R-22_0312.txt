[u]Very high luminosity[/u]: white, snow-white (pure), [del]milk[/del] [ins]lily[/ins]-white (yellowish), milk-white (yellowish), silvery (still yellowish), pearl white (darker)

[u]Moderate luminosity[/u]: [ins]gray (general),[/ins] ash-colored (highly luminous) = cinereous = ashen, [del]grizzly (dark)[/del] [ins]slate-color (darker),[/ins] leadcolored

[u]Very low luminosity[/u]: black (general), swart (lightest), pitch-black, ebony, inky, jet-black, sooty, lampblack, velvet black, coalhole black.
[A black hole is the blackest attainable, black velvet comes next, lamp-black is much less black.]

[u]Emotions[/u]: White is the color of innocence and joy; [ins]and is used at Christmas, weddings, etc.[/ins] but with many peoples it is a color of death. Gray is a subdued and serious color. Black is the color of despair and mourning; and is used on Good Fridays, funerals, etc.
