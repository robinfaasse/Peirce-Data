The different ways of making the first moves of the game
are called the [u]openings[/u]. A [u]gambit[/u] is a game in which a
pawn is sacrificed in the opening for the sake of [ins]an[/ins] advantage
in position. The chief openings are as follows, where the moves
of the first player ("white") are recorded above the line and those
of the second player ("black") below that line:

1. [u]P-K.4[/u]
   P-K.4 ; 2. [u]Kt-KB3[/u] ; King's Knight's opening.

2. [u]P-Q3[/u] ; Philidor's defence.

2. [u]Kt-KB3[/u] ; Petroff's defence.
3. [u]Kt-B3[/u] ; Three Knights' game, or Petroff's defence.
3. [u]Kt-B3[/u] ; Four Knights' game.
