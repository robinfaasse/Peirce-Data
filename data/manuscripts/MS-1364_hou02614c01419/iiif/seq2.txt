The ROTATIONAL DISPLACEMENTS of
crystallography may conveniently be symbolled[del]ized[/del]
by a notation of Leibniz independently invented and improved
by me in my memoir of 1870, and applied to the expression
of dyadic relatives in their relation to their elements, or sin-
gular relatives, Capitals being used to denote single objects,
(A:B) is such a relative that (A:B)B = A and (A:B)C = 0 unless
C = B; and (A:B)(B:C) = A:C but (A:B)(C:D) = 0 unless C = B. Such
relatives as A:B can be added as well as non-commutatively
multiplied.
