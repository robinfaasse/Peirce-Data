As with other combustible gases, the [pn]98[/pn]
maximum combustibility is with
an excess of the combustible gas.

The temperature of inflammation is
only 480°. For most gases it is about 600°

Explosive mixtures of acetylene contained
in glass tubes are very readily inflamed by
heating them some instants [ins]over[/ins] [del]in[/del] an alcohol
lamp. The explosion comes long before
the glass softens.
