[u]Bromides[/u]
When $C_2H_2$ is passed through $Br$, Raboul (C.R. LIV [ins]122[/ins])
and Sabanejeff (Annalen CLXXVIII 112) what is
obtained is the tetra bromide $C_2H_2 Br_4$ and
a small quantity of $C_2H Br_3$ solid laminae melting 76°
So Plimpton. But Berthelot got dibromide boiling 130°
Tetrabromide mixed with own vol. alcohol and
zinc dust (Sabanejeff Berichte IX. 1441) gave
dibromide boiling 110°-111° Sp. Gr. at 0° 2.268 Did
not solidify at -17°
