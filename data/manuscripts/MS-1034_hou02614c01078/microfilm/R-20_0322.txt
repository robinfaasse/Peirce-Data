[date]1893 Jan 19[/date] ([u]Chem News LXVII 24[/u]) [pn]89[/pn]

[del]Morris W Travers presented to Chem[/del]
[del]Soc. a method for preparing[/del]
[del]acetylene[/del]

He remarks that Wöhler made
$\text{Ca}\text{C}_2$ by heating a calcium
zinc alloy with carbon in a
graphite crucible.

45 grams Na are placed at the
bottom of a deep iron bottle and an
intimate mixture of powdered gas
carbon and $\text{CaCl}_2$ well dried in a hot
iron dish is then introduced

Top of longnecked bottle screwed
on it is heated to bright redness
for half an hour. Then removed
stoppered & cooled
