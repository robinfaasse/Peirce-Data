[date]1892 Oct 17[/date]
C. Maquenne (C. R. cxv 558)
gives a new process [del]for preparing CaC[sub]2[/sub][/del]
by replacing [del]the alkaline earths[/del] [ins]precipitated[/ins] [del]by[/del]
[del]magnesium in presence of C.[/del]

Pure carbonate of baryta 26 grams.
Mg in powder 10.5
Animal carbon [ins]previously[/ins] calcined 4

Put into an iron bottle 700[sup]cc[/sup] capacity
furnished with an iron tube 30cm long
and 2cm internal diam. Heat to
bright redness.
After 4 minutes there is a lively
reaction. Immediately close the tube
and throw cold water on the bottle.
Open the bottle. It is MgO with 38%
BaC[sub]2[/sub] a little C and a trace of Ba(CN)[sub]2[/sub]
