[u]Reduction of acetylenedicarboxylic acid[/u].
Acetylenedicarboxylic acid was dissolved in glacial
acetic acid, zinc dust in excess added, and
heated 50°-60° for 2 days. White flocks came
down. They were dissolved in Aq, the solution
acidified by H₂SO₄ and shaken up with ether.
On dilution the ether gave up pure succinic acid.
The liquid was filtered from the zinc dust and decom-
posed by acetate of Pb. This precipitated the
acetylenedicarboxylic acid. No further pre-
cipitate appeared on standing. Hence no maleic acid.
The liquid was evaporated to get rid of the
acetic acid, the residue acidified with dilute
H₂SO₄ & shaken up with ether. On evaporating ether
succinic acid was got. The zinc dust gave no
fumaric acid.

Phenylacetylene with zinc dust and glacial
acetic acid gave styrol.
