New method for forming Acetylene-dibromide
He acts with Zn on tetrabromide of acetylene
in presence of alcohol and with refrigeration.
The dibromide boils 106° and 109° and smells
a little like chloroform.
Decomposed by alcoholic potassa forming
bromacetylene (spontaneously combustible)
