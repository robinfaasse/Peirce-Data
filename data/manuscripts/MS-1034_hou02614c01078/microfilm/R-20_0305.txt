<u>Plimpton</u>     <u>Bromiodide</u>

Treat I with Br mixed with 4 or 5 vols H₂O.
Warm mixture, leave it [del]at rest[/del] [ins]overnight[/ins]. Pour off from
excess of I. Pass C₂H₂ through the sol. shaking
continuously. Black oil separating is washed
with Thiosulphate solution. Distill in a current
of steam.

There remains reddish oil whi refuses to vola-
tilize.

Distillate freed from I and fractionally distilled
After 4 distillations, [del]the[/del] chief fractions were
110°-115° C₂H₂Br₂
145°-150° C₂H₂Br I
160°-190° solidified in ice Probably mostly C₂H₂Br₂I

C₂H₂Br I Heavy, colorless liquid becoming red
on exposure to light. Sp. Gr. at 0° (solid) 2.750 at
17.5° (liquid) 2.6272 Boils without Decomp at 150°
solidifies about 8°
Heated with alcoholic soda gives off gas having
properties of bromoacetylene
