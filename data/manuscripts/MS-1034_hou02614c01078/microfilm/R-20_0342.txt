0.1 gram fulminate will explode 25cc
acetylene
[u]$\text{C}_2\text{H}_2 = \text{C}_2 + \text{H}_2$[/u]

The paper that wrapped the fulminate was
not burned, having been only $\frac{1}{30,000,000}$ of a
second exposed to the flame.

The carbon obtained is amorphous. But it
contains a trace of graphite due to the high
temp. Amorphous carbon heated to $2500^\circ$
begins to change into graphite.

The same thing happens to cyanogen
[u]$\text{C}_2\text{N}_2 = \text{C}_2 + \text{N}_2$[/u]
