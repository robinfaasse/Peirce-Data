[u]J. Coquillion[/u] has note on action of vapor of water on the hydrocarbons at red heat.

He uses Pd wire, heated almost to white heat.

Marsh gas is decomposed its volume doubled
[u]CH₄ = C + 2H₂[/u]

Ethylene is decomposed its volume doubled
[u]C₂H₄ = 2C + 2H₂[/u]

With moisture
Marsh gas CH₄ + H₂O = [del]C₂O₂[/del] [ins]CO[/ins] + 3H₂ quadrupled vol.
Ethylene C₂H₄ + 2H₂O = 2CO [del]O₂[/del] + 4H₂ sextupled vol.

Marsh gas & oxygen
2CH₄ + [del]3[/del] [ins]O₂[/ins] = [del]CO₂[/del] [ins]CO[/ins] + [del]C₂O₂[/del] [ins]CO[/ins] + H₂ + 3H₂O

Acetylene has also two phases in its combustion. It deposits C more easily than does ethylene.

[date]1878 Nov 18[/date] [ins]Coquillion[/ins] makes another communication showing same thing takes place with Pt. only latter is apt to make explosions.
