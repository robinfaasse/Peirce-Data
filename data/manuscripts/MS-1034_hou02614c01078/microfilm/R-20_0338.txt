YC₂ and LaC₂ in electric furnace.
They are crystalline and brittle and have
a golden yellow color. Surface almost
as rapidly attacked by moisture of air as
that of Na. The carbides are decomposed
by H₂O with evolution of H₂ and "carburetted
hydrogen", the hydroxide of the metal
and graphitic carbon being formed.
