[u]Doumer, continued[/u]

[del]Loiscau[/del] [ins]A crusher[/ins] [del]mixer[/del] is made by M. F. Weidknecht:
built on principle of breaking on the fly by movable
jointed hammers. The pieces that wear out are
cheap & easily replaced.

A [del]mixer[/del] [ins]crusher[/ins] of capacity of 100 Kilos needs 4 horse power.

The quicklime and coke are introduced by "pelletees"
into the hopper (trémie) of the crusher [del]which plays[/del]
[del]the part of a[/del] ventilateur soufflant and throws
the crushed mixture into a box (caisse) covered
with with a cloth (toile) which communicates
with a conduit ending at the hopper of the chaine
à godets. After the formation of the carbide, there
remains a considerable waste (déchet) coming from
uncombined matters and containing cinders. These
residues have to pass to the blutoir (bolter?) before
going back into the electric furnace. Otherwise inferior
carbide would be obtained, giving 100 litres of gas
per kilo. A second Archimedean screw can carry
these residues to the bolter.

The Patin furnace thus described needs a
current of 1200 ampères at least and 60 volts.
It can produce 400 Kilos in 24 hours.
