[u]Acetylenechloriodide[/u] C₂H₂Cl I
Iodine powder with 6 times its weight Ag
has Cl passed in and kept cool until I
completely dissolved. Acetylene is led in.
Action very slow and requires continual shaking.
Never entirely loses color. Wash the heavy oil
with weak soda lye, dry, and distill. At 100°
a colorless liquid goes over. Then the distillate
begins to color and at 120° strong decomposition
takes place so that crystals of Iodine appear
in the cooling tube. There are then 3 substances.
One boils under 100° one at 114°-116° (Plimpton says 119°)
A third not distillable.
