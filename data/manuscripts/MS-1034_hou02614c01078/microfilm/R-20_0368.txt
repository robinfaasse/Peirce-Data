Chem. News. LXXIV. 268 (Proc. Chem. Soc.)

The Explosion of Acetylene with less than its own vol. O₂

Used leaden coil 5 metres long internal diam
13 mm. closed at each end by steel tap.
at one end stout glass firing piece, in which 2 Pt
wires were fused fused between steel tap & coil
other end connected with Hg manometer
Coil immersed in [del]bucket cold water[/del]

Filled with explosion mixture by displacement.
Mixture fired by electric spark. In all cases considerable
increase of pressure (after cooling) 260 to 370
mm of Hg. Products analyzed in a modified
McLeod apparatus.

Chief products CO and H but also C₂H₂ and CO

No methane.
C₂H₂ + O₂ = 2CO + H₂
2C₂H₂ + O₂ = 2CO + 2H₂ + 2C
3C₂H₂ + O₂ = 2CO + 3H₂ + 4C

Yet some steam formed

Electric arc makes & decomposes C₂H₂
