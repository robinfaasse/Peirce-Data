A glass combustion tube was heated
[del]to highest temperature attainable by[/del]
a big Fletcher blowpipe while pure acetylene
was flowing through. As the temp reached
[del]softening point of glass[/del], the C₂H₂ burst
into a lurid flame at point where it entered
zone of heat & cloud of carbon was swept
forward. But although cloud had to traverse
an inch or more of more highly heated tube
it was only at the point where it began that
luminosity was developed, proving it was
the heat of decomposition that did it
