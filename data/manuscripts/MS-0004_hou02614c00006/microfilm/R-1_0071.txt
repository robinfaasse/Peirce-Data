[u]The First Theory.[/u]

[u]Definition 1.[/u] Any [u]Blank[/u] is a symbol which could not be vaguer than it is (although it may be so connected with a definite symbol as to form, with it, a part of another partially definite symbol), yet which has a purpose.

[u]Axiom 1.[/u] It is the nature of every symbol to be blank in part.

[del]Example.[/del] [ins]Illustration.[/ins] The Library of the British Museum is a pretty full symbol; yet it is a blank as to what I had for breakfast this morning or whether I had any.

[u]Definition 2.[/u] Any [del]Sheet[/del] [ins]complete[/ins] [del]of[/del] [ins]that element of[/ins] an entire symbol which is the subject of whatever definiteness it may have; and any such element of an entire symbol would be a [u]Sheet[/u].
