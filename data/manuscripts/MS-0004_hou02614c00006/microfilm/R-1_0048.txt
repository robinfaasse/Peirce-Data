I shall prefix to each article a descriptive heading, inventing terms for the purpose when necessary, but as far as possible availing myself of [del]those[/del] [ins]these[/ins] found in editions of Euclid's Elements; viz., Definition, Postulate, Axiom, Theorem and Demonstration, Problem and Solution, Corollary, and Scholium. The first three of these are technical terms whose signification could not be changed without a violation of the ethics of terminology. To the others, I consider myself at liberty to attach definite meanings not in violation of their present vague meanings.
