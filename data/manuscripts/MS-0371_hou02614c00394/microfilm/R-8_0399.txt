[u]Logic — Chapter IV.[/u]
[u]Of Reality.[/u]

Investigation supposes a true and a false, truth and
falsity being independent of all opinion upon the matter.
The name [u]real[/u] is applied to that which is independent
of how you or I or any number of minds think it
to be.

It is a truism to say that the character of what I think
depends entirely on what I think it to be. The real is not,
therefore, [u]per se[/u] an immediate object of thought, even though
my thoughts may happen to coincide with it. Yet the real
must influence thought or I could not by following any
rules of reasoning arrive at any truth.
