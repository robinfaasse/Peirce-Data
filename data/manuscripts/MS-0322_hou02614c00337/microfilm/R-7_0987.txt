Nevertheless, it is possible to detect pragmatistic tendencies in Kant, who derived them from Berkeley, a very distinguished master of the pragmatist mode of thinking. The illuminating definitions that are scattered through Locke's "Essay concerning Human Understanding" are pragmatistic. Some wishes to go further back, Aristotle had a tinge of this quality of thought, which came to him, in part at least, from Socrates.
