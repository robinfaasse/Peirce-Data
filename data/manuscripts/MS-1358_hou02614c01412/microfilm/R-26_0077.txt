[fig]Diagram of a hanging link structure with angles[/fig]

Now the angles $\theta_0$ and $\theta_N$ may vary -
First suppose $\theta_0$ and $\theta_N$ to be fixed as before -

We must first find the geometrical conditions determining
$l_0$ and $l_N$ the first & last link lengths

$$l_0 \cos \theta_0 + \sum_1^{N-1} l_n \cos \theta_n + l_N \cos \theta_N = a$$
$$l_0 \sin \theta_0 + \sum_1^{N-1} l_n \sin \theta_n + l_N \sin \theta_N = b$$

$$\begin{array}{r}
\sin(a+b) + \sin(a-b) = 2 \sin a \cos b \\
\sin(a+b) - \sin(a-b) = 2 \cos a \sin b
\end{array}$$

$$l_0 \sin (\theta_N - \theta_0) = a \sin \theta_N - b \cos \theta_N - \sum_1^{N-1} l_n \sin (\theta_N - \theta_n)$$
$$= \sqrt{a^2+b^2} [del]\sin \Theta[/del] \sin (\theta_N - \Theta) - \sum_1^{N-1} l_n \sin (\theta_N - \theta_n)$$

$$l_0 = \sqrt{a^2+b^2} \frac{\sin (\theta_N - \Theta)}{\sin (\theta_N - \theta_0)} - \sum_1^{N-1} l_n \frac{\sin (\theta_N - \theta_n)}{\sin (\theta_N - \theta_0)}$$

[del]$$l_N = \frac{\sqrt{a^2+b^2} \sin (\Theta - \theta_0)}{\sin (\theta_N - \theta_0)}$$[/del]  $$l_N = \sqrt{a^2+b^2} \frac{\sin (\Theta - \theta_0)}{\sin (\theta_N - \theta_0)} - \sum_1^{N-1} l_n \frac{\sin (\theta_n - \theta_0)}{\sin (\theta_N - \theta_0)}$$

$$l_N \sin (\theta_N - \theta_0) = b \cos \theta_0 - a \sin \theta_0 + \sum_1^{N-1} l_n \sin (\theta_n - \theta_0)$$
