[marginalia]Morison Bridge [date]1899 Feb 23[/date][/marginalia] [pn]3[/pn]

[fig]Line drawing of a bridge profile[/fig]

$y = a + bx + cx^2$
$D_x y = b + 2cx = \tan \theta$
$b + 2cx = \theta$

[fig]Line drawing of a bridge profile[/fig]

$D_x \theta = 2c$
$\int \frac{B}{2dx} [del]\left(\frac{d\theta}{dx}\right)^2[/del] (d\theta)^2 = \int 2Bc^2 dx = 2Bc^2 x$

From $-N$ to [del]$E$[/del] [ins]$I$[/ins] $y = a + bx + cx^2$
From $I$ to $J$ $y = a' + b'x + c'x^2$
From $J$ to $+N$ $y = a'' + b''x + c''x^2$

Energy of the bending $= 2B [del]c^2[/del] [ins]$[c^2(I+N) + c'^2(J-I) + c''^2(N-J)]$[/ins]$

$\int w y dx = w(a'x + \frac{1}{2}b'x^2 + \frac{1}{3}c'x^3)$
Energy of the lifting $= w \left[ a'(J-I) + \frac{b'}{2}(J^2-I^2) + \frac{c'}{3}(J^3-I^3) \right]$

$D_x y = 0$ when $x = -\frac{b}{2c}$
Then $y = a - \frac{b^2}{2c} + \frac{b^2}{4c} = a - \frac{b^2}{4c}$

$y - a + \frac{b^2}{4c} = 2p \left(x + \frac{b}{2c}\right)^2$
$b + c + \frac{b^2}{4c} = 2p \left(1 + \frac{b}{2c}\right)^2$

$p = \frac{1}{2} \frac{b + c + \frac{b^2}{4c}}{\left(1 + \frac{b}{2c}\right)^2}$

$\begin{aligned}
-b + c + \frac{b^2}{4c} &= c \left(\frac{b}{2c} - 1\right)^2 \\
&= c \left(\frac{b^2}{4c^2} - \frac{b}{c} + 1\right) \\
&= \frac{1}{4} \frac{b^2}{c} - b + c \\
&= \frac{1}{2} \frac{4bc^2 + 4c^3 + b^2c}{4c^2 + 4bc + b^2} \\
&= \frac{1}{2} c
\end{aligned}$
