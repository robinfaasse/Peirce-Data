<u>Catenary</u>

Let $p_0$ $p_1$ $p_2$..
be the pulls along string
$\phi$ $\chi$ $\psi$ inclinations to horizon
$m$ $n$ the weights

$-p_0 \sin \phi + p_1 \sin \chi = m$
$-p_1 \sin \chi + p_2 \sin \psi = n$

$p_0 \cos \phi = p_1 \cos \chi = p_2 \cos \psi$

$-\tan \phi \cos \chi + \sin \chi = \frac{m}{p_1}$
$-\sin \chi + \tan \psi \cos \chi = \frac{n}{p_1}$

[del]$\tan \chi$[/del]
$\tan \phi = \tan \chi - \frac{m}{p_1 \cos \chi}$
$\tan \psi = \tan \chi + \frac{n}{p_1 \cos \chi}$

$\tan \phi - 2 \tan \chi + \tan \psi = 0$

$\frac{d^2}{ds} \tan \chi = 0$
