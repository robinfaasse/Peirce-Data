$$= \frac{\splitfrac{\sqrt{a^2+b^2} \sin \Theta \cdot \sin (\theta_N - \theta_0) - \sum_{m+1}^{N-1} n \sin \theta_n \cdot \sin (\theta_N - \theta_0)}{-\sqrt{a^2+b^2} \sin \theta_N \sin (\Theta - \theta_0) + \sum_1^{N-1} n \sin (\theta_n - \theta_0) \sin \theta_N}}{\div \sin (\theta_N - \theta_0)}$$
