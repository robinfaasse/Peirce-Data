$D_s \cos \theta = D_s \theta \cdot \sin \theta$
$(D_s \theta)^2 (1 - \cos^2 \theta) = (D_s \cos \theta)^2$
$D_s^2 \cos \theta = - D_s^2 \theta \cdot \sin \theta - (D_s \theta)^2 \cos \theta$

$b + 2cs + 3ds^2 = - D_s \theta \sin \theta$
$D_s \theta = - \frac{b + 2cs + 3ds^2}{\sin \theta}$
$(D_s \theta)^2 = \frac{(b + 2cs + 3ds^2 + \text{etc})^2}{1 - \cos^2 \theta} = \frac{(b + 2cs + 3ds^2 + 4es^3 + \text{etc})}{(1 + a + bs + cs^2 + ds^3 + \text{etc})^2}$
