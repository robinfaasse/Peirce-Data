As for my idea for weakening the truss, it is of no
immediate importance. Still, you do not seem to
"catch on." My notion is to allow quite large
deflections of the truss but to so adjust the floor
or road bed that [del]to[/del] when the train is on a given part
of it, its weight brings that part into a very level, or
so that the centre of gravity of the train is not
rising [ins]the trains on each track moving only one
way; [del]and[/del] [ins]and when the train comes forward to the next part, that becomes level.[/ins][/ins] Since you have four cables there might be prac-
tically two bridges, as far as flexures are concerned. The
train could gain grade before reaching the bridge, making
larger [del]changes[/del] deformations permissible.
