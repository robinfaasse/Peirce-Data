$= \frac{1}{P_0} (1 - R_0 [(x_1-x_0)^2 + (y_1-y_0)^2]^{-\frac{1}{2}})$

$\delta \boxed{\rho_0} = \frac{R_0}{P_0} [(x_1-x_0)^2 + (y_1-y_0)^2]^{-\frac{3}{2}} [(x_1-x_0)(\delta x_1 - \delta x_0) + (y_1-y_0)(\delta y_1 - \delta y_0)]$

$\boxed{\rho_1} = \frac{1}{P_0} (1 - R_1 [(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_1-y_2)^2]^{-\frac{1}{2}})$

$\delta \boxed{\rho_1} = \frac{R_1}{P_0} [(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_1-y_2)^2]^{-\frac{3}{2}} [(x_2-x_1)(\delta x_2 - \delta x_1) + (y_1-y_2)(\delta y_1 - \delta y_2)]$

Consequently the differential equations are

$\frac{\delta x_0}{\Lambda_0} - \boxed{\rho_0} (\delta x_1 - \delta x_0) - \frac{R_0}{P_0} \frac{\boxed{x_1-x_0}}{\boxed{\boxed{x_1-x_0} + \boxed{y_1-y_0}}}^{-\frac{3}{2}} (\delta x_1 - \delta x_0)$
$- \frac{R_0}{P_0} (x_1-x_0)(y_1-y_0) \frac{\boxed{x_1-x_0} + \boxed{y_1-y_0}}^{-\frac{3}{2}} (\delta y_1 - \delta y_0) + \boxed{1} = 0$

$(\frac{1}{\Lambda_0} + \boxed{\rho_0} + \frac{1}{P_0} \frac{R_0}{R_0} \frac{\boxed{x_1-x_0}}{\boxed{\boxed{x_1-x_0} + \boxed{y_1-y_0}}}) \delta x_0 + [ - \boxed{\rho_0} - \frac{1}{P_0} \frac{R_0}{R_0} \frac{\boxed{x_1-x_0}}{\boxed{\boxed{x_1-x_0} + \boxed{y_1-y_0}}} ] \delta x_1$
$+ \frac{1}{P_0} \frac{R_0}{R_0} \frac{(x_1-x_0)(y_1-y_0)}{\boxed{\boxed{x_1-x_0} + \boxed{y_1-y_0}}} \delta y_0 - \frac{1}{P_0} \frac{R_0}{R_0} \frac{(x_1-x_0)(y_1-y_0)}{\boxed{\boxed{x_1-x_0} + \boxed{y_1-y_0}}} \delta y_1 + \boxed{1} = 0$
