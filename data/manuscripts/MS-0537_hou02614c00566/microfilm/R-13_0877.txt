[del]and that it is the duty of every citizen, not only to refrain from giving aid and comfort to the enemy, but also to support, with all his power, the government of the country in going through the struggle. It is manifest, and indeed, now universally admitted, that the rebel cause is hopeless, that it cannot succeed, and that the longer the contest is prolonged, the greater will be the misery and ruin of those states which are the theatre of war. It is, therefore, the interest of the people of those states, that the authority of the government should be quickly and entirely restored; and every citizen who is loyal to his country, and who desires the welfare of his state, should lend all his aid and countenance[/del]
