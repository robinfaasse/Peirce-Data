[del]This work is intended to be a companion to my
other works, the [u]Prior Analytics[/u] and [u]Posterior
Analytics[/u], which I expect to publish soon.[/del]

The work consists of a translation of the text,
with a commentary intended to explain the meaning
of the author, and to show how his views are
related to those of modern logicians.

The translation is based on the text of Bekker,
but I have not hesitated to depart from it
when there seemed to be good reason for doing so.
In the commentary, I have endeavoured to avoid
all unnecessary technicalities, and to make the
meaning of the author as clear as possible.
