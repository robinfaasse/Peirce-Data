We may denote the self part and alio part
of a relative by a capital S and A before
the symbol of the relative. Thus,
$$l = \text{S}l + \text{A}l.$$
Every relative [del]may[/del] is in like manner, in one
way only, the non-relative product of
the negative of a self-relative and the nega-
tive of an alio-relative. Thus,
$$l = \bar{\text{S}}l \times \bar{\text{A}}l.$$
In fact,
$$\bar{\text{S}}l = \underline{n} + \text{S}l$$
$$\bar{\text{A}}l = 1 + \text{A}l$$ [fig]crossed-out symbol[/fig]
