Let $A, B, C,$ etc. denote all the individual things in the universe. Let $m$ [del]denote[/del] [ins]be a[/ins] general term, as 'man'. Then $m$ is an aggregate of some of the letters $A, B, C,$ etc. This may be represented by the expression
$$m = (m)_a A + (m)_b B + (m)_c C + \text{etc.}$$
Here, $(m)_a$ etc. are numerical coefficients which are either zero or unity. If any co-efficient say $(m)_k$ is zero, then the individual $K$ does not belong to the class denoted by $m$. If on the other hand $(m)_k$ is unity, $K$ is an $m$. $$A = 2A = 3A = \text{etc.}$$
