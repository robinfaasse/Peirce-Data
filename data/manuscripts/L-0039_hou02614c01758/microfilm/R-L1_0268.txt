DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
UNITED STATES GEOLOGICAL SURVEY
WASHINGTON, D. C., [date]Feb 3, 1897[/date]

My dear Charles;-

The vouchers have come and the check will be mailed before noon.

You say that I did not have the vouchers made out as I promised. Your telegram urging immediate payment came Saturday afternoon at 3:30. The chief [del]clerk[/del] disbursing clerk was already gone. By favour, I secured vouchers with merely the amount filled in and got them into the mail before four oclock. I could do no more.

I promised [del]that I would[/del] [ins]to[/ins] do my best to have the vouchers assimilated to that used with other members of the Academy. The vouchers, I find, must be made out in terms of the special authority granted by the Sec'y of the Interior. They read:- "For services rendered in preparing an expert report on the origin of slaty cleavage during the period from Jan 5 to Jan 30." I do not think you can possibly find that objectionable.
