142 Hill Street,
Newport, R.I.

My dear Charles;-

I have been on a journey
or I should have answered your note
enclosing my M.S. before.

It is of no consequence that you
kept the M.S. so long. I rewrote
the paper and, I believe, made the
arguments more cogent. It has
passed through Newton's hands &
is in type. It will appear Nov 1.

The "Cent. Dic" gives [u]dilation[/u] as
a short form of [u]dilatation[/u], and I like
short forms.
