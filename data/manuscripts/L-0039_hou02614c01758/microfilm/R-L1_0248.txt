My dear Charles;-

Have you had time to look at my paper on the finite stress-strain function again? I don't mean by inquiring to hurry you; for I know by experience what a nuisance it is to examine another man's M.S., and how easy it is to postpone it even when one has leisure.

Yours faithfully,
G. F. Becker
