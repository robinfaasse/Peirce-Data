As a chemist mechanical inventiveness and skill in
manipulation distinguish his work. His lamps and his method
of filtration are cases in point. Many of his analytical methods
have failed of wider adoption because few chemists possessed
the skill in manipulation needful for their application.

His recreations were novel reading and travelling, par-
ticularly in Italy.

If he is dead the world has lost one of the most manly
generous and ingenuous of its inhabitants.

Yours ever
G. F. Becker
