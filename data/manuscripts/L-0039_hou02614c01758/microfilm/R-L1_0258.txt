A per diem basis would be much the best for a connection
between you and the Survey. It would leave you free for other
work while a salary, as you know, means devoting 7 hours
each work day to government work.

You can't frighten me about that $50. It was loaned to
you till it should be convenient to repay it, and you would'nt
keep your bargain if you were to repay it earlier. I hope
with all my heart that it may soon be convenient, but
not at all because I want the money. I don't miss it.

This note is merely to prevent you from declining
the perfectly proper fee offered you. The rest of your
letter I will try to answer as soon as I have posted
this.

Yours faithfully
G. F. Becker
