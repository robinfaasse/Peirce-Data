Boston [date]27th Nov. 91[/date]

G. F. Becker Esq.
Dear Sir,

Mrs Agassiz has
[del]sent me[/del] [ins]forwarded[/ins] your letter to her in regard
to Mr Charles Peirce. Nothing would give
me greater pleasure than to engage
Mr Peirce to deliver a course of lectures
before the Lowell Institute during the
winter of 1892/3. This would give
the time I presume that he would
need to write out the lectures, and
I should hope that they would
eventually be given to the public
