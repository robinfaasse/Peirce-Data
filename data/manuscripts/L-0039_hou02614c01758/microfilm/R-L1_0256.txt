DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
UNITED STATES GEOLOGICAL SURVEY

Washington D.C. [date]Jan 13, 1897[/date]

My dear Charles;-

The Director wrote to you last week offering you $150.- for an opinion on a matter of physics. His letter was addressed to you at

"84 Broad Street, Room 6, New York City."

This is the address which Mr Lodge gave me a couple of weeks since.

As there is no reply from you, I fear the letter may have failed to reach you, and I will send this to Milford.

I was much gratified at your seconding my nomination to the N.A.

I sent you some papers to the N.Y. address above, and I have to thank you for your paper in the Monist.

Yours Sincerely,
G. F. Becker

Prof. C. S. Peirce,
Milford Pa.
