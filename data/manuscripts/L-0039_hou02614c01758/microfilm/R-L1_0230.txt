"The Shoreham"
Washington, D.C.
[date]Nov 28, 1891[/date]

My dear Charles;-

Enclosed please find a
very satisfactory letter from Mr
Augustus Lowell offering to engage
you to deliver a course of lectures.
He leaves you free to select your
subject; but I do hope you will
stick to the History of Science
from Galileo to Newton.

matter, and she forwarded my
letter with a note from herself to
