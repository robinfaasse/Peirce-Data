I expect to return to Washington
this week. My future is uncertain; for
my connection with the Geol. Survey
has entirely ceased, and no opening
has appeared. If you hear of any one
who wants to pay a good salary for
a Prof. of Geology, send them my way.

I mean to write up the history of
mechanical geology meanwhile. If
you run across any interesting items
about the subject, please save them.
My first paper will be a review of
Cartesian geology.

My address in Washington will be
"The Shoreham."

Yours faithfully
G. F. Becker.
