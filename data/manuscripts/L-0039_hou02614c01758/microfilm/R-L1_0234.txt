[u]142 Mill Street,[/u]
Newport, R. I.
[date]Oct 1 1891[/date]

My dear Charley;-

I have been asked where
to find "Prof. B Peirce's theory
or suggestion about the trend of
continental outlines in great
circles of the sphere as produced
by the action of the sun."

I am ashamed to say I have
never heard of it. For my sake
as well as that of the inquirer
will you please refer me to the

***

source, if indeed your father ever
propounded such a theory.

The Survey is in a frightful mess.

I hope you are getting on with
that fascinating historical research,
and that I may meet you in
Washington this Autumn.

Sincerely yours,
G. F. Becker
