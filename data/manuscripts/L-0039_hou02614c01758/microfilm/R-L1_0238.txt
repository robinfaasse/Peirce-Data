I. Let the cube be incompressible. Then $k = \infty$ and
the volume, or $x^2y$, is constantly unity. For this case
also $\sigma = 1/2$. For the corner of the cube, or for $x_0 = y_0 = 1$
one may therefore write
$$x y^{1/2} = x y^\sigma = 1 \qquad \therefore - y\,dx/x\,dy$$
