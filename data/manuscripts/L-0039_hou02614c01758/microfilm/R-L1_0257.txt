[u]Private[/u]

DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
UNITED STATES GEOLOGICAL SURVEY
WASHINGTON, D. C., [date]Jan 15, 1897[/date]

My dear Charles;-

I beg you won't refuse the Director's offer of pay and I assure you it will be in no respect "infra dig." to take it. The Survey employs nearly all the professors of geology in the country on odd jobs and pays them a per diem while actually employed. The highest is $[ins]10.[/ins] a day while actually at work. I have good reasons for suspecting that the question referred to you was estimated as a couple of weeks' work and the offer of pay graded to a $[ins]10[/ins] per day basis. Newberry worked for it on the same terms; Shaler and Chamberlin are similarly paid; for some months I too was on this footing; so are Iddings, Penrose and many others. A delicacy on your part to do the same won't be appreciated.
