Va "The Shoreham" Washington DC
[date]Dec 7. 1891.[/date]

Dear Charles;- Did you receive an
offer for lectures from Lowell which
I forwarded to you some ten days ago?
I should hate to hear it miscarry.

I asked you what was the best
translation of Aristotle, but I forgot
to make a note of your answer

Yours truly G. F. Becker
