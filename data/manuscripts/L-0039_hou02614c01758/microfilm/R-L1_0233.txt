DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
UNITED STATES GEOLOGICAL SURVEY
WASHINGTON, D. C., [date]Jan 12, 1892[/date].

My dear Charles, —
I am going to ask you to do me the great favour of reading the enclosed type written M S on the Finite Stress-Strain Function. I have given it great care, but I do not dare to publish it without consultation.
You will see at a glance the great importance of the subject, especially to terrestrial physics. To publish an erroneous discussion would be awful!
Please find every fault you can with it.
I expect to send by the same mail, or the next, a paper referred to in the enclosed. It is called [u]Finite, Homogeneous Strain Flow & Rupture of Rocks[/u] and I hope will interest you. I talked to you about it when we last met.
I hope you History of Science from Galileo to Newton is getting on pleasantly.

Very sincerely yours
G. F. Becker

Prof C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.
