DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
UNITED STATES GEOLOGICAL SURVEY
WASHINGTON, D. C., [date]Feb. 5, 1893.[/date]

My dear Charles;-

I am very glad to hear that the lectures went off pleasantly, and I trust they are to be published. I heard of you among old friends and indulged myself in the hope that renewed contact with the old circle might lead to some appointment at the Observatory, Scientific School, School of Technology, or elsewhere, which would assure you a living and a chance to employ that brain of yours in a manner worthy of its fine quality. Did you hear of any such place? I have not a particle of real influence in Cambridge but, if such a place exists, I could at least draw attention to the fact that you might accept it, and to your qualifications, in terms which modesty would forbid you to employ. I have greatly enjoyed the thought that I was instrumental in bringing you and the Lowell Inst. into conjunction, and should like some more similar sensations.

I enclose a curious historical note on "Potential".
