Let $w = 6k\psi$. Then if $M$ is Young's modulus
$\alpha = \varepsilon^{w/6k}$; $h = \varepsilon^{w/9k}$; $\alpha^2 h = y = \varepsilon^{w/M} = 1 + \frac{w}{M} \dots\dots\dots$
$\frac{1}{\alpha} = x = \varepsilon^{-w/6k} = 1 - \frac{w}{M} \dots\dots\dots$ and $xy = 1$

<u>Value of $w$</u>. Experiment shows that $\alpha^2 h - 1 = F/M$
where $F$ is [ins]being small relatively to $M$[/ins] coincident with stress and with load. It therefore
also coincides with $w$. Finally, since $M$ is a resistance
at zero strain, $w$ is the external force at zero strain,
i.e. the load or initial stress, say $L$, or
$$\underline{w = L.}$$
