[u]1[sup]st[/sup][/u] Anything is itself, or a < a. This is called the principle of identity.
[u]2[sup]nd[/sup][/u] If a is b then whatever is a is b. This is called the dictum de omni. This is as much as to say that [del]it is of[/del] from a < b we can reason thus: -
