C. S. Peirce Esq.
Brunswick Hotel, Boston.

My dear Sir, Thanks for your
kind and sympathetic answer
to my letter. I write this day
sent by express to Boston
a most suggestive series of
photos of my tracings made in
a peculiar case that occur-
red in the Station Hospital
Guernsey, C.I. when I was Surgeon
[del]Major[/del] [ins]Major[/ins] in charge in the beginning
of '88. I had been in full practice
with the "Staggs" all the latter
part of the previous year and
had found out some new things.
The case I refer to was as follows:
Two men in the prisoners' ward
had to stay range in her own room
into which her husband might
unseasonably intrude. Mrs Y. never
saw or spoke to the prisoner. To
my knowledge and all through the
research there was no attempt to
get the better of me that I could
perceive. Sometimes I was with
the men lying on their beds
side by side with them myself
so as to diminish any battery on
the notes or other source of
deceptive noise. At such times
I have heard the knocks in the
room just as I had often heard
in the presence of "mediums".
In fact these people had all
intents and purposes "mediums"
and I have seen the same character
of neurotic trace which they
exhibit as the case of a poor
medium who for a time was under my
observation of abnormal neurotic
pains or expression of energy.
