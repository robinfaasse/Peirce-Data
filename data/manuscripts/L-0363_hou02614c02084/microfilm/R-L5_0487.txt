C. S. Peirce Esq.
Arisbe New Milford, Pa.

My dear Mr. Peirce,

I write to ask
to what address I shall
send some photographs
I have had made. I am leaving,
at once, for Florida,
and shall not been able to
write what I consider the
forced resemblance, [ins]at least in some,[/ins] which
calls for care and time.

These of course you
will understand are only
references to save time
and trouble on the part of
the examiner, who must
use his own judgment and
draw his own conclusions.
