[pn]= G.1893.1[/pn] VE3b#3

Will Prof. Peirce kindly
write on the slip of paper in
the enclosed envelope the name
of the publication, the January
number of which, contained his
article upon "Science in its relations
to Christianity." Trusting he will
excuse the liberty taken by one
who heard his lectures, in the
Lowell Institute, with much
edification & pleasure.

L. M. Alline.

[date]Feb 2nd, 1893.[/date]
