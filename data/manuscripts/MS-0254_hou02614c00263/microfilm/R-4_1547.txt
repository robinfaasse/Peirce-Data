[u]Of Space Measurement[/u].

[u]Art. . Analysis[/u] . Our measures of space are of various
descriptions. Some of them, as square measure, cubic measure,
etc. are composite. Thus, square measure depends upon long
measures of length and breadth. Other kinds of measure are
derivative. Thus, the distance of a point from a surface is the
distance of the point from the nearest point of the surface.
The measures which are neither composite nor derivative are,
two longmeasure between pairs of points and angular mea-
sure between pairs of planes.
