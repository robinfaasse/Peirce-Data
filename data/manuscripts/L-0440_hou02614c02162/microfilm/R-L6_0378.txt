[date]Nov. 23, 1903[/date]

My dear Sir:

I am editing, at Mrs. Fiske's request, the Letters, &c., of John Fiske. Have you such letters of his which you will kindly send me? Can you not recall characteristic sayings, or episodes? Did he ever belong to the old Philosophical Club? How serious was the "ostracism" he endured on account of his supposed Atheism from 1869 to 1872? These are some of the questions I would put.
