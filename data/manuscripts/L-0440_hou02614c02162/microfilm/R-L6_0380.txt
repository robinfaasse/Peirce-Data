[marginalia]8 Berkeley St.,
Cambridge: [date]Nov. 29, 1903[/date].[/marginalia]

My dear Sir:

Many thanks for
your letter. I am glad to know
that you are in Cambridge,
and I shall have the
pleasure of calling at once.

The "ostracism" I referred
to was not social - I
don't think I mentioned that
word - but professional.
