Fiske's reputation for "atheism"
after his lectures on Comte
prevented his getting a position
to teach at Harvard, & the
Lowell Institute was closed
against him. The fact is un-
doubted, & illustrates a state
of mind in our public which
I hope to get a fuller account
of. Very truly yours,
[u]W. R. Thayer[/u]

To C. S. Peirce, Esq.
