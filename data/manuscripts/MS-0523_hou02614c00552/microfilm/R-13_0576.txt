Notes of Schröder's [u]3[sup]d[/sup] Volume[/u].

p. 17. $\Sigma$ and $\Pi$ are not mere abbreviations. There is a great difference between a sum [ins]or product[/ins] of separately designated terms, and a description of a sum of [del]described[/del] [ins]described, not designated[/ins] terms, which are potentially innumerable.

p. 24. Dr. Schröder has perhaps forgotten that in my paper of [date]1870[/date] I wrote $1_{1} = 1$ and generally used a comma to mean the increase by one of the order of a relative by the internal multiplication of "that is". I think his apostrophe better.

But it seems to me [del]not[/del] should not be written the same way, but rather $l^{s}$ where $s$ means [del]$s$[/del] [ins]$\bar{s}$[/ins]. So $l^{s}$ will mean "lover of [del]other[/del] [ins]other[/ins] other."
