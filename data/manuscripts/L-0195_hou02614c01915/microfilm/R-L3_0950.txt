COLLINGWOOD,
HAWKHURST.

My dear Peirce

It is uselessly more than
a twelvemonth since I wrote to
Prof Hilgard about the return
of the R. Soc's invariable pendulums
and though of course I had his
answer promptly enough, nothing
has yet been done towards getting
them returned. Things have
got into a strange deadlock,
it seems, on this side. It is
10 months since my report was
sent in, & I have heard not
a single word as to its fate
from the India office. All

my sent in my report; I can
