My dear Peirce

Will you be so
kind as to send me, as
soon as you can after
getting this, either the ori-
ginal (if not in use) or
a copy (tracing or rubbing)
of the card of discs used
by me for observing coin-
cidences. I very foolishly
trusted to my memory &

[page break]

But I have really no
time to chatter, so excuse
me if I restrict this
note to the one subject
which is — please send
me my discs or a copy
of them, & believe me

Ever yours truly

Herschel
