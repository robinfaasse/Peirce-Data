My dear Peirce

you are too obliging,
more to say kind. I do not see
what right I have to allow you
take so much trouble for us.

However as it is possible you
may wish to go to New York any
way, I must not decline positive-
ly. I will only say that I have
every reason to believe we shall
do very well with such introduction
as we already have. I have
found myself very little able to
take advantage of the admission
to the clubs here, and fear the con-
sequent risk of misconstruction, a
little.
