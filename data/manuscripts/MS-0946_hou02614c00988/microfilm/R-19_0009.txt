An Outline Sketch of the Synechistic Philosophy.

The word [u]synechistic[/u] is not in the dictionary; so that it is fitting to say that it is formed from the ordinary Greek word, [u]συνεχής[/u], for continuous, and naturally means 'given to making continuous.' There are countless instances of precisely similar formations; thus,

from γυμνός naked, γυμναστικός given to athletics,
from [del]ἀγορά[/del] [ins]ἀγορά[/ins] a marketplace, ἀγοραστικός commercial,
from βάδος a walk, βαδιστικός good at walking,
from ἕλκω to draw, ἑλκυστικός attractive,
from δάνος misery, δανειστικός in the money-lending business,
from κήδω to plague, κηδεστικός dealing with relatives by marriage,
[del]etc[/del] from χράω to supply a want, χρηστικός oracular,
etc. The word is thus good in form. It also admits of a cognate opposite [u]diechistic[/u] from διέχω, to keep apart.
