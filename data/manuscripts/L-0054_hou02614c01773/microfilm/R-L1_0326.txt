Follen St. [date]Dec. 23 - 1867[/date]

My dear Sir,

Many thanks for the copy, which you were kind enough to send me of your three able and scholarly "Papers on Logic." I have already bestowed considerable study on them, and intend to examine them still more closely. The first in order I do not meddle with, as it is too mathematical for my taste or attainments.

Your new analysis of the whole doctrine of Syllogism is highly ingenious and apparently, complete; but I am in doubt as to any advantages which may be claimed for it over the old analysis, which appears to me superior to it in simplicity and perspicuity.
