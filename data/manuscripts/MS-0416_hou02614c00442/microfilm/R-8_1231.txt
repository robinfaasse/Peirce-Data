∴ There is a quality of all Ss that is absent from all Ps.
Hence, again, No S is a P. But this last inference would
not hold if the phases of the two premises were not

[u]5th Species[/u]. Every P has ps for its sole qualities,
Every S has some quality or other beside the ps;
∴ Every S possesses a quality unpossessed by any P.
Hence, again, No S is a P, an inference only holding on
account of the antiphasis of the premises. But the general
conclusion of this species is much weaker than the
last which asserts that there is a single quality
which belongs to all Ss and no Ps; while the present
inference is that each S has some quality or another
absent from all Ps.

[u]6th Species[/u] Every P has ps for its sole qualities,
Some S has a quality not a p;
∴ Some S has a quality unpossessed by any P,
Hence, again (on account of the antiphasis) Some
S is not a P.
