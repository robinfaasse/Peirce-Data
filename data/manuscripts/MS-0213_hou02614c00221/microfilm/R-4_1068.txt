I now come to a reason which ought, I think,
to appeal still more strongly to the minds of mathe-
maticians as such. It is founded on the principle
that when an idea is introduced into mathemati-

it will go and not be mixed up with any heteroge-
neous idea that conflicts with it. I will give an
example or two to illustrate my mean. If, for example,
then, there be good reason for counting by tens and
powers of ten, I hold it to be bad mathematical pro-
