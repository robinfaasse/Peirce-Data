[u]A device of Computation[/u]

Suppose we have a series of numbers such that
$$u_{x+2} = a u_{x+1} + b u_x$$
where $a$ and $b$ are rational. Then
$$E^2 - aE = b$$
$$E = \frac{a}{2} \pm \sqrt{\frac{a^2}{4} + b}$$
$$u_x = C_1 \left(\frac{a}{2} + \sqrt{\frac{a^2}{4} + b}\right)^x + C_2 \left(\frac{a}{2} - \sqrt{\frac{a^2}{4} + b}\right)^x$$
$$= C_1 b^{\frac{1}{2}x} \left(\frac{a}{2\sqrt{b}} + \sqrt{\frac{a^2}{4b} + 1}\right)^x + C_2 b^{\frac{1}{2}x} \left(\frac{a}{2\sqrt{b}} + \sqrt{\frac{a^2}{4b} + 1}\right)^{-x}$$

If three rational numbers, $P$, $Q$, $N$ are such that
$$P^2 - Q^2 N = 1,$$
then $(P + Q\sqrt{N})(P - Q\sqrt{N}) = 1$. For any given $P$ there is one largest possible $Q$ and corresponding $N$. [ins]We write these $Q'$ and $N'$.[/ins] We have two functions of which the cosine and sine are special cases, which we may term the P-clos and P-slin, defined by the formulae,
$$\text{clos}_P\ x = \frac{(P + Q'\sqrt{N'})^x + (P + Q'\sqrt{N'})^{-x}}{2}$$
$$\text{slin}_P\ x = \frac{(P + Q'\sqrt{N'})^x - (P + Q'\sqrt{N'})^{-x}}{2\sqrt{N'}}$$

For the [del]cosine[/del] [ins]cosin[/ins] and sine, $P$, $Q$, $N$ are not rational but $P = 1 - \frac{1}{2!} + \frac{1}{4!} + \text{etc}$
$Q = 1 - \frac{1}{3!} + \frac{1}{5!} - \frac{1}{7!} + \text{etc}$. $N = -1$. We shall always have
$$u_x = C R^x \text{clos}_P \left(\frac{x - x_0}{T}\right)$$
for we have $\text{clos}_P\ 1 = P$ $\text{slin}_P\ 1 = Q'$ $\text{clos}_P\ 0 = 1$ $\text{slin}_P\ 0 = 0$

Now since in general $\frac{a A + b B}{2} = \frac{a+b}{2} \cdot \frac{A+B}{2} + \frac{a-b}{2} \cdot \frac{A-B}{2}$
$\text{clos}(x+y) = \text{clos} x \cdot \text{clos} y + N \text{slin} x \cdot \text{slin} y$
$\text{slin}(x+y) = \text{slin} x \cdot \text{clos} y + \text{clos} x \cdot \text{slin} y$
$\text{clos}(x+2) = P \text{clos}(x+1) + N Q' \text{slin}(x+1)$
$\text{slin}(x+2) = Q' \text{clos}(x+1) + P \text{slin}(x+1)$
