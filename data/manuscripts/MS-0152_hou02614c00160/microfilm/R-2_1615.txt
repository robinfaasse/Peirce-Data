If a rule is intelligible, we may always suppose it to be
carried out, and there is nothing absurd or unintelligible in the
result. For example, let the rule be that after each star
was created another was created. The result, in this case,
would be an inenumerable multitude of stars. There is
nothing unintelligible about that, then. Indeed, the cardi-
nal numbers form such a multitude.
