[u]Rule for the addition of several numbers.[/u]

1. Set them down with figures in the same deci-
mal place in the same vertical column.

2. Draw a line under them so as to leave
room for numbers carried.

3. Add the right hand column. To do this begin
[del]at the bottom[/del] look at the first two [ins]bottom[/ins] figures, and imagine
you see their sum. If you like, you may mentally pronounce
the name, without moving the lips; but this is not re-
commended. Put you pen on the third figure from
the bottom, and imagine you see the sum of this
and the [del]sum of[/del] [ins]previous sum[/ins]; so proceed till you have gone
through the column
