P 52] The [del]the truth[/del] [ins]conditional proposition[/ins] which the pragmaticist holds to constitute the
purport of a concept is in the article of Jan 1878 spoken of as expressing a "fact;"
and it does indeed express the actual state of mind of a person who has
made, or is ready to make, a resolve, — not a categorical resolve, but
a resolve conditional upon having a certain purpose. But nobody would
make any difficulty in admitting that every resolve is limited to
future acts, and acts are the most [del]perfectly[/del] individual objects
there are.
