accomplished, certain it is that somehow and in some true
and proper sense general ideas do produce stupendous physi-
cal effects. For it would be a miserable logomachy to deny
that a man's purpose of going down to his office causes him
to go there; well, a purpose is a general idea, and his going
is a physical fact. If it be objected that it is not the general
ideas, but the men who believe in them, that cause the physical
events, the answer is that it is the ideas that prompt men to
champion them, that inspire those champions with courage,
