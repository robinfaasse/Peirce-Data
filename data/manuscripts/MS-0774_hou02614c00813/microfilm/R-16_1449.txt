It would be needless, we trust, to interpose any warning
against inferring that a theory of rhetoric is false because
a given advocate of it exhibits little grace, dexterity, or
tact in the handling of language. For we all know
how seldom an author treating of a particular kind of
skill is found to be remarkably endowed himself with
the skill he discourses about. Many a time, it has been
precisely his consciousness of natural deficiency in that
respect that has led him to study the art.
