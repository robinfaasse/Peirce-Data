3rd. I claim the arrangement by which the solu-
tion is admitted into the decomposing tank between the
electrodes and is drawn out outside of the electrodes near
the one and the other, so as to separate the products of
electrolysis without the use of a diaphragm. In this way
no work is lost in passing the current through, an essential
point of superiority over all methods of electrolysis hith-
erto used for producing decomposition. It is applicable
not only to bleaching but to all electrolytic decomposition.

4th. I claim the process of alternately subject-
ing things to be bleached and cleansed to the repeated al-
ternate action of the two liquors resulting from an elec-
trolysis. The efficiency of bleaching first in an acid
solution, then washing in an alkaline solution, then again
bleaching in an acid solution, and finally washing in alkali
again, is surprising.
