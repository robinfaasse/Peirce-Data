Dear Sir:-

I have the pleasure of presenting you with an in-
vention of the highest value, in bleaching. My claims are
as follows:

1st. The application of a mixed solution of
Glauber's salts ($10. a ton) and common salt in the propor-
tion of 5 to 1, or the inverse ratio of their electrolytic
conductibilities, so as to directly isolate at the positive
pole hypochlorous acid, under the influence of the electric
current, for bleaching and other purposes. This is a chem-
ical action hitherto unknown to science, and consequently
open to a basic patent. If used substantially like Woolf's
solution it has within one per cent, one way or the other,
the same efficiency, with far greater intensity of action;
and the action is then cyclical.

2nd. I claim the use of a liquid which will
directly bleach at the positive pole, by the production of
hypochlorous acid or otherwise, and the drawing off from the
neighborhood of the negative pole of a liquor of greater
commercial value than the solution originally put in. The
advantage of a cyclical process is that the liquor is just
