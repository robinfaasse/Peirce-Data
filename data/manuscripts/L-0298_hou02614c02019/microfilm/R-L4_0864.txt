This simple observation is a complete demonstration that the
bleaching takes place in great measure by direct electrolysis; and
this is confirmed by the fact that there is no substance in particular
which is essential to the process. I find that it is possible to
bleach tolerably well with any of the following:-

Caustic Soda.

Sulphuric Acid.

Glauber's Salts, Sulphate of Soda.

Acetate of Soda.

Nitrate of Ammonia.

None of these substances contain a particle of chlorine.
The reason why chlorine is of advantage in the mixture is that its
tendency to bleach makes a superior electromotive tendency carrying
the current through the coloring matter and not around it.

I have thus proved that as a matter of fact in the Woolf
process the coloring matter is, at least in part, destroyed by direct
electrolysis. I am still ignorant of the nature of this electrolysis
