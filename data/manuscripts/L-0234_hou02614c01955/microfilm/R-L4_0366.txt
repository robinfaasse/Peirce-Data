Mr. Charles S. Peirce.-
Dear Sir:

On page 348 of the "Gypsies" by C. G. Leland, appears the following:
"Ek keri, ak kery, ukery an," etc.
The common version of the rhyme begins with:- "An'eri, two-ry, ikkeri-an."
But [del]one[/del] [ins]ry[/ins] is the exact translation of ikkeri; ek or [del]y[/del]ek being one. Then, on page 369, we have [ins]a complete list of[/ins] the numerals, beginning with
[ins]Ye[/ins] Yean, or hun, One; Do, Two;
Tri, Three; [del]C[/del]ah'air, or k'hair, Four.
etc.
"Eene, meene, moni, mi," is not mentioned in the book, so far as I can ascertain.

Truly Yours
T. J. Kiernan
