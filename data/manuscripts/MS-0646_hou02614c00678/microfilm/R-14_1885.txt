Freedom, in the strict sense should be merely Negative. However, in
literature, we more often than not find Freedom used to simply
spontaneity, or action inwardly caused, which is clearly a Dynamic
idea; and this existence of this literary implication side by side in my mind
with the stricter mathematical sense accounts for my hesitation, in a rapid
review of fifty pairs, to make Freedom either Positive or Negative.
