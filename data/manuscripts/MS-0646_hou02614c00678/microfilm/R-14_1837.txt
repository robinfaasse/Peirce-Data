It has been recognized by Logicians generally, from Plato down,
that the investigation of the Theory of Definition and that of
the Theory of Logical Division, (which is a somewhat different
thing from Classification,) must go hand in hand.

Another subject which [del]may process[/del] goes along with these is
what may be called the Syntax of Thought, or the manner in which
ideas are connected. Before the revolution of Logic (i.e. of Semiotic,)
[del]of[/del] which the study of Relatives effected, the doctrine used to be
that all Concepts were connected only by one operation expressible
by the words "that is". The tree of Porphyry illustrates the doctrine.
