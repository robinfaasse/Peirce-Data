[pn]10[/pn] [del]The order in which the piles after the first are to be taken up, may
be expressed in five forms. Because some persons will prefer one form and others another, and
because there is none of the five to which I do not find it convenient sometimes to resort, I give them all.
The first is as follows:[/del]

[u]Form I.[/u] Counting the original places of piles, whether they are still occupied
or not, pass round and round these places clockwise, or to the right, each
time skipping I places, and taking up the pile in the (I + 1)[ins]th[/ins] place after
the pile last taken.

[u]Form II.[/u] Counting the original places as before, only that you pass round
and round counterclockwise, or to the left, you take the pile that is
in the [del]R[sup]th[/sup][/del] [ins]R[sup]th[/sup][/ins] place after the pile last taken.
