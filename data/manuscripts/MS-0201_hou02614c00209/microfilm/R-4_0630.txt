The simplest way of converting from the decimal to the secundal notation, or the reverse is to proceed by powers of two. Thus, in order to convert express 1907 in secundal notation, [del]remembering[/del] [ins]multiply by 2 and find[/ins] the familiar fact that $2^{10} = 1024$, [del]I know that $2^{11} = 2048$[/del] [ins]8. I attract $2^4$ and get $2^{11} - (2^7 + 2^4) = 1904$ and adding 3, I get 1907.[/del] Now performing these processes in secundal notation, I write
