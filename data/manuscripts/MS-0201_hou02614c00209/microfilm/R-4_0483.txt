when a complete pack of 52 cards and a joker are dealt into 21 piles, the first two lines of the diagram are intended to facilitate the count of the places for piles, in case you should like to use one of the other forms of the rule. The next two lines shows the cards at the tops of the piles, supposing that before being dealt the cards in each suit were arranged, — ace, 2, 3, ... 9, X, J, Q, K while the suits came in the order ♢ ♡ ♠ ♣, and the joker, marked J.M., was the last card. The counts of the piles are shown by numbers in small type, while the numbers of the piles are in large type, and vacant places are filled with Xs.
