In this case, you multiply both the dividend, 1, and the divisor (which must be divisible neither by 2 nor by 5) by such a number as to make the [del]latter[/del] [ins]product[/ins] end in 9. Then, [del]it is[/del] [ins]one tenth of[/ins] one more than this will be the current multiplier, by which you always multiply the figure struck off to the right of the vertical line, setting down the product under the number to the left of that line, and adding the two. Continue until you have only the number with which the process started.
