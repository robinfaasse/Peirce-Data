The first of these propositions is made evident, though hardly obvious, in the following way. Then in case they are unequal, let [u]n/d[/u] < [u]ν/δ[/u], and multiply both sides of this inequality by [u]dδ[/u]. The product will be [u]nδ[/u] < [u]νd[/u]; and conversely if the latter inequality holds, division by [u]dδ[/u] will give the former one. If, on the other hand, [u]n/d[/u] = [u]ν/δ[/u], we have [u]nδ[/u] = [u]νd[/u], and the former equation will follow from the latter.
