14[/pn] The rule ensures a regular arrangement of any prime number of cards after dealing them into any number of piles less than the number of cards. After regathering the cards the card in the last place will [del]obviously[/del] [ins]obviously[/ins] be the very card that was in the last place before the cards were dealt; and consequently the rule holds when the whole number of cards is one less than [del]a[/del] [ins]a[/ins] prime number, provided you simply supply the pack with one more imaginary card which is imagined to come next after the card that is really the last. In that case, the pile to be taken up first is the one upon which the imaginary card falls.

It results from the rule that when 13 cards are dealt into either 2, 3, 4, 6, or 12 piles, they are to be gathered up in succession as they lie, from left to right. When they are dealt into seven piles, these are to be taken up in the order $6^{\text{th}}, 5^{\text{th}}, 4^{\text{th}}, 3^{\text{rd}}, 2^{\text{nd}}, 1^{\text{st}}, 7^{\text{th}}$.
