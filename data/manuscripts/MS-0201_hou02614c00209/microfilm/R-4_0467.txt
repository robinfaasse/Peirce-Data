[pn]3[/pn] [u]number of cards in the packet or pile[/u]; Thirdly, that by the "face-value", I mean, the number of pips on a plain card, the ace counting as one [del]while, as for the picture-cards[/del] [ins]counts,[/ins] for which I write J, [del]counting[/del] as 11, the queen, Q, as 12, and the king, K, as thirteen; and Fourthly, that when I gather up a number of piles that have been formed upon the table, the rule for doing so (though it may appear to conflict with the first of those formulas which by the way I shall refer to as the "Standing Rules") is as follows: the first pile to be taken is to be taken [ins]*[/ins] grasped as a whole and as it lies, faces up, and is to be placed on the second of the piles to be taken. Then these two together are to be grasped as one whole, and placed, faces up, upon the next pile to be taken; and so on, until all have been taken; when, in accordance with the first standing rule, the whole packet is to be turned back up.
