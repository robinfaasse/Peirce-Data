[u]A Second Curiosity.[/u]

A phenomenon much easier to understand, depending on the fact that, in counting round a cycle of 53, $\sqrt{-1} = \pm 30$, since $30^2 = [del]900[/del] = 17 \cdot 53 - 1$, likewise can be shown in the form of a "trick".

You take a pack of 52 cards, after you have told some anecdote in which you two or three times mention the numbers 5 and 6. You ask into how many piles the company would like [del]to[/del] [ins]to[/ins] have them dealt. They will very likely mention one of these numbers; and on your asking into how many piles [del]the cards shall be dealt for a second time[/del] [ins]they shall [del]be dealt[/del] [ins]pairs and[/ins] [del]of[/del] they are likely to[/ins] mention the other. If not, there are many triplets of numbers whose product is $\pm 30$, counting round a cycle of 53; and you can induce the company to let you deal [ins]twice or thrice[/ins] [del]three times[/del] into these numbers of piles. Such are, for example, $17 \cdot 8 = 3 \cdot 7 \cdot 9 = 2 \cdot 11 \cdot 11 = 7 \cdot 8 \cdot 10 = 3 \cdot 4 \cdot 29 = 3 \cdot 13 \cdot 13 = 30$ and $4 \cdot 19 = 2 \cdot 7 \cdot 13 = 3 \cdot 8 \cdot 12 = 5 \cdot 10 \cdot 10 = -31$.
