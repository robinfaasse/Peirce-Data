If one of my classes of prize stupids, to which I gave less than a dozen lessons, two of my pupils afterward led all the rest of the school in mathematics. I once sent De Morgan a copy of a geometry that was required to be used in the New York public schools, — including, as I was informed, the Free Academy; but of that I am not sure, — in the hopes of getting some of his wit in return; but he was so saddened, shocked, and sickened by the exhibit that I got only lamentations.
