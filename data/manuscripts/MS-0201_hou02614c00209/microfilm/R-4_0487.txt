have been dealt into 22 piles. The first two lines of the diagram are intended to aid the count of places of piles, needed
in other forms of the rule. The next line shows the top cards of the piles, supposing that before the deal they were in suits in the
order ♢ ♡ ♠ ♣, and in each suit according to their face-values of Standing Rule III, the joker, marked J.M. being the last
card. In the lines below, you follow between the parallel lines, the small figures being the count, while the large
ones are the ordinal numbers of the gatherings of piles. Xs denote vacant places, and the number in parenthesis between the slant
lines is the number of the count of piles as diminished in passing the place of the last card. In this place, there is no need of an X.
