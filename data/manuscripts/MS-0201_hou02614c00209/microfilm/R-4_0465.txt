[u]A Contribution to the Amuses of Mathematics.[/u]

About [date]1860[/date], I invented a combination of effects of elementary principles of cyclic arithmetic which I have, ever since, occasionally exhibited in the form of a "trick", (though there is really no trick about the phenomenon,) at the end of a sitting at card-play, with the uniform result of interesting and surprising [ins]all[/ins] the company, though whatever their mathematical powers, [del]from those who[/del] [ins]might range barely[/ins] [del]hardly[/del] had enough to care for cards at all, up to some of the greatest mathematicians of the last century, who with a little reflexion would easily discern the rationale of the thing.
