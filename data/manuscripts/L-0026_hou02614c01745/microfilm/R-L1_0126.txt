Raymond H. Arnot,
Attorney and Counsellor at Law,
Rochester, New York.

November 2, 1901.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

I want very much to get your latest or best photograph in cabinet size or larger for the ROCHESTER MODERN HISTORY CLUB, and an accurate account of your distinguished career. I want to inquire also where a photograph and a sketch can be obtained of your father.

Will you be kind enough to tell me where they can be obtained?

Assuring you of my high regard,

I am,

Faithfully yours,

Raymond H. Arnot

Dict. B.
