What I have been saying is intended to throw a light upon the meaning that I attach to the Necessary Modality of a subject. I do not here use 'necessary' in the acception of that which is necessitated but in the sense of that which necessitates, that which through its rationality is fitted more or less to reveal the future, that is to predict future experience. Every law, or regularity, which is not merely a casual uniformity but is a real habit or tendency in things, — as real as a habit is in us, does help to the knowledge of what we have not previously known, and in that sense concerns the future even if it only teaches us ancient history. The great preponderance of testimony is that Pythagoras left Samos and [del]as[/del] removed to Italy about the beginning of the 62[ins]nd[/ins] Olympiad, which began [del]in[/del] the summer of 532 B.C. The story is that he was a wealthy young man of Samos who having been abroad returned to the island and was so disgusted with the tyranny of Polycrates that he sold out and went to Magna Graecia, where he lived for the rest of his life. The date at which Polycrates seized the government of Samos does not seem to be known with precision. But it was before the death of Cyrus 529 B.C. and was not double later than 538 B.C. Pythagoras would require perhaps a couple of years to sell out, to decide where to go, and to go there. This suits well enough the date given for his arrival in Italy.
