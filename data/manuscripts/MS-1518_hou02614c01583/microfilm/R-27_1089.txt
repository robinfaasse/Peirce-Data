[u]The Present Marches (Grenzgebiet) of the Hydrogen Spectrum.[/u]

Beyond the last photograph of the foregoing spectral series I have been able to follow the hydrogen spectrum through an angular space of about 6° but thus far with no good graphical success. Even the most perfect of all the photographs of this region (No. 4222) is not clearly enough defined to bear a twenty fold enlargement. Accordingly they may here be treated only verbally. To begin, I have only been able to secure with great pains, traces of some washy lines in this region.
