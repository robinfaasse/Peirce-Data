Its mechanical arrangement is, in a general way, that of [del]an ordinary spectroscope with collimator and telescope, except[/del] [del]that,[/del] in place of the eye-piece, it has a little photographic camera. In order that the [del]apparatus may be used in vacuo[/del] [ins]air may be exhausted from the apparatus[/ins], its essential parts are joined by air-tight connections; and [ins]by[/ins] [del]a mercurial air pump[/del] a high vacuum can be produced in it.
