$11 \cdot \left| \begin{matrix} 22, 23 \\ 32, 33 \end{matrix} \right| x - \begin{matrix} (12 \cdot 23 \cdot 32 \\ - 12 \cdot 22 \cdot 33) y \end{matrix} + \begin{matrix} (13 \cdot 22 \cdot 33 \\ - 13 \cdot 23 \cdot 32) z \end{matrix}$
$+ 21 \cdot \left| \begin{matrix} 32, 33 \\ 12, 13 \end{matrix} \right| x - \begin{matrix} (22 \cdot 12 \cdot 33 \\ - 22 \cdot 32 \cdot 13) y \end{matrix} + \begin{matrix} (23 \cdot 32 \cdot 13 \\ - 23 \cdot 12 \cdot 33) z \end{matrix}$
$+ 31 \cdot \left| \begin{matrix} 12, 13 \\ 22, 23 \end{matrix} \right| x - \begin{matrix} (32 \cdot 22 \cdot 13 \\ - 32 \cdot 12 \cdot 23) y \end{matrix} + \begin{matrix} (33 \cdot 12 \cdot 23 \\ - 33 \cdot 22 \cdot 13) z \end{matrix} = 0$

The coefficients of $y$ and of $z$ cancell, the second and third, the fourth and fifth, and the sixth and first; and consequently

$11 \cdot \left| \begin{matrix} 22, 23 \\ 32, 33 \end{matrix} \right| + 21 \cdot \left| \begin{matrix} 32, 33 \\ 12, 13 \end{matrix} \right| + 31 \cdot \left| \begin{matrix} 12, 13 \\ 22, 23 \end{matrix} \right| = 0$

which may better be written

$\left| \begin{matrix} 11, 12, 13 \\ 21, 22, 23 \\ 31, 32, 33 \end{matrix} \right| = 0$
