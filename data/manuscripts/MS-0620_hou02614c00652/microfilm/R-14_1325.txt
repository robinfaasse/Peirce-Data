The fourth product is $11\cdot 23\cdot 32\ x + 12\cdot 23\cdot 32\ y + 13\cdot 23\cdot 32\ z = 0$
The fifth is $12\cdot 21\cdot 33\ x + 12\cdot 22\cdot 33\ y + 12\cdot 23\cdot 33\ z = 0$
The sixth is $13\cdot 22\cdot 31\ x + 13\cdot 22\cdot 32\ y + 13\cdot 22\cdot 33\ z = 0$

The sum of these three is $(11\cdot 23\cdot 32 + 12\cdot 21\cdot 33 + 13\cdot 22\cdot 31)x + (12\cdot 23\cdot 32 + 12\cdot 22\cdot 33 + 13\cdot 22\cdot 32)y + (13\cdot 23\cdot 32 + 12\cdot 23\cdot 33 + 13\cdot 22\cdot 33)z = 0$

Subtracting this from the former sum of products and dividing by $z$ (since the coefficients of $y$ and of $z$ cancel) we get
$11\cdot 22\cdot 33 + 12\cdot 23\cdot 31 + 13\cdot 21\cdot 32$
$- 11\cdot 23\cdot 32 - 12\cdot 21\cdot 33 - 13\cdot 22\cdot 31 = 0$

which we may write
$\left| \begin{matrix} 11, 12, 13 \\ 21, 22, 23 \\ 31, 32, 33 \end{matrix} \right| = 0$.
