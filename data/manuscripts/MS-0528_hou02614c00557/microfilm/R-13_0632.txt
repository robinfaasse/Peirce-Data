The first system of relationship that logic studies
is that of an indefinite collection od diconnected units.
It may be represented by this schema.

....................

These objects constitute the universe. To them in various
combinations are attached conventional signs. The study
of this schema gives rise to a variety of the Boolian cal-
culus. Thus, using a, b, c, etc. as names or conventional
signs, we may write IIa to signify that every object in the
universe is an a, and Ea to mean that Some object is an a.
each of which is attached to some individuals of the uni-
verse and detached from others, we first note that the latter
are as much distinguished by this as the former, and we
are thus led to add to our list of names the negative names
a, b, c, etc.
