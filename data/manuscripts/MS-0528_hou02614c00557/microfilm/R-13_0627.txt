When the existence of something is admitted as one of the
postulates of logic, $\Sigma_{i} b_{i}$ follows from $\Pi_{i} b_{i}$. This may
be conveniently written, $\Pi \prec \Sigma$. Even without the postulate
of existence, we have
$$\Pi_{i} \Pi_{j} = \Pi_{j} \Pi_{i} \quad \Sigma_{i} \Sigma_{j} = \Sigma_{j} \Sigma_{i}$$
$$\Sigma \ \Pi = \Pi \ \Sigma \ .$$

The universes of $i$'s, of $j$'s, of $k$'s, etc. are com-
posed of things, of events, of characters, or of any kind
of objects.
