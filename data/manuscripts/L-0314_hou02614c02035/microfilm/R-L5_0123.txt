The fact is, it seems to me doubtful whether we can derive any law of relation between an absolute magnitude of a star, and the number of stars having that magnitude.

Third, the proposition that the proper motions of faint stars are not much smaller than those of bright ones, seems to me not established, except in this sense; that, given a proper motion of two or three seconds or more in a century, the number of stars having it range through a wide degree of brightness. But you would thus exhaust the bright stars much quicker than you would the faint ones.
