Washington, D. C.,

[date]April 23rd, 1895.[/date]

My Dear Peirce:-

I was puzzled by something in the Century Dictionary, which can, I think, be explained by no one but yourself. I refer to the definition of an oddly odd number. As no odd number can have any except odd factors, I do not see what the definition amounts to. It is I know a very old one, but did it originate in anything but a blunder of some ancient editor?

Yours very sincerely,

S. Newcomb

Mr. Charles S. Peirce.
