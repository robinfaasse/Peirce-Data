My dear Newcomb:

Merry Christmas

As you signify in your last that you have got through with your criticisms on my definitions of infinitesimal, limit, and [u]Doctrine of limits[/u], I will summarize them and my answers.

You "object to my definition of [u]limit[/u] totally and of infinitesimal partially, as non-accordant with the best mathematical thought of the day, and savoring too much of notions current a century ago, and current still among men whose ideas are imperfectly coördinated."

Let us see by a review of the arguments whether you have succeeded in justifying this judgment or not.
