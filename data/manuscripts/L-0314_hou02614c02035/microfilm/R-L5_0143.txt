1620 P STREET N. W.
WASHINGTON, D. C.

[date]December 21, 1908[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I beg to invite your attention to the nominations for membership in the National Academy of Sciences of investigators eminent in mathematics or astronomy. I sent you a list of the nominees about December 1, but have received no reply. Two conditions are to be considered.

Firstly, every member who fails to vote [u]practically votes against the entire list[/u] since a majority of all the members [del]irrespective of any[/del] [ins]including all not[/ins] voting is required in the affirmative. For the same reason when a member votes he practically votes against every nominee [u]whom he fails to name.[/u]

Secondly, the list of nominees must be sent in to the Home Secretary by January 1.

Lest you may not have received the list which I sent the names are repeated below.

Very truly yours,
Simon Newcomb
