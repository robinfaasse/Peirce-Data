My dear Newcomb,

The following proposition must be included virtually in Hamilton's discussion of nets in space; but is not stated by him & does not seem to me obvious. It may be well-known.

Through a point in space describe 4 right lines. Assume a point on each and make these 4 points the vertices of a tetrahedron. Assume a second set of 4 points on the 4 right lines & make them the vertices of a second tetrahedron. Then corresponding faces of the tetrahedron, being produced, [del]into[/del] cut one another in 4 complanar lines.
