In the form which you would give to it, the
error is not serious, but I do not think it is
perfectly demonstrative. To say that the error
of a statement is less than any assignable
magnitude is certainly not to say there is
no error: Euclid himself would admit that.

You say, I give no adequate definition of what
the method is. But the truth is you are
mistaken as to what the method of limits
is. It does not consist in reasoning in
a special way, and were I to select two forms
of the method as illustrations, thus greatly leng
thening the definition, neither would be that
which you wish me to give exclusively.

On the whole, I do not think your origi-
