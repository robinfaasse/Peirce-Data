Milford Pa [date]1891 Dec 21[/date]

My dear Newcomb:

One thing is clear. It is that those figures I sent you lend no support whatever to the idea of a positive curvature, but are rather against it. The striking non-decrement of the peculiar motion with the brightness needs to be investigated. But the formula for the surface of the sphere and for the peculiar motion equally involve [u]Sinh[/u] $\frac{r}{2k}$ or [u]sin[/u] $\frac{r}{2k}$.

Very faithfully
C. S. Peirce

I have given a whole day to this. I cannot any longer afford the luxury of unremunerative work but very little.
