425 LAFAYETTE STREET
NEW YORK

[date]November 26, 1908.[/date]

My dear Sir:-

The nominations for membership in the National Academy of Sciences from the section of Anthropology must be transmitted to the Home Secretary not later than January 1, 1909.

By referring to Article IV, section 4 of the Constitution you will see the provisions covering the selection of nominees.

The attempt to have a meeting of the section in Baltimore to consider nominations did not succeed as there was not a sufficient number of the members of the section present to warrant any conclusions.

I shall be glad to receive from you nominations to be sent to the members of the section for consideration.

Dr. Charles S. Minot suggests that it may be desirable to change the name of the section on anthropology to "Section on Medicine and Anthropology" and nominates Prof. W. T. Porter as a candidate for this section.

Mr. Putnam nominates Mr. Charles P. Bowditch.

Hoping to hear from you at your early convenience, I am

Very respectfully,

JSBillings,

Chairman.
