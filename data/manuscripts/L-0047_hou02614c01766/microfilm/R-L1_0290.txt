425 LAFAYETTE STREET
NEW YORK

November 5, 1910.

Charles S. Pierce, Esq.,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:-

Permit me to call to your attention a communication which I addressed to you on May 31, 1910, asking that you would take the matter of a more appropriate division of the committees of anthropology and biology of the National Academy of Sciences into consideration and favor me with your views as to what sub-division, if any, would be desirable in order to secure their main object, i.e., the nomination by experts of persons for members in the National Academy. Not having received any reply, I beg again to call the matter to your consideration and hope that you will favor me with your views.

Very sincerely yours,
JMcKeenCattell
Chairman.
