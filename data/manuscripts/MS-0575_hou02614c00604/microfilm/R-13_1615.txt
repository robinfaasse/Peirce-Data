Theorem VI. (Schröder) If

$x \cdot \bar{y} \cdot \bar{z} \prec y + z$ and $x + \bar{z} \prec y + z$,

then

$x \prec y$

Proof. By III and I

$x \prec x + \bar{z} \prec y + z$

By theorem [del]II[/del] [ins]III[/ins], corollary 5, [del]Second part of theorem 2[/del] and I

$x \prec x \cdot (x + \bar{z}) \prec (y + z) \cdot (x + \bar{z}) \prec x \cdot y + \bar{z} \cdot y + \bar{z} \cdot z \prec x \cdot y + \bar{z} \cdot y \prec$

$\prec y \cdot (x + \bar{z}) \prec y$.
