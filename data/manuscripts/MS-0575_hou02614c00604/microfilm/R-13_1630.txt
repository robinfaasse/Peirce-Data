[del]I define[/del] The copula of inclusion [ins]is defined[/ins] as follows: —
[del]1st[/del] [ins]1[u]o[/u][/ins], $A \prec A$, whatever $A$ may be.
[del]2d[/del] [ins]2[u]o[/u][/ins] If $A \prec B$, and $B \prec C$, then $A \prec C$.

This definition is sufficient for the purposes of formal
logic, although it does not distinguish between the
relation of inclusion and its converse. Were it de-
sirable [del]to introduce do thus[/del] to distinguish, it would
be sufficient to add that the real truth or falsity
of $A \prec B$, supposes the existence of $A$.
