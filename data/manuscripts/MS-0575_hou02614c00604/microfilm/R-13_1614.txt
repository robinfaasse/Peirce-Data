[u]Theorem V[/u] If $x \prec n-y$ then $y \prec n-x$.

[del]Proof[/del] From $x \prec n-y$, we have by theorem 3, and [ins]corollary 9[/ins]
$x \bar{y} \prec \overline{n-y} y \prec 0$

Hence by VII, corollary 7, and [u]III[/u]'
$y \prec x y + \bar{x} y \prec 0 + \bar{x} y \prec \overline{n-x} y \prec n-x$

Whence by corollary 1,
[del]Corollary[/del] If $n-y \prec \bar{x}$ then $n-x \prec y$.
