[del]cances[/del] [ins]The[/ins] first object of logical algebra is
to represent the ultimate analysis of arguments. This
copula, I represent by the sign $\prec$. This is a
different way of writing $\leqq$. The latter sign is logi-
cally objectionable because it involves a definition by
enumeration, and seems to represent inclusion as
less simple than identity.

This reasoning is absolutely irrefragable; and he
who does not accept it simply shows that he does
not comprehend the essential purpose of logic, as
the analysis of arguments, not the art of drawing
inferences.
