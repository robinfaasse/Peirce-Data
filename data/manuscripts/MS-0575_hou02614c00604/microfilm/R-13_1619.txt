[u]First Fundamental definition[/u]. Copula of inclusion. [u]Second[/u]
[u]Clause[/u], being at the same time the definition of negation.
V. $y \prec x, y \text{ to } n-x, y$ (Principle of excluded middle.)
V.' $(x \text{ to } y), (n-x \text{ to } y) \prec y$ (Principle of contradiction.)

[u]Extension of Theorem 1[/u]. This theorem applies equally to
propositions involving the second clause of the definition
of the copula.
