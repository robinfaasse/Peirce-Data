I define the logical sum as follows: —
$1^{\text{d}}$, $A \prec A +_{b} B$
$2^{\text{d}}$, $B \prec A +_{b} B$
$3^{\text{d}}$, If $A \prec C$ and $B \prec C$, then $A +_{b} B \prec C$.

From this one easily proves that logical addition is
associative. For by the first condition
[del]$x \prec x +_{b} (y +_{b} z)$[/del]
and $y \prec y +_{b} z$. (1)
By the second condition
$(y +_{b} z) \prec x +_{b} (y +_{b} z)$. (2)
Hence, by the second condition in the definition
of the copula,
$y \prec x +_{b} (y +_{b} z)$. (3)
But by the first condition of the definition of the sum
$x \prec x +_{b} (y +_{b} z)$ (4)
Hence, by the third condition
$(x +_{b} y) \prec x +_{b} (y +_{b} z)$ (5)
