Accordingly, [u]Camestres[/u] holds when instead of
"other than", or the negative, we put any other
relative term, as "lover of." Thus,
All negroes are men,
God is {[ins]other than[/ins] [del]a lover of[/del]} every man;
[three dots in triangular formation] God is {[ins]other than[/ins] [del]a lover of[/del]} every negro.
But [u]Cesare[/u] only holds provided the relative
is an equiparant. Thus,
All monkeys {[ins]are other than[/ins] [del]resemble[/del]} every man,
All negroes are men;
[three dots in triangular formation] All negroes {[ins]are other than[/ins] [del]resemble[/del]} every monkey.
