[u]Second part of theorem 2.[/u] $x_1(y+z) \prec x_1y + x_2z$. (Boole.)
(Peirce.) $x_1y + x_2\bar{x} \succ (\bar{z}+\bar{x})_1(y+z)$

[u]Proof[/u]. By VII,
$x_1(y+z) \prec y_1x_2(y+z) + \bar{y}_1x_1(y+z)$
[del]$+ (\bar{z}+\bar{x})_1x_2(y+z)$[/del]
[del]$(\bar{z}+\bar{x})_1x_2\bar{x}_1(y+z) + \bar{y}_1x_1(y+z) \prec x_1y + x_2z$[/del]
$\cdot (\bar{z}+\bar{x})_1\bar{x}_2\bar{x}_1(y+z) \prec \bar{y}_1x_1(y+z) \prec (\bar{z}+\bar{x})_1\bar{x}_2\bar{x}_1(y+z)$

Whence by theorem 3, and I
[del]$(\bar{z}+\bar{x})_1\bar{x}_2\bar{x}_1(y+z) + (\bar{z}+\bar{x})_1\bar{x}_2\bar{x}_1(y+z) \prec y_1x_2(y+z)$[/del]
$\cdot (\bar{z}+\bar{x})_1\bar{x}_2\bar{x}_1(y+z) + (\bar{z}+\bar{x})_1\bar{x}_2\bar{x}_1(y+z) +$

By III and II,
$x_1y + x_2\bar{x} \succ$ [del]$x_1y$[/del] $x_1y \succ (\bar{z}+\bar{x})_1\bar{x}_2\bar{x}_1(y+z)$
$x_1y + x_2\bar{x} \succ$ [del]$x_2\bar{x}$[/del] $x_2\bar{x} \succ (\bar{z}+\bar{x})_1\bar{x}_2\bar{x}_1(y+z)$

Hence by IV
$\cdot x_1y + x_2\bar{x} \succ (\bar{z}+\bar{x})_1\bar{x}_2\bar{x}_1(y+z) + (\bar{z}+\bar{x})_1\bar{x}_2\bar{x}_1(y+z)$

Hence, by I,
$(\bar{z}+\bar{x})_1\bar{x}_2\bar{x}_1(y+z) \prec x_1y + x_2\bar{x}$
