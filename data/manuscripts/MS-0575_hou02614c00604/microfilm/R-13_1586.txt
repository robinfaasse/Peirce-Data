The proof of the propositions
$(x +_{,,} y) z = x z +_{,,} y z$
$x y +_{,,} z = (x +_{,,} z) (y +_{,,} z)$
given by me in my paper of [date]May 1867[/date], seems
too obvious for repetition.

The same is true of Schröder's formulae,
$x + x y = x$,
$x (x +_{,,} y) = x$.

[u]Also, Schröder's highly interesting formula[/u]
if [del]$m x \prec m y$[/del] [ins]$m \bar{x} \prec m \bar{y}$[/ins] and $m +_{,,} \bar{x} \prec m +_{,,} \bar{y}$ then $\bar{x} \prec \bar{y}$.
