[u]Definition of the Logical Sum[/u].

($\text{II}$.) $\underline{x} + \underline{y} \prec \underline{y} + \underline{x}$.

($\text{III}$.) $\underline{x} \prec \underline{x} + \underline{y}$.

($\text{IV}$.) If $\underline{x} \prec \underline{z}$ and $\underline{y} \prec \underline{z}$, then $\underline{x} + \underline{y} \prec \underline{z}$.

To apply corollary I, we must have, corresponding to logical addition, another operation which may be called internal multiplication. In analyzing corollary I, these operations [del]are to be interchanged[/del] [ins]are to be interchanged[/ins].

[u]Definition of the Logical Internal Product[/u].

($\text{II}'$.) $\underline{y} , \underline{x} \prec \underline{x} , \underline{y}$.

($\text{III}'$.) $\underline{x} , \underline{y} \prec \underline{x}$.

($\text{IV}'$.) If $\underline{z} \prec \underline{x}$ and $\underline{z} \prec \underline{y}$, then $\underline{z} \prec \underline{x} , \underline{y}$.

[u]Corollary 2[/u]. From $\text{III}$, $\text{III}'$, $\text{I}$, it follows that
$\underline{x} , \underline{y} \prec \underline{x} + \underline{y}$ (1)
