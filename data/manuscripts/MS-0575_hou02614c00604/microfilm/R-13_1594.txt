(V) $x \prec 1$

[u]Definition of Nothing[/u]

(V') $0 \prec x$.

[u]Corollary 4.[/u] From V, it follows that
$x +_1 1 \prec 1$
And by corollary I,
$0 \prec x , 0$
By II and III
$1 \prec x +_1 1$
and by corollary I,
$x , 0 \prec 0$.
