The negative must be defined by the formulae
of contradiction and excluded middle.
[u]x[/u],[u]n[/u]-[u]x[/u] < 0     1 < [u]x[/u] + [u]n[/u]-[u]x[/u] .

Suppose x < y . Multiply by n-y
x,n-y < 0
Add n-x
x,n-y + n-x < n-x
But n-y < x,n-y + n-x,n-y < x,n-y + n-x
<tex>\therefore</tex> n-y < n-x.
