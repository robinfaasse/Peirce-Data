[u]Theorem 3.[/u] If [u]x[/u] < [u]y[/u] and [u]u[/u] < [u]v[/u], then
[u]x[/u] + [u]u[/u] < [u]y[/u] + [u]v[/u]      [u]x[/u] , [u]u[/u] < [u]y[/u] , [u]v[/u].

[u]Proof[/u]. By III and I
[u]x[/u] < [u]y[/u] < [u]y[/u] + [u]v[/u].
By II, III, and I
[u]u[/u] < [u]v[/u] < [u]y[/u] + [u]v[/u].
Hence, by IV,
[u]x[/u] + [u]u[/u] < [u]y[/u] + [u]v[/u].
Whence, by corollary 1,
If [u]y[/u] < [u]x[/u] and [u]v[/u] < [u]u[/u] then [u]y[/u] , [u]v[/u] < [u]x[/u] , [u]u[/u].
