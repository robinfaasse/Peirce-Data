I rushed out, found the same cab, and drove back to the boat, greatly worked up. For the $350 was the smallest consideration. The watch could not easily be matched at any price, and I should feel it as a lifelong professional disgrace, if I failed to restore it in as perfect condition as I had received it. Arrived at the boat, I ran up to my stateroom, and found everything gone. I then made all the colored waiters, no matter on what deck they belonged, come and stand up in a row.
