I at once returned; but the watch was gone. I got all
the stewards, — negroes they were, — in a row. There were
twenty or more. I went back and talked with him a few
minutes; not about my loss, but about anything that he
would be surprised at my talking about. I then, took a little
turn and decided which one I would accuse. I took him
aside and offered him immunity, secrecy, and a fifty
dollar bill if he would produce the watch. "Now," said I, "if
you don't take this offer, you will go to state's prison, sure.
