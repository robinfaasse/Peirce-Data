in Newport or Fall River. I took the detective with me and
found out where he lodged. It was a respectable flat. I went
there with the detective & going to the outer door, I asked him
to go up and get the chain, seal, and an overcoat that had
been taken. He said he dared not do it without a war-
rant. "Very well," said I, "you wait here a few moments
and I will bring them." I went up & knocked at the door.
