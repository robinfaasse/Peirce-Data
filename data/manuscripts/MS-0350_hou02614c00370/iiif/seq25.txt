Accordingly we may grant to Mr. Mill that axioms might
be inferred inductively from some appearance; provided he
will grant that they might be induced from any appearance.
For it will follow that they are as Kant says, involved in the
condition of all appearance. The condition of all appearance
is that our senses should be affected. Hence they are involved
in our sense being affected. In other words, they are part of the
manner in which objects affect us. And this is the transcendental
conclusion.

Universal Causation". The former chapter, begins with the observation that
there is an assumption involved in every case of induction. This
assumption relates to the order of nature. It is hard to express it
precisely but it is to the effect that the course of nature is uniform.
