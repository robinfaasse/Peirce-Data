can be mistaken in an induction. But it is perfectly
essential to a logic of induction that it should give us rules to
prevent our making a complete mistake. For if there may be
one completely wrong conception which we may hit upon, there
may be a million such, and in that case it's a million to

are utterly ignorant how many there may be, & therefore we are
utterly ignorant of the truth of our induction.
