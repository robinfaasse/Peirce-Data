This is a beautifully printed Quarto of 366 pages with a
fine [del]of[/del] [ins]early[/ins] XVIIIth century Full calf binding.

It is not only rare but a book that is very instructive in
more than one way. I should not recommend anybody's trying
the dishes, unless to get an impressive lesson of the strength
of our forefathers' stomachs. It is genuinely an ancient Roman
cook book written by a cook and confectioner. The Latin is very
bad. The piece by Paulus Aegineta (of the VIIth century) is from the Sixth Book
of his work translated from the Greek as is another piece in the volume by
Joannes Damascenus.
