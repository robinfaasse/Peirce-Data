$5^{\text{ly}}$, we connect the two parts of the statement more closely, thus:

Taking anything whatever, C, seven things can be found $\text{A}$, $\text{A}'$, $\text{A}''$, $\text{B}$, $\text{B}'$, $\text{B}''$, $\text{D}$, such that C is identical with $\text{A}$ and $\text{A}'$ [del]and[/del] [ins]while[/ins] $\text{A}'$ is identical with $\text{A}''$ and with $\text{D}$, and if $\text{A}$ is Enoch, then $\text{A}''$ is a man, and C is identical with $\text{B}$ and $\text{B}'$, while $\text{B}'$ is identical with $\text{B}''$ [del]and D[/del], and if $\text{B}''$ is a man, then $\text{B}$ is mortal.

$6^{\text{ly}}$, we separate one extreme from the middle term, thus:

Taking any three things whatever, $\text{C}$, $\text{A}$, $\text{A}'$, five things can be found $\text{A}''$, $\text{B}$, $\text{B}'$, $\text{B}''$, $\text{D}$, such that if C is identical with $\text{A}$ and with $\text{A}'$ and if $\text{A}$ is Enoch, then $\text{A}'$ is identical with $\text{A}''$ and with $\text{D}$ and $\text{A}''$ is a man [del]and[/del] [ins]while[/ins] C is identical with $\text{B}$ and with $\text{B}'$ and $\text{B}'$ is identical with $\text{B}''$ and with $\text{D}$ and $\text{B}''$ is a man then $\text{B}$ is mortal.

$7^{\text{ly}}$, we separate the other extreme from the middle term, thus:

Taking any five things whatever, $\text{C}$, $\text{A}$, $\text{A}'$, $\text{B}$, $\text{B}'$, three things can be found, $\text{A}''$, $\text{B}''$, $\text{D}$ such that if C is identical with $\text{A}$ and with $\text{A}'$ and if $\text{A}$ is Enoch, and if C is identical with $\text{B}$ and with $\text{B}'$ then $\text{B}$ is mortal, unless $\text{A}'$ is identical with $\text{A}''$ and with $\text{D}$ and $\text{B}'$ is identical with $\text{B}''$ and with $\text{D}$ and $\text{A}''$ is a man without $\text{B}''$ being a man.
