[u]habitual intellect[/u] ([greek]νοῦς κατὰ ἕξιν[/greek]) and [u]intellectus adeptus[/u] ([greek]νοῦς ἐπίκτητος[/greek]). These two distinctions have little to do with one another. Still, they were confounded by the Arabians and the confused doctrine suggested to Scotus his brilliant and philosophical division.
