[u]Ascent[/u] is the most unequivocal noun to denote the passage [del]from[/del] [ins]to[/ins] a broader and less deep notion, without change of information; and other words of similar literal meanings are used in the same way. It is the decrease in depth, of course, which is directly expressed, the increase in breadth being implied. [u]Extension[/u], which directly expresses increase of breadth, has a somewhat different meaning.
