Chapter I

The word [u]φανερόν[/u] is next to the simplest expression in Greek for [u]manifest[/u]. Etymologically, [u]evident[/u] is nearer to it, but [u]manifest[/u] and [u]evident[/u], in straining at emphasis, have with us quite interchanged their meanings. They had begun to do so in Cicero's day. Since no pains can be too prodigal in forming a new philosophical word, no deliberation too often revised, no caution too meticulous, I will endeavor to analyze the meaning of [u]manifest[/u].
