On the other hand, starting again with the cards
in their proper order, if we first deal into 5 piles and
then carry 3 cards from back to face, we have
$$y = 5 (x + 3).$$
In short, the order in which the operations are to be tak-
en in the calculation of the face-values is just the reverse
of that of their actual performance. The reason is too ob-
vious to require explanation.
