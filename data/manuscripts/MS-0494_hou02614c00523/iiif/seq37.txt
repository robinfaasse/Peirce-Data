XIV. A graph already written maybe iterated with the
same connections, provided the iteration is not outside
any enclosure that the original graph was within; or,
being so iterated, it maybe omitted.

Proof. 1st. Let the whole graph consist of two unenclosed
graphs, [u]a[/u] and [u]b[/u], together with any number of heavy
lines connecting them. Then I say that Fig 1 is illatively
transformable into Fig. 2. For, by [u]XI[/u], Fig 1 is illatively
transformable into Fig. 3; by IV, Fig. 3 is transformable into
Fig. 4; by X, Fig. 4 is transformable into Fig 5; and by [u]XIII[/u],
Fig. 5 is transformable into Fig 2. Q.E.D.

2nd. By [u]XIII[/u], Fig 2 is illatively transformable into Fig 1.
Thus, when neither the original graph nor its iteration is enclosed
the iteration is [del]an[/del] illative transformation and so is its omission.

indifferent. Then, I say that [u]a[/u] may be illatively iterated
