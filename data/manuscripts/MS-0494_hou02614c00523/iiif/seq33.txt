XIII. Under even enclosures any graph may be erased;
and under odd enclosures any graph may be inserted.

Proof. First, let [del]the[/del] graph, a, [del]be detached[/del] unenclosed and 
either detached or attached by any number of heavy
lines to the rest of the graph, r. Then, I say, that all 
the attachments of a may be [ins]illatively[/ins] cut through and a with
the fragments of its attachments can be illatively erased.
For by III a with its attachments could be erased
were it the whole graph. ∴ by V it can equally be 
erased when it is a part of the graph. [del]Q.E.D.[/del]
