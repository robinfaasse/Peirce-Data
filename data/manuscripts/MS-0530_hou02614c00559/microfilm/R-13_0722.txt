These are terms of DeMorgan, [del]the[/del] who brought the
conceptions they are intended to express for the first time
into view in their true system. Under ordinary circum-
stances, then, our ethical principle would require us to
accept his terminology; and my affectionate admiration
for DeMorgan is so great that if I have [del]only[/del] the warmest
desire that full justice be done to his great services to Logic.

But I am compelled to say that he chooses his words
with too little regard to previous scientific usage, and with
too little sense of the responsibility of saddling his
successors with inconvenient terms.
