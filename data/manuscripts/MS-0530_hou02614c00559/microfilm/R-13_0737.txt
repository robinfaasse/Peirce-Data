classes of man of and of flying things are each 'an
external of' the other, or are 'coexternals'; and that 'humanity
is a repugnant of' the power of flying. 'External' is a very
common term of philosophy, never used in this sense; for
which it is not in the least needed, since [del]exclusive[/del] [ins]these classes are called discrete[/ins] and
coexclusive are common and unambiguous. DeMorgan's
new term [del]will[/del] [ins]will no doubt[/ins] be allowed to fall into desu-
etude; or rather, it will remain obscure, as it has for
[del]a[/del] half a century. 'Repugnant, on the other hand, is a very
old term of logic in the sense in which DeMorgan uses it, ex-
cept that it usually implies logical necessity, conflicting is better.
