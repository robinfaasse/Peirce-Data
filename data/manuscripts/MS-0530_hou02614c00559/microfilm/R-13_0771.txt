[u]Transition[/u]. Petrus Helias says: "Transitio actus dicitur, quando una persona agit in aliam." But for the sake of uniformity, I will change the preposition, and call this mode of construction, [u]conition[/u]. The terms conited will be the [u]coneunts[/u], or the [u]preunt[/u] and [u]adeunt[/u], or [u]eunt[/u] and [u]eund[/u]. The result will be the [u]conit[/u].

In 'lover of something beside a benefactor', I am forced to invent a word, and will call this mode of construction, [u]convasion[/u]; [ins]*[/ins] the terms convaded, the [u]convadents[/u], or the [u]prevadent[/u] and [u]advadent[/u], or the [u]vadent[/u] and [u]vadend[/u]; and the result the [u]convase[/u].
