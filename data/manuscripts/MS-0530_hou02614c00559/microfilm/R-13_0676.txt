If the question be simply what is the easiest way to
take account of the order of succession of the selection
of the different indices, then undoubtedly the best way is
simply to set them down in that order. Let $p$ mean
'praises', $p_j$ 'praises $j$', $p_{jk}$ 'praises $j$ to $k$', $p_{jki}$ '$i$
praises $j$ to $k$'. Then in order to express, [ins]that [del]each person[/del] [ins]everybody[/ins][/ins]
praises some one person to all persons, in a universe
of persons, the [del]usual[/del] [ins]common[/ins] way

$\Pi_j \Sigma_i \Pi_k p_{jki}$

is very good; [del]the[/del] while

$\Sigma_j \Pi_i \Pi_k p_{jki}$

would mean 'There is some one person whom everybody
praises to everybody'; and

$\Pi_i \Pi_k \Sigma_j p_{jki}$

will mean that 'Everybody to each person praises
somebody or other.' From the second [ins]proposition[/ins] the first follows; and
thence the third. For a $\Sigma$ can always be carried
further toward the right and a $\Pi$ toward the left.
