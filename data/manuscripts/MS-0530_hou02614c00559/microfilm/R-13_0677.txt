The precise denials of the three propositions are:

$$\Sigma_{i} \Pi_{j} \Sigma_{k} \bar{P}_{jki}$$

or 'There is some body who fails to praise [del]everybody[/del] [ins]each person[/ins] to some body or other;'

$$\Pi_{j} \Sigma_{i} \Sigma_{k} \bar{P}_{jki}$$

or 'Everybody is unpraised by somebody to somebody;'

$$\Sigma_{i} \Sigma_{k} \Pi_{j} \bar{P}_{jki}$$

or 'There exist two persons (or they may be identical) of whom one praises nobody to the other.'

We come nearer to an analytical expression, if we write the first proposition thus:

$$O_{i} \text{ } P_{j} \text{ } V_{k} \text{ } \Psi \text{ } P_{jki}$$

That is, "For whatever individual there may be designable as $i$, there is an individual designable as $j$ such that whatever individual be designable as $k$, $i$ praises $j$ to $k$." The other five propositions will then be

[del]$O_{i} \text{ } P_{j} \text{ } V_{k} \text{ } \Psi \text{ } P_{jki}$[/del] $V_{j} \text{ } V_{i} \text{ } O_{k} \text{ } P \text{ } P_{jki}$
