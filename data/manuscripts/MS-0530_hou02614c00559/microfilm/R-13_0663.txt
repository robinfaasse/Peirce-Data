$A \ \text{R} \ B$. $A$ is false.
$A \ \text{\cancel{R}} \ B$. $B$ is false.
$A \ \text{\cancel{\cancel{R}}} \ B$. $A$ is true.
$A \ \text{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{R}}}} \ B$. $B$ is true.
$A \ \text{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{R}}}}} \ B$. Either $A$ or $B$ is true.
$A \ \text{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{R}}}}}} \ B$. If $A$ is true, so is $B$.
$A \ \text{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{R}}}}}}} \ B$. Either $A$ or $B$ is false.
$A \ \text{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{R}}}}}}}} \ B$. $A$ is true or $B$ false.
$A \ \text{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{\cancel{R}}}}}}}}} \ B$. Meaningless.
