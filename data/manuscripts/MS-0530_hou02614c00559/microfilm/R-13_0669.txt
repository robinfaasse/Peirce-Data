X. This could be written $\infty$. In the Geometry of Descartes and other writings following that, this sign was used apparently often as a substitute for Recorde's =. But I am inclined to think that what was meant when for example

$\sqrt{4}$ $\infty$ 2

was written was "[u]a[/u] value of the square root of 4 is 2." In that sense the sign might advantageously be retained; for it offends a logician to be told that $\sqrt{4} = -2$.
