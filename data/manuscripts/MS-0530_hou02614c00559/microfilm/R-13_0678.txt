Whether my translation [ins]into words[/ins] of the first of these six forms is a perfectly accurate analysis or not, the notation itself is entirely correct. Of that I feel sure, since it is the same in principle with a mode of writing propositions in the my Algebra of Dyadic Relatives that has been subjected to the most careful scrutiny. But this notation is open to two objections. In the first place, there is too much mere formalism about it: the characters $\Upsilon$ and $O$ have no real meaning.
