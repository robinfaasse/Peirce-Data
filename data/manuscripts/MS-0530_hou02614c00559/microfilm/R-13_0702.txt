The stock instances are those of "much" and "little."
Some things are definitely much; others are definitely
little. Between them is a border to which a man might
at one hour apply the word "much", at another the
word "little," in [del]an off[/del] the endeavor to express the same
truth. Does a man who applies either word, say 'much,'
to a case falling within that border tell the truth or not?
