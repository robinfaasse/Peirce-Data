The mode of construction in 'lover only of benefactors',
which was introduced, but not named, by DeMorgan, and
which in [date]1870[/date], I [del]called[/del] [ins]termed[/ins] [u]regressive involution[/u], a term
objectionable for its numerical and other characters,
I will now term [u]contorsion[/u]. The contorted terms will be
the [u]contorquents[/u], or the [u]pretorquent[/u] and [u]altorquent[/u], or
[u]torquent[/u] and [u]torquend[/u]; and the result will be the [u]contort[/u].

For the uncommon mode of construction in 'not a lover
of any benefactor', I propose the name [u]coamputation[/u].
The coamputated terms will be the [u]coamputants[/u], or
the [u]preamputant[/u] and [u]adamputant[/u], or [u]amputant[/u]
and [u]amputand[/u]; and the result will be the [u]coamputate[/u].
