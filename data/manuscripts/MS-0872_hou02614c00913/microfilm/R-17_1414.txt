[u]XI. What is the real merit or fault of Hume's
argument?[/u]

Hume's argument is one of the strongest applications
that ever has been made of the general method
of judging of the testimonies to ancient history
by means of balancing likelihoods, which is the
method of all the German critics of ancient history
and which has been much employed by writers on
the principles of judicial evidence.
