WASHINGTON, D. C.,

Boston, Mass.,
[date]Oct. 30, 1899.[/date]

My dear Charles:-

I received your letter of September 26th, which had been kept for me, on my return, but I did not get back to this country until about two weeks ago. As soon as I heard of your candidacy for the position in the Coast Survey I wrote to Dr. Pritchett urging your appointment, and received an answer from him that he was very sorry but an appointment had already been made. The letter is with my papers which have gone on to Washington, but as soon as I get them I will forward it to you.

We have had a very pleasant summer indeed. Nannie is at Hamilton with Constance or she would join with me in sending you our best regards.

Sincerely yours,
H. C. Lodge

Charles S. Peirce, Esq.
