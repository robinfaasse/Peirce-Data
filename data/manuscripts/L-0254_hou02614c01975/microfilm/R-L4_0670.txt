My dear Cabot: [ins](Lodge)[/ins]

I take the liberty of reminding you of my strong desire to serve the country in some way at this time, and also to say more explicitly than I did that other things being equal I believe I should be particularly useful where unflurried nerves were desirable in a situation of extreme danger. At the same time, I would not decline [u]any[/u] position in which I should be of use.

I have from boyhood been taught by all our Massachusetts statesmen the the U. S. ought to possess Cuba.
