[u]United States Senate,[/u]
WASHINGTON, D. C.,

Personal.

[date]Dec. 5, 1895.[/date]

My dear Charles:-

I have received your letter of December 1st.
I have no idea what such [del]c[/del]charts as you describe would be worth,
but I will make enquiry at the Hydrographic Office and find out
what the situation in regard to them is.

As for your idea of a newspaper, I can only say from
what little experience I have had that it is something which cannot
be entered into without a very large capital and that it is the
most uncertain of all investments. In regard to the Library, I
will speak to Mr. Spoffard and see if anything can be done there.

With kind regards,

Very truly yours,
H. C. Lodge
