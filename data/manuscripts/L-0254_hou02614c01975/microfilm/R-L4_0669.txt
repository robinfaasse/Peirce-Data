[u]United States Senate,[/u]
WASHINGTON, D. C.,

Personal.

[date]Dec. 26, 1895.[/date]

My dear Charles:-

I have received your letter of the 19th. I am much obliged to you for thinking of me in connection with the pictures, but I am so situated that I could not advance $30,000. --or even a very much less sum--on any pictures, no matter how good. I wrote to the Hydrographic Office as soon as I received your letter, but have not heard from them and just before I got this last letter of yours I had written again. If they do not reply, I will go up there myself personally, but I have been so occupied in the Senate that I have not had time to do this.

With kind regards,

very sincerely yours,
H. C. Lodge.
