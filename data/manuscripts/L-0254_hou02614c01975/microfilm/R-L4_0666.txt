My dear Charles.

I have received both your letters & regret that I must return an unfavorable answer. I have suffered like every one else in these hard times. I have no surplus & am obliged to be very careful. I could of course borrow the money & lend it to you but I do not feel justified in doing for anyone. My own obligations & the instincts of those dependent on me prevent in other words. I am very sorry that this should be so but so it is.

Nannie sends her love.

Sincerely
H. C. Lodge
