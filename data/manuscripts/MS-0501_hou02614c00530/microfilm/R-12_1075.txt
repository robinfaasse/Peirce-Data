[u]Scholium[/u]. Consider the following argument: By
[u]Corollary I[/u], a $\hat{C}$ a is scriptible. Ergo, Something is scriptible.
This is unexceptionable reasoning. Yet since [del]Corollary I[/del] [ins]this[/ins]
is based exclusively upon a definition, it must not be
understood to assert, or imply, the existence of anything;
while the Axiom does distinctly assert existence.
