The necessary and sufficient defence for a vague sign is
an appropriate singular sign; and a general sign is not
sufficient, since there is no guarantee that the interpreter
can produce an object or meaning. The necessary and suffi-
cient attack upon a general sign is an appropriate
singular sign; and a vague sign is not sufficient.
