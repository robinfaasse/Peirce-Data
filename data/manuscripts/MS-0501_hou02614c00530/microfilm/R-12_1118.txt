[u]Theorem VIII[/u]. Whatever i and a may be, if i then (i Ĉ a) Ĉ a.

[u]Proof[/u]. For, by theorem VII, whatever i, a, e may be, if both i
and a Ĉ e then (i Ĉ a) Ĉ e. Let e be a. Then, if both i and
a Ĉ a, then (i Ĉ a) Ĉ a; whence, by Corollary 1, if i [del]then[/del] [ins]and[/ins] (i Ĉ a) Ĉ a.
