[u]Theorem I.[/u] Whatever $e$ may be, if whatever $x$ may be, $x \hat{C} e$, then $e$.
[u]Proof.[/u] For assume that, whatever $x$ may be $x \hat{C} e$. Then I have only to prove $e$. Let $x$ be $a \hat{C} a$. Then, by hypothesis, $(a \hat{C} a) \hat{C} e$. Whence, by Clause 3, if $a \hat{C} a$ then $e$; whence by Corollary 1, $e$. Q.E.D.
