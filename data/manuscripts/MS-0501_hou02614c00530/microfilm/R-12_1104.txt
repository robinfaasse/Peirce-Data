<u>Theorem I.</u> If $e$ be such that, whatever $x$ may be, $x \hat{C} e$, then $e$.

<u>Proof.</u> For assume that, whatever $x$ may be, $x \hat{C} e$. Then I have only to prove $e$. Let [del]at[/del] $x$ be $a \hat{C} a$. Then, by hypothesis, $(a \hat{C} a) \hat{C} e$; whence, by Clause 3, if $a \hat{C} a$ then $e$; whence, by Corollary I, $e$. Q.E.D.

<u>Theorem II.</u> Whatever $a$ and $e$ may be, in case, whatever $x$ may be,
If $x \hat{C} a$ then $x \hat{C} e$,
$a \hat{C} e$ will be scriptible.

<u>Proof.</u> For suppose $a$ and $e$ to be any such things that, whatever $x$ may be, if $x \hat{C} a$ then $x \hat{C}$ [del]e[/del] [ins]$e$[/ins]. Then I have only to show that $a \hat{C} e$. Let $x$ be $a$. Then, by hypothesis, if $a \hat{C} a$ then $a \hat{C} e$; whence, by Corollary I, $a \hat{C} e$. Q.E.D.

<u>Theorem III.</u> Whatever $i$ and $a$ may be, in case, whatever $x$ may be,
If $a \hat{C} x$ then $i \hat{C} x$
$i \hat{C} a$ will be scriptible.

<u>Proof.</u> For suppose $i$ and $a$ to be any such things that, whatever $x$ may be, if $a \hat{C} x$ then $i \hat{C} x$. Then I have only to show that $i \hat{C} a$. Let $x$ be $a$. Then, by hypothesis, if $a \hat{C} a$, then $i \hat{C} a$; whence by Corollary I, $i \hat{C} a$. Q.E.D.
