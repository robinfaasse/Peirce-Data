[u]Corollary 10.[/u] $a\hat{\epsilon}0 = $ If $a$ then everything is scriptible.
For [u]Clause 2[/u] of the Definition of $\hat{C}$ gives
Either $a$ or $a\hat{C}0$

Whence, if one assume
If $a$ then everything is scriptible

one have
Either $a\hat{C}0$ or everything is scriptible. i.e. $0$

But in [u]Clause 1[/u], putting $0$ and $a$ for $i$, we get
If $0$ then $a\hat{C}0$

From these two, it follows that
$a\hat{\epsilon}0$

On the other hand, assuming this, [u]Clause 3[/u] gives
If $a$ then $0$ i.e. everything is scriptible.*
