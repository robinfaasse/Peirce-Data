[u]Theorem III[/u]. Whatever i, a, e may be,
If (i C a) C e then either i or e.

[u]Proof[/u]. For assume
(i C a) C e
Then I have only to prove that either i or e.

By Clause 2 of the Definition of C,
Either i or i C a

By Clause 3 of the Same Definition
If (i C a) and (i C a) C e, then e

Whence by the assumption
If i C a then e

Whence by the [del]former[/del] [ins]result of[/ins] Clause 2,
Either i or e.          Q.E.D
