paper is supposed to represent a definite and individual uni-
verse, "the Truth," so that when a conditional proposition, say
'If i, then e' is written on the sheet, no illative connection is
implied between i and e which should mean that throughout
a general range of possibilities the truth of anything analogous
to i would be accompanied by the truth of something correspond-
ing to e; but all that is meant is that taking the universe of
Truth, [ins]x[/ins] in the state in which it happens to be, although i may
not be true (or applicable, or justifiable, or whatever is the appro-
priate variety of scriptibility,) and should it not be so, nothing
whatever is said about e, yet provided i is true, the interpre-
ter may be sure that e is true. That is in passing from
