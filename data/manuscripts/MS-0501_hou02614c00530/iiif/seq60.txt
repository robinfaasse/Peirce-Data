8

‘Not (Not $i$ but $e$) but $x$’, where $x$ is a sign entirely vague,
will mean the same as ‘If $y$, then (if $e$ then $i$)’ where $y$ is
a sign entirely general.

[fig]figure[/fig]
If (Not $a$, but but $b$) then $c$
$\bar{a} \cdot b$
$a + \bar{b} + c$

If $a$ then Not $b$, but $c$
$\bar{a} + \bar{b} \cdot c$

Not (if $a$ then $b$) but $c$
$a \bar{b} c$

Not $a$ but if $b$ then $c$
$\bar{a} (\bar{b} + c)$

[fig]figure[/fig]
If $a$ then (if $b$ then $c$)
[del]$a + b + c$[/del]

If (if $a$ then $b$) then $c$
$a \bar{b} + c$

Not (Not $a$ but $b$) but $c$
$(a + \bar{b}) \cdot c$

Not $a$ but Not $b$ but $c$
$\bar{a} \bar{b} c$
