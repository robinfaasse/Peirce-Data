[u]Axiom.[/u] Something is non-scriptible.
[u]Scholium.[/u] The following proof might be offered.
Suppose it false. Then it is not scriptible, and con-
sequently something is not scriptible, contrary to the
hypothesis. The hypothesis must therefore be rejected,
and we are forced to the conclusion that something
is not scriptible.
This demonstration is conclusive; but it is a
[u]petitio principii.[/u] Anybody who should maintain
that everything is scriptible, [del]mean[/del] would of course
[del]be[/del] mean by 'everything' everything which could
be expressed in that notation. Now that notation
provides no means of expressing that 'Something
is non-scriptible,' nor indeed of denying anything.
