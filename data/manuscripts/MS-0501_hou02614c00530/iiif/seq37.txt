It is only when a sign for the non-scriptible is introduced
into the notation, and is admitted to have that meaning,
that it becomes evident that something is non-scriptible.
A mere definition may create the sign; but a definition
does not, according to sound logic, declare the existence
of anything. For that purpose positive observation is
required. In this case the substance of that observation
is evident. It ought, therefore, to be termed an axiom.
