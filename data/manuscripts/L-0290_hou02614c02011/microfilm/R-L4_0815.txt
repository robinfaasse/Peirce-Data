312 South Tenth St
Phila. [date]March 17 '99[/date]

My dear Dr. Peirce,

A complication in the arrangements of the Philosophical Club have prevented my writing in answer to your note to assign a date for the lecture that you are generously going to give us - viz the evening of Monday April 11th suit your engagements? We will not have one of the general popular meetings but a smaller session of especially interested people, and send out invitations. If this does not meet your preferences will you be good enough to name a date, - not March 27th nor April 1st nor a Wednesday? In all likelihood it can be adopted.

With hearty thanks for your kindness I am
