312 South 10[sup]th[/sup] St -
Phila. [date]Apr. 6[sup]th[/sup] 1887[/date]

My dear Mr. Peirce,

I am glad that Monday the 12[sup]th[/sup] (next week) will suit you - for I infer that your mention of the 11[sup]th[/sup] (Sunday) was a slip of the pen - Four o'clock in the afternoon, in one of the lecture-rooms in Taylor Hall at Bryn Mawr: such are the time and the place we have to fix upon - If you arrive at Bryn Mawr before one o'clock on Monday I hope you will lunch with me there; in any case I trust you will dine with me in town at the University Club at seven - As may I ask to know beforehand your time of coming? Trains leave the Phila- station, where you arrive, for Bryn Mawr
