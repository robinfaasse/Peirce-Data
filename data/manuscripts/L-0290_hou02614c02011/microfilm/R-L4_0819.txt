312 South Tenth St.
Phila. [date]April 11th '97[/date]

My dear Mr. Peirce,

It is a convenience
to us to be notified at
once of [del]the[/del] [ins]your[/ins] expenses.
As the time is so late I
cash the cheque and en-
close $10 -

If you will be good
enough to inquire for
me at "Taylor Hall" when
you arrive at Bryn Mawr
(the college is but eight
minutes' walk and I
unfortunately don't know
your train) I shall be
on hand - If you reach
there before 1.30 I hope we
can lunch together.

Yours very truly
Dickinson S. Miller
