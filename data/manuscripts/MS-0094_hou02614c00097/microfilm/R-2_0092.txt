[u]Definition #5.[/u] The measure of connectivity is a number [ins]belonging to any surface and[/ins] fulfilling the following conditions:

1. No gradual deformation which does not create nor destroy any contact can alter the measure of connectivity.

2. Every puncture of the surface increases the measure of connectivity by unity.

3. Every cross-cut of the surface diminishes the measure of connectivity by unity.

4. The measure of connectivity of a simple globular surface is zero.

Corollary 6. The connectivity of a [del]dis[/del] closed figure on paper with a number of blots is one more than the number of blots.

Corollary 7. If across a closed figure on paper we make a number, [ins]N,[/ins] of cross-cuts, each cross-cut adds one to the number of pieces. At the same time it diminishes the connectivity by 1. So the number of pieces is N + 1 and the connectivity is 1 - N, or if M is the number of pieces 2 - M is the connectivity. This will generally be a negative number, but that makes no difference.
