[marginalia]§165[/marginalia] [u]Theorem 42[/u]. The angles at the base of an isosceles triangle are equal.

[u]Demonstration[/u]. In the triangle ABC, let

Then, I say, [u]∠[/u]B = [u]∠[/u]C.

The [del]point[/del] [ins]particle[/ins] A will remain at [A].
