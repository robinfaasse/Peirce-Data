_Corollary._ _The sum of the antecedents of a proportion is to the sum of its consequents as either antecedent is to its consequent ; and the difference of the antecedents is to the difference of the consequents in the same ratio._

_Theorem V._ _The sum of the antecedents of a proportion is to their difference, as the sum of the consequents is to their difference._

_Proof._ The proportion

$A : B = C : D$

gives, by the preceding proposition,

$A + C : B + D = A - C : B - D$

whence, by transposing the means,

$A + C : A - C = B + D : B - D.$
