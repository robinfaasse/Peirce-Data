CHAPTER XIII.

AREAS.

[u]239. Definitions.[/u] Equivalent figures are those which have the same surface.

The [u]area[/u] of a figure is the measure of its surface.

[u]240. Definition.[/u] The [u]unit of surface[/u] is the square whose side is a linear unit; so that the area of a figure denotes its ratio to the unit of surface.

[u]241. Theorem.[/u] Two rectangles, as $ABCD$, $AEFG$ (fig. 127) are to each other as the products of their bases by their altitudes, that is,

$ABCD : AEFG = AB \times AC : AE \times AF$.
