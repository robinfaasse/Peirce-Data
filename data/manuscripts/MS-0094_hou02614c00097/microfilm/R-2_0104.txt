A polyhedron is a solid [del]the surface of which is composed of confine polygons called the faces of the polyhedron and the sides of these, each common to two faces are called the edges of the polyhedron, and the extremities which are polygrammata of the network of edges considered as a[/del] [ins]divided into regions of quadruple connectivity, these regions are called the faces of the polyhedron,[/ins] the boundary between two faces is called an edge of the polyhedron, and the extremities [ins]or vertices[/ins] of [del]each[/del] the edges are called the summits of the polyhedron.
