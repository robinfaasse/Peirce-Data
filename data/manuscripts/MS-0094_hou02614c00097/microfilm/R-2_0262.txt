Suppose of three numbers, $x, y, z$, we know that
$$\begin{array}{r}
3x - 2y + 5z = 22 \\
10x + 7y + 2z = \phantom{0}2 \\
5x + 3y + 7z = 20
\end{array}$$

Dividing [del]each syzygy[/del] [ins]each[/ins] by the multiplier of $x$
$$\begin{array}{r}
x - \frac{2}{3}y + \frac{5}{3}z = \frac{22}{3} \\
x + \frac{7}{10}y + \frac{2}{10}z = \frac{2}{10} \\
x + \frac{3}{5}y + \frac{7}{5}z = \frac{20}{5}
\end{array}$$

Subtract the middle one from the first and third, and we get:
$$\begin{array}{r}
-\frac{41}{30}y + \frac{44}{30}z = \frac{214}{30} \\
-\frac{1}{10}y + \frac{12}{10}z = \frac{38}{10}
\end{array}$$

or
$$\begin{array}{r}
-41y + 44z = 214 \\
-\phantom{0}y + 12z = \phantom{0}38
\end{array}$$

Dividing each by the multiplier of $y$, we get:
$$\begin{array}{r}
y - \frac{44}{41}z = -\frac{214}{41} \\
y - 12z = -\phantom{0}38
\end{array}$$

Subtracting the second from the first we have
$$\frac{448}{41}z = \frac{1344}{41}$$

or
$$448z = 1344$$

Dividing by the multiplier of $z$
$$z = 3$$
