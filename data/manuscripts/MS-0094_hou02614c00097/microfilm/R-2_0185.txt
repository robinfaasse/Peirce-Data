[u]§ 93 Definition 138[/u]. [u]Production[/u], [del]otherwise called[/del] [ins]more accurately[/ins] [u]projection[/u], is an operation by which a configuration of [u]Points and Rays[/u], is replaced by a configuration of [u]Rays and Planes[/u], all [del]passing through[/del] [ins]containing[/ins] these Points and Rays and also [del]through[/del] [ins]containing[/ins] one common Point, called the [u]centre of production[/u].

[u]Definition 139[/u]. [u]Section[/u], or [u]plane section[/u], is an operation by which a configuration of [u]Planes and Rays[/u] is replaced by a configuration of [u]Rays and Points[/u], all contained in these Planes and Rays, and also contained in one common Plane, called the [u]plane of section[/u].

[u]Definition 140[/u]. [u]Central projection[/u] is the operation of production followed by a plane section, whereby a configuration of Points and Rays is replaced by a [del]sin[/del] plane configuration of Points and Rays.

There is also an operation of plane section followed by production whereby any configuration of Planes and Rays is [del]followed[/del] [ins]replaced[/ins] by a configuration of Planes and Rays containing one common point.
