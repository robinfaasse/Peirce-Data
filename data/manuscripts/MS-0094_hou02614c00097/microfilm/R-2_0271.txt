let one of the other points of the ray be
$$[ \frac{aA + bB + cC + dD}{a + b + c + d} ]$$
and, if the notation has the property in question, every
other point of the ray may be expressed in the form
$$[ x \frac{aA + bB + cC + dD}{a + b + c + d} + \overline{1-x} \cdot D ]$$
and every such point will be a point of the ray, whatever
$x$ may be. [del]But[/del] Take for the value of $x$
$$x = \frac{a + b + c + d}{a + b + c}$$
Then the point is
$$[ \frac{aA + bB + cC + dD}{a + b + c} - \frac{d}{a + b + c} D ] .$$
But this equals
$$[ \frac{aA + bB + cC}{a + b + c} ] .$$
But this is in the plane $(ABC)$, if the notation has the pro-
perty in question. This proves the third and fourth pro-
positions.
