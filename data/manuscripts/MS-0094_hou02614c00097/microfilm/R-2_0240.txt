[u]§126. Definition 165.[/u] Two [del]lines[/del] [ins]rays[/ins] which lie in one plane, or four points which lie in one plane, or two points and a ray which lie in one plane are said to be [u]coplanar[/u].

[u]Definition 166.[/u] Two rays through a point, or four planes through one point, or two planes and a ray having a common point, are said to be [u]concurrent[/u], or copunctual.

[u]Definition 167.[/u] Three points on one ray, or three planes through one ray, or two points with a plane through them, or two planes with a point on their intersection, are said to be collinear, or better, [u]coradial[/u].
