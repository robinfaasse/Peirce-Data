[u]Definition 144[/u]. A [u]congruence[/u] is a doubly-continuous multitude of rays.

An example is the aggregate of [del]rays along which[/del] [ins]the paths of[/ins] falling bodies at the surface of the earth (conceived as motionless.) So the rays of light from a lens, however bad, or from an irregular mirror lie in a congruence.
