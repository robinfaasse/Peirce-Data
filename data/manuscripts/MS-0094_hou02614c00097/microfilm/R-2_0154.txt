$$\begin{aligned}
1x + 1y + 1z &= 1, \\
2x + 3y + 5z &= 3, \\
7x + 11y + 13z &= 12, \\
px + qy + rz &= px + qy + [del]r[/del][ins]r[/ins]z.
\end{aligned}$$

We drop all the unknowns and simply write,

$$\left| \begin{matrix}
1 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\
2 & 3 & 5 & 3 \\
7 & 11 & 13 & 12 \\
p & q & r & px + qy + rz
\end{matrix} \right|$$

This is called the determinant of the transformation, or of the system of equations.

[u]Subtracting the second column from all the others[/u], we get,

$$\left| \begin{matrix}
0 & 1 & 0 & 0 \\
-1 & 3 & 2 & 0 \\
-4 & 11 & 2 & 1 \\
p-q & q & r-q & px + qy + rz - q
\end{matrix} \right|$$

$$\left| \begin{matrix}
-1 & 2 & 0 \\
-4 & 2 & 1 \\
