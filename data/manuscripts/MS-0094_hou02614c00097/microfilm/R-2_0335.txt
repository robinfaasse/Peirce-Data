[u]Scholium on Plane and Conical Geometry[/u].

We often restrict our considerations to figures all lying in one plane. This is [u]Plane Geometry[/u]. It is specially simple, and the traditional elementary method is to teach this first. This always results in making non-plane or "Solid Geometry" frightfully difficult, even for exceptionally good heads, owing to the [ins]narrow[/ins] habit it impresses on the imagination. Sound psychology shows that it is a prodigally easy way of making the first steps.
