For that reason, one will call the plane $(\omega + \alpha)$ for
every point in it has a designation the sum of the
designations of points in $(\omega)$ and in $(\alpha)$. Thus,
$[O + X + \frac{1}{2} Y + \frac{1}{2} Z]$
is a point in this plane. Its designation is the
sum of $[O + \frac{1}{2} X + \frac{1}{2} Y]$ which is in $(\alpha)$ and
$[\frac{1}{2} X + \frac{1}{2} Z]$ which is in $(\omega)$.

It is [del]plan[/del] evident that [ins]the point[/ins] where this plane is cut by $\{OX\}$
must be called $[O + 2X]$; for being in that ray it must
be $[O + lX]$ where $l$ is some number, and being in
this plane the sum of the multipliers of $X, Y, Z$, must
be $2$. So as the multipliers of $Y$ and $Z$ are [u]zero[/u], that
of $X$ must be $2$, and $[O + 2X]$ is the name of the point.
