CHAPTER XIX.

REGULAR POLYEDRONS.

487. Definitions. — A regular polyedron is one, all whose faces are equal regular polygons, and all whose solid angles are equal to each other. These conditions can be fulfilled in only a small number of cases.

a. If the faces are equilateral triangles, polyedrons may be formed of them, having solid angles contained by three of these triangles, by four, or by five. No other polyedron can be formed with equilateral triangles, for six angles of such a triangle are equal to four right angles, and cannot, by § 339, form a solid angle.
