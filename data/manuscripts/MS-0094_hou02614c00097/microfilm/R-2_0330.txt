If the axis of rotation is removed further and further away from
a given point, and the rotation is made correspondingly slower,
the motion will indefinitely approximate to a motion of translation!

In translation, every accessible particle moves along a ray,
and in a perspective picture all those rays have one vanishing
point. No vanishing points are moved.
