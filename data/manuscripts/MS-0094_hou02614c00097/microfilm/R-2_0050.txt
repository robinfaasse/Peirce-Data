A moving particle describes a line as its ideal wake. That is, a particle being that which at any one instant is entirely contained in a point, [del]the place[/del] [ins]a line[/ins] which a particle occupies at every instant during a motion is a line. A particle could not in all eternity occupy more than a line.

<u>Definition 48</u>. A body which at any one instant occupies a line is [ins]called[/ins] a [u]fibre[/u], or [u]filament[/u]. If it occupies an entire regular line, it is called an entire fibre or filament.

[del]Definition 49. A particle moving so as to produce a line is said to describe (or, loosely, to generate) it.[/del]
