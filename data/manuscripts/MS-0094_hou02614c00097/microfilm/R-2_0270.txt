$[x X + y Y + z Z + \frac{1}{1-x-y-z} W]$,
$[x' X + y' Y + z' Z + \frac{1}{1-x'-y'-z'} W]$,
$[x'' X + y'' Y + z'' Z + \frac{1}{1-x''-y''-z''} W]$.

Do these lie in one ray according to the notation? The condition
of their doing so is that
$\frac{x-x'}{x''-x'} = \frac{y-y'}{y''-y'} = \frac{z-z'}{z''-z'}$.

Now
$x-x' = \frac{\frac{5}{3} - \frac{13}{14} \frac{12}{11}}{\frac{2}{3} - \frac{7}{8} \frac{12}{11}} (z'-z)$
$x''-x' = \frac{\frac{5}{3} - \frac{13}{14} \frac{12}{11}}{\frac{2}{3} - \frac{7}{8} \frac{12}{11}} (z'-z'')$

$\frac{x-x'}{x''-x'} = \frac{z-z'}{z''-z'}$
