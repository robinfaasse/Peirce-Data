168. [u]Problem.[/u] Through a given point [u]P[/u] (fig. 95) in a given angle [u]A[/u], to draw a line so that the parts intercepted between the point and the sides of the angle may be in a given ratio.

[u]Solution.[/u] Draw [u]PD[/u] parallel to [u]AB[/u]. Take [u]DC[/u] in the same ratio to [u]AD[/u] as the parts of the required line. Through [u]C[/u] and [u]P[/u] draw [u]CPE[/u], and this is the required line.

[u]Proof.[/u] For, by § 161,
[u]CP[/u] : [u]PE[/u] = [u]CD[/u] : [u]DA[/u].

169. [u]Corollary.[/u] When [u]DC[/u] is taken equal to [u]AD[/u], [u]PC[/u] is equal to [u]PE[/u].

SIMILAR POLYGONS.

170. [u]Definitions.[/u] Two polygons are [u]similar[/u], which are equiangular with respect to each other, and have their homologous sides proportional.
