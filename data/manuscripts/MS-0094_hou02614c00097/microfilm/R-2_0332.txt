<u>Theorem 40</u>. If two [ins]flat[/ins] [del]planes[/del] laminae [ins]of a rigid body[/ins] are so inclined that the amount of rotation [del]needed to carry[/del] round their common ray as axis needed to carry one into the plane of the other equals the amount needed to continue the rotation so as to bring it into its original plane [ins]they are at[/ins] [del]the inclinat[/del] right angles.

<u>Demonstration</u>. The total amount of rotation [del]is[/del] needed to restore the lamina to its original plane is one turn. If, then, the rotation to carry one into the plane of the other is one half that amount, it is half a turn.
