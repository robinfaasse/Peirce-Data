[u]Inscribed Angle.[/u]

And, in general, an inscribed figure is one, all whose [del]angles have their vertices in the circumference of the circle.[/del] [ins]lie on some curve; and if the curve is not mentioned it is generally understood to be a circle.[/ins] In this case the circle is said to be circumscribed about the figure.

[fig]pointing hand[/fig]

[marginalia]
Definition. A circumscribed figure is one all whose straight lines [del]do[/del] touch some curve, usually understood to be a circle.

Definition. An inscribable polygon is one whose vertices are so placed that a circle may be drawn through them all.

A circumscriptible polygon is one whose sides are such that one circle may touch them all.
