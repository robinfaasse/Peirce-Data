396. *Corollary.* If a section $M'N'O'P'$ &c. is made by a plane parallel to the bases, and passing through the middle point $R'$ of the altitude, it must, by § 336, bisect the lines $AM$, $BN$, &c.; and the area of each trapezoid is, by § 255, the product of its altitude by the line $M'N'$, $N'O'$, &c.

The area of the convex surface of the frustum is, therefore, the product of the altitude by the sum of these lines, that is, by the perimeter of the section made by the plane which bisects the lateral sides of the frustum.

397. *Corollary.* The area of the convex surface of the frustum of a right cone is half the product of its side by the sum of the circumferences of the bases; or it is the product of the side by the circumference of the section parallel to the bases which bisects the side.
