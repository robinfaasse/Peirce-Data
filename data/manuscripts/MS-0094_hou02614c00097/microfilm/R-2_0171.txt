Suppose that, the centre being at the zero-point, the reticulation
were to be rotated round that point through a given angle. Then
the value of R would remain unchanged. Suppose 1 were thus
carried to $p + q \underline{i}$. Then, because R remains unchanged
$$p^2 + q^2 = 1$$
or
$$q = \sqrt{1-p^2}.$$
Then the point $\underline{i}$ will be carried by the same rotation to
$$-\sqrt{1-p^2} + p \underline{i}.$$
