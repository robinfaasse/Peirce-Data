Let us make another application of the same principle

(1) We will take the point [0]

(2) But instead of the plane (C) we will take
$(0+X, 0+2Y, 0+3Z)$

(3) Then the [del]intersections[/del] [ins]pts[/ins] will be
$[X_0] = [0+X]$,
$[Y_0] = [0+2Y]$,
$[Z_0] = [0+3Z]$.

(4) Then we have
$[\{X_0 Y\} \{X Y_0\}] = [0+X+2Y]$,
$[\{X_0 Z\} \{X Z_0\}] = [0+X+3Z]$,
$[\{Y_0 Z\} \{Y Z_0\}] = [0+2Y+3Z]$.

(5) The plane of these will contain
$\{\omega (0+X, 0+2Y, 0+3Z)\}$.

(6) [del]For[/del] Instead of $[\varepsilon \omega (0XY)]$, we shall have $[2Y-X]$,
Instead of $[\varepsilon \omega (0YZ)]$, we shall have $[3Z-2Y]$,
Instead of $[\varepsilon \omega (0XZ)]$, we shall have $[3Z-X]$, or $[\frac{3}{2}Z - \frac{1}{2}X]$.

Then, $[0+2Y+3Z]$, $[0+X+3Z]$, $[2Y-X]$ will be in one ray;
$[0+X+2Y]$, $[0+X+3Z]$, $[3Z-2Y]$ will be in one ray;
$[0+X+2Y]$, $[0+2Y+3Z]$, $[3Z-\frac{1}{2}X]$ will be in one ray,

as they should be according to the notation.
