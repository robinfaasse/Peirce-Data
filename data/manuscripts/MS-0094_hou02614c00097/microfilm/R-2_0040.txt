§ 10
[u]§ Continuity of Space.[/u]

[u]Definition 37.[/u] A place is a part of space.
[u]Definition 38.[/u] A point is a place without parts.
[u]Definition 39.[/u] A body, in geometry, is that which [del]has[/del] [ins]occupies[/ins] a place and cannot be in different places at the same instant.
[ins](But there is nothing in geometry to prevent two bodies from being in the same place [del]at the same[/del] [ins]at any instant[/ins] [del]time[/del])[/ins]
[u]Definition 40.[/u] A particle is a body occupying a point.
[u]Definition 41[/u] [del]Displacement[/del] [ins]A displacement[/ins] is [del]the[/del] [ins]an[/ins] alteration of the place of one body.
[u]Definition 42[/u] A motion is a change in the place of a body.

Between two places which a body occupies at different instants there are intermediate places.
At every instant of time a moving body is in a new place.
