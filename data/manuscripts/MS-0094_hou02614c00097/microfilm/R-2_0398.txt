[del]The[/del] diameter of a circle is [del]the[/del] straight line, as $BD$, drawn through the centre, and terminated each way by the circumference.

87. _Corollary._ Hence, all the radii of a circle are equal, and all its diameters are also equal, and double of the radius.

88. _Theorem._ Every diameter, as $BD$ (fig. 43), bisects the circle and its circumference.

[marginalia]Demonstration[/marginalia]
[del]Proof.[/del] For if the figure $BCD$ be folded over upon the part $BED$, they must coincide; otherwise there would be points in the one or the other unequally distant from the centre.

91. _Theorem._ Every chord is less than the diameter.

[marginalia]Demonstration[/marginalia]
[del]Proof.[/del] Thus $BE$ (fig. 43) is less than $DB$. For, joining $AE$, we have $BD = BA + AE$, but $BE < BA + AE$, therefore $BE < BD$.
