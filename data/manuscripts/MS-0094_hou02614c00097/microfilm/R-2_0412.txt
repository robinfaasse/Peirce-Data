Tangent Circumferences.

126. _Theorem._ When the [del]circumferences of[/del] two circles cut each other, the [del]line[/del] [ins]ray[/ins] _AB_ (fig. 67), which joins their centres, is perpendicular to the middle of the line _CD_, which joins their points of intersection.

_Proof._ For if a perpendicular be erected upon the middle of the chord _CD_, it must, by § 117, pass through the centres _A_ and _B_ of both the circles of which _CD_ is a chord.
