$$\begin{array}{l}

\end{array}$$
Then
$$\begin{array}{l}
(-X_1 - 2B)^2 + 2B(-X_1 - 2B) + C \\

Accordingly, every quadratic which has one root has
another, unless they are both equal, that is, unless
$$X_1 = -X_1 - 2B$$
or
$$X_1 = -B.$$
