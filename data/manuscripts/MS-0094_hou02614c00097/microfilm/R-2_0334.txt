[del]§166.[/del] [u]Definition 189[/u]. A plane triangle, or triangle simply, is a figure determined by any three points in space, called its vertices. It has three sides, which are the stretches from vertex to vertex. It has three angles, which are the rotations in the plane of the triangle about the three vertices from one side to another. They are preferably measured, the [del]three[/del] sides the short way, [ins]and in any case[/ins] so that the rotating fibre shall pass over the [del]side[/del] opposite side.
