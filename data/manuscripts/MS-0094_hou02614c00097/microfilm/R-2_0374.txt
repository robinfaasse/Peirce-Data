[del]41.[/del] [ins]Theorem[/ins]. Of two oblique lines, [u]CF[/u] and [u]CG[/u] (fig. 18) drawn unequally distant from the perpendicular, the more remote is the greater.

[u]GC[/u] + [u]GE[/u] > [u]FC[/u] + [u]FE[/u];
