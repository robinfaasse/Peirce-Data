$[A] = [del]\text{—}[/del] \left[ \frac{1}{2} X + \frac{1}{2} W \right]$,
$[B] = \left[ \frac{1}{3} Y + \frac{2}{3} W \right]$,
$[C] = \left[ \frac{1}{5} Z + \frac{4}{5} W \right]$,

and the second plane [del]contain[/del] contains the points

$[A'] = \left[ \frac{8}{7} X - \frac{1}{7} W \right]$,
$[B'] = \left[ \frac{12}{11} Y - \frac{1}{11} W \right]$,
$[C'] = \left[ \frac{14}{13} Z - \frac{1}{13} W \right]$.

Then in order that a point,

$[D] = \left[ x X + y Y + z Z + \overline{1-x-y-z} W \right]$,

should be on both planes we must have

$2x + 3y + 5z = 1$
$\frac{7}{8}x + \frac{11}{12}y + \frac{13}{14}z = 1$

Dividing by the multipliers of $x$, we have

$x + \frac{3}{2}y + \frac{5}{2}z = \frac{1}{2}$
$x + \frac{8}{7} \cdot \frac{11}{12}y + \frac{8}{7} \cdot \frac{13}{14}z = \frac{8}{7}$

Subtracting, we get

$\left( \frac{3}{2} - \frac{8}{7} \cdot \frac{11}{12} \right) y + \left( \frac{5}{2} - \frac{8}{7} \cdot \frac{13}{14} \right) z = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{8}{7}$

Dividing by the multiplier of $y$ we get

$y + \frac{\frac{5}{2} - \frac{8}{7} \cdot \frac{13}{14}}{\frac{3}{2} - \frac{8}{7} \cdot \frac{11}{12}} z = \frac{\frac{1}{2} - \frac{8}{7}}{\frac{3}{2} - \frac{8}{7} \cdot \frac{11}{12}}$

or

$y = \frac{\frac{1}{2} - \frac{8}{7}}{\frac{3}{2} - \frac{8}{7} \cdot \frac{11}{12}} - \frac{\frac{5}{2} - \frac{8}{7} \cdot \frac{13}{14}}{\frac{3}{2} - \frac{8}{7} \cdot \frac{11}{12}} z$

And so we can find also
