§60. We must not only [del]have[/del] [ins]denote[/ins] the quantities by letters, but
we must have brief signs for the operations. The sign $+$,
read [ins] (latin for more), [/ins] plus, is the sign of addition. Thus,
[u]t[/u] $+$ $40
is the tax with $40 added to it. The sign $-$, read minus
(latin for less), is the sign of subtraction. Thus
[u]x[/u] $-$ $4000
is the income diminished by $4000.

These two signs, $+$ and $-$, plus and minus, are called
the signs, par excellence.
