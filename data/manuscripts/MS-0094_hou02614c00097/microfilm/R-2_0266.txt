For let $s$ be the value of $\frac{a-e}{i-e}$; so that

$$a-e = s(i-e),\quad b-f = s(k-f),\quad c-g = s(l-g);$$

Then

$$\frac{(a-e) + (b-f) + (c-g)}{(i-e) + (k-f) + (l-g)} = \frac{s(i-e) + s(k-f) + s(l-g)}{i-e + k-f + l-g} = s.$$
