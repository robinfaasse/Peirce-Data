$$\left| \begin{matrix}
-1 & 0 & 0 \\
-4 & -6 & 1 \\
p-q & 2p-3q+r & px+qy+rz-q
\end{matrix} \right|$$

This gives

$$\left| \begin{matrix}
-6 & 1 \\
2p-3q+r & px+qy+rz-q
\end{matrix} \right|$$

Adding 6 times the second column to the first we get

$$\left| \begin{matrix}
0 & 1 \\
\left\{ \begin{matrix} 2p-3q+r \\ +6px+6qy+6rz \end{matrix} \right. & px+qy+rz-q \end{matrix} \right|$$

This gives

$$2p-9q+r+6px+6qy+6rz = 0$$

$$2+6x = 0,$$
$$x = -\frac{2}{6}$$
Make [u]q[/u]=1, [u]p[/u]=[u]r[/u]=0, and we get
$$-9+6y = 0,$$
$$y = \frac{9}{6}.$$
Make [u]r[/u]=1, [u]p[/u]=[u]q[/u]=0, and we get
$$1+6z = 0$$
$$z = -\frac{1}{6}.$$
