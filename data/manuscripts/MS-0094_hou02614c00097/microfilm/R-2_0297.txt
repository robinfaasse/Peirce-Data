<u>Corollary 34</u>. Every section of a cone is a conic [ins]or limit of conics[/ins], because the cone is the limit of an endless series of phimi; and a conic is by definition the plane section of a phimus.

It has hitherto been the practice to <u>define</u> a conic as the plane section of a cone. But the definition here used is more convenient and accurate, and facilitates the development of [del]the subject[/del].
