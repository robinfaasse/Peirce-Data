Instead of "quasi-equal" we may say "equal as judged by
the metron." All the "axioms of equality" used in algebra
hold of this "quasi-equality," especially that if equals are subtracted
from equals the remainders are equal, etc. But two places which
are not capable of being occupied by the same part of the
metron, and whose equality cannot be deduced by means of
the axioms of equality, are not equal.
