139. Problem. Through a given point [u]A[/u] (fig. 76), to draw a straight line parallel to a given straight line [u]BC[/u].

[u]Solution[/u]. Join [u]EA[/u], and, by the preceding problem, draw [u]AD[/u], making the angle [u]EAD[/u] = [u]AEF[/u], and [u]AD[/u] is parallel to [u]BC[/u], by § 31.

140. Problem. Two angles of a triangle being given, to find the third.

[u]Solution[/u]. Draw the line [u]ABC[/u] (fig. 77). At any point [u]B[/u] draw the line [u]BD[/u], to make the angle [u]DBC[/u] equal to one of the given angles, and draw [u]BE[/u], to make [u]EBD[/u] equal to the other given angle, and [u]ABE[/u] is the required angle.

[u]Proof[/u]. For these three angles are, by § 25, together equal two right angles.

141. Problem. Two sides of a triangle and their included angle being given, to construct the triangle.

[u]Solution[/u]. Make the angle [u]A[/u] (fig. 78) equal to the given angle, take [u]AB[/u] and [u]AC[/u] equal to the given sides, join [u]BC[/u], and [u]ABC[/u] is the triangle required.

142. Problem. One side and two angles of a triangle being given, to construct the triangle.

[u]Solution[/u]. If both the angles adjacent to the given side are not given, the third angle can be found by § 140.

Then draw [u]AB[/u] (fig. 78) equal to the given side, and draw [u]AC[/u] and [u]BC[/u], making the angles [u]A[/u] and [u]B[/u] equal to the angles adjacent to the given side, and [u]ABC[/u] is the triangle required.
