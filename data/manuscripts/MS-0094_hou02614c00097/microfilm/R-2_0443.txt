220. Problem. To inscribe in a given circle a regu-
lar polygon of 15 sides.

Solution. Find, by § 217, the arc AB (fig. 120) equal
to [fraction]1/10[/fraction] of the circumference, and, by § 214, the arc AC
equal to [fraction]1/6[/fraction] of the circumference, and the chord BC, being
applied 15 times round the circumference, gives the re-
quired polygon.

Proof. For the arc BC is [fraction]1/6[/fraction] - [fraction]1/10[/fraction] = [fraction]1/15[/fraction] of the circum-
ference.
