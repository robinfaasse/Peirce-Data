[u]Subchapter 2. Time[/u].

Definition 17

§ 6. Mathematical characters of time. Time is that by the variations of which individual things have inconsistent characters. Thus, to be alive and to be dead are inconsistent states; but at different times the same body may be alive and dead.

Definition 18.
An alteration is a otherness in the characters of an individual thing.

Definition 19.
A change is a gradual alteration in time.

Definition 20
An instant is a state of time in which there is no room for any alteration.

Definition 21.
A moment is a time in which no [del]sensible[/del] change can [ins]in any way[/ins] be made sensible can take place.

Definition 22
A time, or lapse of time, is any portion of time.

Definition 23
The present, is the existing state of things.

Definition 24
The past is that part of time with which memory is concerned.

Definition 25.
The future is that part of time with which the will is concerned.

Definition 26.
Previous, or before, is that part of time related to any instant as the past is related to the present.

Definition 27.
Subsequent, or after, is that part of time related to any instant as the future is related to the present.
