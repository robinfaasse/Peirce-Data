§147. Take any two points upon a phimus. Let the generators of one set through these points be [u]a[/u] and [u]b[/u], and those of the other set [u]p[/u] and [u]q[/u]. Then the points will be
[ap] and [bq]
joined by the ray {[ap][bq]}
But [u]a[/u] also cuts [u]q[/u], and [u]b[/u] cuts [u]p[/u]. So that there is another ray {[bp][aq]}.
These rays are [u]conjugate[/u], that is, each has the same relation to the other.
