Polyhedra are further distinguished by applying the following [del]names[/del] [ins]epithets[/ins], which indicate the number of summits.

Monoacral, having 1 summit,
Diacral, having 2 summits,
Triacral, having 3 summits,
Tetracral, having 4 summits,
Hexacral, having 6 summits,
Octacral, having 8 summits,
Dodecacral, having 12 summits,
Icosacral, having 20 summits,
etc.

It is also common to distinguish solids by adjectives signifying the number of summits per face. Thus a pentagonal dodecahedron is a solid having 12 faces and 5 summits for each face.
