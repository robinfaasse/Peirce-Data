[del]Definition[/del] [u]Definition 168[/u]. The plane at infinity is called the [u]absolute[/u], or the [u]firmament[/u].

[u]Definition 169[/u]. The perspective delineation of the intersection of a plane with the firmament is called the [u]vanishing-line[/u] of that plane. The perspective delineation of the intersection of a ray with the firmament is called the [u]vanishing-point[/u] of that [del]line[/del] ray.
