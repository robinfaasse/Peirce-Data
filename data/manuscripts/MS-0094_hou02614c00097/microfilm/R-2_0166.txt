§81. Something will be said below about the calculation
of square roots. Mean time, it may be mentioned that unless
$-C > B^{2}$
that is, [del]unless[/del] ([ins]being[/ins] $C$ is a negative quantity), the two roots are nearly
$X_{1} = 2B - \frac{C}{2B}$
$X_{2} = \frac{C}{2B} + \frac{C^{2}}{(2B)^{3}}$

Otherwise, the roots are nearly
$X_{1} = B + \sqrt{-C} + \frac{1}{2} \frac{B^{2}}{\sqrt{-C}}$,
$X_{2} = B - \sqrt{-C} - \frac{1}{2} \frac{B^{2}}{\sqrt{-C}}$.

We can also get an approximate value as follows. Put the
equation into [ins]one of[/ins] the forms
$x^{2} + 2Bx + \mathcal{M}^{2} = 0$
$x^{2} + 2Bx - \mathcal{N}^{2} = 0$

Divide by $x$ and by $\mathcal{M}$ or $\mathcal{N}$, and the equations [del]are[/del]
$\frac{x}{\mathcal{M}} + \frac{\mathcal{M}}{x} = -2 \frac{B}{\mathcal{M}}$
$\frac{x}{\mathcal{N}} - \frac{\mathcal{N}}{x} = -2 \frac{B}{\mathcal{N}}$
