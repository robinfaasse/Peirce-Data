[del]On the contrary,[/del] [del]it is on[/del] It is easy to find these in practice, so that the algebraic condition that an intersection shall be marked [del]is[/del] is hardly [del]req[/del] required. But the condition is that if the numbers attached to the points on one of the intersecting rays are $x_1, y_1, z_1$, and [del]on the[/del] $x_2, y_2, z_2$ and those of the points on the other one of the intersecting rays are $X_1, Y_1, Z_1$, and $X_2, Y_2, Z_2$, then the intersection cannot be marked unless
$$(x_1 y_2 - x_2 y_1)(Z_1 - Z_2) + (y_1 z_2 - y_2 z_1)(X_1 - X_2) + (z_1 x_2 - z_2 x_1)(Y_1 - Y_2)$$
$$+ (x_1 - x_2)(Y_1 Z_2 - Y_2 Z_1) + (y_1 - y_2)(Z_1 X_2 - Z_2 X_1) + (z_1 - z_2)(X_1 Y_2 - X_2 Y_1) = 0.$$

[del]Denoting[/del] the numbers of the intersection be, $x, y, z$,

$$x = \frac{(X_2 - X_1)(x_1 y_2 - y_2 y_1) + (x_2 - x_1)(X_2 Y_1 - X_1 Y_2)}{(y_1 - x_2)(Y_2 - Y_1) - (y_1 - y_2)(X_2 - X_1)}$$

$$y = \frac{(Y_2 - Y_1)(y_1 z_2 - y_2 z_1) + (y_2 - y_1)(Y_2 Z_1 - Y_1 Z_2)}{(y_1 - y_2)(Z_2 - Z_1) - (z_1 - z_2)(Y_2 - Y_1)}$$

$$z = \frac{(Z_2 - Z_1)(z_1 x_2 - z_2 x_1) + (z_2 - z_1)(Z_2 X_1 - Z_1 X_2)}{(z_1 - z_2)(X_2 - X_1) - (x_1 - x_2)(Z_2 - Z_1)}$$

But these cumbrous formulae [del]are seldom[/del] of any use; for the calculation is perfectly simple.
