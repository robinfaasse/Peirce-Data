NOTA BENE.

In this book,
Fixed points are denoted by Roman Capitals, or by combinations
of letters (see below), in Square Brackets, as [A].
Particles are denoted by Roman Capitals in Quotations as "A"
Mobile points (intersections of a fibre moving across a fixed [del]lamina[/del] [ins]surface[/ins] etc)
by Roman Capitals, without special mark.
Plane angles are sometimes denoted thus: $\angle$ A.

[del]Fixed[/del] [ins]Fixed planes[/ins] are denoted by Greek Minuscules, or by combinations
of letters (see below), in Parentheses, as ($\alpha$).
Flat laminae are denoted by Greek Minuscules in Quotations as "$\alpha$".
Mobile planes ([del]moving in[/del] [ins]continuous[/ins] planes of fixed surface [del]passing through[/del] moving
straight fibre, etc) by Greek Minuscules without special mark.
Sides of spherical triangles are sometimes denoted [del]by[/del] [ins]thus[/ins]: |$\alpha$.

Fixed rays (straight lines, etc) are denoted by Lower Case Italics, or
by combinations of letters (see below), in Braces, as {$b$}.
Straight fibres are denoted by Lower Case Italics in Quotations, as "$b$".
Mobile straight lines (intersection of fixed planes, or of flat laminae, etc)
by small letters without special mark.
A Distant ray along the ray is sometimes written |$b$
An Angle of planes around the axis is denoted by $\angle b$.
