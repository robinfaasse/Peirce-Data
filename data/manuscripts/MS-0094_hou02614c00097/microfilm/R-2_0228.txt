If we begin by putting the given number $N$ equal to $0$,
there is but one number which fulfills the conditions; it
is $0$. The following table shows how the answer increases
as $N$ increases

$$\begin{array}{cc}
N & \text{Answer} \\
0 & 0 \\
100 & 0.34885 \\
200 & 0.72525 \\
300 & 1.13539 \\
400 & 1.58805 \\
500 & 2.09641 \\
600 & 2.68168 \\
700 & 3.38199 \\
800 & 4.27999 \\
900 & 5.63634
\end{array}$$

During all this interval there is only one value satisfying
the conditions for each value of $N$. Nor is there any other
until $N$ reaches $968$. For $N = 967$ the only answer is $7.89620$
For $N = 968$, there is the neighboring answer $8.00000$; but
there also suddenly appears another $11.00000$. This new one
instantly splits into two. For $N = 968.01$, [del]one[/del] [ins]the[/ins] answer near $8$ is $8.00111$;
and near $11$ we find $10.94170$ and $11.05719$. For $N = 969$, the
three answers are
$$\begin{array}{ccc}
8.1206[del]6[/del] & 10.34730 & 11.53209
\end{array}$$
For $N = 970$ the answers are
$$\begin{array}{ccc}
8.2679[del]5[/del] & 10.00000 & 11.73205
\end{array}$$
