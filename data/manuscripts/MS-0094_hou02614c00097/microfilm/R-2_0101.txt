Throughout their length, [del]the[/del] no effect is produced upon the connectivity; so that the separation of space into two parts by an [del]annul[/del] annular surface does not change the connectivity.

We [del]infe[/del] can infer from this that the connectivity of space is affected by the creation of surfaces in it precisely as the connectivity of a group of surfaces would be by the addition to it of the same surfaces, except that instead of an increase there is a decrement and vice versa.

It will be convenient to call the connectivity of unbounded space, [u]minus[/u] one. We may also consider the connectivity of any bounded space by itself the same as that of the group consisting of this space and the rest of unbounded space.
