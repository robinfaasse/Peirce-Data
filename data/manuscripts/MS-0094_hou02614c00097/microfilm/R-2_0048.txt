The following is a clearer proof that
space must be conceived to return into itself.

[fig]Diagram showing line AB, a self-returning line, and radiating lines from point O to AB[/fig]

Suppose a line AB to traverse all Space, and another
which returns into itself to touch it; and let lines radiate
from a point, O, within the self-returning line and crossing
that to terminate on the line AB, none of them crossing each
other; and let there be such a radiating line for every
point of AB. Furthermore let the lines that go to the B side of
AB cross the self-returning line at points whose limit is I; and
let the lines that go to the A side cross the self-returning line at
points having the same limit. Imagine all the points on AB to
be marked and then let the self-returning line turn slowly
about O. The radiating lines may turn also [del]but so changing[/del]
so that each takes precisely [ins]the[/ins] position another had. In this way,
the points on AB will be moved along, and if they move from
B to A, it is easy to see that going off in the direction of
A, they will come back from the direction of B.
