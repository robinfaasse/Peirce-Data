$\S 146$ [u]Corollary 36[/u]. Since a section of a cone is a conic, by cutting the cone of the last corollary by a plane, we get the following, called [u]Brianchon's theorem[/u]. The intersections of the three most distant pairs of any six tangents to one conic meet in one point. "Most distant" here, means most distant in any cyclical order in which the tangents may be taken.
