§77 We will now take up single quadratic equations.
A quadratic equation is an equation which contains the
unknown quantities multiplied two (whether the same one
or different ones) together, but which contains no products
of three unknowns. Thus
[u]x[/u]² + 2 B[u]x[/u] + C = 0
is a quadratic equation.
