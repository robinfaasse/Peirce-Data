That is to say that 9 is smaller than 10, but is not smaller
than a number smaller than 10; or, in other words, 9 is next
smaller than ten.

Such a relation may be called a relation of [u]deuterensis[/u]
[From Greek [u]δευτερεύειν[/u], to come next.] We may say, for
commodity, that A is next to B.

The converse of a relation of deuterensis is itself a
relation of deuterensis.
