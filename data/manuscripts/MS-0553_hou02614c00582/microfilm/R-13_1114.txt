Let $u_{pqr}$ mean $p$ urges $q$ to shoot $r$,
and let $v_{pqr}$ mean $p$ abuses $q$ to $r$.

Then the premises are
$$\Pi_i \Sigma_j \Pi_k (\bar{v}_{ijk} + u_{ijk})$$
$$\Sigma_x \Pi_y \Sigma_z \ \ v_{xyz} \cdot \bar{u}_{xzy}$$

We can write the $\Pi$s and $\Sigma$s in two orders either
$$\Sigma_x \Pi_y \Sigma_z \Pi_i \Sigma_j \Pi_k \ (\bar{v}_{ijk} + u_{ijk}) \cdot v_{xyz} \cdot \bar{u}_{xzy}$$
or
$$\Sigma_x \Pi_i \Sigma_j \Pi_y \Sigma_z \Pi_k \ (\bar{v}_{ijk} + u_{ijk}) \cdot v_{xyz} \cdot \bar{u}_{xzy}$$

In the first case, we cannot identify anything but $k$ with $j$
and hence can eliminate nothing. In the second case, we can
identify $i$ with $x$, $y$ with $j$, and $k$ with $z$. We thus get

$$\Sigma_x \Sigma_j \Sigma_z \ (\bar{v}_{xjz} + u_{xjz}) \cdot v_{xjz} \cdot \bar{u}_{xzj}$$
which gives
$$\Sigma_x \Sigma_j \Sigma_z \ \ u_{xjz} \cdot \bar{u}_{xzj}$$
that is, there is a man who has urged one party to
shoot another without having urged the latter to shoot
the former.
