§4. We may use the sign <, writing
A < B
to signify logical consequence, occasionally extending it to other
varieties. The above is read "If A, then B," or "[del]Every[/del] [ins]cases of[/ins] A is [ins]a case of[/ins] B."
At the same time, we may write
A[u]T[/u]B
to express substantial consequence, or either not A or else B
is true.
