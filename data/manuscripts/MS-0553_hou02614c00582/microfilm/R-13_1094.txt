It is one thing to say that there is a man who has benefitted everyman in some respect, and quite another to say that there is a respect in which every man has been benefitted by some man. Either of these propositions might be true though the other were false. This one sees upon reflection, for if a man benefitted one man in one respect and another in another respect, there might be no one respect in which all were benefitted; and so, though all were benefitted, if one were benefitted by one man and another by another, there might be no man who should benefit all.
