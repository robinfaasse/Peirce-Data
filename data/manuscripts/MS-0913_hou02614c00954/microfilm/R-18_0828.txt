This distinction seems to be a sharp one. The distinction
between secondally genuine secondness, where the dyadic
relation is between objects otherwise opposed to one another
and secondally degenerate secondness, where the dyadic
relation is between objects in some sense identical is, for the
most part, a [ins]and[/ins] distinction of more or less.
