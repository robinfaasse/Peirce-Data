[u]Secondness[/u], [pn]2[/pn]. 1. The ordinal position in a series of the second member thereof, whether this position be considered in itself or as appertaining to the second object.

2, (a) The mode of being of [del]that[/del] [ins]an object[/ins] which is such as it is by virtue of being paired, [del]regardless of any triadic relation[/del] [ins]or in a dyadic relation, to an object[/ins]. (b) Also, the special mode of being of the dyadic relation itself, [del]apart from every triadic relation[/del]. (c) Also, in a looser sense, [del]a[/del] [ins]peculiar[/ins] character which belongs to an individual object by virtue of its being in a dyadic relation to an object, apart from any triadic relation.
