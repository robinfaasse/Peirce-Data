Mr C. S. Peirce
U. S. C. S Office
Washington
Dear Sir
Yours of the 27th is
just received. It has been no trouble or
expense to us making the estimates for you,
and we should at any time be happy to help
you. We did not understand the Receiver -
was to be moved about - but thought it was
for the Office in Washington and once in place
would not have to be moved. We stated to
the pattern maker, - when we carried him our
plan, that the Receiver was to stand a
pressure of 15 lbs to the inch and left him to
estimate the thickness, in such a large thing we
apprehend much difficulty in making it perfectly
tight, and therefore the fewer joints the better
we intended to have it cast as nearly whole as
