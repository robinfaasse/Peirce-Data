Boston Dec 31

Mr. C. S. Peirce
Sir
Your favor of the 30th is received.
We are sorry we did not see you, as we could
better have understood what you wanted.
A receiver such as you want would be diffi-
cult to make tight & quite expensive, we
would want good working drawings before we
could undertake it. Why could it not be made
so as to introduce the pendulum at the top?
thus avoiding the necessity of making such a
large door, which would be very difficult to make
tight. Need the sides all be of glass? why not
have two sides of iron & the others of glass?
We can give you no estimate of the cost until
we know a little better what is required, & it would
depend a good deal on the time expended. How good
a vacuum do you require? our test Pumps will
give one very nearly equal to Torricellian, the price
