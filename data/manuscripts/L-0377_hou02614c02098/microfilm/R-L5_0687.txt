Boston [date]Jan 25. 1873[/date]

Mr. C. S. Peirce
Sir.

We have completed our estimate, as far as we can, of the cost of making a receiver, & find it will cost between [ins]$[/ins]275. & $300. We should have the top, bottom, & two sides of iron, cast in one piece, the other two sides of glass, one being a door & swing on hinges, but would have to be fastened when shut, with bolts, the whole thing when finished would weigh about 1,000. lbs. It is rather an expensive piece of apparatus, but we find that we could not undertake to make it cheaper, the pattern alone would cost us between [ins]$[/ins]60 & $70, & it may take some time to make tight, we may have less trouble than we anticipate, in which case we may be able to make it cheaper than the above figures, The above price is for the receiver alone & does not include any attachments. We can finish it in about four weeks, from when it is ordered.

Yours Truly
E. S. Ritchie & Sons
