Many writers, especially in Germany, imagine that that part of logic called [u]“critic”[/u], which distinguishes between good arguments and bad was substantially finished up by Aristotle; and that, in spite of some of these writers having read, understood in the main, and remembered in large part, what Aristotle wrote on this subject. They are prodigiously mistaken, although it is quite true that the Prior Analytics was a great contribution to science. It is quite true that every argument may be split up into arguments of two premisses each, which is what Aristotle [ins]usually[/ins] means by a ‘syllogism’.
