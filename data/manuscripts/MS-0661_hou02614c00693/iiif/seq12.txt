These relations and others show positively that Aristotle's three figures of syllogism are really three different ways of reasoning, while the modern [u]four[/u] figures are mere mechanical transpositions. The fourth figure has properly but three moods; those properly called [u]Celantos[/u] (which [del]properly[/del] [ins]in its nature[/ins] belongs to the second figure), [u]Dabitis[/u] (which belongs to the third) and [u]Frisesomorum[/u] which partakes of the nature both of the second and the third figures.
