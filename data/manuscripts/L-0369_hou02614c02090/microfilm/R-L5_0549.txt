My dear Remsen:

In regard to Brühl's doctrine that a solvent must have a plurivalent atom (which in the case of benzene might be supplied by one of the double bonds) will you tell me whether liquid paraffins cannot act as true solvents?

I shall be greatly obliged by an answer to this obvious question, & I beg you to accept my thanks in advance

very truly
C. S. Peirce
