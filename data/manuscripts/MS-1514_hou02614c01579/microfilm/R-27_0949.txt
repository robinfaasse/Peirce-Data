render certain points of this mass and to perceive the displacements
which they undergo, it is only necessary to put into suspension
in the water small silvered pearls to which have been[/del]

Other phenomena of the same class can be studied
by chronophotography. Thus, a thin inclined plane being
presented to a liquid current, the bright pearls will express
by the direction of their course, the motion of liquid fillets.
By the distances between their images they will express the
rapidity of the current. A scale of millimetres immersed
in the water will measure the extent of the motions, while
