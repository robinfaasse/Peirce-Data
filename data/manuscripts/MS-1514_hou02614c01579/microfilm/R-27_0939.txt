[u]No 15. The Alethorama of Messrs Chéri-Rousseau
and Mortier, [date]1897[/date].[/u] This is a projecting apparatus in which
the [ins]unperforated[/ins] film-ribbons, as they pass along, give reflections of their
pictures from a series of prisms. The projections are [del]most[/del]
exceedingly bright and steady and altogether make a fine
effect. The apparatus, however, seems to be hard to adjust,
and does not appear to have been taken up practically.
