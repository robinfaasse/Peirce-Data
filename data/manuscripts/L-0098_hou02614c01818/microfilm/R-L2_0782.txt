Cambridge Mass
[date]Dec 16, 1872[/date]

Dear Sir

I have filed copy of the Superintendent's letter authorizing the employ of Mr Boyle, under the supposition that Mr. Hein has been advised of the engagement.

The Superintendent retains your second letter of inquiry concerning the backed maps, intending to recover, if possible, a clue by asking Dr. Parsons to name some of the Libraries in Florence.

Yours respectfully
W. W. Cooper

C. S. Peirce Esq
Assistant Coast Survey
