The Logic of Relatives
[del]qualitative and quantitative[/del]

By C. S. Peirce

§1. My purpose is to give as elementary an account as possible of the logic of relations, while carrying the subject considerably beyond the previous bounds of our knowledge. I shall [del]make use[/del] [ins]employ[/ins] of the same algebraical notation I used in my last paper in Vol VII of the American Journal of Mathematics. This requires an acquaintance with the Boolean algebra in the form given to it by Professor Mitchell. Accordingly, my paper will consist of three parts, on the non-relative logic, relative logic, and quantitative logic.
