<u>Rule II</u>. We may insert any expression
under an even number of streamers, or
erase any expression under an odd num-
ber.

Thus, if we have given $\overline{\overline{\text{A}}+\text{B}}+\text{C}$, we may
insert D under two streamers, giving
$\overline{\text{D}+\overline{\text{A}}+\text{B}}+\text{C}$, or under none, giving $\text{D}+\overline{\overline{\text{A}}+\text{B}}+\text{C}$;
or we may erase $\overline{\text{A}}$, which is under one
streamer, leaving $\overline{\text{B}}+\text{C}$.
