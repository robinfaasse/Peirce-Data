[u]Solution of the General Cubic[/u]

$$Ax^3 + Bx^2 + Cx + D = 0$$

Put $$\frac{Q}{2} = \frac{1}{27} \frac{B^3}{A^3} - \frac{1}{6} \frac{BC}{A^2} + \frac{1}{2} \frac{D}{A}$$

$$R = \frac{1}{27} \frac{B^3 D}{A^4} - \frac{1}{108} \frac{B^2 C^2}{A^4} + \frac{1}{27} \frac{C^3}{A^3} - \frac{1}{6} \frac{BCD}{A^3} + \frac{1}{4} \frac{D^2}{A^2}$$

Then let $$i$$ be any cube root of unity

$$x = i \sqrt[3]{-\frac{Q}{2} + \sqrt{R}} + \frac{1}{i} \sqrt[3]{-\frac{Q}{2} - \sqrt{R}} - \frac{B}{3A}$$
