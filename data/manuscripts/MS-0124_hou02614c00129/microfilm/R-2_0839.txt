If $n \le a_0$

Then $W_{n,N}$ is equal to the [del]number[/del] [ins]number[/ins] partitions of the number $n$ into $N$ parts.

Let us therefore consider the partitions of numbers.

The [del]number of[/del] partitions of $n$ into $1$ part equals $1$

Into two parts (including zero) is $\frac{1}{2}(1+R)$ where $R_n$ is zero for an even number, $1$ for an odd number. Excluding zero it is $\frac{1}{2}(1-R)_n$

Into $3$ parts (excluding zero)

[del]No.[/del] No. of partitions of $n$ into $N$ parts

$\Pi_{n,N}$

$\Pi_{n,1} = 1$

$\Pi_{1,1} = 1$

$\Pi_{1,2} = 1$

$\Pi_{n,2} = \Pi_{n-2,2} + \Pi_{n,1}$

$\Pi_{n,3} = \Pi_{n-3,3} + \Pi_{n,2}$

$\Pi_{n,N} = \Pi_{n-N,N} + \Pi_{n,N-1}$

$= \Pi_{n-N,N} + \Pi_{n-N,N-1} + \dots + \Pi_{n-N,N-2}$

$\Pi_{n,1} = \Pi_{n-1,1} + \Pi_{n,0}$
