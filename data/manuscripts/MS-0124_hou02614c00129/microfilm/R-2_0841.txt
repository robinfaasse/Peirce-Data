It appears therefore that every [del]real[/del] plane contains
one real ray.

Let us now see whether every point is
"contained" in a real ray.

Let the point be have for its coordinates
[del]$x$[/del] $x_1 + \xi_1 i$ $y_1 + \eta_1 i$ $z_1 + \zeta_1 i$

Let the real ray be
$x = [del]x' t + x''[/del] [ins]$x' t + x'' (1-t)$[/ins]$ [ins]$x' t + \xi_2 (1-t)$[/ins]
$y = y' t + y'' (1-t)$
$z = z' t + z'' (1-t)$

$x' t + x'' (1-t) = x_1 + \xi_1 i$
$t (x' - x'') = x_1 - x'' + \xi_1 i$
$t = \frac{x_1 - x''}{x' - x''} + \frac{\xi_1}{x' - x''} i$
$= \frac{y_1 - y'' + \eta_1 i}{y' - y''}$
$= \frac{z_1 - z'' + \zeta_1 i}{z' - z''}$
