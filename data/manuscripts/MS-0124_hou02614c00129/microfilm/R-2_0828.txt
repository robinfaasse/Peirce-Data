[u]Parseval's Theorem.[/u]
$$\Phi\theta^{ti} = a_{0} + a_{1}\theta^{ti} + a_{2}\theta^{2ti} + \text{etc}$$
$$\Psi\theta^{-ti} = b_{0} + b_{1}\theta^{ti} + b_{2}\theta^{-2ti} + \text{etc}$$
Multiplying the two formulae and integrating
$$\int_{\alpha}^{2\pi+\alpha}\Phi\theta^{ti}\cdot\Psi\theta^{-ti}\cdot dt = 2\pi\left\{a_{0}b_{0} + a_{1}b_{1} + a_{2}b_{2} + \text{etc}\right\}$$
We can thus get the sum of the infinite series
$$a_{0}b_{0} + a_{1}b_{1} + a_{2}b_{2} + \text{etc}$$
when we know $\Phi\theta^{ti}$ and $\Psi\theta^{ti}$, both series being
convergent.
