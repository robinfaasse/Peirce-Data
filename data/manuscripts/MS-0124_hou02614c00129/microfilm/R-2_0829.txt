We now change the variable by putting
$$\sin \frac{t}{2} = \theta \qquad \cos t = 1 - 2\theta^2 \qquad \sin t = 2\theta \sqrt{1-\theta^2}$$
$$\frac{1}{2} \cos \frac{t}{2} \cdot dt = d\theta \qquad \cos \frac{t}{2} = \sqrt{1-\theta^2}$$

Thus,
$$\frac{\partial^n}{\partial n!} = \int_0^\partial e^{n \cos t} \cos [n(\sin t - t)] \cdot dt$$

becomes
$$\frac{\partial^n}{\partial n!} = \int_0^1 e^{n} e^{-2n\theta^2} \cos [n(2\theta \sqrt{1-\theta^2} - 2\arcsin \theta)] \cdot \frac{2d\theta}{\sqrt{1-\theta^2}}$$

Now
$$\arcsin \theta = \theta + \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{3} \theta^3 + \frac{1 \cdot 3}{2 \cdot 4} \cdot \frac{1}{5} \theta^5 + \frac{1 \cdot 3 \cdot 5}{2 \cdot 4 \cdot 6} \cdot \frac{1}{7} \theta^7 + \text{etc.}$$
$$\theta \sqrt{1-\theta^2} = \theta - \frac{1}{2} \theta^3 - \frac{1}{8} \theta^5 - \frac{1}{16} \theta^7 + \text{etc.}$$
$$\theta \sqrt{1-\theta^2} - \arcsin \theta = - \frac{2}{3} \theta^3 - \frac{2}{5} \theta^5 - \frac{3}{28} \theta^7 + \text{etc.}$$

$$\frac{\partial^n e^{-n}}{2 \cdot n!} = \int_0^1 e^{-2n\theta^2} \cos [2n(\frac{2}{3}\theta^3 + \frac{2}{5}\theta^5 + \frac{3}{28}\theta^7 + \text{etc.})] \frac{d\theta}{\sqrt{1-\theta^2}}$$

We now put $\theta = \frac{x}{\sqrt{2n}} \qquad d\theta = \frac{dx}{\sqrt{2n}}$

$$\theta^5 = \frac{x^5}{4n^2\sqrt{2n}}$$
$$\theta^7 = \frac{x^7}{8n^3\sqrt{2n}}$$
[/marginalia]
