[u]Virial Equation[/u]
[u]$\Phi$[/u] force between molecules.
$\frac{3}{2} pv = \sum \frac{1}{2} m V^{2} - \frac{1}{2} \sum \sum r \Phi r$

Equation of van der Waals
$pv = R\theta + R\theta \frac{b}{v-b} - \frac{a}{v}$

$\theta$ absolute temp.
$R\theta = \frac{2}{3} \sum \frac{1}{2} m V^{2}$
$R\theta \frac{b}{v-b}$ force of repulsion at encounters (collision)
$\frac{a}{v}$ cohesion
