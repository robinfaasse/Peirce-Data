$A$ and $B$ are identical if
there are two numbers $x$ and $y$ such that
$x + y = 0$
$x A + y B = 0$

$A, B, C$ are coradial if
$x + y + z = 0$
$x A + y B + z C = 0$

$A, B, C, D$ are coplanar if
$a A + b B + c C + d D = 0$

The [del]line through $A$ and $B$[/del]
The [del]line[/del] ray through
$p_1 A + q_1 B + r_1 C$
$p_2 A + q_2 B + r_2 C$
is $p A + q B + r C$
where
[del]$\Sigma p A$[/del]
[del]$\Sigma p A$[/del] $x p_1 A + x q_1 B + x r_1 C$
$+ y p_2 A + y q_2 B + y r_2 C$
$+ z p A + z q B + z r C = 0$

This is equivalent to
$x p_1 + y p_2 + z p = 0$
$x q_1 + y q_2 + z q = 0$
$x r_1 + y r_2 + z r = 0$

That is
$\left| \begin{matrix} p_1 & p_2 & p \\ q_1 & q_2 & q \\ r_1 & r_2 & r \end{matrix} \right| = 0$

Which is the equation of the ray.
