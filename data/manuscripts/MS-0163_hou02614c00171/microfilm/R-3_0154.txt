To prove lines joining vertices ABC
of a $\Delta$ to the middle points of sides
are concurrent.

Assume $D = A - B + C$
$B - C$ which is on $BC$
$A - C$ " " " $AB$
$A - B$ " " " $AB$

$|(A+B-C), A| = |B, C|$
$|A+B-C, A| = |2B-C, B|$

$\left. \begin{aligned}
|2B-C, B| &= 2|B, C| \\
|2A-C, C| &= 2|A, C| \\
|2A-C, 2B-C| &= 2|B, C|
\end{aligned} \right\}$ The [ins]double[/ins] triangle

Two of the three rays
$\left. \begin{aligned}
2A-C, B \\
2B-C, A
\end{aligned} \right\}$ intersect at $2A+2B-C$

But $2B-C, A$ also contains $2A+2B-C$
Therefore the [del]three[/del] [ins]rays[/ins] are copunctual
