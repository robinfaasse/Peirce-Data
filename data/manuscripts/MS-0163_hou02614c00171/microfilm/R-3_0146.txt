The Chow were red haired
Kirghizes organized to some
extent.

Lunar eclipse of 1136 B.C. Jan 29
twenty six years before foundation
of the Chow dynasty has been
verified by S. M. Russell.

1100 B.C. Overthrow of the
Shang-Yin dynasty and
conquest of the country by the
Chow with the help of
native tribes. They introduced
ring-money, clepsydra, polarity
of magnet, names of stars,
the 28 lunar mansions, duo-
decenary cycle of animals,
worship of Heaven & earth,
notion of the Yin and Yang

use of the flagrant-ch'ang
"bringing down spirits", dog
sacrifice, dying horses manes
red, eunuchs, slavery,
castration as punishment, 3 holed
flute, etc all notions originating
in Bactria and Khorasmia.

He adopts the chronology
of the Bamboo Annals
somewhat corrected (but
never by as much as 90 years
at one)
