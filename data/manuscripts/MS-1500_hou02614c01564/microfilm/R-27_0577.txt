[u]Flying Vocables and Wingless Reading-Matter[/u]

To a person who is accustomed to read old books [del]there is[/del]
nothing very shocking in Professor Brander Matthews's [del]list[/del]
of reformable spellings. One is rather inclined to ask whether
it was worth while to make such a fuss about so infinites-
imal a result. A good deal of the list simply consists of
Johnson's spellings; for he, too, made an attempt to
induce people to spell a little more phonetically, as
John Milton had done, and other eminent persons before
him. It is a fact to be pondered that they all failed.
It is not supposable that Professor Matthews individually
wields an influence stronger than Dr. Johnson did.
