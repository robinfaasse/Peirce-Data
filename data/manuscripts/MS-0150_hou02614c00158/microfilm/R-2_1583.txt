Begin with $A$, $B$, and then syzygies, such as
[del]$0.99 A + 0.01 B$,[/del] $[0.99 A + B]$, or $[A + \frac{100}{99} B]$,
[del]$1.01 A - 0.01 B$,[/del] etc $[1.01 A - B]$, or $[A - \frac{100}{101} B]$, etc.

The ratio of the multiplier of $A$ to that of $B$, which is the only quantity
in question, since both multipliers may be multiplied by any one
number without change of meaning, may gradually increase from
$0$ through all positive numbers to infinity $\infty$. Since infinity is
$\frac{1}{0}$ and $+0$ and $-0$ are the same, there is no difference between
$+\infty$ and $-\infty$. Thus,
$[1000000 A + B] = [A + \frac{1}{1000000} B]$;
$[\infty A + B] = [A + 0 B] = A$;
[del]$[-\infty A + B]$[/del] $= [1000000 A - B] = [A - \frac{1}{1000000} B]$.

Thus the ratio passes to the negative values and returns through
all the negative values to $0$. This is precisely analogous to the motion
of a point particle along a ray, and shows that on each ray
[del]the[/del] points exactly correspond to the designations.

Next suppose the designation includes $C$. Then any syzygy
between $A$, $B$, and $C$, such as
$[3A + 7B + 11C]$
is a syzygy between $A$ and a syzygy between $B$ and $C$. The syzygy
between $B$ and $C$ represents any point on the ray through $B$
and $C$. The syzygy between $A$ and that syzygy represents any
point [del]C[/del] on the ray [del]through[/del] $A$ and that point on the ray $\{BC\}$.
