[u]Art. 16. Definition 29.[/u] An ordinary point of a line or surface
or an ordinary line of a surface is a point or line whose con-
nections with neighboring points and lines [ins]are[/ins] [del]is[/del] the same as those of
[ins]some[/ins] [del]any[/del] of these neighboring points or lines to one another. A
singular point or line is one whose connections are different
from those of [ins]all[/ins] neighboring points and lines.
