[u]Section[/u] . [u]Measurement on a Perissid Surface[/u] .

[u]Scholium[/u] . The design of this chapter is to trace out those properties of [del]the[/del] [ins]space[/ins] measurement which are independent of the types of measurement used. These propositions hold therefore of [del]lines on[/del] the straightest possible lines upon a [del]hemisphere, or[/del] sphere, supposing that we do not distinguish between points antipodal to one another. We consider chiefly measurement upon a surface; because this is easier than taking account of a third dimension, and leaves not very much to be supplemented to [del]give[/del] [ins]or[/ins] [del]the[/del] fundamental propositions for [del]their[/del] [ins]the[/ins] complete [del]geometry[/del].
