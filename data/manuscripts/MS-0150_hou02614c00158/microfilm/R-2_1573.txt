[u]Illustrations[/u] . Elliptical measurements are common
enough, the age of the moon, the day of the week, etc.
are examples. Put for Sunday 0, for Monday 1, for Tues-
day 2, for Wednesday 3, for Thursday 4, for Friday 5, for
Saturday 6. Then, what is the fourth day after Friday?
Answer: Friday (i.e. 5) + 4 $\equiv$ 9 $\equiv$ 2 (i.e. Tuesday.)

The three lines are the sign for congruency, which means
equality, with a finite round, considered as the same as 0.
