a sort of local habitation & stamping
ground that lies forever quite beyond
the range of either Science or Philosophy,
[ins]but not beyond the range of Knowledge if Knowing = acknowledging.[/ins]
We can then say, The Universe is a real
ordered System of which our idealizing
sense is (most plausibly & workably, I think)
to be regarded as a foreshadowing. We
can say no more than this, but it is
enough. For the rest — pragmatism,
and a closing of the doors of the Hall of
Fame upon Aristotle, Hegel & all the
rest; not to shut them from public gaze but
to keep them securely where they belong, among
the great treasures of the past which have made
things clearer for us today.

Much of very vital practical consequence
turns, as I think, upon remembrance that we
do [del]thus[/del] [ins]must[/ins] acknowledge the incomprehensible
reality of the absolute horn of the ontological
[del]dilemma[/del] as the crude attempt of the envelope
[del]that infolds.[/del] Yours very truly, S.D. Merton
