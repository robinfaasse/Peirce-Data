Now the point which I thought might interest you is
this. Suppose that your Terminology Convention lets
"justice" = social wisdom & endorses a new term
to = the supposititious ethical connotation which
we now impute to the term "justice": what practical
advantage will have been gained? If the Convention
should happily see the point & embrace the opportunity
to puncture the popular ethical superstition, well &
good; but is that a likely outcome?

Somehow I fancy that if you could marshall
half a dozen gentlemen who can speak with
some show of authority in these matters and
lead them down our leading intellectual thorough-
fares displaying certain illuminated and
illuminating placards you would do the
world more & quicker service than could be
hoped for from a convention. A convention that
does anything has to be led by a handful of men.
Why not cut out the convention & the few of you
who are competent lay down the law of the
psychology of language [ins]2[/ins] [ins]This is 51% no joke.[/ins]
