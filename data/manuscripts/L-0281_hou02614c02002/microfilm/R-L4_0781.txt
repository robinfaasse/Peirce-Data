I write as one who would like to see
pragmatism take cognizance of the whole
ontological field & not merely the practical
side of it. And for the life of me I can't
see why we should limit our range.
The proposition that the elimination of the
basic ontological dilemma & would amount
to the elimination of all that we mean
by finitude seems a strange on to most
men — and that seems strange to me.
And the proposition that the frank acceptance
of the dilemma as happily insoluble
seems discouraging to many minds.
To me such acceptance means only
that we give our absolute sense (variously
dubbed [del]our[/del] ideal sense, our abstract sense,
our sense of ultimate perfection, of the Ought
