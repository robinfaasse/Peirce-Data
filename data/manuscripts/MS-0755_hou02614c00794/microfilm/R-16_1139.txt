I call these three ways of reasoning Deduction, Induction, and Retroduction. I propose to show how [ins]and why[/ins] each of these tends to lead us toward the truth.

By deduction I mean that kind of reasoning which, if it be sound, and if its premisses are true, renders its conclusion certain. The conclusion may be that a certain future event has a certain probability, because the premisses may render it certain that it will have that probability.
