* This postulate is usually stated thus: "Through any two points a straight line can be drawn." But there are
two objections to this statement, viz: 1st It is not true that any mathematical line "can be drawn", since a mathematical

such a place in space. Now a line is properly a place that has length without breadth or thickness.
