It is I suppose what Bacon called

Induction does not [ins]often[/ins] amount to
much except in cases in which it
supports some theory [del]to[/del] [ins]toward belief in[/ins] which expe-
rience has inclined us.

That kind of reasoning by which
we are more or less inclined to
believe in a theory because it explains
facts that without the theory would
be very surprising is what I call
Retroduction, or reasoning from
consequent to antecedent. To under-
stand the legitimacy of this kind of
reasoning (often and often as it
deceives us) is to understand the
legitimacy, the truth-leading power
of all reasoning.
