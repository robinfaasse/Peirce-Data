In fact, every elimination in any kind of algebra depends upon the fact that for some particular form of term a general value can be substituted; although in ordinary algebra this is masked by the peculiarity that division is there determinative. However, to stick to the matter in hand, in logic elimination can only take place because some particular form of term some second intentional term is predicable.

All men are mortal animals
$\therefore$ All men are mortal because for animals we may substitute beings.
