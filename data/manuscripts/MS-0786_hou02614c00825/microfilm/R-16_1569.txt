[u]Syllogism[/u]

Your "special kind of syllogism"

All A is B
A is existent
[u]$\therefore$ B is existent[/u]

is monstrous. Existence is not a predicate
as Kant showed & Scotus fully understood. The
form is

All A is B
Something is A
[u]$\therefore$ Something is B[/u]

It is a syllogism in [u]Darii[/u] & presents no great
peculiarity.
