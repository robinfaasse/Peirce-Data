§ 9 Deduction of the [del]ratios[/del] [ins]ratios[/ins] of gravity between the different stations.

The logarithmic lengths of the second's pendulum at the different stations which are given in
§ 85 have been derived by adding to the logarithmic ratios of gravity to gravity at Washington
the logarithmic seconds pendulum at this station as absolutely determined. In deriving these
ratios of gravity, I have combined the ratios obtained from "Heavy end down" and "Heavy end up"
as two distinct invariable pendulums, giving to the former 3 times the weight of the latter,
because the swinging was 3 times as long. It has also been necessary to be on the watch for
changes in the pendulums; and for this reason I have always compared successively occupied
stations. The Smithsonian was occupied both at the outset and at the conclusion of the opera-
tions; but both pendulums altered in the interval. At the first occupation only the Old Stand
was used. The following are the logarithms of the squares of the periods, pointed off after
the fifth place of decimals and marked as seconds.
