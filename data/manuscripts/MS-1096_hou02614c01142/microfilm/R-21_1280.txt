§ 13 Of the atmospheric effects.
For the present, it is necessary to resort to a prior calculation for the effect of the air upon
the period of oscillation. Although it is probable that this effect can be calculated somewhat more
closely than can the decrement of the arc, yet it will be useful to compare theory with observation
