In comparing the Smithsonian and Cornell on the one hand, with Ann Arbor and Madison, on the other,
we have the difficulty that the two heads, H_a and H_s, were reversed relatively to the two stands
We are consequently obliged to compare periods on opposite stands, and we cannot expect the results
with the two heads to agree very well, because any error in the relative flexure of the two stands
will act in opposite ways upon the results with H_a and with H_s. I pass over Cornell, owing to the
abnormal conditions at that station, and compare the Smithsonian and Madison.
