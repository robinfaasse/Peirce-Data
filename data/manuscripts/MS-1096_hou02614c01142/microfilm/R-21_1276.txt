The values of nT in the above table have been reduced to 15°C by an empirical correction dependent upon temperature which I call and treat as a correction for erroneous expansion, although I do not suppose the value of the expansion derived from the measures is really so largely in error as this correction would seem to imply. But such a correction has often been found necessary in pendulum observations. This correction has been ascertained from the observed periods at Cornell where the New Stand was in a cold room. I have not, however, thought proper to use the periods of No.3, because this pendulum shows at every station a very abnormal difference in the time of oscillation with heavy end down on the two heads.
