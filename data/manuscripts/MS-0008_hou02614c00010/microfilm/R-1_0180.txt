For it is the peculiarity of a sign that it can exist
in replicas. Look down a printed page. Every [u]the[/u] you
see is the same word, every [u]e[/u] the same letter. This is
not and cannot be true of anything but a sign. Iden-
tify any other things in this way, and you make a
sign of them. The reacting thing is an absolute indivi-
dual, — an individual system, since we know nothing
that has not parts. It cannot exist twice. The quality,
properly speaking, has no identity. One quality is said to
resemble and to differ from another; but this is a character
of them as [del]of[/del] the meanings of icons. In themselves, one
quality has nothing at all to do with another either as
resembling or as differing from it.

In short, qualities, reacting things, and signs are
three categories of which no two have being in the
same sense. We have direct knowledge of all three.
