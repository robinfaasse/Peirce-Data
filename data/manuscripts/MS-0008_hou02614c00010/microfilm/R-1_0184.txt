In order to bring into prominence a third feature of all
signs, we may consider an instance of still another
kind of sign. Goethe's book about colors is a sign; but it is
so neither because it is itself colored, [del]nor because[/del] it stands
in any [del]sort of[/del] reaction with colors, but simply by virtue of
determining in the mind of the reader a sign of the same
object. Indeed, it distinctly signifies what opinion it is
intended to determine. I call a sign which is such simply
by virtue of its determining a sign, a Symbol. A symbol
