For every thought is a sign, every experience is of a reac-
tion between ego and non-ego, and every feeling is of a
quality. This direct knowledge establishes the categories
for us. Our metaphysics which extends them throughout
the universe is inferential; that is, is knowledge through
signs. Hence, there is a difficulty in making out exactly
what a reacting thing and a quality are like apart from
their being, the one the object, the other the meaning, of a
sign.
