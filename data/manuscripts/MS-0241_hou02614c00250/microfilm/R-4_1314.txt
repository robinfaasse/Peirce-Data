[u]A Mathematical Discussion[/u]

[u]Notation[/u]

Let the letters of the alphabet be objects which may differ in some respects and have some [del]qualities[/del] [ins]attributes[/ins] in common.

Let $A_{B} = B_{A}$ be the form for denoting those attributes of $A$ which it has in common with $B$ & conversely.

Let $A^{B}$ be the form for denoting the measure of those attributes of $A$ in which respects it differs from $B$.

Let the difference between $A$ & $B$ be denoted thus $A^{B} - B^{A}$

Differences between different pairs of objects may differ in some respects and agree in others. Let $(A^{B} - B^{A})_{Ac}$ be that difference between $A$ & $B$ in that respect in which $A$ & $C$ do not differ & let $(A^{B} - B^{A})^{Ac}$ be the difference between $A$ & $B$ in that respect in which $A$ & $C$ differ.

A pair of objects may differ in different respects at once & these different respects may have something in common. In this case that they differ in one respect to a certain amount at least will be implied in their differing in another respect. The amount of which $B$ differs from $A$ in the respect in which $D$ differs from $C$ on account of differing from it in the respect in which $F$ differs from $E$ [del]will be denoted thus[/del] [ins]is $[(A^{B} - B^{A})_{Dc}]^{Fe}$[/ins]

Let $n$ be a number to denote a numerical ratio.
