What is that ring which brings "measureless might" and the very lordship over the world? We must see in it the presentation & sense of that world, from which Tannhaeuser longed to be away, [del]not[/del] from whence he was rescued by Elizabeth, the world of sensual enjoyment, the striving for fame and glory, the all-compelling, world-ruling force of gold. The love which he must renounce who desires to forge the round hoop is not [del]simply[/del] [ins]perhaps[/ins] sexual love, — because Alberich had a son Hagen, — but is rather that ideal, unselfish endeavor, that self-immolating love of Elizabeth.
