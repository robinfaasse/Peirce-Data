But he was already a youth when he first [del]displayed[/del] a decided talent for music. At the concerts of the Leipsic Gewandhaus he first became acquainted with Beethoven's music; and it made a strong impression upon his youthful heart. With burning zeal he entered into a close study of Beethoven; and Dorn says, "I doubt whether there has ever been a young composer more intimately acquainted with Beethovens works than Wagner at the age of eighteen."
