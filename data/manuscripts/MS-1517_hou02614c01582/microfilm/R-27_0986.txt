"Genius is the immediate attraction of an individual in the

Many authors require that genius shall be "epoch-making,"

Gerard demands that artistic genius shall "please" and shall
"gratify the taste." "Objects and circumstances [del]seem[/del] [ins]unfit[/ins] to please

"Genius always imports something inventive or creative, which
does not rest in mere sensibility to beauty where it is perceived,
but which can, moreover, produce new beauties, and exhibit
them in such a manner as strongly to impress the minds
of others."
