I cannot possibly say how my pragmaticism is related to other
kinds of pragmatism, because for the last 18 years (and practically
longer) I have been a recluse, remote from libraries, unable to subscribe
to any journals, and very rarely communicating with any philo-
sophist. I always associate with physicists & chemists rather than
with philosophists. Twice a year I go to the meeting of the National
Academy of Sciences, but I rarely get an opportunity to see anybody
not in the Academy. At the meeting last month in Washington, I did
run over to Baltimore to spend the night with Baldwin. If we had been
able to sell our place here, I would have taken my wife to England this
year & Lady Welby most kindly invited us to Duneaves. But I shall
have to give it up. ¶ Synechism is a doctrine I have long held but
have never had an opportunity to expound. The logic of relations sets
everything in logic in a new light and greatly enlarges & metamor-
phizes [del]all[/del] every conception. Corresponding to generality in nonrela-
tive logic is continuity in relative logic, and the development of
the principle of continuity in the light of that logical view & the adoption
of it as the central principle of metaphysics is an indication of what
I mean by Synechism.
