Milford Pa [date]1905 May 23[/date]

My dear Sir:

I have received today your
sheet of [del]on the[/del] definitions of
Pragmatism and Humanism. If I
were to think only of myself I
should like them both. As nearly
as I can understand you, this
quite my own opinion, including
your subordination of pragmatism
to Humanism, which I quite agree
is fundamental. At least, it was by
that route that I came to pragma-
ticism.

I am not sure of understanding
you; for there is one word whose
meaning in your mouth I cannot
guess. It is [u]reality[/u]. I should be
particularly obliged to you if you
would send me a definition of it.

F. C. S. Schiller Esq. [del]Corpus Christi College[/del]
