My dear Sir:

I did not wait for Carus's
opinion but wrote an entirely
different article in which there
was no room for any allusion
to any other variety of pragmatism
than my own. Just as I was
about mailing the new article
today, I received your paper on
the definition of Pragmatism
& Humanism. As I told you, I think
you say pragmatism is seems
to include one, but it does not
seem to me to include James
& some others of the group.
I am prevented from under-
standing part of what you say
