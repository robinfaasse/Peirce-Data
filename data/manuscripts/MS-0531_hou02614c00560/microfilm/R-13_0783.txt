The logic of conjugative terms has been very little investigated,
but the researches of DeMorgan together with some of my own
have thrown much light upon simple relatives. But as all that
has hitherto been written on the subject has had an algebraical
dress, it may perhaps be well to restate some of the more im-
portant results in ordinary language.
