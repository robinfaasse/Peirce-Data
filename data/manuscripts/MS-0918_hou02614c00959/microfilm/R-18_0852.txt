[marginalia]Ms. 918[/marginalia]

[u]"On the Classification of the Human Faculties"[/u]

Who will give us the true one at last?
Here is one which I offer as possessing
some merits.

Man 1 awakens & rouses himself
2 He sees
3 He thinks
4 He desires
5 He does
6 He enjoys & suffers
7 He respects & loves.

I have elsewhere advanced the classi-
fication of the I-impulse the It-impulse
& the thou-impulse, but that is not a
classification according to faculties. That
the present classification includes every-
thing needs no proof, — everything is there
which has ever been inserted into any
other system.
