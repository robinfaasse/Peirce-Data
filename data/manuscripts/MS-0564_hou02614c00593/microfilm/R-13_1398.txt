If $t=5$, $f=2$, [del]but[/del] $x \prec y$ means that $x$
is at least as small as $y$. Rule 1 will then
obviously be true. Rule 2 that either $x \prec y$ or
$y \prec x$, is also true remembering that each
letter must have one or other of the two values
$2$ and $5$. For $x \prec y$ unless $x=5$, $y=2$; but
if $y=2$, $y \prec z$ whether $z=2$ or $z=5$. Rule
3 simply is that $5$ is not so small as $2$.
