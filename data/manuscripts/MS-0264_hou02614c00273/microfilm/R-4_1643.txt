[u]On the Real Singularities of Plane Curves.[/u]

All the curves are supposed real ovals or unicursal self-returning curves.

①. Curves such that there is a straight line not touching. Make that the line at infinity.

1 It is evident there may be any number of nodes and for each number any number of cusps and for each number any [del]even[/del] [ins]even[/ins] number (but no odd number) of inflexions. Then we have to consider how many double tangents there may be.

2[del]nd[/del] If there are no nodes, cusps, nor inflexions, there can be no [del]any odd number of[/del] double tangents.
