[u]Old Arithmetics, historically valuable.[/u]

[u]Gemma Frisius[/u]. Arithmeticae practicae methodus facilis.
With the additions of Jacobus Peletarius.
8[sup]o[/sup] fol. 96. Paris. [date]1549[/date].
Vellum. A very good copy. Very early & beautiful specimen of Italics.
An important book.

[u]Christophorus Clavius[/u]. Epitome arithmeticae practicae.
8[sup]o[/sup] pp. 323 + Index. Rome. [date]1585[/date].
Vellum. Fine copy. Very early ed. Italics.
An important book. Clavius was a great calculator.

[u]Thomas Hylles[/u]. The arte of vulgar arithmeticke.
4[sup]to[/sup] fol. 266 + Index. London. [date]1600[/date]. Black letter.
Full Calf. Fine ed. Very important.

[u]Gio. Battista Zuchetta[/u]. Prima parte della Arimmetica.
folio. Index, body + pp. 412 + Errata. Brescia. [date]1600[/date].
Vellum. Very handsome. Italics. De Morgan had not seen this.

[u]Pietro Antonio Cataldi[/u]. Prima parte della pratica aritmetica.
fol. pp. 148 Bologna [date]1602[/date]
Seconda parte della pratica aritmetica.
fol. pp. 151 Bologna. [date]1606[/date].
Vellum. No doubt 1[sup]st[/sup] Eds. De Morgan knew only the 4[sup]th[/sup] part.
He speaks highly of Cataldi.

[u]Antonio Pace[/u]. Scorta de' mercanti.
4[sup]to[/sup] pp. 216. Verona. [date]1628[/date]. Italics.
½ Vellum. Price £4 marked in it. This author is
not known to De Morgan.
