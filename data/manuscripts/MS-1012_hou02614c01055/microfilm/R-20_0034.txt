Moreover, below the running-plank at four points "S" hung sacks
each containing 40 kilos of wet sand-ballast, each of which
could be completely discharged by [del]drawing on its line[/del] [ins]pulling a single line[/ins].

All the ballast-draw-lines had their ends [del]at[/del] [ins]on[/ins] the left side of the
forward gondola, the station of the aerostatic leader Baron von
Bassus. In the same place were the five lines of the [del]balloon[/del]
escape-valves. The aerostatic management of the airship was
thus made extremely convenient.

The practical aerostatic training of the crew was chiefly for
the engineers, machinists, and mounter (Monteure), who were to
take part in the ascensions. For both Count von Zeppelin and
Mr. E. Wolff were already sufficiently familiar with the business
in former balloon-ascensions.
