[u]Aeronautic Preparations.[/u]

The building of the air-ship having been completed, the most
important and difficult matter that remained was the training
of those who were to be aboard as well as those on the
raft were to manoeuvre the balloon.

Familiarity with ballooning and the [del]custom[/del] [ins]habit[/ins] of
dealing with gas and ballast were all very well and not to
be despised. Yet there were so many new points requiring
attention in the present air-ship that a theoretical, and so far
as might be, a practical working out of every possible contin-
gency was needed. Success, [ins]safety,[/ins] the very [del]security[/del] of those who were
to be aboard demanded it.
