[u]Report of the First Experimental Run of the [del]Air-[/del] Ship of
Count von Zeppelin.[/u]

of Aeronautics is to build air-ships [del]of promise[/del] and to
ascertain their advantages by experiment. The promoter of
the company is Lieutenant-General Count von Zeppelin,
whose elaborated plan, for which the constructive calcula-
tions had already been made, was deemed worthy of
execution in the interest of Aeronautics.
