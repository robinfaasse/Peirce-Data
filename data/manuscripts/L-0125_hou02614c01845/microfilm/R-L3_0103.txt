16 Broad St. City. Satdy. [date]6 June '96[/date]

My Dear Prof. Peirce:

I had a line fr. d'A. on the matter of his hasty call to Boston. I wrote him of yr. being down + yr. coming again on Monday next at 2.30 P.M. + expressed the wish that he [del]could[/del] [ins]wd.[/ins] be there. Shall probly hear fr. him by that time if he does not come.

Hastily but Sincerely Yours

W. Easton
