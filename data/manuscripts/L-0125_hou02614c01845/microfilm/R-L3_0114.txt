[pn]VBBR[/pn] 16 Broad S. Mf.
[date]14 Oct. '98[/date].

My Dear Prof Peirce

Yours of the 2d
is at hand & I am very
sorry to hear of your illness.
I hope most sincerely that
it is not serious & that you
will soon be if not already,
quite restored.

As to Co. P. Stock. I did not
understand your previous
note on the matter as requiring
any answer — at least until
I should have something definite
to say. So far I have not whipped
matters into shape. Am waiting
Mr Armstrong's arrival from
London. Will then see him & if
agreeable can work up the
matter as talked of. With respectful
compliments to Mrs Peirce

Yours truly W. J. Easton.
