16 Broad St.
[date]8 July 96.[/date]

My Dear Count d'Aulby:

Your esteemed favor of the 6th inst. is at hand this morning & I hasten to reply.

The enclosure to Mr Pintsch is forwarded at once. His address — through which all letters will reach him — is c/o Morgan Harjes & Co., Bankers, Paris, France.

I am distressed to learn that the Countess has had any setback on her way to recovery. I sincerely trust it is but temporary & that she has entirely regained the ground lost, before this.

As to Acetylene: I am writing out just what came to my mind on reading your letter.

"<u>Delays are dangerous</u>." That was my first thought. Then I went to see Mr Stewart & to attend a formal meeting of stockholders. After that I had a chat with him & mentioned what you wrote.
