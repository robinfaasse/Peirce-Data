My dear Easton:

I hate to trouble you again; but I should be glad if this matter of the analyses were not postponed [del]till after[/del] Thanksgiving.

I shall for the best of reasons be sitting in my office here night & day till it comes; and if my wife should die, being without food or shelter, my usefulness to the company would be impaired.

Stewart is so governed by that idiot Patton that he seems to think I am of little value.
