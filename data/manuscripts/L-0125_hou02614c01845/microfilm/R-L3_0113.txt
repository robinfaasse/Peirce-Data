MEMORANDUM.

**To** [u]Prof Chas S. Peirce[/u]
[u]Milford, Pa[/u]

**From** E. C. STEDMAN,
16 Broad Street,
New York, [date]26th Feby 1898[/date]

Dear Prof: I want to make a market for our W. P. Stock
but to do it I have got to have the control of Bierstadt &
your stock. At present there is no market nor likely to be for
a couple of years, for it. Are you willing to pool your stock,
for a while? If so I think I see the way to make something
out of it, which I don't suppose you could object to, to getting back
out of it, & with [ins]no[/ins] appreciable risk of losing your stock. I trust
Mrs Peirce & you are well & enjoying yourselves.

Yours truly
E C Stedman
