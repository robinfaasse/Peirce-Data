[date]11. July '96.[/date]

My Dear Prof. Peirce:

Your favor 10th inst. & [del]unusual[/del] [ins]enclosures[/ins]
found me out. I am down at the shop quite
a bit — very little at 40 Wall, & am
& uncertain about being here.

As to our attitude toward the Wither-
Dickerson people on their patent, the fact
is we have given them but little thought &
we have less reason than ever for doing
so as competitors.

I learned thro' Mr. Leonard that you had
been in with your matter to see him.

If you are down Monday P.M. I should
like to see you. I shall prob'y hear by
then from [del]Council[/del] [ins]O'Reilly[/ins]. In haste

Yours sincerely
N. S. Easton

To
Prof C. S. Peirce,
