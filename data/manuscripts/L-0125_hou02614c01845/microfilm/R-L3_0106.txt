16 Broad St
[date]30th June '96[/date].

My Dear Prof. Peirce:

When you were
down last week I understood
you as expecting to be down
today (Tuesday) but as the day
is pretty nearly over—for business—
I assume you will not be down.

If you are down tomorrow
will you kindly call, say about
2 P.M. as I would like to have
a little chat for a few minutes.

[del]C[/del] [ins]C[/ins]ount d'Aulby has not put in
an appearance yet nor any
word from him. Have you heard
from him since you were here?

Yours truly
M. J. Easton
