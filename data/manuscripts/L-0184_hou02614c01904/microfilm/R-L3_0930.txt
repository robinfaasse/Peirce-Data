I think from your reply you must
have misapprehended the spirit of my
letter, or else, which is quite as probable,
I did not express myself as I intended,
which, of course, I regret.

The copies you have retained and
sent me have been all designed,
to advantage, and the letters of since re-
ceived in reply show that they are
very highly appreciated. As it pro-
bably might interest you I enclose
one, as a specimen, from the Rev.
Mr. Gidney, one of, or rather the leading
man of Westford, a gentleman of
culture, and a warm friend of the
Coast Survey. I have sent them generally
to persons of that class, and the acknow-
ledgements have been pretty much in
the same vein.

Yours Truly
A. D. Bache

C. S. Peirce Esq
Coast Survey Office
Washington D.C.
