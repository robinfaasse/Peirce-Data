Rule VIII. [del]Anything true[/del] [ins]any [del]graph asserting something true[/del][/ins] may be added as an unenclosed part [ins]to[/ins] [del]of[/del] any graph.
Proof. Anything true may be written on a blank sheet; for [ins]by Rule I[/ins] [del]that[/del] merely asserts something true.

Rule IX. Any unenclosed part of a graph may be [del]omitted[/del] [ins]erased[/ins].
Proof. For a whole graph may be erased and the sheet left blank, since by Rule I, a blank sheet asserts nothing.
Hence, by Rule II, the graph may be erased when it forms an unenclosed part of another graph.

Rule X. Any unenclosed line of connexion may be broken.
Proof. For this is merely to omit the assertion of the identity of its parts by erasing a portion of it.
