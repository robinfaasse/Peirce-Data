[u]Rules connecting the Ordinal Numbers of the Elements with the
Characters of their Elements.[/u]

[u]Rule 1.[/u] Odd numbers belong to odd-basic, even numbers to even-basic elements.
[u]Rule 2.[/u] The number 0 belongs to an element which is the only one of its period known.
(Number 1 is left for any other of that period that maybe discovered.) Numbers
exceeding 1 but less than 18 belong to elements of periods of eight. All other
numbers belong to elements of long periods. Numbers exceeding 108 are
believed to belong to essentially radioactive elements, which apparently can
be generated.
