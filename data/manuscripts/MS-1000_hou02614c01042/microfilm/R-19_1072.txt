Cartesianism came; and it was largely an application of mathematics to philosophy. The doctrine of probabilities was created, which may be said to be the logic of modern physics.

Bacon undertook to formulate inductive reasoning. The making of the attempt was a meritorious and valuable thing, however ridiculous its failure.

The Port Royal Logic deserves particular mention on account of its development of the doctrine of logical extension and comprehension.

Leibniz made important contributions to logic, such as his principle of sufficient reason, his analysis of clearness & distinctness, etc. Of the Leibnizian logicians Wolf and Lambert are the most important. Ploucquet may be mentioned as a forerunner of Boole. Euler invented logical diagrams.
