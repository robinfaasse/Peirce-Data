AMERICAN ASSOCIATION FOR THE ADVANCEMENT OF SCIENCE.

L. O. HOWARD,
PERMANENT SECRETARY,

WASHINGTON, D. C.

Dear Sir:-

The Council of the American Association for the Advancement of Science wishes to unite in the membership all those who are engaged in scientific work. Will you not let us present your name for election?

It appears to be a privilege and almost a duty for all men of science to give their support to the Association, which most completely represents their common interests. The annual meeting, at which as many as thirty sections and special societies are repre-sented, is a clearing house for the scientific work of the whole country, and at the same time gives science its due weight in the community. The volume of proceedings and the receipt of the weekly journal "Science" are adequate returns for membership.

Trusting that you will consent to fill in the enclosed blank,

Respectfully yours,

[fig]Signature of L. O. Howard[/fig]
Permanent Secretary.
