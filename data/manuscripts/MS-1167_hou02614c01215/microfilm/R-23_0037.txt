COLOGNE WATER.

This water, compounded of the most subtle aromas
of the vegetable kingdom, united with simples of balsamic
propertie, owes its renown no less to its virtues as a
general panacea than to its well-known superiority in all
the qualities of the best products of Cologne. Upon compar-
ing it with the Original Gegenueber, it will be found to
be still whiter, to evaporate more rapidly, to leave the
hands more dry, to present an equal homogeneity of fragrance
not resolving itself into its constituent parts by evaporation
leaving a more exquisite as well as refreshing perfume
free from the grosser ingredients of the spurious imita-
tions. Suitably diluted, it will be found an admirable
substitute for water in sprinkling the streets; but care
should be taken to avoid its use in fire-engines, for
which it is little adapted. Placed in a basin of hot water,
it serves to fumigate apartments without danger of suffo-
cation to the inmates, and it forms the capital and essen-
tial element in the toilette of the beau-monde. It is pro-
tected against contrefaction by its peerless perfection,
which appeals to every olfactory, and permeates the soul
with the odour of truth and genuineness, thus saving the
cost of distinctive labels and enabling the manufacturer
sto apply the money to the refinement of its etherial and
spirituous excellencies. Articles of glass-ware washed and
polished with this water will be rendered exquisitely clean.
Its intrinsic nature, widely remote from everything like
perfumery, is rather a fragrance of woods, meadows, and
green lanes. Its action is corroborant, restorative, and
prophylactic. It is highly recommended by the Faculty and
its moral-mental tone is eminently salutary.
