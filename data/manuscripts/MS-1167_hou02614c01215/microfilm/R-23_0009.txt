ELASTIC. Strike out the sentence defining modulus of elasti-
city. For the definition of elastic curve, substitute:

Elastic curve, the figure assumed by a thin elastic
plate acted upon by a force and a couple. See the figure.
Perfectly elastic, having the property of resisting a given
deformation equally, however that deformation may have been
applied, whether slowly or suddenly, etc. All bodies, however,
have different elasticities at different temperatures,
and if the deformation is so sudden as to change the tem-
perature of the body and so alter its resistance to defor-
mation, this is not considered as making it
imperfectly elastic.
