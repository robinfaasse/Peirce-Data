Easter, according to the Gregorian calendar, is the
Sunday following the 14th day of that moon whose 14th day
next follows the 21st of March. But the moon spoken of is
not the real moon but a fictitious moon placed for conven-
ience in advance of the real moon and called the ecclesi-
astical moon. In the Greek church, which adheres to the
Julian calendar, Easter is usually a week earlier or later
than in the western churches.
