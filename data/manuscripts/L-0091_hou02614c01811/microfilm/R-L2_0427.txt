amount appropriated during the years re-
ferred to. By inadvertence it includes
appropriation for the current year. I am
assured, however, by the Accountant, by the
clerk of the late Superintendent, and by
others, that the inclusion of appropriation
for the current year is substantially
justified by the omission of a good
many other items of expenditure during
the years referred to, which would make
the aggregate as stated. The evidence
showed that for this expenditure only
about 14 stations had been occupied,
and it seemed that the results of
the experiments had been rendered
so nearly unavailable to the Bureau that
we employed the terms "substantially
destroyed" in describing the consequences
of such failure to comply with the
requirements touching the filing of
records, duplicates, and computations.
