[u]Dear Smith[/u]

Please have him first get the centre of mass as nearly coincident with the centre of figure as possible, and then clean & rough polish so that we can calculate better on the weight. Of course I know that in soldering in the counterpoise we spoil the surface; but still we shall be able to make a better calculation.

Then weigh and swing and find $x+y$, the distance of the centre of $M_{2}$ from the centre of $M_{1}$, by the formula

$$x+y = \sqrt{\frac{M_{1}}{M_{2}} \left(\frac{1}{4}(\text{metre})^{2} - y_{1}^{2}\right) + \frac{1}{4}(\text{metre})^{2} - y_{2}^{2}}$$

This will occupy you till Friday, when I shall be over.

Yours faithfully
[u]C. S. Peirce[/u]
