Sir,
Vouchers Nos. 5 and 6, of your July (1885) account
are herewith returned. They must be sworn to before
competent- legal authority, under seal. If no seal is
used, a certificate from the Clerk of a Court of
Record or County Clerk must accompany the vouchers
as evidence of the official character or authority of
the person before whom the affidavits are taken
to administer oaths. The laws of the State in which
the affidavits are made will govern the charge for
fee,- which is no longer restricted to the sum of
25 cents. Whatever sum you pay for this purpose,
however, must not, in this case, be charged upon
the voucher enclosed, but should be rendered in
a separate account, and transmitted to this office
for settlement- when you return the vouchers. A
