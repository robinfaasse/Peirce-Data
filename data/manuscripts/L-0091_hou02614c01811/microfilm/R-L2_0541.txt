U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,

Washington, [date]D. C., Nov. 13th, 1886[/date].

Prof. C. S. Peirce,
Assistant - C. & G. Survey,
36 West Fifteenth St.
New York City.

Dear Sir,

You will of course understand that the forwarding of your vouchers (referred to in your letter of the 12th instant) with circular letter, was not an impertinent attempt to annoy you by this office, but a service gratuitously assumed in your behalf (identical with that assumed in behalf of most of the Assistants &c.) by this office - Colonna especially - in order to expedite the allowance of vouchers as far as possible by the First Auditor, - to controvert the current slander as to the unwillingness of the Coast Survey to conform to Treasury rulings - and to show the First Comptroller that an earnest effort, at least, had been made to comply with the rigidest requirements of the Auditor whom he must by implication criticize when he allows an account
