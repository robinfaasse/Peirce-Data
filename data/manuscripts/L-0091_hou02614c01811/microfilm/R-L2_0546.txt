U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,

Washington, [date]November 23[/date], 1886.

[u]Prof. C. S. Peirce,[/u]
[u]Assistant C. & G. Survey,[/u]
[u]36 West 15th St. N.Y.[/u]

Dear Sir,

In your letter of Nov. 19, you speak of what should be done in case a Pendulum is swung at Hayti. By reference to my letter of Nov. 16 you will see that it refers to the Hawaiian Islands and not the West Indies. If you have any change to make in your recommendations for Hayti which will make them applicable to the Hawaiian Islands, I should be glad to hear from you at your earliest convenience. You omit to state what head should be used. Please supply this omission.

Very respectfully,
F. M. Thorn
Superintendent.
