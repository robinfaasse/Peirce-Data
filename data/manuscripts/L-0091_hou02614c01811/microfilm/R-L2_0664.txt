Sir:

Please state at once the proper address for returning to you by express the papers &c. relating to your report on Pendulum observations.

State also whether the contents of the 20 volumes of records &c. which formed part of the package are duplicated. Can you permit those volumes now here to remain here in the archives without interfering with the progress of your work on the "report" thus leaving something of a record preserved from the chance of destruction or disappearance?
