This lean-to had its sides banked
up with earth and snow, and
the roof was in like manner
covered with snow, so that the
temperature of the lean-to was
always equable, rarely varying
5° daily, and that of the ice house
in which the pendulum was
placed was even more equable.
A thick plank, three inches thick
(as I now remember) was securely
fastened by cement and spikes
to the top of the piers, and in an
aperture of the plank was securely
placed the pendulum in the
manner directed by Professor
Peirce.
