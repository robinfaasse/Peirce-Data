[u]The Tidal Division[/u], which is under the charge of Mr. R.S. Avery, a good old man, but far behind the times, is in great need of reorganization in a scientific sense. An Assistant well suited for the work has been sent to the field to gain the necessary practical experience with a view to his being placed in charge of and thoroughly reorganizing this department.
