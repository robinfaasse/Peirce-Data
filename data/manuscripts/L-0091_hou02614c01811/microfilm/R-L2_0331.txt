P.S. Before calculating
$\bar{M}_2$ it is necessary to add
to $M_1$ the mass of the lacquer

By close questioning, you
can perhaps find what depth
of liquid in what vessel was
used in lacquering the last.
Then find what weight of
copal or whatever the lacquer
is made of was in that
amount of varnish. Then
set some exact weight which
shall be used for this pen-
dulum.

In like manner, in cut-
ting off the piece of brass to
form $M_2$ the weight of the
solder must be allowed for.
C.S.P.
