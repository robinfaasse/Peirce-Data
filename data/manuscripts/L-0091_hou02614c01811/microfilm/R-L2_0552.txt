Dear Sir,

I have read with much interest your protest against the existing order of stupidity in relation especially to the Gautier Pendulums. Whatever may be the attitude of Congress toward the laws of Nature it is evident that in its present temper there is no shadow of probability of its authorizing the procuring of the Gautier pendulums.
