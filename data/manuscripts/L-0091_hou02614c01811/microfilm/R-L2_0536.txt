U. S. COAST AND GEODETIC SURVEY OFFICE,

Washington, [date]6[ins]th[/ins] November 1886[/date].

[u]Prof. Chas. S. Peirce[/u],
Assistant Coast and Geodetic Survey,
[u]New York City[/u].

Dear Sir,

I transmit, herewith, copy of a
letter from Mr. Henry Farquhar, addressed
to the Assistant in Charge of Office, in
relation to the computation of the Time
Observations [del]made[/del] at Fort Concho. He
asks whether you think it advisable
to suspend this Fort Concho work &
proceed to make the arc-reduction
for the Washington work of June
1881.

Yours respectfully
F. M. Thorn
Superintendent.
