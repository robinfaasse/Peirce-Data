Sir:

In compliance with your request under date of [date]April 12[/date] and instructions from the Superintendent, I have made an approximate comparison of the distance between the 17 cm. and 56 cm. marks on the staff of the pendulum balance with the stops of the Saxton dividing machine, and find, neglecting the corrections for temperature, and error of the stops, that the distance on the staff is about 0.15 mm. longer than 39 cm. standard length. The following are the measurements.

[u]Left.[/u] [u]Right[/u] [u]"[/u]
1.47 .93 .54
1.52 .93 .59
.93 .35 .58

.00583 inches = 0.148 mm.

One revolution = .01 inch.
Higher readings at the left indicate greater length in the thing measured.

Yours respectfully,
Edward B. Lefavour.
