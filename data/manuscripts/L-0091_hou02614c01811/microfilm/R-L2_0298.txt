I beg to state in this connection
that Professors at Harvard University, of
no higher scientific standing than
Assistant Peirce, receive $4,000 per annum;
at Yale College $3,500; and at Columbia
College, New York, from $5,500 up to $7,500,
all with an annual vacation of thirteen
weeks. At other colleges smaller compen-
sations are paid, but such colleges are
unable to retain permanently the services
of men of the highest scientific ability.

Yours
(sd) C. P. Patterson
