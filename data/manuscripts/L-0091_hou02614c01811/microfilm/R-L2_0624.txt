U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, [date]June 11th, 1888[/date].

C. S. Peirce
Assistant C. & G. Survey
Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir,

Replying to your letter of the 8th instant, announcing your purpose to push your work to the utmost, I am compelled to remind you that although nearly two years have elapsed since you ostensibly began this work upon the report of your pendulum experiments under "a careful estimation" that it "might require a year to complete it without assistance", this office contains absolutely no result of your employment during the interval since except an incomplete and imperfect report on the Ft.
