Dear Sir,
In reply to your telegram of March 26th, I would say that the work at the Smithsonian appears to be going on well, and there does not seem to be any immediate necessity for instructing you to return to Washington.
Should such necessity occur you will be duly instructed.

Yours respectfully
F. M. Thorn
Superintendent
