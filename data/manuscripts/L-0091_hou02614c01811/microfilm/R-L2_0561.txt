U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, D. C., [date]May 4, 1887[/date]

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Assistant U. S. C. & G. Survey.

Dear Sir:

On a casual visit
to the office of the Assistant in
Charge, this A.M., a discussion
of the problem of property-accounting
in the Coast Survey, between Colonna
and Mr. Ogden led to my reading
of the press copy of Colonna's
letter of the 30th ult. to you.
Upon my asking Colonna if
he had any response to it, he
picked up your reply to him
and handed it to me with
the remark that he had
read only part of it.
