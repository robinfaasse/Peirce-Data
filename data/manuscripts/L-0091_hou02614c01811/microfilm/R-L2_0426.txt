Prof. C. S. Peirce -

Dear Sir:
Replying to your favor of [date]Sept. 6th[/date] in relation to the report of the Commission so far as it refers to you, permit me to say, that no member of said Commission was actuated by any personal feeling - they deemed, and still deem the statements in their report relating to yourself as substantially supported by the evidence before them. Their reference to the value of your pendulum experiments as having been "meagre", was prompted by consideration of their cost, and in that respect, perhaps, the expression would have been more accurate if the Commission had referred [ins]to them[/ins] [del]to[/del] in the words "[del]a[/del] meagre by comparison with their cost"
