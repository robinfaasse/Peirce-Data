U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,

Washington, [date]September 4th, 1882[/date].

[u]Mr. C. S. Peirce, Asst C.&G. Survey[/u]
[u]McGill College Observatory[/u]
[u]Montreal, Canada.[/u]

Dear Sir:-
Your letter of the 31st ultimo [ins]enclosing[/ins] [del]with[/del] voucher for pay $239.13 and your monthly account amounting to 563.02 has been received. These accounts aggregate $802.15 instead of 812.15 as stated in your letter. The receipt you enclosed was for an advance made by me to you Aug 16th and not for the advance you request. I this day paid your sight draft for $200. Hereafter I will decline to pay similar drafts. I have your approved estimate for month of July 1882 amounting to 380.52. Your expenses for same month exceeded that amount by a few dollars. I have no approved estimate authorizing expenditures for month of August and subsequent months. When I receive your approved estimates I will send you a check as requested.

Yours, respectfully,
Wm Morgan
Disbursing Agent.
