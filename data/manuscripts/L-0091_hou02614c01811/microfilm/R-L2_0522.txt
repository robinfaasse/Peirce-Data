U. S. COAST AND GEODETIC SURVEY OFFICE,

Washington, [date]Sept. 29", 1886[/date].

Professor Chas. S. Peirce
U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey,
N.Y. City,

Dear Sir:

Lieut A. W. Greely writes a
very urgent letter asking if there
is any chance for a preliminary
discussion of his Pend. Work so
that he can get it by Dec. next.

If in the press of your own
work you find that you can do nothing
with the work in time I will endeavor
to have something done here, so far
as the observations will go, if you
will forward the Fort Conger and
Smithsonian records with any other bear-
ing on the subject to the office.

Please give me an immediate
reply to this letter.

Yours respectfully
F. M. Thorn
Superintendent
