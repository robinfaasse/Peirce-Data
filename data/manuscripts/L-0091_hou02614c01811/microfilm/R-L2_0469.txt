Computing Division C.& G.Survey

April 17th,1886.

Mr. F.M.Thorn,Sup' t.

U.S.COast and Geodetic Survey.

Dear Sir:

The new method of reducing transit observations (for
time) was handed to me by the late Sup' t. for an expression of
opinion. I advised him by all means to publish the method so that
its supposed comparative merits may be tested by different compu-
ters.

Respecting the matter of weights Assist.Peirce' s state-
ment shows clearly that here his method possesses an advantage over
the old method although then not sufficiently appreciated by me,and
my statement that the full correction must be applied in some shape
was to be understood as he gives it under § 3. In what I said as
to "the selection of stars is independent of any method of reduc-
tion" I must have been misunderstood,I meant to say that after the
observer has selected his best stars they must be the best for any
proper method and the working up of the observations must lead to
the same result. It may be that after getting familiar with his
method it may be found not to involve more labor than the familiar
method.

Yours respectfully

(SIGNED) Chas.A.Schott

Assistant.

S.B.H.
