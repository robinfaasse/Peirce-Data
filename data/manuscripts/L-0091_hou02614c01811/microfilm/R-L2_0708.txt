C. S. Peirce

Dear Sir,

We have a call from Cornell University for the result of your determination of gravity at that place several years ago.

Please inform me whether or not the computation of that work has been completed, and if so please send me the result that I may transmit it as requested.

Respectfully yours
T. C. Mendenhall
Superintendent
