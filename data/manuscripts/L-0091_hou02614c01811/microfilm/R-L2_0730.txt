United States Coast and Geodetic Survey,
Washington, D.C., [date]February 27, 1891[/date].

C I R C U L A R

Attention of all field officers is called to the follow-
ing regulations and they are directed to comply therewith and
carefully furnish the data called for:

Henceforth all field records must be so arranged that
they can readily be bound at the Office by "States" and "Ter-
ritories", and to this end the observations made in different
states and the computations based thereon must not be entered
in the same field record book or cahier of computations, unless
in special cases when it is absolutely necessary to modify
this regulation, in which event the Chief of Party shall decide
upon the State under which to classify the work and so note
in the preface placed in the record book or on the cahier.

Records and computations of different classes of work
should be kept separately.

Fill in on all blank forms names of stations, counties,
dates, observers &c., which may aid in the identification of
the work to a person not familiar with it.

Special care should be taken to fill in the [u]title page[/u]
on the [u]inside[/u] of the book, as that on the cover is lost when
the records are rebound. Always leave binding margins on
the inside edge of blanks, sheets or books.
