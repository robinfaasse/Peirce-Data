U. S. COAST AND GEODETIC SURVEY,

Washington, D. C., [date]Sep. 3[/date], 189[del]0[/del][ins]1[/ins].

Mr. C. S. Peirce
Assistant, C. & G. Survey,
Milford, Pa.

Sir:

Enclosed please find my check, No. 242,139, drawn this day to your order on the [del]Treasurer[/del] U. S. Washington, D. C. for $132.70, in payment of your account—Balance due—for services as Assistant, C. & G. Survey, for month of August, 1891.

Owing to delay in receiving funds from the Treasury, I am compelled to transmit a "Washington check".

Have you received the duplicate of the lost check mailed you on Aug. 5, 1891? If so, please acknowledge.

Respectfully yours,

John W. Parsons
Disbursing Agent.
