U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, [date]5[ins]th[/ins] November, 1886[/date].

[u]B. A. Colonna Esq., Asst. in Charge.[/u]

Dear Sir,

After receipt of your order assigning me to work on Pendulum observations, I proceeded (under advice of Mr. Schott) to reduction of transit-work for time at Fort Conger. The statement with which I furnished you a few days since, showed the then condition of that work and how much remained to be done. Since making it I have re-read the time-signals on such of the Chronograph-sheets as were brought from the Fort, have made the changes in computation that were necessitated by the discovery of errors in reading (these errors amounted to a second in several cases) and filled up the gap of uncalculated observations - all to two sets.
