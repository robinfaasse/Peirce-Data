Stevens Institute of Technology
Hoboken, N. Jersey
[date]May 2[ins]d[/ins], 1879[/date]

x x x

I take the liberty of stating to you my opinion of the scientific work which Mr. Peirce has been carrying on in our Institute of Technology and in the City of New York during the past two years.

Having given my life to work of a similar character to that which has engaged Mr. Peirce and having been a daily witness of his operations in our own building at Hoboken, my opinion must carry some weight with it.

I think that the results he has even already reached are of the highest importance to the advancement of science and to the interests of the U. S. Coast Survey.

Mr. Peirce's methods are original, and of an accuracy and refinement which are unsurpassed.
