Dictated.

U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, D. C., April 18, 1889.

C. S. Peirce,
25 Lafayette Square,
Washington, D. C.

Sir:
Your note, inquiring for information [ins]and stationery[/ins] from this Office through one of its messengers, is duly received. I have to inform you that we do not do business through such channels.

Yours respectfully,
B. A. Colonna
Assistant in Charge of Office.
