Property returns mentioned under the head of the Mis-
cellaneous Division:

The regulations are all right, but need enforcing.

There is one criticism to make, that necessarily a very
large part of the work of the office is performed for pay of one-
half, or, in some cases, one-quarter of the normal force, and
it is only by the utmost exertion on the part of the execution
of the office that the work is kept up. I mention particularly
the case of the [del]Assistant[/del] [ins]First[/ins] Assistant, who is paid $1500 in-
stead of $3000, the pay of the position. There are other cases,
and there are some cases where the pay is higher, more
particularly in the case of the [del]Assistant[/del] [ins]First[/ins] Assistant in the
[del]Computing[/del] [ins]Computing[/ins] Division, who is paid $2200.

The work, as I have said, is performed, but it is
performed by keeping the office in tension, which is more
than can be expected for the long run, in all cases except on the
part of those who are devoted and enthusiastic, as, I am glad
to say, are most of the assistants in the Coast Survey Building.

According to the present arrangement of the normal force,
there are some who have done little and received much for their
services, while there are others who have done much and received
little.
