Dear Sir:
I have examined Asst. C. S. Peirce's deduction of the lengths of Decimeter Scales Nos. 3 & 4.
Accepting his figures of the results of the comparisons as correct I deduce a different value for the length of No. 4. To assure myself of the correctness of my interpretation of his figures, I request that you will transmit this memorandum to him, inviting his criticism & explanatory comment.
I first quote Asst. Peirce's deduction, starting with the value of Scale No. 3.
"
[u]$\therefore$ 10 x DM Scale No. 3 at 15°C - Meter = +227.0$^\mu$[/u]
[u]Coeff. of expansion per degree C = 5.87[/u]

[u]10 x DM Scale No. 4 - 10 DM Scale No 3 = - 65.5[/u]
$\therefore$ [u]10 x DM Scale No. 4 - Meter = +161.5[/u]
[u]Coeff. of expansion per degree C = 7.27[/u]"
