U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, [date]Feb 14th, 1888[/date].

Mr Charles S Peirce
Asst U.S. Coast & Geodetic Survey
Milford Pa

Dear Sir
Can you tell me anything about the brass meter with steel plugs (mouches d'acier) delivered to the International Bureau in Nov. 1880 and referred to in the Proces Verbaux of 1880 as belonging to the Coast & Geodetic Survey and as being [ins]mm[/ins] 0.211 short at 17.8?
Please furnish me with a copy of Prof Foersters communications to you, relative to Brass meter No 49 or the German meters with which it was compared, subsequent to his letter of [date]Nov 15th 1880[/date]. We want to get at the value of No 49.
I would trouble you further to inform me whether the pieces of steel cut from the state meters and sent to you to New York just prior to your sailing for Europe in [date]1880[/date] reached you in time.
