OFFICE OF THE SUPERINTENDENT
U. S. COAST AND GEODETIC SURVEY,
WASHINGTON, D. C.

[date]Dec, 29/91[/date]

Dear Professor Peirce,

I was absent in New York during the early part of the week, leaving here on Sunday last. When I arrived home I found your letter of the 18th inst.

There are many reasons why it would not be practicable for me to keep you on the list, by giving you furlough without pay, one of the principal of which is that it would be unjust to others in the Corps, who might, if a vacancy existed, receive merited promotions.
