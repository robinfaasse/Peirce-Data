Dictated.

U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, D.C., [date]October 23, 1888[/date].

Prof. Chas. S. Peirce,
Assistant U.S.C. & G.S.,
Milford, Pennsylvania:

Dear Sir:

If you have quite finished with the pendulum observations which you made at Steven's Institute with the Cater pendulums just before returning them to England, please forward them by express to the Office together with any computations which you may have relating to the matter.

The typewriter in your possession which was purchased for you a year or two ago seems to be in little demand for Coast Survey work as up to this time we have received nothing, that I remember, which has been written upon it. If you can spare this typewriter I would thank you to send it to the Office which will save us the purchase of one.

Yours Respectfully,
[fig]signature of B.A. Colonna[/fig]
Assistant in Charge of Office,
for Superintendent, and in his absence.
