House of Representatives U. S.,

Washington, D. C., [date]Dec. 18, 1891.[/date]

My dear Charles,

As I wrote you, I could not get out of the
house to go to the Caost Survey but wrote at once to Pro-
fessor Mendenhall asking that your request might be agreed to.
I enclose herewith a copy of Professor Mendenhall's reply
and regret extremely that I should not have been able to
return a more favorable answer. As soon as I can leave the
house I will see him and find out if anything further is
possible.

Yours sincerely,

H. C. Lodge
