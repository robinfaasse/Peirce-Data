Dear Sir,
The Hawaiian Government contemplates the determination this winter of a number of Astronomical Latitudes, for the purpose of ascertaining the deflections of the plumb line. It has been thought that the occasion will be a propitious one for determinations of Gravity by means of the Pendulum, inasmuch as observations for gravity and latitude could go on simultaneously with comparatively a small increase of expense.
