Dear Sir,

Your attention is called to the enclosed copy of letter of Mr. Wm. Biggs of Ann Arbor, Mich. in reference to his account for labor and material furnished you in August and September of last year.

From the vouchers of Mr. Biggs of which copies are herewith enclosed, it appears that he receipted to you for a total of $127.01. He claims, however, that he received only $118.76, leaving a balance of $8.25 still due him.
