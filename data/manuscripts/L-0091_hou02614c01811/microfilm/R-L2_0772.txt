Dear Mr. Peirce:

My position is now that of Editor of Publications, but I regret to say that my province would only lie in the domain of arrangement after the matter had already been submitted. I did not remember that you had written two memoirs which have not yet been published. These must have been submitted during Mendenhall's administration, if they were sent in at all. Looking over your letter again, I see that you say they "were actually written", but you do not say whether they were submitted or not. In any case the Superintendent would decide in regard to their being published in the Annual Reports, and I would be glad to do anything in my power to help the matter along, providing such a decision was made.

Very respectfully,

[sig]EDPreston[/sig]
Assistant
