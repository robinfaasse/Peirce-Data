Sir

I am sorry I had no
opportunity of seeing you after your
return from Europe. I endeavored to
meet you at the office of Messrs Stevens
in Henrietta St, but missed you by
twenty minutes.

As you are doubtless aware that an
application has been made to us
[del]either[/del] by Mr Simon Newcomb,
Secretary of the Commission for obtaining
instruments for the Transit of Venus,
for prices of astronomical clocks,
asking as also whether we could make
one [ins]or[/ins] five or ten of these clocks.

You will probably have seen our
reply, written by myself, to that
application.
