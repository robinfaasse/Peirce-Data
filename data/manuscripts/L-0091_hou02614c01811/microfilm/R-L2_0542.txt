Equally of course it would not be prudent to
risk the nonpayment of vouchers for your travel
from New York to Ann Arbor, Michigan, to obtain
receipt on Express Co. bill head. Make an earnest
effort by letter to obtain it, and copies of your re-
quest and the Express Agent's reply will probably
suffice. It should be done at once. I will append
a copy of your Instructions of [date]June 30th 1885[/date], to the
sub-voucher 9 - foreman's travel - as requested.

The propriety of sending to the Auditor your
elaborate explanation - the completeness of which may
be admitted - I must consider at leisure, and per-
haps will wait until I hear from you again, after
you have reflected somewhat upon the prudential
considerations involved in the present employment
of even well deserved satire.

Yours respectfully
[ins]F. M. Thorn[/ins]
Superintendent.
