U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, [u]D. C.[/u], [date]Aug. 10[/date], 1886.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Assistant C. and G. Survey,
109 East 15th. Street,
N. Y. City.

Sir:
Yours of 7th. instant just received. Accounts for July should be executed and rendered in precisely the same manner as for any other month, and as usual.

No Estimate for expenditures during July, has been received from you, and I am informed by the Assistant in Charge of Office, that no provisions for such expenditures were contemplated.

Very respectfully,
Geo. A. Bartlett
Acting D. G.
