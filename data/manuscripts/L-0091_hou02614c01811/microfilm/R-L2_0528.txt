U. S. COAST AND GEODETIC SURVEY OFFICE,

Washington, [date]Oct. 26[ins]th[/ins], 1886[/date].

C. S. Peirce
Assistant C. & G. Survey
36 West 15[ins]th[/ins] St.
New York

Dear Sir,

In reply to your letter of the 19[ins]th[/ins] instant, I have to say that it has been determined that a preliminary reduction shall be made of Lieut. Greely's Arctic work, which will enable us to furnish the Signal Service with some evidence of our willingness to perform our part of an agreement entered into some years since, and at the same time to advance our own work of pendulum research.

The desirability of preserving the unity of the work is admitted - and the necessity of getting something done to relieve ourselves of censure in this much delayed matter is asserted.
