This is to be a continuation of the informal vote taken
here, will not be binding on any one, and is communicated to me in
confidence. The votes will be tabulated and the result announced.
It is hoped, should this method develop two or three prominent can-
didates, that further voting will result in a majority for some
one whom all can be asked to support.

Mr. Thorn has informed a committee of our number that his
resignation will be placed in the hands of the President early in
March, the action we desire to take is, therefore, not premature.

Respectfully,
[u]Henry L. Marindin[/u]
Secretary.
