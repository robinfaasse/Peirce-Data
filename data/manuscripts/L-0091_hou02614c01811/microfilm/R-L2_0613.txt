U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey,
James Port Long Island,
October 20, 1887.

Mr B.A. Colonna,
Assistant in Charge of Office
and Topography .
Washington, D C

Dear Sir:

Yours of 18, duly received. On April 28,1886, at your
verbal request as I intended to leave the City for field duty, to
deliver a Barometer to Assistant C.S.Peirce in New York. On the
morning of the 29, the Barometer with address was delivered to me
by Assistant Braid at 9 A.M. In the 11 A.M.train of the same day
I left for New York and on my arrival delivered it in person to
Assistant Peirce, in the presence of his wife. I carefully carried
it from the office to New York in my hands and knew nothing of its
condition when received from Assistant Braid.

Very respectfully,
(Signed) C.T. Iardella,

P.L.
