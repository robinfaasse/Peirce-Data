U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,

Washington, D. C., June 16, 1886.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Assistant C. and G. Survey,
72 Fifth Avenue,
New York City.

Sir:

Your Party Account for May, (in part), amounting to $6.00, has been received and placed to your credit.

Your February Account ($27) is returned for explanation (to accompany the Account), as to the delay in mailing the same for settlement, as required by Paragraph 53 of the Regulations, — to the end that the Superintendent may procure the approval of the Secretary of the Treasury thereto.

Your regular May Accounts are returned for correction of informalities as follows: —

Voucher 1 — Date in receipt represents an advance payment.

Voucher 2 — Dates of employment (to and from) required.
