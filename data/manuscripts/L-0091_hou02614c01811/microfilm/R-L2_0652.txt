U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,

Washington, [date]Sept. 1[/date], 1888.

[u]Mr. Chas. S. Peirce[/u]
[u]Asst. C. & G. Survey[/u]
[u]Milford, Pa.[/u]

Sir.

The articles asked for in your Stationery
requisition of 31st August are forwarded
to your address as above by registered
mail this day, in two (2) packages. You
did not specify the number of sheets
of cross-section paper that you required.
I have sent you one dozen sheets. If
that is insufficient you can order more.
You asked for 12 sheets blotting paper, but
was able to send only 2 sheets at this time.

Respectfully yours
M. W. Wines
Genl Office Asst.
for Assistant in Charge.
