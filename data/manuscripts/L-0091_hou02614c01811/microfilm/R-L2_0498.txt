U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, Aug. 4th, 1886.

Sir,

Please submit as soon as practicable a report of work done by you during the fiscal year ending June 30, 1886, as required by Section 28 of the Regulations.

These reports are needed in the compilation of the Annual Report of the Superintendent.

Where special reports have already been submitted of work done during portions of the year, it is only necessary that the annual report should mention the work done in a general way, giving dates, and with a reference to the detailed report made in each instance.

Yours respectfully,
F. M. Thorn
Superintendent.

C. S. Peirce
Assistant C. & G. Survey
72. Fifth Ave.,
New York
