U. S. COAST AND GEODETIC SURVEY OFFICE,

WASHINGTON, D.C., [date]February 18, 1889.[/date]

At an informal meeting of the officers of the normal
force of the Survey, held at Mr. Ogden's house on the 16th Febru-
ary, where nineteen officers were present, the following resolu-
tions were passed; Mr. H. L. Whiting in the chair:

<u>RESOLVED.</u> That it is desirable that the officers
of the Coast and Geodetic Survey should have a candidate of their
own to fill the vacancy which is likely to occur in the position
of Superintendent. Also, "That an informal vote be taken to
express the preference of each officer as to his candidate." The
ballot resulted in bringing out the following names: Prof. H.
Mitchell, Prof. G. Davidson, Mr. E. A. Colonna, Mr. Herbert Ogden,
Mr. R. M. Bache, Gen. W. F. Smith, Prof. S. Newcomb.

After some discussion which elicited the unanimous opin-
ion that it is most desirable we should concentrate on one candi-
date the following resolution was passed:

<u>RESOLVED.</u> That the Secretary be directed to commu-
nicate with the absent officers to obtain their choice of a candi-
date." It is therefore in pursuance of the resolutions that I
have to request you to send me [ins]by return Mail[/ins] your prefer-
ence for a candidate for Superintendent of the Survey.
