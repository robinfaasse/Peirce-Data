U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, [date]May 15-, 1890[/date]

Mr C. S. Peirce
U. S. C. & G. Survey
109 E 15. St. New York City

Sir

In your letter of May 7th
you "beg [ins]to be[/ins] excused for a few weeks
longer," until you reach your farm
to make out your inventories.
We will grant your request, but hope
that you will not interpret this to
mean an indefinite time, as it
is important that you should give
this matter your attention, without
much more delay.

Yours Respectfully
B. A. Colonna
Asst. in chg of Office
