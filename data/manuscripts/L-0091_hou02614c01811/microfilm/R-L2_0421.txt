This statement of expenditures for
Gravity Experiments by the party of Assistant
C. S. Peirce, taken from the books of the
Disbursing Agent's office, excludes Assistant
Peirce's salary and the salaries of several
Coast and Geodetic Survey officers who
have at times been attached to his party,
namely E. D. Preston (for a portion of his
service), F. H. Parsons, Carlisle Terry,
W. B. Fairfield and H. Farquhar (during a
portion of the past year). It probably
does not include the cost of some of the
instruments purchased for the use of the
Gravitation party and a portion of the
expenses for freight and expressage on
instruments and equipage sent to and
from the office.

* Within some $200 it does.
