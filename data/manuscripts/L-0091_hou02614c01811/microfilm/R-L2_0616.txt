U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey,

Washington, D.C. [date]January 13, 1888[/date].

Mr F. M. Thorn,

Superintendent C & G Survey,

Washington, D C

Dear Sir:

A syphon barometer and a case of thermometers belonging to the Kater pendulum apparatus were lately received at the Coast and Geodetic Survey Office from Stevens Institute Hoboken N.J. I think they should be returned to England with some explanation at an early date.

The thermometer case has places for six thermometers and when delivered to me in 1882, returned to the C. & G. S. Office in 1884, turned over to Assistant Peirce in 1886 and finally again returned to the Office in December 1887, it contained the following viz:

One broken thermometer,

One metal scale of broken thermometer

One thermometer No 3 in good order

One thermometer No 4 in good order

The other two places in the case are for thermometers Nos. 714 & 715. These two thermometers were attached to the dummy pendulum when received by me in 1882 and were so transported over 30000 miles and were so sent from this Office to Assistant Peirce in good order
