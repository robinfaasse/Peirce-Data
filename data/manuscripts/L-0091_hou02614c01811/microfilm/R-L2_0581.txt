Dear Sir: Have just received your telegram of this date saying that you "did not promise the copy for tomorrow." Your letter of the 30th (which I have sent to Gen. Greely for perusal and return), I understood to suggest the sending of the matter "in two or three days", which would expire tomorrow.

However — the main thing is to get it along soon enough to satisfy the exigency in Gen. Greely's case, which appears to have reached the last extremity of pressure. If you will name the day of its transmission — or otherwise — I will advise Gen. Greely of the corrected time.
