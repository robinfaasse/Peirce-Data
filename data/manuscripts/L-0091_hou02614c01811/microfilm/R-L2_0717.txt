U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, [date]Oct. 6[ins]th[/ins], 1890[/date].

C. S. Peirce
Assistant C. & G. Survey
Milford,
[u]Pa[/u].

Dear Sir,
Your letter of the 2[ins]d[/ins] instant referring to Pendulum operations has been received and read with interest.
I was not aware that you had already formulated a scheme for rapid gravitation work, but I am very glad that such is the case. You will please send me the details of this plan at your earliest convenience, that it may receive consideration at once.
It is not desired to sacrifice accuracy, but to get rid of unnecessary refinement, so that the work may be prosecuted with greater rapidity.

Respectfully yours
T. C. Mendenhall,
[u]Superintendent[/u]
