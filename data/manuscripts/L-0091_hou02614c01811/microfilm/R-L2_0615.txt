U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, [date]October 22, 1887[/date].

Mr B A Colonna,
Assistant in Charge of Office,
Washington, D. C.

Dear Sir:

The mercurial barometer belonging with the Kater Pendulum apparatus was examined by me prior to its delivery to Assistant Iardella and was found intact. The examination was made April 28, 1886 as Mr Iardella was to start that day, but he concluded to postpone his departure until the morning of the 29, and asked me to retain charge of it over night. It was locked up in my room and delivered to him early the next morning (the 29) No one had access to the room meanwhile as I was then in the habit of carrying the key with me. I can therefore state positively that the barometer was in good order when it left my hands.

Yours respectfully,
(Signed) Andrew Braid,
Assistant C. and G. Survey.
