U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,

Washington, [date]30 July, 1885[/date].

I was yesterday directed by Mr.
Thorn, the present Supt. of this Survey,
to prepare statement of facts within
my personal knowledge bearing on
the expenditures incurred for the
Survey by Assist. Chas. S. Peirce,
& covering the following points:

1 "A number of trips Mr. Peirce
has made to Europe; that of
1875 & arrangement for traveling
expenses of Assistant,"

2 "Proportional part of the
year devoted to active operations
with pendulums."

3 — "Degree of attention given
by Mr. Peirce to the work while
in operation, & proportion of
work left to subordinates (until
1880, when Mr. Farquhar ceased
to assist Mr. Peirce in this
field)"
