Washington,D.C.,[date]July 30th,1885[/date].

F. M.Thorn,

Superintendent U.S.Coast and Geodetic Survey,

Washington, D.C.

Sir:

In compliance with your instructions to me,dated 27th inst.,

I have the honor to submit this statement,made officially, and sworn

to.

I do not know that I could employ a more suitable introduc-

tion to a history,deliberation,and an earnest desire to do my

whole duty to the public service,to my fellow officers and associ-

ates, and to myself,demands at my hands,than I find written for me

in the preface of the Wars of the Jews,as written by Josephus; and

I beg for it your careful consideration:
