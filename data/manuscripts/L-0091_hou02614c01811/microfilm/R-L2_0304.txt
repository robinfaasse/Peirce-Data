[u]Extract[/u] ([u]Copy[/u]) New York
Columbia College
[date]May 11th, 1879[/date]

C. P. Patterson Esq.
Supt. U. S. Coast Survey,
Sir,
For the last two years I have seen much of Mr. Chas. S. Peirce and have had an excellent opportunity for informing myself about the work which he is now executing under your directions. Of the importance of the work it is not necessary for me to speak to one who is so familiar with all the facts as yourself, and who knows so well the very high estimation in which Mr. Peirce's contributions are held by the scientific men of this country and of Europe. It appears to me that it would be difficult to find another scientist having similar qualifications with Mr. Peirce either in the special education required, or in natural ability.
