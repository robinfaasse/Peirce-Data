I have only to add to Mr.
Fairfield's statement, that no
difference between the two ends
of Pendulum No 1, with respect to
the knife-holding apparatus, is
discoverable. Also that Dr. Clark
has just weighed Pendulum No 2
at my request. This Pendulum
has scales attached to the knife-
edge frames, at end not marked
with name. The weight is now
10635[ins]g[/ins].22, with a plus correction
of a few centigrammes on account
of the higher temperature of the
pendulum. The weighing was
made to see if there could have
been an interchange of figures
between the two pendulums — though
that is out of the question, the result
may be of some use.
Very respectfully
