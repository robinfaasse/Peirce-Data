Voucher 1 - ($19.75) is suspended and returned for Express and Freight receipts to support items of such expenditure charged therein, as this has become a requirement of the Accounting Officers of the Treasury. This Voucher will be re-tendered upon Supplemental Abstract for May.

The Account of $6.60, settled by letter of June 16th, is a [u]March Account[/u].

Your Accounts are settled as promptly as the strained situation of affairs will permit.
