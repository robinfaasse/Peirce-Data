U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,

Washington, January 19, 1888.

Mr B. A. Colonna,

Assistant in Charge of Office,

Washington, D. C.

Dear Sir:

As regards the condition and packing of the Kater Pendu-
lum apparatus when sent to Prof. C. S. Peirce in April 1886, I have
only to say that just prior to the shipment the boxes were opened
and the contents examined (and without unpacking) by Assistant Ed-
win Smith and myself. Everything was found to be in good order
and well packed. On no occasion during my connection with the
Instrument Division were these instruments unpacked and on no other
than the one above referred to, were the boxes (so far as I know)
ever opened.

The Adie Barometer was not packed with the other appara-
tus, but simply in its own case and was carried to New York by
Assistant Iardella.

Yours respectfully,

(Signed) Andrew Braid,

Assistant C. & G. Survey.

H. L.
