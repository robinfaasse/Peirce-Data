Dear Sir

The work at this station is now nearly complete.

The chronograph has not behaved well. It shows signs of wearing out. Had we had time to tinker over it, it would not have got so bad; but that is one of the serious objections to working 24 hours per diem, that you have no time or energy for odds & ends of work.
