Sir:

Enclosed herewith please find my check No. 290393 on Assistant Treasurer U. S., N. Y., for $427.35, for your Party Account for April, as audited in this office, and the balance due you on Salary for same month.

The sum of 25¢ for car-tickets, has been disallowed in Voucher 7, April Account.

Voucher 10 - April Account - $7.50 - is suspended and returned with the request that the same be rendered upon separate Abstracts in your name, as the expenditure being for "Books" or "Publications," is properly chargeable to "General Expenses" of the Office, which appropriation specifically provides for such purchases. This Account when properly executed should be rendered through the Assistant in Charge of Office.
