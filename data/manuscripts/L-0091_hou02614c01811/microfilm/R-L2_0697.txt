[u]Circular.[/u]

U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, D. C., December 2. 1889.

To Field Officers
and all Employes of the
Coast and Geodetic Survey.

Mr. John W. Parsons having been appointed Disbursing Agent
for the U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey, and having qualified as
such, he will, from and after this date, assume the duties hereto-
fore devolving upon Mr. Geo. A. Bartlett, Disbursing Clerk of the
Treasury Department. All vouchers and accounts for expenditures
incurred by date subsequent to and including December 1, 1889,
will be rendered in his name, and all communications relating
thereto must be addressed to him as Disbursing Agent. Blank
forms should be corrected to correspond with the change in title.
Accounts to Nov. 30, will be settled by Mr. Bartlett.

T. C. Mendenhall,
Superintendent.
