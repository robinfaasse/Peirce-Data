U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, [date]April 3rd., 1890[/date]

C I R C U L A R .
- - - - - - - - -

To secure uniformity in the form of the field records you are
instructed to make all records of observations consecutive and
continuous in the order of time in which the observations are
made.
All records must be indelible to secure permanency. If it is
found impracticable to make the originals in ink they can first
be written in pencil but the duplicate must be made from this
pencil record and the two must be compared before inking the
original, and after the original is inked it must, for a second
time be compared with the duplicate, Copying ink must not be
used.

T. C. Mendenhall
Superintendent.
