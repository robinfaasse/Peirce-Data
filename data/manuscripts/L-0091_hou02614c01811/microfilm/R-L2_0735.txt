U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey,
Office of the Disbursing Agent,
Washington, D. C., June 15", 1891.

Mr. C. S. Peirce
Assistant, C. & G. Survey,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:-

Yours of the 8" instant, with enclosure of Bond of Indemnity for lost check, was duly received. My absence from the Office has prevented an earlier acknowledgment.

I note that, in the execution of the Bond, your signature has but [u]one[/u] witness. You will observe that [u]two[/u] are required. You will also note that the post-office address and residence of each witness should be given. The clause in the body of the Bond, giving the date of execution, should also be filled out.

In addition to the foregoing, I beg to call your attention to the requirement of an affidavit to accompany the Bond. I have marked the paragraph, referring thereto, on the first page of the form.
