U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,

Washington, D. C., Mch 29, 1887.

Prof. C. S. Peirce,
Assistant - U. S. C. & G. Survey
* 36 West 15th St.
New York City.

Sir:

You will see by the enclosed copy of letter from General Greely, which is but a repetition of previous urgent appeals, how very embarrassing it is to get from you neither your comments on the Arctic observations nor an intimation of when we may expect them. If you do not intend or have not intended to give the matter consideration - as I am loth to believe - you ought long since to have said so. If you do intend to give it consideration it would have saved us here some embarrassment in our relations with General Greely to have been able to suggest when he might expect us to send it.
