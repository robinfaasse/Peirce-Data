Sir:
As requested in your letter of April 9th, leave is granted you to attend the meeting of the National Academy of Sciences in Washington beginning April 16th, and you are authorized to present to the Academy the results of your work with the invariable reversible pendulum: your absence from your regular office work not to exceed thirty days, the amount of annual leave now due you. Please report the dates of such absence from duty.
