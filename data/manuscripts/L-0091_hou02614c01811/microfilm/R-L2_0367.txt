<u>The Drawing Division.</u> According to paragraph 31, page 30, of the Regulations:

II.- Of the Office and Communications.

31. "The assistant, whose duties are prescribed by Article 10 of the plan of Reorganization, shall be known as the assistant in charge of office and topography, and the force placed under his direction shall be known as the party of the office and topography."

This officer has too many other duties to perform to attend to the minutiae of the Drawing Division. Mr W. T. Bright, who stands in the relation of chief clerk to the division, although capable, is not at all times in condition for work on account of strong drink, and his presence will be an ingredient to a thorough reorganization. One of the draughtsmen, Mr. T. J. O'Leary, is likewise inclined to drink, which I consider detrimental to good order and the best interests of the public service.

The reorganization of this division needs to be thorough, and to do, it must be done under the charge of one who has neither past affiliations nor past associations with its personnel and who is thoroughly acquainted with the field-work of the Survey. There are one or two assistants capable and available for this duty.
