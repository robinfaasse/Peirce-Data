U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, D.C., November 14, 1888.

Mr. Chas. S. Peirce,
Assistant U.S.C. & G. Survey,
Milford, Pennsylvania:

Dear Sir;

The accounts of Mr. W. B. Morgan, late Disbursing Agent U.S.C. & G. Survey, could have been closed some months ago, but I have not been willing to accept on behalf of my associates the official statements so far made, without further effort in our, as I believe, just cause. I have reduced the aggregate of the amounts disallowed over one half and am very hopeful of regaining the whole. In order to know just how to represent your case, will you kindly inform me what amounts are now standing withheld against you for disallowances in accounts rendered up to July 23rd, 1885; which closes Mr. Morgan's term of office. This inquiry does not apply to any disallowances in accounts rendered after July 23, 1885, but it does allude to any stoppage of your pay or other allowance, made at any time to cover disallowance in Mr. Morgan's accounts rendered prior to July 23, 1885.
