Dear Sir:

There has been rec'd at this office an old Caligraph but no transmitting letter comes with it. I presume that you send it in response to my letter of a few days since, asking for the Hammond that was purchased for you and which is needed here if not in use by you. As soon as I get a transmitting letter from you I will credit you with the Caligraph but I do not mistake it for the Hammond that was purchased by the Office [del]and[/del] [ins]and[/ins] delivered to you.

Yours respectfully
B. A. Colonna,
