Treasury Department

Washington D.C. [date]Aug 13, 1879[/date]

Capt. C. P. Patterson
Supt. of U.S. Coast & Geodetic Survey
Washington D.C.

Sir,

As recommended in your letter of the 8th ultimo, the compensation of A. M. Harrison Assistant Coast & Geodetic Survey is hereby reduced from two thousand one hundred and eighty dollars to twelve hundred dollars per annum, to take effect from the first instant.

I have considered the recommendation for the increase of the salary of Chas. S. Peirce, Assistant, from $2,870 to $3,450 per annum, and have to say that, however meritorious this officer may be, I deem it inadvisable to increase his salary as recommended, for the reason that he is now in receipt of a liberal compensation which was fixed at a time
