My dear Sir

I feel impelled to say that one or two things you said to me on Sunday appear to me quite wrong. You mentioned as almost culpable that every head of a bureau wants to set up an investigating laboratory of the branch of science which concerns him. The idea is, I suppose, that he should not try to do his work scientifically, because that might cost money, but must just go on doing it just well enough and no better. That view among civilized peoples does recognize it. Think for one moment of the spirit in which the French bureaus of government are carried on, and of what indig-nation the French people would feel if they were carried on in the spirit that ours are! Does not that reflection make you feel like blushing? It does me.
