U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, [date]March 30, 1886[/date].

Proy. C. S. Peirce,
Assist. Coast & Geodetic Survey.

Dear Sir,

I have returned to the Secretary of the Treasury the communication from you in regard to the testimony taken by the Investigating Com[sup]ee[/sup] - which communication had been referred to me - and have accompanied it by a statement of the situation as I now understand it. In that statement my colleagues, Messrs. Garrison & Huntington, have concurred.

I append a copy of the concluding part of my letter.

Yours respectfully,
F. M. Thorn
Superintendent.
