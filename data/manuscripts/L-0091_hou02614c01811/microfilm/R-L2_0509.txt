In reply to your letter of [date]Aug. 21st[/date]. I have to state that no expenditure will be allowed after [date]Aug. 15th[/date] on Mr. Risteen's account or any other party account.
You will ship the Coast Survey instruments in your charge to this office, and the English instrument to the Royal Society, notifying this office of your action. The expense of transportation will be paid from the office appropriation.
