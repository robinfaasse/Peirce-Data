[u]Memo' for Supt[/u]
of field-work (pendulum) by
Assist. Peirce, between Jan'y 1884
& August 1885.

1884. Feb. March. April. Engaged with pendulum
work at Washington. D.C.

1884. July 23. Arrives at Fortress Monroe, Va.
Aug. 25. Still at work swinging pendulum
at O. P. C.

Sept. 10. At Afton Mountain House Va
prospecting - finds place unsuitable
that time of year.

[bracket]
1884 Oct 1 -
1885 Feb'y 23
[/bracket]
At some time during this
interval swings pendulum at
the Smithsonian - Washington.
No reports or papers or records
on file from which I could
learn of this time when at
work.

1885. March 1 Arrives at Key West Fla &
May 13 completes pendulum there.

Between end of May & Aug 1
no field work was done

(over)
