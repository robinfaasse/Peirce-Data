(Copy)

U. S. Naval Observatory
Washington,
[date]March 12, 1886[/date]

F. M. Thorn Esqr
Superintendent of the
U. S. Coast & Geodetic Survey
Washington D. C.

Sir,

I have the honor to acknowledge the receipt of the letter from Mr. C. S. Peirce of the 10[ins]th[/ins] instant, forwarded by the Coast and Geodetic Survey Office - requesting to be supplied with clock-signals from the Observatory.

The Observatory will be happy to furnish the desired signals whenever it shall be notified that Mr. Peirce is ready to receive them.

Very respectfully
(Sd) Geo. E. Belknap
Commodore U. S. N.
Superintendent
