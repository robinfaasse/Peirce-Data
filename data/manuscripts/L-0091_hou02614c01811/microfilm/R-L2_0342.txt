U. S. COAST AND GEODETIC SURVEY OFFICE,

Washington, [date]Mch 22[/date], 1883.

Dear Sir:

By a ruling of the P. O. Dept. it is forbidden to re-forward printed matter & postal cards which have been delivered to this Office, unless additional postage is added; therefore, it will be necessary for you to make such arrangements as will secure the mailing of your papers, cards &c, direct to you in the first instance, & not thro' this Office. Letters can be forwarded as heretofore, by re-addressing them.

I am

Yours respectfully
Richd. D. Cutts
Assist. in charge of Office
