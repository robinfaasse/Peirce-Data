A Hardy's noddy mounted on a
pendulum support is a special case
of two pendulums on one support. It
will be convenient to obtain a gene-
ral solution of this problem.

I have shown in my paper on Faye's
method of swinging pendulums that two
of the differential equations for this prob-
lem are the same as for a simple pen-
dulum on a flexible support. Namely,
neglecting quantities of the second order
they are
$$l_1 D_t^2 \varphi_1 + D_t^2 s_1 = - g_1 \varphi_1$$
$$l_2 D_t^2 \varphi_2 + D_t^2 s_2 = - g_2 \varphi_2$$

where
$t$ is the time
$\varphi_1$ and $\varphi_2$, the angular displacements from
the vertical of the two pendulums,
$s_1$ and $s_2$, the linear displacements of
