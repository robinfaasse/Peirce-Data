U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,

Washington, [date]Oct 16th 1886[/date]

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Asst. U. S. C. & G. Survey
#272 N. Calvert St
Baltimore, Md.

Sir:
I beg to call your attention to the
fact that, Voucher No. 2, E. Maurer, for services
$15.-, as entered upon your August Abstract,
has not been received by me.

On the 1st inst. I notified you that, the
amount of the Voucher in question would be
held in abeyance until its receipt.

As the first quarter of the fiscal year has
now expired, & my accounts will shortly have
to be forwarded to the Auditor, I have to
request that you furnish me with the above
named Voucher at your earliest convenience,
as in its absence, I will necessarily be
compelled to disallow it, to enable me to
close my accounts.

Very respy,
[del]W. B. Morgan[/del]
