[pn]7.[/pn] Respecting the Paris results, I place no faith in the
accuracy of the time determinations at that place - as
indicated by the recorded rates; - to omit the results or
to repeat the observations would seem to be a violent propo-
sition, but one open to consideration in a question of such
great nicety of investigation. I may remark apart from
this investigation that I have little faith in short swings
where exhaustive accuracy is aimed at; besides the pen-
dulum work should be supported by time observations
at the beginning and end of each swing. The discrepan-
cies at present appearing in the Paris results may pos-
sibly divide themselves, the smaller error attaching to
Assistant Peirce's work and the larger error to the results
of the older observers. The additional pages of Ms., received
this morning and which are to take the place of a part
previously sent, avoid certain entangling questions res-
pecting foreign work which can be considered
much more appropriately in a later part.
