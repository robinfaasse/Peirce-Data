U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey,

Madison, Wis., [date]Oct. 2, 1886[/date].

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Dear Sir,

Enclosed please find
receipt for telegram which was
sent in reply to you, but failed
to reach you. The message was
sent to be paid at your office, but
as it failed to reach you, the
office here required me to
pay it, which I did. The
message was: "C. S. Peirce, Ithaca, N.Y.
Thirteen Dollars and forty-five Cents.
H. W. O'Neill,
Madison, Wis."

The amount charged was 75 cts.

I forward the Ann
Arbor records today.
