UNITED STATES COAST AND GEODETIC SURVEY,

WASHINGTON, D. C., [date]Dec. 19, 1891[/date].

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Assistant U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey,
Milford, Penna.,

Dear Sir:

I send you herewith a list of Property and Books charged to you on the records of this office. Please check the list at once and notify me of any inaccuracy which you may find. Please pack them properly and return to me by freight as soon as possible and send me notice of their shipment with Bill of Lading.

Respectfully yours
T. C. Mendenhall
Superintendent
