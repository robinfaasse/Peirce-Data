U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,

Washington, D. C., [date]June 30, 1880[/date]

Dear Professor Peirce,

A letter was received
from Cornell University a few days
ago asking for the force of gravity
as determined by you at that point.
Thinking that you must have
made a preliminary report a
year or two ago I referred this
letter to this office and learned that
no such report had been
prepared or at least presented.
I afterward received a letter
which you will please return
