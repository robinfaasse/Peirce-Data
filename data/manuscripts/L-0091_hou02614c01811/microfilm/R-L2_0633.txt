U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,

Washington, [date]October 8"[/date], 1888.

Mr. Chas. S. Peirce,
Assistant, C. & G. Survey,
Milford, Pa.

Sir:

I enclose herewith for your information, a list of legal fees for jurats. State laws on the subject are very perplexing, and the list has been compiled with difficulty. It is, however, believed to be correct. Advices to the contrary will be thankfully received at any time.

Yours respectfully,
Geo. A. Bartlett,
Disbursing Clerk.
J.W.P.

C.S.
