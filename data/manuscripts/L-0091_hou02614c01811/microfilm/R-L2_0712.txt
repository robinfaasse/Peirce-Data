it must be "centimetres (or other unit)

rather a clumsy phrase to say the
least. The matter of the proper use
of dynamical units—and dynamical
terms is, of course, of great importance
but I presume that it is fair to say
that neither you nor I acquire special
distinction in that subject and that it
would probably be a waste of time
for us to endeavor to instruct each other.
I cannot have the slightest objection
to your restricting the meaning of "g"
or any other symbol to any way you
please in your personal use of such
symbols. For my own part, while
not feeling obliged to follow any one,
I have found no one more clear
or satisfactory in his treatment

of these matters than Clerk Maxwell & I do
not ask that others shall agree with me, and I only
"wrestle" with those whom I conceive to be erring
through ignorance. Of course I know very well
that the present case is not of that kind and
therefore argument is out of place. A thousand years
of it might not bring us nearer together. When
acting for the public, however, one must be guided
by the general consensus of opinion of those generally
admitted to be the highest authorities; personal preferences
and especially any weakness towards "eccentricity" must often
give way. I am sure you can find no reasonable
objection to this.
