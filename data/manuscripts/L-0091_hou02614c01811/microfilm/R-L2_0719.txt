FORM 148.

LETTER IN REPLY TO REQUEST TO STOP PAYMENT ON CHECK.

[u]Office of Assistant Treasurer U. S.,[/u]
New York, [u]December 3[/u], 1890.

To [u]C. S. Peirce[/u]
[u]Milford[/u]
[u]Pa[/u]

Sir:
Your letter of the [u]1[ins]st[/ins] inst.[/u], requesting that payment be stopped on check No. [u]19711[/u], drawn by [u]John W. Parsons[/u] [u]D. A. U.S. G. S.[/u], dated [u]November 19[/u], 1890, to the order of [u]C. S. Peirce[/u] for $ [blank], has been received. The check has not been paid. If you regain possession of it, please notify this Office at once by letter, written or signed by the same person who signed the letter requesting that payment be refused. No guarantee can be given that payment will not be made, if presented, but due care will be used to prevent it.
[u]This office cannot undertake to notify you should the check be presented hereafter.[/u]

Very respectfully,

[u]M. L. Muhleman[/u]
Acting Assistant Treasurer U. S., New York.
