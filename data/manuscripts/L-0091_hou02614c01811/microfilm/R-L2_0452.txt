U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, [date]D. C., Feby. 24th, 1886[/date].

Prof. C. S. Peirce,
U. S. C. & G. Survey Office.

Dear Sir,

In reply to your letter of the 15th instant, - I have to say that the books belonging to you, but which by a clerical error appear on inventory as Coast Survey property, have been struck from the list as requested by you.

Yours respectfully,
B. A. Colonna,
Asst. in Charge of Office.
