As soon as the pendulum swings were completed, the pendulum was carefully replaced in its original box and sealed up in the tin case. This was done under my personal supervision immediately after the observations were made, so that the pendulum never went except from its box to its point of suspension and thence back to its box.

The extreme and constant dangers which continually beset the party during the retreat from Fort Conger southward necessitated always the utmost despatch in loading or unloading the boats or sledges, and at times everything was thrown on the ice with the greatest celerity. The pendulum, however, was looked after with the utmost care, and the fact that it was secured in its tightly-fitting box, seems a guarantee that no such friction could have taken place as would cause the loss of mass referred to.
