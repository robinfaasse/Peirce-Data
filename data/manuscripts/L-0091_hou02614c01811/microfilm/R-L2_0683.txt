U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, D.C. [date]April 23, 1889[/date].

Dear Sir:

It is my painful duty to announce the death of Mr. Edwin
Hergesheimer, an assistant in this service, which occurred about 4
o'clock this A.M. at his residence in this city Stricken with
paralysis on the 31st March ult. all hope of ultimate recovery was
destroyed by a relapse, with cessation of consciousness, yesterday
morning.

As a man Mr. Hergesheimer was candid, upright, broad-minded,
independent and unwaveringly loyal to truth and right. In his
chosen profession he had no superior and few equals in the country.
He has wrought his own monument in the works of this Survey and
his epitaph is written in the hearts of his associates, in which the
memory of his noble qualities will be long enshrined. These as-
sociates will doubtless soon commemorate his virtues and express
their sense of loss in some appropriate memorial.

Very Respectfully
[sig]F. M. Thorn[/sig]
Superintendent.
