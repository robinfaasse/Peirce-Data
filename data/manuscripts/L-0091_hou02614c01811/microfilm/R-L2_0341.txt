[u]Instructions[/u]

U. S. COAST AND GEODETIC SURVEY OFFICE,

Washington, [date]Feb. 1[/date], 1883.

C. S. Peirce
Assistant C. & G. Survey
Washington D.C.

Sir,

You will prepare the Yard Pendulum to be dispatched with Head No. 3 for use on the eclipse expedition.

You will continue the pendulum comparison of the Yard and Metre.

You will occupy the Smithsonian Institution with all the pendulums used in every way in which they have been used at other stations.

You will re-occupy the Stevens Institute, Hoboken, N. J., as soon thereafter as circumstances will permit, using Pendulums Nos. 2 and 3.

Yours respectfully
J. E. Hilgard
Superintendent
