Sir:

Enclosed please find my check No. 290608 on New York, for $21.50, as an Advance on Party Account.

The amount of $4. for "Advertisements", included in your requisition, is not considered necessary, and the Advance is made in conformity with your requisition, [u]less said sum[/u], as suggested by the Superintendent.

These "new methods", though they may appear "strange and useless" to you, are nevertheless based upon old laws; furthermore, the law makes liberal provisions by which its application may not prove a hardship; nor entail unnecessary expense upon the Government.
