In case of computations, proper titles should be written or pasted on each cahier, name of the locality and kind of work, date, observer's name, computer's name &c. &c.

The following regulations apply especially to hydrographic records:

Tidal Records: When observations at more than one station are recorded in a volume, the beginning and end of each series should be distinctly recorded.

Information called for on pages immediately following the title page should be furnished with sufficient fulness and detail to enable the reduction to be properly made. This information should include a sketch of shore-line showing location of the gauge and benchmarks.

The position of the tidal station should be indicated on the hydrographic sheet.

The plane of reference for the soundings should be given by its reading on the staff, in the tide book and on the hydrographic sheet.
