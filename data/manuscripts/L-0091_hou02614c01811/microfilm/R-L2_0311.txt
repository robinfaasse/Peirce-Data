[u]Instructions from Asst. Peirce in regard to Pendulum[/u].

The metre pendulum is to be made first. As soon as
brass rod is received (which is to weight the pendulum); 1[ins]st[/ins]
cut off piece 20 centimetres long, centre it accurately and
bore out from end to end a core with diameter exactly 2/3 of
the diameter of the rod. This ring-shaped piece is then to be
firmly soldered into the middle of the pendulum tube exact-
ly half-way between the knife-edges, so that it cannot possibly
come loose. Great attention must be paid to having this so
firmly soldered that no jar can ever loose it.

N.B. I consider this as one of the weak points of
the construction, as there is no calculating what jar it may
receive in transportation.

quhar will again proceed with his calculations of time of
oscillation; and also what mass must be placed at 12 centi-
metres outside of one of the knife-edges in order to render the
pendulum convertible. A piece of the rod of the right length
