Professor Chas. S. Peirce,

Dear Sir:

I have letters of yours, all of which I beg leave to acknowledge as follows:

1st To Prof. J. E. Hilgard Supt - July 31st reporting progress &c. This has been previously acknowledged, concerning which I have nothing further to say.

2nd To Prof. J. E. Hilgard Supt. July 31st referring to pendulums made by Gautier previously acknowledged. Have nothing to say further in this matter now.

3rd To F. M. Thorn, Supt. Aug. 5th Referring to detail of Capt. O. E. Michaelis U.S. Ordnance for duty on C.S. in connection with your work, under present circumstances this matter will lay over for a time, Bring it up again when affairs are more settled,
