Dear Sir:
Referring to your letter of [date]Feby 11 /91[/date], and your supplemental Inventory of Books, I would state that the account has been carefully revised, and found to be correct with the exception of those enumerated on the list enclosed.
These volumes were charged to you on the dates indicated, and have not been credited, nor can letters be found on the office file acknowledging them.
If you have any such acknowledgments please give me the dates.

Yours respectfully
