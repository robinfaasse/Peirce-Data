Prof. C. S. Peirce, Asst. etc.
Dear Sir:
In the work at Ebensburg
& York, where arcs & thermometer were observed
by a different time-piece from that by which the
oscillations were registered, the correction reducing
the former to the latter is given. The memor-
-andum "W - 11[sup]s[/sup].4", "Cas. + 0[sup]s[/sup].3, etc., indicates that times
by "W" & "Cas" are to be diminished by 11[sup]s[/sup].4 & increased
by 0[sup]s[/sup].3 respectively, to give Chronometer-times.

Very respectfully
H. Farquhar
