Dear Sir:

It seems to me that your sarcasm concerning the "lawyer's way of meeting a scientific objection," borrows all its force from a vivid imagination. I do not remember having "made light" of any difference in the weight of a pendulum. Your gratuitous fling rather provokes me to say that while you and many of your hysterical clamorous con[del]freres[/del][ins]greg[/ins]y gravity have been [del]irresistably[/del] provocative of levity, I do not think I have ever presumed to "make light" of so momentous a quantity as [del]twenty grammes[/del] enough certainly to unfailingly outwork the profound respect of even so unscientific a creature as a lawyer.

Very Truly
