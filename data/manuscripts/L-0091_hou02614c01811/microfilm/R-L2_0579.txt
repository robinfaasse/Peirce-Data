Washington City, March 28th, 1887.

Mr. Frank M. Thorn,
Superintendent U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey,
Washington, D.C.

Dear Sir:

Referring to the discussion of the pendulum observations of the Lady Franklin Bay Expedition, which was to have been done by the Coast and Geodetic Survey, I write again to urge that special attention be given to this matter and that they be transmitted to me at the earliest possible moment. I am receiving a large amount of proof daily, and it will be impossible for the pendulum observations to appear unless urgent steps are taken by you in the matter.
