Dear Sir:—

I have carefully read over the MS. on the Coast Survey pendulum investigations, which you handed me last Saturday, and beg leave to submit the following notes on points which, in my opinion, would seem to demand some further consideration before they can be published satisfactorily. Possibly I may refer to matters respecting which fuller statements are desirable when all may be contained in part II, but this is unavoidable in the absence of a knowledge of the contents of the entire paper.

1. Year and months of occupation might be added to the C. S. pendulum stations pp. 8, 9, 11, 12.
2. The contents of pp. 41, 42, 43, 49 and similar tables need explanation.
3. Respecting the subjects Nos. 10 & 11 on p. 22, it would be advisable to give some general account in this first part.
