Milford Pa [date]1892 Jan 20[/date]

Dr. T. C. Mendenhall
Supt C & G. Survey

Dear Sir:

In reply to yours of the 19th, I desire to say that in regard to the records you can't identify

No 7 1/2 8 sheets [ins]Washington[/ins] was sent by me
No 8 Large envelope Diagram sheets. Not found.
No 22 1 roll sheets 1883. These are small sheets and were sent in one of the tin boxes.
No 23 1 roll chronograph sheets 1883. Were sent.
Unnumbered 1 roll sheets 1882 I don't see how these can fail to be there somewhere, if they were ever sent to me. They are not here.
1 roll large circles & blanks - Mere stationery.
1 roll sheets 1883 Must be there.
"14th and 6 8vo vols No titles." I cannot say what this means. But I will say that it is utterly impossible there should be 8 octavo volumes
