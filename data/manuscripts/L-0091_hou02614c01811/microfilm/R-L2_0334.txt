Dear Sir,

In examining the list of Appendices
for the 1881 Report, I find that the following
are mentioned as expected from you:
(a) Determination of Force of Gravity at
points in Penn.
(b) Variation of Gravity with the Latitude.
(c) Flexure of Pendulum Supports.
(d) Oscillation Period of the Walls of the
Receiver.

The Report for 1881 has gone to press,
and as the manuscript of none of the
above has yet been received at this
Office, it is scarcely probable that they
can be prepared by the time at which
the Public Printer must know certainly
what is to go into the Report. I would
