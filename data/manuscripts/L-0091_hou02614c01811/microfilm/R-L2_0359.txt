U.S. Coast and Geodetic Sy. Office

Washington [date]July 27th 1885[/date].

B. A. Colonna,

Assistant in charge of Office

Sir:

I have to request that you will furnish me, at your
earliest convenience, with a statement to which you can make oath, of
any and all facts in your possession touching the administration of
this Office during the last five years, whether referring to its
integrity or efficiency.

Very respectfully

F. M. Thorn

Superintendent.
