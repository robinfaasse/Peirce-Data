U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,

Washington, [date]Feb 19, 1889[/date].

Prof. C. S. Peirce
U. S. C. & G. Survey
Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir

Among the articles found at the Smithsonian Institution is a gas stove for which the Gas Co. has rendered a bill of $13.70. It appears that this stove was procured by you some years ago & the a/c never settled. The Office has arranged the matter, agreeing to settle the Gas Co. claim & the stove is therefore now the property of the Office & need not hereafter appear upon your inventories.

Yours respectfully
B. A. Colonna
Asst in Chg. of Office
