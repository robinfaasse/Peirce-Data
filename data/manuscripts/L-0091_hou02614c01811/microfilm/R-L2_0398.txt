U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, [date]31 July, 1885[/date].

Mr. F. M. Thorn
&c — — —

Dear Sir,

I have rec'd thro' Hon.
Farquhar, your instructions to
prepare an estimate of the
reasonable cost of a seasons
work at pendulum-observations.
The estimate must largely be
guesswork, but I think that
the following will not be an
under-statement:

For Living expenses of one
Aid & Artificer $135.00
" Cost of erecting observatory & other
necessary preparations 75
" Travel & expenses of other half
between sta's 100 miles apart 25
" Transportation of Apparatus 35
" Pay of Artificer 45
Total $315.00
That for one seasons work
at six such stations $1890.00
