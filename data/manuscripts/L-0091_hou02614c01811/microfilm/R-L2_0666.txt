Dear Sir,
In reply to your letter of [date]Feb. 9th[/date] I have to say that there is no field work in the South available for you.

I cannot answer [del]your[/del] inquiry as to your taking your present work along until your reply to my letter of the 8th instant as to the practicability of retaining the twenty volumes of records and [del]your written[/del] Ms. report in the archives here for preservation.

I enclose a copy of that letter to enable you the more speedily to reply.
