U. S. COAST AND GEODETIC SURVEY OFFICE,

Washington, [date]Nov 28"[/date], 1890.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Assistant, C. & G. Survey,
Milford, Pa.

Sir:

In reply to your letter of the 26th inst. enquiring about your salary account for the first half of this month, I beg to state that I mailed you my check, No. 19,711, drawn on the Assistant Treasurer, U. S., New York, N. Y. for $120.00, in payment of the account in question, on the 19th inst.

Respectfully yours,
John W. Parsons,
Disbursing Agent.

N.D.L.
