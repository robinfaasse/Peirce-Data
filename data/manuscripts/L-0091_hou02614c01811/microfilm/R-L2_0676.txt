Mr. C. S. Peirce,

Sir:

Your letter of the 27" ultimo, referring to the loss of your check [del]of 120. for account[/del] [ins]of salary[/ins] for March, was received while I was busily engaged in making the monthly payments of salaries; hence the slight delay in response.

Your first course should be to notify the Assistant Treasurer of the U. S., New York, N. Y., of the loss of the check, and request that payment of the same be stopped. A similar notice should be sent to the Disbursing Officer of the C. & G. Survey. The details of the check are as follows: Date of issue, [date]March 20, 1889[/date]; Number, 369.295; Amount, $120.00; Purpose for which drawn, on account of salary.
