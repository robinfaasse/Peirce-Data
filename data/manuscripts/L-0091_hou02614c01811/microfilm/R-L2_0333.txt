U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,

Washington, [u]June 29[/u], 1882.

[u]Chas. Peirce, Esq.[/u]
[u]Asst. C. & G. Survey[/u]
[u]21 Read St[/u]
[u]Baltimore, Md.[/u]

Dear Sir:

The C. & G. Survey Report for 1881 will go to press next week. In the list of Appendices there are four papers marked as to come from you. Of these none have as yet been received. As they are referred to in the body of the Report it is absolutely necessary that we know immediately if they will certainly be forthcoming and, if so, that they be in hand by the 15th of July at the very latest. Please inform me in the matter as early as possible.

Yours respectfully,
[u]Richd. D. Cutts[/u]
Asst. in charge of Office
