U. S. Coast & Geodetic Survey Office
Washington, [date]July 8, 1879[/date]

Hon. John Sherman
Secretary of the Treasury

Sir,

I have the honor to state that A. M. Harrison, Assistant Coast & Geodetic Survey, after having served on the work for more than thirty one years, on all parts of the Atlantic, Gulf and California Coasts, has had his health so much impaired as to be able no longer to execute field work, although quite capable of performing much valuable and important office work. Under these conditions, I have arranged that his pay shall be reduced from $2,180 per annum to $1200 per annum, a reduction of $980 per annum:

Although this reduction does not create a vacancy, I respectfully request and urge that the following advancement for merit be made.
