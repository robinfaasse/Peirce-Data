[u]Circular Letter.[/u]

U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Office,
Washington, [date]October 17, 1884[/date].

Sir:

The following is quoted from a letter received by me from the Acting First Auditor of the Treasury, under date of [date]September 28, 1884[/date], and is furnished you for your information and guidance in the future rendition of your Accounts to this Office.

"In your Account for "Party Expenses" for the fiscal year 1884, I find in the vouchers rendered several items of expenditures for [u]Expressage, Storage, and Forage[/u], which are charged upon personal vouchers of Chiefs of Parties, and which should be covered by bills and receipts; also for [u]telegrams[/u], which, if receipts cannot be obtained, should show the number of words, and the places from and to which they are sent. In the future settlement of your Accounts all items embraced upon personal vouchers, requiring bills and receipts, not so receipted, will be disallowed, [u]unless it can be shown that it was impracticable to obtain them[/u]. Suspensions and disallowances will be made in all Accounts [u]until full evidence is filed[/u] that the service has been rendered or the articles charged for have been received."
