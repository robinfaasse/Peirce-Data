[u]To the President of the United States:[/u]

The undersigned.........................................................................................................................................................................................................................................
being professionally interested in the conduct of the United States Coast and Geodetic Survey,
beg leave to express the opinion that it is most desirable that the office of Superintendent of
that establishment should continue in the hands of a civilian, as from the beginning, and to
state their conviction that MR. JULIUS E. HILGARD is recognized by the scientific world, both
at home and abroad, as the first of American geodesists. They have entire confidence that
under his capable and experienced administration, this great work would fully maintain those
high standards of practical and of purely scientific excellence to which its past history com-
mits it.
