Would rather risk the loss of your
friendship by this endeavor to bene-
fit you than to retain it and
allow you to injure yourself and the
service without speaking.

I return to you herewith your com-
munication to the Sec. Treas. dated
[date]Aug. 10th[/date] with the following comments.

1st Mr. Schott did not disapprove of it on
the contrary he thought it on the whole
a proper statement to make.

2nd Colonna does not agree with Mr.
Schott, he thinks it takes ground
that will not be tenable and con-
tains some sarcasm that should be
excluded from such a paper.

Reconsider the whole matter and do not
write a word for three days after you
get this. After that I will do what-
ever you request me to do that is
consistent with a conscientious performance
of my duty.

Yours respectfully,
B. A. Colonna Assistant Chg.
