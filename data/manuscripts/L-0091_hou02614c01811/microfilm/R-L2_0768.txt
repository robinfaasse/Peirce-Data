Westfield, Pike Co. Pa. [date]1892 Jan[/date]

Dr. T. C. Mendenhall
Superintendent C & G. Survey

Dear Sir:

I send to you by express today boxes containing substantially all the property called for in your list recently sent to me. I desire to call your personal attention to the following exceptions.

[u]1 Baudin Thermometer No 9246[/u]. The number is a mistake for 9245 and should not have been entered on your list as distinct from that. I have not for many years had but this one Baudin Thermometer in my hands.

[u]1 Binocular[/u]. This to the best of my belief has been recently stolen. It did not bear the Coast Survey mark & I request to be allowed to send another in place of it, or else be charged with the value. It was out of order.
