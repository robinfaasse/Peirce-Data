To the Editor of "Mind"
Sir:

I am desirous of publishing a considerable series of articles on the Logic of Relatives, especially in its philosophical aspects. Would you give me space to do so in consecutive numbers of [u]Mind[/u], and if so, how much space?

Independently of the above proposal, I offer the enclosed article, provided it need not wait over more than one issue before the first part of it, at least, shall appear. I am bound, however, to inform you that I was asked [ins](I do not say by whom)[/ins] to furnish material for a notice of [ins]the 3[sup]d[/sup] volume of[/ins] Schröder's Logic for the [u]Monist[/u]; and I sent on something which contained, among other things, some of [del]the[/del] [ins]the[/ins] arguments contained in the paper I now send you, although in a very inferior form. That brief for an article (for it could not be printed as it stood) having reached the editor of the [u]Monist[/u], he wrote me that he wished to have the article appear over my signature. I had already written that I wished to withdraw certain parts (not specified) of what I had [del]sent[/del] [ins]sent[/ins], for publication elsewhere, and at the same time sent other matter, more pertinent to Schröder's third volume, to replace it. When Dr. Carus wrote me that he wished the article to appear as mine, I immediately replied asking him to send me the whole of the MS to prepare for the press. I presume he will do so, although I have not heard from him. He is probably away for the summer. If he does [del]so[/del] [ins]so[/ins], he ought to do and send me the MS., I shall excise everything it contains in common with what is sent herewith (or cut it down to a paragraph or two). Should he put it in print without sending it to me, then [del]it[/del] [ins]a[/ins] part of the [u]Monist[/u] notice will be a brief and imperfect statement of a part of what is here carefully set forth. Should you reject this article, I will forward postage for its return.

your obedient servant,
C. S. Peirce
5 W 50[sup]th[/sup] St. New York City.
