To the Editor of "Mind"

Dear Sir:

I am desirous of publishing a considerable series of articles on the logic of relatives, especially in its philosophical aspects. Would you give me space to do so in consecutive numbers of [u]Mind[/u], and if so, how much space?

Independently of the above, I offer the enclosed article, provided it can be speedily published, that is, provided it is not to wait over more than one issue before the first part of it appears. I must tell you that I was asked to write a notice of Schröder's Logic to be worked over and appear as the work of another person for the Monist. I sent on a sort of brief which contained [ins]among other things[/ins] some of the points contained in the article I now send, but in very inferior form. When the Editor of the Monist received my brief, he concluded [del]it[/del] [ins]the article[/ins] had better appear as mine, after all. I had already sent word that I wished to excise portions [del](not specified)[/del] of what I had sent him & replace [del]it[/del] [ins]them[/ins] by other matter [ins]which I forwarded,[/ins] more pertinent to Schröder's Third Volume; and when he sent me word the article was to bear my name, I wrote to him to send back the whole of the MSS and I would make an article out of it. Thus, if he does as he ought to do, I shall cut out of [del]that[/del] article everything contained in this, unless perhaps [ins]a very[/ins] brief mention of opinions maintained here. Should he, however, make up an article out of my MSS., then that will only contain in brief & imperfect form the argument here set forth at some length.

Your obedient servant C.S. Peirce, 5 W 50[u]th[/u] St, New York City U.S.A.

P.S. In case of rejection, I shall forward the necessary postage for the return of the MS.
