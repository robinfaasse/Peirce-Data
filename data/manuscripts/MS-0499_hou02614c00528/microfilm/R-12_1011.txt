The surrejoinder, such as it is, is this:

A man appears before a notary and
says, I wish to make what I have written on
this paper my act and deed, & [del]so[/del] [ins]will[/ins] swear to
it and [del]be[/del] responsible for it. The notary
takes the paper and finds nothing written
on it but these words - [u]hard, red crystal[/u].
He says you cannot make yourself re-
sponsible nor so much as wish to make
yourself responsible for a crystal of any kind.
