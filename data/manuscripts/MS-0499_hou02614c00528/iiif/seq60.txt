I. An altitude difference.
When such a change is made in the
position of the body as to lower
the head, an increase in the
intracranial pressure is produced
with all its effects, and
the reverse occurs when the body
is inclined.
This is
of course most pronounced when
the change is from the erect
to the inverted position.
A small pressure is however produced
by the mere change from the
erect to the horizontal.
