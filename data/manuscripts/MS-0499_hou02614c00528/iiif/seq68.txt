I was thinking about how much I loved the
people I knew then and how easy and 
pleasant it was to feel so. Now when I love
people I am mostly so self-conscious and
apprehensive that much of the pleasure and
the naturalness are gone. I’m thinking of
K. particularly. In the old days it seemed
natural to tell people I loved them if I
did, and to express affection naturally and
often, and to be so sure they felt the same.
Now it seems extraordinary and almost
scary that K. should care about me. This
is partly because I don’t love myself much
and feel that no one should love me, or
else because I have grown inordinate and
require so much that it is never enough.
The inordinateness seems to be mostly K’s
fault, because she was so much at first.
I hope that I am getting used to it now.
