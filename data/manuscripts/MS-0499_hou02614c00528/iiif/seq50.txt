I am but a poor student, but I believe I can

reach some of these.

It is but a little way further on into the wood

that the flowers are mostly found. 

Will you go with me, and I will show you?

and we can sit down on a fallen tree

that is quite dry, and rest, and I can tell you some

of my stories.

And let us see which can gather the

most flowers on our way.

And then let us think what we shall

do with them.
