[del]This story is of a nature to be quite as interesting to older readers as to children.
But we hope that boys & girls will find it equally attractive.
We commend it to them for the good it should do them. It is intended to teach the necessity of [u]forgiveness[/u]. No matter how much we may have suffered at the hands of others, we should be ready to forgive.
This we are taught in the Lords prayer:
"Forgive us our trespasses as we forgive those who trespass against us."
Without this forgiving spirit, we should never enter into the kingdom of heaven.[/del]
