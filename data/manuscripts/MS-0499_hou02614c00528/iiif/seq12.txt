of some of the following
elements which can already be
determined even from the
scanty material in question.
1. Consistency in orientation
of lines. World-Standard of size.
2. Degree of likeness in some
parts - in some respects -
between the new groups of
Facts and known & standard
groups.
3. Does any particular set or combo-
nation of these new facts lead
anywhere as a whole?
4. Do they act as if they were
true? Do they work?
5. Is the new world - of which
the new facts are parts - a
possible world?
6. Does it fit in with existing
values?
