it? We should say of the author that he must be a culti-
vated Englishman of Shakespeare's time, who should have
taken an interest in the theatre, and who should have had
a good ear for sounds and a faculty for their discrimi-
nating expression. He should be an inhabitant of London
as Shakespeare was, and born in [date]1564[/date], that being the
year of Shakespeare's birth. Since the author would have
to be a contemporary of Shakespeare, the date of his book would
be a small matter; still if we were to give it any birth-year, remem-
bering how apt an orthoepist is to reflect the popular speech as
it was a few years back, we should fix it, we will say, at
three years after Shakespeare's death. Our modern phonotopy
would suggest what the book itself should be. It must con-
tain the principal common and exceptional words printed
in this style, only more phonetically and more accurately.
