[u]Induction[/u]

Aristotle's definition of Induction is perfect. He says:—
"Induction is opposed to syllogism. For the latter, through
the middle term, proves the extreme of the third; whereas
the former, through the third, the extreme of the middle."
That is to say, [ins](less exactly)[/ins] syllogism, through the conception of a class
proves an attribute of an object; while induction through
what is known of an object, proves an attribute of a class.

doctrine of the quantification of the predicate. Yet it is a mean-
