Quality is the only Quantity, which as distinction between the Extension and Intension of Concepts need give to the Predicate.

Note. Though I speak psychologically here (as opposing psychological Logicians) all that I say of concepts may be extended to symbols in general.

[u]Definitions[/u]
1 The reference of a concept to a quality is its intension.
2 The reference of a concept to an object is its extension
3 The determination of [del]the reference of one[/del] [ins]a[/ins] concept [ins]conceived[/ins] by [del]a synthesis of it with the reference of another[/del] [ins]of what it refers to with what another refers to,[/ins] is a judgement.
[del]4[/del] [ins]4[/ins] The determined concept of a judgement is the [u]subject[/u] = S.
5 The determining concept of a judgement is the [u]predicate[/u] = P.

[u]Axioms[/u]
1 In the concept the quality [ins]that is given in the concept itself,[/ins] or intensive object, is directly thought.
2 In the concept the extensive object is, indirectly thought, or thought [del]of[/del] [ins]not seen in the concept itself but[/ins] [ins]by observation[/ins] that is, it is only thought by the reference of the thought to it.
3. A concept may be met with and recognized in the observation of objects.

[u]Theorem I[/u]. The limits of intension are given subjectively; for that we should distinguish what is within the object of intension from what is without, it is only necessary to have a clear notion of that object. But that object is actually thought; hence if the thought is clear the required limits are given without the necessity of referring to external objects of sense.
