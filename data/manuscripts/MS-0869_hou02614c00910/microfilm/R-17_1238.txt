If is an induction, because it is a test of the hypothesis
by means of a prediction, which has been verified. But
it is [del]only[/del] [ins]was[/ins] an abductory induction, because it is a sampling

nor even weighed, except by guess work. It also partakes of the
nature of abduction in involving an original suggestion; while
typical induction [del]makes[/del] has no originality in it, but only
tests a suggestion already made.

In induction, it is not the fact predicted that in any
degree necessitates the truth of the hypothesis or even
renders it probable. It is the fact that it has been predicted
successfully, and that it is a haphazard specimen of all
the predictions which ought to be predicated based on the
hypothesis [del]and which practically[/del] constitute its practical truth.
