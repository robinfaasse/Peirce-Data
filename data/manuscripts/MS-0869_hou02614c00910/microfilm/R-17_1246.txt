certain proportion are of a certain species. Such is the

Such a probability must be distinguished from a mere
likelihood which is nothing better than the expression of
our preconceived ideas. The confusion between these two
kinds of probability is one of the main sources of human
errors, especially in abduction; in which yielding to
judgments of likelihood is a fertile source of waste of
time and energy.
