$\overline{p}$ " " " " " " black

$w$ white balls drawn already
$b$ black " " "

$P_{(w,b,r)}$ probability that the ratio of all the
white balls to all the balls will ever
be as great as $p+r$ or as small
as $p-r$

$P_{w,b} = p P_{w+1,b} + \overline{p} P_{w,b+1}$

of $n$ balls to be drawn as many as $x$ will

$Q(x,n) = p Q(x-1,n-1) + \overline{p} Q(x,n-1)$
