In this paper, [ins]besides very important improvements in the[/ins]
subdivision of the three kinds of simple arguments, with

ness of exposition, I shall have much that is new to say
about mixed arguments, which present many points of
importance and of interest that have never been remarked.
I [del]go[/del] shall give a new classification of them based, not upon

nation. Besides setting forth my own doctrine of the elechi-
ology of argument, I shall examine the most important
of those which are opposed to it.
