They had better be done in their numbered order; but since I ought to procure a great many books (for one cannot venture upon criticizing a book, no matter how well one may be acquainted with it, without having it at hand,) it is desirable that I should receive more aid the first year; and therefore it is desirable that I should accomplish more that year. By selections from the list, I might manage to do nine the first year. I consider this proceeding unfavorable to the best work; but since I understand that the Executive Committee will under no circumstances afford any aid toward procuring books, this method would be unavoidable.
