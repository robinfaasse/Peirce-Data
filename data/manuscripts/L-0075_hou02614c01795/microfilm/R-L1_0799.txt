No 35 [u]On Definition and Clearness of Ideas.[/u]

In [date]January, 1878[/date], I published a very brief sketch of this
subject wherein I enunciated a certain maxim of Pragmatism
which has, of late years, attracted some attention. To that
I fully adhere; but it must be more accurately defined
so as to guard against certain interpretations which
do not follow from the grounds of Pragmatism. More-
over, it allowed it to appear, by my silence in [date]1878[/date],
that pragmatism represents the highest grade of clear-
ness of ideas. This is not true; and the matter will
in this memoir be shown to be a fragment of a
more extended doctrine.
