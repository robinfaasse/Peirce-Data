Dear Prof. James:

The work of Mr. Chas. S. Peirce, so far as I have known it hitherto, is of the kind that benefits not only the student of philosophy but all who work with the tools of science, for his logic is a shaping and sharpening of the tools which all of us use when we attack the problems Nature presents to us.

Any help that he might receive in continuing such work as he brought before us in his lecture at Cambridge, a few years ago, would bring in a return of value to everybody who investigates anything thoroughly.

Yours sincerely,

Richard C. Cabot.

I do not know Dr. Cabot but slightly as having attended my lectures.
