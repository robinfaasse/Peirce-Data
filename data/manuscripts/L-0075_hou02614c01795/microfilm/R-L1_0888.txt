JOHNS HOPKINS UNIVERSITY,
BALTIMORE.

PRESIDENT'S OFFICE.

May 3, 1901.

Dear Sir:

President Gilman wishes me to say that he presented your request to the Executive Committee at their last meeting, and they referred it to the Library Committee to consider and report. You may expect to hear the conclusion before very long.

Yours respectfully,
T. R. Ball
Registrar

Dr. C. S. Peirce.
