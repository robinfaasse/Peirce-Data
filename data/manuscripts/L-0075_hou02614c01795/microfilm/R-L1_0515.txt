I have been called a modern Hume, because Hume denied causality altogether, and I, after calling attention to the fact that all men set some limits to causality, endeavored to define these limits. Because I [del]had[/del] pointed out the insufficiency of existing logical algebra, and have [del]at[/del] used algebra as an aid in explaining the logic of relations, it has been assumed that I regarded logical algebra as the whole, or chief, part of logic, although, in fact, I have protested earnestly against the exaggerated importance attached [ins]by many[/ins] to this instrument of logic.
