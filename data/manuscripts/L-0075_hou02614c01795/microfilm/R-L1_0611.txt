To the Executive Committee of the Carnegie Institution

Gentlemen:

I have the honor to apply for aid from the Carnegie Institution in giving to the world certain researches into logic, chiefly the logic of science. I will proceed

[u]1[sup]st[/sup][/u], to state what these researches are;

[u]2[sup]nd[/sup][/u], to show what their importance is;

[u]3[sup]rd[/sup][/u], to consider how far the aid of the Carnegie Institution is needed, for the purpose;

[u]4[sup]th[/sup][/u], to suggest a plan by which the desired aid might be afforded;

[u]5[sup]th[/sup][/u], to consider what the ultimate total cost is likely to be;

[u]6[sup]th[/sup][/u], to ask what guarantee exists that the work will, in any sense, be brought to a conclusion.
