If I were to believe what I have been told, I should be obliged to admit that it was possible for a member of your responsible body to determine his will to oppose all aid to a certain individual, and to find reasons for doing so where he could. But I have [del]never met with[/del] [ins]detected[/ins] such a spirit in any committee of scientific men, and I do not believe [del]that it could[/del] [ins]for one instant,[/ins] appear in yours. Logic is a "department of study." As to whether I am an "exceptional man" in this department, look to the third volume of Schröder's Logik, where I am named upwards of two hundred times, beginning with page 1, where I am called the "Hauptförderer" of "eine grossartige Disziplin," the "Logik der Beziehungen überhaupt."
