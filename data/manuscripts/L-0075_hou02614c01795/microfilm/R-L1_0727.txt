No 20 [u]Of Critical Logic, in general[/u]. This memoir is intended to establish the precise nature, divisions, and method of critical logic. The nature and conditions of doubt investigated.

No 21 [u]Of First Premisses[/u]. I here undertake to [del]show[/del] [ins]demonstrate[/ins] that the only justifiable first premisses are perceptual judgments, that is, judgments that a present percept presents a certain appearance. Certain well known and obvious objections are examined. Kant's "deductions" examined, and his position shown to be untenable. The test of inconceivability discussed. It is fully admitted that what a man under given conditions cannot help believing is not amenable to criticism, from his stand-point, as long as those conditions subsist.
