Hon. H.D. Peirce,

Washington, D.C.

My dear Sir,

I am informed that an appeal has been made to the Trustees of the Carnegie Fund to further in some way the publication in systematic form of the results of the work of Mr. Charles S. Peirce in logic and Philosophy.

I cannot venture to make a suggestion to the Committee, with whose plans I am, of course, unacquainted. But I feel that it is right that I should express my conviction of the high value of Mr. Peirce's work, and my hope that it may be possible to give him the desired assistance. I am sure that it would meet with the approval of teachers of philosophy all over the country.

If my expression of opinion can be of any service at all in the matter, you are welcome to use it as you see fit.

Believe me,

Very truly yours,

Geo. S. Fullerton.

[date]Jan. 5, 1902.[/date]
