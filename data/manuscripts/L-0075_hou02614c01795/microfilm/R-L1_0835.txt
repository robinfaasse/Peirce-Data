[u]Probable Net Cost[/u].

If the Carnegie Institution should adopt some such
plan as that I have ventured to suggest, they will ad-
vance me something like a professor's salary for
five years, and in return will have the product of
my pen for that time. Now Mill's Logic went through
nine or ten editions. To be sure, it was not so volu-
minous as my memoirs will be, was written by a
literary man, and was just deep enough to please
people who were not very accurate in their thinking.
