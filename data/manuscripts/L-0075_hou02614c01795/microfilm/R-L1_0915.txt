19 East 47th St
[date]June 4th[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce

I am still confined to bed, so have not myself been able to call upon Dr. Billings. But I spoke with Dr. Jacobi about the matter who said that you certainly came under the conditions for the beneficiaries of the Carnegie funds. And then I sent my daughter to see Dr. Billings, and he told her that he knew all about you, and your name was now under one of those now under consideration by the Committee or whatever it is decides.
