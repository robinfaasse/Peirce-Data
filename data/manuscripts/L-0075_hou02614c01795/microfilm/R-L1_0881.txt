No 23. [u]On the Validity of Induction[/u]. This memoir contains
substantially the doctrine of my paper in the Johns
Hopkins "Studies in Logic," but stated more convincingly
and fully in essentials while putting the formalistic
parts into the background. Other theories will be more
fully examined. The theory will be applied to modes of induction
[del]not considered in the old paper and of quite another nature.[/del]
