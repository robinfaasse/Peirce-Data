No 18 [u]On Propositions[/u]. The question of the nature of the Judgment is the question most actively debated today of any in logic. It affords, I think, the most favorable occasion for a description of the present state of logic. I shall endeavor to give a faithful account of the positions on this and allied questions (and almost all the questions that are actively discussed are allied to this) of every logician of importance. I set forth and defend my own position

I shall then pass on to an elaboration of the analysis, classification, symbolization, and relations of propositions.
