40 LAFAYETTE PLACE,
NEW YORK.

[date]June 6 1902[/date]

Dear Mrs Jacobi.

Your note of June 4[ins]th[/ins] in regard to Mr Peirce is received and will be borne in mind when the question of granting aid to him comes up for discussion. When I said to your daughter that I knew all about him I was referring more to his published papers and the character of his studies than to details of his private life, although I have heard much of the latter also.
