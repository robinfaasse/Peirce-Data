No 2 [u]On the Simplest Mathematics[/u]. That is, the system of mathematics which recognizes but two grades of value; a system of high importance for logic.

No. 3. [u]Analysis of the Conceptions of Mathematics[/u]. Number, Multitude, Limit, Infinity, Infinitesimals, Continuity, Dimension, Imaginaries, Multiple Algebra, Measurement, etc. My former contributions, although [del]but partial have attracted attention in Europe; but my priority in respect to some accepted views has been overlooked. I defend the method of infinitesimals while not attacking that of limits.[/del]
