[marginalia]Trowbridge[/marginalia]

Cambridge, [date]Jan. 2, 1903[/date].

Dear Mr. Gilman:

Professor James M. Peirce has asked me to support the claims of his brother Charles for recognition by the Carnegie Institution.

I do not know what Mr. Charles S. Peirce asks for, but I suppose that the philosophers would put him forward as one of their best exponents, and if Philosophy is to be recognized by the Carnegie Institution, I know of no philosopher who comes so near to having a raison d'etre as Charles S. Peirce.

* * * * * * * *

Cordially yours,

(signed) John Trowbridge.
