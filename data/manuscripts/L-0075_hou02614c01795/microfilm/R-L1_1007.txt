The Philosophical Review.
EDITOR'S OFFICE.

CORNELL UNIVERSITY,

Ithaca, N. Y., January 17, 190 5.

Professor James Mills Peirce:

Cambridge, Mass.

My dear sir:-

I was glad to write a letter in behalf of the appeal made to the Trustees of the Carnegie Institute to assist your brother, Mr. Charles S. Peirce, in the further working out and publishing his theories of logic and general philosophy. I heartily concur with those who have presented the appeal, that it is of real importance, both to science and to philosophy, to have your brother's views stated in a clear and systematic way by himself.

I therefore lost no time in dispatching a letter, as your letter suggested, to the Hon. [ins]Herbert[/ins] [del]H.[/del] D. Peirce, Department of State, Washington, D.C.

Yours sincerely,
J. E. Creighton
