Carnegie Institution
WASHINGTON, D. C.

[date]Dec 30 1902[/date]

My dear Mr Peirce:-
I would gladly read and discuss your manuscripts with you but I dare not promise to do so for every day and some nights are filled with duties that must be done. That is why I wish you were comfortably housed right here with books galore at your command.

Have you not certain chapters or books or parts of your great work ready for the printer or at least ready for final revision? If you have and send it [ins]or them[/ins] to me I will store it, in the safe (which has been ordered) when we get it and look it at it when I can.

And meanwhile pray accept the cordial greetings of one who [del]very[/del] [ins]would[/ins] gladly stand as midwife and godfather to your great brain child

Heartily Yours
Marcus Baker
