COLUMBIA UNIVERSITY
IN THE CITY OF NEW YORK
DEPARTMENT OF
PSYCHOLOGY AND ANTHROPOLOGY

Garrison-on-Hudson, N.Y. [date]January 25, 1901[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

It certainly seems to me that some of the income of the Carnegie endowment might be used to advantage in enabling you to complete your work in logic. This will be a great contribution to science. I will write to Dr. Gilman as you request. A letter from me to Mr. Carnegie would, I fear, be of no advantage.

Very truly yours,
[fig]signature of J. McK. Cattell[/fig] J. McK. Cattell

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.
