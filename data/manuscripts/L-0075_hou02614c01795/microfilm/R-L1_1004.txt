Personal.

White House
Washington.

January 12, 1903.

My dear Senator Lodge:

Referring to your letter of the 11th instant, in the case of Mr. Peirce, I beg to send to you herewith a copy of the President's note to Dr. Gilman, as you request.

Very truly yours,

George B. Cortelyou,

Secretary to the President.

Hon. H.C. Lodge,
United States Senate.
