No 1. [u]On the Classification of the Theoretic Sciences of Research.[/u]

"This will be a natural classification, not of possible sciences, but of sciences as they exist today; not of sciences in the sense of "systematized knowledge", but of [del]sing[/del] branches of endeavor to ascertain truth. I shall not undertake to prove that there is no other natural classification of the sciences than that which I give; and this being merely an introductory memoir, cannot have the same convincing character as the others. Every unitary classification has a leading idea or purpose, and is a natural classification in so far as that same purpose is determinative in the production of the objects classified.
