to materialism that matter could not feel, I grant that it is
worthless.

If my arguments are sound, an idea is not a mere aspect.
Then I am bound to say what sort of being it has. This I postpone
to the next memoir. At present I wish to consider objective
logic, by which I mean the logical processes of ideas
acting upon the external world. I promise to give a
sketch of this sort of logic, if it can be called logic.
