18. [u]On Propositions[/u]. No question of logic has of late years
received more attention than that of the nature of the
judgment. I here propose to show that my method
satisfies all conditions. The doctrine is that the pro-
position, that is, the meaning of [ins]the matter of[/ins] [del]a[/del] judgment, is a
sign (regarded as identical with any full interpretation
of it) which separately designates its object. Thus a
portrait with the name of the person portrayed under
it, expresses a proposition. This proposition need
not be asserted. Assertion is a separate act by which
a person makes himself responsible for the truth of
