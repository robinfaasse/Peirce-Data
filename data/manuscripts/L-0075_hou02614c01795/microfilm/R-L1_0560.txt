[u]To the Executive Committee of the Carnegie Institution[/u],
Gentlemen:

I have the honor to apply for aid from the Carnegie Institution in bringing into available form the results of certain researches into logic. I will proceed
[u]1[sup]st[/sup][/u], to explain what these researches are;
[u]2[sup]nd[/sup][/u], to indicate how their importance may be estimated;
[u]3[sup]rd[/sup][/u], to consider how far the aid of the Carnegie Institution is needed or desirable for the attainment of the purposes;
[u]4[sup]th[/sup][/u], to suggest a plan by which suffici[del]ci[/del]ent aid might be extended;
[u]5[sup]th[/sup][/u], to estimate the probable ultimate total cost to the Carnegie Institution of such aid so extended;
[u]6[sup]th[/sup][/u], to inquire whether and how far this plan affords a reasonable guarantee that the work will, in a reasonable sense, be brought to completion.
