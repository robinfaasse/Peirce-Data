[pn]No 12[/pn] [u]On the Definition of Logic[/u]. Logic is [u]formal semiotic[/u].
A sign is something, A, which brings something, B, its
interpretant sign, determined or created by it, into the
same sort of correspondence (or a [ins]implies[/ins] [del]lower[/del] sort) with some-
thing, C, its [u]object[/u], as that in which itself stands to C. This
definition no more involves any reference to human
thought than does the definition of a line as the place
within which a particle lies during a lapse of time. It is
from this definition that I deduce the principles of logic
by mathematical reasoning, [del]which[/del] [ins]and by mathematical reasoning that, I aver,[/ins] will support criticism
of Weierstrassian severity, and [del]is[/del] [ins]that[/ins] is perfectly evident.
