[u]=ine[/u]. Termination belonging to certain elements
forming the 7[sup]th[/sup] Group of the 2[sup]d[/sup], 3[sup]d[/sup], 5[sup]th[/sup], 7[sup]th[/sup] Series.
Fluorine, Chlorine, Bromine, Iodine.

This termination is also applied to
the names of alcaloids, and other organic
bases and derivatives of ammonia; as
[ins]carbamine, acetonine,[/ins]
[del]aniline[/del] aniline, quinine, morphine, creatine, arsine,
etc. The alcaloids formerly sometimes took the
termination [u]ia[/u] or [u]ea[/u] or [u]eia[/u] as morphia,
codeia. The termination [u]=ine[/u] is also [ins](rarely)[/ins] applied
to the acetylene series of hydrocarbons, ethine for
[del]acetylene etc.[/del]

[u]=in[/u]. Neutral substances, especially extracts from
natural substances, as Gelatin, (But irregu-
larly pepsine) fibrin, casein, pectin, stearin
(an ethereal salt), glycerin (an alcohol),
tannin (an acid). [ins]acrolein (an aldehyde)[/ins] To the last three and
many others the termination was assigned
when they were supposed to be neutral &
it has adhered to them.

[u]=on[/u] applied only to the three elements, carbon,
boron, silicon, which are somewhat
similar.

[u]=ogen[/u] applied to the elements Hydrogen, Oxygen,
Nitrogen; also to Cyanogen C[sub]2[/sub]N [ins]glycogen[/ins] etc.
