I can only speak as chemical nomenclature.
For Zoological, [del]biological[/del] [ins]botanical[/ins], mineralogical,
medical consult others.

Chemical nomenclature, ([del]the system[/del] [ins]modern[/ins]) was
invented by M. Guyton de Morveau. First
described in the work "Méthode de
nomenclature chimique, proposé par
MM. de Morveau, Lavoisier, Berthollet,
et de Fourcroy." Paris, [date]1787[/date].

The system has since been much modi-
fied especially by the adoption of proposals
of Kekulé, Hoffmann & others.

[u]ium[/u] or [u]=um[/u], the termination given to the
names of all metals except the ancient
ones. The only metals not carrying it are
Manganese, Iron, Cobalt, Nickel, Copper, Zinc,
Arsenic, Silver, Tin, Antimony, Gold, Mercury,
Lead, Bismuth.
