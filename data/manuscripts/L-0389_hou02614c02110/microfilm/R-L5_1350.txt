San Diego California,
[date]Aug. 30. 1886.[/date]
Professor Chas S. Peirce.
Dear Sir,
The San Diego Society of
Natural History is making a
small collection of likenesses
of men distinguished in the
sciences, and would be
pleased to add yours to the
collection if you will kindly
present it. An engraving or
photograph would be prized.
Very respectfully yours,
G. W. Barnes,
(Pres, Soc. Nat. Hist.)
