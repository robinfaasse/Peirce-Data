Amherst, Mass.
[date]10th July, 1889[/date]

Dear Professor Peirce,

I write this morning
that Mendenhall is appointed to the Coast
Survey.

What likely direction of this is the
swinging of pendulums in West Africa?
Or is it too early for you to say?

The Navy Department has instructed
me to go ahead with the preparations for
the Expedition. A Naval Cruiser will
be placed at our disposal, and I hope
to get away by the 1st October.

If possible, I hope to arrange matters
so that other points on the West Coast
besides St. Paul de Loanda will be
visited; if it so, there will be a chance
for much needed pendulum work.

The Expedition will be absent from the
country for six months, and may touch
at Cayenne, French Guiana, going out—
though this latter is as yet undetermined.

So soon as you are ready to advise,
I shall be glad to hear from you.

Very Truly Yours
David P. Todd.

P.S.
Approximate location of Eclipse-station
$\phi = -9^\circ 15'$  $\lambda = 13^\circ 50'$ E. of Gr.
