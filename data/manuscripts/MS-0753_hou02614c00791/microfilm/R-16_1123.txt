reasoning the justification for it depends upon
the consideration of what the [del]for[/del] process of
inquiry is. For that reason, we must begin by
examining an example or two, — one at the very
least, — of such reasoning. And it must be
a real example and not a humbug example,
such as you will find in Macaulay's Essay on Bacon
which proves nothing at all. No it proves one
