As for deductive, or necessary, reasoning, it
needs no justification at all, because the
conclusion appears evident. That is to say, the
reasoner has no doubt about it. Now a doubt
is a real state of mind. This is one thing to say
you doubt, and another thing to doubt, just
as it is one thing to hold a handkerchief to your
face at a funeral & wait till you are in the
carriage before the jokes begin to fly, and quite
another thing to experience real grief. If you
don't really doubt, there is no use at all in criti-
cizing the procedure; because all that such criti-
cism can do is to remove the doubt one way or
the other. You may reëxamine the reasoning.
