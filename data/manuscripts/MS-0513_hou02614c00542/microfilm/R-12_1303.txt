[u]XII.[/u]

The Algebra of Dyadic Relatives was well explained by me in
outline in the book "Studies in Logic" and Schröder has developed
it in his third volume at such enormous length that it is not worth
while to devote much space to it here.

The Boolean calculus turns upon the three operations of
negation, aggregation, and composition. There are three somewhat
imperfectly analogous operations of a relative kind. By a rela-
tive operation I mean such an operation that the relate and cor-
relate of the function are differently ordered from those of the variable.

The non-relative operation of composition or non-relative logical
multiplication is defined thus:
$(l\cdot b)_{ij} \prec 0$ if $l_{ij} \prec 0$ or $b_{ij} \prec 0$ But
$0 \prec (l\cdot b)_{ij}$ if both [del]$\dagger$[/del] $0 \prec l_{ij}$ and $0 \prec b_{ij}$
The operation of [u]relative multiplication[/u] is defined thus:
$(lb)_{ij} \prec 0$ if [del]all[/del] $\Pi_k\ l_{ik} + b_{kj} \prec 0$
$0 \prec (lb)_{ij}$ if $0 \prec \Sigma_k\ l_{ik}\cdot b_{kj}$
We read $lb$ "$l$ of $b$"

The non-relative operation of aggregation is defined thus
$(l+b)_{ij} \prec 0$ if both $l_{ij} \prec 0$ and $b_{ij} \prec 0$
$0 \prec (l+b)_{ij}$ if either $0 \prec l_{ij}$ or $0 \prec b_{ij}$
The operation [del]of[/del] [ins]of[/ins] [u]relative addition[/u] is defined thus:
$(l\dagger b)_{ij} \prec 0$ if $\Sigma_k\ l_{ik}\cdot b_{kj} \prec 0$
$0 \prec (l\dagger b)_{ij}$ if $0 \prec \Pi_k\ l_{ik} + b_{kj}$
We read $l\dagger b$ "$l$ except of $b$s"
