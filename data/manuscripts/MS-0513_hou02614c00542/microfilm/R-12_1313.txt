The following are the most important formulae:
$$\Pi_u, \Pi_v, \Pi_w$$

If $u \prec v$ $u \cdot w \prec v \cdot w$ $w \cdot u \prec w \cdot v$
" " " $u + w \prec v + w$ $w + u \prec w + v$
" " " $\bar{v} \prec \bar{u}$

$(u \cdot v) \cdot w = u \cdot (v \cdot w)$
$(u + v) + w = u + (v + w)$

[del]Then[/del] $(u + v) \cdot w \prec u \cdot w + v \cdot w$ $w \cdot (u + v) \prec w \cdot u + w \cdot v$
$(u \cdot v) + w \succ u + w \cdot v + w$ $w + (u \cdot v) \succ w + u \cdot w + v$

If $u \prec v$ $u w \prec v w$ $w u \prec w v$
" " " $u \int w \prec v \int w$ $w \int u \prec w \int v$
" " " $\tilde{v} \prec \tilde{u}$,

$(u v) w = u (v w)$
$(u \int v) \int w = u \int (v \int w)$
