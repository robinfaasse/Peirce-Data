Hence we write
$$\Pi_{\alpha} \Sigma_{i} q_{\alpha i} \qquad \text{and } \overline{\Pi_{\alpha} \Sigma_{i} \overline{q}_{\alpha i}}$$
where $\alpha$ belongs to the universe of qualities neither absurd nor necessary.
And in like manner
$$\Pi_{\alpha} \Pi_{i} \Pi_{j} \Sigma_{k} r_{\alpha i j k} \quad \text{etc. and } \overline{\Pi_{\alpha} \Pi_{i} \Pi_{j} \Sigma_{k} \overline{r}_{\alpha i j k}} \text{ etc.}$$
where there is nothing peculiarly necessary nor impossible about any
of the indices. So too,
$$\begin{aligned}
\Pi_{i} \Sigma_{\alpha} q_{\alpha i} \qquad &\text{and } \overline{\Pi_{i} \Sigma_{\alpha} \overline{q}_{\alpha i}} \\
\Pi_{i} \Pi_{j} \Sigma_{\alpha} r_{\alpha i j} \quad &\text{etc. and } \overline{\Pi_{i} \Pi_{j} \Sigma_{\alpha} \overline{r}_{\alpha i j}} .
\end{aligned}$$
[del]But[/del] And so likewise
$$\begin{aligned}
\Pi_{\alpha} \Pi_{i} \Sigma_{\beta} r_{\alpha \beta i} \qquad &\text{and } \overline{\Pi_{\alpha} \Pi_{i} \Sigma_{\beta} \overline{r}_{\alpha \beta i}} \\
\text{and } \qquad \Pi_{\alpha} \Pi_{i} \Pi_{j} \Sigma_{\beta} r_{\alpha \beta i j} \quad &\text{and } \overline{\Pi_{\alpha} \Pi_{i} \Pi_{j} \Sigma_{\beta} \overline{r}_{\alpha \beta i j}}
\end{aligned}$$
Since $\alpha$ can be any character (not peculiarly necessary nor impossible)
let us so take it that
$$r_{\alpha \beta i j} = r_{\beta i} \cdot r_{\beta j} \qquad \text{or} \qquad r_{\beta i} \cdot \overline{r}_{\beta j}$$
and we have
$$\Pi_{i} \Pi_{j} \Sigma_{\beta} r_{\beta i} \cdot r_{\beta j} \quad \text{and } \overline{\Pi_{i} \Pi_{j} \Sigma_{\beta} r_{\beta i} \cdot \overline{r}_{\beta j}}$$
But the last proposition would be selfcontradictory unless $j$ were restricted
to being other than $i$; so that we must write for it
$$\Pi_{i} \Pi_{j} \Sigma_{\beta} r_{\beta i} \cdot (\overline{r}_{\beta j} + 1_{i j})$$
So in the other proposition $\Pi_{i} \Pi_{j} \Sigma_{\beta} r_{\beta i} \cdot r_{\beta j}$, it must be understood
that $i$ and $j$ must not be the very reverse of one another in all respects.
We thus see that such a proposition as $\Pi_{\alpha} \Pi_{i} \Pi_{j} \Sigma_{\beta} r_{\alpha \beta i j}$ will mean
very little when we undertake to bring it down to a special case.
