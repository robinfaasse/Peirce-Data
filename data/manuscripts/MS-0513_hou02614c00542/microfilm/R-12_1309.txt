It will be remarked that
$(\frac{1+G}{2})^{2} = (\frac{1+G}{2})$ $(\frac{1-G}{2})^{2} = (\frac{1-G}{2})$ $(\frac{1+G}{2})(\frac{1-G}{2}) = (\frac{1-G}{2})(\frac{1+G}{2}) = 0$.
$(\frac{A-A^{-1}}{\sqrt{3}})^{3} = \frac{1-3A+3A^{-1}-1}{3\sqrt{3}} = -(\frac{A-A^{-1}}{\sqrt{3}})$ $(\frac{A-A^{-1}}{\sqrt{3}})^{2} = A+A^{-1}-2$
$(\frac{1+G}{2})(\frac{A-A^{-1}}{\sqrt{3}}) = (\frac{A-A^{-1}}{\sqrt{3}})(\frac{1-G}{2})$ $(\frac{1-G}{2})(\frac{A-A^{-1}}{\sqrt{3}}) = (\frac{A-A^{-1}}{\sqrt{3}})(\frac{1+G}{2})$

If therefore we give to $g, h, i, j, k, l$ the values
[del]$g = \frac{1+G}{2}$ $h = \frac{1-G}{2}$[/del] $g = (\frac{1+G}{2})[1+(\frac{A-A^{-1}}{\sqrt{3}})^{2}]$ $h = (\frac{1-G}{2})[1+(\frac{A-A^{-1}}{\sqrt{3}})^{2}]$
$i = \frac{(1+G)}{2}\frac{(A-A^{-1})}{\sqrt{3}}$ $l = \frac{(1-G)}{2}\frac{(A^{-1}-A)}{\sqrt{3}}$
