What shall the notation be? To that question I have never obtained
a satisfactory solution. But I have some hints.

The operation of multiplication generalized to its furthest may
be defined as that operation upon two operands (or if you please
an operator and an operand) by which every unit of the one is
[del]combined[/del] [ins]paired[/ins] with every unit of the other to form a unit of the product.
As there are two kinds of pairs, the ordered and the unordered, so
there are two kinds of multiplication, the non-commutative and the
commutative. Thus, the product of two numbers is the number of pairs
which can be formed each from a unit of the one and [ins]of[/ins] other factor.

The operation of involution, say $b^a$, is the operation [del]for[/del] [ins]of[/ins] which the
units are the different ways in which all the units of $a$ may be
paired each with a unit of $b$. Thus $2^3$ is the number of these diagrams
