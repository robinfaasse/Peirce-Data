But we cannot go beyond two with [del]the[/del] [ins]the algebra of[/ins] dyadic relatives. With the
general algebra we can write $\Pi_1 \Sigma_2 \Sigma_3 \Sigma_4 l_{12} \cdot l_{13} \cdot l_{14} \cdot \Upsilon_{23} \cdot \Upsilon_{24} \cdot \Upsilon_{34}$ and so

So $\Pi_1 \Pi_2 \Sigma_3 \Pi_4 l_{31} \cdot l_{32} \cdot (\bar{l}_{34} + l_{41} + l_{42})$ is beyond the powers of
the dyadic algebra, this expressing that any two persons are loved by
somebody who loves nobody else, and that every person is loved by somebody
who loves nobody else.

assertion to every set of [ins]not more than[/ins] three persons.
