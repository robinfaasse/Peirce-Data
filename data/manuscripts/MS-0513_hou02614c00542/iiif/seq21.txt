For example, suppose
$\Pi_{a} \ \overline{q}_{Pa} + l_{ab} + l_{ac}$
$\Pi_{A} \ q_{QA} + \Upsilon_{AB} \cdot \Upsilon_{AC} \cdot \Upsilon_{AD}$

Then taking the third power of
$\Pi_{\delta} \Sigma_{\gamma} \ \Upsilon_{\delta \gamma} \cdot q_{P \gamma} + \overline{q}_{Q \delta}$

and multiplying by the above we gets
$\Pi_{\delta} \Sigma_{\gamma} \ \Pi_{\delta'} \Sigma_{\gamma'} \ \Pi_{\delta''} \Sigma_{\gamma''}$
$\Upsilon_{\delta \gamma} \cdot q_{P \gamma} \cdot (l_{\gamma b} + l_{\gamma c}) \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta' \gamma'} q_{P \gamma'} (l_{\gamma' b} + l_{\gamma' c}) \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta'' \gamma''} q_{P \gamma''} (l_{\gamma'' b} + l_{\gamma'' c})$
$+ \ \overline{q}_{Q \delta} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta B} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta C} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta D} + \overline{q}_{Q \delta'} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta' B} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta' C} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta' D} + \overline{q}_{Q \delta''} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta'' B} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta'' C} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta'' D}$

which gives
$q_{Pb} \cdot q_{Pc} (\Upsilon_{\delta b B} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta c C} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta e D} + \Upsilon_{\delta b C} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta c B} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta e D} + \Upsilon_{\delta b D} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta e B} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta c C}$
$+ \Upsilon_{\delta c B} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta b C} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta e D} + \Upsilon_{\delta c C} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta b B} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta e D} + \Upsilon_{\delta e D} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta b B} \cdot \Upsilon_{\delta b C})$

But this result cannot be obtained without giving $\Pi_{i} \Sigma_{j} \ \overline{q}_{Pi} + \Upsilon_{ij} \cdot q_{Qj}$
Consequently, there is no way in which the proposition in question can be
necessitated. Hence, it cannot be true.
