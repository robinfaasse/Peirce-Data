Rienzi's story is soon completed. In [date]1351[/date], tired of peni-
tence, he went to Prague, saw Charles IV, and proposed that
they two should share the world between them. The
emperor's answer was to seize Rienzi and send him for
trial to the pope in Avignon. Condemned to death, he
was saved again by Petrarch's intercession, and his
sentence commuted to confinement. At length, a
new pope, Innocent VI, in [date]1353[/date], sent him to second
Cardinal Albornoz in reëstablishing order in Rome.
The Romans welcomed him with enthusiastic affection.
