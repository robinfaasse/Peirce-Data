without bloodshed, without the raising of a finger!
Rienzi now assumed great magnificence, and at the
same time developed marvellous capacity for business.
Clad daily in scarlet apparel, his face and aspect
inspiring awe, he answered every petition before a man
would have believed he could apprehend its meaning,
and the number of letters he wrote was astonishing.

At this time, he organized a "House of Justice and
peace", where enemies came together, acknowledged the
evil they had wrought, asked pardon, kissed, and made friends.
Thus, one man had blinded another's eye. He came, expressed
contrition, wept, and begged the latter to put out his eye. The
other, touched, forgave him, and they departed friends. Al-
together, noble feeling and hope abounded, as in July 1790. Then,
a panic seized upon all criminals and malefactors. Thieves,
homicides, adulterers, and all persons of ill fame fled the
city.
