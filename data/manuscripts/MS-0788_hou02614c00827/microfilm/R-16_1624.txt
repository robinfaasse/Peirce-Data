By the order of a proposition I mean the number
of times it contains the relative p_ or "possessor of"
(in the sense of an object possessing a quality.) Pro-
positions of even orders are either Extensive or Intensive.
Those of odd orders have not this distinction

[u]Propositions of 1[ins]st[/ins] Order[/u]

[col]
1 ⪯ Ă p † β
1 ⪯ Ă (p † β)   1 ⪯ (Ă † p) β
1 ⪯ Ă p † β   1 ⪯ Ă † p β
1 ⪯ Ă p β
[/col]
[col]
1 ⪯ Ă † p̄ † β
1 ⪯ Ă (p̄ † β)   1 ⪯ (Ă † p̄) β
1 ⪯ Ă p̄ † β   1 ⪯ Ă † p̄ β
1 ⪯ Ă p̄ β
[/col]

[u]Syllogisms of the 0-1 Order[/u]
i.e. those having premises of the 0 and the 1[ins]st[/ins] Order.

Suppose one of the premises is β ⪯ γ or 1 ⪯ β̄ † γ.
Then γ may be substituted for β in the other premise
provided β occurs without a dash over it. If in the
premise of the first order β is connected by relative
addition; that is if that premise is one of the
following
[col]
1 ⪯ Ă † p † β
1 ⪯ Ă (p † β)
1 ⪯ Ă p † β
[/col]
[col]
1 ⪯ Ă † p̄ † β
1 ⪯ Ă (p̄ † β)
1 ⪯ Ă p̄ † β
[/col]
