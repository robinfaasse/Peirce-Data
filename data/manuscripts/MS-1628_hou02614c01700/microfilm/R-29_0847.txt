CITY ITEMS.
WE HEAR that the Parade Ground,
east of B. Street is about to be clear-
ed of stumps and drained, in prepar-
ation for the monument to Kosciusco
presented by our patriotic fellow-cit-
izen Silas Dolittle, Esq. We think
the least tribute we can offer in
return will be to name it in his
honor forever, Dolittle Square.
The skating continues excellent in Lafayette
Avenue. As Warsawians we were gratified to per-
ceive that our lovely townswomen approved yesterday
in Polish boots.

WARSAW ATHENAEUM.
The LIBRARY now consists of up-
wards of Forty Volumes, embracing the
whole range of Human Inquiry, with all the
Modern and ancient languages. Catalogues
raisonnés to be had at the door. Price 25 cents.
The Admission to the Library is free to Block-holders;
Admission for the million 25 cents a head.
The Geological, Mineralogical and Botanical Cabin-
ets have been collected with great care by Prof.
SAmboldt. Admission 25 cents.
The MUSEUM of Antiquities and Numis-
matics contains besides innumerable other
articles of interest a complete series of coins
from the earliest ages of our republic. Admis-
sion 50 cents. Children under 12, half price.
There is also a museum of CURIOSITIES
comprising Indian Arrow-Heads, Minute Watches,
Autographs of Distinguished Personages, &c. Ad-
mission 12 1/2 cents.
One of the grand features of the Establish-
ment is a GALLERY OF STATUARY
comprising about 0002 pieces — Originals and
Copies — all executed in wax. Admission 37 1/2 cts.
Visitors are requested not to handle the Statues.
But the pride of Warsaw is the GALLERY
OF PAINTINGS by native artists. Paintings
by GASTON & SOPHIA JUNG, JAMES DEKAY
&c. Admission 62 1/2 cents. Children under four
years of age, not admitted.
Connected with the Athenaeum, is a concert-
hall, where on SATURDAY FEB 14, will be given
