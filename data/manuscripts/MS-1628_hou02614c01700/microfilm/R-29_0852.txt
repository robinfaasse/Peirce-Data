Our readers may not be
aware of the old custom of
sending Valentines on the 14th
of February. We submit the fol-
lowing as a specimen, of the way
they used to do things when the
Editor of this Gazette was not
An old Bachelor.

To P......

My first with motive power
Has lightened many an hour
Writing thy name.
It has not carved with word
Much better than with sword
My way to fame.

My second is no measure
Of how I love my treasure
Yet know I well
That should I give an inch
Of praise, she would not flinch
To take an ell.

My third doth applied
To give my lov'd one's eyes
Unmeasured scope
To drive to desperation
Or, with an aspiration
Changes to hope.

My whole in life and fame
Is worthy the great dame
Whose name she bears.
Oh! to that constant mind.
That loveliest of her kind.
Aspire dares
"ULYSSES"
