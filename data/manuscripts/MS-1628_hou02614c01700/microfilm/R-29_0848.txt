REPORT
OF THE LECTURES
OF DR. P. D. S. B. TIDDLUMKINS
ON BRAIN-FEVER.
LECTURE II.

Miss Tiddleumkins is a strong-minded young
lady, rather above the medium height, imperceptibly
inclined to fulness, and under 21 years of age.
Her delivery is pleasing, but not a gesture or inton-
ation above what the grandeur of the thought required
cast a slur upon her elegance. Her only mannerism
or "prettiness" as they are very improperly called
is perhaps a sparkling of the eyes to hide the flat-
lowness of meaningless remarks; but this is so seldom
called into requisition that a careful observer and un-
usual opportunities are required to observe it.
Miss Tiddleumkins said:
Lady- and Gentleman-friends of truth! Brain-
fever like happiness is only comparative. We none
are destitute of it, we none of us have it. For, as
we say good, bad, and indifferent we are perfectly
authorized in saying well, sick, and so-so. The well
like the good, is ever beyond the actual; and the ut-
terly sick, like the utterly bad, is only the negative
of an idea; but all men are indifferent and so-so.

FASHIONS FOR FEBRUARY, by
Genie C. Scott

At the late ball in Bogusville,
the dresses were truly recherche
and tasteful. Crinoline was large
and fully employed to add grace
and beauty to the lovely forms
of the ladies, and a seal is thus
put upon this hitherto doubt-
ful fashion. Many skirts of the
finest white cambric and
exceedingly full, and trim-
med with row upon row of
of red wafers, were very el-
egant. Others were spotted
all over with this very beau-
tiful ornament and presen-
ted a very novel effect.
Others were fully trimmed
with evergreen wreaths.
The coiffures were gener-
ally exceedingly neat, for
young people. A few dis-
played last winter's combs
and large, and richly or-
namented specimens of
these conveniences gave an
air of true elegance to the
back of the head. The hair
was tied tightly on the very
crown, and a long ringlet
fell on each side of the face.
The caps of the elder ladies
were profusely trimmed
with broad ribbons in prom-
inent bows.
White kid slippers were
seen on many ladies, but
the very beautifully polished
black morocco were the most
common. White silk gloves
and scalloped handkerchiefs
completed the costume.
