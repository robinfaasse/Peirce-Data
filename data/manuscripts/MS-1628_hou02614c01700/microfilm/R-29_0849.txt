It has become our painful duty
to announce to the kind readers of the
Warsaw Times the sudden death of its
esteemed Editor, Charles Joseph Felix Frasto

He was in possession of his faculties up
to this morning when he suddenly ex-
pired in the act of reading a Valentine.
The medical Faculty were at once
called in when Doctor Tiddlewinks
expressed his opinion that the death
was caused by a sudden recurrence
of brain fever, with which he suffered
last winter. An internal examination,
however, showed that this was not the
case, and Doctor Janks on reading the
Valentine was suddenly siezed with
most violent convulsions and palpita-
tions. A servant then testified to hear-
ing Prince Felix previously observe, That
the Valentine was infused with a subtle
and deadly poison. An apparently low
melancholy had lately oppressed him
and the conclusion seems unavoidable
that he selected this awful and impres-
sive manner of ending his existence.
His death will be a shock to all who knew
him, for by all who knew him he was en-
deared by his attention to ingenuous and
even simple nature, and the invariable
kindness of his heart. His life contained
many a thrilling adventure and was passed
in scenes which at once startled and char-
med his feelings. His memoir will probab-
ly be written by some of his friends.
It may be interesting to those who have
wept or laughed over the Warsaw Times, to
read the last production of his gifted pen:-

Eternal terror! tell me pray,
When coldness wraps this suffering clay-
The soul's better half,
When whither strays the immortal mind,
And leaves the beautiful behind,
Ah, can, can it laugh?

To rue, rue its birth?
Or doth some kindly lamp then burnest,

To eye back the earth?

We have clipped the above from
that valuable paper, the Bogusville
Times. We recommend Hypolita to the
Bogusvillains. They attempted to give
a ball the other night, of which the only
full and authentic account is given in
another column. They lodged "the cream
and elite of the best society of the
world" in barns and sheds. The next
morning the Mayor invited those who
had come from London, Paris, and Japan
to stay to breakfast!!
It is notorious that two ostriches
and a griffin live in a rocky cave in
Bogusville. And we understand that
a crusade against the latter is pre-
paring by the young Gentlemen of
the seminary near by.

7.30 AM. COUNTERFEITING
A man named T. Shaw,
alias William, was arrested for
counterfeiting. He was
found with person to the
amount of $10,000,000.

We are gratified to learn
that Simon Spook, Esq., has
taken possession of Budwell's
Lodge, and intends making
Warsaw his home. About
20 English is about to be
naturalized.

These Gentlemen, the
former of whom has a
large family, will add much
to the society of Warsaw.

Mrs. Spook writes pleasing verses
and will no doubt become a
contributor to the Warsaw
Times.
