Special Correspondence of the
Warsaw Times.
Bogusville. [date]Feb 13.[/date]
Dear Mr. Editor
At your request I will
endeavor to the best of my
abilities to give you an ac-
count of the Grand Bogus-
villain Ball. There were 1000
persons present, gathered from
the cream of the best soci-
ety of the world, and con-
centrated in the wealthy (??)
town of Bogusville. The
most conspicuous persons
present were Lady Wash-
ington, Mrs. Hamilton, (in
her green stockings) Prin-
cess Powliskatowski, George

Washington, Mrs Ham-
ilton, Prince Herfou-
bogen, Lord Litch-
field, Lady Arabella
Vicars with her daugh-
ter Miss Abby West-
minster. The even-
ing was beautiful,
the ice-cream de-
licious, and the com-
pany generally in
very good spirits; and
in fact everything
seemed to contrib-
ute except the gas
which gave out -
to make the even-
ing pass off pleas-
ant. The first
dance was led by
Miss Jones, who
was decidedly the
Belle of the even-
ing, eclipsing by

the splendor of her
toilette and beauty,
all elite of the fash-
ionable worlds of Paris
and London. The
supper was very good
as many can testify
consisting of all the
delicacies of the season
including the
of coque robins dressed
à la Pacific, with plen-
ty of wine, which showed
its effects upon the heads
of sundry young captains
and cornets of the Eng-
lish navy. The rest of the
evening was spent in
dancing; and everybody
went home to the
sheds and barns pro-
vided by the inhabitants
of the overcrowded ci-
ty. From
One Present
