Cambridge, [date]June 14/93[/date]

My dear Peirce:

My knowledge of lecture bureaus and of managers is exceedingly slim, my experience of them having been brief and unsatisfactory. One manager of considerable fame let me earnestly warn you against; I mean Major J. B. Pond, of New York. I had him as manager for about which the birds pecked at.

I was in New York in the spring and consumed some clams with Kellar Martin and Otis the prestidigitateur, and we talked of getting you to come in town and join us in a jamboree. But like so many bright thoughts, it didn't come to maturity.

Faithfully Yours
John Fiske.
