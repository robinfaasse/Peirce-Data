Cambridge, [date]Jan. 4/92[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Pray accept my thanks for "A Rich's Storehouse". I shall value it highly, for I have a warm spot in my heart for the old Strasburg rector. It was very kind of you to send it to me.

Sometime when you are here, if I recall it at the right time, I may ask you to embellish the fly-leaf with your calligraphy.

No, I don't bother with agents. I write a few months beforehand to the people in different places, and arrange dates, prices, & subjects; and it is an infernal bore.

The worst part of it is that it is absolutely impossible to make a man comprehend the fact that I lecture in any other town but his town, and therefore can't give him his free sweep through all the dates in the calendar.
