HOUGHTON, MIFFLIN AND COMPANY, PUBLISHERS.

Boston, [date]Jan. 27, 1894.[/date]

Dear Mr. Fiske:-

We are much disappointed at not receiving any more manuscript from you to-day. Mr. Houghton is very nervous in regard to the History. We want to settle at once on the size of page, size of type, etc., etc., etc. May we not receive from you on Monday at latest the last installment of your manuscript? We are all very fearful that the book will have to hang over for another year. Will you not kindly put your shoulder to the wheel and do what you can to expedite matters?

Yours very truly,
Houghton, Mifflin & Co.
H. N. W.

Mr. John Fiske,
122 East 19th Street,
New York City.
