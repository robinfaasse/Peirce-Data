In such a state as that which prevailed in the dark ages, when society was almost broken into small bands, now warring, now allied, now for a time one subordinated to another, man loses his sense of truth and of reason. If he hears one man assert one thing and another man the contrary, he chooses his side and sets to work by all means in his power to silence his adversaries. One has only to read the [u]Historia Calamitatum[/u] of Abaelard to perceive that this tolerably well describes the situation while the dispute between the Realists and Nominalists was at its height.
