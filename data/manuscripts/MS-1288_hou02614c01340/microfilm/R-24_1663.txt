This rule is recognized in the requirement of physicists that a theory
shall furnish predictions, which shall be verified, before any particular
weight is accorded to it. The medical men, too, who deserve special mention
for the reason that they have had since Galen a logical tradition of their own,
recognize this rule, however dimly, in their warning against reasoning
"post hoc, ergo propter hoc."

Some modern Germans refuse [ins]matter[/ins] to recognize the rule; but, whatever
the cause of it may be, it is a fact that Germany has long been the home
of logical fogs and fallacies. In order to show what sort of reasoning
