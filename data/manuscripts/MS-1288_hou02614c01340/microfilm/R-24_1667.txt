Most of the modern systems of metaphysics, [ins]or nature[/ins] such as that of
Hegel, maintain that the world of existence is the total real
world. Very different was the opinion, for example, of so matter of
fact an ancient scientist is Aristotle, for whom there is a first
and second being. Most of the modern philosophers regard time
as false in so far as it represents events in the future as
not [del]being[/del] existing as fully as those in the present, or the present
as not having the [del]generaliz[/del] ripeness of generalization which
belongs to the past. But the successful sciences do not attempt to
criticize our fundamental cognitions. To do so would seem to a physicist
like trying to raise yourself by your own weight.

It is hard to convince a mathematician that there is not
a kind of reality in an imaginary quantity.
