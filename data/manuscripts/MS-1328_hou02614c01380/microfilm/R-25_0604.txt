To my mind the originality of the epoch [date]A.D. 1200[/date] was simply
the logical result of the widespread belief of the tenth century that
the world would be destroyed [date]A.D. 1000[/date]. For in the first place this
belief was bound to be followed by a surprise, and that of an
exalting tone. That necessarily created a rationalistic
tendency which in two centuries greatly developed man's intellect
and [del]is[/del] imparted high selfconfidence - The logical result of high
selfconfidence and pride is humiliation. Humiliation brings self-
consciousness and selfstudy. Selfstudy under humiliation brings
the desire to learn [del]and[/del] to correct one's errors and to [del]enlarge[/del] [ins]remove[/ins] one's
ignorance. Now the earnest wish to correct one's errors is the
principal factor of originality.
