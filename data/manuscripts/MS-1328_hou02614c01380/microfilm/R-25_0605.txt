conclusion, a lofty roof. A lofty roof implied slender columns.
Now how were these slender columns to be acceptably treated. Only
by clusters of cylinders, — another highly original idea, to which
the gothic owes about half of its character. What a wonderful train
of thought this was! How strong & simple in every step. The effect
of a gothic church is to embody that intense yearning for something
higher, that aspiration, that [u]sursum corda[/u], which marks the
fall of pride. In the first simplicity of the style, this sentiment
in it is very touching.
