The Nation, 20 Vesey Street (P. O. Box 794).

New York, [date]March 24 1907[/date].

Dear Mr. Peirce, —

I shall be glad to send you the Roger Bacon if the book comes to us. May I ask you to let me have a card of reminder when you see the title listed among Books of the Week.

Very truly you —

Paul E. More.
