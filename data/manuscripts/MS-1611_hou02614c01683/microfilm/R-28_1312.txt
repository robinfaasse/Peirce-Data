Name in full: Charles S. Peirce [bracket] I am variously known in print as C. S. Peirce, Charles Saunders Peirce, Charles Sanders Peirce. Under the circumstances, the committal S. suits me best [/bracket]

Mode of address: C. S. Peirce, [del]at[/del] P. O. Milford, Pa. Express address: Port Jervis, N. Y. Wells Fargo Express

Home address: Arisbe, P. O. Milford, Pa.

Dep't of study: Logic

Place and date of birth: Cambridge, Mass. [date]1839 Sep. 10[/date].

Education and degrees: Brought up in Cambridge in the scientific circle, Epes S. Dixwell's school, Harvard Undergraduate course, Scientific school, Chemistry. Three months with Agassiz studying classification. Educated in metrology etc. by my father and others. Profited much from studies with Chauncey Wright. German chiefly by ear, Italian of Ferrero & Frankfort, Greek by self. Mathematics of my father, Clifford, Sylvester, Story, etc. Metaphysics chiefly from books and reflection. Other branches by self. A.B. and A.M. and S.B. (in chemistry summa cum laude)

Positions with dates: [del]Assistant in the U. S. Coast & Geodetic Survey for 30 years[/del]

Temporary or minor positions: Lecturer in Harvard & Johns Hopkins, and Lowell Inst. Commissions, U. S. Delegate to Gradmessung, etc.

Membership in societies: American Academy of Arts & Sciences, and National Academy of Sciences.

Honors conferred: Never any, nor any encouragement or aid of any description in any life work, excepting a splendid series of promises.

Books with publishers: Photometric Researches (Leipzig: Wilhelm Engelmann) Edited Studies in Logic by Members of the Johns Hopkins Univ. (Boston: Little, Brown & Co.) [del]B. Peirce's Linear Associative Algebra[/del]

Chief subjects of research: Logic.

Where chiefly published: Not published except in slight fragments. See Baldwin's Dict. of Philosophy & Psychology, art. Logic.

Researches in progress: In logic will continue as long as I retain my faculties and can afford pen & ink.
