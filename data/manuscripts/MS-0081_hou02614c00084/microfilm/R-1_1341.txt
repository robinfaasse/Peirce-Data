Notes on the Fundamentals of Algebra

The copula is the first symbol. Supposing there is but one copula
it can be shown that its definition would be,

1. $x \prec x$ whatever $x$ may be
2. If $x \prec y$ and $y \prec z$, then $x \prec z$.
3. If $x \prec y$ and $y \prec x$, then $x$ and $y$ are indistinguishable,
([ins]and we write[/ins] $x = y$.)

The letters usually have material significance. But some have a
merely formal significance being evolved from algebra itself. Such
are
$0$, or zero, which is thus defined,
$0 \prec x$ whatever $x$ may be.

$\infty$, or general infinity, which is thus defined,
$x \prec \infty$ whatever $x$ may be.
[del]If[/del] $x \prec 0, x = 0$. If $\infty \prec x, \infty = x$.

[u]Ligation[/u]

Ligations of letters are either simple or branching.
Example of simple ligation $x \prec y \prec z \prec u \prec v \prec w$
Example of branching ligation $x \prec \left\{ \begin{matrix} y \\ z \end{matrix} \right\} \prec u \prec \left\{ \begin{matrix} v \\ w \end{matrix} \right\}$

[u]All branching ligation may be reduced to bifurcation[/u] $x \prec \left\{ \begin{matrix} y \\ z \\ w \end{matrix} \right\} = x \prec \left\{ \begin{matrix} y \\ x \prec \left\{ \begin{matrix} z \\ w \end{matrix} \right. \end{matrix} \right.$

[u]Simple Ligation[/u].

Simple ligation is of the following kinds
Supposing [del]l[/del] $l \cdot m \prec x \cdot y$
1. Such that $l \cdot x \prec m \cdot y$. This is called [u]multiplication[/u].
2. Such that $l^y \prec m^x$. This is called [u]progressive involution[/u].
3. Such that $^m x \prec ^l y$. This is called [u]regressive involution[/u].
[del]Such that $m \cdot y \prec l \cdot x$.[/del]
