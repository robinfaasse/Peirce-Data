Without your permission I did not
want to take the responsibility of
leaving the book in the hands of
the book-binder, and out of my
sight for ten days. But I can
see no other way of having the
work done. The vellum has to be
soaked and it takes long to dry.
The binder, a respectable party
recommended to me by Miss Borden
of the University Library for whom
he does work. Shall I leave
the book in his hands?

Hoping to hear from you

I am Respectfully Yours,
Isaac Myer.
(Over)
