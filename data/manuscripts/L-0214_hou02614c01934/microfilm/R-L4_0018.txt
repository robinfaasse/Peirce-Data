[pn]V E[/pn]

Milford Pa
[date]1910 July 24[/date]

My dear Sir;

I desire to thank you for sending me your examination of Neumark, so that you may understand that anything from your pen would interest me. I am totally ignorant of old & century Jewish philosophy and nearly so of Arabic philosophy. I deplore my ignorance but being now 71 years of age, with much unpublished of my studies of reasoning, and being dreadfully trammelled in thinking them owing to my inability to [del]of[/del] quote the pro-

-vovals, naturally have been at about the distance of a sheet of papyrus; and I guess (since it is of course necessary to begin with a guess) that the average length of his shortest pieces (which are not far from being of equal length) may indicate how large his [del]pieces of papyrus were. If[/del] [ins]sheets[/ins] they were placed side by side and pasted together, [del]they would[/del] and then rolled up, the juxtaposed pieces would again be likely to be at [del]the[/del] distance having some relation to the size of a sheet. I have far too much on hand to think of undertaking such a study even if I had a fine enough source of languages. But it strikes me as a possible way, I mean as possibly being a way to improve some readings.
