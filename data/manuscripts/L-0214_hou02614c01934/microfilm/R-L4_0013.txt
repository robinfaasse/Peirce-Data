Phila. [date]June 22, 1904[/date].

Mr. Chas. S. Peirce,

Dear Sir,

I appreciate highly your kindness in allowing me to keep the Ockham till Oct. 1st but as I do not indeed, for the present at least, to make use of it I shall return it to the proper address in a few days. I should have liked to have it bound, but as you advise me in your last letter to send it as it is if I do not keep it till October, I shall follow your advice.

I am not at present particularly interested in Ockham and do not intend to pursue this line
