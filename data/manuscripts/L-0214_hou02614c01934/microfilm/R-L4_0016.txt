My dear Doctor Harris:

I have read your paper on the Categories only once, and while I was myself driving a horse. I intend, as soon as I have time, to go through both treatises in order to get all I can from your observations. Meantime, I will say that it is [del]so[/del] long since I have read a [del]criticism[/del] [ins]paper[/ins] so thoroughly sound.

There are only two points that I think weak. In the first place, although the [ins]strong[/ins] presumption is that the postpredicaments is by Aristotle, yet it certainly does not seem here on a sheet. As well as I remember it was 65. Then the question arose, whether these often received injuries beginning [del]1[/del] [ins]e.g.[/ins] at the last sheet and extending to the second & third. I made some progress but had not the leisure to continue. I mention it only as a suggestion that you may some time feel like taking up.

very truly
C. S. Peirce
