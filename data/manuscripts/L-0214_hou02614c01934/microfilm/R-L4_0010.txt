Mr. Chas. S. Peirce,
Dear Sir,

On receipt of your
letter, in which you very kindly offer
to lend me the rare volume of Occam
in your possession, I went to see Dr.
Smith of the University, who showed
me the written promise [ins]had[/ins] [del]you[/del] sent him.
I promptly refused the promise after having
read it over, but had omitted to make a
copy for myself. Will you be kind
enough to leave a copy of the promises
enclosed in the Occam volume.

Allow me once more to express my
obligations to you and with this to remain

Respectfully Yours,
Isaac Husik.
