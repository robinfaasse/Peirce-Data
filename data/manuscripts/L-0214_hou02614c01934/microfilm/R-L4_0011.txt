616 Morris St.
Phila. [date]June 15, 1904[/date].

Mr. Chas. S. Peirce,

Dear Sir,

I am through with the [del]Ocean[/del] [ins]new[/ins] edition which you were kind enough to lend me. I want to have the binding repaired as you request and with this object went to see a book-binder this afternoon. He says that the book has to be rebound, a new leather back will have to be made and therefore will have to be packed on top of that. The process, he says, takes ten days.

P.S. As ten more days will bring it very close to the end of the month and I have to return the book, as per promise, before the fourth of July, a prompt reply will be gratefully appreciated.

J. S.
