By C. S. Peirce

Existential graphs is not an entire new language, but is a new grammar, or syntax. But it is not a lingual syntax, but a diagrammatic syntax. It is a system of diagrams built out of proper names, verbs, heavy connecting lines, and enclosing ovals.
