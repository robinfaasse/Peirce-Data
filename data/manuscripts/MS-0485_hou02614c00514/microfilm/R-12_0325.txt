Existential Graphs.

Rules of Illative Transformation.

[u]Fundamental Rules[/u].

[u]Rule I[/u]. [del]The[/del] [ins]The[/ins] effect of writing down a graph is to assert it; and nothing not written is asserted.

[u]Rule II[/u]. The effect of writing two graphs together is such that every unenclosed part can be transformed just as if it were alone.
