[u]§2. What assertion is.[/u] [del]Signs[/del] A sign, or representamen, is [del]some-[/del]
[del]thing[/del] which brings an [ins]interpreting[/ins] idea into relation to an [del]thing or[/del] object.

Signs are of three kinds, [u]icons[/u], [u]indices[/u], and [u]symbols[/u]. An [del]icon[/del]
is [ins]a sign[/ins] related to its object by resemblance or analogy. An icon does [del]not[/del],
in itself, discriminate between itself and its object, [del]and[/del] [ins]or[/ins] does not repre-
sent its object [ins]in so far as different from itself[/ins] to be anything more than an idea. An [u]index[/u] is [del]a[/del]
sign related to the interpreting mind by forcing an experience
upon [del]to[/del] it. There can be no force without resistance; there is always
a bearing down of the will of the mind to which the index is presented.
