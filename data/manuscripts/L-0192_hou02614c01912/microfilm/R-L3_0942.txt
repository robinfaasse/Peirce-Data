Boston, 17 October, 1898

Mr. Chas. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:

We have a contract with Professor Royce of Harvard to make a logic for us, but he finds it more and more difficult to undertake the work, and has suggested that you might be willing to, and thinks you would make us a good and usable book. Do you care to consider it?

Yours respectfully,

DC Heath
