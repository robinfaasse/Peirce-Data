The ordinary course is designed to
meet the needs of those young men and
women who have been debarred from
the privileges of a College education, and
even from the training preparatory to it.
Although it is not pretended that this
praxis [del]is[/del] [ins]is[/ins] an adequate substitute for a
full education, yet such persons will
find that faithful performance of it will,
in some important respects, more than
compensate them for what they have
missed. Abridged and modified courses
are arranged for college graduates, law
students, clergymen, etc.
