36 W. 15[ins]th[/ins]. St. New York
[date]1887 Jan. 11[/date].

Right Rev[ins]d[/ins]. and dear Sir:

Permit me to draw your favor-
able attention to my enclosed circular
concerning instruction by correspondence
in the art of reasoning. I address
myself particularly to the needs of
those who are unable to obtain a
regular college education. For them,
I know well, my course is of more
real service than any secular in-
struction within their means. Indeed,
for those who are destined to a hard
struggle with the world, it is perhaps
more useful than all they could
