<u>Note A</u>.

In order to show that our spelling was substantially
the creation of the century following the [del]general[/del] [ins]multiplication[/ins]

tables the history of the spellings of certain words
as given in the Oxford Dictionary. These words are
the first words, on or following the round hundredth

and which are not marked as obsolete or unnatu-
ralized.
