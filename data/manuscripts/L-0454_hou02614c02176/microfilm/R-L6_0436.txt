Experiments by Dr. Sorby and by Daubrée have been adduced
showing that such independent rotation of particles may take place in
consequence of a mass in which they are contained being subjected
to certain forces; but I think it is pretty clear that nothing of the
sort happens, but that the particles are merely turned in the rotational
movement of the whole mass, or of that part of it in which they are
situated.

If, however, a mass contains aeolotropic bodies and is subjected to une-
qual pressures in different directions, there would be a tendency for them
to turn into the position of minimum stress. But such a tendency would
probably be less than the friction to be overcome.
