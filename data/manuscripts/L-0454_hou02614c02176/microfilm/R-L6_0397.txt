DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
UNITED STATES GEOLOGICAL SURVEY
WASHINGTON, D. C., [date]Jan. 16, 1897.[/date]

Sir:

In compliance with your letter of January 14th, consenting to
inquire into the difference of opinion which has arisen between
Messrs. Becker and Van Hise as to the origin of slaty cleavage, I
have forwarded to your address the following memoirs:

Principles N. A. Pre-Cambrian Geology, by C. R. Van Hise,
1896, with appendix by L. M. Hoskins;

Finite Homogeneous Strain, by G. F. Becker, 1893;

Finite Stress-Strain Function " " 1893;

Schistosity and Slaty Cleavage, " " 1896.

I also draw your attention to a memoir by Mr. Alfred Harker,
Brit. Assoc, Rep., 1885; p. 813, which contains a very full ac-
count of inquiries on this subject up to the time of its publica-
tion.

I request that you will prepare your report with a view to its
being published.

Yours very truly,

Director.

Prof. Charles S. Peirce.
