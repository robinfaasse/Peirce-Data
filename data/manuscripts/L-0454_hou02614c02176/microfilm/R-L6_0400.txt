I have not at any time questioned Mr. Becker's mathemat-
ical work. The difference is in the application of the mathemat-
ics to a complex geological problem. This, it seems to me, is
fundamentally a geological question, to be decided by geologists
in the field rather than by [u]a priori[/u] reasoning. I shall be very
glad to know what Mr. Peirce concludes for I have the highest
opinion of his mathematical ability, but I still think that the
difference between Mr. Becker and myself must be decided by geolo-
gists. I therefore am content to await the consensus of opinion
which they may reach.

Very respectfully,

(Signed) C. R. Van Hise,

Geologist in Charge.
