[u]II 2 [greek: gamma][/u].

What miscellaneous consequences of the theory that the same parallelism
of cleavage-planes and flatness is largely due to a flattening [ins]out[/ins] of the
whole mass have been shown to agree with observed features of the
phenomenon?

The distortions of fossils seem to show this incontestibly, as Sorby
stated the case; and Daubrée has artificially produced the same effect
[ins]nearly[/ins] in accordance with this theory.

Besides, Diller (p. 639) and Keith (p. 640), as quoted by Prof. Van Hise,
give examples of crushing planes 45° from the planes of rock-cleavage,
which Van Hise shows are the planes of crystal-cleavage and flatness.

There are other evidences; but the above appear to be sufficient.
