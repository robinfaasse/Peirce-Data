Case 31. The second player occupies a corner square
adjoining the occupied square. The first player has a choice
between 7 plays, as numbered in the figure, making cases 311,
312, 313, 314, 315, 316, 317.

Case 311. The first player occupies the corner adjoining his
side square. The second player makes tit-tat-toe by occupying
the side square adjoining his corner.

Case 312. The first player occupies the vacant side square
adjoining the occupied corner. There is no tit-tat-toe unless
the second player occupies a corner, when the first player makes
tit-tat-toe.
[fig]3x3 grid with O, X, X, and O[/fig]

Case 313. The first player occupies the centre. The
second player occupies the side square opposite that
of the first player. There is no tit-tat-toe.

Case 314. The first player occupies the side square
having its adjoining and opposite squares vacant. The
second player makes tit-tat-toe by occupying first
the corner adjoining two vacant side squares, and then the centre.
