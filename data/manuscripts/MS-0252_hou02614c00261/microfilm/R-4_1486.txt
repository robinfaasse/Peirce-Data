Case 11. The second player occupies a corner square
There are now 4 essentially different situations which
the first player can occupy which are numbered in
the figure. We distinguish them as cases, 111, 112, 113, 114.

Case 111. The first player occupies a compartment [del]next to[/del] [ins]adjacent[/ins]
adjoining that of the second player. Then, to prevent an im-
mediate tit-tat-too, the second player must occupy the op-
posite side compartment. [fig]3x3 grid with X, O, and numbers[/fig] There are now five squares
[ins]either of[/ins] which the first player can occupy, as numbered in
the figure. They make cases 1111, 1112, 1113, 1114, 1115.

Case 1111. The first player occupies the [ins]other[/ins] side
square [del]next to[/del] [ins]adjoining[/ins] the second player's corner square. Then the second
player, to prevent instant tit-tat-too must occupy the opposite
compartment. Now the first player may either oc-
cupy one of the corner [fig]3x3 grid with X, O, and numbers[/fig] squares adjoining a side
square occupied by him (Case 11111) or the corner
square opposite the second player's corner square (Case 11112.)

Case 11111. The second player is obliged to occupy the opposite
corner square and the first player to occupy the last, and there
is no tit-tat-too. [fig]3x3 grid with X and O[/fig]

Case 11112. The second player occupies any square, and
there is no possibility of tit-tat-too. Thus there is no tit-tat-too
in Case 1111.
