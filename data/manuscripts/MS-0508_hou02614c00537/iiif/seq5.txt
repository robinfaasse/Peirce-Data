<u>Permission No 8.</u> [u]All the above permissions apply to all spots and[/u]
[u]to the line of identity, as Graphs; and Permission No 2 is to be under-[/u]
[u]stood as permitting the erasure of any portion of a line of identity[/u]
[u]on the sheet of assertion, so as to break it into two. Permission No. 3[/u]
[ured]is to be understood as permitting the extension of a line of identity on the[/ured]
[ured]sheet of assertion to any unoccupied part of the sheet of assertion.[/ured]

<u>Permission No. 9.</u> [u]It is permitted to [del]draw[/del] [ins]scribe[/ins] an [del]unattached[/del] line of[/u]
[u]identity on the sheet of assertion, and to join such unattached lines in any[/u]
[u]number by spots of tri-identity. This is to be understood as permitting[/u]
[u]a line of identity, whether within or without a cut, to be extended to the cut,[/u]
[u]although [del]at[/del] [ins]at such[/ins] [del]the[/del] extremity of a it is to be understood to be on both sides of the[/u]
[u]cut. But this does not permit a line of identity within a cut that is on[/u]
[u]the sheet of assertion to be retracted from the cut, in case it extends to[/u]
[u]the cut.[/u]

<u>Permission No. 10.</u> [u]If two spots are within a cut, (whether on its area or[/u]
[u]not), and are not joined by any ligature within that cut, then a[/u]
[u]ligature joining them outside the cut is of no effect and may be[/u]
[u]made or broken. But this does not apply if they spots are joined[/u]
[u]by other hooks within the cut.[/u]

<u>Permission No. 11.</u> [u]Permissions Nos. 4 and 5 do not cease to apply[/u]
[u]because of ligatures passing from without the outer of two cuts to within[/u]
[u]the inner one, so long as there is nothing else on the annular area.[/u]

Permission Nº3 must not be understood that because it is permissible to 
scribe a graph without certain ligatures therefore it is permissible to 
scribe it with them, or the reverse.
