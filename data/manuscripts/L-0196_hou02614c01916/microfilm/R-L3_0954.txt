Prof. C. S. Peirce
Hotel Brunswick
Boston.

Dear Sir

Your brother tells me that you have worked on electric bleaching and have some valuable knowledge on the subject. As I am at present engaged in the same line, I would like to talk to you & see if you can help me out. I may be able to find an outlet for your process. I am at home 13 Lexington Ave. any afternoon by 5.30. Please let me know when you are here & I will call on you.

Yours truly

Peter Cooper's Glue Factory,
pr Edward R. Hewitt.
