My dear Sir:

I am sending you a copy of the
Monist for October on account of its
mentioning a division of Signs put forth
by me in [del]1867[/del] [ins]May 14, (Proc. Am. Acad.[/ins] [del]of Arts & Sci. p. 294 et seq)[/del] [ins]and which[/ins]
seems to me to be substantially the
same as a division made by you
& defined psychologically. I am
opposed to any psychological
definition of such a thing; but
if the psychological definition could
be abandoned, it would greatly
please me if the division could find
so reputable a sponsor as to be
called Stout's division. This is
one of ten according to me; but it
is in certain respects particularly
important. In the interest of Science,
