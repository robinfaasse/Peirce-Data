[u]UNIVERSAL EXPOSITION, ST. LOUIS[/u]

[u]1904[/u]
[u]INTERNATIONAL CONGRESSES[/u]

HOWARD J. ROGERS, DIRECTOR OF CONGRESSES

ST. LOUIS, U. S. A.

Cambridge, Mass., July 12, 1904.

Dr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

My dear Sir:--

The Congress of Arts and Science is now starting to make preparations for the short papers, of which five or six are desired in every Section. The official addresses, which are to form a unity and which are thus on prescribed topics, have been arranged for; the programme, with the names of these Speakers and with the names of the Chairmen, has been sent to you, or will be sent, to you in a short while, from the central bureau. The short papers are intended to form such parts of a connected whole, but are to be specialistic contributions on topics to be chosen by those who receive official invitations as speakers. I have been authorized to send to you herewith such an invitation to give a paper of perhaps ten minutes in one or both of the Sections: 1c, Logic; and 1d, Methodology. I beg that you will let me know soon whether you will be able to accept this invitation, and, if possible, what your topics will be.

Very truly yours,

Hugo Münsterberg
