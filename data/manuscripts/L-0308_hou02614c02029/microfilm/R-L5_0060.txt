Clifton, Mass., [date]August 6, 1904.[/date]

My dear Mr. Peirce:--

I am extremely sorry to be obliged to report to you, as to many others, a recent vote of the Committee of the Congress excluding all papers not presented by the authors themselves. The Committee is afraid that papers read by proxies will lack that element of personal interest which ought to stimulate the meetings. We thank you sincerely for your kind offer, but, as we must have uniformity of treatment, we are obliged not to accept it. I am very sorry that you cannot come out.

Yours very sincerely,

Hugo Münsterberg
