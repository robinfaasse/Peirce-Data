Induction & Hypothesis have been very much confused by
writers on the subject; but it seems only necessary to call
attention to the vast distinction between them to have it recog-
nized at once. Hypothesis asserts something known to be unknown.
We know that we do not know light to be ether waves. Induction
only carries an fact to what may be unknown. If there are
any cloven hoofed animals besides neat swine sheep & deer then
they also are herbivorous. Thus, induction is a conditional hy-
pothesis.

Induction substitutes a wider subject for a narrower one.
Hypothesis substitutes a more determinate predicate for
