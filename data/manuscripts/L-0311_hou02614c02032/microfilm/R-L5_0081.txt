December 26, 1923

Mrs. Charles S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Madam:

Your letter of last month regarding the biography of your husband, the late Professor Pierce was duly received. We are sorry to hear of your accident and trust that your recovery will be speedy.

The newspaper clipping which you enclose gives scarcely anything regarding Professor Pierce's family history, and I am very much disappointed that you do not send the questionnaire with the information supplied, as you promised to do in a previous letter. I am preparing a very complete and interesting statement of his professional and philosophical work but this Cyclopedia is essentially a collection of histories of our famous American families and I assure you it is customary to give the marriage records in all instances. I have the name and date of marriage of his first wife. This newspaper clipping gives your maiden name.

Wont you kindly reconsider my request in the light of the above information and send me the following facts:

The date of your marriage to Professor Pierce.

Your home before coming to this country.

The date of the death of his first wife, Harriet Fay, and the name of the children, if any.

Yours very truly,

GEORGE DERBY [ins]George Derby[/ins]
Managing Editor.
