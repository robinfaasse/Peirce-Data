THE NATIONAL CYCLOPEDIA
OF AMERICAN BIOGRAPHY.

July 16/96.

#287 B.

Prof. Charles S. Peirce,
P.O. Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:-

Enclosed please find a typewritten copy of the Biographical Sketch of yourself prepared for the National Cyclopaedia of American Biography. It is so important a publication that we do not think it proper to print it without having it revised, and we beg to ask you to co-operate with us in perfecting it. There may be some facts that we have overlooked in our endeavor to keep it within our space, and which upon consultation it might be desirable to add. Will you therefore please make any changes necessary and return it to me at an early date.

As Portraiture is the demand of the day, and makes a Biography so much more complete and intelligible, it is a feature of the work to have nearly every sketch embellished with a Portrait, making it a National Portrait Gallery. These are not cheap process plates, but are reduced from large pen and ink drawings, made by skilled artists, which gives them their fineness.

To have your Biography properly presented and in keeping with this plan, I ask you to enable the Publishers to make this Portrait. They are not willing to charge you for it, but as it costs quite an amount, they feel that if they assume the expense, they are justified in asking you to co-operate with them at least to the extent of taking a copy of the work, which you will be glad to possess for its intrinsic worth, and will be sure to purchase when placed upon the market.

Your Biography will appear whether a subscription is given or not, but in such a lasting work which has already been placed in nearly all the Great Libraries here and abroad, and which the Press has endorsed as the Biographical Authority of the Century it is a mistake to have it issued without the Portrait. I very much want this Portrait, and I have induced the Publishers to consent to make your Portrait for an order payable at the rate of $10.00 per month for the Volumes now published, and the balance as issued, about one a year, which will not tax any income. See accompanying Descriptive Prospectus. We also send you under separate cover, an Index of our Published Volumes to show you the plan, magnitude and scope of our work.

Please return the corrected Sketch at once, as I wish to have it appear in the next Volume, and I trust you will enclose a Photograph and Autograph for the Portrait.

Very truly yours,
James E. Homans
Managing Editor.
