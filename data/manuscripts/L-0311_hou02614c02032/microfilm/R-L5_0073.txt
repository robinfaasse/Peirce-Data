Mrs. Peirce had another
severe fall injuring her
right hand again, there-
fore the delay.

I found in your proof
sheet no mention was
made of the fact that
Mr. Peirce was one of
the oldest members of
the National Academy
of Science. He lectured
at Harvard 64-65, 69-70.
