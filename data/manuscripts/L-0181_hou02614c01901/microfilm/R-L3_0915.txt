C. S. Peirce,

My dear Sir,

In the light of Lobatschewsky & Bolyai, there must be some easy way of showing the fallacy in the following pseudo-proof of Euclid's Axiom.

Can you give me a hint or a reference?

[fig]Geometric diagram with horizontal lines and a transversal[/fig]

Band AOPB is infinitesimal compared with quadrant of infinite plane.

For AOK any finite angle, surface AOK has a finite ratio to the [del]latter and[/del] [ins]quadrant[/ins] is [ins]therefore[/ins] infinite compared with [del]same quadrant[/del] [ins]band AOPB[/ins].

$\therefore$ surface AOK > band AOPB.

$\therefore$ Euclid's postulate.
