[date]Dec. 17th 1895[/date]

Charles S. Peirce,
My dear Sir,
I send you by this mail, care of "The Nation", a copy of my Bolyai's Science Absolute of Space entirely rewritten.
I have been so fortunate as to be able to announce important additions to the knowledge of the history of these most interesting researches and the lives of the two Bolyais. With high regard
Yours always sincerely
George Bruce Halsted.
