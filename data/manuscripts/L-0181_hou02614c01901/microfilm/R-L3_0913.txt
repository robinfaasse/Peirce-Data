Austin, [date]Dec. 7 1891[/date].

Charles S. Peirce,
My dear Sir,
I was very much interested in your paper on "Astronomical methods of determining the curvature of space," read before the National Academy of Sciences, and if you distribute any reprints of it, I would be much obliged for a copy.
Yours very sincerely
George Bruce Halsted
