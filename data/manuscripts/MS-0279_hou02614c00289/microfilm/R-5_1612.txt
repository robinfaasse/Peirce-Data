Scholastic Latin furnishes us with a beginning of a termi-
nology of logic that is excellent as far as it goes, — not in an
absolute sense, but as languages go. It is, for example,
far better than the traditional terminology of grammar. The
English language is remarkable, as compared with French and
German, for two qualities that concern us. The first is, that it
