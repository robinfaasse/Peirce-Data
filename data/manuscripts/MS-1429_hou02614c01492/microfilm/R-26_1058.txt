Whether theorizing about duties ever can do enough good to outweigh all the crimes that are chargeable to it, or not, it cannot be denied that there are two delightful and innocent [del]part[/del] branches of prolegomena to ethics, the description of the facts of moral life and the logical analysis of the different conceptions relating to it.
