[pn]Review of Scott's Letters[/pn] [pn]N-1894-3[/pn]

These letters unequally cover the time from [date]1797[/date], when
Scott, twenty-six years old, was known only as a young
barrister of fair prospects and the author of some
poetical translations from the German, down to [date]1825[/date],
the year before his earthquake of calamity. They are
conveniently separated into periods, at the beginning
of each of which is inserted a little chronological
table of family events and literary achievements
