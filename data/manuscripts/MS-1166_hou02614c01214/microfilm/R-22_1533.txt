The following phrases are used in metaphysics. [u]Active power[/u] [Gr. dynamis tou poiein, poietikē, poiēsa] power in an agent of creating or of working some change in an object, operativity. [u]Agonistic power[/u], power in strife. [u]Animate power[/u], a faculty of the soul or mind. [u]Appetitive power[/u], a faculty of desiring. [u]Apprehensive power[/u] [Gr. dynamis mathēsei] faculty of cognition. [u]Artificial power[/u], an art considered as a power. [u]Augmentative power[/u], the power of growth. [u]Cognoscitive power[/u], the same as apprehensive power. [u]Commanding[/u], [u]directing[/u], and [u]executive powers[/u], three faculties of the mind, in the psychology of Aquinas, of which the first determines what shall be done, the last does it, and the second secures the correspondence of the action with the intention. [u]Connate power[/u], [Gr. dynamis suggene] a faculty possessed from birth, not developed by education. [u]Power of contradiction[/u], the power in an individual of being determined to one or the other of two contradictory predicates; the corresponding power in a genus to be determined to one or other of two species is not called by this name. [u]Corporeal power[/u], the virtue of an inanimate substance or thing. [u]Creative power[/u], the power of creating. [u]Entitative power[/u], the same as existential power, which see. [u]Essential power[/u], power in an essence to receive actual existence. [u]Existential power[/u], power in a thing that actually exists to do or become something. [u]Free power[/u], a faculty which the mind is free to exercise or not. [u]Generative power[/u], the faculty of propagating the kind. [u]Habitual power[/u], [Gr. dynamis ethei] power resulting from custom. [u]Impassive power[/u] [Gr. dynamis ametablēte] the power of resisting a force tending to produce a charge. [u]Inanimate power[/u], a power not belonging to the soul. [u]Irrational power[/u] [Gr. dynamis alogos] [del]pøøøøøøøøøøøø[/del] a faculty not belonging to the part of the soul that distinguishes man from the beasts. [u]Locomotive power[/u], the power of locomotion in animals.
