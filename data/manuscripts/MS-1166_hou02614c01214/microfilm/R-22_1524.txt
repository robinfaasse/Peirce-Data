it might mean the assumption (from the beginning of the argumentation) of the conclusion; the clas-
sical English rendering is begging the question. A fallacy or fault of reasoning belonging to ar-

degree of probability pretended, the fault consisting in the assumption of a premise which no
person holding the [del]REPRESENT[/del] antagonistic views will admit. This is, therefore, a fallacy which
one cannot fall into in sincere reasoning with oneself, but only affects controversial reasoning;
and controversial writings are without exception a mere tissue of such fallacies.
