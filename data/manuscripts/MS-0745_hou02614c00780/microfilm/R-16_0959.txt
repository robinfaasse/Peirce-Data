In dual relatives, there are four species of propositions:
$$\Pi_i \Pi_j u_{ij} \quad \Sigma_i \Pi_j u_{ij} \quad \Pi_j \Sigma_i u_{ij} \quad \Sigma_i \Sigma_j u_{ij}.$$

We have, in general,
$$\Pi_i \Pi_j = \Pi_j \Pi_i$$
$$\Sigma_i \Sigma_j = \Sigma_j \Sigma_i$$
$$\Sigma_i \Pi_j \prec \Pi_j \Sigma_i.$$

The last is the most important formula of logic.
But we have
$$\Sigma_i \Pi_j u_i v_j = \Pi_j \Sigma_i u_i v_j$$
$$\Sigma_i \Pi_j (u_i + v_j) = \Pi_j \Sigma_i (u_i + v_j).$$
