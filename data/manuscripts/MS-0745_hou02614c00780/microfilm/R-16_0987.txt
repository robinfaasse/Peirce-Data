[u]Syllogistic, or the algebra of the copula[/u]. The
type of inference is
P
[three dots forming a triangle, meaning "therefore"] C

The two types of proposition are
P [copula symbol, looks like a curved <] C      P [copula symbol with a horizontal bar over it] C.

The properties of the copula are
1[sup]st[/sup]. If A [copula symbol] B and B [copula symbol] C, then A [copula symbol] C.
2[sup]nd[/sup]. A [copula symbol] A.
3[sup]d[/sup]. If A [copula symbol] B and B [copula symbol] A, then A = B.

Contraposition and indirect syllogism.
Properties of the negative. The principles
of contraposition, contradiction, and ex-
cluded middle. All syllogism depends on
the principle of contradiction, all dialogism
