In dual relatives there are four species of propositions; viz:

$$\Pi_{i}\Pi_{j}\ l_{ij}\ \ \ \Sigma_{i}\Pi_{j}l_{ij}\ \ \ \Pi_{j}\Sigma_{i}l_{ij}\ \ \ \Sigma_{i}\Sigma_{j}l_{ij}\ .$$

We have, in general,

$$\Pi_{i}\Pi_{j} = \Pi_{j}\Pi_{i}$$
$$\Sigma_{i}\Sigma_{j} = \Sigma_{j}\Sigma_{i}$$
$$\Sigma_{i}\Pi_{j} \prec \Pi_{j}\Sigma_{i}\ .$$

[marginalia][u]The last is the most important formula of Logic.[/u][/marginalia]

But we have

$$\Sigma_{i}\Pi_{j}\ u_{i}v_{j} = \Pi_{j}\Sigma_{i}\ u_{i}v_{j}\ .$$
$$\Sigma_{i}\Pi_{j}\ (u_{i}+v_{j}) = \Pi_{j}\Sigma_{i}\ (u_{i}+v_{j})$$
