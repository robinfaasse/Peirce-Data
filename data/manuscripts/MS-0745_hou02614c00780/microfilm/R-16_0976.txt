Lecture I.

[u]Physiological and psychological basis of logic[/u]. Thinking, as cerebration, subject to the laws of nervous action. Elementary properties of nerves.

1. Connection with external world through contractile cells.

2. Connection with world through irritable cells.

3. Capacity for acquiring habits. "All vital processes tend to become easier on repetition." Along whatever path a nervous discharge has once taken place, along that path a new discharge is more likely to take place. The direction along the path determinate.

Intricacy of connections an essential feature of the nervous system. Inhibitions.
