Introduction to the [u]logic of relatives[/u]. Individuals. Nominalism and realism. The principle of individuation.

Universal and particular propositions represented by the two types

$$\Pi_i u_i \qquad \Sigma_i u_i \text{.}$$

This supposes that 'something exists' is a principle of logic.

Definition of a relative term. The universal block:

$$\begin{matrix} A:A & A:B & A:C & \text{etc.} \\ B:A & B:B & B:C & \text{etc.} \\ C:A & C:B & C:C & \text{etc.} \\ \text{etc.} & \text{etc.} & \text{etc.} & \text{etc.} \end{matrix}$$

Representation of a relative in the form

$$l = \Sigma_i \Sigma_j \ l_{ij} \ (I:J)$$
