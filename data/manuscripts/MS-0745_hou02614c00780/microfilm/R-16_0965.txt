The most perfect method of doing this, consists
in finding a system of relatives [del]w + w' + w'' + w''' + etc[/del]
$w$, $w'$, $w''$, $w'''$, etc.
such that
$\Pi_i \Sigma_j w_{ij}$ $\Pi_i \Sigma_j w'_{ij}$ $\Pi_i \Sigma_j w''_{ij}$ etc.
$\Pi_i \Pi_j \{ w_{ij} + (wn)_{ij} \}$ $\Pi_i \Pi_j \{ w'_{ij} + (w'n)_{ij} \}$ etc.
$\Pi_i \Pi_j (w_{ij} + w'_{ij} + w''_{ij} + \text{etc})$

Then we have
$\Sigma_j u_j = u_i + (wu)_i + (w'u)_i + (w''u)_i + (w'''u)_i + \text{etc.}$
$\Pi_j u_j = u_j \times (wu)_j \times (w'u)_j \times (w''u)_j \times (w'''u)_j \times \text{etc.}$

After this, we shall use the process of elimination
of the Boolean algebra.
Required, for instance, to eliminate $s$ from the
premises
$\Sigma_h \Pi_i \Sigma_j \Pi_k (\alpha_{hik} + s_{jk} l_{ji})$
$\Sigma_u \Sigma_v \Pi_x \Pi_y (\varepsilon_{uyx} + \bar{s}_{yv} b_{vx})$.
Multiplying, we have
$\Sigma_u \Sigma_v \Pi_x \Pi_y \Sigma_h \Pi_i \Sigma_j \Pi_k (\alpha_{hik} + s_{jk} l_{ji}) (\varepsilon_{uyx} + \bar{s}_{yv} b_{vx})$.
