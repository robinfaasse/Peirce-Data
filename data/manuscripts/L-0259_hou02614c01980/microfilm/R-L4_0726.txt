Leipzig [ins]Garnisstr 27[sup]IV[/sup][/ins] – [date]Dec 8[sup]th[/sup] 1898[/date]

Dear Sir, I receive your letter of [date]Nov 20[/date]
and I shall answer it at length later.
Now I wish only to draw your special
attention to the general principle
of stylometric calculation as set forth
p. 140 sqq. The materials are quite insufficient
for determining chronology only according
to style, and the inferences from style
must be supplemented by a compa-
rative study of contents. Your diagram
does not essentially differ from mine
if this is taken into account. The chief
points agree: Crat. Symp. Pdo after Gorg. and
other lower dialogues – before Rep. and Theaet.
– then the dialectical dialogues before Tim.
An exact order of the single dialogues
would require more ample stylometric
observations and would never be con-
vincing if it contradicted the relation of
contents. However, your calculations
are very interesting. Yours sincerely
W. Lutoslawski
