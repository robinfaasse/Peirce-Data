[pn]No. 55[/pn] Computations for a Table of Secundal Antilogarithms, Sheet 2

Explanation. R being the approx. toward $\sqrt{2}$ already reached [fig]black circle[/fig] Approximation
$R + \frac{1}{2^n}$ being the next approximation, its square is $R^2 + (\frac{1}{2})^{n-1} R + \frac{1}{2^{2n}}$
Therefore from the last remainder, which was $\sqrt{2} - R^2$,
we must now subtract $(\frac{1}{2})^{n-1} R + (\frac{1}{2})^{2n}$.

The $R$'s are written [del]The remainders are written in Blue[/del]
The first part of each subtrahend in Green, i.e. $(\frac{1}{2})^{n-1} R$
and the last part, $(\frac{1}{2})^{2n}$ in brown

The rule for subtraction here used is as follows
Units in the same secundal place being always written in the same vertical column
write the subtrahend under the minuend and between them draw horizontal
lines beginning at the extreme left of right-handmost column that contains a unit of the
subtrahend but no unit of the minuend. Continue this first horizontal line toward the left
until it has passed through a column containing a unit of the minuend but none of the
subtrahend, and there let it stop. Then pass on to the right until you have past [del]another[/del] [ins]another[/ins]
column containing a unit of the subtrahend but none of the minuend and
proceed as before, until there remains no column to the right [del]containing a unit of the[/del]
subtrahend and none of the minuend, Then write the remainder by putting [del]a unit of it[/del]
below the subtrahend [del]by putting[/del] a unit in each column where the sum of the units in
the minuend and subtrahend increased by 1 in case there is a part of the column is traversed
by the horizontal line be odd, Where this sum is 0 or two, leave the place of the remainder
vacant, The horizontal line [del]between minuend and subtrahend will be drawn in black[/del]
