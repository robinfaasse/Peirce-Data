My dear Hastings:

Living so long in the country
away from the world, I have fallen
behind in my physics & positively
must go through the whole of
general physics afresh. I want
you to recommend a book, at
the most recent in all its presen-
tations, containing all the facts
as fully or fuller than Wüllner
used to do, and all the theories
in outline with sufficient mathe-
matical definiteness to convey
distinct ideas of how they work.
