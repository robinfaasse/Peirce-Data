I must explain that I use the word "unit" to denote the "figure one", or short
vertical line which expresses in any secundal place that the coefficient of the
corresponding power of .1 is unity; just as "dot" denotes the little spot that
shows that that coefficient is nothing. Every number is represented by a
series of units and dots, which, placed in any successive series of secundal
places, I call "its series,"

The Rule, to which I think it is best scrupulously to adhere, is as follows:

1[ins]st[/ins], mentally shift the thickening that marks the unit or dot in the zero
place (or, as we say, the units' place), through such an even number of places as to
make series denote a number less than four but not less than one.
