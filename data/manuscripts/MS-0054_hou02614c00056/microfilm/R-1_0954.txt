[u]3[sup]rd[/sup] process[/u]. With still another kind or appearance of ink, repeat the second process, treating all units previously cancelled as zeros, but treating the unit that was introduced when the previous fine line was brought to an end just as if it had originally stood where it is; and let it be so treated in subsequent repetitions of the process. Go on repeating the second process until the absence of any column containing more than unit renders any further repetition of it impossible. In these repetitions use the two varieties of ink already used for fine lines alternately. The object of using the different varieties of ink is to enable the computer readily to review [del]at[/del] his work at any stage of it, and to correct any mistakes he may have committed.
