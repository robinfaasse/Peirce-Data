[u]A Rule for Addition.[/u]

1. Write the secundal expressions of the numbers to be added in such wise that all figures belonging in any one secundal place shall lie in the same column

2nd, Begin with the right-hand-most of those columns which contain each more than one; and, with an ink distinguishable in colour or appearance from the figures, draw a fine line across the second unit in this column (so as to cancel it), and then prolong and bend this fine line so as to cancel the first unit in the same column, and thence continue the fine line to the left, making it cancel one unit in each [page break] it bring down in each column whatever unit may remain in it uncancelled; inserting zeros in all other places that [del]have units[/del] [ins]are at once[/ins] to their right and to their left.

[u]A Rule for Subtraction.[/u]

1st process. Write the secundal expression for the minuend and under it that of the subtrahend.
