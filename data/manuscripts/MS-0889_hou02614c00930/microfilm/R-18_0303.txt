As an illustration of the degra-
ded religion [del]—[/del] [ins]or[/ins] rather the uneleva-
ted, because undeveloped, religion
— of the Book of Psalms, — lofty though
it be in comparison with the hideous
mythology of European antiquity,
especially that of Greece, [del]I — I[/del]
point out that, throughout the book,
the Creator is praised for being vir-
tuous. Now what is meant by virtue
in this connection? Merely the human
ideal of conduct, To praise the Creator
for conformity to that, — He having
created that ideal in Man, as
commensurate to Man's grade of
mind, — what is it but a blasphemous
insult to the Creator, — were it possible
for Man to insult his Creator? There is
no grade of Human conception of merit
that has the slightest application to the
Creator of that ideal, although that
ideal as being the relic of an implan-
tation by the Creator demands reverence
from Man as applied to his own Conduct.
The Creator is above all ideals possible
to Man. It is an abomination to apply
any human conception of Merit to Him.
