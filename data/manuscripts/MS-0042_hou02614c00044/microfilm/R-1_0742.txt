Whole numbers, or, as a mathematician would say, positive integers,
maybe treated from two singularly different points of view; namely,
as cardinal or as ordinal numbers. Psycho-genetically, the
cardinal numbers, the adjectives applied to classes of [del]all[/del] [ins]plural[/ins],
are the primitive ideas. From that point of view, one escapes
altogether the necessity of supposing the things counted to be
ranged in any particular order as soon as one can con-
vince oneself that if the counting process ends at all,
it will always end at the same number.
