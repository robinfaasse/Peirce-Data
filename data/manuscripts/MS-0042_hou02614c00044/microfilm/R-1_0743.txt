Whole numbers, or, as [ins]a[/ins] mathematician would say, positive integers,
may be studied from two curiously different points of view; namely, either
as cardinal [del]or[/del] [ins]and[/ins] as ordinal numbers. Cardinal numbers are adjectives
applicable to different classes of plurals. Thus the days of the week are
seven; and so are the planets of the ancients, the Sun, the Moon,
Mars, Mercury, Juppiter, Venus, and Saturn. The ordinal numbers
are a succession of vocables applicable [ins]one by one[/ins] to the successive objects
of [del]a[/del] [ins]any finite[/ins] linear succession; the first, the second, the third, the fourth,
the fifth, the sixth, the seventh, etc.
