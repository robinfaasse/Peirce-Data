The second failure of Selectives to be as analytical as possible lies in their encouraging the idea that negation or denial, is a relatively simple concept, and their representing that the concept of Consequence, is a special composite of two negations, so that to say, "If in the actual state of things A is true, then B is true," is correctly analyzed as the assertion, "It is false to say that A is true while B is false." I fully acknowledge that, for most purposes, and in a preliminary explanation, the error of this analysis is altogether insignificant.
