Prof. C. S. Peirce

Dear Sir_

I am unable to determine from your note, just what form of color samples you desire. However I shall send you by Express tomorrow a set of our Artists' colors in oil_ These have been laid out on a large mill board in about inch squares to the number of about 150_ This set I prepared for my laboratory use, a few months ago, and as I thus happened to have it on hand, I send it to you, hoping it may fulfill your requirements_ If you find it does not and will indicate how you want the samples prepared, I shall take pleasure in giving such assistance as lies in my power_

Respectfully
I. Wyman Drummond_
