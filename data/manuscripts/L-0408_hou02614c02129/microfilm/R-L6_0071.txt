[date]8 - 10 - 12[/date]

My dear Mr. Peirce,

Attempting some serious work this year with a class in Recent Philosophy, I feel keenly the importance of putting into their hands, if possible into the hands of each member, a copy of your epoch-making papers on the Logic of Science in the P. Sc. My of 1878, [del]P[/del]etc, etc. If you could furnish me one or more deprints, I should be glad to pay any reasonable price therefor, or if you could refer me to a source of probable supply I should be much obliged.
