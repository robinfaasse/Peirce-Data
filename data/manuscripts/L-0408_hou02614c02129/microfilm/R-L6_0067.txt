My dear Mr. Peirce,

you may be surprised
to receive a letter dated as
above. For many weeks
I have been very keenly
conscious of dereliction
in our correspondence,
but there was no remedy.
Last summer, as you may
know, I was fully occu-
pied with work at Co-
lumbia University till mid-
August. Immediately after
close of the Summer Session
I plunged into study on my
paper for the Scientific
Congress. As the work was
exacting, overmindful
phrase, I perhaps over-
studied a little; at least
I was compelled to desist
by a pain in the back of
my head, which I inter-
preted as a friendly warning
from Nature. I then went
