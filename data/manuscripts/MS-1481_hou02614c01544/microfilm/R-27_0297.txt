Mr. Rider, the chief electrical engineer of the London tramways, has written a fairly readable book which must interest [ins]every[/ins] anybody who likes to know something of the practical questions connected with things that he is using every day. We do not know what other book covers the same ground so well. It discusses such questions as controllers, rolling stock, conduit systems, accumulators, etc. from the point of view of a man in the responsible position of Mr. Rider.
