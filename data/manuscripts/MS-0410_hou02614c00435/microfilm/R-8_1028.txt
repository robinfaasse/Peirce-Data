For example, to express with precision the fact that
"all men die," we may say; Take any object whatever (no
matter what); then, either that object is not a man, or
else it will die.

Again, to express the fact that "some men are killed,"
we may say; It is possible so to select an object that
is at once a man and is killed.

Again, to express that "few men are six feet high,"
we may say; Take any large number of objects just as
they occur in experience; then of those which are men
but a small proportion are six feet high.
