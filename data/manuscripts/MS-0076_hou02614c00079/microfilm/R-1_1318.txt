Ordinary algebra with imaginaries, considered as a double algebra, is in relative form
[del]1 = A:A + B:B, J = X:B - B:A. Quaternions in relative[/del] 1 = (X:X) + (Y:Y), J = (X:Y) - (Y:X).
This shows how the operation J turns through a right angle in the plane of X, Y. Quaternions in relative form is
1 = (W:W) + (X:X) + (Y:Y) + (Z:Z),
i = (X:W) - (W:X) + (Z:Y) - (Y:Z),
j = (Y:W) - (Z:X) - (W:Y) + (X:Z),
k = (Z:W) + (Y:X) - (X:Y) - (W:Z).
We see that we have here a reference to a space of four dimensions X, Y, Z, W. [[ins]C.S.P.[/ins]]
