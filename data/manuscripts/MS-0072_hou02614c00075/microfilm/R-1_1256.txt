[u]On the Combinations of Six Things[/u].

The following is somewhat less trivial from a logical than from a mathematical point of view. A collection of which any part is (so far as considered) constituted of monads related to one another exactly like every equal part [del]or[/del] [ins]it[/ins] may be called as [u]strage[/u] ([u]I. strages[/u]). By a [u]monad[/u] I mean one of a number of objects supposed to be distinct from one another but [ins]between[/ins] of which no definite differences are considered. I call a strage of [del]two monads[/del] [ins]two objects[/ins], a [u]couple[/u] i.e. an unordered pair, a [del]couple[/del] [ins]couple[/ins]; a strage of three [del]monads[/del] [ins]objects[/ins] a [u]trine[/u]; a strage of four [del]objects[/del] [ins]objects[/ins] a [u]quatern[/u], etc.

Of 6 monads [ins]a, b, c, d, e, f say[/ins] each [del]forms[/del] [ins]has[/ins] 5 couples with the others [del]ab, ac, ad, ae, af[/del]; and since there are 2 monads to a couple, there are 5 x [frac]6[/frac]/2 = 15 couples.

If [del]I call[/del] two couples [del]having[/del] [ins]have[/ins] [del]an monad[/del] [ins]objects[/ins] in common, I say they are [u]allied[/u]. If not, I say they are [u]alien[/u].

Each of the 15 couples, say ab
is identical to 1 couple, ab.
is allied to 8 couples, ac, ad, ae, af, bc, bd, be, bf.
is alien to 6 couples, cd, ce, cf, de, df, ef.

Of the 8 couples to which any given couple is allied, any one, say ac,
is identical to 1 couple, ac.
is allied to 4 couples, ad, ae, af, bc
is alien to 3 couples, bd, be, bf.

Of the 6 couples to which any given couple is alien, [ins]any one, say cd,[/ins]
is identical to 1 couple, [del]cd[/del] [ins]cd[/ins]
is allied [del]to[/del] 4 couples, ce, cf, de, df
is alien [del]to[/del] 1 couple, ef.

Of the four couples which are allied to both of 2 given couples, there is one which is alien to all the others, which are all allied by the same monad.

The 3 couples that are alien to one of two allied couples and are [del]alien to the other[/del] [ins]allied to the other[/ins], are all allied by the same monad.
