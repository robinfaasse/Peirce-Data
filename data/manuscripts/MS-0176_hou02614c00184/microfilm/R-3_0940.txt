Having learned how to read numbers, the pupil has next to practice writing them, a far more important matter. The following rules should be observed.

1[sup]st[/sup] In practising arithmetic, pen and ink should be used.

2[sup]nd[/sup] To the left of every number write, the name of the quantity which it counts or measures. Write this in a very legible and compressed hand. Round hand is very suitable.

3[sup]d[/sup] Before writing a number have the [del]situation[/del] [ins]so as to give them equal spaces[/ins] situation of every decimal place determined; and when several numbers are to be written, be careful to place figures in the same decimal place [del]of[/del] exactly in vertical columns.

4[sup]th[/sup]. Study a method of forming figures, which shall be very legible, and like print, and without flourishes or unnecessary lines or quirks. The strokes should be rather light and the forms somewhat open; so that erroneous figures may be corrected by [del]writing[/del] [ins]writing[/ins] over them, without making erasures.
