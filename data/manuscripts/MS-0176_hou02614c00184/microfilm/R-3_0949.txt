[u]Rule for adding a column[/u]

Look at the first two bottom figures and imagine you see [del]the figures represented[/del] [ins]the sum in Arabic notation[/ins]. Put your pen on the third figure from the bottom and imagine you see the sum of this added to the previous sum. You may mentally name the number [ins]if necessary,[/ins] but must never move the lips. So proceed till you have gone through the column. Set down the result, and immediately begin at the top and add downwards as a check upon the first result.
