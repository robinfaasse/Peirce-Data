[u]C.S.P.'s Small Inventions in Arithmetic & Algebra.[/u]

[u]No 1.[/u] To arrange all the [del]positive[/del] [ins]not negative[/ins] rational fractions in the order of their values without calculation.

[u]Rule[/u]. Write those of lower denominator before those of higher denominator. Of any one denominator write those of lower numerator before those of higher numerator
