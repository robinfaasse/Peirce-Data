By means of leashes and the leashing of leashes,
we can express any relation between any definite
and finite number of members. But in regard to
indefinite numbers it is not so. Here we must
generalize. We have to consider the order of the
world. We no longer say, John Doe does so and so,
but "every man", "one man in so many, on the average,
as they [del]person[/del] come along in a certain range of expe-
