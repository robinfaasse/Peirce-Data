The art of reasoning is a skeleton key to unlock all pro-
blems; and good logical thought must be skeletal thought,
with all that conceals the articulations torn away. More
especially is this true of the necessary argumentation which
we are now alone to consider. We won't say, Man is mortal,
but all S is P; or, still better, we will let S denote man,
[u]h[/u] having a character, P mortality, and will write ShP, to
show that these three are connected. I will term three
demonstratives so connected a [u]leash[/u]. It may easily be proved
that mere [u]couples[/u] will not suffice. If the reader cares to
think this out, let him take this hint: if you try to make
couples suffice, you will find you are giving a [u]special sig-[/u]
[u]nification[/u] to coupling. Now this special signification is
equivalent to the third element of a leash. It is not nec-
