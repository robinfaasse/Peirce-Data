3rd. Multiply, or compound, all the premises into one; and any premise may be multiplied into itself (each time with different letters for demonstratives*) as many times as may be desired. Different conclusions will commonly result, according to the "poner" of the premise used. (Insert here Copy A.)

Bring all the quantifiers to the left of the whole; and, without disturbing the relative order of such as belong to one premise, transpose those of different premises, so as to carry the prone letters as far as possible to the left.
