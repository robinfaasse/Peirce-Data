[marginalia]Ms. 1259[/marginalia]

Rules for editing Midsummer Night's Dream.

[u]In Cases of slight differences[/u]

Where each of two suppositions is possible

If [del]Fisher's 4[sup]to[/sup][/del] [ins]they[/ins] agree with folio — they are right

If [u]4[sup]tos[/sup][/u] are opposed to fol. & I have a decided preference
for folio's reading I then take that reading un-
less it is certainly a misprint, but if I have
no such leaning to folio's reading, if it seems
to be a case of misprint I follow the quartos.

If [u]folio[/u] follows Roberts' quarto. If I have a strong pre-
ference, I follow my preference; otherwise
I follow Fisher's quarto unless it is hard to sup-
pose it a case of misprint.

Collier's folio emendations are to be placed on
the same footing as Theobald's Capell's & Tyrwhitt's.
