Every perlation is necessarily, a juxtareperlation; although not every juxtareperlation is a perlation; and similarly, every reperlation is a juxtaperlation. So, every contraperlation is an [del]extraperlation[/del] [ins]extrareperlation[/ins]; and every contrareperlation is an extraperlation. The converse of none of these propositions is true.
