[u]Second [del]Mode[/del] [ins]System[/ins] of Divisions[/u].

An existential relation may be termed a [u]suilation[/u], if, and only if, [del]from[/del] every individual of the universe stands in that relation to itself. An existential relation may be termed an [u]ambilation[/u], if, and only if, every individual of the universe stands in that relation to every other.
