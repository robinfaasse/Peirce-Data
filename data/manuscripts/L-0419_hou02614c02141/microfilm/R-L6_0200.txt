[ins]U.S. Government Despatch Agency[/ins]
London [date]18 July 1872[/date]

Prof Peirce
Assistant in Charge
U. S. Coast Survey

Dear Sir,

I enclose invoice
of books from your order of June
5th which are sent with your July
magazines by the "City of Paris"
today. The remainder of
the books on your order are out
of print but I shall use my
best endeavours to find them
and send them to you. The
British Association Catalogue of
Stars and Mosbach's Mechanics
vol 1 are both especially scarce
and high priced.

I remain faithfully
B F Stevens
