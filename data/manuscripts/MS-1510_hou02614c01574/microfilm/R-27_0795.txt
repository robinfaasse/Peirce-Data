Two things, however, we are constrained to grant:
that energy is not a mere figment, and that all
modern philosophers insist that there is but one
mode of being, that of individual things. Yea,
even Hegel himself does so; and the [ins]only[/ins] exceptions
we can think of are the dreamy Schelling, the
sharp-witted Bolzano, Dr. Francis Ellingwood
Abbot, and a pragmatist or two. Further than that
we could not go without ourselves crossing
the threshold of that house divided against
itself, the household of the metaphysicians.
