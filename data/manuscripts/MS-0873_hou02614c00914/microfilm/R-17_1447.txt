On the other hand, it is [del]an ob[/del] even greater mistake
to say that cessation from action is in any degree
desirable, — which seems to be the hedonistic conception.
Almost every moralist outside the ranks of the
hedonists has remarked that these philosophers
confound two different things, pleasure and satis-
faction. Pleasure is a special quality of organic feeling:
satisfaction is the conception of conformity to an end.
Pleasure, in limited doses, certainly does produce some
degree of satisfaction, and ought to do so, since nobody
doubts that it indicates a healthy state of the body.
