My dear Professor Keyser:

I intimated to you, before you went upon your vacation, that I wished to propose to Mr. Jacks a series of four articles to be written by me. Being on the verge of 70, and having yet before me the duty of completing a book which may help some people to reason effectively, I should not undertake a series of articles which will engross my mind almost wholly for a year if I did not think them of great importance. The [page break] (or they might be so, if one could hear them) appear to contradict one another or themselves, and to be mere misinterpretations of propositions of philosophers
