I must now be very brief as this is my last sheet of paper. The Feelings
(which includes Sensations) which cropped up sporadically in time as actua-
lities, were of vastly greater variety than those we know, the latter being the
few relics of a "struggle for existence" which destroyed the infinitely larger
part of them. The Feelings generally became, in the temporal evolution, the
Objects, and the Activities the Interpretants of Thought. I shall show how this
occurred, and it agrees with the doctrine of Pragmaticism. As the Feelings became
thus subject to habit (Thought being essentially of the nature of Habit, because of
its generality[del], which will be made perfectly clear[/del]), the vividness of the Feelings
([del]as contradistinguished from their Qualitative, or Ideal, intensity[/del]) continually dimi-
nished, since vividness is nothing but the irregular disturbance occasioned by
Feeling. Consequently, although in matter there are still Feelings, [ins]yet[/ins] their
vividness, — their "consciousness", — is reduced to next to nothing. The $\psi$-parts which
become souls, somehow (I am not yet prepared with a hypothesis of how) become
attached to Bodies, [del]not[/del] [ins]or[/ins] $\phi$-parts. At least, all we know do so, since otherwise
we should not know them. In this attachement the greater part of their
Feelings lose their vividness. This I can explain. The consequence is that we
only know a few Sensations, i.e. Feelings excited by Bodies; namely, the 3 ele-
ments of color, a certain number, not over 2000, of sounds; and still fewer of smell,
about 4 of taste, [del]or[/del] [ins]possibly a few more[/ins] of temperature, and a very few others.
[del]The[/del] Internal Feelings, such as Grief, Pity, Shame, Hope, Joy, etc. are also few.

I have only room [del]on this page[/del] to say that the evolution of Space is [del]partly[/del] [ins]pretem-[/ins]
poral, and I think so for as to reduce it from infinite dimensionality to a [del]quasi-dimensionality[/del]
in which every pair of dimensions is unalterably connected with the other pair. The reason
for thinking so seem very strong. The further development, which reduces it to three dimen-
sions must be supposed temporal.

The type of all temporal evolution I take to be what I call the Darwinian Type. Variety
has arisen [del]in Mind and is[/del] [ins]quite[/ins] constantly increasing [del]under Continual Creative acts.[/del]
[del]These can only appear from the scientific point of view as unexplained residual[/del]
[del]phenomena, or in a collective view as purely fortuitous events. In this view of chance[/del]

[del]reply, and then would only allow me 1000 words in which to complete the account of my[/del]
[del]theory, which was impossible in that limitation.[/del] By the Darwinian Type, I mean that
by the action of probability combined with habit, taking certain habits are eliminated by
the disappearance of the substances in which they inhere.

[del]My[/del] [ins]A[/ins] fourth paper would be devoted to the development of the necessary consequences
of the hypothesis and the comparison of them with the facts of observation. This paper
will be a surprise to almost everybody. I cannot understand why anybody
should be jealous of an old recluse like me; and yet I do not see any other
motive that should set up the difficulties I find in getting my studies printed.
From my theory it neither results that there must nor that there cannot be occasional
communications from the dead. But it does result that if there be such they would probably
have all the general characteristics of "spiritualistic" phenomena, and that those
communications would violate the laws of dynamics.
