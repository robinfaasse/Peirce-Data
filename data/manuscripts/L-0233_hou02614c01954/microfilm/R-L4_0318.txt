Corresponding Member of the American Editorial Board
Professor Cassius J. Keyser, Ph.D., Columbia University, City of New York.

Dear Mr. Peirce:

A letter just received from Mr. Jacks concurs with my opinion written to him that your article is a contribution of "permanent value". He has observed that an understanding of it requires "digging" on the reader's part and is pleased on that account. He requests me, however, to ask you whether it is possible, and, if so, whether you would be willing, "to summarize the article in a concluding page or two, to be added to the article, in order to forestall careless cavillers who might say, 'what, then, precise[ins]ly[/ins] is your neglected argument?'" I submit his suggestion to you without commentary. Your article has gone to the printer and the proofs will doubtless soon arrive. —

Very truly yours
C. J. Keyser

Mr. C. S. Peirce
