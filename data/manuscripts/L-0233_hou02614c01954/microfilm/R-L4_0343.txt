My dear Professor Keyser:

[marginalia]Start[/marginalia] Before you went away on your vacation, I
had intimated to you my intention of proposing
for the Hibbert Journal a series of four articles
by me. I postponed actually making the proposal
until you should have returned. I now do so;
and will state in brief what their contents would
be, sufficiently to enable the managers of the
Journal to decide upon their appropriateness.
