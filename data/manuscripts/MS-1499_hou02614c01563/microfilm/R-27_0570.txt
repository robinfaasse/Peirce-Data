[u]Dalecampius[/u] Jacques Dalechamp 1513-1588
Physician & botanist. His edition of Athenaeus
[del]with latin tr. was[/del] long the best.

[u]Nicander[/u] Νίκανδρος, a medical poet of the
IInd Century B.C. wrote two poems Θηριακά
and Ἀλεξιφάρμακα. Both pretty bad. Both
published by Aldus 1499.

[u]Gorraeus[/u] Jean des Gorris 1505-1577, published
in 1543 a translation of the two extant
poems of Nicander.

[u]Arduynus Grevinus[/u]. There should be a comma
between these two names. Arduini was an
Italian physician & poet of the XVIth century.
[ins]Jacques[/ins] Grévin, 1539-1570 was a Frenchman
of whom Ronsard said,
"A-t-on-vu dans notre France un seul Grevin assemblé
La docte medecine et les beaux vers ensemble."

[u]Oppianus[/u], Ὀππιανός, is most conveniently
supposed to be two different persons, since style,
date, and all circumstances, except the name
and positive testimony, point that way. An
earlier poet wrote the Ἁλιευτικά, a later
and inferior one the Κυνηγετικά. The
former seems to say he was born in Corycus
