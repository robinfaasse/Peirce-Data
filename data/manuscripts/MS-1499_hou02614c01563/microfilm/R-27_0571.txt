[u]Vincentius Belluacensis[/u] or Bellovacensis.
1190-1264 author of a great cyclopaedia
[del]the Speculum triplex. He is believed to
derive his surname from having long
lived in a house of his order, the Dominican,
in Beauvais.[/del]

[u]Gulielmus de Conchis[/u], i.e. of Conches in Normandy
1080-1150 or after 1154. Hauréau has in-
vestigated his writings. But his opinion,
[del]which[/del] he admits is doubtful, that certain
Glosses on Priscian de constructione are
by him has been refuted by Thurot. He
wrote De philosophia Mundi, Dragmaticon
philosophiae, Secunda and Tertia Philoso-
phia, etc.

[u]Bartholomaeus Glanvil[/u]. Lived about 1350.
Wrote a very celebrated [del]a very silly[/del] book
called De proprietatibus rerum. He no
doubt belonged to the Anglo-Norman
family of the medieval earls of Suffolk
from which came the celebrated legal
writer and also in the XVII[ins]th[/ins] century,
the author of The Vanity of Dogmatizing.
