[marginalia]ct.[/marginalia] [u]Jeronymus Cardanus[/u] 1501-1576. A
contemptible character who contrived
to make a good deal of noise in the
world. His work [u]De Subtilitate[/u] 1550,
is his best. It has 21 books; the 1st on the
principles of things, matter, form, vacuum,
movement, space; the 2d on the elements
earth, air, water; the 3d on the heavens; the
4th on light; the 5th on mixtures; the 6th on metals;
the 7th, on stones; the 8th, on plants; the 9th
on spontaneous generation of animals;
the 10th on perfect animals; the 11th on
the form of man; the 12th on man's tempera-
ment; the 13th on feeling; the 14th on the soul;
the 15th on useless subtleties; the 16th on
science; the 17th on inventions; the 18th
on marvellous things; the 19th on devils;
the 20th on angels; the 21st on God.
His next most celebrated book is [u]De
Varietate Rerum[/u] 1557, It is a farrago
without method; He was an original
mind, untrammelled by anything
& has some valuable ideas.
