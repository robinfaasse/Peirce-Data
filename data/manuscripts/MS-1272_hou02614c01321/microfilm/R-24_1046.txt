These directions are considerably better approximations to N.W. and
N.E. than those of the sides of the core to the cardinal points. They
must, therefore, be supposed to rest upon a second and more
careful determination of the azimuth. This determination must,
then, have been made after the core was complete; otherwise, being
the more careful of the two determinations, it would have been
used in erecting the core. Therefore, the sockets must have been
cut after the core was complete. This, indeed, would naturally
be the order of proceeding; [del]for, why[/del] [ins]the casing could not be begun till the core was complete, and[/ins] should the sockets be cut
before they were wanted, [del]when[/del] [ins]considering that[/ins] the presence of the core would
