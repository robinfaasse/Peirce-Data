A pyramid was simply a tomb with a secret chamber in which
was to be deposited the mummy of a king, because of some belief
connected with a resurrection. That was doubtless the king's
sole purpose; and though his priests may have had some ulte-
rior views, yet there is not the slightest reason to think that
any astronomical, metrological, geodetical, physical, or other
scientific idea governed the construction of any portion of the
pile or its contents. [del]But[/del] The numerous hypotheses of this charac-
