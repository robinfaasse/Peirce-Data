This very readiness to accept the wisdom of the ancients showed how little
impulse there was to investigate truth independently, while it equally
betrays the decadence of the intellectual life of the race. Concerning
history as such the Egyptian had little curiosity. A king would cover
his monument with accounts of his own prowess and divinity; or,
if these topics failed him, he would show how he proposed to occupy
himself in the world to come. It never occurred to him to inform posterity
how many years after any epoch in the past any event of his own reign
had occurred. True, some priests had chronological tables of reigns;
