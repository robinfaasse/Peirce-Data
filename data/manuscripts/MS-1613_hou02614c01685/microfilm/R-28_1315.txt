HARVARD UNIVERSITY

LAWRENCE SCIENTIFIC SCHOOL,
CAMBRIDGE, MASS.

MY DEAR SIR:—

In order to make the Lawrence Scientific School card catalogue of graduates as accurate as possible, and enable us to keep on file the addresses of all living graduates, I ask you kindly to fill out the attached blank and return it to me as soon as possible.

If you happen to know the present address of any graduate who has changed his address during the past three years, or, if you have knowledge of the death of any graduate during this time, it will oblige us very much if you will add this information. Let us know, if possible, the date and place of death.

We will esteem it a favor if you will keep us informed of changes in your address, as well as of important changes in your life—such as promotions, new titles, degrees, or honors, etc.

Very truly yours,

J. L. LOVE, *Secretary*.
