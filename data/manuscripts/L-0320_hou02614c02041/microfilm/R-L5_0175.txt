My dear Sage,
After I last wrote you, I was
very ill. A kidney trouble (not offhand)
my mind. Ideas, "cut me dead", and
I lived in a muck. For a time
I thought my time had come. But
a wise physician pulled me back, and
while I am yet weak and dull, I have
hopes of doing better.
I am very grateful for the Monist
containing your "What Pragmatism Is" —
I had been wishing for just this
statement from you.
To understand any man accurately,
or perhaps at all,
one must know and bear in mind
his whole system of philosophy. Royce,
told me you were a "pure Schellingian."
I doubted it. What you say, in
the above mentioned paper, about Hegel
and your own "Triune Being" opens
you up to me, in some measure; while
before, you were to me a vast uncertainty.
