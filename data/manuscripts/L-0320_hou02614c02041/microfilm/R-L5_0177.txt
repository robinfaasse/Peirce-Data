Milford Pa [date]1905 Nov 22[/date]

My dear Doctor Nichols:

Here is pretty fact! I
wonder what you have been
thinking of me.

I receive today from the
Milford P.O. yours of Oct 16
last received at Milford P.O.
the same evening 8 PM
but not sent to me till today!

I am too busy to write much
& it would not be easy to
express myself on this
