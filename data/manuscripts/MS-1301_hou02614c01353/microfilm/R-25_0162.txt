And seven paths aloft men say they take,
Yet six alone are viewed by mortal eyes.[sup]1[/sup]
From Zeus’ abode no star unknown is lost
Since first from birth we heard,[sup]2[/sup] but thus the tale is told: [pn]260[/pn]
These seven are called by name Alkyonê,
Kelainô, Meropê, and Steropê,
Têygetê, Elektrê, Maia queen.
They thus together small and faint roll on,
Yet notable[sup]3[/sup] at morn and eve through Zeus, [pn]265[/pn]
Who bade them show when winter first begins,
And summer, and the season of the plough.[sup]4[/sup]
