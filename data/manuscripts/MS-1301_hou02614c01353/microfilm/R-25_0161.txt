THE HEAVENLY DISPLAY. [pn]31[/pn]

Each joined with each on each side as they go.
These have a star both beautiful and large,
And this men call the tail-connecting link.
Let the left shoulder of [u]Andromede[/u]
Mark the more northern [u]Fish[/u], for it is nigh.
And both her feet her husband [u]Perseus[/u] show,
For o'er his shoulders they are ever borne.

FIG. XX.—THE CLUSTERERS.

Taller than others [pn]1[/pn] moves he in the north;
Towards the seat of his wife's mother's chair
His right hand stretches; wide his steps, like one
Hasting, dust-covered even in realms of Zeus.
Near his left thigh together sweep along
The flock of [u]Clusterers[/u].[pn]2[/pn] Not a mighty space
Holds all, and they themselves are dim to see.
