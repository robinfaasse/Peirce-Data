Nearer the north. And nigh a second sails [sup]1[/sup]
Lesser in size, but dangerous to come
From ocean when night flies; the *Eagle* named. 315
Now near the *Goat* the *Dolphin* speeds along,
Dim in the mist; and round it lie four stars

To see his better though he search the sky. 325

Varied, [sup]2[/sup] nor wholly seen; upon his belly
Revolves a dim star; his portentous jaw
