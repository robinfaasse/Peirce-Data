Seek the _Snake-holder's_ [ins]¹[/ins] head; and then from it [marginalia]75[/marginalia]

FIG. VII.—THE SCORPION.

With each foot presses on a monster huge,
The _Scorpion_, o'er eye and breast-scale standing [marginalia]85[/marginalia]
Upright, the _Snake_, meanwhile, in both hands writhing;
Less in the right, most holds the left on high.
By the _Crown's_ side the jaw-end lies along;
Beneath the coils search for the mighty _Claws_, [ins]³[/ins]
