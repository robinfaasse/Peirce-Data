But there are other things than facts
in my universe. There are Laws. For
example, I do not say that [del]it[/del] [ins]there[/ins] will ever
be a flash of lightening in my universe.
The creator of this universe has not made
up his mind whether it ever shall lighten
or not. But to one thing he has made
up his mind, and it may be set down
as true of this universe; namely, that if
ever it shall lighten, then it shall thunder.
But we have, as yet, provided no means of
asserting this analytically. It might be
written down as a whole. But it is neces-

between the fact of lightening, should it
lighten, to the thunder which the law
would then compel. I propose then a
