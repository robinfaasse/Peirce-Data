When Stevinus came to reflect upon
the principle of his demonstration, he
saw that it amounted to this, that the weight
of a system of [del]men[/del] bodies does not tend
to move them in any way which does
not lower them on the average.

Suppose [del]5 links[/del] [ins]5 inches[/ins] on the side AC are equal
in height to [del]4 links[/del] [ins]4 inches[/ins] on the side CB. Let
those five inches be raised by $\frac{1}{5}$ of an
inch. That will let the 4 inches sink by $\frac{1}{4}$
inch. The total rise will be $\frac{5}{5}$ inch. The total
fall $\frac{4}{4}$ inches. And on the average there
will be neigh neither rise nor fall.
