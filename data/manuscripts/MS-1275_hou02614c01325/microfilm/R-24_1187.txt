Should be considered as infinitely small.

[fig]A very long, narrow wedge-like quadrilateral diagram[/fig]

Now the figure being infinitely small although
the two opposite sides meet at a finite distance
the angle between them is infinitesimal,
and the displacement of the fourth apex
of the quadrilateral is only an infinitesimal
of the second order. Hence a violation of the
principle of similar triangles is unimportant.
