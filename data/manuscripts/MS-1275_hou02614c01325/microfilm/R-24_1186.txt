[del]the combination of forces, which was worked[/del]
[del]out independantly by several men, especially[/del]
by Varignon is that of the parallelogram of
forces. It were better called the parallelogram
of motions. If a man is walking on the deck
of a vessel, so that in a second he is carried

not moving. But if the vessel is so moving

the parallelogram he is carried to D. This seems
at first sight to involve the principle of similar
