There is one objection, however, which I am
bound to mention. It is that Neanthes of
Cyzicum, an esteemed historian of about
[del]the middle of[/del] the 3[ins]rd[/ins] Century before Christ
says that he understands that the early Pytha-
goreans did not keep their doctrines secret.
This is admitting they did so in his day. Now
it is not possible to publish a thing first
and afterwards keep it secret. Besides, he
only says this in order to find an excuse
for the conduct of two Pythagoreans who
wrote books. But because they wrote
books, it does not follow they gave away
any important secrets.
