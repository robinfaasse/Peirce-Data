My dear Sumner

You must have been much surprised not to hear from me but the truth is that I was much perplexed what to write. You held forth some hopes that we might see you this summer, and this was what we both desired most ardently; but there were a number of circumstances which caused me to delay urging you to come right on. These have now mainly been conquered; and now I can tell you truthfully how deeply we shall feel the pleasure & privilege of receiving you.
