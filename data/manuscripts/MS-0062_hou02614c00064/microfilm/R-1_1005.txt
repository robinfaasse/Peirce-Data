To extract the square root of a number

1[u]st[/u], [del]Change the place[/del] Move the bar an even number of places to the right or left so as to make the resulting number > 1 and < 100.
Then the first approximation to the root is 1, and the square of this is 1.

2[u]d[/u], Go through the following operation as many times as may be necessary to obtain the required approximation to the root.

i. Under the square of the approximation already obtained, copy the approximation itself beginning at such a place that when this is added to the square above it the sum shall be as little less than the number whose root is to be extracted as possible, [del]and[/del]

ii. If this number ends [ins]n-1[/ins] [del]n[/del] places to the right of the end of the number above it, write after it n zeros and then a 1

iii. Add the last two numbers and the sum will be the square of the next approximation, which approximation will be the last with a 1 in the n[u]th[/u] place following the last 1 of the previous [del]has[/del] approximation

3[u]d[/u] Move the bar in the opposite way and by half as much as it was previously moved.
