[date]Dec. 29, 1913[/date]

My dear Mr. Peirce, —

Very many
thanks for your very complete
& painstaking letter, which
has just been forwarded
to me from Cambridge. I
shall go over the proof with
the greatest interest as soon
as I return, & can compare
the references. It was very
good of you to take so much
[del]trouble[/del] pains about it.

Sincerely yours,
Edward V. Huntington
