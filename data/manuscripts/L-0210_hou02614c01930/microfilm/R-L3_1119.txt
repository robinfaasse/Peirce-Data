My Dear Mr. Huntington:

I fulfill my promise of sending you that proof of the distributive principle which in the [u]Am. J. Math.[/u] III. p. 33 I omitted as being too "tedious." I now offer it for what it is worth, my mind being at present far away from this subject.

One half of the proposition is admitted to follow from my premises; but I have repeated the proof. The other half depends on certain principles of the "algebra of the copula" which precede p 33 in my paper.
