My dear Mr. Huntington:

I fulfill my promise & send you that proof (or supposed proof) of the distributive principle of Boolean algebra which in the [u]Am. J. Math.[/u] III p 33 I omitted as too "tedious." My mind is now on other things & I make no new criticism of the argument but give it for what it is worth. The first half is admitted to follow; but I repeat it. As to the second half, it depends on certain principles of the Algebra of the Copula which precede p 33 in my paper.

In this copy numbers enclosed in parenthesis are the numbers of formulae in my paper. Numbers in square brackets indicate formulae in this proof
