35 Fairfax Hall
Cambridge, Mass.
March 1, 1904.

My dear Mr. Peirce, -
I must
apologize for not sooner
acknowledging your very
valuable + kind letter
concerning the postulates
for the algebra of logic. It
has given me considerable
"food for thought" which will
take some little time to digest.
But I hope to be able to write
to you more fully in a few
weeks.

Very sincerely,
E. V. Huntington
