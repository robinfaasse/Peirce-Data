My dear Mr. Weiss:

I am very sorry to say that I am not able to put my hand on the original of the C. S. Peirce letter to which you refer but my recollection is that the printed letter followed the original with great exactness.

Regretting that I cannot reply more satisfactorily to your inquiry and with cordial regards, I am

Very sincerely yours,
Edward V. Huntington
