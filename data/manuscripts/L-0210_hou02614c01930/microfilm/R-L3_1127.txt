contradiction can have no being even in thought.
The principle of [del]excluded middle[/del] [ins]contradiction[/ins] is of quite
another character. I can think a triangle, I can
make a supposition that an object is a triangle,
without making any supposition, without its
occurring to me to ask, whether one of its angles
exceeds 57°3 or not. If the principle of contradiction
is a law of thought, then the principle of excluded
middle is a law of existence. The former warns us
against predicating too much, the latter tells us
there is more to be said. Suppose a man says
