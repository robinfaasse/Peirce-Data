[u]Definition[/u]. A projector is a
(1) an optical body,
(2) capable of any motion for which any point taken at will is focus and any plane taken at will is bottom, and
(3) capable of no other motion.

[u]Problem[/u]. The definition of a projector is consistent. It is possible there should be such a motion.
