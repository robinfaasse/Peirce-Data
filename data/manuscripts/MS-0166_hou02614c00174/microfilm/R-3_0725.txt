Corollary 110. In like manner we have
$$\Upsilon(CDF) = 1$$
[ins]unless $\Upsilon\{CDF\} = 1$[/ins]
if $[C]$, $[D]$, $[F]$ exist; and we may write
$$\Upsilon\{CDF\} \times \Upsilon(CDF) = \Upsilon[C] \times \Upsilon[D] \times \Upsilon[F].$$

Corollary 111. So also
$$\Upsilon[\beta\gamma\delta] = 1$$
[ins]unless $\Upsilon\{\beta\gamma\delta\} = 1$[/ins]
if $(\beta)$, $(\gamma)$, $(\delta)$ exist; and we may write
$$\Upsilon\{\beta\gamma\delta\} \times \Upsilon[\beta\gamma\delta] = \Upsilon(\beta) \times \Upsilon(\gamma) \times \Upsilon(\delta).$$
