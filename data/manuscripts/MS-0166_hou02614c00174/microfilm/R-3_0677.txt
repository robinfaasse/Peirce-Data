[u]As[/u]. [u]Theorem[/u]. [u]In a linear configuration with hammata, the station-number of the whole line equals the sum of the station-numbers of the portions cut off by hammata diminished by the sum of the valencies of the hammata.[/u]

[u]Demonstration[/u]. For let the hammata be placed upon the line successively, after the latter is described. Then the effect of each hamma is the same as if instead of placing it there all the stretches radiating from it except one were cut. Namely the hamma separates [ins]all[/ins] those stretches; and that is precisely what such cutting would do. Now each such cutting over and above what go to open rings cuts off [del]one[/del] [ins]a[/ins] piece. Every one which opens a ring reduces the cyclosis [ins]by one[/ins]. Every one which cuts off a piece increases the chorisis by one. Either increases the station number by one. Hence, the station-number of the whole line increased by the sum of the valencies of the hammata gives the station-number of the group of portions [del]cut off by[/del] [ins]into which[/ins] the hammata cut the line. Q.E.D.
