[u]Art. . Theorem . In a linear configuration with hammata, the station-number of the whole line equals the sum of the station-numbers of the portions cut off by hammata diminished by the sum of the valencies of the hammata.[/u]

[u]Demonstration[/u]. For if [del]a[/del] [ins]the[/ins] hammata are put upon the line successively then each one which does not increase the chorisis or number of pieces of the lines by its valency must reduce the cyclosis by the number by which its reduction of the chorisis falls short of its valency. Thus, let

[fig]Diagram of a vertex A with radiating lines B, C, D, E[/fig] Figure 122

A be the last hamma inserted in the process. Then, if A does not separate A [ins]from AB[/ins] [del]and[/del] make it a new piece, it must have been before a ring, which being cut through diminishes the cyclosis. Either way, therefore, each hamma increases the station-number by one less than the number of its radiating stretches: By one less, because [del]every[/del] [ins]if any[/ins] is cut away, it is cut away from some one.
