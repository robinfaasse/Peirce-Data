§ [u]Definition[/u] . A [ins](plural, foci)[/ins] focus is a point all the flat laminae through which, during a [ins]motion or part of a[/ins] [del]motion[/del], for which that point is a focus, remain in their planes and the particles of them only move along rays through that focus.
