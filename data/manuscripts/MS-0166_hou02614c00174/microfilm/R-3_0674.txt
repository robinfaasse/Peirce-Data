[u]Definition 127[/u]. The [u]Listing valency[/u] of a place is the number
of simple places of the same dimensionality for which it has
to count in Listing's theorem which is
[u]Listing Valency[/u] = 1 + [u]Chorisis[/u] - [u]Cyclosis[/u] + [u]Periphraxis[/u] - [u]Immensity[/u]
And the valency of the whole equals the sum of the valencies of the separate
parts.
