In general if $Y$ is odd and larger than 5
$E - Z = [del]E[/del] (Y - 2) x$
$EZ = 2Yx$
$(E + Z)^2 = (Y - 2)^2 x^2 + 8Yx$

We have for one solution
$E + Z = (Y - 2)x + 4$
for this makes $(E + Z)^2 = (Y - 2)^2 x^2 + 8Yx - 16x + 16$
[del]and putting[/del] or $x = 1$
whence $E = Y$, $Z = 2$. But this solution gives nothing new.
To find whether there is or is not another according as
there is a positive [ins]whole[/ins] number $Q$ such that
$E + Z = (Y - 2)x + 4 + 2Q$
$(E + Z)^2 = (Y - 2)^2 x^2 + 8Yx - 16x + 16 + 4(Y - 2)Qx + 16Q + 4Q^2$
or $0 = -16x + 16 + 4(Y - 2)Qx + 16Q + 4Q^2$
or $\{ [del]4[/del] - (Y - 2)Q \} x = (Q + 2)^2$

Now it is evident that if $Y$ is odd and exceeds 5, $Y - 2 > 4$
and the first member of this equation is negative, there is,
[del]therefore, no possible solution[/del], except those already obtained.
