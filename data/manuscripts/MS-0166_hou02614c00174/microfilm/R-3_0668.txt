The surface of one side of a sheet of paper has its cyclosis equal to 0.

The surfaces of both sides of a sheet of paper, considered as a group of two surfaces, have their combined cyclosis equal to zero.

The surface of a baby's ring has a cyclosis equal to 2. For after the ring has been cut through it must be slit its whole length to enable every filament to contract to nothing.

The space occupied by the substance of such a ring has its cyclosis equal to 1.

The cyclosis of an unbounded plane is equal to 1.

[u]Art. Definition[/u]. The station number of a line, or linear configuration is its chorisis diminished by its cyclosis.
