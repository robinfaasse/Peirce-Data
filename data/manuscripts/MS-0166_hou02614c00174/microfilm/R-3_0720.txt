Should still more [del]letters[/del] [ins]characters[/ins] be wanted, the signs of the zodiac are very suitable. They are as follows:
$\curlyvee$ Aries, $\sigma_{0}$ Cancer, $\simeq$ Libra, $\vee\rho$ Capricornus,
$\succ$ Taurus, $\Omega$ Leo, $m_{\ell}$ Scorpio, $\approx$ Aquarius,
$\Pi$ Gemini, $m_{\ell}$ Virgo, $\uparrow$ Sagittarius, $\mathcal{H}$ Pisces.

The following planetary signs are convenient for various purposes: $\odot$ Sol, $\$ Mercury, $\venus$ Venus, $\oplus$ Terra, $\mars$ Mars, $\jupiter$ Jupiter, $\hbar$ Saturn, $\text{\H}$ Uranus, $\Psi$ Neptune.

We further distinguish a [u]point[/u] by putting the signs indicating it in square brackets. Thus $[\underline{b}\gamma]$ will be the point where the ray $\underline{b}$ cuts the plane $\gamma$, and $[\delta\epsilon f]$ will be the point of intersection of the planes, $\delta, \epsilon, f$.

We distinguish a [u]plane[/u] by putting the signs indicating it in parentheses. Thus [del]$\mathcal{H}$[/del] $(\underline{b}\text{C})$ will be the plane joining the point C and the ray $\underline{b}$, and $(\text{C}\text{D}\text{F})$ will be the plane of the three points, C, D, F.

We distinguish a [u]ray[/u] by putting the signs indicating it in braces. Thus $\{\text{Q}\text{R}\}$ will be the ray through $[\text{Q}]$ and $[\text{R}]$, while $\{\pi\phi\}$ will be the ray through $(\pi)$ and $(\phi)$. Again
$\{(\underline{b}\underline{c})(\underline{d}\underline{e})\}$
will be the ray [del]connect[/del] [ins]of intersection[/ins] of the plane of the two rays $\underline{b}, \underline{c}$, with the two [del]of[/del] rays $\underline{d}, \underline{e}$.
