[u]Art. 24[/u]. [u]Scholium[/u] 19. Another prominent part of topical geometry is the theory of knots. But, at present, no way of presenting this subject in [ins]an[/ins] elementary way suited for an elementary treatise is known.

[u]Definition 46[/u]. A [u]knot[/u] is a filament which without rupture or self-penetration cannot be laid without crossing itself upon a simply connected surface.
