166. Upon a globular surface how many [ins]topologically[/ins] forms of regular polyhedra are there? That is, polyhedra of which all the faces have the same number of edges [ins]round them[/ins], and all the summits have the same number of edges radiating from them.

Let $E$ be the number of edges, [del]$Y$[/del] [ins]$Z$[/ins] the number of edges to a face, $Y$ the number of edges to a summit.

$2E = Z \times \text{Number of faces}$, or [del]faces[/del] [ins]Faces[/ins] $= \frac{2E}{Z}$

$2E = Y \times \text{Number of summits}$, or Summits $= \frac{2E}{Y}$

Hence
$$2E \left( \frac{1}{Y} + \frac{1}{Z} \right) = E + 2$$

[del]Multiplying by $YZ$,[/del]
[del]$E [ (Y-2)(Z-2) - 4 ] = 2YZ$[/del]
[del]$E [ 4 - (Y-2)(Z-2) ] = 2YZ$[/del]

Dividing by $2E$
$$\frac{1}{Y} + \frac{1}{Z} = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{E}$$
