[u]Theorem[/u]. [u]A particle may so move upon any ray on[/u]
[u]which it lies, that after performing a circuit it shall have[/u]
[u]occupied all the points upon the ray[/u].

[u]Demonstration[/u]. For a ray being a line is generated by
the motion of a particle; and by the principle of the limit
there must be some limit to the motion. But there is
no limit furnished by the nature of the line itself and
therefore the motion must be capable of continuing until
the return of the particle to its initial position gives a limit.
