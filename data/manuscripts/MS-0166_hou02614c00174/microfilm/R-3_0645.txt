§ [u]Definition[/u] A solid body is that which occupies a solid space, and can only move from solid to solid.

[u]Definition[/u] A lamina is a body, or boundary between parts of a body, which occupies a surface and can only move from surface to surface.

[u]Definition[/u] A fibre, or filament, is a body, or boundary between parts of a body, which occupies a [del]surface[/del] [ins]line[/ins] and can only move from [del]surface[/del] [ins]line[/ins] to [del]surface[/del] [ins]line[/ins].

[u]Definition[/u] A particle is a body, or boundary between parts of a body, which occupies a point and can only move from point to point.

[u]Definition[/u] A flat lamina is a lamina in a plane.

[u]Definition[/u] A straight fibre is a fibre in a ray.

[u]Definition[/u] An optical body is a body filling all space in all the motions of which every straight fibre remains straight.
