[u]Art.[/u] 100. [del]Definition 128. A hamma is a point upon a line not interrupting the line having a number [ins]other than two[/ins] of sketches of the line extending from it.[/del]

[del]Definition 129. The census valency of a hamma is the excess of the number of sketches proceeding from it over two.[/del]
