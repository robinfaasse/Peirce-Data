four quantities constant let the fourth [del]change[/del] [ins]increase gradually[/ins] from a [del]large[/del] [ins]small[/ins] negative to a very large positive quantity, and the anharmonic ratio will move round the ring of quantity

Take for instance $P = -10$, $Q = 0$, $S = 10$, and let $R$ in the first ratio change. With $R = -1000000$, the ratio, which we may denote by $\alpha$, equals $\alpha = -\frac{10001}{9999}$ or nearly $-1$. For $R = -100$, $\alpha = -\frac{11}{9}$. For $R = -20$, $\alpha = -3$. For $R = -11$, $\alpha = -21$. For $R = -10$, $\alpha = \frac{1}{0}$. For $R = -9$, $\alpha = 19$. For $R = -8$, $\alpha = 9$. For $R = -6$, $\alpha = 4$. For $R = 0$, $\alpha = 1$. For $R = 6$, $\alpha = \frac{1}{4}$. For $R = 8$, $\alpha = \frac{1}{9}$. For $R = 10$, $\alpha = 0$. For $R = 12$, $\alpha = -\frac{1}{11}$. For $R = 20$, $\alpha = -\frac{1}{3}$. For $R = 100$, $\alpha = \frac{9}{11}$, etc.
