[u]Art. 22. Definition 22.[/u] A [del]simple[/del] [ins]complex[/ins] sparse sequence [ins]of the first kind[/ins] is a
sparse sequence consisting of two [del]parts[/del] [ins]partial[/ins] sequences, such that every
object of the second comes after every object of the first, while
the first has no last object.

A complex sparse sequence of the second kind is a sparse sequence
consisting of two partial sequences, such that every object of the
second comes after every object of the first, while the second
has no first object.

A simple sparse sequence is such a sparse sequence that if
it is separated into two partial sequences, every object of the
second coming after every object of the first, the first [ins]partial sequence[/ins] will
always have a last object, and the second partial sequence
will always have a first object.
