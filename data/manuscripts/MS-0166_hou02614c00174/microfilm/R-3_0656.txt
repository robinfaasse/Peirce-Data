77. The [u]inverse[/u] of a ratio, is the ratio of its consequent
to its antecedent. That is, it is the reciprocal of the fraction.

78. A compound ratio is a product of ratios; thus 35 is to
12 in the compound ratio of 5 to 3 and 7 to 4.

79. A duplicate ratio is a ratio of squares, thus 50 is to 18 in
the duplicate ratio of 5 to 3. A triplicate ratio is a ratio of cubes, etc.

80. A subduplicate ratio is a ratio of square roots.

81. Extreme and mean ratio is the relation between two
parts of a whole such that the greater is the mean proportional
between the less and the whole.
