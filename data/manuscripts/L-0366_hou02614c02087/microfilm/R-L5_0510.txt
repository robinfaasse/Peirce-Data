which an audible sound, visible shape, or other sensible object
may conform to, or be it a property or habit of behaviour
of something either experienced or imagined, — which is
on the one hand so determined (i.e. affected either by

so, that this mind is thereby itself mediately determined
by the same object. This is a very broad conception,
