If anybody will procure ten half-eagles at each of a hundred
ordinary places, — banks and reputable moneychangers, — and keep-
ing each decad separately, will toss up each half-eagle 250000 times
and give me his thousand numbers of heads, I will tell him what
the probability is that a half eagle, obtained in any prudent way
will have a probability differing not more than a given amount
of coming up heads. For I shall know what he means by that
question.
