[del]D.[/del] There seems also to have been a bath of 28 litres.
1st. Epiphanius always says the bath is 50 sextarii, which
would be 28.2 litres by the Farnese congius.
2nd. 2 Chronicles iv. 5 compared with 1 Kings vii 26 seems
to indicate a second bath about 2/3 of the first, or about
24 litres.
3rd. Josephus says 70 cors of flour make 41 Attic
medimni. Supposing he meant to say 7 cors or
70 ephas, this would make the epha 28.4 litres.
