§9. Epiphanius says the [del]Sath[/del] [ins]seah[/ins] was a heaped measure,
and contained 1½ Hebron modius. He also says that
it contained 1½ Roman modius or 24 sextarii. According

making 32.51 litres by the [del]Roman[/del] [ins]Fannian[/ins] congius and

§10. The Septuagint makes the Hebrew cor = 6 Egyptian
Artabs. Taking the great Artab at 36.176 litres, this
would make the bath 21.71 litres. This agrees with
Eupolemus, cited by Eusebius, and others.
§11. Solomon's molten sea is said by Josephus to
have been strictly hemispherical, and is said to
have been 10 cubits in diameter and to have held
2000 baths according to Kings, 3000 according to
Chronicles. Taking the sacred cubit as 0.525 metre,
the solid contents would be 37900 litres, making
or 18 litres or 12.6 litres for the bath.
