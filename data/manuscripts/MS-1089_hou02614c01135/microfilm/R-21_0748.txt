§1 Josephus says the hin was equal to 2 Attic χόες.
Now 1 χοῦς = 6 ξέσται or κοτύλαι. I follow Hultsch in
making it 3.03 litres. The measures found in Naxos (Dumont,
Revue archéologique 1873. Vol XXVI. p.43) make the χοῦς a little
less than this or say 3 litres. [ins]But this is not quite certain.[/ins] I make the hin [del]would thus be[/del]
it from these measures 2.96 litres. The hin would thus be
6 litres, and the Bath 36 litres.

§2 Josephus also says the gomor was 7 Attic κοτύλαι.
This would be 1.75 litres, or about 1/2 what would be expected
from §1. If we knew the explanation of this difficulty
this passage would probably make the Bath 35 litres.

§3. Josephus says the sath = 1 1/2 modius Italicus. Now
there was a wet and a dry modius Italicus, one is 1 1/2
times the other. Epiphanius quoting Josephus makes
him say the sath is one modius Italicus. He therefore
understands Josephus to mean the 1 1/2 of the smaller modius.
The small modius Italicus is 16 sextarii, the larger 24 sextarii.
The Farnese congius contains according to Hase, 3.387 litres.
As this is 6 sextarii, the sath would be 13.547 litres, [ins]and the bath 40.64[/ins] [del]would
be 40.64 litres.[/del] But modern writers make the congius
3.186 litres, making the epha only 38.23 litres.

§4. Josephus makes two other statements which are unintel-
ligible as they stand, and it is unnecessary to resort to con-
jectural modifications.
