Ladies and Gentlemen:
You may perhaps gain some useful
hints if I describe to you how I go to work
in studying philosophy. I shall merely sketch
the outline of the proceeding without going
into details. I mostly work pen in hand
and although important steps are taken
while I am away from my writing-table,
they are recorded at once. A given question
in philosophy comes up for discussion,
never mind how. I begin by writing out
a Collation upon it. That is, I begin by
setting down briefly yet sufficiently and
as formally as possible all the arguments
which I have seen used on the one side
or which seem to me likely to be used on
that side; and then I do the same for the
