It appears, then, that logical goodness is simply the
excellence of argument; — its negative, and more funda-
mental, goodness being its [del]vali[/del] soundness and weight, its
really having the force that it pretends to have and that
force being great, while its quantitative goodness consists in
the degree in which it advances our knowledge. In what
then does the soundness of argument consist?
