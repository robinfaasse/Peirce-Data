The modern psychological position
is that of structuralism –
structural psychology.
Mental contents are the data of
psychology, no matter what
other names they may bear.
Mental contents are the data of
psychology. In form they are
the conscious experiences of the
individual.
In psychology we look at the
data differently. We look at
experiences as such. mental
processes which are
actually at work and going on.
The very fact of consciousness
shows that at any given moment
a mental content exists. It will
not continue always to exist, but
it exists. It can be further analyzed.
