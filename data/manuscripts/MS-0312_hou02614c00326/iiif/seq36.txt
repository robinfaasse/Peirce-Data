And let us trust, also, that even when the
reforming energy is most intense, the sense
of common purpose and common
citizenship will still control.
We must have some degree of faith in that
natural disposition toward moderation
and compromise, which has hitherto enabled
this nation to overcome the bitterness of
extreme parties, and to steer a steady
course through all the various dangers
of its career.
