Beautifully it is said,
The mountain top is cold,
But there the morning's light
Is first and latest gold.

Where high the spirit's peak
Does to the heavens rise,
And catch the dawning's glow
And keep the sunset's dyes.

In world of thought and heart
The same law is confessed,
The cold and snowy peak
Is with the glory blessed.

Tis lonely on the heights,
And biting is the air,
But first and last the light
Of God is shining there.
