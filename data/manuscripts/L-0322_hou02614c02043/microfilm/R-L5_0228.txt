Ithaca N.Y. [date]25. II. 93[/date]

My Dear Peirce:

This is little more than preliminary to a possible later answer to yrs. of last week. Any more satisfactory reply would require careful re-reading of whatever I did say in Phil. Rev., and then a certain amount of expectant meditation till a good statement of the true theory (i.e. truer than previous statements in its relation to my own mental necessities & outlook, or to some phase of them) — should come to me. But the conditions just now don't permit this.
