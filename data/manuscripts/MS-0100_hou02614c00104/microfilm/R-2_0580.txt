[del]Postulate 2[/del]
[del]Scholium[/del] Then the [del]relation[/del] difference between C and A may be said to be twice that between B and A. And thus all geometrical relations are quantitative & infinitely.

[u]Postulate 2[/u]. This quantity is always continuous.

[u]Postulate 3[/u] Such relations may differ in quality as well as in quantity
