The ambition of Lyon Playfair (born 1818) was at first directed toward scientific eminence. He was the pupil of the great chemists, Graham and Liebig. He made discoveries of which the chief was the nitroprussides. It was already known that the coffee-colored solution obtained by treating ferrocyanide of potassium with nitric acid contained something peculiar and interesting.
