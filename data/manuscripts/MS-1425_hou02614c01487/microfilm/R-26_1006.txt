The following must serve. It shows, at least, that he knew how to treat a given person.
"The [del]S[/del] rule [ins][of the Athenaeum Club][/ins] requires that the committee shall be unanimous in its choice of the member upon whom it confers the honor of a special election. Playfair heard that there was one member of the committee, and only one, who had expressed some feeling of hostility to him. He wrote to this gentleman, and, telling him that he knew he was to be nominated for election, asked him to do him the honour of being his proposer."
The election, it is needful to add, was unanimous. It was not an unusual device; [del]but it requires accurate knowledge of one's man[/del] in this case it certainly could [del]do no harm[/del] [ins]only[/ins] do good.
