It is said by Logicians that the extension & comprehension of [del]terms[/del] [ins]terms[/ins] are in inverse relation; and if logic, as they maintain, has nothing to do with material truth, this [del]rule[/del] [ins]statement[/ins] is true. Extension in this case means the number of classes subordinate to any class; while comprehension, [ins]in any case,[/ins] means the number of characters of any class. In logic, as we conceive it, however, a different rule is needed; [del]To[/del] investigate it let us first define the connotation & denotation of symbols.

[del]But[/del] Logic is concerned only with the reference of symbols to objects. But as objects have three elements matter form & accidents, we must consider the reference of symbols to each of these.
