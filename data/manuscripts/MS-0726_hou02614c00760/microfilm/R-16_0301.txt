From the assertion of a rule and of a case under a rule, the affirmation of a result [ins]would[/ins] follow necessarily. Such a conclusion would belong to the first figure of syllogisms. The terms [u]rule[/u], [u]case[/u], [u]result[/u] are here employed to express facts in reference to the character of the propositions which express them and the relations of these propositions to each other. A [u]rule[/u] is a fact expressed in a universal proposition whose predicate is the predicate of some [del]other[/del] [ins]fact[/ins] included under it called a [u]result[/u].
