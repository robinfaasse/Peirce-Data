or          $kl_{\prime} - l_{\prime}k = 0$
But we have seen that
          $kl_{\prime} - l_{\prime}k = S^{\prime\prime\prime} + V^{\prime\prime\prime} - S^{\prime\prime\prime} + V^{\prime\prime\prime} = 2 V^{\prime\prime\prime}$
Hence $V^{\prime\prime\prime} = 0$. Then these vectors are not linearly independent, and a fourth unit vector is impossible.

But this proof does not apply when the [del]number[/del] [ins]multitude[/ins] of linearly independent expressions is endless; [ins]such algebras are non-linear.[/ins]

We thus see that even when we annul the commutative law of multiplication, there are but three linear algebras, [del]real algebra[/del] single algebra, ordinary imaginary algebra, and the algebra of real quaternions which obey all the other algebraic laws. The law which so limits the number is that

          if $xy = xz$, then $y = z$, unless $x=0$ or $x=\infty$.
