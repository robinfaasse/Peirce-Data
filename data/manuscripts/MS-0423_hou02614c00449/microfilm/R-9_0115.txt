Now the fact that in this case the numbers happened to be
rational fractions had nothing to do with the result. It is plain
that in every case, when between two values we insert
two, and between those two, two more again, and so on
indefinitely, there remains a limit which is never
reached; and the multitude which includes all such limits
cannot be made to correspond, one to one, to any denumerable
collection. [ins]$\checkmark$ ([pn]139[/pn])[/ins]
