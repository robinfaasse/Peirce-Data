$$l + \breve{l}$$

is all-important, inasmuch as it is the basis of all
quantitative thought. For the relation expressed
by it is a transitive relation. [del]Now,[/del] By a transitive
relation, we mean a relation like that of the copula.
If A be so related to B, and B [del]& C[/del] [ins]be so related[/ins] to C, then A
is so related to C. In other words, if t is a transi-
tive relation,

$$tt \prec t.$$

Now, this is the case with $l + \breve{l}$. For that

$$(l + \breve{l})(l + \breve{l}) \prec l + \breve{l}$$

is obvious. Though the reader sees how, I will, in conside-
ration of the importance of the matter, set down the
steps:

$$(l + \breve{l})(l + \breve{l})$$

$$\prec l + \breve{l}(l + \breve{l})$$

$$\prec l + \breve{l}l + \breve{l}$$

$$\prec l + r + \breve{l}$$

$$\prec l + \breve{l}.$$
