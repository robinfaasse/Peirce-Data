Such a relation may well be termed a [u]comparative[/u] relation. If Samson can lift something Ajax cannot lift, then Samson can lift everything Ajax can lift. Such a relation underlies all measurement; and the propriety of the designation I propose will be allowed.

With this conception, quantitative science begins. Note well how it has been suggested to us.

A trial of strength must begin by young Ajax, the challenger, doing various things which he "stumps" the champion, Samson, to imitate. If Samson cannot perform all of Ajax's feats, that settles it. But if it seems that Samson can do all that Ajax [ins]can do[/ins] [del]himself[/del] then he will in his turn [del]for[/del] [ins]do[/ins] something which he proposes that Ajax shall imitate. If Ajax cannot do all that Samson can do, that again settles it. But if it seems that each can repeat all the performances of the other, one concludes that they are equally strong. Thus equality is a complex relation.
