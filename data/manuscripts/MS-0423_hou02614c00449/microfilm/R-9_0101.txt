The syllogism is

For every memorandum of a purchase there is a counter-
cheque or stubb involving the drawing of a cheque;
Some purchases do not involve the drawing of a cheque;
[three_dots_triangular] Some stubbs are not memoranda of purchases.

Another example of De Morgan (Syllabus of Logic § 85) is
as follows:

For every man in the house there is an aged person;
Some of the men are not aged;
[three_dots_triangular] Some persons in the house are not men.

his best exertions, one at least might be pointed out who
abandoned the trial before the result was known. And yet,
