Chapter XVII. The Logic of Mathematics.

Pure Reason, started an extremely important question
about the logic of mathematics. He begins by drawing
a famous distinction, as follows:

"In [del]all[/del] judgments wherein the relation of a

which the [del]relation[/del] [ins]connection[/ins] of the predicate with the subject is thought
