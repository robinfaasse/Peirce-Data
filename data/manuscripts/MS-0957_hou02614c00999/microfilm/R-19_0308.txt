There is a charming branch of [del]mathematics[/del] [ins]geometry[/ins]
called the Theory of higher plane curves. In
treating it, mathematicians almost remove the
subject from out of the field of [del]at-[/del] looking by
introducing imaginary points. They give various
profound and weighty reasons for this course;
but I can whisper one in the reader's ear
which is better than all the rest; it is that
that is the only way in which they know
how to treat the subject.
