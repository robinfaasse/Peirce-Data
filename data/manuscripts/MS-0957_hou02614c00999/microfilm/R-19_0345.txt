therefore, make pretension to being
competent to discuss the mode of evolution

to say that having already made a deep
study of the reasoning in several branches of
science, I have seriously devoted my whole
time during some months, and this repeatedly,
under illustrious teachers, to gaining such an
acquaintance with biological research as

of biologists. I am, therefore, somewhat less
