Much is to be said on both sides. I have not
concealed, I could not conceal, my own
passionate predilection. This strong feeling
is in itself, I think, an argument of some
weight in favor of agapistic evolution, —
that is, provided it bespeaks the dictum
of the Sensible Heart. True, it throws one
side of the shield in strong sunshine and
leaves the other in the gloom; but I
shall study to be fair.
