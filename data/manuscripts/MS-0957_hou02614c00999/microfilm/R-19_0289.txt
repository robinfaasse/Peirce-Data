[ins]Second aspect. Resemblances of the theories.[/ins]

little more accurately the logical affinities
of the different theories. Natural selection,
as conceived by Darwin, is a mode of
evolution in which the only positive agent
of change is fortuitous variation. But to
effect any progress, chance has to be
seconded by some action which shall
hinder the propagation of some kinds of vari-
