AMERICAN MATHEMATICAL SOCIETY,
41 EAST 49TH STREET,
NEW YORK. [date]23 October, 1894.[/date]

My dear Mr. Peirce,

I was delighted to learn from your note, just received, that you had a paper (or perhaps two) in readiness for presentation to this Society. I am also much interested in your proposal of a Mathematical Dictionary —

I hope that you may be able to attend the meeting next Saturday, or, if not, the following one ([date]November 24th[/date]). Mr. McClintock & Mr. Hill have both asked for an hour at the meeting this week, and I am afraid that the time available for your communications would, not be so extensive as we should wish to have it —

If you should not be able to come next Saturday, may I not announce your communications for the November meeting?

Hoping that it may be possible for you to come on next Saturday, I am

Very truly yours
Thomas S. Fiske
