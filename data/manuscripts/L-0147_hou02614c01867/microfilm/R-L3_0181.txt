NEW YORK MATHEMATICAL SOCIETY,
41 EAST 49TH STREET,
NEW YORK.

[date]February 22, 1894[/date]

C. S. Peirce, Esq.,
"Arisbe," Milford, Pa.;

My dear Sir:

It gives me great
pleasure to become a subscri-
ber to the work you have
in preparation upon "<u>the
Principles of Philosophy</u>."
I trust, I shall receive
information as to when
the first payment becomes
due. I have long wished
that you might find an
opportunity to give,
in a single continuous
work, expression to the
results of your many
philosophic and scientific
labors.

Very truly yours
Thomas S. Fiske
