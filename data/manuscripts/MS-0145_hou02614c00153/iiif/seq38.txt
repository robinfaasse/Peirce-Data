A somewhat analogous substance
has been obtained from a very
common British plant, the Scurvy-grass
(Cochlearia officinalis). It is a volatile
oil, called Oil of Scurvy-grass, and
is a butyl-mustard oil (Secondary Butyl-
Isothiocyanate), [ins]C₄H₉NCS[/ins] having the same 
composition as the mustard oil of the 
Mustard seeds, but a different 
constitution. In chemical composition
this is a fundamental, but at the
same time a very simple body.
