It is commonly said that geometrical propositions
are either [u]Metrical[/u] or [u]Descriptive[/u]. But this offends
against what are called [u]the Rules of Division[/u]; but this is too
modest a title; they are essential principles, not mere
rules. The fault of the division in question is that the
department of [u]Descriptive Geometry[/u] is merely an unde-
scribed residue after removing [u]Metrical propositions[/u].
It consists of two different classes of propositions
capable of affirmative definitions and differing from
each other in respects as important as these which
differentiate either class from the metrical propositions.
