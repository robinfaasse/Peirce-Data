However, in rejoicing that that definition has disap-
peared let us not forget that after all, though quantity is
not of the essence of the object of mathematics, there is mighty
little mathematics in the mathematics that does not involve
quantity; so that it is convenient enough for many purposes
to think of mathematics as mainly a science of quantity or of
quanta. In so far as we do this geometry may be called the science
of dimensional quanta. For there is even less left of
geometry when the number of dimensions is reduced to one
than there is left of mathematics when the system of values
is reduced to two, - the true and the false, - as in the Boolean
algebra of logic.
