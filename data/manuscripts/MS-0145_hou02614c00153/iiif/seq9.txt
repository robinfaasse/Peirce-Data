Preface.

The definition of mathematics as the science of quantity seems
now at last to be happily exploded. In its later years it was a
capital example of the disposition of the "vulgar" among the
educated to hold on to phrases without minding much whether
their meanings be retained or no. For the three principal terms
in this definition, [u]mathematics[/u], [u]science[/u], and [u]quantity[/u] had
long been completely revolutionized in meaning before the
phrase itself lost any of its favour. The definition is given by
Boethius, with whom "mathematics" [del]included[/del] meant the
whole quadrivium, including astronomy, music, and [del]an[/del]

while it excluded "logistic", or what we call arithmetic
