There is, in the first place, Projective Geometry, or, as Cremona
states it, the geometry of rectilinear projections from points
followed by sections of such projections by planes. This is
obviously a development of linear perspective. In it the
ray or unlimited right line, is the dominant conception. Now
a geometrical definition of a ray is impossible. We simply
know it by experience as the optical line. This conception
does not suppose [ured]any definite individual[/ured], but it is a particularly good
example of that logical genus [del]the[/del] to which Hegel gave the wretched
subjective designation of [ured]Begriff[/ured], that is a whole class of definite
possibilities filling all space with a reticulation. There might be an
