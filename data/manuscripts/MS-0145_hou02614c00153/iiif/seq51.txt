The operation of hypostatic abstraction consists in substituting
for any predication that of possessing a quality, or that of
standing in a relation. And so that in mathematics an
operation is itself made the subject of operations. The hypo-
statically abstract quality or relation is necessarily [u]general[/u],
that is, is not subject to the [u]principle of excluded middle[/u].
The universe of characters is thereby quite outside the universe
of existents.
