[pn]126[/pn] and medium of communication is a species of [del]triad[/del] [ins]genuine[/ins] triad. A third is something which differs from a first in one respect and from a second in another respect. If this other respect is a contrary respect, the third is a medium. Hence in Latin, where "medium" is a vernacular word, the distinction between it and "tertium" is slight. Thus, the principium exclusi medii is indifferently, though more correctly, called principium exclusi tertii.
