The representation of logical by spatial extension is excellent; for
it applies the principle of geometrical diagrams in the purest and most
elegant possible way; and this system is particularly good as
showing the spurious inferences from the particular propo-
sitions. If it could only be extended so as to represent the logic
of relations, it would be superior to every other method. But
until that can be done, its utility is lamentably narrow.
