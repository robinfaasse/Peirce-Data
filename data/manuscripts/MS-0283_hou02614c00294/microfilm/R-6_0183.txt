Superman. What then, are pleasure and pain? The question has
been sufficiently discussed, and the answer ought by this time
to be ready. They are secondary feelings or generalizations of
such feelings; that is, of feelings attaching themselves to, and
excited by, other feelings. A toothache is painful. It is not
