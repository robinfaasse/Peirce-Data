Leo a sort of [del]rocking-horse[/del]

with a woman holding his tail

& a bird under the tail all in one

boat. This boat looks like a snake

Virgo is a woman, standing

up (not lying as Ptolemy represents

her) holding a spika of corn. She

occupies but a small place in the

zodiac. She is followed by Anis-Osiris

(or it might possibly be Saturn)

Libra is a pair of scales (not Greek)

Scorpio has no large claws

Sagittarius is plainly Babylonian
