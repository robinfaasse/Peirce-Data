[u]12[sup]th[/sup] Species[/u]. Premises: $\breve{p} \dagger \bar{h}\mu$ and $\breve{\mu} \dagger \breve{h}s$, or
$(\breve{p} \dagger \bar{h}\mu)(\breve{\mu} \dagger \breve{h}s)$.

Again there are two ways. On the one hand,
$\breve{p} \dagger \bar{h}\mu(\breve{\mu} \dagger \breve{h}s)$,
$\therefore \breve{p} \dagger \bar{h}\breve{h}s$,
Whence $\therefore \breve{p} \dagger \Upsilon s$.

On the other hand,
$(\breve{p} \dagger \bar{h}\mu)\breve{\mu} \dagger \breve{h}s$,
$\therefore \breve{p} \dagger \bar{h}\Upsilon \dagger \breve{h}s$.

That is, every P wants some quality other than any that no
S has. But this is deducible from the first.
