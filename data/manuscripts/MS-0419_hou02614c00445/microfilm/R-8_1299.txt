To the principles of contradiction and excluded middle,
[del]in the forms[/del]
[del]$\infty \prec l \cdot \bar{l} \prec 0$[/del] [del]$\infty \prec l + \bar{l}$[/del]
correspond the highly important formulae: —

[del]$\breve{l} \bar{l} \prec \top$[/del]
$\breve{l} \bar{l} \prec \top$ $1 \prec \breve{l} \dagger \bar{l}$

§201. The best way to use this algebra is to consider each
entire formula written down as an assertion to be a
description of the relation which everything bears
to itself. Thus if $h$ means "possesses as a [del]character[/del] [ins]quality[/ins]"
the six propositions of the first order in the logic of
the universe of finite marks are
$\breve{s} \dagger h \dagger \bar{\pi} = \Pi_{i} \Pi_{j} (\bar{s}_{i} + h_{ij} + \bar{\pi}_{j}) =$ Every $S$ has every $\pi$.
$\breve{s} (h \dagger \bar{\pi}) = \Sigma_{i} \Pi_{j} s_{i} \cdot (h_{ij} + \bar{\pi}_{j}) =$ Some $S$ has all $\pi$s.
$\breve{s} h \dagger \bar{\pi} = \Pi_{j} \Sigma_{i} s_{i} \cdot h_{ij} + \bar{\pi}_{j} =$ Every $\pi$ is in some $S$ or other.
$(\breve{s} \dagger h) \pi = \Sigma_{j} \Pi_{i} (\bar{s}_{i} + h_{ij}) \cdot \pi_{j} =$ Some $\pi$ is in all $S$s.
$\breve{s} \dagger h \pi = \Pi_{i} \Sigma_{j} \bar{s}_{i} + h_{ij} \cdot \pi_{j} =$ Some $\pi$ is in some $S$s.
