§203. Propositions are divisible into four classes according as they are expressible in one or other of the four forms
$0 \dagger a \dagger 0$
[del]$(0 \dagger a) \omega$[/del] $(0 \dagger a) \omega$
[del]$0 \dagger a \omega$[/del] $0 \dagger a \omega$
$\omega \dagger a \omega$


§204. A [del]very[/del] good excercize for the student will be to trace out the conclusions to be drawn from one of the above [del]four[/del] propositions as one premise and for the other one the four propositions resulting from substituting $b$ for $a$ in the above four forms. This had better be done by the regular method with $\Pi$'s, $\Sigma$'s, and indices.
