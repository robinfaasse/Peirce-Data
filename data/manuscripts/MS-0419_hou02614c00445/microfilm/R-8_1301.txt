[del]Second[/del] [ins]2nd[/ins] Species. Premises: $\breve{p} \dagger \bar{h} \dagger \mu$ and $\breve{s} (h \dagger \bar{\mu})$.
Converting major and [del]relatively multiplying[/del]
[del]$\breve{s} (h \dagger \bar{\mu}) (\breve{p} \dagger \bar{h} \dagger \mu$[/del]
$\breve{s} (h \dagger \bar{\mu}) (\breve{\mu} \dagger \breve{\bar{h}} \dagger \bar{p})$.

Hence, as before
$\breve{s} (h \dagger \breve{\bar{h}} \dagger \bar{p})$.

3rd Species. Premises: $\breve{p} \dagger \bar{h} \dagger \mu$ and $\breve{s} h \dagger \bar{\mu}$.
Converting major and relatively multiplying
$(\breve{s} h \dagger \bar{\mu}) (\breve{\mu} \dagger \breve{\bar{h}} \dagger \bar{p})$.

Hence, as before
$\breve{s} h \dagger \breve{\bar{h}} \dagger \bar{p}$.
This follows from the conclusion of the second Species.

4th Species. Premises: $\breve{p} \dagger \bar{h} \dagger \mu$ and $\breve{\mu} (\breve{h} \dagger \bar{s})$.
Multiplying relatively
$(\breve{p} \dagger \bar{h} \dagger \mu) \breve{\mu} (\breve{h} \dagger \bar{s})$.

Hence, as before
$(\breve{p} \dagger \bar{h}) (\breve{h} \dagger \bar{s})$.

Hence again
$\breve{p} \dagger \bar{s}$,
or by conversion
$\breve{s} \dagger \bar{p}$.

To make sure of the meaning of this, put it in the form
$\Pi_i \Pi_j \bar{s}_{ji} \mathbin{\psi} \bar{p}_{ji}$
$= \Pi_j \bar{s}_j \mathbin{\psi} \bar{p}_j$.
That is, whatever $j$ maybe, it is either not $s$ or, if it is, is not $p$.
