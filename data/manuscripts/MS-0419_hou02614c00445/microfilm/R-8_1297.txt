[u]§197. Relative addition and multiplication are defined as[/u]
[u]follows[/u]
[ins]$$l \dagger b = (l \dagger b)_{a\omega} = \Pi_i \ l_{ai} + b_{i\omega}$$[/ins]
[ins]$$l b = (l b)_{a\omega} = \Sigma_i \ l_{ai} \cdot b_{i\omega}$$[/ins]

[fig]Printed text block with handwritten annotations[/fig]
Relative addition and multiplication are subject to the
associative law. That is,
$$l \dagger (b \dagger s) = (l \dagger b) \dagger s,$$
$$l (b s) = (l b) s.$$

Two formulæ so constantly used that hardly anything
can be done without them are
[del][u]Two formulæ.[/u][/del] [ins]$$l (b \dagger s) \prec l b \dagger s,$$[/ins]
[ins]$$(l \dagger b) s \prec l \dagger b s.$$[/ins]
[del]We may call them[/del] [ins]the all-important formulæ.[/ins]
The former asserts that whatever is lover of an object
that is benefactor of everything but a servant, stands to
everything but servants in the relation of lover of a
benefactor. The latter asserts that whatever stands to
any servant in the relation of lover of everything but its
benefactors, is a lover of everything but benefactors of
servants. The following formulæ are obvious and triv-
ial:—
$$l s \dagger b s \prec (l \dagger b) s$$
$$l \dagger s \cdot b \dagger s \prec (l \dagger s) \cdot (b \dagger s).$$

Unobvious and important, however, are these:—
$$(l \dagger b) s \prec l s \dagger b s$$
$$(l \dagger s) \cdot (b \dagger s) \prec l \cdot b \dagger s.$$

§198. — There are a number of curious development formulæ.
Such are
$$(l \dagger b) s = \Pi_p \{ l (s \cdot p) \dagger b (s \cdot \overline{p}) \}$$
$$l (b \dagger s) = \Pi_p \{ (l \cdot p) b \dagger (l \cdot \overline{p}) s \}$$
$$(l \dagger b) \dagger s = \Sigma_p \{ [ l \dagger (s \dagger p) ] \cdot [ b \dagger (s \dagger \overline{p}) ] \}$$
$$l \dagger (b \dagger s) = \Sigma_p \{ [ (l \dagger p) \dagger b ] \cdot [ (l \dagger \overline{p}) \dagger s ] \}.$$

The summations and multiplications denoted by $\Sigma$ and $\Pi$
are to be taken non-relatively, and all relative terms are
to be successively substituted for $p$.
