[u]Pythagoras[/u] Born 569 or. 4070.
Native of Island of Samos. His
father was Mnesarcus, who re-
ceived citizenship of Samos for
having provisioned island at
moment of disette. Appears
to have been a rich, & impor-
tant man. Fond of music.
Fled from Samos 551 to study
in foreign parts. Stopped at
Lesbos, to see his uncle Zoilos.
There met Pherecydes, & 549
went to Miletus to follow lectures
of Anaximander & Thales who
advised him to go to Egypt. Touched
at Sidon. Arrived to Amasis
to be admitted to priesthood. Priests
tried to frighten him by hard
novitiate. No go. Remained
there 22 years. Carried captive
to Babylon. There allied himself
with Chaldee priests & magi
Learned Astronomy & Medicine
