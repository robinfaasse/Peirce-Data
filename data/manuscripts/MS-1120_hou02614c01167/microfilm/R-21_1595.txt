He asked Charles to send him
to Rome as his representative.
Charles imprisoned Rienzi,
and put him in power of Pope

He substantially retracted
and in July 1351 was sent
to Avignon.

A very mild accusation
was got up. He was condemned
to death, but Petrarch took
up his case, & the sentence
was commuted to detention

The Pope in July 1353 sent
him to second Cardinal
Albornoz in reestablishing
order in Rome. In June
1354, the war being finished
he was made Senator in Peroscia
