Reconciled with cardinal by intervention
of Petrarch returned to Rome as notary
of urban chamberlain. 1344. Tried
vainly to bring magistrates to his idea
of reform. Made no mystery of it. Day
of Pentecost 20 May 1347, collected citizens
without arms at the Capitol. Heard
30 masses during the night. Made great
speech proposing new laws. Senators
out of way & Rienzi made Dictator
Tribune of liberty, Peace, & Justice.
Nobles driven out. Urban militia
created. Brigandage repressed. 1800
mortal hatreds healed. Prices of
provisions lowered by order of police
& general bliss. Pope confirmed
his authority without difficulty.

Required all Italian states to send
two Plenipotentiaries to Congress at
Rome to bring about Italian Unity.
Messengers everywhere received with
enthusiasm. The Queen of Naples
& King of Hungary submitted a
dispute to his arbitration.
