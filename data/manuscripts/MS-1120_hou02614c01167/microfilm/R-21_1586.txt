Dispute with Stefano de la
Colonna who was driven
out of the city with other
barons & Rienzi assumed
for himself and the pontifical
vicar the title of Tribuni de
lo puopolo e liberatori.

The barons quarrelled
among themselves & nothing
was done. Rienzi then
summoned them to come
before him. The first to come
was Stefanello de la Colonna
son of Missere Stefano and
took oath of [del]adherence[/del] [ins]obedience[/ins]. Then
came Missere Rannallo de li
Orsini, puoi Janni Colonna,
puoi Jordano, puoi Misser Stefano,
then all the barons.
