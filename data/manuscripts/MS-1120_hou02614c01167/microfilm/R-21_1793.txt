civil war. Sister became mistress of
Duke of York. He was uneducated but
of natural eloquence, good judgment,
calm, courage

[u]Oxenstierna[/u]. Showed eminent talent before 20
but no strong bent. A cousin & grand
nephew showed some talent & force.

[u]Palestrina[/u] Obscure parentage. Family name
doubtful. First work at age of 30.

Grandfather & grandmother's father public
men. From earliest age showed ex-
traordinary wit. So much so that his
father gave up all business to devote
himself to son's education. At 12 wrote
treatise on sound (experimental)
Strong bent for mathematics, notwith-

hidden. At 16 wrote treatise on
conic sections which astonished Descartes
