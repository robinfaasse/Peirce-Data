§4. The two real functions in the expression
for $e^{yi}$ are called the cosine and sine of $y$.
That is
$e^{yi} = \cos y + \sin y \cdot i$
$\cos y = \sum_{0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{r} y^{2r}}{(2r)!}$ $\sin y = \sum_{0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{r} y^{2r+1}}{(2r+1)!}$

equation
$y - \frac{1}{3!} y^{3} + \frac{1}{5!} y^{5} - \frac{1}{7!} y^{7} + \text{etc} = B$
