29 WEST TWENTY-THIRD STREET,
NEW YORK.
[date]Nov. 24, 1893[/date]

Dear Mr. Garrison:

May I inquire to whom who
wrote the review of "Darwin & Darwin" in
the last Nation? I do not know who had
any hand in the obituary, except perhaps John
Fiske, who could do it? Is there a new
light on the horizon?

Sorry I never see you, but I still
manage to keep,

Very truly yours,
Henry Holt.

W. P. Garrison, Esq.
