All mathematicians are great admirers of what they call "elegance". By this they seem to mean a [del]method[/del] [ins]style[/ins] of exposition in which, a fertile form of representing [ins]certain positive[/ins] mathematical objects having been found, all the use is made of that form before introducing any form foreign to the idea of that one. The quality is not only pleasing, but usually facilitates the development of a mathematical subject [del]up[/del] to a certain point. But pushed too far, its practical inconveniences may become extreme.
