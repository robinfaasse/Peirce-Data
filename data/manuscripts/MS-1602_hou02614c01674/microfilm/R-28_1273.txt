I remember Dr. Gould coming to my father
to take lessons, and he left college in 1844.

I remember driving with father to Dr. Bache's
camp at Blue Hill, in [date]1845[/date]. I well remember
the Quincy's who left Cambridge in [date]1845[/date]. I also
remember a great deal about the building of the
New House. I recollect the discussion of the plans
before it was begun, & the building of it. Then
it was not finished in time, & the Bartletts
who were to take the old house, moved in and
we boarded with them, & John Bartlett made
[del]for[/del] a wonderful trick cabinet of drawers out
of [del]tobacco[/del] cigar boxes. I remember eating my first
meal in the new house of oysters, seated on the
back stairs. I think we must have moved
in [date]1845[/date]. I remember in that year Judge Story's
funeral, the Irish Famine, the Mexican War, and
the retirement of Professor Treadwell. I soon
after began going to Miss Ware's school.
