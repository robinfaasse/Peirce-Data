I divide Ethics into
A. Private Ethics
B. Public Ethics

I divide Arts into
A. Arts practiced by individuals
B. Sociology, or public arts.

I divide Policy into
A. Policy toward men
B. Religion, or policy toward superior beings
C. Policy toward lower animals.

No further subdivision of Philosophy is requisite.

I divide Psychics into
a. Psychology proper, or mind viewed partly at least from an internal standpoint
b. Anthropology etc (say Empsychonomy) or mind viewed wholly from an external standpoint
[ins]$\alpha$[/ins] in individuals, men, spiritual intelligences, animals
$\beta$ in families
$\gamma$ in communities & races.
