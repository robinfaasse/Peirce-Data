[u]Step 5. Pragmatics[/u]. This is the study of how we ought to [del]act[/del] behave in view of the knowledge of our surroundings afforded by episcopy. Pragmatics has three branches, namely,
A. [u]Ethics[/u], or the general principles of conduct,
B. [u]Arts[/u], or the study of general problems not going back to first principles, and
C. [u]Policy[/u], or the study of the course proper to be pursued in special cases arising in history.

Ethics subdivides into —
a. Private Ethics, and
b. Public Ethics.

Arts subdivide into —
a. Private arts, whether
α. Teleological, or studying how to attain given ends,
א Of bodily well-being,
Food and drink, health, [ins]burial,[/ins] clothing, [del]shelter[/del], light, warmth, furniture, house-keeping.
ב Of general mental needs, especially
Communication and
Entertainment.
ג Special mechanical ends, such as
Locomotion, etc. etc.
