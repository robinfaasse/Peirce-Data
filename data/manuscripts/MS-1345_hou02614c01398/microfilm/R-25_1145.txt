1. [u]Philosophy[/u], or the science of the universal characters of phenomena;

2. [u]Nomology[/u], or the study of these characters of phenomena which are general, without being universal, in the hope of accounting for them by connecting them with these universal laws which philosophy has brought to light;

3. [u]Episcopy[/u], or the description of individual things with a view to explaining them by these laws which nomology has taught us.
