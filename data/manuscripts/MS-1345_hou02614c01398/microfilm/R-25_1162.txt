[u]Synopsis of Logic[/u].

[u]Chapter I. The Place of Philosophy among the Sciences[/u].

[del]Art.[/del] [ins]Art. 1.[/ins] [u]Division 1[/u]. The [u]Sciences[/u] are divided according to the distance at which they paint nature into:
1. [u]Mathematics[/u].
2. [u]Philosophy[/u].
3. Nomology: general physics and general psychics;
4. Natural History, the descriptions of classes;
5. Sciences descriptive of individual objects, geography, astronomy, ordinary history, etc.

[u]Definition 1[/u]. [u]Mathematics[/u] is the development of hypotheses.
[u]Definition 2[/u]. [u]Philosophy[/u] is the science which deals with the general phenomena of life.

[del]Art 2.[/del] [ins]Art 2.[/ins] [u]Division 2[/u]. [u]Philosophy[/u] is divided into
1. The philosophy of thought: the [u]Philosophical Trivium[/u].
2. The philosophy of action: [u]Ethics[/u], etc.
3. The philosophy of being: [u]Metaphysics[/u].
