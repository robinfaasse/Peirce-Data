B. [u]Empsychography[/u], or the account of intelligent

C. [u]Cosmography[/u], or the account of inanimate nature.
[u]Ergography[/u] subdivides into —
a. The account of achievements of individuals,
b. The account of achievements of families, and
c. The account of achievements of communities.
[u]Empsychography[/u] subdivides two ways; first, into —
a. [u]Biography[/u], the account of individuals,
b. [u]Genealogy[/u], the account of families, and
c. [u]History[/u], the account of societies;
and second, into —
a'. [del]Zoögraph[/del] Theriac empsychography, the account of
lower animals,
b'. Human empsychography, the account of men,
c'. Pneumatic empsychography, the account of spiritual
intelligences.
