Charles Pierce, Esq.,
108 West 89th Street,
New York City.

My dear Mr. Pierce:-

Your esteemed letter safely to hand. I find no difficulty whatever in any part of it, but am able to follow it, as I think, in the most satisfactory manner. I am quite in agreement as to the necessity of basing these things on mathematics. I shall now eagerly look forward to the second instalment, as there is every hope of my being able to follow all the successive steps.

Thanking you very much, I am, dear Mr. Pierce, with kindest regards,

Yours very truly,

W. T. Frankland
[del]Associate Actuary.[/del]
