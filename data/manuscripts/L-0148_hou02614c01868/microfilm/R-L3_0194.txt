NEW-YORK LIFE INSURANCE COMPANY,
346 & 348 BROADWAY.

New York, [date]May 4th, 1897[/date].

Charles S. Peirce Esq.,
No. 108 West 89th Street,
New York City.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

Please accept my best thanks for the three instalments of the Exposition, which severally reached me in due time, and from which I promise myself a very great deal of pleasure.

I am truly glad that Mrs. Peirce is recovering, and earnestly trust that you will ere long be entirely free from anxiety.

Believe me,

Very sincerely yours,

W. Frankland
