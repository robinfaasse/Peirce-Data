✓ 393 West End Avenue

Monday Evening

My dear Mr. Peirce

I have received your welcome note, and would like to accept your suggestion to place myself under your kind tuition in the matter of the "Logic of Relatives," more especially as I feel certain I have not at present the surplus energy to master the German author. I therefore enclose herewith a check for $30, which I trust will safely reach you, and would suggest that

[page break]

my first step should be to ponder, in solitude and at leisure, some very elementary exordium, either printed or written, on your logical theory—something that would serve merely to introduce me to its study.

Very many thanks for the loan of James. I have been glancing at him this evening and admire his calibre. And his private letter to you enclosed in the book, seems to show that he is a good fellow.

With kind regards

Very truly yours
F.W. Frankland
