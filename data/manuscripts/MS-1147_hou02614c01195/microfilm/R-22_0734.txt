Copula in exact logic is any relation introduced into all
propositional forms. A transitive copula is one which renders
the mood [u]Barbara[/u] valid. Schröder has proved that for
any such copula there is a relative term [ins]r[/ins], such that
writing "is" for the copula, the proposition "$S$ is $P$" is equi-
valent to "$S$ is at once $r$ to, and $r$ to everything [ins]$\bar{r}$ is by[/ins], $P$."
A copula of correlative inclusion is one for which, not only
[u]Barbara[/u], but also the principle of identity, hold good. For
every such copula there is a relation, $r$, such that "$S$ is $P$"
is equivalent to "$S$ is $r$ to everything [ins]$\bar{r}$ is by[/ins] $P$." If the
copula is such that [del]this equivalence holds[/del] [ins]the latter proposition follows from the
former[/ins], no matter what relative $r$ may be, the copula is
[u]the copula of inclusion[/u].
