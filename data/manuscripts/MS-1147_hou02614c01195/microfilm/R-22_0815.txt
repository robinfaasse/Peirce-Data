it follows that

$$A + 3B + 3C = Xp^{-1} + Yq^{-1} + Zr^{-1}$$
$$B + 3C + 3(A + 3B + 3C) = Xp^{-2} + Yq^{-2} + Zr^{-2}$$
$$C + 3(A + 3B + 3C) + 3[B + 3C + 3(A + 3B + 3C)] = Xp^{-3} + Yq^{-3} + Zr^{-3}$$

and, in short, we may go on calculating as many members
