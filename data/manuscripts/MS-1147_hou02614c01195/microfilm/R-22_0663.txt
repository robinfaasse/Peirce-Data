which date from early times began in forms of the crudest
nonsense, medicine as magic, astronomy as astrology, arithmetic
as Pythagorean metaphysics, chemistry as alchemy. Aristotle
calls Zeno of Velia the inventor of logic; and for the Eleatics
logic seems to have been an art of paradox. For could any man
