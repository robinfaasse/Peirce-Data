These rules thus implicitly define the effect of the junction by any heavy
line that does not cross the line of an oval. It only remains to define
the effect of a heavy line that does cross the line of an oval. This can
conveniently be done by amending Rules IV and V.

[u]Amended IV[/u]. Any two mutually transformable indices may be
joined by a heavy line, provided that this, if it pass out of one oval
and into another, shall pass so far out as to be unenclosed.
