With this qualification, the formula, if restricted to individuals,

[u]Socrates is a cousin of a cousin of Socrates, if there be a cousin of Socrates.[/u]

If however, the formula is to be understood as applicable to
general terms, as well as to individuals, an additional explanation
is required to the effect that "not-A" does not mean "[del]not[/del] [ins]some[/ins] A",
but "not any A there may be". At the same time, this "any there may
be" enables us to dispense with the corresponding qualification of
the formula of contradiction itself, which now becomes analogous
to the following:

[u]Every man is similar to whatever there may be that is similar
to whatever man there may be.[/u]
