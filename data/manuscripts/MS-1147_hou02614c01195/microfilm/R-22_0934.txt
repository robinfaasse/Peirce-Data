[u]Norm[/u]. Prof. Baldwin. In my opinion [u]norm[/u] is not a term of philosophy. It may be that a few German logicians may receive it after Wundt. It may be a term of Natural History. But I am confident that in the sense in which Windt uses it it cannot be received into English. By "cannot" I mean it is impossible it should happen. Still, try to give what my impression of the meaning would be in English.
