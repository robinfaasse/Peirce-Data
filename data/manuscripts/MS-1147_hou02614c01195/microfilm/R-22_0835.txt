As matter the conception of matter was in large measure the creation of Aristotle, so was that of form due to Plato. The morality mingled with doubt which characterized Socrates was a mixture favorable to the development of that notion. The morality tended that way; because form is what a thing ought to be; and [del]the[/del] [ins]moderate[/ins] scepticism tended that way, by leading to a distinction between those elements of experience which brutally force themselves upon the mind, and those elements of truth which recommend themselves by their reasonableness.
