[u]II[/u]. Two graphs severally scriptible are scriptible together,

This depends upon the logical principle that if everything
that a proposition asserts, [del]implies[/del] [ins]is true[/ins], the proposition is

is true, then the proposition "A and B" is true.

Considered as a part of a definition, the rule asserts
that juxtaposition without contact has such a signification
that if all the parts are true, the whole is true. This sort of juxta-
position is thus completely defined; but contact is only par-
tially defined, as yet.
