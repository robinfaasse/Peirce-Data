[u]Every man is lover of whatever man there may be[/u].

But a different interpretation may equally be put
upon the principle of contradiction, which equally removes
the vicious circulus in definiendo. Namely, it may be under-
stood thus:

[u]Whatever there may be which is, at once, A and not-A is X[/u],
no matter what term X may be. The second [u]not[/u] is thus
avoided, by substituting a formal definition of "that
which is nothing." Here, as before, if A is to be a general, an
additional explanation must show that not "any" is meant.
