A highly practical improvement on this method has been invented by Venn ([u]Symbolic Logic[/u], ch. XI.) If four classes are involved in the premises, Fig. 20, is first drawn, representing the sixteen classes into which the four classes by affirmation and negation divide the universe. Then those that the premises render non-existent are blackened.
