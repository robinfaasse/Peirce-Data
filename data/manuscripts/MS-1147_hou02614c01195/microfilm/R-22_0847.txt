3. Simpliciter [u]feci[/u], convertitur [u]Eva[/u] per acci.
[u]Astō[/u] per contra; siccat fit conversio tota.
Asserit [u]A[/u], negat [u]E[/u], sed universaliter ambae;
Asserit [u]I[/u], negat [u]O[/u], set particulariter ambo.

[ [del]Some copies correct the quantity by putting sunt for the first
which and these verses always make long.[/del]
[ins]sed[/ins]], [u]feci[/u], [u]Eva[/u], [u]Asto[/u], mean the pairs of propositional forms
signified by their vowels. E and I are converted simply; E
and A, per accidens (acci); A and O, per contrapositionem.

4. Prae, contradic.; post, contra.; prae postque, subalter.
'Non omnis', 'quidam non'; 'omnis non' quasi 'nullus';
'Non nullus', 'quidam'; sed 'nullus non' valet 'omnis';
'Non aliquis', 'nullus'; 'non quidam non' valet 'omnis';
'Non alter', 'neuter'; 'neuter non' praestat 'uterque'.

[The first verse only is in Shyreswood. Verses similar to the others
are given by B. and I. The first verse means that [u]non[/u] placed
before [u]omnis[/u] or [u]nullus[/u] gives the contradictory proposition;
placed after, the contrary; placed before and after, the subaltern.]
