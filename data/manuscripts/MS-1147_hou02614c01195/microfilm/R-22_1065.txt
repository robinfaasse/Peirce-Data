What is here required is to put the [del]conditions of the[/del] [ins]statements of the[/ins] given form of relationship into a comprehensible form. For example, given that a relation [u]r[/u] is transitive, that is, is such that A, B, C being any individuals whatever, if A is in the relation [u]r[/u] to B, and B in the same relation to C, then also A will be in the relation [u]r[/u] to C. That is, the things [u]r[/u]'d by anything, A, include everything [u]r[/u]'d by any of those things.
