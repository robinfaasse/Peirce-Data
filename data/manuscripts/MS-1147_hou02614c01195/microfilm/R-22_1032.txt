And as 'happy' and 'man' can be aggregated to make
'either happy or a man', (where being both is not excluded)
so 'lover' and 'servant' will make 'lover or servant of'
and also 'lover of everything except servants of'. Triadic
relations may be compounded in more intricate ways.
Thus, 'giver of — to —' and 'betrayer of — to —' may
make 'giver of everybody to somebody betrayed by him to —' and
'giver of somebody to everybody except those betrayed by him to —'.
Also, such combinations as 'person to whom somebody, X,
calumniates all except such as are given by — to
somebody to whom X betrays —'.
