in logic, "in order," as he says, "to free the science from the trammels
of speech." Of perhaps the majority of logicians it may be said
that they absolutely know no other way of deciding many questions
of logic. Some treatises contain little else than discussions
concerning Aryan speech. Perhaps one reason why the Irish and
Gaels have produced so many fine logicians is that they were con-
versant with a language in which the subject of a sentence is not
usually put in the nominative case.
