Elemente]] or mounts, which are distinct from and independent of one another; and the being of the collection consists in the possibility of considering those members as thus demning a derivative individual, without being drawn into absurdity. By saying that A is [del]distinct from[/del] [ins]a[/ins] B, is here meant that A has some character, a relative one, if not an absolute one, which B has not. By saying that A is independent of B, is here meant that the possession or non-possession of any character by B does not logically prevent A from possessing or not possessing any character, unless [del]the two[/del] there is such a relation [ins]direct or indirect[/ins] between the characters themselves, [del]one that[/del] [ins]the same thing would be true of[/ins] any two subjects [del]would be subject to the[/del] whatever. Thus, all shades of red do not make up a collection, because shades of red are not individual; [del]all the[/del] [ins]all the males and negroes in the U.S.[/ins] [del]do not make a collection, because they[/del] are not distinct; a spherical shape and its surface do not make a collection because they are not independent.
