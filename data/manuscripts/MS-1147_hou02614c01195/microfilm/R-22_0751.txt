[u]Logical[/u] [[u]L.[/u] [u]logicalis[/u], from [u]Logica[/u], [u]Logic[/u]] Ger. [u]logisch[/u]; Fr. [u]logique[/u]; It. . This adjective enters into many technical terms of logic in the sense of irrespective of special facts or of any facts except those which are involved in there being any [del]true[/del], doubt, reasoning, [ins]truth, falsity,[/ins] assertion, general signs, perception, and in short, those facts of which logic is forced to take cognizance.

[u]Logical possibility[/u] is freedom from self-contradiction, explicit or implicit. Thus, a space of four dimensions is logically possible.
