[marginalia]Logical Graphs[/marginalia]

An evenly enclosed graph may be thrust within any ovals outside of which it stands.

If an oval contains only ovals, it makes no difference whether a graph is written outside the whole or inside each of the inner ovals.

The innermost efficient bond between two graphs [del]enclosed[/del] [ins]is enclosed[/ins] in their innermost common enclosure.

That is to say, in such a graph as that of Fig. 45, the
