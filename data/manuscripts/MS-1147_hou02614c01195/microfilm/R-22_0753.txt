[marginalia]Logical[/marginalia]

[pn]2[/pn]

is useless without a special explanation.

1. It is most commonly defined as consistency, thus making it a synonym of logical possibility. But it may be doubted whether except in a definition, it ever has this sense.

2. Conformity to the rules of logic; consistency in a concept; validity in a reasoning, and in a proposition, sometimes possibility, sometimes the being a necessary consequent from premises.

3. The conformity of a proposition to the facts, so far as it asserts anything about the facts.

4. The conformity of a communication to the judgment of the person making it.
