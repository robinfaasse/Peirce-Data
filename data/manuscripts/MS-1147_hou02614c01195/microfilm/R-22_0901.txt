A infima species of syllogism. The traditional moods of categorical [ins]deductive[/ins] syllogism are, in the first figure, Barbara, Celarent, Darii, Ferio, [del]viz. Ferio,[/del] together with the indirect moods now reckoned as composing the fourth figure; in the second figure, Cesare, Camestres, Festino, Baroko; in the third figure, Darapti, Disamis, Datisi, Felapton, Bocardo, Ferison; and in the fourth figure, Bramantip, Camenes, Dimaris, Fesapo, Fresison. But of these Darapti, Felapton, Bramantip, and Fesapo are not valid if the universal propositions are taken in their simple, or Philonian, sense. In addition to these, there are moods with weakened conclusions [ins]of which the same remark[/ins] applies.
