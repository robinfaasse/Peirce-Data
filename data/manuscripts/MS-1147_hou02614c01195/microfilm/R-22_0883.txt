[pn]Modality[/pn] [pn][1][/pn] [[Ger. Modalität] Ger. Modalität

To define modality, in the sense of stating what it essentially consists in, would be to adopt some theory of the subject, [ins]and none is[/ins] universally accepted. We can only say at the outset that it is either the logical qualification of a predication [ins]or proposition[/ins] [ins]consequence, copulation,[/ins] as possible or necessary (or their negatives) or the metaphysical qualification of an inherence [ins]or fact[/ins] [ins]causation, coexistence,[/ins] in ways analogous to those.
