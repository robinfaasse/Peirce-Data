In order to complete the doctrine of negation, it is
necessary to remark that two propositions or two predi-
cales can be united, either by aggregation or by
composition. Aggregation takes place when we say,
"Select any object, X, you please. Then either X is compound or X is simple",
or "Select any objects, X and Y, you please. Then either X is compound or Y is simple".
In ordinary language, we should express the latter proposition in
the form "All things are simple or all things are compound"
