Many of the best logicians hold that logic must rest upon an
Erkenntnisstheorie, or doctrine of the nature and possibility of
knowing, which must, in its turn, be rest upon psychological inves-
tigations. It would certainly seem that if logic is to have any
other basis than the immediate dicta of consciousness, that is to
say, if we are to have any further reason for believing anything
than that it is agreeable to our inclinations to do so, then that
basis must be some theory of the nature of truth. A theory of truth
