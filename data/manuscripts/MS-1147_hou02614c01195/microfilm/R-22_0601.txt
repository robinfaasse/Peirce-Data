Energy. It is contrary to usage to restrict potential energy to
one of seven forms of energy. The doctrine is that
energy is either kinetic or potential. Potential energy
includes the energy of elasticity which has been proved
not to be mere energy of distance and which is therefore
not included in SN's seven forms of energy.

Besides that, the article is utterly unsatisfactory in
not explaining that energy is, as a result of integration,
affected with an arbitrary constant, & that the whole doctrine
of energy merely means that the component accelerations
of particles depend on their positions relative to other particles
and nothing else. In short the article fails to convey to the student
of philosophy what it concerns him to know and is radically bad.
