[marginalia]Logical Graphs.[/marginalia] [pn]10[/pn]

The following are the fundamental rules of illative transformation:

I. Any unenclosed partial graph can be erased.

II. Graphs separately scriptible are scriptible together.

III. Every heavy line can be joined to some index, after all less enclosed lines are so joined; but the individual denoted by the index may not be known. [The more enclosed lines must wait for the less enclosed ones, because the indices to which they are to be joined depend upon those to which the others are joined. Fig. 30 asserts that some woman fills a certain condition; but, though I know this, I may not know who she is.]

IV. Mutually transformable indices may always be joined by a heavy line. [Enclosures make no difference.]

V. Indices joined by a heavy line that is unenclosed and uncrossed [ins]by the line of an oval[/ins] are mutually transformable.
