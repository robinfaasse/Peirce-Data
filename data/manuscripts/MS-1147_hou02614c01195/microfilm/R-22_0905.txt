[del]Celarent[/del] is not at all a matter of the "turn of expression." [del]But[/del]
It is, therefore, pretty clear that it came to be applied to a syllogistic
form, from having first been applied to the way of expressing such
forms by substituting letters for the terms. This, we are informed by
several authorities, was called λόγοτρόπος by the Stoics, while
the mode of statement by speaking of the terms as first, second,
and third, was called τρόπος.
