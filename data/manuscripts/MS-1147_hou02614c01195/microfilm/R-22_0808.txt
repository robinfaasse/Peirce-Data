as to permit formal deductions. For that purpose, it must be
definitely supposed that certain forms of relation subsist between
objects. In other respects, the less definite the hypothesis is, the better.
Thus, it would be a hindrance rather than a help to suppose a geome-
trical figure to have any particular color. Finally, the hypothesis of
