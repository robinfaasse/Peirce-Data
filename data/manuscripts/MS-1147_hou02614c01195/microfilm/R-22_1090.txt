[u]Theory[/u] [[u]Gr.[/u] [u]θεωρία[/u], from [u]θεάομαι[/u], to see, with a suffix -[u]ια[/u]-]
An outline statement, presenting to contemplation
a sort of ideal diagram, or construction, which is
to serve to represent [ins]a series of[/ins] facts, not as they
at first present themselves, but so as to render the
relations between them comprehensible. [u]Theory[/u] is
used in opposition to fact, and also in opposition
to practice.
