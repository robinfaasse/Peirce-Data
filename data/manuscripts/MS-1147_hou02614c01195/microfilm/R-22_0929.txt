[del]Conversion by[/del] [ins]negation[/ins] is another name for contraposition.

Negation is used in another slightly different logical
application, to mean a predication in which the
copula (or predicate, if you like) is affected by an expressed
sign of negation, or an odd number of such signs. Thus
"Man is not immortal" is a negative proposition and
the mode of predication is the negation of immortality
to man.

(2) In the metaphysical sense, negation is the
mere absence of any character or relation regarded
as positive, without any further implication.

Spinoza's celebrated saying of which the Schellings have
made so much that all deter. omnis determinatio est
negatio, has no other foundation than the logical
fact that determinatio [ins]alternative[/ins] [del]of one thing[/del] excludes another.

Another aspect of the same profound truth is expressed by the
philosopher Mother Goose: "You can't have your cake and eat
it too."
