I have always suspected that Darwin
must have been influenced by Malthus
and the political economists. Political
economy, in so far as it is a scientific
doctrine, is merely the theory of the
production of wealth. It does not come
strictly within its purview to pronounce
as to what is beneficent and good. But
almost every branch of science is
surrounded with a hazy cloud of opinions,
not scientific, — too general to be by
any means sure, — philosophical beliefs
which the science tends to encourage.
