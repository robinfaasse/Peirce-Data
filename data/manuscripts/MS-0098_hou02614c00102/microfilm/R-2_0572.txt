[u]The Axioms of Geometry[/u]

1. The properties of each point are the same as those of
every other.

2. The properties of each pair of points are the same as those
of every other pair.

3. Space has three dimensions.

4. [del]Space is a simple continuum[/del] Space has no ring properties?

5. Space admits of motion of translation

6. Space admits of motion of rotation.
