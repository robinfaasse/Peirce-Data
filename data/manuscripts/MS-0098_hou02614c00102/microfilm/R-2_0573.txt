[u]The Axioms of Geometry[/u]

Axiom 1. All the points of space have the same properties. Thus, denote any point by A and construct any proposition whatever which shall relate only to geometrical properties of objects defined without [del]any other denotation[/del] introducing any other purely denotative sign but A, and that proposition will be true or false independantly of what point A denotes.

Axiom 2. All pairs of points have the same properties.

Axiom 3. Space has three dimensions. That is, three numbers are [del]necessary and[/del] sufficient to define any point.
