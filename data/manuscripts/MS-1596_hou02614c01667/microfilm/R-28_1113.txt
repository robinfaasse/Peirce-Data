[u]Byrne, Oliver[/u]. [u]Tables of Dual Logarithms[/u]
Hto. Cloth, cut. London: [date]1867[/date]
{Simple} Old type-fashioned numerals. Antilogarithms printed in red. A dual number is composed of a series of digits which are powers of 1.1, 1.01, 1.001, 1.0001, &c. in the continued product which gives the value of the number. A dual logarithm is a hyperbolic logarithm.
