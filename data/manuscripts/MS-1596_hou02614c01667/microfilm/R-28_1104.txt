[pn]Association[/pn]
The so-called [u]law of association[/u] has never been enunciated so that it determines what ideas shall follow upon another; for example, whether a suggestion by means of similarity or one by means of causality. A law which does not aid prediction must be regarded as of little value. C.S.P.
