[u]Probability[/u]
[u]Kant (K.ü.V. 258)[/u] distinguishes probability and
verisimilitude. A thing is probable if the reasons
for it have a greater ratio to sufficiency than the reasons
for the opposite. It is likely if the reasons for it are
more than the reasons against it. He combats
the theory of Wolff but is own is not nearly as good.
