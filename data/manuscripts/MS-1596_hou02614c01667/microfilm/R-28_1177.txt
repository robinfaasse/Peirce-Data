[u]Division of[/u]
5 orders (according to Alfarabi) 1. What is truly credible, without
doubt or deception. 2. What can hardly involve a fallacy of opinion. 3. opi-
niable in the opinion of the many who are not learned. 4. those which
are similar to the truth 5 those which are known to be false. Hence
13 kinds of propositions 1. Insensible. 2. Sensible. 3. Experimental 4. Famous
