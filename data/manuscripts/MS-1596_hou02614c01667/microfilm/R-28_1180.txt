[u]Reduction of Syllogisms[/u]
Arist. remarks 29b1 that all syllogisms
can be reduced to Universal syllogisms of the 1st figure.
The Univ. syll of the second fig. by ostensive red. the partic.
by red. per imp. The partic syll. of the 1st fig. by red. per
imp. to Univ. syll of the 2nd fig & then ostensively back. Those
of the third fig by red. to first &c.
