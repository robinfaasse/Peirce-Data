The idea of Thirdness is so familiar to us as hardly to call for any
explanation. It is a modification of the being of one subject which
is a mode of a second in so far as it is a modification of a third.
It is thus a reason. The dormitive virtue of opium by virtue of which
it puts this man to sleep, is not a mere word. It merely means there
is some reason, or regularity, by virtue of which it acts in this way.
