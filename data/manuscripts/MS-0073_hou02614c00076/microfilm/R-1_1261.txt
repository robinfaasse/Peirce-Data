If [u]n[/u] be the number of points, as I shall say for
short instead of punctuation marks, then the number
of letters will be [u]n[/u] + 1. Let [u]Fn[/u] be the number
of trees with [u]n[/u] points, or say of [u]n[/u]-point trees.
Then, if [u]i[/u] be the number of letters to the right of
the highest punctuation mark, so that [u]n[/u]+1-[u]i[/u]
will be the number to the left, the number of
trees which can be formed from these [u]i[/u] letters
to the left is [u]F[/u]([u]i[/u]-1), and the number to be formed
from the [u]n[/u]+1-[u]i[/u] letters to the right is [u]F[/u]([u]n[/u]-[u]i[/u]).
