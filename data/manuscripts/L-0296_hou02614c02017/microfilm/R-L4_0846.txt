[u]Ursinus College[/u]
[u]Collegeville, Pa.[/u]

[date]June 21, 1898.[/date]

OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,

Milford, Pa.

My dear Sir,

May I trouble you for an opinion as to the adapta-
bility of W. P. Montague, of Harvard University, for a College
Professorship. I do not doubt that he has ample preparation
and scholarship, but do not feel certain that he would be suited
to the environments of a Pennsylvania College. Any information
you may be willing to give me with reference to the man's personal
history, &c., I shall greatly appreciate.

Very truly yours,

Henry T. Spangler
President.
