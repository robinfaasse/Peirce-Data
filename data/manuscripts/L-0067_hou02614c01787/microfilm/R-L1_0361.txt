About there I lose the power of recognizing my reasoning
and the next thing I am aware of is that I am moving
across the room. My muscles seem to be making complicated
systematic contractions, but I am sure I do not know what
they are doing. I have only in mind my purpose to have
the air let in. I forget even that, my mind going back
