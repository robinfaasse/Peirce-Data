Boston, Mass., 14 Dec., 1901.

Mr. Charles S. Peirce,

Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:--

In conformity with the plan on which we are preparing
"Lamb's Biographical Dictionary of the United States," we send herewith
for revision a typed sketch intended for Vol. V. The sketch is
sent to you in order that no facts of historical importance be omitted
or misstated, and that such genealogical data as come within our plan
be included.

As reference works of this character are published only once
in a generation, any errors that occur here are liable to be perpe-
tuated in succeeding works and for this reason we urge that you give
the matter your careful attention.

We beg to state definitely that by complying with our
request you will be placed under no obligation to the publishers.

As the work is alphabetically arranged, you will appreciate
the importance of returning the sketch at once, in order that all
correspondence may be completed before the volume goes to press.

Yours very truly,

John Howard Brown,

Managing Editor.
