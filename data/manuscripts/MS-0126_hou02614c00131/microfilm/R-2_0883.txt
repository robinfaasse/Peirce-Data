Given 4 endless straight lines (no two
in one plane) how many endless straight
lines can cut, each, all four?

The phrase "endless straight line" being
somewhat unwieldy, let us replace it by one
word having no more letters than that
phrase has of words, — say "ray".

On the ray (b) take any point [B] you please. Then
there will be a plane {aB} containing [B] and the
whole of (a).

Principle No 1. Given any point and any ray, there
is a plane that contains them both.
