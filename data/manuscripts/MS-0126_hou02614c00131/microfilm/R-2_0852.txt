Following the example of good geometers, I have removed from office, in my writings, the phrase "endless straight line," and for cause. It was too clumsy; and like those I imitate, I have appointed in place of it a word containing no more letters than that phrase contains of words, — the word "ray". I may mention that in the same spirit I use the word "plane" to mean an unbounded plane surface.
