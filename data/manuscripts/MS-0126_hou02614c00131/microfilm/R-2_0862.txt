A [u]GEOMETRICO-LOGICAL DISCUSSION[/u].

How many endless straight lines can each
cut four given endless straight lines, in the
different possible relative situations of
these latter?

I will begin by dismissing the clumsy
phrase "endless straight line", and will
appoint to do its service a word of no more
letters than that phrase has of words, — namely,
the word [u]ray[/u], which has often been used in this
sense. I will likewise use the single word [u]plane[/u]
to mean an unbounded plane surface.

I select this question as a good one to
illustrate the logic of geometry; and it will
be with a view to that that I discuss it. I shall
begin, then, at the very foundations of the
logic of geometry.
