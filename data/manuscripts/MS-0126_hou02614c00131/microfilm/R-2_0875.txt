How many endless straight lines
can each cut four given endless
straight lines?

I will begin by dismissing the clumsy
phrase "endless straight line" and appointing
in its place a single word of no more letters than
that phrase has of words, — [u]ray[/u]. And by the
way I will use the word [u]plane[/u] to mean an
unbounded plane surface.

I will now lay down a full list of the
first principles of geometry, or properties of
space, taking for granted the properties of
time.

[u]Topical Properties[/u].

1. A [u]fixed point[/u] is a Place, or part of Space,
which itself has no parts. It is an [u]ens rationis[/u],
or ideal creation of thought. A [u]particle[/u], or [u]movable[/u]
[u]point[/u], is a movable object, not generally material,
which at any one instant of time is confined to
a fixed point.
