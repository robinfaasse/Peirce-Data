Our knowledge of the heat properties of acetylene is
still very imperfect. We do not know, its diffusibility,
nor the velocity of sound in acetylene, nor other facts
of fundamental importance in acetylene-engineering.

Liquid acetylene is said by Cailletet to be a very mobile
