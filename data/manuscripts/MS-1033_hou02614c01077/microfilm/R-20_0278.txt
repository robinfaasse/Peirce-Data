Three days before this last communication of Sawitsch, 1861 Jan 25, Miasnikoff
announced ([u]Bulletin de la Société chimique[/u], p. 12) that acetylene could be ob-
tained by leading the vapor of monobromethylene or monochlorethylene into
a cooled solution of nitrate of silver mixed with ammonia, and decomposing
the grey precipitate so formed with boiling hydrochloric acid. This seems
the substantially the same action as that of Sawitsch, the ammonia, aided
by the affinity [ins]May 26[/ins] of the bromine for the silver, acting as potassa would do.
