[date]Dec 2 72[/date]

Chas S. Peirce Esq.
Washington D.C.

My Dear Sir:

Thank you
of Nov. 22 about the books: the whole
blunder is in Mr Stevens' agent, not
marking my books, and I went "after
him until I got the clue to them. As
I ordered them & many others in September
1871 I was somewhat annoyed at
their being so long in transitu to New
York (until last June,) and the subsequent
delay was no fault of yours. We are
so far from Scientific pabulum on this
Coast that I am almost disheartened
at the meagerness of our knowledge.
