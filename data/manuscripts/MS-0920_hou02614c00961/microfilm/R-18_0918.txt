In reflection the mind travels from the impression by three
paths of "Association of Ideas," viz. — Resemblance, Contiguity,
Causality. [ins]*[/ins] It is necessary to enquire whether all pretended
thoughts exist in the nature of things as manifested in [del]real[/del]

not examined. Causality has the idea of Necessary Con-
nection. Necessity cannot be discovered in any external or
internal impression. Hence causality or power is no thought
at all.
