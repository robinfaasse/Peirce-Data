[u]II[/u]. Psychological Objection. All our knowledge of things as they exist,
is by & in the modifications of our own consciousness.

1. Metaphysics is antecedent to all other science. Hence metaphysic-
al knowledge is by & in modifications of our own consciousness,
antecedent to all other modifications. This antecedence is an ante-
cedence of principle. But no one phenomenon is antecedent to
another in the sense of being its principle. Hence, metaphysics does
not afford science.

2. Metaphysics studies only the consciousness as such. But if only
as such, it refers to nothing else & can be knowledge of nothing [del]but[/del]
what is in the consciousness; that is what we already know.
