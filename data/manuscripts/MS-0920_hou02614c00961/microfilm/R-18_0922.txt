Book I. Principles of Metaph. Investigation.

Chapter I. [u]Man the Measure of Things[/u].

In the Introduction we have considered the Nature of
Metaphysics, and have rejected certain false notions of
its procedure.
In our investigations, metaphysics is to be taken as
the analysis of Conceptions.
We need not ask the critical question; but still
there is a question of uncritical transcendentalism
with which every method of philosophy must open:
It is, How should the conceptions which spring up
freely in our minds by virtue of the constitution there-
of be true for the outward world?
In this question we detect three leading concep-
tions [u]Truth[/u], [u]the Innateness of Ideas[/u], [u]Externality[/u]. Let us
analyze each of these.
