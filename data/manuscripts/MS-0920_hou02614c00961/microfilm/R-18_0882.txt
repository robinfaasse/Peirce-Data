INTRODUCTION.

Chapter First

The Domain, the Basis, and the Fabric of Metaphysical Thought.

The word metaphysics is derived from the title of a work of Aristotle which treats, according to its author, of that science which is desireable on its own account and for the sake of knowledge, and which is preëminent over the others. (Arist. Metaph. i. 2)

In the following three sections, I shall be forced to consider metaphysics in three lights; viz
in § 1 As Philosophy
in § 2 As a Branch of Psychology
in § 3 As the Analytics of Conceptions.
