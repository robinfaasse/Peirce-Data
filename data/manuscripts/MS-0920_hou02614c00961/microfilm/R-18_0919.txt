[u]III[/u] Mansell.

Mansell's scepticism consists in denying that the uncon-
ditioned is either A or not-A.

$\alpha$. He concludes with a system of credulity.

$\beta$. While his conclusion is that something is neither A nor
not-A, his argument never the less depends upon a presupposi-
tion of logic one of whose principles is the contrary of this,
and consequently he must accept logic without reason or
on faith.

$\gamma$. His criticism does not apply to any new system because
new systems do not admit contradictions [del]of[/del] [ins]in[/ins] themselves.
