We shall have four expressions all equated to zero, one or other of which is [del]finally[/del] equal to zero; and therefore the product of them is certainly equal to zero.

When x = 1 y = 1 everything except the coefficients of xy will disappear and we shall have
(1-s) + t + w = 0

When x = 1 y = 0 everything except the coefficients of x (1-y) will disappear and we shall have
(1-s) + (1-t) + (1-w) = 0

When x = 0 y = 1 everything except the coefficients of (1-x) y will disappear and we shall have
(1-s) + (1-t) + (1-w) = 0

When x = 0 y = 0 everything except the coefficients of (1-x) (1-y) will disappear and we shall have
s + (1-t) + w = 0

We must now multiply these four equations together. I will not stop to perform this process but will merely give the result which is
st (1-w) + s (1-t) w + (1-s) t (1-w) + (1-s) (1-t) w + (1-s) (1-t) (1-w) = 0

And this by a very simple reduction gives
w = [u]st + (1-s) t + (1-s) (1-t)[/u]
    st + (1-s) t - s (1-t)
