If she has occasion to mention that the parallax of Procyon is 0".325 according to the Yale astronomer, she will say that "Dr. Elkin has measured for it a parallactic shift of 0".325." If she wants to suggest that every star presumably turns on an axis, the word "rotation" will not be elegant enough for her; but she must say it "has a movement of gyration." In short bits, like those of Smythe's Celestial Cycle, such rococo expressions may be rather enlivening; but when it comes to five hundred solid pages of detail to be read consecutively, the effect of them is that the reader has to stop and think what is meant.
