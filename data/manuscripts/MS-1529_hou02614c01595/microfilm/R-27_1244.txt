There is here reason to suspect that the direct comparison is vitiated by some
circumstance. If L - T > 2, then L > P; but if L - T < 2, then L < P.
[ins]If we give half weight to the direct comparison of L-T, we get P-L = 1/6 and T-S = 2/3.[/ins]
It is almost certain that L - T < 3; whence, S < T, and L - P < 1/2.
