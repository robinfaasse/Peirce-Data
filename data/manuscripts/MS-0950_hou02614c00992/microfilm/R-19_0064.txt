The phenomena are of two varieties, consisting either of the sifting out of heterogeneous multitudes, or of the emigration of parts of more homogeneous collections. As an example of the sifting process we may take a prism upon which white light falls. This light is heterogeneous. But, after all, the multitude of waves all [del]come[/del] [ins]travel[/ins] in one direction, and while they are of different kinds the average composition continues the same. It falls upon the prism, which is composed of a stupendous collection of molecules of one and the same kind. The action of these molecules upon different waves of light being different, the different kinds of waves follow different paths and a striking spectrum is produced. Although there is a sifting, it will be perceived that likeness plays a much greater part in it than unlikeness. So it is when coal is sifted by a series of screens. It is the equality of the meshes of each sieve that effects the result.
