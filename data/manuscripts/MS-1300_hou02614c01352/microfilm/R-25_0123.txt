The constellation Libra, or rather
the figure of the Scales, is of a different
origin from the rest. To the Greeks, it
was known as the Claws of the Scorpion.

about the constellations called the Phe-
nomena of Aratus. Of course, the word
Phenomena does not carry its modern
signification. Still, it has a special
scientific meaning. It denotes the
configurations and places of the stars.
