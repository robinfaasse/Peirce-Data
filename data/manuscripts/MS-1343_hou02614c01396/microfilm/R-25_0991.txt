1ca. Balloons are of two classes; those which are compressible
and are therefore allowed to be open at the bottom, and those which are
rigid and taut. A compressible balloon is either a Montgolfier
or [del]so[/del] gas balloon. A Montgolfier is [del]so[/del] a cheap and simple affair,
usually made of duck or canvass, and ascending only so long as
the air remains hot (though in early days fire was carried up) after
which the aeronaut descends under a parachute and the balloon is
abandoned [del]or[/del] to be picked up afterward. This is so cheap that there is little
temptation to steal it, nor would it be a vast loss if never recovered;
but it cannot go far, for ascensions are not made in high winds.
