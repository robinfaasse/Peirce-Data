The [u]Gore-Instinct[/u], the instinct of combat and ruthless destruc-
tion, is the most strenuous of all the instincts. It has much
affinity with the Getting instinct and like that has two varieties,
the adventurous, glorious variety, mostly aggressive, notwith-
stand Marathon, Thermopylae, and other splendid gests, and its
selfcontrolled, butchering variety. Irascibility, captiousness, contrariness
[ins]are effects of the Gore instinct.[/ins]

The [u]Gamic instinct[/u] is the instinct which causes the produc-
tion, rearing, and training of children and other pets. It is the
most elevated of the Civicultural instincts.

The [u]Grouping instinct[/u] is the general intellectual instinct, which
causes all association and associational suggestion, all
generalization, classification, grouping, and systemati-
zation, [ins]all imitation of oneself or of others[/ins] all habit forming and habitual action, all ardor of pursuit.
