By the Imitation-Instinct is meant the tendency to ape

By the Conversation-Impulse is meant the love of ex-
pressing our thoughts feelings, passions, and thoughts,
and of entering into, or interpreting the feelings, passions,
and thoughts of others.

By the War-Instinct is meant the aggregate of all impulses
to destroy and to kill, [del]all rage,[/del] and all delight in cruelty.

By the Magic-Instinct is meant that bundle of instincts
which cause men to endeavor to bring about results by means
of rites, incantations, and prayers. It includes all acts of
religion.

By the Progeny-Instinct is meant the desire to have
children, [del]to[/del] to rear them, and to educate them, and
the love of children and of pets generally.
