[u]Descriptive Definition of a Human Instinct[/u], as the term will here be used.

An animal instinct is a natural disposition, or inborn determina-
tion of the individual's Nature ( [del]his[/del] [ins]"native"[/ins] being that within
him which causes his behaviour to be such as it is), manifested
by a certain unity of quasi-purpose in his behaviour. In
men, at least, this behaviour is always conscious, and not
purely spasmodic. More than that, unless he is under some
extraordinary stress, the behaviour is always partially
controlled by the deliberate exercise of imagination and
reflexion; so much so that to the man himself his action
