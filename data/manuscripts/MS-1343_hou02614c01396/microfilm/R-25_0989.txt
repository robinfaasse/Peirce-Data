[pn]12y[/pn]. Terrestrial motor construction and adaptation is
either the harnessing of animals or the construction of
[del]motors, as[/del] locomotives, [ins]and the adaptation[/ins] or [del]of[/del] stationary engines or dynamos.
The harnessing of animals depends on whether they are to
be used with or without vehicles. If, is the affair of the
spur-maker, whip maker, saddler, harness-maker, farrier,
horse-shoer, etc. The locomotives are either steam, [del]gas[/del] [ins]gasolene[/ins], or
engines or they are electric motors or are run by com-
pressed air. The adaptation of stationary engines and dyna-
mos is either by tubes in which the air is compressed or
exhausted, by cables, or by trolley. To these we must add
[ins]land[/ins] transportation by gravity, as in the case of the railway
near Mauch Chunk, and also in the case of the leading
of water and oil through pipes and in canals and
ditches.
