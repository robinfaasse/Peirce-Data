Ithaca, N. Y., [date]Jan. 22/92.[/date]188

Dear Sir:--

Your letter of December 19th - which I infer was sent under cover to Professor Fuertes - seems to have been mislaid and has just come to my hand. I am not at the present moment able to answer your question. This, however, I think I ought to say: the present year, owing to the necessity of erecting several buildings, the appropriation for non-resident lecturers was very small, and it is not easy to see just how any other fund could be provided for the payment. I shall, however, confer at once with the Dean of the School of Philosophy, Professor
