[u]AMERICAN JOURNAL OF MATHEMATICS[/u]
JOHNS HOPKINS UNIVERSITY
BALTIMORE.

[date]19 Oct 1902[/date]

Dr. C. S. Peirce

Dear Sir

I send you a complimentary number of the Journal in the hope that Whitehead's memoir may call forth one by you.

Speaking as an advisor in mathematics to the Carnegie Institution, I may say for the advisors that a series of lucid and brilliant articles, such as one may hope for from you would be likely to call for special consideration though of course nothing can be arranged in advance.

Yours sincerely

[u]Frank Morley.[/u]
