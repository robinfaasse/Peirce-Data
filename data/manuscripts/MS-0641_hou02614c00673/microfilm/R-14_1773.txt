But tell me, my Reader, this how do you know that [u]all[/u] Indefinites are Factitious? It is as important a question, [ins]very nearly,[/ins] as the Universe suggests to us; and you and I want to know the Real Truth of the matter; and of course we propose to put aside all prejudices, [ins]and all traditions,[/ins] and all authorities, who know no more of such a subject as this than you and I may. There are no specialists in Metaphysics; for that science reasons from those phenomena that are known equally to all men. Your Conominalist, Jeremy Bentham, a mighty mind, undoubtedly, called Metaphysics "Coenoscopy"; for [del]How is it[/del] [ins]that very reason.[/ins] How is it that you and I who are equally earnest to know the Real Truth of this matter should think so differently on this subject? If we can find that out, perhaps we shall find our way to an agreement.
