An argument is, of course, a Sign; namely, a Sign of
the truth of its conclusion. For it is a Sign [del]in the first place,[/del] of
the truth of its premises; and then this truth being a Sign of
that of the conclusion, the Argument itself is a Sign of that same.
I do not say that every Sign of another Sign is, [u]ipso facto[/u], a Sign of that of
which the latter is a Sign; but it is so in this case.
