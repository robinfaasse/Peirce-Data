the copulative proposition whose members are so many distinct Premises [del]going[/del] [ins]serving[/ins] to justify

clusion itself; and a variant of this sense (unadvisable, [del]I am inclined to think[/del]) consists in regarding
anything as an Argument which [del]proposes to[/del] [ins]is taken for[/ins] [del]is proposed as such[/del]; 2[ins]nd[/ins], for [del]the[/del] [ins]an[/ins] Argument in
the first sense plus its Conclusion; 3[ins]rd[/ins], for a middle term or something nearly equivalent. The
third sense is hardly English. The [del]first[/del] [ins]recommended[/ins] is best supported by usage; but the second will here be adopted.
