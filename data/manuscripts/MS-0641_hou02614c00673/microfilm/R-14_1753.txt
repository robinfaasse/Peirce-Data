[ins]by some[/ins] Mode of May-be. Common nouns and adjectives have always be called
Generals or Universals by Logicians, merely because they are
"apta nata praedicari de pluribus", without observing that
the words "apta nata" explicitly classes them as Indefinites, or
May-bes, — a terrible blunder. A true General is [del]a Would-be[/del] [ins]what-is-true[/ins]
[del]or Whensoever-should[/del] a Whatever-should-be or Whensoever-should-there-be,
which will impart its Generality to the following would-be or there-would-be.
Just as the Principle of Contradiction does not apply to that-which is
asserted as a May-be, so the Principle of Excluded Middle does not
apply to a Would-be.
