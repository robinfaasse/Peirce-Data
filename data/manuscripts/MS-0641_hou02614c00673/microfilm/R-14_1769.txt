Objects, except [del]the[/del] [ins]by the[/ins] Relation of being Other than them. [del]I[/del] [ins]I[/ins] call
a Sign "Indefinite" if it does not distinguish each Single Object
it Denotes from every one which it does not Denote or De-
notes differently. Thus, [del]the[/del] [ins]any[/ins] Proposition is a Sign; and the Proposition
"Cain [del]killed[/del] [ins]killed his brother[/ins] Abel," or [del]its[/del] [ins]precisely[/ins] equivalent "Abel [del]was[/del] [ins]his brother[/ins] was killed by Cain" is
a Definite Sign; but the Proposition "One of the [del]all three[/del] Brothers Cain and Abel
killed the other" is Indefinite, though both denote the same pair of
Objects, Cain and Abel. Every non-relative Sign Denotes a Single Object;
every Relative Sign denotes a set of Single Objects; that is, such is my
form of stating the matter. The Proposition [del]Name[/del] "Some peas are green"
is precisely equivalent to "Some green pea is other than Some green pea;"
for "some" implies Existence at some time. It is also equivalent to "There
is a pea that resembles another pea in being green."
