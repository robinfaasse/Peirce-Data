My Dear Peirce =

This old town, "the Rose of New
England", was my place of exile in ten years
of my boyhood. Here I lived, in charge of
a stern, old Puritan great-uncle, until
I went to Yale in 1849— my sixteenth
year. I came here last night, to help
in burying Isaac H. Bromley, the journalist,
today. He was my roommate, classmate,
etc., and a "most" unique personage.

Have brought your letter on with me, as
I had no chance to answer it yesterday.

Imprimis: When you write me
confidentially, you would do well to
inscribe the envelope as "strictly personal". Mr.
