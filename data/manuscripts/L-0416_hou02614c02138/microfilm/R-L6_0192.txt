As to the loan on Mtpr: it seems
to both of us that it would be easier
to make such a loan in St Louis
with a Bank or Trust Co. It is so
small a matter that it will be
difficult to get it here except
through some personal influence.
Mr S. is staying in town during the
week, for treatment. He is so far
from Mr Pinchot that he try to see him.
I will keep the matter in mind &
inquire if I can do anything & if so
will let you know.

The Power Co. is promising but there
is no market yet for the stock. Stewart
& Co have all gone to pieces but there is
another channel in which possibly
a market can be found but it would be
at low price if at all. Yours truly
W. J. Barlow
