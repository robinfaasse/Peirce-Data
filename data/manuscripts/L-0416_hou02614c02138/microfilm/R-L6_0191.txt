Office of E. C. Stedman,
Broker,
No. 16 Broad Street.
New York, [date]7 Febry. 1900[/date].

My Dear Professor

Your favor of the 5th inst
to Mr Stedman has been turned over to
me with a request to write you
for the reason that he is used up
with the day. As you will remember, he
had a long sickness in '98. During
the last 8 mos. he has had another
trying time during which it was a
great question if he were going
to pull through. He is better but
far from a normal status of health,
was obliged to sell his seat & get off
the floor of the Exchange & free from
strain of its excitement.
