[del]Art[/del] . In the problems of this article, the student is supposed to be provided with a ruler and a triangle, and to be able

1. To draw a [del]straight[/del] ray through any two given points

2. To find the point common to any two given rays.

3. Through any given point to draw a ray parallel to any given ray. [ins](Put the side of the triangle in coincidence with the given ray, and the ruler against another side. Slide the triangle along the ruler, till the point is on the first side.)[/ins]

Moreover, the student is [del]expected[/del] [ins]supposed[/ins] to be provided with [u]one circle[/u] drawn upon his paper (by a half dollar, or otherwise), without having its centre given.
