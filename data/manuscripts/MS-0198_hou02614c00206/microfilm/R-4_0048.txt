[u]Problem[/u] . Given two points, [A] and [B] : required
all the points the squares of whose distances from these
points have a given difference.

[u]Solution[/u]. Divide the given difference of squares by the
distance AB, [del]and[/del] add and subtract the quotient to and from
the same distance AB, and halve the results. These halves
are distances from [A] and [B] to be measured along
{AB} to a point at which a perpendicular to {AB} is to be
raised. The points of this perpendicular are all the points
which satisfy the condition of the problem.

proposition.

. Given two circles with their centres: required to find
the points from which the tangents to the two circles are
of equal length.

. Given three circles with their centres: required the
points from which the length of tangents [ins]to those circles[/ins] to their points
of tangency are all equal.
