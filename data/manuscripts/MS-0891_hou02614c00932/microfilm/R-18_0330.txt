When man gets to heaven, he is made perfect.
He was already the image of his God; then he is made
perfect. He is the Son of God arrived at manhood.
And Christ says he is put over ten cities. What are

govern his own creation? Whose creation shall
an angel govern but his own?

There are ten thousand creations which bear

[u]On the Classification of the Human Faculties[/u].
Who will give us the true one at last? Here is
one which I offer as presenting some merits:

Man 1 awakens and rouses himself
2 He sees
3 He thinks
4 He desires
5 He does
6 He enjoys and suffers
7 He respects and loves

I have elsewhere advanced the classification of
the I-impulse, the it-impulse and the Thou-im-
pulse, but that is not a classification according to
faculties. That the present classification includes
everything needs no proof, everything is there which
has been inserted into any other system
2 Every faculty is a real one
3 No faculty is the same as another, for arousing
is not volition, nor enjoyment perception.
