I.

[date]1853.[/date]

Everyones mind has a certain basis, which can't be decreased or enlarged. This can be filled, covered with mind or with memory, And everyone can take their choice & cover their Foundation with MIND or MEMORY; or PASSION or GENIUS But here I mean mind in contradistinction to memory. Ought mind to be used in contradistinction to memory?
