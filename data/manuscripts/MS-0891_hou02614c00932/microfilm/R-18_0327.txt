XLI.
[date]1856 August 12[/date].

The "soul" is that which can move. According as a soul is greater or less, it possesses more or less of this power. According as at any time it is exercising this power or moving, it is more or less "excited".

When the soul is in an active state the repetition or continuance of the same thought or notion (to be distinguished most carefully from [del]ordinary[/del] [ins]many[/ins] notions which leave to each [del]little effect[/del] [ins]little weight[/ins]) will pass that notion naturally up through the soul.

But what puts the soul in this active state? [u]Beauty[/u]. In this (which I shall term the [u]Automatic[/u]) method of excitement, it is necessary that the patient notion should outweigh all others — which can happen in two ways: — first, by the superiority of the Notion itself [a thought is [u]caeteris paribus[/u] superior to an idea] and
[u]2[sup]nd[/sup][/u] by the attention given to it.
In order that our Automatic [del]notion may be of[/del] any use it will be necessary to devise some means by which [del]to put it[/del] in practice

All superior notions may be expelled
The principal part of the attention may be given to the patient that is to say
1[sup]st[/sup] That the attention may be drawn from all other notions
(2[sup]nd[/sup]) to the patient notion.

Now then we have decided that 3 things are to be done 1 to render the soul sensitive and active 2 to empty the attention, 3[sup]rd[/sup] to put the Patient Notion in.

XLII
[date]1856 September 3[/date].

Excitement in two ways
1[sup]st[/sup] by an irritant
2[sup]nd[/sup] by an allayer of irritation
Note the same object may belong to both classes
As to the 1[sup]st[/sup] see XL.
2[sup]nd[/sup] Way. It is necessary
1[sup]st[/sup] that the excitability should be great
2[sup]nd[/sup] that the subject should be conscious of the Narcotic powers of the object.
