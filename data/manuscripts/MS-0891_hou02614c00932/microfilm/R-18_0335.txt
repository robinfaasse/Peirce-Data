LXXII.
[date]1867 June 18[/date]

Man is nature's first essay towards the
production of an intellectual animal.
He is not that, but is a prophecy of it,
perhaps.

LXXIII
[date]1868 Feb 17[/date]
Positively the most important part of the
most advanced sciences, Mechanics & Astron-
omy, has been merely metaphysical, twist-
ing things into shape. So morphology.

LXXIV
[date]1869 Sep 28[/date]
We do not value life for what is pre-
sent to our consciousness at any one mo-
ment. That is very little. Nor should we
gain by having more present at once. Nor
even for what we can readily call up.
This is another of the many illustrations
of the small part consciousness plays
in our higher life.
I wonder whether there be not a self subsis-
tent which knows all but itself & becomes con-
scious only in [del]being connected with body[/del].

LXXV.
[date]1888 March 17[/date].
The best maxim in writing, perhaps,
is really to love your reader for his
own sake.
