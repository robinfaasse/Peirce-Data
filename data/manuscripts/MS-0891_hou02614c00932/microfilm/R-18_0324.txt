[u]XXXII[/u]

Worship is the recognition of any being as him to
whose will, as such, we ought to be entirely submissive.
Therefore, we cannot worship any but the one true
God (to others we only pay honor) and the first com-
mandment means we shall hold the truth which
we do know in recognition.

[u]XXXIII[/u] [date]1860 September 15[/date].
To sin is to act contrary to our higher motives.
It is to have our lower impulses stronger than our
higher ones. It is to not love what we recognize as
most loveable. It is to follow after idols.
