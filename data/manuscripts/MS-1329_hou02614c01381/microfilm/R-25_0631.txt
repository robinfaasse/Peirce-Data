After we had the thing running in such modest
way as we could, the first improvement I should
try to make would be a way in which the com-
munity could make money so as to enlarge itself
and provide laboratories and libraries. A library
of twenty thousand dollars I would have carefully
selected at the very beginning. But this would be
only a beginning & chiefly for our own needs.

It would not be my notion to teach men and
have them go out to become anything else; though
I might do a little of that in order to sow good seed
in the outer world. But it would be to a very limited
extent. The regular thing would be for the students
to come and take hold of the problems we were
all engaged in working out together & staying all
