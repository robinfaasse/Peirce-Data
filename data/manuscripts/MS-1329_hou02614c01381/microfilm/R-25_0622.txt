an education? That question is capable of a variety of
answers; but the manner in which that end has been
imposed upon our universities, as one not to be looked
beyond, points to, and is [del]only[/del] consistent with one
answer, namely, that it is for the individual advan-
tage of those students. And what, I pray, is individual
advantage which refuses to look beyond the individual?
What sort of morality is that teaching [del]the[/del] those young
men? If it teaches them that what is meant by their indi-
vidual advantage, which if a vast institution takes as its
ultimate end, they will surely be warranted in taking for
theirs, is that they should lead luxurious lives. Wretched
business! They had better spare themselves their years of
bad habits at the university, content themselves with a
course at a commercial college, go about the world,
and look for a chance to make money.
