Yet I must make an exception in one case &
find room to correct an expression I have used
which you would be quite justified in misunder-
standing. I said that university lectures should be
eloquent. I had in mind a truth about Memory
which I am confident I never saw set down in
any of the dozen books devoted to the subject which
I have read nor in any of the treatises on psychology.
We are, in the first place, to distinguish that sort
of memory which only lasts for a month or a year
or two, according to the differences of different
people, from that which is indelibly impressed.
