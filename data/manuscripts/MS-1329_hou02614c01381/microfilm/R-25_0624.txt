I venture to think that, while it is difficult to trace causes
in such matters with any certainty, it is quite a probable
opinion that it is due to the limitation of the purpose of
these universities.

The idea was that something was the matter with the
state of "Kultur" in Germany, and that what it was, and
its remedy, would be discovered in the universities.
This made research their principal duty.
