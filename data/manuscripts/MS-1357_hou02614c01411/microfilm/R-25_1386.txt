It would be a safeguard against errors in algebraic work if
every alphabet or other group of characters associated in our
minds should be appropriated to quantities of the same di-
mensionality. This is a serious reason for adopting this
plan. The objection to it is that the extensive use of it would
require a large number of groups of characters, each of
which characters must be readily written and must have
a name. Let us see what our available resources are.
