<u>Notation</u>

The number of different quantities requiring distinct signs is large, so that it is difficult to hold the notation in mind. The best way will be to make it as descriptive as possible.

Letters on the line will refer each to a class of quantities.

Subscript indices will be attached to them to show what verticals they refer to, in case they refer to verticals, or to what intervals between the verticals they refer in case they refer to such intervals. An index referring to a vertical will be followed by a [del]point[/del] period. When the verticals are numbered successively, the lesser of the two numbers defining the boundaries of the interval between two successive verticals will be chosen as the index of the interval.

Accents will be attached to letters to make them [del]refer[/del] [ins]denote[/ins] other analogous quantities.
