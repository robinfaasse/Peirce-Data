To integrate
[u]$\frac{dx}{\frac{ga^2}{8\mathcal{L}\mu} + \sqrt{1 + \frac{64\mu^2}{a^4}(x-x_0)^2}}$[/u]

Put
$\xi = \frac{8\mu}{a^2}(x-x_0)$
$K = \frac{8\mathcal{L}\mu}{ga^2}$
$d\xi = \frac{8\mu}{a^2} dx$  $dx = \frac{a^2}{8\mu} d\xi$

$= \frac{a^2}{8\mu} \frac{\frac{1}{K} d\xi}{1 + \frac{\sqrt{1+\xi^2}}{K}}$
$= \frac{a^2}{8\mu} \frac{\frac{1}{K} \sqrt{1+\xi^2} d\xi}{1 + K\sqrt{1+\xi^2}}$
$= \frac{a^2}{8\mu} \left\{ \frac{1}{K} \sqrt{1+\xi^2} d\xi - \frac{(1+\xi^2) d\xi}{1 + K\sqrt{1+\xi^2}} \right\}$

$z = \sqrt{1+\xi^2} - \xi$
$\sqrt{1+\xi^2} = z + \xi = \frac{1+z^2}{2z}$
$\frac{1-z^2}{2z} = \xi$
$d\xi = \frac{-2z(2z) - 2(1-z^2)}{4z^2} dz = -\frac{1+z^2}{2z^2} dz$

$\frac{1}{K} \sqrt{1+\xi^2} d\xi = -\frac{1}{K} \frac{(1+z^2)^2}{4z^3} dz = \left( -\frac{1}{4K} \frac{1}{z^3} - \frac{1}{2K} \frac{1}{z} - \frac{1}{4K} z \right) dz$
$\int \frac{1}{K} \sqrt{1+\xi^2} d\xi = \frac{1}{2K} \xi \sqrt{1+\xi^2} - \frac{1}{2K} \log [\sqrt{1+\xi^2} - \xi]$

$\frac{1}{z} = \sqrt{1+\xi^2} + \xi$
$\frac{1}{z} - z = 2\xi$
$z^2 = 1 + 2\xi^2 - 2\xi\sqrt{1+\xi^2}$
$\frac{1}{z^2} - z^2 = 4\xi\sqrt{1+\xi^2}$

$-\frac{(1+\xi^2) d\xi}{1 + K\sqrt{1+\xi^2}} = \frac{\frac{(z^2+1)^3}{8z^4} dz}{1 + K \frac{z^2+1}{2z}} = \frac{(z^2+1)^3 dz}{4z^3(2z + K(z^2+1))}$
$= \frac{\frac{1}{4K} (z^2+1)^3 dz}{z^3(z^2 + \frac{2}{K}z + 1)} = \frac{1}{4K} \left( Az + B + Cz^{-1} + Dz^{-2} + Ez^{-3} + F \frac{1}{z^2+\frac{2}{K}z+1} \right)$

$= \frac{1}{4K z^3 (z^2 + \frac{2}{K}z + 1)} \left\{ \begin{aligned}
& A [ z^6 + \frac{2}{K}z^5 + z^4 ] \\
+ & B \dots [ z^5 + \frac{2}{K}z^4 + z^3 ] \\
+ & C \dots \dots [ z^4 + \frac{2}{K}z^3 + z^2 ] \\
+ & D \dots \dots \dots [ z^3 + \frac{2}{K}z^2 + z ] \\
+ & E \dots \dots \dots \dots [ z^2 + \frac{2}{K}z + 1 ] \\
+ & F \dots \dots \dots \dots \dots z^3
\end{aligned} \right\}$

$\begin{aligned}
& A = 1 \quad E = 1 \\
& B = D = -\frac{2}{K} \\
& [del]B[/del] -\frac{4}{K^2} + C = 3 \\
& C = 2 + \frac{4}{K^2} \\
& -\frac{4}{K} + \frac{4}{K} + \frac{8}{K^3} + F = 0 \\
& F = -\frac{8}{K^3}
\end{aligned}$

$= \frac{1}{4K} \left[ z - z^{-3} - \frac{2}{K}(1+\frac{1}{z^2}) + (2+\frac{4}{K^2})\frac{1}{z} - \frac{\frac{8}{K^3}}{z^2+\frac{2}{K}z+1} \right]$
$= \frac{\begin{aligned}
z^6 + \frac{2}{K}z^5 + z^4 \qquad\qquad\qquad + z^2 + \frac{2}{K}z + 1 \\
-\frac{2}{K}z^5 - \frac{4}{K^2}z^4 - \frac{2}{K}z^3 - \frac{2}{K}z^2 - \frac{4}{K^2}z \\
+ (2+\frac{4}{K^2})z^4 + (\frac{4}{K}+\frac{8}{K^3})z^3 + (2+\frac{4}{K^2})z^2 \\
-\frac{8}{K^3}z^3
\end{aligned}}{z^3} \dots$
