The moving load which the cables are capable of carrying is
equivalent to 11 000 pounds per linear foot over the entire
structure, and it is assumed that this load is distributed over
the equivalent of six railroad tracks, corresponding to 1833
pounds per foot of track. In estimating the effects of an un-
equal load a weight of 12 000 pounds per lineal foot is taken in
accordance with the provisions stated, at the beginning of this
paper. While it may be considered that the load per foot on one half or one
third of a span ought to be more than 10 per cent. greater than the load
per foot on the whole span, it must be remembered that the peculiar
conditions of this bridge are such that it is only under very
rare conditions that any considerable portion of the moving load
must be distributed by the stiffening truss. In fact, two passen-
ger trains could cross this bridge side by side without disturb-
