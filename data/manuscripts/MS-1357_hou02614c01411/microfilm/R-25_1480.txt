[u]§ 6. Horizontally uniform distribution of the load.[/u] When [del]w/x[/del] is
constant, we may write
(18) $w' = \kappa x' = Q \tan \tau$.
Then, (14), independently of the constancy of $\kappa$, gives
(19) $q = \kappa g \cos^2 \tau \qquad Q = \kappa g \cos^3 \tau = \frac{\kappa}{d^2y/dx^2}$.
The integration of this gives
(20) $\begin{cases} \tan \tau = \frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{\kappa x'}{Q} \\ \frac{\kappa y'}{Q} = \frac{1}{2} \left(\frac{\kappa x'}{Q}\right)^2. \end{cases}$
