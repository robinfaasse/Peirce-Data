By (20)
dx/ds = cos τ     dy/ds = sin τ
whence, by (29) and (31),
dx/dτ = (dx/ds)(ds/dτ) = [fraction]Q/k[/fraction] sec τ,
dy/dτ = (dy/ds)(ds/dτ) = [fraction]Q/k[/fraction] sin τ sec² τ.
Integrating these equations
x = [fraction]Q/k[/fraction] [ log tan (45° + ½τ) - log tan (45° + ½τ₀) ]
