We are in possession of no other way of solving problems
with plural solutions than by assuming equivalents for
the unknown quantities and from them calculating equi-
valents for as many given quantities. At least, this is
true for the algebraic part of the work, where the said
"equivalents" may be simply, [u]x[/u], [u]y[/u], etc. It is the essence of the
method called Analysis.
