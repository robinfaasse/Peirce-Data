Formulæ which this investigation has developed, the strains in the stiffening truss have been calculated for three different conditions. In the first of these, a moving load of one third the length of the span (3100 feet,) is assumed to occupy the first third of the 3100 feet. In the second supposition it is assumed to occupy the second and third sixths of the 3100 feet, the end of the load being at the center. In the third supposition it is supposed to occupy the central third of the 3100 feet. The moments developed by these calculations are given graphically on Sheet 20. They are a satisfactory confirmation of the capacity of the stiffening truss.
