[fig]Diagram showing a weighted point on a cord with tension forces acting on it.[/fig] At each weighted point, say the $n^{\underline{\text{th}}}$, are three forces, the weight, $w_n - w_{n-1}$, whose horizontal component is zero, and the reactions from the tensions on the stretches, namely a force equal to $q_n$ acting at an angle $90^\circ + \tau_n$ to the weight, and a force equal to $q_{n-1}$ acting at an angle $270^\circ + \tau_{n-1}$ (reckoned the same way) to the weight. These three forces being in equilibrium, we have
(3) $w_n - w_{n-1} = q_n \sin \tau_n - q_{n-1} \sin \tau_{n-1}$,
(4) $q_n \cos \tau_n = q_{n-1} \cos \tau_{n-1}$.
