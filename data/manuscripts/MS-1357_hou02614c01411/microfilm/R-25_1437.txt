If it be deemed necessary to take account of the extensions
of the Suspenders under the live loads, it will easiest be

As long as the truss rests upon its four supports, and
is not lifted from any of them by the live load, we need
consider nothing about it, except its elasticity under the
live load. For it is in equilibrium and consequently

deformations produced by the live load, will produce a balance
between the weight of the live load, and the elasticities of truss,
cable, and backstays, and though the centre of gravity of the
truss is lowered, that need not be considered, since it is
balanced by the elasticities. We may, therefore, imagine
that the truss is weightless.
