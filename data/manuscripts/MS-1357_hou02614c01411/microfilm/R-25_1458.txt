From equation (3), it appears that

The constant is determined by the fact that
(a) $$w_0 = 0,$$
so that
$$w_1 = q_1 \sin \tau_1 - q_0 \sin \tau_0 .$$
Hence
(7) $$w_n = q_n \sin \tau_n - q_0 \sin \tau_0$$
$$= Q (\tan \tau_n - \tan \tau_0) .$$
Supposing all the weights positive (i.e. no buoyancy or levitation), it follows that
(8) If $m > n$ then $\tau_m > \tau_n$.
Since $w_N$ is the total weight, which we may denote by $W$, we have
(9) $$W = Q (\tan \tau_N - \tan \tau_0) .$$
The two terms are the downward pressures on the towers or the lift by them on the cord. If the cord descends from A, $\tau_0$ is negative.
Substituting (1) in (7) we have,
(10) $$\frac{w_n}{Q} + \tan \tau_0 = \frac{\Delta y_n}{\Delta x_n} .$$
