§ 8. We now consider the more important case in which the distribution of the load is horizontally uniform, that is, in which K is constant, so that, integrating (28) and determining the constant by (6),

(40) $$w = \kappa x.$$

The integration of (34) gives
$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{\kappa}{Q} x + \tan \tau_0$$

(41) $$y = \frac{\kappa}{2Q} x^2 + x \tan \tau_0.$$

Substituting (40) in (25), this latter becomes

(42) $$\frac{\kappa y - \kappa x_H}{Q} = dy/dx,$$

whence

(43) $$y = \frac{\frac{1}{2} \kappa x^2 - \kappa x_H x}{Q}$$

so that

(44) $$\tan \tau_0 = - \frac{\kappa x_H}{Q}$$

By definition and (40)

(45) $$\bar{x}_n^m = \frac{\int_n^m x dw}{\int_n^m dw} = \frac{1}{2} (x_m - x_n)$$

Putting $n = H$ $m = 0$

(46) $$\bar{x}_H^0 = - \frac{1}{2} x_H$$

Hence, by (19)

(47) $$Q = \frac{\kappa x_H^2}{2 y_H}$$

Substituting this in (43) we get
