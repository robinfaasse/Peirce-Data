87. If the distribution of the load is horizontally uniform,
we may write
$$\omega = Kx$$
where $K$ is constant. Then (19) and (15) give
$$Kx = \omega_R + Q \frac{dy}{dx}$$
or
$$\frac{1}{2}Kx^2 = \omega_R x + C + Qy.$$
But the attachment of the cord being at the origin, $C = 0$,
and the equation of the curve is
$$y = \frac{K}{Q} (\frac{1}{2}x^2 - x_R x)$$
or
(25)
$$\frac{y}{y_R} = 2 \frac{x}{x_R} - \frac{x^2}{x_R^2}.$$
This is a parabola with vertical axis.
Since $y_R$ is negative, we may put
$$\Upsilon = - y_R = \text{the versin (when the towers are of equal height)}$$
