In the differentiation of equations (2) - (7), the only point requiring notice is that
$$\delta (1 - \frac{R_{0}}{\mathbb{R}_{0}}) (x_{1} - x_{0}) = (1 - \frac{R_{0}}{\mathbb{R}_{0}} + \frac{R_{0}}{\mathbb{R}_{0}} \frac{|x_{1} - x_{0}|}{[\mathbb{R}_{0}]}) (\delta x_{1} - \delta x_{0}) + \frac{R_{0}}{\mathbb{R}_{0}} \frac{(x_{1} - x_{0})(y_{1} - y_{0})}{[\mathbb{R}_{0}]} (\delta y_{1} - \delta y_{0})$$
$$= (1 - \frac{R_{0}}{\mathbb{R}_{0}} \frac{|y_{1} - y_{0}|}{[\mathbb{R}_{0}]}) (\delta x_{1} - \delta x_{0}) + \frac{R_{0}}{\mathbb{R}_{0}} \frac{(x_{1} - x_{0})(y_{1} - y_{0})}{[\mathbb{R}_{0}]} (\delta y_{1} - \delta y_{0})$$

By way of abbreviation let $\heartsuit_{1}, \heartsuit_{2}, \heartsuit_{3}, \heartsuit_{4}, \heartsuit_{5}, \heartsuit_{6}$ denote the values of the [ins]left-hand members of the[/ins] [del]six[/del] equations, (2) - (7), when approximate values are substituted for the unknown quantities. Let us also use the following abbreviations

$$\alpha_{0} = \frac{\cos^{2} \Phi_{0}}{\Lambda_{0}} + \frac{\sin^{2} \Phi_{0}}{M_{0}}$$
$$\beta_{0} = \frac{\sin^{2} \Phi_{0}}{\Lambda_{0}} + \frac{\cos^{2} \Phi_{0}}{M_{0}}$$
$$\varepsilon_{0} = \sin \Phi_{0} \cdot \cos \Phi_{0} \cdot (\frac{1}{\Lambda_{0}} - \frac{1}{M_{0}})$$

$$\lambda_{0} = \frac{1}{P_{0}} (1 - \frac{R_{0}}{\mathbb{R}_{0}} \frac{|x_{1} - x_{0}|}{[\mathbb{R}_{0}]})$$
$$\mu_{0} = \frac{1}{P_{0}} (1 - \frac{R_{0}}{\mathbb{R}_{0}} \frac{|y_{1} - y_{0}|}{[\mathbb{R}_{0}]})$$

$$\alpha_{2} = \frac{\cos^{2} \Phi_{2}}{\Lambda_{2}} + \frac{\sin^{2} \Phi_{2}}{M_{2}}$$
$$\beta_{2} = \frac{\sin^{2} \Phi_{2}}{\Lambda_{2}} + \frac{\cos^{2} \Phi_{2}}{M_{2}}$$
