Owing to the awkwardness of this, Jevons, first, and
subsequently nearly every writer on the subject (several
of them unaware of what others had done) discarded
the operation of addition, and substituted another
operation, usually called logical addition, but otherwise
termed aggregation. Denoting this, as Jevons came to do,
by a sign of division turned round 90°, it may be
defined by the equation
