transposing [ins]a quantity[/ins] from one side of a congruence to the other, we need
not take the trouble to reverse the sign. Thus, if
[u]a[/u] ≡ [u]b[/u]

In short, we can take our congruence-sign up and put it
down where we like. It makes no difference where it is put.
In fact, in most cases it is needless to write that sign; only, it
will be found more convenient to let any expression
written without it, as
[u]a[/u],
mean, not [u]a[/u] ≡ 0, but [u]a[/u] ≡ 1.

[del]As to[/del] We have no need of the operation of subtraction
which has the same effect as addition. For if the dif-
ference of two numbers is odd, or is even, so likewise
is their sum. As for division it is useless, for division
by 1 has no effect at all, and division by 0 is either
absurd (when the dividend is 1) or indeterminate
(when the dividend is 0.)

It is curious, that in the dyadic value-system, addition
is not simple an operation as multiplication. Thus,
[u]x[/u] [u]y[/u] ≡ 1
is equivalent to
[u]x[/u] ≡ 1
[u]y[/u] ≡ 1
