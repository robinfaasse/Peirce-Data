A congruence is but an equation. If we write this in the form
[u]x[/u]² = [u]x[/u],
it expresses the principle of excluded middle. For, being a quadratic equation, it implies that there are but two values that [u]x[/u] can take, and that is the principle of excluded middle. Solving the equation, we find those values to be 0 and 1, the very values that the remainder after division by 2 can have.

[u]x[/u] + 1 ≢ [u]x[/u].
