§1
Der Mächtigkeitsbegriff oder die Cardinalzahl.

He defines a 'Menge', that is, a Collection, as follows:
"jede Zusammenfassung M von bestimmten wohlunterschiedenen Objecten m unserer Anschauung oder unseres Denkens zu einem Ganzen."

This is remarkably accurate. No need of the subjectivism of "unserer Anschauung oder unseres Denkens." He notes that the members which he calls 'Elemente' are [del]singular[/del] [ins]individual[/ins] definite [ins]"bestimmten"[/ins] [del]but not that they are also singular[/del], and that they are independent, "wohlunterschiedenen". But he does not [del]say in[/del] analyse that character, and say in what it consists. He implies that the relation of the collection to a member is that of inclusion but this he does not analyse.
