The other paper is one which, at the desire of my students, I have contributed to the collection. It contains a statement of what appear[del]s[/del] to me to be the true theory of the inductive process and the correct maxims for the performance of it. I hope that the thoughts that a long study has suggested to me may be found not altogether useless to [ins]those who[/ins] [del]such as[/del] occupy themselves with the application of this kind of reasoning.

C. S. Peirce
