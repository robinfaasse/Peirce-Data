[u]Preface[/u].

These papers, the work of my students, have been so instructive to me, that I have asked and obtained permission to publish them in one volume.

Two of them, the contributions of Miss Ladd and of Dr. Mitchell, present new developments of the logical algebra of Boole. Miss Ladd's paper may serve, for those who are unacquainted with Boole's [u]Laws of Thought[/u], as an introduction to the most wonderful and fecund discovery of modern logic.
