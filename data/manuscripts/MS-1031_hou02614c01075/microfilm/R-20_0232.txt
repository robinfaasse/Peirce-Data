$\cdot$ Berthelot (Bulletin de la société chimique [2] xxii p. 441) replies
that if $\text{H}_{2}\text{O}$ is subtracted from the double of Blochmann's formula
it will give his. The reason to that Blochmann dried in the cold over
$\text{Ca}\text{Cl}_{2}$ while his own analyses were made with a Gay-Lussac drying
stove at $110^{\circ}$ to $120^{\circ}$. The relation is just that between a metallic oxide
and its hydrate.
