an oil $\text{C}_2\text{H}\text{Br}_3$ and white crystals $\text{C}_2\text{H}\text{Br}_3, \text{H}\,\text{Br}$. The oil has a not
unpleasant smell, cannot be distilled [del]as[/del] [ins]so[/ins]. In that respect it resembles
Liebermann's tribromide of allylene. The crystals smell pleasantly, easily
soluble in ether alcohol $\text{C}\text{S}_2$ chloroform benzol etc melt at $42^\circ$. Treated
with reducing agents they yield acetylene.

The bromooctylide of silver was analyzed and found [ins]to be[/ins] $\left.\begin{array}{r}\text{C}_2\text{H}_2\text{Br}_2 \\ \text{C}_2\text{H}\text{Ag}\end{array}\right\} \text{Ag}\text{Br} + \text{H}_2\text{O}$.
It is in delicate white silvery crystals which on friction or contact with strong acids
