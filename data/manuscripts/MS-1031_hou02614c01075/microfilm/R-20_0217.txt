In [del]the[/del] [ins]the[/ins] Journal P. de Wilde presented to the Belgian Academy (Bulletins de l'Academie royale de Belgique [2] XIX p. 75) a paper in which reasoning from Berthelot's discovery that [del]all other[/del] olefiant gas sent through a red hot tube was partly converted into acetylene he [del]inferred[/del] [ins]inferred[/ins] acetylene ought to be a product of the incomplete combustion of ethylene and found it to be so.
