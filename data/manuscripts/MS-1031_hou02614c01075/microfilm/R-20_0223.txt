He also has a memoir on action of heat on hydrocarbons, acetylene
heated to point of softening of glass [del]is[/del] [ins]wholly[/ins] converted into chiefly into
benzene C₆H₆ but also into Styrolene C₈H₈ etc.

Iron when present determines the complete destruction of the
acetylene.

Mixed with N, CO, or CH₄, or Ethane, acetylene undergoes
the same transformation somewhat more readily.

Acetylene mixed with ethylene and heated to softening of glass gives
crotonylene or something like it.

Acetylene mixed with benzene & heated as above gave styrolene, which
is rather remarkable. For styrolene heated with hydrogen gives benzene and
acetylene.

Acetylene & naphthalene makes a very rapid reaction. What it makes
he don't say.
