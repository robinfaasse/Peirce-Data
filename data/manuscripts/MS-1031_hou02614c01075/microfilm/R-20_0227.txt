[date]26 Jan. 1874[/date] P. and Arm. Thénard (C.R. LXXVIII 219) announced to
French Academie that under the influence of "electric effluvium" acetylene
condenses to a horny substance. Also, under certain conditions to a liquid.
Dungas at a subsequent meeting exhibited one of these. The horn is
singularly insoluble.
