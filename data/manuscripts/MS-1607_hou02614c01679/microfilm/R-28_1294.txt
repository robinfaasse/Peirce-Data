1892.[/date] 12:05 Am

I have a hard year, a year of effort before me; and I think it will help me to keep a diary. My greatest trial is my inertness of mind. I think I shall very soon be completely ruined, it seems inevitable. What I have to do is to [del]peg[/del] [ins]plod[/ins] away and try to do my duty, and starve if necessary. One thing I must make up my mind to clearly. I must earn some money every day.
