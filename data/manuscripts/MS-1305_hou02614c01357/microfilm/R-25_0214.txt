Aratus does not profess to have any information
regarding the origin of the constellations. Like other
ancient poets, he generally writes as if they were due
of divine decree. Thus, in the prelude he says that
"Zeus himself hath fixed in heaven these signs, which
separate the stars." Yet in another place, in a more
naturalistic spirit he suggests that

"Some man of eld,
A nomenclature thought out and devised,
And shapes sufficient found."
