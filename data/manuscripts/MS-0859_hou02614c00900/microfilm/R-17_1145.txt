[u]Proof of the Infinite Nature of the Creator[/u]

from time, therefore, this began when time
began & therefore time [ins]also[/ins] was created when
the Universe was created. Therefore this
whole sequence of Causation in time has the
dependence of Creation, which is Influx.
Therefore since the Universe has been a-going
from everlasting the amount of spiritual
Manifestation is Infinite. Q. E. D.
