Zoological Hall [date]Dec. 5th / 87[/date].
Dear Sir

Allow me to thank you for the copy of your "Three papers on Logic": much of them is of course beyond my limit of those who are already acquainted with the science - but they are very suggestive and I shall be obliged to you for your following papers on the subject.

Very truly yours
Burt G. Wilder,

C. S. Peirce Esq.

P.S. I should be glad to see you at my Lectures in the museum which begin tomorrow at noon.
