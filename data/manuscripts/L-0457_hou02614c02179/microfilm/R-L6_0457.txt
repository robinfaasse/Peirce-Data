Lanthorn, Bucks Co, Pa.
Saturday, [date]Jan. 3, 1914[/date]

Prof. Charles Sanders Peirce
Milford, Pike Co, Pa.

My dear Mr Peirce

Here's a genuine, hearty, whole-souled good wish and New Years greeting to thee and thine for 1914.

A letter to me from Gifford Pinchot tells me you are still in Pike County.

Stanley Hall's "Founders of Modern Psychology" and Royce's "Problem of Christianity" are among the best books I read in 1913.

Bless! — Bless! — your royal soul! You threw a bright light of Reason on the dark path of your age.

Affectionately, with tenderest memories
A. H. Halton
