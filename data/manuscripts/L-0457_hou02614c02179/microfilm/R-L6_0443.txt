[date]Oct. 10[/date].

To C. S. Peirce
Westfall, Pennsylvania

My dear Mr Peirce

I thank you very
very warmly for your
new philosophy & logic
running through your ar-
-ticle on "the Reality of God"
in the Hibbert Journal
of October 1908.

I send you also by
this mail a brochure
written under the
nom de plume of "Skelarana"
which may do no
harm if it does no good.

[pn]3[/pn]

I trust I may have the plea-
-sure and profit of reading
other matter from your pen.
Your article on the "Reality of
God" is the best thing in
its line I ever read.

I wonder where "Westfall" is?
I could not find it on the
map. The brochure sent you
is a vague rough, popular
attempt to make plausible
the "Mind Cure's" empiricism
as an explanation of the
cures of Jesus. It may do
no harm if it does no good.

Again thanking you for your
new philosophy & logic and
hoping some day to see you and
thank you in person

Remain
Very truly yours
Henry G Walters
