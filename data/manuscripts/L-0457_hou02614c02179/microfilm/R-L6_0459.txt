Once in a century I suppose there is such an un-
-perverted extraordinary emotional soul born into
the world like myself. Let the curtain fall on this
with a Chopin nocturne. nevertheless it may serve to
"explain" why I - I - I - wrote the "Wisdom of Passion".
Following indirectly - indirectly along the line of thought
of the "Wisdom of Passion" let me say that within an
hour of receiving your letter (after having read some
of the "things of Ibsen") I had thought of putting in the
rest of the winter on an article on "Henrik Ibsen's
[ins]Healing[/ins] Art of Love", for a London magazine. Only one thing in
Christianity am I inclined to believe: - "God is Love",
and that when the magnificent soul of Charles Sanders
Peirce passes beyond the smiling and the weeping
of earth, it will be into the power and influence
of this sort of deity. So with Browning I say to you: -
"The best is yet to be, the last of life for which the
first was made. Our times are in His hand Who
saith: 'A whole I planned, Youth shows but
half. Trust God; see all, nor be afraid.'"

Cordially & Affectionately Yours
Henry Guy Carleton
