Dear Sir:

A good way of putting all positive rational quantities into one-to-one correspondence with all ordinal numbers, so that the ordinal number of any given quantity or the quantity in any ordinal place can readily be calculated, is this:

Work the usual algorithm for finding the greatest common measure of the quantity and
