Princeton, [date]Jan 28, 1901[/date].

My dear Professor Peirce,

I have read your unfinished paper on fractions – [ins]some of the last –[/ins] with a great deal of interest. It brings a host of curious relations to light and the detail to which you have carried the investigation is simply astonishing.

But as you see I am returning it. I cannot take seriously your suggestion that I have it published for you – unless you suppose – as I hope you do not – that I am a member of the publication committee of the American Math. Society.
