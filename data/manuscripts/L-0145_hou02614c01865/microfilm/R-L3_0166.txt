You did not quite understand my
question about the ordinal arrange-
ment of your [del]streamer[/del] [ins]points[/ins], [del]nos.[/del] [ins]nos[/ins] or points:
I meant what is the rule in accor-
dance with which they are to be
[u]ordered[/u] among the assign. points;
in other words, how would you order
them ordinally?

I hope sometime to hear in extenso
your views on the foundation of
topology; they would interest me tre-
mendsouly.

In conclusion, let me tell you how
deeply I appreciate your writing me
these two letters, not only because
of their profound interest but quite
as much because of the expression
of hearty good will & friendly feeling
which they contain. I can assure
you that those feelings are reciprocated.

I hope that Mrs. Peirce has recovered
and that all the attendant troubles
are at an end. Sincerely Yours,
H. B. Fine
