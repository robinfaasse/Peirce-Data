Princeton Dec. 19th 1900.

My dear Professor Peirce,

The strictures contained in
your letter were so just that I
at once proceeded to read them to
our Librarian, only to be told that
I had misrepresented the Princeton
Library shamefully [ins]managingly[/ins], since it is
always ready to send books to
scholars not in Princeton. I
therefore hastened to make what
reparation I could by having
the two volumes of the
Annalen packed up & sent
you. I have only one request
