which it is the replica, and such that to each unit of either

[u]related[/u] As used by Kempe and others, but inadvisedly, interdepen-
dent, that is, not independent.

[u]interdependent[/u] Not independent.

[u]Independent systems[/u], in the terminology of Kempe, two mutually ex-
clusive systems such that the product of the numbers of self cor-
respondences of two respectively is equal to the number of self-cor
respondences of the two taken together as one system.
