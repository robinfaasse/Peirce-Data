rotatory-reflexion An operation equivalent to a rotation
about an axis through any angle followed by a reflexion in a
plane perpendicular to that axis; the operation by which a
figure is brought into coincidence with an enantiomorphous
figure. It translates G. Drehspiegelung, which might conveniently
be imported into English.

drehspiegelung Proposed as a shorter synonym of rotatory-reflexion.

link A plain line of a graph connecting two spots of the
graph. (Kempe) In existential graphs, a link may be composed
of two or more lines of identity, in which case it is not itself
a partial graph.

sort Add: Places of the same sort, in Kempe's terminology, correspon-
ding places; as the first, the second, etc.

unified In Kempe's logical terminology, regarded as a single unit.

focus of a self-correspondence An identically-correspondent
element of the self-correspondence. (Kempe)
