[u]Cantorian ordinal[/u], (a) [del]a position[/del] the ordinal position of a
member of a Cantorian series; (b) one of a system of numerals
expressing ordinal positions in a Cantorian series. The
system begins with the finite ordinals, 1, 2, 3, 4, etc., every one
having another next after it. These are the [u]Cantorian ordinals of
the first class[/u]. This endless series of numerals is immediately
followed by a numeral written as [ins]$\omega$[/ins], which indicates a position
next after an initial [del]simple[/del] endless series that does not consist
of one endless series followed by another. Thus, in the Achilles, the
rapidly moving point is represented as passing over an endless
series of stretches, and [del]at[/del] when it attains the position next follow-
ing them all, according to the same law, it will be just as far
advanced as the slower point. Next after $\omega$ follow successively
