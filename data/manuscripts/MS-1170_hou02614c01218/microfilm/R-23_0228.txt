[u]ambilation[/u] A relation in which every individual object
of the universe stands to every other; also called a
[u]penecoëxistence[/u]. A relation in which no individual
stands to any other is a [u]contrambilation[/u], [ins]otherwise a concurrency;[/ins] [del]or[/del] [ins]a[/ins] relation in
which some individual stands to another is a [u]juxtambila-[/u]
[u]tion[/u], [del]otherwise an opponency;[/del] [ins]or[/ins] a relation in which some individual does not
stand to every other is a [u]contrambilation[/u].

[u]self-relation[/u] A relation in which something stands to itself,
a [u]juxtasubilation[/u].

[u]aliorelation[/u] A relation in which nothing stands to itself,
a [u]contrasubilation[/u] or [u]extra-self-relation[/u].

[u]concurrency[/u] Add: 2. A relation in which nothing stands
to anything but itself, such as "being at once black and",
a [u]contrambilation[/u] or [u]peneincompossibility[/u].

[u]opponency[/u] Add 2. A relation in which something stands
to something else, a [u]juxtambibation[/u].
