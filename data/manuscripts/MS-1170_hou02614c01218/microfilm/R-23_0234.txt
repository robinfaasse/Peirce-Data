[u]appreciation[/u]
The [del]world of appreciation[/del], the world as it appears to spiritual insight, or [del]to[/del] the broad[ins]est[/ins] conception of human good sense, in which there are esthetic and moral values. The term introduced by Royce in 1892, in his "Spirit of Modern Philosophy," was more fully explained in the second series of "The World and the Individual," but only in close connection with the doctrine of absolute idealism.
