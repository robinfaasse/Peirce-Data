[u]Cantorian[/u], a. Due to the logico-mathematical researches
of Dr. Georg Cantor, professor in Halle on the Saale.

[u]Cantorian series[/u], a series of objects, called its [u]members[/u],
so arranged that every part of the series has a [u]first[/u]
member. This is called by Cantor himself a [u]well-ordered[/u]
collection.
