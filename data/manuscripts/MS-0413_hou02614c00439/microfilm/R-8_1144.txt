[del]The affirmation of the rule is always called the major premise, the affirmation of the case, the minor premise. The denial of the result is minor premise where there is a major (in the second figure) and is major premise where there is a minor (in the third figure)[/del]

[u]The moods of the Second Figure are Cesare, Camestres, Festino, Baroco.[/u]
