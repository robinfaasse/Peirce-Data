The four propositional forms are customarily denoted
by the vowels A, E, I, O
A. Universal affirmative. Any S is P.
E. Universal negative. Any S is not P.
I. [del]Universal[/del] [ins]Particular[/ins] affirmative. Some S is P.
O. Particular negative. Some S is not P.

Some writers think these vowels came from the
words affirmo and nego. At any rate, these words
will serve to remind the student of the significations
of [del]these[/del] [ins]the[/ins] letters.
