[u]Of the First Figure.[/u]

§ 119. Since the S and P in Barbara may be any terms, we may make the S particular, or the P negative, or both; and we thus get three modified forms called [u]Darii[/u], [u]Celarent[/u], and [u]Ferio[/u], being the remaining three "direct moods of the first figure."
