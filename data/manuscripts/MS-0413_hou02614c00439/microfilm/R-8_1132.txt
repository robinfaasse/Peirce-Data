The most primitive of all eliminative inferences is
[u]Barbara[/u], a mnemonic word whose three vowels
signify that its two premises and conclusion are all
[del]other of[/del] [ins]of[/ins] the form A, or universal affirmative. [del]This[/del] [ins]So the mood next[/ins]

in O. Barbara runs thus:
Any M is a P,
Any S is M;
[u]∴[/u] Any S is a P.

The middle term is subject of the major premise and predicate
of the minor.

For example: "A desire to gain by another's loss is
a violation of the tenth commandment: all gaming,
therefore, since it involves a desire to profit at the
