We now consider [del]Camestres and[/del] Baroko. These are

[del]Any[/del] [ins]Some[/ins] M is P,
[del]Any[/del] [ins]Some[/ins] S is not P,
[del]Any[/del] [ins]Some[/ins] S is not M.

The long reduction of [del]Camestres[/del] is very simple. We simply
convert the minor, and by Celarent conclude,
Any M is not S,
whence [del]∴[/del] Any S is not M.

To get the long reduction of Baroko, we divide the
minor premise, thus:
Some S is not P,
∴ Any some-S is not P; and an S exists.

Proceeding then as with [del]Camestres[/del] we get

Any some-S is not M; and an S exists;

and this is the same as

Some S is not M.
