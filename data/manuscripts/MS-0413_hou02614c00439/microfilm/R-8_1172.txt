[pn]18[/pn]. A fallacy is a deceptive argument.

The division of arguments into good and bad, is rather
childish. It reminds one of Longfellow's little girl, who had
little curl right in the middle of her forehead.
When she was good, she was very very good,
But when she was bad she was horrid.
