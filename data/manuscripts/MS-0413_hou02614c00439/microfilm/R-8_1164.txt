The two moods [u]Datisi[/u] and [u]Disamis[/u] are as much
alike as [u]Cesare[/u] and [u]Camestres[/u]. Yet they [del]really[/del] [ins]formally[/ins] belong
to different genera. This we see by putting a different relation
for [u]is[/u]. Thus,

[u]Disamis[/u].
A certain lover of an S is a P,
Any S is an M;
A certain lover of an M is a P.

[u]Datisi[/u].
Any S is a P,
A certain lover of an S is an M;
[three dots forming a triangle] A certain thing loved by an M is a P.

The reasoning of [u]Bokardo[/u] and [u]Fesapo[/u] are respectively precisely like
that of [u]Disamis[/u] and [u]Datisi[/u]. Thus,

[u]Bokardo[/u].
A lover of an S is not P,
Any S is an M;
[three dots forming a triangle] A lover of an M is not P.

[u]Fesapo[/u].
Any S is not P,
A lover of an S is an M;
[three dots forming a triangle] A lover by an M is not P.
