[del]∴ Some M is not P.[/del]
[ins]∴ Some M is P.[/ins]

The long reduction of [u]Datisi[/u] follows the lines of that of [u]Cesare[/u]. We first lexically contrapose its major, thus:

Any S is P
∴ Any some-P is some-S (i.e. the only Ss.)

We then divide the minor into two propositions, thus:

Some S is M
∴ Any some-S is M; and an S exists.

Thence we infer by the first figure

Any some-P is M; and a P exists.
And this is the same as

Some P is M.
