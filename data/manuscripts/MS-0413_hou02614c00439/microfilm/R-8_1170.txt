Many logicians reject all or some of these moods as useless. Of those who retain them, the greater part regard them as belonging to the first figure; because they conceive the [del]terms[/del] major and minor to be better determined by the premises than by the conclusion, and they [ins]hold the[/ins] [del]do not[/del] figure to be defined by the premises alone. Others, headed by Galen, constitute a fourth figure of these moods.
