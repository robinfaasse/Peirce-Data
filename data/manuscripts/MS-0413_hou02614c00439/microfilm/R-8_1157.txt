From these two conclusions together we infer by the fourth figure
[ins]Any[/ins] [del]M[/del] [ins]M[/ins] is not-S; and S has real significance
which is equivalent to
Any M is not S
and this, by simple conversion, finally gives
Any S is not M.

The long reduction of [u]Festino[/u] is still more troublesome. We
first divide its minor premise, thus:

Some S is P
[triple_dot] Any some-S is P; and [ins]an[/ins] S exists.

Then proceeding exactly as with [u]Cesare[/u] we reach

Any some-S is not M; and an S exists.

But this is equivalent to
Some S is not M.
