In this scheme, each mood of the second and third
figure is next that mood of the first figure to which
the short reduction reduces it. Thus, the short reduc-
tion shows the true nature and analogal affinities
of the moods. Remember; moods whose names contain
an m are allied to Barbara. Moods whose names
contain k are allied to Celarent or Darii, according
to the situation of the a.
