This, though not in Aristotle, as far as I remember, is
in modern logics as a "disjunctive syllogism."

"He is either a fool or a knave;
"He is not a fool;
"$\therefore$ He is a knave." [u]Fowler Deductive Logic, [date]1867[/date].[/u]
