[u]Darii[/u]
"All virtues are laudable;
"Some habits are virtues;
"Therefore, some habits are laudable." [u]Bachmann Logik[/u].

"Omnis homo est rationalis,
"Aliquod animal est homo,
"Aliquod animal est rationale". [u]Eustachius à S. Paulo[/u].

[u]Ferio[/u]
"No boaster deserves respect;
"Some Heroes are boasters;
"$\therefore$ Some Heroes do not deserve respect." [u]Bowen Logick p 191[/u].

"No sensible body is a stone,
"But some man is a sensible body;
"Ergo, some man is not a stone." [u]Blundevile Arte of Logicke[/u].

"Nullum vitium est positum in medio;
"Aliquus habitus est vitium;
"Ergo aliquis habitus non est positus in medio."
[u]Heereboord Hermeneia[/u]
[u]Logica. 1657[/u]

"Quisquis non credit, damnatur:
"Aliquis Judaeus non credit:
"Ergo aliquis Judaeus damnatur."
[u]John Milton. Art. Log. l. I. xii.[/u]
