[u]Some M is not-P, and P has real significance[/u],
[del]which is the same as[/del]
[u]Some M is not P.[/u]

The short reductions of [u]Disamis[/u] and [u]Bokardo[/u], being precisely
alike, may be effected together, just as were those for
[u]Camestres[/u] and [u]Baroko[/u]. The minor premise is lexically
contraposed, thus:
Any S is M ;
[u]..[/u] Any some-M is some-S (i.e. the only Ss).

The major premise is divided thus:
Some S [ins]is not[/ins] [del]is[/del] P
[u]..[/u] Any some-S [ins]is not[/ins] [del]is[/del] P, and an S exists.

Then, by the first figure,
Any some-S [ins]is not[/ins] [del]is[/del] P, and an S exists,
which is the same as
Some S [ins]is not[/ins] [del]is[/del] P.
