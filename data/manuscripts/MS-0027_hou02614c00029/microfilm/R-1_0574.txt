§ 2.4.3 The theory of Multitude must begin by defining itself. What is its purpose? Its purpose is to state under what conditions [del]one[/del] [ins]a[/ins] collection is [u]as great as[/u] or not as great as a collection. For the [u]Multitude[/u] of a collection consists in its system of relations to each collection as being as great as or not as great as that collection and, denoting by $g$ the relation of being [u]as great as[/u], its being $g$'d or not [del]not[/del] $g$'d by that collection. Then we must define what we mean by a collection and what we mean by the relation $g$.

A collection is a particular kind of whole.
