I cannot conceive[del]d[/del] that the mill of the mind can treat any material which comes to it in more than one way. To my understanding Morality is simply Ethics faithfully followed. Conscience is an unconconscious reflex directing one's conduct in accordance with rules of life, either adopted from others or modified by one's own reflection. Religion is emotion plus your favorite fairy story of the cosmogony. God is the universal X. of all systems.

If you really believe that I have drawn "a hair splitting distinction on p. 5 to maintain" my "creed of page 4, so as to put down Heber Newton", I shall have done wrong in answering your letter at all, because such an act would be simple dishonesty with the miserable motive of a spurious success in dialectics. I cannot think you intended to impute this to me whatever may be the apparent signification of the phrase, for even if you had unjustly thought so ill of me I have given you no provocation to express your thought to me. Moreover the use of the words "your creed", which is the very thing the lack of which, in common with others of my class, it was one object of the article to avow, lends support to the view that you are not charging me with an unworthy offence. In conclusion it is not likely that Heber Newton himself to whom I sent it and who sent it to the magazine could have taken the view of my words which your postsc script seems to express.

Very truly
