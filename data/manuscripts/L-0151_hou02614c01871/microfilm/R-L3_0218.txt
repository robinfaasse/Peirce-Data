My dear Dr. Frazer: (of Bibliotheca)

May I ask you kindly to give me a bit of information. When I was a boy I used to write with Vanadium ink made by an uncle of mine. Tell me, please, is there any vanadium ink on the market, and if so what is it called? If not, please tell me how to make it.

I usually write with Carter's Black Letter Ink. Do you know what that is? I am now writing with Higgins's ink, which I only half believe in. At one time I tried nigrosine, but gave it up.
