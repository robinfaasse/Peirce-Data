Prof. C. S. Peirce
Milford. Pike Co. Penna.

My dear Prof. Peirce

The morning after Dr Mitchell's dinner (yesterday) I sent my son to seek you at Houston Hall and invite you to dine "en famille" but he could not find you. Dr Edgar Smith told him you had left without giving an address.

If you hear anything of the condition of my property above you I would be glad to know it. Dunham Gregory has charge of it but I suspect, visits it very seldom.

With kindest regards
Very truly
[u]Persifor Frazer[/u]
