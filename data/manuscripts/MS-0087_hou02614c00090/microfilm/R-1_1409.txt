[pn]10[/pn] The [u]Dimensionality[/u] of a [del]place[/del] [ins]figure, or place,[/ins] is the number of different orders of motion that can take place in the figure. A point-figure, which is composed [del]of[/del] a collection of points is of dimensionality zero. A line-figure, which is composed of lines and points, has dimensionality 1, because a particle can move in it. A surface-figure, which is composed of [ins]of[/ins] surfaces, may be, more, and possibly also of lines and points has dimensionality two, because particles and stresses can move in it.
