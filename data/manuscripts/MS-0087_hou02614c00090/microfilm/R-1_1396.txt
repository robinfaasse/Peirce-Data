[pn]23[/pn] A cap placed on a disk gives a closed surface. This is the type of periphraxy one. The periphraxy is on the surface itself not in the space in which it is. Therefore, it does not consist in the enclosure of [ins]three dimensional[/ins] space [del]by the surface;[/del] for it would not enclose [del]space in four[/del] if it were in a space of four dimensions. The periphraxy must consist in the incapacity of the species occupying it to shrink to a stress by a mere [del]motion[/del] [ins]movement[/ins] in the surface; or must consist in something like this in relating to what is possible in the surface itself. The surface has no cyclosy because a self returning stress in it can shrink to a particle by a mere [del]motion[/del] [ins]movement[/ins] in the surface. Its chorisy is one because it [del]ha[/del] is all one piece, - that is, a particle occupying any point in it can by mere movement in the surface come to [del]occupy[/del] any other point. Its census-value, according to this, is
$$X_2 + \Pi_2.$$
