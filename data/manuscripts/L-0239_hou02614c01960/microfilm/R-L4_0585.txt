58, University Street,
Montreal, [date]25th April 1901[/date].

Dear Sir,

As I happen to have a spare
moment or two, I will answer
your letter at once, for it has sug-
gested to me what I had never
dreamed of in working over the
alleged fallacy in your example -
namely, a fundamental differ-
ence of conception as to the
nature and function of
geometrical reasoning. What
you point out as the crucial
fallacy would never have
crossed my mind, even
had I poured over the

[page break]

thing for years, nor
(consequently) would the point
which you charge me with
having missed.

Yours truly
[u]Paul T. Lafleur[/u].

Hon. C. S. Peirce,
Milford.
