ever in the consideration I was slowly

When the two right-angled triangles,
with a common side, are brought
under consideration, their
equality is not directly proved.
It could be, by appeal to the 4th prop.
of Euclid Bk I; but this prop. is pas-
sed through I. 29, which called
for the original postulate —
in other words, returns to the very
position for which proof is
demanded.

Other flaws may perhaps be
