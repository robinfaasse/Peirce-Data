Dear Sir,

I am truly sorry to have delayed for a whole month the acknowledgment called for by your preferred illustration of a fallacy. During that interval, I was intensely occupied first with official and other work; and a close examination of the triangle was thereby postponed.

I must admit that such broken reasoning as I can detect in the problem was not discovered by my unaided self. Problems of mathematics and more congenial fields — literature

Yours very truly
Paul T. Lafleur.

Hon. C. S. Peirce
Milford.
