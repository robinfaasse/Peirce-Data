Dear Sir:

I beg to suggest an addition for a future edition of [u]Illustrations of Logic[/u]:

The denials of equivalent propositions are equivalent;

The propositions ‘A is true’ and ‘A is true and I say so’ are equivalent;

∴ The propositions ‘A is not true’ and “If A is true, I do not say that it is” are equivalent.

yours truly
C. S. Peirce

Mr. Lafleur
Mc Gill University
Montreal
Canada.
