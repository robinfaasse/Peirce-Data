Dear Sir:

Will you permit me to suggest an addition for a new edition of [u]Illustrations of Logic[/u]?

The denials of equivalent [del]assertions[/del] [ins]propositions[/ins] are equivalent.

The propositions ‘A is true’ and ‘This proposition asserts that A is true’ are equivalent.

∴ The propositions ‘A is not true’ and ‘This proposition does not assert that A is true’ are equivalent.

The propositions ‘A is true’ and ‘A is true and it is written that A is true’ are equivalent;

∴ The propositions ‘A is not true’ and ‘Either A is not true or it is not written that it is true’ are equivalent.
