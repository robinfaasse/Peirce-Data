OHIO STATE UNIVERSITY,

DEPARTMENT OF
PHILOSOPHY.

COLUMBUS, [date]Nov. 16 1903[/date]

Charles Saunders Peirce, Esq.
Milford, Pa.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

Will you please inform me as to whether your lectures
delivered at Harvard University last year, on "Pragmatism as a Princi-
ple and Method of Right Thinking" have been pub-
lished? If so where? And if not, is there
any way in which a friend of mine here, who is
making a special study of Pragmatism, can get
hold of the matter? He (and I as well) is
very desirous of having this your latest sys-
tematic deliverance on the subject.

Will you kindly let me know immediately.

Very truly yours
J. Harvey Haines.
310 W 7th Ave
Columbus
Ohio
