310 West Seventh Ave.
Columbus, Ohio
[date]Nov. 23. 1903[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce:—

I thank you very kindly for your offer to allow me to have the privilege of reading your lectures on Pragmatism which you gave in Cambridge last year. I shall be pleased to read them when you can conveniently send them. I wish very much for the privileges of moving in the philosophical circles in Cambridge again, amongst which privileges would be that of hearing you lecture again.

I enclose a program of our Philosophical Society here at O. S. U.

I shall send you a catalog of the University, as requested.

Very truly yours,
T. Harvey Haines
