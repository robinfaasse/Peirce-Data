[u]2[ins]nd[/ins][/u], Every character possessed by a number is either such that any number whatever is smaller than some number possessing that character or else there is a number possessing that character which is either larger than or identical with every number possessing the character.

Or, every collection of numbers which has a number larger than any of them has a number which is the largest among them.

It is easy to conceive of a universe of numbers of which this is not true. Thus if to the whole numbers we add

[u]11[/u], and [u]11.1[/u], and [u]11.11[/u], and [u]11.111[/u], and [u]11.1111[/u], and [u]11.11111[/u], etc without end, then although every single number would have a number next greater than it, yet the class of numbers smaller than [del]12[/del] [ins]12[/ins] has no largest.
