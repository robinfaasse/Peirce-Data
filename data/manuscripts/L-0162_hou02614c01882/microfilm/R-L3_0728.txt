[marginalia]JW Gibbs[/marginalia]

New Haven Dec 1 1881

My dear Sir

I am much gratified that you have found sufficient interest in my pamphlet to criticize it, & to communicate your criticism to me. I feel quite in the dark in respect to the question of the best signs, & am glad to get all the light I can on that point. And there is no one whose opinion I should value more than yours.

How little stress I would lay on the particular signs I employed, you may best judge when I tell you how hesitating & vacillating my usage has been. The notation of my pamphlet was, I think, the first I ever used (except the Quaternionic, wh I never used much). I adopted it somewhat at random & probably applied the more cumbersome sign to the external product as representing the operation (arithmetically) the longest in computation.
