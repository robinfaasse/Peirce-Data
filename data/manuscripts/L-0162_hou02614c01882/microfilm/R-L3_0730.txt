Thus I wrote $\alpha\beta.\gamma$ for the internal product
of $\alpha$ and $\beta$ applied as coefficient to $\gamma$. I
also wrote (by exception & for brevity) $\alpha\beta\gamma$
for the scalar product of the three vectors.
This economized space & [del]was[/del] was pretty
perspicuous. Yet when I returned from
my summer vacation, I went over my
manuscript & changed everything back to
my original notation, among other reasons,
because I thought it would present less
difficulty to the student if a definite sign
was always used for a definite operation.
