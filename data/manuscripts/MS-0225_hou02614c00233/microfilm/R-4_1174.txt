[u]Given Three Sides[/u]

$1^{st}$ $\sin \frac{1}{2} A = \sqrt{\frac{(s-b)(s-c)}{bc}}$

$2^{nd}$ $\cos \frac{1}{2} A = \sqrt{\frac{s(s-a)}{bc}}$

$3^{rd}$ $\tan \frac{1}{2} A = \sqrt{\frac{(s-b)(s-c)}{s(s-a)}}$

$4^{th}$ [fig]triangle with altitude[/fig] $d = \frac{(b+a)(b-a)}{c}$ $AP = \frac{1}{2}(c+d)$ $BP = \frac{1}{2}(c-d)$

The third method is always the best.
