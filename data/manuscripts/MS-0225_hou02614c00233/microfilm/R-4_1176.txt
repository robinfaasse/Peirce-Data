$e_n = \frac{\eta [A^n]}{m}$

$\begin{aligned} r &= 0.845347 \ e_1 \left\{ 1 \pm \frac{0.509584}{\sqrt{m}} \right\} \\ &= 0.674489 \ e_2 \left\{ 1 \pm \frac{0.476936}{\sqrt{m}} \right\} \\ &= 0.577190 \ e_3 \left\{ 1 \pm \frac{0.497199}{\sqrt{m}} \right\} \\ &= 0.512502 \ e_4 \left\{ 1 \pm \frac{0.550719}{\sqrt{m}} \right\} \\ &= 0.465583 \ e_5 \left\{ 1 \pm \frac{0.635508}{\sqrt{m}} \right\} \\ &= 0.429497 \ e_6 \left\{ 1 \pm \frac{0.755776}{\sqrt{m}} \right\} \end{aligned}$

The general formula is
$\begin{aligned} r &= \rho \sqrt[2n]{\frac{2}{(2n-1)!!^2}} \ e_n \left\{ 1 \pm \frac{\rho}{\sqrt{m}} \frac{\sqrt{2}}{n} \sqrt{\frac{(2n-1) \cdot (2n-3) \cdots 1}{2^n} - 1} \right\} \begin{cases} \text{when} \\ n \\ \text{is} \\ \text{even} \end{cases} \\ &= \rho \sqrt[2n]{\frac{2^n}{(2n-1)!!^2 \cdots 1}} \ e_n \left\{ 1 \pm \frac{\rho}{\sqrt{m}} \frac{\sqrt{2}}{n} \sqrt{\frac{(2n-1) \cdot (2n-3) \cdots 1}{2^n} - \frac{1}{2}} \right\} \begin{cases} \text{when} \\ n \\ \text{is} \\ \text{even} \end{cases} \end{aligned}$
