Numerical Equation

$Ax^2 + Bx + C = 0$

$x = -\frac{1}{2} \frac{B}{A} \pm \frac{B}{2A} \sqrt{1 - 4 \frac{AC}{B^2}}$

$1 > 4 \frac{AC}{B^2}$ or the roots are imaginary.

$x_1 = -\frac{B}{A} + \frac{C}{B} + \frac{AC^2}{B^3} = -\frac{B}{A} + \frac{1}{\frac{B}{C} - \frac{A}{B}}$

$x_2 = \frac{1}{\frac{A}{B} - \frac{B}{C}}$

When $C$ is large and negative

$Ax^2 + Bx - C^2 = 0$

$x = -\frac{1}{2} \frac{B}{A} \pm \frac{\sqrt{B^2 + 4AC^2}}{2A} = -\frac{1}{2} \frac{B}{A} \pm \frac{C}{\sqrt{A}} \sqrt{1 + \frac{B^2}{4AC^2}}$
$= -\frac{1}{2} \frac{B}{A} \pm \frac{C}{\sqrt{A}}$
