$\underline{m}$ corresponds to $x = \frac{1}{2}\delta$. This is therefore the true
value of $m$. Making then $x = \frac{1}{2}\delta$

$-\frac{1}{2} \cdot 10^{-N} = \frac{1}{2} [ \log \sin (m + \frac{1}{2}\delta) - \log \sin (m - \frac{1}{2}\delta) ] + \log \sin (m - \frac{1}{2}\delta) - \log \sin m$
$= \frac{1}{2} [ \log \sin (m + \frac{1}{2}\delta) + \log \sin (m - \frac{1}{2}\delta) ] - \log \sin m$

Doubling and taking the anti logarithm

$10^{-10^{-N}} = \sin (m + \frac{1}{2}\delta) \sin (m - \frac{1}{2}\delta) \div \sin^{2} m$

$\sin^{2} m 10^{-10^{-N}} = \sin^{2} m \cos^{2} \frac{1}{2}\delta - \cos^{2} m \sin^{2} \frac{1}{2}\delta$

$10^{-10^{-N}} = \cos^{2} \frac{1}{2}\delta - \cot^{2} m \sin^{2} \frac{1}{2}\delta$

$\cot^{2} m = \frac{\cos^{2} \frac{1}{2}\delta - 10^{-10^{-N}}}{\sin^{2} \frac{1}{2}\delta} = \frac{1 - 10^{-10^{-N}}}{\sin^{2} \frac{1}{2}\delta} - 1$

$1 + \cot^{2} m = \frac{1}{\sin^{2} m} = \frac{1 - 10^{-10^{-N}}}{\sin^{2} \frac{1}{2}\delta}$

$\sin m = \frac{\sin \frac{1}{2}\delta}{\sqrt{1 - 10^{-10^{-N}}}}$
