[u]Right Plane Triangles[/u].
Given two legs. To find Hypothenuse.
1[sup]st[/sup] method. [u]log h = ½ log (a² + b²)[/u] log a² = 2 log a log b² = 2 log b
a² and b² to be got and added together
2[sup]nd[/sup] method log h = log b + ½ G = 2 (log a - log b)
3[sup]rd[/sup] method log h = log b + log sec A log tan A = log a - log b.
