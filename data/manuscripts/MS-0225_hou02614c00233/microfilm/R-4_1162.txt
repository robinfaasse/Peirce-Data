[u]Notes on the Calculation of the
Orbits of double stars.[/u]

1. If during a long time the distance changes very
little, it may be assumed to vary proportionally
to the time. Then,
$$r = a + bt$$
And by the law of areas
$$D_t \varphi = \frac{c}{r^2}$$

$$\varphi = c \int \frac{1}{(a+bt)^2} = - \frac{c}{ab+b^2t}$$
or as only two independent constants are here in-
volved we may write
$$\varphi = \frac{1}{a+bt} \text{ or } = \frac{1}{a} + \frac{b}{a^2}t + \frac{b^2}{a^3}t^2 + \text{etc}$$
$$\text{hence if } b \text{ is very small } \varphi = A + Bt$$

If there are 3 obs.
$$b = \frac{\varphi_1^{-1} - \varphi_2^{-1}}{t_2 - t_1} \quad a = \frac{t_2\varphi_1^{-1} - t_1\varphi_2^{-1}}{t_2 - t_1}$$
