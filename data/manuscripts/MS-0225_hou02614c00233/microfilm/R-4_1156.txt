[u]To draw a map in which all great circles appear as
straight lines.[/u]

That is the sphere is projected onto a tangent plane
[del]by[/del] [ins]for[/ins] lines running from its centre.

Let $\Phi$ be the latitude of the tangent point (which
should be in the middle of the map) and let
all longitudes be counted from this point.

The meridian of $\Phi$ [del]may[/del] is a straight line. Put
this down first of all.

Let $g$ equal the radius of the earth on the scale of the map.

Then $g \tan \Phi = \text{distance to equator}$ $g \cot \Phi = \text{distance to pole}$.
