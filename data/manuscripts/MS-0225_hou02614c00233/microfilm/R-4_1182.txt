To calculate values of a function from a slowly
convergent or even slowly divergent series.

If the series is so slow, let the values of the last
two terms used be $u_n$ and [del]not[/del] assume the
others will be $u_n + u_n \frac{u_{n+1}}{u_n} + u_n \left(\frac{u_{n+1}}{u_n}\right)^2 + \text{etc}$
so that the sum is $u_n \frac{1}{1 - \frac{u_{n+1}}{u_n}} = \frac{u_n^2}{u_n - u_{n+1}}$

Suppose we [del]take[/del] [ins]yields take[/ins] [del]have taken[/del] into account 4 terms
$u_0 + u_1 + u_2 + u_3$
We shall thus get the approximation
$\frac{u_0^2}{u_0 - u_1} - \frac{u_1^2}{u_0 - u_1} + \frac{u_2^2}{u_2 - u_3} = u_0 + u_1 + \frac{u_2^2}{u_2 - u_3}$

We may treat these in the same way, getting
$\frac{\frac{u_0^4}{(u_0 - u_1)^2} // \frac{u_0^2}{u_0 - u_1} - u_0 - \frac{u_1^2}{u_0 - u_1}}{\frac{u_0^4}{(u_0 - u_1)^2} - \frac{u_2^4}{(u_2 - u_3)^2}} = \frac{\frac{u_0^4}{(u_0 - u_1)^2} (u_1 - u_2)}{\frac{u_0^4}{(u_0 - u_1)^2} - \frac{u_2^4}{(u_2 - u_3)^2}}$
$= \frac{u_0^4 (u_1 - u_2)}{u_0^4 - u_2^4 \frac{(u_0 - u_1)^2}{(u_2 - u_3)^2}} = \frac{u_0^4 (u_2 - u_3)^2 (u_1 - u_2)}{u_0^4 (u_2 - u_3)^2 - u_2^4 (u_0 - u_1)^2}$
$= \frac{u_0^4 (u_2 - u_3)^2 (u_1 - u_2)}{[del]2 u_0^2 (u_2 - u_3) (u_1 - u_2) - u_0^2 (u_2 - u_3)^2 - u_2^2 (u_0 - u_1)^2[/del]}$
$= \frac{u_0^4 (u_2 - u_3)^2 (u_1 - u_2)}{[del]u_0^2 u_2^2 (u_2 - u_3)^2 + 2 u_0^2 u_2^2 (u_2 - u_3)(u_0 - u_1) + u_0^2 u_2^2 (u_0 - u_1)^2[/del]}$
