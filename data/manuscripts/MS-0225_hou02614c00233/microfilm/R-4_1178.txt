To convert Heliocentric Lat & Long. into Geocentric R.A & Dec

$\lambda$ Hel Long $\hslash$
$\beta$ " Lat $\hslash$

$\alpha$ Geoc. RA $\hslash$
$\delta$ " Dec

$\Lambda$ Long. of Sun
$r$ = Radius Vector of Saturn.

$P$ Dist of Sun
$\Delta$ Dist of Saturn

Project $\hslash$ onto plane of ecliptic let $\Delta'$ and $r'$ be projected
lengths & $t$ the distance of $\hslash$ from Elliptic

$t = r \sin \beta$
$\Delta \cos \beta' = \Delta'$
$\frac{r \cos \beta}{\Delta \cos \beta'} \sin (\lambda - \Lambda) = \sin (\lambda' - \Lambda)$

$\frac{r}{\Delta} \sin \beta = \frac{\sin \beta'}{\cos \beta'}$ (where $\beta'$ is Geoc. Lat)
$\frac{r}{\Delta} \cos \beta = \frac{\sin (\lambda' - \Lambda)}{\sin (\lambda - \Lambda)}$ (where $\lambda'$ is Geoc Long)

Then from $\beta'$ and $\lambda'$ calculate $\alpha$ and $\delta$. These formulae
are extemporized and very likely could be improved.
