To solve numerical equation

Transform so that one root shall have smallest
modulus. Then put in form
$a_0 x^n + a_1 x^{n-1} + a_2 x^{n-2} + \dots - a_{n-1} x = 1$
Then assume any numbers (almost) for
$u_0, u_1, u_2 \dots u_{n-1}$
and calculate others by recurrent formula
$u_{i+n} = a_0 u_i + a_1 u_{i+1} + a_2 u_{i+2} + \dots - a_{n-1} u_{i+n-1}$
Then if $s$ is a large number
$x = \frac{u_s}{u_{s+1}}$ nearly.

Thus to find $\sqrt[3]{10}$
$\frac{1}{2} x^3 + 3 x^2 + 6 x = 1$
$\sqrt[3]{10} = 2 + x$
