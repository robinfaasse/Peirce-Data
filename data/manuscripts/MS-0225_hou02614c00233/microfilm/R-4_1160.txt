[u]My method of calculating moon's Rising & Setting.[/u]

[u]1[sup]st[/sup][/u] I copy out from "Moon Culminations" of the American Ephemeris the meridian passage of moon. [del]Put[/del]ting it down to tenths of an hour.
[u]2[sup]nd[/sup][/u] By a little table and the Moon's Declination I next write down to tenths of an hour the hour angle at the Horizon increased by [fraction]1/30[/fraction] part of itself. This [fraction]1/30[/fraction] takes account of the motion of the moon.
[u]3[sup]rd[/sup][/u] From these & the longitude of Greenwich. I get the Greenwich time of the event.
[u]4[sup]th[/sup][/u] I next write down the declination for this time to the nearest minute.
[u]5[sup]th[/sup][/u] I next write down in the margin of my moon-culminations, the product of the hour angle by the excess of the hourly motion over 2[sup]m[/sup]. [ins]Call this c[/ins]
[u]6[sup]th[/sup][/u] For the days on which the declination is S I next write down in the margin c + 12[sup]h[/sup] 24[sup]m[/sup] = c'
