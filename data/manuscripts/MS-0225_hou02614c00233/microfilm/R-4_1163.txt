[u]To take Moon's Place from Ephemeris[/u]

Here there are given the differences for $1^{m}$.

Now suppose
$$f = a + bt + ct^{2} + dt^{3}$$

This is given for hours, & the differences for $1^{m}$ are therefore
$$\frac{1}{60} D_{t} f = \frac{1}{60} b + \frac{2}{60} ct + \frac{3}{60} dt^{2}$$

For $t = 0, 1, 2$ these give
$$\begin{array}{lll}
\frac{1}{60} b & & \\
\frac{1}{60} b + \frac{2}{60} c + \frac{3}{60} d & \frac{2}{60} c + \frac{3}{60} d & \\
\frac{1}{60} b + \frac{4}{60} c + \frac{9}{60} d & \frac{2}{60} c + \frac{6}{60} d & \frac{3}{60} d
\end{array}$$

Then [del]the diff[/del] the value for $t$, we may do as follows
$$a + t \left( \frac{1}{60} b + \frac{1}{2} t \left( \frac{2}{60} c + \frac{3}{60} d \right) \right)$$
$$= a + bt + 2ct^{2} + \frac{3}{2} dt^{2}$$

Then the correction of this is
$$dt^{2} (t - \frac{3}{2}) \quad \text{This can never be greater than } \frac{3}{2} d \text{ or than}$$
$\frac{\frac{3}{2} d}{\frac{3}{60} d} = 30$ times the second difference of the differences for one minute.
