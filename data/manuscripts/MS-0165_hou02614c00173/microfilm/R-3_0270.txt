Case 112. The first player occupies one of the corner squares
not opposite the second player's corner square. The second player
is obliged to occupy the [fig]Tic-tac-toe grid with O's and X's[/fig] opposite corner square. The first
player is obliged to occupy the side square between
the two corner-squares of the second player, and the second
player is obliged to occupy the opposite side square. Only one
play remains for the second player and he cannot make tit-tat-toe;
nor can the first player, unless the second player absurdly
occupies the corner square. Thus, there is no tit-tat-toe in this
case.
