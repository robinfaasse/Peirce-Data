[u]Art. 124.[/u] But by far the best way will be to express by means of this notation the conditions of Definition 162. For this purpose it will be well to make the following addition to our notation.

[u]Notation 16.[/u] We will use the sign of inclusion, [u]≺[/u], to signify that the point or ray written before it is contained in the ray or plane written after it.

[u]Corollary 105.[/u] If then Γ, Δ, Θ being any places whatever we have Γ ≺ Δ and Δ ≺ Θ, it will follow that Γ ≺ Θ.

Γ ≺ Γ.
