But Desargues had set his heart upon doing something
to benefit the class of mechanics. He had already been attracted
to them; and he now had the idea, — original and useful as
were all his ideas, — of establishing evening schools for
adults. He published his discoveries in graphics, by teaching
them in his evening school. Doubtless, Richelieu was dis-
pleased at the resignation of Desargues; and Richelieu was
now at the summit of his power. Besides, the passion of
Desargues for the lower classes put a sort of social taboo
upon him. This drove him all the more to repose upon
