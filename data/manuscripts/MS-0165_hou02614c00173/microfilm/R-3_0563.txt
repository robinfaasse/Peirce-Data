<u>Scholium 64</u>. The subject-matter of geometrical study is the
geometer's hypothesis of space. He frames this idea so that the
points on each ray are arranged like the real quantities of algebra
including $\infty$, and form a ring. Anybody is at liberty to form a
different hypothesis; [del]and[/del] [ins]and[/ins] by simply leaving out the point which
corresponds to $\infty$, he will come nearer to the hypothesis of common sense.
