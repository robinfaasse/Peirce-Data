[u]Art. 20[/u]. [u]Definition 16[/u]. The objects of a collection are said to be arranged
in a [u]sequence[/u] when of every pair of them one object comes [u]after[/u] the other.

[u]Theorem 1[/u]. [u]In any sequence there can be but one object that comes after no other[/u].

[u]Demonstration[/u]. For let A and B be any two objects of the same sequence. Then
I say that one of [del]these[/del] [ins]the[/ins] two objects, A and B, comes after some other.

For, by Definition 16, of the pair of objects, A and B, one of them comes after the
other. Q.E.D.
