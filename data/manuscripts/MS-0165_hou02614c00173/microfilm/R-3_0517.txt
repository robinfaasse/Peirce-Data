[marginalia]330[/marginalia] [u]Definition 164[/u]. A [u]ray[/u], or [u]unbounded straight line[/u], is a line of the system of optical homaloids; that is, it is a line which the geometer assumes to be a possible path of light in vacuo.

[marginalia]331[/marginalia] [u]Definition 165[/u]. A [u]plane[/u], or [u]unbounded flat surface[/u], is a surface of the system of optical homaloids. That is, it is one of the homaloidal system of surfaces any two of which have in common an assumed possible path of light.

This was not understood by the ancients; and they did not speak of [del]the[/del] straight lines as the paths of light.
