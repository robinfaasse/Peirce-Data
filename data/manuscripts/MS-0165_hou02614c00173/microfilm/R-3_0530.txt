<u>Corollary 109</u>. In like manner, we have
$$\Upsilon \{\beta \gamma\} = 1$$
if $(\beta)$ and $(\gamma)$ are two different planes; or if they are collections
of planes
$$\Upsilon \{\beta \gamma\} = \Upsilon (\beta) \times \Upsilon (\gamma).$$

<u>Corollary 110</u>. In like manner we have
$$\Upsilon (CDF) = 1,$$
[del]if[/del] unless
$$\Upsilon \{CDF\} = 1,$$
or unless one of the three numbers $\Upsilon [CD]$, or $\Upsilon [CF]$, or $\Upsilon [DF]$
equals unity, or unless one or more of the points have
no existence. Generally, we have for collections of points,
$$\Upsilon (CDF) = \Upsilon [C] \times \Upsilon [D] \times \Upsilon [F].$$

$$\Upsilon [\beta \gamma \delta] = 1.$$
