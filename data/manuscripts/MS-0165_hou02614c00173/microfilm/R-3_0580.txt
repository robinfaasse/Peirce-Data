[A-D]. The same ray cuts {AD} where? Answer: D = A+B+C; whence
{AD} = uA + vD = (u+v)A + vB + vC. Put, then,
(x+y)A + xB + yC = (u+v)A + vB + vC.

This gives
y = v  x = v
x+y = u+v = 2v

and the point is v(2A+B+C) or simply 2A+B+C
