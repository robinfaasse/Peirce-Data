To show precisely how the signs were used, we may take the following, from the Vulgar Arithmeticke of Hylles (London: 1600):

"There are 3 merchants, to wit, A. B. C.
Who parting an 100 crownes do agree,
That B shall have mo crownes then A by three,
And C shall have 4 crownes mo then B.
Now how many crownes each Merchant must have,
Is all the question and thing that I crave."
