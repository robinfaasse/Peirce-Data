66. (Old Greek.) Make me a crown of gold, and copper, and tin, and
iron, weighing 60 minae; [del]of which[/del] [ins]of which[/ins] [frac]2[/frac]/3 shall be copper and gold,
[frac]3[/frac]/4 gold and tin, [frac]3[/frac]/5 gold and iron. How much gold, copper,
tin, and iron in the 60 minae?

Let [fig]circle with dot[/fig] be the minae of gold,
♀ the minae of copper,
♃ the minae of tin,
♂ the minae of iron.

The equations are [fig]circle with dot[/fig] + ♀ + ♃ + ♂ = 60,
[fig]circle with dot[/fig] + ♀ = [frac]2[/frac]/3 · 60 = 40,
[fig]circle with dot[/fig] + ♃ = [frac]3[/frac]/4 · 60 = 45,
[fig]circle with dot[/fig] + ♂ = [frac]3[/frac]/5 · 60 = 36.

Solving for [fig]circle with dot[/fig]
[fig]circle with dot[/fig] = 60 - ♀ - ♃ - ♂,
[fig]circle with dot[/fig] = 40 - ♀,
[fig]circle with dot[/fig] = 45 - ♃,
[fig]circle with dot[/fig] = 36 - ♂.
