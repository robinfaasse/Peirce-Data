Second, the sequence may close up by alternation from the two sides.

But in both these kinds of approximation, there is a part of the
sequence which continually approaches the limit in one direction; as,
$\frac{1}{0} > \frac{3}{2} > \frac{17}{12} > \frac{99}{70} > \frac{577}{408} > \frac{3363}{2378}$ etc.
