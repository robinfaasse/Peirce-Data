In order to effect [del]the[/del] vanishing of everything above
the dexter diagonal, it will be best to begin by making the
numbers in the top row all alike, by increasing one
column by a multiple of another. That done, we have
only to subtract the first column from all the others
to make all [ins]the numbers in[/ins] the top row, except the first, vanish. We
then treat the second row in the same manner.

But after a little practice it will be unnecessary
[del]to follow any[/del] fixed rule. We shall readily see different
ways of effecting our purpose.

Let us [del]add[/del] [ins]join[/ins] to the three equations, above, the identical
equation
$$A\underline{x} + B\underline{y} + C\underline{z} - (A\underline{x} + B\underline{y} + C\underline{z}) = 0.$$
The letters $A, B, C,$ will at the end be made $1$ or $0$, or
other simple values be given to them according to the
quantity we desire to calculate. For the sake of abbreviation,
we write $M$ in place of $(A\underline{x} + B\underline{y} + C\underline{z})$, and so have

$$\left| \begin{matrix}
+3, & -2, & +1, & -6 \\
+4, & -1, & -2, & +7 \\
+2, & +3, & -4, & +7 \\
A, & B, & C, & -M
\end{matrix} \right| = 0.$$
