[u]Scholium 36[/u]. The usual mode of language is that the moving object in generation is a place, instead of a body. Undoubtedly, the moving body carries a moving place with it. Still, this conception is somewhat complicated, and in cases in which it can, as easily as not, be avoided, it is best avoided, for the sake of perspicuity. For this reason, in the present revision, a moving body is spoken of where it is convenient to do so. But in case the motions of the bodies are different from that of the moving place considered, we shall speak of a moving wave.
