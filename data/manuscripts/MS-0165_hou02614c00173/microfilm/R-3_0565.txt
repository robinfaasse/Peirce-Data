We are now to take up the practical problem of making a perspective drawing, and for that purpose must undertake a part of the mathematician's business of which the scholar sees but little, but which calls for as much skill, at least, as the part he is instructed in. We have to consider what hypothesis, apart from absolute truth, will simplest represent the facts with sufficient accuracy.
