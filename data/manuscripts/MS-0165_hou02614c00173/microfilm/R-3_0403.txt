Since the third column has the smallest number in the
first row, it will be convenient to make it the first column,
thus:
$$\left| \begin{matrix}
+1, & +3, & -2, & -6 \\
-2, & +4, & -1, & +7 \\
-4, & +2, & +3, & +7 \\
C, & A, & B, & -M
\end{matrix} \right| = 0.$$

We will [del]subtract[/del] [ins]add to the[/ins] fourth column twice the second:
$$\left| \begin{matrix}
+1, & +3, & -2, & 0 \\
-2, & +4, & -1, & +15 \\
-4, & +2, & +3, & +11 \\
C, & A, & B, & 2A-M
\end{matrix} \right| = 0.$$

We will add to the second column the third, and diminish by the first:
$$\left| \begin{matrix}
+1, & 0, & -2, & 0 \\
-2, & +5, & -1, & +15 \\
-4, & +9, & +3, & +11 \\
C, & \begin{matrix} A+B \\ -C \end{matrix}, & B, & 2A-M
\end{matrix} \right| = 0.$$

We will add to the third column twice the first:
$$\left| \begin{matrix}
+1, & 0, & 0, & 0 \\
-2, & +5, & -5, & +15 \\
-4, & +9, & -5, & +11 \\
C, & \begin{matrix} A+B \\ -C \end{matrix}, & \begin{matrix} B+ \\ 2C \end{matrix}, & 2A-M
\end{matrix} \right| = 0.$$
