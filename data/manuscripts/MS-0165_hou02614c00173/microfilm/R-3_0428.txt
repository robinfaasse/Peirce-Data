[u]Corollary 56[/u]. Two proportions may be multiplied,
since they are equations. So that, if
[tabular]
& A : B :: C : D \\
and & E : F :: G : H, \\
\hline
then & AE : BF :: CG : DH.
[/tabular]

[u]Art. 73[/u]. [u]Definition 82[/u]. An expression of the form
[fraction]
[numerator]A - B[/numerator]
[denominator]A - C[/denominator]
[/fraction] . [fraction]
[numerator]C - D[/numerator]
[denominator]B - D[/denominator]
[/fraction]
is called an [u]anharmonic ratio[/u] of the four quantities A, B, C, D.
