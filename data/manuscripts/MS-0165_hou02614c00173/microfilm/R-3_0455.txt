<u>Corollary 63</u>. Hence, after having determined the value of
$r_1$, the root of smallest modulus, if the equation, brought
to the form in which one member is zero, be divided by $x-r_1$,
[del]the[/del] [ins]root of[/ins] next smallest [del]root[/del] modulus of the original equation
is the root of smallest modulus of the equation resulting
from that division.

<u>Art 82</u>. <u>Exercises</u>. 92. Determine all the roots of
the equation
$x^6 + 3x^5 - 7x^4 + 6x^3 - 7x^2 + 3x + 1 = 0$.
It is evident that $x=1$ is one solution. Hence [del]dividing[/del] [ins]we divide[/ins] by
$x-1$. [del]we get[/del]
