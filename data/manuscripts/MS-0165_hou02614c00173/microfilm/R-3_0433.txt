that the numerator of each shall be any constant number of
times the numerator of the one just before it increased by
a second constant number of times the denominator of that
preceding fraction, while the denominator of each shall be
the first constant number of times the denominator preceding
diminished by the second constant number of times the
numerator preceding. To express this algebraically, let
the fractions be $\frac{P_{0}}{Q_{0}}$, $\frac{P_{1}}{Q_{1}}$, $\frac{P_{2}}{Q_{2}}$, $\frac{P_{3}}{Q_{3}}$ etc. Let any one be $\frac{P_{n}}{Q_{n}}$,
and the next following $\frac{P_{n+1}}{Q_{n+1}}$. Then, [del]that[/del] taking $q$ and $p$ for
the two constants.

$$\frac{P_{n+1}}{Q_{n+1}} = \frac{q P_{n} + p Q_{n}}{q Q_{n} - p P_{n}} .$$

If we take $q = 19$ and $p = 10$, and take $\frac{P_{0}}{Q_{0}}$ as $\frac{0}{1}$, the
other fractions will be found on calculation to be
