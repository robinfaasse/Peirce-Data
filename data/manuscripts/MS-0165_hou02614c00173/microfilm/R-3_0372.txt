[u]Art. 59[/u]. [u]Definition 57[/u]. The system of rational, or
mensural, numbers consists of the datary numbers to-
gether with all quotients of one such number divided by another
not themselves such numbers, the moduli of their dividends
being prime to and not divisible by those of their divisors;
and each such quotient is a single number.

of the quotient of [del]a[/del] [ins]one[/ins] datary number by another by writing
[del]the[/del] dividend, called the [u]numerator[/u], over a horizontal line, called the
[u]virgula[/u], and the divisor, called the [u]denominator[/u] below that line.
