47. (By Bhaskara) Two lovers broke a string of pearls, of
which [fraction]1/6[/fraction] fell to the ground, [fraction]1/5[/fraction] remained on the sofa, [fraction]1/3[/fraction] were
caught by the girl, [fraction]1/10[/fraction] were taken by her sweetheart, and
6 remained on the string. How many were there?
[math]x = \frac{1}{6}x + \frac{1}{5}x + \frac{1}{3}x + \frac{1}{10}x + 6[/math]
[math]x = \frac{6}{1 - \frac{1}{6} - \frac{1}{5} - \frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{10}} = \frac{180}{30 - 5 - 6 - 10 - 3} = 18[/math]

48. (By Metrodorus.) Demochares has lived [fraction]1/4[/fraction] of his life
as a boy, [fraction]1/5[/fraction] as a youth, [fraction]1/3[/fraction] as a man of military age,
and 13 years as [del]a[/del] superannuated. How old is he?
Let [u]x[/u] be his age. Then the equation is
[fraction]1/4[/fraction][u]x[/u] + [fraction]1/5[/fraction][u]x[/u] + [fraction]1/3[/fraction][u]x[/u] + 13 = [u]x[/u],
which gives
[math]x = \frac{13}{1 - \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{5} - \frac{1}{3}} = \frac{13 . 60}{60 - 15 - 12 - 20} = \frac{13 . 60}{13} = 60.[/math]
