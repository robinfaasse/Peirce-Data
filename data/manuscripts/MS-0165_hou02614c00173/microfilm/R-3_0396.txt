We are further told the number is not 5. Hence we

Hence x-1 = 0, or x = 1.

The excess of 1 over 3 is -2, of which the cube is -8.
The excess of 1 over 1 is 0 of which the square is 0, and this
diminished by 8 gives -8. Either 4 or 5 would have satis-
fied the equation, since it was found divisible by both. In
fact, the equation was equivalent to
(x-4)(x-5)(x-1) = 0
and either of the three factors could vanish, and one
must vanish.
