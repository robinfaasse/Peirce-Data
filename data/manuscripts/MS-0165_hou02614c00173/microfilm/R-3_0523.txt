It frequently happens in mathematics that additional letters
are wanted; and it is desirable they should correspond to the
Greek and Roman letters, yet have distinctive names. The
Hebrew and [del]other[/del] Arabic alphabets are very objectionable,
because the letters look so much alike. The neatest
alphabet in the world is the Indian alphabet employed
by Asoka in the [ins]3[u]rd[/u][/ins] Century before Christ. It is, here,
given, as a mine of handy shapes.
