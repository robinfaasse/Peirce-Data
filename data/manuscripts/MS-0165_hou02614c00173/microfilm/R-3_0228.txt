Chapter I.

INTRODUCTION,
on Mathematics in General.

[u]Art. 1. Definition 1.[/u] Mathematics is the science which draws necessary conclusions.

[u]Gloss 1.[/u] "Mathematics" (pronounced mathəmæt'iks, as it is written in Ellis's Glossic) is a noun plural in form, but now used with a verb in the singular number. A few old-fashioned people still say "the mathematics are an important science." [del]Sixteenth century writers[/del]

The Mathematickes, and the Metaphysickes
Fall to them as you finde your stomacke serves you.
(Taming of the Shrew I. i)

Earlier writers use the singular form, [u]mathematike[/u].
