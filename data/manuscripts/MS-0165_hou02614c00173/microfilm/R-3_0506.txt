[date]1783[/date]) who during his 56 years of activity made more numerous

statement was
Faces + Summits = Edges + 2,

published in [date]1794[/date], gave this (Book VII. [del]Theorem[/del] [ins]Proposition[/ins] 25). From Legen-
dre it has been copied into most subsequent elementary text books.
