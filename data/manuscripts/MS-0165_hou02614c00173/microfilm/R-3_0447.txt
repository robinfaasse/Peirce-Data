87. Two drapers, [del]each[/del] [ins]A and B[/ins] have the same cloth, but B has 3 yards more of it than has A. They sell at different prices and their aggregate receipts are $35. A says, "At my price, I should have received $24 for your piece." B replies, "At my price, what you had would only have fetched $12.50." Let $x$ be A's yards. Then B's were $x+3$.
