[u]Corollary 64.[/u] Every equation has as many roots
as [del]the[/del] [ins]its[/ins] degree, though some may be equal to one
another.

[u]Corollary 65.[/u] If $x_1$, $x_2$, $x_3$, etc are the roots of
the equation
$y = x^n + a_1 x^{n-1} + a_2 x^{n-2} + a_3 x^{n-3} + \text{etc} \dots + a_{n-1} x + a_n = 0$,
it follows that
$y = (x-x_1)(x-x_2)(x-x_3)(x-x_4), \dots (x-x_n)$
and consequently
$a_1 = -x_1 - x_2 - x_3 - x_4 - \dots - x_n$
$a_2 = +x_1 x_2 + x_1 x_3 + \dots x_2 x_3 + x_2 x_4 + \text{etc}$
$a_n = [del]\dots[/del] (-)^n x_1 x_2 x_3 \dots x_n$.
