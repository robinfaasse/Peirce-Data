[del]Art 83.[/del]
[ins]Scholium 34.[/ins] [del]Imaginary[/del] [ins]Imaginary[/ins] quantities do not come within the scope of an elementary treatise. But since we cannot avoid their cropping out from time to time, being, as they are, the bed-rock of mathematical truth, it is best to make some slight explanations about them.

There is nothing mysterious nor impossible about imaginary quantities. [del]They[/del] Like other numbers, they are mere expressions of relative position. But instead of expressing order along a line, as other numbers do, they express positions on a surface.
