For suppose $u, v, w, t$ were the multipliers that made the horizontal rows sum up to $0$. Then $\frac{u}{t}, \frac{v}{t}, \frac{w}{t}, 1$ would have the same effect, since $\frac{0}{t} = 0$.

But we have just seen that by multiplying each [del]equation[/del], that is, each horizontal row, by a suitable multiplier, the sum of all the numbers in every vertical column can be made zero.

Note, then, this reasoning:

The whole meaning of the equations is, that suitable multiplication of each column will make the sum of every row zero.

No general rule can be true of the array which does not follow as a necessary consequence of this.
