[u]Art[/u] 134. [u]Corollary 114[/u]. The plane central projection, or perspective delineation, of a point is a point, and of a ray is a ray. For the result of producing the point from the centre is a ray; and the plane section of that ray is a point. So the result of producing the ray from the centre is a plane; and the plane section of a plane is a ray.

[u]Theorem 55[/u]. [u]Any four points on a ray have the same arrangement as their plane central projections on that of the ray, admitting that every ray returns into itself.[/u]
