It may be remarked that the same results as far as lines are
concerned could be reached by counting each detached piece of
the line as a piece and reckoning each singularity as adding as much as
the excess of the number of ways of leaving it on the line over two. Thus,
a figure of 8, reckoned the first way has 2 pieces, Cyclosis 0, singularity
1, making its Census number 1. Reckoned the second way, it has 1
piece, cyclosis 2, singularity +2, again making its census-number 1.
