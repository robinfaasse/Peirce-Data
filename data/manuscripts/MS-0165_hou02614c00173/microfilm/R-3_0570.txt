[u]Art. 136[/u]. [u]Definition 191[/u]. In a perspective delineation the [u]vanishing[/u]
[u]line[/u] of a plane is the ray which in the picture represents the ray
in which that plane cuts the plane of the firmament; and the
[u]vanishing point[/u] of a ray is the point which in the picture
represents the point in which that ray cuts the plane of the
firmament.

[u]Definition 192[/u]. In a perspective delineation, the [u]trace[/u] of a
plane is the ray in which that plane intersects the plane of the
delineation; and the [u]trace[/u] of a ray is the point in which that
ray intersects the plane of delineation.

[u]Definition 193[/u]. In a perspective delineation, the [u]ground[/u]
[u]line[/u] of a plane is the [del]line[/del] ray which represents the ray in
which that plane intersects the ground, and the [u]ground point[/u]
of a ray is the point which represents the point in which that
ray intersects the ground.

[ins]Direction 2. To draw in a given plane through a given point a ray intersecting another
ray in that plane at infinity, lay one side of a wooden triangle against the given ray and place a wooden
straight edge against another side of the triangle, then slide the triangle until the first side comes to the given point.[/ins]
