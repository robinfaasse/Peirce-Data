77. A certain whole number consists of six digits. Its triple
is got by transferring its first digit to the end, and its quintuple
by transferring its last digit to the beginning. What is the number?

As there are here but 2 equations to determine 6 unknowns, we have
to take into account that they are whole numbers less than 10.

Let the number be

[u]x[/u] + 10 [u]y[/u] + 100 [u]z[/u] + 1000 [u]u[/u] + 10000 [u]v[/u] + 100 000 [u]w[/u].

Then its triple is

3 [u]x[/u] + 30 [u]y[/u] + 300 [u]z[/u] + 3000 [u]u[/u] + 30000 [u]v[/u] + 300 000 [u]w[/u].

This is said to equal

[u]w[/u] + 10 [u]x[/u] + 100 [u]y[/u] + 1000 [u]z[/u] + 10000 [u]u[/u] + 100000 [u]v[/u];

So that

7 [u]x[/u] + 70 [u]y[/u] + 700 [u]z[/u] + 7000 [u]u[/u] + 70000 [u]v[/u] = 299999 [u]w[/u].

Its quintuple is

5 [u]x[/u] + 50 [u]y[/u] + 500 [u]z[/u] + 5000 [u]u[/u] + 50000 [u]v[/u] + 500 000 [u]w[/u].

This is said to equal

[u]y[/u] + 10 [u]z[/u] + 100 [u]u[/u] + 1000 [u]v[/u] + 10000 [u]w[/u] + 100000 [u]x[/u];

So that

99995 [u]x[/u] = 49 [u]y[/u] + 490 [u]z[/u] + 4900 [u]u[/u] + 49000 [u]v[/u] + 490 000 [u]w[/u].
