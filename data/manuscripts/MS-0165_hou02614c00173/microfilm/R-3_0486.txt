[u]Art. [del]17[/del] [ins]19[/ins]. Scholium 39[/u]. The generation of a surface by a filament can begin or end either at a line or at a point. As an example of the first mode, imagine a round filament to be carried about and finally to return to its original position. It will thus, usually generate the surface of a ring. As an example of the second mode, imagine a particle while descending to become a [ins]horizontal[/ins] ring-shaped filament and this while continuing to descend to first expand and then shrink to a particle and disappear. A ball-shaped surface may thus be generated.
