$$2(-a+b)P_8 = 2b P_6 - 2a P_7$$
$$2(a-c)P_9 = -2c P_5 + 2a P_7$$
$$2(b+c)P_{10} = 2c P_5 - 2b P_6$$

Now adding these equations together we have

$$2(-a+b)P_8 + 2(a-c)P_9 + 2(b+c)P_{10} = 0$$

which shows that $P_8$, $P_9$, $P_{10}$ are on a line.
