[u]Now, then, I draw a ball. It turns out to be white and ivory.[/u]
I have then, three [del]arguments to show that it is[/del] [ins]reasons to believe it to be[/ins] auriferous.
1[ins]st[/ins], 5 to 1 of all the balls are auriferous;
2[ins]nd[/ins], 2 to 1 of all the white balls are auriferous;
3[ins]rd[/ins], 5 to 1 of all the ivory balls are auriferous.
Now, [del]with fact[/del] we may just slip in the remark that I might not know that the ball was white and ivory by my personal inspection, but by the testimony of [del]two[/del] [ins]several[/ins] witnesses of perfect competence and veracity, who should severally testify to the ball being auriferous one judging by one sign and the other by the other.
