We should pass our lives in amaze-ment if we wondered why there should be no connection between such things as my getting an even number of letters by mail and my seeing the next night an even number of shooting stars.

When there is a breach of an existing regularity, however, there is always surprise and [del]an inquiry[/del] [ins]a demand[/ins] for an explanation. There is no report of a centenarian without people's wanting to know what caused him to live so long; al-though the phenomenon is simply what the mortality-curve ought to have led us to expect.

When we expect regularity and find only irregularity without any breach of regularity, we are simply induced to revise our reasons for expecting the regularity, but are not stimulated to seek any positive cause for the irregularity.
