[ins][del]To worship[/del] for example[/ins] [del]because[/del] [ins]if[/ins] it is a tradition of his house, and he is
determined to maintain the family traditions, or
from sympathy with his neighbors who take the
same view, or from reverence for the teacher who
has commanded him to believe it, or because he
thinks it is a good, wholesome belief that will
bring him content and happiness. But such a man
has but a vague notion of what truth is; and that
sort of thing is not what I mean by the "adoption of
an assertion as true," whether in small or in great
measure.
