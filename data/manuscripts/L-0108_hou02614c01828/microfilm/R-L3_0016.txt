[fig]The Sun logo[/fig] New York, [date]May 10, 1892[/date]

Dear Sir: I have carefully examined
the three manuscripts you kindly favored
me with on Friday last, two of them
having been put in type by one of
my assistants. My conclusion is
that they will not answer the purpose
of a daily newspaper at all. They
are too general, too abstract; they
lack that evident relation to the
interests and events of the present
hour, which is essential to laying
hold of the public attention.

Yours truly,

Charles S. Peirce Esq
