[u]The Phyllotactic Numbers[/u]

Let the $m^{\text{th}}$ phyllotactic number be written '$\Phi_m$' and read 'Phyl $m$'.

<u>Definition</u>

(1) $\Phi_0 = 0$
(2) $\Phi_1 = 1$
(3) $\Delta^2 \Phi_m = \Phi_{m-1}$

<u>Theorem</u>

If the formula
(4) $\sum_i A_i \Phi_{B_i + a} + (-)^a \sum_j A_j \Phi_{B_j - a} = 0$
holds good for two successive values of $a$ it holds for all values.

For the sum of the equations for $a$ and $a+1$ will give the equation for $a+2$, because by (3)
$\Phi_{B_i + a} + \Phi_{B_i + a + 1} = \Phi_{B_i + a + 2}$
$\Phi_{B_j - a} [del]+[/del] [ins]-[/ins] \Phi_{B_j - a - 1} = \Phi_{B_j - a - 2}$

And the equation for $a$ diminished by that for $a-1$ will give the equation for $a-2$, because by (3)
$\Phi_{B_i + a} - \Phi_{B_i + a - 1} = \Phi_{B_i + a - 2}$
$\Phi_{B_j - a} + \Phi_{B_j - a + 1} = \Phi_{B_j - a + 2}$.
