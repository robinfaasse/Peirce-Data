Corollary. $^1\mathcal{O}_{m+0} = ^1\mathcal{O}_1\ ^1\mathcal{O}_m + ^1\mathcal{O}_0\ ^1\mathcal{O}_{m-1}$
$^1\mathcal{O}_{m+1} = ^1\mathcal{O}_2\ ^1\mathcal{O}_m + ^1\mathcal{O}_1\ ^1\mathcal{O}_{m-1}$

Consequently,
(6) $^1\mathcal{O}_{m+n} = ^1\mathcal{O}_{n+1}\ ^1\mathcal{O}_m + ^1\mathcal{O}_n\ ^1\mathcal{O}_{m-1} = ^1\mathcal{O}_m\ ^1\mathcal{O}_n + ^1\mathcal{O}_m\ ^1\mathcal{O}_{n-1} + ^1\mathcal{O}_{m-1}\ ^1\mathcal{O}_n$

From (6) we have again
$^1\mathcal{O}_{m+n+0} = ^1\mathcal{O}_1\ ^1\mathcal{O}_{n+1}\ ^1\mathcal{O}_m + ^1\mathcal{O}_1\ ^1\mathcal{O}_n\ ^1\mathcal{O}_{m-1} + ^1\mathcal{O}_0\ ^1\mathcal{O}_{m+n-1}$
$^1\mathcal{O}_{m+n+1} = ^1\mathcal{O}_2\ ^1\mathcal{O}_{n+1}\ ^1\mathcal{O}_m + ^1\mathcal{O}_2\ ^1\mathcal{O}_n\ ^1\mathcal{O}_{m-1} + ^1\mathcal{O}_1\ ^1\mathcal{O}_{m+n-1}$

Consequently,
$^1\mathcal{O}_{m+n+p} = ^1\mathcal{O}_{p+1}\ ^1\mathcal{O}_{n+1}\ ^1\mathcal{O}_m + ^1\mathcal{O}_{p+1}\ ^1\mathcal{O}_n\ ^1\mathcal{O}_{m-1} + ^1\mathcal{O}_p\ ^1\mathcal{O}_{m+n-1}$
