[marginalia]Ms. 1056[/marginalia]

[u]Phyllotactic Numbers[/u].

Write
(1) $\vartheta_{0} = 0$ (Read, "Thyl zero = 0")
(2) $\vartheta_{1} = 1$ "Thyl first"
(3) $\vartheta_{m+1} = \vartheta_{m} + \vartheta_{m-1}$

It will often be convenient to refer to (3) under the forms

a $\vartheta_{m+2} = \vartheta_{m+1} + \vartheta_{m}$
b $\vartheta_{m+1} = \vartheta_{m} + \vartheta_{m-1}$
c $\vartheta_{m} = \vartheta_{m-1} + \vartheta_{m-2}$
d $\vartheta_{2-m} = \vartheta_{1-m} + \vartheta_{-m}$
e $\vartheta_{1-m} = \vartheta_{-m} + \vartheta_{-1-m}$
f $\vartheta_{-m} = \vartheta_{-1-m} + \vartheta_{-2-m}$

g $\vartheta_{m+2} - \vartheta_{m+1} = \vartheta_{m}$
h $\vartheta_{m+1} - \vartheta_{m} = \vartheta_{m-1}$
i $\vartheta_{m} - \vartheta_{m-1} = \vartheta_{m-2}$
j $\vartheta_{2-m} - \vartheta_{1-m} = \vartheta_{-m}$
k $\vartheta_{1-m} - \vartheta_{-m} = \vartheta_{-1-m}$
l $\vartheta_{-m} - \vartheta_{-1-m} = \vartheta_{-2-m}$

If $\vartheta_{i} = (-)^{i+1} \vartheta_{-i}$ then putting $m = i$ in (3[del]f[/del][ins]k[/ins]) and (3j)
[del]$\vartheta_{1-i}$[/del] $\vartheta_{1-i} + \vartheta_{i-2} = (-)^{i+1} \vartheta_{2-i} + (-)^{i} \vartheta_{1-i}$
