Suppose it possible to find $i$ such that
$$(\phi_{\infty})^{i} = \mathcal{O}_{1-i} + \mathcal{O}_{-i} \phi_{\infty}$$
$$(\phi_{\infty})^{i+1} = \mathcal{O}_{-i} + \mathcal{O}_{-1-i} \phi_{\infty}$$

$$(\phi_{\infty})^{i} - (\phi_{\infty})^{i+1} = \mathcal{O}_{1-i} - \mathcal{O}_{-i} + (\mathcal{O}_{-i} - \mathcal{O}_{-1-i}) \phi_{\infty}$$

But by (11), (3k), and (3l) this reduces to
$$(\phi_{\infty})^{i+2} = \mathcal{O}_{-(i+1)} + \mathcal{O}_{-(i+2)} \phi_{\infty}$$

This is of the same form as the two hypothetical equations. Consequently if any such value of $i$ can be found a similar equation holds for every value of $i$. But putting $i = 0$, the hypothetical equations reduce to
$$(\phi_{\infty})^{0} = 1 \quad (\phi_{\infty})^{1} = \phi_{\infty}$$
