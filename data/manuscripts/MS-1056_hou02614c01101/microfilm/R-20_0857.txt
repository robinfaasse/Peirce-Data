[u]Problem[/u]. Let there be [u]n[/u] groups of stars numbered from 1 to n. Let $M_i$ denote the mean magnitude of the $i^{\text{th}}$ group. Let every group say the $i^{\text{th}}$ be compared with the $(i+1)\text{st}$ and $(i+2)\text{nd}$. Only the $n^{\text{th}}$ cannot be compared with any following group[ins]s[/ins] since there are none nor the $(n-1)\text{th}$ with any [ins]following one[/ins] but the $n^{\text{th}}$. Denote observed values by writing the quantity observed in parentheses. Thus $(M_i - M_{i+1})$ is [del]observed[/del] obtained for every value of $i$ from $1$ to $n-1$ and $(M_i - M_{i+2})$ for every value of $i$ from $1$ to $n-2$. Denote the best value [ins]of $M_i - M_{i+1}$[/ins] concluded by least squares from observations of all groups from $1$ up to $i+1$ by $\{M_i - M_{i+1}\}$. Then the problem is to find a general expression for $\{M_n - M_{n-1}\}$.

In order to solve this problem we must first have a notation for the phyllotactic numbers, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, etc.

(1) Write $\Phi_0 = 0$
(2) " $\Phi_1 = 1$
(3) " $\Phi_m = \Phi_{m-1} + \Phi_{m-2}$ [del]$\Phi_m - \Phi_{m-1} = \Phi_{m-2}$[/del]
(Read "$\Phi_m$", "Phyll "$m^{\text{th}}$")

Then $\Phi_2 = 1$ $\Phi_3 = 2$ $\Phi_4 = 3$ $\Phi_5 = 5$ $\Phi_6 = 8$ etc.
$\Phi_{-1} = 1$ $\Phi_{-2} = -1$ $\Phi_{-3} = 2$ $\Phi_{-4} = -3$ $\Phi_{-5} = 5$ $\Phi_{-6} = -8$ etc.
