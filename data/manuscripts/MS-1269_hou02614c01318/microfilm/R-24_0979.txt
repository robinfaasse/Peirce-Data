In the entrance passage, we are told that great care must be used in passing

A pyramid was simply a tomb with a secret chamber in
which was to be deposited the mummy of a king, because of some
belief connected with a resurrection. Such was doubtless
the king's sole purpose; and though his priests may have
harbored some ulterior views, yet there is not [del]a scintilla[/del] [ins]the slightest[/ins]
of reason [ins]metrological[/ins] to think that any astronomical, geodetical, physical,
or other scientific idea governed the construction of the whole
or of any portion of the pile or of its contents. The numerous
hypotheses of this character which have been proposed have
no other value than as illustrations of fallacious reasoning.
