The title of the fifth chapter may be read "On making the mean

word, reads "Anfang vom machen den mittleren Unterschied." [ins]p. 33[/ins] The

This ends the purely arithmetical part of the book; and mensu-
ration follows. There are first some problems on the solid contents
of granaries, [ins]both round and square,[/ins] the diameter and [del]heights[/del] [ins]heights[/ins] being given. The horizontal
area having been calculated from the diameter, the Egyptian mul-
tiplies this by the height, and then by $1\frac{1}{2}$. Although the sides were
very likely sloping, and the diameter given may be the diameter
at the top, it is very unlikely that granaries were so made as to
render this singular rule correct; for this would make the
sides of high granaries steeper than those of low ones, the diame-
ter at the bottom being always about $1\frac{3}{4}$ of that at the top.
