all Egyptian fractions having denominators less than a hundred. These
are regularly arranged, and the results being written in red ink form
a table quite indispensible to the Egyptian computer. This table occupies
eight columns, or more than a quarter of the book. Each column is headed
"Inoutke 2"; and the different divisions begin, "in front of 3," "of 5," etc.
