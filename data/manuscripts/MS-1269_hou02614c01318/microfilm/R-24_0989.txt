As the pyramid grew out of the mastaba, so the [del]slope[/del] [ins]slope[/ins] of the pyramid [del]may have of[/del] 14 in 11 may have grown out of the slope of the mastaba of 4 in 1 by simply adding 10 to each term; thus 4+10 : 1+10. Now the early pyramids were simply a pile of mastabas, and were built in [fig]sketch of a stepped mastaba structure[/fig] the same way.
