No doubt, every art, useful, aesthetic, or moral, has its peculiar methods.
But, for the most part, these methods are not so remarkable as to
repay that labor of acquiring that mastery of a multitude of techni-
cal details, which is indispensible to appreciating those methods. It
is true, there are some exceptional arts which are exceedingly in-
structive from a logical point of view; but it has seemed best to
construct this history upon a plan susceptible of very simple defini-
tion, and not to complicate it by exceptions.
