A Diagrammatic Syntax.
My dear Risteen:
I mentioned to you, while you were last year, that I have a diagrammatic syntax which analyzes the Syllogism into no less than six inferential steps. I now describe its latest state of development for the first time. I am glad to think that my account of it will have one such a reader as you. C. S. P.
I.
