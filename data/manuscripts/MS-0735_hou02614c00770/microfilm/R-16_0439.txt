[u]Introduction. What is Logic?[/u]

Logic is the [u]Theory of Reasoning[/u]. That is to say, its main business is to ascertain the conditions upon which the just strength of reasoning depends. This is the statement of what logic has always been understood to be, at least approximately, and of how the word ought always to be used, so far as the exigencies of science will permit. It [ins]embodies[/ins] the original charter of the term; [del]its[/del] and though it may be superseded as a scientific definition, must, like the definition of a fish as any aquatic vertebrate, forever be [del]accepted[/del] [ins]respected[/ins] as alone authoritative in a rough way.
