The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Aug 25, 1898[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I send you A. R. Wallace's "Wonderful Century" — a "wonderful" book when we consider the chapter on "Vaccination an delusion." There will be many topics ready to your hand, but try & hold yourself in on regards space.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. Garrison
