The Nation, 206-210 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Jan 30. 1903[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I am sending you Rowland's
Physical Papers, by express. Here is a
fine opportunity to make a real
note, not a review: say 300 or 400
words, description of the contents, with
any personal touch that
you choose. I believe the Nation
was derelict in being silent when
he passed away.

Mellish will come to you direct
from Macmillan.

Yours always,
WPG.
