Marietta, Ohio, [date]Nov. 4, 1903[/date].
Editor of "The Nation";
Dear Sir:
On p. 263 of your paper for [date]Oct. 1, 1903[/date], is an article entitled "British and American Science", in which occurs the following sentence;-
"We forgot to mention one of the greatest discoveries of all, made by an humble clergyman of the Church of England, Gay - the discovery that the association of ideas is the autocrat (or, at least, the first of two consuls) that governs all the activities of the human mind, so far as they are subject to any mental law."
Will not the writer of the article be so kind as to tell me where I can find an account of the discoverer and discovery referred to?
Yours sincerely,
Charles H. Goddard.
