**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]March 22. 1902[/date]

My dear Peirce:

There was stupidity in our adver-
tising department over the answer to
"Unique." The letter was sent up to me.
I failed to recall your nom de plume,
& returned it, saying that it must
concern some advertiser. Now I have
given instructions to send all replies
to me, & I have had type-written copies
made of your form.

I share your feeling in contemplating
the inevitable —

"The houses that were our destination
be ours."

Yours faithfully,
W. P. G.
