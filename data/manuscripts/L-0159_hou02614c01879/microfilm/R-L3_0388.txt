My dear Peirce:

Have you not heard that we are not fit to be judges in our own case? Now I maintain that the two articles I returned were among your worst, not because of any holding diff-erence from you, but because they were a poor kind of book review — a great deal about yourself, & very little about Ward & Chanute.
