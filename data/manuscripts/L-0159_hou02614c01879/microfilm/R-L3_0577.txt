**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]March 2 1902[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I will discharge your advertising
account with credit for matter now
in hand, & make you payment
besides, shortly. I hope to see
Paterson this week.

Your waterfalls must be perfect
at this moment. Had I been a bird
yesterday, I would have flown to
Paterson to view Sam Patch's falls
— and the surrounding misery of fire
& flood.

Yours truly ever,
W. P. G.
