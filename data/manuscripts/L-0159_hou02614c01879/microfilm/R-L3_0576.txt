**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Feb. 2 1902[/date]

My dear Peirce:

You have only overlooked the
proof of "X-rays," but the fact that
the notice was published several
weeks ago. At this moment, I don't
recall whether you have returned the
proof of Fielding. But I think so.

There is, I assure you, nothing in
the Poe matter. The poem cannot be
his.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. Garrison
