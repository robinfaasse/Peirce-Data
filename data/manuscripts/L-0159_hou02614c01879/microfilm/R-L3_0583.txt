Your wife took me into her confi-

health, & I must say that I was
as much pained by the revelation as
I was struck by her courage in facing
it. Those she loves may be enabled
to go abroad, is my sincere desire.
There, being there, you could write an
agreeable [del]station[/del] [ins]station[/ins] letters, I do not
