The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]July 12 1901[/date]

My dear Peirce:

After editing all of yours in
hand at this moment, I enclose proof
of Berkeley, & append an exact puissance
of $50, + $10, + $5, + $3, + $1.50 returned
in one form or another from February to
date. I am now in an acute stage
of indigestion, with a mass of matter
(some, 7 months old!) awaiting its
chance to be worked off in print. All
my collaborators are therefore being put
on short commons & reduced to "Notes"
or less.
