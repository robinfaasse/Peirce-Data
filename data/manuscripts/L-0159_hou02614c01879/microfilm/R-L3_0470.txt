Colorado College,
Colorado Springs,
[date]May 22, 1899[/date].

To the Editor of the Nation,
Dear Sir:

Your reply to my letter, printed on p. 376, is extremely unsatisfactory, for the reason that it obscures the question as to the exact nature of Galileo's explicit assumption. The point at issue is one of considerable importance, for if you are right, then one of the most important historical works ever written on the Science of Mechanics (Mach's) contains a vicious mistake. For that reason I trust that you will consent to publish the enclosed note. I for my part am, of course, anxious to clear myself of the charge that I blindly copied the mistake of another writer. This will be my last communication to you on this subject.

Trusting that you will grant my request, I am,

Respectfully yours,
Florian Cajori.
