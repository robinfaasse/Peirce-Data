The Shelburne
Atlantic City, N. J.
[date]March 24th 1894[/date]

To the Editor of the Nation.

Sir,

I enclose a few lines which I hope you will be able to insert in the Correspondence Column of the Nation. I enclose my card with my permanent address — this of course not for publication.

Yours truly
Charlotte Angas Scott.

[u]Permanent address[/u],
Bryn Mawr College
Bryn Mawr, Pennsylvania.
