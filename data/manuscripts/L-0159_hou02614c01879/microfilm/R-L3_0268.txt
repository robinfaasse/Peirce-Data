Enlightened men cannot but expect considerable, perhaps great, changes in religious beliefs during the course of the coming century. To [del]wish[/del] [ins]fear[/ins] these changes may break up the churches would be frivolous. Yet there must be the effect upon every denomination which pins its existence upon an unyielding creed in the manner you say morality requires it to do.
