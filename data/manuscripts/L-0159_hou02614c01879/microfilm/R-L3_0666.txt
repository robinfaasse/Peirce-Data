The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Jan. 21 1904[/date]

My dear Peirce,

Whatever Newcomb's faults of temper may be, he is a genuine man, & I'm glad his autobiog. partly abates your disapprobation of him. He has one great merit for a scientific man: he keeps alive his political interests, and often writes forcibly for the Nation in that line.

You will surely find Tarde among the mass of books I sent you. I hope to print your Galileo soon.

Yours always,
W. P. G.
