The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]July 10. 1898[/date]

My dear Peirce:

To yours of July 8: —

1. You have your bag by this time.

2. Van t'Hoff has been printed, with
its [u]vis[/u] on its head.

3. Noel Winter is quite unknown
to me.

4. As to stocks, I have nothing
to invest, & am a poor financier.
I hope your Water Power Co.
holdings will realize what you
estimate. Thanks for the offer to
myself.

Sincerely yours,
W. P. G.
