**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Oct. 28. 1904[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Here is the card of a brother
of the Mr. Turner whose philosophy
you noticed at length for us, to
his gratification. He asks after the
authorship, & I told him I would
leave it [ins]you[/ins] to reveal yourself.

Yours always,
WPG.
