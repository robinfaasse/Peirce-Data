New York, [date]March 22 1899[/date].

My dear Peirce.

I am going to offer you
an article which I think will
interest you & put money in
your purse. [u]Confidentially[/u]: The
Evening Post is undertaking a
special supplement for the end of
the year, reviewing the century philo-
sophically. There will be 20 or 30
first-rate writers, whose names will
be signed. Pay will be liberal and
not delayed till publication. The unit
of length is two pp. of the [u]Nation[/u],
or 3600 words circa.

Now the subject I propose for you
