Dear Mr. Garrison,

As I have just got back from my
house, I hasten to clear myself once more
of a misapprehension on the spacing writer.
My view is direct and unequivocal:
his drawings as the Paris Expos. are so
bad there is no excuse for publishing any of
them unless it is "copy" choice; besides,
I know that one of your correspondents
has more than once forestalled me in
discussing the Beaux Arts at Paris
— the public is collective of the temper,
any way, in my view.
