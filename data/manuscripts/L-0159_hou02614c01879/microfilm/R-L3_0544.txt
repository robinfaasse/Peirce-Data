40 LAFAYETTE PLACE,
NEW YORK.

[date]Oct 18. 1900[/date].

Dear Mr Garrison,

That is a very good lot of books and pamphlets that you sent me, and many of them just the sort of things that one does not come across in trade lists - just what we want to complete our collection on the Boer War and the Philippines.

I thank you very sincerely for them. The clipping articles of Albert Billings is a decided cure - probably he was one of the family which went all over the country free.
