The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]July 21 1893[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I enclose a check for
$25 on account.

Remembering my conservative
nature, keep a little rein on
your praise of Gould. I have,
in fact, but little intellectual
sympathy with men who are
disturbed by their inability to
"find out God."

Yours faithfully
W. P. Garrison
