The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Sept. 17 1892[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce:

Our account stands at present with you debtor about $15. But I have considerable of yours in hand & in type, & will make a nominal advance of $100. I have just sent a check for that amount to the 5th Ave. Bank.

My wife wrote me of her very pleasant call on you. She now telegraphs me that the house will remain open till Oct. 1.

In great haste,
Yours truly
W. P. G.
