**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Aug 16 1901[/date]

My dear Peirce,

Your notice is here, & I don't
know whether to look upon it as a
godsend or view it with a critic's eye.
I am at the parting of the ways —
nearly caught up with my accumula-
tion of belated notices, & not quite
certain how I shall get over the Septem-
ber-October bar. You give me no
date to this publication. Is it no
later than 1900? A line on the en-
closed card, if you please.

Not a Logic book have I to my
name at this instant; & why belabor
Lafleur for our brief notice of Sidgwick?
