Book-review Editor of the "Nation", New York

Dear Sir: Let me thank you for your very kind notice of my work on Solar Energy, in your issue for [date]July 11th[/date], I wish the book were better, but no such better book will ever be written by any one whose objects in life are confined to one especial science. As all great inventions arise from without, so all great discoveries arise, and my only claim is that I have coordinated in a single focus the light of all the natural sciences, which, hitherto, has not been done. That I lack the knowledge required in its full extent is very true, but where is there one who does not lack this knowledge? I do not write, in my book, my own investigations, experiments, and discoveries, though I have been a hard worker in the natural sciences for twenty-five years.
