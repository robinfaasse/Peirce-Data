The International Calendar Co.
Incorporated under the Laws of the State of New York.
Capitalized at $10,000, Full Paid and non-Assessable Stock.

VICTOR O. A. STRAUSS, President.
HARRY KETLER, Secretary and Treasurer.

BIBLE HOUSE, NEW YORK.
General Publishers, Advertising Brokers, and Designers of Trade Circulars.

New York, N. Y., November, 1895.

Notice is hereby given that the

INTERNATIONAL CALENDAR COMPANY

has been reorganized, Mr. Victor O. A. Strauss having accepted the
Presidency and Mr. Harry Ketler the Secretaryship of the well-
known concern.

The undersigned, having retired from the management of the
corporation, hereby returns his sincere thanks for the many and
various courtesies extended him by our patrons in the past, and, well
appreciating the ability and character of the gentlemen now con-
ducting the affairs of this Company, takes the liberty to earnestly
request a kind continuation of all the good offices and favors he
has hitherto been privileged to enjoy.

Very truly yours,

PAUL F. MOTTELAY.
