The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Dec. 25 1870[/date]

My dear Peirce,

Many thanks for research. You shall receive a proof early next week.

And thanks for your better news about your wife's health. I fear you have been terribly off-ridden, & the end of our cold season is not yet. When it does leave its victim, I may feel the tramp's yearning, & turn my face Milfordward, but I had better make no promises.

Yours always,
W. P. G.
