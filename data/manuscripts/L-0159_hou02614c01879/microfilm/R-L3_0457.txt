My dear Garrison:

I have just received [del]your[/del] from you Holman's communication. I have lettered the points he makes in the margin & therefore return his type-written MS with the following replies. If any notice is to be taken of it, you will please return it to me for more careful rejoinders. I take his points in his order although that is not the order I should prefer.

A. Of the eleven physicists he names, I only know what one, Mendenhall, has really said; and he is so utterly stupid about the kind of things [ins]discussed[/ins] [del]men[/del] that what he says has no weight. I have not found Wolcott Gibbs always perfectly clear about such matters & E. C. Pickering still less. So far as I know of the others only Trowbridge has ever set up as an authority about such things & I have never seen his book. No doubt as many of them as knew about Holman's state of health would be as tender as they could in their criticisms, as I should have been myself had I known about it. But any way, it is no answer to my criticism (which was made as brief as possible) to tell me that eleven gentlemen none of whom are deeper students of these matters than myself do not agree with me, even if it be a fact.
