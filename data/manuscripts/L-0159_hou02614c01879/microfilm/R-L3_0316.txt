The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Sept. 27 1893[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I am back again after the close of my tragedy.

I am sorry you had the idea that it was urgent to reply to Prál. Such matters always allow plenty of time & deliberation. I think if I had seen his letter I should not have printed it, though I might have sent it to you with protest. Your rejoinder, like his letter, seemed to me to call for a little cutting.

Yours always,
W. P. G.
