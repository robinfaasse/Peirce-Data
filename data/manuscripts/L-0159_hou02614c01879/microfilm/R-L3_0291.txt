**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Oct. 18, 1892[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce:

With me anything is possible, and it is probable that I have muddled the Tyndall parcel. I seem to remember addressing it to you at Port Jervis, with or without adding Milford, & so indicat-ing the express; but then we have no voucher for any such dispatch on the 8th inst. Again, I seem to re-member having the two volumes done up separately for the mail. In that case it would have been folly to send to Port Jervis, the having perhaps ascertained at first the weight precluded sending by post,
