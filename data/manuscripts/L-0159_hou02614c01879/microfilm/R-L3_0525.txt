The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]June 28 1880[/date]

My dear Peirce,

I find that the Association
has been covered by the Post's reporters,
so that I can offer you nothing
there; but I enclose $25 in full
of your final share of the Century
article.

Thanks for the good note on
Boethius, & regards & best wishes
to your wife.

Faithfully yours,

W. P. Garrison
