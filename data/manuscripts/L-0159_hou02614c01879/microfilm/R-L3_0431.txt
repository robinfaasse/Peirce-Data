**Northwestern University,**
**Evanston, Illinois.**

COLLEGE OF LIBERAL ARTS.
DEPARTMENT OF MATHEMATICS.

[date]May 11th 1898[/date]

To the Editor of the Nation
New York.

Dear Sir:— A few days ago I asked the Macmillan Co. to send you a copy of my translation of Reye's 'Geometry of Position' which has just appeared, with the hope that a notice in your columns would bring the book prominently before the public interested in it and would do valuable service in the development of [ins]the interest in[/ins] pure geometry in this country.

Yours respectfully,
Thomas F. Holgate,

I send you this; but as it is a translation of a standard work, something brief — a Note? — will do.

W. P. G.
