The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Jan. 21 1892[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce:

I hope I shan't be
always apologizing to you, but
I sent off Mendeleef through
a accustomed channel in forgetful-
ness of your application for it,
which I chanced upon a day
or two ago. I haven't got
used to regarding you as
chemist yet.

By way of compensation I
turn over to you rather than to
your rival Tyndall's new book,
which has several "men of
genius" in it & other good matter.
