The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Dec. 1 1903[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Clark had sold his Mätzner
& your chance was lost.

I send you (or shall to-morrow
if this beastly rain stops) a couple
of books by express — on Comte and
on the principles of mathematics. At
this time, very brief notices will
satisfy me.

Your "leader" has attracted
wide attention. The most "British"
Tory female I know, and a woman
of culture & wit, writes me that
