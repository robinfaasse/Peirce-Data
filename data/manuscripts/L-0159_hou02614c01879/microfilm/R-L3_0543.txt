Mr. Horace White,
Editor "The Post,"
206 Broadway, New York, N. Y.

Dear Sir:-
We are sending you by express paid [ins]from here[/ins] today a copy of the New Edition of Webster's International, thinking that you may be glad to examine the new matter and give it fitting review in "The Post." We inclose sheets giving the gist of Dr. Harris's Preface to the Supplement and the Publishers' Statement, as they give a general idea of what has been done, the reasons for doing it, and the principles followed.

While we hesitate to burden competent critics with the opinions of others regarding the dictionary, we cannot but think that you will be interested in the compliment to American scholarship contained in the extract from Dr. Jas. A. H. Murray's "Lecture on the Evolution of English Lexicography." As you doubtless know, Dr. Murray is the editor-in-chief of the many-volumed English Dictionary that has been in preparation for more than twenty years. Such praise from such a source is peculiarly gratifying to the publishers as showing that their ideals have to a certain degree been realized.

Yours truly,
G. & C. Merriam Co.
