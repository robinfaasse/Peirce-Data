The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Sep 14 1902[/date]

My dear Peirce,

I send you a volume of
Annales containing sundry papers
any one of which is fair game
for a [u]Note[/u] (strictly), & call
your attention to that on the
metric system at p. 82.

I believe I warned Mrs. Peirce
that if I ever became her pedes-
trian guest, I must be frankly
tolerated as a Tramp — with the
clothes on my back & maybe
change in my satchel; no style.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. G.
