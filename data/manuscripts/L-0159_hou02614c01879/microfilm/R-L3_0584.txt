My dear Peirce:

It is long since you have given me such a nut to crack as this. That excuse of a "general reader" would account for my publishing what I have extracted, I cannot guess. He may feel flattered if he is defied. He will have to infer that Royce is engaged in logicizing a Supreme Being, for you nowhere give a bird's-eye view of his purpose.
