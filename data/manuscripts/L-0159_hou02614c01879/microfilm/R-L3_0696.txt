May 25, 1905.

Dear Sir:

The enclosed note, prepared by Professor Norton, is mailed to you as a contributor to "The Nation"; and you are requested to sign it, if it meets with your approval, and return it, as soon as convenient, to Prof. J. H. McDaniels, Hobart College, Geneva, New York, (U.S.A.),

It has been suggested that many of the contributors might like to join in presenting to Mr. Garrison some token of their regard, a loving-cup, or other piece of plate. Gifts for this purpose need not, unless the giver desire it, exceed the amount of two dollars. They should be sent to Prof. McDaniels at the earliest convenience. For obvious reasons this matter should be kept strictly private for the present.

CHARLES ELIOT NORTON,
CHARLES FRANCIS ADAMS,
JAMES FORD RHODES,
GOLDWIN SMITH.
