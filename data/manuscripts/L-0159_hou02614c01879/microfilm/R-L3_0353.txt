The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Feb 1,[/date] 1894

My dear Peirce:

Obediently to your telegram, I send you not only Lockyer but a piano-teacher's book on the Great Pyramid. In his own craft Mr Parsons ranks high; but I fancy the Pyramids are too much for him. Perhaps the two books can be bracketed, perhaps not. If there is much folly about either, what Newcomb calls rot, pray be as brief as possible.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. G.
