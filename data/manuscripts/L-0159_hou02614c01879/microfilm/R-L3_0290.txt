The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Oct. 14, 1892[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce,

I need not enumerate
the liberties I have taken
with your text. I cut out
some anti-English remarks, for
I desire to do nothing unneces-
sary to foster antipathy between us
& our British cousins, of which
we have a standing source in our
4th of July. At the end, I forbore
to call Sidgwick a dunce, &
this perhaps leaves the notice
somewhat abrupt; but I could
not make an artificial tail
to it. Apologies & regards.

W. P. G.
