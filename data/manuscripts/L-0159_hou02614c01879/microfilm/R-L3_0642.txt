**The Nation,** 206-210 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]April 22 1903[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I ought to have your Academy proof back at once — i.e. by Wednesday a.m. Two of the topics are "To be continued" of last year; hence I felt obliged to curtail the catalysis.

Schiller can wait, and I think it is in pretty good shape now if I have left no breach in the continuing of my butchering.

I waited for you here to the last moment on Saturday, with all the lights put out and all the guests departed, and still sought you by walking down to the Cortlandt St. ferry, in vain.
