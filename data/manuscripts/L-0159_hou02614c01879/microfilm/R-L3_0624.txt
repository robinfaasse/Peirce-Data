The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Dec. 24 1902[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Had you spoken earlier, I could have served you — oh so well! With one volume I have already anticipated your request for books, and this will perhaps provide for your youngest. For the other two a deus ex machina is clearly needed (our last book sale having come off). Indelicate as it is, I send you two books of my own making — the Darwin a compilation from the Grand Old Man, the Rambles my own notion. I think they will do good, I hope
