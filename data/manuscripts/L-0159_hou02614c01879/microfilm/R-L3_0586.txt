The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]June 18 1902[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I have just deposited in
5th Ave. Bank $27.20 for
the [u]C.S.[/u] or [u]J.P.[/u]; have had
a very pleasant interview with
your wife, & have fixed a pro-
visional date for a little tramp
in your neighborhood.

To-morrow I will try to get
to the books you ask for — or rather
I can say now that nothing is in
hand except Atkinson & the Charades,
which I will send for silence on the
minimum of notice. Muirhead is here,
