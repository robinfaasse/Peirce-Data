New York, [date]Oct. 28 1901[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I find that the Post is supplied by Associated Press with report of Nat. Academy of Sciences, supplemented to a very trifling degree by a cheaply paid correspondent (according to space). If you think you could make a fairly popular report for the Nation, of about a page or so, I would take it, & guarantee you [del]the usual[/del] [ins]your name[/ins]. This would mean a letter in hand if possible on Monday, Nov. 18.
