Llewellyn Park,
Orange, N. J. [date]May 15. 1902[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I had time yesterday only to
set your advertisement in motion. It
will appear on the next two Saturdays.

I have never seen Guff but once, some
years ago at a private reception to [del]R[/del] Ruskin.
I should describe him as a slender & perhaps
tall Scotch gentleman, with full gray beard, &
head not bald, a kindly looking man. He is
not of my acquaintance at all. Nothing
could be done by me family addressing him
on the subject.
