I may have put on the [del]wrong[/del] address.
My mind often works that way. Finally,
the address may have been all right
and our wretched N.Y. P.O. may be
at fault; if so, I despair. I lost
a book to Boston the other day.
They hunted, but to no effect.

Now, [del]que faire[/del] [ins]que faire[/ins]? Will you drop
a line to Port Jervis P.O., and to
Chr. Expr. if perchance the books went
that way? Or shall I assume
what I can't affirm, and row up
our N.Y. P.O.? In peace, I apologize
as if the culprit.

My wife received your [del]three[/del]
Cents, but I preceded her in the
reading of them. They are a revelation
as regards your penological views, which
I find such as Geo. Combe might have
arrived at, barring, perhaps, the phrenology.
