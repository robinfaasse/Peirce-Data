The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794).

New York, [date]April 26. 1890[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I don't relish the duty of expressing my disappointment in your last draft, but I am fortified by Mr Ogden, who has read of the ms, & written down his comments. Is it worth while to try again? (Mr O. is confining to Century series to which you were asked to contribute.)

Yours always.
W. P. G.
