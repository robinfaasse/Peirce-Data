The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794).

New York, [date]Feb. 12 1898[/date]

My dear Peirce:

At last you are the right man in the right place, & I am heartily glad to hear that you are speaking ex cathedra in Cambridge.

Lutoslawski has not yet reached us. His Plato tests & studies were set forth in the Nation by Louis Dyer some months ago, who, however, entered no claim to the forthcoming book. I may have to consider another claim, but you are now the only one on the list of applicants.
