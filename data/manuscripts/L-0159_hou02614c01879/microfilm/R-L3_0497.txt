**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794).

New York, [date]Jan 17, 1894[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I am sorry that the enclosed check for Playfair is my only mode of testifying my sympathy for you in the extraordinary trying circumstances from the midst of which you write to me. I wonder that you can put pen to paper even as a mode of forgetting.

Perhaps already your wife's health will have taken a turn for [del]the[/del] better. I hope so with all my heart.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. G.
