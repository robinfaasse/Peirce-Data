The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Dec. 1. 1900[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Mr. Ogden bids me say that the time is closing when he can avail himself of your emendations to the Centennial article. He does not himself think much change necessary or desirable; but now is the time for it.

ever faithfully
W. P. G.
