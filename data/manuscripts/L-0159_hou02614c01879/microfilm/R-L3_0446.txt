HARVARD COLLEGE LIBRARY

CAMBRIDGE, MASS., [date]Dec. 3 1898[/date]

Dear Sir,

In reply to your enquiry
I beg to say that there is no com-
plete edition of Arnauld in this
library. We have another edition
of the "Logique" (Paris, 1843) ed. by
Jourdain which may be more useful
to you than the other. The only complete
ed. of "Fénelon" here is in 19 Vol. Paris 1810-
12. & contains letters to the Duc de
Chevreuse, but not from him.

The only complete edition of Arnauld
noted in "Brunet" is: in 45 vols. Lausanne
1775-83. The "Logique" is given: first ed. 1662. [ins]Paris[/ins]
2nd 1664, [ins]Paris[/ins] 3rd. 1668, [ins]Paris[/ins] 4th 1674, [ins]Paris[/ins] dernière ed. Amst. 1675.

Mr. C. S. Peirce.

Truly Yours, T. J. Kiernan
