**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794).

New York, [date]Jan. 25 1900[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I am glad you can report
improvement in your wife's health.

A parcel just made up includes
all your books, except the Theism,
which is non inventus. Apparently
the "Kinetic Theory of Gases" was never
noticed by the Nation. It is by
S. H. Burbury, & published by
Macmillan. It is just possible I
sent it out for review, but if so,
to a source from which I habitually
expect a lingering or no return.
Do you want me to order for
you?
