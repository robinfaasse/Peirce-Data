**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794).

New York, [date]April 20 1892[/date]

My dear Peirce:

If, as I trust, you have not destroyed your last draft of Spinoza, returned with Mr Rollo Ogden's comments, please return it here to him — who knows, as I am going East in a day or two. He thinks he can piece it usefully with your final cast, just received.
