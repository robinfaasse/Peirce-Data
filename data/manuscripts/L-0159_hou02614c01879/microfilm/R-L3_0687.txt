**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Oct 25 1904[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I send you

Cajori N-1904-19
Murray N-1904-19
Mendeléeff N-1905-11

— really indifferent to anything
but Notes on any of 'em, unless
the Russian deserves it. Now is the
dragging period for any reviews
having a commercial utility.

It is almost impossible for me
to keep in mind the books already
reviewed, even within 10 years. I
looked to see if we had noticed
Britten, & could not discover.
(He changed his title.)
