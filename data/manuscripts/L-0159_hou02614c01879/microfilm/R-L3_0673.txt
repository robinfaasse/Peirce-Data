**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Mar. 3, 1902[/date]

My dear Peirce,

Your Metric System is here, and I will read it at my first opportunity, hoping to discover some way to use it without abridgment (unless I discover an irrelevant dead-eye in the middle of it!).
