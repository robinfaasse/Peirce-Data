New York, [date]Sept 13. 1904[/date]

My dear Mr. Garrison:

Would it be contrary to your rule to let me know who wrote the review in the Nation of July 28 of Dr. Allbutt's "Notes on the Composition of Scientific Papers"?

I was going to send you a note on "Lord Acton's letters to Mary Gladstone", which I bought in London, but as I see there was a notice of it by Paul Finley in "The Lamp" for August I presume it would be a little late.

Yours truly.
Arnold Tanzer
