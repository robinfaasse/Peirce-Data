My dear Peirce:

I had to cut out the
detail re vaccination, for I
am convinced that our experience
during the civil war is conclusive
as to the small amount of
mischief wrought by the practice.
It was then applied on probably
the largest scale ever known, in our
armies.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. G.
