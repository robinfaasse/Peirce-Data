**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Oct. 11, 1902[/date]

My dear Peirce:

The major surgical operation
turned out minor, or even minimus.
You shall have a proof soon.

The Leslie Stephen has gone from
one or you should have it; and I think
one of the books on linear perspective.
The second [del]is[/del] [ins]has[/ins] sold, but I can't see
[del]more than a Note in it.[/del]

Have I been abusing Poe? Truly
I don't care for him as poet or
man, & rather guess I should save
his prose stories if I were jettisoning
in a storm.

I send you Stout, which offers a
fine opportunity for selective reviewing —
