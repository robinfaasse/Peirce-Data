Dear Mrs. Peirce:

Such was my faith in you that,
had I heard that horn, I should
have taken it for one "from
elf-land blowing;" but I was already
lost in thought. The farewell, like
the entertainment, shall live long for-
getful.

I forgot, in my note yesterday
about the heel-plugs, to say that
they [del]ought not[/del] [ins]ought[/ins] to project more than
3/16 inch beyond the heel.
