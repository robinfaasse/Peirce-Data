The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Aug 20. 1898[/date]

My dear Peirce:

This will square us for what has appeared — is it 4.70?

I'm now plethoric again, as regards copy, & can't let you go at Hodgson. The other books will doubtless turn up for you.

Our first fine day in weeks. The naval parade just over (1.15 p.m.)

Yours faithfully,
W. P. G.
