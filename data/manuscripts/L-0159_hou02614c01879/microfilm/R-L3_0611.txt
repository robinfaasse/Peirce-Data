The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Oct. 3 1902[/date]

My dear Peirce.

I trust you may take kindly to
the two volumes on air navigation I
am sending you, though quite in igno-
rance of the subject. As for me, I
hope to live long enough to see the
dream realized, but [del]we[/del] [ins]who[/ins] knows that when
it is, it will be a good time for all
of us to get out of the world.

This infernal coal strike fills me
with horror at the possible consequences.
If your situation is in a woody region,
I should be distressed for you. I am,
in spite of a pretty good coal pile, pre-
paring to burn some wood.

Yours for freedom & truth.
W. P. G.
