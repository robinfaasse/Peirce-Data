My dear Peirce:

This is further from the mark than the first draught of Leland, & cut it as I might there would be nothing coherent left in it. My indulgence about Diterici is your bane, just as the referendum is the bane of careful law-making. I had a right to expect that you would recall our long estimate of Scott a few months ago, apropos of the new edition of Waverley (from the pen of Goldwin Smith - the notice).
