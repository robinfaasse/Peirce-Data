The man appointed is without any practical experience
in such an office, and without any but the most insigni-
ficant scientific standing. It would be favoritism to give
him as much as 70 on the first two counts. Of course they could
mark his composition as they fancied. Supposing his mark
on that was 100 and 70 on the others, his average would be 82.
