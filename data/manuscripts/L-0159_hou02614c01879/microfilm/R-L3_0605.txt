The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Aug. 23 1902[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I think it possible to push
may use one of your paragraphs,
that on variable stars.

Lack of funds makes it impracticable
now to consider outside editing of
a scientific column. I am well
aware that this is one of the weak
sides of the paper.

I wonder if your thermometer dropped
below 50° this morning, as my sister's
did on the North River, at, say, 450
feet above tide-water?

Yours faithfully,
W. P. G.
