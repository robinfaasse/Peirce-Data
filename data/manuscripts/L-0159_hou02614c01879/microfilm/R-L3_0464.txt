The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794).

New York, [date]April 1. 1891[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I send you a heterogeneous
volume that may prove amusing
reading or browsing to you;
but will also surely contain tempt-
ations of comment. So

"Choose well, your choice is
brief......"

yours always,

W. P. G.
