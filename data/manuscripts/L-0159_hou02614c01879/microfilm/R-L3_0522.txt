New York, [date]May 12. 1890[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I had supposed Ogden had satis-
fied you on the question of acceptance,
as I had supposed I had communi-
cated with you. He has not yet
tackled the job of pricing, but you
are quite right to ask for an advance
on account. I am to-day placing
$75 with the Fifth Ave.

I send you Bottoms.

Yours faithfully
W. P. G.
