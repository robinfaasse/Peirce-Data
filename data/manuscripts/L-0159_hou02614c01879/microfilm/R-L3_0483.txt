New York, [date]Dec. 8 1899[/date]

My dear Peirce:

The amusing thing about this is,
that if you ever penned the above
lines, it was not in connection with
"Through Nature to God"; hence it is
rather sharp practice in the publishers
to couple them with the title.

Your recollection is quite right:
I said nothing to you about the
originality of Fiske's pet theory — I
sent through my brother that J.F.
was anxious to learn your identity.

Here is a proof of Dana. Thanks,
[del]Garrison[/del]

Yours always,
W. P. G.
