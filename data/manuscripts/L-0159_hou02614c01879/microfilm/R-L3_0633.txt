The Nation, 206-210 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Feb 21 1903[/date]

My dear Peirce:

As I have overhauled this
myself somewhat, it being too
long, perhaps you will find it
acceptable as it stands.

For the elect's sake, I hope your
cold days will be shortened. We
seldom experience colder in Jersey.

Yours always,
W. P. G.
