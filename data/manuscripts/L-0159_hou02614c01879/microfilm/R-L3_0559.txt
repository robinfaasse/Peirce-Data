The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Feb. 5. 1901[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I have complied with your
request about the Bank.

Since my return from Chicago,
I have been extremely busy in
catching up, but had my book in
which I could afford space suggested
you, it should have gone to you.
It was intended for the Utilitarians,
& in the end I consented to lend
articles by two different hands. It
was very early bespoken, evaded,
withdrawn, & assigned anew.

Any important work for which you
can "show cause" is yours on demand
if in season, or if there be one
