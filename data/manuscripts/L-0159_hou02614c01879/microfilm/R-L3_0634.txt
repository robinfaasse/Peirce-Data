The Nation, 206-210 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Feb. 24 1903[/date]

My dear Peirce:

There cannot be much of an
advance in this payment, but I
am so desirous that I have sent
you ten to take up your
Baldwin, 4. Meanwhile your
present hardships, I hope the
worst is over, though we shall
need this for three months yet.

Yours always,
W. P. Garrison
