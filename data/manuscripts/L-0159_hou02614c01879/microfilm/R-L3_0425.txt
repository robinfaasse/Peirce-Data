The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Oct. 15 1897[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Your wife brings me, in her face & in her words, the most welcome news of the great physical change which surgical science has wrought in her. She also gives me good tidings of yourself & your joint prospect of a return to Milford.
