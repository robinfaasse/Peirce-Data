The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, Oct. 16, 1895

My dear Peirce:

I have been absent for 10 days.

Herewith I send three books, of which the biggest may prove the least valuable. I should prefer notices of moderate length.

Schröder's Logik is of course unknown to me, and comes at a bad time to compete with holiday English books. Here again a summary notice is desirable for your sake and mine.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. Garrison
