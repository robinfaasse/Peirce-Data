The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]April 6 1901[/date]

My dear Peirce,

I am loth to renew my old role of deus ex machina, not because I doubt getting a greater return than the proposed advance from so many books, but because the relation is a false one. However, I have deposited $50 as indicated.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. Garrison
