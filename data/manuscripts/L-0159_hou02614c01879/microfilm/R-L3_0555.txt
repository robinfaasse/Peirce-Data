The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Dec. 17 1900[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Our last book-order has been rec'd,
& I can only send you some clippings
to which your wife is welcome.

You had better borrow my Wilson,
& mark it to be returned some day.

Don't go far afield with the
Weber, but keep your eye on the
Supplement as the chief novelty.

Thanks for Wallace.

Yours always:
W. P. G.
