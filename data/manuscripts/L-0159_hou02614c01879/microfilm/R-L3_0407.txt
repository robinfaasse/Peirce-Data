The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]July 5. 1895[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I would have spared you
the trouble of revising your notice
of Huxley, if I could have
foreseen an editorial article
in the Post, but it is hopeless
to try to bring your views of science
into line with my own at
all points.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. Garrison
