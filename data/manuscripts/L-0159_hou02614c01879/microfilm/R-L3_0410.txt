The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Dec. 22 1895[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I have deposited $7.00 to your credit, for the Stanley, at the 5th Ave. National.

Your instinct about curtailing was right. My audience would not have comprehended the introductory discussion before you attack the book itself.

Sincerely yours,
W. P. G.
