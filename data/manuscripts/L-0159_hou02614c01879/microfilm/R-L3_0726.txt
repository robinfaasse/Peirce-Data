Nov 27 1901

C. S. PIERCE ESQ
Milford Penna.

Dear Sir:

I observed in the New York Nation a review of the pro-ceedings [ins]of[/ins] the recent meeting of the American Academy, and notice that you read a paper upon The Logic of Drawing Historical Conclusions.

I am indebted for your address to my friend, Vice Provost Edgar F. Smith, of the University, who tells me that he thinks your paper has not been published. If it is published I should be very much obliged to you if you will let me know how I can obtain a copy, as I think I would be interested in it.

Yours very truly
John M Gest
