The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Jan. 4, 1894[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I shall deposit to-morrow $50 on a/c of your Helmholtz, with which I quarrel very little except where the materialistic bee gets into your bonnet — or gets out of it. Lass sie ruhn, die Todten! I have mentioned his appointment to be president & director of the Physikalisch-Technische Reichsanstalt — I fancy within the past 3 years.

Again a happy New Year to you!

Faithfully yours,
W. P. Garrison
