Office of "The Nation," VB3b #2
No. 5 Beekman Street. (P. O. Box 6732.)

New York, [date]Jan. 10 1872[/date]

Dear Sir:

I send you a couple of books which you can perhaps notice for us briefly.

Referring to your note of Dec. 8, let me express again my desire to promote the popularization of the Coast Survey maps like that of Newport. I hope you haven't been reproaching [ins]me[/ins] for not noticing the first sheet, & waiting for the second to appear.

Very truly yours,
W. P. Garrison
