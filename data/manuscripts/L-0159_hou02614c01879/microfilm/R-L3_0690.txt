My dear Peirce:

This is agreeable reading, but it has for me the fatal defect of not giving me the faintest illumination about the nature of the Phase Rule. A Phase I know (in one sense, at least), and a Rule I know; but a phase rule passes me. You ought to define Phase near the beginning.

Yours,

The Man in the Street.
