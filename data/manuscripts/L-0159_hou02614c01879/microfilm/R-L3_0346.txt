My dear Peirce:

Forgive me if I wrote anything irritating in my last. You can hardly appreciate the extra strain on the nerves imposed by correction of what is in the main or partly acceptable.

It is easy to say, "Draw your pencil through this or through that - there is matter enough to hold out"; but this may be fatal to structure, and sometimes it may be impossible as to substance also. Your second notice
