The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Oct 21. 1877[/date]

My dear Peirce:

As often happens, your
urgent letter, reached me at
my pinching time as regards
excess of occupation. However,
receipt to express my deep
regret at your wife's misfortune,
of which I had heard nothing. On
referring to our "forward," I
find a notice of Stokes already
prepared in your own hand.
I regret that more books in
your line do not present them-
