The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Dec. 17 1902[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I enclose a check for your
Sundials, & am sending you a
book as a venture for one of
Peirce protégés.

If our late storm covered you,
it must have washed nearly you
snow into the Hudson.

Yours always,
W. P. G.
