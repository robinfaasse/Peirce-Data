The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Sept. 2 1902[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Your [del]P[/del] paper is your master-
piece. It led me to turn back
to good old Dr. Hagen's familiar
account of Kant in No. 551 of
the Nation, of which I am
sending you a copy. From it I
drew the item (on the margin)
about his never having visited any
other city than that in which he was
born.

Yours always,
W. P. G.
