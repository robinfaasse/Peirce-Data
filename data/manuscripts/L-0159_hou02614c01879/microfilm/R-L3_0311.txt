The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Sept 9 1892[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I am very grateful for your telegram with its kind offer of help, but Mr Ogden is my locum tenens when I am disabled, & since my wife has rallied I have been able to get to the office & keep up with the correspondence & proofs. She is still very weak & cannot sit up & be dressed, but is otherwise fairly comfortable. Perhaps another week must elapse before we can determine her fate.

I refer,
