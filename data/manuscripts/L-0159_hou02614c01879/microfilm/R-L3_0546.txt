The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Dec. 19. 1900[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I will assume $20 is the
current value of the reading
of "Chalon" &c., entailed upon you,
& am depositing that sum in the
Second National, to your wife's
credit.

Perhaps you can answer this
query re Dr. Billings's (of the Astor
Library). If not, no matter.

I have not found time to thank
you for your sympathizing letters
in my distress. They were appreciated
& accomplished their end. The wound was
very deep.

Faithfully Yours,
W. P. Garrison
