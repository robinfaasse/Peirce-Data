The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Aug. 6. 1898[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I am sorry indeed that your season has turned out so poorly, but hope that the remaining two months of hot weather may do something for you.

Enclosed is a check for your matter in type, & I will try to work off the rest as soon as I can.

Faithfully yours,

W. P. G.

I send you Carveth Read.
