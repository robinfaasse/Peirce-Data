The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Jan. 25 1896[/date]

My dear Peirce:

When your wife brought
me your MS. I did not
observe the letter accompanying
it. I now enclose check
for the batch.

Books in your vein seem
wanting. I am so stuffed
I grudge the German pamphlet
notes you propose.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. G.
