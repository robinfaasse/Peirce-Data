Great Successor,
I beg, and this is the purpose of my letter, to
call your attention to two or three points,
for I am desirous of winning your good scientific
as well as literary opinion.

My basic principle, which cannot be contro-
verted, is that all concrete systems originated from
an originally diffused, and equally diffused, but
enormously attenuated medium composed of the vapors
of all substances which, afterwards combined, resulted
in the visible universe. The nebulae are our pre-
cessors, and will be succeeded by others. The uniform-
ity of light proves that this medium is neither
expanding, contracting, dispersing, or condensing,
and yet it is in molecular motion and under
tension. Hence we know that space is in-
finite and is a plenum, not a vacuum.
