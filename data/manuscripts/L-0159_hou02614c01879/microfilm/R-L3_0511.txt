The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794).

New York, [date]April 2 1890[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I am sorry enough that sickness still reigns in your home.

Your paper on Spelling commands my sympathy so far as your ambition goes; but, if I may say so, I think the treatment too ponderous for a magazine — probably too lengthy also. I could not undertake to blue-pencil it. Try on approaching the subject from the side of the print-ing-office which must be persuaded that a fluctuating phonetic system is impossible.
