The Nation, 206 to 210 Broadway (P. O. Box 794), New York.

[date]July 22. 1894[/date]

My dear Peirce:

On general principles I should
decline to print this as being a review
of our review. Still, I think you ought
to see it. The comment, if any, should
be very brief.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. Garrison
