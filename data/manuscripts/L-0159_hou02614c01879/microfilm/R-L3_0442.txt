The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Oct. 28. 1898[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I am sorry enough to learn of your late illness, & with some hesitation comply with your wife's request to tax your brain once more. Herewith I mail you Darwin's "Tides", an important book, which, by. Newcomb resigns in your favor if you feel equal to undertaking it.

With best wishes,

yours faithfully,
W. P. G.
