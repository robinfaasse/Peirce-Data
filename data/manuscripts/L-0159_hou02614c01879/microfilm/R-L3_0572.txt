**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Oct. 29, 1901[/date]

My dear Peirce:

In my letter yesterday, I did not say, what I have felt, that there was something fishy about Holden. I now enclose proofs of this, just come to hand. I should add that he wrote a reclamation about our notice, but it was defective in tone & not otherwise suitable to publish.

You may destroy these documents.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. G.
