**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Jan. 8 1904[/date]

My dear Peirce:

With the [del]mass[/del] [ins]run[/ins] of books
sent you of late, you must
not be thought to be ready for
Dewey, which was shelved, not
thrown away, & has now gone to
you.

I wish you would pick
out Newcomb's Memoirs for first
treatment. You must have got it,
I think, in October last.

Thanks & reciprocation for
the New Year's wishes.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. Garrison
