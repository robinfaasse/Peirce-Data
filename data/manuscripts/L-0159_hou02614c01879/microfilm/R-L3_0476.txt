The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Sept. 16. 1891[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I will gladly credit you with the stamps.

If I do not send you more books, it is because none needing your "signature" have come to hand. The first interest has been along quite other lines.

The poor books you have may be neglected, unless a summary judgment of a few lines in the Notes will serve.

Yours always, W. P. G.
