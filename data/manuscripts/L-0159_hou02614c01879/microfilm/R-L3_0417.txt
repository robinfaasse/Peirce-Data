The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Feb. 18 1896[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I am sorry to hear you have been ill. From what your wife told me, I feared you were overworking yourself.

Conant is on its way to you, but do not make your notice too long. I am overwhelmed.

The advertisement shall go in. You do not say how many times.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. G.
