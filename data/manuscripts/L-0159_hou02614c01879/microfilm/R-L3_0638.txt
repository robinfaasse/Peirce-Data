**The Nation,** 206-210 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Mar. 16 1903[/date]

My dear Peirce:

The case is not so bad
after all. Your confession shows that
you were competent to notice, &
that the Nation still only com-
petent for its reviewing.

Your report of the Academy
last year was of a kind to
justify a repetition. Let me hear
from you again by April 1,
if skies are brighter & think I
can arrange with you.

Deep regrets for your wife's ill
health & trust it improves.

Heartily yours,
W. P. G.
