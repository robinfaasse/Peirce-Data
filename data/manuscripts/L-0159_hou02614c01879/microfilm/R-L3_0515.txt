The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794).

New York, [date]April 17 1890[/date]

My dear Peirce,

I send you Höffding & Lockyer, with a prayer for brevity in each case; and I throw in (since you seemed to desire it) the Clark University volume already noticed, [del]by[/del] all 3 of them.

Your new draught is a much better return for the books than the Note, & yet I meant nothing more than that you should expand the Note a little. However, I will use the longer notice.

Your Frommeleste is at last in the press.

Very truly,
W. P. G.
