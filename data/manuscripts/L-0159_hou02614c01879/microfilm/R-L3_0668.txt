**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Feb. 1. 1904[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Your depressing letter
greets me on my return from
burying my eldest brother in
Boston. To-morrow I will look over
what I have in hand & if I
can make as large a deposit as
I can, & let you know.

With much sympathy,

Yours,
W. P. G.
