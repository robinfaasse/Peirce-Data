Dear Garrison:

I have been studying that "Alalah". I am
thinking of writing a lecture about it, because one
must hear the proper readings in order to under-
stand its relation to Poe's other things.

The screed I sent you was poor enough. Still,
it would be useful; and if you can lay hands on
it, I wish you would send it back.

by Poe; and facts being so damnably assertive as

what the [del]fact[/del] [ins]evidence[/ins] really is ought to be most cau-

the evidence is, I have to go by what you say.

But when you tell me, — and in the
very same breath, — and with such nonchalance,
— that "there is nothing in it" I can only
say — Great Garrison nods. Were it by Poe, then
indeed one might say there was not much in it.
