The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Jan. 13, 1894[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I send you Vol. I
(one-half) of Funk & Co.'s
Dictionary, which is a much
smaller affair than I had
anticipated. As an etymological
authority it is worthless. In what
you say of it, remember that
we have had trouble with the
publishers, & must not show
temper or bias. I think 1 1/2 pp.
of Nation should suffice for
your notice.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. G.
