The only illustration of Dr. Hill's "incompetence" to write
upon philosophy given by his critic shows that this censorious
judge has wholly misapprehended or perverted the statements of the
author. Dr. Hill is cautioning the reader [u]against supposing that[/u],
because chemical elements have been discovered whose probable ex-
istence was indicated by Mendelejeff's periodic law of the ele-
ments, Mendelejeff's law must be correct; for Bode's law led to
investigations regarding an unknown planet, subsequently found by
Dr. Galle, and yet the position of this planet so far as the calcu-
lations are concerned might have been one hundred and eighty de-
grees from the place where Neptune was found. Bode's law served
to suggest, but has been proved to be erroneous. So, argues Dr.
Hill, Mendelejeff's law may not be true, although elements have
been discovered possessing qualities which according to this law
they should have. And after all this, your critic proceeds to
emphasise the fact that Bode's law has been proved erroneous, as
if this had been denied!

A LOVER OF JUSTICE.
