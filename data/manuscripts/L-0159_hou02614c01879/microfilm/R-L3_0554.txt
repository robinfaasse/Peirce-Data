My dear Peirce:

I now answer your inquiry
for our copy of Webster, & enclose
[del]L[/del] Murray's compliment, which [del]per-[/del]
haps is modified by the new Supplement;
having compared by the recent parts of
the Oxford Dictionary and of the Century.
They have not proved by comparison,
& probably you are not in a position
to do so. Don't make the article too
long.

Yours faithfully
W. P. G.
