The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]March 31, 1892[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I can take one letter on the
Academy. My space is too limited now
to consider the others you suggest.

A trip to Chicago to see my
daughter's latest grandchild was
on the cards for tomorrow, but I
have been called [del]by[/del] [ins]to[/ins] see my
movements are rendered uncertain
by my grandchildren in Florida. I doubt
if I shall be free to stir from
home. It will seem hard if I may
not meet your wife again except
in your home; but illness makes it
seem that. My thanks & regards to
her.

Sincerely yours,
W. P. Garrison
