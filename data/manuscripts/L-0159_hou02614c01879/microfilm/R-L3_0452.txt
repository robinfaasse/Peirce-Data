The real question is whether he has produced any conception
which, if it had been clear to the Leibnizians, would have enabled
them to see what it required the efforts of Helmholtz, Clausius,
Thomson, and others to make plain that resistance is due to the
action of energy. My remark that there is no such short cut
as Holman imagines is perfectly correct. He is trying to conjure
facts of nature out of definitions of words. He defines energy
as that which produces change of motion, and then because
resistances produce changes of motion, syllogistically infers that
resistance is due to the action of energy. But syllogism is not
"sufficient evidence" or any evidence; it is mere word-conjuring.
The only evidence lies in the fact that heat is produced, and that
that heat is proportional to the loss of mechanical energy.
