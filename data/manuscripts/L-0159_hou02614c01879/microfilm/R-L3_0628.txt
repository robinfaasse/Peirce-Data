The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Jan. 12 1893[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I send you herewith ten
dollars on account of Personal
Idealism. I see you have fallen
foul of my warm friend Schiller,
a constant contributor to the Nation,
now of Corpus Christi. I must
modify somewhat here.

I'm glad you liked my little
Parables from any point of view.
The has sold out barely one edition,
& I doubt if it will go to press
again. Yours always
W. P. G.
