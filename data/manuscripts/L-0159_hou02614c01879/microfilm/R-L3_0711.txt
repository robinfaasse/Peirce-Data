To the Editor of the New York Evening Post

[u]Sir[/u]: —

The last part of Mrs. Frances Hoadley's little tractate seems to be excellent. It seems certainly to be worth while to take pains to use words in the precise senses in which they will be understood to bear, and this can in some measure be ascertained by attending to phrases and passages generally familiar in which these words occur.

But in the matter of pronunciation, Mrs. Hoadley seems to assume that there is some authoritatively correct standard. Is that so?
