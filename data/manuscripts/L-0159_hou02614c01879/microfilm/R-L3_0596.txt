Llewellyn Park,
Orange, N. J.
[date]July 27, 1902[/date]

Dear Mrs. Peirce:

It was an added kindness of
yours not to rise this morning to speed
me on my journey, and it was very
that your husband would not forego
the formality of host and parting guest.
I was just an hour in reaching the
Delaware, and ten minutes more brought
me to the Erie depot. The ride was
altogether pleasant. I got off at
Paterson, and was three hours in reaching
my home afoot, in ample season for
dinner. My children were the confi-
dents of my punctuality that they
walked a mile to meet me.

My thanks for your hospitality.
