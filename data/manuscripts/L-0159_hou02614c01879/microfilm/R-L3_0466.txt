The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794).

New York, [date]April 10. 1899[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Our sporting editor shows me a little manual which declares it impossible to describe the laying out of links, & recommends professional assistance. He has an employer who understood the thing — and counsel for the protest perhaps. No two courses are alike — and that is really the zest of the game when going to a new links. Is there none near you which you could inspect?

Yours truly
W. P. G.
