The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Oct. 10. 1893[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Your swollen hand had a
bad look, & your diagnosis of
it has a bad sound, — worse, I
hope, than the reality will prove.

All I can send you to-day
is Werner Siemens's Personal
Recollections. (I met the old
gentleman in 1884.) They are
perhaps not exactly light reading,
but they are not like Todhunter,
which I resigned to you yesterday.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. G.
