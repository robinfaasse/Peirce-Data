There has been no other change
in our arrangements, as you allege,
— it is not becoming — or modest,
if you prefer — to stigmatize
my editorial judgment as a whim.
I have always paid you promptly
and generously, on the assumption
that you were giving me not
waste paper but Nation matter
in my judgment. No other arrange-
ment would have been possible.

The result is, that your debit
is $400, & your credit as at 
present is incalculable sum, which I
have not read. as the sheets on
which I have confidingly advanced
you money. I submit that if there
is to be any concern of mind or
any coolness, it should be on
my part rather than yours.
I notified you long ago that you
were leaving me (i.e., the Nation)
too heavily; this fact ought to have
prevented you taking — or giving —
offence.
