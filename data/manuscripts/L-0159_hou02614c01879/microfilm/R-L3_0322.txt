The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Oct 2 1893[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I am dismissing Abrantès in ten lines, which is all the lady is worth at this late day.

Baker's Argumentation, if we ever had it, & Samuel's Lockianism certainly, have passed out of my hands.

Funk & Wagnall's Dict'y is probably hung up. When the financial stringency began, the corps was either broken up or greatly reduced, & I hear no more of it.

You did notice Love in No 1480.

The other books I'll procure of and for you.

Yours,
W. P. G.
