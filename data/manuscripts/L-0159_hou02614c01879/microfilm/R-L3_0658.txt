THE REEF,
NEWPORT, R. I. [date]Oct. 10, 1903[/date]

Dear Mr. Garrison.

We landed
two days ago, &
came here at
once. My general
address in this
country will
be c/o Messrs.

[marginalia]
Thanks to the writer
of the article on British
& American science in
yr. issue of Oct. 1. One
seldom gets anything
so good outside
the columns of
the Nation. I think
by the way that are
those alive & very
active who soon will
soon make it impossible
for any one to say that
mathematics is
dead in England
[/marginalia]
