The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Dec. 20. 1900[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Here is Wallace transmitted with Bank paper.

In another envelope I send you some observations of mine on French, German & Italian punctuation. These you need not return, but some day I should like the Wilson back.

Yours,
W. P. G.
