My dear Peirce:

I send you the only book on
your list (Webb) not already
noticed or in hand for review
or (in one case) lost sight of.
I have forborne to write about
the Almanack because our pub-
lisher was going to inquire whether
other publishers made any profit out
of such advertisements of their
papers. This I can say - though
you must not take it as an
indication of intention to labor
over a project - that the scheme
to get ads of an almanac maker.

I enclose a letter from Dr.
Carus, which will amuse you.

Newcomb will read the proofs
of your Lockyer, which I shall
now begin to put into type.
