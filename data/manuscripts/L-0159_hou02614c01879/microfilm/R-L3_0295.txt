**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Jan. 14 1893[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce:

I thought my half-line from Tacitus, on the proof of your Sentences, would have drawn you out of your shell or from under the Delaware ice-sheet, or wherever you lodge just now in [del]cosmite[/del] [ins]cosmite[/ins]. How can I print your notice without your approval, & when are you going to descend on N.Y. for the winter? Or are you already here?

Yours interrogatively,
W. P. Garrison
