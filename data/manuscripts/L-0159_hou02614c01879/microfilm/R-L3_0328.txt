Your credits, meantime, for Nos.
1469-1482, have been $126, and

As regards more books, I should
of course send you those of your mis-
cellaneous kind if I had not
fear a misadventure like that with
Leland. (Your revised notice, though
readable, has somewhat the air of
penance, but it is, for Leland,
better than a warmer notice would
have been, in my opinion. It is now
in the press.) However, I am now
forwarding a parcel which will
show the amount of risk I itself
incur. It contains a collection
volume of those legs & the Letters
of Walter Scott. An entertaining
suggestive notice can be made
out of either, with a due observance
of [u]dicenda tacenda[/u]. I hope you
will justify my boldness.
