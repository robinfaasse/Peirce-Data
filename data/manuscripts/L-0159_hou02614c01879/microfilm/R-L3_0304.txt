The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]July 27 1893[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I accept your insertions
without further alteration. It seems
odd to have you, [ins]L[/ins] Royce [ins]&[/ins] W.
James writing at the same time
for the Nation on kindred themes.

There is still a complete dearth
of new books available for you
— hardly any for any body. As for
general articles, they would be
welcome, but I have no suggestion
of a topic.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. G.
