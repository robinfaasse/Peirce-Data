**The Nation,** 206-210 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Oct. 14, 1903[/date]

My dear Peirce:

As delays are dangerous
in mailing letters to the flooded
districts, I shall hold this open till
I can enclose proof of a rejoinder
to your article on Brit. & American
Science, as I do B. Berenson's approval.
If it seems desirable to add an editorial
note to the Cornell letter, be free to
do so, but calculate on sending a
comment to reach me by Tuesday [ins]noon[/ins]
next at latest.

I hope you have had no distress.
By experience, tho' I can imagine the
raging Delaware almost up to your
front door. Sincerely friend of yours
