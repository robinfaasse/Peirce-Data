58, University Street.
[date]2d. April 1901[/date].

Dear Mr. Garrison,

Many thanks, not only, for the three books dealing with French life and literature, but for the very kind attention of Linacre. My English-speaking and Italian friends in Palermo have written pleasantly about my little paper; and as they saw it in copies of [u]The Nation[/u], I trust their good judgment will lead them to take that most excellent periodical.

Notices of the books will follow.
