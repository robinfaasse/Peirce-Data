**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794).

New York, [date]April 7 1891[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Your advt shall be attended
to; & your subscription.

A parcel of books is on the
way from Port Jervis. All the mathematical
are for your archives, rather than
for notice, unless the Kepler calls
for a descriptive note. I don't
know what you will make of Cro-
zier on your friendship book. The
briefer the better in, either case.

Faithfully yours,
W. P. G.
