New York, [date]July 31 1902[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I send you by this mail the promised rubber heel plugs. If they seem unsuitable to your wife's shoes, I dedicate them to you, & have another pair in reserve if you will like them also.

By Monday you will probably receive (also by mail) the electric flash lantern I spoke of. Press the ring on the metal arms, & you will have the connection.

Your wife flatters my youthful appearance, but if she will put [ins]up[/ins] with the photo. of 1894, I am content to be [del]thought[/del] [ins]thought[/ins] younger-looking.
