My dear Mr. Garrison:

Many, many thanks for the lovely photo! Of all our numerous [del]visits[/del] [ins]visits received[/ins], past, present, and (I feel sure) future, there will have been none to make us so thoroughly happy, except for its short duration, as this last. I could not at first realize that you were not still here. Alas, my experience of life is that good things come and go like dreams.

I will now confess that I was not quite so obedient as you credit me with being, for I could not resist the temptation to run to the front balcony wrapped up Indian fashion to send you a parting horn-blast and towel handshake. But you were away before I could get there. Now I have patiently bide the time, hoping that soon you may be able to come again, when your dear family and your business can spare you for a little. We look eagerly forward to that long tramp to the Water Gap in your companionship. Very hopefully Juliette Peirce
