The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Oct. 8. 1898[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I approve of your decision about your mathematical critic — an incommensurable topic for my brains, truly. Also, I'm glad you'll do Darwin. The book seemed fitted to your hand.

I send you the Marco Polo for children I had in mind when I put my query to you. It note is all I ask.

Yours, for your speedy recovery,
W. P. G.
