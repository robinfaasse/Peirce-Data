The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Nov. 26 1902[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I am much too hurried
to write today, but I enclose
a check on account of what
I have in hand from you. I
send another book, & if you can
touch it lightly & [u]briefly[/u], so
much the better chance of printing
before Christmas. It is on gastronomy.

In great haste,

W. P. G.
