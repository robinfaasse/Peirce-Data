argument clear to you. The conservation of energy became
the prevalent doctrine about 1850. Though the word [u]energy[/u] was not
then in use (Young had applied it to living force), yet it is convenient
to speak as if it were, since the idea of it is clearly in Helmholtz's memoir.

energy; but those who followed Leibniz (e.g. B. Peirce) [del]plealt[/del] [ins]dealt[/ins]

was that they held the principle did not apply to resistances.

into heat. It was one of the greatest steps human science has ever
