To The Editor of the Nation:

My dear Sir:

The Clark Univ. authorities are very anxious that I shall review their Decennial Volume for the [u]Nation[/u]. They know, of course, that I am in sympathy with their general aims; and my association with Stanley Hall on the [u]Journal of Psychology[/u] has brought me into intimate relations with the staff. Just how or why they came to the idea that I could review for the [u]Nation[/u] at all, I can't guess. However, I write to you to express my willingness to undertake the review, should you see fit to entrust it to me.

Yrs sincerely,
[u]EBTitchener[/u].
