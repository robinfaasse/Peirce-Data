New York, [date]Feb. 9 1899[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I send you Cajori.

My little theological tract is the
publisher's venture, & has fallen flat
with οἱ πολλοί as I expected. Nature
has a few good-natured words about
it.

The Boston Courier was a Bourbon
paper. As the advocates of national
reform we have had to be perpetually
in opposition, and as strict construction-
ists we must hold the nation to its
fundamental pledges & ideals. Time
will show if this persistence is Bourbonish.
