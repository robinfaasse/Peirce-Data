The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794).

New York, [date]Jan. 10 1890[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Your tale of political hugger-
mugger is a sorry one, but there
is no appeal to the immoral
head of the present Administration.

I could not give you vols. 1, 2
of Chonel without writing over to
the other side, & I must avoid
inviting too much attention from
the [ins]Custom-house[/ins] Continent. The technical
words cannot obscure the general
meaning. Yours hastily

W. P. G.
