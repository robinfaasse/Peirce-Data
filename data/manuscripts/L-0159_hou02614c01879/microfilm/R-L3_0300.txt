The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]July 17 1893[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Your readiness to write co-
incides with an alarming bareness
of my pigeon-holes. I send you
accordingly a heterogeneous lot
of books — all that I can lay
my hands on — with power of
life & death over them. Some
may suggest a note, others
silence, others a long or longish
review.

Speaking of lectures, have you
ever lectured before the Peabody
