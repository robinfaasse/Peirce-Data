The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]My 11 1905[/date]

My dear Peirce:

This I call an ideal notice, got out on which Vases to ignorant Editors all: make rest.

Your "Lallie" is here, & I will take it up as soon as I can.

Pray look at this marked astronomical passage in the proof of Shorthouse, & if it be right say nothing & I will return.

In haste. W. P. G.
