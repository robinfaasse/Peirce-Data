The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794).

New York, [date]Sept. 4. 1890[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I will look out for the
two books you name for the "Nation",
and give you a fat one on
Acetylene, which I think you
bespoke sometime ago. But be
brief if you want to get printed,
for the crush is on.

Yours always,
W. P. G.
