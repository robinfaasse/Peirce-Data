The Editor of the Nation,

Dear Sir:- Anonymous criticism has, no doubt, certain advantag-
es, but it sometimes misses its force through the doubt awakened
in the reader's mind regarding the critic's impartiality. Polit-
ical, religious, and philosophical questions which create lines of
cleavage among men often leave them in battle array rather than in
a judicial frame of mind. The follower of Herbert Spencer is not
expected to show much quarter to a full blown Hegelian, [ins]or vice versa,[/ins] and if the
public could know the position of the critic what might be styled
the "controversial" critique might be more justly estimated.
