The Nation, 206-210 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]July 8. 1903[/date]

My dear Peirce:

If I could have responded
favorably to your request for more
work, you would have heard from me
at once. But I have been and
still am engaged in clearing off
notices, & have been exceptionally
busy. The best service I could
render was to get into type
your notices on hand, & place
you on a paying basis once more
on my books.

All debts now wiped out, &
