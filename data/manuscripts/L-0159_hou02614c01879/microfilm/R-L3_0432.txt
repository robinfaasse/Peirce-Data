The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]July 8 1898[/date]

My dear Peirce:

By giving you another
rendezvous on Wednesday, I caused
you to forget your bag, and
yesterday I did not come to
the office. I now repair my
fault by expressing the bag
to you in the old way (Post
Lewis, Wells, Fargo), which I
forgot to use in sending you the
parcel of books.

With apologies,
Yours,
W. P. G.
