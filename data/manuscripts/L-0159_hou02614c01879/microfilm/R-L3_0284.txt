**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Sept. 3 1892[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce,

I have sent you three books more, hoping you may like to own them, & desiring only brief notices — in the case of Kant, of the translation & not of the original work. They go by express to Port Jervis.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. G.
