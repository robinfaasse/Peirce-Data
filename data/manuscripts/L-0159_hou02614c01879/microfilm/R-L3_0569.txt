**The Nation,** 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Oct. 16 1901[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I send you by this mail a
box of books, in one, Conybeare's, connected
with a former volume which I think
you noticed. All will be thankfully
noticed if curtly. "Notes" will satisfy
me for any and all, as to crush
mind is now on.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. Garrison
