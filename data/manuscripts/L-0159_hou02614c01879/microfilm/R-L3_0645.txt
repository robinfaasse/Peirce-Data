My dear Peirce,

Your chemical notes are
models, but don't waste yourself
on Welby or the Soul-Body man.
The latter I glanced over without
deeming it of much account. The
former [del]do[/del] [ins]do[/ins] bear out in general

You should hear my daily groan
over my congested pigeon-holes. If
there is [del]no[/del] [ins]no[/ins] present for the notices
you propose, there is certainly a
future. My floor-tide is now too
constant.

Yours always,
