The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Mar. 27 1897[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I dare say you may
have surmised that you were
criticising Newcomb (in the
notice of Berry). By this I
do not mean that your comments
are not sound. They belong to
a world of knowledge which
I can only admire from the out-
side.

Yours faithfully,
W. P. Garrison
