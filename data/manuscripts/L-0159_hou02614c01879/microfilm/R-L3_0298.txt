The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]July 1 1872[/date]

My dear Peirce,

I was sorry to miss your
call at the office (having taken
my turn to go West), & the ex-
pectation that you might shortly
call again has delayed my
answering your note of June 11.

For that part of it conveying
a kind desire that it were
practicable for you to have my
wife as your guest, I will
say at once that her condition
is such that she cannot well
