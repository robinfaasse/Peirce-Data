The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]June 29 1891[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I mail you the reissue of De Morgan, lest I can take of a Note about it; also a curious book on Old Clocks, of which you can say something brief, perhaps.

The heat, at last, has gone refreshing as we have.

Yours faithfully
W. P. Garrison
