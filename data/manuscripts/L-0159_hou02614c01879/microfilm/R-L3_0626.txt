The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Dec. 30 1902[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I send you three of the books on your list. The rest are gone beyond repair. I was diverted from thinking of you for the Aristotle by the review in tomorrow's paper. And then, I only wanted a few lines about the merits of the translation, not about the stuff itself. But had you come first, you would have been served first.

You received, I trust, the Darwin & the Parables for your boys.

I wish you a New Year —
