The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Mar. 15. 1904[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I am sending you Poes in
Nations as requested.

I shall finish your Metric System
to-day, & am going [del]to have a copy[/del]
[ins]sent[/ins] to every member of Congress.
If you still wish half-a-dozen copies,
you shall have them.

There is [del]no[/del] obstacle to your reprint-
ing to-day. My recollection is,
that I advanced you $50 on account,
As the time approaches, let me know
if that is right.

Very glad to hear of your wife's
improvement.

Yours always.
W. P. G.
