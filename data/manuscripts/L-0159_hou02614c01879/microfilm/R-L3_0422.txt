My dear Garrison:

I suppose I shall follow your advice and go across the water and from there address my proposals to American newspapers; although if I can get any engagements before I go, I shall do so. What I want to know is this. Looking to a clientele of say 10 to 15 newspapers, and guaranteeing not to take more, and no two of them within 50 miles of one another, would $12 for a weekly column go? I want to set the price somewhat high, because my expenses will be very large, — travel, entertainments, clerk, etc., and also because the character of the matter will be altogether exceptional. Is $12 low enough? Is it high enough? If I were to confine my reports to France, 25000 fr a year would be enough. But I intend to go to all the laboratories in Europe; and it will certainly cost me 30000 francs. Thus, unless I have an average of over ten newspapers, I shall lose money. Shall I find a dozen newspapers which will pay $620 a year each for a weekly column which is not addressed to one paper but to a possible 15?

Yours very truly
C. S. Peirce
