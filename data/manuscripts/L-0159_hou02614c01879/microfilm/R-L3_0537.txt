The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Sept. 10. 1890[/date].

My dear Peirce:

I am depositing $10 on account of Acetylene.

Sympathizing with you in all your distresses, I can at least congratulate you on not having typhoid fever like my oldest son — and away from home, while visit-ing a friend. But it is a light attack, & the crisis is past.

Yours constantly
W. P. G.
