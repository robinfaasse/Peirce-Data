The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]July 17, 1902[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Moré meo, I am gradually
dragging myself to the brink of
separation from this office for the
better part of next week. If all
goes well, I shall reach Branchville,
N. J., some time in the forenoon of
Wednesday next, & walk straight to
Dingman's Ferry. That will be 8
miles, & I think no more than
10 intervene between you & D. F.
So I ought to come upon you early
by six P.M. How long I shall stay,
will depend on circumstances beyond your
