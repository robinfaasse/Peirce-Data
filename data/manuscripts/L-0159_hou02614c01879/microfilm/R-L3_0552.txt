The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 749),

New York, [date]Dec. 6. 1900[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I could not wait for your
proof of Agon, but your corrections
shall be made for use in the
Post.

You do not reflect that, keeping
books out of sight, you cannot under-
stand my withdrawing them altogether
from review. Gray's Electricity seemed
to me not for us. Sellers I did
recall, but it is not in sight, & pro-
bably was too technical for us. Wallace
I send you, with some misgivings -
but I should have misgivings in the
case of any other reviewer. The book is
a collection of essays, & more must
be the man whose knowledge [fig]line pointing down[/fig]
