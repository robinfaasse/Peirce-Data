The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Feb. 21. 1899[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I did not mean to trouble you so much — or at all; but of the medium of laying Holman's complaint before you. I wrote him that if we had done him wrong, it will react upon our reputation with the scientists he names as his endorsers.

My Hittite reviewer is Prof. Sterrett of Amherst, who has explored Asia Minor with Ramsay.
