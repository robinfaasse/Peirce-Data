The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Nov. 1. 1898[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I thought I had written that
Hodgson had been forespoken and
sent out. I now send you Leibniz,
which I think is but a translation,
& invites, not much remark, & two
other books in your line, also to be
discussed briefly if at all.

I am sorry you can't under-
take Darwin's Tides. Do me the
favor to remail it to Prof.
Newcomb, 1620 P St., Washington.
I enclose stamps that will about
cover the carriage.
