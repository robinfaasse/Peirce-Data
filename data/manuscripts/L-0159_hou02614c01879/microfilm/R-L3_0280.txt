The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Aug 23. 1892[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce:

I walked 50 miles last week for the sake (inter alia) of looking down on Milford; & on my return I find your notices & note. The approach of the full tide of publication warns me not to solicit books not voluntarily sent to us; otherwise I would try to oblige you in the matter of the four you name. I have neglected to report that I was disappointed to find your notice of Tyndall's mostly about the man & not the matter, though there were some very interesting topics, like Goethe's color theories.
