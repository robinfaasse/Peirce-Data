The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]May 4 1871[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I am sorry that your
troubles have recommenced. You
will have received a small check
for Bourgeois, &c, & I now enclose
one for Rouge et Noir. Other
remains, & will put into type
at the first opportunity & remit
for, but (unmarried as it is) I
do not see more than $60
coming from it.

James escaped from us
last yesterday, & I see nothing
