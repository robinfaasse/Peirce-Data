The Nation, 208 Broadway (P. O. Box 794),

New York, [date]Aug. 28 1892[/date]

My dear Peirce:

The Nation shall go to
you as requested, from Jan. 1
last.

Do not, on my account, seek
to replace the Du Magnet. That
is, you need not tax yourself
to replace it as if in duty bound
to renew the book. Otherwise,
ut placet.

I am pained to hear such
a report concerning your wife,
but mine is low enough in bodily
