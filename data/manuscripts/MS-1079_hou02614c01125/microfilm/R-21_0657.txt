As to the first question, it does not appear that
scientific men as such, are particularly qualified to
express an authoritative opinion. They always use
the metric system for scientific purposes and the con-
veniences of uniformity for many, perhaps most
purposes, are obvious.

For the most important purposes, those of
machinery, the gravest differences of opinion
are found among those scientific men who
have devoted most time to the study of the
question; and under those circumstances, it would
probably be the judgment of the majority that precipitate
action should not be taken.

Nichols
