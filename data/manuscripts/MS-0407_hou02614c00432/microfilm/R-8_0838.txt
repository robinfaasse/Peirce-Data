§4. The irritation of doubt causes a struggle to attain a state of belief. I [del]shall[/del] term this struggle inquiry, though it must be admitted that this is sometimes not a very apt designation.

The irritation of doubt is the only immediate motive for the struggle to attain belief. It is certainly [del]best for us that[/del] our beliefs should be such as may truly guide our actions so as to satisfy our desires; and this reflection will make us reject [ins]every[/ins] [del]any[/del] belief which does not seem to have been so formed as to induce this result. But it will only do so by creating a doubt in the place of that belief. With the [del]doubt[/del] therefore, the struggle begins, and with the cessation of doubt it ends.
