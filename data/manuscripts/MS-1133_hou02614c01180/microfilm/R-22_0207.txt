My list of human motives (Pop. Sci. Monthly. 1901 Jan. pp. 298 et seq.) may be better stated. I will number them by [del]the[/del] [ins]a peculiar[/ins] system of arithmetical notation,

0. The zero of intelligence is seen in a quasi-hypnotic response to an instant command.

1. Obedience to a command [del]not instant[/del] put into concrete shape, if it is not to involve any reference to a general rule, can only take place by some vague personification of [u]other people in general[/u]. It will be action from dread of blame.

10. But instead of this personification, the man may act upon a [u]general rule[/u] for which he has an unreasoning reverence.

[del]11.[/del] [ins]11.[/ins] Instead of acting from obedience he may act from conformity to a norm. If this is done without any reference to a general rule, it must be by [u]retroconsciousness[/u], by which I mean putting oneself into another's shoes. In that case it will be instinctive imitation.

12. Instead of such retroconsciousness, the man may act with conscious reference to a general rule, by the personification of other people in general. That is, his norm will be [del]the[/del] custom.
