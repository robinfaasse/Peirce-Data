PUBLICATION HOUSE OF
FUNK & WAGNALLS,
18 AND 20 ASTOR PLACE.

EDITORIAL ROOMS OF THE STANDARD DICTIONARY.

NEW YORK, [date]Aug. 19th[/date] 1891.

Dear Sir;

Yours of the 15th, inst, to hand. We do not see how it would be practicable for us to agree to your proposition. Did we not see fit to accept your offer after you had made known to us your plan, it would tie our hands in the future. We have scores of the ablest scholars in this and other countries at work upon the dictionary. All are seeking to embody in the dictionary everything that would improve it. We would not care to tie our hands or the hands of any of our editors as to the future; nor could we consent to pay you five thousand dollars for suggestions before we know what those suggestions are worth.

Thanking you for your proposition, we remain,

Most respectfully,

Funk & Wagnalls
B.F.

To - C.S. Peirce. Ph. D.
Milford, Pa.,
