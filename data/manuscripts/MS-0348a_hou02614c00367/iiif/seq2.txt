Agassiz holds that an individual lobster would belong to the same Order, Family, Genus, & Species, though there were no other Crustaceum in existence. This makes the [ins]kind[/ins] [del]species[/del] constituted by the [ins]mode of[/ins] existence of the individual. From these two conceptions of natural classification the notion of quiddity easily follows. [ins]Suppose[/ins] If I ask what is this? [del]for[/del] You answer a Lobster. If I ask what makes it to be a Lobster, you must give the definition of a Lobster. Now suppose [ins]instead of this[/ins] I ask the two questions in one & say "What makes this to be what it is?" You must answer me still by the definition of the lobster. This conception of something in the individual which determines what kind of a thing it is & which therefore raises the conception of a kind far above that of a mere collection of things is the conception of quiddity.
