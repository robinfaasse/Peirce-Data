[date]June 29, 1901.[/date]

Dear Sir:-

We are obliged to you for your letter of June 25th suggesting that you should prepare for the Atlantic an article on the Philosophy of Religion, but regret to say we have some misgivings whether we should find such an article as you have in mind entirely suited to our plans, and cannot, therefore, encourage you to undertake it for us.

Yours very truly,
The Editors
W. H. P.

Mr. C. S. Pierce.
