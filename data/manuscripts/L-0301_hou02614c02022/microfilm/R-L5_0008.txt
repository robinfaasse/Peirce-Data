My dear Professor Morley:

I am very much obliged to you for your information. I certainly shall not print anything upon the subject unless I am more fully up in the theory than I have time now to make myself and unless I have something better worth pointing out than the trivial errors of the history that I find in Michelson's book.

I see my use of the phrase "common sense," due to my philosophical studies, has misled you into understanding me to say that common sense "settles the matter." But I always insist that common sense is a mere impertinence in an exact science & ought not to settle anything except practical questions. With renewed thanks,

Yours faithfully, C. S. Peirce
