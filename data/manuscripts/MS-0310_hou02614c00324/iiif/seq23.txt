10.

Only a few generations ago, the Alps, which so
elevate our souls to a sublime sense of
operative Thirdness had been regarded by every
man who had looked upon them, even the
dwellers in their vallies, as Nightmare terrors.
The oppressive sense of Secondness was there,
while their minds were not awake to the
Thirdness.
The problem of ethics is simply to define
what it is that each man is deliberately pre-
pared to accept as the object of his endeavors.
