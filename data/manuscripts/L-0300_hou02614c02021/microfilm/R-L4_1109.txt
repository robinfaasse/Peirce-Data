GEORGE S. MORISON
CIVIL ENGINEER
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK

New York, May 17, 1899.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Penn.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I arrived here yesterday morning from the southwest having been as far as Sabine Pass. I did not pass through Port Jervis on my return. I send you by express the original copy of the report on the suspension bridge, not having anyone with me who is capable of making a copy. Kindly make such notes from it as you need for your work and return the same to me.

As regards your place it certainly is of value and you ought to be able to get the mortgage of $3 000 which you speak of. It seems to me however that your best course would be to begin by selling the two lots which you tell me that some Port Jervis people want to buy. I should think that you would be able to get some payment down almost immediately. There are certainly people who would like to own your place and I will try to think it over.

The two bijous are in a place where they can be seen on an order
