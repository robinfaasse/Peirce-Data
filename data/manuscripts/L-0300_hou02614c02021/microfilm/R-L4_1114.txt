Milford Pa [date]1900 Jan 15[/date]

My dear Mr. Morison

I don't know whether I wrote to you that in May I was privily informed from Washington that the man for the Weights & Measures place had been decided upon before the examination was announced in April, that he was a young physicist, a pet of a person high in the Treasury, and that the salary was to be $4000. I did not believe it, but I mentioned all this in some letters, & part of it in others. The examination, — and the announcing of the salary at $3000 — was, it was said, only for effect.
