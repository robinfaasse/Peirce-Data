New York, Sept. 22, 1896

**MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,**
**Care Albert Stickney, Esq.,**
**35 Nassau Street, City.**

**Dear Mr. Peirce:**

**I returned here today and have your letter of the 9th.**
**While I am somewhat appalled at the amount of work which you have had**
**to do and at the 169 numbered sheets, I am very glad that you have been**
**going through it in this way. Your work will undoubtedly be very val-**
**uable and it will be especially gratifying to have an American study of**
**this subject on independent lines come out now, when all that we have**
**is practically from German authors and really adapted only to struc-**
**tures of small size.**

**My paper, to which this will be an appendix, is to be discussed on**
**the 21st of October and I hope that you will be able to come to the**
**meeting at which it is discussed, and I should be very glad if your work**
**could be completed so that it could be put in press before that meeting,**
**but I fear there will not be time.**

**I leave town tonight but shall return here about the middle of**
**next week.**

Very truly yours,
G. S. Morison
