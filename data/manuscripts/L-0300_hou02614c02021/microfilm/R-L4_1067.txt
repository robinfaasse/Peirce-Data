[u]GEORGE B. MORISON,[/u]
CIVIL ENGINEER,
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK.
1742 MONADNOCK BLOCK, CHICAGO.

New York, [date]May 2, 1896[/date]

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
No. 160 West 87th Street, City.

Dear Sir:

Will you please allow me to introduce to you Prof. Wm. H. Burr, whom you already know by reputation. Prof. Burr is Chairman of the Committee on Publications of the American Society of Civil Engineers, and as he lives in your neighborhood and I have to go to Boston tonight, I have suggested that he call on you and ask you how soon you will have your paper in regard to the study of the Suspension Bridge ready for our use.

My movements have been affected greatly by my father's death last Sunday and I do not yet know definitely where I shall be for the next two or three weeks.

Yours truly,
Geo. S. Morison
