C. S. P. 2

a valuable paper.

I hope that I may be able to come out and see you in Milford some
time before the summer is over, but I am going to New Hampshire to-
morrow and do not know when I can go. I am very sorry to hear what
you say about Mrs. Peirce being very ill and I sincerely hope that she
may already be improving.

Very truly yours,
[fig]signature of Geo. S. Morison[/fig]
