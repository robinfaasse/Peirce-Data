GEORGE S. MORISON
CIVIL ENGINEER
49 WALL STREET, NEW YORK

New York, Oct. 4, 1901,

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

The missing letter has at length arrived with your letter of September 27th. I have not had time to examine it as yet. However, I have made some inquiries and find why I did not receive it. The reason does not seem to me the credit of the Post Office; it was delivered here when I was absent from the city and Joseph Deegan, who has authority to receipt for registered packages for me was also absent on his vacation. The carrier refused to deliver it to any one else and took it back to the Post Office leaving no notice to call for it there. Had the customary notice been sent here eight days after it was offered the letter would have been received.

On thinking the matter over I have about decided to pay you $200 for the work you have done in this connection, for one-half of which I will send you my check and the other half I will credit on your note which I hold. Kindly advise me if this arrangement will be satisfactory.

Very truly yours,
G. S. Morison
