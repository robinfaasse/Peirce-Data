New York, May 31st, 1898.

MESSRS. CLARK, DODGE & CO.,
     51 Wall Street,
          New York City.

Gentlemen:-

     I herewith hand you two certificates, one for 50 shares of
preferred stock and the other for 50 shares of common stock in the
St. Lawrence Water Power Company of Massena, New York, in the name of
C. S. Peirce with power of transfer endorsed. Will you please to
hold this stock in my account, having it transfered to your name. If
either Mr. or Mrs Peirce calls for this stock you may deliver them 40
shares of each class of stock, holding ten shares of each for my benefit.

     Yours truly,
          Geo. S. Morison

Enc.
