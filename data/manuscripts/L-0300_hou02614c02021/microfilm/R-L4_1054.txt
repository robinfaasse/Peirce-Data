GEORGE S. MORISON,
CIVIL ENGINEER,
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK.
1712 MONADNOCK BLOCK, CHICAGO.

New York, July 10th, 1895.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:

I have your postal card but do not quite under-
stand it as apparently it was written after receiving my letter of June
27th but before receiving my letter of yesterday, but it appears to me
that I should have received it earlier. I will however have the depos-
it of $50 made at once and you may count upon its being in the Second
National Bank tomorrow. I am sorry to hear of Mrs. Peirce's continued
illness.

Very truly yours,
G. S. Morison
