Chicago, [date]February 13, 1896.[/date]

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,

160 West 87th Street, NEW YORK.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

I have your letter of the 10th. I wrote to you on the 10th addressing you at 74 West 53rd Street and trust that the letter may have reached you.

My attention has recently been called to a book entitled "Der Portland Cement und seine Anwendungen im Bauwesen" which is prepared by the direction of the Association of German Portland cement manufacturers. It has seemed to me that this book contains a great deal of matter which it would be well for the American Portland cement dealers to publish and I have written to day to the agent of the Alsen cement, who are among the parties who got out this book in Germany, suggesting that they have a translation made for American use and also suggesting that you would be an excellent man to do the work. I trust something may come of it. It covers a quantity of interesting subjects and contains about 300 pages, a considerable portion of the text being occupied by illustrations.
