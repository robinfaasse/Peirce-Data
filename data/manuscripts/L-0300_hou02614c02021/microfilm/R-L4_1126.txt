GEORGE S. MORISON
CIVIL ENGINEER
49 WALL STREET, NEW YORK

New York, January 7, 1903.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Pennsylvania.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

You will remember that there are in the hands of Clark, Dodge & Company, holding them nominally on my account but really for you, certificates for fifty shares of common and fifty shares of preferred stock of the St. Lawrence Power Company. I presume that you know of the unfortunate termination of this company, the mortgages having been foreclosed and a new company organized representing the creditors only and entirely eliminating the old stockholders. Under these circumstances I should be glad to know whether you would like to have anything done with these certificates.

I am sorry to begin the new year with so unpleasant a subject, but I send my best wishes for the season to you and Mrs. Peirce.

Very truly yours,
G. S. Morison
