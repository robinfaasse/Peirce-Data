GEORGE S. MORISON,
CIVIL ENGINEER,
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK.
1712 MONADNOCK BLOCK, CHICAGO.

New York, [date]November 24, 1896[/date]

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Room 6, No. 84 Broad Street, City.

Dear Mr. Peirce:

I have your letter of today. I leave, myself, for Chicago at two p.m. and so shall not be able to see you. I have seen Stickney and he will advise Sypher that he may send for the picture.

I have not had time to do anything about your other matter but spoke of it to Charles Fairchild, whom I think you know. I would suggest that you call on him at his office No. 29 Wall Street.

Very truly yours,
Geo. S. Morison
