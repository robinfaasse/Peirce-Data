GEORGE S. MORISON,
CIVIL ENGINEER,
1742 MONADNOCK BLOCK, CHICAGO.
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK.

Chicago, October 12th, 1896.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,

Stuart House, Broadway & 41st St.,

NEW YORK.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

I have your letter of the 10th. For various reasons it seems to me best that all our financial arrangements should pass through Mr. Stickney for the present; I therefore, write to him about the matter. I am very anxious to get your paper as early as I can and hope that you will at any rate be in New York on the 21st and can go with me to the meeting of the American Society of Civil Engineers.

As regards your picture, I have had this in mind and have started an attempt to get it sold here, if this succeeds at all it will bring a much larger price than you mention, $500.

Yours truly,
G. S. Morison
