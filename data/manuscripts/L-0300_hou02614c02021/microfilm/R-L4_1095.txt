Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I have your letter of the 26th. You may certainly send the box which you wish to have opened to me here and I will send it to a locksmith, have it opened and a key fitted. I am glad to hear that the preliminary report is approaching completion. As regards arching the stiffening truss there are two or three pretty serious objections to this; the first is that, while the stiffening truss has to be made of metal, unless the arching is very excessive there is great difficulty in providing for changes in temperature; the second is, in bridges like the one we are considering navigation interests require a maximum clearance for the whole length of the span; unless the stiffening truss is carried clear through to the anchorages there would be nothing to take the thrust of the arch. My impression is that the arch stiffening truss should be heavier rather than lighter.
