GEORGE S. MORISON
CIVIL ENGINEER
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK

New York, [date]April 26, 1899[/date].

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Penn.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I am again back in New York and have your letter of the 18th and your two letters of the 20th. I have requested Clark, Dodge & Co. to deposit the $60 to the credit of Mrs. Peirce to-morrow April 27th instead of waiting till the first of May.

As regards going out to Milford I think I can probably arrange to do it at the end of this week, leaving here Saturday afternoon, probably by the fast train which leaves at 2 P. M. and returning Sunday evening. If this is convenient to you and Mrs. Peirce please let me know and I will try to shape my plans accordingly.

Your method of solving equations given in your letter of the 20th is novel to me and certainly is a useful method. In the hands of some people of defective judgment it might be interminable, but such people do not reach the mathematical rank which this method implies.

Very truly yours,
G. S. Morison
