GEORGE S. MORISON
CIVIL ENGINEER
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK

New York, Dec. 15, 1898.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Penn.

Dear Sir:-

Mrs. Peirce has brought to me this morning the articles provided for in sections 3 and 5 of my letter to you. She has requested me to return them to her instead of keeping them as provided for in that letter. I have told Mrs. Peirce the conditions under which this special provision was made; it was solely to relieve you of the high interest charged by the pawn broker. The principal object in making the arrangement was to make sure that you had funds on which you could live while working for a year. If you would prefer to go back to original arrangement and retain these articles, returning to me $180 which will be paid to you on the first of August, September and October I am entirely ready to do so. Furthermore, I will return these articles to Mrs. Peirce without any conditions whenever the bridge work is completed.

Very truly yours,
G. S. Morison
