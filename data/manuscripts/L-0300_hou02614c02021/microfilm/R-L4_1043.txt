Chicago, May 20th, 1895.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,

Milford, PA.

Dear Sir:

I have your letter of the 16th.

The weight of one cable per lineal foot of cable including wrapping and clamps, is 2630 lbs. This is a single cable, there are two on each side of the bridge making the weight for one side 5 260 lbs and for the whole bridge 10 520 lbs., this is per foot of cable and not per foot of bridge.

The dead weight per lineal foot of bridge for the entire bridge and for one side are as follows:
