MRS. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Penn.

Dear Mrs. Peirce:-

I have your letter of the 3rd. I decided to take the diamond and sapphire ring out of pawn some days ago in order that a friend of mine from St. Louis might see them. They were therefore redeemed on the following basis.

Pawn ticket $475.00
Interest 11 1/2 months 92.62
[u]--------- [/u]
$567.62

After redeeming them we examined them and thought it best to have them appraised and I am sorry to say that the appraiser places the value considerably lower than I expected, the cash appraisal being as follows:

1.3ct Sap. and 10 Diamond Cluster Ring $210.00
1.7 to 8ct. Diamond Arrow Brooch $250.00
[u]--------- [/u]
$460.00
