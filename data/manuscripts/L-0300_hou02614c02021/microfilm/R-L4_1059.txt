GEORGE S. MORISON,
CIVIL ENGINEER,
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK.
1742 MONADNOCK BLOCK, CHICAGO.

New York, [date]November 6, 1895.[/date]

MR. C. S. PEIRCE,
158 West 58th Street, City.

Dear Sir:

I enclose a duplicate of the voucher which was sent to you last August. Can you have the kindness to sign it and return it to this office. It will keep my records straight.

I have talked about the work which you spoke of last Sunday to Mr. Forney of the American Engineer and to Col. Prout the editor of the Railroad Gazette; the latter took your address and said he hoped to be able to make some use of you. I have also written a letter to Mr. Wm. H. Wiley recommending you for any work of the class we talked of, and I hope that something may come of it, but of course at present I can go no farther than hopes.

I leave for Chicago tomorrow.

Yours truly,
G. S. Morison
