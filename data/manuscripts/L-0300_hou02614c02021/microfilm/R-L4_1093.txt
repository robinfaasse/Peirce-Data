Milford Pa [date]1899 Jan 26[/date]

My dear Mr. Morison

Why would it not be a good idea to arch your truss so as to support it in part by its thrust, and at the same time to diminish or annul the upgrade of the trains in approach ing the piers? If the trains only run one way on each side, that might be pass from grade.

I want you to do me a favor. In this part of the report which I am sending you, I want to use some mathematical instruments. Now I have some; but the box has lost the key, & the lock is a small 2 or 3 tumbler lock which the people up here are not expert enough to open. I propose to express the box to you & get you, kindly, to get a New York locksmith to open it and make a key without injuring the lock. I hope he wont steal any instruments. There are a few places vacant. Please send it back to me by Wells Fargo to Port Jervis NY.

This preliminary report will be ready some time in February, I hope. To get it done before the first, but at the beginning of this month I was really not fit for such work & lately our old gardener has been ill which has thrown all his work on me together with the nursing of him, which takes me away from my work. Mrs. Peirce is also under the necessity of economizing her forces.

Could not a cable be stiffened so as to have it stand in an arch shape & so save the expense of the towers?

very truly & gratefully C S Peirce
