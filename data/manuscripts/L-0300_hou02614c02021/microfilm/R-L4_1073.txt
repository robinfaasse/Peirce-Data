C.S.P. 2.

Bladgen (H.U. 1857) of Clark, Dodge & Company and who on receipt of this deed will pay you one hundred fifty dollars ($150). As soon as I reach New York, which will probably be on the 9th of this month, I will have the matter of title looked up, put the deed on record and pay you the balance, two hundred and eighteen dollars ($218).

If you will show this letter to Mr. Blagden at the time you give him the deed he will pay you the $150. As I am at a distance I do not see how I can make any better arrangement.

Yours truly,

G. S. Morison
