be taken to see that the weight was kept as nearly uniform as possible.

This is the method in which I should expect to erect. It
would certainly be expedient when the whole truss was completed to
adjust all the suspenders to something like a uniform tension under a
uniform load, but I do not feel clear that it would be quite practica-
ble to screw the cables into perfect parabolas, though possibly this
could be done.

In all such works there are hypotheses which are not strictly
correct. Whichever of your two suppositions is the simpler might be
used, I am inclined to think that the supposition that the stiffening
truss is a continuous beam laid in position may be the better. I
should be willing, however, to have the present calculations made on
whichever basis is the simpler.
