MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
No. 160 West 87th Street, City.

Dear Sir:

I arrived here this morning from Boston and am to return there Saturday afternoon. Can you kindly advise me whether your portion of the work on the paper on Suspension Bridges is ready?

Yours truly,
Geo. S. Morison
