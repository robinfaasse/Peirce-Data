GEORGE S. MORISON
CIVIL ENGINEER
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK

New York, [date]Feb. 8, 1899[/date].

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Penn.

Dear Sir:-

I return to you at Port Jervis by express to-day the box of drawing instruments which arrived yesterday morning having had a new key fitted which key I enclose in this letter.

On undoing the package the case was found to be open and a key inside, which key however fitted the lock very imperfectly. I trust everything will reach you in good condition.

Yours truly,
G. S. Morison

Enc.
