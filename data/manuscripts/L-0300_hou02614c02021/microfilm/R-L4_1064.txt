C.S.P. 2.

If I were in New York I should be very glad to come around on Sunday afternoon and hear you converse about philosophy, even if you bring that sublime subject within the mental capacity of "giddy worldings".

I trust that you will find it convenient to have the other matter ready when I return from Mexico as outlined in my previous letter.

Yours truly,
[fig]signature of G. S. Morison[/fig]
