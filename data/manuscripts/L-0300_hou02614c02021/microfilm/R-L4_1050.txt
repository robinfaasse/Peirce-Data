New York, June 27,1895.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:

I am at length back here and have today sent a check for $100 to the Second National Bank to be deposited to the credit of Mrs. Peirce.

I find on looking up my records that this check makes $900 paid to you. I hope that [del]you[/del] [ins]you[/ins] may be able to complete your work without calling for more than $100 more, making the total payment $1000. While I do not mean to say that your work has not been worth even more than this it is more than I had expected it would come to.

I do not know what the future of the scheme will be, as I have not seen any of the people since I arrived in New York.
