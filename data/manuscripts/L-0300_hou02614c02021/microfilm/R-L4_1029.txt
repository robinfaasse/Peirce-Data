New York, [date]Feb. 13th., 1895.[/date]

PROF. JAMES M. PEIRCE,
4 Kirkland Place,
Cambridge, Mass.

Dear Sir:--

Your favor of the 12th inst., reached me this morning, and I have also a letter from your brother dated from the Brevoort House. I will write to him to-day and try to make an appointment with him. He seems to like the idea of taking up this subject.

Thanking you for your trouble in this connection,

I remain.

Yours very truly,
Geo. S. Morison
