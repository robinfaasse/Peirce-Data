GEORGE S. MORISON
CIVIL ENGINEER
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK

New York, Nov. 3, 1898.

MR. CHAS. S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Penn.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I have been here but little of late and have not answered your letter of the 2nd. I sent a very breif reply on October 20th to Mrs. Peirce's letter of the 17th. I was very glad to hear that you had got fairly to work again on the suspension bridge study, though you apparently had done only about one-third of what you expected to do when you were taken sick again. I sincerely hope that Mrs. Peirce's opinion was correct, that you were out of danger when she wrote me, and that you have now recovered and are able to resume the completion of what some of us hope to be a very valuable work. As regards the mortgage which you speak of, of course the sale under foreclosure is followed by an equity of redemption, the time of which varies in different states but is usually about a year. Am I to understand that you have reduced the mortgages on your whole place to $1 000 and that this will become due on the first of December? If this is the case it ought to be easy to straighten it out.

Very truly yours, G. S. Morison
