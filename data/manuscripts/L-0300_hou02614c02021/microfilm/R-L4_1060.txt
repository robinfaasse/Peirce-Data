Chicago, February 10, 1896.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,

74 West 53rd Street, NEW YORK.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

Your postal card has just reached me here. I am sorry to say that I am not likely to be in New York before the middle of April. I am making my plans for a trip to the city of Mexico, which I have intended to take for several years and now for the first time have a good opportunity. Please give my regards to Mrs. Peirce and tell her that I am very sorry that I am not to have the pleasure of calling on her.

As regards the completion of your report, I have been revising my report on the North River Bridge and putting it in the form of a paper on the general subject of suspension bridges of this magnitude. I shall have the paper in shape to be written out before I leave for Mexico and shall probably present it to the American Society of Civil Engineers immediately after my return.
