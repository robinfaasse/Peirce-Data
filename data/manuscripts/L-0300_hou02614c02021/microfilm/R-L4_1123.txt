GEORGE S. MORISON
CIVIL ENGINEER
49 WALL STREET, NEW YORK

New York, Sept. 6, 1901.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Penn.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I have your letter of the 3rd which this time reached me safely. As before, it reaches me just as I am about to leave town; I shall take it with me and will write to you again on my return.

Very truly yours,
Geo S. Morison
