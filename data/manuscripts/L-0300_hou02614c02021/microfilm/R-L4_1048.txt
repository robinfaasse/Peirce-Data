Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I received your letter of the 7th yesterday on my re-
turn from Lake Superior.

I note what you say about making the towers lean back.
Something of this kind can be done and should be done; there are several
ways of doing it. The motion, however, required in the tops of the
towers will not be much unless the backstays are erected without any
supports, if temporary supports are put under the backstays I think it
will be possible to draw the towers over as much as is necessary, sim-
ply by straining the separate ropes which form the backstays. At all
events I think that you are correct in saying [del]that the[/del] [ins]that the[/ins] hypothesis
that every suspender carries the weight of its own panel will be nearer
than any other hypothesis.
