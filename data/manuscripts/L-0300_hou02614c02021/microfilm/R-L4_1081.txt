New York, Dec. 5, 1898.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Penn.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

[del]Yo[/del]Your letter of the 19th ult., arrived at a very unfortunate time. I left for the west the day after it was written and returned by a somewhat roundabout way. The letter was forwarded to me, but it was impossible for me to answer it until I could get at my papers here. I have now looked up the matter and have studied it in some detail so as to see how the account between ourselves really stands.

I understand your proposition to be briefly that I extend the time for the payment of what you now owe me three years or say till January 1st, 1902; that I advance you about $700 more; that you agree under a penalty of $1 000 to have your report ready within that time; that as additional collateral to that which I now hold, you give me 40 shares of common and 40 shares of preferred stock of the St. Lawrence Water Power Company.
