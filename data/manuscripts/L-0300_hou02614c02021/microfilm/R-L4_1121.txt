GEORGE S. MORISON
CIVIL ENGINEER
49 WALL STREET, NEW YORK

New York, August 16", 1901.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I have your letter of the 15th. I will read it and read it carefully, but I have been very busy for the last few days and have not had time. I am sorry to say that I did not receive the diagram which you sent me.

The young man named Woodard can be no relation to Prof. Woodward; observe the difference in the spelling of the names. He is a young man in the employ of the Commission who is a good mathematician for a person of his age and experience, that is all.

I leave town this afternoon to be gone 10 days and will write you on my return. Of course I expect to pay you for any work you may do in this connection, but it will be my affair and not that of the Commission.

Very truly yours,
G. S. Morison
