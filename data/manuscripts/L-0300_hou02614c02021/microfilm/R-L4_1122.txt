GEORGE S. MORISON
CIVIL ENGINEER
49 WALL STREET, NEW YORK

New York, August 29, 1901.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I have just returned from New Hampshire and while there I examined carefully the papers accompanying your letter of August 13th. I have also your letter of the 18th. I return herewith the papers which accompanied the earlier letter. I never received the diagram in five colors which you mention and the only letter on smooth blue paper which I received was the one accompanying the six sheets which I now return.

I shall be here for the present though not all the time, and if you will drop me a line saying what you think will be a fair payment for the work you have done I will send you a check at once. I will explain my peculiar position in this matter when we next meet.

Very truly yours,
G. S. Morison

Enc.
