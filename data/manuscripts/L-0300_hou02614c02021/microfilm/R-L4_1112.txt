GEORGE S. MORISON
CIVIL ENGINEER
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK

New York, July 14, 1899.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Penn.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

Will you kindly drop me a line saying how you are getting on with the report and with other matters. Moreover, if you have made such notes as you desire from the copy of your report which I sent you can you have the kindness to send it back.

I believe that the seven monthly payments of $60 each have now all been made and that nothing is due on the first of August. The jewelry which I hold in my safe deposit box should be returned and I am ready to return it. Will you kindly advise me how you wish this to be done.

Very truly yours,
G. S. Morison
