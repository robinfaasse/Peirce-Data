GEORGE S. MORISON
CIVIL ENGINEER
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK

New York, [date]Oct. 20, 1898.[/date]

MRS. CHAS. S. PIERCE,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Mrs. Peirce:-

I am just passing through the city, having arrived here Tuesday night and leaving for Chicago to-day. I have your letter of the 17th and am very sorry to hear of Mr. Peirce's sickness and trust that your hope that he was out of danger is now an accomplished fact. I should have deferred writing until I returned, but think that perhaps a breif note now is better than more later.

Sincerely yours,
G. S. Morison
