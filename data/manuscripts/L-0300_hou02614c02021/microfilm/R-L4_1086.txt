New York, [date]Dec. 14, 1898[/date].

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Penn.

Dear Sir:-

I have this day received from you a note for $3 000 dated [date]December 15, 1898[/date] and payable three years from date. This note is issued in accordance with the general provisions stated in my letter of the 5th and the arrangement is made expressly to enable you to complete your partially finished work on the suspension bridge which was begun about three years and a half ago. I am willing, however, to have you devote the greater part of your time during the year 1899 to the work outlined in your letter of December 7th, but this is done however on the condition that before taking up this other work you will complete your general demonstration of the suspension bridge study, this being without special application to the bridge which we were considering, (whether that special application be algebraic or numerical) but to be complete in itself so that it may be used by competent men and will be preserved in case anything should happen to yourself.
