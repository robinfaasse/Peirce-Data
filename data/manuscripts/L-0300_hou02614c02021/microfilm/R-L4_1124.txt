GEORGE S. MORISON
CIVIL ENGINEER
49 WALL STREET, NEW YORK

New York, Sept. 18, 1901.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-
I returned here yesterday after an absence of several days and find you registered letter of the 3rd and the large package of the 5th. I have not time to examine them now as I leave again to-day, but you will hear from me further pretty soon.

Very truly yours,
Geo S. Morison
