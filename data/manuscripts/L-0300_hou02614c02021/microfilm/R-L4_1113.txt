GEORGE S. MORISON
CIVIL ENGINEER
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK

New York, Oct. 31, 1899

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Penn.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

I have your letter. I shall be glad to receive the analytic report. As at present advised I expect to be in New York for the next ten days, probably till Friday, November 10th. However, I have been very irregular of late and I may have to go to Washington meanwhile.

Very truly yours,
G. S. Morison
