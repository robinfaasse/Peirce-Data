[u]GEORGE S. MORISON[/u]
CIVIL ENGINEER
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK

New York, June 1, 1898.

MRS. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Penna.

Dear Madam:-

Your husband received from me through Mr. Stickney in July and October 1897 $350, for which I hold a picture by Bierstadt as security. He has also received from Clark, Dodge & Co. on my account $50 for which I am entitled to 10 shares of common stock of the St. Lawrence Water Power Company as collateral. I have this day paid to you an additional $100 taking 10 shares of preferred stock as collateral and taking your note due in six months for One hundred dollars. This makes the total indebtedness $500 besides interest.

I have received from you 50 shares of preferred stock and 50 shares of common stock which I have placed with Clark, Dodge & Co., under instructions of which I enclose a copy.

Very truly yours,
G. S. Morison
