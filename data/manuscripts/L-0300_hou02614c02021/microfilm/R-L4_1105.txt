GEORGE S. MORISON
CIVIL ENGINEER
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK

New York, March 9, 1899.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Penn.

Dear Sir:-

I write to inform you that the package which you sent Mr. Morison by express has arrived. The package will be handed to Mr. Morison as soon as he returns from the South, which will probably be in about three weeks.

Yours truly,
