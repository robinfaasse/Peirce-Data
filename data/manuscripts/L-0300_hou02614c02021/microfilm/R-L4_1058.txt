GEORGE S. MORISON,
CIVIL ENGINEER,
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK.
1713 MONADNOCK BLOCK, CHICAGO.

New York, October 17, 1895.

MR. C. S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Pa.

My dear Mr. Peirce:

Can you have the kindness to look up your pa-
pers and see if you have not still the voucher which I enclosed to you
in my letter of August 13th, and if you have it will you kindly sign
the same and return it to me here.

I have been spending a large portion of the summer in New Hamp-
shire but shall probably be in and about this city for the next three
weeks. I should like to have a further talk with you about the suspen-
sion bridge study and if you are to be at home on Sunday the 27th per-
haps I could arrange to go out and spend that day at Milford, that is
if it is perfectly convenient to you and Mrs. Peirce.

Very truly yours,
G. S. Morison
