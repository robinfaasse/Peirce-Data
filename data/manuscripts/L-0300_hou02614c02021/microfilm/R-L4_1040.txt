New York, May 10,1895.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Pa.

My dear Sir:

I leave for Boston tonight but shall probably be here on Monday and return to Chicago on Tuesday.

I have been looking over the papers which you left me, giving moments and deflections. It is hard to tie the moments with the memorandum in your note of May 6th. The deflections seem to be about what they should be except that in case B the deflection upward when x = [ins]11/12[/ins] seems excessive; if it were 2.40 instead of 3.40 it would be apparently correct. I think that perhaps it would be best, instead of sending me the moments, to send the value of [ins]3H/1q[/ins] for each arrangement, A, B & C, and then make a table showing in two successive columns the moments divided by Hl and the deflections for each of the three arrangements. I can have the arithmetical work done without trouble, and can prepare the curves both of moments and deflections which will show whether there is any probable error.
