New York, [date]April 6,1895.[/date]

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Penna.

Dear Sir:

I have your letter. I leave tonight for Buffalo but will make the deposit of $100 in favor of Mrs. Peirce on my return, probably on Tuesday, possibly on Monday.

I note what you say about the delays which you have met with and hope you will pardon my saying that I am getting a little anxious for some results; I hope I may hear from you early in the week. I am specially anxious to know what you make the moment at the center of the stiffening truss and also on the quarters, or say at points 700 feet each side of the center, these being the quarters of the suspended portion of the span. Perhaps your work is in such shape that you can send me these results immediately.
