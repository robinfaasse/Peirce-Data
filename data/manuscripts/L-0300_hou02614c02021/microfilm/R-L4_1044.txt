There are three panels to each 100 feet and the suspenders on
each side at each panel point have a cross section of 24 square inches
and the same modulus of elasticity as the cables.

The cables on one side of the bridge, as already stated, have
a section of 1500 square inches and a modulus of elasticity of
26 000 000 pounds.

The moving load as we have assumed it is 15 000 pounds per
foot of bridge or 7 500 pounds on each side for a length 1033.3 feet,
occupying the three positions, A, B and C previously arranged.
