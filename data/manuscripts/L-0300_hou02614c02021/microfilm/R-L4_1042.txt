GEORGE S. MORISON,
CIVIL ENGINEER,
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK.
188 MONADNOCK BLOCK, CHICAGO.

New York, May 14,1895.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:

I have your letter of the 10th. I have written to Meyer & Müller in Berlin asking them for the two books which you name. I presume they should be here in about three weeks; I will let them come to this office and leave instructions to have them forwarded to you.

You understand of course that the change from 30 000 000 to 26 000 000 in modulus of elasticity relates only to the cables and not to the chords of the truss; this was the reason why I suggested reducing the section of the cable in your calculations instead of changing the modulus.

The deposit of $50 in the Fifth Avenue Bank is made today.

Your letter of the 13th reached me this morning. I think that it would be best for you to take a couple of weeks of rest before doing anything further and I will hope to hear from you some time towards the end of June. I shall probably be here during the last week of that month.

Yours truly,
G. S. Morison
