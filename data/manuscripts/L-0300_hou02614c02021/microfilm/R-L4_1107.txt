C. S. P. 2.

"
Four Hundred and Twenty Five Dollars ($425) on account of the
principal of this note [del]and[/del] as of date of maturity of the same paid March
6th, 1899."

This being in accordance with my letter of December 14th, 1898.

I have to-day your letter of the 7th. I should like to go up to
Milford very much and perhaps I can do so within the next two or three
weeks. My movements just now, however, are somewhat uncertain, as
they are dependent on certain important matters which have not yet
taken shape.

I had a very interesting visit to Cuba; it was very instructive
and in the main agreeable. It is an island of immense possiblities
if it is properly handled.

Very truly yours,

[fig]signature of Geo. S. Morison[/fig]
