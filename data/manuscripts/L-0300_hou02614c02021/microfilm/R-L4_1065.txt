GEORGE S. MORISON,
CIVIL ENGINEER,
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK.
1742 MONADNOCK BLOCK, CHICAGO.

New York, [date]March 23, 1896[/date]

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
160 West 87th Street, City.

My dear Sir:

I have been unexpectedly called east, but leave for the west this evening. My paper on the Suspension Bridge is ready and only waiting for your portion of the work. How soon can you send it to me?

I have advices from Meyer & Muller of Berlin that there are still 11.75 marks remaining there to my credit. As this, though in my name, is really your account you have the right to order books from them to this value.

Very truly yours,
G. S. Morison
