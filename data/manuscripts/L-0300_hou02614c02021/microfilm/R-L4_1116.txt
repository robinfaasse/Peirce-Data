GEORGE S. MORISON
CIVIL ENGINEER
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK

New York, April 17, 1901.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Penn.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I am about moving my office and I find that I have never returned to you the book which you sent to me of the Clark University. I did not quite understand from your letter whether you expected it to be returned or not, but as the book is of much value and evidently of more value to you than to me I send it to you now.

I have not heard from you for some time and I trust that you and Mrs. Peirce may have passed a comfortable winter and have fairly good prospects for the coming summer.

Very truly yours,
[fig]Signature of Geo. S. Morison[/fig]
