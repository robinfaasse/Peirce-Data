GEORGE S. MORISON
CIVIL ENGINEER
PETERBORO N. H.

Peterboro, August 25, 1898.

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, PA.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I have your letter of the 24th. As I understand you, what you wish is to make sure that the 100 shares of stock (50 preferred and 50 common) now held by Clark, Dodge & Company will not be thrown on the market to interfere with the stock which Mr. Easton proposes to sell. It does not seem to me that you require any action in this matter beyond my own assurance that I will not have this stock offered for sale before January 1st. As the stock is only held as collateral I should not really have the right to order it sold without giving you notice but I assure you now that it will not be offered for sale and it seems to me that this is all you will require. If anything more is needed please advise me.
