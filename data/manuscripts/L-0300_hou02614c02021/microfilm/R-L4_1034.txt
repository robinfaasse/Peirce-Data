GEORGE S. MORISON,
CIVIL ENGINEER,
35 WALL STREET, NEW YORK.
204 LA SALLE STREET, CHICAGO.

New York, [date]March 9,1895.[/date]

MR. CHARLES S. PEIRCE,
Milford, Penna.

My dear Sir:

I have your letter of the 8th and am glad to hear that you are getting on so well with the work.

I will have a deposit of $150 made to the credit of Mrs. Peirce in the Second National Bank on Monday. When your work is done I hope that I may see you here as I should like to talk it over with you. I write this answer in haste as I leave for Boston this afternoon but shall be back on Wednesday.

Very truly yours,
G. S. Morison
