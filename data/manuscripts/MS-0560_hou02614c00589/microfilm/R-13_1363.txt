The theory of combinations
of two terms is highly impor-
tant. But the whole thing
reduces to this.

There is an essential dif-
ference between asserting
that something exists & saying
that something doesn't exist.
That is between propositions
of the types
$$\sum x \quad \prod x$$
If the $x$ is itself a sum or product
we have 6 types
$$\sum_i \sum_j \quad \sum_i \prod_j \quad \sum_j \prod_i$$
$$\prod_j \sum_i \quad \prod_i \sum_j \quad \prod_i \prod_j$$
