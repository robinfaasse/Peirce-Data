We have therefore three
distinct signs $\Sigma$ $\Pi$ and the
line denoting negation, when
only two are requisite. But
we cannot get along without
a sign of negation and we
have no reason for choosing
one rather than the other of
the other two signs $\Sigma$ $\Pi$.

Some crows are white is
written $\Sigma c w = \overline{\Pi(\overline{c}+\overline{w})}$

No crows are white is
written $\overline{\Sigma c w} = \Pi(\overline{c}+\overline{w})$

Every man is either a Knave or a fool
is written $\Pi(k+f) = \overline{\Sigma \overline{k} \overline{f}}$

Some man is neither a fool
nor a Knave
$\overline{\Pi(k+f)} = \Sigma \overline{k} \overline{f}$
