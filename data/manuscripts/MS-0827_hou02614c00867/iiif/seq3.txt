It is illogical, then, to hold that any given
truth is purely objective, definitive, exact, or
certain. You must not, for example, hold to
the law of gravitation in any such sense, nor
to the principle of energy, nor to the law of
action and reaction, nor to the first law of
motion, nor to the exactitude of any physical
law, nor to the absoluteness of any form
of necessity, [ins]nor to the axioms of geometry,[/ins] nor to the principle of excluded
middle. You may hold that black is not
white, that twice three are six, etc.; because
these are not matters of fact at all, but mere
matters of consistency of nomenclature.
