PEABODY ACADEMY OF SCIENCE.

Salem, Mass.,
[date]June 23ᵈ 1869[/date]

Charles S. Peirce
Dear Sir

I send you
to day by express three blow pipes
which Mr Emerson said he
should like me to give you
as a souvenir.

They are of no value
except in this respect.

I do not know
what your relations were with
the Emerson but mine were of
the most intimate & friendly descr-
-tion and his loss was irreparable
to me.
