These things were received
by me with others to be
distributed among his friends
and I laid them aside intending
(to hand them to you personally.
They were covered up and for-
gotten for eighteen months
and now after still another
month of delay I send them
in despair of being able to
see you myself.

I have read with much pleasure
and profit Mrs Peirce's essays
in the Atlantic. I am a faithful
believer in all well directed
efforts for the amelioration of
our social condition and I hope
with all my heart that your
wife's theory may meet with
the success its earnestness deserves.

If you ever visit Salem I shall
consider it as a great favor if you
would call upon me.

Yours very truly
A. Hyatt
