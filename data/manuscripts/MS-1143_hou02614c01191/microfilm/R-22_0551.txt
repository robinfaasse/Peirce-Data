Abeyance is another legal term. It means a state
of temporary non-existence, or being in the future or
in expectation. "A peerage that is in abeyance is dormant
only, not dead," says Boutell's Heraldry. "Abeyance" is [ins]a[/ins]
word just fit for use in such nonsense subjects as
heraldry is.
