[pn]Ms 1143[/pn] [u]A Little Dictionary of Choice[/u]
English Words, comprising all those that
everybody who proposes to use the language
at all must perfectly understand and be
familiar with, together with others that
are particularly useful; but excluding
all that are inelegant or to be shunned
for any reason, as, for example, on account
of their ambiguity.

I have no intention of completing
this dictionary or of going far toward
doing so, but only intend to carry it
so far as to serve as a specimen
by which the merits and faults of
the plan [del]can[/del] [ins]can[/ins] be easily judged.
