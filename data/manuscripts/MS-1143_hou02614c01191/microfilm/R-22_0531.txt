[u]Abbot[/u] The ruler over an abbey, or house
of monks or nuns. It is a special
title. It is not every religious house
whose superior is an abbot or an
abbess. I don't know whether there
are any at all in English speaking
countries now-a-days.

[u]to Abear[/u] To bear, to submit without resistance
to any burden or disagreeable condition.
Many persons, especially in England,
consider this word to be vulgar; but
there is no good reason for it: it
is truly vulgar to object to this
innocent and good old word.
The past tense is [u]abore[/u], and the
participle is [u]aborn[/u]. Thus one might
say "She said she could not abear the
man. Yet at last she abore him well
enough to marry him, and so, un-
abearable as he [ins]had[/ins] seemed, he came
to be aborn by her and [del]by[/del] all her family.
