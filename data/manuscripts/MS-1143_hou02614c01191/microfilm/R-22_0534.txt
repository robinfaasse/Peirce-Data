Ablaze is a modern word formed from
the old phrase "on blaze" which is
no longer said. Thus Gower, a poet
of Chaucer's time said "That casten
fire and flame aboute, so that they
setten all on blaze" which we should
write "who toss fire about so as to
set everything ablaze."
