Pliny says "quod ego abominor"
where we should say "which I,
for my part, pray that God may
turn away from us," or "Away
be the sign." Abominable means
[del]something[/del] [ins]properly[/ins] so horrible as to make
ones flesh creep to think of it, like
unnatural vice; but the word has
been monstrously perverted by many
people. Thus a lady who lived
somewhere in the 14 hundreds
wrote about what she called a
"a bomynable syght of monks"
meaning merely a large body
of them.
