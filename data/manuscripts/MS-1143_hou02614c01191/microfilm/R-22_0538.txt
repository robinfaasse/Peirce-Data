meaning about means on all
sides, all round, so when motion
is spoken of, a motion "about"
anything is to move all round it,
as say from North to North East
then to East and [del]continuing[/del] [ins]continuing[/ins] in
the same sort of motion, going on
from a little north of east to a
little south of east to East South

to South East, and South East by
South, and so on to South South
East to South by East and to South
and on to South by West and
South West, and due West,
and West by North, and North
West and at last back to
West, thus having "boxed the
