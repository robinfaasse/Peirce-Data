But where shall the line be most truly drawn? I reply that the purpose of
this sentence being understood to be to communicate information, any
thing belongs to the interpretant that describes the quality or character
of the fact, anything to the object that, without doing that, distinguishes
this fact from others like it; while a third part of the proposition,
perhaps, must be appropriated to information about the [del]assertion[/del]
manner in which the assertion is made, what warrant is offered for
its truth etc. But I rather incline to think that all this goes to the subject.
