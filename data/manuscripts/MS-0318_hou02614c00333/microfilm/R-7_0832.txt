* I well remember when I was a boy, and my brother Herbert, now our minister at Christiania, was scarce more than a child, one day, as the whole family were at table, some spirit from a "blazer," or "chafing-dish", dropped on the muslin dress of one of the ladies and was kindled; [ins]and[/ins] how instantaneously he jumped up, and did [del]the[/del] the right thing, and how skillfully each motion was adapted to the purpose. I asked him afterward about it, and he told me that since Mrs. Longfellow's death, [del]it was that he had[/del] [ins]often run over in imagination all the details of what ought to be done in such an emergency.[/ins] It was a striking example of a real habit produced by exercises in the imagination.
