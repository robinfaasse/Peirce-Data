When [del]I[/del] my reflections had [ins]once[/ins] reached this point, I was ready to enun-
ciate the principle of pragmatism; and it only remained to decide
upon the proper form of expression. Owing to the intensely practical
nature of the doctrine I chose, as most appropriate, the form of the
maxim, and consequently expressed my pragmatism as follows:
"Consider what effects that might conceivably have practical
bearings you conceive the object of your conception to have; then
[del]these effects[/del] the general mental habit that consists in the
production of these effects is the whole meaning of your concept."
