[pn](Prag 22)[/pn] By calling this quaesitum an ingredient of the
utterer, I mean that where this quaesitum is absent the
utterer cannot be present; and further that where there is no
utterer, it cannot be that this quaesitum together with all the
others of a certain body of "ingredients" should all be present.

so nearly self-evident that I need not insist upon it. A fact

of all study, is that, because this quaesitum will [del]be[/del] function
