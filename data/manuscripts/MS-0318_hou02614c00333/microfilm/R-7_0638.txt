owing to vagueness, is an intellectual interpretant of the term
it defines. But it is equally evident that it cannot be the [u]ulti-[/u]
[u]mate[/u] intellectual interpretant, inasmuch as it is itself a sign, and
a sign of the kind that has itself an intellectual interpretant, which
is thereby an intellectual interpretant of the term [del]which[/del] defined. This
consideration compells us to seek elsewhere than among signs, or
or among concepts, since they are all signs, for the ultimate
intellectual interpretants. This same consideration cuts off
