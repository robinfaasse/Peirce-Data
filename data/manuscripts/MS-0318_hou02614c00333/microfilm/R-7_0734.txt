Now not only is an agreement a mental act, but legal right is a [del]creation[/del] [ins]relation[/ins] of minds. If A lays down B for C to find and take, the [del]mental[/del] purpose is part of the object. If A lays down B, [ins]which[/ins] [del]and[/del] C subsequently picks up, there are either two disconnected facts here, or they are made one by the action of the mind that conceives them as one. If A is composed of B and C, it is by the action of the mind that the two separate objects B and C are regarded as forming one, no matter what reason there may be for [del]so[/del] so conceiving them.
