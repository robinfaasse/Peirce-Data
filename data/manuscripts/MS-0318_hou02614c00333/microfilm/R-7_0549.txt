performance of a piece of concerted [u]music[/u] is a sign. It conveys
and is intended to convey, the composer's musical ideas; but these
usually consist merely in a series of feelings. If a sign produces
any further proper significate effect, it will do so through the
mediation of the emotional interpretant, and such further effect
will always involve an effort. I call it the energetic inter-
pretant. The effort may be a muscular one, as it is in the case
of the command to ground arms; but it is much more
usually an exertion upon the Inner World, a mental effort.
It never can be the meaning of an intellectual concept,
since it is a single act, which such a concept is of a general
nature. But what further kind of effect can there be?
