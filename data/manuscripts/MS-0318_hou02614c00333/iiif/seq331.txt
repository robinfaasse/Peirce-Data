They are the "Listing Numbers" of lowest "grade"; — the chorisy of grade 0, the cyclosy of grade 1, the periphraxy of grade 2, the Apeiry of grade 3, etc. They may all be defined in one statement, thus: The Listing number of grade G of a place of dimensionality [del]D[/del] [ins]D[/ins] is the number of interruptions by obstacles of dimensionality D-G that would have to be established in that place in order that the uninterrupted part of it should be incapable of containing a [del]x[/del] continuously deformable thing of dimensionality G that, while remaining all the while in the uninterrupted part of the place, could not, without rupture or welding of parts, shrink to an indefinite approximation toward losing a unit of dimensionality.
