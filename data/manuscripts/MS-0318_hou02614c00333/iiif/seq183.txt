(Bag 90) the order of a line-singularity suddenly changes. Thus, if the
edge of one part of a surface lies for part of its length along another part
of the surface, its order will be, where it remains an edge, - 1, but where
it becomes a nodal line, + 1, and there will be two points where its order
suddenly changes. The direct point-singularities of a surface are points
where two or more [del]different[/del] [ins]otherwise separate[/ins] sheets are tacked together. They may be called [u]tacks[/u].
