     I now reach the point where I must gather my courage and risk an attempt to define a sign, though the task calls for a subtler intellect than mine. Premising that by [u]intelligence[/u] I shall mean the character common to intelligent feelings, — such as those evoked by listening to a piece of concerted music, — intelligent actions, — though they rise no higher than a horse's ordinary responses to [del]his[/del] [ins]even a[/ins] driver's touch, — and intelligent thought, I will suggest that a sign is anything which being intelligently determined by an Object in its turn intelligently determines an Interpretant, which thus becomes mediately determined by the Object.
