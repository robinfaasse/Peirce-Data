Still less [del]does[/del] [ins]would[/ins] it suit my way of thinking to say that the meaning of a concept expresses itself in “experience to be expected.” It is obvious that nothing expresses itself in anything that it does not create or determine; and therefore a concept cannot express itself in any “experience”; though it may partially express itself in a muscular effort whose consequences may be experienced. To this, it is true, it may very justly be replied that the phase does [fig]figure[/fig] not represent the meaning as expressing itself in the experience itself, but in the [u]expectation[/u] of the experience; and this comes so extremely near what I apprehend to be the genuine pragmatistic position, as to show that the author of the phrase must be a genuine pragmatist.
