larity is the excess of the number of ways
of leaving it over the number of ways of
leaving an ordinary place. Thus the order
of an extremity of a line, or the edge of a surface,
or the bounding surface of a solid is -1. The
order of a fork of a line [del]Y[/del] is 2 less than the
number of branches meeting at the fork. The
[del]number[/del] order of a split of a surface is 2 less
than the number of sheets that [del]meet are[/del]
[ins]joined[/ins] along the split. The order of a tack may
be taken as one less than the number of
