The periphraxis of a surface is the number of times it must be
pierced to render it incapable of containing any edgeless surface.
The cyclosis of a surface is the number of times, after its periphraxis
has be destroyed by holes, that it must be cut from edge to edge to
render it incapable of containing a non-singular line that cannot
shrink to a point by a continuous deformation within the surface.
