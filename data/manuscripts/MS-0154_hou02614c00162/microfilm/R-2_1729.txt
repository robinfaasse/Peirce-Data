[u]On the Problem of Coloring a Map.[/u]
[del]X[/del] ———

Let us define a [u]point[/u] as an indivisible place; a [u]particle[/u] as a movable thing that at any one instant is contained in a point. A [u]line[/u] as a place that can contain all the places where a particle can be during a time and no others; a [u]filament[/u] as a movable thing that at any one instant is contained in a line but at no instant in a point. A [u]surface[/u] as a place that can contain all the places where a filament can be while it moves out of its place.
