Anything which has ultimate integrant parts may
be called a [u]collection[/u] of those parts. This explains
how I propose to use the word "collection", but it does
not analyze the idea. I know of no idea of logic
more difficult to analyze. A collection is an object
distinct from its parts. A [del]city[/del] nation is not a man.
It is an individual object whose existence is constituted
by the existence of certain men. The existence of the
