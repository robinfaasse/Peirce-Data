Anything which has ultimate integrant parts may
be called a collection of those parts. This explains
how I propose to use the word "collection," but it does
not analyze the idea. I know of no logical idea more
difficult to analyze. The first point to be noted is that
a collection is a single object distinct from its parts.
A nation is not a man, nor is it all the men it contains;
[del]for[/del] the men are many but the [del]co[/del] nation is an indi-
vidual object. The second point to be noted is that the
mode of being of a collection is such as results from a
possibility, — namely, the possibility of our regarding
many objects as constituting a single object; while the
mode of being of the ultimate parts may be of this kind
or they may be arbitrary independently existing things.
