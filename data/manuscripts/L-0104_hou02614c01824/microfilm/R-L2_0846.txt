[date]May 1st / 96[/date]

AMERICAN JOURNAL OF MATHEMATICS
JOHNS HOPKINS UNIVERSITY
BALTIMORE

My dear Peirce:—

I wrote you first about Pierpont's paper a year ago. You replied briefly to that note. After waiting several months I wrote you again & still later a third time, but receiving no answer to either letter.
