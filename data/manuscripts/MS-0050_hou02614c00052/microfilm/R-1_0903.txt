(8) Let $r_a$ be the distance from the centre at which
the upward motion ceases; so that for larger values of $r$
there is no velocity. This will be expressed by
$$v_a = 0 = \sqrt{\frac{k}{r_a^2} - C}$$
whence
$$C = \frac{k}{r_a^2}$$
and we have generally
$$v = \sqrt{k \left( \frac{1}{r^2} - \frac{1}{r_a^2} \right)}.$$
The initial velocity is
$$v_c = \sqrt{k \left( \frac{1}{r_c^2} - \frac{1}{r_a^2} \right)}.$$

(9) Since $v = \frac{dr}{dt}$ we have from the above value of $v$
$$dt = \frac{dr}{\sqrt{k} \sqrt{\frac{1}{r^2} - \frac{1}{r_a^2}}}$$
The integral of this gives
$$r = \sqrt{r_a^2 + 2 \sqrt{k} \sqrt{1 - \frac{r_c^2}{r_a^2}} (t - t_c) - \frac{k}{r_a^2} (t - t_c)^2}$$
