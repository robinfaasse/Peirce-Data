(116) The rules of integration require us, in the first place,
to ascertain certain relations between the magnitudes of
the constants, since the formulae applicable in calculation
vary with these relations. We found in (13) that
$$\frac{A^2}{k} = (\frac{1}{v_c^2} - \frac{1}{v_a^2}) P^2$$
and in (12), we found $P < v_c$. Hence
$$\frac{A^2}{k} < 1 - \frac{v_c^2}{v_a^2}$$
and $v_c < v_a$, since $v_a$ is the maximum value that $v$ can
ever attain. Hence $\frac{A^2}{k}$ is a positive quantity less than
unity.
