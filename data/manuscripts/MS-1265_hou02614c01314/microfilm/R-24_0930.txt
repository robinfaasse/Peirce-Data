II. Recorde's second edition added finger counting.
Revised and enlarged the chapter on [del]besize[/del] [ins]the size[/ins] of wheat.

III. Dee's first edition seems to have simplified some parts. Thus he omits
(I suppose) "Then will I caste the whole charge, of one monethes commons at
Oxford with battelyng also", because students of arithmetic had not yet
gone to Oxford.
He rewrites the proofs of addition.
In subtraction (p. 86 of ed. of 1594) Dee adds 2" line from bottom "between the double line"
inserts 1 after "article" and changes "places" to "place". Page 87, 4" line, he adds
"and so it appeareth I have delivered in all 1452 crownes". And so on.
The two examples on p. 92 are changed.
Under multiplication he draws a cross etc.

IV Dee's second edition I think added everything in the Ed of 1594 from p. 521
to the end, with other matter suppressed by Mellis.

V. VI. VII. I cannot distinguish between Mellis's three editions.
