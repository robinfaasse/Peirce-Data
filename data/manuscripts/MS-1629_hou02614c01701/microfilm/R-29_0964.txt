[pn]§30.5[/pn] [u]Integrate[/u] $\frac{(\sqrt{ax+b} + c)^n}{\sqrt{ax+b}}$

Let $y = \sqrt{ax+b}$ $x = \frac{y^2-b}{a}$ $D_y x = \frac{2y}{a}$

$\int_{x} \frac{(\sqrt{ax+b} + c)^n}{\sqrt{ax+b}} = \int_{y} \frac{2}{a} (y+c)^n = \frac{2(y+c)^{n+1}}{a(n+1)} = \frac{2(\sqrt{ax+b} + c)^{n+1}}{a(n+1)}$
