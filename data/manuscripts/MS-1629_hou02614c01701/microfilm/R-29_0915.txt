[u]The Parthenon.[/u]

The Parthenon was a temple of the goddess Minerva the guardian deity of Athens. It was situated in that city on the summit of a lofty hill called the Acropolis, which contained most of the temples and public buildings of the city. It was the most beautiful building in the world, and standing as it did among the monuments of the art of the most tasteful of nations, it stood distinguished among them as a thing even more divine.
