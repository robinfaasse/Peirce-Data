[u]§28.1[/u] To integrate [del]$\frac{x^2}{(x^2+2x+2)^2 (x+1)^3 (x-1)}$[/del]

Represent this by $\frac{x^2}{A^2 (x+1)^3 (x-1)}$
[u]First[/u] Let $x_0=1$ $n=1$ $\varphi'x_0 = V = \frac{x^2}{A^2 (x_0+1)^3}$
$A=5$ $A^2=25$ $(x+1)=2$ $(x+1)^3=8$ $A^2(x+1)^3 = 200$
$\varphi'x_0 = \frac{1}{200}$ $\int^1 = \frac{1}{200} \log (x-1)$
[u]Second[/u] Let $x_0=-1$
[u]1st[/u] $n=3$ $\varphi'x_0 = V = \frac{x^2}{A^2 (x-1)}$
$x_0^2=1$ $A=1$ $(x_0-1)=-2$ $\varphi'x_0 = -\frac{1}{2}$ $\int^3 = -\frac{1}{4(x+1)^2}$
[u]2nd[/u] $n=2$ $\varphi'x_0 = DV$
$\log V = 2 \log x_0 - 2 \log A - \log (x_0-1)$
$D \log V = \frac{DV}{V} = \frac{2x_0}{x_0^2} - \frac{2DA}{A} - \frac{1}{(x-1)}$
$DV = \frac{2x_0}{A^2(x-1)} - \frac{2VDA}{A} - \frac{V}{A^2(x-1)^2} = -x_0 - 2DA + \frac{1}{2}$
$DA = 2x+2 = 0$ $DV = \varphi'x_0 = \frac{3}{2}$ $\int^2 = +\frac{3}{2(x+1)}$
[u]3rd[/u] $n=1$ $\varphi'x_0 = D^2V$
$D \frac{DV}{V} = \frac{D^2V}{V} - \frac{(DV)^2}{V^2} = \frac{2x_0}{x_0^2} - \frac{2D^2A}{A} + \frac{2DA^2}{A^2} - \frac{4x_0^2}{x_0^4} + \frac{1}{(x-1)^2} =$
$-\frac{D^2V}{2} + 9 = -2 - 2D^2A - 1 + \frac{1}{4} = -2D^2A - \frac{11}{4}$
$-\frac{D^2V}{2} = -\frac{49}{4}$ $D^2V = \frac{49}{2} = \varphi'x_0$ $\int^1 = -\frac{49}{2} \log (x+1)$
