§78 Ex 2

Find the length of the arc of the parabola whose equation
is $y^2 = 2px$ counted from the vertex.

Since at the vertex $y=0$ $x=0$

$$s = \int_{x_0} Ds$$

$$Ds = \sqrt{1 + (D_y)^2}$$
$$y = \sqrt{2px}$$
$$D_y = \sqrt{\frac{p}{2x}}$$
$$(D_y)^2 = \frac{p}{2x}$$
$$\sqrt{1 + (D_y)^2} = \sqrt{1 + \frac{p}{2x}}$$
$$\frac{1}{x} = z$$
$$\sqrt{1 + (D_y)^2} = \sqrt{1 + \frac{p}{2} z}$$
$$\int \sqrt{1 + (D_y)^2} = \int \sqrt{1 + \frac{p}{2} z} \ D_z \frac{1}{z} =$$
$$\int \sqrt{1 + \frac{p}{2} z} \ (-\frac{1}{z^2}) =$$
$$\int v \left(-\frac{p^2}{4(v^2-1)^2}\right) \frac{4v}{p} = \int - \frac{p v^2}{(v^2-1)^2}$$

[marginalia]
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{p}{2} z + 1}$$
[/marginalia]

To perform this integration.

$$- \frac{p v^2}{(v+1)^2 (v-1)^2}$$

$$x_0 = -1$$
$$n = 2 \ \ \ f'x_0 = - \frac{p}{4}$$
$$- \frac{p}{4(v+1)}$$
