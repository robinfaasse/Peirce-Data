[del]Valentine to[/del]
[del]Miss Lily Greenough[/del]
Cheeks where rose and lily blend,
Coral lips, and eyes which send
Lightning glance or liquid beauty
These but poor attractions seem
Till the soul their grace inform
And these outward beauties warm.
Thou in whom all charms combine,
Be thou but my Valentine!
What could maddest bard or boy
Dream beyond this crowning joy?

[u]Valentine[/u]

Nor wintry sky, nor chilling air,
Nor frost, nor sleet, nor [del]snow[/del]
Congeals my truest love for thee
I laugh at 5° below.

The flame that glows within my heart
Ah! let it reach thine own.
'Will make all seasons warm for thee
Blot out the frigid Zone.
Plant a fair tree of living green
On every frozen heath,
Verdure will hang upon the boughs
And roses bloom beneath.
