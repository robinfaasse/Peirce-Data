[date]21 Janvier.[/date]
Left Strassburg 10 1/2 Am Ar. Würzburg 9 PM
Long journey, [del]not very[/del] tiresome
Expected to go on to Nürnberg
at one stretch but found it more
convenient to put up at the Rus-
sischer Hof in Würzburg. Beautiful
station in Würzburg.

[date]22 Janvier.[/date]
Left Würzburg 4 Am Ar. Nürnberg 8 Am
Having been called at 3 we caught
the train. C had slept well I wretch-
edly. Got to Nürnberg tried our Hotel
Had good room & wretched breakfast,
so I who tried for tea. Good says C.
I half sick but dressed & went to St. Sebaldus
& picture gallery. Consequently made sick
She saw also Lorenzkirche, Frauenkirche
& streets etc. Nürnberg wonderful
place.

[date]23 Janvier[/date]
Left Nürnberg 12 M Ar. Leipzig 9 1/2 PM
I sick all night & morning with headache
Took opium pill. C. went & bought ivory
carving etc. At last moment was
found able to start. Journey very
tedious. Magnificent apartment at
Hotel Hauffe. Amy came at mid-
night.

Leipzig. [date]24 Janvier.[/date]
Showed Amy things & moved to
another Apartment.
