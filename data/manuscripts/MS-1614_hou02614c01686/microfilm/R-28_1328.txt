[date]1er Février.[/date]
London
I unsuccessfully attempted
to get her clothes.
Evening went to Hermann's. Much
entertained.

[date]2 Février.[/date]
London.
Walked in Bond St. Continued
investigations by L. [del]sh[/del]
Evening to Gaiety again. I
much pleased with Toole.

[date]3 Février.[/date]
London.
I in house all day. Miss Cook
called in morning. I bought dress.
C. went with Winlock to Browning's.
I wrote to her father. Took walk
together Pall. Weather as everyday
this year cloudy.
Evening at home.

[date]4 Février.[/date]
London.
I went to National Gallery, then
to Popular Concert Pall.
Evening at home.
