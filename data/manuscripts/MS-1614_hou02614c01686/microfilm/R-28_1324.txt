In pocket 520 fr.

[date]18 Janvier.[/date]
Left Bern 1 3/4 PM Arrived Basel
Spent morning looking about Bern. Funny
fountains. One with ogre, another with bear
etc. Bears everywhere. Bought one. Saw
clock strike. Bears & cock. Visited
cathedral. Gothic in its decadence. Not
cared for. Some fine old glass & curi-
ous door. Also catholic cathedral miniature
of Rheims. Bern very interesting town.
At Basel put up at Trois Rois

[date]19 Janvier.[/date]
Left Basel 2 1/2 PM. Arrived Strasburg 9.
[del]Looked about Basel[/del] Got left in morning
owing to not knowing there were two sta-
tions. Snowing. Visited Münster. Fine &
very old cathedral in the early Romanesque
style. Quite in love with Trois rois. Ar-
rived in Strasburg with but little de-
tention, trunks all right, comfortable
rooms, grateful hearts, put up at the
Stadt Paris.

In pocket 356 fr.

[date]20 Janvier.[/date]
Strasburg
Nice room. Coffee in room. Z in
bed. C. sick all day but able to
crawl about. Saw only cathedral.
Did not ascend tower. C bought
a lorgnier. In evening I read
Lord Whewell's Notes on German
Churches. Very pleased. No dinner
for much job for luncheon.
