[date]25 Janvier.[/date]
Leipzig

In evening opera. Charming
& artistic. C. did not go. Nursing
his horrid cold.

[date]26 Janvier.[/date]
Leipzig

In evening concert. Very fine.
In morning I went to hear Amy
play.

[date]27 Janvier.[/date]
Left Leipzig at 7 P.M.
Amy got off at noon, after a plea-
sant meeting.

[date]28 Janvier.[/date]
Ar. Cöln at 8 A.M.
Terrible journey. No heaters in
cars. Excessively cold. I increased
the cold she caught on 25th. Went
to Hotel Holland but did not like
it & changed to Hotel du Nord. Much
better. Nice room.
