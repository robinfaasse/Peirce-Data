[date]29 Janvier.[/date]

Left Cöln 11 AM Arrived Dover 2 AM.
C. went out & saw S. Gereon St. Mar-
ria im Capitol SS Aposteln. Then
started off. Hotel Disch very good.
Got Johann H Gulichsplatz without duty.
Very tiresome journey especially
on boat. Put up at Lord Warden
Hotel.

[date]30 Janvier.[/date]

Left Dover. 9 AM Arrived Canterbury 10 AM
Left Canterbury 4 PM Arrived London 6 PM
Lord Warden Hotel very comfortable
At Canterbury Royal Fountain
our headquarters. Cathedral
visited. Put up in London at
Sherman's lodging house 1 Clarges St
May Fair.
C. went to Royalty Theatre Aladdin 2nd
Delighted
