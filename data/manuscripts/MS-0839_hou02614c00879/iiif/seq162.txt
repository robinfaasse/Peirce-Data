[u]Liberties of the Cross and Stigma.[/u]

Liberty 1. The cross and stigma are each associative;
that is
(x + y) + z = x + (y + z),
(x . y) . z = x . (y . z).

Liberty 2. Any aggregant may be inserted [ins]in any complicant or in the whole proposition[/ins] or any complicant dropped [del]from any aggregant or from the whole proposition[/del] without loss of truth. Any aggregant may be dropped or any complicant inserted without gain of truth.

Liberty 3. An aggregant [ins]or an aggregant[/ins] of which the whole or a complicant is identical with a coördinate aggregant, or a complicant of which the whole or an aggregant is identical with a coördinate complicant, [del]is[/del] without effect.

Liberty 4. The negative of an aggregation is equivalent to the [del]complicate[/del] [ins]complicate[/ins] of the negative of the aggregants and the negative of a complicate is the aggregate of the negatives of the complicants. That is
[u]x + y[/u] = [u]x[/u] . [u]y[/u],
[u]x . y[/u] = [u]x[/u] + [u]y[/u].

Liberty 5. [u]w[/u] + x = [u]w[/u],         0 . x = 0,
w . x = x,              0 + x = x,
x + [u]x[/u] = [u]w[/u],             x . [u]x[/u] = 0.
