$$\begin{pmatrix}
r \cos \theta & r \sin \theta & 1 \\
r_1 \cos \theta_1 & r_1 \sin \theta_1 & 1 \\
r_2 \cos \theta_2 & r_2 \sin \theta_2 & 1 
\end{pmatrix} = 0$$

$r \cos \theta (r_1 \sin \theta_1 - r_2 \sin \theta_2) + r \sin \theta (r_2 \cos \theta_2 - r_1 \cos \theta_1) + [ins](r_1 r_2 \cos (\theta_1 + \theta_2)[/ins] [del]r_1 r_2 \cos \theta_1 \cos \theta_2[/del] = 0$

$r r_1 \sin (\theta_1 - \theta) + r r_2 \sin (\theta - \theta_2) + r_1 r_2 \cos (\theta_1 + \theta_2) = 0$

If $r = \infty$ $\tan \theta = \frac{r_1 \sin \theta_1 - r_2 \sin \theta_2}{r_1 \cos \theta_1 - r_2 \cos \theta_2}$ $\frac{\sin (\theta + 90^\circ)}{\cos \theta + 90^\circ} = - \frac{\cos \theta}{\sin \theta} = - \cot \theta$
