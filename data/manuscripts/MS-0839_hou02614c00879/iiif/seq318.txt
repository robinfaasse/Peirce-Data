What algebra represents all the diagonal
products of a determinant, considered as so
[del]many[/del] matrices formed each of them of all
the constituents of a diagonal product of a
determinant?

[u]1[ins]st[/ins][/u]. Consider the matrix

A B
B A

Let [math]\alpha[/math] [math]\beta[/math] be such matrices that the one just written
is

A[math]\alpha[/math] + B[math]\beta[/math].

Then evidently if one write

[u]h[/u] = [frac]1[/frac]/2([math]\alpha[/math]+[math]\beta[/math])
[u]i[/u] = [frac]1[/frac]/2([math]\alpha[/math]-[math]\beta[/math])

[u]h[/u] and [u]i[/u] have the following multiplication table

[fig]
   h i
h |h 0|
i |0 i|
[/fig]

The algebra is therefore the compound of two ordinary
single algebras.
