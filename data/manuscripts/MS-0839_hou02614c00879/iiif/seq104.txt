Besides the principal motive for the study of logic, namely,
to acquire the art of reasoning, there is another secondary
motive, namely to understand how other people reason,
and what they mean when they talk about reasonings.
This makes it desirable to mention a good many things
which, in themselves, are hardly worth notice; and, in particu-
lar, to explain all the phrases of logic which are in men's
mouths, although they [ins]often[/ins] express quite superficial conceptions.
