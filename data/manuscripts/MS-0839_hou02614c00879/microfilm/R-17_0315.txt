A polar graph is a graph which, if it has more spots than
one, is composed of two parts, both of which are polar graphs,
and one of them, which is [u]negative[/u], is encircled by an oval line
(not a bond.) The other part, which is [u]positive[/u], and includes the
bonds connecting its spots with those of the negative part, is
not encircled. A bond both whose extremities lie within the
same oval is encircled by that oval; otherwise not. In the in-
terpretation of a polar graph, those spots and bonds which are
encircled by an odd number of ovals have a different character
from those which are encircled by an even number of ovals.
