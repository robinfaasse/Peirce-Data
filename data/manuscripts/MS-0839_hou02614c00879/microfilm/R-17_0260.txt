What algebra represents all the [del]diagonal[/del] [del]products of a determinant, considered as so[/del] [del]many[/del] matrices formed each of them of all the constituents of a diagonal product of a determinant?

[pn]1[u]st[/u][/pn] Consider the matrix

$$\begin{matrix} A & B \\ B & A \end{matrix}$$

Let $\alpha$ $\beta$ be such matrices that the one just written is
$$A \alpha + B \beta.$$

Then evidently if we write
$$h = \frac{1}{2} (\alpha + \beta)$$
$$i = \frac{1}{2} (\alpha - \beta)$$
$h$ and $i$ have the following multiplication table

[fig]Multiplication table for h and i[/fig]

The algebra is therefore the compound of two ordinary single algebras.
