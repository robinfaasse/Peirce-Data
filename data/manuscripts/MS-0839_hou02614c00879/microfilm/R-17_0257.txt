$$\nu + \xi + o + \pi = \rho + \sigma + \tau + \upsilon = \phi + \chi + \psi + \omega.$$
Also,
$$\begin{array}{ccc}
\alpha + \beta = \nu + \xi & \varepsilon + \xi = \nu + o & \iota + \kappa = \nu + \pi \\
\alpha + \gamma = \rho + \sigma & \varepsilon + \eta = \rho + \tau & \iota + \lambda = \rho + \upsilon \\
\alpha + \delta = \phi + \chi & \varepsilon + \theta = \phi + \psi & \iota + \mu = \phi + \omega
\end{array}$$
It is plain that these equations are all independent, and it is not difficult to see that no more independent equations subsist. These equations being 14 in number, a ten-fold algebra is required for the expression of the substitutions.

Assume the ten relatives [u]$h$[/u], [u]$i$[/u], [u]$j$[/u], [u]$k$[/u], [u]$l$[/u], [u]$m$[/u], [u]$n$[/u], [u]$o$[/u], [u]$p$[/u], [u]$q$[/u], such that
$$\begin{aligned}
h &= \tfrac{1}{4} (\alpha + \beta + \gamma + \delta) \\
i &= \tfrac{1}{4} (\varepsilon + \xi + \eta - \theta) \\
j &= \tfrac{1}{4} (\alpha - \beta + \gamma - \delta) \\
k &= \tfrac{1}{4} (\iota - \kappa - \lambda + \mu) \\
l &= \tfrac{1}{4} (\iota - \kappa + \lambda - \mu) \\
m &= \tfrac{1}{4} (\varepsilon - \xi + \eta - \theta) \\
n &= \tfrac{1}{4} (\iota + \kappa - \lambda - \mu) \\
o &= \tfrac{1}{4} (\alpha - \beta - \gamma + \delta) \\
p &= \tfrac{1}{4} (\alpha + \beta - \gamma - \delta) \\
q &= \tfrac{1}{4} (\varepsilon - \xi - \eta + \theta)
\end{aligned}$$
