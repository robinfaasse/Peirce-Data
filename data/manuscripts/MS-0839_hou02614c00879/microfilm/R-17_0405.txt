[u]Liberties of the Cross and Stigma[/u].

[u]Liberty 1[/u]. The cross and stigma are each associative;
that is
[indent]
$(x + y) + z = x + (y + z)$;
$(x \cdot y) \cdot z = x \cdot (y \cdot z)$.
[/indent]

[u]Liberty 2[/u]. Any aggregant may be inserted [ins]in any complicant or in the whole proposition[/ins] or [del]from any aggregant or from the whole proposition[/del] any complicant dropped without loss of truth. Any aggregant may be dropped or any complicant inserted without gain of truth.

[u]Liberty 3[/u]. An aggregant of which the whole or a complicant [ins]or an aggregant[/ins] is identical with a coordinate aggregant, or a complicant of which the whole or an aggregant is identical with a coordinate complicant, is without effect.

[u]Liberty 4[/u]. The negative of an aggregation is equivalent to the complication of the negatives of the aggregants and the negative of a complicate is the aggregate of the negatives of the complicants. That is
[indent]
$\overline{x + y} = \overline{x} \cdot \overline{y}$,
$\overline{x \cdot y} = \overline{x} + \overline{y}$.
[/indent]

[u]Liberty 5[/u].
[indent]
$\infty + x = \infty$,
$\infty \cdot x = x$,
$x + \overline{x} = \infty$,
$0 \cdot x = 0$,
$0 + x = x$,
$x \cdot \overline{x} = 0$.
[/indent]
