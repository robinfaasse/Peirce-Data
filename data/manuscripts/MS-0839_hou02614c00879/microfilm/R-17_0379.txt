Suppose
$u^2 = 0$     $uv = vu$

$u(u + v + w) = uv + uw$
$(u + v + w)u = uv + wu$

Suppose $uw$ and $wu$ dissimilar
$uv = p uw + q wu$
$(p+1) uw + q wu \approx p uw + (q+1) wu$ [fig]Concentric circles with tangent line[/fig]
[del]$\approx$[/del] $1 + \frac{1}{p} = \frac{q}{1 + \frac{1}{q}}$
$\frac{1}{p} + \frac{1}{q} + \frac{1}{pq} = 0$
$p + q = -1$

$u(v + rw) = uv + r uw$ [fig]Intersecting lines[/fig]
$(v + rw)u = uv + r wu$

$(p+r) uw + q wu$
$p uw + (q+r) wu$
$\frac{p+r}{p} = \frac{q}{q+r}$     $r(p+q) + r^2 = 0$
[del]$\leftrightarrow$[/del] $p+q+r = 0$

$(u + nv)(v - (p+q)w) = uv - (p+q)uw + nv^2 - n(p+q)vw$
$(v - (p+q)w)(u + nv) = uv - (p+q)wu + nv^2 - n(p+q)wv$
$q \frac{wu - uw}{uw - wu}$
