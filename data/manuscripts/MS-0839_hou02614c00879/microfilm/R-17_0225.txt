[u]Com of Notion[/u]

We cannot think two distinct propositions
at the same time. $\therefore$ we cannot think
two predicates directly of the same subject.
We cannot think without thinking a sub-
ject, i. e. we cannot think two independent
predicates at once.

Hence all composition of ideas is a thought
of our thought. [del]Hence[/del]

the pred is [ins]not[/ins] contained in the subject. For we
uphold the unity of all substance. But it
is when pred is not contained in our thought
of the subject.
