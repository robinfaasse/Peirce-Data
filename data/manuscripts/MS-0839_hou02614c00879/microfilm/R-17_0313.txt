A few pages appear to be missing. It is not so.
Merely, a few rejections have been made.

After [u]the work is accepted[/u], I shall be willing to
make a few additions; and especially a Glossarial
Index, which my contract with the Century Company
enables me to do.

I retain one chapter only till tomorrow or next
day to work up the style a little.

The [u]Exercises[/u] were accidentally left at home.
They will be sent on my return. That makes an appen-
dix of a few pages.

In printing formulae I desire all l.c. letters
not otherwise indicated to be Italics. All Capitals
to be Roman of an Antique Pattern.

My other logical writings have been much admired.
I am sure the whole world must admit this book
is from every point of view. Substance, style,
suggestiveness, awakening influence, the best of all
my writings by far.
