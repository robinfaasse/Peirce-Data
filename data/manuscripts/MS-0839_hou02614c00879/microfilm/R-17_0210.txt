A very pretty illustration of the [del]importance[/del]
[del]of distinguishing intensive & extensive meaning[/del]
is this:—

Every quadrilateral triangle is three-sided.
Every quadrilateral triangle is four-sided.
<span style="padding-left: 20px;">∴ Some four-sided things are three-sided.</span>

This conclusion is false; yet the premises are true.
The premises are understood [u]intensively[/u], and
the conclusion [u]extensively[/u].
<span style="padding-left: 150px;">C. S. P.</span>
