(5). If $A \prec B$ is scriptible and $A$ is any necessary formula then $B$ is scriptible. Thus if any of the following is scriptible
$(A \prec A) \prec B$
$[A \prec (B \prec B)] \prec B$
$B$ is scriptible

(6) If $A \prec B$ is scriptible and $A$ is any necessary formula then
$B \prec Z$
$B \prec (C \prec Z)$
and so on are scriptible.

(7) Hence the following are scriptible
$Z \prec (A \prec Z)$
$Z \prec [A \prec (B \prec Z)]$
and so forth
$[(A \prec A) \prec Z] \prec Z$
$\{ [A \prec (B \prec B)] \prec Z \} \prec Z$
$[(A \prec A) \prec Z] \prec (B \prec Z)$
$[(A \prec A) \prec Z] \prec [B \prec (C \prec Z)]$
