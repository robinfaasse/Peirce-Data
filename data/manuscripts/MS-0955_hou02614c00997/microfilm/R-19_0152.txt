But, it will be said, You forget the laws which

there is the most positive historic proof that
innate truths are particularly uncertain, [ins]and[/ins] mixed
up with error, and therefore [u]a fortiori[/u] not without
exception. This historical proof is, of course,
not infallible; but it is very strong. Therefore, I ask
how do you know that apriori truth is certain, excep-
tionless, and exact? You cannot know it by
reasoning. For that would be subject to uncertainty
and inexactitude. Then, it must amount to this that

judgments at their own valuation, without cri-
