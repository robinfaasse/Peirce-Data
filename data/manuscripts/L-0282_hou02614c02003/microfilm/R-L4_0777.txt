THE FORUM.
253 FIFTH AVENUE, NEW YORK.

[date]June 21st[/date]

Prof. Charles S. Peirce,
Dear Sir:

If you are endeavoring to carry out the idea that you proposed to me, of writing a new article, will you please let me know when you will be able to do so. To date, they hope now that the papers on Spiritualism follow one another quite closely, and already several months have passed since Mr. Hodgson's appeared. And the length — please remember not to exceed 5000 words.

Yours very truly,
L. S. Metcalf
