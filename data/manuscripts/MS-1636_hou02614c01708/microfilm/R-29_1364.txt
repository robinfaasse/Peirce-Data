[u]Article IV Meetings[/u]. §1
Regular meetings of the society shall be held once in two
weeks during term-time, beginning with the second Mon-
day of the first term & the first monday of the second
term.

The time of meeting shall be 7.30 P.M. and that of
adjournment 10 P.M.
The meetings shall be strictly private & the proceedings
secret.
The order of business shall be as follows:-
1 Calling of roll
2 Record of last meeting
3 Editor's Paper
4 Business in this order.
1 Election of officers
2 Votes on amendments to constitution
3 Election of members
4 Reports of Committees
5 Miscellaneous Business
