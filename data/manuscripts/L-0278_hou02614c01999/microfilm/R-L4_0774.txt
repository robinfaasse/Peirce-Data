[u]Association of Ohio Teachers[/u]
Mathematics and Science
Thos. E. McKinney, Sec'y

Marietta, Ohio, [date]Dec. 7, 1904[/date]

Professor C. S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Professor Peirce:-

In a recent issue of the "Nation" is a brief notice of a remarkable memoir on Logical Geometry read by you before the National Academy of Sciences at its last meeting. May I venture to inquire who publishes or will publish the memoir. While I may not be able to follow your investigation, I infer from the notice that your researches have the most profound significance for the science of Geometry and desire to follow them more closely if I can.

Very truly yours
Thos. E. McKinney.
