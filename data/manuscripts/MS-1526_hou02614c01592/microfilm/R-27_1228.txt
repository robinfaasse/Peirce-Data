It follows from these facts that the most important
consideration in playing backgammon is that of obstruc-
tions, namely, obstructions to entering and obstructions to
play after entering. The player should endeavor to create these
obstructions for the adversary while avoiding them himself.

Since these obstructions entangle matters and are dangerous
for both parties, if a player has decidedly the best of the
game, yet not overwhelmingly so, he should [del]direct[/del] [ins]pay his[/ins] chief
attention to avoiding obstructions himself; while if he is
somewhat behind he should care less for that than de-
taining his adversary at all hazards; for if the two sets of
men are once separate, he will probably be beaten.
