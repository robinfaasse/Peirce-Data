[u]Initial Furnishing of the Board.[/u] At the beginning of the
game each player has five men on his own size-point and
three on the deuce-point of his outer table, two on the adversary's
ace-point and five on the size-point of the adversary's outer table.

[u]Counting the Games.[/u] The player who bears off all his
men first wins. If the other player has borne a man, the game
is called a [del]hit[/del] [ins]or single game[/ins], and the winner throws first in the following game.
If the losing player has not borne a man but is out of the
winner's inner table and has no man up, the game is called
a [u]gammon[/u], or double game; and the players "throw for throws,"
that is throw as at the beginning to determine who throws first.
If the losing player has [del]not borne a man or[/del] [ins]not borne a[/ins] man and has a man
"up" or in the adversary's inner table, the game is called a
[u]backgammon[/u], or triple game; and the losing player throws
first in the succeeding game.
