[u]Bearing, or throwing off.[/u]
Throwing off, or "bearing." When a player has all of his men "home", that is, in his own inner table (none, therefore, being "up") he may, instead of moving, throw off, into the middle of the board, a man from that point of his table [del]what[/del] whose number is the same as that he has to play. Moreover, if he has no man on a point numbered as high as that, he may throw one off from his highest point, instead. This is called "bearing" or "bearing off" the men. A player is not obliged to bear if he can play; nor is he obliged to wait till he cannot play. Doublets have the same effect in throwing off as in other parts of the play.

* Since the game gains no interest by this violation of a general principle, players are counselled to agree "that a player shall not be compelled to play [del]so[/del] so as to be able to play thereafter."
