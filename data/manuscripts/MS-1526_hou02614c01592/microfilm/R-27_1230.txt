[u]BACKGAMMON[/u]

Backgammon is a game for two, celebrated for its respectability.

It is played upon a Backgammon-board. This is a box covered with leather inside and out. Laquered [del]boxes[/del] [ins]boards[/ins] are intolerable for the racket the dice make upon them. The Backgammon board when closed imitates two volumes. These are always labelled on the back "History of —" some country. The different sizes are regularly labelled with the names of different countries. The "History of England" is a small size, the "History of China" is a very agreeable one for use. Two volumes are hinged opposite their backs, and being opened forms two trays. Upon the outside, or bottom, of the trays is a checker board. This is turned down in playing Backgammon. The board is placed between the two players with the line of hinges running from one to the other. From the side of the board nearest each player in each tray [del]are run[/del] wedge shaped pieces of leather, six in each tray [ins]placed side by[/ins] alternately red and black, run a third of the way toward the other player. They are called points. The six points in one tray near one player are called his "table", and the six points opposite them in the same tray make the other player's "table".
