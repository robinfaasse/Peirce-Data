Dear Sir:

I am sorry to say that our Backgammon experts agree that there is nothing worth while in the manuscript by the late Professor Peirce.

At the time of its writing it must have been quite remarkable, but now it looks quite different. The early stages of the article are to elementary, while the latter part of it is something that has been figured out more thoroughly. The mathematical laws are now covered in a number of books which have been published.

Thank you, however for submitting the matter to me. I return it herewith.

Very truly yours,
Julian S. Mason,

Professor James H. Woods
Emerson Hall
Harvard University
Cambridge, Mass.
