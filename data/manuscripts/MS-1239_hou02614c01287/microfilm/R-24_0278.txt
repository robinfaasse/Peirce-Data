The following verbs are exceptions to this rule.
Renoncer, tarder, attendre, balancer, [ins]hésiter[/ins] répugner, Haïr.

Verbs of desiring and purposing some of them take [u]à[/u] and some [u]de[/u]. The following take [u]à[/u].
aimer, aspirer, chercher, conspirer, pencher, penser, persévérer, persister, songer, travailler, tenir.

The following take [u]de[/u]
bruler, désirer [generally no preposition], souhaiter [ditto], choisir, tenter, parler, entreprendre, jurer, méditer, menacer, présumer, promettre.

When the [del]subject[/del] [ins]principal verb has[/ins] [del]to[/del] [ins]a meaning[/ins] such as to permit the subject of the infinitive to be different from the principal subject.

Verbs of accusing and punishing [del]& pardoning[/del] are followed by [u]de[/u] with an infinitive expressing the fault. Verbs of condemning [del]& pardoning[/del] by [u]à[/u] with an infinitive expressing the punishment.
