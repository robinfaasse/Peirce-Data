[date]March 2, 1901.[/date]

Prof. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Professor Peirce,

Pray pardon the delay in the matter of the Dictionary. I have been so busy with other things that it has escaped my mind again and again.

There is just now nothing that we can ask you to do in the way of work. We are simply selling the Dictionary and making a few trifling repairs from time to time. Besides I have never had a chance to fairly examine and make use of your notes. How would it do for me to send you your copy for you to use until we need it, as we may in a general revision of the Dictionary—something which, however, will doubtless not occur for several years. You could write me a note stating that it is the property of The Century Co., so that it could be returned to us without trouble in case of your death. Will this arrangement meet your wishes?

Sincerely yours,
Benjamin E. Smith
