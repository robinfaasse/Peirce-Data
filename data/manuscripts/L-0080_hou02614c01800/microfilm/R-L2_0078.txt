--Newton's rings, first column of page 5185.
Make it read "These rings, in the case of
white light, may be seven in number, and the
order of color follows that known as Newton's
scale of color.
Omit the next clause.
"Newton's scale of colors" is not the same thing
as the order of colors in the spectrum, the
colors of the rings being due to interference,
and depending, in the case of all the rings but
the first, partly on the effect of overlapping
spectra.
See Newton's Opticks, Book II, part I and II,
or Edinburgh Encyclopedia, Optics, page 696.
In the case of colored light the number of
rings observable greatly exceeds [u]7[/u].
