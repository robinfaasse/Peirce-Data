I should propose that it be made a two column book
something like Smith's Dictionaries. I should calculate
to get it into 500 pages of that exclusive of Introductory
matter.

By proceeding in this Procrustean method as to time
and space, — substantially the method followed by Skeat
in his Etymological Dictionary, — a satisfactory performance,
we could keep our promises. If there was anything that
seemed to have suffered particularly by the proceeding, we
could slip in the time on Sundays etc. to fix it up. ([del]I have[/del]
[ins]been cultivating[/ins] the American dialect lately.)
