Then, would begin the great labor of writing the book. The work
being all prepared and put in shape by a competent and drilled assis-
tant before it came to my hands, I should undertake to do a page
and a half a day, — a great deal, considering the intellectual effort
required, — but not more than I could be sure of accomplishing.

There would remain 10 weeks to complete my 2 1/2 years, or
23 weeks to complete my 3 years. I should need the whole of this
time for improvements of special parts and revision.

This distribution of time looks very different from that of my
previous letter; but it is not really so. I have merely restated
the matter, so as better to be able to take account of the progress
made from time to time.
