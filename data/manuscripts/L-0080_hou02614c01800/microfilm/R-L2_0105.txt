[date]June 17, 1902.[/date]

Prof. C. S. Peirce,
#108 West 89th St., New York City.

Dear Professor Peirce,

It was only yesterday that Mr. Scott was able to be at his desk in consultation with other gentlemen in the business office, and to give your scheme a preliminary consideration. It impresses him favorably, as it did me, but I cannot say what the practical decision of the matter will be. He will communicate with you in regard to it as soon as possible.

Very truly yours,
Benj. E. Smith
