April 18, 1902.

Professor C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Professor Peirce,

I am sorry to say that I am not authorized to spend any money at present upon the general revision of the dictionary. How soon such a revision will be undertaken I cannot say. The matter has been thought about a good deal but no definite conclusion has been reached.

I sincerely hope that you will be able to work your material into shape for independent publication, for I know how valuable it must be.

I have noted what you say with regard to a copy of the Cyclopedia and Atlas.

Very truly yours,

Benjamin E. Smith.
