June 3, 1897.

Prof. Charles S. Peirce,
#109 West 39th St., New York City.

Dear Prof. Peirce,

Mr. Scott has only just returned to
the office after a two-weeks illness, and I
shall lay your scheme before him to-day.

Have you had time to look up that
matter of the league?

Very truly yours,
Benj. E. Smith

Later. Am sorry to say that he has not
been able to consider the matter to-day, and
has left for a week or so more of
rest.
B.E.S.
