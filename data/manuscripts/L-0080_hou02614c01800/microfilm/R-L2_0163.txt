October 6, 1905.

Professor Charles S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Professor Peirce,

The delay in answering your note of September 23rd has been due to my absence from the city. It might, as you say, be a good thing to overhaul the philosophical definitions of the Century Dictionary in view of your later researches and Professor Baldwin's work, but it will not be possible to do so for some time to come. Any revision which we may undertake in the immediate future will be of subjects in which time has brought about greater changes. If, however, there are any changes which you particularly desire to have made, I will try to make them.

Very truly yours,

Benjamin E. Smith.
