[date]August 31, 1904.[/date]

Professor Charles Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Professor Peirce,

I am sorry that the matter of your books has been delayed so long. Miss Jackson has made enquiries concerning the placing of the orders enumerated in the accompanying copy of your letter, but as the Century Company has no retail trade and as we have, moreover, at the present time, no London agent, it will, I am sure prove more satisfactory to you if you place the orders yourself. If we undertook it, it would necessitate a good deal of complicated correspondence which would prove tedious to you and we should, I believe have to pay duty on all the books. Of course, being second-hand, we should receive no discount on them. Would it be possible for you to place the order through some library or college and thus avoid the duty?

Stout's "Analytic Psychology" has been sent to you and the bill charged to your account here.

Very truly yours,
Benjamin E. Smith
