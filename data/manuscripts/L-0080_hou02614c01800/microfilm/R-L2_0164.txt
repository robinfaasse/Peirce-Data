June 23, 1904.

Professor Charles S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Professor Peirce,

I am sending you by express to-day your annotated copy of the dictionary. I have in hand the making of a supplement and shall be glad if you can give me the new logical and metaphysical terms that should be included in it. Are you in a position to do this? Are there many of these new terms? If so, I shall be able to offer you a rate per hundred definitions. The work will be subdivided somewhat more than was originally the case. Mathematics, astronomy, weights and measures, [del]etc.,[/del] [ins]etc.,[/ins] for example, being assigned each to a different specialist.

Please let me hear from you about this matter, which kindly regard as confidential.

Very truly yours,

Benjamin E. Smith.
