Under this head, it will be necessary to explain Klang-Farbe,
or timbre, and to give all the words expressive of it. Also
all words for noises, bang, snap, rap, tap, tick, with abbrevi-
ated but accurate statements of just what they are, Then phone-
tics will be treated, the sounds of vowels & consonants. So I shall
go on to tastes & smells, incorporating the whole of the little that
is known of the analysis of smells, of which I have made some studies
for the purpose. Having done with the peripheral sensations, I shall
pass to the visceral sensations, — the different kinds of physical
pain, etc. Then to the emotions. In short I shall give a complete
table of all the feelings we know.

I shall next pass to the elementary experiences and actions.
By "elementary", I mean those acts and sufferings which we
perform and suffer at one impulse. [del]All[/del] the motions of the parts
of the body, as nodding and shaking the head, kissing, striking,
cutting, running, snoring, etc. Much of this seems to have its
place in physiology. But it is no so; we do not in using these words
consider them in their physiological aspects.

These two large sections having been [del]formed[/del] [ins]disposed of[/ins], all the
rest of the ideas are to be classified according to the branches
of science to which they belong. For after we have disposed
of Feeling and the elements of human life, all the other
ideas relate to Knowledge.
