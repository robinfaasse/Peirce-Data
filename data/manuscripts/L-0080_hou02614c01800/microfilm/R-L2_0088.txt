[date]Jan. 27th 1892[/date]

Dear Prof. Peirce:

I am very sorry to
hear of your experience of the
grippe and of your other
troubles. If I could do anything
to aid you it would give
me great pleasure. Unfortunately
— this, of course, in confidence —
my work during the past nine
years has left me poor — so
much so that even the small
loan you ask for it is now
out of my power to make.
