[date]August 13th, 1902.[/date]

Prof. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

My dear Prof. Peirce:-

Mr. Benjamin Smith has handed me your letter in regard to Mr. Sommer and I sent to him yesterday a letter signed by the President of our Company stating that he had made a large number of mathematical and astronomical drawings for our Century [del]Magazine[/del] [ins]Dictionary[/ins], which have been entirely satisfactory and that we could cordially recommend him to anyone desirous of availing themselves of his services.

Very truly yours,
A. W. Drake
