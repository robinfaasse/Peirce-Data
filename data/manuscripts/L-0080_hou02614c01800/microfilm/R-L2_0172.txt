Professor Charles S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:-

Mr Smith has to-day forwarded to me your letter to him of September 3rd, asking me to say to you that if your work reaches him by November or December 1st it will be satisfactory to him. He also adds that he will answer your letter at length when he returns to New York.

Very truly yours,

Margaret Jackson
