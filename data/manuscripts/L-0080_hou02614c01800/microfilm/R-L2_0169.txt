[date]July 26, 1904.[/date]

Professor Charles S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:-

At the request of Mr Benjamin E. Smith I have made enquiries concerning the purchase of the Arabic-English portion of Steingass's Student's Dictionary. I have up to the present date failed to find a copy in New York; either new or second-hand. If you wish us to import it from the publishers, Sampson Low, Marston and Co, London. [del]Their[/del] [ins]Their[/ins] listed price is 50 shillings and the duty on books is 25 per cent additional. We can order it and charge it to your account, allowing you, of course, whatever discount is allowed to us if you wish us to do so. I am told that an advertisement placed with Parker's in Oxford, England would probably produce a second-hand copy but this would of course take some time. Will you kindly write me your wishes in the matter?

Very truly yours,

margaret jackson.
