[date]Oct. 12 1891[/date]

Dear Prof. Peirce:

Please send at once the corrections for your article on "hyperbolic geometry." You will remember that it contains an error. The paper is wanted for the press.

I am just back from a little vacation. Will write you in a few days.

Very truly yours
Benj. E. Smith.
