Metanoetic (meta-no-ét-ik), [u]a[/u] and [u]n[/u].

(< Gr. [u]μετανοητικός[/u],— beyondknowing, beyondknown, beyondknowable < [del]μετα[/del] [ins]μετα[/ins]-beyond + [u]νοητικός[/u]-knowing.)

I. [u]a[/u]. Beyondknowledge; transcending knowledge as limited to that which lies within the sphere of sense; belonging to the religious consciousness.

II. [u]n[/u]. One who believes that the Ultimate Power transcends knowledge; that the religious consciousness is concerned with that which lies beyond the sphere of sense and knowledge.

Unlike the ordinary consciousness, the religious consciousness is concerned with that which lies beyond the sphere of sense. [u]H. Spencer[/u], Pop.Sci.Mo., XXIV, 340.
