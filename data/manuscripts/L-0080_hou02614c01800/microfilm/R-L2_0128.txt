The next step of the work, which would be going along almost
simultaneously with the above, would consist in gumming these sheets
on the back, cutting them up into strips and entering them in scrapbooks
each under its proper head. This step, I am satisfied, could perfectly
well keep pace with the last. It would be performed by an assistant,
working by piece-work. He would gum them first & dry them. Cut them up. Sort them
while cut out. Last enter them. He would take 750 sheets of over 20000 entries
at one job. He would be paid by the job & fined for errors.
