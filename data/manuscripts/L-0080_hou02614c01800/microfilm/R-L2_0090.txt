Dear Prof. Peirce:

I had not noticed, and do not now find the criticisms in the "Monist", to which you refer in a recent letter. It is a matter of interest to me to keep track of such things. Will you kindly tell me in what number it can be found?

Very truly yours

Benj. E. Smith
