In order to show the minuter subdivisions, I have selected that
one of the branches [del]of the[/del] shown on B² which seemed to me the most
difficult to produce any systematic classification of, and have,
on the 9 sheets marked B³, drawn up, somewhat hastily, and
without consulting any books, a first sketch of a subdivision of it.
This might be a good deal modified when I came to closer study. The catch-
word [u]Amusement[/u] would in particular be changed, as it hardly expresses
the idea. It is rather shocking to call a [u]Prayer-Meeting[/u] "amusement," but it
is the kind of activity which I define, for which I hope to find a better word.

If you compare this table B³ with the corresponding part of
Roget, to wit, his articles 838-841, chiefly 840, you will perceive
that my table would really be of considerable service in helping a
person [u]to think[/u]. If he was, for instance, laying out a plan of amuse-
ment, he would find such a table quite an aid, furnishing various
suggestions and showing in what directions he need rack his brain no
more. Besides, it furnishes some information about facts. Roget, on
the other hand, offers very little assistance in thinking and hardly
any information; and merely as a repertory of words, it is very defective.
