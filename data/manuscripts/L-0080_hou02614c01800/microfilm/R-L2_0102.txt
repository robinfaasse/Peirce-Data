Dominion of Canada.
Department of Agriculture
(Archives Branch)
Ottawa. [date]20th May, 1897[/date].

Geo A Baker
Dear Sir,

I am in receipt of your letter of the 18th. There were several varieties of "league", but the one that Charlevoix undoubtedly meant was the ordinary league of 84 arpents that will give 3.051+ statute miles. There was besides the nautical mile, but you need have no hesitation, I think, in assuming Charlevoix league to be = 3.051+ statute or (English miles) I shall be glad to receive the publication your society is preparing on the subject.

Yours truly
Douglas Brymner
