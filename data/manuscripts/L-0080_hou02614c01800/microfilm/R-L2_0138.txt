March 13, 1901.

Professor C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Professor Peirce,

We sent you yesterday by Wells Fargo Express your copy of the Century Dictionary. It was addressed, as you directed, to Port Jervis, N.Y. I have placed your note on file so that in case we should have need of your copy at some future time the purport of our present correspondence about it can be definitely recalled.

My note about cube root was certainly obscure. The point is whether the reference to Horner's method is not more confusing than helpful, since so little information is given about the method itself.

Very truly yours,

Benjamin E. Smith
