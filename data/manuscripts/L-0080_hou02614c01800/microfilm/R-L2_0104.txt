South Bend is located on the St. Joseph River, one and three-quarter miles above the ancient Portage to the Kankakee River, a connecting link between the basin of the Great Lakes and that of the Mississippi. LaSalle and his followers made use of this historic highway.

SOUTH BEND, INDIANA, [date]June. 9 1897.[/date]

Mr Benj E. Smith
New York -

Dear Sir
I am in receipt of the communication from Prof. Peirce - for which I am very much obliged. With your permission, I will keep it a few days and make a copy of it for our "Society."

I have taken the liberty to address Mr. Peirce. Enclosing some data. letters &ts regarding the league of 3.051+ English miles - which bear on the use of the league in our own locality. I will be pleased to have you read same, and if you think best, to forward to Prof Peirce. It seems that the Canadian authorities & some others differ with Mr Peirce and I believe it worthwhile to go into the matter further

Thanking you for your prompt attention I remain with much respect

Respy
Geo. A. Baker
