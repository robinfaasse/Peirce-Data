[date]July 10/02[/date]

Prof. Chas. S. Peirce
Dear Sir

Our Mr. Benjamin Smith
suggests to me that you
would put me in the
way of getting some
information as to the so-
called "Power of nine",
why a transposition of any
combination of figures should
be always divisible by nine.
If so, I shall be greatly
obliged if you can direct
me to some text book
which treats on this
subject.

Very truly Yours
M. J. [unclear]Giles[/unclear]
