[u]macroscope[/u] (mak'rō-skōp), [u]n.[/u] [[u]Gr.[/u] [u]μακρός[/u], long, large, + [u]σκοπεῖν[/u], view.] An imaginary instrument, antithetic to the microscope, which should bring vast regions of the universe within the range of vision. — See the extract.
We may imagine a [u]macroscope[/u], which should shrink experience as the microscope expands it, thus disclosing to us more and more of immensity at once.
— [u]C. A. Strong[/u], Why the Mind has a Body, p. 220.
