NORTHERN INDIANA HISTORICAL SOCIETY,
(INCORPORATED.)

SOUTH BEND, INDIANA,

[u]Copy of letter from[/u]
[u]N. E. Dionne. Quebec.[/u]
[date]June 2. 1897.[/date]

Dear Sir,

After having consulted persons of great ability on the question, I may state.

1º The [u]arpent[/u] used in the French Canadian Colonies in their early history was the same as the one in use at the present day.

2º The arpent which is still the legal measure in use in all the Signiories of the Country, the Province of Quebec, is the arpent de Paris, equal to 180 pieds de roi. (French feet) or to 191.85 English feet.

3º The French league is equal to 84 arpents de Paris

Yours truly
N. E. Dionne.
