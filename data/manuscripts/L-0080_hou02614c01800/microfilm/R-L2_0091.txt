Dear Prof. Peirce,

Pardon the delay in answering your letter of Oct. 23d. We know that Funk & Wagnalls have made free use of the "C.D." in compiling their own work, but it is, of course, impossible to object to this in advance of the publication of "The Standard;" and I presume that they have covered their tracks pretty carefully, so that when that book appears it will be difficult to prove that undue use has been made of our dictionary. As regards the second edition of The Century Dictionary, nothing whatever has yet been done, and nothing is likely to be done for some time, not certainly until the outlook in the business world is a little brighter. I am sure that when anything of that kind is seriously undertaken your accumulated notes will be of great service.

I have always regretted the misunderstanding between you and Mr. Scott and never quite knew how it arose. I am sure that there was not the slightest personal feeling against you on his part.
