My dear Mr. Smith

The work that I have done on the C.D. since you sent for the volumes has chiefly (not wholly) consisted in comparing the A's with Baldwin's Dict'y with a view to answering the question of how it stood the comparison. The answer is that it stands it far better than I had supposed it would, the C.D. being right when Baldwin's Dict'y is wrong far oftener than the reverse. Unless one were to break the plates to such an extent as to involve new pagination, the improvements called for are not very numerous. The fourteen years that have elapsed have been chiefly occupied by me in reading & study, so that I am much more competent for such work now than I was then. If the paging were broken, there are many things that I could improve. It would be more than 6 months' solid work to do this. I have declined work on more than one encyclopaedia; but for the sake of perfecting the work on which I spent so much time I would endeavor, if desired, to take up this job, if it is not postponed too long, feeling that owing to my special qualifications I could do it better & cheaper than anybody else.
