Dear Prof. Peirce,

I have been so very busy that I have been unable to answer your letter before. With regard to the advertisement[del]s[/del] which you speak of, the publishers tell me that they are obliged to maintain their regular rates in all cases with great strictness. I sincerely hope that the scheme for your work which you have outlined can be carried out. The book will be one of great value and one that I must possess.
