Milford Pa [date]1905 Oct 11[/date]

Dear Sir:

Your probability
curves of both kinds, the little
table and the rule for using it
are ready; but it is absolutely
indispensable that I should, for
the present, devote myself to
something not eleemosynary. I
will send the curves etc. when I
find time. Very truly

C. S. Peirce

Prof B. E. Smith
Century Co.
