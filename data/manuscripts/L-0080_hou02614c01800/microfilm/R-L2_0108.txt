June 30, 1897.

Dear Professor Peirce,

A perfect copy of Volume V has been sent to you this afternoon.

I leave the city tomorrow night to be gone for some time, but your report on the Thesaurus will be sent to me and will be attended to promptly.

Very truly yours,

Benj. E. Smith

Prof. C. S. Peirce,
109 West 39th St.,
New York City.
