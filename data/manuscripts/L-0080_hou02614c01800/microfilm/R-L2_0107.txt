Dear Professor Peirce:

The copy of The Century Dictionary which you ask for, and also Roget's Thesaurus, will be sent to you this afternoon or tomorrow. I shall not be in the office myself again until Monday afternoon.

The Atlas to which I referred is almost done. It is a purely commercial matter; the maps are made by the Matthews-Northrup Co., of Buffalo, and are for the most part based upon [ins]authorized[/ins] photographic reproductions of the latest official maps.

Very truly yours,
Benj. E. Smith
