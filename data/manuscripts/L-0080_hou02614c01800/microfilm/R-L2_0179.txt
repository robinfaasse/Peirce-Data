My dear Mr. Smith:

I am going to propose, in the Monist of next month, a term of philosophy, [del]which[/del] I think of sufficient importance to be included in your supplement. I enclose a definition of it. very truly C. S. Peirce

[u]common-sensism[/u], [u]n.[/u] The doctrine that every man believes [ins]general[/ins] [del]some[/del] propositions and accepts some inferences without having been able [ins]genuinely[/ins] to [del]doubt them, and[/del] consequently without being able to subject them to any real criticism, [del]and that these must appear to him to be perfectly satisfactory and manifestly true.[/del]
