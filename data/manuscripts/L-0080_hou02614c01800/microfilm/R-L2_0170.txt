Copy of extract from letter of Professor Peirce.

I shall be greatly obliged to you if you will try to purchase of Parker, Oxford (England) and of Macmillan and Co of Cambridge ( a shop there where second-hand books are sold, connected in some way with the publishers but not the same concern) Steingass's Arabic-English Dictionary, if necessary with the English-Arabic volume

[del]Either Wright's Arabic Grammar or Palmer's larger Arabic grammar[/del]
for any price less than new copies or if not obtained within one calendar month from time of reception of order then I will thank you to get for me
Steingass's Arabic-English Dictionary (without the Eng-Arabic) and Palmer's larger Arabic grammar, new. If Palmer's grammar cannot be had, then I would like Wright's.

And may I trouble you also [del]in the interest of the C.D[/del] to try to procure for me at not more than one guinea each volume from Macmillan and Co, Cambridge, copies of the following books that are out of print
Boole's Finite Differences (3rd ed. preferred)
Cremona's Projective Geometry
