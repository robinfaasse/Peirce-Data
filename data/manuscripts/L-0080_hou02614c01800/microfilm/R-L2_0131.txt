When we came to the revision, I would, in like manner, send on the revised copy once in 6 weeks.

Of course, I am not to be understood as insisting upon this plan of examination, but merely as suggesting it. I would propose to have the company pay me the $5000 or $6000 for the preparation of the copy, out of which I to pay all expenses of assistance, typewriting, purchase of books (except a copy of the C.D. and the loan of books if convenient) and I to receive share of profits or royalty besides, with option of investing money in payment of part of the $5000 or $6000 on receipt of further share or royalty. I would suggest as a fair arrangement that the money should be advanced every 6 weeks, 3 weeks in advance of the day of examination.

In addition to the sheets I have mentioned, I forward certain others marked [u]I[/u] treating of the philosophy of the classification.

Yours very truly
C. S. Peirce
