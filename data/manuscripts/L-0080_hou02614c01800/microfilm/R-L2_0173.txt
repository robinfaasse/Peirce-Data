October 5, 1904.

Professor Charles S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Professor Peirce,

I send you by express to-day a batch of new terms and senses, good, bad and indifferent, which have turned up in our reading. Some of them may be of value. It is, of course, necessary to compare the Century Dictionary before accepting a word or sense as new. The work of the collectors has not been criticised. Those that do not belong to you kindly lay aside and return to me.

Very truly yours,
Benjamin E. Smith
