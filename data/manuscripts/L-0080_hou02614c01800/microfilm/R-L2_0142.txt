I happened last night, to want some additional in-
formation about the Society of the Lesina, of
which one of the rules was that the members
must mend their own shoes, giving French and
Italian words for parsimony (I refuse to write parsimoney,
though editors and proofreaders will, I dare say, make me seem
to do so). I would wager ten thousand pounds to one penny
that you did not find room for what I wanted to know, nor should
I know where to look for it, unless possibly in Lerouge.

I cant afford to buy your Cyclopaedia. If your people choose
to deposit a copy with me, I will paste in slips of memoranda or keep
them in a separate book, or send to them when they want it, gratuitously
or otherwise as they may desire. I prefer the former. very truly C. S. Peirce
