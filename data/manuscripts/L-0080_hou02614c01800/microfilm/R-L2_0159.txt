I have plenty of work for the rest of my life. I am not trying to make myself a job. But having put many of my best years into this work, I have a lively interest in its perfectionment; and I do not think you could easily find another person who had all my qualifications, — neither now nor at any time in the next half generation. That is my candid opinion. It is utterly impossible that any business man, — unless he were a Roswell Smith, — should understand my motives in saying this; and therefore I might as well leave it unsaid perhaps. But I discharge my conscience.

Yours very faithfully
C. S. Peirce
