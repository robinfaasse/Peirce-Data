August 20

Prof. C. S. Peirce,
Dear Sir,

I enclose proof of
Virgo. If any corrections are
necessary kindly let us have
them by Saturday morning
as the plate is to be cast then.

Yours sincerely,
W. Lewis Fraser
(per J.)
