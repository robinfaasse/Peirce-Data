Professor Charles S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Professor Peirce,

I do not think that we desire to sell that copy as suggested in your note of January 27th, but my need of it is not immediate. Suppose you send it to me some time next summer and let me copy out the notes that I need. I can then return it to you. In this way we shall both be accommodated.

What you say of Baldwin's dictionary is, I believe, quite true though I have not attempted to read much of it.

In haste,

Sincerely yours,

Benjamin E. Smith
