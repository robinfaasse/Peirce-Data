[date]May 22nd, 1905.[/date]

Professor C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Professor Peirce:-

Your note of May 16th reached me after my last to you was written. I am much obliged to you for your offer in regard to the proofs. [u]I shall not begin to get them until about June 1st.[/u] I do not yet know to what extent I can send them out.

Your capacity for work is certainly astonishing and I wonder that you retain it, though sixty-five is not old nowadays. I must admit, however, that I feel pretty old, though you have fifteen years the advantage, or the disadvantage of me.

Sincerely yours,
Benjamin E. Smith
