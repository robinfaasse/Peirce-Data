[marginalia]V.P.2c[/marginalia]

[date]July 9" / 1897[/date]

Dear Prof. Peirce:

Your note has been
forwarded to me. You
speak of sending us some more
samples of classification etc.
So I will delay reporting
to the Co. Co. until these
come to hand.

I have transferred my
work here for the summer
in order to escape the heat
and the malaria which has
been bothering me for several
years.

Sincerely yours
Benj. E. Smith

Twilight Park
Haines Falls
N.Y.
