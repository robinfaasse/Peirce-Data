In order to show what my book would be like, I in the first
place, present on sheets marked [u]B¹[/u] the general scheme of
classification. The System of Numbering the divisions here used
is essentially provisional, being designed to be superseded in the
completed work by some other system now under consideration.
It is well-adapted for use during the compilation of the work, since
it renders alterations of classification practicable with little modi-
fication of the numbering. The General scheme of classification
may possibly be altered, as far as the three widest divisions go, but
in other respects, will be little modified. At the time I wrote my
previous letter, I was still in doubt over the classification of the
Arts, [del]upon[/del] [ins]to[/ins] which I had devoted weeks of labor without suc-
ceeding in producing a satisfactory system, nor even a [del]satis fac-[/del]
[del]tory[/del] principle of classification. Most of the time I have spent on
these papers since has been devoted to the study of this question; and
I am pretty confident, for reasons I shall give, that not much more
time need be given to that. The remainder of the General scheme
of classification has been well and maturely digested. It will,
I am sure, prove readily intelligible as well as profoundly philoso-
phical.
