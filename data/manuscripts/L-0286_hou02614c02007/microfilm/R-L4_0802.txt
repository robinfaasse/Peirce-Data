8th Oct. 1872.[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Of the many visionary
schemes that have at various
times found a temporary resting-
place in my brain - that, stated
in my last letter, was the most
extravagant! Of course I saw
its ridiculousness at once, &
need not have asked for it.

I have made application
for 4 mos leave - but am not
certain about getting. Col. L.
has returned, and does not
seem favor [del]ay[/del] [ins]my[/ins] going as he did
before his trip to Washington. He
[del]has[/del] somebody else's op that time.
He endorsed my application only
formally.

Yrs sincerely,
O. E. Michaelis.
