My dear Peirce;

I enclose herewith a
brief account of the use of two chronometers
simultaneously on the Schultz. Read it
if you have time and inclination, and
then please return it. I have two boxes
(about 50 lbs each) of E.B. tea left - came
as sample sent Mrs. Peirce - at York.
It is very good - and hear let
you have one chest. Rather a
step from "time" to tea!

When do you expect to be
on? Mrs. Michaelis joins me
in kind regards to Mrs. Peirce and
yourself.

Yours sincerely,
O. E. Michaelis.

Mr. Chas. S. Peirce.
