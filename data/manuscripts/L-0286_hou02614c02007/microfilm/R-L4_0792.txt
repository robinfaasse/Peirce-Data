My dear Peirce:

[ins]And Mrs. Peirce[/ins]

And not believe that I have
forgotten all about the tea,
and coffee I promised to
send her. The delay has been
caused by the non-arrival
of the tea until Saturday.

It is a beautiful "chop" -
fragrant and bright - I
