Watertown Arsenal,
[date]March. 1872.[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I have this moment received a telegram from Col. Laidley, who is at present in Washington, to the effect, that if I go at all, I must go on leave. Three field officers are going - none of whom know a word of German - and I believe the Col. is at the head of the Commission. I had long since given up all idea of being sent over - and had decided to go - on my own hook - provided I could go without a formal leave of absence. For if I went on leave - I would not only lose half my pay - which I might stand - but also all right to quarters, fuel, and forage, which I'd feel.
