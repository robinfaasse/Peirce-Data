Watertown Arsenal, Mass.,
[date]11th Sept., 1872[/date].
[/marginalia]

My dear Peirce:

Your umbrella
was sent to your father's house,
as requested, during my absence
at the Pier. Yesterday I sent
you a copy of the "Lighthouse",
& have also found a man
who treats his friends to Chateau
Yquem, the brand of Cruse fils -
almost equal to the [del]one[/del] [ins]acid[/ins] discussed
mines. Mrs. M. and myself will make
the "physiological" observations.

If I remember correctly, the curve
is formed with "times" abscissae, and
the "number" of equal times for ordinates?

Please send me a copy of your
[del]l[/del]iged Boston Harbor chart -
especially that part lying between
Quincy and Strawberry Hill.
