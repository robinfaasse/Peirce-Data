Watertown Arsenal,
[date]July 8th, 1872[/date]

My Dear Peirce:

Col. Montgomery
looked at the lime, and
was pleased therewith, but
at this "opportune" moment
he received an official
letter from Newburg tn - which
changes all his plans. He
will not remain here long.

Allyn was married
the other day, with Cummings
