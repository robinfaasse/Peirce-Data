Watertown Arsenal, Mass,
[date]June 25th, 1872[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Col. Montgomery
has arrived, and will, I
think, like to occupy your
house for such length of time
as you might deem proper.
He is an old officer,
and has only a wife, and
grown up daughter for family.
I don't conceive of more
eligible tenants for a house
containing such valuable
books, &c as yours. Will
you please drop me a line
in regard to terms, &c —
With kind regards to
Mrs. Peirce

Yrs. Sincerely
O. E. Michaelis
