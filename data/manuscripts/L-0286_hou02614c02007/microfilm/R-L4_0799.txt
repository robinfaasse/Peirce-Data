Watertown Arsenal,
[date]20 Sept. 1872[/date].

Dear Peirce:

Your favor with check
came duly to hand - accept thanks.
Suppose Henry has received
key from Prof. Newcomb to get the
phy. time observation. By-the-way,
Gen'l. Belknap has accepted Mr.
Krupp's invitation to visit Essen &
Essen; so if you can say a good
word for me - if you have the
opportunity.

Yours sincerely,
O. E. Michaelis.
