6 Prescott Hall, Cambridge Mass
[date]1903 Dec 7[/date]

My dear Sir;

I send you one of a dozen copies left after distribution to my audience of the first pages of a Syllabus. The printer did well, considering that I never saw a proof. It was written in extreme hurry & piece-meal & sent to the printer without any copy being retained. Hence there are a few formal inconsequences.

I had better take this occasion, late as it is, to thank you for kindly
