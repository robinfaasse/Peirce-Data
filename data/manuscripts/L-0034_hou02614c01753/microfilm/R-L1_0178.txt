Dear Dr. Peirce

I send certain matter (delayed by domestic distractions, from wh. I am here recuperating):—

1). I return [u]Multiplication[/u] with explanatory note.

2). [u]Mathemat. Logic[/u], ditto.

3). [u]Modal[/u], ditto

4.) I return certain parts of the glossary to [u]Logic (exact)[/u] which I cannot use. I can not enlarge that art. to such an extent. I have put in a few of the terms of the glossary (seeing they are in the early letters wh. are already printed.). Any later terms should go in as separate topics, each being agreed upon before it is written. You will see proofs of the-art.
