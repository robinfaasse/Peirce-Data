Milford Pa [date]1901 March 15[/date]

My dear [del]Prof[/del] Baldwin:

I have your check for $50
and enclose the receipt. I thank
you very much, & trust I may be
able to keep the money.

But I must ask you kindly
to relax your rule about notation
in my case. If you wanted to
lay down rules as to [del]office boys[/del],
you would choose office-boys as
your contributors. But that would
not suit you. However, you need
not do that. There are plenty of
men who will be able to express
all they have to say about such
subjects as Relatives & Scientific
Method under any restrictions you
may choose to impose. I could not.
