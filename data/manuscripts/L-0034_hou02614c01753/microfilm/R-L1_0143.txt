Milford Pa [date]1900 Oct 20[/date]

Prof Baldwin
Princeton N. J.

Dear Sir:

I will do the work you write about. Mrs. Franklin has sent this list, but no proofs. It is, of course, indispensible that I should have [u]all[/u] the logical articles and, if possible, all the rest that is printed, before me. Many of the topics require sub-topics other than those contained on this list. It may be said, in a general way, that the entire terminology of "exact logic" is omitted, as well as a variety of [del]other[/del] [ins]older[/ins] terms of the old logic, and terms of modern systems which have had sufficient vogue to call for their insertion, such as [u]Quantified Predicate[/u]. I should think you had better let me add both sub-topics and principal topics as they seem to me called for, since it is better to err on the side of insertion than of omission.
