DICTIONARY OF
PHILOSOPHY AND PSYCHOLOGY,
MACMILLAN & CO.

J. MARK BALDWIN, EDITOR.

PRINCETON, N. J., [date]Jul 9 01[/date]

My dear Dr. Peirce

Since writing you, a lot of material has come to me and I have accordingly had to revise your list, striking out certain topics covered by others. Enclosed I send the list as amended (from L in). I send the same to Mrs. Franklin and I am asking you to arrange with her to divide the work and get them all to me at once. I must deposit the mss, with the printer by [date]Aug. 15[/date]: the R & S terms if possible [date]Aug 1[/date]. Everything is now written on this list of arts.

Please consider this my last arrangement, and to be rigidly lived up to, I can't get the publishers to give me any grace (try as I have).

Yours truly
J. M. Baldwin
