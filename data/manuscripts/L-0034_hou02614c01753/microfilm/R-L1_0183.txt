I can't treat scientific method in any
decent way without having to use
some such form as "$\sin x$" which
your "conservatism" forbids, since
writing one sign after another without
any intervening sign is to be the sign
of logical composition.

Conservatism in its proper place,
and in moderation, is something I
very much approve of. It may be
defined as yielding to a timidity born
of a consciousness that one does not
fully comprehend the situation. That
is often wise in practical matters, but
in pure Theory it is out of place. Your
rule may lay some claim to conserva-
tism on the purely theoretical side
of the matter to which it relates. At
any rate, it is based upon a pretty
thorough want of understanding of the
situation. But upon the practical side
it is as contrary to the spirit of conservatism, which seeks
to allay difficulties, not inflame matters, as anything
could be.
