[u]Milford[/u] [date]1901 March 23[/date] V2

Dear Sir:

I have the words in N and O done, & most of those in P. Your decision about relatives does not particularly stimulate my zeal about the Dictionary. Still, I will write to Mrs. Franklin & say that I should like to write the article symbolic logic myself. If she consents, I can without the use of any symbols, explain the general principles of the application of algebra to Logic, & state the grounds for such differences as exist (and which you seem very desirous of [del]entering into something more[/del] [ins]satisfactorily[/ins] [del]in a nutshell[/del] [ins]all round[/ins]). I shall await her reply before taking further steps.

Very truly C. S. Peirce

Prof. Baldwin.
