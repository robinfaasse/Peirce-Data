Since symbols written together without
an intervening sign must be understood
as being relatively multiplied, it is neces-
sary to use a different sign for logical
composition. I use a rather heavy dot
and think others had better fall in with
that unless some good [del]solid[/del] reason
can be alleged against it.

As to [u]limiting notion[/u], I can't for the
life of me call to mind the term. In
what connection is it used?
