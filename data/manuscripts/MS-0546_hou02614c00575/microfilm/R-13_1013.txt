Art 24. The compound of a k-fold and an l-fold relation is a k+l fold relation if they are independent.

Art 25. It is not quite correct to say that the composition of relations corresponds to intersection. He means of independent relations. Neither does aggregation necessarily mean congregation of loci. When one speaks of a curve through n points, that is not a mere congregation of the n points. But it is properly aggregation. It is true only the n points are definite. But it is supposed they are connected by others. If this is anything more than aggregation, then intersection is something more than composition.

Art 28. A k fold and an l fold condition cannot be both satisfied if k+l > m, unless they are not independent. In like manner if aggregation is properly understood they cannot be aggregated if k+l < m. There is no [del]definite[/del] locus between two planes, except space itself.
