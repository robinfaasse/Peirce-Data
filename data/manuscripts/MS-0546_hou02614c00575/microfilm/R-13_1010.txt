Art 6. A number of one fold relations, which Cayley calls a plexus,
may [del]not of course not taken together reduced[/del] restrict the

Art 7. But if more than a k fold Plexus is required to state a

Art 8. We can have an m-dimensional space. In that case there

Art 9. We now very arbitrarily restrict the quantities to point
coordinates. I do not mean that this is a restriction because
they might be coordinates of other loci [u]instead[/u], but because
[del]they[/del] he might wish to consider at once the relations between
different kinds of coordinates. When he calls them coordinates does he
mean they must refer to a fixed figure?

Art 11. Cayley seems to be doubtful whether to call the point a locus,
