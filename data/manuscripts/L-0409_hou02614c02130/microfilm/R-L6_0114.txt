Relations entre la Physique Experimentale et la
Physique Mathematique. By Poincare.
Rapport Congrés de Physique, Vol. I,
pp. 1-29. Abridgement to be made. 15 pp.

The Secretary requests that the papers be trans-
lated as they stand, except Brandt's article "La Vie dans
les Mers", which is to be translated omitting the notes,
and that by Poincaré, entitled "Relations entre la Physique
Experimentale et la Physique Mathematique", which you are
requested to abridge in your discretion so that it will not,
when set in type, occupy more than 15 pages of the Smith-
sonian Report, or what is equivalent thereto, 7500 words.
It will be necessary to have the translations made and re-
turned to the Institution, together with the originals, not
later than March first. The allotment of space in the re-
port volume for these articles is indicated for each paper
in the list above given, and it is hoped that the transla-
tion will not materially exceed this. The estimate is based
in each instance on 500 words to the report page.
