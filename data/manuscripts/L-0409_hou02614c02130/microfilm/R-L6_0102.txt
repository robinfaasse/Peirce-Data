SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON, D. C.

[date]May 31[/date], 1900.

Dear Sir:

I am directed by the Secretary to advise you, in
reply to your letter of May 28th, that translations, if sub-
mitted in a clear and legible handwriting, are acceptable to
the Institution; in this form a deduction of 7 1/2 cents being
made on the allowance of fifty cents per hundred words of
the original. This is necessary to defray the cost of
typewriting, which is perhaps slightly higher than the
commercial rate, on account of the technical nature of the
articles and the need of extreme care in revising, owing to
the lack of familiarity of the typewriter with the subject
matter treated in the translation.

Very respectfully yours,

[ins]Richard Rathbun[/ins]
Assistant Secretary.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Pennsylvania.
