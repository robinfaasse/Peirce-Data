BROWN'S HOTEL,
LONDON. W.
(DOVER ST. & ALBEMARLE ST.)

[date]June 24th, 1899[/date]

My dear Peirce,

I answer in haste yours of the 10th, to say that while I am forbidden to answer such questions officially, I shall be very glad indeed to have you refer to me in the present case for an expression of my personal opinion of your scientific qualifications and experience.

I beg my respects to Mrs Peirce,
and am, very truly yours,

S P Langley

Chas. S. Peirce Esq.,
Milford,
Philadelphia. U.S.A.
(c/o R. Rathbun Esq.)
