SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON, D. C.

[date]January 17, 1901.[/date]

Dear Sir:

The Secretary directs me to acknowledge the receipt
of your letter of January 16, expressing your willingness to
undertake certain translations for the Smithsonian Institu-
tion, and to announce the transmission to you for this pur-
pose of the following articles:

Les Progres de L'Aeronautique. By Janssen. Annuaire
du Bureau des Longitudes, 1901. 8 pp.

Antiken Desemer ans Chinsi und ubernaloge Desemer.
Zeitschrift fur ethnologie. 8 pp.

La Vie dans les Mers. K. Brandt. Rev. Sci. (4)
513-526, 1899. Without the notes. 15 pp.

L'elevage du renard bleu. Par Henri de Varigny.
Revue Scientifique, Sept. 22, 1900. 6 pp.

L'oeuvre de Lavoisier. By M. Berthelot. Revue
generale des sciences, Aug. 15, 1900. 10 pp.

{ Les Formes diverses de la phosphorescence. By M.
Gustav Le Bon. Revue Scientifique, Sept. 8,
1900. 20 pp. }
You will please not translate this paper without
further instruction.
