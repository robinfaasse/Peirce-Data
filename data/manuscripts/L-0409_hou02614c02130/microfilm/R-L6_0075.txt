Allegheny Observatory
[date]Nov 17 1875[/date]

Dear Sir

I regret to have been the
unintentional cause of trouble over the
various accounts given of the [del]clock[/del]
[ins]Chronometer[/ins]. There is some mistake about
the "Clock under a new method
of decrease of rate" but I mentioned to
Mr. [del]P[/del] [ins]C[/ins]ontact that recent additions
of Electric apparatus had rendered our
Break Circuit Chronometer by Frodsham
nearly superfluous, and that I would
sell it. I presume this is what you
refer to, but I have since concluded
to retain the instrument. The arrange-
ment [ins]respecting[/ins] altering the Error without stopping
the Chronometer is I believe peculiar to it, and
works well.

Yours truly
S P Langley

C. S. Peirce Esq.
U.S.C.S.
