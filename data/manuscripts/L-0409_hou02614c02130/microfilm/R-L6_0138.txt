SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON

S P LANGLEY,
SECRETARY

[date]April 22, 1901.[/date]

My dear Mr. Peirce:

Your paper "On Two Map-
Projections of the Lobatschewskian
Plane" only reached me the day after
the Academy had completed its session.
What would you like to have me do
with it?

Very truly yours,
[ins]S P Langley[/ins]
Secretary.

C. S. Peirce, Esq.,
Milford,
Pennsylvania.

W-P
