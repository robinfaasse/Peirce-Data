I inclose a copy of a letter I
had written, [del]to[/del] before I had heard
from you, that you may see I am not
indifferent to his interests. If

I beg you to believe that apart
from my desire to serve your cousin,
nothing will be more agreeable to
me than to comply with any request
of yours if I can.

Very truly yours,

Captain C. H. Davis, U.S.N.,
