SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON, D. C.

February [ins]2[/ins]0, 1901.

Dear Sir:

I am authorized by the Secretary to acknowledge the receipt of your letter of February 12th, and of the translations which you have recently completed for the Institution.

A voucher and check to your order for One Hundred Dollars and forty-nine cents ($100.49), in payment for the above, is enclosed herewith. Please return the voucher, when signed, in the accompanying franked envelope.

Very respectfully yours,

Assistant in Charge of Office.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Pennsylvania.
