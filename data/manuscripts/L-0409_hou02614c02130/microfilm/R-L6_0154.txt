WASHINGTON, D. C.

ALL CORRESPONDENCE

TO THE SECRETARY
S. P. LANGLEY.

July [ins]18[/ins], [date]1901[/date].

My dear Sir:

Secretary Langley left Washington at the end of June, without finding time to reply to your letter, and as the subject was unfamiliar to me, I had to go over the correspondence before being able to write you.

Your manuscript "Hume on Miracles and Laws of Nature", was received, and the Secretary went over it himself, marking such passages as he thought should be included. These have now been type-written, and I enclose a copy to you. In addition to this, the Secretary himself inserted an occasional remark of his own, which you will readily recognize.
