SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON

S P LANGLEY,
SECRETARY.

Oct. 3, 1894.

My dear Peirce:

I have just returned from Europe, and am glad to hear from you in yours of the 28th ultimo.

I send you the "Experiments in Aerodynamics" by post, and I also send you a copy of the Smithsonian Report for 1893, in the appendix of which you will find something about Lilienthal, though something which may possibly [ins]not[/ins] interest you since it is not new.
