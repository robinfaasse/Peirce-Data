[date]February 3 , 1911.[/date]

Dear Sir:

Referring to your letter of January 30, I beg leave to say that your name is on the list to whom the Smithsonian Report is annually sent, and to thank you for your comments on the Poincaré article in the 1909 Report. It is very difficult to secure strictly accurate and polished translations of highly technical papers.

Very respectfully yours,

[ins]C D Walcott[/ins]
Secretary.

Dr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Penna.
