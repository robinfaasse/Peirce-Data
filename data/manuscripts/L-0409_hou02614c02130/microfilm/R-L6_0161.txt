SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON, D.C.

ALL CORRESPONDENCE
SHOULD BE ADDRESSED
TO THE SECRETARY
S. P. LANGLEY.

[date]October 31, 1901.[/date]

Dear Sir:

In relation to the publications of the Smithsonian
Institution, the Secretary authorizes me to say that one of
the functions of the Institution is the diffusion of knowl-
edge in language "understanded of the people"; so that al-
though most of its works are intended primarily for the
specialist, there is an exception made by the Secretary in
publishing with his Annual Report material addressed to that
large body of the public which has a general interest in
scientific matters without special knowledge of them. In
this connection he has taken the liberty of reprinting your
valuable article on "The Century's Great Men in Science",
and has had forwarded to you a copy of the volume contain-
