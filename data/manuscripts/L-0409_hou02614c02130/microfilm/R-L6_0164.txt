ALL CORRESPONDENCE

TO THE SECRETARY

S. P. LANGLEY.

[date]December 17, 1901.[/date]

Dear Sir:

I am authorized by the Secretary to acknowledge the receipt of your letter of December 14th, and the translation of Marey's paper on "Cronophotography", and to say that your account of Twenty-five dollars ($25.00) has been placed in course of settlement. A check for that amount will be forwarded to you during the present week.

Very respectfully yours,

[signature]
Assistant in Charge of Office.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Pennsylvania.
