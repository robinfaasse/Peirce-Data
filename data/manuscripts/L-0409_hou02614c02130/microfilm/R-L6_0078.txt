ALLEGHENY OBSERVATORY
ALLEGHENY PENNSYLVANIA

[date]Dec 10/84[/date]

Dear Peirce

St Thomas Aquinas
somewhere, I believe, says
that "there is no light without
heat - not even in the rays
of the moon", or words to that
effect. Can you help me
to the original citation and its
context? The statement, if so
made, is certainly a very curious
anticipation of modern views.

Yours most truly
S. P. Langley

P.S. I think the quotation begins
"Lux quantum est de se semper est
effectiva caloris" etc."
