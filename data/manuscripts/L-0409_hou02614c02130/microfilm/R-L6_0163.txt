SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON, D.C.

ALL CORRESPONDENCE
SHOULD BE ADDRESSED
TO THE SECRETARY

S. P. LANGLEY.

December [ins]10[/ins], 1901.

Dear Mr. Peirce:

I am authorized by the Secretary to express appreciation of your prompt response to his telegram of yesterday, asking if you would undertake the translation of Doctor Marey's article. The pamphlet will be mailed to you tonight, and the Secretary will be very greatly obliged if you will return it when the translation is finished, which, it is understood, will be in two days from the receipt of the printed original.

Very truly yours,

Assistant in Charge of Office.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Pennsylvania.
