SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON. D. C.

ALL CORRESPONDENCE
SHOULD BE ADDRESSED
TO THE SECRETARY

S P LANGLEY.

May 7, 1902.

My dear Peirce:

I am sorry to have to say, in answer to your letter of the sixth instant, that I have nothing to do with the assignment of moneys under the Carnegie Institution, which lies wholly in the hands of the Executive Committee. It is to these gentlemen that you will have to address yourself.

I shall bear your wishes in mind and when I see anything in connection with which the Institution can use your abilities I shall gladly let you know.

Please present my respects to Mrs. Peirce, and believe me,

Very sincerely yours,

[ins]S P Langley[/ins]
Secretary.

Professor C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pennsylvania.

W-C
