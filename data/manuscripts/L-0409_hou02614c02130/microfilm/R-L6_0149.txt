Now suppose that before we have been able to eliminate all
hypotheses but one, a practical emergency arises in which
several lines of conduct are open to us which will have
various different advantages and disadvantages according
to which one of the still unrejected hypotheses is correct.

all we can practically apply. The point is that the factor
of likelihood is a subordinate one unless the likelihood is
quite overwhelming; otherwise, the factor of safety is the
important consideration.
