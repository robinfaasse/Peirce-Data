ALL CORRESPONDENCE
SHOULD BE ADDRESSED
TO THE SECRETARY

S P LANGLEY

October [date]31, 1900.[/date]

Dear Sir:

I am authorized, in the absence of the Secretary, to acknowledge the receipt of your letter of October 29th, and of the manuscript translation of the report of the flight of the Zeppelin balloon, which will be brought to Mr. Langley's attention upon his return.

A voucher and check in payment for this translation amounting to $33.07 is enclosed herewith. A franked envelope for the return of the voucher when signed is also enclosed.

Very respectfully yours,

Assistant Secretary.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Pennsylvania.
