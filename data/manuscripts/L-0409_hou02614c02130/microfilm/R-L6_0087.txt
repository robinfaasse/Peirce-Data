SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
Washington U.S.A.

[date]Jan.17,1894.[/date]

My dear Peirce:

My delay in answering your note of the 6th,has not been at all from reluctance to give you every encouragement in your undertaking,but I am so busied by urgent routine matters at this time,that I have hesitated as to what expression of my opinión would be of most service to you,particularly as a resolution of the Board of Regents inhibits the Secretary from endorsing any work whatever.

I have,then,strained a point in writ ing what I now inclose,and you will,of course,understand that it is sent,and should be published,only as an expression of my private opinion, and without any reference to my official capacity.

I have sent to DeVinne an order for your "Earliest Work of Experimental Science",which I shall look for with interest.

Very sincerely yours,
S. P. Langley

Mr.C.S.Peirce,
Milford,
Pa.
