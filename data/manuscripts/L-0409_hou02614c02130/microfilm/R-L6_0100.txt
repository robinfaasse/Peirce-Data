Dear Sir:

The Secretary directs me to acknowledge the receipt
of your letter of May 14th, and to say that he regrets that
the Institution has at present little to translate.

I am instructed to enclose, however, an article by
Otto Mannesmann, entitled "Luftwiderstandsmessungen mit
einem neuen Rotationsapparat", which you will please trans-
late, writing on paper the size of the original so that
these may be bound up together.

I enclose also a paper by Hermann Strebel, "Die
Stein-Sculpturen von Santa Lucia Cozumahualpa", and an
article by Xavier Raspail on the Sense of Smell in Birds,
published in the Revue Scientifique, July 29, 1899, pages
144 to 148.
