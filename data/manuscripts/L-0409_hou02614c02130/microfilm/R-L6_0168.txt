ALL CORRESPONDENCE
SHOULD BE ADDRESSED
TO THE SECRETARY

S. P. LANGLEY.

September 16, 1902.

Dear Sir:

In the absence of the Secretary, I am authorized to ask whether you care to translate for the Institution a paper in German of about eight thousand words, by Doctor Victor Schumann. It is important that the article be translated within ten days after its receipt. Should you desire to undertake this commission, the Institution will pay its customary price of thirty-five cents ($.35) per hundred words for such work.

Very respectfully yours,

Assistant in Charge of Office.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Pennsylvania.
