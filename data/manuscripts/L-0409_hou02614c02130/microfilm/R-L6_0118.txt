SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON, D. C.

February 11, 1901.

Dear Mr. Peirce:

In reply to your letter of February 2d, I have much pleasure in sending you copies of the Smithsonian Report for 1896, 1897 and 1898. The reports for 1896 and 1897 were, as shown by our records, transmitted to you in June last, but possibly they miscarried in the mails, and I have therefore sent duplicate copies.

I have directed that the volumes in this series be sent to you regularly in the future.

Very respectfully yours,

Secretary.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Pennsylvania.
