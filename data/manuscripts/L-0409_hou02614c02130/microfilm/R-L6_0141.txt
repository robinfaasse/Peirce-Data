S P LANGLEY

[date]May 18, 1901.[/date]

My dear Sir:

I am instructed by the Secretary to acknowledge the receipt of the manuscript of your article on "The Proper Treatment of Hypotheses", which he has read through with much interest. The Secretary agrees with the statement which you make in your accompanying letter, however, that the article is "hard reading", and quite beyond the scope of the readers for whom the Appendix to the Secretary's Report is intended.
