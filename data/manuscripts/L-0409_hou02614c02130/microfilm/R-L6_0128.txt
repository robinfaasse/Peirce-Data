My dear Mr. Peirce:

I have your letter of the first, and I enclose you a circular used here in obtaining the counsel of persons attached to the Institution, in selecting magazine and newspaper articles already published, as an appendix to the Secretary's Report, to explain to you what that is.

I make no question whatever of your eminent fitness to treat, in an article for matured scientific minds, the subject you mention. I feel equally certain, however, that you do not appreciate the incapacity in the audience to whom the Appendix to the Secretary's Report is addressed, to understand any such subject, which, indeed, could hardly be made intelligible to them by the most extreme skill in popular elucidation.
