SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION.

S. P. LANGLEY, Secretary.

BUREAU OF INTERNATIONAL EXCHANGES.

March [ins]15[/ins], 1894.

Dear Sir:

Your letter of March 3d was duly received and in reply I am authorized by the Secretary to say that owing to a mistake of the binder not discovered until volume VI. of the Memoirs of the National Academy of Sciences was forwarded to you, a "Correction sheet" became necessary which was sent to the recipients of the volume for incorporation in the proper place.

This "Correction sheet" is returned herewith.

Very respectfully yours,

W. C. Winlock

Ass't in Charge of Office.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,

Milford,

Penn.
