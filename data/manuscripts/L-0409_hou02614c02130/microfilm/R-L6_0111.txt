SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
Washington, U.S.A.

[date]Nov.2,1900[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I have your letter of Sep-
tember 30th, on my return from
Europe.

I have noted your excellent
translation of the Mannesmann
paper, and since then you have had
a commission from me to translate
an article by Count Zeppelin, which
translation I have also received
and duly appreciate. I may per-

haps observe that the customary
payment by the Institution is thirty
cents instead of fifty by the hundred
words, the extra amount paid you being
in acknowledgment of the excellence
of the work.

I have said a word to the Coast
Survey, though I cannot expect that
it will have any weight.

Very truly yours,

[del]S P Langley[/del]

C.S.Peirce, Esq.,
Milford, Pa.
