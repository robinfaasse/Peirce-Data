ALL CORRESPONDENCE
SHOULD BE ADDRESSED
TO THE SECRETARY

S P LANGLEY

June 28, 1900.

Dear Sir:

I beg leave to acknowledge the receipt of your letter of June 25th, and of the manuscript of your translation of the Mannesmann's paper, voucher and check for the payment of which will be forwarded in a short time. A franked envelope for the return of the originals is enclosed, as you request.

The Secretary is now absent in Europe, but your personal letter addressed to him will be brought to his attention upon his return.

Very respectfully yours,

Richard Rathbun

Acting Secretary.

Professor C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Pennsylvania.
