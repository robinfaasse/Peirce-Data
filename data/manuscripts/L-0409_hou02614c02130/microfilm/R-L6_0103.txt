SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON, D. C.

ALL CORRESPONDENCE
SHOULD BE ADDRESSED
TO THE SECRETARY.

S. P. LANGLEY

June [ins]5[/ins], 1900.

Dear Sir: --

I am directed by the Secretary to acknowledge the receipt of the translations of the articles by Strebel and Raspail recently forwarded by you, and, as it may be several weeks before they are put in type, I have sent a check for $41.93 in payment for them, at rate of fifty cents per hundred words, less 7 1/2 cents per hundred words for cost of typewriting. Please return the originals of these articles, and they will be sent to you again with printer's proof for your revision, in accordance with the terms of the original order.

Should there be any further translations desired, the Secretary will be glad to bear your request in mind.

Very truly yours,

[signature]

Assistant Secretary.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Pennsylvania.
