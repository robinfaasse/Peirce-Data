SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON, D. C.

[date]January 27, 1901.[/date]

Dear Sir:

I am authorized by the Secretary to say that he has received your letter of January 20th and has now transmitted to you a complete copy of the article by Soleland on "Dose etc".

The Secretary is interested by your criticism of Poincaré's article but thinks that it is hardly necessary to bring the shortcomings before the kind of public for whom the translation is made. He may perhaps add a note of two or three lines only, when the time for publication comes, for the purpose of advising the reader that the article does not embody an all-around view of the subject.

Very respectfully yours,

[u]R. Rathbun[/u]
Acting Aid in Charge.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Pennsylvania.
