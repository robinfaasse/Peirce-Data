SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
Washington U.S.A.

[date]Jan.29,1900[/date]

Dear Captain Davis:

I must begin this acknowledgment of your note by asking you to pardon me for employing a type-writer,as my eyes do not allow me to write with my own hand.

I have known Charles Peirce for a large part of my life,and am sincerely desirous of getting a man of his real genius a position in which he may be able to do himself justice.
