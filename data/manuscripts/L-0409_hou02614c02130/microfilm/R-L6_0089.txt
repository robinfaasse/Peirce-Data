Smithsonian Institution
Washington, D.C., [date]February 23, 1894.[/date]

S. P. LANGLEY,
Secretary.

Dear Sir:

The Secretary directs me to subscribe, for the Smithsonian Institution, to your forthcoming work, entitled "The Principles of Philosophy, Or, Logic, Physics, and Psychics", in twelve volumes.

Will you be good enough to instruct that they be forwarded, addressed to the Smithsonian Institution, Washington, D.C. (For Secretary's Library.)

Very respectfully yours,

W C Winlock

Asst. in Charge of Office

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pennsylvania.
