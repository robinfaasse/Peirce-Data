Dear Sir:

I [del]enclose[/del] [ins]send[/ins] herewith the type-written copy of my paper, with such patchings as seemed called for. You can make further excisions if you like. [del]Of course,[/del] I cannot do my best when hampered by a composite subject imposed from without[ins]*[/ins], as every artist knows. But under the circumstances, I think I have [del]contrived[/del] to make a paper not entirely without value. You need not hesitate to call upon me for any further improvements. Of course, you won't expect me to change my doctrines; but I dare say you may suggest improvements in the statement, — at any rate for your publication. Meantime, I should not object to the money, or some of it. I will enclose the original MS & two useless typewritten sheets.

very respectfully
C. S. Peirce

[marginalia]*It would therefore not be fair to me to change the subject, for that is to write on the theme I accepted a commission for.[/marginalia]
