SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON, D. C.

ALL CORRESPONDENCE
SHOULD BE ADDRESSED
TO THE SECRETARY

S P LANGLEY

[date]April 23,1901.[/date]

My dear Mr.Peirce:

I have your letter of the 20th, which I have read with interest. I only write now to enclose you the $4. you refer to, and to say that the manuscript which you offered, I will take, and that it was returned to you through some error. I do not know how many words there were in it, but if you will accept an honorarium of [del]Twenty[/del] [ins]twenty[/ins] Dollars ($20.00), with the understanding that this is quite independent of the payment for the new article, I am pleased to enclose it.

Very truly yours,

S P Langley
Secretary.

Charles S. Peirce,Esq.,

Milford, Pennsylvania.
