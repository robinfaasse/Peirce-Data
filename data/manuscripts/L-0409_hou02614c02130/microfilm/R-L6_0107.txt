SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON, D. C.

ALL CORRESPONDENCE
SHOULD BE ADDRESSED
TO THE SECRETARY.

S P LANGLEY

July 17, 1900.

Dear Sir:

I beg leave to acknowledge the receipt
of your letter of July 14, in regard to the pay-
ment for the Mannesmann paper, and to say that
the enclosure addressed to Secretary Langley will,
in accordance with your request, be brought to his
attention upon his return.

Very respectfully yours,

Richard Rathbun

Acting Secretary.

C. S. Peirce, Esq.,
Milford, Pennsylvania.
