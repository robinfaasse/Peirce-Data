SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON, D. C.

ALL CORRESPONDENCE
SHOULD BE ADDRESSED
TO THE SECRETARY.

S. P. LANGLEY

October [date]6[/date], 1900.

Dear Sir:

The Institution desires to obtain a translation of the article entitled "Bericht über den ersten Fahr-Versuch mit dem Luftschiff des Grafen von Zeppelin", contained in the accompanying August number of the "Illustrirte Aeronautische Mittheilungen", and I beg to inquire if you would be willing to undertake this small commission at the price customarily paid by the Institution for such a technical article, namely, fifty cents per hundred words for a typewritten translation, less a deduction of 7 1/2 cents per hundred words, if the copy is submitted in manuscript.

Very respectfully yours,

Acting Secretary.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Pennsylvania.
