SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON D C

March 7 1901.

Dear Mr.Peirce:

I have got your translations. I see that Le Bon does not know what he is talking about in Physics, and I intend to add a good natured word to that effect in the text.

I have not yet had time to read your paraphrase of Poincaré, but not doubting that you have brought it your usual conscientious care, I consider it worth the price that you mention, and without waiting the usual routine of payment, have much pleasure in enclosing herewith a check for the sum of $210.87, which embraces the payment of $188.00 for the articles for the Appendix to the Secretary's Report and $22.87 for the report by Mr. Victor Schumann on investigations made by him with the aid of a grant from the Hodgkins Fund.
