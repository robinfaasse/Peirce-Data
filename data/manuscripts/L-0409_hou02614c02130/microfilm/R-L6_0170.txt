SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON, D. C.

ALL CORRESPONDENCE
SHOULD BE ADDRESSED
TO THE SECRETARY

S. P. LANGLEY

October 3, 1902.

Dear Sir:

I am authorized, in the absence of the Secretary, to acknowledge the receipt of your translation of the additions to Doctor Schumann's report and also of the original German manuscript. Your account has been placed in course of settlement and a check will be forwarded to you as soon as practicable.

Very respectfully yours,

Assistant in Charge of Office.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Pennsylvania.
