SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON, D. C.

January [ins]15[/ins], 1901.

Dear Mr. Peirce:

There are a few untechnical articles, chiefly in French, which I should like to have translated for the possible use of the Secretary in the Appendix to his annual report for 1900. I suppose there may be as many as twenty or thirty thousand words. The articles, I repeat, are wholly popular and untechnical, but if you would like to do them I should be very glad indeed to engage your aid, paying in your case a special honorarium of five ($5.00) dollars per thousand words of the translation, which includes typewriting. Please let me hear from you.

Very truly yours,

[ins]S P Langley[/ins]
Secretary.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Pennsylvania.
