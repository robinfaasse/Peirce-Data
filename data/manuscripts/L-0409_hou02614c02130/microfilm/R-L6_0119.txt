SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON, D. C.

February [ins]11[/ins], 1901.

Dear Sir:

I am authorized by the Secretary to send to you herewith a paper by Professor Hertwig on "Die Entwicklung der Biologie im 19. Jahrhundert", and to say that if you can translate it by March first, in addition to the papers you already have in hand, he will be pleased to have you do so.

He also desires me to say that he will be glad to have you translate the paper on "Les Formes diverses de la phosphorescence", which you were requested to hold for further instructions, if you can complete this also within the time limit, the fee for both of these articles to be at the same rate as that stated in my letter of January 18th.
