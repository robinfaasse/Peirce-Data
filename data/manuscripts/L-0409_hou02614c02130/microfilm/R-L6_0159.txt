TO THE SECRETARY

S P LANGLEY

September 7, 1901.

Dear Sir:

In the absence of the Secretary, who is now abroad, I beg leave to acknowledge the receipt of your letter of the fifth instant, in which you enclose the revised manuscript on "The Laws of Nature and Hume's Argument Against Miracles". The letter and the manuscript will be placed in the hands of the Secretary immediately on his return to Washington.

Very respectfully yours,

[signature]

Assistant in Charge of Office.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Pennsylvania.
