SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
Washington, U.S.A.

January 20,1900

My dear Peirce:

I am sorry to hear what you tell me. I have at this moment nothing to offer, but in case you care to do some translating, and the occasion presents, I shall very gladly avail myself of your aid.

I will only now venture the suggestion that McClure, who has consulted me about his great encyclopaedia, ought to know that such services as yours can be obtained for it.

I write briefly because in haste,
