S. P. LANGLEY.

October 3, 1901.

My dear Mr. Peirce:

I find your letters of the fifth and twenty-fifth of September on my return, and I send you an unpublished article that I first wrote with the intention of making it a letter to you to explain my wishes; which I think I did not make clearly intelligible.

Your present article on the Laws of Nature and Hume's Argument against Miracles will do very well, and I do not know that any changes are necessary or desirable; if you should care to make any, in view of the present paper, there is still time to do so.

I have given instructions that the whole of the $50.00 should be handed to you, and you will find a check for it in this letter.

I am

Very truly yours

S P Langley
