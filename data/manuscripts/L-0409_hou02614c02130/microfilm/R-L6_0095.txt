SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
Washington, U.S.A.

[date]Jan. 27, 1900[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I fear that for years to come,
there is nothing to be hoped for in
the proposed University plan which
would provide a place for you.

I have written very specially
to McClure in your behalf, and hope
you will hear from him.
