SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION,
Washington, U. S. A.

January 6, 1903

My dear Peirce:

I receive yours of December twenty-third, after some delay. If there is anything that I can get you to translate next month, I will be glad to send it to you. I would willingly do more, but that is all that is possible for me at present.

Will you please give my respects to Mrs. Peirce, and believe me,

Very truly yours,

C. S. Peirce, Esq.,
Milford, Pa.
