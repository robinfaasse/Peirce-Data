SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
WASHINGTON. D. C

ALL CORRESPONDENCE
SHOULD BE ADDRESSED
TO THE SECRETARY
S. P. LANGLEY

September 19, 1902.

Dear Sir:-

Replying to your letter of September seventeenth, I am authorized, in the absence of the Secretary, to forward to you herewith, for translation, the four additions to the paper on the spectrum by Dr. Victor Schumann.

You will note that the time specified for the completion of this translation is ten days after the receipt by you of the paper, which is transmitted, as you suggest, by registered mail.

Yours very respectfully,

[signature]

Assistant in Charge of Office.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Pennsylvania.

(Four enclosures.)
