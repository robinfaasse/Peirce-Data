Allegheny Observatory,
Allegheny, Penn[ins]a[/ins]
[date]Feb[rua]ry 20. 1873[/date]

C. S. Peirce Esq
U. S. C. S. office

My dear Sir,

I regretted to be
obliged to leave New York,
before your arrival, and to
miss the pleasure of dining
with you. I should
earlier have acknowledged
your letter, but I had delayed
a reply, wishing to be
prepared to inform you
