[u]Copulas[/u]

$\infty\ (\ )\ x$ means Something is $x$
$0\ (\cdot)\ x$  "  Everything is $x$

$x\ (\ )\ y = \infty\ (\ )\ x \times y$
$x\ (\cdot)\ y = 0\ (\cdot)\ x + y$

$x\ (\ )\ y = y\ (\ )\ x$
$x\ (\cdot)\ y = y\ (\cdot)\ x$

$0\ (\ )\ x$ is absurd
$\infty\ (\cdot)\ x$ is meaningless

$\infty\ (\ )\ \infty$ is meaningless
$0\ (\cdot)\ 0$ is absurd.
