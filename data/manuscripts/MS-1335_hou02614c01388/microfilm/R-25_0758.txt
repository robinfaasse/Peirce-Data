James Harris wrote a book in the last century called [u]Philosophical Arrangements[/u]. The phrase is a very pretty translation of the word [u]Categories[/u], and as [del]everyone[/del] [ins]grown person[/ins] knows the importance of arranging one's ideas, it suggests what the study of the Categories can do for a man.
