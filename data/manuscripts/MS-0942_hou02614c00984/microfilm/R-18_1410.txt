Hence the first dimension of the continuum of possible quantity
is of the nature of a hyperbolic sequence.

In a continuum of two dimensions, if there is any sequence,
there are certain lines along which there is no sequence, unless
it be secondary, [ins]and[/ins] independent of the principal sequence.
These lines of no sequence may all intersect at two points,
or at one point or not at all.
