So the accidents of reaction, which are making consciousnesses
of pairs of qualities, may be expected to join themselves into
a continuum.

Let me remind you that three things do not necessarily
make a genuine triad, or have what I call [u]Threeness[/u],
except in a very modified and reduced sense. By a
genuine triad I mean three things in a genuine triple
relation which is not a mere mixture of dual relations.
Hence, when I say that accidents of reaction are pairs
of qualities, I do not mean to deny [del]that they may[/del]
[del]be composed of more than two qualities, since in general[/del]
every quality is concerned, but merely that the mode of
combination is strictly combination, such a relation as
two things can have. So there maybe a large family
of brothers and sisters, but their relation[ins]ship[/ins] is nothing
but the mixture of the relations of the different pairs.
