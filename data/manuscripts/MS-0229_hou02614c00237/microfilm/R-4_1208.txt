My father's definition "mathematics is the science which
draws necessary conclusions" at least implies the truth.
Modern logic shows that all necessary inference is really
mathematical; and no inference could be necessary if
it related to anything more than a hypothesis.

But the best definition is "mathematics is the science
of hypotheses," or of precise hypotheses. For one important
part of the mathematician's business is to [u]frame[/u] his hy-
pothesis, and to generalize it. [del]The drawing of inferences from[/del]
conclusions about it is not all.

matician. A man comes to me with a complicated and
often confused statement of facts and asks a question supposing
them to be true. It is not my business to consider their truth;
but the first thing I have to do is to frame a simple hypo-
thesis which shall represent those facts as nearly as I can
make it while yet being simple enough for my mathema-
