In the first place, it is necessary to understand the
general nature of mathematics and its reasoning. Mathe-
matics, speaking broadly, is historically the earliest of the sciences.
Unless a collection of absurd medical prescriptions be counted for
science, the earliest scientific treatise which has come down
to us is on mathematics. Pythagoras was a true mathematician
ages before there was any true physics or psychology.
Astronomy became scientific very early; but it used mathematics
from the outset.

The morphologic biologists tell us that the development of

development of the race. Some great pedagogists make this

tics than anything else, except mechanics; and it is almost
the only study which will remain a valuable accomplishment
