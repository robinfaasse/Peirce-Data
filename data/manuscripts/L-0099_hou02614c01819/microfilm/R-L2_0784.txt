OFFICE OF
F. J. DEL CORRAL,
CIVIL ENGINEER & SURVEYOR,
DRAUGHTING OF ALL KINDS
A SPECIALTY.

REFERENCE No. ........................

Saratoga, N. Y. November, 28th 1889.

To Mr. C. S. Peirce.
Assist. U.S. Coast,
and Geodetic Survey.
Washington, D.C.

Dear Sir:
I write to beg the favor of information concerning the "quincunical projection" invented by you. I am in possession of the plate; and tables of rectangular co-ordinates for the same, published in U.S.C.&G.S. Rept. for 1877, appendix #15. Wishing to use the projection for a star chart, I undertook to learn the method of making the projection; as indicated in the above paper, but though I am a draughtsman of considerable experience, and familiar with the polyconic, and Mercator's projections, I am forced to admit my inability to discover the method of using the co-ordinates, as published in appendix #15.
It is on that account that I take the liberty of asking you to indicate; plainly the successive steps to be followed in making the projection upon paper.
If you will kindly accommodate me with the desired information, you will very greatly oblige,

Yours most respectfully.
F J del Corral.

# 125 Phila St.
Saratoga, N.Y.
