I shall reply to these two criticisms, which are
quite unrelated, separately. I first consider his
attempt to show that I am "led into inextricable
confusion" by my tychism. His papers on the
subject contain a great deal which is perfectly
irrelevant and merely serves to hide the nakedness
of his argument. I shall begin by analyzing each
section of his papers, omitting all this matter.
