Section VI raises a very broad objection, indeed.

by which science has been advanced; but that a
radically different, and thoroughly positivistic
method is required. This question has been the
chief study of my life. I wrote a book [ins](never published)[/ins] on the
history of scientific methods, twenty-five or thirty
years ago, and have been studying the subject ever since.
I am now revising for the press, the first of three
volumes about it. I am prepared to maintain
