Section 7. [u]Lopping off the absolute[/u]. This argument
plainly depends upon a confusion between the two
meanings of the word absolute. I ask Dr. Carus
to lop off the heads of all absolute propositions. Of course,
I mean that we cannot attain perfect certainty, nor,
in a certain sense, perfect exactitude even of
probable knowledge. Dr. Carus replies that he
has lopped off all absolutes. If he means to admit
that law is not perfectly exact, I do not see why
he objects to my doctrine. If he does not, he seems
to be playing with two meanings of the word absolute.
