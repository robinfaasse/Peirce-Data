Dr. Carus lays down, as unquestionable, the proposition that "science constantly operates on the basis of the maxim that there is no chance." He, thus, calmly passes [del]without notice[/del] [ins]by[/ins], my argument, four pages long, to show that scientific reasoning postulates no such thing. This is criticism made easy.
