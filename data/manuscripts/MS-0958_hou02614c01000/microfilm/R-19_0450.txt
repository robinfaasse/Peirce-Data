Dr. Carus now defines necessity as the
inevitable, This is what is usually called
fate. Few people believe that anything
is absolutely and unconditionally inevitable;
and I should never have troubled myself
[del]to argue against that[/del] [ins]to argue against the[/ins] doctrine that everything
is so. But that, Dr. Carus distinctly says is
what he is to be understood to mean by
the necessary.

The idea of the absolutely inevitable is, he
says, closely allied to that of agreement. This is
true of what philosophers generally mean by
necessity but not, I think, of Fate.
