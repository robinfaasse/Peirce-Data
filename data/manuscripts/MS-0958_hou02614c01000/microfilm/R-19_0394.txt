§ 25. [del]If[/del] I offered, in my paper attacking
the doctrine of necessity, four positive reasons
for believing in real chance. They were,

1. The general prevalence of growth, which
seems to be opposed to merely mechanical law.

2. The variety of the universe.

3. That law can thus only be explained.

4. That, thus only, can the connection of
matter and mind be so stated as to do justice
