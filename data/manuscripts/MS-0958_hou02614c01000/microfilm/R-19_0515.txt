I desire to deny that my logic of science is very
positivistic. Such a charge is most unjust. My
theory, like my practice, condemns excessively positivistic
methods, and shows from the principles of pro-
bability why they must naturally be as sterile
as they have proved to be historically. My theory
and practice alike favor a method which holds
the balance pretty even between constructionism
and positivism.
