Namely, I hope my style of writing may never be liked by anybody who admires his. He leaves a hard smooth surface, over which the loose thinker glides easily, and upon which the close thinker can find no projection to lay hold upon. I do my best to help close critics of my thought, and to make my writing agreeable to them alone.
