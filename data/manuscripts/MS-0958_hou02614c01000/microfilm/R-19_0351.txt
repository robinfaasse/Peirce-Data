[u]Reply to the Necessitarians[/u].

The doctrine of necessity was attacked by me in the [u]Monist[/u] of January, 1891, and April, 1892, and its partisans were courteously challenged and besought to attempt an answer to my arguments. But this, so far as I have learned, Dr. Carus has alone, [ins]publicly[/ins] vouchsafed, in the [u]Monist[/u] of July and October, 1892. A number of other philosophers, however, some of them minds of great force, have had the kindness to endeavor to explain to me privately the reasons for their belief in necessity. I will now endeavor to reply [del]as to those objections[/del] [ins]well[/ins] to the objections to my arguments, so far as I am able to comprehend them.
