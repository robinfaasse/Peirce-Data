The correct definition of necessitarianism is quoted from The Century Dictionary; but in these articles I use the word in a slightly more comprehensive sense to mean the doctrine that whatever happens is [del]so[/del] absolutely determined by law. This includes a little more than the doctrine [ins]which[/ins] properly constitutes necessitarianism.

The definition of determinism is criticized as absurd. It may be so; but that is the fault of the necessitarians. They distinctly maintain that [del]that[/del] the denial of their doctrine involves contradiction, and themselves state their own doctrine as a truism. But what really characterizes them is their [del]doct[/del] conception of "motives," upon which the question turns.
