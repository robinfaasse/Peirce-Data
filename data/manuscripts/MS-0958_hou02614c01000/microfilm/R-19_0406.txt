Section 8. I hold that all knowledge of fact
falls short of complete certainty. But this does
not apply to 2x2=4; because numbers are
only a system of our own creation. It is a ques-
tion of fact whether there are two persons in a
room. That is uncertain. It is a question of fact
whether each has two eyes. That is uncertain. It
is, therefore, a question of fact whether there are
just four eyes in the room; and that is uncertain.

there, and each has two eyes, there are or are not
four eyes, is not a question of fact, but a question
