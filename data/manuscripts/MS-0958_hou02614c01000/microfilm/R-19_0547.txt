Is $7+5=12$ a synthetic proposition?
It seems to me it can clearly be proved
not to be so in several ways. In the first
place, I assume the "fundamental proposition
of arithmetic," namely, that if the counting of
any lot of things stops by the exhaustion of
the things at any given number, then the
counting of this same lot will stop for the
same reason at the same number. I assume
this, because I do not suppose that anybody will
maintain that the synthetic character of
$7+5=12$ depends solely upon this propo-
