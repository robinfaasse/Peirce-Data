Dr. Carus now defines necessity as the
inevitable. This is the proper definition of
fate. It is an [del]m[/del] erroneous definition of the
conception which is generally attached to the
world, and it is particularly unserviceable
for his purpose. If this were all necessity
meant, I never would have taken the
trouble to attack the doctrine of necessity,
because hardly anybody believes in the
absolutely inevitable fate.

But this he declares is the sense in which
the word ought to be used and in which he
is to be understood to use it. This clear and
emphatic declaration, having been made,
is [del]also[/del] promptly withdrawn.
