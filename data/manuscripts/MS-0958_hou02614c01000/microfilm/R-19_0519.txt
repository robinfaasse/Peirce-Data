the entirety of the All as an inexorable and
uncompromising sovereign, or as a severe educator,
a stern father who unfalteringly clings to his
principles." In short, the God of the determinist
is Law. Those who adopt indeterminism, do
so [del]not[/del] merely on account of its attractiveness
to a superficial view. Science "operates on the
basis of the maxim that there is no chance." The
question is, is this true, and how can it be proved?
