Dr. Carus thinks me a nominalist. What
is a nominalist? He is a person who holds
that only individual things exist, and considers
laws and types, since they are of the nature
of thought, to be unreal. The realist admits
that laws and types are of the nature of thought,
but [del]he[/del] holds them to be at least elements
of the real, even if not also in some sense
real when before they are embodied in
individuals. My view is that the real is
nothing but the immediate object in a
true cognition, and that the true cognition
is simply the settled opinion we are striving
after. This settled opinion is something we
hope and strive for; but we cannot be
sure that it will be nearly attained about
[del]all subjects[/del], or even that it is in regard to
