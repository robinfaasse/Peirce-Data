[u]Rejoinder to Dr. Carus[/u]

Dr. Carus's review of my philosophy in the Monist for July is capable of a more succinct [del]series of[/del] reply than his previous two articles. He frequently objects to my style as "stilted." The passages to which he applies that epithet are those in which I aim at a high degree of logical accuracy of statement. I, on the other hand, find the greatest difficulty in understanding the views of Dr. Carus, [del]for[/del] on account of his lack of that quality.

[ins]For the sake of brevity[/ins] I shall omit reference to points which seem to me unimportant, for although [del]in so[/del] I shall thus pass by [ins]very numerous[/ins] [del]many[/del] [del]repeated[/del] misrepresentations which would be important if they were not easily detected by a careful reader.
