[date]June 21st, 1895[/date].

Professor C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pike Co. Pennsylvania.

My dear sir:

I have indeed been lost to
express to you my great appreciation of
your letter of the 20th inst. relative to the
paper "What is Math?" & thank you heartily
for the commendation and the suggestions.

As to the "group" I wish to explain that the
word was used in its general sense
here, although I was under the impression that
some mathematicians limit the term
"group" to a set subject to but the
single operation I call a factor, and a
single factor. This would exclude the
process of "addition" from such an algorithm,
and these algorithms. The term "algebra"
I use of a set of units subject to two operations,
factors, & modes of combinations thus
defined. These are associative, distri-
