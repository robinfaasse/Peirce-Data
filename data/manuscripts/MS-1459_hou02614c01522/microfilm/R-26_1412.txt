His real name seems to have been Brillat-Savarin.
Presumably, the family name had been Brillat to which
Savarin had been tacked on as a sort of quartering.

The celebrated poem of Berchoux is stated to have
been first published in 1801; although we doubt not
that Mr. Ellwanger is perfectly aware that it went
through three editions in 1800. But probably at the
moment of writing that sentence he had in mind some
statement than the poem made its appearance in
the first year of the nineteenth century.
