If a pack is dealt into N piles, the place of each card
is multiplied by $\frac{1}{N}$ (mod M).
[ins]of M-1 cards[/ins]

If a pack is dealt into N piles and the last card is
made the last card of a new pack, the last card of a
second pack being substituted for it, and so on until
the packs are interchanged

the $(-1)^a$ card is the $(-1)^a$ of the new pack
the $(-\frac{1}{N})^a$ card is the $(-2)^a$ of the new pack
the $(-\frac{1}{N})^2$ th card is the $(-3)^a$ of the new pack
$\overline{\text{the } (-\frac{1}{N})^{M-1} \text{ th card is the } \frac{(1-M)^a}{= 1^a} \text{ of the new pack}}$

If the order of the pack dealt out is first reversed
while the other cards are [del]taken up in their[/del]
[del]order[/del], and the order of the pack finally remaining
in hand is reversed, [del]then[/del] the [del]new[/del] place of card of
pack in hand is (mod M) $N$ [del]if (mod M-1) N[/del]
