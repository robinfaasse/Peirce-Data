Maximus Tyrius Διαλέξεις trans-
lated into Latin by Cosmo Pazzi,
archbishop of Florence. Rome. [date]1517[/date].
[del]Stained with water.[/del] Impressum Romae apud Jacobum
Mazochium. Small folio, Bound in old vellum
[del]Stained with water.[/del] This is the first
edition. The translator had died two
years before and his brother Pietro
supplies a preface. Often reprinted.

A volume of 154 leaves folio containing
14 celebrated Astrological writings:
2 by Ptolemy, 2 by Hermes, 3 by Bethem,
1 by Almansor, 3 by Zahel, 4 by
Messahallach. Venice: [date]1492 the
20th of December[/date], just two months
after the discovery of America.
[The [date]20th December 1492[/date] is called in the
colophon the 13th day before the Kalends
of January [date]1493[/date].] Old wooden
covers with pigskin back.

There are two separate translations
from Ptolemy, one direct from the
Greek, the other through the Arabic; which
points to translation in the 13th Century.
