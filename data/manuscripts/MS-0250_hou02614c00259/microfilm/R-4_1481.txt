In like manner let [ins]$\text{X}\vert\text{Y} = \text{Z}$[/ins] [del]$\text{X}\vert\text{Y}$[/del] express that if $\text{X}$ is $\text{F}$ and $\text{Y}$ is $\text{F}$ then $\text{Z}$ is $\text{F}$,
but if $\text{X}$ is $\text{V}$ or $\text{Y}$ is $\text{V}$ then $\text{Z}$ is $\text{V}$. Then $\underline{\text{Z}} = \underline{\text{X}}\vert\underline{\text{Y}}$, $\overline{\text{Z}} = \overline{\text{X}}\vert\overline{\text{Y}}$.
Thus [del]$\overline{\text{X}\vert\text{Y}} = \overline{\text{X}}\vert\overline{\text{Y}}$ and $\underline{\text{X}\vert\text{Y}} = \underline{\text{X}}\vert\underline{\text{Y}}$[/del] $\overline{\text{X}\vert\text{Y}} = \overline{\text{X}}\vert\overline{\text{Y}}$ and $\underline{\text{X}\vert\text{Y}} = \underline{\text{X}}\vert\underline{\text{Y}}$
