[pn]p. 63.[/pn] Nothing can be more exaggerated than to say that modern
research "establishes" a connection between the Akkadians
and the Chinese. Philologists were at one time inclined
to think the Accadian language had affinities with
the Hungarian and Finnish; but that theory has lost much
ground of late years. M. Terrien de la Couperie, an arrant
theorizer, has shown some reason to think the old Chinese
people came from the West and had been in contact with
the Sumerians. Whether there is any linguistic or race
connection between all those tribes called Turanian is
excessively doubtful; and the Chinese seem to belong
to a different family. The whole matter is in the darkest
doubt. Nothing is "established."
