The scholastic philosophy proper, in which quiddities did
not, however, play quite such a rôle as is represented, really
did not begin till after Abelard. Alexander of Ales and
Albertus Magnus may be reckoned as scholastics properly
enough; though the latter was not wholly given over to
theology. But Thomas Aquinas was about the first charac-
teristic scholastic doctor.

The authorities cited here, Whewell, Hallam, Tennemann,
& Ranke are as bad as could be; for they all wrote before modern
explorations into scholasticism.

p. 122. It is extreme flattery of Neckam's book on the one hand
and great injustice to it on the other to compare it with
St. Isidore's Etymologies or any other encyclopaedia. Neckam
expressly says in the preface it is only a book of moral reflections
