Required to multiply 23 by .0434. This is
$(223 + 7) (449 - 15) = 223 \times 449 \times (1 + \frac{7}{223}) (1 - \frac{15}{449})$
$= 1000 \times (1 + 7 \times .0434 - 15 \times 23)$
$= 1000 \times (1 + 0.314 - .335) = 1000 (1 - 0.021) = 0.979$

The true answer is 0.9982.

Had we inscribed the scale with an additional figure, we
should have had for 223     222.8
for 449     448.9
and the answer would have been .9981
