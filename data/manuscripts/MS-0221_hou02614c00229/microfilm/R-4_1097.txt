Old things may be patentable very properly as new
inventions. For seven hundred years all the world,
even the most ignorant, have been in daily use
of logarithms, — nay, of a logarithmic scale, — nay, of
the very system of measuring down to a unit of mea-
surement and then using computation, which is pre-
cisely what we propose to patent in our scale table.
I mean the decimal places of the Arabic system of
numeration.
