I have probably been near a year off and
on reading Sextus adv. Math., and this contains
much about the Epicureans. Also the Hypotyposes.

I have given a good deal of time to other Hercula-
neum papyri of Epicurean contents (besides the
[u]σημεῖα καὶ σημειώσεις[/u])

Have read Gassendi's Exercitationes and
Syntagma Epicuri.

Of modern books on Epicurus, I have
read nothing except such things as Zeller's
[del]sketched account of the doctrine[/del]. The truth
is the doctrine is [del]not[/del] little understood or
appreciated. A doctrine so unboastful, so
emphasizing uncertainty, cannot be valued
except by those who go to the substances;
and that only highly trained scholars are
able to do with perfect confidence.
