THE MC CLURE ENCYCLOPAEDIA

141 East Twenty-fifth Street,

New York, [date]Nov.29th,1899.[/date]

Dr C.S.Pierce,

P.O Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:-

I am glad to have your letter of a few days ago, and I am sorry that I
can as yet write you nothing definite--that is, nothing definite along the lines that you
would obviously best like to work.

I am just on the point of going away from home for several weeks on
various errands connected with the Encyclopaedia, and when I return I shall take up your
letter again and see if there be any practical way for the enterprise to get the benefit of
your services in its present state.

With thanks for your courtesy, I am,

Very truly yours,

Walter H. Page
