logical goodness is. We have seen that any
kind of goodness consists in the adaptation
of its subject to its end. One might [del]say[/del] [ins]set this[/ins]
[del]that[/del] down as a truism. Verily, it is scarcely more,
although circumstances may have prevented
it being clearly apprehended.
