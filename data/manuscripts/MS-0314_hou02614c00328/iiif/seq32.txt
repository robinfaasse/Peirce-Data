The observations on this and following
pages were made with the 4-inch Meridian
Circle.
Between Feb. 11 and March 8, the [del]teles[/del]
pivots were [del]supported[/del] cleaned, and also
the V-supports. Before cleaning, the 
friction was so great that the telescope
would remain in any position without being
clamped. After cleaning, it was nicely
balanced. It was also found that the
object glass was loose in its cell. It
was tightened Feb. 11.
On March 8, the following test for
flexure of the telescope tube was made.
A small electric lamp was placed at the
eye end, and its light was reflected from 
the objective into the telescope tube.
By means of a series of diaphragms,
the light was [del]ma[/del] narrowed to a small beam
which fell on a scale at the eye-end.
The telescope was turned into various
positions, and the position of the light-
spot on the scale was noted.
