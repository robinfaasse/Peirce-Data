The following night she related to him
the adventures of Prince Ardeshir and
the Princess of the Island of Camphor.
He did not see her again on the
two next nights - but she appeared
on the evening of the fourth and
related the history of the King of
Samarcand. It was thus that she diverted
his mind from the thoughts that tormented
him. She related stories to him almost
every night for several months - he felt
that he loved her - but she always
evaded every question he asked her in
regard to herself.
At length the winter passed away, and
it was on a beautiful evening in the
month of May, as she was relating
the history of Prince Camaralzaman
and the Princess Badoura, she suddenly
stopped and looked around her - she turned
pale and appeared about to faint - but
the Prince came to her assistance -
"Adieu my Prince" she cried - " we part
to meet no more - and I have but time
to ask you whether you love me enough
to leave your father and your friends
and go with me to a far distant
country?"
