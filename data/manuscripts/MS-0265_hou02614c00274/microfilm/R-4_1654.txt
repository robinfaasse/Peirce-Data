The semidirect point singularities are
Points where attached lines meet sheets, which may be called [u]seals[/u]; and these may be either at ordinary points of sheets, or at bounding edges, or at lines of splitting, and in this last case either a ordinary points of these lines, or at their extremities, or at their nodes.

The direct points singularities are
Tacks, or [del]topical[/del] conical points, where two or more sheets are joined at single points; and these are either
[u]1[sup]st[/sup][/u], [del]Double[/del] Edge tacks, where [del]several sheets are joined at their edges[/del] [ins]an ordinary point of one[/ins]
[u]2[sup]nd[/sup][/u], Edge-sheet tacks, where the edges of some sheets are tacked to ordinary points of others;
