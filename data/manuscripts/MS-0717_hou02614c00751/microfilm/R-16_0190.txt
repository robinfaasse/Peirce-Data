The ideas in which Thirdness is predominant are, [del]naturally[/del] [ins]as might be expected[/ins], more complicated, and mostly require careful analysis to be clearly apprehended; for ordinary, unenergetic thought slurs over this element as too difficult. There is all the more need of examining some of these ideas.

The easiest of those which are of philosophical interest is the idea of a sign, or representation. A sign stands for something to the idea which it [del]modified, or[/del] [ins]produces, or modifies[/ins]. Or, it [del]conveys[/del] [ins]is a vehicle for[/ins] conveying into the mind something from without. That [ins]for[/ins] which it stands is called its [u]Object[/u]; that which it conveys, its [u]Meaning[/u]; and the idea [del]to[/del] which it gives rise, its [u]Interpretant[/u]. The object of a representation can be nothing but a representation of which the first representation is the interpretant. But an endless series of representations [del]each representing the one behind it[/del] may be conceived to have an absolute object at its limit. The [del]meaning[/del] of a representation can be nothing but a representation. In fact, it is nothing but the representation itself conceived as stripped of irrelevant [del]mall[/del] clothing. But this clothing never can be completely stripped off; it is only changed for something more diaphanous. So there is an infinite regression here, too. Finally, the interpretant is nothing but another [del]idea[/del] [ins]representation[/ins] to which the [del]torch of truth[/del] is handed along; and as representation, it has its interpretant again. [del]Thus[/del], another infinite series.
