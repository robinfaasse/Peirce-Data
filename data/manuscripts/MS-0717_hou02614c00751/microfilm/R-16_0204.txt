As there are various ways of thinking a judgment, so there are various
ways in which an inference may be suggested. Logic, as the art of rea-
soning, ought, at least, to find out the most convenient of these; but
for the present it may suffice that [del]the[/del] attention becomes fastened
upon a special feature of the icon, and the rest are dropped from
attention. In logic, we avoid the difficulties of psychology
as much as possible by treating of [del]judgments[/del] [ins]propositions[/ins] in place of
judgments, and to some extent of argumentations in place
