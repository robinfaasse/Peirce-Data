[u]Thought and Things: A Study of the Development
and Meaning of Thought; or Genetic Logic.[/u] Vol. I.
[u]Functional Logic, or Genetic Theory of Knowledge.[/u] By
James Mark Baldwin. London: Swan Sonnenschein & Co. New
York: Macmillan Co. [date]1906[/date]. 8vo. pp. 273.

We will say at once that this is a most earnest, profound,
laborious, systematic analysis of cognition, such as cannot
fail to be of the greatest utility to psychology, even if
not also to logic and metaphysics. Its motto is most
inspiriting too: [u]Τὸ καλόν πᾶν[/u], though we must confess to
not understanding the accentuation: it is doubtless our
ignorance.
