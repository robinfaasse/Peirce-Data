In reasoning, however, your opinion is that we have the
singular phenomenon of a physiological function which is
open to approval and disapproval. In this you are supported
by universal common sense, by the traditional logic,
and by English logicians as a body. But you are in
opposition to German logicians generally, who seldom notice
fallacy, conceiving human reason to be an ultimate tribunal
which cannot err.
