This question is a question of fact, and experience alone can settle it. [ins]and cognition generally[/ins]
Experience is that determination of belief which the course of life has forced upon a man. One may lie about it; but one cannot escape the fact that some things [u]are[/u] forced upon his cognition. There is the element of brute force, existing whether you opine it exists or not. Somebody may object that if he did not think so, he would not be forced to think so; so that it is not an instance in point. But this is a [ins]double[/ins] confusion of ideas.
