The German mind is extremely subjective. [del]Their
view[/del] The German view of reason as supreme arbiter
makes a question of truth a matter altogether
analogous to a question of taste. In that view, Truth,
in the sense of something which is [u]so[/u], whatever may
be thought of it, is a chimera. The German practice
is for everyman to say what appears to him reason-
able. Reason being the supreme arbiter, there is, as
a matter of fact, no such thing as genuine logical
criticism in Germany. The consequence is that
opinion there is chiefly a matter of fashion. In one
generation, all Germany regards the rest of the
world as benighted, because it is not Hegelian.
