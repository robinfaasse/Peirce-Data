Logic
II. ii
24

My imagination is a feeling which I can compare
with a percept, not in all respects, but in respect
to the emergence of a little cake of a certain size
and shape, done up in tin foil, which when removed
displays a certain chestnut brown smooth body,
easily bitten and with a certain taste and flavor. This
expectation, it will be observed is an inference; and
any [del]inference[/del] [ins]inferential conclusion[/ins] resembles it in respect to being a
habit of imagining.
