The popular notion is that Reason is far superior to any
instinctive way of reaching the truth; and from your
desire to study logic, I am perhaps warranted in pre-
suming that such is your opinion. If so, in what respect
do you hold reasoning to be superior to instinct? Birds
and bees decide rightly hundreds of times for every time
that they err. That would suffice to explain their imperfect
self-consciousness; for if error be not pressed upon the atten-
tion of a being, there remains little to mark the distinction
between the outer and the inner worlds. A bee or an ant cannot
