5.

It would seem from your desiring to study Logic that you are imbued with the popular notion that Reason is superior to any Instinctive way of getting at the truth. Really one would like to know in what respect you hold reasoning to be superior to instinct. You certainly must know that birds and bees [del]your[/del] judge rightly hundreds of times for every time they err. As for the conduct of men, we must distinguish. Do you suppose a bee or ant is aware that he acts from instinct, or that he would be if he were able to indulge in the pastime of reflecting upon why he acted as he does? Not at all, [del]he thinks[/del] it seems to him, or would seem to him if he reflected, that he is guided entirely by reason; and indeed so he is, in point of fact.
