But while there is no independent instinctive sense of logicality, it is impossible to deny that there are instinctive ways of forming opinions, especially if we continue to take instinct in that broad sense in which it will include all habits of which we are not prepared to render an account, [del]in short[/del] or in one word all that goes by the name of the rule of thumb. [del]In applying[/del] In applying such instincts, such habits of unknown parentage, we do reason a little. But that little reasoning is based on some axiom or impression of opinion which we adopt uncritically, with out any assurance that it is rational.
