Division of Signs according to their relations to their [del]45[/del] [ins]Triadic[/ins] Interpretants. As an example of a quality which is a sign, take [u]heat[/u] as a figure of speech used to convey the idea of an excited state of mind. I term these three kinds, [u]Qualisign[/u], [u]Sinsign[/u], [u]Legisign[/u].
