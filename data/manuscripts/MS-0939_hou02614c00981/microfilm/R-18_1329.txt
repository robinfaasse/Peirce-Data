A triadic relation cannot be reduced to dyadic relations,
[del]For[/del] if it were, it would be a compound of such relations.

Now the relation of a compound to its components is itself
a triadic relation. Therefore, even if a triadic relation
be a compound of dyadic relations, it [del]is not[/del] [ins]does[/ins] thereby
consist of dyadic relations only. But a relation
