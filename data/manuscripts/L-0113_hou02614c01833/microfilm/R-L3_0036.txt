Navy Yard Norfolk
[date]3 April 1894[/date]

My dear Charley,

I have just received the enclosed letter from Sigsbee which I suppose is the original and that you have received the charts he sent you. The Pilot chart I regard as the merest cheap trap. It is a sort of chromo to popularize the office. Its scientific value is nil; and it is a very poor substitute for the guarantee of the correctness of the principles of your plan.

Your affectionate cousin
C. H. Davis
