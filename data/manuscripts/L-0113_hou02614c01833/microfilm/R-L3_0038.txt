U. S. NAVAL OBSERVATORY,
GEORGETOWN HEIGHTS,
WASHINGTON, D. C.
[date]November 4 1897[/date]

My dear Charley

Your letter is received, and I knew that you were an applicant for the Inspectorship of Standards, Office of Weights and Measures, and I have been doing what I could to help along your chances, by personal intercession with Dr. Pritchett, but, I am bound to say, with little prospect of success.
