U. S. NAVAL OBSERVATORY,
GEORGETOWN HEIGHTS,
WASHINGTON, D. C.

[date]Feby. 8, 1900[/date]

My dear Charley

I have your two letters of the 1st and 4th, and with regard to the first, you need not give yourself the slightest uneasiness about Nannie's feelings in the matter you refer to. I did not mention the subject itself to her, but I satisfied myself that there was not the slightest feelings about that or anything else. Both she and I hope I shall have a chance to do it. Back of the whole move-ment is Newcomb, whose course for the last thirty years has been dishonest, malicious, and unprin-cipled. But I must admit that I am getting tired of the fight, and shall be glad when I can turn the whole business over to other hands.
