Navy Yard Norfolk
[date]21 Mar. 1894[/date]

My dear Charles,

Your letter of the 14th inst. was forwarded to me here, where I am engaged in fitting out my new ship. I shall be in Washington again next week, and if you don't object I should like to consult with Sigsbee, who is the Hydrographer to the Navy Dept., on your plan. It seems to me a very brief summary of your plan I won't attempt to discuss it here. The fundamental principle seems to me good.

I will write again from Washington. If I can't be of service to you in this matter it won't be because I don't want to

Your affectionate cousin
C. H. Davis
