I return the
photograph of your house.
It has a very attractive look,
and if I might proffer a word
of advice, I would not sell it
or the place unless I got a
very good price for it. It is
a pied-à-terre at any rate and
that is something, and a
house and land stay in sight

when other investments disappear.
I found that out in the disastrous
years after [date]1893[/date].

Julie joins me in love,
and I am always

Your affectionate Cousin

C. S. Peirce

P.S. I am sorry to say that there
is not a single copy of my paper

I can come across one I will send
it; and I will put your name
on the list to receive the appen-
dices.

C.S.P.
