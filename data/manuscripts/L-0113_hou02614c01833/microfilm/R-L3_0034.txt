1705 Rhode Island Ave
Washington
[date]31 March 1894[/date]

My dear Charley,

Your letter was
received last Monday. I
am going back to
Norfolk tomorrow. I
should have written you
before, but have not
heard from Sigsbee yet.
I gave him your papers
early in the week and
expected an opinion

minor point. I will let you hear
again as soon as I hear from
Sigsbee

Your affectionate cousin
C. H. Davis
