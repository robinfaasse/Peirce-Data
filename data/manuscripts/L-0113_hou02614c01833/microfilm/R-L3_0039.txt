Even in the matter of appoint-
-ments to the Observatory staff,
I have no power or influence
whatever. The minor places
are all in the classified service,
and the greater places go by in-
-fluence which I can neither
control or direct. In the cases
of vacant Professorships for
instance, I can only put the
candidate whom I favor in the
way of having his application
considered with others.

Having so little weight
in my own department it is
not likely that I can exert much
influence in others. Still I have [ins]some[/ins]
and shall continue to do, all that
I can to further your interests,
as far as my power goes, and I
only hope you will believe that I
wish I could do more.

Your long connection with
the Coast Survey ought to give
you consideration and a prior
right over candidates who are
not so well known.

Your affectionate
cousin

C. H. Davis
