Telephone messages to
are decidedly uncertain affairs, as
they are received and delivered by
the watchmen in the hall, who
have a constitutional objection to the
telephone, and in their dealings
with the instrument adopt the
simple expedient of remembering
only a few words of an important
message, leaving it to the recipient
to piece them together, and
determine their true import, as
intelligently as he can.

I am exceedingly sorry that I should
have missed the chance of seeing you.

Pray, give my kindest regards and
regrets to your wife

Your affectionate cousin

C. H. Davis

[date]April 17th[/date]
