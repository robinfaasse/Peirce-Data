A is analyzable into the simpler conceptions of
A1 [u]Me[/u] - Subject
A2 are conscious of - apperception
A3 [u]that objective[/u] - Object

A2 belongs to the class (B) of [u]acts[/u] which are the
relations between subjects and their objects.

Subject & Object belong to the act & are not A1
& A2 in themselves but only in [del]relation[/del] reference to the
act. A1 & A3 are, first, words expressing a
relation; & second a relation in regard to B.

Hence the same existence may be both subject &
object at once. The object which is subject is called
[u]self[/u].

Hence also the not-subject - the not-I may be [u]self[/u].

Every subject affects its object.
I kill him dead

His dead had no existence till the killing but
before the killing [del]him[/del] existed.
