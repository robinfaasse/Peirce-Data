The Infinite, the Type of the Perfect.

We [del]have[/del] have thus seen that the Perfect is not a conception existing by itself but that it is merely the stage of thought to which anyone of several kinds of conceptions may be raised and that it enters into these various Categories of thought in precise [del]ly[/del] the same way. It will be quite as well therefore to take one of these conceptions as they type of the Perfect & continuing our researches on the peculiar nature of that in the Perfect stage as distinguished from the same Conception in the stages of Positivity & Simplicity, whatever is true of that will hold good for the Perfect stage of the other conceptions. The Obvious Selection as [ins]such[/ins] a Type is of the Infinite.
