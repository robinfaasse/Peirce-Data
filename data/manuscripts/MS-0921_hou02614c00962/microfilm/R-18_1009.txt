[u]Elucidation of the Essay, headed[/u]
[u]All unthought is thought-of.[/u]

The foregoing essay is on a very abstruse subject, &
was worded paradoxically so that it would either
not be understood at all or [ins]be[/ins] rightly understood. I
think, however, some of the obscurities may be cleared
up.

1. "We either think-of the unthought as thought" We cer-
tainly can think of the unthinkable as thought, we can
think of the Infinite as thinkable, and in this case we
have a positive conception of the thought [del]INFINITE[/del] [ins]INFINITE[/ins] tho'
we do not think the INFINITE.

2. "We have only negative conceptions of things out of us"
You may say you are thinking of the table, but in re-
ality you are only thinking of the phenomenon table. Things
out of us we cannot think-of as thought. Noumena are
not to be thought-of positively.
