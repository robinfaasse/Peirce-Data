To learn how to analyze idea, therefore, and to analyze them - in short to study metaphysics - is par excellence education.

But I will go further than this and say that all wisdom is derived from [ins]the study of[/ins] Metaphysics. For what is [ins]the study of[/ins] metaphysics but reflection, meditation? And what is wisdom? Knowledge is that which we get empirically, but wisdom is wrought by the unfolding of the mind. Now the terms of every proposition are presupposed to be comprehended; therefore, no proposition can give us a new conception, & wisdom is not learned from books.

You cannot read Metaphysics. It isn't in the books, but in the mind.
