[u]Of Pantheism[/u] [date]1857 October 25[/date]

What is that portion of the soul of which we are not conscious? How does it differ from other mens souls? If consciousness is not our limit[del]s[/del] we have no limits but are agglomerated.

Another man's consciousness however is not part of me. Neither is a [u]noumenon[/u]

This is formal pantheism.
