We can think of the neumenon. Can we predicate of the neumenon? Not blue; since it can only be the phenomenon which is blue if by blue is meant any quality we can think of. We cannot [del]put[/del] assign any quality which we can think upon an exterior neumenon or upon the soul, for unless the neumenon is a thought it can possess no quality of thought. Now there are no qualities except [del]these[/del] thought, since only by being thought does it become a quality. Nothing can be predicated either of the soul [ins]or of any external neumenon[/ins], - much less, infinitely.

The phenomenon, my thought, can that be infinite? It cannot; & no more can the thinking ego.

Apart from my soul there is no infinite. In my immediate consciousness, I cannot realize any quality infinitely. But everything which is in my consciousness is not in my immediate consciousness. The infinite therefore, if it exists, exists in [u]idea[/u]. The idea may be infinite and the thought finite.
