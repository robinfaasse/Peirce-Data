[u]The Great Subject of Metaphysics.[/u]

I assert that it is only conceptions of the PERFECT
that afford any great doubt to the Metaphysician.

[u]The Fundamental Distinction of Metaphysics.[/u]

It is usually conceded or assumed that all Meta-
physics is based on some fundamental distinction. The
fact is that every new system of metaphysics has
some particular merit which is based on some par-
ticular distinction. The distinction upon which my
system is based, obviously is between the thought
and the thought-of or rather between the thinkable &
the thinkable-of.
