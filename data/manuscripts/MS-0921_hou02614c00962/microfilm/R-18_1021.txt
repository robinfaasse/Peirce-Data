[marginalia][date]1861 Aug 11.[/date][/marginalia] [u]INTRODUCTORY TO METAPHYSICS.[/u]

The Logical and the Psychological Treatment of Metaphysics.
Metaphysics, the science of the thoughts, is obviously
a branch of Psychology. Yet Psychology rests on meta-
physics - Rational psychology is a branch of Meta-
physics, if the latter is received as the analysis of
Conceptions. These two views of Metaphysics give rise
to two modes of treating it. One starts by drawing the
Conceptions from the system of psychology & reasoning
to their logical relations & meaning; the other draws
the conceptions from no system but from the
thoughts as they present themselves in their Logical
form - defining them examining them in every
way and finally put them in their right place
in the mind.

The latter seems to me to be the truly meta-
physical course. It may be called the Logical
Treatment of Metaphysics.
