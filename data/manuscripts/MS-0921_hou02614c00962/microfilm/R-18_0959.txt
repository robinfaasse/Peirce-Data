Proposition [date]1859. July 25.[/date]
All unthought is thought-of.
<u>Proof</u>.
We either think of it as thought[del]s[/del], when we have a
positive conception of it or as unthought, when we have
a negative conception of it.
Corollary I.
We have only negative conceptions of things out of us.
Corollary II.
All noumena are unconditioned
Corollary III.
All things in Heaven or earth are thought of, for the
unthought of is [del]neither[/del] thought of [ins]$\therefore$ not unthought[/ins].
[del]All thought is thought-of[/del] $\therefore$ No unthought of is thought
$\therefore$ [ins]the unthought of[/ins] [del]It[/del] is neither thought or unthought $\therefore$ it is not.
Corollary IIII.
Whatever is thought of is apprehended; otherwise though
we think we are thinking of it, we are not.
Whatever not subject to the conditions of thought is unthought
