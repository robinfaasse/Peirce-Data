The Ground of dogmatical dialectics is that the light of necessary truths is a participation of the infinite reason.

<u>Proof</u>. According to dogmatical dialectics that which is antecedent to science is itself science; that is, there are certain major premisses from which (by means of the minor premisses of experience) all other knowledge is deduced.

No knowledge can be deduced from premisses whose truth is not given.

$\therefore$ The truth of the first principles is given

No truth can be given by that which is deduced from it

$\therefore$ the truth of a first principles must be given in the very proposition itself.
