A definition is an analysis. Now we may not [ins]have[/ins] [del]have[/del] the conception defined and yet may have all the elements of which it would be composed. That is the synthesis of some of our conceptions is impossible. I will give two illustrations, one simple and the other practical.

Suppose somebody should talk about [del]a[/del] [ins]as[/ins] & when we asked him what they were he should say they were four-sided triangles. The definition would be but too clear. And we should proceed to show that he had no such conception. This case is too simple.

Suppose someone should say [del]they[/del] he could imagine two persons interchanging personalities. I should tell him [del]how[/del] I knew what he wished to imagine & that it could not be imagined.

These [del]ideas[/del] [ins]conceptions[/ins] are unthinkable from their own nature. Infinity however is unthinkable from our nature. Still its definition is obvious. It is that influxual dependency of quality which [del]from transcends reality[/del] surpasses reality.
