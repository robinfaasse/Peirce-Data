[u]That There is No Need of Transcendentalism[/u]

There can be no need of a Transcendental Philosophy if [del]reason[/del] right reason does not lead to contradictions of [u]a priori[/u] principles

For the principles of the Syllogism itself we must assume to be right in all cases since it is that which we use & the only thing we can use in refuting [del]the use[/del] any use of reason.

Therefore the premises must be false, that is to say the observation is false, Not the [del]facts[/del] in themselves for facts have no truth or falsity but only [del]conceptions[/del] [ins]judgements[/ins]. Therefore the observing power or [ins]by[/ins] that [ins]which[/ins] the mind modifies the fact the [u]a priori[/u] conception must contain the falsity

The falsity must be [ins]made[/ins] evident by the transcendentalist to a mind still using the [u]a priori[/u] conceptions that is they must be made to contradict each other.
