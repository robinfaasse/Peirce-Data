That the PERFECT is the great Subject of Metaphysics.

I assert [del]that[/del] there is only one conception whose analysis [ins]or explanation[/ins] affords any difficulty to the metaphysician & that is the Perfect. I will enumerate four kinds of perfection

Quantity (I use this as a concrete term) is always Substance. This is not because the word is a concrete; for Quality on the other hand is always Accident. Now Quantity like every thing else has three stages, the Null, [del]in the sense of that[/del] [ins]the[/ins] Positive, & that which is so Positive that it becomes Null & [del]which[/del] is therefore contradictory both of Nullity & Positivity — the Perfect. Null Quantity is Unity, Positive Quantity is Plurality. Perfect Quantity is Totality, Hence there is no totality of [del]Accidents[/del] [ins]Accidents[/ins]

So with Quality. Null Quality is Negation. Positive Quality is Affirmation. Perfect Quality is Infinity. [del]Qualit[/del] Hence there is no Infinity of Substance
