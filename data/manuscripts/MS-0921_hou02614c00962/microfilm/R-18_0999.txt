Things in my consciousness are always in the concrete

blueness only expresses my capacity of thinking
a blue thing. I say I have the conception blueness
because I have a capacity which enables me to
think of a blue thing.

I say I have not the conception 4 sided triangle
because I cannot think a four sided triangle

A two sided figure is in a different position,
I can think indefinitely near to it, [del]namely very[/del]
[del]obtuse triangle[/del]

[del]I[/del] [ins]In[/ins] the one case I undertake to think [del](4)[/del] what
I do not think (triangle). In this case I have a
right to say, not only I can't think it, it cannot
be, [del]for a quality exists only applied[/del] a quality is a
mental view.

In the other case, I have no right to say it cannot
exist, for when I lengthen the base of a triangle, I
gradually approach it & if I do not reach it it is
obviously because I cannot think long enough. There
is nothing impossible about the qualities.
