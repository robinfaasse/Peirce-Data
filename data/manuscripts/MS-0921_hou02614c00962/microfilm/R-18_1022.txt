[u]Transcendentalism.[/u]

This indicates a peculiar treatment which leads
to a particular kind of results. It begins in
the Logical Method. It then examines exactly how
the Notion was introduced into the mind. And it
then asks whether the Notion is true to the object
or not — whether it is [u]objectively valid[/u]. If it finds
that the Notion is a necessary condition to knowing
the object it decides that it is [u]objectively valid[/u]. Other-
wise it gives no answer; [u]speculatively[/u] it doubts; [u]prac-[/u]
[u]tically[/u] it believes or not on practical considerations.
