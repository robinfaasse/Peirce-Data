Of Objects [date]1857 Oct 25[/date]

When we think of a mind thinking of a thing, our
thought has 5 elements
1 the noumenon or the thing regarded simply
2 the object or the thing regarded as thought of
3 the act of thinking
4 the phenomenon or the thought
5 the thinker or the Ego.

1 The noumenon is not even in the system of formal pan-
theism, part of the soul.
2 [del]Inasmuch as[/del] the existence of the mind is a necessary condition
for the existence of the object. It is not mind then nor is it matter
What other thing can we think of but an idea.
3 The act of thinking is implied in the thought. But the [del]of[/del] Influx is not
implied in the idea
4 The phenomenon which I call the thought is a representation of the
object
5 The Ego consists of the field of consciousness, but the thinker is
the soul itself.
