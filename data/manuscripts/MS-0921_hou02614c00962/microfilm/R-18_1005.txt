There are only three ways in which thoughts can
be compounded viz:
1 By conjunction or the thinking both at the same time
2. By adjection or the thinking of one in the other
3. By synthesis or the thinking of both in a third.
[del]Two thoughts, however, cannot be present to the mind[/del]
We cannot, however, be conscious doubly at once
hence two thoughts cannot be present as two to the
mind at once.
Synthesis is a compound action of conjunction &
adjection. Hence, in all composition of thought there
is adjection.
