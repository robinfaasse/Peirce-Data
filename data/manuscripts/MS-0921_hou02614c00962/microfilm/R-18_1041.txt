C. As an intention.

Finally, dialectics is fundamentally wrong in idea.

1. It seeks to substitute common-sense or what we know already for observation of what we have to learn. It is lazy.

2. It substitutes human speculations for fact. It is arrogant.

3. It thinks by dividing, analyzing, knowledge to add to it. It is trifling.
