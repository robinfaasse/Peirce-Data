Again the fundamental distinction of psychology is be-
tween the soul & the body, to show that this is a me-
taphysical distinction. The soul is that which moves
itself; the body is that which moves but does not move
itself. When I say that the soul moves itself, I do not
mean that it originates any force, but that a certain
amount of force which is in it always moves in it
& never out from it, [ins]*[/ins] so that every force passing
through it is subject to modification by a force within
it & belonging to it. In this respect it is that the soul
