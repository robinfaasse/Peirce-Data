[u]Domain of Metaphysics.[/u]

Metaphysics is the philosophy of all that we are conscious of.

A definition of any science or philosophy must not be expressed in terms of that philosophy, but must contain the ideas which suggest the science.

A philosophy differs from a science inasmuch as observation is no part of its procedure. The philosopher as such only reflects on the results of science.

The science of the thoughts is a branch of psychology.

Such is pure metaphysics. It may also be defined as all [del]knowledge[/del] truly & entirely [u]a priori[/u], since the consciousness is the only source of what is [u]a priori[/u].
