<u>The Use of Metaphysics as a Study.</u>

All proveable propositions have for their ancestors a
major premiss & a minor premiss, a major premiss
and a minor premiss, a major premiss & a minor
premiss—until we come to their very progenitors which
are original truths. Some of these come from ex-
perience but the original major premiss cannot
come to us so, for a major premiss "distributes
the middle term" & must therefore be either
Universal (All Y is X) or negative. Now experience
unreasoned upon (which has no ancestry) cannot
be universal. Neither can it be negative; for instance,
the Proposition this is not Green cannot be an experience
for it is a thought of green & that green not given by
the experience.
