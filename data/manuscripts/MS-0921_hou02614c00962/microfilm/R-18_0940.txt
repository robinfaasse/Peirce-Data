[u]Of the Stages of the Category of Modality or Chance.[/u]

The arrangement of the stages of this category
strikes us at once as peculiar. In the other cate-
gories we begin with the [del]Base[/del] [ins]Predicable[/ins].
That is the first stage. Then we pass through
the space of the Positive and finally arrive at the
Perfect. But here we have six stages [ins]Impossibility[/ins]
Possibility, Non-actuality, actuality, [del]Contingency[/del], necessity.
How is this explained?

In the first place is the Possibility & Necessity
of the Category, physical or logical.

1 It cannot be logical. For logical Possibility
& Necessity have no actual between them but only
the probable.

2 All the non-actual must be physically im-
possible, [del]because[/del] [ins]for[/ins] it is non-actual because nothing
made it exist and it is physically impossible that
anything should be which nothing has caused to
exist. None of the actual can be possible.
