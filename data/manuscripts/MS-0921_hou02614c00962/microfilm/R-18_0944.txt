[u]Explantions of The Categories[/u].

The [ins]first[/ins] [del]stages[/del] [ins]is[/ins] [del]are[/del] the Bare Category. [u]Unity[/u] is that Number which makes Number conceivable without itself realizing that conception. It is the Negative of number. [u]Negation[/u] is that quality which has not enough quality to exist as positive quality; it is unpre-dicated quality. Mere, simple quality. [u]Community[/u] is that relation which makes dependence conceivable but [del]has[/del] [ins]is[/ins] not that which is necessary to positive dependence succeedence in time. It is the negative of dependence. It is such a mere dependence that it ceases to be such. [u]Conceivability[/u] is that possibility which has not that which makes possibility possible. It is mere simple possibility. The negative of any compatibility. A merely formal possibility.
