[u]That Infinity is an Unconscious Idea[/u] [date]Oct-25. 1859[/date]

The conception infinite is not in the [del]mind[/del] [ins]at[/ins] consciousness any more than that of a foursided triangle is. The four-sided triangle however is not in the soul at all. Is infinity? If not what follows?

When a thing influences the soul, its effect comes into the field of consciousness or it does not. In the former case we call the modification of consciousness a true thought; in the latter we may call the effect of the influence an unconscious idea. Now Faith says, the infinite does influence the soul — as infinite. It follows that we have an unconscious idea of it. Here then we have found where the infinite resides in the mind.

But the infinite is merely the degree to which we can never think. If, then, [del]infinite[/del] qualities are not in the mind or field of consciousness, [del]quality[/del] the conception quality is not in the mind. If quality is not in the mind unity plurality totality are not in the mind.
