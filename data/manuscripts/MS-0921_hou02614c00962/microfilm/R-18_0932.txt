[u]New Names & Symbols for Kant's Categories.[/u]

Quantity, Quality, Relation & Modality are not very
good names for the Categories. They should express
four conceptions which have each three stages,
but the do rather express the stage of the Con-
ception. Quality is a good name. But Quantity
is the stage of perfection of Thing. [del]That also is
perhaps objectionable.[/del] I will therefore give them
new names, and lest these should be objected
too I will invent symbols to express them.

[del]Nullity[/del] Simplicity I will represent by [fig]circle[/fig]
Positively [fig]dot[/fig]
Perfection [fig]circle with dot[/fig]

[del]Thing[/del] Quality Dependence Fact I will represent
by letters which shall not stand for any partic-
ular names, nor interfere with logical letters
but which yet shall be easy to remember viz:

S P Q R
