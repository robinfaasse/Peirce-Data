1. A special faculty gives us the infinite.

A power which we must exert may be viewed on two sides. On the one hand it is a [u]faculty[/u] & on the other it is a [u]constraint[/u]. So Kant, seeing that it is not the Understanding which gives us the infinite, makes a special faculty therefor. Sir W. Hamilton shows how it is an inability of the understanding. Still it may be a special inability.

It is only qualities that are infinite. It is only those things that may be predicates that are negative and they are only negative in predication. It is certain that infinity & negation are related. Thus

The Quality A is either $\begin{cases} \text{1 Infinite } \begin{cases} \text{when its essential} \\ \text{limitation is unre-} \\ \text{alized} \end{cases} \\ \text{2 Positive \& Finite - when it is realized}^{\text{x}} \\ \text{3 Negative } \begin{cases} \text{when its positivity is} \\ \text{unrealized.} \end{cases} \end{cases}$

What is thus expressed may be elucidated as follows. It is the nature of our thought of qualities that A when realized should contain A & $\frac{1}{\text{A}}$ as for instance the size of an object is large or small. Now the negation of a is the infinite of $\frac{1}{\text{a}}$ & vice versa [u]$\infty\text{A} = \frac{1}{\text{oA}}$[/u]

$^{\text{x}}$ I call a quality realized when it is really made to belong to a substance, even in thought.
