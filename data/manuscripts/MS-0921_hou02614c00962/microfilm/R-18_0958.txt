[u]Proposition[/u]. All unthought is thought-of.[ins]x[/ins]

x The words [del]thought[/del], thought-of, unthought, unthought-of I use in a [u]Potential[/u] sense.

[u]Proof[/u].

We either [ins]think[/ins] of it as thought, when we have a positive conception of it; or as unthought, when we have a negative conception of it.

[u]Corollary I[/u].
We have only negative conceptions of things out of us.

[u]Corollary II[/u].
All noumena are unconditioned.

[u]Corollary III[/u]
All things in heaven & earth are thought of, for the un-thought-of is not thought-of [u]∴[/u] not unthought. All thought is thought-of [u]∴[/u] No unthought-of is thought [u]∴[/u] the un-thought-of is neither thought or unthought [del]So[/del] it is not.

Whatever is not subject to the conditions of thought is [del]unthought[/del]
Ergo, All the Unconditioned is apprehended, or
