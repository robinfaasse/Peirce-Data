SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
BUREAU OF AMERICAN ETHNOLOGY
WASHINGTON, December 23, 1895.

My dear Sir:

I trust you will pardon my delay in replying to your letter of August 7th last, which unfortunately was mislaid and has only now come to light. I have pleasure in directing the transmission to you of the seventh to the twelfth annual reports of this Bureau, as well as a copy of the Bibliography of the Eskimo Language, and a list of our publications.

If you have observed an apparently definite relationship between the Tatar and Eskimo languages I shall be very glad indeed to receive further word from you in regard to the matter. It is a well known fact, however, that the Yuit of extreme northeastern Asia belong to the Eskimauan stock.

In the Eskimo bibliography you will find numerous references to works which will aid you in your linguistic investigations.

Yours very truly,

[fig]signature[/fig]
Director.

Mr C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pennsylvania.
