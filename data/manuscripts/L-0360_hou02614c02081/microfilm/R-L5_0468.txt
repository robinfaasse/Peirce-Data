U. S. Geological Survey
Washington, D. C.

[date]June 19 1900[/date]

My dear Peirce:

I have failed to reply to your letter written me long ago about Herbert Spencer's Philosophy, from the fact that I have been engaged in a contest which has occupied all my time and thought, and have not been able to give your letter due consideration until now.

For several years I have been thinking of writing a criticism on Spencer's work in anthropology for the purpose of showing his failure to recognize essential facts, his use of the statements of facts that are worthless, and his employment of miscellaneous trivial facts. But I do not know when I can find time, as the years of my life increase, work piles on me. Some time when we meet I should like to confer with you on this matter more fully.

With many thanks for the article you send, believe me,

Cordially yours,
[del]J W Powell[/del]

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Century Club, N. Y.
