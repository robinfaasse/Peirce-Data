Drayton has,
Heaven on her shape such cost bestow'd,
And with such bounties blest,
No limb of hers but might have made
A goddess at the least.

In such easy stanza perfect rhymes may be expected. Herrick has,
Yet let him keep the rest -
But keep them with repining restlessness -
Let him be rich and weary; that, at least,
If goodness lead him not, yet weariness
May toss him to my breast.

We shall see that breast was short. It hardly seems that imperfect rhyming can be accepted as a sufficient explanation of the tendency to rhyme least with syllables in est.
