<u>Footnote continued</u>

Steal was drawled by Cheke, 1550, and was regularly spelled Steale.

Stealth was sometimes spelled stelth.

Weal was pronounced 'weel' by Gill.

Wealth was pronounced 'welth' by Gill, pp. 39. 77. 85. 87. 89; and by Butler, pp. 2, 20, 38, 139, 141.
