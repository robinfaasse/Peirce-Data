[del]Thou, Collatine, shalt oversee this will,[/del]
Yet die I will not 'till my Collatine
Have heard the cause of my untimely death;
That he may vow, in that sad hour of mine,
Revenge on him that made me stop my breath.
My stained blood to Tarquin I'll bequeath,
Which by him tainted shall for him be spent,
And as his due writ in my testament.

Breadth. Generally spelled without the [u]a[/u] in the XVIIth century.
[ins]Shortened by [del]Butler[/del] [ins]Butler[/ins], pp. 13, 18, 43, 44.[/ins] / by Gil, p. 125, and by Butler, pp. 11, 13, 26.
Breath. Shortened by Bullokar. Stated by the Oxford Dictionary to
have been "only recently shortened." But it was [del]usually[/del] [ins]more commonly[/ins] spelled without
the [u]a[/u] than with it in the XVIIth Century; and was mostly rhymed with
short syllables. The Oxf. Dict. statement alone prevents us
from holding that it was invariably short.
