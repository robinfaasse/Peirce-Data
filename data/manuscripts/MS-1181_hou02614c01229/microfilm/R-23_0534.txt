Except when final, the use of y and w as vowels has been much
restricted since 1600. We now spell rice (never ryce), ripe (never
rype), inch (not ynch), irk (not yrk), iron (not yron), satire (not satyre)
cinnamon (not synamon), poison (not poyson), boil (not boyle), sail (not
sayl), fuel (not fewel), suet (not sewet), persuade (not perswade).
But y is now almost invariably used to represent the Greek υ. We
write amethyst (no longer amethist), crystal (not cristal), clyster
(not clister), etymology (not etimologie), hyssop (not hisope), mystery
(not misterie), thyme (not thime), type (not tipe). We also spell oyster
(no longer oister), royal (not roiall).
