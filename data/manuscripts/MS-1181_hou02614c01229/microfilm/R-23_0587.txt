Beest, was sometimes spelled best in the XVIth century, and
was often rhymed with words in est. Shakespeare has the following:

In food, in sport, and life-preserving rest,
To be disturb'd, would mad or man or beast.

Lady. You Sheep & I pasture: shall that finish the jest?
Boyet. So you grant pasture for me.
Lady. Not so gentle beast.

There are several similar rhymes of beast in Shakespeare. From
several by Lord Brooke, [del]some[/del] one may be quoted which ends an act.
