The letters i and u do end a few, — the pronouns I, you, thou, also
salmagundi, broccoli, alkali, and some words adopted from
foreign languages unchanged, as alibi, vermicelli, spaghetti,
spermaceti, bureau, adieu, lieu, portmanteau, beau, etc. But
if and v end no words, at all. They can perfectly well end
syllables; and v does end many, j very few except maj-esty.

How our Spelling has been determined.
