A and e are frequently found interchanged, according to our notions; as in chaw, clarke, currant (for current) osiar, palfray, rabate, rapiar, regratar, ship-wracke; and bedlem, beggersbrier, frier, lettise (for lattice), salet (for salad), pedler, etc.

So also are o and u; as in encomber, mosell (for muzzle), pommell, sodeine, sommer, stirrope; and bumbast, burrage, furrier (for forayer), slubber, turbut, etc.

We also find such spellings as cabanet, canapie, dandraffe, harth, squamish, entrals, gage (for gauge), gard, carret (for carrot), querister, sexten, satterday, iubile, cushen, gesse, surfet, camphire, venime, frontire, shrike, capston, banquetor, bason, bollyne (for bowline), embroder, alcumie, durt, chuse, bruse, sutable, mustache, maister, flea (for fly), shest (a rope), hey (for hay), conceave, hainous, fraight, retrait, beesome, seeing, reeke (for rick), cloas, peece (for peace), threed, harneis, piepe, forspeed
