[u]Weapon[/u] was drawled by Butler, 1633; Jones, 1701, shortens it; but the Expert Orthographist, 1704, pronounces it 'weepon'. Lord Brooke has

For thought is not the weapon
Wherewith thoughts-ease men cheapen.

This seems to [del]signify[/del] [ins]signify[/ins] that the word was drawled, though it is not quite certain how cheapen was pronounced.
