Least was drawled by Cheke, 1550, Smith, 1568, Bullokar, 1580,
and Gil, 1619, (p. 34). But Hodges, 1643, says it is pronounced exactly
like lest, with which it would easily be confounded. Rhymes
of least do occur, but not where an imperfect rhyme is impera-
tive. Shakespeare has

Wishing me like to one more rich in hope,
Featur'd like him, like him with friends possest,
Desiring this mans art, and that mans skope,
With what I most injoy contented least.

George Herbert has,

Yet let him keep the rest_
But keep them with repining restlessnesse_
Let him be rich and weary; that, at least,
If goodness lead him not, yet wearinesse
May toss him to my breast.
