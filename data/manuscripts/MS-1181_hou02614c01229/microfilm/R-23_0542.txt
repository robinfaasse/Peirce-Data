Chapter I. Spelling.

[u]§ 1. The Aim of Orthography.[/u]

[u]Art 1. What it is.[/u] The sole object of spelling is to render words recognizable.

The use of a system of orthography is to render words recognizable with the greatest possible ease, and without distracting the reader's attention from the substance of what is printed (or written) to the spelling.
