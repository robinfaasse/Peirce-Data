[u]Endeavour[/u] was drawled by Gill (p. 82) and by Butler (p. 49). But it was regularly spelled without the u.

[u]Jealous[/u] was shortened by Gill and was regularly spelled jelous.

[u]Treachery[/u] is a word concerning the Shakespearian pronunciation of which [del]no[/del] [ins]other[/ins] evidence is at hand [ins]than that it was rhymed by Shakespeare with the short lechery.[/ins]. It was regularly spelled with the ea.

Against such Lewdsters, and their lechery,
Those that betray them do no treachery.
