The Alphabet.

We use the 23 Roman letters together with [ins]W, Y, J.[/ins] [del]W, Y, J.[/del]

Our lower-case letters are slightly modified from a mode of writing nearly allied to that of our Anglo-Saxon ancestors. The [u]futhore[/u], or alphabet of runes, the prechristian letters of the Norse was the first that was used in England. The Roman alphabet came with christianity, about [date]A.D. 600[/date]. Two styles of writing were brought in, one into Northumbria by the Irish, the other into Wessex by from Gaul. But there was a strong resemblance between them. In writing Anglo-Saxon, the letters q and z were rejected, and k was scarcely ever written.
