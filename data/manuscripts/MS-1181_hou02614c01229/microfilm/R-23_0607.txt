5th, Final ie is changed to y; as in try, body, mortify, bray.

6th, Y and w, when vowels and not final, are, except in monosyllables, generally changed to i and u respectively (except that y representing the Greek [del]u[/del] [ins]υ[/ins] remains) or are otherwise eliminated. Thus, we spell persuade (no longer perswade), fuel (not fewell), poison (not poyson), boil (not boyle), sail (not sayle), [ins]adieu (not adiew).[/ins]

7th, T before i followed by a vowel, and s in -ise are in many words changed to c; as vicious (no longer vitious) and practice (not practise).

8th, U following a or o is frequently dropped; or ou is changed to oo or to u; as in danger (not daunger), orator (not oratour), troop (not troupe), abundant (not aboundant).

9th, E is not omitted before the termination -red, [ins]-ring, etc[/ins] nor is e inserted before -led, [del]-ling, etc,[/del] as [del]ministered (no longer blustring)[/del] [ins]blustering (no longer blustring)[/ins], bridled (not brideled).
