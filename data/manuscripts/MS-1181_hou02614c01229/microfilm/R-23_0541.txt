9th, Modern spelling is [del]much[/del] [ins]more[/ins] strict in following the
etymology of words derived from the classical languages.
This is particularly true of words easily recognizable as Greek.
In the case of words coming from the Latin through the French,
while we more usually follow the Latin, yet in many cases
our spelling is French while that which was predominant
in 1600 was Latin.
