[fig]dagger[/fig] Bread was drawled by Cheke, 1550, Smith, 1568 (fol. 11, 41), Gil, 1619 (pp. 28, 37, 73), and Coote (15th Ed. 1624), Hodges, 1643, shortens it.

Death Deaf was drawled by Cheke and Smith (fol. 24). It remained long until, with other words in ea, it took the sound of ee. It was shortened in the XVIIIth century.

Dread was drawled by Smith and by Butler, 1633 (p. 129).

Heavy was drawled by Gil (p. 119) and by Butler (p. 43).

Lead, the metal, was drawled by Smith (fol. 11, 43), by Gil (p. 39), and by Butler (pp. 43, 119)

Meant was drawled by Butler (p. 57.)

Pleasure was drawled by Gil (pp. 89, 140) and by Butler (pp. 19, 24, 43, 55, 107, etc.)

Spread, present tense, was drawled by Cheke and by Butler (pp. 90, 118.)

Sweat, present tense, was drawled by Smith (fol. 20) and Gil (pp. 48, 111.)

Threat was drawled by Gil (p. 99.)

Weapon was drawled by Butler (pp. 8, 60).
