Sir John Davies ends a stanza thus:
That when the air doth dance her finest measure,
Then art thou born, the gods and mens sweet pleasure.

Drayton ends a stanza thus:
She [ins]all[/ins] one of her kinde
Knoweth true measure,
And her unmatched minde
Is heavens treasure.

The following is earlier:
So long as I was in your sight
I was your heart, your soul, your treasure;
And evermore you sobb'd and sigh'd
Burning in flames beyond all measure.

Two imperfect rhymes in one quatrain would be too much. In
order to appreciate the next example, it should be known that
