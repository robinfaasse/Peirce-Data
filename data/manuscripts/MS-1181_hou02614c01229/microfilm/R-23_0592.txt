East was sometimes spelled est in the phonetic writing of the
XV century; and it is usually rhymed with words in est. Shakespeare
has, for example,

O weary night, O long and tedious night,
Abate thy houres, shine comforts from the East
That I may backe to [u]Athens[/u] by day-light,
From these that my poore companie detest.

Lord, how mine eyes throw gazes to the East!
My heart doth charge the watch; the morning rise
Doth cite each moving sense from idle rest.
