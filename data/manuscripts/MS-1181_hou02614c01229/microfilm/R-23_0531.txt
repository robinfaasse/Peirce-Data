The entire class of words now spelled with [u]ea[/u] but having
the sound of short [u]e[/u] has now been reviewed; and they show
clearly that, in the first fixation of our spelling, as little regard
was paid to the pronunciation as possible. Many words had
perforce to be spelled phonetically, since there was no other principle to
determine the spelling, but wherever etymology could be used
for this purpose it was preferred to the pronunciation.
