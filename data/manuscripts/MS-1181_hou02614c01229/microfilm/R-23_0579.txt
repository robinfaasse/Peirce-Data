Weal was regularly spelled weale, and must have been drawled. But
wealth is made short by Bullokar (1580), by Gil (pp. 39, 77, 85, 87, 89), and by
Butler (pp. 2, 20, 138, 139, 141). Thus, its [ins]a[/ins] is purely etymological.
