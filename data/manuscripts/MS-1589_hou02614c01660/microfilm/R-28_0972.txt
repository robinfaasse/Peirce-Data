CHAPTER I.
[u]OUTLINE OF THE IMAGINARY GRAPHIST'S[/u]
[u]PROCEDURE.[/u]

§1. A person who desires to put this system to use will usually have to imagine that a Graphist is using it as a calculus, although he himself is presumably using it for the purpose for which it is designed, namely, as an aid in the analysis of reasoning and not at all as a calculus, for which purpose it is not at all adapted.
