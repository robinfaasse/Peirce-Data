[pn]Preface.[/pn]

The system of existential
graphs is intended to afford a
method for the analysis of
all necessary reasonings into
their ultimate elements. No
transformations are permitted
except [u]insertions and omissions[/u],
and the results of series of per-
missible insertions and omissions.
The peculiar formal signs are
the fewest with which it is
possible to represent all the
operations of necessary rea-
sonings.

The system was invented in
[date]January 1897[/date]. The Monist refused to
publish an account of it.
