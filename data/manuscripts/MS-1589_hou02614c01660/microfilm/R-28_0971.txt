A [u]scroll[/u] consists of two cuts, one within the other. The outer one is called the [u]outloop[/u], the inner the [u]inloop[/u]. The area of the outloop when there is only the enclosure of the inloop upon it is called the [u]outer close[/u] of the scroll.

A scroll with nothing on its outer close unless it be lines running from the inloop to the outloop is called a [u]double-cut[/u].

A graph not connected with any other by [ins]any[/ins] ligatures is called a [del][u]whole[/u][/del] graph; if so connected, an [u]alligate[/u] graph.
