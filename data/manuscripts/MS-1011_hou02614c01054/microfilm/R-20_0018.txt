The brass-scale, [ins]M₁[/ins] is a circular arc, 2 cm wide and
40 cm long and is divided into 200 divisions.

For small velocities power was derived from a turbine
of [del]1/5[/del] [ins]1/10[/ins] horse-power, using the water of the water-supply,
under a pressure of 30 pounds per square inch. Mid-
dling velocities were obtained with a Siemens electro-motor
of 1/4 horse-power maximum, and for velocities of 15 to
25 meters per second a larger Siemens electro-motor of 1/2
horse-power maximum was used. The current for the motors
was furnished by a storage-battery of 36 elements of 72 volts
tension.

For the intended experiments it was requisite to be able to vary
the rotary speed of the electro-motor through wide limits.
Attention had, therefore, to be directed to the resistances
in the circuit.
