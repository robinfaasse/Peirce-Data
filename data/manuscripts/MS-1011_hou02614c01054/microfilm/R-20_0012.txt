In the place where it is desired to determine the
pressure, a round hole is made and is connected
by means of an airtight tube, with a closed vessel in
the centre of the rotating apparatus. From this vessel
an india-rubber tube leads to a differential manometer,
which measures the difference of pressure between the
air brought to the centre of rotation and that of the
surrounding atmosphere.
