experiment was continued through 500 revolutions. It was
consequently noticed that the strong heating of the wires
during the experiment increased the resistance and
caused a falling off of the velocity. [del]Exact observation[/del]
[del]thus became impossible;[/del] for the aluminium pointer was

was almost entirely conquered by interposing a combina-
tion of fluid and solid resistance. Since the resistance
of liquids diminishes with increasing temperature, in-
stead of increasing as that of solids does, it follows
that [ins]by[/ins] a suitable [del]combined[/del] arrangement of the two an almost constant
