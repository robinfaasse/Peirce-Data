It is an important fact that 65537 (one more than the number of code-words) is a prime number, that is, is exactly divisible by no whole number, while 65536 itself is exactly divisible by no odd number.

If, then, the result of any arithmetical operation had more than sixteen figures, all the figures to the left of the right hand sixteen should be cut off and being written under the others [ins]at[/ins] the extreme right hand places should be subtracted from them.

If in performing the inverse operation one had to subtract a greater number from a smaller one, then it would only be necessary to add certain figures to the left of the smaller, every figure so added being also added to the [del]place[/del] figure sixteen places to the right.
