When you say "6 times 7," [del]so[/del] 6 is called
the [u]multiplier[/u] and 7 the [u]multiplicand[/u];
but if we turn it the other way and
say 7 times 6, though that is the same
thing, then 7 is the [u]multiplier[/u] and 6 the
[u]multiplicand[/u]. Since 6 times 7 make 42,
we say 42 is the [u]product[/u] of 6 and 7. We
say, also, that 6 is a [u]factor[/u] of 42; and so is 7;
but 42 is a [u]multiple[/u] of 6; and so it is of 7.
