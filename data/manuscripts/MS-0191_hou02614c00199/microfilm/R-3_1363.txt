[fig]A horizontal scale divided into 10 numbered segments from 0 to 10[/fig]

Here is one of the measures used by people who know a great deal. Each of the spaces is a cent measure, just as a penny is a money cent. A cent measure is called a centimetre. Every scholar should copy this scale [ins]20 times[/ins] [del]every day[/del] by his eye and his pencil on his slate. [Whoever has the sum of the errors of the 200 centimetres the least should receive a [del]small[/del] decoration.] Thus, at the end of a few weeks, you will know a centimetre well.

[fig]A small horizontal scale with subdivisions[/fig]

When this has been learned tolerably well, you must begin to learn to divide a centimetre into ten equal parts. Each of these equal parts is called a millimetre.
