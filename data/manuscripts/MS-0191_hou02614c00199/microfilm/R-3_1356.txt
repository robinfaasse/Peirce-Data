If we were to put the new chalk in
the left pan and the old chalk in the
right pan, they would balance, because
they are just alike. But if we were now
[del]to[/del] replace the new chalk by the tin, since
they have the same weight, the whole would
[del]still be in balance.[/del] So it makes no difference
whether the chalk is on the left and the tin
on the right or whether the tin is on the left
and the chalk on the right.
