COMMONWEALTH OF MASSACHUSETTS.
SUPREME JUDICIAL COURT,
COURT HOUSE, BOSTON.

[date]February 10. 1894[/date].

My dear Mr Peirce

I have seen with interest
your prospectus for 12 vols Principles of Philos-
ophy at $2.50 per volume and beg you to
enter my name as a subscriber. The mathematical
portion I should not understand. From the rest
I should expect pleasure and instruction.

Very truly yours
Oliver W. Holmes, Jr.

C. S. Peirce Esq
