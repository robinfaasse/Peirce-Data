[u]Rollandus. De Arismetrica[/u] ([date]1424[/date])

MS. on paper. XVth Century folio. 32 lines to page

Height 7 [ins]27[/ins]/48 x 11 in

Pars Theorica (presumably from Boethius) takes up about 1/3 of the volume.

The operations are numerare, addere, demere, duplare, mediare, multiplicare, dividere, et radicem extrahere.

Teaches cross multiplication of several figures

$(a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$

extraction of roots
