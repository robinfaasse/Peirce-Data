its generating tangent with infinity is reversed on that line at the crossing; if, then, the curve
stops before that intersection reaches its initial position, no element of the curve enfilades
another; in that case, because of the crossing infinity, the point running along the final tan-
gent reaches the intersection of the initial tangent at a point in advance of the point of inci-
dence without crossing infinity, and at the same time the initial tangent has to be turned against
the direction of generation to reach the line joining the extremities, while owing to the change
in the direction of the turning, the intersection of the tangents is still in advance on the ini-
tial one, which now has to be turned backward to be brought into coincidence with the junction of
the extremities.
