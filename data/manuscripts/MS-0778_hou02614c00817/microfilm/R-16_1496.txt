Of such dough philosophers are baked. Thank God they
don't take to logic. They naturally would not like it. I will
write the truth about them in one damning sentence. It
is so atrocious you would hardly believe it, but this
is true. —
Their method is first to make up their minds what they are
going to support and then cast about for reasons to prove it.
I am not joking. I am not exaggerating!
