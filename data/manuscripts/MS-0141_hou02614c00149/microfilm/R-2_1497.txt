As far as I am aware little has been made out concerning topical geometry except the census-theorem of Euler and Listing, with some partial extensions. But this does not embrace the whole doctrine. For instance, the census-theorem would per-mit a net upon a spheroidal surface having 12 pentagonal faces, or regions, one hexagonal face, 22 coigns, and 33 boun-daries. But this is easily seen to be impossible. Moreover, Listing, among other faults, has committed that of neglecting all topical singularities except [del]edges and extremities[/del] [ins]limits[/ins].
