Of these three, Se[ins]c[/ins]ondness is the easiest to comprehend, being the element that the rough-and-tumble of this world renders most prominent. We talk of [u]hard[/u] facts. That hardness, that compulsiveness of experience, is Secondness. A door is slightly ajar. You try to open it. Something prevents. You put your shoulder against it, and experience a sense of effort and a sense of resistance. These are not two forms of consciousness; they are two aspects of one [del]double[/del] two-sided consciousness.
