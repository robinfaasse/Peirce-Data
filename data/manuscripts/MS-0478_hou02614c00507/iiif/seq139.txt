Graphs; and no Graph is partly inside and partly
outside any cut, but is wholly in one Area or on
the sheet. Every Area has its Entire Graph, like the sheet,
be it only a blank. A graph-replica which has no
parts connected by Forms of Connection is a Spot. Beta
Graphs differ from Alpha Graphs, in that some of their
