The Rule of Iteration and Deiteration does not apply
to the broken cut.

does not apply[ins]to broken cuts[/ins]. For although two broken cuts one within
the other, [del]or[/del] both otherwise vacant, or a broken and a
full cut so circumstanced, may be written unenclosed,
yet, owing to the failure of Iteration and Deiteration,
this leads to nothing.
