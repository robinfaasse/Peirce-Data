[u]Speculative Grammar[/u]. A [u]sign[/u], or [u]Representamen[/u], is 
anything which [del]so[/del] stands in such a triadic relation to a 
[u]Second[/u], called its [u]Object[/u], as to be capable of determining 
a [u]Third[/u], called its [u]Interpretant[/u], to be in the same triadic 
relation to that Object [del]to[/del] [ins]in[/ins] which it stands itself. That is to 
say, the Interpretant must itself be a Representamen, copy-
ing the Sign and representing it to be a sign of its Object. 
Thus, the [ins]complete[/ins] Representamen represents the Object, not only 
as the sign [del]of[/del] which it is the copy does, but also inde-
pendently and [del]generally[/del] vaguely in a second Represen-
tamen.
