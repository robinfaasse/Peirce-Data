[ured]Corollaries[/ured]. Two graphs [del]well[/del] scribed on the sheet are,
both of them asserted; and any entire graph implies the truth of all 
its partial graphs.
Every blank part of the sheet is a partial graph.
[ured]Convention No. III[/ured]. ¶ 1. By a [del]cut[/del] shall be understood to 
mean a self-returning linear separation (naturally repre-
sented by a fine-drawn or peculiarly colored line) which 
severs all that it encloses from the sheet of assertion on 
which it stands itself, or from any other area on which it 
stands itself. The [ins]whole[/ins] space within the cut (but not com-
prising the cut itself) shall be termed the [u]area[/u] of the 
cut. Though the area of the cut is no part of the sheet of 
assertion, yet the cut together with its area [ins]and all that is on it,[/ins] conceived as 
so severed from the sheet, shall, under the name of the 
[u]enclosure[/u] of the cut, be considered as on the sheet of 
assertion or [ins]as on[/ins] such other area as the cut may stand upon.
