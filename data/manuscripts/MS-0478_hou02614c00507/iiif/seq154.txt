[ured]XII[/ured]. If a Cut contains only Enclosures, it makes no
difference whether any Graph is scribed just outside
it or in the Area of all the Enclosures.

[ured]XIII[/ured]. A Ligature is equivalent to a Selective at all
the places where its extremities and outermost parts
are.

[ured]XIV[/ured]. Two Graphs not connected in their innermost common
enclosure and not so connected without [ins]their ligature[/ins] [del]entering[/del] any other
cut are virtually not connected at all, not even through
another Graph.

[ured]XV[/ured]. A Branch of a Ligature extending into the Area of
a Cut without there being connected with any Hook is
of no effect.

[ured]XVI[/ured]. If two Selectives, Proper Names or other Individual
Symbols are joined by a Ligature passing from
without to within a Cut, it makes no difference
whether both, only one, or neither are within the
Cut.
