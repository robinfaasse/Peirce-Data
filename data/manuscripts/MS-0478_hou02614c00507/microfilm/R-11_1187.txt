[u]VIII[/u]. Any enclosure on the Area of which carries at once a Graph, A, and also an Enclosure consisting of a Cut whose Area carries nothing but A may be erased.

[u]IX[/u]. In any Area it is permitted to insert an enclosure containing at once [del]a[/del] [ins]any[/ins] graph, A, and also an enclosure consisting of a Cut carrying on its Area nothing but A.

[u]X[/u]. If [del]one[/del] [ins]any[/ins] graph, A, could, [del]be if scribed unenalised[/del] be transformed into another, B, then it is [del]anywhere[/del] [ins]permitted[/ins] in any area to insert an Enclosure consisting of a Cut carrying [del]on its[/del] A on its area together with an Enclosure consisting of a cut carrying only B on its Area.

[u]XI[/u]. Any Graph may be iterated [ins]with the same ligatures[/ins] within any number of additional cuts; and if any Graph occurs in Two Replicas [ins]with the same ligatures[/ins] one of which is within all the Cuts that the other is within the former Replica may be erased. But Iteration and De-iteration are commonly only useful within an even number of additional Cuts.
