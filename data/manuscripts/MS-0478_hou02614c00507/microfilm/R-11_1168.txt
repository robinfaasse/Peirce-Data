[u]Convention No. IX[/u]. ¶1. By a [u]rim[/u] shall be understood an oval line making it, with its contents, the expression [ins]either[/ins] of a rhema or a proper name of an [u]ens rationis[/u]. Such a rim may be drawn as a line of peculiar texture, or a gummed label with a colored border may be attached to the sheet. A dotted rim containing a graph some part of which is itself enclosed by [del]another[/del] [ins]a similar[/ins] inner dotted oval and with heavy dotted lines proceeding from marked points of this graph to hooks on the rim, shall be a spot expressing that [del]what is[/del] [ins]the individuals[/ins] denoted by lines of identity attached to the hooks (or the single such individual) have the character constituted by the truth of the graph to be possessed by the individuals denoted by those points
