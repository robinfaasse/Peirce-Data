[u]Speculative Grammar[/u]. A [del]sign[/del] [ins]sign[/ins], or [del]Representamen[/del] [ins]Representamen[/ins], is
anything which [del]is[/del] stands in such a triadic relation to a
Second, called its [u]Object[/u], as to be capable of determining
a Third, called its [u]Interpretant[/u], to be in the same triadic
relation to that [del]Object[/del] [ins]Object in[/ins] which it stands itself. That is to
say, the Interpretant must itself be a Representamen, copy-
ing the Sign and representing it to be a sign of its Object.
Thus, the [ins]complete[/ins] Representamen represents the Object, not only
as the sign [del]of[/del] which it is the copy does, but also inde-
pendently and generally vaguely in a second Represen-
tamen.
