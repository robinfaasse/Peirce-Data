[u]Convention No. VII[/u]. ¶ 1. The following spot-symbols shall be used, as if they were ordinary spot-symbols, except for special rules applicable to them: [Selectives are placed against [del]the[/del] hooks in order to render the meanings of the new spot-symbols clearer.]
[u]A[/u]q, A is a monadic character;
[u]A[/u]r, A is a dyadic relation;
[u]A[/u]s, A is a triadic relation;
X[u]A[/u], X is a proposition or fact;
X[u]A[/u]Y, Y possesses the character X;
X[u]A[/u][ins]Y[/ins]Z, Y stands in the dyadic relation X to Z;
X[u]A[/u][ins]Y[/ins]Z[ins]W[/ins], Y stands in the triadic relation X to Z for W.
