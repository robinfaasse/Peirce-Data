A Symbol is a Representamen whose Representative Firstness consists precisely in its [del]deter[/del] power of determining an Interpretant. Such, for example, is any word [ins](sentence, book, or other)[/ins] [del]or[/del] conventional sign. [del](The word 'man', for example,[/del] We speak of writing, or pronouncing the word 'man'. But, strictly speaking, the very word itself never exists. It consists in a [del]succession[/del] [ins]general[/ins] mode of succession of sounds, or representamens of sounds, which, according to the habits of our people, will [del]make them think[/del] call up in their minds the idea of a man. Thus, [del]the essence of[/del] a Symbol strictly consists in a law, governing existent things, but not itself existing.
