[u]II. Considerations concerning the Ethics of Terminology.[/u]

Certain thoughts on the subject of my heading have
forced themselves upon my reason, and appear to me
to be very solemn. Of course, I may be quite mistaken
about the matter. At the outset, therefore, I will assert
nothing, but will simply ask the reader, in aid of his
own deliberations, to set down a cross in [del]that[/del] [ins]of the[/ins] affir-
mative or negative column opposite to each [del]question[/del]
following questions [del]in[/del] [ins]as he comes to it,[/ins] case it should appear to him
[del]certain[/del] reasonably certain that the affirmative
or negative answer is the correct one. He will then
know whether he can deliberately accept or reject the
propositions which follow the questions. I do not
ask him to publish his answers, but merely to answer
the questions to himself in the order in which they
are put, with a view to aiding his own deliberations.
