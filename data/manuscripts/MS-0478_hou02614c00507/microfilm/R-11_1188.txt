[u]XII[/u]. If a Cut contains only Enclosures, it makes no difference whether any Graph is scribed just outside it or in the Areas of all the Enclosures.

[u]XIII[/u]. A Ligature is equivalent to a Selective at all the places where its extremities and outermost parts are.

[u]XIV[/u]. Two Graphs not connected in their innermost common enclosure and not so connected without [del]entering[/del] [ins]their ligature entering[/ins] any other cut are virtually not connected at all, not even through another Graph.

[u]XV[/u]. A branch of a Ligature extending into the Area of a Cut without there being connected with any Hook is of no effect.

[u]XVI[/u]. If two Selectives, Proper Names or other Individual Symbols are joined by a Ligature passing from without to within a Cut, it makes no difference whether both, only one, or neither are within the Cut.
