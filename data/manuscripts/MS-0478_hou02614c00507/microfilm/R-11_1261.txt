$A_{\underline{r}}$, $A$ is a dyadic relation;
$A_{\underline{s}}$, $A$ is a triadic relation;
$X A_{\underline{p}}$, $X$ is a [del]graph[/del] [ins]proposition[/ins]; [del]or symbol;[/del]
$X A Y$, $X$ is a quality, or monadic character, of $Y$; or $Y$ [ins]possesses the character[/ins] $X$.
