¶ 2. [del]And[/del] It is agreed that a certain sheet, or blackboard, shall, under the name of [u]The Sheet of Assertion[/u], be considered as representing the universe of discourse, and as asserting whatever is taken for granted between the graphist and the interpreter to be true of that universe. The sheet of assertion is therefore, a graph. [del]Certain parts being severed from the sheet will not be regarded as any part of it.[/del]

The graphist may place replicas of graphs upon the sheet of assertion; but this act, called [u]scribing[/u] a graph on the sheet of assertion, shall be understood to constitute the assertion of the truth of the graph scribed.
