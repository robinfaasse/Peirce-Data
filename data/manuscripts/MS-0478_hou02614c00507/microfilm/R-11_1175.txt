<u>Rule III</u>, called The Rule of Immaterial and Pseudographic Forms.

An unenclosed vacant cut may have any graph added to it; and
the enclosure of an oddly enclosed cut on whose area is a vacant
cut may be erased.

Further, two cuts the one containing [ins]only[/ins] the other, and the latter con-
taining nothing, can be added unenclosed to the Entire Graph of
the sheet; and two cuts, one enclosing nothing but the enclosure
of the [del]latter[/del] [ins]other[/ins] can be removed, leaving what was on the area of
the inner cut unenclosed by either of those cuts.
