Now everybody knows that the execution of a purpose has [del]three[/del] at least three very distinct parts. Some [del]part[/del] minds may insist upon a larger number; but there are, at least, these three. The first is the working out of the [ins]desire[/ins] [del]purpose[/del] itself; the second is the devising of a course of procedure by which the means at one's disposition at the outset, together with those which may be calculated to be forthcoming can be applied to the attainment of the desire; and the third is the energetic performance of the acts that this scheme requires.
