National Academy of Sciences. [ins]Peirce[/ins]

ARNOLD HAGUE,
HOME SECRETARY.

WASHINGTON, D. C.,
[date]April 25, 1902.[/date]

Prof. Chas. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Prof. Peirce:

Immediately upon the adjournment of the National Academy of Sciences I left town, and was absent several days, returning yesterday. I found your note asking for a card to the Cosmos Club, and I presume that you have obtained it from other sources.

Trusting that you will understand my delay in attending to the matter, believe me,

Yours very truly,
Arnold Hague
