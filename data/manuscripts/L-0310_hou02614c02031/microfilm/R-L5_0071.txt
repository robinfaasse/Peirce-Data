[u]National Academy of Sciences.[/u]

Office of the President.

Baltimore, [date]March 12, 1908[/date].

Ira Remsen,
President.

My dear Professor Peirce:

According to information received from the Secretary of the National Academy you were selected by my predecessor, President Agassiz, to prepare a biographical memoir of Henry Mitchell. May I ask whether this information is correct, and if so whether you have made any progress in the work? It is desirable that this memoir should be presented to the Academy as soon as possible.

Yours very truly,
[ins]Ira Remsen[/ins]
President.

Professor C. S. Peirce.
