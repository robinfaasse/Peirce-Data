[u]National Academy of Sciences.[/u]

ARNOLD HAGUE,
HOME SECRETARY.

WASHINGTON, D. C.,
Feb. 4, 1903.

Mr. Wendell P. Garrison,
The Nation,
206-210 Broadway,
New York, N. Y.

My dear Mr. Garrison:

I have to acknowledge your check for $5.00, in payment of the annual dues of Charles S. Peirce as a member of the National Academy of Sciences. I have handed your check to the Treasurer, and he will forward receipt to Mr. Peirce at Milford, Pa.

very truly yours,
Arnold Hague
Home Secretary.

My dear Peirce:
I have your Hague, [del]w.[/del] & am depositing $20 in the Bank.
Yours WPG.
