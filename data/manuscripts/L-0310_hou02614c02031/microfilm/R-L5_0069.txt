DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

UNITED STATES GEOLOGICAL SURVEY

Newport R. I. [date]Aug 20, 1905[/date]


Dear Prof Peirce. —

Mr Agassiz, president of the
National Academy of Sciences appointed you
to prepare a biographical sketch of the late
Henry Mitchell of the Coast Survey.

In May 1903 Mr H. L. Marindin read an
obituary notice of Prof Mitchell before the
Philosophical Society of Washington. The original
copy has been mislaid and cannot be found.

I am informed that a duplicate was
sent to me to serve as a basis for the
Academy memoir. It occurs to me that
I mailed this to you at the time for your
aid. If I am correct and you have
used such material as you require can
you return it to me here, that I may
