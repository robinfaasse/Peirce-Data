[u]National Academy of Sciences.[/u]

E.M.S.

OFFICE OF THE TREASURER.

S. F. EMMONS, TREASURER,
U. S. GEOLOGICAL SURVEY.

WASHINGTON, D. C., Feb. 29, 1904.

Dear Sir:

You have perhaps forgotten that your dues to the National Academy for the year 1903-4 have not yet been paid. It would be a personal convenience to me if you could send me a check before the April meeting instead of paying in cash at that time, as the latter practice makes a complication in my accounts.

Very truly yours,
S. F. Emmons
Treasurer.

Prof. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa
