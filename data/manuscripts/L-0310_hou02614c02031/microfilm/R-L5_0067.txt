[u]National Academy of Sciences.[/u]

Office of the Home Secretary.

Arnold Hague, Home Secretary,
U. S. Geological Survey.

Washington, D. C., [date]May 28, 1903.[/date]

Prof. Chas. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

My dear Mr. Peirce:

At the last meeting of the National Academy of Sciences, you were appointed to prepare a biographical memoir of the late Henry Mitchell. I believe you accepted the task. I therefore mail to you a typewritten copy of a paper read by Mr. Marindin of the Coast Survey before the Philosophical Society of Washington. I also send a copy of the list of publications of Mr. Mitchell. These will be of great service to you in the preparation of your memoir. The enclosed letters from the Coast Survey will explain matters. I am quite sure they will be ready to aid you in any way in their power. If by any chance you are unable to prepare the biography, please return the papers to me here.

Very respectfully yours,

Arnold Hague
Home Secretary.
