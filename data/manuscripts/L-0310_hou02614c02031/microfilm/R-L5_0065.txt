[pn]National Academy of Sciences.[/pn]

Office of the Home Secretary.

Arnold Hague, Home Secretary,
U. S. Geological Survey.

Washington, D. C., [date]Jan. 28, 1903.[/date]

Prof. Charles S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Professor Peirce:

I have not yet received your ballot for members of the National Academy of Sciences from the sections of Anthropology and Mathematics. I forwarded to you some time ago for your consideration the name of Professor Osgood of Harvard from the Section of Mathematics, and the names of Prof. W. H. Holmes and William James from the Committee on Anthropology. Of course if you do not wish to vote for any of these candidates, you have simply to regard this letter as a friendly one calling your attention to the fact that your vote had not been recorded.

Very respectfully yours,

Arnold Hague
Home Secretary.
