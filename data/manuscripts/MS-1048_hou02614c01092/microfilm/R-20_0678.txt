If the Sulphuric Acid & water merely mixed without any
sort of combination the curve of their density would be a straight line.
If they formed only one [ins]or two[/ins] compounds we should have on pouring
water into Sulphuric Acid

1[sup]st[/sup] Pure Sulphuric Acid
2[sup]nd[/sup] Mixture of Sulphuric Acid & Compound No 1
3[sup]rd[/sup] Compound No 1
4[sup]th[/sup] Mixture of Compound No 1 & Compound No 2
5 Compound No 2
[del]Mixture of Compound No 2 & Water[/del]
7 Water

Instead of 3[sup]d[/sup] we should have Sulphuric Acid & Compound No 2
Compound No 1 if the Mixture was not thoroughly made.

We see in the Curve, these straight lines, indicating mixture
of two bodies & between them curves indicating mixtures of
[del]three[/del]. If the straight lines ran out until they met at the junc-
tion the existence of a pure compound would be indicated.
