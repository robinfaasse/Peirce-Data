[del]three trials of successive approximations giving[/del] will furnish [ins]three successive approximations of the second[/ins] series; and from these three, the same rule will show how to obtain a still better approximation of a third series. It will be still better to begin with seven [ins]or even nine[/ins] [del]successive[/del] approximations of the first series, when one can make series after series of approximations. [del]From[/del] [ins]ordinarily[/ins] [ins]corresponding actual approximations of[/ins] three successive series, a still better approximation can often be obtained; but the advantage of this procedure is dubious and at best very slight.
