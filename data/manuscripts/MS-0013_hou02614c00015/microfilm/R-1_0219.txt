The first prime division consists of Mathematics, which merely frames
hypotheses and tracks out their consequences, and alone of all the sciences
does not concern itself with [del]real[/del] [ins]positive[/ins] truth. Although the mathematician is

ous quantity. The [del]purpose[/del] [ins]problem[/ins] of the present paper is to ascertain why
