$E = \frac{B+C}{2} \pm \sqrt{\frac{(B+C)^2}{4} - BC + AB}$

$\alpha + \beta = B+C$ $\alpha\beta = \frac{(B+C)^2}{4} - \frac{(B-C)^2}{4}(1 + \frac{4AB}{(B-C)^2}) = B(C-A)$

$\psi^{n+2} = B \frac{A+B \frac{A+\psi^n}{C+\psi^n}}{C+B \frac{A+\psi^n}{C+\psi^n}} = B \frac{A(B+C) + (A+B)\psi^n}{AB+C^2 + (B+C)\psi^n}$
