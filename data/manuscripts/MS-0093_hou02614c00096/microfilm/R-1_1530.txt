[del]Art.[/del] [ins]1.[/ins] The calculus of finite differences is an algebraic method

certain street and $u_x$ the number of people living in that
house, the relation between $x$ and $u_x$ illustrates the kind
of dependence considered. There may be no discernible law

usually be general relations between two such quantities,
as $u_x$ [del]and[/del] [ins]or[/ins] $v_x$. In some cases, however, $u_x$ is calculable by an
arithmetical rule from $x$.
