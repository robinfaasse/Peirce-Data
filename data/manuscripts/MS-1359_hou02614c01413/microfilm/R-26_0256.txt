$W_N$ and $W_{-N}$ are the downward forces at $N$ and $-N$ due to the weight of the unsuspended parts of the truss; but when the truss rests on all four piers or is held down to its bearings, it is unnecessary to consider these forces.
$\Omega$ is the vertical resistance of the unsuspended parts of the truss to any deflection at $n = \pm N$
$\beth$ (Beth) is the resistance of the same parts of the truss to change of $\Delta_N$ or $\Delta_{-N}$.
