Equating to zero the differential coefficient of the whole energy
relative to $\eta_N$, we have
$$0 = \mathcal{B} D_n^2 \eta_N + \Omega \eta_N + 2 D_n \eta_N + K \bar{\xi}_R'' \left[ - \frac{R-N}{r} - \left(\frac{R+N}{r}\right)^2 \frac{\eta_N}{R-N} + \eta_N \right]$$
Equating to zero the differential coefficient relative to $\eta_{N-s}$ we have
$$0 = \mathcal{B} (-D_n^2 \eta_N - D_n^2 \eta_{N-s}) - 2 D_n \eta_N + K \bar{\xi}_R'' r^{-1}$$
But the formulae for the part between $s$ and $N$ involve and apply
to $\eta_N$ and $\eta_{N-s}$; and therefore by substituting in the two equations
just obtained the values these formulae give for $\eta_N, D_n \eta_N, D_n^2 \eta_N, D_n^3 \eta_N$,
we get two more [del]equal[/del] equations for determining the constants
of integration. We shall have two others at $-N$, and in that way all
the constants will be determined.

The rougher approximation should be used in the first computation.
