We have furthermore
$$\xi_{N} - \xi_{-N} = \sum_{-N+S}^{N-S} \left\{ \left[ \sqrt{1 - 2 \frac{\eta_{n} - \eta_{n-s}}{S} \tan \varphi_{n} - \left( \frac{\eta_{n} - \eta_{n-s}}{S} \right)^{2}} - 1 \right] S \right\}$$
Neglecting $(\eta_{n} - \eta_{n-s})^{2}$ in comparison with unity, we have, since
$\tan \varphi_{n} = \frac{n}{R}$,
$$\xi_{N} - \xi_{-N} = \sum_{-N+S}^{N-S} \left\{ - \frac{n}{R} (\eta_{n} - \eta_{n-s}) - \frac{1}{2} \frac{(\eta_{n} - \eta_{n-s})^{2}}{S} (1 + \frac{n^{2}}{R^{2}}) \right\} S.$$
If we neglect the second term, we shall only be in error by a small
percentage of the small quantity $\xi_{N} - \xi_{-N}$, so that we may adopt
this as a first approximation. This gives,
$$\xi_{N} - \xi_{-N} = \frac{2N}{R} (\eta_{N} - \eta_{-N}) + \frac{1}{R} \sum_{-N}^{+N} \eta_{n} [ins]d[/ins]S.$$

The two previous equations give
$$\xi_{R}^{u} = \frac{1}{2} (\xi_{N} - \xi_{-N}) - (R-N) + \frac{1}{2} (R-N) \sqrt{1 + 2 \frac{R+N}{R} \frac{\eta_{N}}{R-N} + \left( \frac{\eta_{N}}{R-N} \right)^{2}} + \frac{1}{2} (R-N) \sqrt{1 + 2 \frac{R+N}{R} \frac{\eta_{-N}}{R-N} + \left( \frac{\eta_{-N}}{R-N} \right)^{2}}.$$
