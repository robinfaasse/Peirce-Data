(neglecting the square and products of b and c) so that the elongation is
\frac{1}{r} (bx + 2cx^2) dx = \sqrt{\frac{1}{r^2} (b^2x^2 + 4bcx^3 + 4c^2x^4) (dx)^2}
and the energy is
\frac{E}{2r^2} \int \frac{b^2x^2 + 4bcx^3 + 4c^2x^4}{\sqrt{1+\frac{x^2}{r^2}}} dx = E [ ( -\frac{1}{6}b^2r^2 + \frac{1}{4}b^2x - \frac{3}{4}c^2r^2x + \frac{2}{3}bcx^2 + \frac{1}{2}c^2x^3 ) \sqrt{1+\frac{x^2}{r^2}}
+ ( -\frac{1}{8}b^2r^2 + \frac{3}{8}c^2r^4 ) log ( \sqrt{1+\frac{x^2}{r^2}} + \frac{x}{r} ) ].
