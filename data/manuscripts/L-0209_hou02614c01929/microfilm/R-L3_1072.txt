[marginalia]
Wollaston
[date]April 11. 1873[/date]
[/marginalia]

Dear Charley I cannot tell
you how much I should
enjoy accepting immediately,
your very kind invitation
to visit you at Milford.
I shall pack my
grip-sack with eagerness,
this very morning, and start
on the next through train;
but, alas, I am afraid
to leave home just at
this time. I have been
having a siege of over-
duty, not for ever, for you
know, but to little limits,
and I dreadfully need
some healthful place, with
the chance of amusing
thoughts, and cheer for
a continued lease of life.
Do be perfectly frank
with me regarding this
plan of a later visit,
(a week, in ten days, hence),
for I am asking much.
With my warmest regards
to your wife I remain,
Your afft Cousin,
H. F. J. Huntington
