I know you will
pardon what must be
apparent as weak writing,
when this reaches you.
This afternoon when
your last dispatch arrived
I was feeling better, and
my wife was willing I
should venture — Hence
the enthusiastic telegram;
but the next morning
could barely lift my
head from the pillow.
We have moved into
town and are pleasantly
situated in the flat,
and we lost strength for
several days, to
be expected. Though we
have engaged our passage
for the steamer "Peytona"
which sails June 24, I
cannot think of going
abroad in my present
