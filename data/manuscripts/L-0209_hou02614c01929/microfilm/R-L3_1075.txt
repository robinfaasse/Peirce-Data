And I shall follow that
dispatch.

If your own plans render
this arrangement inconvenient
drop me a line. I shall,
of course, write again before
next Saturday.

I wish indeed you
could have your longing
for France gratified. Why
do you not damn the
expense, as we are so
recklessly doing—allow for—
to attend to the future—and
give yourself and Mrs Peirce
the three months vacation.

You know that I dread
the stars. I can assure
you of the perfect safety
of satisfying your longing.
If I visit you I
shall insist that my
company in your house
shall not interfere with
any of your working
hours. I shall find
my perfect happiness
in the change of scene,
rest, and quiet reading
of some few of your
books.
