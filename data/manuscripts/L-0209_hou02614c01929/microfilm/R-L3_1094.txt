THE FLORENCE CLUB
5 BORGOGNISSANTI.
[/marginalia]

Florence [date]Dec 8/93[/date]

Dear Charley,

Although you
have paid no attention
to my last letter, written
from Geneva, or Florence,
I forgive you. Why should
you care to write, even
on ink or me?

I do most of my
reading in the long night
watches, as I never sleep
anyway before 5 AM,
and am up with the lark.
I have enjoyed the last
copy of "The Nation" &c.

a month in this most
delightful city, and shall
probably stay a month
longer before going to
Rome, where I fancy
Mary and the Rodney
family are settled.
I am too weak to
do much sight-seeing,
and spend most of my
time at this Club. I
meet quantities of bright
fellows, mostly Englishmen
of rank, and the best
class of Americans.
