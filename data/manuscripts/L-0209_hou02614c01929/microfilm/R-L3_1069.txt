[marginalia]V8[/marginalia] Wollaston [date]Feb 14/93[/date]

Dear Charley,
I thank you most heartily for
your thoughtfulness in sending me three copies
of "The Monist." I am reading your articles first,
but I notice many other essays on subjects
which interest me and it will enlarge my
narrow philosophic culture very much to study
the topics treated in these various numbers.
I understand and, (am pleased to record), appreciate
a large portion of your thought as presented in
your different papers. I consider the ideas con-
tained in "Evolutionary Love" as sound, and in
accordance with some wise (?) ponderings of my
own. Not being accustomed to the strict nomenclature
of monistic reasoning, however, I am obliged at
times, during this reading, to look up the meanings
of some of your terms.
"The Open Court" is coming regularly, also, for
which my thanks are due. I shall expect to
pay the subscription price for this paper, and if
these copies of "The Monist" have been any expense
to you, please let me know that I may refund.
I was much disappointed in not being
able to attend Jim's gathering of friends to hear
your novelette. You know at that time I was
in the worst throes of my dreadful treatment.
