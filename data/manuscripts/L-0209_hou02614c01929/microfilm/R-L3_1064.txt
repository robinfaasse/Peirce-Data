With more skill, I might have avoided a
good deal that dissatisfies my friends and
slightly disgusts myself; but at the same time
I should have probably destroyed what little
merit the book possesses — the quality of
realism, and natural easy narrative.

I was so doubtful about any publisher taking
the thing (I had no money [ins]myself[/ins] to take the risk) that
very little care was bestowed. It went to the
printer exactly as I first dispatched it off
last summer.

I shall be most happy to explain any
passages that need clearer statements, and feel
glad that you find the book worth any
attention.

I suppose the cloth copy reached you
safely. Do write again.

Yours affectionately
E. S. Huntington
