Wellesley Hills Mass
[date]Sept 2/1902[/date]

Dear Charley, I am glad at last
to get your correct address and
to know you have some idea of how you
are. I can picture exactly the
outward surroundings of your residence
as they used to be, but not
the interior for that is new. I am
very glad that you are so comfortable
and have such a cozy room.
James and I have been writing you
a joint letter, but it is a long one
and I will send this first. I am
agreeably situated with a very pleasant
woman who has a house on a
hill, and is very quiet. My health
is much improved, and I am
gaining strength. Please give my very affectionate
remembrance to Juliette. I am very glad she is better.

We, the family and I, read your
pathological article with great
interest, & there really was much
better news & tell me what it
is. I am so much interested in trying
to find out the theory of the light
phenomena. We are going to have
studies in color to see if we
can find out.

I wrote you the other day, and I
wrote you a letter about some of
the "tracts", the "Theology" & a few
books, but they say that I did not
write it, but that a fourth person,
being confused with us, & gave him
a letter to you, but as you are
