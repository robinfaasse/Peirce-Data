Joe & Jenny sail for
New York the latter part of
the present month and will
join Harriet and Helen in
Florence. You know they left
here a month ago, and are
at present enjoying the new
delights of Italy, landing
at Genoa.

In conversation about our
common relatives, you
and your wife were
mentioned. Daisy said she
was perfectly charmed with
Juliette, — thought her so
bright, lively, and so entirely
an "actress of self", so
different from the set
type of the Boston woman.

You may be very sure
that I more than agreed
with her, and my regret
is sharp that my wife
should not have had
the opportunity of becoming
closely acquainted with her.
