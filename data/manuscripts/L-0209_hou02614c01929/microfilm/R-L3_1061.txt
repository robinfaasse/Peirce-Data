Wollaston [date]Aug 3[ins]rd[/ins]/92[/date]

Dear Charley,

In a certain sense it is flattering to find from your note of the 31[ins]st[/ins] that you consider any of my assertions, as given in the Arena Journal, worthy of serious attention; at the same time I am somewhat ashamed to own that I cannot much more firmly, on the ground of logical discussion, to answer your very natural objection, to my rather sweeping statements regarding the lagging pace of educated science in keeping step with the beliefs (always termed superstition) of the common people.

I must plead guilty to the fault of careless rhetoric. I wished to express briefly what I think is true, that careful scientific methods, undoubtedly of the first importance in probing all natural phenomena on the material side, have failed, through lack of faith or interest, in giving sound explanations of psychic or spiritual phenomena.
