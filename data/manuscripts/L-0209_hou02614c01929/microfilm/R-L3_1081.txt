Cannot doubt for one
moment our perfect
sincerity. I can see now,
(after the event has occurred),
that the state of my health
should have convinced
me from the very first
that I could not gratify
my strong desire to visit
you and Mrs Peirce.
You will know that a
man cannot go through
a siege of eight weeks
and expect to recover
strength at once.
My wife was right, as

she always is in matters
concerning my personal welfare,
on the receipt of your [ins]kind[/ins] invitation
in advising me to give
up my idea of a visit:
but my health-appetite
such self gratification, I
was obstinate, and when
your last telegram arrived,
braced up by stimulants, felt
sure I could go and so
notified you. As things
turned out it was fortunate
for all of us that my
temporary fictitious energy
did not last long enough
for me to take the train.
