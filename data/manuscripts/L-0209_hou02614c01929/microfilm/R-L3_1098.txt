My Dear Mr. Peirce

Your last letter is
just received, and it
is needless to say that
I did not get the
other, or I should have
thanked you at once
for your hospitable invitation.
We are most sorry that
just now it will be
impossible for us to
go to you, attractive as
the picture you draw is,
