Wollaston
[date]April 17/93[/date]

Dear Charley,

If my wish could be gratified the first train for New York and Milford would bear me toward you, but a wise regard for the possibilities of further tissue troubles, (needing medical attention), and one or two domestic drawbacks, delays my departure for another week.

Under present hopes you may expect a telegram from me next Monday, [date]April 24[/date].

Miss Abby Quincy has at last departed, at the age of 91 years, to the great relief of all of Ganny's family.

With my warmest regards to your wife, (I wonder if you suspect how much I like her!)

Your aff Cousin,
E. S. Huntington
