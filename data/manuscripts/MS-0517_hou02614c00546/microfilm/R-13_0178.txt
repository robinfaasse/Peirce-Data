A scholium is a comment upon the logical structure
of the doctrine. This preface is a scholium.

1. I now proceed to explain the difference between a
[del]practi[/del] theoretical and a practical proposition, together with
the two important parallel distinctions between [u]definite[/u]
and [u]vague[/u], and [u]individual[/u] and [u]general[/u], noting, at the
same time, some [del]conne[/del] other distinctions connected
with these. A sign is connected with the "Truth", i.e.
the entire Universe of Being, or, as some say, the Abso-
lute, in three distinct ways. In the first place, a sign
is not a real thing. It is of such a nature as to exist
