This is, strictly speaking, the total Universe [ins]allowing[/ins] as it is, was, and
will be. But an event, just as it is, in its entirety is just the
same thing. It, therefore, follows from Mill's use of the words
that the only effect is the entire Universe and its only
cause is itself. He thus deprives the word of all utility. But when
everybody else uses it, that which is caused [del][unclear]is represented[/unclear][/del] is, not the entire
event, but that abstracted element of it which is expressed in
a proposition; and by the cause of it is meant that other
abstracted fact, which existing, in every possible case, within
[ins]expressed or[/ins] understood limits, the fact caused or influenced would exist.
