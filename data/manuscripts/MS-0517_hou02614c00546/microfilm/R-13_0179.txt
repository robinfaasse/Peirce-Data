of a sign is merely the being represented. Now [del]really[/del] [ins]being[/ins]
and [u]being represented[/u] are very different. Giving to the

literature is a sign. The sentence 'Roxana was the queen

though there is a grammatical emphasis on the former,
logically [del]'Alexander'[/del] [ins]the name 'Alexander'[/ins] is as much [del]the[/del] [ins]a[/ins] subject as is the

sufficiently complete refers to sundry real objects. All these
