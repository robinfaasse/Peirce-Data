rightly by everybody to be a reason for predicting that an
event will have a certain character although the events
known to have that character have no other real connection
with it than the law. This shows that the law is not a
mere uniformity, but involves a real connection. It is
true that those metaphysicians say that if $A, B, C,$ etc. are
