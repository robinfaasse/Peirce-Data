Leaving that line of thought unfinished for the present
owing to the feeling of insecurity it provides, let us note, first,
that it is of the nature of a symbol to create a tabula rasa & therefore
an endless series of tabulae rasae. Since such creation is merely
representation, the tabulae rasae being entirely indeterminate except to
be representation wherein is a real effect; but a symbol cannot
be without that power of producing a real effect. The symbol represents
itself to be represented; and that representedness is [del]some[/del] real being
pure owing to its utter and vagueness. For all that is represented
must be thoroughly borne out.

For reality is compulsive. But the compulsiveness is
absolute, hic et nunc. It is for an instant & it is gone.
Let it be no more and it is absolutely nothing. The
reality only exists as an element of the regularity.
And the regularity is the symbol. Reality, therefore,
can only be regarded as the limit of the endless
series of symbols.
