I have made poor work of my attempts at ideally perfect
symbols. But my motive is very serious. We have some
half dozen metaphysical theories to account for the rela-
tions of body and soul, all entirely destitute of founda-
tion. We have not, however, any logical account of the
same thing. That is what is wanted. That symbols really do
cause real facts we have seen; and indeed, it is quite
manifest. But what the logic of the process is, is not
so easily made out.
