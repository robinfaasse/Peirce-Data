I deem it useful to say a few words about this piece. Some years ago I wrote a book entitled 'New Elements of Mathematics.' It was such a book as a man with considerable natural aptitude for logic and mathematics, who had devoted [del]the best of his time[/del] [ins]for forty years[/ins] to the study of the former and all that has been written about it, and had not neglected the latter, was able to write by devoting a year exclusively to it.
