What is the difference between '— is a man' and 'man'?
The logicians hold that the essence of the latter lies in a
definition describing its characters; which doctrine vir-
tually makes 'man' equivalent to 'what is a man'.

[ins]both named[/ins] indefinite pronoun, [del]what[/del]. The rhema '— is a man' is
