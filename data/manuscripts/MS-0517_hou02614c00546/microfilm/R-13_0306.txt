A symbol is defined as a sign which becomes such
by virtue of the fact that it is interpreted as such. Why
are symbols interpreted as such? Complex symbols
follow rules of syntax. But simple symbols receive their
interpretation for the most part from some accidental
cause. Are there no symbols that would necessarily
be interpreted to signify the characters that they do sig-
nify? It might be suggested that such a symbol would
necessarily be an index, But that is not so, since it
would not have necessarily have any real connec-
tion with its object. Take a bird's signal of alarm.
Why might it not be employed quite mendaciously?
