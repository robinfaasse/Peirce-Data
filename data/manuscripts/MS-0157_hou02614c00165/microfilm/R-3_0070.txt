<u>Question 1</u>

[fig]Simple chain of 5 links[/fig] With In how many ways, with $c$ colors, can a simple chain of $l$ links, be colored, [del]so[/del] no two adjacent links being colored alike?

Let $p_l$ be the number of ways when the two extremities are colored alike
$q_l$ the number of ways when the extremities are different colors

$E p_l = p_{l+1} = q_l$     $E q = (c-1) p + (c-2) q$

$E^2 p = (c-1) p + (c-2) E p$
$p = C_1 (c-1)^{l-1} + C_2 (-1)^{l-1}$
$p_1 = C_1 + C_2 = [del]c[/del]$
$p_2 = C_1 (c-1) - C_2 = 0$

$C_1 c = c$     $C_1 = 1$     $C_2 = c-1$

$p_l = (c-1)^{l-1} + (c-1) (-1)^{l-1}$ [del]$= (c-1) \{[/del] $= (c-1) \{(c-1)^{l-2} - (-1)^{l-2}\}$
$q_l = (c-1) \{(c-1)^{l-1} - (-1)^{l-1}\}$

It may be remarked that $(c-1)^l + (-1)^{l+1}$ is always divisible by $c$.
