[pn]32[/pn] Feeling is absolutely simple and without parts, — as indeed, [del]it[/del] [ins]is[/ins], since it is whatever it is regardless of anything else, and therefore regardless of any part, which would be something other than the whole, — it follows that if the [del]color-red[/del] color-sensation was not the whole feeling of the instant it has nothing in common with the feeling of the instant. Indeed, although a feeling is immediate consciousness, that is, is whatever of consciousness there may be that is immediately present, yet there is no consciousness in it because it is instantaneous. For we have seen already that feeling is nothing but a Quality, and a Quality is not conscious; it is a mere possibility.
