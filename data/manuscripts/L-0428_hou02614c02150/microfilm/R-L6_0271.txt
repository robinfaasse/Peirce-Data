Lake Forest, Illinois [date]12 April 1902[/date]

C. S. Peirce Esq
Milford, Pa

My Dear Sir: I should long since have answered your very in-
teresting letter of last September communicating certain
remarks and suggestions concerning my discussion of "coefficients
of sharing" in "Studies in Logical Theory". At that time I
was commencing work in a new position and was in the
midst of preparation of another study which had to be
ready against a certain early day. Your letter therefore had to
be put aside for the time and I regret very much that I
have since then, in the press of many cares and distrac-
tions attending my regular work, let it lie unanswered.
