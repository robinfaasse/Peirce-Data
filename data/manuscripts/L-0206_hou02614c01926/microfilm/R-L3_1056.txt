My dear Peirce,

Thank you for the
kindly things you say.

The idea "Wineland" only [del]comes[/del]
[ins]arises[/ins] [del]from[/del] the [del]English[/del] [ins]mistranslation[/ins] of the word
corn as the glader [del]did[/del] [ins]does[/ins] — including
[del]wheat[/del] [ins]maize[/ins] & wheat. The Icelandic word
means strictly "wheat, [del]not[/del] [ins]rare[/ins] of corn." The
"corn" found on Cape Cod, was gathered
at the time of grapes, with ripe — sometimes
after wheat would have been harvested
yet while the corn was white.
Moreover wheat was not known here
till after Champlain's time.

About the 12 days for Biarni's voyage
there is no mention of the time he
required to run from a point some
three days out from Iceland. On his
return, he was two days from Cape Cod to
Cape Sable — three from Cape
Sable to Cape Race — the time from
Cape Race to Belle Isle, when he had
found Newfoundland to be a Island,
is not given, & the time from Belle Isle
to Cape Farewell was four days.
