Young's Hotel,
Boston, [date]Dec 3 1891[/date]

My dear Professor Horsford:

I was greatly struck by your argument concerning the location of the wintering of Leif. It seems to me demonstrative, or as near to that as on such a subject it is possible to come. You plainly show that that is the only possible theory which will suit at once the Saga and the geography. That Leif came up to Symonds' Hill seems to me as certain as a fact in the tenth century could be.

There are some difficulties. How could Bjarni sail so far in 12 days? Then the wheat! I see no propriety in calling maize white-corn
