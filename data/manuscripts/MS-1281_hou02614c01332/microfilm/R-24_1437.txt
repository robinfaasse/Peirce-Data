Moreover, Copernicus commits [pn][14][/pn]
a very grave but very common error
of rhetoric, in trying to do several
different things in one book.

He ought to have written three
different books; one to show as simply
as possible the advantage of his theory over
the Geocentric system; a second to improve
upon the representation of the planetary
motions as given by Ptolemy; and a
third upon [ins]other[/ins] improvements in the methods
of astronomers. He jumbles these all
together; and the result is that his theory
seems to be far more complicated
than Ptolemy's and not so satisfactory.
