$5^{\underline{\text{th}}}$ The period of rotation of Venus and
Mercury is just a year.

$6^{\underline{\text{th}}}$ The line from the centre of the
epicycle of a superior planet to the planet
is just parallel to the line from the earth
to the sun.

$7^{\underline{\text{th}}}$ The line from the earth to the
centre of the epicycle of Venus or Mercury
strikes the Sun.

$8^{\underline{\text{th}}}$ The sun alone has no epicycle, and
consequently, by simply supposing it the
centre, all the motions of the planets can
be represented precisely as in the Ptolemaic
System while suppressing all their epicycles.
