[u]Chapter IV[/u].

[u]The Conception of Time essential in Logic[/u].

Investigation, if pushed far enough, will carry all minds to the same belief, independently of what their opinions were at the outset. The process [del]must[/del] [ins]is[/ins] there[del]fore[/del] clearly one which introduces new elements of thought; and these are termed [u]sensations[/u]. Every thought not a sensation is determined by something previously in the mind. Such determination of one thought by another must also enter into the process of investigation, or we should be perfectly passive in the business and there would be no distinction of a right and a wrong method of research, which is presupposed in logic.
