Mass. Institute of Technology.
PHYSICAL LABORATORY.

Boston, [date]June 26th 1872[/date]

My dear Sir,

In reply to
yours of the 23rd, I send
you by this mail two articles
I have written proposing two
different methods of obtaining
achromatism. In the first
the two prisms are turned in
opposite directions from the
minimum of deviation which
thus increases the dispersion of
one while diminishing that of
the other. A 45° prism may thus
give as great a dispersion as one
of 60° with much less deviation.
This same idea seems to have
occurred independently to [del]Babe[/del] [ins]Babi[/ins],
Brewster, Amici and quite recently
