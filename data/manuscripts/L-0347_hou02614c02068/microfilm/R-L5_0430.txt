Harvard College Observatory,
Cambridge, Mass.

April 26, 190 2

Dear Mr. Peirce,

The enclosed explanatory circular was omitted by oversight from the list mailed to you yesterday. If you will kindly correct and return the list, I shall be glad to see what can be done about supplying some of the back numbers.

I have had your name placed on our list for future publications.

Very sincerely yours,
E. C. Pickering

Mr. C. S. Peirce.
Milford, Pa.
