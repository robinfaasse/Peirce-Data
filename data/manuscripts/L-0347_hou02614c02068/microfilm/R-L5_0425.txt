As you will see from the Treasurer's report, any
extension is not now justifiable. Although
the increased rate of interest has increased
our income more than two thousand dollars,
the deficit exceeds a thousand dollars, and
much care will be needed to make the necessary
retrenchment. I shall be glad to aid in utilizing
the great ability of your brother, and do not
forget the many kindnesses of your father to
me, or the friendship of our families extending
over nearly a century, in Salem and Cambridge.

Very sincerely yours,
[ins]Edw. C. Pickering[/ins]
