Cambridge, Mass.

[date]April 16, 1900[/date].[del]189[/del]

My dear Mr. Peirce,

Mr. Pritchett writes, April 13, "with regard to the matter of Mr. Peirce's employment I have only time to say that I will take this matter up at the end of a week and if after consideration, it seems proper and fair to make such an arrangement I shall be glad to do it. I appreciate Mr. Peirce's abilities and should be glad to help him in his need if it can be done properly". I hope that this plan will be carried out.

Very truly yours,
E. C. Pickering
