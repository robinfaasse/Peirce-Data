[u]All S is P[/u] and [u]Some S is not P[/u]
as well as [u]No S is P[/u] and [u]Some S is P[/u]
are said to be [u]contradictories[/u], while
[u]Some S is P[/u] and [u]Some S is not P[/u]
are said to be respectively [u]subaltern[/u] to
[u]All S is P[/u] and [u]No S is P[/u].
But in the system here used, while the contradictories
remain such, all other pairs of propositions are [u]subcon-[/u]
[u]traries[/u].
