translated into English by
Sir Henry Billingsley, Lord
Mayor of London. The transla-
tion was from the original
Greek.

by a woodcut border, and
was as follows: —

of Euclide now first trans-

by H. Billingsley. Whereunto
are annexed certaine an-
notations of the best Mathe-
maticians. With a [del]preface[/del] [ins]preface[/ins]
made by M. I. Dee, specifying
the chiefe Mathematicall
