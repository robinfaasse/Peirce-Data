There is a dark brown powder, almost black, called argentic oxide.
93.10 % of it is silver. If this is stirred or shaken up in a solution
of iodide of potassium it turns to a straw color. The straw colored
powder contains all the silver that was in the blackish powder;
but only 45.95 % of its weight is silver. If it is heated in
caustic lye, it will gradually change back to argentic
oxide, all the time retaining the same silver.

Now the question is, What is the percentage of gain in
weight of the blackish powder in being changed into the
strawcolored powder? Also, what is the percentage of loss of
weight of the straw-colored powder in being changed back to
the blackish powder?
