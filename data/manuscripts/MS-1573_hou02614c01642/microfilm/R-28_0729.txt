But it is not predominant, as long as each term of a series simply has another like it following next after it. But when we find an endless sequence inevitably brings in the idea of a limit which lies beyond it, and that is the essential idea of continuity, there the idea of the Third is the most prominent one. Growth [del]contains[/del] [ins]involves[/ins] the same idea with equal prominence; for it is the production out of what went before of more than the past contained. Intelligence [del]is[/del] [ins]shows[/ins] the same feature; for mind looks upon a senseless thing and derives from it an idea, which though strictly true of that thing, embraces, as idea, that which that thing does not contain. The reader will not readily recognize the Thirdness here; and it cannot be made quite plain without a more exact analysis than would be appropriate here. But he will see that as long as a series of points is not continuous, we can always reason by what the logicians call the "principle of excluded third," that is, that because [u]this[/u] is not the thing, [u]that[/u] must be it. If a number is less than 2, it must be precisely 1. But in [del]the[/del] the case of a continuous sequence we cannot reason that way.
