The [ins]ideas of[/ins] [del]primary qualities[/del], bulk, figure, number, situation, motion, are likenesses of the qualities themselves. The ideas of the secondary qualities, as colors, smells, tastes, etc. are no more like the qualities themselves than the words that denote these qualities are like the qualities themselves. The tertiary qualities are the powers or occult properties of bodies, as of the sun to bleach wax. The idea of thinking embraces perception, memory, and discernment. Complex ideas are [del]either[/del] [ins]of[/ins] modes, [ins]of[/ins] substances, [del]or[/del] [ins]and of[/ins] relations. Modes are either simple (which are complications of one idea) or mixed (which conjoin several ideas).
