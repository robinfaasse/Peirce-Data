It is of importance for SCIENCE to publish prompt and reliable news of a character that will promote the objects of the Journal. Such news includes announcements of deaths among scientific men, academic appointments and positions to be filled, elections to offices, the conferring of prizes and honorary degrees, scientific expeditions, meetings of societies and congresses, gifts and other accessions to institutions, important advances in science and the like. Men of science and those interested in science will greatly assist the Journal by sending news of this character.
