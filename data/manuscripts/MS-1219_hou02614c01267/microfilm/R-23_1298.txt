"A man renown'd for repartee
Will seldom scruple to make free,
With friendship's finest feeling.

G.B. puts a comma after "repartee". Quite uncalled for.

"In vain the sage, with retrospective eye,
Would from the apparent What conclude the Why."

So G.B. Quite right; for Pope did not mean to call up the
horrid image of a sage with a retrospective eye!

Thus, the above simple considerations cover every case
more clearly and easily than G.B.'s seventeen rules and
fifteen exceptions!
