[u]5th[/u] Draw a horizontal line under the array, and bring down into a row beneath it the 1s remaining in the array, without altering their secundal places, and filling [ins]with 0s[/ins] the vacant places between them and in the last places, if [del]they be[/del] [ins]any[/ins] vacant, [del]to[/del] the right of all the 1s. Mark the same places as barred and dotted. The result is the sum required.

Example. Add the first sixteen odd numbers after attaching [u]10[/u] to the [u]1st[/u], [u]5th[/u], [u]9th[/u], and [u]13th[/u], [u]101[/u] to those next following, [u]01[/u] to those next following, and [u]010[/u] to the rest. I set down these numbers in black, enlarge the circulates with lead pencil, connect the cancellings with lines and use brown, blue, red, green, ink in cyclical order, so that it shall be easy to see what has been done.
