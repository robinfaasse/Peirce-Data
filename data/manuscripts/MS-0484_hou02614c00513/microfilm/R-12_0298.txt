[u]Rule XXV[/u]. ([u]Rule of Protraction and Retraction[/u]). An evenly enclosed
heavy line (i.e. so enclosed as to its outermost part) may be [del]retracted[/del]
[del]into[/del] [del]within[/del] an even number of ovals while an oddly enclosed heavy
line may be protracted out from any number of ovals.

[u]1[sup]st[/sup][/u]. This holds of unenclosed heavy lines. For by [u]VII[/u] an individual

index can be erased.

[u]2[sup]nd[/sup][/u]. If the rule, so far as it relates to retractions and protractions

holds good so long as it is not enclosed in more than N+1 ovals.
