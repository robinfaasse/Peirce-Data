Besides the Listing theorem, the following second census proposition holds of valental graphs: The valency of a graph equals the sum of the valencies of its spots less the number of extremities of connecting bonds. It follows, as a corollary, that no perissid can be composed exclusively of artiads.

Between Listing's theorem and the second census proposition the number of connecting bonds may be eliminated. Let a graph be composed of $n_0$ medads, $n_1$ monads, $n_2$ dyads, $n_3$ triads, etc. Let $\kappa_0$ be the sum of the cyclosis of the medads, $\kappa_1$ that of the monads, etc and $V, N, K$ denote respectively the valency, chorisis, and cyclosis of the graph. Then,

$$V - 2N + 2K = \sum_0^\infty v n_v - 2n_v + 2\kappa_v$$
