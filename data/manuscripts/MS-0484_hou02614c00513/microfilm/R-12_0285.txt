The whole graph may be conceived as connected by a heavy line with an index of the individual state of things described. But such line is not drawn.

4. The field within which it was understood that what was to be written should be interpreted as an existential graph may be conceived as marked off by a lightly drawn line surrounding it. That line may be folded in in places so as to produce cells and cells within cells, thus:

[fig]A diagram showing a large outer boundary with a canal folding inward to form an inner cell.[/fig]

In the practical delineation the canals may be omitted, thus:

[fig]A diagram showing a small circle nested inside a larger outer boundary.[/fig]

Thus whatever is written inside an odd number of lightly drawn ovals is to be conceived as not asserted but merely as describing what is intended [ins]and any thing answering the description is intended[/ins]. But further every heavy line crossed by the oval, including the imaginary heavy line which unites that which is within with the index of the state of things, is to be taken as signifying the non-identity of that which is denoted by the parts on the two sides. Hence, on the whole the oval denies that which is within. Thus
