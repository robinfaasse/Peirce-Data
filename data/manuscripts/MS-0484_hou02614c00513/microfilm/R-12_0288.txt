The following graph
[fig]graph with line through loves[/fig]
means, there is something which is not identical with anything
which loves something; that is, something loves nothing.

The following graph
[fig]graph with line through loves and benefits separately[/fig]
means, there are two things, A and B, (which may be identical), such that
taking any four things X, Y, U, V such that X loves U and Y benefits V,
[del]either A is not X or Y[/del] A and B are not respectively identical with
X and Y; that is, either A is not X or B is not Y, In other words,
either something loves nothing or something benefits nothing.

The following graph
[fig]graph with line through loves and benefits together[/fig]
means, something loves nothing and something benefits nothing

The following graph
[fig]graph with line through loves and benefits joined at the end[/fig]
means, there are two things, A and B, (which may be identical) such that taking
any three things X, Y, Z, such that X loves Z and Y benefits Z, A and B
are not respectively identical with X and Y. That is, there are two things, one of
which loves nothing benefitted by the other.
