[u]§2. Of Logical Graphs.[/u]
Of systems of graphs for the representation of logical
relations, the simplest in construction, inasmuch as it
makes use of but one general sign, is that of Entitative
Graphs, sketched by me in the Monist. But while simple
in one sense, that system is complicated in its mode of
representing many logical relations, and it is also
quite unnatural in some of its leading conventions. The
system of Existential Graphs herein described is very
much like that, but [del]it[/del] [ins]this[/ins] corrects the faults of that, while
it misses its chief merit.
