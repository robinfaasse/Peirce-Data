4. The field within which it was understood that what was
to be written should be interpreted as an existential graph
may be conceived as marked off by a lightly drawn line
surrounding it. That line may be folded in in places so
as to produce cells and cells within cells, thus:
[fig]diagram showing nested boxes with a canal connecting the inner and outer areas[/fig]
In the practical delineation the canals may be omitted, thus:
[fig]diagram showing an oval inside a rectangle[/fig]
Thus whatever is written inside an odd number of lightly drawn
ovals is to be conceived as not asserted but merely as describing
what is intended [ins]and anything answering the description is intended[/ins]. But further every heavy line crossed by the
oval, including the imaginary heavy line which unites that
which is within with the index of the state of things, is to
be taken as signifying the non-identity of that which is
denoted by the points on the two sides. Hence, on the whole
the oval denies that which is within. Thus
