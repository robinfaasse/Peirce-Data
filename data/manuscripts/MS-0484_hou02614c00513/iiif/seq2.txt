Besides the Listing theorem, the following second Census
proposition holds of valental graphs: The valency of a
graph equals the sum of the valencies of its spots less the
number of extremities of [u]connecting[/u] bonds. It follows
as a corollary, that no perissid can be composed exclusively
of artiads.
