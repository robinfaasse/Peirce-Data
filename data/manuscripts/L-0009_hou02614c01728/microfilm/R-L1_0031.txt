Milford Pa. [date]1890 Aug 25[/date]

Mr. John B. Alden
393 Pearl St. N.Y.

Dear Sir

In reply to yours of [ins]11th[/ins] inst.
will say that my P.O. address is
C. S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

Your magazine "Knowledge" seems to me a
good idea and the article Mormonism valu-
able. I enclose 75 for Vol I in cloth. I should
be happy to contribute myself at $25 per 1000
words.

In answer to your slip.

1. My name is Charles Sanders Peirce.
2. Born [date]1839 September 10. Tuesday[/date].
3. " Cambridge, Mass.
4. My father was Prof Benjamin Peirce, a very
eminent mathematician.
Grandfathers: Benjamin Peirce, author.
E. H. Mills, U.S. Senator from Massachusetts
Brother: James Mills Peirce, Professor in Harvard.
Uncles: Charles Henry Peirce, author.
E. H. Mills Jr. author.

5. Graduated Harvard College, [date]1859[/date].
B.S. (summa cum laude in chemistry), [date]1863[/date].
6. Scientific and literary.
7. No permanent or regular public office.
