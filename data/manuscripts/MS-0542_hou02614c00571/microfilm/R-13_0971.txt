[del]Unique and reunique relations have been investigated by
Schröder[/del]

An [u]individual dyadic relation[/u] is a relation at once unique
and reunique.

A [u]repeating relation[/u] is a relation [del]where[/del] such that there is
an individual which [del]is both relate and correlate[/del] [ins]stands in this relation to something[/ins] while
something stands in this relation to it.

An [u]exhaustless relation[/u] is a relation of which some
relate belongs to a class of individuals every one of which
[del]belongs[/del] occurs as a relate to some individual of that
class.
