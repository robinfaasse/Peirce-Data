[u]Logic — Chapter IV.[/u]
[u]Of Reality.[/u]

There is, then, a reality or something independent of what you or I or any number of men, may think about it. What is the mode of existence of this reality?

It is a truism to say that what I think depends entirely on what I think it to be. The reality [ins]therefore[/ins] [del]there[/del] is not, [ins]per se,[/ins] an immediate object of my thought, though my thought may happen to coincide with it. Yet reality must have some intimate connection with what is in the mind or it would be vain for [del]me[/del] [ins]us[/ins] to hope by following [del]a[/del] [ins]rules[/ins] certain method of reasoning to arrive at the truth.
