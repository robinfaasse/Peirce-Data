It may be admitted that all will be right angles, so that the four angles of [del]a[/del] [ins]the[/ins] quadrilaterals are right angles, and the the two triangles into which any such quadrilateral is cut [ins]by a diagonal[/ins] will have the sum of its angles equal to two right angles, whence, (or otherwise, without room for cavil) the postulate is proved.

The fallacy lies in this, that though the line $E'$ $E''$ cuts [del]C[/del] $CE'$ and $C'E''$ at equal angles, this does not prove that it will cut [u]all[/u] the perpendiculars at equal angles.
