[u]On Logical Graphs.[/u]

I.

The so-called diagrams of Euler are well-known. They have been traced back to Laurentius Valla in the fifteenth century, and there is no knowing how much older they may be. It certainly does not seem at all unlikely that Aristotle aided his syllogistic by diagrams; for such a hypothesis would explain a number of his expressions.

Euler's diagrams will here be described in the improved form given to them by John Venn, adding an additional improvement. The system of representation being so modified, the conventions are as follows:
