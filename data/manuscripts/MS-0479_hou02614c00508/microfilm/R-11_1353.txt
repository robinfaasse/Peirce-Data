In order to illustrate the [del]method[/del] [ins]utility[/ins], I will apply it (without
any preconsideration at all) to the following problem
by Mrs. Franklin, which, being more complicated than
anybody is likely to meet with in a long life, elsewhere
than in a logic-book, will be satisfactorily treated if
it is easily solved, [del]or[/del] regardless of the solution being
a little longer or a little shorter:

"Six children, A, B, C, D, E, F, are required to obey
the following rules:

1[sup]st[/sup], On Mondays and Tuesdays, no four can go out together;

2[sup]nd[/sup], On Thursdays, Fridays, and Saturdays, no three can stay in
together;

3[sup]rd[/sup], On Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Saturdays, if B and C are
together, then A, B, E, and F must remain together;

4[sup]th[/sup], On Mondays and Saturdays B cannot go out
unless either D or C, E, and F stay at home.
