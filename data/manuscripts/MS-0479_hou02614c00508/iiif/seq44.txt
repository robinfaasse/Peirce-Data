[ured]Rule [del]4[/del] [ins]3[/ins][/ured]. In the same compartment repetitions of the same sign, whether [ins]mutually[/ins] attached or detached, are equivalent to one writing of it. Two different signs in the same compartment, if attached to one another are equivalent to no sign at all, and may be erased or inserted. But if they are detached from one another they constitute an absurdity. All the foregoing supposes the signs to be unconnected with any in other compartments. If two contrary signs are written in the same compartment, the one being attached to certain others, P, and the other to certain others, Q, it is permitted to attach P to Q and to erase the two contrary signs.
Thus, Fig. 33 can be transformed into Fig. 34.
