B and F are first to decide what they will do, and C makes
his decision before A, D, or E.
Find [u]($\alpha$)[/u] when C must go out,
[u]($\beta$)[/u] when he must stay in,
[u]($\gamma$)[/u] when he can do as he pleases.
We begin by restating the rules, so as, to exhibit their
effect on C's conduct.
[ured]I. [ins]Applies to Mondays and Tuesdays only.[/ins][/ured]
1. If B and F choose to go out, and either A, D, or E is
forced to go out, C must stay in;
2. If either B or F chooses to go out, and any of the pairs,
AD, AE, DE are forced to go out, C must stay in;
3. If A, D, and E are forced to go out, C must stay in.
[ured]II. Applies to Thursdays, Fridays, and Saturdays only[/ured]
1. If B and F choose to stay in, C must go out.
2. If either B or F chooses to stay in, and either A, D, or E is
forced to stay in, C must go out.
3. If either of the pairs AD, AE, DE is forced to stay in,
C must go out.
