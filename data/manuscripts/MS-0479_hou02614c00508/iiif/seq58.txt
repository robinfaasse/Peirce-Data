This is the very most highly organized system of quantity that mathematicians have ever succeeded in definitely conceiving. On the other hand, the very simplest [del]one[/del] and most rudimentary of all conceivable systems of quantity is that one which distinguishes only two values. This the system of evaluation which ethics applies to actions in dividing them into the right and the wrong and which [ins]non-relative[/ins] logic applies to assertions in dividing them into the true and the false. The mathematics of such a system, — dichotomous mathematics, — amounts to very little. Those who seek to make a calculus or the algebra of logic struggle vainly after mathematical interest by complicating their problems. They do not succeed: mere complication has not even a mathematical interest.
