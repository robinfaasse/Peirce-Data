I. [u]Of Eulerian Diagrams[/u].
The general attention of logicians was called
to these diagrams by the description of them in
1772 in the second volume of Leonard Euler's [u]Lettres
à une princesse d'Allemagne sur quelques sujets de physique
et de philosophie[/u]. In order to exhibit the nature of the
syllogism, — say of this one
. All men are mortal;
All saints are men;
$\therefore$ All saints are mortal; —
