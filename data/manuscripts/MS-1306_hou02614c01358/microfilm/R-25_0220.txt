[u]The Ptolemaic System[/u]

What was the [del]great[/del] [ins]chief[/ins] error of the ancient system of astronomy? Most people will say without hesitation that it lay in [del]making[/del] [ins]supposing[/ins] the earth at rest while the Sun moved, instead of supposing the Sun at rest while the earth moves. This was certainly a great error of physics, due to imagining the stars and Sun to be imponderable bodies. But if it be imagined, as one believe it generally is, that that error had anything to do with the failure of the ancient theory to represent the motions of the planets, it is a total misapprehension, as we shall proceed very simply to show.
