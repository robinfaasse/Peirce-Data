[date]TUESDAY 30[/date]

[date]WEDNESDAY 31[/date]

In the afternoon
I moved me down
into the music-room.
Our thoughtful friend
Mrs James Pinchot had
sent a large package
Juliette took off the
wrapper & found a
large pasteboard
box containing a
sort of basket-bag
full of Park & Tilford's
goodies; and especially
a box of peppermints
which proved good
for my everlasting
stomach ache.
