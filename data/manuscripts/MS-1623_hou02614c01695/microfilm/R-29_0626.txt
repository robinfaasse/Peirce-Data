[date]MONDAY 2[/date]

[u]Lent Florence Ball[/u]
[u]Coleridge's Poems.[/u]

P.M. 12:45 Opened 5[ins]th[/ins] box
peaches  4[ins]d[/ins] 16[ins]h[/ins] 30[ins]m[/ins]

Mrs Blood came &
stayed long, breaking
up my work

I had much trouble
with the fire.

[date]TUESDAY 3[/date]

Decker came just
as I got out of bed
and the jabber all
day prevented my
getting anything
done

Driller came late
in the afternoon
