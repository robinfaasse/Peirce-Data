[date]TUESDAY 21[/date]

Don told [ins]F.[/ins] that when he went out this morning with Zola he heard our neighbour Blond who was on his wagon with his hired man, say to him that as soon as [del]I[/del] [ins]Mrs Peirce[/ins] went out there he would do something to drive us away for good.
Blond said
"When Mrs Peirce goes away again I'll fix the dam place for good."
I gave me a good warm bath.
