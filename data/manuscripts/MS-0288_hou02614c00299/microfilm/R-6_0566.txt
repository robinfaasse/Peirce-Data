[date]1905 Apr 27[/date]

I will begin this book by stating what I propose to put into it. Last September I wrote an article as the first of a series of three on Pragmatism. The first was to [del]state[/del] [ins]explain[/ins] what I conceive pragmatism to be, the second to show what it would lead to and the third to argue its truth. The first of the three articles, the only one yet written, was published in this month's Monist. I have received so many letters about it, that I am puzzled to contrive to get all that is suggested to me (by the objections and queries) into two more Monist articles. [del]Yet[/del] I detest longwindedness in philosophy. I therefore propose to use this book to set down in it things to be said to be afterward rewritten and synthetised. Very likely I shall need more than one volume
