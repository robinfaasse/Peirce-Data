The instructions to develop a plan which
will keep
all necessary records of each individual
and show that each person is accounted
for and has had necessary care.
From our records here we have
little record of what has been
done for the individual and practically
no record to show that his physical
needs are met.
It should be possible to have such a
record as will tell at a glance
what has happened to and been
done for each individual.
The individual records which we now
have are inadequate. There should be
one page for each child on which
is record of his health and physical
condition. This will have to be
carefully worked out.
