"Every part of me is either in Albemarle or Augusta.
So if you don't cut what is in Albemarle nor what is
in Augusta, you don't cut me at all," said Tom. "But
really the boundary must be in both counties; for otherwise it
would be in neither; and then there would be a place between
Albemarle and Augusta in neither; and that is not so."

"You see that [del]stone[/del] red stone in the brook?" asked
Euclid. Tom did see it. "Now I want to know whether
there is any place between that stone and the water,"
said Euclid.

"Perhaps there is, and perhaps there is n't," replied the white, "I
don't know. But this I know, either there is a place
where there is both stone and water, or there is a place
