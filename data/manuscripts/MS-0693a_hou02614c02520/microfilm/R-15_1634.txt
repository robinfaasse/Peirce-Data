§11. The ladder of the sciences, as well as I have been
able to work it out, is as [del]follows[/del] [ins]here exhibited[/ins]:

A. SCIENCE OF DISCOVERY, embracing:

I. MATHEMATICS, embracing:
a. Dyadics,
b. Arithmetic,
c. Synectics.

II. PHILOSOPHY, embracing:
a. Phenomenology
b. Normative Science, consisting of
i. Esthetics
ii Ethics
iii Logic, composed of
1. Speculative Grammar,
2. Speculative Critic,
3. Methodeutic.
