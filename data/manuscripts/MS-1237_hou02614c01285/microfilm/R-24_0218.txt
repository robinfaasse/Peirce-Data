[u]Genus C. Verbs of hesitating.[/u]

[u]Species a[/u]. Most of these imply an involuntary mental resistance, and the rule requires [u]à[/u].

[u]Examples[/u]

Il barguigne [u]à[/u] se marier.
Elle ne balance pas un instant [u]à[/u] ouvrir la lettre.

[u]Species b[/u]. In case of a decided disinclination the resistance is that of the will and the rule requires [u]de[/u].

[u]Example[/u].
Pourriez-vous un instant douter [u]de[/u] l'accepter?

[u]Species c[/u]. A verb may vary its signification and construction.

[u]Example[/u].
[u]Hésiter[/u] generally takes [u]à[/u], but occasionally [u]de[/u].
