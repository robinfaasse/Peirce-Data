[u]Forcer[/u]. When the force is physical or that of authority [u]de[/u] is
more appropriate; but when it acts through the heart of the
subject of the infinitive [u]à[/u] is preferable. But the distinction is
hardly observed.

[u]Obliger[/u]. When this verb implies compulsion [u]de[/u] is more appropriate.
When it means to incite [u]à[/u] is better. When it signifies an obligation it
depends upon the point of view. But little attention is paid by
the majority of good writers to such fine distinctions.
