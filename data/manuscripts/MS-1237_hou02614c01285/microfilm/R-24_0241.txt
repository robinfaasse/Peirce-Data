[u]for the use of à and de with French infinitives following personal verbs.[/u]

[del]The principles which have historically determined the different constructions of different verbs have been various and their action has been largely fortuitous; consequently, any single rule covering all cases must necessarily be artificial and vague. But for all that, it may be very useful as an aid to the memory. It is as if the locks on the doors in a large house were of various makes. Any one skeleton key that would open them all would fit many of them very imperfectly, and require to be forced this way or that, in order to work at all. Still, if one could manage to open all the doors with it, one would only be too glad to be so fortunate as to have it. In the same way, the rule I am about to give, though faulty enough, will I hope be found valuable.[/del]
