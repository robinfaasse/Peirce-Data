Genus B. The principal verb expresses the moral attitude of
the person who performs the action denoted by the infinitive. This
attitude is conceived as resulting in the infinitive action and there
are only subjective obstacles, so that the rule requires [u]à[/u]. Most of
these verbs are reflexives.

Pasquier s'abaissa jusqu'à réprimer l'insolence absurde
du jésuite Garasse.
