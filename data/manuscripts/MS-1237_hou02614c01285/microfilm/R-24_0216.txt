Family 3. The signification of the principal verbe is such as to render the subsequent performance of the action denoted by the infinitive uncertain.

Genus A. Verbs signifying delay.

Species a. If the intention to perform the delayed act later is implied, the delay is an involuntary mental resistance, and the rule requires à.

<u>Examples</u>.

Ne tardez pas à m'envoyer de l'argent.
[But il [del]me tarde de[/del] [ins]me tarde de[/ins] mettre ma tête sur mon oreiller.]
Le cordonnier s'attarde à m'envoyer mes bottines.
Il attend à faire son testament.
