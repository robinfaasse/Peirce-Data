[u]Family 4[/u]. The action expressed by the principal verb [ins]tends to[/ins] cause the state of things denoted by the infinitive by influence on the heart and reason. [ins]In accordance with Preliminary Explanation 3 the figurative sense follows the construction of the literal.[/ins]

[u]Genus A[/u]. The majority of verbs of this family signify an effectual influence on the heart and reason and do not emphasize the external action so much as the effect on the person. The reaction against such [del]influence[/del] [ins]influence[/ins] is that of the heart and intellect; so that the rule requires [u]à[/u].
