Y X,

A A becomes
All Y is X
(All Z) is Y
$\therefore$ (All Z) is X

This mood is called [u]Barbara[/u]. The three vowels of the word signify that all its propositions are A.

It is clear that we can draw no other inference from a thing's being in a class other than what is directly expressed [del]by Barbara[/del] namely that whatever is true of [u]an[/u] entire class is [ins]true[/ins] of [u]every[/u] member of the class (Aristotle's Dictum); hence all other syllogisms may be reduced to Barbara.
