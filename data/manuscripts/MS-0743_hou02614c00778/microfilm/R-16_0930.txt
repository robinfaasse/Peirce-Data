That which propositions predicate is either some general predi-

The quantity & quality of propositions are designated by
letters; thus,

A is Universal Affirmative; as All Y is X.
E is Universal Negative; as No Y is X
I is Particular Affirmative; as Some Y is X
O is Particular Negative; as Some Y is not X.
