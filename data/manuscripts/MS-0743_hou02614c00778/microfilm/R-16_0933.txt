In the 4[ins]th[/ins] place, it follows from the above remark, that the
circles of major and minor terms must not both cut the
circumference of the middle term, otherwise the latter would
bound nothing and would limit nothing out of itself and
no conclusion could be drawn, with regard to the respective
boundaries of major & minor. This, expressed in logical
language, becomes: [u]The Middle Term must be distributed at[/u]
[u]least once in the premises.[/u]

A term is said to be distributed when it is spoken of
altogether. The subject is distributed in Universals, and the
Predicate in Negatives.
