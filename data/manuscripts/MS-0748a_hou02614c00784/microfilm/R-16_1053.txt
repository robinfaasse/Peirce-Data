Any of the categorical propositions to which a hypothetical
proposition can be reduced asserts that a course of events
of one description is within a certain range of possibility
always a course of things to which another description will
apply. A still wider class of categorical propositions,
including these and also many others, [del]consistsin[/del] assert
that [ins]to[/ins] objects of one general description another general
description is applicable. This is a matter of fact which
may be true in one world and not in another. It may be true
of all things t at exist now or of all that have ever ex-
isted or of all that can exist according to the known laws
of nature : and many other worlds, or as they are called
by the logicians many universes, may be supposed as the
one to which the proposition is supposed to apply.
