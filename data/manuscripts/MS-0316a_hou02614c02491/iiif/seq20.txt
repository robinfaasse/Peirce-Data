[del]I do not like the phrase "the soul of a religion".
There is no soul in religion: only things to be 
done and things to be thought & felt.[/del]

[del]It is only when a man takes some portion of the 
religious life & thinks of it by itself, as if it were 
independent of the body of his life, that he calls it 
the soul of religion.[/del]

[del]He does so because he thinks it is something
invisible, as the soul is thought to be, & that it 
is the reason & foundation for all other 
parts of the religious life.[/del]

[del]There is no more soul in religion than there is in 
morality. In both there are simply certain
courses of action & certain ways of feeling &
thinking. There is no core, separate from these 
outer manifestations.[/del]
