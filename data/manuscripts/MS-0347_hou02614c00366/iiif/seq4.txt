[ins]^ It is universally held that extension & comprehension are in reciprocal relation[del]s[/del]; thus if Horse be divided into [u]Black Horse[/u] & non-black horse, black horse has more intension & therefore less extension than Horse.[/ins]

We [del]are[/del] [ins]have[/ins] already familiar with the distinction between the extension & comprehension of terms. A term has comprehension in virtue of having a meaning and has extension in virtue of being applicable to objects. The meaning of a term is called its connotation; its applicability to things its denotation. Every symbol denotes by connoting. A representation which denotes without connoting is a mere sign. If it connotes without there by denoting, it is a mere copy.[ins]^[/ins]
