6 Prescott Hall Cambridge
[date]1903 Dec 5[/date]

My dear Mr. Lowell:

I find myself obliged to ask you
kindly to advance me another sum of
$130 making the honorarium for
two lectures in all.

The truth is I find there are some
payments to be made in Pike County
that I thought had been settled; for
I have been all summer so absorbed
in my work that I left Mrs. Peirce
to attend to the business. I find that
