HARVARD UNIVERSITY
CAMBRIDGE

[date]February 15, 1911[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

It was a pleasure to get your letter, and I have read it with very great interest. I earnestly hope that you will write such a book on the subject that you have in mind. Your studies of logic put such a new light on the probity value of thought, that to turn it in this way on the questions that lie at the foundation of everything is of immense importance. My opinion is of course of no value; but the argument in your letter impressed me very much.

Very truly yours,
A. Lawrence Lowell.

Charles S. Peirce, Esq.
