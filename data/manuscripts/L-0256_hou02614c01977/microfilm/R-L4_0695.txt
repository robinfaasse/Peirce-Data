HARVARD UNIVERSITY
CAMBRIDGE

PRESIDENT'S OFFICE

September 26, 1931.

Dear Dr Woods:

I am afraid I can do nothing
about helping you with reminiscences of
Charles S. Peirce. He gave some
lectures at the Lowell Institute, and
I met him, but he was a man who knew
few people and I am not aware who knew
him well.

Yours very sincerely,

A. Lawrence Lowell.

Dr James H. Woods
Department of Philosophy
Emerson Hall
