Denigration. I admit might
cause a man to wonder what
the force of [u]de[/u] is in it. For there
is, I take it, really no such
Latin word, [ins]even[/ins] though it be found
once or twice. But meticulous
and timorous have obvious
meanings, and are really good
words.

With great respect, and
since I have had no occasion
to say so before, let me venture
to express my particular sa-
tisfaction at the last very happy
choice of our Harvard President.

C. S. Peirce
