Let us use the word "dyad" to signify an
ordered pair. That is, AB is a different dyad from
BA. Then, if there N individuals in the universe,
there are $N^2$ individual dyads. This is supposing
objects are paired with themselves, as AA. We
may term such a dyad, a [u]self-dyad[/u], as opposed to
an alio-dyad. An important mode of classification
of dual relations is in regard to their subsisting
between the members of [u]self-dyads[/u] and [u]alio-dyads[/u].
