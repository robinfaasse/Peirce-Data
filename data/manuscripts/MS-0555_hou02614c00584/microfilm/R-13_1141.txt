I now propose to introduce the definition
of a character, $\kappa$. I note that a definition
consists of two propositions, the first of
the form
$D.. D\bar{R}\kappa + \bar{I},$
stating that if anything has the character
defined, a certain fact, $I$, is true; and
the second of the form
$D.. D\bar{R}\kappa + I,$
stating that if that fact, $I$, is true, $D$ has
the character, $\kappa$. Ordinarily, $I$ will involve
$D$, in some way; but even if it did not, we
should merely have a definition of a strange
use of language, without any violation of
logic. We are always at liberty to intro-
duce new characters, with two propositions
constructed as we like, so long as they are
of the above form. My definition of $\kappa$, then,
shall be
