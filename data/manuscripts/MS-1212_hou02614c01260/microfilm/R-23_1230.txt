[u]Reply to the Etymologist[/u].

1[ins]st[/ins]. I prefer not to undertake to answer inquiries in zoölogy, botany, mineralogy, and medicine.

2[ins]nd[/ins]. In regard to chemistry, we have a singular invented language or jargon. It is not English for it is nearly the same in every country; that is it has like gipsey talk a sprinkling of the words of the country, as [u]acid[/u]. It is not even an inflectional but a sort of agglutinative tongue. Accordingly, no regard is paid to the order of the constituents of a word, in many cases. The same writer will on one page speak of dimethylethylmethane on another of ethyldimethylmethane.
