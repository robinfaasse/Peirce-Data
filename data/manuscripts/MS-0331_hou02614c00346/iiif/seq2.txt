(but only used by him in conversation, for it does not appear in the
Century Dictionary in which he looked after the philosophical terms,)
to express his conviction that a practical maxim could embody
the entire genuine intellectual purport of any concept. Peirce
meant by a practical maxim a rule for attaining a definite
purpose, but [ins]Professor[/ins] James gave the matter a slightly different turn
