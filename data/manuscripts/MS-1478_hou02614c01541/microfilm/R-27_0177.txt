The adoption of the metric system would have real
advantages. It would also have real disadvantages. [del]The[/del]
[del]question first to be[/del] [ins]Are the former so assured and so great[/ins]
[del]as unmistakably to overbalance the latter?[/del] This is a pure
question of how much, a question of economy. Economy
is not a matter to be discussed in a passionate and partisan
way. Above all, let us not hear from the one side that the
advantages or from the other that the disadvantages are
"incalculable." Economy is a matter of calculation. Especially
those who advocate the change should remember that scien-
tific logic and good sense equally throw upon them the
burden of proof. Moreover, since we could not favor the
metric system more than we have already done without
severe infringements of natural rights, its advocates must
show clearly that the prosperity of the republic is so depen-
dent upon the course they advocate that such lamentable
infringements of private rights are imperatively required.
