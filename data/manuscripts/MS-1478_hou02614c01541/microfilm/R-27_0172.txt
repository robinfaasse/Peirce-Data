One of these is that "on and after [date]Jan. 1, 1907[/date], the weights and measures of the metric system shall be the legal standard weights and measures of and in the United States." We doubt whether this amounts to anything. Those countries which have succeeded in introducing the metric system have found it necessary to make it a penal offence to express weights and measures otherwise or to have a foot-rule in one's possession. Without some [del]law[/del] law to that effect, it seems reasonable to suppose that a contract which should speak of inches, and should simply define an inch as 2.54 centimetres, would be enforced by the courts.
