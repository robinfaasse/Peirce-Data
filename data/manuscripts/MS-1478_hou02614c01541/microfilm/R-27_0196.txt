reasons against our government's going further than it has already
done toward the adoption of the system here are as follows:

Firstly, the introduction of metric units would not, for a longer
time than it is wise to attempt to legislate for the future, free
people from the necessity of being familiar with the English
inch, pound, etc.; so that whatever evils attach to a superfluity
of units being employed in one country would be augmented by
the introduction of the kilo, centimetre, etc. Secondly, the metric
system is far from being [del]the[/del] of all possible systems the one
which is economically the best; and it would be quite as easy
to bring about a universal adoption of one of the best systems
as it would be to bring about the universal adoption of the
metric system, provided our government goes no further in
the direction of the metric system than it has already gone; while
if it does go further it [ins]will[/ins] destroy all hopes of such better system.
