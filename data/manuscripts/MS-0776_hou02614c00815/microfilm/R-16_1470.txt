earnest study has been bestowed upon different
questions concerning the best modes of communicating
discoveries. This, in many branches it is generally
acknowledged that as soon as a discovery has reached
a certain [del]point[/del] stage of its development it should be
communicated to a certain journal or [del]to[/del] some one of
several; and this is of the nature of a rule of
rhetoric. Of late years, particular attention has
been paid to the important matter of titles of com-
munications. It is certain that some sciences have
suffered grievously from the neglect of it. Thus,
