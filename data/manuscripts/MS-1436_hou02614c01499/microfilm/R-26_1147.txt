Shaftesbury's morals were an object of solicitude [del]and[/del]
while before he was born; for his grandfather, the First Earl,
dispatched the philosopher Locke into the provinces to search
out a suitable mother for the model who was to be.
She was found in the person of a daughter of the great
Dr. Cudworth, him who taught the existence of a Plastic
Nature and an Eternal Morality, [del]was[/del] [ins]runs[/ins] upon principles partly
anticipating Kant's. His education was absolutely under
the superintendence of Locke.
