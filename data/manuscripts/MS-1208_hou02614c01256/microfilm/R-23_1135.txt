-[u]atile[/u] from Latin [u]-atilis[/u], added to a stem, as [u]volatile[/u], [u]versatile[/u], always takes a final [u]e[/u].

-[u]ation[/u] is occasionally appended to English words, as starvation, flirtation, botheration. When the word to which it is appended ends in a caudal consonant following a single vowel, the consonant should be doubled, as in, [u]installation[/u]. If there were such words as biassation, channellation, they should double the consonant. But the termination is usually attached to a Latin stem.
