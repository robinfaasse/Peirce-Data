[u]-ary[/u] ends a few adjectives from Latin [u]-aris[/u], where
one would not expect the [u]-y[/u], as military, [del]capillary,[/del]
exemplary, salutary. The [u]-y[/u] is getting dropped in Modern
English; as scalar for scalary, tutelar for tutelary, granular
for granulary, annular for annulary, insular for insulary,
titular for titulary. A number of these words are due
to medical men, as mamillary, capillary, maxillary,
medullary, axillary, papillary, fistulary.
