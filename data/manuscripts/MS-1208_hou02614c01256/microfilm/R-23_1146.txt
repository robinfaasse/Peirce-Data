-dom, an Anglo-Saxon suffix, representing the word dōm, which

denoted by such nouns; but in the old words it signifies a
state or quality, as princedom, freedom (A.S.), dukedom, popedom,
boredom, whoredom, sheriffdom (where inexorable usage insists
on two f's, although one would think the two consonants together
were enough, and that sherifdom would not be amiss), kingdom
