be connected with naked. The latter means 'stripped',
so that a neck would be a connecting strip or strap.
We do not know what the original form of the in-
strument was; we only know that it pinched the
animal's nose so as to make it easy to hold him.
It is possible the construction was such as to make
the word neck particularly appropriate; if we knew all
the senses the Anglo Saxon hnecca had. At any rate,
that the earliest recorded form of barnacle was equiva-
lent to modern 'bear-neck', is an indisputable fact.
