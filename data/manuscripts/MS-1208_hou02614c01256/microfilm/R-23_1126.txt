[u]buzzard[/u] means, for one thing, an insect that buzzes,
but as the name of a bird, the first syllable is supposed
to be derived from [ins]Latin[/ins] [u]buteo[/u], the same bird.

[u]coward[/u] was probably first applied to the hare, as
having a large backside, [u]coe[/u], from Latin [u]cauda[/u]; but
this is not certain;

[u]mustard[/u], is supposed to be so called because formerly
medmixed with the must of wine; but this may be
doubted;
