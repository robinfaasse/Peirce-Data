THE TIMES OFFICE,

New York, [date]March 17 1890[/date]

Dear Prof. Peirce:

I wanted to hold the Spencer article until I could be assured of something in reply to or in support of it for the following Sunday, Prof. Marsh and Prof. Dana, — I think I said Prof. Gray the other day when I meant to speak of him to you —
