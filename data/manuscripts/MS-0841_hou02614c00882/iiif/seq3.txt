An "Experience" is a brutally produced conscious effect that contributes

to a habit, self-controlled, yet so satisfying, on deliberation, as to be

destructible by no positive exercize of internal vigor. Take for illustration

the sensation undergone by a child that puts its forefinger into a flame with

the acquisition of a habit of keeping all its members out of all flames.
