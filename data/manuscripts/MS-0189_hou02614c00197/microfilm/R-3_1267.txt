<u>Lesson II</u>.

Lydia took pleasure in teaching the children whatever
they wished to learn and could learn, as well as some
other things that they would have great reason to wish
they had learned if they should not learn them. Among
other things Benjy had been carefully trained to distinguish
the mushrooms which could be found growing in places
from poisonous toadstools. Therefore, when he said
one afternoon "Gramma Lydia, I know where I
could find some mushrooms for supper," she was glad
to hear it. "But how will you find the place," she asked.
