[fig]Four numbered cards[/fig]

Now there is no other to be turned over. So the last number
that I called, Four, is the number of cards.

Have you never picked the petals from a daisy and
said, "Big-house, little-house, pigsty, barn; big-house,
little-house," and so forth. Then the last one called is
supposed to be your future home. That is like counting.

To find out who shall be It, one can put all
the children in a row, and point at them with the words,
