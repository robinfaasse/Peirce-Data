Lydia. You will see that in counting by nines the last figure
goes down while the last but one goes up one each
time, unless the last figure is 0 when it changes to 9
and the figure before it is unchanged.

I want you all to practice counting beans by nines. It
is a very good way. You will begin by counting simply
for a few beans, as I tell you, and then will count by nines.
To do this you shove three beans at a time, and at every
third shove, you call the number.

The children all practiced this for the rest of that
lesson. Each child was provided with about a pint
of coffee berries which had been counted before hand.
