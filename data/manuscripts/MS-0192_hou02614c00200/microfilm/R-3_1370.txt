[u]Art. 6[/u]. The two real functions of $y$ in the series for $G^{yi}$ are termed the [u]cosine[/u] and [u]sine[/u] of $y$. That is
$$G^{yi} = \cos y + \sin y \cdot i$$
which is called DeMoivre's Principle.
$$\cos y = 1 - \frac{1}{2!} y^2 + \frac{1}{4!} y^4 - \frac{1}{6!} y^6 + \text{etc} = \sum_r^\infty \frac{(-)^r y^{2r}}{(2r)!}$$
$$\sin y = y - \frac{1}{3!} y^3 + \frac{1}{5!} y^5 - \frac{1}{7!} y^7 + \text{etc} = \sum_r^\infty \frac{(-)^r y^{2r+1}}{(2r+1)!}$$
It is evident these series are convergent for all [del]real[/del] real values of $y$. We have to study the properties of these functions. These properties all spring from two, as follows:

$1^{\text{st}}$. It is evident from the series that
$$\cos(-y) = \cos y \qquad \sin(-y) = -\sin y$$
and $\cos 0 = 1 \qquad \sin 0 = 0$

$2^{\text{nd}}$
$$G^{(y_1+y_2)i} = \cos(y_1+y_2) + \sin(y_1+y_2) \cdot i$$
$$= G^{y_1 i} \cdot G^{y_2 i} = (\cos y_1 + \sin y_1 \cdot i) (\cos y_2 + \sin y_2 \cdot i)$$
$$= \cos y_1 \cdot \cos y_2 - \sin y_1 \cdot \sin y_2$$
$$+ [\sin y_1 \cdot \cos y_2 + \sin y_2 \cdot \cos y_1] \cdot i$$
That is $\cos(y_1+y_2) = \cos y_1 \cdot \cos y_2 - \sin y_1 \cdot \sin y_2$
$\sin(y_1+y_2) = \sin y_1 \cdot \cos y_2 + \sin y_2 \cdot \cos y_1$
This is called the Addition Principle.

From the two principles we have
$$1 = G^0 = G^{0i} = G^{(y-y)i} = \cos y \cdot \cos(-y) - \sin y \cdot \sin(-y)$$
$$+ [\sin y \cdot \cos(-y) + \sin(-y) \cdot \cos y] i$$
$$= \cos^2 y + \sin^2 y$$
