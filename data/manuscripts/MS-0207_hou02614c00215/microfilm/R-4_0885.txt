Finite multitude has the following properties which distinguish it from some other kinds of quantity:

[u]1[ins]st[/ins][/u], if one discrete collection is greater than another, there [del]are[/del] [ins]are is[/ins] some collections than which it is [u]next greater[/u], and [del]there are collections next greater than[/del] the second collection. By saying that A is "next greater" than B, we mean that A is greater than B but is not greater than anything greater than B.

[u]2[ins]nd[/ins][/u], every character which belongs to a collection, A, and which belongs to every collection next greater than any collection to which it belongs
