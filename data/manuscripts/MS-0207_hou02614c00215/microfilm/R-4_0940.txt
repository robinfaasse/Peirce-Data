We first write the fractions of denominator $1$, with the sign $<$ between every successive two, thus:
$$\frac{0}{1} < \frac{1}{1} < \frac{2}{1} < \frac{3}{1} < \frac{4}{1} < \frac{5}{1} < \frac{6}{1} < \text{etc.}$$

[del]We then mark every interval for the insertions[/del]

After all the fractions of any denominator $d$ have been inserted, we mark every $d\text{th}$ interval from the beginning for the insertion of the fractions of denominator $d+1$.

We then insert these fractions; and if any fraction is inserted immediately after a sign, the same sign is to be repeated after it. But if a fraction is inserted immediately after [ins]another[/ins] fraction, they are to be connected by the sign of equality.
