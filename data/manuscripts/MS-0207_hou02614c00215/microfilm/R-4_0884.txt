or otherwise brought into notice, in their proper sequence, so

viduals have been marked so far. In accuracy, zero must
be brought into notice before any individual is marked;
that is to say, we must notice that at the outset no indi-
vidual bears a mark as if it had been already counted.
If the collection is finite, the operation of counting will

collection, and if that is the case, the count must stop
