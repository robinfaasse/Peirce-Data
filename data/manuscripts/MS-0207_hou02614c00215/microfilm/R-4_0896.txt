Q(RN - MS) = N(RQ - PS) + S(PN - MQ)
and then reduce the fractions to a common denominator and form
the similar equation, we shall simply have every term multiplied
by N²Q²S². But if we form a similar equation from \frac{A}{B} < \frac{B}{B} < \frac{C}{D}
we get
D²(C-A) = D²(C-B) + D²(B-A)
Hence, in the first equation above, the three terms must be propor-
tional to the three differences
(\frac{R}{S} - \frac{M}{N}) = (\frac{R}{S} - \frac{P}{Q}) + (\frac{P}{Q} - \frac{M}{N}).
Hence, in following the above rule, where RN - MS = RQ - PS = PN - MQ,
we have
(\frac{R}{S} - \frac{M}{N}) : (\frac{R}{S} - \frac{P}{Q}) : (\frac{P}{Q} - \frac{M}{N}) = Q : N : S.
Hence in following the rule, the difference between any two successive
