[u]Process no. one[/u]

Hold the pack back upwards pass the cards
one by one into the other hand [del]making[/del] [ins]putting[/ins] the even
cards at the bottom and the odd ones at the top.

The cycle will be completed by a repetition
of the process such a number of times to that
[del]2[ins]t[/ins] ± 1 = (2[ins]t[/ins] + [gap])(2n - 1)[/del]
2[sup]t[/sup] ± 1 = l(2n + 1)
where n = number of cards in pack & l any integer
This formula applies only to even numbers of cards.
When n is odd it is 2[sup]t[/sup] ± 1 = l(2n - 1).
