[marginalia]MS 1539[/marginalia] Tricks

[u]No. 1[/u]. Take 52 cards. Milk them 3 times or Perform
process No. 1 9 times. Then perform process No. 1 taking
four cards at once & reversing the order of those
cards within those fours which are put on the
top. Then deal out in 4 piles face up.

No. 2. Divide the cards into three packs of 20, 21 & 11
cards respectively. Give the packs of 21 & 11 cards to two
persons, each to select a card. Let the one who had the
pack of 21 lay his card on the pack of 20 & put the rest
of his pack on it. Let the one who had the pack of 11 put
his card at the bottom of the pack of twenty & put the rest
of his pack under it. Perform process No. 2 & take 20
cards from the top & 10 from the bottom. The cards will
be found to have changed places.

No. 3. Illustrates formation of a novel. Take two ladies & gentlemen
suited to each other & a [ins]5[/ins] card to represent their felicity
