* [u]Foot-note.[/u] I know no question of metaphysics so [del]imp[/del] pressing as this of whether or not there be a maximum multitude capable of linear arrangement. There appears to be a proof that there is none; but owing to the extreme liability to fallacy of reasoning concerning this question, I have some doubt of its validity. I found what appeared to be a proof to the same effect. After careful and repeated scrutiny, I discovered no fallacy in it; and there was no great intricacy in the proof. I submitted it to two persons, [del]either[/del] [ins]each[/ins] of whom had devoted so much study to the general subject that I had a right to presume that not a dozen persons living were as competent in the premises as he. Neither could discover any fallacy. Yet I subsequently found, myself, that my proof was fallacious. Such an experience naturally renders me very cautious.
