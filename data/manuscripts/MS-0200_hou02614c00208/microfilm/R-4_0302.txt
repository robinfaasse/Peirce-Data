Nevertheless, in some respects the mathematician's time differs essentially from the river. For this latter is an existential form, while the time is a mere possibleness, and as such, being supposed at all, it must be supposed in its entirety. What I mean is this: suppose I arbitrarily suppose the centaurs to exist.
