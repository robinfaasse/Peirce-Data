E51[/pn] A Plural is a thing of reason, an ens rationis, which has, like many other things, two orders of Being; an entity and an existence. [del]The[/del] [ins]An[/ins] entity of anything belongs to it by virtue of its being intelligible as such and such a sort of thing. If this intelligibility belongs to it regardless of whether it actually is understood, as often is the case, it [del]go[/del] constitutes a Reality, a real nourisher of thought. But it does no more than that. It makes it intelligible as that [del]sort of[/del] [ins]genus[/ins] of thing, but it does not contribute to make it of this or that species of the genus, far less to give it individual identity, or to make it diverse from another individual of the genus. The entity of manhood, (whatever it consists in perhaps a naturalist may know, nor I) constitutes an object a man, and does no more.
