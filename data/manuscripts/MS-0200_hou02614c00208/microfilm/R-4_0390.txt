many persons whose notion of hard thinking was that of some
great straining, some violent action. Violence is the last thing that
I should recommend to you. The difficulty of getting a definition
to fit such a case as that before us, may be likened to that of
fitting a large screw stopper with a fine screw thread cut upon it
into its female correspondent that allows not the slightest
back-lash. To permit yourself any, the least violence of
effort is the way to spoil your job for good and all. What is
wanted is delicacy of touch, nice attention, and the smoothest-
running, endless patience.
