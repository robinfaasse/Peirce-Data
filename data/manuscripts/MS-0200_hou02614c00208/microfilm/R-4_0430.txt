[del]As far as positive whole numbers are concerned,[/del] This shows that zero multiplied by or into any quantity gives zero as the product. For $M\cdot P = (M+0)\cdot P = M\cdot P + 0\cdot P$ and transporting $M\cdot P$ in the equation $M\cdot P = M\cdot P + 0\cdot P$ we get $0 = 0\cdot P$, and we can equally prove $M\cdot 0 = 0$. As far as whole numbers are concerned [ins]together with $1\cdot x = x$ whatever $x$ may be[/ins] the distributive principle [del]is[/del] [ins]expressed[/ins] by saying that the effect upon a product of substituting for either factor the number that comes next after that in the natural succession of numbers is to add the other factor to that product. Thus in the product $5\times 8$, if we put $6$ instead of $5$, as the multiplier, it will add $8$ to the product, while if we put $9$ in place of $8$, it will add $5$.
