      I shall show the reader, when the proper time comes, how much reasoning is dependent upon Graphical Signs. By "graphical" I mean capable of being written or drawn, so as to be spatially arranged. It is true that one can argue [u]viva voce[/u]; but I do not believe one can go very deeply into any important and considerably large subject of discussion with calling up [fig]figure[/fig] in the minds of ones hearers mental images of objects arranged in ways in which [u]time[/u], without [u]space[/u], is incapable of serving as the field of representation, since in time[ins] of[/ins] two quite distinct objects one must be antecedent and the other subsequent.
