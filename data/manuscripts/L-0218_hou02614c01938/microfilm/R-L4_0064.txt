The Independent.
130 Fulton St.
New York.

Nov. 18, 1893.

Prof. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

My dear Sir:-

Dr. Twining has handed me with the highest commendations your article on "The Connection Between Mind and Matter." I have read it and found it very able and valuable, but I am sure that to the bulk of our readers its spesific gravity will seem to be above twenty-five, and I therefore am reluctantly obliged to decline it.

Yours very truly,
Wm Hayes Ward
Superintending Editor.
