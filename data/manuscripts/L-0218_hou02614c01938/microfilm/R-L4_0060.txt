The Independent,
251 Broadway,
New York.

May 26, 1892.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,

12 W. 39th St., N. Y.

My dear Sir:-

I must cry [u]Peccavi[/u]. I was sure I had returned your article, not feeling able to use it, but here I find it with some correspondence which I had put one side in another room to attend to, and I supposed it had been attended to. I can only express my sincere regret and beg your pardon.

Yours very truly,

Wm Hayes Ward
Superintending Editor.
