The Independent,
130 Fulton St.
New York.

Nov. 2, 1893.

Prof. C. S. Peirce,

Milford, Pa.

My dear Sir:-

The difficulty with the Napoleon article has been not that it was too heavy but that it was too long, [del]and[/del] [ins]and[/ins] I have looked it over and thought that I might divide it and publish it in two parts. I hope to be able to do so, and will let you know if I can as soon as possible.

As to the scientific column, I am afraid you do not examine our issue very often. We have a scientific column edited by as good scientific men as we know, and I find on talking with Dr. Ward that neither he nor I feel that we ought to do anything to disturb the arrangement as it exists in its present form.

Very truly yours,

Kinsley Twining.
