My dear Professor Peirce:-

Dr. Ward is obdurate and remains un-
moved by your propositions to tell us what the
world has been thinking of through 1893 in a
series of articles. As to the advertisements
you will have to deal directly, I suspect, with
the advertisers, who are stupid enough to pre-
fer their own method of praising their goods.
I think on the whole that I should be willing
to pay some money to see you acting in the role
which you propose.

Yours very truly,

Kinsley Twining
