Can the infinite be defined? Is it not a simple
conception? The term simple conception has not its
metaphysical sense in Logic. In Metaphysics it signi-
fies one that is formed by the energy of a single faculty.
And in this sense most simple ideas may be defined. For
they may have complex & crossing relations by which we
can draw their coordinates. And in this way I propose to
define the infinite.
