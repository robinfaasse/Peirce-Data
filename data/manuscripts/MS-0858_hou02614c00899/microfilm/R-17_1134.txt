We can therefore comprehend definitions, when we
cannot conceive of that which they define. That is to
say; [del]certain[/del] a synthesis of some conceptions cannot be
made. Now in all cases there is but one cause of this
and that is that our faculties will not make the synthe-
sis. Nevertheless, the reason divides the cases into two,
and these I shall speak of hereafter. It is sufficient
at present to conclude that whatever can be defined can
be discussed.
