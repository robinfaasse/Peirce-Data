What can be discussed? We [del]can[/del] syllogise on whatever we can define. We can define many things of which we cannot conceive. What we define is never a thing but [del]any[/del] idea or a pretended or supposed idea. Now we can define ideas which we cannot think. Thus a foursided triangle is a defined pseudo-idea. We can reason upon it & show that it is an absurdity.

What is infinity? [del]How[/del] is that defined? What is infinity? It is not [ins]the conception of[/ins] a thing, neither is it the [del]caption[/del] conception of the quality of a thing. If we can think of a good man, it is because in the first place we have a notion of man & in the second place we have the conception of good and the third place we can combine one conception with the other.
