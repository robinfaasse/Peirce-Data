[u]Ordinal Numbers.[/u] The word number is used in three senses; namely,
1[ins]st[/ins], to mean a [ins]finite[/ins] collection, as when we say "I had a number of callers
yesterday"; 2[ins]nd[/ins], to mean the multitude, or maniness, of a finite
collection; as when we say "The number of stars easily visible
to the naked eye is not so great as one would think, [del]not[/del]
[ins]much[/ins] more than [del]three[/del] half a thousand, [del]s[/del]—not thousands upon thousands,
as people often guess"; 3[ins]rd[/ins], to mean one of several classes of
words and symbols, better called [u]numerals[/u], which are used
in discriminating between one finite multitude and another.

There are several kinds of numeral words and phrases, of
which the chief are the cardinal numbers and the ordinal numbers.
