It is important to notice that some wholes are not col-
lections of their parts. Thus, a mass of gas is a whole of
which the molecules are the parts, But if it were a [ins]the[/ins] collec-
tion of these molecules, then the gas being hot would con-
sist in all its molecules being alike hot. Such is not
the case. On the contrary, the gas being hot consists in its
molecules moving in opposite ways. It is also important to
bear in mind that the parts of any whole must have the
