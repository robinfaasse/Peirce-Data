[u]Numeration[/u]

What is meant by 'numeration'? The operation of counting and the naming of numbers.

[u]1[sup]st[/sup][/u], [u]Number[/u] sometimes means a [u]finite[/u] collection, that is what is named by a plural noun, provided the taking one of the objects so named away could not be repeated endlessly, because at last none would be left. Thus one may say "I saw a number of [del]friends[/del] [ins]new books[/ins] yesterday." But one could not with propriety call all possible books a number, since of [del]writing[/del] [ins]making[/ins] possible books "there is no end."
