[u]Numeration.[/u]

What is meant by "numeration"? It is that part of arithmetic which teaches how to count.

Some arithmetic-books distinguish between [u]numeration[/u], which teaches the use of the words called numbers, from [del]arithmetical[/del] [ins]or characters[/ins] [u]notation[/u], which teaches the use of the [del]figures[/del] [ins]characters[/ins] called numbers. But [del]the to[/del] one can count with figures, without words, or with a tally, without either figures or words; and the use of the numeral words and that of the numeral figures ought to be learned together, in learning to count. For these reasons, this book will follow the usage of other arithmetic-books which treat notation as a part of numeration.
