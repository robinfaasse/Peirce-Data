ment such that if we take any two different members of the
series, the difference between them is that one is in that way
attached to something which the other is not attached.
A very natural and convenient way of thinking of a series is
to compare it with the series of instants of all time by
imagining that something changes so as to be at different
instants attached to all the different members of the series
