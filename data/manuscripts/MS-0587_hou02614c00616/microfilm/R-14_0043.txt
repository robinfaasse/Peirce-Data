This is obviously much too vague
& shows us the necessity of beginning
with a systematic analysis of the
conception of a sign.

But before proceeding to such an
analysis we may make a certain
use of this vague proposition.

This shows that we must come to an
idealistic doctrine concerning truth. We
must mean by how things are, how we
are affected by them.

Yet there is a distinction between a
true and false idea, also. Then by the
truth concerning a thing we do not
mean how any man is affected by
a thing.

Nor how a majority is affected
But how a man would be affected
after sufficient reflection & experience
