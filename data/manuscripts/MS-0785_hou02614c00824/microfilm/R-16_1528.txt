[u]Note 1[/u].

The proof offered that
$\phi x \equiv \phi(1) , x + \phi(0) , \bar{x}$
is fallacious. For the identities
$\phi(1) \equiv i$   $\phi(0) \equiv j$
cannot be obtained by simply substituting
[del]1[/del] [ins]1[/ins] and [del]0[/del] [ins]0[/ins] for $x$ in the identity
$\phi x \equiv i , x + j , (1-x)$
inasmuch as $i$ and $j$ have not been
proved to be independent of $x$. Boole's
proof is the same.
