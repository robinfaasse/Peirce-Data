But Solomon is a singular. Let $m$ denote
man. Then shall $w^m$ denote wiser than
every man or wiser than [del]some[/del] [ins]some[/ins] man? In
either case, $n^{w^m}$ will be ambiguous. For
it may be taken as $n^{(w^m)}$ or as $(n^w)^m$.

Now, if we simply adopt the formula
$$w^{(a+b)} = w^a, w^b$$
which is imitated from algebra, then $w^m$
must be wiser than every man, and $(n^w)^m$
must be other than wiser (no more wise)
