[del]No. 1206[/del] [u]Standard Orthography[/u].

The written and printed representation of the sounds of language
by means of characters
which are insufficient
both in kind and number,
[del]and which must therefore be combined and modified,[/del]
if we would give a graphical symbolization of the phonetic elements,
with only some degree of [del]exactness[/del] [ins]exactitude[/ins] and convenience,
has been, from all time,
for nations as well as individuals,
linguistical students not excepted,
one of the most [del]necessary[/del]
and one of the most difficult [ins]of[/ins] problems,
and has consequently
scarcely ever been happily solved.

[u]Mr. H. Sweet[/u].
