[marginalia]VA2[/marginalia]

Odessa, Mo.
[date]June 8th/93[/date] —

Dear Sir: Will you kindly
inform me, where I may
obtain a copy of the paper
on Linear [del]Associative[/del] Algebra
by the late distinguished
Prof. Benjamin Peirce — your
father, I believe? I understand
that the theory of Quaternions
is developed and illustrated
in this paper.

I have Studies in Logic,
in which book I find
much of interest and
value. —

Very truly yours,
Thos. M. Johnson

To
Prof. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.
