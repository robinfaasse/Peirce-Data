C. S. Peirce Esq., Ph.D.,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir: Accept my hearty
thanks for the vol. of the [u]An[/u]
[u]Internat. d' Histoire Congres de Paris[/u]
[u]1900[/u] which, with your kind
letter, came safely. I enclose
$1.00 for the same, which I
trust will be satisfactory.
(The vol. is of value to me only
because it contains the tract
by Anatolius). If not, however,
I will of course return
the work to you. With
