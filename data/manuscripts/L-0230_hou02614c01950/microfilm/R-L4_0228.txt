[marginalia]VB3b#3[/marginalia] Osceola, Mo. [date]Feb. 4th 1893[/date]

Dear Sir:-
Thanks for your favor of the 26th of Jan. ult., which came in due time. (Your former letter must have gone to Florida, as you suggest, or elsewhere - it never reached me, I am sorry to say).
I have the Studies in Logic by members of the Johns Hopkins University - I procured it since last writing you - and shall give your paper a careful study. I have also the Jour. of Spec. Phil. and the Monist, and have read your articles in those periodicals.
Somewhere I saw a reference to a paper by you in the Cambridge Philosophical Society. From what you say I infer that this was a fake reference. I read Greek, Latin and French but (unfortunately) not German, and therefore am unable to avail myself of Schröder's Algebra der Logik, according to your suggestion.

Sincerely Yours,
Thos. M. Johnson
