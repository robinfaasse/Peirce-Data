Osceola, Mo.
[date]Jan. 21st 1893[/date].

Dear Sir:

Several weeks ago I wrote you inquiring as to where I could procure your papers on Symbolic Logic. Not having heard from you I presume that my letter went astray or was [del]been[/del] overlooked.

I am very anxious to obtain the following papers by you:
(1) On the Syllogism and the Logic of Relatives (Trans. of Cam. Phil. Soc. 1864).
(2) The Logic of Relatives (3d Vol. of Amer. Jour. of Mathematics, 1880).
(3) Paper or Series of Papers in Amer. Journal of Mathematics for 1885.

If you have copies of these or other papers treating of the same subject for sale, I will on notification of price, take pleasure in remitting the same.

Very truly Yours,
Thos. M. Johnson

To Prof. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.
