[date]Aug. 15th 1902[/date]

[u]Ed. of The Nation[/u]
New York City, N.Y.

Dear Sir: In the issue of your paper for [date]Aug. 7th[/date], you note (3rd. col. p. 115) the Annales of the Paris Inter. Congress of 1900, and mention the names of the various contributions. One of these—the Greek text of Anatolius on the first ten numbers, edited by Heiberg—I am anxious to get, and I should be glad to know whether you would dispose of your copy of this tract? If not, kindly give me the full title (in French)
