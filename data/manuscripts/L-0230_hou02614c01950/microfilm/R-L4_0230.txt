[pn]VA2[/pn]

Osceola, Mo.
[date]Nov. 17th 1895[/date].

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:- I hasten to send you money order for $1.50 in payment of sub. to [u]The Pike County Press[/u] (beginning with Nov. 15th), in order that I may secure your articles on the "General Aspects of the Logic of Relatives". I anticipate much pleasure and profit from the reading and studying of them.

With best wishes and regards,
I remain,
Faithfully and Platonically Yours,
Thomas M. Johnson.
