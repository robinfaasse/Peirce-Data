$$\tan \tau = \frac{v}{u}$$

$$ux + vy + 1 = 0$$
$$u dx + v dy = 0$$
$$dy = - \frac{u}{v} dx$$
$$(dx)^2 + (dy)^2 = (1 + \frac{u^2}{v^2}) (dx)^2 = (ds)^2$$

$$d \tan \sigma = \sqrt{1 + (D_x y)^2} dx$$
$$(D_x \tan \sigma)^2 = 1 + (D_x y)^2$$
$$D_x (\tan \sigma + y) \cdot D_x (\tan \sigma - y) = 1$$

$$(du)^2 + (dv)^2$$
$$= (d \frac{1}{a})^2 + (d \frac{1}{b})^2$$
$$= \frac{(da)^2}{a^4} + \frac{(db)^2}{b^4}$$

$$\tan \varphi = \frac{y}{x}$$

$$du \cdot x + dv \cdot y = 0$$
$$dv = - \frac{x}{y} \cdot du$$
$$(du)^2 + (dv)^2 = (1 + \frac{x^2}{y^2}) (du)^2$$
$$\frac{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}}{y} du = \sec \varphi \cdot du$$

$$d \tan \tau = d \frac{v}{u} = \frac{u \cdot dv - du \cdot v}{u^2}$$
$$= \frac{u \frac{dv}{du} - v}{u^2} du$$
$$= - \frac{u \frac{x}{y} + v}{u^2} du$$
$$= - \frac{\frac{xu}{y} + \frac{1}{y}}{u^2} du$$
$$d \tan \tau = \frac{\frac{u}{y} D_u v - 1}{v} du$$
$$\tan \tau = \int \frac{\frac{u}{y} D_u v - v}{v^2} du$$
