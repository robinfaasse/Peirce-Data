My dear Mr. Peirce.

I am in receipt of your letter of [date]Jan. 12[/date], the circulars and also the copies of the Monist. I can fully appreciate your position and I very greatly wish that I could furnish you material aid in publishing your magnum opus. But - unfortunately income is diminishing while expenses are increasing. I dislike the way of the world. We have lost a great deal in these hard times, and I am compelled to husband my resources to meet the claims of those dependent on me. The
