[date]Jan 1873[/date]

At a meeting of the
[u]Stevens Institute of Technology[/u]
held [del]this[/del] [ins]on the[/ins] [u]twenty-third[/u]
day of [u]January[/u] in the year
of our Lord [u]one thousand eight hundred[/u]
[u]and seventy-three[/u], it was unanimously
Resolved, That the degree of
Doctor of Laws
be, and the same is hereby, conferred upon
Professor JOHN TYNDALL,
of London, in testimony of our high appreciation of
his services to Science, and of the great value of
his labors in the discovery and dissemination of
scientific truth, and especially of his efforts to
popularize the study of Physical Science, and to
awaken a love for its pursuit in the minds of the
young, and of his generous devotion of the
proceeds of his lectures in this country to the
advancement of scientific research in the United
States.

ALFRED M. MAYER, Secretary.

Attest: A. M. MAYER, Stevens Institute, Hoboken, N. J.
