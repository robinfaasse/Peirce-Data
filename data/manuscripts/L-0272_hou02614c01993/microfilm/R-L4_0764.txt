Stevens Institute of Technology.

Hoboken, N. J., [date]February 10th 1894[/date]

My dear Mr. Peirce

I send you with this mail
a paper or a pamphlet in which I have
endeavored to arrive at the law connecting
the pitch of a sound with the duration
of its residual sensation.

I hope that you will find the
paper worthy of perusal and my
conclusions correct, especially since
I shall be very much pleased to
hear from one whose criticism I
have always held in great respect.

I already wrote to ask you
to place my name as a subscriber
to your proposed work on the
"Principles of Philosophy". I should have
written sooner but I have been ill.
