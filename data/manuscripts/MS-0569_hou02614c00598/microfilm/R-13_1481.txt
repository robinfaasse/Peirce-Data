The sign [u]⤙[/u] is subject to three algebraical rules,
as follows: —

[u]Rule 1[/u]. If [u]x⤙y[/u] and [u]y⤙z[/u], then [u]x⤙z[/u]. This is
called the [u]principle of the transitiveness of the copula[/u].

[u]Rule 2[/u]. Either [u]x⤙y[/u] or [u]y⤙[del]z[/del][ins]x[/ins][/u].

[u]Rule 3[/u]. There are two propositions, [u]u[/u] and [u]v[/u], such
that [u]v⤙u[/u] is false.

rule is readily proved as follows. In the state-
