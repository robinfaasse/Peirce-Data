I further use the symbols [u]\Sigma[/u] and [u]\Pi[/u] as follows. [u]\Sigma_{i}[/u] means
that [u]i[/u] is [del]anything[/del] [ins]something[/ins] suitably selected from the universe of [u]i[/u]'s,
while [u]\Pi_{i}[/u] means that [u]i[/u] may be anything however taken
from the universe of [u]i[/u]'s. For example, [u]\Sigma_{i}\Pi_{j}[/u] [u]l_{ij}[/u] will
mean that an object [u]i[/u] can be found such that, taking
any object [u]j[/u] whatever, [u]i[/u] and [u]j[/u] are related in the way
signified by [u]l[/u]. While [u]\Pi_{i}\Sigma_{j}[/u] [u]l_{ij}[/u] will mean that any
object whatever, [u]i[/u], being taken, an object [u]j[/u] can then
be found such that [u]i[/u] has the relation to [u]j[/u] signified by
[u]l[/u].
