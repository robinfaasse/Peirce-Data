The nucleus was situated about 70' S.W.
of $\kappa$ Serpentis and the tail curving round
by $\beta$ and $\gamma$ Herculis which it just about
touched with its convex edge reaching up to
$\eta$ Herculis. In its widest part it stretched
from $\beta$ Herculis to $\kappa$ Coronae.
This observ. was rough and the estimates
consequent accordingly.
