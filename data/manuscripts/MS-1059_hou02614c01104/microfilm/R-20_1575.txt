Good. Upper edge of tail (southern) distinct as before; lower edge also distinct
about as far as the line marked across, where it is merged in fringes
of light as represented. The maximum breadth of the bright portion is
thought to be a little beyond $\delta$ Serpentis. The black line down the centre
of the tail appears to follow its direction, partaking of its curvature.
The intense brightness about the nucleus extends about twice as far
on the northern or indistinct side as on the southern. A small
star marked in front of the head seems to be involved in nebula.
Several faint branches of the tail may be seen on the indistinct
side extending [del]about[/del] as far as $\eta$ Hercules. The faint southern
straight tail can be imagined; but is very uncertain.
(The branches mentioned above certainly were seen, but were
not counted, neither was their position accurately estimated.)
