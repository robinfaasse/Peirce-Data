$$(x^2+y^2)(y+c z) + 2 \overline{c} x y z = 0$$

Put $z = c z \qquad e = \frac{2 \overline{c}}{c}$

$$(x^2+y^2)(y+z) + e x y z = 0$$

To form line equation
$$\begin{aligned}
u &= 2x(y+z) + e y z \quad = 2(xy+xz+yz) + a y z \\
v &= 2y(y+z) + x^2+y^2 + e x z = 2(xy+xz+yz) + a x z + x^2+y^2 \\
w &= x^2+y^2 + e x y \qquad\qquad = \qquad\qquad\qquad (a+2)xy + x^2+y^2
\end{aligned}$$

Put $a = e-2$

The cubic having a node, the line equation is of the form

$$\begin{aligned}
&A u^4 + B v^4 + C w^4 \\
&+ D u^3 v + E u^3 w + F u v^3 + G v^3 w + H u w^3 + I v w^3 \\
&+ [del]J u^2 v w + K u v^2 w[/del] \\
&+ J u^2 v^2 + K u^2 w^2 + L v^2 w^2 \\
&+ M u^2 v w + N u v^2 w + O u v w^2 = 0
\end{aligned}$$

$$\begin{aligned}
u^2 &= 4 x^2 (y+z)^2 + 4 e (y+z) x y z + e^2 y^2 z^2 \\
v^2 &= 4 y^2 (y+z)^2 + 4 y (y+z)(x^2+y^2) + 4 e (y+z) x y z \\
&\quad + x^4 + 2 x^2 y^2 + y^4 + 2 e x^3 z
\end{aligned}$$
