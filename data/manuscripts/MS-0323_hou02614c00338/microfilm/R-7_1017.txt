The writer has taken part in this movement since its first
inception in the early seventies, and has watched all its phases.
He long entertained serious doubts of the doctrine, owing to a
personal peculiarity which prompts him to struggle desperately
against every philosophical opinion that has recommended
itself to him before he definitively surrenders himself to it.
Perhaps this may fit him to speak of pragmatism with
less bias than if he had not himself felt the force
of the objections to it.
