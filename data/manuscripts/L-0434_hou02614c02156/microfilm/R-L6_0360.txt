Editor's Office of The Sun,

New York, [date]Oct 11 1892[/date]

Dear Mr Peirce_

I learn that the delay in answering your letter is due to waiting for the answer to your criticisms on an article heretofore published in The Sun.

Mr. Dana further says that any contribution you may offer for the paper will receive favorable attention, and if used will be paid for.

Yours faithfully
Thomas Hitchcock
