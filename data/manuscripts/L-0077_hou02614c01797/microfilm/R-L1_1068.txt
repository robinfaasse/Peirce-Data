CHICAGO, [date]July 28 1890[/date]

Charles S. Peirce Esq.

Dear sir; The Open Court Publ Co intends to
publish a new Quarterly for which I have
enlisted as contributors the very best names -
among them Max Müller, Prof. Mach of Prague, Prof.
Sigwart of Tübingen, Prof. Romanes of London and
others. I wish that in the new Quarterly our
most prominent American authors should
be represented and shall be greatly indebted
to you for an article from your pen on
"Modern Logic" or some similar topic -
perhaps "Logic and Ethics". You may choose
any theme with which you are engaged
at present.

I shall be much obliged to you if you
can send your MS. soon so that I can publish
your essay in the first number. If you will
kindly let me know the price of your contribution,
with which you may be pleased to favor us, I shall
send remittance at once.

Respectfully yours
P Carus
