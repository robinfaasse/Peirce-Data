My dear Doctor Carus:
I now will begin the explana-
tion of the corrections I deem in-
dispensable in the reprint of
my Pop. Sci. papers.

The first two constitute one
Essay upon what I call
Pragmaticism or the doctrine
of pragmatic clearness which
consists in a clear conception
of that Habit of Conduct [del]to[/del]
in which any given concept
would work out its actualiza-
tion.
