My dear Professor,

We have now
proposals for the printing, etc.
of the books from New York
and Chicago - but also
the information through Mr. Sooksteder
that a copyright runs for
28 years - so that Appleton's copyright
is not expired on the Pop. Sc. Monthly
articles; and their written consent
or, another agreement made at the time
of the publication of the articles
seems to be a necessity now.

Hoping yourself and Mrs. Peirce
are well,

I remain
Sincerely Yours,
[u]F. C. S. Schiller[/u].
