Milford, Pa.,

My Dear Sir:-

Your article is duly received.

Mr. Hegeler wishes me to ask you, before its publication, what your conception concerning the honorarium would be; and I should like to know whom you address in the article by "you". If you mean us, viz., the management of The Open Court, would it not be advisable to express it more plainly, so that among our readers there would be no mistake about it?

Your MS. and copy of Shelley announced by you have not as yet arrived. [ins]With kind regards[/ins]

Yours very truly,
P. Carus
