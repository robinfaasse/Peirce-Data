For the publication on
my part of the papers that
appeared in the Pop. Sc. M. the
permission of the firm Appleton & Co
in writing is necessary, as I
wrote you already on the [date]24th[/date]

Your photogravure has arrived.
It is a fine picture. Accept
my thanks for the same.

I read with sympathy of your
care for Mr. Peirce's health.
Is she at Lakewood now as thought
of by you in your former letters?

The Chicago prices for getting up the
book are lower than Little's and as
good work as theirs is likely to be
expected; but I am undecided yet.
