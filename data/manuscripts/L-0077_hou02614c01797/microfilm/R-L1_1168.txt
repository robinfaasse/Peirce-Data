The Monist,
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT,
LA SALLE, ILL.

La Salle, Illinois, July 25, 1904.

Chas. S. Peirce, Esq'r.,
Milford, Pa.,

Dear Sir:-

Your request concerning payment for an article still
due you which appeared in No. 353 of The Open Court under the ti-
tle "The Marriage of Religion and Science," touches matters of
The Open Court which I, as editor, can directly attend to. The ar-
ticle containing 1095 words, I send you a check for twenty-seven
dollars and fifty cents($27.50) in payment for it.

Hoping that you will soon overcome your present difficulties,
I am,

Yours very truly,

P. Carus
