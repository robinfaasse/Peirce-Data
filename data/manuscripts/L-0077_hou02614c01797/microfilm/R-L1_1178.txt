The Monist,
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT,
LA SALLE, ILL.

August 10, 1896.

Chas. S. Peirce, Esq.,
C/O Albert Stickney, Esq.,
35 Nassau St.,
New York City, N. Y.

Dear Sir:-

Your MS. was received some time ago and I replied in a letter of July 24th in which I made a definite offer to pay you for the MS. $250. The letter was sent to your last address, "35 Nassau St, New York." Not having heard from you I was at a loss what to think of it. [u]If you wish the MS. returned, please let me know a reliable and secure address and I shall send it to you by express.[/u] Of course the offer made in my letter of July 24th holds good still, and I should be glad to have a definite reply at your earliest convenience

Hoping to hear from you soon, I remain, with kind regards,

Yours very truly,

P. Carus
