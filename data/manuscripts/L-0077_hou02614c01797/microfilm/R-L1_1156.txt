La Salle Ill. July 13. 93

Professor C. S. Peirce
Milford Pa.

Dear Sir

Referring to my letter of June 22. 93 I will say that I am still without the catalogue of your Library which you was to sent me, neither have I received from you acknowledgement of receipt for the $ 250⁰⁰ remitted to you in the beginning of the month through the First National Bank of Chicago.

Respectfully
Edward C. Hegeler
per C. Diesterweg
