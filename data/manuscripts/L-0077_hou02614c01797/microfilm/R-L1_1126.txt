[date]LaSalle April 2nd/89[/date]

Professor C. S. Peirce
on Erie R. R. Port Jervis,
New York State

My dear Professor,

I wrote you April 1st
enclosing check for $250.00/100

This morning, my letter to you
of the 16th ult is returned to
me. For explanation I enclose
the envelope, I also send the letter.

As the letter of April 1st may
have been misdirected in a similar
way, I cause to be [del]written[/del] [ins]and telegraphed[/ins] to
the Postmaster at Port Jervis, [ins]N. Jersey[/ins]
to forward same to Port Jervis, New York
