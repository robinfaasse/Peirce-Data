Dear Sir:— Your letter, etc., received.
Did you mean to say that you would like
to get hold of the Kant "translation"? Well,
it was my intention months ago to send
it to you, but the tenor of your letters led
me to believe that you do not like to do
anything in the matter at all, and I did
not want to bother you too much. However,
I resolved to make use of the "translation"
[ins]myself[/ins] some day in the way indicated in some
of my first letters. the result may be small,
but it is better than nothing. I intend to
try my game in October when I am settled
down in Chicago, but in the meantime I am
quite willing to listen to you if you have
anything new to say.

a copy of the Dedekind book, and one of
our catalogues. I have almost a complete set
