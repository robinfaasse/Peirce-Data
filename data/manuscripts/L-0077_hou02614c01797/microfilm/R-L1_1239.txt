THE MONIST,
THE OPEN COURT,
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT,
LA SALLE, ILL.

DR. PAUL CARUS.

September 15, 1904.

C. S. Peirce, Esq.,

Milford, Pa.

My dear Sir:

Your Ms. just reaches me on the day on which I intend to leave for St. Louis. I have no time to look at the Ms. but I expect that your plan to have your treatment of pragmatism divided into a book review and an article is proper. You will notice in the same [del](viz.,[/del] [ins]next)[/ins] number that I have done so with Paul Rée, a philosopher of second rank, of whom I published an article and a review of his work. The article is critical, but of his achievements, and I have to let the public know that Paul Rée is not up to the mark while for private reasons he deserves all honor as a generous benefactor, as a promoter of, philosophical truth.

I shall attend to the business part of our transaction at my earliest convenience and send you the promised check for $50.00 as soon as I can have it made out from Chicago.

I remain with kind regards and best wishes,

Very truly yours,

Paul Carus
