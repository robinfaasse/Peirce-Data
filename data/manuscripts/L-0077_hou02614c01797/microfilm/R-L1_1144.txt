Dear Sir:

The following MS. is in my hands for my arithmetics

[u]Copy for Primary Ar[/u]
About 50 pages, with very numerous rough sketches for illustrations, equivalent to about 5000 words

[u]Copy for Advanced Ar.[/u]
About 20 pages typewritten 60 lines, or 1000 words to page, say 20 000 "
About 50 " written, about 120 words to page, say 6 000 "
Total copy about 31 000 "

[u]Preparation for copy[/u]
Detailed notes for Primary Arithmetic 50 pages, about
Examples mostly statistical for Advanced Ar. 50 " "
Calculations for physical examples 30 " "

In addition to the above, there is a quantity of MS, which, though it will not serve for copy for the arithmetic, was prepared to guide me in writing it, and out of which one or two articles can be made. Namely, there are

Notices on previous arithmetics. About 40 text books now in the schools are carefully noticed (I much regret not having German, French, & Italian text books). About 200 older books are noticed. In all about 150 pages MS.

Notes on apparatus to be used in teaching arithmetic, on the psychology of the subject, on my method of teaching each operation, various attempts at laying out the work so as to get the whole subject into moderate compass. Memoranda of matters to be introduced, etc. About 100 pages MS.

In all there are about 500 pages MS.

The work is in rather a backward condition, but by no means desperately so. Yours very truly C. S. Peirce
