Dear Sir:

I have postponed answering yours [ins]of [del]ult.[/del][/ins] — without meaning, however, to put it off so long, — because I was anxious Dr. Carus should have before him a substitute which he may desire to use instead of what I have said about logical graphs in the MS forwarded. This I have now sent to him.

The most difficult characters to reproduce are those which I use for the copula as in this formula [u]A⤙B⤙C⤙D⤙E[/u]. The cost of justification, etc. in those cases, together with the facts that the copula would have to be of several sizes, and that the number of such formulas are few, and the sign is not intended to remain permanently in [ins]each[/ins] [del]the[/del] matter, me think the cheapest way would be to have the whole [del]formula[/del] drawn as a cut.

[fig]Two logical formulas with copula signs connecting letters[/fig]

These should be sufficient hints to an intelligent draughtsman. If my sent MS. on graphs is used, the figures are well enough to serve the purpose of the draughtsman, especially if he reads the article.
