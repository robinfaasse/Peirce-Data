[marginalia][date]Oct. 14. 1896[/date][/marginalia]

Dear Sir:

There are a number of persons to whom I should like to have copies of my article [u]The Regenerated Logic[/u] go. I do not know whether you continue your liberal practice about sending copies or not; but if not I suppose you will send them & charge me the wholesale price. I am sorry you do not send separate articles, on account of the expense of the whole numbers; although the whole number is exceedingly interesting.

I should like a copy to be sent without delay to M. le Comte d'Aulby 18 W 32[ins]nd[/ins] St. New York City; because he sails next week. The other persons to whom I should like the article to go are:—

Dr. W. K. Otis 5 W 50[ins]th[/ins] St. New York
Dr. A. Jacobi 1 W 34[ins]th[/ins] St. New York
Mrs. James M. Barnard, Milton, Mass.
Geo: S. Morison Esq. 35 Wall Street, New York
Albert Stickney Esq, 35 Nassau Street, New York
Mr. A. D. Risteen, Hartford Steam Boiler Insurance Co., Hartford, Conn.
Prof J. M. Peirce 4 Kirkland Place, Cambridge Mass
H. H. D. Peirce, Esq, American Legation, St. Petersburg, Russia.
[del]E. S. Holden[/del] [ins]Edward S. Holden[/ins] Esq 16 Broad Street, New York.
Professor E. S. Holden Lick Observatory, Mt. Hamilton, Cal.
Father Geo: M. Searle, Washington University, Washington D.C.
Prof. Ferdinand Becker U. S. Geological Survey, Washington D.C.
