Milford, Pa.,

My Dear Sir:-

Shelley, and also the book containing a number of your articles, were duly received Saturday, May 6th. I am very much obliged for the beautiful edition of Shelley, and have noticed with pleasure that it contains more [ins]material[/ins] than other editions.

Will you kindly let me know whether the book containing your articles is intended as copy for your book, [ins]to be published[/ins] or as a personal gift? In case you intend it to be a copy for the printers, I should be much obliged to you for further information concerning the title of the whole book, arrangement of the articles, etc., etc.

With kind regards,

Yours very truly,
P Carus
