La Salle, Ill., July 27, 1892.

Mr. Charles S. Peirce,
Milford, Pike Co.,
Pa.

Dear Sir:

Your letter of July 25th. stating that you will commence the articles for The Open Court, received. When you send the first article will you also kindly write [ins]each[/ins] a short note to be published in The Open Court a week or so in advance of the [ins]first[/ins] article[del]s[/del], stating what the articles will treat of, their plan, etc. ?

We have ordered a copy of "Fundamental Problems" to be sent to you.

Enclosed you will find, on a separate sheet of paper, a translation of the title of Mach's work. Do you think it will do ? We must decide upon one now, as the page-headings are to be set up in advance.

The page-corrections you suggest have been noticed.

Very truly yours,

Thomas J. McCormack
