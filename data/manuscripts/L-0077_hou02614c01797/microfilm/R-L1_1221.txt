La Salle, Ill., [date]Dec. 23, 1907[/date]

Dear Sir:- On the 19[ins]th[/ins] inst. I wrote a postal card to you in regard to a matter relating to Dr. P. C. of the "O. C." and "Monist," but I am sorry to say that you did not answer. Kindly tell me if you are not interested in the concern at all, which would be a great mistake, as there is a bushel of fun and perhaps a few hundreds in it for the right party, - "a man with the "proper philosophical comprehension."
Please favor me with a reply!

Resp.

Fred. Sigrist
La Salle, Ill
