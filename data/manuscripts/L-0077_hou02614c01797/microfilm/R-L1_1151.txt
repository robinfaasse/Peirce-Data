Milford, Pa.,

Dear Sir:-

You ask, in a letter to Mr. Hegeler, whether The Open Court desires further articles from you in the "Critic of Argument" series, and I have to reply that you are welcome to send articles but that unless a special arrangement be made, we preserve the liberty of rejecting them.

There are three articles of yours in my hands; one on "Immortality in the Light of Synechism", which I intend to publish in The Monist; another, "What is Christian Faith" is in the hands of the compositors, and you may expect proofs in a few days. The third one, entitled "The Critic of Arguments" was handed me by Mr. Hegeler some time ago for my inspection only. It was not sent as a contribution. If you mean it as a contribution, I should suggest ([u]at least[/u] for its republication as a part of the whole series in book or pamphlet form) the omission of one or two passages. I think, mainly of the footnote on "uniformity of nature". We shall be ready to publish this third article (viz., on the Critic of Arguments) in about two months.

I enclose the two articles which are not as yet in the hands of the compositors.

Please return them in case you are satisfied with this arrangement.

Yours very truly,
P. Carus
