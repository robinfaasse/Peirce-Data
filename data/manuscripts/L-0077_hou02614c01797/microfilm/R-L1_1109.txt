LaSalle [date]Nov. 20th 1891[/date]

Professor C. S. Peirce
Milford Pa.

My dear Professor,

Your kind favors of the 11th and 15th inst. have both reached me in due course of mail.

From M. G. Co. I have not heard yet, but it may be that a proposal from them has arrived in the Chicago office, as Mr. Lathrop the office manager was here, from Thursday to Saturday.
