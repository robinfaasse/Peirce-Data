La Salle May 15" 1893

Professor C. S. Peirce
Milford Pa.

My dear Professor,

I received your favor
of the 6th inst in due course of
mails.

It seems that you are under the
impression that I or my secretary
had misdirected a letter to whereby
you had received it much later. [del]than[/del]
We hold this to be a mistake on
your part. My secretary has written
details on a special sheet which I
enclose.

The cordial invitation from Mrs. Peirce
and yourself to Mrs. Hegeler and
myself to visit you at your home
we cannot follow for the present.
Please express our thanks to
Mrs. Peirce.
