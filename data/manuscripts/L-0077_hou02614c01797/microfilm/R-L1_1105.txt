LaSalle [date]Jan 25 1893[/date]

E. C. Hegeler,
La Salle Illinois.

C. S. Peirce Esq
New York.

Dear Sir,

Your article for the Monist came
to hand. It is intended to publish the
same in the July number. I enclose my
check for $200.00 on account as desired.

Think it would be a help to our work
if we became nearer acquainted and invite
you to visit me here at LaSalle. I expect
to be home for some time from now.

Shall be glad to hear favorably from you.

With best regards from Dr Carus

Yours truly
Edward C Hegeler
