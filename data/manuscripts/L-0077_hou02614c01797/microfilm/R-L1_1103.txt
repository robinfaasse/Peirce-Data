E. C. HEGELER, President.
DR. PAUL CARUS, Editor.

Chicago, [date]October 19th 1892[/date]

Charles S. Peirce Esq.
Buckingham Hotel
New York, City.

Dear sir: Your letter was received today, also your telegram. Since you seem to be in a great hurry to have your bill attended to, I asked Mr Hegeler to be so kind to make out the check, which I herewith enclose.

Your 4 Open Court Articles contain about 10.400 words
= $ 262.50

your Monist Article contains about 10.000 words
= $ 250.00

You have received on account 250 Dollars, which leaves a balance of 262.50 Dollars in your favor.

Twice, when passing through New York, I tried to see you; but in vain. I am sorry having missed you. With kind regards from Mr Hegeler

Yours very truly P. Carus
