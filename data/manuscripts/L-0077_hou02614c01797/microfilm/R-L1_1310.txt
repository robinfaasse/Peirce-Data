My dear Doctor Carus:

When I came to look at
what you wanted me to sign, I
found it did not quite agree
with my understanding. I have
therefore written out my own,
which I trust will satisfy you.
If not, I beg you will [del]return[/del]
send it back to me saying how
you wish it changed.

I sent you today a long
letter that should have gone
two days ago, but I was not
equal to going out to the
road & waiting for the mail, & I
can't stand still long yet, though
I am, I think, getting better &
should soon be fit for action.
