4 Trafalgar Square Charing Cross London Eng.

and articles published by you and

for a customer of Mr Stevens who
wishes also to secure all of
the other contributions from your
pen. if you should be able to
supply these pamphlets it would
save time if you sent them at once
with your bill as the order is
final and for every thing you have done.

works for this purpose it would be

for again and we would be able to give
definite information without troubling
you
