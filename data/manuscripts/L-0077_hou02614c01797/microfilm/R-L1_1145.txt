Chicago, [date]May 23, 1893.[/date]

Chas. S. Peirce, Esq'r,
Milford, Pa.,

Dear Sir:-

Enclosed you will find a literal translation of Prof. Mach's Preface to the translation of his book. At Prof. Mach's suggestion I should also have inserted your name where the thanks to the Translator are mentioned. But knowing your opinion of the work I have thought it best to leave this to you to decide. Will you kindly return it as soon as possible?

Very truly yours,
T. J. McCormack
