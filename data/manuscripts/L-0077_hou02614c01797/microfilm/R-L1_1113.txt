[date]Seattle Feb 27. 1891[/date]
Prof C S Peirce
Milford Pa
My dear Professor,
Your letter of the 24th inst. reached me yesterday morning. In my letter of the 20th inst. I planned [del]with[/del] to acknowledge[del]ing[/del] the receipt of your letters of the 11th and 15th insts. I wrote you again on the 24th inst. on business matters.
In your letter of the 11th you say:
"Yesterday, Friday, I went to see, Mr. Willie Appleton, and also Mr. Vail the head of the Editorial Department of the Book Company, or school-book publishers trust.
