I think I could write an acceptable article or even two for the Open Court on the following materials

1. Personal experience has a [del]real[/del] positive value always. This is greater the more unusual the experience. That which I have to report seems [del]to be[/del] worth mention.

(A.) Of late years I have suffered extreme adversity & affliction, being (B.) In the somewhat [del]unusual[/del] situation of a student of philosophy, laboratory-bred, who holds on [del]not[/del] essentially to the creed & communion of the church.

(C.) Now, the facts which seem worth reporting are [ins]1[u]st[/u],[/ins] what kind of reflections I have found really consoling, [ins]2[u]nd[/u],[/ins] how the different literary works addressed to these in such circumstances sound.

2. Of course, it is nothing but the experience of a single individual; still, it is out of individual experiences that general experience is built.
