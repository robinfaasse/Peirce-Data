**The Monist,**
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT,
LA SALLE, ILL.

December 11, 1896.

Charles S. Peirce, Esq'r,
84 Broad St.,
New York City, N. Y.,

My Dear Sir:-

Your letter of December 8th was duly received, and I spoke with Mr. Hegeler concerning your proposition. He read your letter and told me that he was not inclined to venture into the enterprise. This afternoon we received your telegram which O. K's the diagrams of your article and gives us authority to go ahead with the printing of your article.

Hoping that you will be prosperous in your business undertakings, I remain, with kind regards,

Yours very truly,

P. Carus
