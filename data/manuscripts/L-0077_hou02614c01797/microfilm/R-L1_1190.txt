The Monist,
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT,
LA SALLE, ILL.

December 9, 1896.

Charles S. Peirce, Esq'r,

64 Broad St.,

New York City, N. Y.,

Dear Sir:-

Galleys 2 to 6 inclusive of your article were received to-day. The new drawings of the graphs which you sent differ from those in the copy. If in any way possible, please approve the drawings which you find in the last batch of galleys sent to you. It takes several days to send copy to Chicago and have it returned with the cuts. All the drawings sent to you had to be made in Chicago by a draftsman, photographed, and electrotyped at considerable expense. The Monist is quite late already, and even if we were inclined to make the changes we should not have time. [ins]unless really important.[/ins]

Yours very truly,

The Monist
