My dear Carus:

I am moved to write to you primarily to ask a question (if you are at liberty to answer it) prompted by the October number of The Monist, which I have just received & have not fully examined. I turned first to Borinski's review of "Der vorchristliche Jesus," because I have a great admiration for the reasoning of W. B. Smith both in that book and still more in his dealing with MSS, F and G. But what struck me specially was the [u]life[/u] of that translation (as I take it to be) of parts of Borinski's review. It strikes me as extraordinarily good, although I have not seen the original. For that reason I have a curiosity to know who did it.
