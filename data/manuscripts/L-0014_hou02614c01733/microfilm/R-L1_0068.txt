Milford Pa [date]1901 May 17[/date]

Prof. Albert Bushnell Hart
Cambridge Mass

Dear Sir

The Am. Hist. Rev. of which you are an editor is the best worth reading of any review [del]I see[/del]. It relates to a science which, I should suppose, would interest more people than any other. There are many rich historians in the country who are disgraced if the Review is disgraced.

The Review offers $3 per thousand words for book notices & then either does not pay at all or only after the most extraordinary dunning
