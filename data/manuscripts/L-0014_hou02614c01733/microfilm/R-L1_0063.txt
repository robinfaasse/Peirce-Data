The American Historical Review.

J. FRANKLIN JAMESON,
MANAGING EDITOR,
196 BOWEN ST., PROVIDENCE, R. I.

December 10, 1897.

Professor Charles S. Peirce:

My dear Sir:

I am glad to know that we may have your kind services in the review of the edition of Bacon's Opus Majus. No arrangement was made for its notice in our January number. As it goes over to the next number, I will not be rigid about the extent of space. Let it be twice as great, 800 words, if you like. I shall see that the book reaches you in two or three days at the address you have given, and shall wish to have the review, if you please, by the 10th of February. The usual circular to reviewers is enclosed.

Very truly yours,

JFJameson.
