Milford Pa [date]1901 May 17[/date]

Sir:

The Am. Hist. Rev. of which you are an editor is the best worth reading of any review I see. It relates to a science which, I should suppose, must interest more people than any other. There are many rich historians in the country each of whom bears a share of any disgrace falling on the Review.
