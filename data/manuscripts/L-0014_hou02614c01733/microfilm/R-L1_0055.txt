Cosmos Club,
Washington, D. C.,
[date]May 6, 1896[/date].

Professor Charles S. Peirce:

My dear Sir:

I am very much obliged to you for undertak-
ing to review the book and will have it sent to you at once. Our
page of review matter contains about 570 words. I send herewith
a circular regarding reviews; attention is particularly called to
the paragraph respecting the limits of length.

With many thanks, I am,

Very truly yours,

J. F. Jameson.
