Now let me remark, — not at
all by way of excusing myself,
for I am much to blame, — but
by way of instructing you, that
when you offer a man three
dollars a thousand, you make
him look upon the work, not as
he would a scientific contribution,
but as something he is employed
to do; and yet it clearly can-
not pay him, say to go to New
York to the Astor or Columbia
library to brush up his knowledge.
It is just enough to tempt him
to write something that he regrets.

You historians are all rolling in wealth, up to
your necks in gold, Bancroft with [del]not[/del] [ins]near[/ins] a million
ruins. Lodge is that over & over. So you all
see no doubts. If you want really first rate
notices I should think that multiplying the
honorarium say by 8 would help.

However, I repeat that nobody but me is to
blame in this business. I much regret it, especially
as the exactness of part of what I have said will
detract from the weight of the rest.

very repentantly
C. S. Peirce
