Prof. Geo: B. Adams
New Haven, Conn

Dear Sir:

You are one of the editors of the Am. Hist. Rev. It is the best worth reading of any review I see. It relates to a science in which I should suppose more people must be interested than any other. Moreover there is a large number of rich historians in the country whose duty it is to prevent the Review from failing in its contracts.
