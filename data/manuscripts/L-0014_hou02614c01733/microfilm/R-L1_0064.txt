My dear Professor Jameson:

Since writing my notice
of Harriott I have become
somewhat dissatisfied with it.

There is no doubt that Wallis
overpraised him in the most
unconscionable manner; and
several things which I have
referred to as generally ascribed
to him are no more his discoveries
than they are yours or mine.

it go. I simply state the maximum
