[u]The American Historical Review.[/u]

**J. FRANKLIN JAMESON,**
**MANAGING EDITOR,**
**196 BOWEN ST., PROVIDENCE, R. I.**

April 30, 189[del]6[/del][ins]6[/ins]

Professor Charles S. Peirce:

My dear Sir:

I hope you feel an interest in the infant American Historical Review, and I know that you feel a great interest in the history of Science. Therefore I take the liberty to ask you if we may not have a little aid from you in the preparation of our October number. Could you undertake to write for us a review of President White's Warfare of Science? I should be glad to make it one of our longer reviews (say 2,000 words) and I should not need to receive the manuscript until the 1st of September. I should be exceedingly gratified if you would undertake to do us this service. Our payment for reviews is at the rate of $3.00 per page. The book would be sent you. Please address me at The Cosmos Club, Washington, D.C.

Believe me,

Very respectfully yours,

JFJameson
