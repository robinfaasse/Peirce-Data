The next simplest of the [ins]universal[/ins] negative syllogisms [del]is[/del] (for there is no need of considering particular syllogisms) is Camestres, which runs thus
Any M is P;
Any S is not P:
$\therefore$ Any S is not M.

Substitute [del]for is not[/del] injures for is not, and we get
[del]Any[/del] [ins]Every[/ins] M is a P;
[del]Any[/del] [ins]Every[/ins] S injures every P:
$\therefore$ Every S injures every M.

This obviously holds, because the injury is to every one of the [del]class[/del] forming the correlate. It would not be correct to reason
Every M is a P;
Every S injures a P;
$\therefore$ Every S injures an M.

We see then that the [del]principal[/del] [ins]al[/ins] condition of the validity of Camestres is that not means not any and not not some. In logical lingo, negative predicates are distributed. But the condition that the copula expresses a sib-relation is also involved.
