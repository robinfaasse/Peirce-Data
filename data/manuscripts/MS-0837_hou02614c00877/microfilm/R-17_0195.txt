In short, an induction may be said to be the inference
of the major proposition of a syllogism of the 1[ins]st[/ins] or 3[ins]rd[/ins] figure.
Their follow would be its moods

$A, B, C$ etc are [del]selected as[/del] [ins]being[/ins] $Y$
$A, B, C$ etc are found to be $X$ (or not to be $X$)
$\therefore$ All $Y$ are $X$ (or No $Y$ are $X$)

$A, B, C$ etc are selected as belonging to a genus of which $Y$ is a species
Among $A, B, C$ etc are found individuals having the character $X$ [ins](or non-$X$)[/ins]
$\therefore$ All $Y$ are $X$ (or no $Y$ are $X$)
