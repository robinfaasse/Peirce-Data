As for those who are engaged in systematic
psychical research, they should receive every
encouragement. They seem to me to have
reached little or no result, so far; and I
do not believe they will, until they dismiss the
telepathic hypothesis from their minds. But
they must [del]go on[/del] develop their own methods,
they are scientific men, working in something
like scientific ways, and they must ultimately reach
scientific results. Two generations hence the
wonders of psychology will occupy the popular
imagination as the wonders of electricity do now.
