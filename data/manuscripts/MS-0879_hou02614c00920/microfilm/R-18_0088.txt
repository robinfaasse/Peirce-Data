If it be urged that neither common sense nor
good sense can be made to favor the doctrine
the consciousness is an accident or "ulti-
mate property" of nerve-matter, I willingly grant
that. Sooner than make mind a modification
of-matter, I would make matter a modification
of mind. But no [del]such[/del] speculations about the
connection of body and soul can shake the
proposition of the dependence of feeling and action
upon the nerve-matter in which it resides; for this
stands solidly upon universal experience.
