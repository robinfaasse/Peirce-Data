[u]Logic of Quantity[/u]

In order to answer that question let us state that [del]an ass[/del] [ins]a collection[/ins] is in no semicorrespondence to some other, and see whether it be possible to eliminate all reference to that other. Let [u]m[/u] be the collection of which the statement is made. Then, the statement is

(9) $\Sigma_n \Pi_i \Sigma_e \Sigma_f \Sigma_g \Sigma_s \Pi_r \text{v}_{ief} \cdot \text{v}_{ieg} \cdot \text{r}_{fg} \cdot \text{q}_{ns} \cdot (\bar{\text{q}}_{mr} + \bar{\text{r}}_{irs})$.

Since [u]n[/u] only occurs once in this statement we lose nothing of the assertion in simply dropping it. In fact, when we consider that [u]n[/u] might be {$\infty$}, the character of existence, we see that the same thing is asserted when $\text{q}_{ns}$ is dropped.
