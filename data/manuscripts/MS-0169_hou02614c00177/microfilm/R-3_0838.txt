We count round and round, as the figure shows. The 8th day is the same as the 1st day, if we do not care what week it may be in, but only want to know the day of the week; and 9th day will be the 2nd day. Therefore, the 5th day after the 4th day will be the 9th day, which is the 2nd day, or Monday.
