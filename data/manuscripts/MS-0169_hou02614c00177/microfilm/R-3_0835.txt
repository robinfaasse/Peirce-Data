The process of counting consists in successively starting to pronounce single numerals of the series, while marking, or noting, single objects of the lot to be counted. The count ought to begin by carefully observing that none of the lot are already marked or placed as if counted; and it is proper in doing this to begin to pronounce a [ins]preliminary[/ins] numeral equivalent to "None". It is somewhat simpler to regard the series of numerals as beginning with one to be pronounced before [del]to be[/del] marking any object, because the number [ins]denoted by[/ins] [del]will, in general, be[/del] the last numeral used in counting.
