11[/pn] draws attention at once to the singleness and to the plurality.

The word 'object' means that of which, on any occasion, one speaks or thinks. Accordingly, if I say, or even think that, from where I sit, a star, a caterpillar, and a blot of ink can be seen, the star, the caterpillar, and the blot of ink forthwith make up a single object.
