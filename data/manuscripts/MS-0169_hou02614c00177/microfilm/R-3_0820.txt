[del]I shall[/del] [ins]I allow myself, however,[/ins] in this statement, the use of one
abridged expression, because it will render the statement
decidedly clearer and easier to comprehend. Namely, I
shall speak of one numeral, call it N, "supposing" another,
which we may call M, which will be an abbreviated form
of words employed in place of saying that "the use of N,
in counting, supposes the use of M"; that is to say, there
could not possibly be a count in which N should
be used but M not be used, for the reason that N
comes later in the series than M, and therefore, in counting,
is not used until after M has been used. But the best expla-
nation of my phrase "N supposes M" is that it means [del]that[/del],
"take whatever [ins]and[/ins] possible count you may, if N is used in it, so is M."
