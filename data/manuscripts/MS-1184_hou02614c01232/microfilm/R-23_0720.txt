A certain class of words may be called Brachystic.
It embraces interjections, conjunctions, prepositions, the
most frequently used particles, pronominal words, auxili-
ary verbs, and articles. No consonants are doubled in
these words; as alas!, if, as, of, yes, us, his, this, thus,
is, was, has, an.
