3. Words [del]recognized[/del] [ins]regarded[/ins] as compound or formed by the loose suffix -ship or the loose prefixes fore-, [del]mis-[/del] [ins]re-,[/ins] un- negative, un- reversing, follow the spelling of their components. Examples are instil, pellmell, zigzag with zigzagging, periwig with periwigged, humbug with humbugging, kidnap with kidnapper; in -ship, friendship, worshipper; in fore-, forestall, foretell; in mis-, miscall, misfall, misspell, mistell, misworshipper; in re-, recall, retell; in un- negative, unwell, unbiased*; in un- reversing, unroll, unspell, unwell. Of course such words as rebel, repel, instil, are not compounds, since there are no such words as bell, pell, still (verb).

* Some editors spell biased; but biassed is both more usual, and more regular.
