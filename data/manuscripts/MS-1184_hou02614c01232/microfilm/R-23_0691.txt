Moreover, æ and œ are now unnecessary, used in a few
words rather irregularly formed from the Greek, in which no
such diphthongs occur, though they are not used in words
from the Latin, in which they do occur. Thus, we write
encyclopædia instead of encyclopædia, although we write
pedobaptism; and we write œcumenical for ecumenical,
although we write economy.
