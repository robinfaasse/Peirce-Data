[u]-est[/u] and [u]-eth[/u] seldom occur in modern English, but
no doubt should follow the rule for [u]-ing[/u].

[u]-ed[/u] is often used where the verb without this termi-

supposes a verb, as in dishevelled, jewelled, rowelled,
postilled, devilled, trellissed (better than trellised),
unbiassed (better than unbiased), addressed (also
caddised and caddices), etc.
