3. Words recognized as compound follow the spelling
of their components; and for the purpose of this rule,
words beginning with the negative prefix [u]un-[/u] and
with the reversing prefix [u]un-[/u] are regarded as compound.
But words beginning with [u]un-[/u] from Old Low German [u]und[/u]
are not so regarded; as [u]until[/u]. Examples are [u]unwell[/u],
[u]unroll[/u], [u]unspell[/u], [u]unwill[/u]. [u]Unbiassed[/u] is always spelled with
[u]ss[/u], although some editors spell [u]biased[/u]; but [u]biassed[/u] is
more usual and regular.
