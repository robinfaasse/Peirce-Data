3. Words regarded as compounds or formed by
attaching the loose suffix -ship or the loose prefixes
[u]re-[/u], [u]un-[/u] negative, [u]un-[/u] reversive, to existing English
words follow the spelling of their components; except
that words compounds [ins]well (add)[/ins] of [u]all[/u], [u]full[/u], [u]mass[/u], [ins]miss[/ins], [del]if[/del]
they are felt to be closely enough united to be written
without a hyphen, drop or double the last consonant
of these words; and if three consonants of the same kind
come together [ins]without a hyphen[/ins] one is dropped. Exceptions are chilblain,
[del]welld[/del] pastime, fulfil, well-being, [ins]farewell[/ins] shepherd, wherever,
whosoever, wherefore, therefore.
