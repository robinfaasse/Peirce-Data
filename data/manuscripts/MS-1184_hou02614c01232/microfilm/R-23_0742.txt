[u]G[/u]e is used instead for [del]double[/del] [ins]d[/ins]ge. Hence we write badge, edge,
hedge, ledge, sledge, pledge, knowledge, dredge, sedge, wedge, midge,

ridge, bridge, porridge, cartridge, partridge, dodge, lodge, budge,
