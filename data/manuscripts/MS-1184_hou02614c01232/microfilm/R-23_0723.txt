The following rules for the doubling of consonants
conform to the best present usage and are much
simpler than those usually given:

1. Words recognized as compound follow the spell-
ing of their components. For example, kidnapped doubles
the p, being recognized as kid-napped. So l is doubled
in miscall, forestall, recall, install, foretell, pell-mell
