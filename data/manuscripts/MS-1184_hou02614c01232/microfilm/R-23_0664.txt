The following rules for the doubling of consonants conform
to the best present usage and are decided[ins]ly[/ins] simpler and
more complete than any code hitherto drawn up. In
[del]the[/del] case two rules conflict the one first given takes the
precedence.

retain their spelling, or regular transliteration. Thus, from
