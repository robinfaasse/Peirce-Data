6. When a word ending in a final consonant immediately
following a single vowel receives a termination or suffix
beginning with a vowel,

i) if that consonant is [u]s[/u], it is doubled, and if it is [u]c[/u], it
is virtually doubled by taking a [u]k[/u] after it;

ii) if the consonant is [u]l[/u], and the word without the
termination is a verb, the [u]l[/u] is doubled, but if the word is
a noun or adjective the [del]and[/del] [ins][u]l[/u][/ins] remains simple; so that
before the terminations [u]est[/u] (2[sup]nd[/sup] person singular), [u]eth[/u], [u]ing[/u], [u]ed[/u],
[u]er[/u] (agent or instrument), [u]es[/u], [u]able[/u], [u]ous[/u], the [u]l[/u] is doubled
before [u]ish[/u], [u]ize[/u], [u]ist[/u], [u]ism[/u], [u]ity[/u], [u]ic[/u], [u]ian[/u], [u]ian[/u], [u]ine[/u], [u]iferous[/u]
it is not doubled, and before [u]um[/u], [u]ous[/u], [u]ary[/u], [u]ery[/u], [u]ate[/u]
[u]ation[/u], it may or may not be doubled;

iii) if the consonant is other than [u]s[/u], [u]c[/u], [del]nor[/del] [ins]neither[/ins] [u]l[/u], it is doubled
if the augmented word is accented on the vowel preceding the
consonant, but not otherwise.
