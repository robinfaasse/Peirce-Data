The law of ancestral heredity, a law originally put forth by Francis Galton and proved by him in certain cases, [ins]divergence from the general[/ins] to the effect that which any individual inherits is derived one half from its parents, one fourth from its grandparents and [del]more than[/del] [ins]in general[/ins] [del]one half the[/del] [del]ascent from any generation of ancestors is compared with[/del] [ins]inheritance from[/ins] that the [del]difference of[/del] any generation of ancestors divided by the mean amount of the inherited character that those ancestors possess is one half the [del]independent[/del] inheritance divided by from the next following generation divided by the mean amount of the character which this generation possesses. This is the law as originally formulated; but though it sounds very precise, further definition is required in most cases.
