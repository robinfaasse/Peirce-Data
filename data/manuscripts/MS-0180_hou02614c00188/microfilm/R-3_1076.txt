The drill is to be carried so far that given
any number under ten, the pupil immediately
proceeds from that with perfect fluency, adding
successive 1s, 2s, 3s, 4s, 5s, 6s, 7s, 8s, 9s, 10s,
etc. up to 101. This drill is the foundation of
all facility in arithmetic. Competition & prizes.
