June 28", 1911.

Mr. Charles S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir;-

Will you kindly advise me if the following articles which appeared in The Monist, 1892-3, have ever been published in book form, viz:-

"The Architecture of Theories";

"The Doctrine of Necessity Examined",

"Reply to Necessitarians".

Yours Truly,

Warren Bigler
