[fig]circular diagram with crosshairs[/fig] New York, [date]November 28th, 1893[/date].


Prof. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

My Dear Sir:-
I make this tardy acknowledgement of the telegram
which came to the house in my absence upon Election Day. I wish I
could have met you, as you suggested. If you will kindly state
when you are to be in town, I will be glad to make an appointment
with you for dinner or otherwise, that we may have another inter-
change of views relative to Gilbert, etc.

Very sincerely yours,
[fig]sketch of a sailboat[/fig]
[u]R. J. Mettelar[/u]
