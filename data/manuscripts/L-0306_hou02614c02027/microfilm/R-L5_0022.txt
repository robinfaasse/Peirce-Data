[marginalia][date]7/27/92[/date][/marginalia]

My dear Mr Peirce,

Your letter, covering the
MS. came to hand yesterday, upon
my return from a final visit to
my mother on Long Island.

I thank you very much for
its contents and I shall at
once begin the translation, with
the view of finishing as much of
it as I can while on shipboard.

It is my intention sailing next
Saturday, & while abroad, I shall
endeavor to get photo-litho's of
