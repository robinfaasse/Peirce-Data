My dear Mr Peirce

Thanks for yr postal with the desired information concerning De Vinne, &c.

You wrote that it would not be much trouble for you to finish the translation, if desired, and that in writing it out many other remarks (Notes) would no doubt occur to you. I wish I could translate it as readily and do justice to your notes — no 61, page 11, in particular.

For above reason, I send you herewith, as requested, and duly prepaid, all your papers — in even better condition than sent me some time ago. I have carefully mended some & have numbered all pp. in pencil.

I enclose notice of Santayana's work: it may interest you.

Let me hear from you soon, that I may act upon your suggestion re Century Co &c & believe me

truly yours
P. Amstutz
