Dear

I trust this last effort of
mine to hear from you, after so long a delay, will
prove successful, and that it will not turn out
you have been too ill to write. If so, my hopes are
naturally for an early and permanent recovery.
No need then to bother about looking over my sheets.
Send them back, please, so that I may at once
give them to publisher for examination. I enclose
stamps for the purpose.

When you are again dans votre assiette
ordinaire, drop me line or two giving particulars, and,
meanwhile, believe me truly yours

P. F. Mottelay

276 W. 152d St
New York Oct 11th [date]Oct 11th[/date]
