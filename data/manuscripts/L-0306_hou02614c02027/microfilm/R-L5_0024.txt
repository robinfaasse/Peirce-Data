C. S. Peirce, Esq,

Dear Sir,

I send you, as promised, (by Express, prepaid) a copy of Gilbert & your MSS re Purpureus — in accordance with my recent letter to you acknowledging yours of 25.

In further reply to the latter, permit me [del]to say[/del] [ins]to[/ins] assure you that you are surely in error when stating that I failed to acknowledge receipt of the Ms. "Atmos. etc". in due course, like all the rest of my mail matter, before my departure for Europe, in a note asking you for [del]the promised[/del] information re the Vt. letters [del]which I never received[/del].

What I have of Purpureus is, I regret to say, not in a shape to be readily availed of, consisting as it does of references attached to the Continuation of my Chol. History, which I am now assiduously at work upon. You are, however, welcome to it.
