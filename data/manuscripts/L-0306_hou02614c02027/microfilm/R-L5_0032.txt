New York, [date]January 15th, 1894[/date].
2337 Seventh Avenue.


Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

My Dear Mr. Peirce:-
Your letter of the 11th inst. reached me to-day with prospectuses of your Peregrinus and Philosophy, both of which I have perused with much interest.

I appreciate your kindness putting me down as Honorary Subscriber to the Peregrinus. Thanks.

Your philosophy, I observe, is to be in twelve volumes. Its price, $2.50 per volume, places it way beyond my reach, I regret to say, for I would much like to have it. I will bear it in mind for a little later period, when I may feel better able to draw a check therefor.

I regret exceedingly that you are so far advanced with the translation, whilst my collection here consists mainly of notes. I would like very much to have been able to supplement your own, and if it is yet time and convenient to send me advanced sheets, pray, do so. I will then look them over without delay, making such suggestions as I think advisable, and which may possibly help you in the end.
