New York, [date]October 12th, 1893[/date].

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:-
Pardon this forced tardy acknowledgement of your two late favors.

I am extremely obliged to you for calling my attention to the many shortcomings in my translation of Gilbert. I have carefully looked over all your notes, and while, thanking you for the spirit that prompted them, am very anxious to discuss them with you, whenever you come to the City. Your Ptolemy extract especially claims my best thanks, as the book is not now for me within easy reach. Some errors throughout the book, which I am heartily ashamed of, I need hardly add, are the result of the extraordinary haste to which the publishers suddenly subjected me just as the long promised English translation began to be feared.

I shall have a fight with you on the [u]virtus convertens[/u], [u]coitio[/u], etc. [u]Coitio[/u] is explained fully at page 97 (Chapter III).
