New York, [date]February 7th, 1894[/date].
2337 Seventh Avenue.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

My Dear Mr. Peirce:-
I reply to your last letter by saying, that I feel disposed to contribute a memoir in the manner you suggest, and I will give it my attention at the very earliest moment. It would be in my line, as you say, to work on the "History of Science" and I hope that not only will your Philosophy succeed as it promises, but that your next ship will come in long before the last volume has appeared.

The only thing that delays my trip to Europe is, the quibus. When I do go over, I will attend to the photographing alluded to, and I trust I will be "flush" enough to have photographs made, besides, of the attractive title pages of Taisnier, as was my intention when last at the British Museum, and when I intended publishing the Peregrinus with the Gilbert.

Can I not be allowed to see your review of my volume; do I need a word from you to the Editor of the "Nation" for that purpose; if so, kindly send it to me. I will read it hurriedly, and since it has not been published, may make some suggestions that may be of value to me hereafter.

Meanwhile believe me as ever,
Very faithfully yours, [fig]signature of G. T. Mottelay[/fig]
