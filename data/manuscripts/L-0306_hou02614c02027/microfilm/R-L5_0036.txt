In answer to your inquiry regarding the 1558, I will say, that the English Institution of Electrical Engineers on Victoria Street, London, has a copy of the 1558 Peregrinus and two copies of the Taisnier 1562. The British Museum has the 1558 Peregrinus and the Taisnier of 1562 and 1579. This, I find on my library [ins]"[/ins]notes[ins]"[/ins] made at the Museum. At Caen, I think it was, I came across Taisnier of 1559 and 1560. It is now my purpose running across the water next month or early in March. Even if I were not to hear from you in the meantime, which is of course doubtful, I would extend my [ins]"[/ins]notes[ins]"[/ins] or rather make a new research expressly for the purpose of adding, if possible, to the work you have already done. Goodness knows, I feel there is enough to gather to make up a good sized octavo[ins]![/ins]

I will write you further in a little while, and duly advise you of my prospective movements as well as of my visit to the city.

Wishing you to believe me as ever, I remain,

Very sincerely yours,

P. F. Mottelay

(Dictated.)
