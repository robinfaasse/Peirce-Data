to stop trying. But if when he is asked for security he replies
"Sir, I postulate this loan; for if I [ins]not[/ins] get it, I can prove

considered as security, at all. Yet this parallels the course of
those philosophers who try to prove the truth of natural beliefs
by showing that they are "presuppositions" of our indispensable acts.
