This, as well as singular, ought to be understood as applying to

<u>Identical</u>. 'Identical' in this sense is probably quite recent.
The "identical propositions" discussed by older logicians were those
in which subject and predicate expressed precisely the same thing.
See commentaries on I Post. anal. iii.
