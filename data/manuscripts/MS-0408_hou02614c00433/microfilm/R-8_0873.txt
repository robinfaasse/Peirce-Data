In regard to the [u]De Consolatione[/u], there are several rejoinders to
make. First, if Boetius was a Christian, he was an orthodox
Christian; and he may have thought it imprudent for him
in prison at that juncture to take a tone hostile to Arians,
which he could not have sincerely avoided if he had not
kept his Christianity in the background. Second, Boetius
may have been a decided Christian in his youth and may
subsequently have become disgusted with Christians and Christi-
anity. Third, the [u]De Consolatione[/u] may have been written by
