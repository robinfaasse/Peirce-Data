The University of Paris was the most important throughout the
period of scholasticism; and during the most flourishing
times, its importance quite out-weighed all the others put together.
There, Theology was the great subject; though faculties of
law and of medicine also existed. But law was better
studied in Italy, medicine in Spain. Before entering these
professional schools, the student took his degree in
arts; and in the faculty of arts, logic was the leading
subject.
