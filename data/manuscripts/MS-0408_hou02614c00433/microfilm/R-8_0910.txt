[u]Copula[/u]. This word first occurs in the [u]Dialectica[/u] of [u]Abaelard[/u]. He speaks of
"praedicatum et subiectum atque ipsorum [u]copula[/u], secundum hoc scilicet, quod
verbum a praedicato seorsum per se accepimus." The fact that Abaelard
in an advanced treatise like his [u]Dialectica[/u] is forced to explain the mean-
ing of the word [u]copula[/u] rather favors the idea that he himself was the
inventor of it. Prantl's notion that he borrowed it from some imaginary
Greek source is quite unfounded, and almost ridiculous. Prantl has
himself pointed out a passage in Boethius which would give all the
hint required, if not too much.
