By an [u]Area[/u] is meant so much of the exposed surface as lies wholly within and without the same identical Cuts. The Area within a Cut is called "the Area of" that Cut, while the Area in which the Cut has been made is called "the Place of" that Cut.

An Area that is enclosed in an even number of Cuts, or in none at all, is said to be "evenly enclosed." Any other is "oddly enclosed", since no Cut is allowed to intersect another.
