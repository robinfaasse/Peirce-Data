By an [u]Area[/u] is meant so much of the exposed surface as lies wholly
within and without the same identical Cuts. The Area within
a Cut is called "[u]the Area of[/u]" that Cut, while the Area in which
the Cut has been made is called "[u]the Place of[/u]" that Cut.

An Area that is enclosed in an even number of Cuts, or in none
at all, is said to be "evenly enclosed." Any other is "oddly enclosed", since
no Cut is allowed to intersect another.
