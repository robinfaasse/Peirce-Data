of subjects, [ins]called the correlates.[/ins] A [u]proper relation[/u] is a relation whose
subsistence involves a common mode of being
among the correlates. An indirect, [del]proper[/del] rela-
tion, as opposed to a [u]directly proper relation[/u], is a
proper relation which consists in improper re-
lations.
