§2. In order to avoid the danger of being misled by
associations of words, let us take an otherwise mean-
ingless vocable as the designation of what is supposed in
§1. I propose, then, to term anything which is such as it is
entirely regardless of anything else, a [u]swa[/u].

§3. A [u]swa[/u] is something definite and positive so far
as it is.
For it is not relative to anything else.

§4. A [u]swa[/u] is nothing but what it is of its very nature.
For all that it is, it is in itself.

§5. A [u]swa[/u] cannot involve an contradiction.
This has already been shown.

§6. A [u]swa[/u] cannot be affected or modified by
anything.
For it is such as it is in itself.

§7. A [u]swa[/u] cannot have any individual identity.
For individual identity consists in continuing the
same under all circumstances, which are other things.
