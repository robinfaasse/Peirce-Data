[pn]Two swas.[/pn]

§11. Any swa is immutable.

For if a swa were subject to change, its being could be determined by something other than that wherein its being consists. But a swa is such as it is regardless of anything else there may be than that which constitutes its being. Consequently, though facts may be connected with swas, and indeed must be so if swas are to be assistant to the universe, yet they cannot affect swas in the being of the latter.
