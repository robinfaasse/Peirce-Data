Additional Note on the Method of Coincidences.
By C. S. Peirce.

The old method of coincidences employed a single disk fixed to the clock pendulum. Let [ins]the[/ins] abscissas [ins]of rectangular coordinates[/ins] represent distances to the right or left and let ordinates represent velocities. Then a [del]motion[/del] uniform circular motion having its centre on the axis of abscissa will represent the motion of any point on either pendulum.
