[marginalia]November 22, 1916
VB[/marginalia]

[fig]Coat of arms of Parker House[/fig]
Parker House,
Boston.

Prof. C. S. Peirce,
The Commonwealth,
Boston, Mass.

Dear Sir:-
At the request of Dr. A. Graham Bell, I enclose a copy of his paper read before the National Academy of Sciences yesterday afternoon.
Dr. Bell would be very much obliged if you will kindly return it at your convenience to his residence, 1331 Connecticut Avenue, Washington, D.C.

Very truly Yours,
Charles R. Cox
Secretary.
