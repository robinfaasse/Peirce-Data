Milford Pa [date]1908 March 25[/date]

My dear Colleague:

I do not remember that you have ever met Mrs. Peirce & beg, then, to recommend her to your favour. For she is in Washington at this moment, and writes asking me to give her an introduction to you. I do not know why she desires this; but I have some guess; and I need not say that anything you may do for her will be ten times as much done for me. It is rather a ridiculous thing for a husband to say, [marginalia] very truly
C. S.
