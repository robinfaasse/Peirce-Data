[u]The BOOKMAN[/u]
AN ILLUSTRATED JOURNAL
OF LITERATURE AND LIFE
"I am a Bookman." — JAMES RUSSELL LOWELL

DODD, MEAD & COMPANY, Publishers
372 FIFTH AVENUE, Corner 35th St.

New York. April 18, 1900.

Professor Charles S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

My dear Sir:-

Mr. Crowninshield has referred us to you as one thoroughly qualified and wishing to review books. We forward to-day, under another cover, [u]The Spiritual Life[/u] and [u]Introduction to Ethics[/u], for that purpose. We should like a BOOKMAN'S Table treating them both of about one thousand words in length, although if you think it necessary there would be no objection to your using a little more space. We shall pay you $10.00 for this review, and a cheque will be sent to you on the day of publication.

Yours very truly,
The Editors of the Bookman
Arthur B. Maurice
Junior Editor

H.
