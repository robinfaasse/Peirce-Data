I believe it is a current opinion that we are aware
of our own existence from the very moment of birth
or earlier; though Kant seems to think we do not
realize the fact till we are three or four years old.

As nobody now supposes that there are actually any
innate ideas and especially as the existence of any
individual mind is a contingent fact, it must
be granted that the knowledge of it comes by ex-
perience. But many philosophers hold that any
thought at once informs us that we exist. To know
