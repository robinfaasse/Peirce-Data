[u]Multiplication[/u]

Again "inductive exercises" and an insufficient drill.

A circle with numbers arranged 1. 7. 3. 11. 5. 2. 6. 4. 10. 12. 8. 9. There is no sense at all in this. Were the arrangement 1. 7. 10. 5. 9. 11. 12. 6. 3. 8. 4. 2, there would be a glimmer of sense in it. Or 1. 6. 10. 8. 9. 2. 12 7. 3. 5. 4. 11.
