[del][u]Franklin Written Arithmetic[/u][/del]

By [del]E. P. Seaver & Walton[/del]
I knew Seaver well. Was a good teacher.

Very practical, common-sense affair.

Decimals introduced at once  Good!!

Definitions concrete & good.

No adequate drill in the elements. No real computation
taught. This surprises me.

A lot of out of the way things are put into
an appendix.

A good book. Quite a model in
some respects. In others easily distanced.
