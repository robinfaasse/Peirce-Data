In order to form the equation of the rosette, we need three equations to determine $a$, $b$, and $c$ in this equation
$$\frac{x_i^2}{a} + \frac{y_i^2}{b} + \frac{z_i^2}{c} = 1$$

Of these the first expresses the fact that the rosette passes through the point of the helix to which it belongs. That is
$$\frac{x_i^2}{a} + \frac{y_i^2}{b} + \frac{z_i^2}{c} = 1$$

The second is that it passes through the axis at the same value of $z_i$. That is
$$\frac{z_i^2}{c} = 1$$

The third equation expresses the leaning back of the rosette
