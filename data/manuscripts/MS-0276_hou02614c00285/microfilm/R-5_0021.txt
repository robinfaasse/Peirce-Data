The only things that it concerns
us to know are how to conduct
ourselves on future occasions

Now our knowledge multiplies
so that if [del]at[/del] at a given time
$t = 0$ its value is $v$, then at
the end of time $t$ it is $v a^t$ where
$a$ is a constant; and if by an
unusual effort at the end of
$t_1$ we can make its value to be
$v b^{t_1}$ where $b > a$ then at the
end of time $t$ its value will be
$v b^{t_1} a^{t - t_1} = v (\frac{b}{a})^{t_1} a^t$ But if we can anyhow at the end of
a still shorter time $t_2$ we
can make our knowledge
equal in value to $v (\frac{b}{a})^{t_1}$
Then we have $(\frac{b}{a})^{t_1} = (\frac{c}{a})^{t_2}$
and $(\frac{b}{a})^{t_2} a^t = (\frac{b}{a})^{t_1} a^{t - t_2}$

$c = (\frac{b}{a})^{\frac{t_1 - t_2}{[del]t_2[/del]}}$

$c = a (\frac{b}{a})^{t_1 - t_2}$
$= a^{1 + t_2 - t_1} b^{t_1}$
$b^{t_1} = a^{t'}$

[del]Then we have $t' < t_1$[/del]
