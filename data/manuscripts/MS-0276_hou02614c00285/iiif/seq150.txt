In order to form the equation
of the rosette, we need three equations
to determine a, b, and c in that equation
$$\frac{x_i^2}{a} + \frac{y_i^2}{b} + \frac{z_i^2}{c} = 1$$
Of the the first expresses the fact
that the rosette passes through
the points of the helix to which it
belongs. That is
$$\frac{x_i^2}{a} + \frac{y_i^2}{b} + \frac{z_i^2}{c} = 1$$
The second is that it passes
through the axis at the same
value of $z_i$. That is
$$\frac{z_o^2}{c} = 1$$
The third equation expresses
the leaning back of the rosette
