THE AXIOMS OF INTUITION.

AFTER KANT.

All Intuitions are Extensive Quantities.

An extensive quantity is that wherein the representation of parts renders possible (and therefore necessarily antecedes in the synthesis) the representation of the whole.

[u]Axiom I.[/u]

Space has three dimensions.

[u]Proof.[/u] Space is the form of the external sense. Our knowledge of external things can only be of qualities; and since all knowledge is discrimination I can only know them by their difference of quality. And this difference of quality must exist at each moment of time. It cannot be a mere difference in quantity, because different quantities differing also in quality must be observable at [del]different[/del] [ins]the same[/ins] times.
