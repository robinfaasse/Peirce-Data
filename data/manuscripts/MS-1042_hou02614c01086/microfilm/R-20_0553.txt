[pn]6[/pn] Moreover, it is found that every atom possesses a definite
valency, which is the maximum number of other atoms
with which it may be combined in chemical equilibrium.
H, F, Na, K etc have valency 1, or are univalent; Ca, Mg, Cu,
etc have valency 2, or are bivalent; B, Al, etc have valency 3,
or are trivalent; C, [del]Si,[/del] [ins]Si,[/ins] Sn, Pb, etc have valency 4, or are quadrivalent;
N, P, As, etc. have valency 5, or are quinquivalent; S and probably O
have valency 6, or are sexivalent; Cl, I, etc. have valency 7,
or are septivalent; Fe, Ni, Pt, etc have valency 8, or are
octovalent; He, Ar, etc have valency 0, or are nullivalent. The
