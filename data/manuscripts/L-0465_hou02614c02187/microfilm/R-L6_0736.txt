[u]Glenmore,[/u]
HURRICANE, ESSEX CO., N. Y.

Dr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Dr. Peirce:

I trust you will pardon my delay in answering your letter. I find that the total expense of your coming here, including stops and expense here for a week would be not less than $35.00. I regret that our finances this year will not justify the expense. Most of our lecturers are those who want a week in the mountains and share in a small lecture fund. This will probably not be over $25.00 this year. I hope that at some other time we may be able to enjoy the pleasure of hearing your lectures.

Very Sincerely,
Stephen F. Weston
