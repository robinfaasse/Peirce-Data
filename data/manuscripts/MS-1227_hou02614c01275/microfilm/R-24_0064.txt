[u]Lt. Col. C. R. Conder[/u]
[u]The Hittites and their Language[/u]

Disputes Petrie's chronology because it
will not agree with the Assyrian.
Petrie places accession of Amenophis IV
1383 B.C. thirty years later than the latest
date we can assign for the last years of
Burnaburias. Yet these two kings were
contemporaries & Burnaburias was the
former.

Adolf Erman. [u]Egyptian Grammar[/u].
Translated by James Henry Breasted
Williams & Norgate [date]1894[/date]
