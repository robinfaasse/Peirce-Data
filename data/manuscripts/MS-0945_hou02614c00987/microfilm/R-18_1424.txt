[u]Mems for Lecture[/u]

We will go back to the very beginning, before time or space was organized.

What have we?

Nothing. Absolutely nothing.

Not however the nothing of negation. For not means simply other and other is the numeral second. A second implies a first. So we are long, long before such complex nothingness as that.

That would be the nothing of Death. This is the nothing of not yet being.

There [del]are[/del] [ins]is[/ins] no individual thing, no [del]force[/del] [ins]compulsion[/ins], no law. For law is regularity and supposes a multitude.

Since there is no law and no compulsion there is the freedom of boundless possibility.

So of potential being there was no lack.

What logically resulted from that state of things? Nothing, you will say. True, according to deductive logic, nothing would result. But that is not the logic of life, nor the logic of freedom or possibility. The logic of freedom, or possibility, is that it shall annul itself. For if it doesn't annul itself, it is [del]an idle possibility[/del] [ins]a completely idle possibility[/ins]; and [del]an idle possibility[/del] [ins]a completely idle possibility[/ins] is annulled by its complete idleness. That is the logic of possibility, and we know that [del]the[/del] [ins]something[/ins] universe did not remain [del]and[/del] a completely idle possibility. So that in point of fact that is the logic which it must both a priori and a posteriori have obeyed.
