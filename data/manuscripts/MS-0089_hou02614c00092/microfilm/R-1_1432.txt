Suppose
$$h = A x [del]+[/del] [ins]+[/ins] B y + C z + E t + G (x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - t^2) + H$$

$$D_t h = E - 2 G t$$
$$-D_x h = +A - 2 G x$$
$$-D_y h = +B - 2 G y$$
$$-D_z h = +C - 2 G z$$

Then what is $u$, If we write $u = A' x + B' y + C' x + E' t + G' (x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - t^2) + H'$

[del]Since $D_t h = D_x u$[/del] it is we have

$$D_t u = E' - 2 G' t$$
$$D_x u = A' + 2 G' x$$
$$-D_y u = -B' - 2 G' y$$
$$+D_z u = C' + 2 G' z$$

Hence
$$A' = E \quad G' = G = 0$$
$$E' = +A$$
$$C' = +B$$
$$B' = C$$

Put $v = A'' x + B'' y + C'' z + E'' t + H''$

$$D_y v = B'' = E$$
$$-D_z v = -C'' = +A$$
$$D_t v = E'' = +B$$
$$D_x v = A'' = +C$$
