[u]Theory of Quaternion Functions[/u]

Let the variable be $t + xi + yj + zk$
The function $p + ui + vj + wk$.
Suppose there is a derivative
$F(t + xi + yj + zk + \delta) - F(t + xi + yj + zk) = F'(t + xi + yj + zk) \cdot \delta$
$[F(t + xi + yj + zk + \delta) - F(t + xi + yj + zk)] \frac{1}{\delta} = F'(t + xi + yj + zk)$

$= D_t p + D_t u \cdot i + D_t v \cdot j + D_t w \cdot k$
$= D_x u - D_x p \cdot i - D_x w \cdot j + D_x v \cdot k$
$= D_y v + D_y w \cdot i - D_y p \cdot j - D_y u \cdot k$
$= D_z w - D_z v \cdot i + D_z u \cdot j - D_z p \cdot k$

$D_t p = D_x u = D_y v = D_z w$
$D_t u = - D_x p = - D_y w = - D_z v$
$D_t v = - D_x w = - D_y p = - D_z u$
$D_t w = - D_x v = - D_y u = - D_z p$

$F'' = D_t^2 p + D_t^2 u \cdot i + D_t^2 v \cdot j + D_t^2 w \cdot k$
$= - D_x^2 p - D_x^2 u \cdot i - D_x^2 v \cdot j - D_x^2 w \cdot k$
$= D_x D_y w - D_x D_y v \cdot i + D_x D_y u \cdot j - D_x D_y p \cdot k$
$= - D_y D_x w - D_y D_x v \cdot i - D_y D_x u \cdot j + D_y D_x p \cdot k$
