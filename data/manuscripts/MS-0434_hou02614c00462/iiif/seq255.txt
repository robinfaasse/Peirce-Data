with the state (which must subsist) there will be corresponding
excellencies in the individual. Corresponding to the Wisdom of the
state, Reason in the individual ought to have sway over Passion
and Appetite. And he will be courageous whose Passion obeys the
behests of reason as to what he ought or ought not to fear. And he has
Selfcommand in whom there is a harmony and acquiescence on
the part of Passion and Instinct as to the proper supremacy of
Reason. And Righteousness consists in each of the three parts
of the soul doing its own business with all its might. It is, therefore,
from this point of view absurd to say that Righteousness is not
a good in itself.
