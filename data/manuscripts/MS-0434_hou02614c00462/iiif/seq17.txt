Phillip sober. The only strict individual is an event,
and a sudden event. Therefore, the only good is a [del]at[/del]
single event. Criticism of this position may be
postponed until we have examined different kinds
of simple events considered as good.
[u]Good No. 1. The simple desideratum[/u]. It may be said
that we mean nothing by "good" except what we shall [del]deliberately[/del]
upon full deliberation desire, so that desire
is what constitutes the good. Nothing can be good,
according to this definition, unless it be desired; and
if a thing is [del]good[/del] desired then, in itself, it is good.

such that what we imagine as desirable cannot be
brought about, without bringing about something
else that we do not desire. But still, what we desire
