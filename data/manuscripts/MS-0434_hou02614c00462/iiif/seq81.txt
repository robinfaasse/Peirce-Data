Logic [ured]reason not easily fathomed the Young Socrates[/ured], — no relation to the
IV 226 [ured]great Socrates,[/ured] — is substituted for Theaetetus as respondent. Considering that
there was not the least personal color to Theaetetus, one is surprised at this.
Sciences are divided into that practical (πρακτική) and the gnostic
(γνωστική). (258 E) There is a science of government. Of gnostic
there are two parts, Epitactic (τὸ ἐπιτακτικόν), which orders things, and
critic (κριτική), which judges (260 A). The epitactic is divided
into that which transmits others' commands and that which
commands of oneself. (αὐτεπιτακτικὸν γένος)
