Another way of defining Sophistry is now proposed. He now
starts with a new branch of art, without obtaining it by any
process of dividing art in general. It is diacritic (διακριτική),
the art of dividing. This [del]is[/del] [ins]either[/ins] separates like from like, [del]which[/del] [ins]as such[/ins]
which has no name or it separates good from bad, throwing
the latter away. This is cathartic (καθαρτική) or the art of purification.
There are two kinds; that concerned with the purification of the body, and
that concerned with the purification of the soul. Now evil in the
soul is either deformity, or disease. The former is ignorance, the
latter vice. The cathartic of the body is either gymnastic which
purifies from deformity, and iatric (ἰατρική), or therapeutics,
which purifies from disease. In like manner the cathartic
of the soul is either didascalic (διδασκαλική) which purifies from
