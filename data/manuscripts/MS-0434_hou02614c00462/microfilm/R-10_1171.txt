But if animals who have habitations and remain in or
about them are tame, a great many other tame animals
are hunted, beginning with oysters (which are however agestic),
such as rabbits, bees, etc. The hemerothereutic art, or hunting of
tame animals, is again of two kinds. Lestic ([u]ληστική[/u]), or piracy; andra-
podistic ([u]ἀνδραποδιστική[/u]), or kidnapping; tyrannic ([u]τυραννική[/u]); and
polemic ([u]πολεμική[/u]), or the art of war, make up violent hunting
of tame animals; while dikanic ([u]δικανική[/u]), or pettifogging; dema-
goric ([u]δημηγορική[/u]), or demagogy; and prosomiletic ([u]προσομιλητική[/u])
or the art of private negotiations, may be called in one word, [del]a single[/del]
[ins]pithanourgic[/ins] ([u]πιθανουργική[/u]) art. Of pithanourgic there are
two genera, the private ([u]ἰδίᾳ[/u]) and the public ([u]δημοσίᾳ[/u]). Private
pithanourgic, or idiotherautic ([u]ἰδιοθηρευτική[/u]), is again of two
kinds: the mistharnetic ([u]μισθαρνητικόν[/u]), which [del]receives hire[/del] [ins]is paid[/ins],
