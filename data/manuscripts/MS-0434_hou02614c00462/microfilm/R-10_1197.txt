[u]C.S.P.[/u] And the predicate must be signified by some word or other sign, must it not? [u]Velian[/u]. Yes.

[u]C.S.P.[/u] If it is said that the predicate-sign is applicable to something [del]design[/del] to which the subject-sign is applicable, that must be true or false, must it not? [u]Velian[/u]. Yes.

[u]C.S.P.[/u] If [ins]this[/ins] is false then whatever there may be to which the subject-sign is applicable the predicate-sign is also applicable, is this not so? [u]Velian[/u]. Yes.

[u]C.S.P.[/u] While if the former is true, the latter is false? [u]Velian[/u]. Yes.
