— For never may you subdue this, that nothings are;
But keep your mind from this path of inquiry.

The first line here is all right; the second is nonsense. Nothing is simply that of which everything is true. That is wherein its nothingness consists. It therefore exists. That is true. It also does not exist. Why not? The principle of contradiction does not stand in the way; [del]for that[/del] [ins]for the[/ins] [del]things which contradict each other are non-existent. The principle of con-[/del]tradiction does not say that of that of which contraries are true contraries are not true; but merely that those things of which contraries are true are nothing. Here is is first and chief reasoning, mark it well:
