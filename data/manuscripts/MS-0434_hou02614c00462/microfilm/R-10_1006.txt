[u]Rule VI.[/u] If an existing term of philosophy is so
inappropriate as to be a positive hindrance to philo-
sophical study, a new term to replace it will not be
superfluous.

[u]Rule VII.[/u] Since these rules are intended to further the
advance of philosophy, they must not be allowed to
stand in the way of any unmistakable and urgent
need of philosophy. But this rule is not to be used as
[del]an excuse for evading the spirit of the other rules.[/del]
