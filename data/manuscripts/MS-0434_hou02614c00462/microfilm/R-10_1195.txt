My dear Veblen, some god suggests to me that thousands
of years after you and I have left this earth, a physicist
by the name of Thomson will prove that all matter consists
of corpuscles. Now each of those corpuscles must, I suppose,
have centre of mass, which is an absolute point. But no
matter how infinite the multitude of those centres, they cannot
in all time, pass through every place. There must, then, remain
places where no centre has been or ever will be. Yes, for
my part, I [del]can[/del] [ins]will[/ins] believe, that the laws of motion, the law
of gravitation, etc. are as real in those places as anywhere.
