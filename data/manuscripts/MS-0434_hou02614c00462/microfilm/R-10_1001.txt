branch of science. It is most desirable to bring about
a general usage and to adhere to it. After much re-
flection, I have come to the conclusion that the
only way to bring about such an agreement in
philosophy is to induce philosophers to adhere
strictly to reasonable rules; and [del]them[/del] [ins]as such[/ins] a
