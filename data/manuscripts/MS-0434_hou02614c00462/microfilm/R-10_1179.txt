So far, this reëxamination is futile. It simply adds more
of the same uncritical stuff that was found to need reëxamination
and increases instead of diminishing the amount of matter to be
determined. But now he dismisses all that method and breaks
into mild Hegelianism.

How is it possible for a thing to appear or seem and not
be so, or for a thing to be said and not be true? (τὸ γὰρ

τῷ πρόσθεν χρόνῳ καὶ νῦν. 236E) There is no harm in the question,
