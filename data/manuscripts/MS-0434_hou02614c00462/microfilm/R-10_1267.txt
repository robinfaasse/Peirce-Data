Sciences are divided into that practical ([greek]πρακτική[/greek]) and the gnostic
([greek]γνωστική[/greek]). (258E) There is a science of government. Of gnostic
there are two parts, epitactic ([greek]τὸ ἐπιτακτικόν[/greek]), which orders things, and
critic ([greek]κριτική[/greek]), which judges (260A). The epitactic is divided
into that which transmits others' commands and that which
commands of one self. ([greek]αὐτεπιτακτικὸν γένος[/greek])
