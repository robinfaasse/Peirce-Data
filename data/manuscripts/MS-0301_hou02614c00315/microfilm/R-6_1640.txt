Every metaphysician is supposed to have some
radical fault to find with every other, & I
cannot find any direr fault to find with the
new pragmatists than that they are lively. In
order to be deep it is requisite to be dull.

On their side, one of the faults that I think
they might find with me is that I make
pragmatism to be a mere maxim of logic
instead of a sublime principle of speculative
philosophy.
