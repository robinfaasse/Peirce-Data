[u]Unofficial[/u]

U. S. Coast Survey Office
Washington [date]Sept. 10 1872[/date]

Dear Sir

A friend of mine who spent the summer with a gentleman belonging to the Boston yacht club reports a rock in the channel between Thompson's Island and Squantum, bare at low water with a buoy on it — not marked down on our chart — he made a mark on the accompanying chart and it would be well to have this examined & if facts are as stated to have it marked on our chart

Yours resp[ins]y[/ins]
Ch. A. Schott.

Chas. S. Peirce Asst in
charge Office
