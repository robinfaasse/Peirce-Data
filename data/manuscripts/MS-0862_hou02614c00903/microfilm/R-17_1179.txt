As for Inspiration, that must be a peculiar experience. Though
it differs from other experience in being the exclusive pri-
vilege of the [del]few[/del], still it is experience, and does not

Tradition may be taken off the list for a similar
reason. There are peculiar experiences, those of hearing
narratives. There are peculiar [del]suggestions[/del] — namely of the
meanings of the sounds heard. Finally, reasoning es-
timates the value of these suggestions.
