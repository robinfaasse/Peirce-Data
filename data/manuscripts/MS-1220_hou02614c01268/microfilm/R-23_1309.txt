<u>IV.</u>
<u>Of the Colon and Semicolon.</u>

Most writers on punctuation tell us that a colon is
placed between two sentences to indicate a pause less than
a period, and a [del]comma[/del] semi-colon to indicate a pause less
than a colon. This is all poppy-cock. Any elocutionist
would tell these writers that sentences requiring periods
between them are often properly read with less pause
between them than [del]all[/del] [ins]some of[/ins] the commas in these sentences.
