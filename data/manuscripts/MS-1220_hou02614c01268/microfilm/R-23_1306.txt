"Saying with a loud voice, [u]Worthy is the Lamb that was slain to[/u]
[u]receive power, and riches, and wisdom, and strength, and honor,[/u]
[u]and glory, and blessing. And every creature which is in heaven,[/u]
[u]and on the earth, and under the earth, and such as are in the[/u]
[u]sea, and all that are in them, heard I saying, Blessing, and[/u]
[u]honor, and glory, and power, be unto him that sitteth upon the[/u]
[u]throne, and unto the Lamb, for ever and ever."[/u]

Were this the writing of a modern English author, I should say
that when a man deliberately and intentionally abandons common
sense he must be the sole judge of his own mystical punctuation.
But I think it ought to be printed,

Saying with a loud voice, "Worthy is the lamb that was slain
to receive power and riches and wisdom and strength and
honor and glory and blessing." And every creature which
is in heaven and on the earth [ins]and under the earth[/ins] and such as are in the sea, and
all that are in them, heard I saying, "Blessing and honor
and glory and power be unto Him that sitteth upon the
throne, and unto the lamb, forever and ever.
