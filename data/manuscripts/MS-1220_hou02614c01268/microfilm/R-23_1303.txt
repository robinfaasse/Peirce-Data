Here, there must be no comma before pendant since the
phrase that now introduces is not explanatory but modificatory.
For the absence of a comma suggests that it is the hazels that
are pendant instead of the roots. An awkward way of avoiding
this inconvenience would be by hyphens, Thus:
Among the roots-
Of-hazels pendant o'er the plaintive stream.

The above simple principles seem to me to be quite
sufficient to make it easy to determine where commas
should be placed. The result may differ slightly from
the prevailing practice, but not enough to overcome the
advantage of acting upon simple and rational principles.
