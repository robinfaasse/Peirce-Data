I give some examples:

The thought is always prior to the fact; all the facts of history preëxist in the mind as laws.

All history becomes subjective; in other words, there is properly no history; only biography.

Every law which the state enacts indicates a fact in human nature: that is all.

There is at the surface infinite variety of things; at the centre there is simplicity of cause.

Thus, of the genius of one remarkable people, we have a fourfold representation: and to the senses what more unlike than an ode of Pindar, a marble centaur, the peristyle of the Parthenon, and the last actions of Phocion?

Nature is full of a sublime family likeness throughout her works; and she delights in startling us with resemblances in the most unexpected quarters.

Common souls pay with what they do; nobler souls with that which they are.

Xenophon arose naked, and taking an axe, began to split wood; whereupon others rose and did the like.
