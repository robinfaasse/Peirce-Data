[u]From Landor, whose penetration is, however, bad.[/u]

He who would strike out a novelty in architecture, commits a folly in safety; his house and he may stand: he who attempts it in politics, carries a torch, from which, at the first narrow passage, we may expect a conflagration.

I have performed one action; I have composed some few things, which posterity, I would fain believe, will not suffer to be quite forgotten.

Aristocracy is not contemptible as a system of government; in fact, it is the only one a true gentleman can acquiesce in.

Experimentalists may be the best philosophers; they are always the worst politicians.

It is only a free city that is strong; for it is only in a free city that the mass of the people can be armed.

You write upon language and analogy; no man better.

Principles are of slower growth than passions: and the hand of Philosophy, holden out to all, there are few who press cordially.
