$$\Sigma_{i} a_{ii} \prec \Sigma_{i} \Sigma_{j} a_{ij} \text{.}$$

But an index particularly quantified, which is preceded by one universally quantified, cannot be identified in the different factors unless the latter is so. Thus, from
$$\Pi_{i} \Sigma_{j} l_{ij}$$
we cannot infer
$$\Pi_{i} \Pi_{k} \Sigma_{j} l_{ij} l_{kj} \text{;}$$
for this would imply that there were two objects [u]i[/u] and [u]k[/u] in the relation [u]l[/u] to the same correlate j.
