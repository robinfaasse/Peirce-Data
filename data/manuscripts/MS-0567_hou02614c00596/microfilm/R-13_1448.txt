Let us now seek for all propositions
resulting from the elimination of $\kappa$ from
$$\Pi_{\kappa}\Sigma_{a}\Pi_{b}\Sigma_{c}\ u_{a}(\overline{v_{b}} + \overline{r_{\kappa ab}} + r_{\kappa cb}\overline{r_{ac}}).$$
Owing to the universal quantification of $\kappa$, we
have a right to multiply this by any true pro-
position in $\Sigma_{\kappa}$. It is easy to prove
$$\Pi_{\mu}\Sigma_{\lambda}\Pi_{i}\Pi_{j}\left\{\overline{r}_{\lambda ij}(\overline{r}_{\mu ij} + \overline{u}_{i}) + r_{\lambda ij}r_{\mu ij}u_{i}\right\}.$$
First, if $\lambda$ be at once eliminated, the re-
sult is an identical proposition.
