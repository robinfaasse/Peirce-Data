$$= (a_1 + a_2 + a_3 + \text{ etc}) (a_1 + a_2 + a_3 + \text{ etc})$$
$$= \Sigma_i a_i . \Sigma_j a_j = \Sigma_i \Sigma_j a_i a_j .$$
