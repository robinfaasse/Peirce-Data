$$\Pi_{\kappa} \Sigma_{a} \Pi_{b} \Sigma_{c} \ u_{a} (\overline{W}_{b} + \overline{\gamma}_{\kappa ab} + \gamma_{\kappa cb} \overline{I}_{ac}).$$
Owing to the universal quantification of $\kappa$

line of p. 201. It could easily be proved by
involution. As a special case put $u_{i}$ for $\gamma_{\beta ij}$.
