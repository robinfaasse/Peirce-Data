There is, besides, a sort of development of the Quantifier, consisting in substituting two indices where one had been used, according to the formulae
$$\Pi_i x_i y_i = \Pi_i x_i . \Pi_j y_j = \Pi_i \Pi_j x_i y_j$$
$$\Sigma_i (x_i + y_i) = \Sigma_i \Sigma_j (x_i + y_j)$$
$$\Sigma_i l_{ii} \prec \Sigma_i \Sigma_j l_{ij}$$
$$\Pi_i l_{ii} \prec \Pi_i \Sigma_i l_{ij}.$$
