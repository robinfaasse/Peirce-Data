$$\Phi_{i} u_{i} = u_{1} * u_{2} * u_{3} * \text{etc.}$$

$n+1$ indices of which $i$ is the first. When

$\underline{u}$. Then the square of the whole expression
with identification of $i$ in the two factors
is
$$\Phi_{i} u_{i}^{2} = u_{1}^{2} * u_{2}^{2} * u_{3}^{2} * \text{etc.}$$

[del]$u_{1} \prec u_{1}^{2}$[/del] $u_{2} \prec u_{2}^{2}$ $u_{3} \prec u_{3}^{2}$ etc.

$$\Phi_{i} u_{i} \prec \Phi_{i} u_{i}^{2}.$$
