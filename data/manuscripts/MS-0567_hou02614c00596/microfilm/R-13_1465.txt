We have thus shown that

$$\Psi_j w_j \prec \Psi_j \Psi_k w_j w_k$$

and

$$\Phi_i \Psi_j u_{ij} \prec \Phi_i \Psi_j \Psi_k u_{ij} u_{ik} \text{ ;}$$

and by similar reasoning it follows that

$$\Upsilon_h \Phi_i \Psi_j u_{hij} \prec \Upsilon_h \Phi_i \Psi_j \Psi_k u_{hij} u_{hik} \text{ ,}$$

and in short that the last index of the quantifier [del]may[/del] [ins]can[/ins] always be diversified, provided all that may precede it be identified.
