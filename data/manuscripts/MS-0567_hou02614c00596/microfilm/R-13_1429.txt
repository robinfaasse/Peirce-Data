After the involution has once been performed, further identification and diversification of indices may be effected by applying the following formulae.

[u]For identifications[/u].
$\Pi_{i} \Pi_{j} l_{ij} \prec \Pi_{i} l_{ii}$
$\Sigma_{i} \Pi_{j} l_{ij} \prec \Sigma_{i} l_{ii}$

[u]For diversifications[/u].
$\Pi_{i} l_{ii} \prec \Pi_{i} \Sigma_{j} l_{ij}$
$\Sigma_{i} l_{ii} \prec \Sigma_{i} \Sigma_{j} l_{ij}$
