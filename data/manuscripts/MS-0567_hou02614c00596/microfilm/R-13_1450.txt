We now assume the following
$$\Pi_{m} \Pi_{n} \Pi_{p} \Pi_{q} \Sigma_{\delta} ( I_{mn} + I_{pq} + \overline{r_{mp}} \overline{r_{nq}} \overline{r_{mq}} \overline{r_{np}} ) .$$

The truth of this is evident because one term consists wholly of r's, and this cannot be made to disappear in any power because the $\delta$ in the different factors would be discriminated unless all the letters were the same. Hence, elimination of $\delta$ must always evacuate the proposition.
