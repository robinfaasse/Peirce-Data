AMERICAN METROLOGICAL SOCIETY,
OFFICE OF SECRETARY,
Columbia College, Cor. 49th St. & Madison Ave.

New York, [date]Jan. 20th 1885[/date]

My dear Sir:

I sent you some time ago to the office of the U. S. C. & G. S. all the boxes, books &c that you left with me, I trust that they have come to hand in good order. All charges were paid on them so that you should incur no expense. The Publication Committee would gladly receive very soon your paper on the pendulum

Yours very truly
J K Rees
Secy

Prof. C. S. Peirce
U. S. C. & G. S. Office
Washington D.C.
