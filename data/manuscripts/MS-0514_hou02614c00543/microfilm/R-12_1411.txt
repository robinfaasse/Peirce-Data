Any graph-instance may be iterated (i.e. duplicated) in the same area or in any area enclosed within that, provided the new lines of identity so introduced have identically the same connections they had before the iteration. And if any graph-instance is already duplicated in the same area or in two areas one of which is included (whether immediately or not) within the other, [ins]their connections being identical,[/ins] the inner of the instances ([del]or either of them if they are in the same area[/del]) may be erased. This is called the Rule of Iteration and Deiteration.
