This system of Graphs, as distinguished
from other systems of graphs, is called
Existential Graphs. Before long, I shall
be able to explain why it is so named.
What is called a sentence in Grammar
is a sign sufficient for one person to
influence another; although if he merely
says “mermaid” or “heroic selfsacrifice,”
he does inevitably excite an idea in
the person who heard him. Sentences
[del]are[/del] either make inquiry and are called
Interrogations or Interrogatories, or they
are intended to make the person addressed
perform a single definite voluntary act,
and are called Commands or Imperatives,
or they are intended to influence the
conduct of those they address by bringing
their thought to bear upon something; as
when I say “The planet Mars is easily seen
from the Earth, but the Earth is very badly
