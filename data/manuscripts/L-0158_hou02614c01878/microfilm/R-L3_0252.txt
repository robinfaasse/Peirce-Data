Roscoe, So. Dakota,
[date]Mch. 18th, 1890[/date].

To Prof. Peirce
My Dear Friend:

I recd sometime
since from our friend Prof. E.
N. Horsford, a copy of his
wk. entitled "The Discovery
of the Ancient City of Norumbega,"
&c. In acknowledg the gift, I
said to Prof. Horsford that I had
lent you his former work, & had
left it with you, because of yr.
estimate of it, and also on ac-
count of [del]his[/del] [ins]your high[/ins] regard for him
as [del]his[/del] [ins]your[/ins] former instructor. I also
said that I shd. be glad to receive
another copy of each so that
I might still leave with you the
one you have, and send a copy
of the other. The Prof. evidently
