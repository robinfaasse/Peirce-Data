§152. Mr. DeMorgan conceives of his own system as a
system of "quantification of the predicate" or as I
should phrase it, of predicative metalexis. [ins]or this applying same end to finite[/ins] But [del]still[/del]
it seems to me clear that it is simply a system
of [u]subjective metaphasis[/u]. [ins]That is varying the subject by not.[/ins] This is shown by the
following table.

$S))P$ Any $S$ is $P$. This is the $A$ of Aristotelian syllogistic.
$S)\cdot(P$ Any $S$ is not-P. This is $E$.
$S(\cdot)P$ Any not-$S$ is $P$; i.e. Everything is either $S$ or $P$. Denote
this by $H$ (to be called Eta), a modifier $E$.
$S((P$ Any not-$S$ is not-P, i.e. Any $P$ is $S$. [del]Denote by[/del] [ins]$\aleph$ (aleph)[/ins] [del]which corresponds to A.[/del]

$S((P$ Some $S$ is not $P$. This is $O$, contradictory of $A$.
$S()P$ Some $S$ is $P$. This is $I$, contradictory of $E$.
$S)(P$ Some not-$S$ is not $P$, i.e. There is something besides $S$ and $P$. Denote
this contradictory of $H$ (a Greek letter) by Greek $\Upsilon$ (Upsilon), [ins]which recalls I.[/ins]
$S))P$ Some not-$S$ is $P$, i.e. Some $P$ is not $S$. Denote this
contradictory of $\aleph$ (a [del]Greek[/del] [ins]Hebrew[/ins] letter) by Hebrew $\mathcal{y}$ (Ayin), [ins]which corresponds alphabetically to O.[/ins]
