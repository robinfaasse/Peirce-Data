[u]I[/u] and [u]$\Upsilon$[/u]. [del]$\Xi$[/del] [ins]$\Xi$[/ins] is merely a converted A, [del]$\Psi$[/del] [ins]$\Psi$[/ins] a converted O.

A by predicatal metaphasis becomes E,
by subjectal metaphasis H;

A by subjectal metalexis becomes I,
by predicatal metalexis $\Upsilon$;

$\Xi$ by predicatal metaphasis becomes H,
by subjectal metaphasis E;

$\Xi$ by subjectal metalexis becomes $\Upsilon$,
by predicatal metalexis I;

E by subjectal metalexis becomes O,
by predicatal metalexis $\Psi$;

H by subjectal metalexis becomes $\Psi$,
by predicatal metalexis O;

I by predicatal metaphasis becomes O,
by subjectal metaphasis $\Psi$;

$\Upsilon$ by predicatal metaphasis becomes $\Psi$,
by subjectal metaphasis O.
