[u]§ 118. De Morgan's Propositional Scheme[/u].

I have [del]many[/del] [ins]that[/ins] times had occasion to make use of [del]this system, and[/del] [ins]in[/ins] fields and [del]by[/del] for purposes [del]of which[/del] its author hardly dreamed, and certainly had not in mind when he contrived his scheme. It has never failed to do me good service; and though for many years I was disinclined to advocate its general adoption into logic, I am completely convinced now of its great utility, and that the forms of inference cannot be properly stated without it. For the Aristotelian system has no dilemmatic reasoning.
