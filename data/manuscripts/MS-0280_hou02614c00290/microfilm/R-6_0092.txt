must function as signs. The function of a sign is to convey
an idea. The idea must spring from some source of or
factory of ideas, which we may call a mind without begging
the question of its being personal. The sign must also be
interpreted; so that there must be an interpreting agent. This
understands the source of ideas. The two must come to
an agreement or convention. They must, therefore, be of the
same general nature, and we may call the interpreting agent a
mind likewise. Not only must they come to an understanding
but must have the power of observing when it is reached. This
