Now whatever is connected with any point is connected with every
point in the same place. Hence it further follows that if two points
heavily marked are connected by a heavy line or continuum
of heavy points, since every point of the line stands for a single
[fig]large black dot[/fig] individual, all the points of the line stand for the same indi-
vidual, and the line [u]asserts[/u] that the individuals denoted by its
extremities are one and the same. Such a line is called a [u]line
of identity[/u]. A capital letter may be attached to a marked point to render it a designate individual.
