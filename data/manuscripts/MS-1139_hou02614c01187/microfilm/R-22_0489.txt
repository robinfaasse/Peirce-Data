Lange follows Kant (in his Geschichte des Materialismus [date]1866[/date])
in admitting forms of Intuition & of Judgment as basis of all experience.
But he does not admit their "Deduction", Kant's "zukünftige Metaphysik" as
impossible as the old metaphysics. He thinks the highest concepts of the
understanding [del]are[/del] [ins]which are[/ins] grounded in the original & invariable
[del]laws[/del] of the foundation of the understanding can only be discovered
by induction aided by Critic & by psychological [del]knowledge[/del] [ins]enlightenment[/ins].
The whole world of sense is the product of our organization, although unknowable
things that action our organization are undeniable [This perfect nonsense] and
claptrap.] The physiology of the organs of sense is Kantian corrected.
Truth only comes from experience; but this is our property [Eigenthum.] Materialism
is a sure cure [eine wahre Wohlthat] [del]or[/del] for every metaphysical system that
seeks to draw from concepts a knowledge of the essences of things that can only
be learned from experience. But this will not do as a Weltanschauung
"since it leaves the chief difficulty untouched. Our apparent bodily organs
are themselves mere phenomena and the [del]transcendental[/del] foundation of our
organization remains as unknown as the so-called outer world [Yes, no more
& no less] It is the cornerstone of the Kantian critic that we only know
ourselves as we appear not as we are. This brings materialism to naught.
