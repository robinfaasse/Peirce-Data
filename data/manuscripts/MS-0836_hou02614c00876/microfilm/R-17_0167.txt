There are commonly said to be three 'normative' sciences, or studies of the necessary conditions of excellences and virulences. They are enumerated as esthetics, ethics, and logic. It is a merely empirical enumeration, but, like the trivium, it has a basis in the nature of things. Esthetics is said to be the science of beauty and ugliness, ethics of virtue and vice, logic of truth and falsity.
