[u]Fechner's Law
applied to Color[/u]

If color arises from three
independent excitations, the
sensations from each are
proportional to logarithm of
the excitation, and if the total
sensation is simply the sum
of the three sensations, this
should be,
$\log x \cdot \rho + \log y \cdot \gamma + \log z \cdot \beta$
if the color mixture is
$x\text{ R} + y\text{ G} + z\text{ B}$
provided R gives sensation $\rho$
" G " " $\gamma$
" B " " $\beta$.

Then the effect of increasing
the brightness in ratio $r$
is to add to the sensation
$\log r (\rho + \gamma + \beta)$
