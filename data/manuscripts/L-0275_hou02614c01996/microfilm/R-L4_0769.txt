Whitten, Hardin Co., Iowa, [date]June 7, 1901.[/date]

Prof. Mathematics & Astronomy,
Cambridge, Mass,

Dear Sir:—

Will you kindly give me any information in your possession as to literature concerning the Translation of the Earth's Axis? — or

Secondly, what year (or years) and the [u]time of year[/u] such translation is said to be at a maximum? — that is, when the circle as described in the earth by the "adjusting pole" is of greatest diameter.

Do you still write for the Monist?

Yours very respectfully,
L. S. McCoy.
