Suppose there are certain individual
objects called dogs and certain indivi-
dual objects called boys. Suppose
that on a certain date all [del]the[/del] dogs
belong to one boy; and that, by
the nature of the relation no dog can
belong to two different boys. Sup-
pose, however, that on a future date
it is possible that each dog should
belong to a different boy, independ-
ently of [del]the[/del] the belonging of other
dogs to this boy or that, the same
dogs and the same boys existing
on the two dates.
