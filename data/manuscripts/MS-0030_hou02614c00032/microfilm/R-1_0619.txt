Using the word [u]dyad[/u] to mean an
ordered pair (so that there are two
different dyads of any two different
individual objects), suppose that (by
Omnipotence) a list has been formed
of some dyads of proper dog-names.
Then by a "[u]form of dyadic relationship[/u]
[u]among dogs[/u]" is meant a general
type of collection of dyads of dogs
which such a list might come to
denote by an interchange of dog-tags.

Conceive that (by Omnipotence) a
complete list has been made of
all possible forms of dyadic re-
lationship among dogs.
