NEW YORK MATHEMATICAL SOCIETY,
41 EAST 49TH STREET,
NEW YORK.

[date]6 April, 1894[/date]

My dear Mr. Peirce,
I am very glad to learn that you are going to be present at the meeting tomorrow — There will be time at the meeting for your account of the Arithmetic of Rollandus, in which, I am sure, the Society will be much interested — Would you be willing to throw your remarks into form for publication in the [u]Bulletin[/u]?

Very truly yours
Thomas S. Fiske
