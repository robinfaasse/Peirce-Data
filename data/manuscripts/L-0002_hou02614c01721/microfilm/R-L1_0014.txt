The censorship is complete and
absolute. There is no means open
by which a man can get a hearing
who opposes the governing monied
interest of the world. Both in
America and Europe the system

Against this great power it is use-
less for the men in my position
to strive by means of investments, for
we shall be broken down. My
opinion is that time must work
out its own phenomena.
Money put into a publication
which runs counter to the govern-
ing class is simply absorbed by
them. I am inclined to the opinion
that they have enough already.
It need hardly be added by me
that it gives me the greatest
pleasure to have any work of
mine commend itself to a mind
as acute and highly trained as
yours. Nor can I suppress a natural
feeling of regret that such intellects
as yours, in this economic age, do
