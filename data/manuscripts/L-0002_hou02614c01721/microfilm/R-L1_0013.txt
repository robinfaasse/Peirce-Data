My dear Mr Peirce.

My delay in
answering your flattering letter
of Dec 1 comes, as you see, from
distance. I have now been in
India some weeks, and I do not
expect to reach home before May.
Of course my absence makes it
impossible for me to do anything
about a review, and from my own
experience I should fear such a
venture might not succeed.
