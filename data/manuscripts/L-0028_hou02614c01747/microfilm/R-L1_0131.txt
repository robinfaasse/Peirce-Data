The Astor Library
[date]December 6 1895[/date]

My dear Professor

I am sorry to hear of your
losses, and should think that
in the proposed expansion there
might be an opportunity to
avail ourselves of your talents
and acquirements. At present
there is no vacancy here. The
staff is prescribed and appointed
by the Trustees. I shall be glad
to file your letter for future
reference.

Yours very truly
Robbins Little
