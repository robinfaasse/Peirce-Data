[u]Three Preliminary Reflexions.[/u]

[u]First Consideration[/u]. Every kind of mind, whether human or brute[ins], still[/ins] with which I have any sufficient acquaintance, appears to me to possess three classes of powers. The first class consists of powers of [u]Feeling[/u], or [u]Consciousness[/u]. I use these two words as synonyms; for though there may be other modes of consciousness than feelings, yet these others, if they exist, are so swamped in excess of Feeling that I am not able clearly to discern them.
