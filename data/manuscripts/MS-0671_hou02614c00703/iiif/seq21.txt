brute, [del]still[/del] with which I have any sufficient acquaintance, appears
to me to possess three classes of powers. The first class consists
of powers of [u]Feeling[/u], or [u]Consciousness[/u]. I use these two words as
synonyms; for though there may be other modes of consciousness
