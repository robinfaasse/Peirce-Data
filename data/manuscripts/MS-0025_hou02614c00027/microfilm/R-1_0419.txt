Art. 9. For example, the relations of equality and excess of multitude having been defined after Cantor, philosophy cannot avoid the question which instantly springs up, Must every two collections be either equal or the one greater than the other, or can they be so multitudinous that the units of neither can be in a one-to-one relation to units of the other?
