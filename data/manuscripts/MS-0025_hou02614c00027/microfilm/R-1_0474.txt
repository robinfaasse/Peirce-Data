[marginalia]Multitude[/marginalia] [pn]62[/pn]

I will now describe a certain collection, A, whose units [del]I[/del] [ins]I[/ins] will call the Ps.

[u]1[sup]st[/sup][/u], The [del]Ps[/del] [ins]$\Pi$s[/ins] can be arranged in linear order. That is, there is a relation, r, such that taking as you will any $\Pi$s, individually [del]designated as[/del] [ins]designable as[/ins] $\Pi_1$, $\Pi_2$, and $\Pi_3$, [ins](if $\Pi_1$ is to $\Pi_2$ and $\Pi_2$ is to $\Pi_3$)[/ins] either $\Pi_3$ is not r to $\Pi_2$, or $\Pi_2$ is not r to $\Pi_1$, or $\Pi_3$ is r to $\Pi_1$;

[u]2[sup]nd[/sup][/u], The line of arrangement of the $\Pi$s can be taken so as not to branch. That is, taking as you will $\Pi$s individually designable as $\Pi_4$ and $\Pi_5$, either $\Pi_4$ is r to $\Pi_5$ or $\Pi_5$ is r to $\Pi_4$; [Of course this permits both to be true, [del]b[/del] but that I proceed to forbid.]

[u]3[sup]d[/sup][/u], The line of arrangement of the $\Pi$s can [ins]further[/ins] be so taken as not to return into itself, circularly. That is, taking [del]as[/del] [ins]you[/ins] will any $\Pi$, individually designable as $\Pi_6$, $\Pi_6$ is not r to $\Pi_6$;

[u]4[sup]th[/sup][/u], There are certain parts of A called "packs" of $\Pi$s, which are mutually exclusive. That is, taking any pack whatever and any unit of that pack, that unit is a $\Pi$; and taking as you will any packs individually designable as $\Box_7$ and $\Box_8$ and any $\Pi$ individually designable as $\Pi_7$ and $\Pi_8$, either $\Box_7$ is identical with $\Box_8$, or $\Pi_7$ is not a unit of $\Box_7$, or $\Pi_8$ is not a unit of $\Box_8$, or else $\Pi_7$ is not identical with $\Pi_8$;

[u]5[sup]th[/sup][/u], The packs can be arranged in linear order. That is, there is a relation, s, such that taking as you will any $\Box$s individually designable as $\Box_1$, $\Box_2$, and $\Box_3$, either $\Box_3$ is not s to $\Box_2$, or $\Box_2$ is not s to $\Box_1$, or $\Box_3$ is s to $\Box_1$;

[u]6[sup]th[/sup][/u], The line of arrangement of the packs can be taken so as not to branch. That is, taking as you will any $\Box$s individually designable as $\Box_4$ and $\Box_5$ either $\Box_4$ is s to $\Box_5$ or $\Box_5$ is s to $\Box_4$;

[u]7[sup]th[/sup][/u], The line of arrangement of the packs can be further taken so as not to return into itself. That is, taking as you will any pack[del]s[/del] individually designable as $\Box_6$, $\Box_6$ is not s to $\Box_6$.
