These may be some of you to whom this theorem in arithmetic is unfamiliar.
I only became aware of it by finding it out for myself. It helps to bring home
to us a philosophical proposition of which I was well aware before I discovered
this, namely, that [del]numbers in themselves cannot possibly signify any magnitude[/del]
[del]other than the magnitudes of collections, or multitude; but what they principally[/del]
[del]represent is[/del] [ins]place in a[/ins] [del]serial order.[/del] Numbers do not contain the idea of the equality of
parts and consequently [del]they[/del] a fraction [del]cannot[/del] in itself signify anything involving
equality of parts. They merely express the [ins]ordinal[/ins] place in such a uniformly condensed series.
