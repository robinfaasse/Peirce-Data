[del]Article[/del] RULES OF SIMPLIFIED IDEOGRAPHS.
Preamble. Definition of the Simplified Ideographs.
1. A [u]logical graph[/u] is a diagram standing either for an assertion or
for a [u]relative[/u], or assertion incomplete only in wanting certain subjects.
A [u]logical ideograph[/u] is a logical graph constructed and intended to
be interpreted upon the principles [del]above[/del] set forth in previous sections of
this paper.
A [u]simplified logical ideograph[/u] is a [ins]logical[/ins] graph constructed and intended
to be understood in accordance with the present rules.
Article I. Of the Constitution of Graphs.
