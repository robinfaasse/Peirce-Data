from                       A non est B Premiss

In this case A is the minor extreme. Which is the major? The
conclusion implies the premiss. Hence the comprehension of the 
predicate of the latter is less and its extension is greater. B there-
fore is the major & non-B the middle. We have then inferred a 
minor proposition & have consequently made a hypothesis.
