We found representations to be of three kinds
Signs Copies Symbols.
By a copy, I mean a representation whose agree-
ment with its object depends merely upon a sameness
of predicates. By a sign, I mean a representation
whose reference to its object is fixed by convention. By
a symbol I mean one which upon being presented to the
mind - without any resemblance to [del]the[/del] [ins]its[/ins] object & without
any reference to a previous convention - calls up a
[del]mental symbo[/del] concept. I consider concepts, themselves,
as a species of symbols.

thing. Second it must be a manifestation of a Logos.
or represented & [del]material[/del] [ins]realizable[/ins] form. Third it must be
[del]equi[/del]translatable into another language or system of symbols.
