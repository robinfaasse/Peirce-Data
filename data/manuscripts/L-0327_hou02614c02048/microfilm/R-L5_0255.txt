Cambridge
[date]Jan 3. 1873[/date]

My dear Mr Peirce

I received your note the
other week together with one from
the Secretary of the P. E. T telling
me that Lieut. Steever had
started — I was sorry to have
missed him but shall drop
him a line & hope that we will
meet in fellow traveller [ins]skin and then[/ins] fashion.

Since I saw you I have married
& about 2 months ago Mrs Palmer
presented me with a daughter —
[del]both[/del] mother & child are I am
happy to say extremely well.
