[u]Notes on Kempe's Paper[/u]

It is odd that Kempe should say his entities are undistinguished, just in the same breath in which he distinguishes them by letters.

His Law V is excessively vague; but if it means anything, laws I and II are superfluous. For they only state that [del]q[/del] exist under certain circumstances and Law V states that [u]any entity[/u] exists that is not explicitly or implicitly forbidden to exist.

He does not say that ab.c and ba.c are substantially the same assertion. But he evidently means them to be so.

Let us see what sort of an assertion this is.
