Having arrived at this point we have only to notice
whether the [u]Rule is universal[/u], the [u]case affirmative[/u]; the
[u]Result of the same quality as the Rule & the same quantity[/u]
[u]as the case[/u]. In this case the rule is All men of God
are hearers of me. This is universal. The case is All of
you are men of God this is affirmative. The result is All
of you are hearers of me. This is affirmative like the rule
& universal like the case. Hence the syllogism is good.
And it does follow since All that are of God hear
my words & ye hear them not, that you are not of God.
