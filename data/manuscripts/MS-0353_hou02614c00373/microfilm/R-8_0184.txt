The syllogisms which we then took up divided
themselves in Aristotelean and Theophrastean. I hope
however, I did not emphasize this division too strong-
ly. These Theophrastean moods have been the great puzzle
of logicians. The manner of regarding them which I
suggested satisfies me better than any other; but still
it does not satisfy me. I shall therefore, say no more
about these very unusual forms of argument; only to or
three instances of the use of which can be found in all liter-
ature.
