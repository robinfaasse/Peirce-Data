[u]Lino's[/u] [ins]4[sup]th[/sup][/ins] Argument. If a man takes 5 [del]strides[/del] [ins]steps[/ins] towards the [del]West[/del] [ins]East[/ins] each of 3 feet each, he goes 15 feet to the West. But if he is upon a vessel which is sailing rapidly to the West, he [del]really[/del] [ins]moves[/ins] toward the West; but if he is not very near the pole the motion of the Earth carries him much more swift-ly to the East again. And thus all motion is relative, to something else; And therefore a body may appear to move and yet in reality be stationary.
