Reasoning is that mental occupation in which one searches out arguments and judges of them[ins] as such[/ins]. In saying that Reasoning judges, we place it among conscious actions, that is, voluntary [del]actions[/del] [ins]controlled[/ins] or [ins]those[/ins] actions[del],[/del] in which Free Will, rightly understood, comes into play.

An Argument, in the sense in which the word will uniformly be used throughout this book, is a real Fact which is a sign of the reality of another fact.

The word Sign, as it will here be used, denotes [del]anything[/del] object of thought which excites any kind of mental action, whether voluntary or not, concerning something
