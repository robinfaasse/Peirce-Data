Every object of experience excites an idea of some sort; but if that idea is not [ins]associated,[/ins] [del]as[/del] sufficiently and in the right way, [del]as[/del] with some previous experience, so as to narrow the attention, it will not be a Sign.

[fig]black circle[/fig] A Sign necessarily has for its Object some fragment of history, that is, of the history of ideas. It must excite some idea. That idea may go wholly to narrowing the attention, as in such signs as "man," "virtue," "manner."
