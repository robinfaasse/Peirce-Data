7:25[/date][/marginalia] as to which of them. That which a Sign "stands for," or "refers to," that is to say which it recalls to the mind, or, to use the technical [del]term[/del] [ins]usage[/ins], that which it [u]denotes[/u], is otherwise called its [u]Object[/u]. [del]It[/del] [ins]We[/ins] might, equally well, have made us call it the Subject, since there is no logical distinction between the two words. The student of Logic rarely, if ever, has any occasion to think of a word [u]as[/u] a word, — at any rate, not in the sense in which the phrase "a word as a word" contains only [u]three[/u] words, in contradistinction to the sense in which it might be said to contain [u]five[/u] words, that is, five occurrences of words, or in my nomenclature, five [u]Instances[/u] of words.
