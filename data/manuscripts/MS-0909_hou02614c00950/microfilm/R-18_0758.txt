[ins][u]General Summary:-[/u][/ins]

Here then, we have indubitably three radically different elements of consciousness, these and

no more. And they are evidently connected with the ideas of one-two-three. Immediate feeling is

the consciousness of the first; the polar sense is the consciousness of the second; and syn-

thetical consciousness is the consciousness of a Third or medium.
