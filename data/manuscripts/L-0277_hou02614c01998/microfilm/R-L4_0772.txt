UNITED STATES
INLAND WATERWAYS COMMISSION
WASHINGTON, D. C.

September 21, 1908.

My dear Sir:

Pursuant to oral assurance, please find herewith one of the few remaining advance copies of the preliminary Report of this Commission, together with two or three expository utterances of my own; also advance copies of such of the opening statements at the Governors' Conference as remain available. On their issue it will be a pleasure to send you both the final edition of the Report of this Commission (which with the appendix will form a lusty volume) and the equally bulky volume of Proceedings of the Governors' Conference.

Greatly regretting the emergency that robbed me of the pleasure of seeing you again, I remain,

yours sincerely,
W J McGee
Secretary.

Professor Charles S. Peirce,
Milford, Pike County, Penna.
