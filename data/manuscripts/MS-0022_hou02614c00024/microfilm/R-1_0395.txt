[u]Systems of Quantity.[/u]

A [u]relation[/u] is a character of an object
consisting in its forming with others
an ordered set of objects belonging
to a general class of such sets

I use our English word [u]relationship[/u]
to denote a system of relations among
a collection of objects.

By a [u]ring-relationship[/u], I mean
such a system of quaternary relations
that of a [del]certain[/del] collection of things
which are said to form a [u]ring[/u], taking
any four, A, B, C, D, there are two
pairs, say AC and BD, the objects of
[fig]small circular diagram with letters A, B, C, D[/fig] each of which have a reciprocal,
or equiparant, relation to each other
