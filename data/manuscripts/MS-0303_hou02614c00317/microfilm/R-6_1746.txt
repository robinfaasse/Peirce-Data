in the relation of relative product of P into Q

This means that if anything A is in [del]the[/del]

to C then [del]there is nothing[/del] [ins]taking anything[/ins] whatever [del]B[/del] [ins]B[/ins] [del]such[/del]
[del]that[/del] either A is not P to B or else B is not
Q to C.

we get the [del]idea[/del] conception of identity
