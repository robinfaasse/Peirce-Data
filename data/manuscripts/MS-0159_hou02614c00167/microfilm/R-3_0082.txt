The memoir running to p 182 and therefore 86 pages consists of introduction
46 articles, a Glossary, & 2 plates of [del]C[/del] 64 figures.

The words in the [u]Glossary[/u] p. 181 are as follows (I anglicize them)

[u]acyclodic[/u] ([ins]better[/ins] [u]acyclic[/u])

[u]amplex[/u], the surrounding space extending "ins Unbegrenzte" Art 5 [marginalia]Art 15 hang.[/marginalia]

[u]anastasis[/u] eine Umstellung der Complexe oder ihrer Theile ohne Eingriff in ihren Zusammen-

[u]aperiphractic[/u] Having 0 periphraxis. [marginalia]Art 37[/marginalia]

[u]attribute[/u], a number depending on cyclosis, periphraxis, and "unendliche Ausdehnung".

[u]Ausgang[/u] a node of a line. Art 19

[u]Census[/u] the relation between the number of points, lines, surfaces, and spaces and the attributes
of their constituents.

[u]Complex[/u] an aggregate of points, lines, & surfaces, whether including or excluding
the tridimensional space they enclose Art 1 and 102

[u]constituent[/u] Bestandtheile eines Complexes (Art. 1)

[u]curia[/u] Class to which the constituent of a complex belongs, i.e. whether of the
nature of point, line, surface, or [del]volume[/del] volume = Dimensionality [marginalia]Art 1[/marginalia]

[u]diagram[/u]. A linear figure to which a constituent is reduced by shrinkage
or gradual retraction of its limits. Arts 13 et seqq. [marginalia]Art 26[/marginalia]

[u]diacrisis[/u] a [del]constant[/del] number dependent on the Beschaffenheit of the complex

[u]dialysis[/u] annulment of the cyclosis Art 9 [marginalia](adits?) Art 7[/marginalia]

[u]diaphragm[/u], oder Zwerchfläche, an acyclic surface bounded by a cyclic line

[u]diatresis[/u] annulment of the periphraxis Art 37.
