[u]On a Neglected Argument for the Reality of GOD.[/u]

The first proposition of Natural Theology; what single other subject has been worn so threadbare! Moreover, whoever, from prelate to plough-boy, has deeply [del]though[/del] pondered the matter is, I suppose, familiar with the train of thought [del]which[/del] [ins]which this paper is to consider, and[/ins] which it will be convenient to designate as "the neglected argument."
