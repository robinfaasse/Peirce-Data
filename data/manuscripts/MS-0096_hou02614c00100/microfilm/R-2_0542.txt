[u]Preface[/u].

What I purpose writing in this book is a rough draught, even more hurried than hasty, (suggested by Professor Cook Wilson's book on Traverses,) of an exposition I never expect to find time to finish of ideas that for years and years have been dismissed from my mind. That it should be crammed with mistakes and other vermin of stupidity is inevitable under the circumstances.

[u]Geometrical Topics[/u] is that branch of Pure (or Formal) Geometry which is related to [u]Geometrical Graphics[/u], (as Clifford conveniently called strictly Projective Geometry) as this is to [u]Geometrical Metrics[/u].
