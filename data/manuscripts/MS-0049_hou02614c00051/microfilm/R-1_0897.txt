But if the latter pair be supposed to constitute an organic whole constituted, not by a mere fact of representation, but by some fact of being, then there is certainly no reason why they should not form a pair. But the members of a collection must not only be independently existing objects, but objects independently existing in the same sense. In short, I would define a collection as [ins]a single[/ins] object whose existence is constituted by the facts of the [del]independent[/del] existence or non-existence [ins]in one sense[/ins] of certain objects represented to be its members and by the fact that these objects which, so far as they exist, exist in the same sense, can be represented as constituting a single object.
