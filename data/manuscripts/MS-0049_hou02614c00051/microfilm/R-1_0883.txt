Speaking of a real collection. Of course, a collection
can be imagined, as well as an elephant. Just so far
as the collection is real, that is, in all respects except
its unity, it is existential. Only its existence is of a
derived nature, consisting in the existential facts
that each item of its definition exists or does not exist.
But here it behooves us to begin to tread gingerly. There
are several questions [ins]in regard[/ins] to which we must take care not
to commit ourselves unawares. Is there such a thing as
a collection of only one member? If so, is it identical with
that member? Is there a collection which comprises no
member? If so, how many such are there? If there
be such a collection, does it exist? Is the collection which
includes the daughter of Queen Elizabeth identical with
the collection which differs from it only in excluding
that [del]the[/del] member? Here are questions to be pondered.
Upon what principle shall they be answered? [del]Ones[/del]
