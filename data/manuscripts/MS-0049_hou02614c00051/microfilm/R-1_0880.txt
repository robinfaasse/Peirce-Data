Why all this? Because to explain infinitesimals,
I must explain quantity to some extent. To explain
that, I must explain number and multitude. But
"multitude," in the sense in which I use the word, is that
character of a collection which makes it greater or
less than another collection, or equal to it. I must,
therefore, define a collection. This is a difficult
task, and its difficulty lies in elucidating the mode
of being of a collection. By a collection, I do not
mean an actual whole. The Empress of China's nose,

oldest daughter, form a collection; a collection
