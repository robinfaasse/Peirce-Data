The University of Chicago
FOUNDED BY JOHN D. ROCKEFELLER

Chicago, January 11 1904.

Mr. C. P. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

I am taking very great pleasure in sending you a copt of the Logical Studies. It is a very poor return for all my indebtedness to you. I should not have mentioned the fact of my having to pay for the book at all except to indicate that if you were going to get it through the Nation I should prefer to have you get it that way.

I wish very much we could have you here some time to deliver some lecture, and shall make it a point to see if something can be done. Unfortunately, excepting in the Summer Quarter (and then the lectures are pretty popular) the custom here is rather against than in favor of these outside lectures.

Yours very truly,

Dict.J.D.

John Dewey
