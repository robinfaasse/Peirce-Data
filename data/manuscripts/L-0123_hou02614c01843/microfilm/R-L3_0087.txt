Dear Mr Peirce

I want to express my appreciation of your article in the current Monist. It would be impertinent in me to praise it, but I may express my conviction that it ought to go far in clearing [del]up[/del] [ins]and away[/ins] a lot of current misconceptions, & beating of the air — with air.

The terminology matter is of great interest to me. I have sent a little article to Woodworth's journal, which touches incidentally on the difference between -ive and -al, as endings (cognitive & cognizal) I am writing another which bears on the difference between -ive and -ic (or -ical) or -al, as cognitive and cognitional. With the tendency of philosophy to treat of specific situations in experience, it ought to be possible to find a terminology which should define situations in certain of their class characteristics

Yours very truly
John Dewey

Mr. C. S. Peirce
