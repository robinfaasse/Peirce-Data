If it were not for this uncalled for intolerance of
your logical theory, I should have no serious objec-
tion to it; and there are parts of it that seem to me
admirable and of great value. I regretted your
making everything turn on Lotze, as if he were a
Hume. He was in his day a very careful, serious
inquirer. But he was never a thinker of great
subtlety, and he is now so entirely left behind, that
I thought you might have left his doctrines to be
disposed of by Jones and men of that calibre; and
that he was rather small game for you. Whenever
