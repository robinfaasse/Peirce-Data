[pn]Point[/pn] [pn]23[/pn]

([u]h.[/u]) In backgammon, one of the narrow pieces of
leather tapering to an apex, upon which the men are
placed. [u]To make a point[/u] is to get two men upon it
when it is secured, and they cannot be taken up by
the adversary; hence, figuratively, to consider as
a matter of importance.

The rain always [u]made a point[/u] of setting in just as
he had some outside work to do.
Irving, Rip van Winkle.
