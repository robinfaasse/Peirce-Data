[del]point[/del]

(a)
14. A unit of count in a game. Originally, because it was usual to play for tagged laces.

Hence, an advantage in anything likened to a game, as "I have gained a point;" "he can give you points in boxing."

Any average Eton boy could give [u]points[/u] to His Holiness in the matter of Latin verses. [u]The American[/u] ([date]1873[/date]) [u]VI[/u]. 333.

Charles's imprudence and bad character are great [u]points[/u] in my favor.
[u]Sheridan[/u], School for Scandal, iv. 2.
