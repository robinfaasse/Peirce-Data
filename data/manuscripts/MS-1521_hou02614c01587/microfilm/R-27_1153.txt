39. Formed long ago.
Yet made to day.
I most am used
while others sleep;
What few would wish
To give away.
And fewer still,
Would wish to keep.
Ans. A bed.

40. Why is a boy doing his first sums, like
a serpent-erect?
Ans. Because he is an adder up.

41. What word of three syllables is that, the
first of which means a [del]woman[/del] mother, the
two last a woman, and the whole a des-
troyer of mankind?
Ans. Malady.

42. Why is a doctor like Job?
Ans. Because he is nothing without patients.

43. In what month do ladies talk the least?
Ans. In February.

44. What is an old woman like in the sea?
Ans. She is liked to be drowned.

45. Why is a dancing master like a tree?
Ans. Because he is full of boughs.

46. Why are two laughing girls like a chicken's
wing?
Ans. Because their is a merry throat between them.

47. Why is the North star like pen, ink, and
paper?
Ans. Because it is stationary.

49. Full of holes! Full of holes! and yet it
holds water!
Ans. A sponge.

50. Why is a person looking through a key hole
like a great city of China?
Ans. because he is peeking (Pekin).
