[u]Riddles, Conunundrums &c.[/u]

1 What was the first thing that Adam set in
the garden of Eden?
Ans. His foot.

2 What most resembles a cat looking out of a
window?
Ans. A cat looking in.

3. Who is he that has married many a wife
and yet remains single?
Ans. An unmarried clergyman.

4. Why does a miller wear a white hat?
Ans. To cover his head.

5. What tree is surrounded by the Mediterranean
Ans. The Cyprus.

6. Why is France like mince meat?
Ans. Because it has lost Buonaparte.

7. When is a door not a door?
Ans. When it is a-jar.

8. What two letters denote improvement?
Ans. X. L. (excel)
