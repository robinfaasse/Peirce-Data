Scene —
The Isle of Athelney

Alfred — How retired and quiet is every thing
in this little spot! The river winds its silent
waters round this retreat; and the
tangled bushes of the thicket fence
it in from the attack of an enemy.
The bloody Danes have not yet pierced
into this wild solitude. I believe I am
safe from their [del]attack[/del] [ins]pursuit[/ins]. I hope I
shall find some inhabitants here
otherwise I shall die of hunger. Ha!
