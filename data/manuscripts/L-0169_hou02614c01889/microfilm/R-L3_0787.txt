Dear Prof. Peirce :-

Many thanks for your letter just received, concerning your brother's book. It is gratifying to us that it meets with your approval and that you will be willing to aid in the revision of it. We shall be very glad to see the MS., and there can hardly be a doubt but that we shall want to publish it. If it were not in some respects a matter of form we would settle the question now.

Very truly yours,
Edwin Ginn.
