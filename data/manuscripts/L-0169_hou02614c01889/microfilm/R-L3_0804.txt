Dictated by

Mr. C. S. Peirce,

Dear Sir :-

We send you your brother's letter. Will you not please make this announcement read so that we shall be under obligation to publish only the Metrics now, and should not be obliged to publish the other books until we are able to do so. We should like this announcement for our forthcoming catalogue, which is just about ready to go to press.

Very truly yours,
Edwin Ginn.
