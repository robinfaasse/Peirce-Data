[date]April 12,1900[/date]

Mr. C.S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Professor Peirce:-

You are quite right about my title to the Pike County land. I shall have to do something to perfect it before I can sell it; but I hope yours is all right.

There is an old friend of mine who wants to know what objection there is to his definition of time, namely: that it is the measure of motion. Wont you give me your ideas about this definition so that I can send your letter to him?

I am going to do my utmost to get that position with the Grolier Club for you, and I think that if you get it you can make yourself invaluable to lovers of old books in this country.

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A Plimpton.
