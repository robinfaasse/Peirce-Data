Dictated by

Dear Mr. Peirce :-

Enclosed please find check for $60., which I hope will facilitate your closing up the contract for the sale of the land. I should think that if Mr. Bull would let me pay him, the cheapest way would be for you to send the warrant to me and I will deduct what I have advanced you and send the balance to you.

Very truly yours,

George A. Plimpton.
