Professor C.S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

It will not be possible for me to send the books up to you, so that is out of the question, and it will not be possible for Mrs. Plimpton and myself to go up, either.

Enclosed is a note from Dr. Pritchett. I will write to the President in your behalf. What would be better would be to get Tom Platt interested in your case.

Very truly yours,
Geo. A. Plimpton H.
