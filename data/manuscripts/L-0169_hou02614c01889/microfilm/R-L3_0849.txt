29 Beacon Street, Boston, July 13, 1903.

Professor C. S. Peirce,

Dear Sir:

We are very glad that you can find time to look over the MS. which we are sending you by express prepaid. If you can give us an opinion as to its availability for class use, this will be perhaps more nearly what we should like to know than an opinion as to its salability. If, in your opinion, it would not be available for class use, we should be glad to know if you think it could obtain any general sale among scholars.

The MS. is rather formidable and we think you will probably not need to read it all in order to gain sufficient opinion to advise us on the points which we have suggested.

Very truly yours,

GINN & COMPANY.
