My dear Mr. Peirce:-

Is there anybody under the heavens that will give you more than one dollar an acre for your land up there in Pike County? If there is, all right; but if not, I should sell it for a dollar. It is a great deal better to do this than to keep the land, which as I understand it does not bring you in anything at present.

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A Plimpton
