9-13 Tremont Place, Boston, Feb. 10, 1900.

My dear Mr. Peirce :-

Your letter I have just read. The man for you to write to in regard to your land is J. T. Rothrock, Supt. of Forestry, Harrisburg, Pa. In fact, I will write to him of you, telling him that I think he can get hold of the land, and possibly I can get him to pay more than he paid me for it.

Now you need not trouble yourself about remitting any money for the books. You are welcome to them.

I note what you say about getting somebody to take a second mortgage for you. Mr. Lothrop came in to see me about it, and he is interesting himself to see if he cannot find somebody to do it in case it is necessary. I should think the security was ample. I am sorry I cannot do anything myself, but I am all tied up.

Sincerely yours,
George a. Plimpton
