70 Fifth Ave., New York, August 11, 1903

DICTATED BY G.A.P.

Mr. Chas. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

I think you make a great mistake in increasing your mortgage on your property. I should advise you rather to pay it off than to increase it. Whatever repairs have got to be made, I should make them myself. If the house needs painting, you can get ready mixed paint and with a brush and striker you could paint the house in no time yourself.

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A. Plimpton
