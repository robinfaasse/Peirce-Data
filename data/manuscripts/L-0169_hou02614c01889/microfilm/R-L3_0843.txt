My dear Mr. Peirce:-

I will see Dodd, Mead & Co. at once, but I understand that they have a man by the name of Herbert Wade to edit the scientific department of the cyclopaedia. He was formerly a tutor at Columbia. At the same time, they may be looking for somebody else.

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A Plimpton. F.
