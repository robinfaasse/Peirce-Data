This circumstance is that we have to give away a very large number
of all our publications, and, as we give them to teachers, the net
result is, in the case of a book like yours which would be a
teachers' book rather than a text-book, that we kill the author's
market. While, therefore, we strongly advise you to have the book
published, and do not hesitate in the least to predict for it a
cordial welcome and the highest esteem of those most competent to
judge of the work, we feel equally clear that it would be better,
both for you and us, that it were placed in the hands of a different
sort of a house. We regret very much that our investigations have
led to this conclusion. We are much disappointed that we must fore-
go the pleasure of issuing the book for you, but it is clearly for
your interest that we commend the work to some other house.

Thanking you for the high compliment you have paid us
in proposing that we issue it, we remain,

Very truly yours,
