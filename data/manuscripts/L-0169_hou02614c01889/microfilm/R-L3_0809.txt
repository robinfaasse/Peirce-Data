70 Fifth Ave., New York. March 21,1896

Mr. C. S. Peirce
160 West 87th Street

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

I am sorry to have missed you when you called. Now the reason why I have not written you about making a catalogue of my books is that I haven't any money. I have been at tremendous expense this year, and it is on that account that I feel as though I must economize in every way I can. I wish I might have you make the catalogue for me.

I trust Mrs. Peirce is well.

Very truly yours,
Geo. A. Plimpton
