Milford Pa [date]1899 Nov 15.[/date]

My dear Mr. Plimpton:

That Washington place has been filled. The appointee is a person of no experience and of [del]small[/del] trifling achievements. The promise solemnly made that "no consideration whatever except ability should have the slightest weight" has been shamelessly broken, and I have been kept on tenterhooks since April 20 when the appointee had been decided upon, as I am informed, in advance. A blow has been struck at the eminence of American science.

The Cyclopaedia of Practical Information appears more useful the more I examine it. I shall commence a systematic examination of it at once.

You think the best course is to organize a corps of 25 road agents. So do I. At any rate, I will at once begin the work. They will insist on 40%, though I shall struggle to make them take a round dollar and freight free. I am assuming the price is to be $3 a copy.
