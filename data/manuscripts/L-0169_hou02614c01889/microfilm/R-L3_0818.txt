Mrs. C.S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Mrs. Peirce:-

Enclosed is a letter from C.W. Bull. I wrote you some time ago as to the importance of putting this property into the hands of someone else than Mr. Peck. I hope you can bring this about very soon. Mr. Peck is no longer in the city, and I cannot very well look after the matter. If I were in your place, I should have just as little as possible to do with lawyers.

I enclose also a note from Philbin & Beekman.

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A. Plimpton
