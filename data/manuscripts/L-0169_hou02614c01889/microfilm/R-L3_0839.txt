My dear Mr. Peirce:-

In regard to your letter about the price of the land, they offered two dollars for my land, and I accepted the offer; but I imagine that when they came upon the ground they found they could buy plenty of the same kind of land for a dollar and a quarter,- and so they crawled out of the bargain on that account. I understand the state has taken a lot of land which has the same sort of title that mine has. Why don't you say to him that you would be willing to submit an offer of $1.25 to your principal, if they would agree to pay that?

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A Plimpton
