July 14, 1894.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear sir;-

Your valued letter of July 10th is duly received.

Mr. Plimpton, like the rest of the firm, will regret very much being obliged to forego the pleasure and satisfaction of working for a book of yours, but, as we endeavored to explain, our decision seemed to be forced upon us.

It is, in a way, gratifying to us to see that the conclusions of our reader were not wrong, that is to say, you write yourself that the book is not a text-book. We can agree with you perfectly, for we said, as you say, that your work is one of great and mature thought.

You ask, at the conclusion of your letter, why we do not return the programme of your Index Raisonnée. We enclose this.

Very truly yours,,

Ginn & Co
