Mr. Charles S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

If you will have Mr. Bull draw up the affidavits, I will see that Mr. Peck signs them and executes the same; also the deeds. I am mighty glad that you are to get the money for this property so soon, and I hope this will relieve you of your embarrassment.

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A Plimpton
