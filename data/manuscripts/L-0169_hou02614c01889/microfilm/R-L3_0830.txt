70 Fifth Ave., New York.

Dictated by G.A.P.

Professor C.S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Professor Peirce:-

Your letter is at hand. My mathematical books are still in storage. I expected to have been in my house the first of December, but there has been this delay.

I did not understand that you wanted any books. If I had I should have been glad to send them to you. I am perfectly willing to let you have these books at almost any price that is at all reasonable. You can pay me a dollar or .75 a volume, and all above that you can get you are welcome to. If you will send me orders, I will send the books to you, and then when you collect the money from these men you can send it to me.

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A. Plimpton
