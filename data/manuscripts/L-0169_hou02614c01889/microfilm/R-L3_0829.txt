Nov. 28,1899

Mr. Charles S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

Enclosed find my check for twenty-five dollars which I think is what you need to help you out on your mortgage. I don't want to see your roof sold over your heads.

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A Plimpton
