_Dictated by_

Dear Prof. [ins]J.[/ins] Peirce :-

I send you your brother's MS. with a copy of our letter to him, wishing that you would look the MS. over to see what adjustments and adaptations are necessary to make the book a practical text-book. What we both want is the same thing, - a good working book for schools and colleges, leaving for a later period the philosophical portion.

Very truly yours,
Edwin Ginn.
