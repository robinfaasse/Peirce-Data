Dictated by G.A.P.

Mr. C.S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Prof. Peirce:-

I have just arrived home, and find your letter. Enclosed please find the release executed.

I am sorry that you have had hard luck this summer. I bought quite a number of very interesting books abroad. I have a copy of the manuscript of Euclid, written in 1300; a complete set of Dutch elementary mathematical books; a copy of the first Euclid printed in England and also a copy of the first Euclid printed in English. I should like very much to have you look these books over.

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A Plimpton
H.
