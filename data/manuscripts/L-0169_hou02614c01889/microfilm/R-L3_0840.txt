My dear Mr. Peirce:-

I should be very glad to let any of your friends have the whole lot of those encyclopaedias at $1.00- It is my impression that there are nearly 1500 of them. The books are so that a new title-page can be inserted. There are no maps to go with the book, and that mention should have been omitted.

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A Plimpton
N.
