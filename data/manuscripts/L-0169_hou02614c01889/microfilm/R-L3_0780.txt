Professor C. S. Pierce

Milford, Pa.

Dear Professor Pierce:-

I will send your table of antilogarithms together with your letter to Boston, and I will see what we can do in regard to the matter. I am afraid, however, that you have put a rather high estimate on the amount of money there is in something of this sort; but I will see what we can do.

I looked over that book which you spoke of, and found the paper giving the list of the authors and remarks about them.

We enjoyed your little paper on Thessaly the other evening very much.

Believe me,

Sincerely yours,

George A. Plimpton
H.
