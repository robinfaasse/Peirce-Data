Oct. 26,1899

Mr. Charles S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Professor Peirce:-

My books are all in storage. I have given up my house on 35th street and have purchased one on 33d street, which I am fixing over. If I could get hold of my books, I would gladly utilize you until January 1st. The trouble is, I cannot get hold of them.

While in Europe this year I bought quite a number of interesting mathematical books and manuscripts, which I should like to have you look over. For instance, I got a MS. of Euclid written in 1300; a copy of the first Euclid printed in England in Latin and the first one printed in English also; and I happened to be in Amsterdam and bought the collection of Mr. Müller, who had been collecting Dutch arithmetics for thirty years,- also bought a lot of very rare books at the Hague. But I cannot do anything about them probably until the first of December. I have written to Secretary Gage and also to Dr. Pritchett, as per the enclosed, which I hope will be of some use to you.

I am very sorry to hear about the accident to Mrs. Peirce's hand.

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A Plimpton
