My dear Prof Peirce:

Your card is at hand.

I am off for the South Saturday & shall
be gone until March 1st. If you are here on
my return I shall like then to do some
work for me on the mathematical books.

I trust Mrs Peirce is better. Believe me

with kind regards, yours
