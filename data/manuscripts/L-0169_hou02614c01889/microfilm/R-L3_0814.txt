_70 Fifth Ave., New York._ [date]Oct. 19,1898[/date]

_Dictated by_ G.A.P.

Mrs. C.S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Mrs. Peirce:-

Your letters I find upon my return to the city this morning. I have sent check for $52.86 to B.C. Totten, Collector, addressing him at Milford, as you did not state where to direct the letter. I have said that the indebtedness tax is paid under protest. I am sorry that you cannot get someone to transfer the property to. Mr. Peck, you know, has not been well, and is away, and for that reason I think the quicker it is transferred out of his hands, the better.

I am sorry to hear that Professor Peirce has been so ill. I will try to look up this man, to whom you refer, General Terrero_

Sincerely yours,
[fig]signature of Geo. A. Plimpton[/fig]
