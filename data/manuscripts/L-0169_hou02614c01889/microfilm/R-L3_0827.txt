Mr. C.S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

What I am willing to do on this cyclo-
pedia is just this. You pay me a dollar each, and whatever you
get over and above that, you are welcome to. I have plenty of
books on hand, and I would let them go for cash. I am so fixed
that I cannot go into any business with these books; in fact, I
cannot do anything with it at all myself. I have been hoping
that some fellow would come along and take the whole thing off
my hands. I am not sure myself whether there is anything in it or
not. You could probably tell yourself better after you have tried
it. What I should do would be to take it myself and try and see
if I could make it go, and if I could I would then see if I could
not find some other fellow that could make it go, and begin that
way.

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A Plimpton
N.
