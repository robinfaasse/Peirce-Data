Mr. C. S. Peirce,

Dear Sir :-

I forward your letter to your brother, Mr. J. M. Peirce, this morning, and I wish you and he would largely work out this problem together, for I feel that you can [del]yoursel[/del] [ins]yourselves[/ins] do it more intelligently than it is possible for us to do it. What we want is a good revision of your father's book, with such additions as will make it even more popular than his was and cause it to meet with even a greater sale, perhaps.

Very truly yours,
E. G.
