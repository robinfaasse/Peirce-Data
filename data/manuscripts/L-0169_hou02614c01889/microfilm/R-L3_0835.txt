*Dictated by* G.A.P.

Professor C.S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Professor Peirce:-

I return the receipts which you'd better file away.

Last night I was at the Grolier Club. The librarian is goin to leave, and I suggested that you were the man above all others who ought to have that position; that you could read medieval Latin, etc. They do not think of filling the position until fall, and I imagine they would not pay much over $1500 or possibly $2000; but they would want only a few hours a day of your time, and I think it would be a good thing if you could get that and then have something else to occupy the rest of your time. I told them I would write to you and see how you felt about it.

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A. Plimpton
H.
