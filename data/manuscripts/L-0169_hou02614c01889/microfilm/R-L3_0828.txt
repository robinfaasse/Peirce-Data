Dictated by G.A.P.

Mr. Charles S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

Since writing you this morning I have had a talk with Walter Page, who you remember was editor of the Atlantic Monthly, and also editor of the Forum for many years. He is now coming with Doubleday & McClure to edit the great encyclopaedia. I tell Page that I think he can get hold of you, and if he does he will get a very rare, strong man. I wish you would send to Mr. Page an abstract of what you have done. His address is care of Doubleday & McClure, 141 E. 25th street. Please do this at once. I have shown him your article on Ford's Franklin.

Sincerely yours,
[ins]per[/ins] G. A. Plimpton
H.
