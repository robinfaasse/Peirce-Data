Jan. 18,1900

Mr. Charles S. Peirce
Milford, Pa.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

I like your idea about the Columbia Senate Committee's Reports, and will do what I can to favor it.

I note what you say about your land. It is possible that I may be able to get you an offer of two dollars an acre for it. As I understand it, you have 900 acres. Don't place any hopes upon this, however. Perhaps you can do a great deal better than this.

Sincerely yours,
Geo. A Plimpton
