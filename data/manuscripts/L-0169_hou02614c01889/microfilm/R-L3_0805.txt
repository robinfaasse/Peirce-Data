Dictated by

Dear Prof. Peirce :-

I have asked the opinion of a good critic on the child's book in Geometry proposed by your brother, and he hardly dares recommend the publishing of a work of this kind without seeing the whole of it. It is a very difficult task, and one of the hardest books in the world to write is a child's book, especially on a subject so difficult to handle as Geometry. I cannot make a recommendation, for I partially realize the great difficulty in undertaking anything of the kind. I thank you, however, for your kindness in forwarding the sample pages. I wish I could say something more definite.

When the book is completed we shall be very glad to examine it.

Very truly yours,
Edwin Ginn
