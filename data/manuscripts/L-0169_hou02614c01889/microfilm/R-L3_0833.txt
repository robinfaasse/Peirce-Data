70 Fifth Ave., New York. March 9, 1900.

Mr. Chas. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

My dear Mr. Peirce:-

I find your letter on my return from Boston. Now, I have sent to the Second National Bank of this city $100. to be deposited to [del]your[/del] [ins]her credit[/ins] [del]credit.[/del] This will enable Mrs. Peirce to pay taxes on that land. I have not made much headway yet in regard to selling the land to the state, but I hope to have some good news to report before long. I am glad you are all right about the mortgage.

I wish you would send me the manuscript of your editor's manual whenever it is ready. What I would like mighty well is to put you in our Boston house in the editorial department, if in no other capacity than that of simply seeing that our books have good English and are properly punctuated, etc.; but this is not so easy to do.

Sincerely yours,

Geo A Plimpton

G.
