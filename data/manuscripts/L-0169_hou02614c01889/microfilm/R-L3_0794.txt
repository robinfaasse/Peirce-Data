Ginn & Company, Publishers
OF
SCHOOL AND COLLEGE TEXT-BOOKS.
BOSTON. NEW YORK. CHICAGO. LONDON.

7-13 Tremont Place, Boston, March 8, 1895.

Dictated by

Prof. J. M. Peirce,

Dear Sir :-

I forward you your brother's letter, together with a copy of my reply, wishing that you and he would bring this matter into the best form possible for publication as a practical book, and leave the theoretical portion for a second volume. I am very much afraid that if we put too much of the theoretical into a book of this kind, it might frighten the ordinary teachers so that they would not use the practical part.

Very truly yours,

Edwin Ginn.
