My dear Mr Peirce

Your note of 18th came duly and
it has given me great pleasure to
write to Mr Brett, the President of the
Macmillan Co, in reference to the matter
of work on the “International Monthly”

I hope you will have an opportunity to
make this a really valuable Review

Yours sincerely
Henry Rutgers Marshall

Chas. S. Peirce Esq
