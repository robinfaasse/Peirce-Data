Milford Pa [date]1904 Apr 16[/date]

My dear Cam:

I enclose you a piece by me on the Metric System, although you may have seen it. It is a matter I am very much in earnest about, and I should be glad to know that my arguments met your approval.

I am very sorry that I have never seen your book. I should have liked to read it.
