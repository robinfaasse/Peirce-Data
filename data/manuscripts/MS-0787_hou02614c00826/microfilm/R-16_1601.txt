This is the nature of the assertory act, which logicians have found
great difficulty in describing and which some of them have pronounced
to be [u]sui generis[/u] and unanalyzable. To a sign which gives reason to
think that something is true, I prefer to give the name of a [u]symbol[/u];
although the words [u]token[/u] and [u]symptom[/u] likewise recommend themselves.
That symbol which thus forms a principal part of an assertion may
be termed its [u]copula[/u], or asserting verb.
