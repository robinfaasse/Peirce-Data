To this opinion, Prof. Schröder, following Sigwart, dissents. He
makes the validity of necessary inference depend upon a necessity
of thinking. He makes (p. 13) the correctness of a feeling "the last
anchor-hold of all certainty." It is true that he endeavors to dis-
tinguish logical necessity of thought from psychological neces-
sity. But he does not succeed in establishing the distinction.
He seems to think that it helps the distinction to say that the laws
of logic are not natural but normative laws; but that is quite
beside the question.
