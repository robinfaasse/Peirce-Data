Naval Observatory
[date]1872. May 8[ins]th[/ins][/date]

Mr. C. S. Peirce
Dear Sir:

A short time ago
Professor Winlock informed me that
you are about to mount at the Office
of the Coast Survey one of Zöllners
photometers. When the instrument is ready for
use I shall be very much obliged if you
will permit me to see the operation of
it.

Very respectfully yours
A. Hall
