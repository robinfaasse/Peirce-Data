[u]Solutions of Spherical Triangles[/u]
[u]without logarithms[/u]

1 [u]Given two sides, [u]b[/u] and [u]c[/u] and the included angle A[/u]
[u]Required to find opposite side a[/u] -

Calculate subsidiary angle N, by formula
2 cos N = [del]1/2[/del] cos (b-c) - [del]1/2[/del] cos (b+c)
Then
2 cos a = cos (b+c) + cos (b-c) + cos (N+A) + cos (N-A)

2. [u]Given two angles A and B and side a opposite one of them.[/u]
[u]Required to find side opposite the other[/u]
Calculate two subsidiary angles M and N by
formulae
2 sin M = cos (a-B) - cos (a+B)
cos N = sin A - cos M
Then 2 sin b = tan [u]M+N[/u]/2 + tan [u]M-N[/u]/2
