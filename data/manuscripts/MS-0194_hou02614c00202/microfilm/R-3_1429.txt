$\cos A = \frac{\cos a - \cos b \cos c}{\sin b \sin c}$

$\cos C = \frac{\cos c - \cos a \cos b}{\sin a \sin b}$

If $c = 90^\circ$
$\cos A = \cos a \cdot \csc b$
$\cos h = \cot a \cot b$

If $C = 90^\circ$
$\cos a = \cos A \csc B$
$\cos h = \cot A \cot B$
