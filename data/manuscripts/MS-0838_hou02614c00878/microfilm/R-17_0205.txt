[u]The Justification of Reasoning[/u]

The purposes of this essay are
1[ins]st[/ins], to render evident the nature, degrees, and conditions of the different kinds of inference;
2[ins]nd[/ins], to disprove certain current beliefs on the subject;
3[ins]rd[/ins], to draw from the true doctrine, herein set forth, some important consequences.

The subject will not be developed from its elements; but the different modes of inference, taking the more evident before the less evident, will be successively examined, and the different conceptions they involve will be introduced as they come to be needed.
