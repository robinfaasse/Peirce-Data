Coexistence is the only [del]Tr[/del] [ins]dyadic[/ins] (anti-alio-anti-concurrent) relation,
that is, the only relation between [del]pairs[/del] all pairs of the form
A:A and all pairs of the form A:B. Incompossibility is
the only [del]alio[/del] concurrent alio relation; that is the only relation
which subsists between no pairs of the form A:A and between
none of the form A:B.

A notation for these two logical relations is thus
provided. But there are two others for which notations
have to be provided. These are the concurrent anti-alio-
relation, which I term [u]identity[/u], in which every indivi-
dual stands to itself alone, and the anti-concurrent
alio-relation, [del]is[/del] which I term [u]otherness[/u], in which
every existing individual stands to every one except
itself.
