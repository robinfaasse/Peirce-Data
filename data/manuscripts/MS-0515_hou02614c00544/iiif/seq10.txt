[u]Corollary[/u] 8. If an enclosure contains nothing unenclosed, and
if the same proposition is a component of every the contents
of every enclosure once enclosed, this same proposition
may be iterated unenclosed. That is, from
[fig]((XY)(XZ))[/fig],
we may infer
X [fig]((XY)(XZ))[/fig].
For if [u]XY · XZ[/u] is true, by the definition of a conditional pro-
position, if XY is true XZ is true; whence by Cor. 3, X is
true. But if XY is not true, XY is true, whence again X is
true. Hence, by Rule II, X may be inserted.
