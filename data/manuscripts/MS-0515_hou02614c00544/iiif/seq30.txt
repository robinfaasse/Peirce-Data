The notation which I employ in my general algebra of
logic for such a proper name applied to an indefinite indi-
vidual is this. If p means ‘is a patriarch’ and ‘is
translated,’ instead of writing
[del]e-p-e-t[/del] p-e·t-e
I write
Σₑ pₑ·tₑ
The sign Σ [u]simulates[/u], and merely simulates, a sign of logi-
cal aggregation. If there are only 5 individuals in the
universe, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, we should have
[del]p[/del] p-1·t-1 + p-2·t-2 + p-3·t-3 + p-4·t-4 + p-5·t-5 = Σₑ pₑ·tₑ
and so it would be for any multitude whatsoever, assuming
that [del]a collection[/del] [ins]any multitude[/ins] is capable of linear arrangement.
But if I were talking of the points of a line, there could be no
logical aggregate of these, since they do not constitute a collection;
