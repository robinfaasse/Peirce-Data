'Something is an orator and Something is a Roman,' we
should have no right to unite [del]them[/del] the two lines of identity,
since 'something,' though individual, is indefinite.

How shall we express analytically 'Some Roman is not
an orator'? One excellent way, quite in the spirit of
mathematics is to suppose the indefinite Roman
who is not an orator to be definitely known, and write
