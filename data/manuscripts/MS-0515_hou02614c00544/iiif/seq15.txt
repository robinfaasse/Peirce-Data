[ured]Corollary 14. Aggregation and composition are mutually distributive; so that[/ured]
[ured]x · (y + z) = x · y + x · z[/ured]
[ured]x + y · z = (x + y) · (x + z).[/ured]
The [ins]the latter formula[/ins] proof was given by me in 1867, the former having been substantially given by Boole.
