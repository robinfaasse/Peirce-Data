I could not, within reasonable limits, put everything I have to say in an evidently convincing [ins]shape.[/ins] [del]manner.[/del] I shall go much further than is expected in a mathematical paper, but I hope not further than is warranted.

Looking at experience in the widest possible sense, to include all that appears to us even in our mathematical hypotheses, — therefore at what is better called the phenomenon, in general, — we discern three radically different kinds of elements in it. The first kind may, by generalizing the meaning of a word, be called 'flavors'. Take blue, for example. It may be that we only remark it by its contrast to other colors; but certainly its blueness is not mere not-redness; it is as it is in itself.
