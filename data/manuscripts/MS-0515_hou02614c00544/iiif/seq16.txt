of the relate are included among the relates of the correlate;
so that	If [u]M[/u] is [u]r[/u] to [u]P[/u],
	and [u]S[/u] is [u]r[/u] to [u]M[/u],
	then [u]S[/u] is [u]r[/u] to [u]P[/u].
