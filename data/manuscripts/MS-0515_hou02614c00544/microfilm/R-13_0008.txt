We provide ourselves, therefore, with a surface
which we call the [u]sheet of assertion[/u]; and pretend to
hold ourselves responsible for the truth of whatever
we may write upon it.

But as long as it remains blank we are irrespon-
sible. Hence, the first rule of transformation will be,

[marginalia]
Rule I.
[/marginalia]

[del]Everything[/del] [ins]The whole of what is[/ins] written on the [u]sheet of assertion[/u] may be
erased, without danger of falsehood.

a proposition, A, on the sheet of assertion, and that
we should be justified in writing a proposition,
B, on the sheet of assertion. It will then be very
natural to write A and B in different parts
of the same sheet, as if they were written on
different sheets. Hence, we adopt as the second
rule:
