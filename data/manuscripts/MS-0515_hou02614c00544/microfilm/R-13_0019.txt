[u]Corollary 10[/u]. An aggregant can be inserted under even en-
closures or vincula, erased under odd enclosures or vincula.

[u]Corollary 11[/u]. Aggregation is commutative.

[u]Corollary 12[/u]. Aggregation is associative. These corollaries
appear from the corresponding properties of composition, as soon
as the aggregates are written in their full form.
