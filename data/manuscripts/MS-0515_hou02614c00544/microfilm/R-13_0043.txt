[pn]§7.[/pn] There is a certain logical relation of the utmost importance,
We may denote it, for the moment by I. It is not only transitive, so
that
If X is I to Y,
and Y is I to Z,
then X is I to Z;
but it is also continuous, so that
If A is I to Z,
then A is I to something (say B) that is I to something (say C) ---
--- that is I to something (say X) that is I to something (say Y) that is I to Z;
and in short, any multitude whatsoever of intermediaries might
truly be inserted. This relation, which is that of absolute identity, so
that each individual is so related to itself alone, is most appropriately
represented by a continuous heavy line which may be ever so long
or may be shortened to a hyphen. Thus A---B will mean
A is B, C--- that C is something, [fig]three-way line junction[/fig] that some three
things (or designations) are identical, [fig]line with branch[/fig] is a mother of a son that is ---,
--- is a black thing that is ---, having obvious interpretations.
