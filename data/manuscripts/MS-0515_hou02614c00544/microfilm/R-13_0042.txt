[u]§4.[/u] Suppose we wish to express, "If X is true, Y is
true," and that analytically, so as to exhibit iconically the
relation [del]of[/del] [ins]between[/ins] X and Y. We must, then, in some way, write
down X and also Y. But neither being asserted of itself,
neither can be written unenclosed and detached from
the other.
