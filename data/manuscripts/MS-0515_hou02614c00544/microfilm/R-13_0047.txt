Another logical relation, that of coexistence is represented
by the blank. Thus from
Tully — Cicero
"Tully is Cicero" we can infer
Tully — [del]Cicero[/del] [ins]— Cicero[/ins]
"Tully is coexistent with Cicero" i.e. Tully exists and Cicero exists.
