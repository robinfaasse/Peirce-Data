§7. There is a certain logical relation of the [ins]utmost[/ins] impor-
tance. We will denote it [del]for the moment[/del] [ins]for the moment[/ins] by I. It is not only transitive, so that
[del]if[/del]
If X is I to Y
and Y is I to Z,
then X is I to Z;

but it is also continuous so that
If A is I to Z
then A is I to B, B is I to C, - - - - X is I to Y, Y is I to Z,
and, in short, any multitude whatsoever of intermediates exists.
This relation, which is that of [ins]absolute[/ins] identity, is most appropriately
[del]rep[/del] represented by a continuous heavy line between its relate
and correlate; thus:
A [u]   [/u] B [u]   [/u] C [u]   [/u] D [u]         [/u] X [u]   [/u] Y [u]   [/u] Z.

it reads 'A is something', 'something is B', 'something [del]is[/del]' 'something
