[u]Coexistence[/u] is the only [del]anti-alio[/del] [ins]anti-concurrent[/ins] relation, that is, the only relation between [del]pairs[/del] all pairs of the form A:A and all pairs of the form A:B. [u]Incompossibility[/u] is the only [del]alio[/del] concurrent alio-relation; that is the only relation which subsists between no pairs of the form A:A and between none of the form A:B.

A notation for these two logical relations is thus provided. But there are two others for which notations have to be provided. These are the concurrent anti-alio-relation, which I term [u]identity[/u], in which every individual stands to itself alone, and the anti-concurrent alio-relation, [del]in[/del] which I term [u]otherness[/u], in which every existing individual stands to every one except itself.
