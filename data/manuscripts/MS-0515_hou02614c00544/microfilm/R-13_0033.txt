[del]An undetached line of identity may be written unenclosed[/del]

We see that every unenclosed point of a line of identity denotes an indefinite individual, "something." Consequently, two [del]non-definite but[/del] non-individual terms being joined by a line of identity, the meaning will be that there is some individual which both represent.

[fig]Line connecting "is a patriarch" and "is translated"[/fig]
