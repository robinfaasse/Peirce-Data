But two such propositions simultaneously asserted [del]thereby parts[/del] [ins]are combined[/ins] into one copulative proposition by logical composition. We have, therefore, in Rule II, adopted a blank space as the sign of logical composition of propositions.[ins]*[/ins] As an alternative sign, I use a heavy dot above the line.

[u]*My habit has been to use the blank as the sign of relative multiplication, as it is used when in trigonometry one writes "sin x." It goes against the grain to change this habit, but the above reason forces me to do so.[/u]
