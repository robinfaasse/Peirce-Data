The axiom therefore that all [del]propositions[/del] are convertible fails if the distinction of subject & predicate be kept up. Now suppose that the distinction is abrogated. Then it follows immediately that a proposition & its converse are logically identical. True that it will be necessary in considering syllogisms to distinguish All A is some B from Some A is all B but this is a subsidiary distinction altogether dependent merely upon the transposition of the matter of the terms; the two being All A is some B & All B is some A. Distinct forms of propositions they are not. Hence these two forms should be struck out.
