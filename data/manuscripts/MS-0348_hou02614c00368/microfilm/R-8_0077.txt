Denotation, Information, Connotation.

Corresponding to these [ins]three[/ins], are evidently certain conceptions of objects in general. To denotation corresponds the conception of an [u]object[/u], to information the conception of a real kind, and to connotation the conception of a [del]logos[/del] [ins]character[/ins] or quality. So to Induction corresponds the conception of a [u]Law[/u], to Hypothesis the conception of a Case under a Law, and to Deduction the conception of a Result.

There are also principles of the Judgment corresponding to these conceptions of which we have instances in the laws that all things, forms, symbols are symbolizable.
