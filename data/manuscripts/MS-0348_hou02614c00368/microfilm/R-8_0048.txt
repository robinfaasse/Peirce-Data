There are certain pseudo-symbols which are formed by
combinations of symbols [del]or[/del] and which must therefore be
considered in logic which lack either denotation or conno-
tation. Thus, [u]cats and stoves[/u] is a symbol wanting in con-
notation because it does not purport to relate to any de-
finite quality. [u]Tailed men[/u] wants denotation; for though
it implies that there are men & that there are tailed things;
it does not [del]deny[/del] say that these classes are mutually exclusive.
All such terms are totally wanting in information.

In short the formula
[u]Connotation[/u] x [u]Denotation[/u] = [u]Information[/u]
holds good thoroughly.
