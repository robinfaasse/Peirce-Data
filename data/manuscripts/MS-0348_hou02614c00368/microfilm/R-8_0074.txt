A or B or C is Some A is B
B or C or D is Some A is not B

I have described this scheme of the thorough-going qualification
of the subject in order to show how easy it is to produce a system
quite as thorough-going and far more rational than Hamilton's.
There is no definite quantification of the predicate in either affir-
matives or negatives. Definite quantification is such as determines
limits of extension. Now no affirmation or negation has any
thing to do with the limits of the predicate.

The scheme of thorough-going qualification is a consistent so-
lution of the problem set before us namely to produce a uniform
system of propositions introducing the formula of definition.
