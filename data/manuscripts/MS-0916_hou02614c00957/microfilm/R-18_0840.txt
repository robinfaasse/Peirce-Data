[u]The Modus of the TT.[/u]

There are three Celestial Worlds. 1 that whose Heaven is a speck, or the manifold of sense 2 that whose Heaven is of extensive manifestation or the world of consciousness 3 that whose heaven is of immense manifestation or the world of abstraction.

Consciousness is the only one of the worlds which is real & tangible to us. How shall sense become consciousness? This is not meant as a skeptical question; I ask it of faith.

[del]This triad are alike in being worlds[/del]

The relations of the triad being apprehended, it will be clear that which is in the sensible world can only enter the mental world by having in it a [u]revelation[/u] which is in the abstract world [del]This quality of abstract revelation is dependence.[/del]

Now there are three abstract revelations; that whose abstraction is [del]in a world[/del] [ins]of time[/ins] [del]is of a temporal world[/del] or arbitrariness, that whose abstraction is [del]of[/del] a world of space or dependence, & 3 that whose abstraction is [del]in a world of[/del] [ins]in a[/ins] celestial world or absoluteness. Of these only dependence is revelation to us. [del]How then was the abstraction prevented from sinking to mere temporal abstraction & turning to revelation[/del] [ins]Why, we may ask, is it that [del]was[/del] [ins]the[/ins] abstraction [del]was[/del] [ins]was[/ins] [del]more than as written[/del] [del]not mere[/del] [ins]and[/ins] [del]of mere[/del] spacious not temporal[/ins] & the revelation dependence not arbitrariness? Because to the arbitrariness was joined absolute existence.
