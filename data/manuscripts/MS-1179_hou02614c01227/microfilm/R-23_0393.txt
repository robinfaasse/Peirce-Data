If the principle that spelling ought to be phonetic were once admitted, a dozen different [del]sys[/del] systems of making it so would at once compete for the preference. This is certain, because, even at present, without the smallest prospect of adoption, there are the Philological Association's proposal, Mr. Ellis's "glossic," Mr. Melville Bell's "Visible Speech," Pitman's "phonotypy," and several others.
