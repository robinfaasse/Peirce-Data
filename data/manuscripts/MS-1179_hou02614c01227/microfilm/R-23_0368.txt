[pn](6)[/pn] [u]An Apology for English Spelling.[/u]

[marginalia]Ms. 1179[/marginalia]

The learned body of linguists, the modern scientific
substitutes for the philologists of other days, men who
set amazing store by such tongues as Lithuanian, Moeso-
gothic, Irish, Basque, and Old Egyptian, and who would
gladly exchange all our knowledge of classical Latin for
an equal acquaintance with the plebeian speech of ancient
Rome, have small love for modern English, unless it [del]is[/del] [ins]be[/ins]
dialectical; and they tell us that "our standard typographi-
cal spelling is a mishapen changeling, a standing
disgrace to our literature." To me, I confess, it [del]does[/del] [ins]hardly[/ins]
seems [ins]highly[/ins] becoming in a scientific person to fly into a
passion with the facts that it is his business to study.
