The whole system of dyadic monosynthemes of
the sixth order may be obtained as follows:

Write down any one of them, separating it into
3 pairs, one above and two below. Call the
upper one "pair 0" the two below "pair 1" and "pair 2"
There are, of course, three ways of doing this, [del]from[/del]
[del]the[/del] without distinguishing pair 1 from pair 2. From
this, according to a rule I will state presently, you
form 4 others to be called the N, E, S, and W of the
first; and these 5 will make up a pentakis-
syntheme, of which there are 6 in all. Since the
[del]pr[/del] operation of forming the N, E, S, W, is ambigu-
ous, it will give two different pentakis synthemes
and any one of the 5 will have its two systems
of N, E, S, W, of which one will be the same pen-
takis-syntheme and the other a new one. The
rule for forming the N, E, S, W, will be as follows.
Let $\alpha$ be the primitive syntheme. Then the first and
second letter of pair 0 will be respectively the
last letter of pair 1 of [del]the N, E,[/del] $N\alpha$, $E\alpha$, $S\alpha$,
and $W\alpha$ and the first letter of pair 2 of the same,
and conversely, the last letter of pair 1 of $\alpha$ and
the first letter of pair 2 will [ins]taken[/ins] in this order, be the
first and last letters of pair 0 of $N\alpha$, while the
last letter of pair 0 and the first of pair 1, of $N\alpha$,
$E\alpha$, $S\alpha$, $W\alpha$, respectively will, in this order form
pair 0 of $E\alpha$, $S\alpha$, $W\alpha$, $N\alpha$; the last letter of pair 2
of $N\alpha$, $E\alpha$, $S\alpha$, and $W\alpha$ respectively will [del]be[/del] the first
letter of pair 1 of $E\alpha$, $S\alpha$, $W\alpha$, and $N\alpha$; and the first
letter of pair 0 of $N\alpha$, $E\alpha$, $S\alpha$, and $W\alpha$, respectively
will be the last letter of pair 2 of $E\alpha$, $S\alpha$, $W\alpha$, $N\alpha$
[del]and the first letter of pair 1 of $\alpha$ will be the last of[/del]
pair 2 of $\alpha$ will have the same places in $N\alpha$.
In general if we write $E = EN$, $S = EE$, $W = ES$, $N = EW$,
and write [del]p[/del] $f_n$ and $l_n$ for the first letter of the
