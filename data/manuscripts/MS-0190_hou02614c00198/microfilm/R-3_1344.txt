<u>Divisor 7.</u> Beginning at the right hand of the [del]augend[/del] number separate it
into periods of six figures. Write the different periods [ins]are[/ins] under [del]another[/del] [ins]each other[/ins] and
add; and repeat the operation until there [del]is[/del] [ins]are[/ins] no longer more than one
period. Separate the six figures into two periods of three figures
each, and subtract the left hand period from the right hand [ins]period[/ins] adding
7 to the first figure of the latter if necessary. Having thus obtained a
number less than 1000, multiply the hundreds by 2 and the tens by
3, and add the products to the units. Then the remainder of
the original number after division by 7 will equal that of the
sum last obtained.
