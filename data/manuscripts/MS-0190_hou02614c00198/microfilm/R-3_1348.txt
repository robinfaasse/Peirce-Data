[u]Arithmetic. Integers[/u] [u]Cyclic Numeration.[/u]

Suppose we want to know on what day of the week will fall a day
which is, say, 143 days later than a Wednesday. We reckon as follows:

Wednesday is the 4th day of the week, or 0 weeks + 4 days

from the previous Saturday,

142 days are 20 weeks + 2 days

Adding we have 20 + 6 days, from that first Saturday.

The day in question is therefore the 6th day of [del]the[/del] [ins]a[/ins] week, or Friday.
