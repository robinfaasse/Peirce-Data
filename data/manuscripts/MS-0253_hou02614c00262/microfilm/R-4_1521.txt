This equiparance involves the [u]Commutative Principle[/u] as an immediate consequence.

It does not involve the [u]Associative Principle[/u]; but the [del]Associative[/del] [u]Principle[/u] follows from the fact that the conjunction merely unites the conjugends, without modifying them.
