The letters of the Boolean that are written on the line are equivalent to the Spot-graphs ([ins]Convention IV, §1, [/ins] Syllabus, p. 17) or Existential Graphs. The indices are equivalent to the Selectives (Syllabus, Conv. VI) and the [del]pla[/del] several places adjacent to the letters on the line which are suited to indices of the subjects are equivalent to the hooks of the Spot (Syll_._ Conv. IV, ¶ 1). The attachment of an index to a letter on the line fulfills the function of a line of identity.
