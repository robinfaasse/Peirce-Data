[fig]Cross-shaped logic table with letters E, A, I, O[/fig]

In this table you will perceive that the three figures are entirely
separated. the middle part is the 1[sup]st[/sup] figure, the top & bottom wings
the 2[sup]nd[/sup] figure, and the side wings the 3[sup]d[/sup] figure. Consequently, there can
be no inference in two ways from the same data. This is made
still more clearly if we have three tables instead of one so as to
bring the inferences into the middle in all cases. The symmetry
is thereby made very striking.
