A pyramid was simply a tomb with a secret chamber in
which was deposited the mummy of a king, because of a belief
[ins]in[/ins]
[del]about[/del] resurrection. That was no doubt the king's sole purpose.
The priests who counselled him had, of course, like all priests,
their personal reasons for what they did. It was very convenient for them
that every king should require a separate college of priests for all
time to take care of his corpse. Any belief which involved the em-
ployment of exceptional skill and learning and made a great deal of work
would be encouraged by them. But there is not the slightest reason
