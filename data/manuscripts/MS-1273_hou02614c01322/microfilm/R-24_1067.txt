The casing-stones of the pyramid (at least, the lower ones) were
cut with great perfection. Mr. Petrie says: "the mean variation
of the cutting of the stone from a straight line and from a true square, is
but 0.01 inch on a length of 75 inches up the face, an amount of
accuracy equal to most modern opticians' straight-edges of such
a length."[ins]‡[/ins] The stones weighed from 10 to 20 tons. They were wholly
transported from Turra and Maesara, ten miles away [ins]and across the river.[/ins][ins]§[/ins]
