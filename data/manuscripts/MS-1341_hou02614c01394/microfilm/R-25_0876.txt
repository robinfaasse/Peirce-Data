No wonder, then, that the classifications are various:
the different writers are classifying different things.
But this is not all. Even where the things classified
are the same, what one writer is trying to do in
classifying them is entirely different from the
aim of another. Every classification whatever is necessa-
rily governed by a purpose, or idea, however loose a one.
