Ethic is divided ([u]VII[/u] i) into the Doctrine of Exemplar
and the Georgic of the Soul. Good Exemplar is divided into
the Simple Good and the Comparative Good. The Simple Good
is divided into Individual Good and the Good of the Commu-
nity. The Individual Good is divided ([u]VIII[/u] ii) into the Active and
the Passive. The Passive Good is divided into Conservative Good
and Perfective Good. Good of the Community is divided into
General and Respective Offices. The Doctrine of the Culture
of the Soul is divided ([u]VIII[/u] iii) into the Doctrine of Characters of Souls,
the Doctrine of Affects, and the Doctrine of Remedies.
