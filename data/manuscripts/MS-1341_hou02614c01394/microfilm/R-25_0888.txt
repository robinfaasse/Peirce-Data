Elenchs of Sophisms, Elenchs of Hermenia ([del]a doctrine[/del]
For avoiding errors of reasoning depending on the con-
ceptions of more, less, same, other, power, act, habit,
etc), and Elenchs of Images (clutes & Idols of the Tribe, of
the Market place, of the den, of the theatre.) An appendix to
the art of Judging relates to the Analogy of Demonstration
for the Nature of the Subject. The Art of Retaining is
divided ([u]Vv[/u]) into the Doctrine of Aids to Memory and the
Doctrine of Memory itself. The Doctrine of Memory itself
is divided into Prenotion and Emblem.

The Art of Importing is divided ([u]VI[/u]) into the Doctrine
of the Organ of Speech, the Doctrine of the Method of Speech,
and the Doctrine of the Illustration of Speech [ins]or Rhetoric[/ins]. The Doctrine
of the Organ of Speech is divided into the Doctrine of the
Marks of Things, and Locution, and Scription. The two last
together make up Grammar. The Doctrine of the Marks
