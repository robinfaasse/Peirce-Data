The symbol [u]E[/u]ₙ has no effect upon a constant, nor
upon a [del]member of a succession which varies[/del] [ins]independently of n.[/ins] Thus,
[u]E[/u]ₙ [u]a[/u] [u]u[/u]ₙ = [u]a[/u] [u]E[/u]ₙ [u]u[/u]ₙ
[u]E[/u]ₙ [u]u[/u]ₙ₋ₘ [u]v[/u]ₘ = [u]u[/u]ₙ₋ₘ₊₁ [u]v[/u]ₘ .

The subscript to the enlargement is always a single
letter. If it were a complex expression, it would be
necessary to state what was to be remain constant.
Thus, we should have
[u]E[/u]ₘ₊ₙ [u]u[/u]₂ₘ [u]v[/u]₂ₙ = [u]u[/u]₂ₘ₊₁ [u]v[/u]₂ₙ₊₁ .
