We shall have to do with a mode of dependence of
one quantity upon another constituting the former what
may be called a [u]logical function[/u] of the latter. In order to
get a distinct notion of the difference between a [u]logical[/u]
and a [u]mathematical function[/u], it may be well to note,
first, some features of mathematical functionality.
