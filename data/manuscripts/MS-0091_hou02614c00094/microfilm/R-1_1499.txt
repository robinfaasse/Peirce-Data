[del]ones[/del] of values that are neither values of [u]u[/u] nor values of [u]v[/u].

Let us place a sign of multitude in any angle to signify that there are at least as many values of the description belonging in that [del]enclosure[/del] [ins]region[/ins], as that sign of multitude indicates. As such signs of multitudes, we may use [del]one[/del] [ins]one[/ins], two, or three dots for one, two, three, the Arabic numerals for larger finite multitudes, $\infty$ for the denumeral multitude [fig]diamond with one dot[/fig], [fig]diamond with two dots[/fig], [fig]diamond with three dots[/fig], [fig]diamond with four dots[/fig], etc for [del]the[/del] successive ab-numeral multitudes, ①, ②, ③, etc for continua of 1, 2, 3, etc dimensions. Thus, [u]u[/u] [u]v[/u] will signify that [u]u[/u] and [u]v[/u] have at least one [del]common[/del] value, etc.
