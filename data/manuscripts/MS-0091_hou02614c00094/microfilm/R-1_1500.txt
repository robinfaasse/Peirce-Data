We have hitherto regarded $u_1, u_2, u_3, \dots u_m \dots$ as
[del]making up a particular[/del] [ins]quantities[/ins]. But the [del]former[/del]
conception must be taken that each member of the
succession is a general description which may be
applicable to many quantities, to one only, or to none.
Here the following rule of notation is called for

<u>Rule of Notation 1</u>. An equation between two ex-
pressions each of which is a general description of
quantity shall signify that whatever quantity there
may be which either member of the equation will
describe is among the quantities which the other
member of the equation will describe.
