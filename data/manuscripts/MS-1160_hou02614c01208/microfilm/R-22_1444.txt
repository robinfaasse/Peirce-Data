Baker's Chronicles says
of Henry VII
No man ever more loved
or less doted upon a Wife
than he.

The bird called a dotterel
is a bird that apes whatever
you do according to Bacon
a Drayton

So in May's Old couple dotterel
means a very pliable, acquiescent
person

When a tree is called dotard
or doted, the word is a corruption
of doddered.
