[u]dote[/u]
What is it to dote? Our phrase-
ology "He dotes" "A doting
old man" "A dotard" seems
to imply that it is doing something.
So too in French we have it in
a frequentative form radoter.
Now frequentatives generally signify
some way of acting.

In Chaucer's Chanouns Yemannes
Tale, we read
In al this world of falshede nis his peer;
For in his termes he wolde him wynde,
And speke his wordes in so sly a kynde,
Whan he commune shal with any wyght,
That he will make him [u]doten[/u] anon ryght,
But it a feend be as him-selven is.
This means to [del]make the par[/del] [ins]do as[/ins] whatever the
other party [del]wishes[/del] suggests.
