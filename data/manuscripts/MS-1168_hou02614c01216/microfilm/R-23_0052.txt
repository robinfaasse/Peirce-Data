PHOENIX. A southern constellation introduced by Theodori, or Keyser, at the end of the XVIth century. It is situated south of the tail of Cetus, and north preceding alpha Hydrus. It contains one second, two third, and two fourth magnitude stars.

PISCES. A constellation and sign of the zodiac, the fishes. The figure represents two [del]fishes[/del] fish united by a ribbon attached to their tails. One of the fishes is east and the other south of the square of Pegasus.

PISCIS AUSTRINUS. An ancient southern constellation, the southern fish. It contains the 1.3 magnitude star fomalhaut, which is 30 degrees south of the equator and is in opposition on the third of September. The figure represents a fish which swallows the water which is being poured out of the vase of Aquarius.

PISCIS VOLANS. Otherwise called Volans, the flying fish, one of the southern constellations introduced by Theodori, or Keyser, at the end of the XVIth century. It is situated west of the star [del]bet[/del] beta Argus, and contains two fourth magnitude stars.

PICTOR. An abbreviated form of Equuleus pictoris, the painter's easel, a southern constellation introduced by Lacaille, about the middle of the last century. It is situated west of Argo, and contains one star of the fourth magnitude.
