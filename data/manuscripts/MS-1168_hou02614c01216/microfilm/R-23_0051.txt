[u]Power[/u] In math., the result of multiplying a quantity into itself a number of times. The Nth power of X, X taken N times as a factor. In this sense power is a translation of Latin potentia and Greek dynamis, which seems to have had the meaning of a square in ancient Greek. However, it usually means the side of a square, not the area, a square root, or surd; and the same is true of the latin word, even down into the XIVth century; the meaning of a quantity multiplied into itself is however also ancient.
