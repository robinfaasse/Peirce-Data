Laertius and other collectors of philosophical nugae quite often
refer to sophisms and such trifles as having some connexion
with Megara, and that the insane doctrines of the old philosophers
(which no human being can ever have really believed,)
of Velia, or Elea, seem to have been [del]once[/del] in vogue there and to have
been upheld by Euclides, which sufficiently shows his utter incapacity as a
reasoner.
