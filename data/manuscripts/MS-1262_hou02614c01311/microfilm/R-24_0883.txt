Do you ask where Chorasmia is, or was? It was
the kingdom of Khiva, south of (and earlier extending
also to the westward of) the Sea of Aral. It was bounded
on the south by Bactria, and was, in all probability,
included in that great Bactrian Empire which, as
you perhaps remember from your Sunday-school lessons,
existed down to the time of St. John. You will
soon see why I mention its neighborhood to Bactria.
