We next come to a great mathematician, François Viète. He
was a wealthy lawyer who wrote eleven or more mathematical works
which he had privately printed in small numbers and distributed
them as gifts. They were, therefore, not widely known; and his
[del]Canon Mathematicus[/del] of [date]1579[/date] was so full of misprints that
Viète almost immediately endeavored to recover and destroy
all the copies, although there is one in [ins]Cassel.[/ins] Stockholm. In this almost
unknown book [del]we[/del] it is said that true decimals occur, or some-
thing very like them. But they remained unheard of.
