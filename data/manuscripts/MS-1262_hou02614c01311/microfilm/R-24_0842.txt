I have set these down at the risk of boring you, reader, not to speak of myself, for the sake of enforcing the proposition that absolutely nothing is required for numerals except that the sequence of them should be perfectly familiar to both interlocutors. This being the case, it is a very remarkable fact that, among all the races of mankind that have adopted systems of numeration, there should not be a single one which has not constructed its system of numerals with a view to counting upon the fingers. Of course, I am not speaking of the degraded or primitive races which appear to have no systems of numeration, but only a few names for the lowest numbers. That what I say [del]is true[/del] of the others is true I have convinced myself by examining about 150 different systems, most of which are to be found in a little book entitled 'The Number Concept' by Dr. Levi Leonard Conant, a work of [ins]very[/ins] [del]some[/del] convenient for the purpose, although the author's ideas do not always seem to me to be very clear.
