[pn]24[/pn] It is by supposing that he, or still better that Gerbert himself, (who, though the boldest of men, was also extremely prudent, was so apprehensive of the [u]sequelae[/u] of the stories about his having learned the black art from the Saracens that he (whichever of the two it was) determined, at least so far as it was possible without a direct lie, to convey the idea that Gerbert never had any intercourse with Arabians. This decidedly complicates the theory but not in a way difficult to [del]be[/del] believe.
