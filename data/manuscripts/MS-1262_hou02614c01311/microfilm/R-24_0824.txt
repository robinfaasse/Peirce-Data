Him the Khaliyfeh commanded to prepare a [u]popular treatise[/u] on computation. The Latin translation of this, which is believed to be by Adelard, and which has been printed entire by Prince Boncompagni (in his [u]Trattati d'aritmetica[/u]), beginning "Dixit Algoritmi", presents a remarkable clear and terse exposition of the general system of computation that we still use. It need scarcely be said, that the [del]method of handling the system[/del] is different in some details. Thus, the old "scratch" rule of division is alone given, and the old rule for extracting the square roots, and there are special rules for [del]halving[/del] [ins]doubling[/ins] and halving, as there are in all the earliest text-books. Nor are there any decimal fractions.
