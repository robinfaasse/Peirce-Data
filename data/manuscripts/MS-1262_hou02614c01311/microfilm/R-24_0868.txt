Do you know why [ins]it is that[/ins] a trade secret is so well kept? Of course you do, — or will as soon as you think about it. It is because it is a source of profit to whoever knows it, on condition that it be kept from the public. Hence, whoever finds it out, instead of making it public, goes into the business, and makes a profit of his knowledge.

Can you tell me, then, [del]under what[/del] [ins]define the[/ins] circumstances under which a trade secret comes to be common property? Do you say that it is when so many know it that their competition destroys the profit? That does not cover the majority of cases; but all cases are covered in the statement that it becomes public as soon as there are many people who are interested in knowing it, without having any particular interest in preventing others from knowing it.
