At this point we enter upon a great battle-ground of the
historians of arithmetic. The general question is whether our
numerical figures came to us from any other source than from
the Arabians. The special question that we now encounter is
whether or not Gerbert studied at the Moorish university of
Cordova. I deduce my logical principles from certain facts
open to the observation of all men and from certain proposi-
tions in mathematics. I am not going to inflict upon the
reader a treatise upon logic; but I will say that my general
method of investigating logic once admitted, and especially
the principle that whether a given mode of reasoning is sound
