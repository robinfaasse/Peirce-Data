Gerbert that I took from the [u]Fasciculus Temporum[/u], this will have
the advantage of affording you some information about his
character and environment which will be needed in forming an
opinion about the source of his nine figures; and this course
particularly recommends itself because the [u]Fasciculus[/u] confines its
account to his [del]early[/del] [ins]later[/ins] life, so that in forming the acquaintance of
the man you will not need to prejudge any question that be-

with the evil one is as far as possible from being a surprise. The
same thing was said of Roger Bacon in the relatively cultured
age of Henry III and Edward I. How could it be otherwise with
a true scholar in spirit who found himself immersed in the
deepest blackness of the dark ages, when reading seemed to

plishment? Even in the reign of Queen Elizabeth, the learned
Dr. Dee was forced to leave house and home and become a
