Marietta, Ohio, [date]Nov. 24, 1903[/date].
Prof. C. S. Peirce;
Dear Sir:
Prof. Mitchell and Mrs. Ladd
Franklin (who, according to Prof. Syl-
vester, were of the same order of mind)
became acquainted at Johns Hopkins.
They esteemed each other very highly,
and, during Prof. Mitchell's residence
here, exchanged letters now and then; —
once, I remember, in regard to Prof. Mitch-
ell's short controversy with Prof. Venn. I
think it was Mrs. Franklin who wrote
the obituary notice in the Nation.
Yours most truly,
Charles H. Goddard
