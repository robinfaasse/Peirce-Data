As each Conception is only for the purpose of bringing the mani-
fold of intuition to Unity, it is clear that were it not for this con-
ception either the intuition itself would fall apart or else some in-
dispensable conception would detach itself from the intuition. This
conception which would [ins]thus[/ins] detach itself, is clearly the one which enables the
conception [del]which unites it with the intuition[/del] [ins]other conception[/ins] to be applied. Hence, if in-
quiring what, each conception unites with the object immediately perceived,
we find what is the next conception in order, in passing from the
unity of Being to the manifold of sensation.

Being itself unites subject & predicate, or the thing and its attributes
or quality. The subject or thing is the immediate object; while the conception
of quality or character, [del]this is[/del] is the reference of the thing to its ground
or reason of being. A thing, we say, is, by virtue of being some how.
But somehow is not immediately perceived, because it is general. Thus,
when I say the [del]stone is black[/del], I conceive the stone as having a quality
which may belong to other things. In short, it is a thought and there is no
thought in sensation. Reference to a ground, then, is the first conception
under being.
