The proof of (83) and (84) is simple enough. By (77) 
and (78), if x ~ y ~ z is scriptible, then if z is scriptible

(78) if x is both individual and definite the statement 
just made is equivalent to x ~ y ~ z, which is (84). Thus, 
the whole theorem is proved.
