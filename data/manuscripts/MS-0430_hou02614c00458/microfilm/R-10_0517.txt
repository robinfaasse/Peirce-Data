We now commence observations from the vital definitions.

I shall begin with giving interpretations of the simpler algebraical consequences in case [del]x[/del] [ins]v[/ins] is individual and in case it is definite. I shall then set down some corollaries independent of the characters of v and some other elementary propositions; after which the general algebraic problem of the algebra will be stated and solved.
