[u]Theorem II[/u]. The consequence, [u]x y z[/u], with two antecedental propositions has the following equivalents:

[marginalia]
(79)
[/marginalia]
1[ins]st[/ins], with [u]v[/u] individual, it equals the proposition that if both [u]x[/u] and [u]y[/u] are severally scriptible, then [u]x[/u], [u]y[/u], [u]z[/u], are all jointly applicable to [u]v[/u];

[marginalia]
(80)
[/marginalia]
2[ins]nd[/ins], with [u]v[/u] definite, it equals the proposition that if either [u]x[/u] or [u]z[/u] is applicable distributively to [u]v[/u], and if either [u]y[/u] or [u]z[/u] is applicable distributively to [u]v[/u], then [u]z[/u] is scriptible;

[marginalia]
(81)
[/marginalia]
3[ins]rd[/ins], with [u]v[/u] both individual and definite, it equals the proposition that if both [u]x[/u] and [u]y[/u] are applicable to [u]v[/u] severally, then [u]z[/u] is applicable to [u]v[/u].

[u]Demonstration[/u].
