$r - r = 0$ or $r = r$;
That is, it does not rain.

Let us write the two premises
If it rains, it snows;
If it snows, it hails.

The expressions will be
(4) $(r - s)(v - s) = 0$
(10) $(s - h)(v - g) = 0$.

Multiply (4) by $(v - g)$ and (10) by $(r - s)$. We get,
(11) $(r - s)(v - s)(v - g) = 0$
(12) $(r - s)(s - h)(v - g) = 0$

The sum of (11) and (12) is
$(r - s)(v - s)(v - g) = 0$
which by (a) gives
(13) $(r - h)(v - g) = 0$
