1[u]st[/u], if [del]invariable[/del] neither [u]x[/u] nor [u]y[/u] is [del]applicable[/del] [ins]scriptible[/ins];

2[u]nd[/u], if it sometimes happens that both [u]x[/u] and [u]y[/u] are

3[u]rd[/u], if [u]x[/u] is sometimes scriptible, but [u]y[/u] never is so.

This amounts to saying that [u]x[/u] [u]y[/u] is scriptible in case,
it is true that if [u]x[/u] is sometimes [del]applicable to v[/del] [ins]scriptible[/ins], then both
[u]x[/u] and [u]y[/u] are sometimes so applicable, just as with an
individual definite [u]v[/u] the consequence means if [u]x[/u] is
[del]applicable to v[/del] [ins]scriptible[/ins], then [u]x[/u] and [u]y[/u] are so applicable.

For example, let [u]x[/u] mean 'it rains', and [u]y[/u] 'it blows'.
