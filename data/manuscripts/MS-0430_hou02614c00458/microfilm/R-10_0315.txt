[u]Theorem VIII[/u]. The scriptibility of $x \prec y$ and $y \prec z$ severally implies no other relation between $x$ and $z$, independent of $y$ than that if $x$ is scriptible, so is $y$, so that, $V$ being both individual and definite, $x \prec z$ is scriptible. This last may be called the Rule of Transitivity.
