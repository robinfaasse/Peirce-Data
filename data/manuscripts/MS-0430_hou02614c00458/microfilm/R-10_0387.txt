The Haupt-Satz, or Principal Theorem, of the algebra is,
that any consequence capable of being completely written
out is scriptible, whatever the several significations of its
letters, if, and only if, in every one of its ultimate conse-
quentials, some evenly antecedental element and
some antecedental proximate constituent element are
the same letter.

But before attacking this proposition, it will be
well to prove some subsidiary theorems.

(67) Every consequence of the form [u]x[/u] [u]x[/u] is scriptible.
Proof. By (64) either [u]x[/u] is scriptible or [u]x[/u] [u]x[/u] is scriptible.
But, by (65), if [u]x[/u] is scriptible, [u]x[/u] [u]x[/u] is scriptible.
Hence, in any case, (67) holds.
