two ovals one inside the other, any unenclosed part of it can
be iterated within the two ovals. For let the unenclosed parts
to be iterated be A, and the rest that is unenclosed be B.
Let the singly enclosed part be C, and the doubly enclosed
part be D. Then the graph before iteration will be

while after the iteration it will [del]be[/del] [ins]have[/ins] become

[fig]Existential graph with nested ovals containing letters A, B, C, D, A[/fig]

I have to prove that if what the former asserts is true, so is
what the latter asserts.
