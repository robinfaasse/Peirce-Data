[pn](74)[/pn] Hence, if any consequence, $y \prec z$, is scriptible, then [ins]any[/ins] consequence [del]$(x \prec y) \prec (x \prec z)$[/del] [ins]of which $y$ is the antecedent of this scriptible consequence,[/ins] is the consequent of the antecedent, [del]the consequent of the scriptible consequence,[/del] [ins]and $z$[/ins] is the consequent of the consequent, and the antecedent of the antecedent and the [del]consequence[/del] [ins]antecedent[/ins] of the consequent are the same expression, is scriptible.

[pn](75)[/pn] Further, if any consequence, $x \prec y$ is scriptible, then any consequence, $(y \prec z) \prec (x \prec z)$, of which, $y$, the consequent of the scriptible consequence is the antecedent of the antecedent, and $z$ the antecedent of the scriptible consequence, is antecedent of the consequent, while the consequent of the antecedent, and the consequent of the consequence are the same, is scriptible.
