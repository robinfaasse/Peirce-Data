A. If [u]x[/u] [del]is[/del] [ins]y[/ins] is scriptible, then [u]y[/u] is at least as scriptible

[pn](68)[/pn] B. If [u]y[/u] is at least as scriptible as either [u]x[/u] or [u]y[/u], taken

"it rains [fig]symbol[/fig] it blows" is scriptible. This will be false in case
it always rains or blows but sometimes rains without blowing.
The distributive sense of either [u]x[/u] or [u]y[/u] being scriptible, is that
of each individual [ins]of[/ins] to which the interpreter of the proposition
has a right to consider it applicable, either [u]x[/u] is applicable
or [u]y[/u] is applicable.

[pn](69)[/pn] C. If both [u]x[/u] and [u]y[/u], taken jointly, are at least as scrip-
