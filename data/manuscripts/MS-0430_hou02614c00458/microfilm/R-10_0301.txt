scriptible, then both $\underline{x}$ and $\underline{y}$ are applicable to $\underline{v}$ jointly, is

true in case $\underline{v}$ is individual.

Assume, then, that if $\underline{x}$ is scriptible, both $\underline{x}$ and $\underline{y}$ are
applicable to $\underline{v}$ jointly.

$\underline{x}\ \underline{y}$ is scriptible.

But $\underline{v}$ being individual there is no distinction between
the distributive and collective sense.

$\therefore$ Either $\underline{x}$ or $\underline{y}$ is applicable to $\underline{v}$ collectively or $\underline{x}\ \underline{y}$ is
scriptible.
