[pn](20)[/pn] $v - \frac{(v-p)(v-n)(v-g)}{(v-f)^2} = f$; that is, $\frac{v-p}{v-f} \cdot \frac{v-n}{v-f} \cdot \frac{v-g}{v-f} = 1$

Its assertion is asserted by

[pn](21)[/pn] $\frac{v-p}{v-f} \cdot \frac{v-n}{v-f} \cdot \frac{v-g}{v-f} = 0$

These forms speak to us. We assert that it rains, snows, and hails in the equation

[pn](18)[/pn] $\frac{p-f}{v-f} \cdot \frac{n-f}{v-f} \cdot \frac{g-f}{v-f} = 1$.

We assert that it both rains and snows in

[pn](22)[/pn] $\frac{p-f}{v-f} \cdot \frac{n-f}{v-f} = 1$.

We assert that it rains in

[pn](23)[/pn] $\frac{p-f}{v-f} = 1$.

[pn](24)[/pn] $r = \frac{p-f}{v-f}$;

$s$ for it snows, making

[pn](25)[/pn] $s = \frac{n-f}{v-f}$;
