(79) If $(x\bar{y})\bar{y}$ is scriptible, either $x$ or $y$ is scriptible.
For, by (66), if, [del]$(x\bar{y})\bar{y}$ being scriptible, $x\bar{y}$ is
scriptible, then $y$ is scriptible, that, by (64), either $(x\bar{y})\bar{y}$[/del]
[ins]$x\bar{y}$[/ins] is scriptible. For, by (64), either $x$ is scriptible or
$\bar{x}\bar{y}$ is scriptible, when, by (66), $(\bar{x}\bar{y})\bar{y}$ being
scriptible, $y$ is scriptible.

(80) If either $x$ or $y$ is scriptible, $(y\bar{x})\bar{x}$ is scriptible.
For, by (64), if $x$ is scriptible $(y\bar{x})\bar{x}$ is scriptible. And
if $y$ is scriptible, and likewise $y\bar{x}$, then, by (66), $\bar{x}$ is
scriptible, and consequently, as just seen, $(y\bar{x})\bar{x}$ is
scriptible. But, by (64), either $y\bar{x}$ is scriptible or $(y\bar{x})\bar{x}$
is scriptible. Hence (80).
