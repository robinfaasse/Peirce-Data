By the proximates of an algebraical consequence shall be
meant those constituents which are not constituents of other
constituents. Thus, in $(a \prec b) \prec (c \prec (d \prec e))$, the proximates are

constituents which would be proximates if the consequence were in
standard form.

By the ultimate of an algebraical consequence shall
