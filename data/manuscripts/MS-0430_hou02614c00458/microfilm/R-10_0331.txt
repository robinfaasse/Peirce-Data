a quadratic equation of the form

[u](v - x)(x - f) = 0[/u]

The first member of this equation is a product; but a pro-
duct cannot equal [u]zero[/u] unless one or other of its factors
equals [u]zero[/u]. Hence, the equation implies that either

v - x = 0, [del]or[/del] [ins]or[/ins] x = v,
or x - f = 0, or x = f.
