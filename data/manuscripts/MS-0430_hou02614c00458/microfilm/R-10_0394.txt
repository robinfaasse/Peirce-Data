[pn]Logic
III
68[/pn]

[pn](67)[/pn] consequences having several proximate antecedents,
any consequence [del]that is in the written out,[/del] [ins]whatever[/ins] is scriptible unless all its
proximate antecedents are scriptible.

<u>Proof</u>. Assume, for the moment, that this proposition
is true for every consequence having fewer than a certain
number, N, of proximate antecedents. Let us, then, con-
sider a consequence having N antecedents, which
we may designate as $\Omega_1$, $\Omega_2$, $\Omega_3$, etc up to $\Omega_N$,
and let $\Psi$ be its ultimate consequent. We assume, then,
that either $\Omega_2$, $\Omega_3$, etc up to $\Omega_N$, are all scriptible, or
else $\Omega_2 \prec \Omega_3 \prec \dots \prec \Omega_N \prec \Psi$ is scriptible. But, by
(65), under the last alternative $\Omega_1 \prec \Omega_2 \prec \Omega_3 \prec \dots \prec \Omega_N \prec \Psi$
is also scriptible. So that either $\Omega_2$, $\Omega_3$, etc up to $\Omega_N$ are
all scriptible or $\Omega_1 \prec \Omega_2 \prec \Omega_3 \prec \dots \prec \Omega_N \prec \Psi$ is
scriptible. But, by (64), either $\Omega_1$ is scriptible or
$\Omega_1 \prec \Omega_2 \prec \Omega_3 \prec \dots \prec \Omega_N \prec \Psi$ is scriptible. Hence,
either $\Omega_1$, $\Omega_2$, $\Omega_3$, etc up to $\Omega_N$ are all scriptible
or else $\Omega_1 \prec \Omega_2 \prec \Omega_3 \prec \dots \prec \Omega_N \prec \Psi$ is scriptible.
