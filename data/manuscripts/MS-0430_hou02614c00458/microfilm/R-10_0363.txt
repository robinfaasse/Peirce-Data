Let us call this the [u]Rule of Transposition[/u]: namely, that antecedents of one consequent can be transposed. Whatever follows by virtue of this rule may be said to hold "by transposition."

(70) [[u]x[/u] < [u]y[/u] < [u]z[/u]] < [u]y[/u] < ~ [u]x[/u] < [u]z[/u]
