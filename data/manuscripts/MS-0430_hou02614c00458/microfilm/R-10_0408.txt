(84) If $(x \prec z) \prec z$ and $y \prec z$ are both scriptible, then
$(x \prec y) \prec z$ is scriptible.

Proof. For, by (79), if $(x \prec z) \prec z$ is scriptible, either
$x$ is scriptible or $z$ is scriptible. But if $x$ is scriptible,
then, by (66), if $x \prec y$ is scriptible $y$ is scriptible; whence,
if $y \prec z$ is scriptible, again by (66), $z$ is scriptible. Thus,
in any case, if $(x \prec z) \prec z$ and $y \prec z$ are both scrip-
tible, then if $x \prec y$ is scriptible $z$ is scriptible. Hence,
by (68), if $(x \prec z) \prec z$ and $y \prec z$ are both scriptible,
$(x \prec y) \prec z$ is scriptible. Q.E.D.
