"Sometimes it is both hot and cold" is not necessarily true, although it be true both that it is sometimes hot and that it is sometimes cold. To say that $V$ is definite, is to assert that the principle of contradiction applies to its predicates; i.e., that, whatever $X$ may be, $V$ is $X$ and $V$ is not $X$ cannot both be true.
