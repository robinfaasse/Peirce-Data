scriptible, it will be true that $\mathfrak{S} \prec \mathfrak{T}$ is scriptible.

<u>Proof</u>. For, by (65) if $\mathfrak{T}$ is scriptible $\mathfrak{S} \prec \mathfrak{T}$ is scriptible.
Hence, if under the hypothesis if $\mathfrak{S}$ is scriptible, $\mathfrak{S} \prec \mathfrak{T}$
is scriptible. But, by (64), either $\mathfrak{S}$ is scriptible or $\mathfrak{S} \prec \mathfrak{T}$
is scriptible. Hence, $\mathfrak{S} \prec \mathfrak{T}$ is scriptible in any case.
