On the other hand, the following [ins]do[/ins] are not generally hold:

Either [u]x[/u] is scriptible or [u]x[/u][u]y[/u] is scriptible.

This fails if [u]v[/u] is not individual. For in that case the form
becomes: Either it always rains, or else sometimes neither rains
nor blows, or else always blows. This is not true, since we
have seen, under (70), that in this case, it may always rain or
blow without either always raining or always blowing. But
by (70) it must hold when [u]v[/u] is individual.

[pn](76)[/pn] If [u]y[/u] is scriptible, [u]x[/u][u]y[/u] is scriptible.

This fails if [u]v[/u] is not definite. For in that case the form
becomes: If it sometimes blows, then if it sometimes rains,
it sometimes both rains and blows. But, as seen under (71),
when [u]v[/u] is indefinite, it may sometimes rain and sometimes
blow without doing both. But, by (71), when [u]v[/u] is definite,
the (76) must hold.
