[u]The Vital Definitions of the Algebra of the Copula.[/u]

I. The letter [u]v[/u] may be taken to denote any object conforming to the following two conditions:

[marginalia]
(64)
[/marginalia]
[u]a[/u]. [u]v[/u] is individual; so that whatever is ever true of it, is always true of it.

[marginalia]
(65)
[/marginalia]
[u]b[/u]. [u]v[/u] is definite; so that whatever is [del]not[/del] [ins]of it in some sense[/ins] is true in every sense.

[marginalia]
(66)
[/marginalia]
II. There is supposed to be a [u]sheet of assertions[/u] on which any proposition that is written is [u]asserted[/u] to be applicable to [u]v[/u], or as we shall say, is asserted to be [u]scriptible[/u]. The signification of any term is its signification when asserted.

[marginalia]
(67)
[/marginalia]
III. The sign [u]⤙[/u], or the [u]Copula of Inclusion[/u], signifies that on whatever occasion and in whatever sense, the antecedent, or term to the left of [u]⤙[/u], [del]is[/del] [ins]maybe[/ins] scriptible, on that occasion and in that sense, the consequent, or term to the right of [u]⤙[/u], is scriptible. It is said that if the antecedent is scriptible, the consequent [del]must be[/del] [ins]is[/ins] scriptible; or is the [del]consequence[/del] [ins]algebraical consequence[/ins], or whole composed of antecedent, copula, and consequent, rendered false [del]in[/del] [ins]but[/ins] in case the antecedent is never in any sense, scriptible; [del]for[/del] on the contrary, in that case the consequence is true.

[marginalia]
(68)
[/marginalia]
IV. The term [u]x ⤙ y ⤙ z[/u], whatever terms [u]x[/u], [u]y[/u], [u]z[/u], may be, is equivalent to [u]x ⤙ (y ⤙ z)[/u].
