The letter [u]v[/u] is scriptible.

That is, by (66), it is applicable to that to which [u]v[/u] is applicable.

(70) [del]Indeed[/del] Any term, [u]x[/u], is equivalent to [u]v[/u] [u]x[/u].

For, by (66), the signification of any term is that which it represents is applicable to [u]v[/u]; and by (67) the signification of [u]v[/u] [u]x[/u] is that [u]x[/u] is as applicable to [u]v[/u] as [u]v[/u] itself is.

(71) Whether any term, [u]x[/u], be scriptible or not, [u]x[/u] [u]v[/u] is scriptible.

(72) Whatever terms [u]x[/u] and [u]y[/u] may be, supposing [u]x[/u] [u]y[/u] to be scriptible, if [u]x[/u] is scriptible, so is [u]y[/u].

This follows from (67), regardless of what the character of [u]v[/u] may be. Suppose, for example, that [u]v[/u] were what sometimes happens, referring to an individual instant but an indefinite instant.
