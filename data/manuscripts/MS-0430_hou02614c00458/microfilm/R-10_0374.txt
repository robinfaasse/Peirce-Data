By an [u]ultimate form[/u], I mean the form of a consequence
every one of whose first antecedental constituents is a
prime antecedental constituent.

By [u]an ultimate consequential of a consequence, C[/u], I
mean any consequence, D, in ultimate form which
corresponds to C in the following way:—

Every oddly antecedental element of C that has
anything in D corresponding to it, is represented in D
by the same letter as an oddly antecedental element;
and every evenly antecedental element of C (excluding
it ultimate consequent) that has anything in D corre-
sponding to it, is represented in D by the same letter
as an evenly antecedental element. The letter which
is the ultimate consequent of C is the ultimate conse-
quent of D and likewise of every antecedental
constituent of D that is a consequence.
