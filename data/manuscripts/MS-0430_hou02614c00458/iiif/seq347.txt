The Vital Definitions of the Algebra of the Copula .
I. The letter [u]v[/u] may be taken to denote any object conforming to the 
following two conditions:
[indent]$\alpha$. [u]v[/u] is [u]individual[/u]; so that whatever is ever true of it, is always true of it.
$\beta$. [u]v[/u] is [u]definite[/u]; so that whatever is true of it in [del]some[/del] [ins]some[/ins] sense is true in every [del]some[/del] [ins]sense[/ins].[/indent]
II. There is supposed to be a [u]sheet of assertions[/u] on which any proposition
that is written [u]is asserted[/u] to be applicable to [u]v[/u], or as we shall say, is
asserted to be [u]scriptible[/u]. The signification of any term is its signification
when asserted.
III. The sign [u]$\prec$[/u], or the Copula of Inclusion, signifies that on what-
ever occasion and in whatever sense, the antecedent, or term to the left
of [u]$\prec$[/u], [del]is[/del] [ins]maybe[/ins] scriptible, on that occasion and in that sense the consequent,
or term to the right of [u]$\prec$[/u], [u]is[/u] scriptible. It is said that if the antecedent
is scriptible, the consequent [del]must[/del] [ins]be[/ins] scriptible; [del]nor[/del] [ins]algebraical[/ins] is the [del]consequence[/del],
or whole composed of antecedent, copula, and consequent,
rendered false [del]if[/del] [ins]in case[/ins] the antecedent is never in any sense,
scriptible; [del]for[/del] [ins]but[/ins] on the contrary in that case the consequence is true.
IV. The term [u]x $\prec$ y $\prec$ z[/u], whatever terms, [u]x[/u], [u]y[/u], [u]z[/u], maybe,
is equivalent to [u]x $\prec$ (y $\prec$ z)[/u].
