(71)       It follows as a corollary [del]that[/del] from the last two propositions
that the proximate antecedental constituents of a conse-
quence can be transposed in any way, without affecting
its scriptibility. This is called the rule of transposition.

(72)       If x⤙y and y⤙z are scriptible, then x⤙z is
scriptible.
       For, by (64), either x or x⤙z is scriptible. But
if x and x⤙y are scriptible, by (66), y is scriptible;
and y and y⤙z are scriptible, again by (66), z is
scriptible; and if z is scriptible, by (65) x⤙z is
scriptible. Therefore, in any case, (72) holds.
