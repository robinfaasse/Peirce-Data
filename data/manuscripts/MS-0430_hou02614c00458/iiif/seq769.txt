the algebra.

(67) p -< q is scriptible except in case p is so, while q is not so.
For by (64) [del]p -< q[/del] [ins]p -< q[/ins] is scriptible, if q is so; while, by (65), p -< q is scriptible if p is not so.

(68) If p -< q is scriptible, but q is not so, neither is p.
For were p scriptible, while p -< q is scriptible, so likewise, by (66), would be q.

(69) Either p -< q or q -< r is scriptible.
For if q is scriptible, so, by (64) is p -< q; while if q is not scriptible, by (65) q -< r is scriptible.

(70) p -< p is scriptible.
For (69), being a general formula, holds good whatever expressions are substituted for p, q, and r. [del]And[/del] But make these all p, and it reads 'Either p -< p or p -< p.' That is p -< p is scriptible in any case. (70) is the [u]formula of identity[/u].
