We must be on our guard against iterating any part of a graph in an enclosure created for the purpose. Thus, given
[fig]man --- (disgraced / wounded)[/fig]
or "some man is disgraced if he is wounded," one may iterate and write
[fig]man --- (man -- disgraced / wounded)[/fig]
whence,
[fig]___ --- (man --- disgraced / wounded)[/fig]
"There is something which if it is wounded is a disgraced man." But one cannot from, the first of these three iterate thus
[fig]man --- (wounded) / (man --- disgraced / wounded)[/fig]
and infer "some man if wounded is a disgraced non-man."
