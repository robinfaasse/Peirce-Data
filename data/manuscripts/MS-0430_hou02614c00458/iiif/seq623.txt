By the rule of iteration, we now get
[fig]catholic –adores– woman[/fig]
and by Rule I
[fig]catholic –adores– woman[/fig]
or "Any catholic there may be adores some woman or other."
The beginner should be on his guard against creating
an enclosure in order to place something within it. That
cannot be done by any rule. It may be that the whole en-
closure with contents can be inserted; but otherwise insertions
can only be made in places already existing or [ins]previously[/ins] [del]first[/del] created
according to some rule.
Rule V. A detached line of identity may be drawn
unenclosed.
Thus given the graph
[fig]–wounded[/fig]
or "nobody is wounded," we get by this rule
[fig]– –wounded[/fig]
