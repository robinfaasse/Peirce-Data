(81) If (x [u]v[/u] y) [u]v[/u] z is true, then (x [u]v[/u] z) [u]v[/u] z is true
For if (x [u]v[/u] y) [u]v[/u] z is true, by (65), unless z is true, (x [u]v[/u] y) is
false. But, by (64), if x [u]v[/u] y is false, x is true. Hence, unless
z is true, x is true. But if x is true, [del]by[/del] [ins]& by[/ins] (64), x [u]v[/u] z is
false, unless z is true; and if z is true, by (64), (x [u]v[/u] z) [u]v[/u] z
is true. This may be expressed: The consequent of the antecedent
may be identified with the antecedent.
