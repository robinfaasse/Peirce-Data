Logic [u]consequences having several proximate antecedentalts.[/u]
III (69) Any consequence whatever [ins]that can be written out,[/ins] is scriptible unless all its
  68   proximate antecedentals are scriptible.
          Proof. Assume, for the moment, that this proposition
       is true for every consequence having fewer than a certain
       number, N, of proximate antecedentals. Let us, then, con-
       sider a consequence having N antecedentals, which
       we may designate as $\Omega_1, \Omega_2, \Omega_3,$ etc up to $\Omega_N,$
       and let $\mathcal{V}$ be its ultimate consequent. We assume, then,
       that either $\Omega_2, \Omega_3,$ etc up to $\Omega_N,$ are all scriptible or
       else $\Omega_2 \curlyprec \Omega_3 \curlyprec \dots \curlyprec \Omega_N \curlyprec \mathcal{V}$ is scriptible. But, by
       (65), under the last alternative $\Omega_1 \curlyprec \Omega_2 \curlyprec \Omega_3 \dots \curlyprec \Omega_N \curlyprec \mathcal{V}$
       is also scriptible. So that either $\Omega_2, \Omega_3,$ etc up to $\Omega_N$ are
       all scriptible or $\Omega_1 \curlyprec \Omega_2 \curlyprec \Omega_3 \curlyprec \dots \curlyprec \Omega_N \curlyprec \mathcal{V}$ is
       scriptible. But, by (64), either $\Omega_1$ is scriptible or 
       $\Omega_1 \curlyprec \Omega_2 \curlyprec \Omega_3 \curlyprec \dots \curlyprec \Omega_N \curlyprec \mathcal{V}$ is scriptible. Hence,
       either $\Omega_1, \Omega_2, \Omega_3,$ etc up to $\Omega_N$ are all scriptible
       or else $\Omega_1 \curlyprec \Omega_2 \curlyprec \Omega_3 \curlyprec \dots \curlyprec \Omega_N \curlyprec \mathcal{V}$ is scriptible.
