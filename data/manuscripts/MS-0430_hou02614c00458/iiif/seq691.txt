[u]Reasons for the Notation for the Boolean algebra here used.[/u]
In the first place, I ask whether our expressions are to signify numbers, and through those numbers are to represent logical relations and propositions, as was the case with Boole's original algebra of whether they are to signify the logical relations directly, [del]and[/del] but in such a way [del]that[/del] the analogy of those relations to numerical operations may be brought out. both for Theoretical reasons and because we thus utilize our associations with algebraical signs to facilitate the use of the new algebra.
