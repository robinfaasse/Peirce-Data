By a [del]second[/del] antecedental element, I mean a first
antecedental element of a first antecedental constituent.
By a [u]n[sup]th[/sup][/u] antecedental constituent, I mean a first
antecedental constituent of an [u](n-1)[sup]th[/sup][/u] antecedental constituent.
By an [u]n[sup]th[/sup][/u] antecedental element, I mean a first ante-
cedental element of an [u](n-1)[sup]th[/sup][/u] antecedental constituent.
By an [u]oddly[/u] antecedental element, I mean a [u](2n+1)[sup]th[/sup][/u]
antecedental element.
By an [u]evenly[/u] antecedental element, I mean a [u](2n+2)[sup]th[/sup][/u]
antecedental element.
By a [u]simple consequence[/u], I mean a consequence,
having but two elements.
By a [u]prime [del]antecedental[/del] [ins]antecedental[/ins] constituent[/u], I mean a first
antecedental [del]element[/del] [ins]constituent[/ins] which is either an element or
is a simple consequence whose consequent is the same
letter as the ultimate consequent of the whole consequence.
Thus, in (a—b)—(c—e)—d—e, c—e [del]is a[/del] [ins]and d are[/ins] prime
antecedental constituents.
