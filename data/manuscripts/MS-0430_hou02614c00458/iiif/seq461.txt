(94) [u]Theorem VIII[/u]. If [u]x[/u] ⋌ [u]y[/u] and [u]y[/u] ⋌ [u]z[/u] are scriptible, so also
is [u]x[/u] ⋌ [u]z[/u]. This may be called the Barbara [del]Theorem[/del] [ins]Rule[/ins].
