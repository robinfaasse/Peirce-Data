he is impressed with the idea that great ingenuity is required
to work this calculus. But I shall now proceed to show that
none is needed; that he has only to follow a straightforward
method. The method I shall develope is not the most expe-
ditious possible; but in my opinion it is the best on account
of its extreme simplicity.
