The Haupt-Satz, or Principal Theorem, of the algebra is, that any consequence capable of being completely written out is scriptible, whatever the several significations of its letters, if, and only if, in every one of its ultimate consequentials, some evenly antecedental element and some antecedental proximate constituent element are the same letter.

But before attacking this proposition, it will be well to prove some subsidiary theorems.

(67) Every consequence of the form [ured]x[/ured] ⤙ [ured]x[/ured] is scriptible.
Proof. By (64) either [ured]x[/ured] is scriptible or [ured]x[/ured] ⤙ [ured]x[/ured] is scriptible.
But, by (65), if [ured]x[/ured] is scriptible, [ured]x[/ured] ⤙ [ured]x[/ured] is scriptible.
Hence, in any case, (67) holds.
