"It either rains, snows, or hails," we require a function of
p, n, and g which shall be equal to v when either p, n,
or g equals v, that is when
(16)        (v - p) (v - n) (v - g) = 0
and which shall be equal to f when p = n = g = f. The
first condition is satisfied by
            v + (v - p) (v - n) (v - g) D
where D is a finite constant; [ins]while[/ins] [del]and[/del] the last condition re-
quires that D shall be determined by the equation
(17)        v + (v - f) (v - f) (v - f) D = f
so that D = - (v - f)^{-2}.
