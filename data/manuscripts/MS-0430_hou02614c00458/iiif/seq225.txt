(81) The expressions (x⤙y)⤙y and (y⤙x)⤙x are
equivalent.
For by (78) and (80), if (x⤙y)⤙y is scriptible, so is
(y⤙x)⤙x; and since the letters may have any signi-
fication, muting x for y and y for x, the converse holds.
Thence, by (68), we have [del](x⤙y)⤙y[/del] ([x⤙y]⤙y)⤙(y⤙x)⤙x
and ([y⤙x]⤙x)⤙(x⤙y)⤙y; so that, by (77), the truth of (81)
follows.
