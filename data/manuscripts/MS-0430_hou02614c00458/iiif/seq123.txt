I am sorry to say that I am forced to impose upon the reader (if he is to continue to be a reader) the trouble of mastering some further terminology. The reason is that (as will be proved in due course) there [del]is[/del] [ins]turns out to be[/ins] hardly any difference between a consequent and a consequent of a consequent, or between an antecedent and an antecedent of a consequent; and the consequent of an antecedent partakes of the nature of an antecedent and the antecedent of an antecedent partakes of the nature of a consequent. This circumstance makes the following designations needful.

A [ured]constituent[/ured] of an algebraical consequence is any letter of it or any [del]term[/del] [ins]part of it[/ins] enclosed by itself.
