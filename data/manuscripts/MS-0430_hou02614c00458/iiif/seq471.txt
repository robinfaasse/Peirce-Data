[ured]dental proximates has the following equivalents:[/ured]

[u]x[/u] and [u]y[/u] are severally scriptible, then [u]x[/u], [u]y[/u], [u]z[/u], are all jointly

(80) 2[ins]nd[/ins], with [u]v[/u] definite, it equals the proposition that if either
[u]x[/u] or [u]z[/u] is applicable distributively to [u]v[/u], and if either [u]y[/u] or [u]z[/u] is
applicable distributively to [u]v[/u], then [u]z[/u] is scriptible;
(81) 3[ins]rd[/ins], with [u]v[/u] both individual and definite, it equals the

rally, then [u]z[/u] is applicable to [u]v[/u].
