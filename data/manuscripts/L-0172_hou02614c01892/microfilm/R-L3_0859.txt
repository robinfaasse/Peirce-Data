My dear Professor Goodwin:

Though I seldom have time to
read any classical Greek except Plato,
yet two or three years ago I procured
& read with great pleasure & profit the
1897 edition of your Moods & Tenses; and
today, coming across an expression that was
perfectly intelligible & indeed familiar, it struck
me that I [del]know[/del] remembered no principle or
rule of grammar according to which it
would have that meaning, & accordingly
pulled down the M. & T., and having found
the desired rule, it occurred to me, the
principal word being an aorist subjunctive,
to turn to what you said about that tense,
although your rule which you taught me in
college has never yet failed to explain an
