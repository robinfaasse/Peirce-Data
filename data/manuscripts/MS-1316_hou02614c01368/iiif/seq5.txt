5 / The amount of it seems to be very limited, and there is no
particular difference between this case and those of sundry
other minerals which emit different gases, which have
presumably been occluded, or condensed upon the surfaces
of their particles. But radium has now been obtained in
a state of purity, - in the sense in which chemists judge
of purity. It has as good a claim to be considered as a
chemical element as have a large proportion of those
which are regarded as established elements. Its atomic
weight has been as well determined as have theirs, and its
chemical characters are more distinct than those of
quite a number.
