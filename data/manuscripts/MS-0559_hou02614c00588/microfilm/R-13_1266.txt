Two corollaries from these rules require particular notice.
If we have given a consequence with two antecedentals, as
[u]P[/u] [u]Q[/u] R [del]true[/del],
by Rule II, we can write
Q [u]P[/u] [u]Q[/u] R [del]true[/del];
And since by Rule I
[u]Q[/u] Q true,
we are get by Rule IV,
[u]Q[/u] [u]P[/u] R true.
That is, the order of sequence of [u]coördinate antecedentals[/u] is
indifferent.

Again, if we have
[u]P[/u] Q [del]true[/del]
and [u]Q[/u] R [del]true[/del],
then by Rule 4 we have
[del]Q[/del] [u]P[/u] R [del]true[/del].

[del]By[/del] Rule IV makes the following form of inference
valid
[u]P[/u] Q true,
[u]Q[/u] R true,
[u]P[/u] R true.

This is the canonical form of the syllogism Barbara.*
