This has been deduced from the proposition that to say a proposition is false is as much as to say that everything follows from it. It comes to this: [u]not[/u] means [u]other than every[/u]. This, to say that man is not an angel, means that man is other than every angel. Then it follows that every angel is other than every man just as from the proposition that every man loves every angel it would follow that every angel is loved by every man.
