$$\overline{X}\ \overline{Y}\ Z$$
to infer
$$\overline{Y}\ Z.$$
But from the same premise we can equally infer
$$\overline{X}\ Z,$$
since, as we have just seen, from $X$ we can infer $X|Y$.
