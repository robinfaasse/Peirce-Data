I have set forth the matter in this way, partly because it aids us to comprehend the subject, and partly because it is an excellent practical exercise in reasoning. But this method, though searching, does not lead to a neat presentation of the rules.

I propose to go over the same matter from another point of view, after taking into consideration the infinite formulae which we have, so far, omitted.

I only want to note here that all the rules above given are balanced, as I here show in parallel columns.
