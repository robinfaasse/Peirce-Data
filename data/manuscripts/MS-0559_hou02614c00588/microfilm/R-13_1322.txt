$1^{\text{st}}$. Some antecedentials, $I_{\text{f}}$, may reduce to the single letter $A$; [del]as[/del] [ins]as[/ins] in $A\overline{\text{I}}\ \overline{\text{I}}\ A$.

$2^{\text{nd}}$. Some of the second antecedentials may be necessary formulae; as in $\overline{C\overline{I}C}\ \overline{\text{I}}\ A\overline{\text{I}}A$.

$3^{\text{rd}}$. Some of the fourth consequentials, may like the second consequentials to which they belong be identical with the first consequential; as in $\overline{A\overline{\text{I}}C}\ \overline{\text{I}}\ A\overline{\text{I}}A$.
