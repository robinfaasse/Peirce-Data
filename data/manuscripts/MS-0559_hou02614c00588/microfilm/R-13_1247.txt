A question of notation here arises. Shall
$$A\vert B\cdot C$$
be understood to mean
$$(A\vert B)\cdot C$$
or
$$A\vert (B\cdot C)\text{ ?}$$

It will be found convenient to [del]consider[/del] [ins]consider[/ins] the vertical
