Corresponding to the inference
[u]S | P[/u] true,
<span style="padding-left: 30px;">$$\therefore$$</span> [u]x | S[/u] | [u]x | P[/u] true.

we have
[u]S [ins]~[/ins] P[/u] false
<span style="padding-left: 30px;">$$\therefore$$</span> [u]S [ins]~[/ins] x[/u] [u]P [ins]~[/ins] x[/u] false.

Corresponding to the contrapositive inference
[u]S | P[/u] true,
<span style="padding-left: 30px;">$$\therefore$$</span> [u]P | x[/u] | [u]S | x[/u] true,

we have
[u]S [ins]~[/ins] P[/u] false,
[u]x [ins]~[/ins] P[/u] [u]x [ins]~[/ins] S[/u] false.

Corresponding to the inference
[u]x | y[/u] | [u]z | w[/u] true,
<span style="padding-left: 30px;">$$\therefore$$</span> [u]x | y[/u] | [u]z | w[/u] true,

we have
[u]x [ins]~[/ins] y[/u] [u]x [ins]~[/ins] w[/u] false.
<span style="padding-left: 30px;">$$\therefore$$</span> [u]x [ins]~[/ins] y[/u] [u]x [ins]~[/ins] w[/u] false.

Other analogies the student can trace out for himself.
