under it; and to reform our notation we must examine
the consequents of negation.

Now we have seen that to deny a proposition is
to assert that every proposition follows from it as a
consequent, or, what is the same thing, that every
proposition is in antecedent to it. To assert, then,
that from $M$ the negative of $N$ follows, is the same as
to assert that from $M$ it follows that from $N$ any
proposition we please, say $O$, follows. This we write

[u]$M \bar{|} N \bar{|} O$.[/u]

But we have seen that this is equivalent to

[u]$N \bar{|} M \bar{|} O$,[/u]

which expresses that from $N$ the negative of $M$ follows.
Let us draw a line over a letter to express its negative.
