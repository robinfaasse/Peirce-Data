The one property of [del]the[/del] denial is that from the denial of A, it follows that something follows from A. If you ask what it is that follows from A, the reply is that anything you please follows. Consequently, the impossible and absurd follows. In other words, if the denial of A is [del]true[/del] [ins]This is called the principle of contradiction![/ins] and if A is true, then we are landed in absurdity. [del]But[/del] if we abstract from, or abstain from asking what it is that follows, this property consists in this, that from the denial of A it follows that from A something follows.

The other property of denial is, that from something [del]being true[/del] [del]being possible[/del] without A [del]being true, follows[/del] [ins]would follow[/ins] the denial of A. If you ask what this something is, it is what is true. In other words, in the existing [del]case[/del] [ins]state of things[/ins], [del]if[/del] [ins]either[/ins] A is [del]not[/del] true or the denial of A is true. This is called the principle of excluded middle.
