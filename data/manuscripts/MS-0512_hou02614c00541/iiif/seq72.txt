That (6) cannot reduce to (5) is evident when we examine the last
six $\Sigma$s followed by only two $\Pi$s and see that the two indices of these
two quantifiers are nowhere indices of the same letter. Hence, it is evident
that if (5) can be demonstrated at all, it must be by the help of some
other premise. As a first such premise, I write (7):
(7) $\Pi_{\mu} \Pi_{\nu} \Sigma_{\gamma} \Pi_{x} \Pi_{y} \bar{q}_{\mu x} + \bar{q}_{\nu y} + r_{\gamma x y}$.
