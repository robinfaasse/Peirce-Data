The following are the most important general formulae. There is a
sort of balance of non-relative and relative formulae; [del]provided[/del] [ins] But it will be [/ins] noted
that the negative converse of a compound reverses the order of factors and

tive formulae. In short, the balance is imperfect. From the formulae here given all
others can be deduced. $\Pi_u, \Pi_v, \Pi_w$
