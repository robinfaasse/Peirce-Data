How shall we define a [u]collection[/u]? That is a hard question.
The collection of New Yorkers is an object such that to say that it
has any character is the same as to say that all New Yorkers
have a corresponding character. In the case of some characters,
there is no difficulty with this. To say that any New Yorker is polite
is the same as to say that New York is a collection of polite men.
