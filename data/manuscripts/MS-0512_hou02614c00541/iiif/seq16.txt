This result is confirmed by analyzing assertion from another point of view. A asserts B to C when, and only when, A makes C believe that A is trying to make C believe that A believes B to be true. Now to believe anything to be true includes acting consistently as if it were true. To assert the assertion itself to be false is for A to pretend to try to make C believe that A does not believe what he pretends to try to make C believe. Then, clearly, A is not really trying to make C believe anything. His pretension is therefore false, and the proposition is false.
