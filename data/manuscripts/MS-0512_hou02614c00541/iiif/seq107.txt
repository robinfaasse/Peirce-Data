Now among those distributions there must be one in which
no two Ps are assigned to the same Q, unless this involves a con-
tradiction. For every distribution logically possible is among
the distributions. But the only way in which it can be impossible
that there should be a distribution in which no Q should have
two Ps is that it may be that in every way in which it could
be brought about by the variation of the Qs to which given
Ps are assigned, a state an intermediate distribution would
be reached in which every Q was assigned to some P. For if
there is a distribution in which every Q is assigned to some
P and some Qs to more than one P then it may be
impossible to alter that distribution so that no Q is assigned
to different Ps.

to different Ps or there is a distribution in which every Q is
