$$\begin{array}{ll}
\text{IJE} = \frac{1}{3} (x + \eta^2 y) & \text{KLH} = \frac{1}{3} (-x - \eta^2 y) \\
\text{IJG} = \frac{1}{3} (-x + \eta^2 y) & \text{KLF} = \frac{1}{3} (x - \eta^2 y) \\
\text{ABI} = \frac{1}{3} (y + \eta^2 z) & \text{CDL} = \frac{1}{3} (-y - \eta^2 z) \\
\text{ABK} = \frac{1}{3} (-y + \eta^2 z) & \text{CDJ} = \frac{1}{3} (y - \eta^2 z) \\
\text{EFA} = \frac{1}{3} (z + \eta^2 x) & \text{GHD} = \frac{1}{3} (-z - \eta^2 x) \\
\text{EFC} = \frac{1}{3} (-z + \eta^2 x) & \text{GHB} = \frac{1}{3} (z - \eta^2 x) \\
\text{AEI} = \frac{\eta}{3} (x + y + z) & \text{DHL} = \frac{\eta}{3} (-x - y - z) \\
\text{CFL} = \frac{\eta}{3} (x - y - z) & \text{BGL} = \frac{\eta}{3} (-x + y + z) \\
\text{DGJ} = \frac{\eta}{3} (-x + y - z) & \text{AFK} = \frac{\eta}{3} (x - y + z) \\
\text{BHK} = \frac{\eta}{3} (-x - y + z) & \text{CEJ} = \frac{\eta}{3} (x + y - z)
\end{array}$$

The faces are opposite one another in pairs as shown above.

Every face has [del]four[/del] [ins]3[/ins] adjacent. If it has two terms change the sign of the one with coeff $\pm 1$, or add the third with either sign, and make all the coeffs $\pm \eta$ with unchanged signs. If it has 3 terms, suppress any one and give previous one (in cyclic order $x,y,z$) coeff $\eta^2$ and one before it with unchanged signs.

Every face has 6 periadjacent, 3 remote, 6 periremote.
