Some will ask whether if every tetrad can be built up out
of triads, it must not be equally true that every triad can be
built up out of dyads. The reason has already been
stated, namely, that nothing can be built up out of other
things without combining those other things, and combination
is itself manifestly a triad. But those who do not see the
force of this reason had better try to build up a chemical
triad, that is, a connected group with three free bonds, out
of chemical dyads, while observing the law of valency.
