[u]Examples of Mathematical Definitions[/u]
[u]suitable for Imperial Dictionary[/u]

[u]Deficiency of a curve[/u] is the number by which its double points are fewer than the maximum for a curve of the same [del]order[/del] [ins]degree[/ins].

[u]Curve[/u], in Analytical Geometry, is the path of a moving point. A curve is, therefore, not necessarily curved, since the straight line is the simplest kind of curve. But in ancient geometry only a line whose direction varies continuously is termed a curve.

[u]Degree of an algebraical curve[/u], is the number of points in which a straight line can cut it. Every straight line cuts it in the same number of points, real or imaginary.
