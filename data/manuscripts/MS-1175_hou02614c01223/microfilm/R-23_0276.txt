[u]Continuant[/u] ([u]kon-tin'-u-ant[/u]) [u]n.[/u] A deter-
minant all whose constituents vanish
except those in the principal diagonal and
the two bordering minor diagonals.

[u]Continued fraction[/u], in alg., an expression
[ins]ordinarily[/ins] of the form
$$a + \frac{1}{b + \frac{1}{c + \frac{1}{d + \text{etc.}}}}$$
But if the one's were replaced by any other
quantities, the expression would [del]be[/del] still be
called a continued fraction. The quantities
[u]a[/u], [u]b[/u], [u]c[/u], [u]d[/u], etc. are usually understood to
represent whole numbers and are called
the [u]quotients[/u] or [u]incomplete quotients[/u].
