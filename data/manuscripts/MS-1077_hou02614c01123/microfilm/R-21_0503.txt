[ins]Derbyshire:[/ins] of barley, often 50 pounds.

Stockton: of oats, 35 pounds.

Gloucestershire: commonly 9 1/2 gallons, but varying from 9 and

Herefordshire: of grain, 10 gallons.

Lancashire: of potatoes, generally 90 pounds not cleaned.

Liverpool: of barley, beans, and oats, 34 1/2 customary quarts,

Leicestershire: of grain, 8 1/2 to 9 gallons.
of malt, 8 gallons.
of potatoes, 80 pounds.

Middlesex: of potatoes, 56 pounds.

Northumberland; the Winchester bushel, variously subdivided.

Somersetshire: of coal, at the pits, 9 gallons.

Staffordshire. Wolverhampton. Of barley, beans, oats, and pease,

Sussex: of wheat, in some parts 9 gallons.
