subtle, less ready, less [del]inerrant.[/del] [ins]unerring.[/ins] The one respect in which
it is superior is in being controlled, checked, criticized. This

condemns; and were it not so, reason would be without its
solitary advantage. It follows that [del]reason[/del] the excellence of
