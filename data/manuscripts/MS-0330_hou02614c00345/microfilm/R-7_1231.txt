5. Is the Satisfactory meant to be
whatever excites a certain peculiar feeling
of satisfaction? In that case, the doctrine
is simply hedonism in so far as it affects
the field of cognition. For when hedonists
talk of "pleasure", they do not mean what
is so called in ordinary speech but what
excites a feeling of satisfaction.
