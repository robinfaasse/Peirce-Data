I hope to obtain shortly
the requisite leisure to
study the several
papers minutely. If
the Bibliography of 1870
is intended to be
exhaustive, I think that
notice of my work
on the Algebra of
Logic should have
been inserted. It is
at least as important
a contribution to the
subject as many of
those mentioned.

Private business calls
me to cross the Atlantic
this summer, and it
is possible that I
may have an opportunity
of visiting some of
the colleges of the
States. I am, dear Sir,

Yours sincerely

Alexander Macfarlane.

Professor C. S. Peirce
