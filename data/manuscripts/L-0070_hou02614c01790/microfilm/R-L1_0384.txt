**Auchinbreck.**
Cherry Valley N.Y.
[date]Oct. 4th 92[/date]

My dear Peirce.-

Many thanks for your prompt response to my letter. I do not wonder that you think some of my chapters so long, those which are devoted to well-known history. Had I been writing only for scholars like yourself, I could have cut the whole argument into one volume. But in enlarging it, I have made no mistake from a commercial standpoint.
