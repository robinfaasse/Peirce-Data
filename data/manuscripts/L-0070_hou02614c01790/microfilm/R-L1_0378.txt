My dear Peirce,

Can you tell me
anything about the pedigree of
Pres. Franklin Pierce? I wish the
information for the final chap-
ter of my book in which I have
something to say about the in-
fluence of the Scotch-Irish el-
ement in our population, a sub-
ject of great interest and im-
portance, but but little under-
stood. This race carried Penn-
sylvania and Virginia for
independence, did most of the
Revolutionary fighting for the
country, furnished five out of
the first thirteen governors,
largely shaped the constitution
