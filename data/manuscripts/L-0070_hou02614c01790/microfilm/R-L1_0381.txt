In this connection I have
been tracing out the pedigree
of our twenty-three presidents,
with results which are very
striking. The work has not been
an easy one, but I believe
that I am entirely correct as
to the following.

[u]Scotch-Irish[/u]; Jackson, Polk,
Taylor, Johnson, Buchanan,
Arthur.

[u]Scotch[/u]; Monroe, Grant, Hayes.

[u]Welsh[/u]; Jefferson.

[u]Dutch[/u]; Van Buren.

Garfield's ancestors on his father's
side came from England, but
were of Welsh descent. His mother
was a French Huguenot.

Grover Cleveland writes me that
he has no information about
his ancestors, but Appleton's
Biographical Encyclopaedia
says that his mother's father
was an Irishman. Cleve-
land by the way is the only
person to whom I have written
upon this subject who has
not responded to my inquiries,
Hayes, Harrison, Mrs. Garfield,
and others being extremely
courteous.
