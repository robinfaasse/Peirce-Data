Buchanan, Johnson, Arthur;
while the Portes have given us
Monroe, Grant and Hayes;
the Whigs, Jefferson, and the
Latter Van Buren. Some of
the others I am uncertain about
as e.g. Tyler, Pierce, Fillmore,
Garfield, and Cleveland.

With the exception of a few
blunders in the last chapter,
the book is finished, and the
Harpers, who are my publishers,
have the first of two volumes
in type.

My health, I regret to say, is
pretty bad. I have hardly
been out of the house for weeks
and only from my bedroom
across the hall to my library.
Still I am pretty comfortable
in the main and hope to get
out again with the return of
warmer weather.

I feel keenly the depriva-
tion of companionship such
as I used to have in the
Century but try to bear the
inevitable with philosophy.

Yours sincerely
Douglas Campbell
per M. S. C.
