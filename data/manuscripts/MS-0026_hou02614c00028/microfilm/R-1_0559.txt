A heavy horizontal line, called an obelus, written over a proposition, has the effect of negativing it, that is, of precisely denying it, and also asserting its negative.

There are certain terms [del]of the[/del] meaning of which logic is obliged to take cognizance; and as they may occur in any discussion, the convention which determines by what characters they shall be signified belongs to the general convention which settles the shapes of the logical signs of operation, although these signs of logical terms, [ins]being predicates,[/ins] may be classed along with the letters which are written on the line. The Arabic figures may conveniently be used to [del]represent[/del] [ins]stand for[/ins] these predicates, since [del]their meaning of any one of them either is that its indices[/del] [ins]each one of them either asserts or denies that the individuals[/ins] denoted by its indices form a collection of a certain number of distinct individuals, or units, in [del]the[/del] universe of discourse and having the mode of existence which belongs to that universe.
