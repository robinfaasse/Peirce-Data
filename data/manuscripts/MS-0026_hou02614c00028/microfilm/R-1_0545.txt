Now the suggested form of the second axiom, expressed in the General Algebra,
is
$$(44) \qquad \Pi_{\alpha} \Sigma_{\beta} \Pi_{\underline{x}} (q_{\alpha \underline{x}} + \bar{q}_{\beta \underline{x}}) \cdot (\bar{q}_{\alpha \underline{x}} + \bar{q}_{\beta \underline{x}});$$
while if $\underline{c}$ is the relation which every collection whatever bears to everything what-
ever, the same proposed axiom is expressed in the dyadic algebra as follows:
$$(44') \qquad \underline{c} \breve{\underline{c}} \cdot 1 \prec (q J \breve{q}) (\bar{q} J \breve{q})$$
Squaring (44) and dropping the last compredicant of each compredicant,
we get, substituting $\gamma, \delta, y$ in one compredicant for $\alpha, \beta, x$,
$$\Pi_{\alpha} \Sigma_{\beta} \Pi_{\underline{x}} \Pi_{\gamma} \Sigma_{\delta} \Pi_{y} (q_{\alpha \underline{x}} + \bar{q}_{\beta \underline{x}}) \cdot (q_{\gamma y} + \bar{q}_{\delta y}).$$
Identifying $\gamma$ with $\beta$, dropping the first compredicant and then the quantifi-
ers whose indices do not occur in the Boolian, we have,
$$\Sigma_{\beta} \Sigma_{\delta} \Pi_{y} (q_{\beta y} + \bar{q}_{\delta y})$$
But this is the aggregate of a proposition with itself, so that
$$\Sigma_{\beta} \Pi_{y} q_{\beta y}$$
and by carrying the $\Sigma$ to the right and changing $y$ and $\beta$ to $x$ and $\alpha$, we
get (43).
