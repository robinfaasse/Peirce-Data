Secondly, the figure of the number $N$, negatived by having an obelus attached to it, and having $N+1$ indices, [ins]or more,[/ins] means that [del]these[/del] [ins]every[/ins] $N+1$ [ins]of those[/ins] indices denote [del]N+1[/del] [del]differ[/del] distinct individuals existing in the universe of discourse with the mode of existence proper to that universe.

Applying this general definition, we find as follows:

$\bar{0}_x$ means "$x$ is an existing individual of the universe of discourse", which is meaningless;

$\bar{1}_{xy}$ means "$x$ and $y$ are two different objects", that is, $x$ is not $y$;

$\bar{2}_{xyz}$ means "$x$, $y$, and $z$ are three different objects";

etc.
