<u>Fundamental Rules of the Signs</u>. The rules of the copula of inclusion are [del]two,[/del] [del]the[/del] as follows: First, whatever proposition $\underline{x}$ may be,
(1)
$$\underline{x} \prec \underline{x} \text{ ;}$$

Secondly, whatever propositions $\underline{x}$, $\underline{y}$, and $\underline{z}$ may be,
(2)
$$\text{if } \underline{x} \prec \underline{y} \text{ and } \underline{y} \prec \underline{z} \text{ then } \underline{x} \prec \underline{z} \text{ .}$$

[del]Thirdly, whatever propositions $\underline{x}$ and $\underline{y}$ may be, if $\underline{x} \prec \underline{y}$ and $\underline{y} \prec \underline{x}$ then $\underline{x} = \underline{y}$.[/del]

(3) The rules of the sign of aggregation and the sign of compredication are as follows: First, [del]these[/del] [ins]identifying[/ins] signs are [del]commutative[/del], that is, whatever propositions $\underline{x}$ [del]and $\underline{y}$[/del] may be
(4)
$$\underline{x} + \underline{x} = \underline{x} \qquad \underline{x} \cdot \underline{x} = \underline{x} \text{ ;}$$

Secondly, these signs are <u>commutative</u>, that is, whatever propositions $\underline{x}$ and $\underline{y}$ may be
(5)
$$\underline{x} + \underline{y} = \underline{y} + \underline{x} \qquad \underline{x} \cdot \underline{y} = \underline{y} \cdot \underline{x} \text{ ;}$$

Thirdly, these signs are separately <u>associative</u>, that is, whatever propositions $\underline{x}$, $\underline{y}$, and $\underline{z}$ may be
(6)
$$(\underline{x} + \underline{y}) + \underline{z} = \underline{x} + (\underline{y} + \underline{z}) \qquad \underline{x} \cdot (\underline{y} \cdot \underline{z}) = (\underline{x} \cdot \underline{y}) \cdot \underline{z} \text{ ;}$$
