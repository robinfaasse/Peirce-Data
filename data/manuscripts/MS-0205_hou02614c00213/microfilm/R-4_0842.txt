[u]Corollary 4[/u]. The product of any cardinal number, $N$, multiplied into
$G0$, or $1$, is $N$. For suppose $N$ to be a cardinal number possessing
this character, that $N \times G0 = N$. Then by the second condition
$GN \times G0 = G0 + N \cdot G0$ which by the third condition of Definition
$= G(0 + N \cdot G0)$ which by a former corollary
$= G(N \cdot G0)$ which by hypothesis
$= G(N)$

Thus, if $N$ possesses the character, so does $GN$. But by the first condition
$0 \times G0 = 0$, so that zero possesses the character. Hence, by the Fermatian
Principle, every cardinal number, $N$, multiplied into $G0$ gives $N$ as the
product. $N \times 1 = N \times G0 = N$.

[u]Corollary 5[/u]. Whatever cardinal numbers $M$ and $N$ may be, $M \times GN$
$= M + M \cdot N$. For suppose $M$ to be of cardinal number having the character.
Then $GM \times GN = GN + M \cdot GN$ by the second condition
$= GN + (M + M \cdot N)$ by hypothesis
$= (GN + M) + M \cdot N = G(N + M) + M \cdot N = (N + GM) + M \cdot N$
$= (GM + N) + M \cdot N = GM + (N + M \cdot N)$ by former results
$= GM + GM \cdot N$ by the second condition.

Thus, if $M$ possesses the character, so does $GM$. But zero possesses the charac-
ter since $0 \times GN = 0 = 0 + 0 = 0 + 0 \cdot N$. Hence, by the Fermatian Principle,
every cardinal number possesses the character.
