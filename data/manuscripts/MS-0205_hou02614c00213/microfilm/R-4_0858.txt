<u>Corollary</u>. The sum of any cardinal number added to a greater number is greater than the sum of the same number added to a lesser number.

<u>Corollary</u>. The sum of two greater numbers is greater than the sum of two lesser numbers.

<u>Corollary</u>. The sums of two different numbers added to the same number are not the same.

<u>Corollary</u>. If the sum of two cardinal numbers, A and B is greater than the sum of two cardinal numbers, C and D, either A or B is greater than C and than D or else C or D is less than A and than B.
