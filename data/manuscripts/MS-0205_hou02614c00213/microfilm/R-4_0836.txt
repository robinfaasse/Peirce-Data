[u]Corollary 6[/u]. Whatever cardinal numbers [u]M[/u] and [u]N[/u] may be, [u]M[/u] + [u]N[/u] = [u]N[/u] + [u]M[/u].
For suppose [u]M[/u] to be a cardinal number having this character, that, if
whatever cardinal number [u]N[/u] may be, [u]M[/u] + [u]N[/u] = [u]N[/u] + [u]M[/u]. Then

G[u]M[/u] + [u]N[/u] = G([u]M[/u] + [u]N[/u]) by the third condition
= G([u]N[/u] + [u]M[/u]) by hypothesis
= [u]N[/u] + G[u]M[/u] by the above corollary

That is if [u]M[/u] + [u]N[/u] = [u]N[/u] + [u]M[/u] then G[u]M[/u] + [u]N[/u] = [u]N[/u] + G[u]M[/u]. But by the above corollaries
0 + [u]N[/u] = 0 = [u]N[/u] + 0. Hence, by the Fermatian Principle, the formula holds
for all cardinal numbers.
