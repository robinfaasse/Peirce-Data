Definition 4. A divisor of N is any [ins]cardinal[/ins] number which multiplying
[del]a[/del] [ins]any cardinal[/ins] number gives N as the product. The number is said to be a multiple of its divisor.

A part, or aliquot part, of a cardinal number N, is [del]any cardinal[/del] [ins]either a cardinal[/ins]
number [u]integral part[/u] or a [u]fractional part[/u]. The former is a cardinal num-

A factor of a cardinal number, N, is a cardinal number which
multiplied by an integral part of N gives N as the product.
