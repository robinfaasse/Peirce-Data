[u]Corollary 2[/u]. The double of a cardinal number, N, is [del]equal[/del] to N+N, the sum of N added to itself. For GGO [del]x[/del] [ins]·[/ins] N = N + GO · N = N + N. In other words, the double of a number minus the number itself is the number itself.
