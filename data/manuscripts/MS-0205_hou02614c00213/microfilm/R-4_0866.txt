[u]Theorem[/u]. Every fraction is as great as $\frac{0}{G0}$ and as small as $\frac{G0}{0}$.

[u]Proof[/u]. I will first prove that $\frac{G0}{G0}$ is not so small as $\frac{0}{G0}$ and not so great as $\frac{G0}{0}$. For this purpose take any two cardinal numbers, $M$ and $N$. The fraction $\frac{0}{G0 \times M}$ is [ins]by the last corollary but one[/ins] both as great and as small as $\frac{0}{G0}$. By adding $M + [del]G0 \times N[/del]$ to the numerator and $G0 \times N$ to the denominator, we get $\frac{M + [del]G0 \times N[/del]}{G0 \times (M+N)}$. By again adding the same numbers to numerator and denominator we get $\frac{M + M + [del](G0 \times N) + (G0 \times N)[/del]}{G0 \times (M+N+N)} = \frac{G0 \cdot M + M + G0 \times N + G0 \times N}{[del](G0 \times M) + (G0 \times N) + (G0 \times N)[/del]} = \frac{(G0 \times N) + (G0 \times N) + (G0 \times N)}{(G0 \times M) + (G0 \times N) + (G0 \times N)}$ $= \frac{G0}{G0}$. Therefore, unless $\frac{0}{G0}$ is [del]as small but not as great[/del] [ins]as great but not as small[/ins] as $\frac{M + (G0 \times N)}{G0 \times (M+N)}$, the latter is as [del]great[/del] [ins]small[/ins] as $\frac{G0}{G0}$. Let us now begin with $\frac{M}{0}$ which is both as great and as small as $\frac{G0}{0}$. Let us add $N$ to the numerator and $M+N$ to the denominator we get $\frac{M+N}{M+N} = \frac{G0}{G0}$. By again adding the same numbers to the numerator and denominator we get $\frac{M+N+N}{M+M+N+N} = \frac{M+N+(G0 \times M)}{M+N+G0 \times (M+N)} = \frac{M+G0 \times N}{G0 \times (M+N)}$. Thus, unless $\frac{G0}{0}$ is as [del]great but not as small[/del] [ins]small but not as great[/ins] as $\frac{G0}{G0}$, the latter is as [del]small[/del] [ins]great[/ins] as $\frac{M+G0 \times N}{G0 \times (M+N)}$. Combining these results, the logical consequence is that either $\frac{0}{G0}$ is as small but not as great as $\frac{M + (G0 \times N)}{G0 \times (M+N)}$, while the latter is as small as $\frac{G0}{G0}$ and the last is as small but not as great as $\frac{G0}{0}$ or else $\frac{0}{G0}$ is as great but not as small as $\frac{M+G0 \times N}{G0 \times (M+N)}$
