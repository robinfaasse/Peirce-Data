[u]Corollary 1.[/u] If the cardinal number, N, is less than the modulus, M, [ins]its[/ins]
remainder, [del]R[/del] [ins]Q[/ins][sub]M[/sub]N, is N. [del]For N = N + 0·M.[/del]

[u]Corollary 2.[/u] The remainder of the sum of two numbers [ins]N and N'[/ins] is the remainder
of the sum of their remainders. [del]For if one of the numbers is divisible by
the modulus, the remainder of the sum [ins]is the[/ins] sum of the remainders. For[/del]
[del]N +[/del] N + Q·M = R[sub]M[/sub]N + [del](Q[sub]M[/sub]N)[/del] [ins](Q[sub]M[/sub]N)[/ins]·M + Q·M = R[sub]M[/sub]N + [del][(Q[sub]M[/sub]N + Q][/del] [ins][Q[sub]M[/sub]N + Q][/ins]·M.
Hence, the remainder of a number [ins]N - Q'M[/ins] is the same as the remainder of that
number diminished by a multiple of the modulus less than N. Hence,
the remainder of N + N' is the remainder of N + N' - (Q[sub]M[/sub]N + Q[sub]M[/sub]N')·M.
But this is R[sub]M[/sub]N + R[sub]M[/sub]N'.

[u]Corollary 3.[/u] The remainder of the product of two numbers, N and N',
is the remainder of the product of the remainders. For
N·N' = (R[sub]M[/sub]N)·(R[sub]M[/sub]N') + [(Q[sub]M[/sub]N)·N' + (R[sub]M[/sub]N)·[del](Q[sub]M[/sub]N')[/del] [ins](Q[sub]M[/sub]N')[/ins]]·M.
Therefore, by subtracting the multiple of M, which does not affect the remainder,
the product of the remainders remains.

[u]Corollary 4.[/u] The quotient of the sum of two numbers is the sum of the
quotient of the sum of the remainders added to the sum of the quotients
of the numbers; that is Q[sub]M[/sub](N+N') = Q[sub]M[/sub](R[sub]M[/sub]N+R[sub]M[/sub]N') + Q[sub]M[/sub]N + Q[sub]M[/sub]N'.

[u]Corollary 5.[/u] The quotient of the product of two numbers, N and N', is the
sum of the product of N by Q[sub]M[/sub]N', the quotient of the other added to
the quotient of the product of N by R[sub]M[/sub]N', the remainder of the other. Or
Q[sub]M[/sub](N·N') = N·Q[sub]M[/sub]N' + Q[sub]M[/sub](N·R[sub]M[/sub]N').

[u]Corollary 6.[/u] Given any cardinal number, N, and any modular number, M,
there is a multiple of M greater than N. For (Q[sub]M[/sub]N)·M is such a multiple.
