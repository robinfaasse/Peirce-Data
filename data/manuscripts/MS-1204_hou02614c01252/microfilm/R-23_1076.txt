[marginalia]Mo. 1204[/marginalia]

[u]A PROMISE AS TO TWELVE WORDS[/u]

In order to testify my approval of the principle of a sim-
plification of English spelling, and to encourage the practice,
I agree to adopt for customary use in my own personal
correspondence the following twelve spellings, here-
tofore recommended and used by the National Educational
Association, namely,

[tabular]
program	prolog	tho	thorofare
catalog	demagog	altho	thru
decalog	pedagog	thoro	thruout
[/tabular]
