<u>II</u>.

In English, then, everything belongs to punctuation
in a broad sense except the letters, the ranging of them
closely one after another to form a word, and the [del]senseless[/del]
breaking a word at the end of a line, where our [del]normal[/del]
insistence upon vertical edges to the text necessitates it.

The devices of punctuation are, then, as follows: 1[sup]st[/sup],
the capital initial, and 2[sup]d[/sup], the hyphen, within a word;

whole word; 4[sup]th[/sup], the blank spaces between words [ins]and before[/ins]
