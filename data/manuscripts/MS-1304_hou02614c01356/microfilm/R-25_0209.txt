uniformly, [ins]and retrogressively[/ins] [del]round[/del] [ins]the centre[/ins] [del]an eccen[/del] of the ecliptic

round the centre of the ecliptic. He does
not say, as far as I see, how the epicycle

described. In short, the whole effect of the
new circle is to give the centre of the epicycle a
