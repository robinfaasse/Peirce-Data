[u]Brief Sketch of a Proposed Series of Articles
on the Cosmology of the Here and Hereafter.[/u]

The series would consist of four articles. The first would begin by explaining the purpose of the series; namely, to determine how our physical and psychological hypotheses would require to be modified in the event of its being admitted that occasionally communications are received from the dead. I shall show that the greatest modifications are such as we ought to make in any case, and that there would be nothing really to revolutionize either physical or psychological science so far as either has to do with phenomena of [ins]our[/ins] this life that there is the slightest probability of observing within many centuries.
