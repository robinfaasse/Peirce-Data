[u]Translation of Dedication[/u].

To the most illustrious and most serene Prince, the most dread[del]ed[/del] Lord, the Lord John, paternal uncle of our Lord, the King of France and England, ruling the realm of France, Duke of Bedford, Rollando, physician to the writer of your Highness, of Lisbon, wrote [this] in fulfilment of a vow.

Most famous Prince, under whose happy dukedom all France happily increases with a certain high consent of the people as becomes a prince ruling righteously, in you charity burns within, piety shows without, and science sheds its rays both ways. Who purer in feeling? Who more abundant in well-doing? Who more certain in clearness of counsel? I say, weighing my meaning, that you spend your treasures as if you were scattering ashes. You immolate yourself as a sacrifice in [del]continual[/del] [ins]incessant[/ins] labors. In order that you may prepare peace for those subject to obedience to our Lord, the King of France and England, and as duke may lead the realm of France out of the whirl of mars, to the rule of that realm alone you are deputed by a divine arrangement with salutary satisfaction.
