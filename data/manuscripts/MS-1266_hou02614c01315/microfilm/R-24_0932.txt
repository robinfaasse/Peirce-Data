[u]Mr. Plimpton's MS. Arithmetic circa 1430.[/u]

The MS. is written with remarkable care, and in places blanks are left, as if it were intended to be corrected by the author. The dedication is as follows:

[fig]Large decorative initial I[/fig]llustrissimo ac Serenissimo principi metuendissimo domino domino Johanni patruo domini nostri regis ffrancie et anglie regenti Regnum ffrancie, duci bethfordie Rollandus scriptoris vestre celsitudinis phisicus vliebonensis se ipsum ex debito iuramenti ¶ In vobis inclitissime princeps sub cuius felici ducatu tota feliciter accrescit francia quadam excellenti prerogativa sicut recte regentem decet principem Caritas intus ardet, Pietas foris nitet, Sciencia vero radiat utobique. Quis magis affectu purus? Quis beneficio magis fecundus? Quis consilij claritate magis certus? Dico in libramine consiliorum expenditis Thesauros vestros quasi cinerem spergitis. Vos ipsum quasi victimam continuis laboribus immolatis. Ut domini nostri regis francie et anglie obediencie subditis patere paretis et regnum francie extra turbinem guerrarum ducatis.
