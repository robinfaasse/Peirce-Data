Taking any three rays
Let $a, b, c$ be any three rays [del]of which[/del]
[del]no two[/del] no two intersect

Let $x, y, z,$ be any three rays
each cutting the three rays
$a, b, c$.

Let, $d,$ be a fourth ray cutting
$x, y,$ and $z$ and let, $w$ be a
fourth ray cutting $a, b, c$.
Then
$w$ cuts $d$.
