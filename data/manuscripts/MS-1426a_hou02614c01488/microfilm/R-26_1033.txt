The volume is remarkably handsome, owing to its
pages approaching just nearly enough to the shape of
[del]the[/del] a quarto. Allowing for the exaggeration by the eye of
the vertical dimension, the diagonal of the solid text
appears to divide the right angle between bottom and
side into angles one of which is twice the other. The
water-lines of the laid paper run as in a quarto.
