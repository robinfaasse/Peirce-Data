What is "[u]Experience[/u]"? Surely, it is important that this question
should be answered with precision, as long as we hold that all human
knowledge, and especially all assurance of knowledge, spring out of it.
My answer is this: Experience is that state of cognition which, [del]the[/del]
[ins]under conditions in most cases partly due to the action of the object apprehended,[/ins]
course of life or some part of it (however minute,) has forced upon
the person who is said to undergo the experience.
