21 | It is generally recognized, however, and is true, that by means
of experience alone we get the idea of the [u]probable[/u]; and it is further
true that experience [del]alone[/del] can furnish grounds for induction
proper. This last is partially recognized, and is [ins]generally[/ins] [del]supposed[/del] to be
due to a certain order in the facts of experience. I shall
show that both characters are principally due to an absence
of order, a promiscuity, in the facts of experience. However,
this phrase must not go without proper explication.
