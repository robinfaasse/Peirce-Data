[pn]21[/pn] It is generally recognized, however, and is true, that by means
of experience alone we get the idea of the probable; and it is further
true that experience [del]alone[/del] can furnish grounds for induction
proper. This last is partially recognized, and is [ins]generally[/ins] supposed to be
due to a certain order in the facts of experience. I shall
show that both characters are principally due to an absence of
order, a promiscuity, in the facts of experience. However,
this phrase must not go without proper explication.
