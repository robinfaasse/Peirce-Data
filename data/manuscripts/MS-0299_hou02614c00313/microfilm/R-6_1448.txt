8.[/marginalia] a single sentence, — which does not rest upon experience at all, and consequently is not a science of the Real. This is Mathematics, which studies only [del]what[/del] [ins]most[/ins] to discover what would be true under conditions which in [del]many[/del] cases can probably never be fulfilled; and even if they can be, that circumstance can in no wise modify the mathematician's conclusions.
