§2. Precision, [del]and[/del] Discrimination, Dissociation.

Precision and distinction are two names for the same process, which we
now analyze not merely as separation by the mind, but, to a particular
degree, which is separation, namely, that my attention is one point and ne-
glects another. That which is attended to is said to be prescinded, and that
which is neglected is said to be abstracted from. This definition should
be strictly adhered to, however narrowly it may limit the application of
the terms. Attention is a absolute condition of attention to one element to
the exclusion, without any mental supposition, of the other.
