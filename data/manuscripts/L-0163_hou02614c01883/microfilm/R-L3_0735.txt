[pn]V2[/pn] Newport March 14 [pn]15[/pn] / 89

Dear Sir

Your letter of yesterday is at hand & I write that you have misread the object of my enquiry. What the readers wish to know is simply whether you have [u]published[/u] any results obtained by the use of money from the Bache Fund. If so when and where you published them - that is in what Journal Book or Periodical with Vol & page & usually the name

If you have any doubt about the amount assigned to you Dr Billings can certainly find all the information you desire.

Very truly yours
M C Meigs

Charles S Peirce Esq
Dr Billings' address is
Dr John S Billings
Army Medical Museum
Washington
