[u]Commandant's Office,[/u]
NAVY YARD,

Norfolk, Va, [date]1st July 1872[/date].

My dear Charley:-

I received your note and the accompanying article, yesterday (Sunday). If there were an opportunity and means to do so, it would give me great pleasure to render any service to Mr. Dana - and his pleasure would be a sufficient incentive to do the work.

paper is mainly, if not wholly, a transcript of the old article - Don't mention my name in connection with this paper. My object in this note is to shew you, that it is out of my power to be of any service to Mr. Dana.

Aunt Harrie and I are very curious to know if Xena is with you - she has not been mentioned in any of your Mother's letters, If she is with you, give her our best love - Aunt Harrie sends her best love to you - Yours, most affectionately -

Uncle Henry -
