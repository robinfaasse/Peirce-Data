But I may say to you,
strictly in confidence, that
this paper, which may have
been full and satisfactory
when it was originally
written, has now fallen
behind the times. It treats
of the Gulf Stream, at
length - and yet makes
no mention of the recent
papers of Petermann, Von
Freeden, Dr. Mühry and
Colding, nor of the
expeditions of Weyprecht,
Payer & others.

These investigations, now in progress,
profoundly interesting as they are in their
geographical & physical relations, derive
their primary source & origin from the
Gulf Stream.

It is also a defect of this paper,
in my opinion, that it makes no mention
of the labors of Pourtales, which now that
they have been extended into another
hemisphere and another ocean, must
furnish many characteristic features and
distinctions of the highest interest & importance.
