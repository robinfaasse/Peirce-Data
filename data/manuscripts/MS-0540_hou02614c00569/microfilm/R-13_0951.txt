10[u]th[/u]. A Argument is a sign whose interpretant represents
its object as being an ulterior sign through a law, namely, the
law that the passage from [ins]all[/ins] such premises to such conclu-
sions tend to the truth. Manifestly, then, its object must be
general; that is, the Argument must be a Symbol. As a Symbol
it must, further, be a Legisign. Its Replica is a Dicent [del]Indexical[/del] Sinsign.
