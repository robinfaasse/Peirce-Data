If parts of a proposition be erased so as to leave blanks
in their places, and if these blanks are of such a nature
that if each of them be filled by a proper name the result will
be a proposition, then the blank form of proposition which
was first produced by the erasures is termed a [u]rheme[/u]. Accord-
ing as the number of blanks in a rheme is 0, 1, 2, 3, etc.
it may be termed a [u]medad[/u] (from [u]μηδέν[/u], [del]nothing[/del]), [u]monad[/u],
[u]dyad[/u], [u]triad[/u], etc. rheme.
