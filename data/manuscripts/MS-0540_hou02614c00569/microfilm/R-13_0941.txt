A [u]Legisign[/u] is a law that is a sign. This law is usually established by men. Every conventional sign is a legisign. It is not a single object, but a general type which, it has been agreed, shall be significant. Every legisign signifies through an instance of its application, which may be termed a [u]Replica[/u] of it. Thus, the word 'the' [del]may occur[/del] [ins]will usually occur[/ins] [del]hourly[/del] from fifteen to twenty-five times on a page. It is in all these occurrences one and the same word, [del]one[/del] [ins]the same[/ins] legisign. Each single instance of it is a Replica. The Replica is a Sinsign. Thus, every Legisign requires Sinsigns, but these are not ordinary Sinsigns, such as are peculiar occurrences [ins]that are[/ins] regarded as significant. Nor would the Replica be significant if it were not for the law which renders it so.
