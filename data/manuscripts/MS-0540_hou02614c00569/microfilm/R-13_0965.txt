[u]8th[/u], a Rhematic Symbol is a sign connected with its
object by an association of general ideas so that its replica
calls up an image in the mind which, owing to certain habits
of that mind produces, or tends to produce, a general concept,
and the [del]sign[/del] [ins]replica[/ins] is interpreted as a Sign of any object of that
concept. In short, the Rhematic Symbol is a General Term.
The Rhematic Symbol, like any Symbol, is necessarily itself
of the nature of a general type, and is, therefore, a Legisign.
Its Replica, however, is [del]an[/del] [ins]a Rhematic[/ins] Indexical Sesisign, and its peculiar
power of producing a general [del]caps[/del] concept, which some Rhe-
matic Indexical Sesisigns do not, is owing to the action upon
the image it suggests upon a Symbol already existing in the
interpretant mind.
