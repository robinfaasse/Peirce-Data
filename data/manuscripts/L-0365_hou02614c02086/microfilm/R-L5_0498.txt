THE LIBRARY OF CONGRESS,
WASHINGTON.

S.

THE LIBRARIAN'S OFFICE.

[date]December 20, 1901.[/date]

Dear Sir:-

I have your letter of [date]December 18th[/date].

Heiberg's edition of Euclid is not in the Library.

Steingass' Arabic and English Dictionary has just been

purchased for reference and, we fear, could not well be spared.

With sincere regret that we are unable to comply with

your request, I am,

Very truly yours,

[signature]Herbert Putnam[/signature]
Librarian of Congress.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford,
Pennsylvania.
