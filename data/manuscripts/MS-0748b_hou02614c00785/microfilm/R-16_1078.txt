become easier on repetition. Along whatever path a nervous
discharge has taken place, along that path a new discharge
is the more likely to take place. Habit plays somewhat
the same part in the history of the individual that natural
selection does in that of the species.: namely, it causes
actions to be directed towards ends. When an irritation of
