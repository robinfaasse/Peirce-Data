(5) $[(X \prec Y) \prec X] \prec X$

<u>Proof</u>. For assume $(X \prec Y) \prec X$. Then if we are forced
to admit $X$ is true, the formula holds by A. But
by B if we assume $X \prec Y$ we are bound to admit
$X$ is true. And if we assume $X$ is not true by
A' we are to take $X \prec Y$.
