<u>Rule 3: principle of insertions and omissions.</u> Any
antecedent may be inserted under an even num-
ber of parentheses (or none) or dropped under an
odd number. For if b is true anyway, it is true if a
is true, hence by proposition A, if b is true a < b is true.
