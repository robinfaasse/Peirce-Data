1. When a parenthesis ends with the formula
[ins]or part in another parenthesis[/ins]
it is omitted. Thus

$$\begin{array}{lcl}
\text{for } a \prec (b \prec c) & \text{we write} & a \prec b \prec c \\
a \prec [b \prec (c \prec d)] & \text{"\quad"} & a \prec b \prec c \prec d \\
(a \prec b) \prec (c \prec d) & & (a \prec b) \prec c \prec d \\
a \prec [(b \prec c) \prec d] & & a \prec (b \prec c) \prec d \\
[a \prec (b \prec c)] \prec d & & (a \prec b \prec c) \prec d
\end{array}$$

\end{array}$$
