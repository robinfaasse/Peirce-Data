[u]Algebra of the Copula[/u]

The copula of consequence, $\prec$, may be defined
as follows:
$\alpha$. If $b$ is true, then $a \prec b$ is true.
$\beta$. [del]If[/del] [ins]Either[/ins] $a$ is [del]or[/del] $a \prec b$ is true.
$\gamma$. If $a \prec b$ and $a$ are true, $b$ is true.

The letters $a$ and $b$ may be replaced by proposi-
tions, that replacing $a$ being written in parenthe-
ses. Thus, we shall have the forms following:

With 0 copula: $A$.
With 1 copula: $A \prec B$.
With 2 copulas: $(A \prec B) \prec C$
$A \prec B \prec C$.
With 3 copulas: $[(A \prec B) \prec C] \prec D$
$(A \prec B \prec C) \prec D$
$(A \prec B) \prec C \prec D$
$A \prec (B \prec C) \prec D$
$A \prec B \prec C \prec D$.
