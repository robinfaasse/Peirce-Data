1 The copula is the sign [fig]copula sign resembling a fishhook or scroll[/fig]. Two letters with the copula between them make a proposition, as a[fig]copula sign[/fig]b. The preceding letter is called the antecedent, the [del]latter[/del] [ins]following one instead of a letter[/ins] the consequent of [del]the[/del] [ins]a[/ins] proposition. A proposition [ins]may[/ins] be consequent or antecedent of another proposition. A proposition which is the antecedent of another is to be written in parentheses. But when a series of letters or propositions are written with alternate copulas without parentheses everyone which is immediately preceded by a copula is called an antecedent
