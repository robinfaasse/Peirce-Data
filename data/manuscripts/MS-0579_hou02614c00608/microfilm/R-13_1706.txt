In like manner a consequent can [del]be dropped[/del] [ins]be dropped[/ins]
precisely as an antecedent except that the
next following one must be put in its place.

Thus
$$[(a \prec b) \prec c] \prec b = [(a \prec c) \prec c] \prec b$$
$$\{[(a \prec b) \prec c] \prec d\} \prec b = \{[(a \prec c) \prec c] \prec d\} \prec b$$

So
$$(a \prec b) \prec b \prec c = b \prec c$$
$$[(a \prec b) \prec c] \prec b \prec d = c \prec b \prec d$$
