[u]Algebra of the Copula[/u]

The copula of consequence, [u]⤙[/u], may be defined as follows:
$\alpha$. If $b$, then $a \mathbin{\} b$.
$\beta$. Either $a$ or $a \mathbin{\} b$.
$\gamma$. If $a \mathbin{\} b$ and $a$, then $b$.

The letters $a$ and $b$ may be replaced by propositions, that replacing $a$ being written in parenthesis. [del]The letter or proposition preceding[/del] [ins]What precedes[/ins] the copula is called the antecedent [ins]or thesis[/ins].
