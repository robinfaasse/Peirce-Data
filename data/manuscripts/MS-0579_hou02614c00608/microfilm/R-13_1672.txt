[u]Rule 1.[/u] The order of antecedents is immaterial.

[u]Rule 2.[/u] Any antecedent may be inserted under an even number of parentheses, or none, and dropped under an odd number.

[u]Rule 3.[/u] The insertion of an antecedent a second time either as antecedent of the whole proposition or of a parenthesized part of it has no effect [del]and the parenthesized antecedent may[/del] [ins]be dropped[/ins].

[u]Rule 4[/u] [del]If the consequent of a proposition is also antecedent of a parenthesized part, this part has no effect.[/del] The insertion of a parenthesized part having for [ins]identical with[/ins] antecedent [del]the[/del] the consequent of the proposition has no effect; and a proposition having its consequent [ins]identical with[/ins] [del]for[/del] one of its antecedents is identically true.
