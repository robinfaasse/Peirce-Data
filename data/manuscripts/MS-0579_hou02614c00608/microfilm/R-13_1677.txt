The copula may also be considered as de-
fined by the following two propositions.

[u]Proposition A[/u]. If from a proposition, P,
a proposition, Q, follows, we may write
P ⤙ Q.

For, by $\beta$, either P or P ⤙ Q is true; and [del]since[/del]
if P is true, since from P follows Q, then Q is
true, in which case, by $\alpha$, P ⤙ Q is true. So that
this is true whenever Q follows from P.

[u]Proposition B[/u]. Conversely, if P ⤙ Q is true, then
if P is true, Q is true. This is the same as $\gamma$.

From these propositions we deduce the algebraic
rules.
