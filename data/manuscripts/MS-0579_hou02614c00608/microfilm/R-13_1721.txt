[pn]V[/pn] $[(a \prec b) \prec c] \prec d = (\bar{a} + b)\bar{c} + d = (\bar{a} + b + d)(\bar{c} + d)$
$= \bar{a}\bar{c} + b\bar{c} + d$
[del]$= [b + (d \prec a)][/del]

Special cases
1 $[(a \prec b) \prec c] \prec a = c \prec a$ NB
2 $[(a \prec b) \prec c] \prec b = \bar{a}\bar{c} + b$ NB $= [(c \prec b) \prec a] \prec b$
3 $[(a \prec b) \prec c] \prec c = \bar{a} + b + c$ NB $= [(a \prec c) \prec b] \prec b$
4 $[(a \prec b) \prec a] \prec d = a \prec d$ by $2\ \text{II}\ 1$
5 $[(a \prec b) \prec b] \prec d = \bar{a}\bar{b} + d$
$= [(a \prec d) \prec b] \prec d$ NB
6 $[(a \prec a) \prec c] \prec d = c \prec d$ by $2\ \text{II}\ 3$
7 $[(a \prec a) \prec c] \prec a = c \prec a$ by $2\ \text{II}\ 3$
8 $[(a \prec b) \prec a] \prec b = a \prec b$ by $2\ \text{II}\ 1$
9 $[(a \prec b) \prec b] \prec b = a \prec b$ NB
10 $[(a \prec a) \prec c] \prec c$ Necessary by $2\ \text{II}\ 3$ and $1\ \text{I}\ 1$
11 $[(a \prec b) \prec a] \prec a$ Necessary by $2\ \text{II}\ 1$ and $1\ \text{I}\ 1$
