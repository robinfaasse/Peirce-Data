[u]Rule 2[/u] = principle of permutation. The order of the different antecedents is indifferent. Thus.

$$abcde = [cb(ef)ad]\dagger$$

[u]Rule 3: transitive principle[/u]. If we have given any proposition

$$abc\dots hijk\dots xyz$$

and if we also have

$$(abc\dots hi\dagger)\dagger$$

then we have further

$$jk\dots\dots xyz$$
