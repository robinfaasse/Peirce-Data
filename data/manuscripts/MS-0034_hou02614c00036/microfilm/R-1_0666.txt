Yet we have seen that the syllogism of transposed
quantity [del]is not applicable[/del] does not hold good of
the entire collection of numbers. This illustrates
one of the elementary principles of the logic of
relatives. Let $N$ be a number, $C$ a stage of
counting, $R$ the fact that a number is reached
in counting. Then,
$$N \Omega .. NRC$$
is true; that is, taking any number whatever,
[del]sometime[/del] there is a stage of counting which
reaches it. But
$$C \Omega .. NRC$$
is not true; that is, we cannot say [ins]that there
is[/ins] a stage of counting [del]such[/del] that reaches every
number.
