Let us now consider the doctrine of collections. A collection
differs from a quality only in this particular, that given any
quality, or combination of qualities not contradictory, it is
mathematically possible that there should be an object having that
quality or that combination of qualities. But given a collection,
its subjects are supposed to be fixed, and it ceases to be the same
collection if a new subject is added or subtracted.
