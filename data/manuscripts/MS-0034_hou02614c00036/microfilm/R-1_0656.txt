The second property of numbers is that the
Fermatian reasoning applies to them; that is to say,
that a certain relation, that of being next following
in a certain arrangement, and also a certain number,
the first in that arrangement, can be found, such
that, taking any predicate, $\beta$, no matter what, if
the premises of the following inference are true,
the conclusion is also true.

$U$ (the first number) has the [del]property[/del] [ins]character[/ins] $\beta$;

Taking any number, $H$, no matter what, either
it has not the [del]property[/del] [ins]character[/ins] $\beta$, or some number $Q$
(next following) to $H$ has this [del]property[/del] [ins]character[/ins].

$\therefore$ Every number has the [del]property[/del] [ins]character[/ins] $\beta$.

This Fermatian property of numbers may be written
thus:

$\propto C \beta \text{ I } \text{ N } .. U \underline{\bar{R}} \beta + H \underline{\bar{R}} \beta . I Q H . I \underline{\bar{R}} \beta + N \underline{\bar{R}} \beta$.
