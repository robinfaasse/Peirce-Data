§55. The fixed laws are not incompatible with the spiritual origin of force.
I have in this chapter taken up the subject of the Forces of Nature & I have treated it from a point of view which I think is the natural one for a thinking person. It carries the subject back as nearly up to metaphysics as possible. Seeing that force was sometimes spiritual in its origin I concluded that this was always the case, and that matter was only the medium which has the capacity to receive this will, life, force. Now to receive it under the form of motion is not more difficult than to receive it under the form of gravitation Remarks on the Conservation of Force.
