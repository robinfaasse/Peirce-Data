$$\text{D}_x \tan^{-1} x = \frac{1}{1+x^2}$$

$$\sin^{-1} x = \sum_n^\infty C_n x^n$$
$$\text{D}_x \sin^{-1} x = \sum_n^n C_n [del]x^{n-1}[/del] = (1-x^2)^{-\frac{1}{2}} = 1 + \frac{1}{2}x^2 + \frac{1\cdot 3}{2\cdot 4}x^4 \text{ \&c}$$
$$C_1 = 1$$
$$C_3 = \frac{1}{6}$$
$$C_5 = \frac{3}{40} \text{ \&c}$$

$$\cos^{-1} x = \frac{1}{2}\pi - x - \frac{1}{6}x^3 - \frac{3}{40}x^5 \text{ \&c}$$
