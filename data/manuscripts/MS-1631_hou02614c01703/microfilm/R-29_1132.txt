$D_t^2 x = v D_t \sin \frac{s}{g} = v \cos \frac{s}{g} D_t \frac{s}{g} = \frac{v^2}{g} \cos \frac{s}{g}$
$s = \frac{ds}{d \frac{s}{g}}$ $D_t \frac{s}{g} = \frac{v}{g}$ $R = \frac{v^2}{g}$
