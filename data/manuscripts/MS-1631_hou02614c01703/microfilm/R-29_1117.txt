Heat shall be taken as the emanation. We found that $\nabla^2 \Omega = 0$ represented fully the idea of emanation & represented nothing more — "the amount that passes out = what passes in + the amount generated within". Heat is a substance perpetually travelling from the origin — the heated masses; and which coming upon a geometrical surface covers that surface according to certain laws of intensity, &c. We wish to prove, now, that it is the same thing if it had been considered as emanating from one of these isothermal surfaces and that the external manifestations would be the same as if it came from the original masses. Certainly, in every elementary case it must be true that heat would take the same course in the two cases, owing to the law of uniform distribution [fig]concentric circles[/fig] or [fig]curved parallel lines[/fig]

But what is true for the elements is true for the whole. $\therefore$ The second prop. is demonstrated. It is demonstrated that the level surface distributes the heat, just as the original masses would.
