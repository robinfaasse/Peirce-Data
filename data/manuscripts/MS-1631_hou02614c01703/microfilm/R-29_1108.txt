[u]Gravitation & the Force of Statical Electricity.[/u]
[u]§102[/u] [u]Gravitation.[/u] Each particle of matter is an elemen-
tary centre of action for the force of gravitation, & all
the level surfaces for each particle are spherical
surfaces, of which the particle is the centre. The value
of the potential for any particle, is inversely propor-
tional to the distance from the particle, & for dif-
ferent particles it is proportional to the mass of
the particle.
