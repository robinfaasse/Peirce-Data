[u]Corollary 10[/u]. Within an even number of enclosures, anything may be [del]illustrat[/del] carried into two ovals, one within the other.

For example, from [u]Fig. 159[/u] "There is a prophet who if he is disobedient is swallowed by a whale," we can infer [u]Fig. 160[/u] "Somebody if he is disobedient is a prophet swallowed by a whale." This is a bad example. Take the following. [u]Fig 161[/u] means "There is a person who loves a brother of a person, yet who loves only servants of that person (if he loves anybody)." Hence we can infer [u]Fig 162[/u], "There is a benefactor of a [ins]person[/ins] [del]of a[/del] who loves [del]only[/del] servants of [ins]of[/ins] [del]that[/del] [ins]whom[/ins] [del]person[/del] [ins]that man is a brother[/ins]."
