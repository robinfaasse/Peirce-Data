The diagram represents the Scheme of the Possible. The class terms at the top of the diagram exhaust the universe. The probability that the generic description [ins]$\mathcal{G}$[/ins] which covers the universe will when determined turn out to be a [ins]$\mathcal{G}_x$[/ins] is indicated by the heavy line $\mathcal{G}_x - r$ with the light line under it. The probabilities of $\mathcal{G}_y$ and of $\mathcal{G}(\bar{x}\bar{y} + xy)$ are indicated in the same way.

If we add the probabilities [ins]$x$[/ins] and [ins]$y$[/ins] and subtract the probability $x\bar{y} + \bar{x}y$ and divide by two we have the probability [ins]$xy$[/ins] as shown in the diagram. This represents the probability that [ins]$x$[/ins] and [ins]$y$[/ins] agree in their statement and that the statement is true [ins]$\frac{x+y-r}{2}$[/ins]. Dividing this probability [ins]$(1-r)$[/ins] which is the probability that they agree in their statement, we obtain the conditioned probability required.

The heavy broken line at the lower part of the diagram illustrates this limited universe of discourse and the required conditioned probability.
