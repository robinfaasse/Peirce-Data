Eliminating $s$, $x$ and $y$ we obtain the value of $w$ in terms of given probabilities:

$$w_1 = \frac{p + q - r}{2}$$

$$\frac{w_1}{1-r} = \frac{p + q - r}{2(1-r)}$$
