[u]Relatives of Second Intention[/u]

Of absolute terms we have
$1_{a}$ or $a$ exists
and $0_{a}$ or $a$ does not exist

Of dual relatives
$1_{ab}$ or $a$ and $b$ are identical
$0_{ab}$ or $a$ and $b$ are not identical

Of triple relatives
$1_{ab} + 1_{ac} + 1_{bc}$
$1_{ab} 1_{ac}$
$0$ etc

$1_{a} \chi_{a} = \chi_{a}$
$\Sigma_{b} 1_{ab} \chi_{bc} = \chi_{ac}$
$\Pi_{b} ( \bar{1}_{ab} + \chi_{bc} ) = \chi_{ac}$
$\chi_{bc} \bar{1}_{ac} \prec \bar{1}_{ab}$
$\bar{1}_{ab} \prec \chi_{bc} + \bar{\chi}_{ac}$
$\bar{\chi}_{a} \chi_{a} \prec 0_{a}$
