To deal with a non-ultrainfinite
we have
$\phi x (\overline{\phi x} + \phi' x)$
or
$\phi x \phi' x$
but $\phi x$ implies $\phi x$ and therefore
$\phi' x$
may be assumed at once.

The same applies whatever the
meaning of $\phi x$ and shows that
any proposition involving $\Sigma \alpha$ later
than any $\Pi \beta$ is true provided it
is so when $\alpha$ is eliminated, and
provided $\alpha$ is defined only by the relation
between which it subsists.

Another thing which might do would
be to form a systematic list of
all such propositions.

But there seem
insuperable dif-
ficulties to this,
at least as a
thing to be put
into practice.
