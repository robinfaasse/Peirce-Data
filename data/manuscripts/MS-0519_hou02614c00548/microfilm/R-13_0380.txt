[u]Method of Elimination[/u]

Before the fifth step in my
rule (Adm. Jour. Math. vii. 198)
I should insert a step to use
as many separate indices as
possible to express the meaning.

For example, suppose we have
the premise
$\Pi_i \Pi_j (m_i + l_{ij} + \bar{m}_j)$.
The tokens [del]m[/del] cannot be directly
eliminated but we have the
above
$= \Pi_i \Pi_j (m_i + \bar{l}_{ij}) (l_{ij} + \bar{m}_j)$
$= \Pi_i \{ \Pi_j (m_i + \bar{l}_{ij}) \Pi_j (l_{ij} + \bar{m}_j) \}$
$= \Pi_i \Pi_j \Pi_k (m_i + \bar{l}_{ij}) (l_{ik} + \bar{m}_k)$
