[u]Use of General Formulae in $q_{\alpha}$ and $r_{\alpha}$[/u]

Suppose we have the premise
$\Pi_{k} (q_{ki} + \overline{q}_{kj})$

Now take the identical formula
$\Pi_{l} (q_{li} + \overline{q}_{li}) (q_{lj} + \overline{q}_{lj})$.

Introduce into this $q_{mi}$ and $q_{mj}$ in any way thus
$\Pi_{l} \Sigma_{m} (q_{li} \overline{q}_{mi} + \overline{q}_{li} q_{mi}) (q_{lj} \overline{q}_{mj} + \overline{q}_{lj} q_{mj})$

Multiply by the original premise
$\Pi_{l} \Sigma_{m} \Pi_{k} (q_{li} \overline{q}_{mi} + \overline{q}_{li} q_{mi}) (q_{lj} \overline{q}_{mj} + \overline{q}_{lj} q_{mj}) (q_{ki} + \overline{q}_{kj})$

By the fifth step
$\Pi_{l} \Sigma_{m} \Pi_{k} (q_{li} \overline{q}_{mi} + \overline{q}_{li} q_{mi}) (q_{lj} \overline{q}_{mj} + \overline{q}_{lj} q_{mj}) (q_{ki} + \overline{q}_{kj}) [ins](q_{li} + \overline{q}_{lj})[/ins]$
$= \Pi_{l} \Sigma_{m} \Pi_{k} (q_{li} \overline{q}_{mi} \overline{q}_{mj} q_{mj} \overline{q}_{lj} q_{mi} q_{mj}) (q_{ki} + \overline{q}_{kj})$

Eliminating $m$ [del]again[/del] and $k$
$\Pi_{l} \Pi_{k} (q_{li} + \overline{q}_{lj}) (q_{ki} + \overline{q}_{kj})$
$\Pi_{l} (q_{li} q_{lj} + \overline{q}_{li} \overline{q}_{lj})$

Try to do this with the identical formula
$\Pi_{k} \overline{q}_{kj} + q_{ki}$
$\Pi_{l} (q_{li} q_{lj} + \overline{q}_{li} \overline{q}_{lj})$
This does not do.

$q_{li} \overline{q}_{mi} \overline{q}_{mj} q_{lj} + \overline{q}_{li} q_{mi} \overline{q}_{lj} q_{mj} + \overline{q}_{li} q_{mi} \overline{q}_{lj} q_{mj}$
