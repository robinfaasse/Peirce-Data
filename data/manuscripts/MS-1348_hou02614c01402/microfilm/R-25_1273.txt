The even numbers of atoms in our formulae are the natu-
ral result of binary combinations.

Get rid of the even numbers by division. You will then
have fractions of atoms but the whole number of
atoms in the formula will be [del]even[/del] [ins]integral[/ins]. Thus
$\text{C}\ \text{H}_{1\frac{1}{2}}\ \text{O}\ \text{H}_{\frac{1}{2}}$     4 atoms
will be Marsh Gas.

Take the usual table of equivalent weights with the
following modifications:-
$$\begin{array}{ll}
\text{O} = 16 & \text{S} = 96 \\
\text{P} = 62 & \text{B} = 21 \\
\text{As} = 150 & \text{Si} = ? \\
\text{Sb} = 244 & \\
\text{Bi} = 420 &
\end{array}$$

Now make formulae as above. These modified letters
will have fractional indices which will not be com-
pensated for. These [del]exponents[/del] [ins]denominators[/ins] of these fractions, how-
ever, will be constant, and may be represented by
dots under the letters. We shall now find that
the equivalent weight of every compound is equal
to its vapor density.

N.B. It is necessary to give $\text{Hg}$ always an
even no. of atoms e.g. $\text{Hg}_2\text{Cl}_2$ not $\text{Hg}\text{Cl}$ and then
divide by 2.
