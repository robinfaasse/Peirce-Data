The oxygen evolved falls very little short of the
oxygen required for the combustion of the
hydrogen; so that no amount of chlorate
worth considering is formed. A solution
of hypochlorite in the electric circuit is
soured about the anode by electrolysis.
The chief advantage of the carbonate over caustic
alkali is that it does not interfere with this
souring. It must be remembered that only a
very small amount of hypochlorous acid needs
to be set free for the efficient souring of bleach;
since this same amount will always be free
as long as the bleaching goes on. For as fast as
the hypochlorous acid bleaches it forms hydro-
chloric acid which sets free an equivalent
amount of hypochlorous acid again.
