That the action of the carbonate is essentially

I believe. When chlorine acts upon hydrate of
sodium, one atom of the molecule of chlorine
takes the place of an atom of hydrogen in one
of the molecules of the hydrate, and this atom of
hydrogen takes the place of an atom of sodium in
another molecule of the hydrate, and this atom of
sodium takes the place of the lost atom in the
molecule of chlorine. Thus, these three atoms
