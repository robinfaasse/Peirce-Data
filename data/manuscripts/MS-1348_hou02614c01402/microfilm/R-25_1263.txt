Water may contain
1. Scale forming materials,
2. Frothmaking substances.

1. The scale-forming materials are chiefly
Sulphate of Lime $\text{Ca SO}_4$
Bi Carbonate of Lime $\text{Ca H}_2\text{ C}_2\text{O}_6$
(the last of somewhat uncertain composition.)
Salts of magnesia are also commonly enumerated but they are of little importance.

2. There are many frothing salts. The worst is sulphate of soda $\text{Na}_2\text{SO}_4$
Common chloride of sodium $\text{NaCl}$
also has this character in some degree.

The sulphate of lime is deposited as anhydrous gypsum
