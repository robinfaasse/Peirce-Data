It was found that the Javelle water bleached in every respect as well as Woolf's process. Hence, there was no sensible advantage from the direct electrolysis. In point of fact, the current passes almost wholly between the electrodes, and consequently the coloring matter in Woolf's process is little exposed to direct electrolysis. But in direct electrolysis lies the future of electrical bleaching.
