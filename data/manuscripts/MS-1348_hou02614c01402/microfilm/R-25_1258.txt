The same thing is still more true of the electrolysis of chloride of aluminium. But upon the addition of baryta water, the formation of [ins]free[/ins] chlorine is checked. Alumina is not precipitated by an excess of baryta water.

In the electrolysis of the chlorides, a small amount of chlorate is formed, sufficient to reduce the bleaching power by from 5 to 15 per cent. If the chloride of potassium were used, the chlorate would accumulate in the solution until it could be crystallized out to advantage. The chlorate of potassa is worth 8 cents a pound, and the manufacture of it would be profitable.
