Practically all that Mr. Woolf's process
does is to produce a bleaching solution. This
is shown by the fact that it bleaches no better
than Javelle water of the same oxidizing
power. The only questions, therefore, which
my contract calls upon me to answer are
in what manner, as far as reasonable
research can show, does Mr. Woolf produce
a bleaching solution, and how can then
process be modified by varying the in-
gredients of the solution, etc.
