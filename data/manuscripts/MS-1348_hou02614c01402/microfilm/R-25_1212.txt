[u]Electrolysis of solutions of chlorides[/u]. Whenever
a salt is electrolysed, the first, or direct effect,
is that its metal is liberated at the cathode and
the residue of the salt at the anode.

When a solution or mixture is electrolysed
the different ingredients are decomposed in
the same proportions as they would be in
branch circuits in similar solutions.

Hence, water cannot be decomposed by
direct electrolysis; since when pure it is
not an electrolyte.
