I find that a mixture of nitrate and chloride
of sodium or potassium bleaches with great
rapidity and efficiency. But the process is
not quite cyclical. It should be patented.

All mixtures of [ins]alkaline[/ins] salts for bleaching are
improved by the addition of carbonate in small
quantity. This should be patented.

I have examined several hundred other
combinations of salts, but am satisfied that
there is nothing else practical. Of course, very
high priced substances are out of the question.
