Process for intimately mixing large quantities
of water with a solution.

The solution is admitted through a tube
into the middle of the vein of water pouring
from the [del]main[/del] [ins]pipe[/ins] into the tank, the rate of
flow of water and solution being rendered
severally uniform.

These rates of flow are rendered uniform
by their flowing from tanks of large superficies
where the level of the water is automatically
adjusted by a ball floating on the water and
regulating a stopcock or by other automatic
contrivance to keep the level constant. These
stop-cocks are in the pipes supplying these tanks.
