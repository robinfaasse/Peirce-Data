The magnesia salts tend to become basic
by surrender of their acids to stronger bases; and these
basic salts are mainly insoluble; though soluble
in ammoniacal salts.

2. The most frothing salt is sulphate of
soda $\text{Na}_2\text{SO}_4$, the behaviour of which in solution
exhibits several anomalies. Its solubility undergoes
several sharp changes with temperature, and its
crystallization is accompanied by a large evolution of
heat.
