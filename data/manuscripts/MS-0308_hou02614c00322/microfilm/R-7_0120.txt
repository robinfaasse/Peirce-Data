The three categories furnish an artificial classification of all possible systems of metaphysics which is certainly not without its utility. The scheme is shown in [del]Fig. 5.[/del] [ins]Fig. 5.[/ins] It depends upon which ones of the three categories each system admits as important metaphysico-cosmical elements.

One very naturally and properly endeavors to give an account of the universe with the fewest and simplest possible categories.

Praedicamenta non sunt multiplicanda praeter necessitatem.
