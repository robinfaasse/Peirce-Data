New York, [date]Feb. 13th, 1894.[/date]

My dear Professor Peirce:

I was sick when your letter came and unable to reply to it, but now I wish to become a subscriber to the first volume of your projected work on the "Principles of Philosophy."

Please give my kind regards to Mrs Peirce -

Sincerely yours,
Ogden N. Rood -

Prof. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa -
