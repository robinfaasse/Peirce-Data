Columbia College,
New York, [date]Oct. 17th, 1890[/date].-

My dear Professor:

Since you were so kind as to be interested in the ancient coins the other night at the Century, I now send you some antique trash that may amuse you for a moment, and then find a place in a corner of your pocketbook.-

First a bronze of Alexander the Great, with his portrait arranged to personate his ancestor Hercules in the lion skin - It is so battered and worn by time and water as to preclude any idea of modern imitation, and of the inscription only two letters (AN) remain.-
