Columbia College, N. Y., [del]1[/del]
[date]March 14[ins]th[/ins], 1894[/date].

My dear Mr Peirce;

I have received your letter of March 12[ins]th[/ins] and in reply have only three things to say -

First: when I wrote you my two recent letters I suspected I was making a fool of myself, but now I know it.

Second: it is not my habit to address communications of any kind whatever to persons to whom I am hostile.

Third: I have already refused to use my influence in the Century Club against your reinstatement, but I certainly shall not use it in favor of such reinstatement -
