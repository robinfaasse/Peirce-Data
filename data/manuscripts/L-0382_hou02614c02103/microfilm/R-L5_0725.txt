Columbia College, N.Y.
[date]Nov. 4th 1886.[/date]

My dear Peirce:

Mrs Rood was at the college a few minutes after your letter came. The house is in great disorder, but if you and Mrs. Peirce are willing to pardon it, she says she will be glad to see you there.
(1067 4th Ave near 62nd St.)

Your experiment I will think about.

Sincerely,
Ogden N. Rood
