Columbia College,
New York, [date]Nov. 16th,
1885[/date]-

My dear Peirce;

I have received your
two notes, and have made some
inquiries regarding your project
or proposal. As far as I know
or can learn, all the kind of
work you speak of is done
within the companies or firms, by
their own men. The only field
open would be in patent cases
or law suits, which would not
amount to much. I would
advise you to try to attach
yourself to some organization
