That is to say, on the third system of projection the distances of the point representing a mixture from two points representing its constituents are inversely as the masses of the points representing the constituents. In other words, the point which represents a mixture is laid down at the centre of mass of the points representing its constituents. From the sum of (4) and (5), it follows that the [del]weight[/del] [ins]mass[/ins] of the point representing the mixture is equal to the sum of the masses of the constituents. A diagram constructed on this system is called [del]a[/del] Newton's diagram.
