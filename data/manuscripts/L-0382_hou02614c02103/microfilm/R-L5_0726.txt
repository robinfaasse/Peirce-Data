Stockbridge, Mass.,
[date]July 31[ins]st[/ins], 1889[/date].

My dear Peirce:
I inquired of several ladies concerning "Isabel color", and finally visited Stewart's store in New York during a recent trip in that direction. The people there had not heard of it, and then I examined the pattern books with "shades" of color and names, many of which were quite new to me, but the term "Isabel" did not make its appearance.

What do think of Mendenhall as superintendent of the Coast Survey?

Affectionately,
Ogden N. Rood_
