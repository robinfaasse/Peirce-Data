New York, March 3[ins]rd[/ins], 1890.

My dear Peirce:

I was very glad to get both your notes, and have been intending daily and weekly to answer the first and thank you for it.

I shall not be at the College on Tuesday afternoon, but will come down to the Century on the following Saturday evening and bring along with me a good cigar, which you certainly will enjoy if you are able to be present.

Sincerely,
Ogden N. Rood.

Prof Chas Peirce,
109 East 15[ins]th[/ins] St.,
New York.
