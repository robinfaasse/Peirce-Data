[u]Dichotomic Mathematics[/u].
By C. S. Peirce

[u]Hypothesis I[/u]. 1. We are to be supposed to have to do with [u]symbols[/u], which are certain objects of a general nature whose being lies in being represented, like purposes.

We shall also have to do with [u]replicas[/u] of symbols. A replica is a definite individual belonging each to one symbol. Each symbol may have a multitude of replicas.
