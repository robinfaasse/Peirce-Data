I shall make use of the headings found in editions of
Euclid's Elements, viz., Definition, Postulate, Axiom,
Theorem and Demonstration, Problem and Solution,
Corollary, and Scholium, understanding these terms
as follows:

[u]A Scholium[/u] is a comment elucidating the structure
of the discussion.

[u]A Definition[/u] analyzes a conception signified by a
term, the definitum, and has two members of which the
first states that anything to which the definition [del]words[/del]
apply would have the characters signified by the defini-
tion, while the second states that [ins]of[/ins] anything having the
characters signified by the definition the definitum
could be predicated; and observe that no definition as-
serts the existence of anything.
