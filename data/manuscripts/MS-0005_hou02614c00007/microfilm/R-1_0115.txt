¶ 2. Any transformation of a replica of a good symbol
into a replica of a bad symbol would be bad; and no
good transformation would be bad.

[u]Corollary 4[/u]. A transformation of any replica into a
replica of a good symbol would, by Def. 1 ¶ 7, be good.

[u]Corollary 5[/u]. A transformation of a replica of a bad
symbol into any replica would, by Def. 1 ¶ 7, be good.

[u]Corollary 6[/u]. By Def. 1 ¶ 7, any transformation of a
replica of a symbol, A, into a replica of a symbol
agreeing with A in respect to being good or bad, would be
good.

[u]Corollary 7[/u]. From Corollaries 1 and 6, it follows that if
there is any replica whatever, there is a good transforma-
tion.

[u]Corollary 8[/u]. By Cor. 6, if all symbols are alike in
respect to being good or bad, all possible transformations
are good.
