A Quoddam, or Matter, (one might call it a place, if from this word we were to eliminate, without excluding, all implication of space and of continuity,) of which a non-ego, or resisting something, is an example, is [ins]such[/ins] [del]that[/del] [ins]its[/ins] [del]whose[/del] being consists entirely in its reactions with other quoddams. As reacting, it really exists and is individual; that is, in respect to every quality, its every part contains some determination in one or other of two ways, possessing or not possessing that quality. But since its whole being consists in reaction, in itself it is nothing but the mutual reaction of its parts, and they are of the same nature. It is, therefore, [del]inevitable[/del] [ins]vague[/ins]; that is, nothing prevents its [del]being[/del] containing contrary determinations; and however homogeneous it may be, this characteristic will always cling to it.
