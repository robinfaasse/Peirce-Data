[u]First Theory.[/u]

[u]Postulate 1[/u]. Let it be supposed that we are acquainted with certain meanings, called [u]Symbols[/u], each of which is capable of embodiment, to indefinite multiplication, [del]each embodiment taking place in a[/del] [ins]at definite[/ins] individual object[ins]s[/ins], called a [u]Replica[/u] of that symbol, so that [del]each[/del] [ins]the[/ins] replica shall convey the meaning of which it is a replica to anybody who interprets it.

[u]Postulate 2[/u]. Let it be supposed that we have a power, such that, when a replica exists, we can do what may be necessary so that, in place of that replica, a replica shall exist, especially, a replica of a different symbol; and let the preëxisting replica be then said [del]to be[/del] [ins]to be[/ins] [u]transformed[/u] into the post-existing replica.

[u]Corollary 1[/u]. A replica which remains unchanged is transformed into itself.
