Transformation supposes a junction of two jungent
replicas to form a [del]jungent[/del] replica. If we distinguish ar-
bitrarily between the two jungents, calling one [del]the[/del]
jungend, or prejungend, and the other the adjungent,
then every junction must be related to the prejungend
in one or other of four ways; namely, either the right
to create the adjungent determines a right to transform
the prejungend into the junct, or determines a right
to transform the junct into the jungend, or both, or
neither. But the third case is best treated as complex,
while the fourth does not admit of any regular rule.
