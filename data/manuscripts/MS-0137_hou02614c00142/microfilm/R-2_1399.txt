happen to be. To how many is it in the nature of the general word
to be applicable? To any multitude whatsoever. But any word,
even a proper name, is general in another sense. For the word
itself is not anything written or spoken. If it were, there would be
not one definite article in English, but I know not how many
millions. There is only one word "the". It is the general type, not
an existent object, but a type, a general rule. People are in
the habit of speaking of "mere" words, as if nothing of the nature
of a word could be of much consequence. The notion is that that
