The prevalence through the mathematical world of a conception of the continuity of Space which deprives topics of its logical cohesion seemed to exact the above lengthy introduction, from whose logical subtleties, utterly foreign to the manner of thought of topics, we hasten to free ourselves. We shall be able to do so as soon as we can enter upon topics. But topics presupposes the doctrine of Time; and the doctrine of Time certainly presupposes the doctrine of Multitude and the doctrine of Characters. It would seem that it must also presuppose the doctrine of Ordinal Number. How these three doctrines are related to one another and to the doctrine of Logic is not, at present, entirely clear. This article does not aim at systematic perfection, by any means; and I will limit myself to setting down what it seems the most desirable to have explained in regard to these preliminary matters.
