The prevalence in the mathematical world of [del]the[/del] [ins]a[/ins] conception of the continuity of space which deprives topics
of its logical cohesion seemed to necessitate that long introduc-
tion, whose logical subtleties are as foreign to topics as anything
could well be. We here bid them a glad adieu for the rest
of this article, retaining however the notion that the
instants of time are logically welded together so as to lose
their separate being except so far as marked by events.
The [ins]true[/ins] continuity of Time of course necessitates the true
continuity of Space.
