People think it is of no consequence because it needs to be embodied in a passing thought or in the habit of some person before it can be effective. [del]That[/del] That undoubtedly is true. But the image in the mind would be of no consequence if it were not governed [ins]or conformed[/ins] by the thought. The two factors are both indispensable. But the difference is, that given the word, it matters comparatively little whether it be embodied in this momentary thought [del]while[/del] or in that; the effect will be substantially the same. But it makes a good deal more difference whether the image in the mind be joined by its associations with this concept or with that. This would seem to make the general factor of effectiveness far more important than the individual element.
