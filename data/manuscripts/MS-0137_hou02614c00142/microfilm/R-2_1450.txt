The instantaneously present state of things is, at once, just beginning
to be and just ceasing to be. Now what mode of being is it which
evades the principle of contradiction? It is possibility. It is
possible for me to sin and possible for me not to sin. The mode-
rately skilled logician will call this [ins]instance[/ins] [del]to be[/del] puerile. He will deny
that there is any contradiction here. I myself used to be
merry over similar remarks in Hegel. I now say that the
regulation logician both right and wrong. You know what
the doctor of the middle ages used to say when he was [del]partly[/del]
caught in a flat contradiction: he used to say [u]Distinguo[/u],
and invariably emerged triumphant from the snare.
