It will [del]that[/del] exemplify the method I propose to use, if I begin by deducing from the enunciation of the theorem the definition of an aggregate, assuming it to be like a sum. Therefore, assuming 'aggregate' to be the same as 'sum' when there are no common parts, we must say that when two aggregants have a common part, the aggregate is the sum less that common part.
