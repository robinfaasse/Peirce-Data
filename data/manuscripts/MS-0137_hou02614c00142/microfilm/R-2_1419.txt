Non-singular places have their highest Listing number equal
to their chorisy. That is a non-singular line has cyclosy one for each
piece, a non-singular surface has periphraxy one for each piece,
and non-singular space (boundless space) has apeiry one. An
interrupting point [del]place[/del] put upon a non-singular place diminishes
the highest Listing number by unity, reducing it to zero; while
every subsequent point increases the next lower number by
unity. This shows that the census-number of the unit of the highest
Listing number is the negative of that of the unit of the next
Listing number; or CAs = -CTs, CTr = -CKr, CKq = -CXq.

A [del]sing[/del] non-singular surface of chorisy 1 has periphraxy 1. Its
cyclosy may have any [del]posi[/del] integer value from zero up. The surface
of an ordinary body has cyclosy 0. The plane of projective geometry
has cyclosy 1. If two holes be made in a surface, and a filament
[del]occupying[/del] [ins]lying on[/ins] the edge of one hole moves [del]som[/del] away from the surface
and then down upon it so as to occupy the edge of the second
hole, the [del]cy[/del] cylindrical part of the surface so generated [del]formly[/del]
[del]form a[/del] [ins]is a[/ins] bridge as viewed from one side of the surface with
a tunnel beneath the bridge, but as viewed from the other
