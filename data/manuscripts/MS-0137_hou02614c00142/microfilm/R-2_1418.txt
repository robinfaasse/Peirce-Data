P, q, r, s; point, line, surface, space.

Subjacent numbers attached to P, q, r, show that the point, line, or surface is a singularity of that grade belonging to what is described in the following letters. Thus [del]P[sub]2[/sub]q[/del] P[sub]2[/sub]q[sub]0[/sub]r[sub]0[/sub]s would be a point of crossing of two three-way splits of an outlying surface considered as a singularity of a solid. A brace is used used where the [del]bounding[/del] singularity belongs to the place of meeting of different [del]to[/del] kinds of places and a letter may be repeated to show that the same place has different characters as belonging to one or another place. Thus P[sub]0[/sub]{P[sub]0[/sub]q[sub]0[/sub]r[sub]0[/sub]s[sup]2[/sup]} would mean a point which is a three-way furcation of an outlying line of a surface intersecting that surface at an otherwise ordinary point of it.
