The utility of the theorem that the census-number of a
whole, so defined, equals that of its parts is very great.
Let us illustrate this by tracing out one or two applications of it.
The entire universe of space must be regarded as all one
piece; that is, the other pieces, which presumably exist, are
in no spatial relation to the only one we know, in this life.
