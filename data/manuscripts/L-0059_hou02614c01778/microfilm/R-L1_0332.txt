Prof. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Pa.

Dear Sir:-

We beg to apologize for not advising you concerning our decision regarding the publishing of your proposed book, to be entitled "Calculations of Chances." The Autumn and Holiday business has prevented us conferring together concerning it.

We have considered the matter very thoroughly, and while we think there is a [del]field[/del] [ins]field[/ins] for the book, we do not believe that it will have as great a succes through us as through some other publishing house. If you will permit us, we would suggest your offering the book to Messrs Scribner's, 5th Avenue and 19th Street, New York, or The Frederick A. Stokes Co., West 23rd Street, New York.

We thank you very much for submitting the plan to us, and beg to return to you herewith the copy of Bertrand's "Calculul des Probabilités," by express.

Yours very faithfully,
Brentano's Publishing Department.
