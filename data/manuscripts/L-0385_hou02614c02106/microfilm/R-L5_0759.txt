My dear Professor Royce:

I have half a dozen very particular
things to say to you, and I shall have
to beg you to read a long letter.

[u]1st[/u], Please know that I never received
from your publisher Vol I of your [u]World[/u]
[u]and Individual[/u]. I should not have omitted
to write and thank you for one of the most
valuable works on my shelves. You seem
to me to have penetrated metaphysics deeper
than anyone. I am not at all sure you
will not make your notion of reality more
distinct: I think you will do us that service.
That it is in the right road I feel sure. I am
going to send you Vol II to have my name
written in it by you.

[u]2nd[/u], I have been extremely touched by your
kind references to me. I even wonder at
them: for men, I fancy, are able to say where
they first got hints which they have since
greatly elaborated. Witness Aristotle at the
end of the Sophistical Elenchi.
