In repeating this now weary tale to you, I only want you to consider, before you write again, whether, in view of these facts, the expression "brutal life-and-death fight from the first," expresses justly the true situation. There was a contest. I have had more than one in my life. But it was a contest about a matter of scholarship, not about anybody's life or death as to any other matter than just this question of scholarship. On my side I tried to observe the rules of the game. Dr. Abbot was equally sincere.
