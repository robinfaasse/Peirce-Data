There are microscopical microbes
in this world whose powers of
mischief are proportional to the
extent to which they make their
living of doing their business. They
are adroit and energetic. They
live in an atmosphere of lies &
they won't scruple at anything.
So if you will aid me, it will
need an exertion of the will.

C. S. Peirce
