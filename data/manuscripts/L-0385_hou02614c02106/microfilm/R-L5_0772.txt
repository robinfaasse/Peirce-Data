My article on Symbolic Logic has been greatly
hampered, because, although I offered to supply the
types, Baldwin would not consent to my using my
notation, he being guided by Mrs. Franklin. I have
seen nothing of the logical articles except three I wrote,
which have been edited, — by Mrs. Franklin, I suppose.
As I thought it included in my engagement, that I
should keep the peace with them, I have not expressed
myself fully to them; but shall do so, ultimately.

My article on Symbolic Logic, as it was origin-
ally written, would have been of very little use
to your class, because Baldwin had forbidden me,
any use of symbols, unless I used Mrs. Franklin's
to which I have very solid objections. It was, and is,
therefore, about Symbolic Logic, but hardly goes into
the subject. Baldwin would not even allow Euler's
diagrams to be treated. But Baldwin submitted
my article to one Louis Couturat for his judgment.
It came back to me with a great quantity of objections
of just that degree of stupidity & ignorance to be
useful to me, [del]as[/del] [ins]so as[/ins] to give me an excuse for enlarging
upon them. I fear Baldwin will not print what
I have, in consequence, inserted; but if he does, I think
the article might be of some use, as a side reference
under a teacher like you.
