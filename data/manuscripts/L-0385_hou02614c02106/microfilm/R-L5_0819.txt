But now it is time to look out to see whether owing to the accidents of our form of expression, these transformations may not have produced some unintended and unjustifiable limitations on account of which correcting modifications are called for. In point of fact, this has occurred in two ways. For, in the first place, we have investigated $E$, and have found that $E(\alpha)$ does not place the slightest limitation upon the character of any element of $\alpha$ considered by itself. Now our last expression
$\sum_{\omega}\Pi_{i}\Pi_{j}\bar{\alpha}_{i}\bar{\alpha}_{j}\psi E_{\omega i}\cdot E_{\omega j}$ does assert something concerning the elements simply namely $\sum_{\omega}\Pi_{i}\bar{\alpha}_{i}\psi E_{\omega i}$. To correct this we must write $\sum_{\omega}\Pi_{i}\Pi_{j}\bar{\alpha}_{i}\bar{\alpha}_{j}\psi E_{\omega i}E_{\omega j}\cdot \bar{\psi}_{i}\bar{\psi}_{j}$
