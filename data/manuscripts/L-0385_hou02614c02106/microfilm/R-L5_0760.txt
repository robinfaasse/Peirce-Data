I thought it involved in that promise to keep
the peace; and therefore I ought not to com-
plain to others. But as bearing upon your
request for extra copies, it is pertinent to say
that I should not wonder if you got them; and if
you do, I should be much obliged to you if you would
send me a copy. I offered to pay for the press-
work, paper, & binding of a copy of the diction-
ary; but they said I must pay the trade price,
which I cannot afford. Although the agree-
ment was that I should have charge of the
logic, I have seen no articles but my own
and do not know what else there is, as I have
no proofs of my own articles.
As for my article on Symbolic Logic, Baldwin
would allow no notation to be used not approved
by Mrs. Franklin, & the rules laid down have had
the effect of effectually preventing the expression
of my [del]own[/del] doctrines. In fact, I could not go
into the algebra at all, but only speak generally
concerning it. I was not allowed to give my
reasons for preferring one notation over
another. I was not allowed even to intro-
duce Euler's diagrams. I offered to supply
the types for a few formulae of my own;
but this was not permitted.
