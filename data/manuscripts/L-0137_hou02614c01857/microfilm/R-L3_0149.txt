"Arisbe" P.O. Milford Pa. [date]1904 March 18[/date]

My dear Sir:

The editor of the [u]Nation[/u] encloses to me your Post Card of Feb 4 asking for a copy of a notice of your Galileo that I contributed to that journal. I am writing to ask whether he has sent you one. If not I will; and if you like it I shall feel that a part of my indebtedness to you for much pleasure and information has been discharged.

The long delay has been due to an act of God, a great flood of the Delaware River (in the valley of which my place is) having carried away 3 bridges & a ferry with loss of half a dozen lives & thus having caused us to be for a long time completely cut off from the outer world.

very truly
C.S. Peirce

J.J. Fahie Esq
Rapallo, Italy
