[u]English Spelling[/u].

about other matters have proposed eccentric
spellings in a more or less wholesale way "for gene-
ral adoption." But they do not commonly go
to the length of [ins]themselves[/ins] spelling [del]with these[/del] as they recommend
others to do. No practice can come into "general
adoption" except by some individual's adopting
it and being admired for doing so by others.
