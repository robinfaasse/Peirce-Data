I did not go back, as I had planned, 
the next evening to the little 
church in the village. It seemed 
better to keep the memory of that 
one hour and the music, and not 
to risk its being changed or spoiled.
Later I felt glad that I had not 
gone back, for the memory of 
that hour of music has stayed 
with me, and I can hear it 
now, whenever I close my eyes.
