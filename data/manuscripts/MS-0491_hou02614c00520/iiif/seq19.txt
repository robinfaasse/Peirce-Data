No I. On Existential Graphs.
Part I.

A [u]Graph[/u] is a diagram consisting of no more than, first, the sheet upon which it is written, secondly, spots (or their equivalents) [del]variously distinguished[/del] [ins]having various visible qualities[/ins] (as colors, etc), third, lines of connection (commonly of only two kinds, those that are drawn and those that are left undrawn), and fourth enclosing ovals.
An [u]entire graph[/u] is a graph on a sheet otherwise blank. A [u]partial graph[/u] is a part (possibly the whole) of an entire graph, which [del]part if it were alone upon the sheet would be diagram[/del] [ins]part if it were have a meaning also[/ins].
