A [u]symbol[/u]* is a representamen whose representative force depends on how it is interpreted.

This sounds like nonsense; for what else is the interpretative force of a representation but its interpretation? But an example will at once show what is meant. The word “man” has the meaning it has simply by virtue of there being a general law, or habit, among English speaking interpreters, to which the interpretations of it will conform. Not only is “man” a “general sign” [u]formaliter[/u], or in its signification, but it is also general [u]materialiter[/u], in its mode of being as a sign.
