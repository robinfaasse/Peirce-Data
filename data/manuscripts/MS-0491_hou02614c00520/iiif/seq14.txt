PART I.
A [u]pure[/u] icon [ins]or image,[/ins] would be a [del]representation[/del] [ins]men[/ins]* whose [ins]special[/ins] representative character should depend entirely upon qualities or characters which it would possess although its object had no existence. It would therefore represent whatever was like it or analogous to it.
A geometrical figure of a triangle may be taken as an example of an icon. To be sure, no representation can actually take place until there is an object and an interpreter. But still the shape of the figure belongs to [del]the it[/del] [del]shape[/del] in itself, as much as any character belongs to a thing
