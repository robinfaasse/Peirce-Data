by this system of Existential Graphs.

Principle I. [u]Of the Sheet[/u]. The sheet on which the graphs
are written (called the [u]sheet of assertion[/u]), as well as each
portion of it, is a graph asserting that a recognized
universe is definite (so that [del]no[/del] [ins]no[/ins] assertion can be both
true and false of it), individual (so that any assertion is
either true or false of it), and real (so that what is true and
what false of it is independent of any judgment of man
or men, unless it be that of the creator of the universe; in
case this is fictive); [del]and[/del] any graph written upon this
sheet is thereby asserted of that universe; and any [del]number[/del] [ins]multitude[/ins]
[del]or continuum of graphs written disconnectedly upon the sheet are all asserted of the[/del]
[ins]unfigured[/ins]
