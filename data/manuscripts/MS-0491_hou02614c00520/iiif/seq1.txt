LOGICAL TRACTS.
By C. S. Peirce.
№ I. On Existential Graphs.

PART I.
Representation is a relation of one thing, — the [u]representamen[/u], or [u]sign[/u] — to another, — the [u]object[/u], — this relation consisting in the determination of a third, — the [u]interpretant[/u] representamen, — to be in the same mode of relation to the second as the first is to that second.

There are three modes of representation and three corresponding genera of representamens, these being [u]icons[/u], [u]indices[/u], and [u]symbols[/u]. ([u]Proceedings[/u] of the [u]American Academy of Arts and Sciences[/u], in [u]Boston[/u], for 1867 May 14. Vol VII, p. 294.)
