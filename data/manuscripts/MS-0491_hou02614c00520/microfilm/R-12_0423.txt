[u]N[sup]o[/sup] I. On Existential Graphs.[/u]

PART I.

[u]Representation[/u] is a relation of one thing, — the [u]representamen[/u], or sign, — to another, — the [u]object[/u], — this relation consisting in the determination of a third, — the [u]interpretant[/u] representamen, — to be in the same mode of relation to the second as the first is to that second.

There are three modes of representation and three corresponding genera of representamens, these being [u]icons[/u], [u]indices[/u], and [u]symbols[/u]. ([u]Proceedings of the American Academy of Arts and Sciences[/u], in Boston, for [date]1867 May 14[/date]. Vol VII, p. 294.)
