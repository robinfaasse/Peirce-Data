A [u]symbol[/u]* is a representamen whose representa-
tive force depends on how it is interpreted.

This sounds like nonsense; for what else is the
interpretative force of a representation but its inter-
pretation? But an example will at once show what
is meant. The word "man" has the meaning it has
simply by virtue of there being a general law, or habit,
among English speaking interpreters, to which the
interpretations of it will conform. Not only is "man"
a "general sign" [u]formaliter[/u], or in its signification,
but it is also general [u]materialiter[/u], in its mode of
being as a sign. It is certainly not an individual
existent individual. A book of which millions of
copies are in existence contains this word in
