Under these circumstances I for one
am not going to give my energies
for nothing to this work.

To oblige you, I will write one
letter to Science to declare my
convictions & to say that the criticisms
of your work have been one-sided
& partisan, although their authors
have had no personal interest to
blind them, in a measure to
excuse partisan talk. Further
than that I will not go down in
to the wet trenches. Let the great manufac-
turers to whom this question means hundreds
of thousands of dollars take some interest in
their own business & pay for what is to save
them so much. I wouldn't turn my hand over to save them all
from ruin.

A young man whose energy is more than
he knows what to do with may perhaps see
his way to benefit himself by going into
such a controversy without any remuneration
at first. He may hope it will lead to something.
But a man in my situation would be a
simpleton to do so.

Yours very faithfully
C. S. Peirce
