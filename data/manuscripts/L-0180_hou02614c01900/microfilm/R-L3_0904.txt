356 W 120th Street
New York
[date]Sept 23 1904[/date]

Dear Mr Peirce

I am greatly obliged for your word of encouragement. Of course I expect the thing will come up again though I think not for a few years. There are underground forces at work that should have an influence.

I sent you a copy of the [u]Sun[/u] [u]mail[/u] this morning containing the notice of the British Knight and Monies Association which I helped to join. This has a fuller notice week after next which I will send you. Not much of my stuff appears in the Machinist — Editor in chief won't allow it.
