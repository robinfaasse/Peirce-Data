P.O. Milford Pa [date]1905 Jan 15[/date]
VB

My dear Mr. Halsey:

Finding myself overrun with more work than I could possibly do, I fear I never answered your last.

I attacked the project of making the metric system compulsory mainly on the ground that it would gravely injure the machinery interests without much advantage to anybody. But nobody in these interests ever thanked me for my Nation article & my inference is that they are paying as they can well afford to do, for all the aid they desire & looked upon my article as an officious & impertinent meddling with their affairs.

Any way I will not turn my hand over to further oppose the proposed measure unless the particular action in which I am asked to take part is authorized & somebody is properly paid for it. As a general rule, I do not think it fair to workers in any department to offer to do such work for nothing. I hope you are receiving the rich reward that you have amply earned very truly C.S. Peirce
