That two [del]writers[/del] [ins]thinkers[/ins] of such disparate types should reach any common conclusion was hardly to be expected; that that conclusion should be so contrary to what had been generally [del]assumed[/del] [ins]believed[/ins] by men of great ability for more than a generation, and at the same time that its truth should be put so completely beyond dispute as it is, is one of the most singular occurrences in the history of letters.
