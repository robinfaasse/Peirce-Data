Mr. Gannett _ [date]1880 Apr 1[/date]

Qu 1. What one among the
topics lectured upon by
me do you understand best?

I think I know most about
probabilities & the Logic of
Relatives. Don't know
much about "Least Squares".
Think I have a very
fair knowledge of Syllogism
& induction & hypothesis.
Have also a general
idea of [del]of[/del] Breadth &
depth; don't know much
about "Quantified predicate".
