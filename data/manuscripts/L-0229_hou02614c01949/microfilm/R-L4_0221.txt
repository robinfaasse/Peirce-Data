[u]Deduction[/u].
R. Most beans in this bag are white.
C. This handful is from the bag.
Re. Probably most of the handful are white.

[u]Induction[/u]
Re. Most beans in this handful are white
C. These are from the bag.
Rule Probably most beans in the bag are white.

[u]Hypoth.[/u]
Rule Most beans in this bag are white.
Re. Most beans in my hand are white.
Case. Probably these beans were taken from the bag.

In Deduction we reason from the Rule & Case to the Result.

In Induction, from the Case and result to the rule.

In Hypothesis, from the Rule & result to the Case.

Deduction cannot go beyond the premises without becoming bad; Induction & Hypothesis are of little account unless they go beyond the premises.
