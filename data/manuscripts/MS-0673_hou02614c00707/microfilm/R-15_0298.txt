This, I fancy, is a fair statement of the reason, or the first part of the reason why you think Achilles never will overtake the tortoise. If we divide 10 by 9 by long division, we get precisely such a quotient, proving that no such number as 1.11111 etc is large enough to express [del]by[/del] ten nineths; and it is evident, is it not, that the distance Achilles will have to run to overtake the tortoise will be ten nineths of a stadium, and that it will take him one minute and one nineth to do so it. Is not that so? But your friend will say, "This is mathematical, and I have never been able to comprehend mathematics."
