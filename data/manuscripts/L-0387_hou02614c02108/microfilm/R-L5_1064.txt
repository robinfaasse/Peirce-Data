[date]May 13 / 1896[/date]

My dear Russell

I have no time to explain the terrible effects which have resulted from the breach of Carus's promises. For one thing my wife is turned out of the hospital in a state of imminent danger to her life as the doctors themselves say having given up the room where she was and with no place to go & money in advance is required everywhere. She will simply have to die in the streets. If this cursed treacherous promise had never been made, — which Carus no doubt chuckles at the idea of my believing, — I should have been decently [ins]off[/ins] by this time. But I cannot believe you will not take steps about it. I will have my lawyer write to Chicago if something is not done soon.

The problem $a \prec x \bar{y}$ can be solved and $a$ eliminated if there are more than 3 objects in the universe. Namely $1,2,3,4$ denoting 4 individuals and $5$ any 5th whatever

$x = 1:2 + 1:4 + 4:2 + 4:4 + 2:1 + 2:3 + 3:1 + 3:3 + 3:4 + 5:1 + 5:2$
$\bar{y} = 1:1 + 1:3 + 4:1 + 4:3 + 2:2 + 2:4 + 3:2 + 3:4 + 4:4 + 1:5 + 2:5$

It does not matter whether $3:5, 4:5, 5:5, 5:3, 5:4$ go in $x$ or in $\bar{y}$.

Obviously [del]1:1[/del] $1:1 \prec (1:4)(4:1) \prec x\bar{y}$ $1:2 \prec (1:2)(2:2) \prec x\bar{y}$ $1:3 \prec (1:4)(4:3) \prec x\bar{y}$ $1:4 \prec (1:2)(2:4)$
$1:5 \prec (1:2)(2:5) \prec x\bar{y}$

$2:1 \prec (2:4)(4:1)$ $2:2 \prec (2:3)(3:2)$ $2:3 \prec (2:3)(1:3)$ $2:4 \prec (2:3)(3:4)$ $2:5 \prec [del](2:1)[/del](1:5)$
$3:1 \prec [del](3:1)[/del](1:1)$ etc like $(2:1)$ etc $(1:3)$ etc like $(2:1)$ etc.
$5:1 \prec (5:1)(1:1)$ $5:2 \prec (5:2)(2:2)$ $5:3 \prec (5:1)(1:3)$ $5:4 \prec (5:2)(2:4)$ $5:5 \prec (5:2)(2:5) + (5:1)(1:5)$

Whoever works up the copy sent into an article, I or you, which can be done only when it is decided just what form the article is to take will insert the above. The solution for 3 objects though Schröder seems to think it complicated is really very easy.

Don't fail somehow to remit at once, so that I may possibly still save my life from the consequences of that lie or if that is too late, may at any rate do something for my creditors. I intend the thing shall be know all over the world.

very faithfully
C. S. Peirce
