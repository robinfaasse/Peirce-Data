Anyway you and Schröder do what you think best; and be careful my brother James has no say in the matter. I owe him about $250 [u]at most[/u]. He employed a lawyer named Sykes, employee of Taylor, firm of Hornbeck or Horn-something (proposed for U.S. Supreme Judge) [del]to[/del] who made me sign a note for $2300 as a "mere form", and for my interest.
