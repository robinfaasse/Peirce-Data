My dear Judge:

I don't hear a word from you or from Carus. I suppose it is hot in Chicago.

I wonder if my article about which I wrote to you ever came to hand to Carus. I am awfully in want of cash notwithstanding I have two great companies trying to get my patent & various other things that promise well. If I could get, - say, $ dollars - why even that would be something provided $ > 0.

very faithfully
C S Peirce
Care Albert Stickney
35 Nassau St.
N.Y.
