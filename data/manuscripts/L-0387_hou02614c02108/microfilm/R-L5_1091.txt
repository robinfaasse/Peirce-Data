I am a Democrat in many things
while convinced that socialism
must and ought to be extended
much further than at present.
My natural sympathies are with
the poor rather than the rich; and
I favor what will alleviate the
[del]miseries[/del] [ins]lot[/ins]. For the same reason I
should be very sorry to see them
commit an act of great folly
which [del]will[/del] [ins]would[/ins] reduce them to the
condition of the poor in silver
countries. Besides, while honesty
is the best policy in almost all cases,
it is especially so on the part of
those who need help. I do not
think the Free Silver idea is honest.

I think it would be the most disastrous thing possible
to all classes; but it would almost enslave the poor,
while producing terrible want & many deaths [ins]in the coming winter[/ins] and
unspeakable woe of every kind. I think it would
be the final knell of the cause of intellect in this
country, — if there is any life in that cause now.

All this, my dear Judge, makes no pretence to
being argument. It only explains to you, as my
friend, what you wanted to know, namely, how my
favoring gold coheres with my declared sentiments.

Very faithfully C. S. Peirce
