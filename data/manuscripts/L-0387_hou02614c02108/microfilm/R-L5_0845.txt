Milford Pa
[date]1889 Jan 8[/date]

My dear Sir:

I received yours of the 22nd last night. It had followed me here to my farm. It gives me great pleasure to form the acquaintance of another student of logic. I send you by today's mail, 3 separate papers on the logic of relatives, — all I can lay my hands on. If they do not reach you, let me know, please.
