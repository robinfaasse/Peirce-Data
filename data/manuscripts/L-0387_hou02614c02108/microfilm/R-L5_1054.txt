Dear Judge

I enclose my article on Schröder. I have counted the words on 6 pages as follows

[u]page[/u] [u]words[/u]
2 301
5 286
10 333
15 317
20 227
25 278

The mean is 290 words to the page. (there are really 300, page 20 being exceptional) since there are 29 pages there are 8410 words at that rate. The pay should be $210 and the need is pressing. Please endeavor to have a New York draft sent to me at once.

very faithfully
C. S. Peirce

It is fearfully long; but I would have gladly written much more.
