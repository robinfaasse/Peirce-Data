"Beware" says Emerson "How you
unmuzzle a valetudinarian" and as I
start to write I fancy you in dizing at
the possible reward of your graciousness
to me in unmuzzling a logical tyro
who is presuming on your courtesy. —

So let me say at once that this letter
expects no answer — I am too sensible
of the favor accorded me too sensible of the
value and engrossment of your time
to ask any such thing. —
