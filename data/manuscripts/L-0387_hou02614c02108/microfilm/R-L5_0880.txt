[ins]Mr[/ins] Harper is almost a stran-
ger here and has very little
here for mere local influ-
ence. How do you stand
with Stanley Hall, Pres.
Gilman, William Gibbs.
There must be a host of
people who can speak of
your distinguished fit-
ness, and who would be
glad to do so if asked.

I have not gone far with
the reading & shall go no
further according to your
request. Whenever I can
do anything for you freely
call upon me.

Very Respectfully
Francis C. Russell
