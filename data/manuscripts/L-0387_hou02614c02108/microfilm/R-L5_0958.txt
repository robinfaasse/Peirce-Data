Chicago [date]Sept 19th 1894[/date]

Dear Mr. Peirce

I have not as yet received
a single word from Mr. Hegeler in
answer to my representations in the
matter of your affairs. I hardly know
what to think. I cannot believe that
he will flatly ignore and contemn
my intervention. Perhaps I ought to
argue favorably from his deliberation.
He is an awful slow man to make
up his mind sometimes. But at any
rate I did what I could and not
(as yet [del]Emmet[/del] [ins]I trust[/ins]) hoping for the best.

glitters is gold" or Not all glitterers
are gold or [del]glit[/del] [del]glit[/del] [del]glit[/del] glitters are gold.
What is the converse of glitterer.

Also take the relation "between"
What are the [del]two[/del] converse relations.
Also take the plural relation "among"
and then the relation "in". I have little
doubt but that my questions "give me [del]dead[/del]
away" but please understand I make no pre-
tension whatever only to those who are just
as big hunks as [del]I am myself[/del]

Cordially Francis C. Russell
