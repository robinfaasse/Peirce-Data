OFFICE OF
FRANCIS C. RUSSELL,
COUNSELOR AT LAW,
No. 150 West Madison Street,

Chicago, [date]Jan 2nd, 1889[/date]

Prof. C. S. Peirce
Esteemed Sir -

I feel almost like an [del]in-[/del]
truder but really unless men like
you are a little gracious I don't know
how men like me would get along
at all - Will you kindly inform me
if your paper on the Logic of Relatives
in Vol IX of American Academy is
substantially reproduced in the "Stud-
ies of Logic" - The latter I have as well
as Van Nostrands edition of the "Linear
Associative Algebra" but I find it prac-
tically impossible to get at the Me-
moirs of the "American Academy" here
in Chicago - No Library has them ex-
cept that of the "Academy of Science"
and that institution has been a tramp
for nearly ten years and its library
is packed away in a remote corner
