My dear dear Mr Peirce

I have just received your chapters last sent and have read your letter accompanying. I am deeply grieved over your troubles and never felt the curse of material impotence so deeply as now when all my soul bids me to fly to your aid. I will not admit that your straits are really so desperate and you must not give up [del]in any event[/del]. Forgive me if I put the matter in this way that you ought [del]not[/del] to abandon the mission God sent you to fulfill. No [del]personal[/del] reasons of any [del]sort[/del] even the most desperate possible must prevail. You do not belong to yourself but to humanity [del]and to the future[/del].
