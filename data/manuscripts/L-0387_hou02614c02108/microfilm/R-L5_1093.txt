The real danger of our affairs is that
such ignorant men are sent to congress; also,
that when we make some headway against
protection, we are thrown back and have
all our work undone by the extreme folly
of the democrats. When I review the history
of that party from the time I was a boy,
say from Pierce's election to this day, the
way they have set back their own cause
"time, times, and half a time," by some
error strikes me forcibly.
