Dear Judge

[pn](12)[/pn] I have received yours in reply to some of mine. Don't try too hard! I wouldn't have you get yourself into bad order. That I could never forgive myself. Besides, if a [del]skeptikos[/del] cool presentation don't work, nothing will. Nor do I care to be a suppliant! I shouldn't wonder if one had the silly notion one could starve me into acceptance of a given philosophy. I will write for a paper without writing on philosophy. That is of course the furthest I will go. You'll endeavor not to express my opinion of Hegeler and Carus as philosophers, but if the occasion comes directly [del]on[/del] [ins]across[/ins] my path I shall do so. C.S. Peirce
