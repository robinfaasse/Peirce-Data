[date]Oct 18th 1907[/date] copied [/marginalia]

As to three curiosities of Math-
ematics, I have a lot more
in my budget, and am think-
ing of making a volume of them.
If I do, of course I must write
all that is to go into the volume,
although I propose to put the
heavier parts into a little
smaller type. But there is
no reason why those parts
should not be altogether
omitted from the [del]Monist[/del]
publication. At any rate, if you
print my explanation of the first
curiosity (which I see no sign
of your doing) — It is the part
of the M.S. marked B — I would
insert it after the second cu-
riosity, marked C. I have sub-
stantially finished a fifth
