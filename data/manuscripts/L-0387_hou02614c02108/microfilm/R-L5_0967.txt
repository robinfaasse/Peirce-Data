To say that A is superior to B in reference to the [del]given[/del] relation is to say that taking any third thing, C, either A is in the given relation to C or B is not in that relation to C, and C can be so chosen that both alternatives are true.

If A is superior to B, A is not B.

Superiority is a transitive relation.

If A is superior to B, then B is superior to A in reference to the negative relation.

Everything is [ins]$l \dagger \breve{\bar{l}}$[/ins] to itself that is
$$1 \prec l \dagger \breve{\bar{l}}$$

In a universe of more than one individual nothing is $l \dagger \bar{l}$ to anything
$$l \dagger \bar{l} = 0$$

But it is possible to find such a relation $l$ that everything is $l \dagger \breve{\bar{l}} \dagger l$ of itself. Such a relation $l$ I call a "foundation of subsequence." For such a relation
$$l \breve{l}$$
$$\therefore l \breve{l} (l \dagger \breve{\bar{l}} \dagger l)$$
$$\therefore l (\breve{l} l \dagger \breve{l} \breve{\bar{l}} \dagger \breve{l} l)$$
$$\therefore l (\breve{l} l \dagger \bar{l} \dagger \breve{l} l)$$
$$\therefore l \breve{l} \dagger l \bar{l} \dagger l \breve{l}$$
$$\therefore l \dagger \bar{l}$$

The relation $l \breve{l}$ where everything is $l \dagger \breve{\bar{l}} \dagger l$ of itself I call a subsequence.

Every subsequence is a superiority.

The converse of a subsequence is a subsequence in negative reference.

The negative of a subsequence is a relation of covering in reference to the negative of the foundation.

For a foundation in reference to which things which cover one another are considered as identical, a relation of subsequence is said to be [u]linear[/u].

[u]More[/u] and [u]less[/u] are examples of linear subsequence.

In all cases, $(l \dagger \breve{\bar{l}})(l \dagger \breve{\bar{l}}) \prec l \dagger \breve{\bar{l}}$

For relations of subsequence $l \breve{l} l \breve{l} \prec l \breve{l}$.

But if $l \breve{l} \prec l \breve{l} l \breve{l}$ the relation of superiority is said to be [u]concatenated[/u].

A relation of superiority for which everything superior to another is superior to something without being superior to anything superior to that thing is termed a [u]deuterensis[/u].
