THE MONIST,
THE OPEN COURT,
EDITORIAL DEPARTMENT,
LA SALLE, ILL.

DR. PAUL CARUS.

November 7, 1904.

Mr. Francis W. Russell,
Chicago.

My dear Mr. Russell:

Mr. Peirce sent me a communication concerning himself which ought to be "fathered" by someone. It contains quotations from James and references to Mr. Peirce's "three papers on logic" and seeing that Mr. Peirce cannot sign the communication himself and as the editor should not publish it under his own name it seemed to me best to have some one of Mr. Peirce's friends who would unhesitatingly endorse his views, sign the paper. If you are willing to let it appear under your name I will have it set and send you a proof for your [ins]final[/ins] approval. I enclosed Mr. Peirce's letter and would enclose his [ins]too[/ins] Ms. if I did not hesitate to risk the Ms. in the mails. Otherwise I would have to go to the trouble of having it registered.

I remain, with kind regards,

Very truly yours,
Paul Carus
