I would issue the three as separate works with distinct titles.

If you will negotiate the sale of the first volume to the Open Court
they to pay me a sum down and the rest in the future, I will give
you a commission of 10%. I am awfully in want of money. I must
have $100 or $150 at once or go under.

You still have some [del]things[/del] [ins]MSS.[/ins] of mine which I should propose

would enable me to sell my place, which is just now in Demland.
I cannot now go near it or do anything; and it has been broken
open & things are disappearing fast.

very faithfully
C. S. Peirce
