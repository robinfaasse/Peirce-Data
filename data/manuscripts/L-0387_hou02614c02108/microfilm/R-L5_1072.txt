My dear Russell

[pn](34)[/pn] I sent Carus [ins]second[/ins] article (long enough for two) on the Logic of Relatives. I think he seemed to wish to take one if Hegeler would consent. But I have not heard whether the MS came to hand.

With a moderate amount of help at this crisis, it looks strongly as if I should be all right in future & be able to pay all debts. But just now I fear all will be lost for want of a small amount. If you can persuade Carus to advance $100, I beg you to do so.

Very truly
C. S. Peirce
Care A. Stickney, Esq 35 Nassau St
New York

[marginalia]
P.S. I have a much more formal and exact statement of all the properties of the graphical method, if such a preferred status merits.
[/marginalia]
