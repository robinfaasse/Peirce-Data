Now if the [u]Open Court[/u] were made as large as the [u]Nation[/u], a first rate critical journal, advocating Mr. Hegeler's views of Religion, matters, & moderately, but distinctly, republican in its tone, giving attention also to Science, to Intellectual Amusement, theatres, chess, whist, etc., and paying also more attention to the tastes of women, there is no doubt in my mind its subscription list would equal or surpass the [u]Nation's[/u] very soon. Meantime, it would gain advertisements which would also swell the treasury. The [u]Nation[/u] gets as much from advertisements as from Subscribers. The [u]Nation[/u] is [ins]in[/ins] not conducted in a very economical manner, & various respects, which I don't care to speak about. In my opinion it could be better done for less money; if in fact, I guess the editors are quite aware of that. But various things are done for the sake of prestige.
