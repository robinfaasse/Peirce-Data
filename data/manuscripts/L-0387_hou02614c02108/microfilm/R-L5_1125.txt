[date]1906 Dec 6[/date]

My dear Russell:

Owing to my not knowing
before what my address would
be, I have only this minute
received your letter here,
where I expect to remain for
the winter. But as I have
overdrawn my bank account
owing to the apparent breach
of understanding of a newspaper
manager & have only a loaf of
bread and $1.50 cash to go on until
I can make further deposits, I shall
