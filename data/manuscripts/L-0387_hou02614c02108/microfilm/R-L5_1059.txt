[date]May 12/96[/date]

My dear Russell:

You have had those chapters of mine a long time. Please send them at once to me at 5 W 50th St New York City.

Can you not contrive to communicate in some way with Carus and have something sent to me at once, — telegraphically if possible.

Here was a plain promise made me that I should be paid "as soon as MS. was sent in" It was sent in just 13 days ago. If it had not been for that I should have done some other work which would have brought in what was absolutely essential. Also trusting in that promise, — which it never occurred to me was a mere trap, — so innocent am I of German ways, — I sent my wife to a hospital.

The result will be that they will kick her into the street & die. I have [del]no[/del] had nothing to eat this week except a sandwich which was given me yesterday morning. I shall have no place to sleep after last night, except the bench.

I could have made your fortune, if I had been properly backed up. Or even independent of any backing.
