**123 and 125 La Salle Street,**

Chicago [date]Oct 27th 1894[/date]

Dear Mr Peirce,

I received yours enclosing the "Achilles Tortoise" paper several days ago & ought to have noticed sooner. But a pressure of litter affairs, visitors from the East & etc have reduced me into delay.

I have altered the paper not a little mostly in the way of expansion. Very possibly you will be vexed to see it in the shape in which it will appear and think it a pity to spoil a good thing in such a way but I thought I had to alter it in order to avoid a "dead give away". I have little doubt but that it will appear soon.
