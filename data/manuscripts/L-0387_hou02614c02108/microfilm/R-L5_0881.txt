Your letter was directed
to me via, the Open Court
office; therefore delays
one of my cards. I have
no regular connection
with the Monist and
Open Court although I
am quite intimate with
Dr Carus and Mrs Heg-
eler and know pretty
well what is going on
as regards these period-
icals. The Dr. is a fine
good fellow that you
will be sure to like.
He is going to Europe
in June if he can get
prepared so to do. He is
therefore obliged to settle
