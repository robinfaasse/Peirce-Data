These papers show, that I
have had no time to work
on it for the last 5 years.

I am trying to write a book
setting forth my theory of the
universe & I hope it may
appear in the Spring, under
the title of Reflections on the
Logic of Science. There you
will find the significance
of my remarks in the Sym-
posium which surprise you.
I could give you no such hint
in a few words as you ask
for, — there is a long train of
thought involved. Suffice it to
say that I have not sacrificed
any of the more fundamental
of my younger opinions so
far as I recollect them,

but am perhaps more sceptical & materialis-
tic. I am sorry I cannot just now write
you more. I am kept [del]very[/del] busy all day
writing various kinds of things & making
mathematical computations & have to
run my farm, carry on building etc.
at the same time. I am consequently
not very good company and a detestable
correspondent.

Yours very Truly
C S Peirce

Francis E. Russell Esq
