I now take up alternative № 2. As to this, I say that an idea
representable by a graph of more than 3 pegs cannot be an element
because it can always be analyzed into a trivalent idea and an
idea of valency [del]one[/del] lower by one than that of the idea to be analyzed.
Take for example the idea of selling, where the graph would have
4 pegs to denote respectively the seller, the buyer, the thing whose
ownership is transferred and the price. Take any two of these
you like, say the seller and the buyer. Then the idea that the
seller transfers to the buyer the ownership of the subject of the sale
in consideration of the price may be analyzed [del]from[/del] [del]The seller[/del]
