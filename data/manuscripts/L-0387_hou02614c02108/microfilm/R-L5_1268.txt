P.O. Milford Pa
[date]1908 Oct 7[/date]

Dear Russell;

Thank you very
much for yours of the
2nd. I have not received
the Monist of October but
I corrected and returned
the proofs of my article.

This will be the third
of mine that has been
printed since I received last
any money from Carus
which was $100 on Oct 21
of last year. I have an
extremely important article
showing the proper method
of making logical analyses

I have my tax bill of a hundred dollars to
pay for and ruinedly before long for interest
on Mortgages, at Co., [del]for[/del] [ins]was[/ins] swindled out of
three hundred in the spring, & am in a bad
way. Twenty-five boxes of books from my
late brother's library are on their way to me
& I have not five dollars in the world, to pay
the freight. Do try to persuade Carus to send
some cash & also to explain how I came to
owe him so much. If he will pay, I shall
be glad to write for him & the Hibbert Journal both.
