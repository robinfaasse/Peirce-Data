(Letter to Peirce to which
his No 50, is a reply)

1268, Jackson Boul.
Chicago [date]Sept 16th 1908[/date]

Dear Prof. Peirce,

Before I go very far in
the preparation of any logical
text I feel that I must clear
up, some points that I am
fearing I may be in serious
error about, that is to say
I fear so because (and because
only) I have seemed to draw
from divers passages in your
papers that you were of a
different opinion.

points I will dwell upon
