Passing to the subject of true continua. The true definition of
a continuum should be given; namely a continuum is an object
all of whose parts are alike in their relations to their parts. So Kant.
Then Listing's great memoir, I mean the one on the Census Theorem
in the Göttinger Abhandlungen. But this should be annotated and
brought into connection with projective geometry on the one hand &
quaternions on the other. Besides which, the doctrine of Topical
Singularities should be treated. And reference should be made to space
of more than three dimensions. I haven't time to write more at present.
