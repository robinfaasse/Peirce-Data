My dear Russell:

I receive your witty & valued letters
I am in the midst of things getting ready a long
paper for the Nat'l Academy of Sciences which
I am told a quantity of people have been invited to
come in to hear. For that reason I cannot
now finish reading your letter. I only read
as much as I could read while drinking a cup
of tea. I have been at work on this for eleven con-
secutive hours today & shall work till I go to
sleep.
