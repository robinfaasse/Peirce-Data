To say that a thing exists is to say that it reacts
with other things. It is not destroyed in this
action but preserves its identity. Probably,
therefore, in so far as it reacts, it never will
be entirely destroyed. [del]Water[/del] A mass of water,
in reacting with the earth, has weight. It may
be decomposed; but its elements will have the
same total weight. Perhaps, in some hitherto
undiscovered way, its weight might change; but
if so, there would presumably be some [ins]conceivable[/ins] way of
identifying the substance. It may be mixed with
