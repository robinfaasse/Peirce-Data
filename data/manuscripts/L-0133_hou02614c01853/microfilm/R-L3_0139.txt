[pn]V8[/pn] 2548 McCulloh St., Baltimore, Md.
[date]February 24, 1905[/date].


Dr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Penna.

Dear Dr:-
I have greatly enjoyed the beginning of your work.
I am greatly bored by many of the lectures I have to hear
at the Hopkins, so that your work is indeed refreshing. It
is delightful to read one who has something [ins]to say[/ins], and who knows
how to say it. I wish I were able to give to this line of
work the attention it deserves. Your letters deserve one's
whole time for consideration and reply, and I can command but
a little part of my time therefor. The change from the
pastorate to the editorship is entailing a multiplicity of
duties in both fields just at this juncture and I am trying
to catch up Hopkins work which I have neglected. I feel
that my answer to your work should be better matured but I
must crave your indulgence.

I hope you have fully recovered your health.
You have your facts and views at your finger ends, so
please let me hear from you at your early convenience.
