Dear Dr:-

I feel almost persuaded to endorse all that you write. True, I have in mind some differences as to the scope of philosophy and some questions as to your conception of metaphysics, but these matters may well go over.

"The Pragmatic Philosophy of Common Sense", one must needs believe a possibility and hope to see developed. That the propositions, whipped into us by all-embracing reality, are surer than those the teacher grinds into us, we may all admit.

That one's stomach can tell him facts as certain and sure for the foundation [del]stones[/del] of a philosophy [ins]as[/ins] [del]or[/del] the weighty deliverances of scientific tomes is not to be doubted. The experience of all mankind seems certainly the best starting point for philosophy.
