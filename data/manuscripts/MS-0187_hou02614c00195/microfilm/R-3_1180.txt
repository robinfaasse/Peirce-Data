It is impossible to count a trillion. Had Adam counted continuously
from his creation to the present day he would not have reached that number
for it would take him over 9512 years.

At the rate of 200 a minute we have for one hour $\frac{200 \times 60}{12000}$

for one day $\frac{12000 \times 24}{\begin{array}{r} 48000 \\ 24000 \\ \hline 288000 \end{array}}$

for one year $\frac{288000 \times 365}{\begin{array}{r} 1440000 \\ 1728000 \\ 864000 \\ \hline 105120000 \end{array}}$
