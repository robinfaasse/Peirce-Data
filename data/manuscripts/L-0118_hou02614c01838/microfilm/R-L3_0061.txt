Hence if you have at hand the work of Coper-
nicus himself, and have also some such work
as that of H. Hüssener, Berlin 1874, I would
thank you more for the loan of the latter than
for that of the former.

I was much amused at the accuracy
with which you spell Polish & Bohemian
book titles, not forgetting the hardening
stroke in 'Mikołaj', or the sign of the
long vowel in Koperník.

If you can send me any thing to answer
my purpose, I shall gladly pay all the postage,
take good care of the work and return it before
the end of the year.

You earn the regard & sympathy of my
wife by disposing of Camille Flammarion
as "ignorant and worthless."

Very truly yours
Lewis N. Dembitz

Louisville Ky. [date]Nov. 19" 1893.[/date]
