[u]Addition in Primary Arithmetic[/u]

In group-counting the child has already
practised addition & knows the table to
perfection. Nothing remains but to
accustom him to add on first one
number and then another, together with
the work.

Introductory Example. Poor family
without food. Each member contributed
something. Put them down — Add
them up. Find the sum total
