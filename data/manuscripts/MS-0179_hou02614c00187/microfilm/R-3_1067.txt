If the product of the two numbers taken
is more than 100, what you will find
is a number which must be added to
101, 202, 303, or some multiple of 101
to get the product. Thus, suppose we want
the product of 19 and 41. This must be
nearly 800; for that is 20 times 40. Now
19 is [ins]in[/ins] the 84th place, and 41 is in the 5[ins]th[/ins]
place. [del]But[/del] The sum of 84 and 5 is 89.
In the 89[ins]th[/ins] place is 72. But the cross shows
the real product ends with 9. This 72 ends with
2, which taken from 9 leaves 7. So we add 707
and find 779, which is the product of 19 and
41.
