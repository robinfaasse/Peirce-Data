[u]Multiplication[/u]

Miss Sessions said:

You have now learned how to count by groups very well. But it is every day important, in order to save time when we are hurried, to be able, not merely to count
7 14 21 28 35 42 49 56 63
but to say at once [ins]without stopping to count[/ins] how many 5 times 7 make, or 35, and so forth. This is called [u]multiplying[/u].
