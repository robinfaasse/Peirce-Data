The speaker also [del]creates two signs[/del] [ins]points out two other[/ins] objects, — intellectual objects, — namely, the special connection of $A_1$ to $B_1$, and that of $A_2$ to $B_2$. The listener is perfectly familiar with the fact that any two things taken in either of two orders forms an ordered pair. Thus, what the speaker says to the listener refers to nine objects of common experience, viz: — $A_1$, $B_1$, $A_2$, $B_2$, the ordered pair $A_1 B_1$, the ordered pair $A_2 B_2$, [ins]and[/ins] the familiar ideas of lover, loved, and love. What he says is that lover represents $A_1$ and $A_2$, that loved represents $B_1$ and $B_2$, and that loving represents the ordered pair $A_1 B_1$ and the ordered pair $A_2 B_2$. Accordingly, a relation is to be defined as a cluster of ideas representing the several objects of an ordered set and the ordered set as a collective object.
