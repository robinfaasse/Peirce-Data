[u]What is a Law of Nature?[/u]

I am to discuss in four thousand words, the following questions:

1[sup]st[/sup], What is a law of nature?
2[sup]d[/sup], What was the opinion in Hume's day in England concerning the first question, — not the opinion of those who wrote upon the subject, but of the mass of educated men who did not write upon the subject?
3[sup]d[/sup], What is the opinion of the same class of persons and of scientific men at this day about the same question?
4[sup]th[/sup], What is the bearing of the [ins]the first[/ins] answer [del]to the[/del] [ins]of the[/ins] question upon Hume's argument against miracles?
5[sup]th[/sup], What was supposed, at Hume's time, by men who did not write upon the subject, of the answer they gave to that question upon Hume's argument as they understood it?
