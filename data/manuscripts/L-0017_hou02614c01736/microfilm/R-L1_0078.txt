Emerson Hall,

Cambridge, Massachusetts.

My dear Mr. Woods:-

I regret to inform you that we have been unable to locate [ins],[/ins] any letter or letters from Mr. Charles S. Peirce.

In your letter of March 6th, you tell me that the letter you want was in reply to an invitation to join the Society in the first year or two of its existence. The records of the early years we do not have as the Society started as a branch of the English Society and several years later became the American Society.

Regretting my inability to furnish you the information and hoping you will call upon me if there is anything I can do for you, I remain,

Very truly yours,
[ins]T. H. Pierson[/ins]
T. H. Pierson.
Secretary.
