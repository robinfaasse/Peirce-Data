[pn]41)[/pn] This is my father's form of biquaternions, which we have already noticed. Biquaternions are thus connected with the permutations of three things. It is true that two more letters are required in order to express the relations of those permutations fully. Let us add two units which shall leave I, J, K, L standing quite apart. For this purpose, we must seek two quantities of the sextuple algebra, call them H and M, such that H[ins]²[/ins]=H, M[ins]²[/ins]=M, and such that Q being any quantity of the algebra whatsoever

Q H = H Q = [ins]ξ[/ins]H,

Q M = M Q = [ins]η[/ins]M,

where [ins]ξ[/ins] and [ins]η[/ins] are numbers depending on [del]Q. Put[/del]

H = [u]q[/u]i + [u]r[/u]j + [u]s[/u]k + [u]x[/u]l + [u]y[/u]m + [u]z[/u]n

Then H[u]l[/u] = [u]x[/u]i + [u]z[/u]j + [u]y[/u]k + [u]q[/u]l + [u]s[/u]m + [u]r[/u]n

[u]l[/u]H = [u]x[/u]i + [u]y[/u]j + [u]z[/u]k + [u]q[/u]l + [u]r[/u]m + [u]s[/u]n

Hence, [u]x[/u] = [ins]ξ[/ins][u]q[/u], [u]z[/u] = [u]y[/u] = [ins]ξ[/ins][u]r[/u] = [ins]ξ[/ins][u]s[/u], [u]q[/u] = [ins]ξ[/ins][u]x[/u], [u]s[/u] = [u]r[/u] = [ins]ξ[/ins][u]y[/u] = [ins]ξ[/ins][u]z[/u].

Consequently,

[ins]ξ²[/ins][u]q[/u] = [u]q[/u], [ins]ξ²[/ins][u]r[/u] = [u]r[/u], etc

Hence, either [u]q[/u] = [u]r[/u] = [u]s[/u] = [u]x[/u] = [u]y[/u] = [u]z[/u] = H = 0, which would not answer the purpose, or [ins]ξ[/ins] = ± 1. thus, either

[u]x[/u] = [u]q[/u] [u]r[/u] = [u]s[/u] = [u]y[/u] = [u]z[/u]

or [u]x[/u] = - [u]q[/u] [u]r[/u] = [u]s[/u] = - [u]y[/u] = - [u]z[/u].
