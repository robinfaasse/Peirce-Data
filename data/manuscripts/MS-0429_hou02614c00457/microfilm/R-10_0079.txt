It is usual to suppress parentheses, etc., first, when
they merely unify combinations of single letters, as in abc, and
secondly, when the operative sign outside the parenthesis is the
same as that inside, and is associative. The former omission is
almost imperative; and I shall always assume there is no such pa-
renthesis, etc. The other omission is another obstacle in writing;
but in speaking about what is written, I shall always assume the
vinculum to be there. I shall always call parentheses, square
