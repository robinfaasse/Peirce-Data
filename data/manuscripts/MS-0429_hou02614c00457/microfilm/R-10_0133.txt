Yet in this particular instance, we can adapt our doctrine better
to thoroughgoing trichotomy by derogating a little from the dig-
nified meaningless of pure algebra. In multiple algebra, it is
generally assumed that the coefficients can be any numbers. My
father, for example, even allowed them to be imaginary; though
I cannot approve of that. But for the purposes of trichotomic
mathematics, it should be recognized that each quantity has one
of three values. Call them for the moment, $0^{\circ}$, $120^{\circ}$, $240^{\circ}$;--
regarding $360^{\circ}$ as the same as $0^{\circ}$. Or one might call them night,
morning, and afternoon. Let us denote the three values by $\bigcirc$
( for "$\ddot{o}\rho\theta\rho o\varsigma$") $\delta$ ( for "$\delta\epsilon\acute{\iota}\lambda\eta$") $\nu$ ( for "$\nu\acute{u}\xi$") Then, we must
adopt the addition table:
$$\bigcirc + \bigcirc = \bigcirc \qquad \bigcirc + \delta = \delta \qquad \bigcirc + \nu = \nu$$
$$\delta + \bigcirc = \delta \qquad \delta + \delta = \nu \qquad \delta + \nu = \bigcirc$$
$$\nu + \bigcirc = \nu \qquad \nu + \delta = \bigcirc \qquad \nu + \nu = \delta$$

For multiplication table of these numbers will be obtained by as-
suming $\delta\delta = \delta$. Then $\delta\nu = \delta(\delta + \delta) = \delta\delta + \delta\delta = \delta + \delta = \nu$.
$\delta\bigcirc = \delta(\nu + \delta) = \delta\nu + \delta\delta = \nu + \delta = \bigcirc$.

For the sake of showing the consistency of the formulae, we may
complete the cycle
$$\delta\delta = \delta(\nu + \nu) = \delta\nu + \delta\nu = \nu + \nu = \delta;$$
$$\nu\nu = \nu(\delta + \delta) = \nu\delta + \nu\delta = \nu + \nu = \delta, \text{ (just as } -- = + \text{ )}$$
$$\nu\bigcirc = \nu(\nu + \delta) = \nu\nu + \nu\delta = \delta + \nu = \bigcirc$$
$$\bigcirc\bigcirc = (\nu + \delta)\bigcirc = \nu\bigcirc + \delta\bigcirc = \bigcirc + \bigcirc = \bigcirc$$

Instead of assuming the distributive principle in its entirety
we might have evolved the multiplication-table from these three
equations
