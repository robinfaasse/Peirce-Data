[u]VI. If a quantity, were it written by itself on a sheet of assertions, could be transformed into another with an obelus over it, then the latter, were it written by itself, without the obelus, could be transformed into the former covered with an obelus.[/u]

[u]Demonstration[/u]. Let x be the former, y the latter quantity. Then it is assumed that x were it asserted, could be transformed into [u]y[/u] without loss of truth. It follows, then, from V, that [u][u]y[/u][/u] [del]COULD BE TRANSFORMED INTO [u]x[/u] WITHOUT LOSS OF TRUTH.[/del] But by III, [u]y[/u], were it written on a sheet of assertions, could be transformed into [u][u]y[/u][/u] without loss of truth. Therefore, [u]y[/u] could be, by the two steps, transformed into [u]x[/u] without loss of truth. Q.E.D.
