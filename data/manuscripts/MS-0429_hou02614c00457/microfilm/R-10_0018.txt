of purely imaginary states of things, poets must be greater math-
ematicians, especially that class of poets who write novels of
intricate and enigmatical plots. Even the reply, which is ob-

ing what is true of them, perhaps does not fully meet the objec-

particular sort of hypotheses, namely, those that are exact, etc.,
as there set forth at some length. The article is well worthy of
consideration.
