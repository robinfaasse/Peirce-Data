not study other branches of mathematics, is so excessively simple
as neither to have much mathematical interest, nor to display
the peculiarities of mathematical reasoning. I shall, therefore,
devote the present chapter, - a very dull one, I am sorry to say,
it must be, - to this kind of mathematics. Chapter IV will treat
of the more truly mathematical mathematics; and Chapter V will
apply the results of the present chapter to the study of Formal
