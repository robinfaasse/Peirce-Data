It is usual to suppress parentheses, etc., first, when
they merely unify combinations of single letters by the dot, and
secondly, when the operative sign outside the parenthesis is the
same as that inside, and is associative. The latter omission is
almost imperative; and I shall always assume there is no such pa-
renthesis, etc. The other omission is unobjectionable in writing;
but in speaking about what is written, I shall always assume the
vinculum to be therea. I shall always call parentheses, square
brackets, braces, or any equivalent sign, a [u]vinculum[/u]. By an [u]enclos-[/u]
[u]ure[/u], I shall mean either a vinculum or obelus.
