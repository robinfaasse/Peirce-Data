[ins]299[/ins] XVIII. The negative of a component may be introduced into
any part of its composite as an aggregant, without loss of truth.
[u]Demonstration[/u]. For if [u]a[/u] has the value [u]v[/u][ins]in,[/ins] its negative is [u]f[/u][ins]in,[/ins]
and as an aggregant can affect the value of nothing. But if [u]a[/u] has
the value [u]f[/u][ins]in,[/ins] being a component, the composite is [u]f[/u][ins]in,[/ins] no matter how
the other components be transformed.
[u]Corollary[/u]. It follows that if the negative of an aggregant
occurs in any part of the aggregate as a component, it may be o-
mitted.
