of mathematics is enwrapped in these trichotomic graphs; and they
will be found extremely pertinent to logic. So prolific is the
triad in forms that one may easily conceive that all the variety
and multiplicity of the universe springs from it, though each of
the thousand corpuscles of which an atom of hydrogen consists be
as multiple as all the telescopic heavens, and though all our
heavens be but such a corpuscle which goes with a thousand others
