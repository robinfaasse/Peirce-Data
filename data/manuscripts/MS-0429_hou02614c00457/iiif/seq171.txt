        Corollary. It follows that every composite is true or
equal to [u]v[/u] only in case all its components are so; being false,
or equal to [u]f[/u], if any one of its components are so; while an
aggregate is true, or equal to [u]v[/u], if any one of its aggregants
is so, and is only false, or equal to [u]f[/u], if all its aggregants
are so. But this applies only to composites and aggregants
which can be written out.
