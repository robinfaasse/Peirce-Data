William Sellers & Co., Incorporated
P.O. Address, 1600 Hamilton St. Station J. Philadelphia.

Philadelphia, February 11th, 1904.

Mr. C. S. Peirce,
Milford, Penna.

My dear sir:-

Yours of the 2nd inst. was duly received and I
have read it over with great interest, but I doubt the possibility
of making an estimate of the annual disadvantages to the people of
the United States that would be accepted by the friends of the
metric system or by any one favorable thereto. Aside from this it
would take no account of the enormous loss in tools and appliances
that must be sustained by changing from one system of metrology to
another, and the enormous loss and confusion that would result in
the diminution of product whilst the change was being affected.
John Quincy Adams report shows that after the promulgation of our
monetary system it had not been universally adopted for a period of
eighty years. I can very well remember when I first went to
Providence, R. I. to take charge of a shop when I was twenty one
years of age, that the accounts were kept in shillings of six to
the dollar; that is to say, the shilling being equal to 16 2/3 cts.
