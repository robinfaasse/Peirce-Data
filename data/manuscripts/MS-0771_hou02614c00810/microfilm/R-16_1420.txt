[u]Essays on the Rationale of Science[/u]

[u]PREFACE[/u].

The track over which the Reader and I shall ramble together in these pages is certainly not one to fill the need of a mind that, fagged out with hard work, wishes to read merely to be rid of disagreeable thoughts or perhaps merely to avoid falling asleep. On the contrary, it is a track attractive to an intellect that delights in activity; and to that delight it [del]offers[/del] adds two other pleasures.
