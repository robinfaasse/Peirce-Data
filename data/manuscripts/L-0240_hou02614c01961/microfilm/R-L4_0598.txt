September 7, 1954


Professor A. Lalande
28 Avenue Casimir
Asnieres (Seine)
France

Dear Professor Lalande:

My friend Professor Philip P. Wiener, who saw you
in France last winter, tells me that you have at least
one letter from Charles Sanders Peirce.

I am working on some additional volumes of the
[u]Collected Papers of Charles Sanders Peirce[/u]. Hence I
should like very much to obtain copies of any correspondence
between you and him.

If this is possible, I should appreciate it very much.

Sincerely yours,


Arthur W. Burks
Professor of Philosophy

AWB:ii

University of Michigan
Department of Philosophy
Ann Arbor, Michigan
U.S.A.
