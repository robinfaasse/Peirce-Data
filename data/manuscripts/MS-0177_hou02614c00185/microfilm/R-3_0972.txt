[u]To square a number[/u]

The square of one more than a number exceeds the
square of that number by one more than double
that number.

The square of one less than a number falls short
of the square of that number by one less than double
that number.

The square of 5 more than a number exceeds
the square of that number by 25 more than ten times
that number.

The square of ten times a number is a hundred times
the square of that number.

[u]Example[/u]
To find the square of 365
The square of 5 (or 5 more than 0) is 25 more than ten
times zero = 25
The square of 4 falls short of that of 5 by 2x5 - 1 or 9 [unclear]∴[/unclear] 4² = 25 - 9 = 16
The square of 40 is a hundred times 4² = 1600
The square of 35 is 1600 - 400 + 25 = 1225
36² = 1225 + 70 + 1 = 1296
360² = 129600
