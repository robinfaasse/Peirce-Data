[u]Of Multiplication[/u]

Children should be taught to play games
with the pack of 101 cards and these
games should be so devised that success
shall be made to depend on being able to
say promptly what card in [del]any[/del] [ins]dealing[/ins]
into any given number of packs will be
in any given place of any given
pack.

When this is done, the child will know
far more than the multiplication table
It will be able to give an instantaneous answer
to the question. How much is [u]U.V+W[/u]?
whatever digits U, V, and W may be

To learn the multiplication table is tolerably
easy when one has learned to count by
every digit. For it is only to be able to
say what card is in each place of the [del]last[/del]
[del]pack[/del] in every dealing.
