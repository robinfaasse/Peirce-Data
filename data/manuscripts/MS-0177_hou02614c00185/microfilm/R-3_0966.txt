Since the warning dots are where the number is
[u]less[/u] than the one above it, and since 4 is at
the head of the next column to the left we
count from that 4 by [u]fours[/u] and set down only
the last figure of each number of the count.
Only where there is a warning dot, we [del]put down[/del] [ins]take[/ins]
the number next greater than we otherwise
should and proceed to count by fours from
that, thus:

$$\begin{array}{c c c c c}
4 & 3 & 8 & 7 & 2 \\
8 & 7 & 7 & 4 & 4 \\
3 & 1 & 6 & 1 & 6 \\
7 & 5 & 4 & 8 & 8 \\
1 & 9 & 3 & 9 & 0 \\
6 & 3 & 2 & 3 & 2 \\
0 & 7 & 1 & 0 & 4 \\
5 & 0 & 9 & 7 & 6 \\
9 & 4 & 8 & 4 & 8 \\
3 & 8 & 7 & 2 & 0
\end{array}$$

If the operation has been
rightly performed the
tenth figure of this
column will be the
same as the first figure
of the column next to the
right of it.
