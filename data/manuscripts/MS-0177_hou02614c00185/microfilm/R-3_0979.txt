[pn]32[/pn] When the last figure of the divisor is 3
we multiply always by 1 more than 3 times
the tens of the divisor. Thus

[fig]Mathematical calculations for 6/53 and 1/73[/fig]

$\frac{6}{53} = \dot{1}13207547169\dot{8}$
There are 13 places = $\frac{52}{4}$

$\frac{1}{73} = \dot{0}136986\dot{3}$
There are 8 places or $\frac{72}{9}$.
