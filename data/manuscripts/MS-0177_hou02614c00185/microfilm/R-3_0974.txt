A number is odd or even according as the
last figure is odd or even.

The remainder after the division of a number
by 3 is the same as that of the sum of
its digits. Thus 365 gives the same remainder
as 3+6+5 or 5. That is 2.

The remainder after division by [del]4[/del] is the
same as the last figure plus twice the last
but one. Thus of 365, it is 12+5 = 17 = 2+7 = 9 = 1

The remainder after division by 5 is the same
as that of the last digit

The remainder after division by 6 is the same
as that of the last digit and four times the
sum of the others

The remainder after division by 7 is the same
as that of the last figure [u]plus[/u] 3 times the second [ins]from the last[/ins]
[u]plus[/u] twice third from the last [u]minus[/u] the fourth [u]minus[/u]
3 times the fifth [u]minus[/u] twice the sixth [del]Thus[/del]
365 = 5 + 18 + 6 = 29 = 9 + 6 = 15 = 5 + 3 = 8 = 1
