The remainder after division by 11 is the
same as that of the sum of the digits in
odd places [del]minus[/del] [ins]minus[/ins] the digits in even places.
Thus 365 = 5 + 3 - 6 = 2

The remainder after division by 13 is the same
as that of the last digit minus 3 times the last but
one plus twice the last but 2 minus the last but 3
plus three times the last but 4 minus twice the
last but 5 etc Thus 365 = 5 - 18 + 6 = -7 = 6
