Consider a system of rectangular coordinates.
[fig]Cartesian coordinate axes with origin O, x-axis, and y-axis[/fig] We may take the [del]zero[/del] origin as the zero of quantity. Then the point $x=x_1$ $y=y_1$ [del]to[/del] is (or has for its affix) the expression
$$y_1 + x_1 [u]i[/u].$$
The letter [u]$i$[/u] is not a number but a peculiar unit; and the expression cannot be further reduced. Thus,
$$2 + 3i$$
cannot be made a monomial. It is (or is the affix of) the point $y=2$, $x=3$.

We have, as naturally springing from the notation, and enlargements of it
$$[u]y + xi = xi + y[/u]$$
$$3 + 5i + 2 - 7i = 1 - 2i.$$

If
$$y + xi = b + a[u]i[/u]$$
Then, [del]From[/del] $y=b$ $x=a$.
Hence an equation in imaginaries is two equations.
