$$= \cos \theta \cos \vartheta + \cos \vartheta \frac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} \cdot i$$

$\sin (\theta + \vartheta) = \sin \theta \cos \vartheta + \cos \theta \sin \vartheta.$
