[u]sin[/u] ([u]x[/u] + [u]y[/u]) = [u]sin[/u] [u]x[/u] . [u]cos[/u] [u]y[/u] + [u]cos[/u] [u]x[/u] . [u]sin[/u] [u]y[/u].

[u]Demonstration[/u]. Let [u]a[/u], [u]p[/u], [u]h[/u] be the legs and hypothenuse of a right triangle of which the angle opposite [u]a[/u] equals [u]x[/u]; and let [u]b[/u], [u]p[/u], [u]k[/u] be the legs and hypothenuse of a right triangle of which the angle opposite [u]b[/u] equals [u]y[/u]. [u]s[/u]/[u]h[/u] = [u]a[/u]/[u]h[/u] [u]p[/u]/[u]k[/u] + [u]p[/u]/[u]h[/u] [u]b[/u]/[u]k[/u] .

The area of the large triangle is [u]sk[/u]. It is also [del]p[/del] [u]p[/u]([u]a[/u]+[u]b[/u]).
Hence, [u]sk[/u] = [del]pa[/del] [u]pa[/u] + [u]pb[/u]
Dividing by [u]hk[/u], we have
[u]s[/u]/[u]h[/u] = [u]a[/u]/[u]h[/u] [u]p[/u]/[u]k[/u] + [u]p[/u]/[u]h[/u] [u]b[/u]/[u]k[/u] . Q.E.D.
