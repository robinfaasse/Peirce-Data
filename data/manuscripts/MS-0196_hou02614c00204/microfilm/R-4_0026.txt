Square of the coefficient of $x$, its solution is plain
$$(x+a)^2 = b^2$$
$$x+a = \pm b$$
$$x = -a \pm b.$$

But if its form is
$$x^2 + 2ax + a^2 + b^2 = 0$$
its solution is
$$x = -a \pm b\sqrt{-1}$$

The square root of [del]$\sqrt{}$[/del] $-1$ here has no direct interpretation in uni-dimensional quantity. If the equation is given in the form
$$x^2 + 2ax + b^2 = 0$$
its solution is $x = -a \pm \sqrt{a^2 - b^2}$.

Here we have the important form $\sqrt{a^2 - b^2}$, with which is [del]naturally[/del] [ins]connected[/ins] the form
$$\sqrt{p^2 + q^2}.$$

This occurs in probabilities, in geometry, in dynamics, and must be recognized in the imaginary calculus.

Let us suppose we have the equation
$$z^2 - 8z + 25 = 0.$$

Its solution is
$$z = 4 \pm 3i.$$
