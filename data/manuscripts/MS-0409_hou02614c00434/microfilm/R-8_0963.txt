[u]§61[/u]. A belief is a habit; but it is a habit of which we
are conscious. The actual calling to mind of the substance
of a belief, not as personal to ourselves, but as holding good,
or true, is a judgment. An inference is a passage from one
belief to another; but not every such passage is an inference.
If noticing my ink is bluish, I cast my eye out of the window
and my mind being awakened to color remark particularly
a poppy, that is no inference. Or if without casting my eye
out of the window, I call to mind the green tinge of Niagara
or the blue of the Rhone, that is no inference. In inference,
one belief not only follows after another, but follows from it. What
