[del]Firstness is the Mode of Being of that which is such as
it is regardless of anything else.[/del] [ins]It is mere possibility.[/ins]
It can therefore only be known [del]to us[/del] immediately.
In fact a quality of feeling as [u]red[/u] is of this nature.
But we may suppose Firsts which are not knowable by
us. (?)

[u]Secondness[/u] is the Mode of Being of [del]that which is such[/del]
as it is relatively to a Second but regardless of any Third. That
makes it of a brute nature.
It is degenerate or genuine.
Degenerate secondness is [del]mutual[/del] relation, for that involves Thirdness
but is, as [del]I originally stated[/del], a relation consisting in each correlate
having a certain character ([del]the same for both[/del]) and that embraces
every [del]equiparance[/del].
Genuine secondness is disquiparance. If it is of mental origin
it involves Thirdness. To [del]must[/del] be a [del]genuine[/del] action, an actual fact.
There are two kinds of secondary firstness, Activity and Passivity.
Each is an [del]actuality but passivity[/del] Actuality. But Passiveness is
degenerate [del]actuality[/del] actuality, not actuality, which is more in the nature
of Firstness. Activity is genuine actuality.
Passiveness is a Firstness as modified by the Actuality.
Activity is Actuality in the aspect of Firstness, [del]or latter is[/del]
[del]Some[/del] Genuine Secondness in so far as it modifies Firstness.
[u]Existence[/u] is the [del]indeterminate[/del] activity of objects in one
universe. There is a corresponding passiveness, but it has not been
noticed.
