CHARLES SANDERS PEIRCE

MS. [del]#399[/del] [ins]319[/ins]

When editing of Peirce's papers was begun, this manu-
script was largely out of proper sequence and was scattered
among several files. During editing an attempt was made to
arrange the papers in proper sequence, and following that
attempt page numbers were assigned to each part. These
numbers appear as lightly pencilled marks on the upper right
corner of each recto.

Subsequent to the above arrangement, some pages were
further rearranged in what was believed to be an improved
sequence. In so doing, however, no changes were made in the
above assigned numbers. A check list of the contents of this
manuscript was prepared in August 1961 by Don D. Roberts. This
check list has been microfilmed preceding ms. [del]#399[/del] [ins]319[/ins] and within
it are indicated some of the sequential changes made later.
In any case, the manuscript has been filmed in the sequence
most recently established, without regard to the numerical
sequence of the page numbers.
