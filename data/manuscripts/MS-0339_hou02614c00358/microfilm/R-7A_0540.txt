There is no subject into which I have inquired more particularly & persistently than the varieties of dyadic and triadic relations without obtaining a satisfactory classification of either. Triadic relations may be divided as follows:
1[sup]st[/sup] those of which at least one correlate is a mere Idea
2[sup]nd[/sup] those of which no Idea but at least one Occurrence is a correlate
3[sup]rd[/sup] those all of whose correlates are [del]Sign[/del] Signs etc.

The 1[sup]st[/sup] of these classes is divisible into
1. Those all whose correlates are Ideas
2. Those of which 2 correlates have that character
3. Those of which all three have that character
