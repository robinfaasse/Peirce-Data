The [del]Priman[/del] may be called a Presentment for the present.

The primum is that which is such as it is regardless of anything else. That is it is complete in itself and simple. But as every body knows in regard to character [del]even[/del] that the only way to be "teres, totus, atque rotundus" is to be [del]profo[/del] stupendously incomplete.

It is vague in the sense of being unanalyzed, and as being unidentifiable is indefinite in logical form. For such is the nature of the indefinite.
