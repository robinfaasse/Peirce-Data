[date]1903 June 9[/date]. Theorem [u]XXVI[/u] may be [del]generat[/del] [ins]extended[/ins] as follows:

Two graphs not connected in their innermost common enclosure [del] (whether through another graph or not) [/del] and not so connected without entering any other enclosure are not connected (even through another graph at all)

Thus [fig]graph of a, b, c connected[/fig] $= \sum_i \sum_j a_i \cdot \bar{b}_{ij} \cdot c_i$

[fig]graph of a, b, c with b in enclosure[/fig] $= \sum_{ij} b_{ij} (\bar{a}_i + c_j)$
$= \sum_{ij} b_{ij} \bar{a}_i + \sum_u \sum_v b_{uv} \cdot c_v$

[fig]two separate graphs a-b and b-c[/fig]

But [fig]graph of a, b, c with b in enclosure and a, c outside[/fig] $\sum_i \sum_j a_i c_j (\overline{\prod_k b_{ij}})$
$= \sum_i \sum_j a_i c_j \bar{b}_{ij} + \sum_i \sum_j a_i c_j \bar{b}_{ji}$
$= \sum_i \sum_j a_i c_j \bar{b}_{ij} + \sum_u \sum_v a_u c_v \bar{b}_{vu}$

[fig]two separate graphs with nested enclosures[/fig]

[fig]complex graph with nested enclosures and connections a, b, c, d[/fig]

$\sum_i \sum_j \prod_k \prod_l d_{ij} (\bar{a}_{ik} + \bar{c}_{jl} + b_{kl})$
$\sum_i \sum_j \prod_k \prod_l d_{ij} (\bar{a}_{ik} + \bar{c}_{jl} + b_{kl}) \cdot (\bar{a}_{il} + \bar{c}_{jk} + b_{lk})$
