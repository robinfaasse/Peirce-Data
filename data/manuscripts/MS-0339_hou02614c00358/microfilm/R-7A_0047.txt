I wish to investigate the nature of a simple concept.

Such a concept first arises as predicated of some
object (occasion of experience)
[ins]S is M[/ins]

On the ground of some previous representation
of the object. (Not immediate)

The predication of the concept is virtually contained
in this previous representation.

[fig]wavy line[/fig]

To say that a simple concept is the immediate
apprehension of a quality is but a mode of
saying that its meaning is given in the repre-
sentation which gives rise to it, inasmuch as it
is as much as to say that that quality is contained
in that representation.
