§1. "Science" has 3 meanings
1. [u]ἐπιστήμη[/u]. The demonstrative character of certain cognition
2. (Formulated by Coleridge at the beginning of the [del]XIXth century[/del])
Systematized & organized knowledge. [ins]Well Ordered knowledge[/ins]
3. By the end of the [del]XIXth[/del] century: An concrete operation
conducted by groups of men. Its spirit which [ins]gives life to every other element of it[/ins] is [del]I have on other occasions defined[/del] singlehearted desire to learn the truth; [del]where the methods of doing so are carefully studied[/del]
& hence each man gets all the help possible from his fellows.

§2. "Philosophy" has 2 principal meanings
1. The cream of the sciences, — synthetic or positive philosophy
2. Cenoscopy, the study of the experience already acquired.

§3. In commencing cenoscopy we are confronted with the
fact that we already believe a great many things. These beliefs
must be [del]criticized. To[/del] reconsidered; and this reconsideration
should be architectonically planned. Every procedure of cenoscopy
should be architectonically planned. This plan should not
be adopted without the severest criticism. Nothing in
cenoscopy should be adopted without criticism.
[del]Every criticism should wait to be planned; the
adoption of each plan should wait for criticism[/del]
Imperfection inseparable. We must go ahead
as little imperfectly as circumstances permit.
