Let the first of a triad of numbers be
1 for a Tone  2 for a Token  3 for a Type

Let the second of the triad show the Object of the Sign [del]The[/del]
1 an Idea  2 an Occurrence  3 a Habit

Let the third number have the same signification for the Interpretant

Then there will be ten classes only
since an Idea can [ins]be[/ins] determined [ins]by[/ins] an Idea only
[ins]and[/ins] a Habit can be determined by a Habit only

222
