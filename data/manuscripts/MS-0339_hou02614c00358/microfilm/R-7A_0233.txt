Let $\phi x = x + a$

$\Delta (x + a) \equiv (x + \Delta x) + a - (x + a)$
$\equiv \Delta x$

Let $\phi x = a_{1}x$

$\Delta (a_{1}x) \equiv a_{1}(x + \Delta x) - a_{1}x$
$\equiv a_{1}\Delta x$

Let $\phi x \equiv 1 - x$

$\Delta (1 - x) \equiv 1 - (x + \Delta x) - (1 - x)$
$\equiv - \Delta x$

Let $\phi x = e^{x}$

$\Delta e^{x} \equiv e^{x + \Delta x} - e^{x}$
$\equiv e^{x} \cdot e^{\Delta x} - e^{x}$
$\equiv e^{x} (e^{\Delta x} - 1)$

Let $\phi x \equiv x^{2}$

$\Delta x^{2} \equiv (x + \Delta x)^{2} - x^{2}$

$\Delta (\phi x + \psi x) \equiv \phi(x + \Delta x) + \psi(x + \Delta x) - (\phi x + \psi x)$
$\equiv \Delta \phi x + \Delta \psi x$
