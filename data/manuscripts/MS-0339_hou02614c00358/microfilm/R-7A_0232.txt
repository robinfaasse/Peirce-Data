$$e^x = y$$ though [del]$$x \times 0 = 0$$[/del] [ins]$$x \cdot 0 = 0$$[/ins] [del]$$y \times 0 = 0$$[/del] [ins]$$y \cdot 0 = 0$$[/ins]
