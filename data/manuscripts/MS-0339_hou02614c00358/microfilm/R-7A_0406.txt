$a_{1} b_{1}$ means some $a$ is $b$
$a_{1} \prec b_{1}$ means every $a$ is $b$
$a_{1} \infty b_{1}$ means something is $a$ & $b$
$a_{1} \infty \prec b_{1}$ means everything is $a$ or $b$

$a \prec x$
$a_{1} \prec x_{1}$ [del]Some[/del] Nothing is $a$

$a \prec b \prec x_{1}$ No $a$ is $b$
$= a_{1} b_{1} \prec x_{1}$

$a_{1} (b_{1} \prec x_{1})$ Some $a$ is not $b$
$= (a_{1} \prec b_{1}) \prec x_{1}$

$a \prec b \prec c$ Whatever is $a$ is $b$ & $c$
$= a \cdot b \prec c$ If both $a$ and $b$ are $c$

$a \cdot (b \prec c)$     $a \cdot (\overline{b} + c)$
$(a \prec b) \prec c$     $\overline{a \overline{b}} \prec c$

$s \dagger m$
$(\overline{u} \dagger p) (s \overline{p} \dagger q)$

$s \overline{p} (\overline{u} \dagger p) \dagger q$
$= (\overline{u} \dagger q)$

$s \dagger (\overline{u} \dagger p) \dagger q$
$= s \overline{m} \dagger q$
$(s \dagger m) \dagger q$

$(a \dagger p) (s \dagger \overline{p})$

$a \dagger b \dagger c$
$(a \dagger b) \dagger c$     $a \dagger (b \dagger c)$
