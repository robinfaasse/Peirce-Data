There are three forms of question

1 Are quadrupeds animals?
2 Are there quadruped animals?
3 Is anything [del]quadruped or animal[/del] [ins]either quadruped or[/ins] animal [ins]without being the other[/ins]?

The affirmative answers will be
To 1 Universal Affirmative. Yes, all Quadrupeds are animals
To 2 Particular Affirmative. Yes, [del]Quadruped animals exist[/del]
To 3 Universal Negative. [del]Yes[/del] [ins]No[/ins]. There is [del]a collection of animals[/del] [ins]no animal[/ins] [del]quadruped[/del] [ins]not quadruped[/ins]

There is no difference logically between hypotheticals & categoricals, the subject is a sign of the predicate, the antecedent of the consequent; and this is the only [del]sign[/del] [ins]relation[/ins] this concerns.
