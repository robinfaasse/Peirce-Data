[u]b. As to the Dynamic Interpretant[/u]
[u]α. In itself[/u]
1. The sign may be such that it can only be interpreted properly in feeling or in a possibility. [del]The reason why an abstract quality is so indefinite is that what I mean by an abstract quality consists in the possibility of something. Now to say that anything is possible is to say that under some unspecified conditions it will occur. The same is the mark of indefiniteness.[/del]
Such a sign may be termed an [u]eidoseme[/u]
2. The sign may be [del]such that it can only be interpreted in[/del] [ins]an[/ins] existential fact.
Such a sign may be termed an [u]ergoseme[/u]
3. The sign may be such that it can only be interpreted primarily in a sign. Such a sign may be termed a [u]logoseme[/u].
[u]β. In the [del]relation[/del] [ins]nature[/ins] of the appeal of the sign to it.[/u]
1. The sign may be such that its [ins]natural mode of[/ins] [del]can only[/del] appeal to its dynamic interpretant is by sympathy
2. The sign may be such that its [ins]natural mode of[/ins] [del]can only[/del] appeal to its dynamic interpretant is by compulsion
3. The sign may be such that its natural mode of appeal to its dynamic interpretant is by reason
