[del]Combination of Arithmetic & Boole's Calculus[/del]

Given $p a_1 \bar{n} = 0$ when $p = \frac{1}{2}$
$q a_2 \bar{n} = 0$ " $q = \frac{1}{2}$
$m_1 n = 0$
$x_1 m = 0$
$x_2 \bar{x} = 0$

We have by ordinary processes

(1) $q a_2 \bar{n} + p a_1 n + p a_1 x + x_1 \bar{a} = 0$

[del]But for $p = q = \frac{1}{2}$[/del]

And from this

(2) $p a_1 q a_2 = 0$
But [del]since $p = q = \frac{1}{2}$ we have[/del]
$p + q = 1$ or $q = 1 - p$
$\therefore p \bar{q} = p \cdot p = p$
& since $p + q = 1$
$p \bar{q} = 1 - p$
But $p = \bar{p}$ & $1 - \bar{p}$
$= 1 - \bar{p} \cdot \bar{q}$
Hence $p \bar{q} = \bar{p} \cdot \bar{q}$
Hence $p a_1 q a_2 = 0$ [del]gives[/del]
