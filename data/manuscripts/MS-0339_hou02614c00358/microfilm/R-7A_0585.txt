By the way, the fact [del]that[/del] [ins]that[/ins] the multitude of $\infty^2 = \infty^1$ is strictly analogous
to the fact that the sum of a semi-convergent series depends on the order
in which the terms are taken, so that we should express both in
the same way, and either say [del]that the multitude of $\infty^2$ may change[/del]
to $\infty^1$ by counting in a different order, or else say [del]that the[/del]
two values of the convergent series are equal. But the latter would
be manifestly inconvenient, and therefore we should say the other.
We might say that $\infty^2$ and $\infty$ are semi-equal.
