We might call the three Interpretants
The Impressional Interpretant
The Factual Interpretant
The Habitual Interpretant

The first member of each triad is distinguished by its
want of definite identity, which only the Dyadic Element
introduces.

All my notions are too narrow. Instead of "Sign",
ought I not to say Medium?

Concerning the Impressional Interpretant, we ask
what is its Nature, Primal, Secundal, or Tertial and what
division of Signs does this give rise to?

[del]There is no other Question to be asked of it for it is nothing[/del]
but the Sign under another form of presentation. Is so the
meaning

Concerning the Factual Interpretant, we ask, First
what is its nature and Second how is it produced
and what divisions of signs result?

Concerning the Habitual Interpretant, we ask
Of what Nature is it, How is it produced? and What
is its Significance? [del]Or Destiny?[/del]

[del]The Sorts of Habits and habits of body, habits of action, Habits of habituation.[/del]
How are habits produced? By simple custom, by forcible experience, by the growth
of one habit influencing another.
What is the Significance of Habit? Producing a state of calm, Achieving
a definite purpose, the Gradual Elevation of ideals.
