It would seem to follow

since [ins]v[/ins] $C_{v,x} \equiv \sum^v C_x$

that $C_{x+x+x+...} \equiv C_{n,x} \equiv n, C_x$

But it plainly does not follow. Or rather that $n$ must be $\equiv 1$.

It is true, however, that
$C_{n,x} \equiv v, C_n, C_x$

for [del]$C_{n,x+u} \equiv C$[/del] putting $u \equiv (1-n), y$
$C_{n,x+u} \equiv C_x \equiv C_{n,x} + C_u$
And so $C_n \equiv C_{n,x} + C_u$
$\therefore C_n, C_x \equiv C_{n,x} + C_u, C_x$
Hence putting $v \equiv [del](1-C_{u,x})[/del] (1-C_{u,n})$
$v, C_n, C_x \equiv C_{n,x}$
