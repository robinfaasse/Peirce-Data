The Final Interpretant is the ultimate effect of
the sign, so far as it is intended or destined, from
the character of the sign, being more or less of a
habitual [ins]and formal[/ins] nature. In respect to the final interpretant
signs are divisible, [ins]1[sup]st[/sup],[/ins] according to the nature of this
interpretant; 2[sup]nd[/sup], according to the relation of it to
the sign; 3[sup]rd[/sup], according to the relation of it to the object.

In the first respect the division is into different forms of good
according as the object is to create or modify an Ideal,
to produce an individual event or process,
or to establish a general habit or law.
