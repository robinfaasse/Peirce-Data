What shall we finally say then? The theory that man
is is incomprehensible & self-contradictory.
But is it not justified? Yes.
Shall we then attempt to satisfy our intellects with an
incomprehensible & self-contradictory hypothesis? Certainly not.
Nay, such a hypothesis is not intellectual by the very definition
of the intellect. It cannot be held in a comprehensible form but
it may be held.
The intellect did not make this hypothesis. The intellect makes
no hypotheses which are incomprehensible. The intellect is not
therefore alone concerned in any propositions.
But we make it. All that the words convey is something in-
capable of being made distinct, since it is emotional in its own
nature. And as an emotion it is justified.
