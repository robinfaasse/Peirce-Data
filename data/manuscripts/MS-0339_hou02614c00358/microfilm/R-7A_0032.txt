[u]Induction[/u]
[pn]27[/pn]

This, this, & this are what have been drawn from this bag
This, this, & this are all balls
$\therefore$ All that is drawn from the bag would be balls.

***

This, this, this, and this have been taken from this room
This, this, this, & this are all the gold balls I know

Non sequitur

***

Some of these things have been taken from the bag
Some of these things are balls
$\therefore$ All that is taken from the bag are balls

Whatever has some character of what is now $S' S'' S'''$
has every character which may be found to be possessed by $M$
whatever has the common characters of $S' S'' S'''$ is $P$
$\therefore$ whatever has some character of what is now $M$ is $P$
