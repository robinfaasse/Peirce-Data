[del][u]New principle[/u][/del] Suppose we have a right to $x \prec (y \prec z)$
Then if we have a right to $x$ we have a right to $y \prec z$
$\therefore$ If we have a right to $x$ and right to $y$ we have right to $z$
$\therefore$ If we have right to $y$ we have right to $(x \prec z)$
$$y \prec (x \prec z)$$
$\therefore [x \prec (y \prec z)] \prec [y \prec (x \prec z)]$
