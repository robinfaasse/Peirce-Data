[u]Liberty & Necessity[/u]

Suppose a man is in a room locked [del]in and does not want[/del]
[del]to go out, so that[/del] [ins]his staying is voluntary;[/ins] is he at liberty? No.
But suppose that if he [del]were to[/del] [ins]had[/ins] wished [del]to go[/del] the door
would be unlocked, was he then at liberty? Yes. [del]Now this is[/del]
