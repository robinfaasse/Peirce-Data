$5^{\underline{\text{th}}}$, let $\bullet$, $\heartsuit$, $\Upsilon$ be [del]two[/del] [ins]three[/ins] mutually exclusive times; [ins]and let P be a certain character[/ins] Then, if it be
true that taking any two mutually exclusive durations $\diamondsuit$, $\clubsuit$,
[del]each[/del] such that $\spadesuit\heartsuit : \Upsilon\diamondsuit$ is the peculiar partition of that tetrad,
and $\spadesuit\heartsuit : \Upsilon\clubsuit$ is the peculiar partition of that tetrad, and
such that every part of $\diamondsuit$ possesses P while no part of $\clubsuit$
possesses that character, it is always the case that $\spadesuit\heartsuit : \heartsuit\diamondsuit$
is the peculiar partition of that tetrad, then there is always an
instant, and not more (mutually exclusive), which we may
denote by $\spadesuit\beta$ which partly possesses and partly does not
possess the character P; and every instant, A, such that
$\spadesuit\heartsuit : \text{A}\Upsilon$ is the peculiar partition of that tetrad, while
$\spadesuit\spadesuit\beta : \text{A}\heartsuit$ is the peculiar partition of that tetrad, [ins]altogether[/ins] possesses
the character P; every instant, $\Phi$, such that
$\spadesuit\heartsuit : \Phi\Upsilon$ is the peculiar partition of that tetrad, while
$\heartsuit\spadesuit\beta : \Phi\spadesuit$ is the peculiar partition of that tetrad, altogether
non-possesses the character P.

No instant exists in Time except so far as it has been
developed like $\spadesuit\beta$, so that duality is essential to an instant.
