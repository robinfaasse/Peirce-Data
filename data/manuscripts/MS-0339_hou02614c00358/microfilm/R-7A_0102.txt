If I had any rel- form of relation
such that
$x \breve{x} \prec x \dagger \breve{x}$

I might have a clue -
The $x$ it would seem must be a relative aggregate

Say $x = p \dagger q$
$(p \dagger q) (\breve{p} \dagger \breve{q}) \prec p \breve{p}$ This looks unpromising

It is true that
$p \breve{p} \prec p \dagger \breve{p}$

Suppose then we let
$x = \xi \dagger 1$
$\breve{x} = 1 \dagger \breve{\xi}$
$x \breve{x} = (\xi \dagger 1) (1 \dagger \breve{\xi})$
$x \dagger \breve{x} = \xi \dagger 1 \dagger 1 \dagger \breve{\xi}$

[u]$p \dagger \breve{p} = (\pi \cdot \tau) \dagger [(\breve{\pi} \dagger 1) \psi \breve{\pi}]$[/u]
$\{ \pi \dagger [(\breve{\pi} \dagger 1) \psi \breve{\pi}] \} \cdot$
$\{ \sigma \pi \dagger [(\breve{\pi} \dagger 1) \psi \breve{\pi}] \}$

$p \breve{p} \prec p \dagger \breve{p}$
$x \breve{p} (1 \dagger \tau) \breve{p}$
$\prec p \dagger \tau \breve{p}$

$p = \pi \cdot \tau \pi$
$p \breve{p} = (\pi \cdot \tau \pi) [(\breve{\pi} \cdot (\breve{\tau \pi} + 1)]$
$\prec \pi \breve{\pi} \cdot \pi (\breve{\tau \pi}) \cdot \tau \pi \breve{\pi} \cdot \tau \pi (\breve{\tau \pi})$
$p \dagger \breve{p} = \pi \cdot \tau \pi \dagger \breve{\pi} \cdot (\breve{\tau \pi})$
$= (\pi \breve{\pi}) \cdot \tau \pi \breve{\pi} + 1$
$\breve{p} = (\breve{\pi} \dagger 1) \psi \breve{\pi}$
$p \breve{p} = (\pi \cdot \tau \pi) [(\breve{\pi} \dagger 1) \psi \breve{\pi}]$
$= (\pi \cdot \tau \pi) (\breve{\pi} \dagger 1) + (\pi \cdot \tau \pi) \psi \breve{\pi}$
