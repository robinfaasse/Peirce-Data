[u]Notes on my Provisional Classification of Signs.[/u]
I have thought of the Object of a Sign as that which determines
the Sign; and this is well thought. I have thought of the Interpretant as
that which the Sign determines or might determine, [del]or should determine,[/del]
but this is not so well. For my idea of determination is dyadic while the
idea of the relation of the interpretant to the Sign is triadic.
Say that Interpretant is that which the Sign [del]determines to be[/del] [ins]builds up[/ins] in correspondence with
the Object.
The Object is plainly Twofold. The [del]Immediate[/del] [ins]Dynamoid[/ins] Object is the Real Object
according to the above definition. The Immediate [del]Real[/del] is the Object as
[del]presented[/del] presented in the Sign.
The Interpretant is Threefold. The Normal Interpretant is the [del]Genuine[/del] [ins]concerning which[/ins]
Interpretant, [del]being[/del] embracing all that the Sign could reveal to [del]a sufficiently[/del] [ins]any[/ins]
penetrating mind, [del]being[/del] [ins]more than[/ins] any possible mind could [del]conclude[/del] [ins]conclude[/ins]
from it, since there is no end to the distinct conclusions that could be drawn
concerning the Object from any Sign. The [del]Dynamic[/del] [ins]Dynamoid[/ins] Interpretant is just
what is drawn from the Sign by a given individual Interpreter. The Immediate
Interpretant is the interpretant represented, explicitly or implicitly, in the Sign itself.
[del]This[/del] I have thus omitted the intended interpretant. So far as the intention is
betrayed in the Sign, it belongs to the immediate Interpretant. So far as it is
not so betrayed, it may be the [del]intended[/del] interpretant of another sign, but it is in no sense
the interpretant of that sign.

As to the Matter of the Sign itself, it is either a Tone or a Token or a Type
The word Tone is a blend of Tone and Tune. It means a [del]sign[/del] quality of
feeling which is [del]a[/del] significant, whether it be simple, like a Tone or complex,
like a Tune. But the latter is not pure feeling. By a Token, I mean
an existing thing or an actual historical event, which serves as a Sign.
By a Type, I mean a general form which can be repeated indefinitely, and
is in all its repetitions one and the same sign. Thus the word [u]the[/u] is a Type.

book, but it is only one word twenty times repeated. The distinction between
Type and a Token is obvious. There may be some confusion between the Tone
