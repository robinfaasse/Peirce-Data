Hence

$$\begin{array}{l} \ell_1 \equiv \ell \text{ to something} \\ \ell_0 \equiv 0 \text{ or } \ell \text{ to everything} \end{array} \qquad \begin{array}{l} 1 \ell \equiv 1 \\ 0 \ell \equiv 0 \end{array}$$

Now if we let

$$\ell'x \equiv 1 - (1 - \ell)x$$

$$\ell'x \equiv 1 - (1 - 1)x \equiv 1 - 0x \equiv 1 - 0 \equiv 1$$
$$\ell'x \equiv 1 - (1 - 0)x \equiv 1 - 1x \equiv 1 - 1 \equiv 0$$
$$\ell'1 \equiv 1 - (1 - \ell)1 \equiv 1 - \text{other than } \ell \text{ to something} \equiv \text{what is } \ell \text{ & everything} \equiv \ell_0$$
$$\ell'0 \equiv 1 - (1 - \ell)0 \equiv 1 - \text{other than } \ell \text{ to everything} \equiv (1 - \ell)1$$

Then $\ell'x$ might be written $\ell^x$

and we should have

$$1^x \equiv 1 \qquad 0^x \equiv 0$$

$$\ell^1 \equiv \ell_0 \text{ which might be written } \ell$$
