Let $\phi$ be such that
$$\phi(x+y) \equiv \phi x + \phi y$$
Then $\phi(x+\Delta x) \equiv \phi x + \phi \Delta x$
But $\Delta \phi x \equiv \phi(x+\Delta x) - \phi x$
$\therefore \Delta \phi x \equiv \phi \Delta x$
$\therefore$ [del]$(\phi + \Delta) (\phi x + \Delta x) \equiv$[/del] $(\phi + \Delta)^n x$ [ins]?[/ins]
Let us scrutinize that.
$$(\phi + \Delta)^2 \qquad \begin{array}{l} \phi + \Delta \\ \underline{\phi + \Delta} \\ \phi^2 + 2\phi\Delta + \Delta^2 \end{array}$$
Let $\phi$ be [del]$\equiv$[/del] $ax+b$
$$(\phi+\Delta)x \equiv \phi x + \Delta x \equiv ax+b + \Delta x$$
$$(\phi+\Delta)(\phi+\Delta)x =$$
$$\begin{array}{rl} & a_2x+a_2b + a_1\Delta x + b + \Delta(\phi+\Delta)x \\ \equiv & a_2x+a_2b + a_1\Delta x + b + (\phi+\Delta)\Delta x \\ \equiv & a_2x+a_2b + a_1\Delta x + b + a_1\Delta x + b + \Delta.\Delta x \\ \equiv & a_2x+a_2b + 2a_1\Delta x + 2b + \Delta^2 x \end{array}$$
$$\phi^2 x \equiv a_2x + a_1b + b$$
$$\phi\Delta x \equiv a_1\Delta x + b$$
