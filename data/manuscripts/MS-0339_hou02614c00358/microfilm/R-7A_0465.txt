A collection which contains no more than is supposed in
supposing that it is as large as every collection of a
collection of collections, cannot be larger than a
collection larger than any collection of that collection
of collections.

The collection of collections each equal to a given
collection, the A's, which collection may be formed
from the A's and one individual besides is not
larger than a collection larger than the collection
of A's.

i.e. If the B's are [del]no[/del] more than the A's, they are as many
as all the different collections of A's, but not as many
as the A's.
