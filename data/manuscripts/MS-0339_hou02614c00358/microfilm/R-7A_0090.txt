[u]Composition of Relatives[/u]

It is better to define the 8 modes of compounding dual relatives by De Morgans 8 forms of proposition

$l\text{s}$ denotes everything of which it can be said that what is $\breve{l}$ by it is partient of servant of $\breve{l}(ls)\prec\overline{s}$

$l\circ s$     $\breve{l}(l\circ s)\prec s$

$l^{s}$     $\breve{l}(l^{s})\prec\overline{s}$

$^{l}s$     $l(^{l}s)\prec s$

Reciprocally one may consider the eight propositions as differing by the different modes of combining relatives

$\begin{array}{lll}
l\prec s & \infty\prec\breve{l}^{s} & lx\prec sx\quad x\prec\breve{l}(sx) \\
\overline{l}\prec\overline{s} & \infty\prec\breve{l}^{s} & \overline{l}x\prec\overline{s}x\quad x\prec\breve{l}^{sx} \\
l\prec\overline{s} & \infty\prec\breve{l}s & lx\prec\overline{s}x
\end{array}$
