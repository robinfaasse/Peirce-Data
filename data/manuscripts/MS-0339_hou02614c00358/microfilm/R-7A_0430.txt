One has to come back to the [u]dictum de omni[/u]

To say that something is true of a general is to
say that it is true of whatever that general is true.
Take the general "massive" to say
All massive is Q
is simply to say
If P is massive, then P is Q

Thus the transitive relation is implied in the
very idea of a general.
