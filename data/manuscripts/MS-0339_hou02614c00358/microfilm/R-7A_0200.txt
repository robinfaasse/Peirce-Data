I wish to investigate the nature of a simple concept.

Such a concept first arises as predicated of some object (occasion of experience)
[ins]S is M[/ins]

On the ground of some previous representation of the object. (Not immediate)

The predication of the concept is virtually contained in this previous representation.

To say, that a simple concept is the immediate apprehension of a quality is but a mode of saying that its meaning is given in the representation which gives rise to it, inasmuch as [del]its[/del] [del]is as much as to say that that quality is contained in that representation[/del]
