such that
$$\kappa \breve{\bar{\kappa}} \prec \chi \dagger \breve{\bar{\chi}}$$
I might have a clue —

The $\chi$ it would seem must be a relative aggregate

Say $\chi = \rho \dagger \sigma$

$$(\rho \dagger \sigma) \breve{\overline{(\rho \dagger \sigma)}} \prec \rho \breve{\bar{\rho}} \text{. This looks unpromising}$$

It is true that
$$\rho \breve{\bar{\rho}} \prec \rho \dagger \breve{\bar{\rho}}$$

$$\chi = \bar{\xi} \dagger 1$$
$$\breve{\bar{\chi}} = \tau \dagger \breve{\bar{\xi}}$$
$$\kappa \breve{\bar{\kappa}} = (\bar{\xi} \dagger 1) \tau \breve{\bar{\xi}}$$
$$\chi \dagger \breve{\bar{\chi}} = \bar{\xi} \dagger 1 \dagger \tau \dagger \breve{\bar{\xi}}$$
