[u]On Logical Extension and Comprehension[/u]

One term is more extensive than another when it is predicable of all that the latter is and more besides. One term is more comprehensive than another when all the [del]qualities[/del] [ins]characters[/ins] predicable of the latter are predicable of it and more besides.

From this it is plain, at once, that the greater the extension the less the comprehension.
