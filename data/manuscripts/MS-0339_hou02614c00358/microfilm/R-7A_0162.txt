$M$ is $P$ in the sense [del]that the[/del] [ins]actual denotation[/ins]
of things taken under $M$ are $P$ (contingent)
and $2^{\text{nd}}$ in the sense that all possible things
taken under $M$ would be $P$ (necessary)

On the same principle

$S$ is $M$ in the senses (attributive)
$1^{\text{st}}$ that $S$ has the qualities taken of $M$
$2^{\text{nd}}$ that $S$ has all qualities of $M$ (subsumption)

Still it may be doubted if hypothesis proceeds by
random selection of qualities of the new predicate.
