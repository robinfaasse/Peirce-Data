In short we need but one logical function to
express everything.

We may write this function $(x,y)$ and
let it mean
$$\begin{array}{rccccccc}
(x,y) & = & i \varphi & + & j \psi & + & k \chi & + & l \omega \\
U & = & \varphi & + & \psi & + & \chi & + & \omega \\
x & = & \varphi & + & \psi & & & & \\
y & = & \varphi & & & + & \chi & &
\end{array}$$

Solving
$$\varphi = \frac{l U - (x,y) + (i-l)x + (k-l)y}{-i + j + k - l}$$

$$x + y - \varphi = \frac{-l U + (x,y) + (k-i)x + (j-i)y}{-i + j + k - l}$$
