The same intensive relation subsists between [del]the reverse of A and[/del]
[del]the reverse of B. For if the reverse of A is not B,[/del]
[del]then B is somewhat that is not A. But B is somewhat[/del]
[del]of which A is not. Therefore B is somewhat that is not A.[/del]
[del]But this is not the case. Therefore[/del]
[del]the reverse of A is B.[/del]

And the following are contradictory:
[del]The reverse of A is B[/del]
[del]The reverse of A is not B[/del]
