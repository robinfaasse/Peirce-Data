[del]There is another point of view[/del]

What is the function of a symbol as subject? It stands for certain things. Then if the predicate be true of all the things that it stands for as yet [del]that is[/del] for all which we yet know it to stand for, the symbol may stand as subject provisionally.

The difficulty with this is that it does not represent [ins]synthetic[/ins] the probability of the inference.

It is however a good idea that a random selection is equivalent to all known. the genesis of these two would show that.
