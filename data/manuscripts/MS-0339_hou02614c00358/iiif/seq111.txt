$C_x$ denotes that which is $C$ to some $x$
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
$C_{(x+_sy)} \equiv C_x +_s C_y$
$\therefore C_x \equiv C_x +_s C_0$  or  $C_0 \equiv \frac{0}{0} C_x$ [del]$\ldots \ldots$[/del]
and $C_1 \equiv C_x +_s C_{1-x}$ [del]or $C_{1-x} \equiv \frac{0}{0}$[/del]

$C_1$ is $C$ of something
$C_0$ covers nothing except what is $C$ of everything.


$1 = (1-C)_x$
$= 1-C_x$
$\therefore$
$\equiv (1-C)_x +_s (1-C)_y$
$\equiv 1-(C_x +_s C_y)$
