Thus it seems that a [u]collection[/u] is just like
say a crystal or a gold ring or any other indi-
vidual thing except that it [del]exists[/del] [ins]is entity[/ins], not as
[del]an atom[/del] [ins]a[/ins] first substance, but by virtue of any
predicate. It does not exist by virtue of its
members; since it is a collection, even if it has
no members, provided any predicate describes
it. But still, when it once does exist, its iden-

predicate which defines it gives it [u]Being[/u]; and
