I jot down a few things relating to Phanerology.

The Primum may be called [u]Besentment[/u] for the present.

The primum is that which is such as it is regardless
of anything else. That is it is complete in itself and
simple. But as every body knows in regard to the character
of men that the only way to be "teres, totus, atque rotundus"
is to be [del]quite[/del] stupendously incomplete.

The only way to be complete, to refer to nothing else, in any
way [del]is[/del], and and the same time to be simple, which is 
essential to this completeness, is to [del]be[/del] have a Protean
indefiniteness.

[del]The[/del] [ins]Any[/ins] primum [del]A[/del] must be simple, Because that which is
composite depends on its parts, and depends upon each
part, and that part is not A itself. [del]To be anything at[/del]
[del]all it must.[/del] It cannot [ins]itself essentially[/ins] possess any character not itself
[del]and therefore for that would be to depend on that character.[/del]
Therefore it must itself [u]be[/u] that character. It must,
[del]therefore[/del] be an abstraction, and has no identity other [del]than[/del>
[ins]exact likeness[/ins]

It is vague in the sense of being unanalyzed, and as being
unidentifiable is indefinite in logical form. For such is the
nature of the indefinite. Its essential characters beyond its
logical form, [del]that is, its[/del] [ins]or rather up[/ins] logically material characters, are
positively, that it has a certain positive character absolutely
peculiar to itself, and negatively that it is devoid of existence.
