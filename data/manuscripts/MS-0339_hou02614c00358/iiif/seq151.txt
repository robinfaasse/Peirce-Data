Combination of [del]Arithmetic[/del] & Boole's Calculus

[u]Given[/u] [space] $pa, \bar{m} = 0$ where $p = \frac{1}{2}$
[space] $qa, \bar{n} = 0$ [space] " [space] $q = \frac{1}{2}$
[space] $m, n = 0$
[space] $x, m = 0$
[space] $x, \bar{a} = 0$

We have by ordinary processes

(1) [space] $qa, \bar{n} + pa, n + pa, x + x, \bar{a} = 0$
[space] [del]But for $p + q$[/del]

And from this
(2) [space] $pa, qa = 0$
[space] But [del]$p + q = p + q - pq$[/del]
[space] $p + q = p + q - p, q$
[space] $\therefore$ $p, q = p + q - p, q$
[space] & since $p + q = 1$
[space] $p + q = 1 - p, q$
[space] But $p + q = 1 - \bar{p}, \bar{q}$
[space] $= 1 - \bar{p}, \bar{q}$
[space] Hence $p, q = \bar{p}, \bar{q}$
[space] Hence $pa, qa = 0 = \bar{p} a, \bar{q} a$
