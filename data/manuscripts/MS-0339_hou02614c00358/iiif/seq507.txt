§1. "Science" has 3 meanings
1. [del]ἐπιστήμη[/del]. The demonstrative character of certain cognition
2. (Formulated by Coleridge at the beginning of the XIXth century)
Systematized & organized knowledge [ins]Well Ordered knowledge[/ins]
3. By the end of the [del]XIXth[/del] century, A concrete operation
conducted by groups of men. The sperm which gives life to every
other element of it is single-hearted desire to [del]have one's errors corrected as speedily as possible[/del] [ins]learn the truth.[/ins]
Hence the methods of doing so are carefully studied
& hence each man gets all the help possible from his fellows,

§2. "Philosophy" has 2 principal meanings
1. The cream of the sciences, — synthetic or positive philosophy
2. Cenoscopy, the study of the experience already acquired,

§3. In commencing cenoscopy we are confronted with the
fact that we already believe a great many things. These beliefs
must be [del]criticised[/del] [ins]reconsidered[/ins]; and this reconsideration
should be architectonically planned. [del]Every procedure of cenoscopy
should be architectonically planned.[/del] This plan shouldn't
be adopted without the severest criticism. Nothing in
cenoscopy should be adopted without criticism.
Every criticism should wait to be planned; the
adoption of each plan should wait for criticism
Imperfection inseparable. We must go ahead
as little imperfectly as circumstances permit.
