Now I do not quite like my definition of a General. I have
not sufficiently distinguished between a General and a Collection.
A Collection consists of Individuals, or rather of whatever in-
dividuals of a certain kind actually exist. If there are no such
individuals, still the collection has a mode of being. A gene-
ral is something capable of determination, and having none
the more being [del]in[/del] or reality that individuals corresponding to it
exist. Future time is just as real as past time.
I must define in terms of my three categories. Quality, Reaction,
Representation. Or that which is [del]that[/del] in itself what it is. That
which whose mode of being consists in its being a member of
a pair. That whose mode of being consists in its being the
limit of an endless series.
