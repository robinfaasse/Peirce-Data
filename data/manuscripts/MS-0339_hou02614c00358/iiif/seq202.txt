Thm $\xi + 1 \prec \xi \top + (0 \dagger 1)$

But in a universe of more than one individual
it is not true that $0 \dagger 1$ or that anything is
coexistent only with itself. Hence in every
such universe

$\xi + 1 \prec \xi \top$ [ins] $\prec \xi \top \prec \xi$ [/ins]

$1 \prec \top \dagger 1 \prec \top \top$

$\xi \prec \xi (\top + 1) \prec \xi \top + 1 \prec \xi \top \top$

$(\xi + 1) \top \check{\xi} \prec (\xi \dagger 1 \top) \check{\xi} \prec (\xi + \top) \check{\xi} \prec \xi \check{\xi} \check{\xi}$

[del] and $\xi \check{\xi} \check{\xi} \prec \xi (1 + \top \check{\xi}) \prec \xi$ [/del]

$\xi \check{\xi} \check{\xi} \prec (\xi \top + \top) \check{\xi} \prec [(\xi \top \dagger 1) \top \dagger 1] \check{\xi} \prec (\xi \top \dagger 1) \top \check{\xi}$

$(\xi + 1) \top \check{\xi} (\xi + 1) \top \check{\xi} \prec$ [del] $(\xi + 1)$ [/del]

$(\xi + 1) \top \check{\xi} \check{\xi} \prec \xi \check{\xi} \check{\xi}$

$(\xi + 1) \top \check{\xi} (\xi + 1) \top \check{\xi} \prec \xi \check{\xi} \check{\xi} (\xi + 1) \top \check{\xi} \check{\xi}$
