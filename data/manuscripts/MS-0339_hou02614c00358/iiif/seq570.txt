first steps toward the formation of a general hypothesis about
matter and the quasi-matter by which spirits seem to communicate
with us in this world, and a hypothesis about the nature of mind
and thought. These hypotheses will have their most general features
deduced from the principles of logic. But they must be carried
beyond those general features, and in so far as they are so carried
