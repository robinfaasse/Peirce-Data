Here is another point of view.

What is the function of a symbol as subject? To
stand for certain things. Then if [del]the[/del] predicate be true of
all the things that it stands for as yet that is for all
which we yet know it to stand for, the symbol may stand
as subject provisionally.

The difficulty with this is that it does not represent
the [ins]synthetic[/ins] probability of the inference.

It is however a good idea that a random selection
is equivalent to all known — the genus of these two
would like that.
