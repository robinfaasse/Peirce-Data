Thus All A is B  &
Some A is not B  are contradictory.

Because if the whole extension of A is in that of B then it is
not true that some of it is excluded from A. & vice versa.

Now take All A is B
& [del]Whatever is in no respect like[/del] [ins]the reverse of[/ins] A is somewhat B

Now if the whole intension of B is in the intension of A, no part of it
is excluded from the intension of A; hence no part of it is in that which
is entirely excluded from A; & hence no part of it is in that from which the
intension of A is entirely excluded. On the other hand take any intension
you please it is either wholly in A or else some part of it is out of A, if it
is out of A it is in what is excluded from A, & hence is in that from which
the intension of A is entirely excluded.

For example let B be taken as quadruped & A as [del]sentient[/del]
Now since quadruped is not in sentient, part of it out of sentient, part of
it therefore is in that in which no part of sentient is. [del]That[/del] reverse of A
has all the qualities which A has not hence it has that part of B which
A has not.

The propositions Some A is not B
& the reverse of A resembles B
will coincide when the former is denotative & the latter
connotative.
