Miscellaneous Observations 1

12 Nov. 1865

What is a real definition? A definition of a word whose
essence lies in its denotation.

Nov. 1865

There are three forms of question

1 Are quadrupeds animals?
2 Are there quadruped animals?
3 Is [ins]Are[/ins] anything [del]quadruped or animals[/del] either quadruped or animal?

The affirmative answers will be

To 1. Universal Affirmative. Yes. All Quadrupeds are animals.
To 2 Particular Affirmative Yes Quadruped animals exist
To 3 Universal Negative. Yes. There is a [del]collection[/del] no animal
quadruped.

Nov. 14

There is no difference logically between hypotheticals & cate-
goricals. The subject is a sign of the predicate, the antecedent of
the consequent; and this is the only point that concerns
logic.
