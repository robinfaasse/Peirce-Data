whatever does not possess $\alpha$ and by nothing that does possess $\alpha$

Given any two characters $\alpha$ and $\beta$ there is a
character which [ins]is[/ins] possessed by whatever possesses
either $\alpha$ or $\beta$ and by nothing else

Given any two characters, $\alpha$ and $\beta$ there is a
character which is possessed by whatever
possesses them both and by nothing else.

Given any character $\alpha$ there is a character $\beta$
such that if anything possesses $\alpha$ something
possesses $\alpha$ and $\beta$ and nothing else possesses
$\alpha$ and $\beta$.
