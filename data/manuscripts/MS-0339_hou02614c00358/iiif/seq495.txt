b. As to the Dynamic Interpretant.
&alpha;. In itself
1. The sign maybe such that it can only be interpreted properly in feeling
or in a possibility. [The reason why an abstract quality is indefinite is
that what I mean by an abstract quality consists in the possibility of something
Now to say that anything is possible is to say that under some unspecified
conditions it will occur. The some is the mark of indefiniteness.]
Such a sign maybe termed an [u]eidoseme[/u]
2. The sign maybe such that it can only be interpreted in an existential fact.
Such a sign maybe termed an [u]ergoseme[/u]
3. The sign maybe such that it can only be interpreted primarily
in a sign. Such a sign maybe termed a [u]logoseme[/u].
&beta;. In the [del]nature[/del] [ins]relation[/ins] of the appeal of the sign to it.
1. The sign maybe such that its [del]can only[/del] [ins]natural mode of[/ins] appeal to its
dynamic interpretant is by sympathy
2. The sign maybe such that its [del]can only[/del] [ins]natural mode of[/ins] appeal to its
dynamic interpretant is by compulsion
3. The sign maybe such that its natural mode of appeal
to its dynamic interpretant is by reason
