A fallacy is a miracle. I cannot admit its existence

as a violation of logical law. A fallacy is a reasoning

from mistaken data. That is 1<sup>st</sup> from insufficient data or

from a fallacious inference, or 2<sup>nd</sup> from using as data some-

thing different from what appeared to be used. Thus for

example if I say for fun

It either rains or it doesn't rain

It doesn't rain

<center>∴ It rains</center>

to fun.
