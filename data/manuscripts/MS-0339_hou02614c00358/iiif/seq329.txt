[Provisional definition of a continuum.] A continuum is a system of

1899 Aug 17.
What I wrote yesterday, although the result of many years meditation,
shows an immature stage of thought. In several places I am endeavoring
to get along with fewer elements than is possible. I ought to
begin by laying down my categories. My definition of a collection
is well enough as far as it goes, but it does not bring out the important
point that a collection consists of ultimate, and therefore irrational,
units.
