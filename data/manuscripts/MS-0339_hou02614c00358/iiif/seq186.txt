The form of a "superiority" is
(l + [u]l[/u]) · l[u]l[/u]

Required to find a form of "succession"
that is a superiority such that either A is
superior to B or B to A.

The negative of it must implicate some
relation l and also [u]l[/u].

This relation itself must be an
aggregate. Now how can an aggregate
be transitive? [del]And how can it be a difference? [/del]

[fig]To be a difference each aggregant must
be a difference. C'est élémentaire.[/fig]

To be transitive
(A + B) (A + B)
= AA + BA + AB + BB.

Each of these four terms must be
included under A + B
