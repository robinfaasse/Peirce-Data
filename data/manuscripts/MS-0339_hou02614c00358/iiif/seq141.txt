In arithmetic
z logₑ mʸ = logₑ mʸᶻ

Now suppose
eˣ = mʸ, ᶻ

Then it does not follow that every x is z
and therefore not that
z logₑ mʸ = logₑ mʸᶻ

This seems to be a down-right exception.

So this very valuable formula, without which it seems
hope less to get any developments, must be dispensed with.
