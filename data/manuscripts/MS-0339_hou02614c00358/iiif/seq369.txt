[(a ⊰ b) ⊰ c] ⊰ a ⊰ (b ⊰ c)
(b ⊰ c) ⊰ a ⊰ (a ⊰ b) ⊰ c
a ⊰ (a ⊰ b) ⊰ (b ⊰ c) ⊰ c

The proof is this:
By syllogism (a ⊰ b) ⊰ (b ⊰ c) ⊰ a ⊰ c
Transposing [del]that[/del] [ins]a ⊰[/ins] (b ⊰ c) ⊰ (a ⊰ b) ⊰ c
By contraposition a ⊰ [(a ⊰ b) ⊰ c] ⊰ (b ⊰ c)
Transposing [(a ⊰ b) ⊰ c] ⊰ a ⊰ (b ⊰ c) i.e. (a̅ + b)⋅c̅ ⊰ [a̅ + b⋅c̅]
by contraposition [a ⊰ (b ⊰ c)] ⊰ [(a ⊰ b) ⊰ c] i.e a⋅(b̅ + c) ⊰ [a⋅b̅ + c]
