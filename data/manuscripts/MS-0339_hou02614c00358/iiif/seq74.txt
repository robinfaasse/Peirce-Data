Let me consider a little about the nature
of truth. [u]             [/u]
First. I notice that if we define an image to
be a representation completely determined in content
so that in it every attribute is affirmed or denied
there is probably no image. And is not this what
is requisite to make an image? What is an image?
There is a good question for dialectical research.
