A. Signs are divisible according to their matter.
1. There are signs each of which has all its significance in its abstract quality, regardless
of the circumstances of its occurrence. Such a sign may be termed a [u]Qualisign[/u].

cance lies in the circumstances of that one occurrence. Such a sign may be called a
Sinsign. But [del]that[/del] it will be allowable to use this term a little loosely for a sign
