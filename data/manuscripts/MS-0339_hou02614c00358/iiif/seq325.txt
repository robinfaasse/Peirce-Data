   If we are to rely upon the affinities and sympathies between
different men to lead us to a natural classification of the
sciences, then we must acknowledge that the essence of
science does not lie in its truth, but in its striving
after truth, and in the breadth and generality of the
truth striven for. Pure science is the striving for [del]after[/del]
broad truth for its own sake; but in its beginning science
is not pure, but is applied science, or art.
   History carries us back before the beginnings of pure
science; but what the first steps in science may
have been we can only conjecture. No better basis for
conjecture can be had as to the remote development of the
race than the history of the development of the individual.
