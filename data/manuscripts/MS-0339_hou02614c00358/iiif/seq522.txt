the sign; and this is well thought. I have thought of the Interpretant as
that which the Sign determines or might determine, or should determine;

As to the Matter of the Sign itself, it is either a [u]Tuone[/u] or a [u]Token[/u] or a [u]Type[/u].

like a tune. But the latter is not [u]pure[/u] feeling. By a [u]Token[/u], I mean
an existing thing or an actual historical event, which serves as a Sign.
