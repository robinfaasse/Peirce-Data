What is true of a pair or larger number of entia is termed a relation. [del]That is true of a pair of entia[/del] A relation which may hold between a pair of entia I shall term a [u]simple relation[/u], but I shall often omit the qualifying adjective, because, [del]as will be seen[/del] for reasons which will soon appear, I prefer to call other relations by another name. [del]And[/del] The pair of entia are called the [u]terms[/u] of the relation. Each term is said to be related to the other. [del]Either term is called the relate[/del] An ens as related to another is called a [u]relate[/u] and that other is its [u]correlate[/u]. The relation has two names according to which term is called the relate; these two aspects of the relation are sometimes considered as two relations bound to together, and each is called the [u]converse[/u] of the other.

The second law of logic is called the law [del]of[/del] [ins]of[/ins] [u]relativity[/u]; and it is, that each quality exists only by virtue of a relation between its subject and something else. A comprehension of the reason of this fact can only be gained later
