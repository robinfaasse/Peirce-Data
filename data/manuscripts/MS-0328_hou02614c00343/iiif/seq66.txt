It contains delicious reflections. He says the more
civilized the nations are, the more the children
resemble their fathers. with the savages they are 
distinct individuals and little like their parents. 
I like this because it is exactly what I have 
observed myself. with the common and rude 
people, there is no family likeness. they are only 
animal souls and each individual is as separate 
and distinct from his parents as one hog from 
another. but in a refined and cultivated 
family, there is a likeness of soul, an intellectual 
resemblance which spreads through it, because 
they are raised from the animal sphere to 
the human, and the human souls being 
unfolded, are according to the law of the 
transmission of souls, as like as their countenances.
