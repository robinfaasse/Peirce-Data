It is very generally and rightly held that the attribution of a work to any given author by the best manuscripts creates a prima facie presumption of its truth, and a pretty strong presumption in comparison with [del]ex-[/del] ordinary such arguments to the contrary as can ordinarily be adduced. Now, if I am rightly informed, all the M.SS., without exception, attribute those theological writings to Boethius. Yet his authorship of them is denied by most critics simply because their theological metaphysics is distinctly Christian, while his De Consolatione Philosophiae written in the last year of his life exhibits no religious faith of any definite kind.
