[pn]13[/pn] to keep accounts. [del]As[/del] The [del]usual[/del] designation of the author
of this treatise [del]was[/del] "the Chorasmian", in Arabic Al-Kharismi, which
in European books written mostly in Latin became "Algorismus"; and
from that form was derived the much better old English name
for the art of Chorasmian art of computation "augrim". This is
a much better name for the art than is "arithmetic", because
"arithmetic" is a Greek word, the meaning of which was
well-established a thousand years before the art of Cho-
rasmian arithmetic became publicly known in Europe,
and, derived as it was, could mean be the name of no
practical art, but only of the purely theoretical science of
numbers, which is its meaning still in the mouths of
mathematicians.
