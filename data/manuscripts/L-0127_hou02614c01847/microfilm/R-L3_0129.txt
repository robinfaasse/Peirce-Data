Harvard University,
Cambridge, October 4th, 1895.

Dear Mr. Peirce,-

The newspaper is not correct in representing that our students are on half rations of Philosophy this year. We are doing as much for them as usual, except that we have lost the presence of a very interesting man, Professor Munsterberg. He is still serving us, but it is from Germany.

When I was in Egypt last February I received a suggestion that you be asked to lecture at Cambridge during the present year. I was told that I must answer the suggestion by cable; so that it was quite impossible for me to discuss the question with you, or indeed with anybody.

Very truly yours,
Charles W. Eliot

Mr. C. S. Peirce.
