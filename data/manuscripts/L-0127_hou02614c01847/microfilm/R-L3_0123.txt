**HARVARD COLLEGE,**
**CAMBRIDGE, MASS.,**
[date]23 May 1872[/date].

My dear Sir,

You were appoint-
ed an assistant in the Observ-
atory Oct. 30 1869 by votes of
the Corporation & Overseers in the
usual manner. The Corporation
feels that on the whole the
interests of the Observatory are
best served by continuing your
appointment and your salary.
