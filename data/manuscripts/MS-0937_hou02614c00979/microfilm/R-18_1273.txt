In short, this hypothesis falls either into the Scylla
of absurdity or into the Charybdis of unverifiable
metaphysics. Besides, its application to the action
of matter upon mind in sensation is unsatisfactory.
For if the energy of the mind is exhausted in volition,
sensation ought to share with nutrition the function
of restoring it; but this we do not find to be the case.

physicists who have examined into the question of
an interchange of energy between mind and
matter have come to the conclusion that no such
phenomenon takes place. Hence the suggestion is
