[u]A The Connection between Mind and Matter.[/u]

[u]S[/u]everal different theories have been urged during the past months to account for the mutual action between mind and matter in volition and in sensation. Little positive evidence has been gathered as yet for any of them; yet three or four hint at some possible eventual solution of this famous, deeply interesting, and dark problem.
