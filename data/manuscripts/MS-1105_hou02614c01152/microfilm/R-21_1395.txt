§4 Second Condition

The meaning in a creation must become notice-
able. This can only be through language.

Language is un abstraction not capable of realization
alone, but combined (in a way of which we shall
think directly) with other abstractions gives them realiza-
bility. Geometrical figures, letters, conversation, music
are such languages. We seem to see their analogues
in Vegetables, Animals, Chemical Compounds, Nebular Sys-
tems &c.
