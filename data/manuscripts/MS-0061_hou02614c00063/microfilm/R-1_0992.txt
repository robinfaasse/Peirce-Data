Let $a$ be an approximate value of $x$ and put $x = a + y$
Then $(y+a)^{r} = y^{r} + r a y^{r-1} + \frac{r(r-1)}{2} a^{2} y^{r-2} + \text{etc} + a^{r} = A$
and whatever be the value of $m$
