“every proposition would be true if it were”
By “not hard” we mean “every proposi-
tion would be true if it were hard”. So to say
that a stone is at once hard and not hard
is to say that if it is hard every proposition
is true, and it is hard. Accordingly this would
be to assert that every proposition is true, —
a super-Hegelian position, — that directly
denies the distinction of truth & falsity,
which we are fully satisfied, exists.
