The Gallas are among the most intellectual of the Negro races. The
stem of the verb in their language can be used as a substantive. It is one
of the few languages which has gender. Its most remarkable feature is
"its prolific production of causative and middle verbal stems." Its nomi-
native and instrumental cases are alike, both ending in [u]-n[/u] or [u]-ni[/u].

In the Basque, the stem serves as subject for an intransitive verb,
