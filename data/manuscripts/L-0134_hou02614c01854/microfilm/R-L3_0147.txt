DEAR SIR:

I have the honor of sending you herewith the first part of a new memoir by me on the _Algebra of Logic_. The manuscript left my hands in April last, before I had seen several important publications,—Mr. McColl's third paper, Prof. Wundt's _Logik_, etc. The following notes and corrections should be inserted before reading the article:—

[ins]✓[/ins] _Page_ 26. Last line but 1, for [del]M[/del] [ins]M̄[/ins] read P.
[ins]✗[/ins] _Page_ 28. An oversight has here been committed. For from [del]A = (A < x)[/del] [ins]Ā = (A < x)[/ins] follows not merely (16) but also (19), (20), and (21), and thus all the properties of the negative which concern syllogistic. But this does not affect the view taken of the subject, nor the division of the moods according to the properties of the negative on which they depend; for whatever is shown in the text to be deducible from [del]A = (A < x)[/del] [ins]Ā = (A < x)[/ins] is in fact deducible from (16).
[ins]☡[/ins] _Page_ 30. Add the formula (3') [del]{(x < y) < z} = {(x < z) < y}[/del] [ins]{(x < y) < z} = {(x < z) < y}[/ins].
[ins]☡[/ins] _Page_ 33. Remark that the proofs of the lettered propositions follow the enunciations.
