But since many may prefer to skip the description of this system, I will facilitate their doing so by [del]prefixing[/del] [ins]placing[/ins] the mark ¶ before each paragraph of the description, and also at the end of it.

¶ In Existential Graphs, any part of an assertion which is not [ins]entirely[/ins] expressible in a way peculiar to the system, and which, at the same time, need not have its meaning analyzed, is simply written in any convenient language, while in [del]most cases[/del] some part of any assertion that [del]requires expression[/del] is expressed by a diagram which is drawn, not written. On account of this feature of the system, it is desirable in the description of the system to have a word that shall [del]mean[/del] "to write or to draw, as the case may be." The word "scribe" is taken as a verb to express that.

¶ It is requisite, in this description of Existential Graphs, to explain what is meant by a "Graph" and what by a "Graph-Instance" or "Instance of a Graph." The distinction between these two expressions is [ins]almost[/ins] precisely analogous to two current meanings of the word "word." In one of these senses it may be said that the word "the" [del]will[/del] occur on the average, over twenty times on an ordinary English printed page.
