My Dear Sir

May I beg your good offices in
spurring up our good, but slow, friend Hein, to
send me funds promptly. My [del]del[/del] accts. for about
$3000 left here Wednesday July 31st, in due course of mail were
in Wash. on the succeeding Monday Aug. 5th. With them went
a letter specifying the days when I must have funds
throughout August. The contractor for grading the base
has some 60 hands at work. They have to be paid
on Saturdays, or they will quit, and our work suffer by
delay. The contractor looks to me every Friday for
means to pay them, & I must pay from $300 to
$500 each week, according to amount of work done.
