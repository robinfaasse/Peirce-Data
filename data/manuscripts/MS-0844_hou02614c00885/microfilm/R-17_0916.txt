Now substitute in this general statement for "tendency to act in [del]some[/del] [ins]one[/ins] way" a tendency to take habits, and we see that that tendency would grow. It would also become differentiated in various ways. But there are some habits that carried beyond a certain point eliminate their subjects from the universe. There are many ways in which this may happen. Thus a tendency to lose mass will end in a total loss of mass. A tendency to lose energy will end in removing its subject from perceptible [del]existence[/del]. A tendency to gain energy will end in the body's shooting through the universe too rapidly to produce any effect, etc.
