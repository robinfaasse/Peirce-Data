Two terms may be related to each other in three different ways either
in extension, comprehension or implication; viz.

1. The extension, comprehension or implication of one may include
the whole of that of the other & something more. This gives the relation of
subordination & superordination.

2. The two quantities may have a part & only part in common. This is
intersection.

3. The two quantities may exclude each other. This is called exclusion.
