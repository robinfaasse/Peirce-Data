[del]2[sup]nd[/sup][/del] [ins]to[/ins] 2[sup]nd[/sup]. Every symbol may be regarded as belonging to its object, just as

3[sup]rd[/sup] Every symbol may be regarded as a store-room of knowledge, &

Logic is the science of the relation of symbols in general to their
objects.

a <u>proposition</u>. If it is intended as a ground of acquiring knowledge, it

a relation to an object, terms and propositions as well as arguments
