29[/pn] Before we go on to further conventions, let us try to what
the three already adopted can lead. The numbered figures
below are supposed to be all scribed on the [u]recto[/u], and there
must be a tacit understanding as to the composition of the
universe of discourse to which everything scribed on the recto refers.
By a tacit understanding, I mean that neither graphist nor inter-
preter are supposed actually to reflect that everything [del]denoted[/del] [ins]signified[/ins]
by graph-instances on the recto belongs to such and such
[fig]solid black circle[/fig] a domain. For so to think would be to think of that
domain as limited and thus, however indefinitely, to make
reference to what lies without it. What I mean, on the
contrary, is that their thoughts are so [del]absorbed[/del] [ins]immersed[/ins]
for the time being in the domain in question that [del]it does not occur[/del]
