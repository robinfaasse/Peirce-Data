[pn]43[/pn] the cut. This reversal shall be made whenever a cut is made, [del]and the area of the reversal so exposed shall be termed the area of the[/del] cut, while the exposed surface outside the cut shall be termed the place of the cut. No two cuts must intersect each other, and if a cut intersects itself, it is to be called a scroll and [ins]and treated[/ins] is to be conceived as consisting of two cuts one within the other.
