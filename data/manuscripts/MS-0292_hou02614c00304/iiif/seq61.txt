This shows [del]that[/del] there are five natural classes of forms of graphs;
namely, medads, monads, dyads, triads and higher perissids (odd-

reason why no chemical element has a minimum valency
greater than 4. In classifications generally, it may fairly
be said to be established, if it ever was doubted, that Form,
in the sense of structure is of far higher significance than Material.
Valency is the basis of all external structure; and where indecomposa-
bility precludes internal structure, — as in the classification of
elementary concepts, [ins]valency[/ins] ought to be made the first consideration.
