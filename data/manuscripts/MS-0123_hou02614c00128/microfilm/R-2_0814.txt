[del]and[/del] the needle point and the [ins]straight[/ins] line on the
ground, a geometrical plane could pass, and
this plane would cut the plane of the glass in
a straight line. But how about the horizon
line? Would this be represented by a
straight line or not? Are we quite sure there
would be any definite horizon line? These
are questions which can only be answered
by experience, direct or indirect; and our
existing experience is not sufficient for
the purpose. The old Euclidean geometry,
indeed, answered them both affirmatively.
Even Lobachevski so answers the second.
But geometers are now agreed that there is
nothing to show that this is so.
