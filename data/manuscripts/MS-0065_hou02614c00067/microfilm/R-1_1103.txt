The Binary Numerical Notation.

In this system every rational number is expressed by a hori-
zontal row in which characters of 2 types follow one another in
a determinate order. In the special form of the notation here to
be treated of, the 2 types of characters are y or Y, signifying
the presence, and n or N, signifying the absence [del]of that[/del] in the
number, considered as a sum [del]of different powers of 2,[/del] of that
power of 2 [ins]to[/ins] which the place of the character in the horizontal
row representing the number causes it to refer. Namely, the
capitals Y and N refer to the zero power of 2, and one or other
must be written in the row expressing the number. Every other
character refers to the power of 2 next higher than the power of
[del]the[/del] referred to by the character next to the right of it in the
row, or next lower than the power of 2 referred to by the charac-
ter next to the left of it in the row.
