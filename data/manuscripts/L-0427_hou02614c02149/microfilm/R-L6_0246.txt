I venture to hope that
you may find time to write
out & publish the interesting
theory of the connection of
mind & body which you indi-
cate. The suggestion is so
new to me & your account
of it so brief, that I am
scarcely able to estimate
whether it would effect a
junction at any point with
my theory. But I hope to
hear more of it.

Should you care to
send me any criticisms of
the views expressed in my
book, they would be most
thankfully received. Letters
sent to Lakewood, New
Jersey, will be forwarded
to me.

Believe me, with re-
newed thanks,

Very sincerely yours,
C. A. Strong.
