But you will see that this method insists at once upon Subgenera. For in the first place, Secondness has two grades. Looking at the Firstness, or internal quality, of the secondness, it is Genuine in real interaction, and Degenerate in mere contrastal qualities; and looking at Secondness in its Secondness, or the relation of relate and correlate, it is Genuine in Relations of one thing to another thing, and Degenerate in such relations as "Being something sweet that is at the same time —", where relate and correlate are [del]the[/del] identical. Consequently there [del]are[/del] [ins]may be[/ins] four [del]kinds of[/del] [ins]possible[/ins] Sinsigns, of Indices, and of Dicisigns, as well as four [del]th[/del] modes of division into Icons, Indices, and Symbols. As for thirdness, it is in three ways of three grades. You will perceive that this method forces me to examine the whole ground with the utmost attention; and hence when I come to general propositions I have some ground for hope that nothing essential has been overlooked.

That which distinguishes signs is their [u]triadic relationships[/u]. All triadic relationships that are not reducible to dyadic relationships involve intellectual elements. The main thing is the reference to an [u]interpretant[/u]. As determining an interpretant every symbol in some sense has a power to determine physical results. The ultimate purport of the symbol lies in the effectual laws which it determines.
