Logic I make to be semeiotics, the doctrine of the necessary conditions
of signs. It has three parts; ‘speculative grammar,’ which describes the
possible genera of signs and analyzes their nature; ‘critic’, which
considers the conditions under which signs can refer to their objects,
and methodeutic which considers the necessary conditions of signs
fulfilling their purposes. In this last part I make some use of facts of
history, facts about the mind that are, some of them, discoveries of psycho-
logy, etc. But I use these only as checks upon possible blunders that
I may have committed.
