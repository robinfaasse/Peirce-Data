II.

The theorem which ought to be taken first, as
being the simplest is obtained by asking whether
a number is as great as itself. In the definition
of $g$
$$g_{il} = \Pi_{\alpha} (\bar{q}_{\alpha i} + \bar{h}_{\alpha} + q_{\alpha l})$$
substitute $i$ for $l$ and we have $\bar{q}_{\alpha i} + q_{\alpha i}$
which is necessarily true. Hence
$$\Pi_{i} g_{ii}$$
or everything is as great as itself.

These two theorems do not involve any of the
properties of numbers, but only the definition
of $g$.
