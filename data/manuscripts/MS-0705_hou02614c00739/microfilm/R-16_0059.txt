By a 'postulate', Dr. Huntington seems to understand any
one of a body of propositions such that nothing can be
deduced from one that can [ins]be[/ins] equally be deduced from
another, while from them all, every proposition of a given
mathematical theory or branch of mathematics might be
deduced. The utility of such a body of premisses for the
logical analysis of the branch of mathematics in question
is beyond dispute. But I think we ought to distinguish
between postulates and definitions. As for axioms, or
propositions already well-known to the student who
takes up the branch of mathematics in question, the
