For my part, when I can get a little money,
though it will come too late to avail my own
studies, I will use it to revive the Pythagorean
brotherhood, based upon celibacy, community
of goods, and obedience, for the furtherance
of science; and in one little century my
followers shall be masters of the world. I
am sorry for the fate that awaits the catholic
church. I wish I could confer upon it [del]our[/del] [ins]its[/ins]
powers, because its ambition is laudable. But
unfortunately it was bound over by its early
fathers to the service of Ignorance, and though
the sun of science, love, and life, that
has made it so great, yet [gap] has long
been sold to Death, and cannot be redeemed.
