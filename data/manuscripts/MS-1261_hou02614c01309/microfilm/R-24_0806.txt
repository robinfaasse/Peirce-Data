25. [u]Rubbing from Shakespeare's Tomb.[/u] Size, 10 inches by 30 inches.
Made specially for the Art Collectors Club by placing a sheet over the tombstone and rubbing it with
black crayon, so as to bring out the following inscription:
Good frend for Iesvs sake forbeare,
to digg the dvst encloased heare:
Bleste be ye man yt spares thes stones
and cvrst be he yt moves my bones.
It makes a very unusual picture, specially fitting to hang over a library mantel.
