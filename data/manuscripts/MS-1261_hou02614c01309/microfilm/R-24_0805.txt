[u]COPIES OF FAMOUS PAINTINGS[/u]
(Arranged in Six Groups)

THIS SERIES of famous pictures is the result of an effort on the part of the [u]Art Collectors Club[/u] to secure for its members the "best things" in pictures, which have ever been offered in this country. We are satisfied that we shall have the "best things," but the expense involved has been wholly out of proportion to the price which many of our members and customers can afford to pay, and for this reason the Club cannot again undertake to reproduce masterpieces in anything like so expensive a way.

The [u]Art Collectors Club[/u] sent its agents to Europe during July and August of last year to visit the most famous galleries and secure new copies of the very best paintings. A great many difficulties had to be overcome, and a large sum of money expended, but the venture, so far as the pictures themselves are concerned, is already an assured success, and we deliver to the members and customers who are fortunate enough to be among the 4000 purchasers, the finest art collection ever produced in this country.

The pictures are arranged in groups. Any one group should make a superb wedding or birthday gift. There are twenty-five pictures in all, and each will be mounted on neutral tint board ready for framing, or for decorative use, unframed. Eleven of the pictures are twice the size of the [u]Murillotint[/u] copies issued last April, and for which the Club has received more than one thousand testimonial letters. All the pictures are being reproduced by our improved [u]Murillotint[/u] process, which has been pronounced equal in effect to the most expensive reproduction work of Paris houses.

The Pictures

NOTE.—The prices attached to each group are printed simply to give members some idea of the value which the Club places upon the pictures. Separate groups are not for sale at any price. Complete sets will not be broken.
