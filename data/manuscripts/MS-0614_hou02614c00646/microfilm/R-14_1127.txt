Everybody knows that people are able to exercise, some consi-
derable control over their habits; and some day I ought to endea-
vour to give you some aid in tracing out the [u]modus operandi[/u] of
that control somewhat minutely. At present, it comes in my
way to direct your attention to certain features of the process only.
In doing so, I shall not meddle with the science of psychology;
and this is a remark that I shall often have occasion to repeat.
