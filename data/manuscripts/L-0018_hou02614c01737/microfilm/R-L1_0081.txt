General Offices,

[date]July 29th, 1891.[/date]

[[Dictated.]]
Dear Sir:-

Mr. George Henderson has requested me to acknowledge the receipt of your favor of July 23rd, regarding your election to membership in the American Society for the Extension of University Teaching. We are pleased to know that you are interested in the movement, and should have been gratified if you had felt as though you could accept fully. We are pushing the movement as fast as possible and are meeting with good success. Some of the Local Centres that we established last year are trying to double their work this year. Everything points towards a prosperous year if we can secure the funds for pushing the work. I think, however, th that this will be forthcoming, as we have a great many friends and they are very generous.

It may be interesting to you to know that the Society is now publishing a journal, devoted to the interests of University Extension. The subscription price is $.500 per year.

If at any time you care to have any information upon this subject, I will gladly furnish it.

Very truly yours,
Willis Boughton

To Hon. C. H. Peirce.
