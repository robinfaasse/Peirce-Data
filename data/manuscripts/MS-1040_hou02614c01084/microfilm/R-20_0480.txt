\end{array}$$

The At. Wts are taken from a list dated [date]1903[/date]
with corrections upon which I have made sundry correc-
tions with very little knowledge of the grounds of them.

As for the probable errors, it is much less important
that they should be absolutely than that they should be
[del]relatively[/del] correct. I have assumed that the probable
error equals a unit in the last place given in either
column of the International table for [date]1904[/date], although I
believe they are really less than half that quantity.
But instead of writing them I have written them as square roots
and have multiplied these square roots by 100. Thus $\sqrt{1}$
means that the probable error is 0.01, $\pm \sqrt{100}$ means that
it is 0.1, and $\pm \sqrt{0.01}$ means that it is 0.001.
