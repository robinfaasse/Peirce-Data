By Information I [del]mean[/del] [ins]all[/ins] that knowledge that we collect from the experience of ourselves or of others. Yet this is not a correct statement, since all our knowledge of whatsoever kind is derived from experience. To be sure Kant endeavours to draw a distinction between knowledge drawn from experience and knowledge that begins in experience.
