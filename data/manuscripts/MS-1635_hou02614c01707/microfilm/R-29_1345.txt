Gentleman Slow? I should think
so. He has a theory of connubial in-
tercourse which is certainly innocent.
He thinks the spiritual union must be
complete before the carnal union takes
place. He therefore says that the first
night he shall kiss his wife upon the
cheek; the 2[ins]nd[/ins] night he shall kiss her lips;
the 3[ins]rd[/ins] he shall feel of her left bub & so pro-
ceed gradually. He says he is endeavoring
to propagate this new doctrine; that he
has already made one convert - a young
lady with whom he discussed the subject
for 3 hours in the dark the other night.
