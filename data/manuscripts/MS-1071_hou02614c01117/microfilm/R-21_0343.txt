$$\frac{d\theta}{\rho} = \frac{\sqrt{(dr)^2 + (dz^2)}}{r}$$

Suppose $r^2 + z^2 = R^2$

$$r dr + [del]z dz[/del] [ins]z dz[/ins] = 0 \qquad \frac{dz}{dr} = -\frac{r}{z}$$

$$r^2 (dr)^2 = z^2 (dz)^2$$

$$(dz)^2 = \frac{r^2}{z^2} (dr)^2$$

$$\sqrt{(dr)^2 + (dz)^2}$$

$$= dr \sqrt{1 + \frac{r^2}{z^2}}$$

$$= dr \sqrt{1 + \frac{r^2}{R^2 - r^2}}$$

$$\frac{\sqrt{(dr)^2 + dz^2}}{r}$$

$$= dr \sqrt{\frac{1}{r^2} + \frac{1}{R^2 - r^2}}$$

$$= dr \sqrt{\frac{R^2}{r^2(R^2 - r^2)}}$$

$$= dr \frac{R}{r\sqrt{R^2 - r^2}}$$
