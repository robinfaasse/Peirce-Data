I like and esteem the man
who knows when to resign a
game of chess, and does not
insist upon protracting it
in tedious and melancholy
mate. I like and esteem the
man who gives Death a
cordial shake of the hand
when the time comes, and
having fought a good fight,
does not finish it with a
feeble, frittering, factious,
fretful, futility.
