230. It is required to inscribe the greatest trapezoid in
a given segment of a circle.

<u>Solution</u> Let the radius be unity, the arc of the segment
$2\omega$. The chord which is placed on base of the trapezoid is
$2\sin\omega$. The other which is parallel to this [del]maybe[/del] is the
chord of an arc [del]which, being[/del] represented by $2\omega'$, gives $2\sin\omega'$
for this base. The altitude is $\cos\omega' - \cos\omega$.
and the surface = $(\sin\omega + \sin\omega')(\cos\omega' - \cos\omega)$.
this, differentiated and equated to zero gives.
$-(\sin\omega + \sin\omega')\sin\omega' + (\cos\omega' - \cos\omega)\cos\omega' = 0$
or. $\cos 2\omega' - \cos(\omega' - \omega) = 0$
$\omega - \omega' = 2\omega'$ $\omega' = \frac{1}{3}\omega$.

233. In a triangle, having given the base, the ratio
of the sides and one of the angles adjacent to the base,
double the vertical [del]tri[/del] angle; to determine the triangle

<u>Solution</u>. Let the base of the triangle be $b$. the
ratio of the sides $\frac{c}{d}$. and the vertical angle $\omega$
$\frac{\sin 3\omega}{\sin 2\omega} = \frac{c}{d}$. $\frac{3\cos^2\omega - \sin^2\omega}{2\cos\omega} = \frac{4\cos^2\omega - 1}{2\cos\omega}$
$4\cos^2\omega - \frac{2c}{d}\cos\omega = 1$.
$\cos\omega = \frac{c}{4d} \pm \sqrt{\frac{1}{4} + (\frac{c}{4d})^2}$.
Let $\frac{c}{2d} = \cot\alpha$
$\cos\omega = \frac{1}{2}\cot\alpha \pm \frac{1}{2}\csc\alpha = \frac{\cos\alpha \pm 1}{2\sin\alpha} = -\tan\frac{1}{2}\alpha\text{ or }\cot\frac{1}{2}\alpha$
