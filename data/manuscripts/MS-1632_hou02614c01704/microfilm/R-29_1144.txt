2. Divide 140 into two such parts that the
greater being divided by the less and the less by the
greater, and the less quotient being multiplied by 9
and the greater by 42, the two products shall be
equal.

Solution. Let $x$ be the greater and $y$ the less.
By the second condition of the problem [del]$\frac{9x}{2y} = \frac{8y}{x}$[/del] [ins]$\frac{9x}{y} = \frac{16y}{x}$[/ins]
Getting rid of fractions. $9x^2 = 16y^2$
Extracting the square root $3x = \pm 4y$ (1)
By the first condition $x + y = 140$ (2)
Multiplying by 3 and substituting $3x$ its value from (1) $\pm 4y + 3y = 420$
Reducing $7y = 420$ or $-y = 420$
$y = 60$ or $y = -420$
From (2) $x = 140 - y$. $x = 140 - 60 = 80$ or $x = 140 + 420 = 560$.
