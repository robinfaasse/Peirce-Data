[pn]239[/pn]. Required the content of the [del]least[/del] sphere that
shall touch three paraboloids given in magnitude
and position.

<u>Solution</u>. Let the equation of one the revolving
parabolas be $y^2 = px$. Let $R$ be the radius of the sphere.
Suppose this parabola to be enclosed by a curve
at distance $R$ from it. the equation of this curve will be
found from the three $y^2 = px$
$(y - y')^2 + (x - x')^2 = R^2$
$y - y' : x - x' = y : \frac{1}{2}p$ by eliminating $y$
and $x$. to be. $\phi(y'.x'.R) = 0$
If this curve is supposed to revolve round the same axis
with the parabola, the resulting surface will contain
the center of the sphere. as we get shall get an
equation of the form $\phi(a.b.c.R) = 0$.
$a, b, c$ being the coordinates of the centre of the sphere.
the same is true of the other paraboloids. We may then
eliminate from these three equations $b.c$ and obtain
$f(a.R) = 0$. the differential of which
gives, by making $\frac{dR}{da} = 0$. $f'(a.R) = 0$. and eliminating
$a$ from these we obtain $R$.
