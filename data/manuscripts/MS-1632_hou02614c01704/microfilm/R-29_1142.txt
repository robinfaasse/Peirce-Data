1. How many words can be made with five letters of the alphabet in each word, there being 26 letters in all, and 6 vowels, admitting that a number of [del]words[/del] consonants alone will not make a word?

Solution. The number of words that can be made with 26 letters and 5 in each word is
$\frac{26.27.28.29.30}{1.2.3.4.5} = 142506$

$\begin{array}{r} 26 \\ \underline{27} \\ 182 \\ \underline{52} \\ 702 \\ \underline{28} \\ 5616 \\ \underline{1404} \\ 19656 \\ \underline{29} \\ 176904 \\ \underline{39312} \\ 570024 \\ \underline{30} \\ \hline \end{array}$

$\begin{array}{r} 2 \\ \underline{3} \\ 6 \\ \underline{4} \\ 24 \\ \underline{5} \\ \hline 120 \end{array}$ | $\begin{array}{r}17100720 \\ \hline 142506\end{array}$

$\begin{array}{r} 24 \\ \underline{23} \\ 72 \\ \underline{48} \\ 552 \\ \underline{22} \\ 1104 \\ \underline{1104} \\ \hline \end{array}$
