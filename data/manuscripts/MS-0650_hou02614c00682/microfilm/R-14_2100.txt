pendent of him, if such there be and we believe
there is, and cannot help believing it, is distinct
from a Feeling. For a Man's having a Feeling is
not, in itself, inconsistent with his being the only
actually [ins]and independently[/ins] existing thing in the universe, since the Feeling is
not independent of him [del]and nothing else is involved in[/del]
[del]Feeling,[/del] [ins]and besides a Feeling is not an individual object but only a kind, altho'[/ins] there is the special capacity for that special feeling;

place between two distinct Existents. But you may ask, what
