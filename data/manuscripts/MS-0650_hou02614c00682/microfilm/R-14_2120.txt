[u]Exposition of the System of[/u]
[u]Existential Graphs.[/u]

Although this system is, I believe, the very simplest of all possible ways of expressing every assertion, [del]with absolute exact[/del] yet, owing to the extreme exactitude that will be requisite in this Exposition, I find it convenient to employ [ins]in it[/ins] nearly two hundred abridged expressions; and though each of these will be clearly explained [ins]on its[/ins] [del]the[/del] first occurrence, yet I am sure that an alphabetical glossary of them will be welcomed. Indeed, I myself sometimes refer to it, to remind me of the microscopically exact meaning of some expression, although I have constantly been employing the system for nearly fourteen years (i.e. since Jan. 1897)
