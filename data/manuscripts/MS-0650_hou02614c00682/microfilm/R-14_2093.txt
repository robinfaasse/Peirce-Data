I am now going to introduce another bit of nomen-
clature. Before I had the concept of a cut, I had
that of two cuts, which I draw at one continuous
movement as shown in Fig. This
I called a scroll, and the areas of the outer and inner

I shall use these two expressions in enunciating
[del]two more[/del] [ins]another[/ins] permission[del]s[/del] [del]of mode[/del] to modify graphs.
[del]Firstly,[/del] [ins]Namely,[/ins] it is permitted to you to draw a scroll on any evenly-enclosed
area, without its having any graph in either close. It is not permissible
to draw a single cut on such an area, unless a false graph be at once
enclosed in it, or else one [del]the[/del] be at once inserted in it by iteration ac-
cording to the rule. You will be able to see for yourself why [del]an[/del] [ins]an empty[/ins] [del]scroll[/del]

inner close is vacant can be removed.
