[u]Brief Exposition of the System of Existential Graphs.[/u]

I mean to make this statement as simple and intelligible as I can, without aiming to make it absolutely accurate. I prefer to correct any minute inaccuracies in a second more formal statement.

You are to take a piece of paper and call one side of it "the sheet of assertion," for that is the name by which I shall call it. Perhaps I should have done better to call it the sheet of [del]affirmation[/del] [ins]assertion[/ins], since whatever state of things you represent on this [del]pa she[/del] page, you will be understood to affirm as existing somewhere, or, at least, consistently to make believe to affirm. You may use any shapes that can be drawn or written or partly drawn and partly written, provided they be not among those to which fixed meanings are attached in this exposition, and may give them such significations as you like. I would suggest the astronomical signs for [del]your[/del] [ins]use,[/ins] [del]scher[/del] they are easily written and will not have any fixed meanings in the system.
