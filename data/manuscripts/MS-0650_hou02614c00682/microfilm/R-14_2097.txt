The simplest form of consciousness is Feeling. Apparently, we are always, except perhaps in deep sleep, in a state of feeling, which is as much as to say that we have a Feeling. Every Feeling is, in itself, absolutely simple. To say that it is compound means that it [del]would[/del] [ins]is or[/ins] could be produced by concurrent circumstances, each of which would produce a Feeling.
