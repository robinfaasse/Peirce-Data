[u]Specimens of bad definitions in the Century[/u]
[u]Dictionary[/u].

[u]plight[/u]¹ . 1. Doubtful whether this is not [u]plight[/u]² (plite).
Even if it be this word, the precise meaning is doubtful.

2. A plight is not a promise, as stated. It is the
staking of the reputation for truth-telling (troth) upon
the [del]execution[/del] [ins]performance[/ins] of a promise.

[u]plight[/u]² (Better spelled [u]plite[/u].) The definition is too wide.
It is the temporary state of an animated being as
to bodily welfare. [A man in love is said to be in
a bad plite only if it affects his health.]

[u]plight[/u]³ v.t. 1. Precise meaning doubtful.
2. A mere metaphor.

[u]Plinian[/u] I a. The definition ought to say [u]Pliny the Elder[/u].

[u]plinth[/u] Awkward, sprawling definition. No need of the restriction
of Roman and renaissance.

[u]plinthoid[/u] Too vague.
