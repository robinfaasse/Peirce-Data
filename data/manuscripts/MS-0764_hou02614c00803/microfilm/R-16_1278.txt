Given any algebraic equation. Di-
vide it by its last term and transpose
all the terms containing $x$. Regard the
exponents as subscripts; so that
$$ax^n + bx^{n-1} + \text{etc} = 1$$
becomes
$$ax_n + bx_{n-1} + \text{etc} = x_0$$
Assume any values for the terms with posi-
tive subscripts. (But I make them all $1$ and $x_0 = 1$)
