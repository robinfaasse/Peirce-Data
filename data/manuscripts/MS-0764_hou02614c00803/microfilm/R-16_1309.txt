<u>The Syllogism</u>
No long lived things have gall
Man, the horse, the mule are long lived
[del]∴[/del] Man, the horse, the mule must be long lived

<u>The Induction</u>
Man, the horse, the mule are [ins][a sample of] things[/ins] long lived
Man, the horse, the mule have no bile
So whatever is long lived is without bile

This is Adduction, though only Crude
Adduction. There is also the germ of a
penetrating truth in the relation which
Aristotle finds between the Syllogism
and the Induction, although in the bald
way in which he states it, there seems
to be little or no reason for asserting any
particular relation between the two.
