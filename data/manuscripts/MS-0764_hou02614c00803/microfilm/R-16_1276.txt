The Rules for it can be made out by
comparing it with the Quantitative
genus. If of a class of things, a known
percentage, $p$, has a certain character, $P$,
and a sample is taken by a process by
which at each drawing of an individual unit for the same, there is the same
probability, $p$, of adding to the sample one
of the $P$ kind, and if moreover whether
the other constituents of the sample are
of the $P$ kind or not, it remains true for
each drawing that the probability is $p$
that the unit added to the sample will be
$P$-kind, then of course we can calculate
the probability that the sample will
not have more than $N_1$ of the $P$-kind
or [del]so on that[/del] [ins]that[/ins] it will not contain less
than $N_2$ of the $P$-kind. There is no sound
objection to using the $\Theta$ integral for this
purpose. Those two probabilities will be
apodictically deduced. Suppose, then, that
the sample is drawn in that same way,
but when the probability, $p$, is unknown,
and it is found on examination that the
sample does have [del]more[/del] [ins]not more[/ins] than $N_1$ [ins]and does[/ins]
have [del]less[/del] [ins]not less[/ins] than $N_2$. One might hastily be
