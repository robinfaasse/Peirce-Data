[u]logarithm[/u]

An artificial number, or number used in computation, belonging to a series having the following properties: First, every [del]ever[/del] [ins]natural[/ins] or ordinary, number has a logarithm or artificial number, and conversely, every logarithm belongs to a natural number; second, as the natural numbers increase geometrically, their logarithms increase arithmetically. This is shown in the following table of two kinds of logarithms of a [ins]geometrical[/ins] series of numbers.

It is seen that each successive vulgar logarithm of the series is greater by 1 than the one above it, and each successive hyperbolic logarithm exceeds that above it by 2.3025851.
