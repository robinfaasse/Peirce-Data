[u]Chemistry. - The Elements[/u]

From the point of view of the valency of the atoms and their modes of combination, the elements are classified as shown in the following tabular array, due to Mendeleeff. These are eight "groups" forming the successive pairs of columns of the table, each of which columns considered as a [del]class[/del] [ins]sub-group[/ins] of elements, will here be termed a [u]file[/u]; while crossing these groups are twelve "series" of which every even-numbered one to'with the odd-numbered one following it will here be termed a "rank," the 2[ins]nd[/ins] and 3[ins]rd[/ins] series constituting the first rank, etc.
