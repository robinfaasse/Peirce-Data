There seem to be no regular basic compounds. A subchloride
of K probably exists but not as a well-defined body.

There are peroxides and persulphides. The principal oxide
of potassium is $K_2O_4$, and $K_2O_2$ and $K_2O_3$ seem to exist. It
is usually said that there are five sulphides, $K_2S$ forming brown
deliquescent crystals, $K_2S_2$ reddish yellow deliquescent crystals, $K_2S_3$
yellowish brown, $K_2S_4$ redbrown, and $K_2S_5$ red. But all these
are very unstable. There [del]are[/del] [ins]is[/ins] also $KI_3$ crystallizing in dark blue
