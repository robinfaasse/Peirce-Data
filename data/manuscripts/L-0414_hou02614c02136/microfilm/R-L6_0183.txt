64 Avenue Road
Regents Park
London, N.W.
March 5, 1894

Dear Sir,

My health is now so bad that I can do scarcely any work, and cannot read any amount worth mentioning. What little I can do I can do only by a rigorous exclusion of all distractions. For this reason you must excuse me if I do not give the requested attention to the documents you have sent.

I am,
Faithfully yours
Herbert Spencer

C. S. Peirce Esq.
