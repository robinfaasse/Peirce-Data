Models trained on 914 dated manuscripts; scores are from grouped 5-fold cross-validation. Years written in the text are masked before prediction. Estimates are clipped to 1850-1914. Ranges are cross-conformal (MAPIE): the estimate plus or minus the 80% or 95% quantile of the out-of-fold errors. Code: github.com/robinfaasse/Peirce-Data.
